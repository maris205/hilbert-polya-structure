\section{Frozen object and determinant convention}

\begin{definition}[Coprime renewal suspension]
Let
\[
\Sigma_{\cop}=\left\{(n_k)_{k\in\mathbb Z}: n_k\in\I,\ 
\gcdop(n_k,n_{k+1})=1\text{ for every }k\right\}.
\]
The left shift is the dynamics and the roof is
\(\tau((n_k))=\log n_0\).
\end{definition}

The edge potential is split symmetrically.  On
\(\mathcal H=\elltwo(\I)\), with counting measure, define
\[
(L_s f)(m)=\sum_{\substack{n\in\I\\gcdop(m,n)=1}}
(mn)^{-s/2}f(n),\qquad \Re s>1.
\]
The real logarithm fixes the complex powers.  A cyclic word
\((n_0,\ldots,n_{k-1})\) has edge product
\[
\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}(n_jn_{j+1})^{-s/2}
=\left(\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}n_j\right)^{-s},
\qquad n_k=n_0.
\]
This identity fixes the clock, normalization, and determinant data type.

\begin{remark}[One ledger]
The only object used below is
\[
D_{\cop}(s)=\detF(I-L_s).
\]
The reciprocal, logarithmic derivative, a scattering determinant, and the
completed Riemann function are excluded from this stage.  Trace class alone
does not authorize a \(\lambda=1\) trace-log expansion unless a separate
spectral-radius or norm bound is proved.
\end{remark}
