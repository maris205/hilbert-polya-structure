# Paper plan

## Claim

The frozen COPRIME-0001 Fredholm determinant has a target-free punctured
scalar continuation through a squarefree-divisor Hilbert--Schmidt lift, but
the same scalar determinant has an unavoidable endpoint zero accumulation at
`s=1`.

## Proof spine

1. Freeze the original kernel, clock, normalization, and determinant.
2. Factor through squarefree divisor coordinates and apply Sylvester.
3. Prove the exact `S_2` Euler product and `det_2` continuation domain.
4. Add label one only as a min--max comparison object.
5. Diagonalize finite prime-coordinate rank-two kernels.
6. Force arbitrarily many crossings of eigenvalue one near `s=1`.
7. Convert crossings to a meromorphic-continuation obstruction.

## Explicit nonclaims

There is no prime-orbit law, von-Mangoldt trace formula, completed-ξ divisor,
root computation, quantization, Route B result, Hilbert--Pólya operator, or RH
claim.
