# Narrative report

The first COPRIME stage established a genuine countable recurrent object and a
Fredholm determinant only on `Re(s)>1`. The natural next question was whether
the scalar determinant could cross that operator boundary.

The answer is split. Möbius factorization moves the problem to squarefree
divisor coordinates, where the auxiliary matrix is Hilbert--Schmidt already
for `Re(s)>1/2`; its 2-regularized determinant gives a scalar continuation on
the punctured half-plane. This does not extend the original `ell^2` operator.

At the missing point, a temporary label-one completion has rank-two local
prime-coordinate blocks. Their positive products grow without bound as
`s downarrow 1`. Removing label one costs one dimension, so every fixed
positive eigenvalue index of the frozen candidate eventually crosses one.
At `s=3` all eigenvalues have modulus below one. Spectral continuity therefore
forces infinitely many determinant zeros approaching `s=1`.

This is a reusable structural obstruction, not a numerical root result. The
candidate is parked under `STOP_SCOPED`; a future reopening must choose a new
source-locked function space or determinant convention explicitly.
