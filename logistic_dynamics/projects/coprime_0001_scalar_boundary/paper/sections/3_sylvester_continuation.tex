\section{Squarefree-divisor continuation}

Let \(\Sscr\) be the squarefree positive integers and define
\[
 V_s(m,d)=\mathbf 1_{d\mid m}m^{-s/2},
 \qquad M(d,d)=\mu(d).
\]
On \(\Re s>1\),
\[
 L_s=V_s M V_s^{\mathsf T}.
\]
The Gram matrix is
\[
 (V_s^{\mathsf T}V_s)_{de}
 =\zeta(s)[d,e]^{-s}-\delta_{d=e=1}.
\]
Therefore, with \(P_1=e_1\otimes e_1\),
\[
 C_s:=V_s^{\mathsf T}V_sM=\zeta(s)T_s-P_1,
 \qquad (T_s)_{de}=\mu(e)[d,e]^{-s}.
\]
Sylvester's identity gives
\[
 \detF(I-L_s)=\detF(I-C_s),\qquad \Re s>1.
\]

Put \(H_s(d,e)=[d,e]^{-s}\).  Squarefree Euler factorization yields
\[
 \|H_s\|_{S_2}^2
 =\sum_{d,e\in\Sscr}[d,e]^{-2\Re s}
 =\prod_p(1+3p^{-2\Re s}).
\]
Thus \(T_s=H_sM\) is a locally holomorphic Hilbert--Schmidt family for
\(\Re s>1/2\).  On
\[
 \Omega=\{s\in\C:\Re s>1/2,\ s\ne1\}
\]
define
\[
 \widetilde D(s)=\dettwo(I-C_s).
\]
For \(\Re s>1\), \(C_s\) is trace class and
\[
 \Tr C_s=\zeta(s)\sum_{d\in\Sscr}\mu(d)d^{-s}-1=0.
\]
Since \(\dettwo(I-C)=\detF(I-C)e^{\Tr C}\), we obtain
\[
 \widetilde D(s)=D_{\cop}(s),\qquad \Re s>1.
\]

\begin{remark}
The continuation is a scalar identity.  It does not say that the original
matrix \(L_s\) is bounded, compact, or trace class below its defining
half-plane.  The \(\dettwo\) representation is kept as a separately named
ledger throughout.
\end{remark}
