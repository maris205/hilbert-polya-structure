\begin{abstract}
We continue the target-free audit of the countable coprime renewal
suspension.  The frozen transfer kernel is
\[
 (L_s)_{mn}=\mathbf 1_{(m,n)=1}(mn)^{-s/2},\qquad m,n\ge2,
\]
with determinant \(D_{\cop}(s)=\detF(I-L_s)\) initially defined on
\(\Re s>1\).  A squarefree-divisor Sylvester lift produces an explicit
Hilbert--Schmidt family \(C_s=\zeta(s)T_s-P_1\), so
\(\dettwo(I-C_s)\) is a scalar continuation on
\(\{\Re s>1/2\}\setminus\{1\}\) and agrees with \(D_{\cop}\) on its
defining half-plane.  A separate min--max argument, using a temporary
label-one completion, proves that infinitely many positive real zeros of
\(D_{\cop}\) approach \(s=1\).  Hence no holomorphic or meromorphic germ of
the same scalar determinant crosses that endpoint.  No zeros are computed,
and no prime or Riemann-zero data enter the construction.
\end{abstract}
