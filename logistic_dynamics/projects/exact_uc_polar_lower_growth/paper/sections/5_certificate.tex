The certificate checks that the algebraic root lies in its sealed bracket,
\(0<\alpha_0<1\), \(2\alpha_0^2<1\), \(2>\sigma_*\), every logarithm has a
positive argument, and the final outward lower endpoint exceeds \(0.0213\).
Its data firewall forbids prime tables, primality predicates, Riemann-zero
tables, zeta or xi evaluation, USTC data, parameter fitting, Fredholm
evaluation, and root searches.

The source, regression, and artifact are synchronized as one provenance unit
under \path{src/}, \path{tests/}, and
\path{artifacts/log_0001_lower_growth/}.  The scalar
program certifies only the numerical inequalities used after the analytic
trace argument; it cannot certify termwise positivity or the Cauchy step by
sampling.
