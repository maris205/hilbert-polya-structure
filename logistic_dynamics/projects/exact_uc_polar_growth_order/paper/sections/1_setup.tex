\section{Frozen determinant and main result}
Let \(u=U_c\) be the real root of
\[
 u^3-2u^2+2u-2=0,
 \qquad \rho=u-1.
\]
The underlying real system is the inherited two-full-branch polar map on
\(I_L=[-\pi/2,0]\) and \(I_R=[0,\pi/2]\). Its inverse branches
\(\phi_L,\phi_R\) extend holomorphically to the fixed radius-\(10^{-3}\)
stadiums \(U_L,U_R\). They share a holomorphic logarithm
\(\ell=\Log a\), with \(\phi_L'=a\) and \(\phi_R'=-a\).

On
\[
 X=A(U_L)\oplus A(U_R),
 \qquad
 B=\{(v_L,v_R):v_L(0)=v_R(0)\},
\]
the frozen transfer family is
\[
 (\LL_s v)_j(z)=\ee^{s\ell(z)}
 \bigl[v_L(\phi_L(z))+v_R(\phi_R(z))\bigr].
 \tag{1}
\]
Both outputs of (1) are restrictions of one holomorphic expression, so
\(\LL_s(B)\subset B\). The preceding analytic stage establishes that
\(\LL_{s,B}:=\LL_s|_B\) is nuclear of order zero and that
\[
 \Dpol(s):=\Det(I-\LL_{s,B})
 \tag{2}
\]
is entire. Equation (2) is retained throughout; neither the operator nor the
determinant convention is replaced.

\begin{theorem}[Growth, counting, and zero-free half-plane]
There exist constants \(C_0,C_1<\infty\), depending only on the frozen
stadiums and \(\ell\), such that
\[
 |\Dpol(s)|\le
 \exp\!\bigl(C_0+C_1(1+|s|)^2\bigr)
 \qquad(s\in\C).
 \tag{3}
\]
Consequently \(\ord(\Dpol)\le2\). Its zero count, with multiplicity,
satisfies
\[
 \Nzero(2;R)=O(R^2)
 \tag{4}
\]
in disks \(|s-2|\le R\), and for every fixed real interval \([a,b]\),
\[
 \Nzero([a,b];T)=O_{a,b}(T^2)
 \tag{5}
\]
in \(a\le\Re s\le b\), \(|\Im s|\le T\). Finally, \(\Dpol\) has no
zeros in
\[
\Re s>\sigma_*:=\frac{\log2}{\log(4/U_c^2)}
 =1.3382657903899534\ldots.
 \tag{6}
\]
\end{theorem}

The theorem gives only upper information. In particular,
\(\ord(\Dpol)\le2\) is not an equality assertion, and (4)--(5) are not
sharp counting asymptotics.
