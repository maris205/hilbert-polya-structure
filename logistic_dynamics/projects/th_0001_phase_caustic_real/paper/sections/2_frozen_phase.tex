\section{Frozen phase and caustic}

Use the fixed generating function

\[
S_a(x,y)=xy-x+\frac a3x^3
\]

with ordered phase

\[
\Phi=S_{1/2}(q_0,q_1)+S_{3/2}(q_1,q_2)+S_{5/2}(q_2,q_3).
\]

Differentiating in ((q_1,q_2)) gives

\[
E_1=q_0+\frac32q_1^2+q_2-1,
\qquad
E_2=q_1+\frac52q_2^2+q_3-1.
\]

The internal Hessian is

\[
\Hint=\begin{pmatrix}3q_1&1\\1&5q_2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det\Hint=15q_1q_2-1.
\]
