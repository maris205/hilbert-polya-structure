\section{Exact on-shell incidence}

Set (t=q_1\ne0) and impose (det\Hint=0).  Then

\[
q_2=\frac1{15t},\qquad
q_0=1-\frac32t^2-\frac1{15t},\qquad
q_3=1-t-\frac1{90t^2}.
\]

Conversely, substituting these expressions into (E_1,E_2) and the caustic
equation gives zero identically.  Thus the full real nonzero-(t) caustic is
stationary.  The endpoint projection of the stationary Lagrangian is

\[
(q_1,q_2)\longmapsto
\left(1-\frac32q_1^2-q_2,\,1-q_1-\frac52q_2^2\right),
\]

and its Jacobian is

\[
\frac{\partial(q_0,q_3)}{\partial(q_1,q_2)}
=-\Hint.
\]

Hence the same equation is the genuine singular set of the endpoint
projection.
