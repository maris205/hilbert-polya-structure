\section{Rational rank-one witness}

Choose \(t=1\) in the exact parameterization.  The endpoint and internal
coordinates are
\[
 (q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)=\left(-\frac{17}{30},1,\frac1{15},-\frac1{90}\right).
\]
Using \(p_{j+1}=q_j\) and the first component of \(F_a\), the corresponding
momenta are
\[
 (p_0,p_1,p_2,p_3)=\left(-\frac{289}{1800},-\frac{17}{30},1,\frac1{15}\right).
\]
For the three parameters in their frozen order, direct substitution gives
\[
 F_{1/2}(q_0,p_0)=(q_1,p_1),\qquad
 F_{3/2}(q_1,p_1)=(q_2,p_2),\qquad
 F_{5/2}(q_2,p_2)=(q_3,p_3),
\]
with all six coordinate residuals equal to zero in exact arithmetic.

At this point
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&1/3\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \operatorname{rank} H_{\mathrm{int}}=1.
\]
The vector \(v=(-1,3)^T\) spans its nullspace.  Since the only nonzero pure
third derivatives of the phase in the internal variables are
\(\partial_{q_1}^3\Phi=3\) and \(\partial_{q_2}^3\Phi=5\),
\[
 D_v^3\Phi=3(-1)^3+5(3)^3=132\ne0.
\]
The witness is thus a regular rank-one caustic in the frozen chart.  This is
an exact algebraic certificate, not a floating-point near-singularity.
