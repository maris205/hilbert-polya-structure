\section{Frozen ordered Fourier-integral object}

For a real parameter \(a\), fix the generating function
\[
 S_a(x,y)=xy-x+\frac{a}{3}x^3.
\]
The associated canonical kick is
\[
 F_a(q,p)=\bigl(1-aq^2-p,\ q\bigr).
\]
The clock is one ordered superstep
\[
 G=F_{5/2}\circ F_{3/2}\circ F_{1/2},
\]
with exactly three factors.  In the iterated kernel, \(q_0\) and \(q_3\) are
endpoints and \((q_1,q_2)\) are the internal variables.  The frozen phase is
\[
 \Phi(q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)
 =S_{1/2}(q_0,q_1)+S_{3/2}(q_1,q_2)+S_{5/2}(q_2,q_3).
\]

Differentiation in the internal coordinates gives
\[
 E_1=\partial_{q_1}\Phi
 =q_0+\frac32q_1^2+q_2-1,
 \qquad
 E_2=\partial_{q_2}\Phi
 =q_1+\frac52q_2^2+q_3-1.
\]
The internal Hessian is
\[
 H_{\mathrm{int}}
 =\Hess_{(q_1,q_2)}\Phi
 =\begin{pmatrix}3q_1&1\\1&5q_2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \det H_{\mathrm{int}}=15q_1q_2-1.
\]

The source lock fixes \(\hbar=1\), phase convention
\(\exp(+iS_a)\), positive-real factor amplitude \((2\pi)^{-1/2}\), and global
phase \(1\).  The factorized operator acts on \(L^2(\R,\dd q)\); its global
Maslov index is intentionally not assigned.  This stage has no determinant
convention: neither a zeta function nor a spectral zero ledger is defined.

\begin{remark}
The factorization is the object.  Replacing it by a single reduced phase would
require a nondegenerate internal stationary projection on the region being
used.  The next section tests that requirement on-shell.
\end{remark}
