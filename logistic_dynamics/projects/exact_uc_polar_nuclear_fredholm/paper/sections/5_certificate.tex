one through eight, for a total of
\[

for an all-order theorem; the all-order statements above come from the
analytic factorization and fixed-point trace theorem. It uses only the
certified \(U_c\) bracket and inherited branch formulas. In particular, it
contains no prime table, Riemann-zero table, \(\zeta\) or \(\xi\) evaluation,
or Fredholm-zero calculation.
