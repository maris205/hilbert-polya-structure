\section{Quantitative period bounds}
\label{app:period-bounds}

Let \(q(t)\) be a nonconstant \(T\)-periodic solution of
\begin{equation}
 q''=-\nabla V(q)
 \label{eq:newton-equation}
\end{equation}
contained in a convex set on which \(\nabla V\) is \(L\)-Lipschitz.  Write
\(\bar q=T^{-1}\int_0^Tq(t)\dd t\).  Periodic integration by parts gives
\begin{align}
 \int_0^T|q'|^2\dd t
 &=\int_0^T(q-\bar q)\cdot\nabla V(q)\dd t\\
 &=\int_0^T(q-\bar q)\cdot
 [\nabla V(q)-\nabla V(\bar q)]\dd t\\
 &\le L\int_0^T|q-\bar q|^2\dd t.
 \label{eq:lipschitz-energy}
\end{align}
The periodic Wirtinger inequality yields
\begin{equation}
 \int_0^T|q-\bar q|^2\dd t
 \le\left(\frac{T}{2\pi}\right)^2
 \int_0^T|q'|^2\dd t.
 \label{eq:wirtinger}
\end{equation}
Cancelling the nonzero kinetic integral proves
\begin{equation}
 T\ge\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt L}.
 \label{eq:universal-period-bound}
\end{equation}

For \(V_0(q)=2\pi e^{\pi|q|^2}\), direct differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \|\nabla^2V_0(q)\|_{\rm op}
 \le E\left(2\pi+4\pi\log\frac{E}{2\pi}\right).
 \label{eq:radial-hessian-bound}
\end{equation}
Using \(\log(1+x)\le x\) through \(E=2\pi+0.010201\) proves
\cref{eq:radial-explicit-bound}.

For the warped potential, the energy equation and the exact inverse
\(\Psi_a^{-1}(u,v)=(v,-c_av-av^2-u)\) enclose every allowed configuration
in
\begin{equation}
 |x|<0.02274,
 \qquad |y|<0.042427.
 \label{eq:warped-config-box}
\end{equation}
Outward rational estimates for \(D\Psi_a\), \(\Psi_a\), and the exponential
factor give
\begin{equation}
 \|\nabla^2V_a\|_{\rm op}<102.494<103.
 \label{eq:warped-hessian-bound}
\end{equation}
The box is convex, so \cref{eq:universal-period-bound} proves
\cref{eq:warped-period-floor}.  The result is only a period floor; it does
not count returns above the floor.
