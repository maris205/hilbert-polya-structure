# Refinement Report

## Starting idea

The initial direction was to pursue a Hénon-based Hilbert--Pólya candidate,
possibly by adding spin, finite-field arithmetic, or random-matrix symmetry
transitions. The broad ambition was useful, but it mixed three logically
different tasks:

- constructing a real self-adjoint spectrum;
- reproducing a mean zero count;
- deriving prime-power arithmetic.

## Refinement decisions

1. **Freeze one explicit operator.** A static confining Schrödinger family is
   used instead of an unspecified map quantization.
2. **Separate the gates.** Q/W/S/P/Z prevent a mean law or an RMT statistic
   from being treated as a prime mechanism.
3. **Preserve the clock analytically.** Area-preserving Hénon warps and
   momentum-fiber translations leave the classical comparison exact.
4. **Prove only two growing quantum terms.** The safe remainder is retained;
   no quantum constant is claimed.
5. **Test the flow, not the map.** The static Hamiltonian flow receives
   direct FTLE/SALI controls.
6. **Use magnetic coupling only as a symmetry deformation.** The scalar
   family is orthogonal-to-unitary, not a spin/GSE realization.
7. **Audit the relative spectrum.** A signed spectral container is rigorous,
   but the ordinary first-resolvent route is disfavored and the arithmetic
   trace is open.

## Resulting paper identity

Paper 7 is a mathematical-physics theory-and-computation article about an
iso-Weyl family, not a numerical zero-fitting paper. Its strongest result is
the fixed-iterate, fixed-field two-growing-term Weyl theorem. Classical and
quantum calculations show that the clock-preserving deformations are active.
The failure to obtain \(r\log p\) periods is promoted to the central open
bridge rather than hidden.

## How the RH/TPC discussion changed the workflow

The project now uses breadth-first discovery for RH:

\[
\text{generate}\to\text{screen}\to\text{replicate}\to
\text{prove the smallest bridge}.
\]

This differs from theorem engineering for a nearly complete conditional
route. Candidate deaths, failed grids, stiff high-distortion branches, and
unsupported trace-class assumptions are retained as research output.
