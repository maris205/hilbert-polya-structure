\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For an explicit radial/H\'enon pair with the same exact phase-volume clock,
we have isolated a nonzero-time spectral difference.  The analytic result is
an eigenvalue-only relative Gutzwiller term from one primitive fast Lyapunov
orbit at every fixed sufficiently small positive energy excess.  The phase,
normalization, action, and stability factor are explicit, and the removal of
the microlocal observable follows from a complete-shell short-return
theorem.

A separate computer-assisted theorem makes the distinguished branch
quantitative.  Validated flow and Krawczyk enclosures, guarded bridge boxes,
and exact-rational replay give one connected primitive local branch over
\(0\le\epsilon\le0.101\).  An invariant-quotient argument and validated
monodromy enclosures prove \(\det(I-D\Pi_\epsilon)>3\) on that branch.
As a bounded fourth result, six validated complement trees close on three
representative parameter slabs at both precisions, with 89,962 independent
exact-decimal checks and no unresolved leaf.  This implementation certificate
does not supply the all-slab complement theorem.
The representative spectral computation at \(\delta=0.01\) is a separate
numerical evidence layer: it tests the coefficient but does not establish that the
energy lies in the current analytic theorem domain.

Two next steps are concrete.  First, an all-slab complement, phase, and
global-shell cover should quantify \(\delta_{\rm tr}\) and connect the
analytic and validated domains.  Second, any Hilbert--P\'olya continuation
must search for an endogenous arithmetic carrier at fixed physical
\(\hbar\), without loading prime times or zeta zeros into the construction.
The present paper supplies a rigorously controlled local trace component for
that search; it does not supply the arithmetic bridge.
