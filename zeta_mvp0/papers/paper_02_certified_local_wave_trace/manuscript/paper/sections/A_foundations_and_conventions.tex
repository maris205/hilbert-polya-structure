\section{Functional calculus and Fourier conventions}
\label{app:foundations}

This appendix records details used in \cref{sec:model-trace,sec:analytic-trace}.
They make clear which relative objects require trace-class comparison and
which do not.

\subsection{Compact resolvent}

The closed quadratic form associated with \cref{eq:semiclassical-operator}
is
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak p_{a,\h}[u]
 =\frac{\h^2}{2}\|\nabla u\|_2^2
 +\int_{\R^2}V_a(q)|u(q)|^2\dd q.
 \label{eq:quadratic-form}
\end{equation}
It is densely defined and lower bounded.  If \((u_n)\) is bounded in its
form norm, then for any \(M>0\),
\begin{equation}
 \int_{\{V_a>M\}}|u_n|^2
 \le \frac{1}{M}\int_{\R^2}V_a|u_n|^2.
 \label{eq:tail-compactness}
\end{equation}
Choose \(M\) so that the right side is uniformly small.  The sublevel
\(\{V_a\le M\}\) is compact by properness, and Rellich compactness applies
there.  A diagonal argument gives a convergent subsequence in
\(L^2(\R^2)\), proving compact resolvent for the Friedrichs realization.

\subsection{Finite-rank cutoff identities}

For \(f\in C_c^\infty(\R)\), spectral calculus gives
\begin{equation}
 f(P_{a,\h})=
 \sum_k f(\lambda_{a,k}(\h))
 |\psi_{a,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{a,k}|.
 \label{eq:spectral-calculus}
\end{equation}
Only finitely many summands are nonzero.  Hence every trace in
\cref{eq:relative-wave,eq:relative-density} exists separately.  We never
deduce trace class of
\((P_{a,\h}-z)^{-1}-(P_{0,\h}-z)^{-1}\), and no such statement is needed.

If \(N_j\) counts eigenvalues with multiplicity, then
\begin{equation}
 \Tr f(P_j)=\int_\R f(E)\dd N_j(E).
 \label{eq:stieltjes-spectral}
\end{equation}
Subtracting, using \(\xi=N_0-N_a\), and integrating by parts proves
\begin{equation}
 \Tr[f(P_a)-f(P_0)]=\int_\R f'(E)\xi(E)\dd E.
 \label{eq:compact-relative-identity}
\end{equation}
Compact support removes all endpoint terms.

\subsection{Fourier factors and orientation}

Substituting \cref{eq:fourier-convention} into each spectral summand gives
\begin{equation}
 g\!\left(\frac{E-P}{\h}\right)
 =\frac{1}{2\pi}\int
 e^{it(E-P)/\h}\widehat g(t)\dd t,
 \label{eq:functional-fourier}
\end{equation}
which proves \cref{eq:density-wave}.  A positive-time cutoff returns the
complex coefficient in \cref{eq:main-trace-formula}.  For a real test with
symmetric positive and negative time supports, the negative-time orbit
contributes the complex conjugate.

Let \(\theta_0=2\pi/\rho_a\in(0,2\pi)\).  Abel regularization gives
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{r\uparrow1}\sum_{m=0}^\infty
 r^m e^{-i\theta_0(m+1/2)}
 =\frac{e^{-i\theta_0/2}}{1-e^{-i\theta_0}}
 =-\frac{i}{2\sin(\theta_0/2)}.
 \label{eq:abel-transverse}
\end{equation}
Poisson summation of the fast quantum number at its first positive recurrence
contributes \(-1/\omega_+\).  Since
\(2\sin(\theta_0/2)=\sqrt{D_+^0}>0\), the combined phase is \(+i\), and
\(1/\omega_+=T_+^0/(2\pi)\).  This locks the normalization used throughout.

\subsection{Support-localized stationary phase}

After coherent-state insertion, the trace phase is stationary only at pairs
\((z,t)\) satisfying
\begin{equation}
 h(z)=E,
 \qquad \Phi^t(z)=z,
 \qquad t\in\supp\widehat g.
 \label{eq:stationary-set}
\end{equation}
When \(\supp\widehat g\subset[-T_0,T_0]\), no fixed point outside that time
interval occurs in the integral.  Compactness of the energy band makes the
remaining stationary analysis local in phase space.  The short-return
classification in \cref{thm:whole-shell} therefore suffices; no assertion
about longer trajectories is silently imported.
