# Hilbert--Pólya Candidate Portfolio

**Research mode:** breadth-first structural discovery followed by narrow
bridge closure.  This prospective portfolio was created on 2026-08-05 and
operationalizes `RH_DISCOVERY_PROTOCOL.md`; it is not a preregistered record
of the historical selection of the current Hénon family.
The purpose is not to make every candidate publishable; it is to make each
candidate cheap to kill and hard to overclaim.

## Immutable target

The programme seeks a fixed mathematical object that realizes as many of the
following requirements as possible without loading a table of primes or zeta
zeros:

1. **Q:** a specified self-adjoint operator (or an honestly labelled relative
   or resonance analogue);
2. **W:** the two growing Riemann--von Mangoldt counting terms analytically;
3. **S:** active dynamics rather than a coordinate relabelling;
4. **P:** endogenous prime-power times and von-Mangoldt-type weights;
5. **Z:** a signed spectral fluctuation capable of carrying the explicit
   formula.

R (finite-window random-matrix/symmetry response) and C (relative-container
admissibility) are recorded as side diagnostics, not as Hilbert--Pólya gates.

A candidate may be valuable after passing only a strict subset of the gates,
but no collection of early passes substitutes for P.

## Promotion policy

The current Hénon exponential family supports a Paper-7 manuscript because Q
and W are analytic and S survives frozen controls.  This is a retrospective
scope judgment, not a claim that the prospective ladder selected the family.
It should not consume all future search effort.  Work proceeds in three queues:

- **exploration queue:** candidates 2--7 receive cheap algebraic and pilot
  screens in parallel;
- **proof queue:** at most two bridge problems are active, currently the
  relative-trace interface and a fixed multiscale operator;
- **replication queue:** only one expensive spectral calculation is active at
  a time and must have an independent numerical family before promotion.

The AI's role differs across the queues.  In exploration it acts as a
structure generator and ruthless search engine.  In the proof queue it acts
as a theorem engineer: write the shortest conditional chain, expose the
minimal missing lemma, and reject circular bridges.  Evidence labels never
move between queues without an explicit promotion record.

## Immediate sequence after Paper 7

1. Freeze and run R200 without primes or zeros.
2. In parallel, generate five mathematically distinct fixed multiscale
   operators and kill any whose W law is not analytic.
3. Promote at most one survivor to a theorem package.
4. Authorize a held-out zero comparison only after an endogenous P mechanism
   has been written and independently audited.

This ordering preserves the exploratory advantage of RH research while
preventing a numerically attractive object from quietly changing the target.
