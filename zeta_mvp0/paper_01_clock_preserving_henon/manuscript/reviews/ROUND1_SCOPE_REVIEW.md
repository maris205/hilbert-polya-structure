## Critical issue

The draft described the breadth-first protocol as the historical mechanism
that selected Paper 7, but no contemporaneous candidate registry or death log
exists and an early idea report required a positive-measure chaos result that
was never obtained.  A protocol formalized after exploration cannot be
presented as a preregistration.

**Required action:** either produce contemporaneous immutable records or state
that the protocol is prospective, was formalized after parts of the family
were explored, and is not a preregistered selection history.

## Major issues

1. **Z-gate drift.**  GOE/GUE and magnetic symmetry diagnostics had been
   renamed “symmetry-level Z,” contrary to the formal definition of Z as an
   explicit-formula/zero fluctuation.  Introduce side labels R for
   random-matrix/symmetry diagnostics and C for relative-container
   admissibility; leave P open and Z untested.
2. **Ledger identifiers.**  The W1--W8 numbering mixed self-adjointness,
   Weyl results, dynamics, RMT, relative spectra, primes, zeros, and RH.
   Rename by logical layer.
3. **Blinding provenance.**  The current code loads no prime or zero arrays,
   but (a=1.02) comes from an earlier RH-motivated, zero-exposed programme.
   Call the current calculation zero-input and prior-frozen, not statistically
   blinded.
4. **Quantum constant.**  The main theorem correctly omitted the classical
   (+1), but supporting files retained displays that could imply the
   constant had been resolved.  Synchronize all authoritative formulas.
5. **Relative-container overinterpretation.**  Heat and higher-resolvent
   differences are trace class because the individual operators are
   summable; this is admissibility, not cancellation, closeness, or
   arithmetic content.  Distinguish Yafaev's weighted SSF (up to a constant)
   from the canonically normalized pure-point staircase.
6. **Title overclaim.**  “Classical Chaos” exceeded the finite-state,
   finite-time evidence.  Use “finite-window diagnostics” or “numerical chaos
   diagnostics.”
7. **Prime-time scaling.**  The natural-time argument was a dimensional
   warning, not a proved uniform period obstruction.  Remove “precise
   obstruction” language.
