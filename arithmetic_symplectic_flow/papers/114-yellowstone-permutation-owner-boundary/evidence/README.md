# Evidence boundary

- [Applegate et al., *The Yellowstone Permutation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.01669): defines the least-unused gcd/coprimality rule, proves the sequence is a permutation of positive integers, and reports finite cycles of the resulting permutation, including the cycle with minimum 6.
- [Prior-work guide](../../../docs/prior_work/README.md): requires a specific prime-symbolic observable and a justified sequence-to-Hénon/conservative bridge. Gcd/coprimality alone is not that lineage.
- [063 monotone-elimination rigidity](../../063-monotone-elimination-periodic-rigidity/paper.md) is a related method boundary but not used as this proof: Yellowstone's monotone observable is its used-history set, not an eliminated sieve set.

Method: definition and owner audit only. No numerical reconstruction of the sequence, prime-table search, cycle enumeration, roof assignment, geometric lift, determinant, or Route evaluation was performed.
