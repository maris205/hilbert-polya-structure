## 2. D HARD-OFF: exact branches, predecessors and terminals

The full carrier C=[0,infinity)^2 retains both axes and the origin. E is defined
only in the interior: E(a,b)=(a-b,b) when a>=b>0, and E(a,b)=(a,b-a) when b>a>0.
The equality case belongs to the first branch and maps (b,b) to (0,b).
Solving these two displayed affine equations gives respectively

    L(u,v)=(u+v,v),  domain u>=0,v>0;
    R(u,v)=(u,u+v),  domain u>0,v>0.

For L, the original first-branch inequality becomes u>=0 and b=v>0.
For R, the strict inequality becomes v>0 and a=u>0. Substitution recovers the
target in each case. Every interior source lies in exactly one original branch, so
these are all predecessors; no third boundary branch is suppressed.

An interior target has both predecessors. A target (0,v),v>0 has only (v,v).
A target (u,0),u>0, and the origin have none. Every axis point is a forward
terminal, but the positive vertical axis is not an incoming-free boundary.
All maps and their actual domains are Borel; no smooth global inverse or
etaleness is asserted across the cut. There is no measure or time prescription.

Complete arrows with source r can be enumerated without a bounded cutoff:
from r, take each legal E^n r and apply any finite word v of L/R whose EVERY
intermediate inverse domain is satisfied. If v has length m and output q,
retain the arrow (q,m-n,r), outgoing from r. Its inverse (r,n-m,q) is incoming
to r; taking all these inverses gives every incoming arrow. The enumeration is complete because
each forward step reverses by one of the two domain-checked branches just proved.
Duplicates are identified only as equal endpoint/lag triples, not as free words.
At a horizontal terminal or the origin only the identity arrow remains; at a
vertical terminal all the source-checked ancestor histories remain.

## 3. Actual retained-lag groupoids and D source isotropy

For D the frozen arrows are (z,m-n,w) with E^m z=E^n w and both histories legal;
source is w and range z. The inverse swaps endpoints and negates the lag.
For composable arrows (z,m-n,w) and (w,p-q,u), compare n and p at their
common intermediate source. If p>=n, extend the first equality by p-n existing
steps to obtain E^(m+p-n) z=E^q u. If n>=p, extend the second by n-p steps to
obtain E^m z=E^(q+n-p) u. The longer intermediate history already licenses
each extension, even when its last point is terminal. Thus composition has
the summed lag, and equality of triples makes it witness-independent and
associative. No illegal continuation past a terminal was introduced.

The same argument applies to any of the three deterministic partial maps.
Domains of finite histories are Borel; equality of their endpoint maps is
Borel, giving the specified groupoids their inherited Borel structures.
The source-fibre enumeration makes those fibres countable, as are their inverses. These are not
free path or germ groupoids, and an identity is not an artificial stationary step.

For every actual E-step, the function s(a,b)=a+b decreases strictly:
s(E(a,b))=s(a,b)-min(a,b), with min(a,b)>0 on the domain. If E^m q=E^n q
with m>n, this strict decrease along the legal segment contradicts equality.
Hence every D isotropy group is {0}: the sole isotropy arrow is (q,0,q).

## 4. S GEOMETRY-OFF: full binary boundary and periodic ledger

For x in {0,1}^Z a gap is the positive coordinate difference between consecutive
ones. X_gap contains exactly those x whose distinct gap VALUES never divide
one another. Equal gaps are allowed. An illegal pair is witnessed inside a
finite coordinate window, so X_gap is closed. Translation preserves consecutive
gaps, hence sigma and sigma^-1 are whole mutually inverse maps of X_gap.
No finite/infinite support, null point or representative is removed; no measure
is assigned, so no atom or typical-state status is inferred.

Use the same lag orientation as section3: (z,k,w) satisfies sigma^m z=sigma^n w,
k=m-n, equivalently z=sigma^(-k)w. These are all arrows outgoing from w;
their inverses are all arrows incoming to w. Labels are retained, and the lag kernel consists of units.
Source isotropy is {0} for a nonperiodic full word, or pZ for a globally periodic
word of least positive binary period p. Indeed any nonzero isotropy is exactly
a global shift period; division by the least positive period proves the claim.
Eventual periodicity of one or both tails alone is not global periodicity.

Allzero and allone are legal and have isotropy Z. The alternating states are
legal and have isotropy2Z. Nonempty finite supports have no nonzero shift
period, since translation would move their least occupied position. These
states remain in the same full source, without selecting only periodic words.
For any nonzero periodic word, list its cyclic gaps INCLUDING the wrap-around
gap. It belongs to X_gap exactly when the distinct cyclic values are mutually
incomparable. Its least binary period is the SUM of a least repeating cyclic
gap block, not the number of gaps: every translation period must send a one
to a one and preserve the ordered gap pattern, and conversely such a block
translation repeats the binary configuration. Allzero is the separate period1 case.
These are complete source-period conventions, not lengths of physical orbits.
