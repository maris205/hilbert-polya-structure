## Candidate and lineage

This candidate is a direct deformation/lift of the prior-work sequence, not a
generic arithmetic insertion. Papers 1–2 supply a prime-sieve symbolic
proposal and its one-dimensional admissibility failures; Paper 3 gives a
mathematical sequential framework; Papers 4–5 motivate non-autonomous
quadratic dynamics and its area-preserving Hénon lift. The exact object is
frozen in [candidate-card.md](candidate-card.md).

The cotangent-lift construction makes the non-autonomous Hénon skew product
into one autonomous symplectic map. This establishes only geometric ownership:
it does not prove that the earlier prime-symbolic comparison is an endogenous
arithmetic source.

## A0 audit result: scoped fail

The frozen candidate has no defined arithmetic observable. Its allowed data are
only \(u_c,c\), the scalar function \(a(q)\), and the map; neither the card nor
the map defines a predicate, coding, orbit statistic, or derivation whose
output distinguishes primes from composites. Prior prime-density motivation is
provenance, not a mechanism internal to this map.

Three adverse controls close the audit without fitted numerical comparison:

- **Label-blindness:** the formula and all orbits are unchanged if external
  validation labels are replaced by composites, shuffled primes, or random
  integers, because none enters the object.
- **Nearby-envelope control:** any comparably decaying nonarithmetic function
  gives the same kind of sequential Hénon cotangent lift; no map invariant
  selects the displayed envelope.
- **Simpler-parent control:** a constant parameter removes the density-shaped
  drift while retaining the same cotangent-lift geometry; the proposed content
  therefore resides only in an externally motivated coefficient schedule.

Absence of an inserted prime table is not positive A0 evidence. With no
endogenous derivation path, A0 is a `SCOPED FAIL`.

## Early A1 stop signal

For all \(n\ge1\), the \(q\)-coordinate of \(F^n\) is \(q+n\); hence \(F^n(z)\ne z\). The frozen base map has no periodic points and its unit-roof suspension has no closed flow orbits. This is a scoped A1 precheck failure, not a reason to change the clock within this candidate. It ensures the strict A0 audit is short and prevents expensive orbit work.

## Decision

`STOPPED`. This direct-lineage control shows that a smooth autonomous symplectic
lift can preserve the historical sequence of ideas while failing both
independent gates: no endogenous A0 mechanism and no periodic points. Any
recurrent-clock modification is a new object and cannot inherit credit.
