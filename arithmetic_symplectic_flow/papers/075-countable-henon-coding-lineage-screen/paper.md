## Question inherited from 046

Record 046 only excludes a standard finite-Markov Hénon horseshoe from having the
complete prime-gap language as its intrinsic code. It deliberately leaves open a
new nonuniform or countable-coded geometric construction. This screen asks whether
available countable-coding Hénon literature supplies that missing direct
prime-symbolic-to-geometric arrow.

## What the sources actually provide

Countable Markov shifts are a genuine symbolic class, and countable Markov
partitions are used for nonuniformly hyperbolic maps. The located Hénon-like
tower source explicitly works under a contraction-dominates-expansion condition
and says it naturally holds for Hénon-like maps with Jacobian less than one.
That is a dissipative condition, not the required area-preserving Hénon carrier.

Separately, the area-preserving Hénon literature gives symbolic codes for
rotational/elliptic and hyperbolic orbits of a selected map. Such a code is
intrinsic to that map after its parameter is chosen, but the source supplies no
Eratosthenes/prime-gap/sieve observable, no rule deriving its parameter from that
source, and no intertwiner with the prior prime-symbolic admissibility.

## Scope control

This is not a finite-type argument in disguise. A countable coding may have
periodic words and may be relevant to a future candidate. The stop is instead an
ownership failure: importing a countable graph from one model and declaring that
it encodes the sieve in another would recreate 012's external-language error.
Likewise, choosing an area-preserving parameter because it happens to exhibit a
desired code would be post-selection unless an explicit prime-symbolic mechanism
selects it.

## Decision

**Portfolio position: fork.** The nonfinite coding lane remains admissible, but
only with a concrete fixed area-preserving/symplectic map and a proved
prime-symbolic coding relation defined before parameter selection. Generic
countable-shift universality, a dissipative tower, or rotational labels on a
chosen Hénon map does not meet that threshold.
