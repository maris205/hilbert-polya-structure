**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-88`

**Route state:** `No classical P0 or formal Route-A coordinate; Route B NOT INVOKED`

## Question and lineage

Can the direct sieve-symbolic source be carried, without an external schedule,
through a substitution-tiling realization to a hyperbolic toral automorphism
and hence to a conservative/symplectic base map?

The proposed arrow is deliberately narrow:

```text
prime/composite sieve -> non-stationary S-adic substitutions
    -> [fixed substitution tiling realization?]
    -> hyperbolic toral automorphism / symplectic base.
```

This would be a genuine continuation of the prior-work symbolic-to-geometric
direction if the bracketed map were supplied for the same action.  The strongest
supported result is a scoped negative: the available realization theorem begins
with one fixed substitution, whereas the operational sieve source has a
prime-dependent directive sequence.  No equality, factor map, or intertwining
between them is supplied.

The geometric theorem is valuable because it identifies a possible route from
symbolic tilings to a conservative toral comparator. It does not itself select
a two-dimensional, determinant-one symplectic map, much less prove that one
owns the sieve action. Selecting a fixed substitution with a desired expansion
would turn the prime directive into a preselected parameter, while freezing
every directive level makes the schedule structural input. The latter is
precisely the owner split stopped in 049.

A focused 2026-09-14 source screen combining prime/Eratosthenes terminology
with substitution tilings and toral or hyperbolic realization located only
general tiling theory and studies where prime data enter as an external
statistical weight or orthogonality observable. It did not locate a source
defining the required action-level sieve-to-tiling map. This is a `NOT LOCATED`
record, not a theorem that such a map cannot exist.

that toral automorphisms have periodic points.  It rejects only the proposed
same-object transfer without a documented prime-sieve intertwiner.  It also
does not reuse a Hénon or cat-map determinant.

prime-sieve-derived substitution or tiling action satisfying the theorem's
