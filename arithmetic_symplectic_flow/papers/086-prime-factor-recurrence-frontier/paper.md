# Prime-factor recurrence frontier: three exact return types and the unresolved reversible-owner gap

**Paper ID:** 086-prime-factor-recurrence-frontier  
**Record ID:** ASFS-FRONTIER-20260914-09  
**Date / status:** 2026-09-14; PORTFOLIO FRONTIER — NO P0 ADMISSION  
**Route state:** Route A unassigned; Route B NOT INVOKED.

## Portfolio result

The latest direct-lineage screens use fixed arithmetic recurrences rather than
an externally supplied prime schedule. They have materially different exact
outcomes and are not alternative descriptions of one dynamical object.

| Object | Arithmetic/return fact | Decisive boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 083 least-odd-factor prime-Fibonacci | every positive forward trajectory terminates at a power-of-two boundary | no closed orbit exists on the faithful partial action |
| 078 GPF-Fibonacci | source theorem gives one prime-only four-cycle | discrete, noninjective carrier; no classical symplectic owner |
| 084 affine GPF | elementary descent gives the unique prime-only two-cycle | discrete, noninjective carrier; no roof or geometry |
| 085 GPF-Ducci vector rule | every prime diagonal is a fixed point | infinite prime-labelled fixed family, not an intrinsic selected packet |

The controls prove a useful narrow fact: prime-factor evaluation can be an
endogenous part of a fixed recurrence and can coexist with exact periodic data.
They do **not** prove an arithmetic clock, a prime-to-orbit correspondence, an
invertible base map, or a symplectic realization. In particular, borrowing
078's four-cycle, 084's descent proof, and a hypothetical geometric carrier
would violate the same-object invariant.

### Broader-family check

Back--Caragiu study the higher-order family

\[
p_j=\operatorname{gpf}(a_1p_{j-1}+\cdots+a_dp_{j-d}+a_0).
\]

Their order-two GPF-Fibonacci result is the same type of prime-only
four-cycle already retained in 078.  For the order-three GPF-Tribonacci
analogue, the reported cycle spectrum is computational and its universal
ultimate-periodicity statement is explicitly a conjecture.  This expands the
search coverage, but supplies neither a new proven complete packet ledger nor
an inverse, conservative deformation, or symplectic owner.  It therefore does
not open a distinct P0 branch or justify a coefficient/initial-data search.

## Search consequence

A new classical screen may be admitted only if it supplies, before long A1
work, all of the following within one construction:

1. a fixed, positive-dimensional invertible map or an explicitly reversible
   encoding whose inverse and phase-space owner are defined;
2. an arithmetic rule that is internal to the map and follows the stipulated
   prime-symbolic to sequential-deformation lineage, not a pre-restricted prime
   state set or an externally indexed factor table;
3. a proof that actual phase-space returns are neither terminal artifacts nor
   a freely indexed family of prime-labelled fixed states; and
4. a direct Hénon/conservative deformation or a stated obstruction to it.

This is a search filter, not a no-go theorem for all factor-based arithmetic
dynamics. It deliberately leaves open a genuinely nonlocal or reversible
mechanism.

## Decision

**Portfolio position: fork.** Stop expanding local componentwise
least/greatest-prime-factor recurrences as main candidates. Resume broad
search among reversible prime-symbolic mechanisms and reject them early if
their inverse, lineage, or all-orbit ownership is external.
