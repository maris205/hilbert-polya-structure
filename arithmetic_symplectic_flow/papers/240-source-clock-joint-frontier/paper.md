## Abstract

This bounded architecture search asks for arithmetic selection, intrinsic
returns and physical time to be tested together. Binary/sequential proposals
do not yet supply a new complete action beyond the nearest local controls.
Source-selective renewal proposals likewise do not yet replace their
engineered admissibility or inherited clock with a specific new source law.
These are no-admission decisions, not impossibility theorems. A third lane
supplies a separately frozen oscillator/continuum-field owner, DRB01 in
Paper 241. Its complete mild owner has exactly one primitive packet per
prime, no composite or stationary packet on energy 1, and actual periods
\(T_p=2\sqrt2\log p+O(1)\). It changes conservative return selection
but retains static divisor incidence. This engineering advance is not an
answer to the source-naturalness question and inherits no prior trace.

## 2. Question, method and claim boundary

The question is whether a changed rule can make desired prime packets
return, rule out unwanted packets and account for elapsed time in one
complete action. The preceding 238 and 239 illustrate distinct failures:
the former retains prime returns at linear-scale time, while the latter
loses all nonzero returns. Neither outcome is a universal obstruction.

Three separate read-only scouts examined proposal directions and nearest
collisions. Root compared the relevant local records and
decided admission. This was not a literature review, a numerical search,
an exhaustive architecture census or an external novelty assessment.
There are no new theorem claims for an unadmitted proposal. Definitions,
exact proofs and limitations of an admitted owner live in its own package.

## 3. Sequential and binary source lane — no admission

The explicit lineage arrow available here is proper-divisor exclusion
to binary witness blocks with a retained scan phase. That arrow already
appears in [143](../143-dyadic-batched-divisor-counter/candidate-card.md)
and its [144 conservative lift](../144-dyadic-counter-henon-lift/README.md).
They use blocks \(2^k\le d<2^{k+1}\) and a complete batch cycle of
\(K_n=\max(1,\lfloor\log_2(n-1)\rfloor)\) macrosteps.

Importantly, **144's macroclock belongs to its actual symplectic action**.
It is not rejected here for failing to be a clock. Its local record already
states the concrete limitations: prime \(p\) contributes \(p\) primitive
packets, composite packets remain, and direct processing still tests all
\(n-2\) proper-divisor candidates. Renaming the batches does not change
this owner or fix those packet limitations.

Two other proposals remain incomplete. A reversibly compiled prime
computation does not, by itself, specify a recurrent all-state action;
the local [093](../093-fractran-primegame-boundary/README.md) and
[099](../099-reversible-sieve-computation-boundary/README.md) records
identify that distinction. Binary acceleration of
[238](../238-nonconfining-source-frontier/candidate-card.md) changes its
transition and available phase ranges when the integer source changes.
This scout did not supply a new transition and inverse covering every
state. No packet or timing theorem for that missing action is asserted.

Finally, a matrix-scale recursion must differ from the exact owner already
audited in [232](../232-source-feedback-frontier/README.md). That package
has its own multiplier/clock obstruction; it is not a theorem about every
possible binary source.

**Decision: no admission / fork.** Reopen this lane with a complete changed
action and inverse, packet convention and same-action time accounting.
Word length, circuit depth and physical period must remain distinct.

## 4. Source-selective renewal lane — no admission

A shift-invariant rule that forces periodic cover-ratio words to be
constant is not yet a new source mechanism. Two exact designs already
exist: [163](../163-ordered-cover-scale-suspension/README.md) uses weak
ordering, whereas [220](../220-finite-defect-cover-flow/README.md) uses
finite total variation and retains nonmonotone finite defects. Their
different carrier rules have positive owned packet results. The unfilled
obligation is why the source selects such a rule and its scale law, not
whether their established flow definitions are valid.

Actual arithmetic updates also exist locally. The
[GPF recurrence control](../078-gpf-fibonacci-a0-a1-control/README.md)
and [affine-GPF natural-path owner](../087-affine-gpf-path-groupoid-flow/README.md)
evaluate arithmetic dynamically, but their recorded recurrent cores are
single small packets. Their separate log-output-clock constructions
[081](../081-gpf-log-roof-path-flow/README.md) and
[094](../094-affine-gpf-log-roof-path-flow/README.md) aggregate to
\(\log210\) and \(\log6\), respectively. This comparison does not
turn their positive arithmetic results into one packet for every prime.

The local reversible-scattering record
[229](../229-chord-ribbon-scattering/README.md) retains composite
primitive packets; 238's actual source updates stay inside the composite
sector. No changed renewal or arithmetic-transition rule was supplied
here that simultaneously closes the return and timing obligations.

**Decision: no admission / fork.** The missing input is a specified new
source/action mechanism and its relation to actual time. This is not a
requirement that every source be a chronological prime generator, and not
a theorem forbidding all admissibility-based constructions. Merely choosing
another periodic-rigidity rule or attaching a new cocycle would not close
the present missing-definition record.

## 5. Conservative oscillator/field lane — separate bounded admission

The [DRB01 card](../241-divisor-radiation-bath/candidate-card.md) keeps
every integer component, its oscillator and all active and inactive
continuous-frequency fields at total energy 1. Proper-divisor symbols
open reciprocal oscillator/field couplings. The squared Hamiltonian
includes its counterterm before proof. There is no outgoing condition,
dissipative reduction, discarded field sector or selected-centre carrier.

This is different from 171's static witness force: the proposed selector
is a full periodic-field solvability condition. It is also different from
237's discrete bound modes and 239's uniform removal of recurrence.
The bounded collision search found no matching local continuum-bath card;
this is not a claim of novelty in the external literature.

The oscillator deliberately uses the same rational barrier formula as
[171](../171-separatrix-witness-hamiltonian-clock/candidate-card.md).
That disclosed design overlap gives no theorem transfer. On the new full
owner the global mild action, full periodic field equations, constant-state
exceptions, multiplicity and physical period must all be established again.

**Admission boundary:** the conserved integer and fixed divisor incidence
do not become a newly evolving arithmetic source. A successful conservative
selection theorem may advance an engineering layer while source-naturalness
remains OPEN. The widened carrier is an infinite-dimensional flow and its
transformation groupoid, not a classical finite-dimensional suspension.

### Result of the bounded first-gate audit

The [241 proof](../241-divisor-radiation-bath/paper.md) constructs a
global mild action on the entire frozen Hilbert carrier. Energy
preservation extends from strong-domain states to all mild states. The
Hamiltonian vector-field identity is asserted on its strong domain,
not as an everywhere Hilbert-valued smooth generator.

An active channel makes a nonconstant periodic oscillator incompatible
with a square-integrable periodic field: a nonzero positive temporal
Fourier coefficient would create a pole at a positive field frequency.
Reality then forces the oscillator to be constant. Constant solutions
have energies 0 or \(c_n>1\), never the frozen energy 1. Thus the
complete composite components have no positive-time returns. On prime
components, full periodicity forces every free field to vanish, leaving
one central oscillator oval and no other periodic packet.

The least time of that actual oval is derived from the new full owner's
equations and satisfies \(T_p=2\sqrt2\log p+O(1)\), with repeats
\(rT_p\). No field is removed to obtain this classification. No exact
\(\log p\) equality, scattering/decay theorem, finite-dimensional
section, trace, determinant or quantum spectrum is inferred.

This is a **conservative engineering advance**, unlike 239's loss of
all desired returns. It does not make the integer label evolve or close
source-naturalness. Arbitrary Boolean channel data select their own
all-zero rows by the same argument, a PROVES_TOO_MUCH control against
claiming that the bath construction canonically singles out arithmetic.

T0 and T2 are established for DRB01, T1 is construction-positive with
naturalness OPEN, and T3 is NOT SUPPLIED. These are 241's broadened
owner labels, not SCJ01 or formal Route coordinates.
