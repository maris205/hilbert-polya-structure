## 2. A whole power-trace class is excluded before further space engineering

[157's proof](../157-power-trace-integrality-audit/paper.md) fixes the entire
all-integer map, unit roof and natural flat-trace sequences from the 153
geometry. No damping, component removal or periodic-sector selection is
allowed in its exact realization question. With lambda=3/2,

\[
N_m=\sum_{p:\,K_p\mid m}K_p,\quad
K_n=\max(1,\lfloor\log_2(n-1)\rfloor),\quad
T_0(m)=T_2(m)=\frac{N_m}{\lambda^m+\lambda^{-m}-2},
\]
\[
T_1(m)=N_m+2T_0(m).
\]

The exact dyadic endpoint count and a bound for every proper-divisor
contribution, together with the prime number theorem, give

\[
\frac{N_m}{2^m}\longrightarrow\frac1{\log2},\qquad
\frac{T_0(m)}{(4/3)^m}\longrightarrow\frac1{\log2},\qquad
\frac{T_1(m)}{2^m}\longrightarrow\frac1{\log2}.
\]

The paper proves the needed spectral lemma for arbitrary complex,
possibly nonnormal Hilbert trace-class operators. Lidskii and absolute
summability isolate a finite peripheral eigenvalue sum; Cesàro phase
extraction shows that a positive finite normalized full-sequence limit
must be the positive integer algebraic multiplicity of its positive real
leading eigenvalue. The coefficient 1/log2 lies strictly between one and
two. No assumption of positive or self-adjoint spectrum is used.

Consequently none of the three exact trace sequences can be the ordinary
traces of all positive powers of any single Hilbert trace-class operator.
The corresponding fixed-operator ordinary determinant germs are excluded
as well. Searching for another Hilbert norm while retaining that same
all-power trace requirement cannot repair this contract.

The scope is important. [153](../153-saturated-sieve-flat-trace/paper.md)
never claimed an ordinary trace-class operator; its actual full-map flat
traces and D_0 D_2/D_1=Z identity remain valid. Non-trace-class,
regularized, local or other meromorphic frameworks are not excluded by
157. Nor is a parameter-dependent family arbitrarily substituted for the
powers of one fixed operator covered by its determinant claim.

An explicit positive control in 157 realizes every finite packet cutoff
by a trace-class direct sum. It can match any prescribed finite prefix
of the complete sequence exactly. This explains why more finite numerical
matching would not address the proved all-power obstruction.

Rank-one components are circles of length L=log p. Their scalar smoothed
trace tends to the actual return distribution L times the lattice delta
comb. Higher-rank dense translation invariance forces basic functions to
be constant, but those components remain in the full direct sum.

The map f to f du pairs the two Hilbert degrees unitarily and intertwines

Independently, infinitely many orthogonal constant channels are fixed by

The specified full ordinary supertrace is undefined, not a legitimized
subtraction of two infinities. Pairing before subtraction gives a genuine

This is a decisive stop of the actual basic-complex contract. No theorem
about every possible relative complex, torsion or groupoid trace follows.
A degree-weighted alternative was left unexecuted, not substituted after
the failed unweighted test.

## 4. Chronological source evolution is real, but its entire conveyor is aperiodic

[159](../159-reversible-sieve-conveyor/paper.md) gives a genuine ordered

The source displacement at the forward scan's top computes the number

The full proof supplies a global integer height h and cumulative test

\[
(h,Q,P)\longmapsto(h+1,Q,P).
\]

Its unit suspension is globally R squared times R with real translation
in the last coordinate. Every full state is therefore aperiodic, not
only the calibrated source path. This is a concrete full-geometry
instance of the known monotone-frontier obstruction, not a new universal
no-go theorem.

Closing a finite list of compute–uncompute blocks is a different owner.
Its total increment cancels, so one full cycle is the identity on every
register plane. The resulting continuum of primitive packets occurs
for composite inputs as well as primes. Neither a finite closing nor a
clean-register selection repairs the frozen chronological object.

## 5. Two owned logarithmic clocks, with different unresolved targets

### 5.1 The serial-scan clock is a transparent engineered control

**Under prespecified dilation/reset timing**, [160](../160-source-geometric-return-clock/paper.md)
constructs a full symplectic divisor-scan suspension with one primitive
orbit per prime and no composite periodic state. The new map has phases
d=1,...,n-1, local witness drift and its complete cotangent action.
The explicitly chosen transit dr/dt=r on d to d+1 gives

\[
\tau_d=\log\frac{d+1}{d}.
\]

An explicit quotient seam resets the final scale n to one and glues
the transverse state by the same map. It is not a positive-time path
that was secretly omitted from a globally defined scale observable.
For each fixed n the finite cyclic roof list has positive minimum and
sum log n. Thus every full trajectory is complete in both time
directions, even though the infimum across different n is zero.

Full configuration and momentum return equations give one intrinsic
prime packet of least map period p-1, actual flow time log p, and
nondegenerate repetitions. Its ordinary unweighted orbit product is
the prime Euler product on Re(s)>1. No transfer operator, trace,
Fredholm realization, continuation or formal target-divisor verdict
is supplied.

The engineering warning is part of the main claim, not a footnote.
For h(n,d,q,p)=log d, the exact same roof satisfies

\[
\tau(z)=h(Fz)-h(z)+\mathbf1_{\{d=n-1\}}\log n.
\]

It is cohomologous to placing the full log-n time at the reset edge.
The dilation law, neutral initialization interval and seam remain
specified design choices, not consequences derived from the divisor
source. The [earlier clock screen](../108-source-action-frontier-cycle-11/evidence/README.md#bounded-next-clock-hypothesis-screen)
already identified this risk. The new full construction verifies
ownership and completeness; it does not overcome that source-clock gap.
Alternative velocities demonstrate design freedom, not a universal
illegitimacy theorem or a new demand for unique geometry.

Accordingly, 160 is retained as an engineered control and stops
