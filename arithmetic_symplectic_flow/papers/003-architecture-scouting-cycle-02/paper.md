## Abstract

The screen targets three architectures that each appear to solve one missing
piece: character varieties provide natural symplectic geometry and integral
Markoff-type points; continued fractions provide strong arithmetic coding; and
adeles expose every prime place simultaneously. None presently supplies the
whole same-object tuple. In particular, Markoff integrality has no prime-varying
clock, branchwise continued-fraction maps are not automatically one global
\(C^r\) symplectomorphism, and an adelic restricted product has no specified
finite-dimensional symplectic base/roof. All are stopped before P0 admission.

## 1. Scope and test

The no-candidate lock is [candidate-card.md](candidate-card.md). The admission
test is deliberately short: a future object must specify one concrete smooth
symplectic base map, positive roof, endogenous varying-prime source, and an
intrinsic primitive-orbit/repetition owner. A compelling property in only one
column is not enough.

### Follow-up all-places/Hénon check

The focused follow-up separated two real but distinct literatures: adelic
rational dynamics coordinates real and (p)-adic components of one rational
linear-fractional action, while Hénon/arithmetic-dynamics work studies
polynomial surface maps over a chosen field.  The search did not locate a
source-defined adelic Hénon action that also preserves a sieve-derived
prime-symbolic observable.  This is `NOT LOCATED`, not a nonexistence theorem.
It rules out neither future adelic geometry nor a future Hénon realization; it
only prevents identifying those currently separate owners by terminology.
