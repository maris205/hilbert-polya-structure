## Explicit checks behind the main boundary

For a finite support S, the positive units are products of primes in S
with arbitrary integer exponents. Thus the time stabilizer is exactly
the additive group generated by their logarithms. This group is not the
isotropy group of an object in the displayed étale groupoid: that latter
group is trivial. The manuscript keeps these meanings separate.

For two different primes p and q, any rational relation between log p and
log q would imply equality of positive powers of p and q. This is
impossible. The small integer combinations from the paper's pigeonhole
argument therefore never vanish, and changing their signs gives arbitrarily
small positive returns. Integer multiples of these returns approximate
every real number. Their countable subgroup is dense but not all of R.

Indeed, the mixed-rank coarse component has the indiscrete topology:
the inverse image of a nonempty open quotient set is a nonempty open set
in R saturated under a dense subgroup. Translates of any contained open
interval cover R, so that inverse image is all of R. This verifies, and
slightly strengthens, the paper's claimed non-T1 boundary. It does not
invalidate the frozen groupoid carrier.

The asserted nonproperness also has a direct check. Fix a mixed-rank A.
The set of arrow pairs with source (A,0) and target (A,v), where
-1 <= v <= 1, is the inverse image of a compact subset of the object-pair
space. It contains infinitely many arrows indexed by distinct discrete q
with absolute value of log q at most 1. This inverse image is an infinite
discrete space and is not compact. Thus the source-target map is not proper.

At time log p, counting one positive unit per distinct returning A already
has infinite mass because all A_(pq) occur. This statement does not depend
on counting every representative u separately. It proves a failure of that
positive unweighted return-counting rule, not divergence of an otherwise
undefined ordinary primitive product. The paper states the latter product
as unsupported and leaves weighted traces and regularizations OPEN, which
is the correct scope.
