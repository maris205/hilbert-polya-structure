## 2. Ordered covers give a full prime-only carrier under a declared design

[163's complete proof](../163-ordered-cover-scale-suspension/paper.md)
starts with positive-rational divisibility: x <=_D z exactly when z/x is a
positive integer. A strict cover has no intermediate element. Its ratio is
a prime by the elementary equivalence between intermediate divisors and
nontrivial factorizations; the source does not receive a prime table.

For all normalized bilateral cover chains y with y_0=1, write
a_j=y_{j+1}/y_j. The newly defined entire carrier and its action are

\[
Y=\{y:a_j\le a_{j+1}\ \text{for all }j\in\mathbb Z\},\qquad
(Fy)_j=y_{j+1}/y_1,
\]
\[
G(y,r)=(Fy,r/y_1),\quad
Q=(Y\times\mathbb R_{>0})/\langle G\rangle,\quad
\Phi^t[y,r]=[y,e^t r].
\]

Weak numeric ordering is imposed before the dynamics is audited. It is not
selection of constant words after examining 161's closed orbits. The full Y
retains mixed aperiodic chains, for example ratio 2 at all negative indices
and ratio 3 at all nonnegative indices. Both F and its inverse preserve Y.
The full quotient is Hausdorff, and the flow is jointly continuous and
complete. The embedded section r=1 has actual first return Fy and roof
log y_1 >= log 2. For every integer m, the exact formula
G^m(y,r)=(F^m y,r/y_m) controls both time directions and the quotient charts.

Every positive closed-flow return forces a positive shift period of the
entire ratio sequence. A periodic nondecreasing sequence must be constant.
Consequently the whole flow has exactly one primitive circle per derived
prime p, with period log p and repetitions k log p. The scale coordinate
parametrizes phases of that one circle, not a continuum of distinct packets.
No nonconstant chain is periodic and no other full state is suppressed.
Bounded-time packets and their repetitions are finite. Thus the same
owner's ordinary unweighted product is

\[
Z_{163}(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})^{-1}
=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s},\qquad \Re s>1.
\]

The paper proves this identity by absolute convergence and unique
factorization, and proves the exact absolute-logarithmic boundary Re s=1.
It does not provide a trace, transfer operator, Fredholm determinant,
continuation, target divisor or Riemann-zero conclusion.

Two controls make the admissibility change explicit. Removing weak order
restores 161's mixed primitive words, including (2,3). Replacing weak order
by strict increase makes the **entire bilateral positive-integer ratio
carrier empty**: infinitely many strict decreases toward negative indices
cannot start below a fixed positive integer. The latter is not a nonempty
aperiodic substitute. More generally, ordered alphabets have the same
constant-periodic-word lemma: its prime specificity lies in the cover
source, not in the order lemma by itself.

The new ANG carrier is permitted on the broadened track. It is not a full
Hénon horseshoe with a favorable subshift selected afterward, the boundary
in [012](../012-sieve-constrained-horseshoe-screen/paper.md), and is not supplied

[041](../041-prime-gap-language-screen/paper.md). Those historical
records do not automatically forbid a new explicitly declared carrier.
Neither the weak order nor the exponential scale law has been proved to
follow from chronological sieve evolution. F shifts an already admissible
chain; it does not execute a sieve. **The positive result is a limited
broadened-owner advance under declared order/scale design; naturalness

## 3. A genuinely different Hamiltonian return closes geometry but not time

[164](../164-witness-hamiltonian-return-clock/paper.md) uses all integer
labels n>=2 and the complete four-dimensional symplectic space at each
label. Let a(n) count proper nontrivial divisor witnesses and set

\[
V_a(q)=(1-a)\log\cosh q+a q,\qquad
H_n(q,p,Q,P)=\frac{p^2}{2}+V_{a(n)}(q)+QP.
\]

The energy surface H_n=1 was fixed uniformly **before** the periodic audit.
It is the genuine complete flow owner, not a favorable energy selected
after examining the answer. Each component's force is bounded, so the full
Hamiltonian flow is complete in both directions. The chosen energy surface
is regular. The entire section q=0,p>0 has coordinates (Q,P) with QP<1 and
induced area dQ wedge dP.

The operational maximal two-sided return domain is determined dynamically.
For composites a>=1, p-dot=-[a-(a-1)tanh q]<=-1, excluding any periodic
trajectory and any repeated positive-p crossing. For primes a=0, the scalar
oscillator energy E=1-QP>0 has a unique periodic oval and an actual period
function T(E). The prime E=0 branch q=p=0, QP=1 is also retained: its
hyperbolic motion is complete but nonreturning and not closed. Thus the
omitted nonreturn states of the complete energy owner are explicitly
classified rather than hidden by passing to a section.

On the entire maximal return domain, the actual return map and roof are

\[
F(Q,P)=\bigl(e^{T(1-QP)}Q,e^{-T(1-QP)}P\bigr),\qquad
\tau(Q,P)=T(1-QP)\ge2\pi.
\]

The full map is a smooth symplectic diffeomorphism. Its suspension is exactly
the return saturation in the fixed energy surface, not the whole surface;
the complement has no closed orbits. This proves an authentic positive-
dimensional conservative/return construction without a reset-assigned roof.

A positive-time closed trajectory forces Q=P=0 because Q and P evolve by
e^t and e^{-t}. At energy 1 it therefore has E=1, so each prime supplies
exactly one primitive oscillator orbit, with all transverse repetitions
hyperbolic. Crucially, **every prime has the same physical time T(1)**.
There are already infinitely many primitive packets at this one bounded
time. The first repetition alone makes the complete ordinary absolute
log-product sum infinite at every finite complex s. Its expected prime-log
clock also fails, since T(1)/log p tends to zero.

This is a decisive STOP of the frozen clock/product proposal. It preserves
the full Hamiltonian owner and the full period classification. It does not
deny all possible analytic frameworks, demand that a legitimate energy
surface cannot be fixed beforehand, or launch energy/potential tuning.

## 4. Factor exchanges produce a real flow with nonordinary returns

without changing r, for every integer i. The quotient is by all finite words
in these generators and their inverses. Scale multiplication e^t commutes
with every generator and descends to a complete jointly continuous action;
an open quotient map proves joint continuity without assuming separation.

The decisive owned return uses only four actual generators, composed right
to left:

\[
G^{-1}H_{-1}GH_0(a,r)=(a,r a_0/a_1).
\]

For the full alternating chain a=(2,3)^infinity, G squared also changes r
to r/6. Its time stabilizer therefore contains

\[
\mathbb Z\log6+\mathbb Z\log(2/3).
\]

Unique factorization proves these generators have irrational ratio; the
elementary pigeonhole argument gives arbitrarily small positive returns
and density. All finite generator words change scale by a rational number,
so the full stabilizer remains countable and proper, not all of R. It has
no least positive element. The quotient fails the topological T1 separation
axiom, which is unrelated to the project label T1 for source/clock audit.

Every constant p chain still has exactly (log p)Z as its return group.
These are valid positive controls, not a permitted replacement of the full
mixed-state owner. The conclusion is **STOP the proposed ordinary complete

## 5. Full unit exterior weighting improves local selection but not the trace

[166](../166-unit-exterior-weighted-trace/paper.md) retains 146's all-integer
actual localizations A=Z[1/n], their positive units U_A, and the real

\[
B_A=C^\infty(\mathbb R)^{\log U_A},\qquad
C_A^j=B_A\otimes\Lambda^j\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(U_A,\mathbb C),
\qquad d=0.
\]

The paper gives the elementary tensor resolution proving this unit-group
coefficient complex. The uniform mean norm completes rank-one coefficients
to normalized circle L2 and higher-rank coefficients to constants; all mixed
components remain. A uniform Gaussian time smoothing R_A and exterior
degree weighting give the maximal direct-sum operator

\[
W=\bigoplus_{A,j}(-1)^j j(R_A\otimes I).
\]

For each fixed component the finite identity

\[
\sum_{j=0}^r(-1)^j j\binom rj
=\begin{cases}-1,&r=1,\\0,&r\ge2\end{cases}
\]

retains the negative rank-one scalar return trace and cancels each higher-
rank component trace. At rank one, L=log p and that actual smoothed trace is
Theta_{L,epsilon}(t)=L sum_k g_epsilon(t-kL), with fixed-L distributional
limit L sum_k delta_(kL). This is a genuine local improvement over 158's
unweighted basic pairing, not a deletion of mixed carriers.

The full operator nevertheless fails at once. Infinitely many orthonormal
degree-one constant vectors satisfy W e_p=-e_p, so the bounded degree-one
restriction is not compact. Constant top-degree vectors on rank-r components
have image norm r, so the entire maximal-domain operator is unbounded,
although densely defined and closed. Its ordinary Hilbert trace is
undefined. Finite-component trace cutoffs at fixed epsilon>0 and real t
tend to negative infinity because every prime component contributes at
least (log 2)g_epsilon(t)>0 before the minus sign. No cancellation of
undefined global traces or new order-of-limits prescription is installed.

The complex does not faithfully encode all dense mixed returns: its
higher-rank coefficient action is the identity at every time. The finite
rank selector is not itself a full dynamical return theorem. Thus 166 stops
its precise ordinary-trace contract while leaving other relative or torsion
owners unconstructed, not universally impossible.

### Screen XN — source necklace fields do not descend to factor multisets

Take nonempty finite words of cover-derived atoms, first modulo cyclic
rotation, then quotient further by all finite permutations to the exponent
multiset q(w). The proposed inherited fields are word primitivity, maximal
word repetition index, and the norm of its primitive root.

The word 2233 has least cyclic word period 4: periods 1 and 2 both fail.
Thus it is primitive, with repetition index 1 and primitive-root norm 36.
The word 2323 is (23) squared, with least period 2, repetition index 2 and
primitive-root norm 6. One adjacent exchange identifies their multisets.
Therefore these three inherited fields do not descend to this quotient.

The full word norm 36 and its logarithm **do** descend. The operation of
repetition also descends: q(w^k)=k q(w). It is the inherited maximal index
and root ledger that fail, not the algebraic repetition operation itself.
An equivariant map R/(log36)Z -> R/(log6)Z is a legitimate degree-two circle
cover, showing that a new quotient may shorten a period. This is not a
proof that every quotient flow is impossible. Nor do finitely many adjacent
exchanges on a bilateral sequence identify 2233-infinity with 2323-infinity:
they disagree at infinitely many coordinates. **STOP the proposed descent
of the old necklace fields.** The actual 165 return proof is separate.

### Screen XC — commutative power-primitivity does not mean irreducibility

As a different definition, use nonzero, finite-support, nonnegative integer
exponent vectors and call one power-primitive when the gcd of its nonzero
exponents is 1. The
vector for 6=2 times 3 is (1,1), hence is a mixed power-primitive object.
The vector for 36 is (2,2), twice that vector. More generally a nonzero
nonnegative integer vector is k times another such vector, for an integer
k>=1, exactly when k divides every coordinate, so this gcd rule is
consistent. It is not descent of
necklace primitivity and does not equal multiplicative irreducibility.
Single-prime vectors of exponent 1 are positive controls and larger pure
powers are repetitions. **STOP the prime-exclusive interpretation of this
new commutative primitive convention.** No flow or time owner is claimed.

### Screen E — actual Euclidean computation is not yet a conservative return

Unlike [127](../127-prime-continued-fraction-digit-boundary/paper.md), the
screen executes real integer division rather than labeling continued-
fraction digits by a prime predicate. For n>=3 and 2<=d<n, set a_0=n,
a_1=d and iterate

\[
a_{j+1}=a_{j-1}-\left\lfloor a_{j-1}/a_j\right\rfloor a_j
\]

until a_{L+1}=0, with a_L=gcd(n,d). The active state's second coordinate
strictly decreases among positive integers, so every active path terminates;
there is no positive active-state cycle. Zero remainder is an endpoint,
not an automatic return. This is a concrete instance of
[073's well-founded execution obstruction](../073-well-founded-certificate-return-obstruction/paper.md),
not a new general theorem forbidding reversible Euclidean geometry.

The exact identity

\[
\sum_{j=1}^{L}\log(a_{j-1}/a_j)=\log(n/\gcd(n,d))
\]

is a positive finite scale cost, not an established closed-orbit time.
Taking d=n-1 makes this log n for every n>=3, including n=4,d=3. The
composite example n=4,d=2 instead gives log 2. Testing every permitted d
can recognize primality, but a family of terminating tests is not one
intrinsic periodic packet. No complete positive-dimensional conservative
return or physical clock is supplied. A reset or reversible extension
would need a new full owner; 159's translation and finite-closure controls
cannot be borrowed as its proof. Nor does
[129's prime-geodesic terminology](../129-modular-geodesic-prime-terminology-boundary/paper.md)
supply ordinary prime times. **STOP this proposal before P0; no candidate
ID is allocated.** The source-lineage connection is genuine and is not
erased by the missing return construction.
