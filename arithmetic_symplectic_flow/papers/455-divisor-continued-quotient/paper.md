## Abstract

On the entire closed square with its original area measure, a partial
reciprocal map admits the current digits \(n,d\) exactly when \(d\mid n\)
and writes their quotient into its second coordinate. We prove its complete
inverse atlas, every-point and every-Borel IMAGE, actual-lag groupoid,
full kernels, isotropy, incoming recursion and height phases.
Writing \(\alpha_j=(\sqrt{j^2+4}-j)/2\), the complete MAIN fixed set is
\(\{(\alpha_{q^2},\alpha_q):q\ge1\}\). Each incoming source class is singleton;
its entire clock image is generated by \(2\log(\alpha_q/\alpha_{q^2})\).
The \(q=1\) packet has zero clock. For every \(q\ge2\), the exponential of
the positive primitive is a nonrational algebraic unit, not an ordinary prime.
The permission-OFF control has explicit predecessor trees, while the
quotient-feedback-OFF control owns an endpoint-potential clock and \(H=0\)
globally, despite nonzero individual clocks. All boundaries and phases remain.
These are exact same-owner statements, not a higher-period or Route result.

## 2. Complete actual inverses and assigned boundaries

For all integers \(a,b\ge1\), propose
\[
 \theta_{ab}(u,v)=\left(\frac1{a+u},\frac1v-b\right).
\tag{3}
\]
At an actual target require \(v>0,0\le u<1\), reconstructed coordinates
in \(B\), and all the owner's source/digit checks and own quotient law.
Necessity follows by solving the two forward equations; sufficiency follows
by substitution, because \(\lfloor a+u\rfloor=a\) when \(0\le u<1\).
Thus \(T_U\theta_{ab}Z=Z\) and \(\theta_{ab}T_Uz=z\) precisely on these
actual domains. Targets outside \(Y\) have no predecessors but remain objects.

There is a useful exact simplification which keeps every candidate in (3).
For \(Z=(u,v)\in Y\), set
\[
 b=\lceil1/v\rceil-1,\qquad y_v=1/v-b,\qquad
 d_v=\lfloor1/y_v\rfloor.
\tag{4}
\]
The interval condition \(0<1/v-b\le1\) has exactly this integer solution;
\(b\ge1,\ 0<y_v\le1\). In particular at \(v=1/k\), \(k\ge2\),
the correct branch is \(b=k-1,y_v=1\), not \(b=k,y_v=0\).
All actual predecessors are exactly the following:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
 M&\theta_{d_vb,b}(Z),\\
 O&\theta_{a,b}(Z),\quad
   a\in C_{d_v,b},\quad
   C_{d,1}=\{1,\ldots,2d-1\},\quad
   C_{d,b}=\{bd,\ldots,(b+1)d-1\}\ (b\ge2),\\
 N&\theta_{b,b}(Z)\quad\text{if }d_v\mid b,\quad\text{none otherwise}.
\end{array}
\tag{5}
\]
For MAIN the source law is \(a=d_vb\). For \(O\), when \(b=1\) its law
is \(\lfloor a/d_v\rfloor\le1\); when \(b\ge2\) it is
\(\lfloor a/d_v\rfloor=b\), giving exactly the sets in (5).
For \(N\), its law forces \(a=b\), followed by its own permission check.
Every listed first coordinate lies in \((0,1]\) and has digit \(a\).
This proves completeness, not a label cutoff. In particular \(M,N\) are
injective on their actual sources; \(O\) may have multiple predecessors.

Every branch domain is Borel by its literal inequalities, ceiling/floor
and divisibility checks. On
\(\Omega_{ab}=\{u>-a,\ 0<v<1/b\}\), (3) is an analytic diffeomorphism
onto \((0,\infty)^2\), with inverse
\((x,y)\mapsto(1/x-a,1/(b+y))\).
All actual branch domains are subsets of \(\Omega_{ab}\), including assigned
square boundaries. Restriction therefore gives Borel images of Borel sets.
This does not assert that the full digit-dependent map is smooth across cuts.

## 3. Its own area IMAGE and clock, at every actual point

Differentiating the prescribed inverse germ, without altering \(\mu\), gives
\[
 D\theta_{ab}=
 \begin{pmatrix}-(a+u)^{-2}&0\\0&-v^{-2}\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad J_{ab}(u,v)=\frac1{(a+u)^2v^2}>0.
\tag{6}
\]
This is finite on every actual inverse domain. For every Borel subset \(E\)
of that domain, change of variables on \(\Omega_{ab}\) proves
\[
                      \mu(\theta_{ab}E)=\int_EJ_{ab}\,d\mu.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus (7) covers boundaries and null sets as well as interiors. The measure
identity alone fixes a density only almost everywhere; the frozen analytic
germ (6) fixes the version at every point, including periodic null states.
No global invariant probability or alternative density is invoked.

At a legal source \(z=(x,y)\) with \(T_Uz=(u,v)\),
\[
 J_{\rm actual}(T_Uz)=x^2/v^2,\qquad
 \kappa_U(z)=-\log J_{\rm actual}(T_Uz)
            =2\log(v/x).
\tag{8}
\]
All signs remain. A legal step has zero clock exactly when \(x(q+y)=1\).
There is no clock for a nonexistent terminal step and no imposed positive roof.

For a legal history \(z_j=T_U^jz=(x_j,y_j)\), define
\[
 P_0(z)=1,\quad
 P_r(z)=\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}\frac{y_{j+1}}{x_j},\qquad
 S_r(z)=2\log P_r(z),\quad S_0=0.
\tag{9}
\]
Every factor is finite and positive, even if the last target has \(x_r=0\);
no terminal step is taken. Iterating (7) proves the IMAGE for every actual
finite inverse history, with factor \(P_r(z)^{-2}\) at its target.
The identity is on its whole Borel history domain, not an interior surrogate.

## 4. Full groupoids, kernels, isotropy and all phases

Let \(D_U^r\) be the Borel set of legal length-\(r\) histories, \(D_U^0=X\),
and \(T_U^0=\mathrm{id}_X\), including all terminal objects.
The complete actual groupoid is
\[
 \mathcal G_U=\{(z,r-s,w):z\in D_U^r,\ w\in D_U^s,
                            T_U^rz=T_U^sw,\ r,s\ge0\},
 \qquad c=S_r(z)-S_s(w).
\tag{10}
\]
Equal triples only are identified; source is \(w\), range \(z\).
Two witnesses for the same triple differ by equal extra depths. Their extra
sums begin at the same meeting state and cancel, proving descent of \(c\).
For composable witnesses \((r,s)\) and \((a,b)\), if \(a\ge s\), extend
the first range history by \(a-s\); the product has depths \((r+a-s,b)\).
Its added sum is \(S_a(w)-S_s(w)\), proving additivity.
If \(s\ge a\), extend the second source history instead.
Reversal negates \(c\), and the forward arrow \((T_Uz,-1,z)\) has
clock \(-\kappa_U(z)\). Borel meeting relations and countably many depths
make \(\mathcal G_U\) Borel; selecting the first witness in an enumeration
makes \(c\) Borel, independently of that selection by descent.

Here are exact global kernel tests in source coordinates, not a finite scan.
For any witness in (10),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (z,r-s,w)\in\ker c
   &\Longleftrightarrow P_r(z)=P_s(w),\\
 (z,r-s,w)\in\ker\ell
   &\Longleftrightarrow r=s,\qquad \ell(z,k,w)=k,\\
 (z,0,w)\in\ker\ell\cap\ker c
   &\Longleftrightarrow
       T_U^rz=T_U^rw,\ P_r(z)=P_r(w)\ \text{for some legal }r.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
All products are the explicit coordinate products (9).
These witness-independent conditions characterize every arrow, including
coalescing equal-depth histories of \(O\).
For \(M,N\), injectivity allows cancellation of the common history:
if \(k\ge0\), an arrow \((z,k,w)\) exists exactly when \(w=T_U^kz\)
legally, and its clock is \(S_k(z)\); for \(k<0\) reverse this description.
Consequently their lag and joint kernels are only the units.
For MAIN its clock kernel consists exactly of those connecting segments
with the corresponding product \(P_{|k|}=1\), in either orientation.

Let \(I_U(E)\) be the union of every predecessor in (5) of every target
in \(E\cap Y\), using each owner's own tests. Then
\[
 [z]_U=\bigcup_{\substack{s\ge0\\z\in D_U^s}}\ 
                    \bigcup_{r\ge0}I_U^r(\{T_U^sz\}).
\tag{12}
\]
This is equivalent to (10), and includes all depths. For a terminal \(t\),
it becomes \(\bigcup_{r\ge0}I_U^r(\{t\})\). No recursion cutoff or termination
claim is imposed. The explicit finite lists (5) make every stage reconstructible.

For a state not eventually on a legal cycle, source isotropy is trivial:
a nonzero isotropy lag in (10) would give two unequal iterates of that state
which agree. If its eventual cycle has least source period \(p\) and cycle
sum \(K\), extending meeting histories beyond first entry proves
\[
 \mathcal G_{U,z}^{z}=\{(z,mp,z):m\in\mathbb Z\},\qquad
 c(z,mp,z)=mK,\qquad H_z=K\mathbb Z.
\tag{13}
\]
Every period of a least-\(p\) cycle is a multiple of \(p\), by division with
remainder; conversely all multiples are realized after entry. Tail sums cancel.
Thus (13) is the entire image, also at incoming states that are not themselves
periodic. Non-eventually-periodic states, including all terminal classes, have
\(H_z=\{0\}\). This is a structural criterion, not a higher-period census.

The real extension keeps all \((z,h)\in X\times\mathbb R\) and arrows
\((w,h)\to(z,h+c(g))\). Its isotropy is the zero-clock source isotropy:
in (13) it is all \(p\mathbb Z\) if \(K=0\), and only the unit otherwise.
Height translation acts on the orbit SET and its stabilizer is exactly \(H_z\).
Over any one source orbit, fix an anchor \(a\), choose an arrow \(a\to z\)
of clock \(b_z\), and retain all phases
\[
                             h-b_z\pmod{H_a}\in\mathbb R/H_a.
\tag{14}
\]
Changing the arrow changes \(b_z\) by \(H_a\), so this is well-defined.
No global measurable selector or nice quotient is asserted.
When \(K\ne0\), primitive means \(|K|\), with all positive integer repeats;
when \(K=0\), source isotropy remains but there is no positive primitive.
Distinct source orbits are not merged for having equal lengths.

## 5. Complete global fixed sets

For every integer \(j\ge1\), set
\[
 \alpha_j=\frac{\sqrt{j^2+4}-j}{2}
          =\frac2{j+\sqrt{j^2+4}},\qquad
 \alpha_j^{-1}=j+\alpha_j.
\tag{15}
\]
These numbers lie strictly between \(0\) and \(1\), decrease strictly with
\(j\), and satisfy \(\lfloor1/\alpha_j\rfloor=j\).
Every fixed state must be legal, with equations
\(x^2+nx-1=0,\ y^2+qy-1=0\).
Each quadratic has exactly one positive root, so every fixed state is
\((\alpha_n,\alpha_q)\), whose actual second digit is \(d=q\).
The remaining own laws now give the complete sets:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Fix}(M)&=\{f_q=(\alpha_{q^2},\alpha_q):q\ge1\},\\
 \operatorname{Fix}(O)&=\{f_{nq}=(\alpha_n,\alpha_q):
                 q\ge1,\ n\in A_q\},\quad
                 A_q=\{q^2,\ldots,q^2+q-1\},\\
 \operatorname{Fix}(N)&=\{g_n=(\alpha_n,\alpha_n):n\ge1\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]
For MAIN, \(n=dq=q^2\) is necessary and sufficient.
For \(O\), \(q=\max(1,\lfloor n/q\rfloor)\) gives \(n=1\) when \(q=1\);
for \(q\ge2\) it gives \(q^2\le n<q^2+q\), exactly \(A_q\).
For \(N\), \(q=n\), and its actual permission \(d=n\mid n\) holds.
Thus every listed point passes its own checks.
This exhausts the full square: axes are terminal, \(x=1\) maps to first
coordinate zero, \(y=1\) maps below one, and (15) lies strictly inside its
assigned reciprocal cells. No boundary root or terminal loop is hidden.

## 6. Complete fixed packets, including the control predecessor trees

MAIN and \(N\) are injective by (5); each fixed core is already its own
predecessor, so induction proves that its entire incoming source class is
its singleton. Put
\[
 L_q=2\log(\alpha_q/\alpha_{q^2}),\qquad
 \lambda_{nq}=2\log(\alpha_q/\alpha_n).
\tag{17}
\]
By (8), MAIN has clock \(L_q\), with \(L_1=0\) and \(L_q>0\) for \(q\ge2\).
At \(f_q\), every isotropy lag \(k\in\mathbb Z\) has clock \(kL_q\);
entire \(H=L_q\mathbb Z\). For \(q\ge2\), clock/lag/joint kernels on this
packet are units, extension isotropy is trivial, and all phases
\(h\bmod L_q\) remain, with primitive \(L_q\) and repeats \(jL_q\).
For \(q=1\), source and extension isotropy are \(\mathbb Z\), the clock
kernel is all of it, lag/joint kernels are units, \(H=0\), phases are all
real heights and no positive primitive exists.

The independently owned \(O\) control has genuine incoming branches.
Write \(h_a(t)=1/(a+t)\). At a target \((u,\alpha_q)\) with \(0<u<1\),
(4) forces the previous second coordinate to be \(\alpha_q\) and the
feedback integer to be \(q\). Formula (5) then permits exactly \(a\in A_q\).
Hence the full source class of \(f_{nq}\) is
\[
 P_{nq}=\{(h_{a_1}\circ\cdots\circ h_{a_l}(\alpha_n),\alpha_q):
                         l\ge0,\ a_i\in A_q\}.
\tag{18}
\]
Both inclusions follow inductively from the complete inverse rule.
All coordinates stay in the open square; their reciprocal digits strip the
word one at a time. Duplicate words are identified as actual points.
Removing trailing \(n\)'s gives a unique reduced word, empty or with last
digit different from \(n\): \(h_n(\alpha_n)=\alpha_n\), and deterministic
digits prove both uniqueness and that its length \(\nu(z)\) is first-entry
time to the core. Thus (18) includes every incoming point and no extra one.
Different cores cannot have a common incoming state under a deterministic map.

On this full packet put \(\lambda=\lambda_{nq}\) and
\[
 B(z)=S_{\nu(z)}(z)-\nu(z)\lambda
     =2\sum_{j<\nu(z)}\log(\alpha_n/x_j).
\tag{19}
\]
Every pair in the packet admits every lag, by extending both histories
far enough at the fixed core. Extending any earlier meeting to that core gives
\[
 \mathcal G_O|_{P_{nq}}=P_{nq}\times\mathbb Z\times P_{nq},\qquad
 c(z,k,w)=B(z)-B(w)+k\lambda.
\tag{20}
\]
Consequently its lag kernel is every \((z,0,w)\); its clock kernel is
exactly \(B(z)-B(w)+k\lambda=0\); its joint kernel has \(k=0,B(z)=B(w)\).
At every incoming point, source isotropy is \(\mathbb Z\) and entire
\(H=\lambda\mathbb Z\). For \(q\ge2\), \(n>q\) gives \(\lambda>0\);
extension isotropy is trivial and all phases are \(h-B(z)\bmod\lambda\).
This is one full packet, not one packet per predecessor or phase.
For \(q=1\), \(A_1=\{1\}\) and \(P_{11}\) is singleton, with the same
zero-clock isotropy/real-phase description as the MAIN unit core.

For every \(N\) fixed core, (8) gives zero clock; its singleton class retains
source and extension isotropy \(\mathbb Z\), entire \(H=0\), full clock kernel,
unit lag/joint kernels, all real phases and no positive primitive.
There is also an exact global control identity, using the original measure.
Define \(\psi(x,y)=2\log(1+xy)\) on all of \(X\).
For each legal \(N\) step, \(q=n\) and
\[
 1+uv=(1+xy)v/x,\qquad
 \kappa_N=\psi(T_Nz)-\psi(z),\qquad
 c_N(z,k,w)=\psi(w)-\psi(z).
\tag{21}
\]
The first identity follows by substituting \(u=1/x-n,v=1/(n+y)\);
the others follow from (8) and telescoping legal histories.
Therefore \(H=0\) on every \(N\) state, not only at its fixed cores;
\(\ker c_N\) consists precisely of actual arrows with \(x_zy_z=x_wy_w\).
Its lag/joint kernels are units by injectivity, and its extension isotropy
is all source isotropy. Over each source class the complete phase coordinate
is \(h+\psi(z)\in\mathbb R\). Individual legal clocks are not all zero:
\((1,1)\in A\) has \(T_N(1,1)=(0,1/2)\) and \(\kappa_N(1,1)=-2\log2\).
This identity changes neither the measure nor the clock; it is not a
classification of any period-\(\ge2\) cycle.

## 7. The MAIN nonprime obstruction and exact limit of the result

An elementary algebraic-unit argument decides the frozen gate.
Each \(\alpha_j\) satisfies the monic polynomial \(t^2+jt-1\), and
\(\alpha_j^{-1}=\alpha_j+j\). Thus \(\alpha_j\) and its inverse are
algebraic integers. Products and sums of these numbers remain algebraic
integers: for two such roots their ring is generated over \(\mathbb Z\)
by \(1,\alpha_a,\alpha_b,\alpha_a\alpha_b\); multiplication, or equivalently
the corresponding monic-polynomial quotient ring and its characteristic
polynomial, gives a monic integral equation for every ring element.
A rational algebraic integer is an integer, by clearing a reduced denominator
in its monic equation. Hence a positive rational number which, together with
its inverse, is an algebraic integer must equal \(1\).

For MAIN \(q\ge2\),
\[
                  \exp L_q=(\alpha_q/\alpha_{q^2})^2>1
\tag{22}
\]
and both this number and its reciprocal are algebraic integers by the preceding
argument. Therefore (22) is irrational, in particular not an ordinary prime.
The same reasoning diagnoses each positive \(O\) fixed primitive independently.
MAIN itself already supplies the adverse primitive, for example at index \(q=2\).
Equation (13) and the complete singleton incoming proof show that it is the
least positive generator, not a repetition hiding a smaller prime clock.
The unit core remains in the ledger; deleting it or the adverse cores is forbidden.

Thus MAIN has a nonempty positive ledger and fails ordinary-prime support.
Same-object ownership is intact; the decision is STOP / FORK without parameter,
source, measure, phase or clock repair. Higher-period occurrence, global prime
coverage and uniqueness are not classified or certified, and cannot rescue this
necessary-condition failure. There is no universal no-go for other reciprocal
systems, no positive classical suspension, operator, trace or formal Route claim.
