# Evidence boundary

- [Ville Salo and Ilkka Törmä, *Direct prime subshifts and canonical covers*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/ergodic-theory-and-dynamical-systems/article/direct-prime-subshifts-and-canonical-covers/D8D649AE0A81FB2ED244BE6192E254BA): defines ``direct prime'' by absence of a nontrivial product decomposition and studies how that structure affects possible reversible cellular automata.  The terminology concerns factors of a subshift, not integer primes.
- [Prior-work guide](../../../docs/prior_work/README.md): the project requires a specific arrow from prime distribution or prime/composite observables into symbolic admissibility; a generic symbolic adjective does not supply that arrow.

Method: terminology and source-definition audit only.  No CA rule was selected,
no arithmetic labels, prime tables, or finite fields were inserted, and no
periodic-orbit, Hénon, roof, determinant, or Route computation was performed.
