In prime-geodesic theory, “prime” means *primitive*. It is the same
non-iteration convention used in dynamical zeta products; it neither indexes
geodesics by ordinary primes nor derives lengths `log p`. The modular flow has
many primitive closed geodesics independently of the ordinary prime sequence.

purported prime mechanism, which is `PROVES_TOO_MUCH`.
