# Evidence and source lock — `ALF-20260913-DEN01`

Primary source: Christopher Deninger,
[*Dynamical systems for arithmetic schemes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06400).
An accessible source-level restatement of the construction and packet theorem is
[*Primes, knots and periodic orbits*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11643).

Relevant source assertions:

- the construction attaches infinite-dimensional continuous-time systems to
  integral normal schemes flat and finite type over `Spec Z`;
- closed points correspond bijectively to compact packets of periodic orbits of
  length `log N(x_0)`;
- each packet is fibred over a compact group with circle fibres, and every
  periodic orbit lies in exactly one packet;
- the source asks for a Fuller-type packet index if a Hasse–Weil/Ruelle zeta
  relation is to be made precise.

Source boundary: the unrestricted rational-Witt carrier has too many periodic
orbits. This card therefore freezes the explicitly described admissible locus
`X_(0,E_in)`, where `E_in` requires injectivity of the character on the roots
of unity, not the unrestricted quotient. The packet theorem classifies its full
periodic set as a pairwise-disjoint union over closed points. This is the reason that
the packet classification is `T2 ESTABLISHED`, while a packet trace weight and
all of T3 remain `OPEN`.

Exact source choices frozen here are `X_0=Spec Z` and `K=Qbar`. No numerical
calculation, representative-orbit selection, prime table, classical roof, or
Route evaluation has been performed.
