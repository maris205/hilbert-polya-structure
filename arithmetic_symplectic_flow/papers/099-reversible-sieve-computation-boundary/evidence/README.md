# Evidence boundary

- A. Sowa, [*The Quantum Sieve of Eratosthenes*](https://researchers.usask.ca/artur-sowa/documents/2016-jpm.pdf), *J. Phys. Math.* 7 (2016), 180, DOI 10.4172/2090-0902.1000180.  Its discussion uses an `n`-qubit finite-register construction and states that reversible classical functions can be converted into quantum computations; no autonomous closed-orbit or symplectic-base theorem is stated.
- M. Vassor, “Reversibility and Predictions,” in *Reversible Computation 2021*, as reproduced in [the proceedings text](https://dokumen.pub/reversible-computation-13th-international-conference-rc-2021-virtual-event-july-78-2021-proceedings-lecture-notes-in-computer-science-12805-1st-ed-2021-3030798364.html).  The application initializes `{2,...,l}` and a finite value set in an oracle, and explicitly says pure causally consistent reversibility does not ensure forward progress.
- [011 reversible sieve simulation control](../../011-reversible-sieve-simulation-control/paper.md) is a related but distinct universal-computation boundary.

Method: source-definition audit only.  No quantum circuit, process calculus, finite orbit calculation, roof, zeta/determinant, geometric lift, or Route evaluation was performed.
