## Question and conclusion

Could the occurrence of MSS words and the phrase “prime period” supply a new prime-symbolic recurrent carrier that advances the project lineage?

**No.** In the cited dynamical usage, a prime period is the least period of a periodic point; it is not a prime integer.  The reported cascades have periods `2^m`, or `p*2^m` after selecting an arbitrary starting period `p`.  The source supplies neither a sieve-derived admissibility rule nor a map-generated prime/composite observable.  Replacing the terminology by prime labels would be manual insertion of forbidden data.

This result is still useful as a narrow comparator: it isolates the part of the legacy chain that is genuinely shared—MSS kneading structure—from the required first arrow, prime distribution to symbolic admissibility.  The latter arrow remains absent here.  The project legacy itself records that its sieve/MSS comparison has finite-stage admissibility obstructions; that historical result is not transferred to this different map family.

## Controls

1. **Semantic control.** Minimal period is a standard dynamical descriptor and exists for composite as well as prime-valued integers; it is not an arithmetic predicate.
2. **Label-blind control.** The trapezoid formula and its bifurcation proof contain no prime list, primality test, or sieve state, so a relabelling of orbit periods cannot become endogenous A0.
3. **Lineage control.** MSS words alone realize only the Logistic/MSS node of the required arrow chain.  They do not supply the prior prime-distribution-to-symbolic map, much less its Hénon/symplectic deformation.
4. **Ownership control.** A chosen map parameter, an unrelated roof, or a later symplectic lift would create a new object and cannot repair the source object's missing A0 owner.
