# Thickening an exact sieve packet destroys its finite orbit owner

**Paper ID:** `033-wheel-packet-symplectic-thickening-screen`  
**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-24`  
**Date / status:** `2026-09-14; PRE-P0 STOP — THICKENING CREATES UNOWNED PERIODIC FAMILIES`  
**Route state:** `No classical candidate; Route A not evaluated; Route B NOT INVOKED`

For a wheel `W_k`, assign one symplectic area disk `D_(k,a)` to each state and
choose area-preserving identifications mapping `D_(k,a)` to `D_(k,R_k(a))`.
Take their cyclic composition to be identity. If `q=phi(P_k)`, the `q`th iterate
is then identity on every disk. Thus the finite wheel packet becomes a continuum
of periodic points.

The 032 gap roof gives time only to finite symbolic states, not this continuum.
Selecting disk centres restores a finite cycle only by an external choice: no
map, area form, or sieve mechanism selects those centres. Primitive multiplicity
therefore changes and the 032 packet zeta cannot transfer.

**Portfolio decision: stop/fork.** A future symplectic lift must intrinsically
retain finite packet multiplicity and the exact gap roof; an embedding followed
by discarding extra periodic points is not a classical ASFS candidate.
