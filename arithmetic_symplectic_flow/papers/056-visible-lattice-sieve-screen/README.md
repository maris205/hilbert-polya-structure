**Record:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-40`  
**Status:** `PRE-P0 STOP — NATURAL SPATIAL SIEVE HULL HAS ONLY THE ZERO FULL-PERIODIC CONFIGURATION`

genuine prime-divisibility spatial sieve. Its translation hull has no nonzero
configuration invariant under a finite-index lattice of translations. This
rules out its most natural periodic packet form, without claiming that every
one-direction shift-periodic point has been classified.
