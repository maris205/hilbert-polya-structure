## Abstract

We test two separately frozen full arithmetic residue owners. The
Logistic polynomial Q(x)=x(1−x) acts on K=Z_hat with additive Haar h.
Its full image is Haar-null and has empty interior; its zero fibre
is uncountable. Thus its full retained-lag relation cannot have the
intended second-countable étale owner, and the full Haar dynamics
is singular rather than a source of the proposed finite IMAGE clock.
The exact two-register Hénon-form replacement
H(x,y)=(y,y(1−y)−x) is instead a full homeomorphism preserving product
Haar. Its actual IMAGE clock is identically zero, hence has no
positive time-return packet, even at discrete periodic states.
Neither result is a universal obstruction to nonlinear dynamics,
other measures or physical clocks. Both supplied admissions stop;
the two owners are never combined into a candidate.

## 2. Arithmetic residue interface and the full quadratic image

For d>=2 let e_d(x)=1 if x=0 modulo d. Then

    e_d(Q(x))=1  iff  (x mod d)(1−x mod d)=0 modulo d.

This is an exact state-generated divisibility rule, not a separate
symbolic label. At a prime modulus the zero alternatives are 0 and
1. They alone do not define ordinary primality: modulus 4 also has
exactly these two alternatives. No Boolean test of primality follows.

### Lemma 1 — every odd-prime image has size (p+1)/2

For an odd prime p, the image of Q on Z/pZ contains exactly
(p+1)/2 residues.

**Proof.** Over this field,

    Q(a)−Q(b)=(a−b)(1−a−b).

Thus two values coincide precisely when b=a or b=1−a. The
involution a->1−a has exactly one fixed point, namely 1/2. Its
remaining p−1 points form pairs. Counting these fibres gives the
stated image size. QED.

### Proposition 2 — the entire Q image is null and not open

Q(K) is compact, h(Q(K))=0 and its interior is empty. In particular
Q is nowhere a local homeomorphism onto an open subset of K.

**Proof.** Polynomial evaluation is continuous, so Q(K) is compact.
For any N distinct odd primes p_1,...,p_N, its reduction modulo
each p_i belongs to the image in Lemma 1. The Chinese remainder
theorem and uniform Haar mass of residue cylinders therefore imply

    h(Q(K)) <= product_(i=1..N) (p_i+1)/(2p_i) <= (2/3)^N.

There are arbitrarily many distinct odd primes, so the measure is
zero. Every nonempty basic open residue cylinder has positive Haar
measure. Consequently Q(K) has no interior. If Q restricted to any
nonempty open neighbourhood were a homeomorphism onto an open set,
that image would be a nonempty open subset of Q(K), impossible. QED.

This is an exact infinite-congruence bound. It does not approximate
K by a finite quotient or assign an extrapolated numerical density.
Each finite image is nonempty, which does not contradict full Haar
nullity.

## 3. Entire fibres and failure of the proposed full owner

### Proposition 3 — Q^{-1}(0) is an uncountable idempotent fibre

There is exactly one element of Q^{-1}(0) for every assignment of
one bit to each ordinary prime. In particular the fibre has the
cardinality of {0,1}^N, not at most two elements.

**Proof.** If x(1−x)=0 modulo p^k, then x and 1−x cannot both be
divisible by p, since their sum is 1. Hence one is divisible by
p^k, so x is 0 or 1 modulo p^k. Compatibility makes the same choice
hold for every k at that prime. Conversely, assign 0 or 1 at each
prime-power tower. The Chinese remainder theorem gives one compatible
residue modulo every positive integer, hence one element of K.
It solves Q(x)=0. Distinct assignments differ at some prime, so
give distinct elements. These exhaust the fibre. QED.

The prime-indexed description here is a conclusion of solving a
frozen equation in K, not a prime-indexed carrier selection. Every
assignment remains. In particular the ordinary integer roots 0 and
1 are not the full fibre; K is not an integral domain.

### Theorem 4 — no admission under NRC-L's full Haar IMAGE recipe

The full arrow set G_Q cannot be made into the intended
second-countable étale groupoid with the stated source map. The
pushforward Q_*h is singular to h. Moreover Q(K) is a null set
whose saturation under the full tail relation is all K. Thus the
frozen full Haar owner does not supply the proposed nonsingular
local-arrow clock.

**Proof.** Each x in Q^{-1}(0) gives a different arrow
(x,1,0) in G_Q, with source 0. Proposition 3 makes this source
fibre uncountable. In an étale groupoid a source fibre is discrete;
a discrete subspace of a second-countable space is countable.
The required two properties therefore cannot both hold here.

The probability Q_*h is supported on Q(K), whereas h(Q(K))=0
by Proposition 2. This proves singularity. For every x in K,
the arrow (x,1,Q(x)) connects x with a point of Q(K). Thus the
full tail saturation of that null set is all of K. This also
precludes the null-saturation property of the intended countable /
second-countable étale measure-class interpretation. It is not a
general criterion for arbitrary non-étale groupoids. QED.

No logarithm of a zero derivative or formal polynomial derivative
is promoted to a finite time cocycle. The conclusion is failure of
this specific full, nonsingular local-arrow construction. It is
not a claim that no non-étale relation or alternative measured
construction can be studied. No packet-time classification is made
for NRC-L because its proposed clock is unavailable.

## 4. Conservative replacement: a complete owner but zero clock

### Proposition 5 — exact inverse and product Haar law

H is a homeomorphism of K² with

    H^{-1}(u,v)=(u(1−u)−v,u).

For every Borel E subset K² and every integer n,

    (h×h)(H^n E)=(h×h)(E).

**Proof.** Substitution checks both inverse identities; the maps are
continuous polynomials. Modulo every m, the same inverse formula
makes H_m a permutation of (Z/mZ)². Thus it preserves normalized
counting measure there. Finite residue cylinders generate the Borel
sets of K², so uniqueness of probability measures extends this
identity to all Borel sets. Apply the inverse and iterate to cover
every integer n. QED.

This is a full joint-measure proof, not an inference from a formal
Jacobian determinant. Here conservative means product-Haar preserving;
it does not identify K² with a finite-dimensional symplectic manifold.

### Theorem 6 — all time-return groups of NRC-H are zero

The full action groupoid consists of (n,z), source z and target
H^n z, with discrete integer labels and the topology of K² on
each sheet. It is second-countable, locally compact Hausdorff and
étale. Its continuous IMAGE derivative is J=1 and clock c=0 on
every arrow. Its real extension has complete continuous translation,
but every full source state's time-return group is {0}. Therefore
there is no primitive positive-time packet under this convention.

**Proof.** The topological claims follow directly from the countable
disjoint sheets and Proposition 5's homeomorphisms. That proposition
also gives IMAGE density 1 on every Borel bisection. It is the
unique continuous version: full support on a sheet makes continuous
functions equal everywhere when they agree almost everywhere.
Hence c=−log J=0, including at null and periodic states.

Extension arrows map (z,u) to (H^n z,u). Translation of u by all
real t is jointly continuous and two-sided complete. Connecting
(z,u) to (z,u+t) requires t=0, irrespective of discrete isotropy.
This proves the full time-return assertion. QED.

For example (0,0) is an actual fixed point of H and has discrete
isotropy Z. All of that isotropy remains at a fixed object in the
zero-clock extension; it still supplies no positive time return.
This control prevents conflating absence of positive time packets
with absence of periodic points. No full H-cycle census is needed.
