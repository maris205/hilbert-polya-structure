## Abstract

We audit one frozen countably disconnected two-dimensional symplectic map
built over the split/merge and Euclid-shear graph of all finite integer words.
The graph has an isolated vertex exactly at each prime singleton. We add one
explicit stationary port per isolated vertex, apply a fixed hyperbolic matrix
there, and apply a symplectic momentum drift at every ordinary directed edge.
The full real periodic set consists of precisely one fixed point per prime;
there are no discarded composite components or selected geometric centres.
This gives a complete owner-level primitive/repetition ledger. It does not
give natural arithmetic time: every primitive of the unchanged unit-roof
suspension has length 1. The ordinary unweighted full orbit logarithm has no
absolute-convergence half-plane. Moreover, the stationary-port completion and
terminal/nonterminal geometric distinction are design choices, not consequences
of the arithmetic relation alone. We retain the exact geometric construction
as an engineering control and stop its promotion to a natural A0+A1 chain.

## 3. The graph and ribbon action are fully defined

### Proposition 1. Finite valence and exact terminal classification

G is a countable locally finite simple graph. Its isolated vertices are
precisely the singleton words (p) with p prime.

**Proof.** W is a countable union of finite products of countable sets. A
word of length k has finitely many proper factor splits at each entry,
k-1 possible adjacent merges, and at most four shear/subtraction moves per
adjacent pair. Hence N(w) is finite. A word of length at least 2 has at
least one merge neighbor, so is not isolated. A singleton (n) admits no
adjacent-pair shear or merge; its neighbors are precisely its proper
two-factor splits. There are none exactly when n is prime. No inverse
shear can create an edge incident to a singleton, since a shear preserves
word length. This proves the claim. \(\square\)

### Proposition 2. Full port bijection and multiplicity

The frozen \(\sigma\) is a bijection of X and preserves both E and L.

**Proof.** Shortlex totally orders each finite nonempty N(v), so c_v is a
well-defined permutation. For any (v,z) in E,

\[
\sigma^{-1}(v,z)=(c_v^{-1}(z),v).
\]

The inverse stays in E by the undirected graph relation. Each \(\ell_w\)
is separately fixed. Equivalently, reversal j on E swaps (u,v) with (v,u),
and the port rotation R sends (v,u) to (v,c_v(u)); extend both by fixing L.
Then \(\sigma=Rj\). Each ordered adjacent pair occurs once, and each
isolated vertex has precisely the one stationary symbol declared in the
card. No edge orientation or state is quotiented after this definition.
\(\square\)

We do not need to classify all cycles of \(\sigma\). Even if the symbolic
action has composite cycles, the geometric drift below prevents their lifts
from becoming full F-periodic points.

## 4. Exact symplectic geometry and the full periodic set

### Proposition 3. Global geometry

M is a Hausdorff second-countable smooth two-manifold without boundary, and
F is a global \(C^\infty\) symplectomorphism. Its unit suspension is defined
for all real times.

**Proof.** X is countable, so the disjoint union of its Euclidean components
is a standard smooth two-manifold; a countable union of countable coordinate
bases proves second countability. On nonterminal components the fibre map
B has inverse

\[
B^{-1}(Q,P)=(Q-P,P-1),
\]

and derivative \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)
of determinant 1. Together with Proposition 2 this defines the full inverse
between the appropriate components. On terminal components

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix},
\]

also has determinant 1. These formulas preserve \(dq\wedge dp\) exactly.
Since components are open and disjoint, the two cases introduce no boundary
or differentiability discontinuity. Global iterates exist in both directions;
unit roof prevents finite-time accumulation of crossings. \(\square\)

### Theorem 4. Complete prime fixed-point ledger

For every m>=1,

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(F^m)
=\{(\ell_{(p)},0,0):p\text{ prime}\}.
\]

Every listed point has primitive map period 1. In particular, the equality
classifies the entire periodic set of the full positive-dimensional map,
not an invariant section selected after construction.

**Proof.** Every orbit starting in E stays in E. Iterating its fibre map gives

\[
B^m(q,p)=\left(q+mp+\frac{m(m+1)}2,\ p+m\right).
\]

The second coordinate differs from p for every positive integer m. This
rules out every nonterminal periodic point independently of symbolic cycle
length, product changes or port order.

Every terminal component is fixed as a component. The two eigenvalues of A
are \(\lambda_\pm=(3\pm\sqrt5)/2\), satisfying
\(\lambda_+>1\) and \(0<\lambda_-<1\). Thus 1 is not an eigenvalue of
\(A^m\), so \((A^m-I)(q,p)=0\) has only the zero solution. That origin
is fixed by A. Proposition 1 identifies the terminal labels exactly with
prime singletons, finishing the full classification. \(\square\)

### Corollary 5. Full suspension primitives and repetitions

The unit suspension owns exactly one primitive oriented circle \(\gamma_p\)
for each prime p, with

\[
T_{\gamma_p}=1,\qquad T_{\gamma_p^r}=r,\qquad
P_{\gamma_p}=A,\qquad P_{\gamma_p^r}=A^r.
\]

There are no composite primitives or continuous families. Indeed, a closed
unit-suspension trajectory must return to the same height modulo 1 after
an integer number m of crossings, with \(F^m z=z\). Theorem 4 therefore
exhausts it. The transverse return determinant is

\[
\det(I-A^r)=2-(\lambda_+^r+\lambda_-^r)\ne0.
\]

This nondegeneracy is only a geometric fact; it supplies no trace formula.
A repeated traversal stays at the same prime port. A word of product p^r
with r>=2 is nonterminal and supplies no new periodic state. The numerical
arithmetic expression p^r does not occur as a time law: the actual repeat
length is r, not r log p.

## 5. Clock and ordinary analytic obstruction

All infinitely many prime primitives have the same frozen period 1. In
particular \(T_{\gamma_p}/\log p\to0\), and no fixed positive rescaling
of time makes these lengths logarithmic-order in p. A roof chosen from p,
a scale quotient, or a new physical-time lift would be a new candidate.

The card's proposed ordinary unweighted repetition logarithm would be

\[
\log Z(s)=\sum_p\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{e^{-sr}}r.
\]

For every finite complex s, its absolute sum already contains
\(\sum_p e^{-\operatorname{Re}s}=\infty\) from r=1. Thus there is no
absolute-convergence half-plane for this ordinary full-ledger definition.
When \(\operatorname{Re}s>0\), the per-prime inner sum converges but is
the same nonzero value for every p, so ordering complete prime factors
does not repair the ordinary sum. This obstruction precedes any attempt
to supply an operator, continuation or determinant. A cutoff, arithmetic
weight, subtraction, or regularization is not supplied by this object and
would need its own scope and identity.
