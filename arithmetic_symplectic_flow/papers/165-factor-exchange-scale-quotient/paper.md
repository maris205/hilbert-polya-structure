## Abstract

We add actual finite adjacent factor exchanges to the complete normalized
divisibility-chain scale carrier. The quotient is by the group generated
by the scale-shift and every finite adjacent exchange, not by a selection
of constant prime words. Uniform real scale multiplication descends to
a jointly continuous complete action on the entire quotient. A direct
four-generator loop, however, returns the chain while multiplying its
scale by the ratio of two adjacent factors. On the alternating 2,3 chain,
that loop and the original two-step return generate a dense subgroup of
time returns. The full stabilizer is countable and proper, so this orbit
has no least positive return, and the quotient fails the topological T1
separation axiom. Constant prime
chains still have exactly the expected discrete logarithmic return group.
The full ordinary primitive/repetition target therefore stops; the
remaining return classification and all analytic owners are unprovided.
The result is about this precise quotient, not all exchange constructions.

## 2. Exact proofs

### Proposition 1 — Source and generator ownership

Each cover ratio is a prime, and every two-sided sequence of these
source-derived atoms determines one normalized cover chain. In ratio
coordinates F is the left shift sigma, and all transformations in (2)
are homeomorphisms of the complete product.

**Proof.** A strict ratio n is an integer at least 2. A factorization
n=bc with b,c>=2 gives an intermediate rational bx. Conversely, any
intermediate rational supplies such a factorization. Thus covers are
exactly the multiplicatively irreducible integers, namely primes.

For any sequence a of such ratios, reconstruct y_j as the product of
a_0,...,a_{j-1} for j>0 and as the reciprocal product of a_j,...,a_{-1}
for j<0, with y_0=1. These formulas and (1) depend on finitely many
coordinates and are inverse continuous maps. The ratio carrier is
therefore the entire two-sided atom shift. No allowed word is removed.

In these coordinates, (2) becomes

\[
G(a,r)=(\sigma a,r/a_0),\quad
G^{-1}(a,r)=(\sigma^{-1}a,r a_{-1}),\quad
H_i(a,r)=(s_i a,r),
\tag{4}
\]

where s_i swaps coordinates i and i+1 and is its own inverse. Each
formula is continuous, and the displayed inverses prove the assertion.
QED.

### Proposition 2 — A genuine complete quotient action

Equation (3) defines a jointly continuous action for every real time.

**Proof.** B_t(a,r)=(a,e^t r) commutes with each G and H_i and hence
with every element of Gamma. It consequently acts on each equivalence
class independently of its representative. The group law and inverse
time -t descend from B.

The quotient projection q is open: for an open U, the preimage of
q(U) is the union of all gamma U, gamma in Gamma, which is open.
Thus id_R times q is an open quotient map. The jointly continuous map
(t,x) -> q(B_t x) factors through this map, proving joint continuity
of the descended action. Every real time already exists before taking
the quotient, so no finite-time endpoint or Zeno suspension needs to
be filled in. This does not establish a positive first-return roof or
Hausdorff topology. QED.

### Proposition 3 — An exact exchange holonomy

Composition is read right to left. On every full state,

\[
G^{-1}H_{-1}GH_0(a,r)=(a,r a_0/a_1).
\tag{5}
\]

**Proof.** Put b=s_0 a, so b_0=a_1 and
sigma b=s_{-1} sigma a. The four steps are

\[
(a,r)\xmapsto{H_0}(b,r)
\xmapsto{G}(s_{-1}\sigma a,r/a_1)
\xmapsto{H_{-1}}(\sigma a,r/a_1)
\xmapsto{G^{-1}}(a,r a_0/a_1),
\]

where the last multiplier is (sigma a)_{-1}=a_0. Every arrow is a
generator of the frozen quotient. Hence log(a_0/a_1) is an actual
return time of [a,r], with either sign permitted in its stabilizer.
QED.

This is not an application of an infinite periodic block permutation.
Only the two displayed finite exchanges and two scale-shifts are used.

### Proposition 4 — A retained mixed state has dense proper returns

Let a_j=2 for even j and a_j=3 for odd j, and let z=[a,r] for any r>0.
Its return group

\[
\operatorname{Stab}(z)=\{t\in\mathbb R:\Phi^t z=z\}
\]

is dense, proper and countable. In particular it has no least positive
element, and Y fails the topological T1 separation axiom.

**Proof.** This alternating chain is in the full source-derived
carrier. Since sigma^2 a=a, (4) gives G^2(a,r)=(a,r/6), so log 6
is a return time. Equation (5) supplies log(2/3). Consequently

\[
\mathbb Z\log6+\mathbb Z\log(2/3)
\subseteq\operatorname{Stab}(z).
\tag{6}
\]

The two generators have no nonzero integer relation: a relation with
coefficients m,n would give 2^{m+n}3^{m-n}=1, forcing m+n=m-n=0
by unique prime factorization. They therefore have irrational ratio.

For completeness, two real generators with irrational ratio yield
arbitrarily small nonzero integer combinations. Divide one by the
other and place the fractional parts of its first N+1 multiples in
N equal intervals; two lie in one interval. Their difference differs
from an integer by less than 1/N and is nonzero by irrationality.
After rescaling and taking a sign, this gives arbitrarily small
positive elements of (6). Integer multiples of those small elements
approximate any real number, proving density.

Every finite generator word changes r by a positive rational factor:
G and its inverse do so, and H_i leave it unchanged. Therefore any
return time satisfies exp(t) in Q_{>0}; the full stabilizer is a
subset of the countable set log Q_{>0}. It is proper in R, even
though it contains the dense subgroup (6). This proves all stated
return properties without classifying every possible arrow.

Finally, the continuous orbit map t -> Phi^t z has preimage
Stab(z) over the singleton {z}. That subgroup is dense and proper,
so it is not closed. Thus {z} cannot be closed in Y. QED.

### Proposition 5 — The constant-atom control retains discrete time

For the constant chain a_j=p, where p is any prime,

\[
\operatorname{Stab}([a,r])=(\log p)\mathbb Z.
\tag{7}
\]

**Proof.** Every H_i fixes this entire chain and the scale. G leaves
the chain fixed and multiplies scale by p^{-1}. Thus every generator
word acts on its scale by an integral power of p, and every such
power occurs. Equality in the quotient is therefore precisely
exp(t)=p^k for an integer k. The least positive return is log p,
and its positive repetitions are exactly r log p for positive
integers r. Different constant primes cannot be identified, since
no generator leaves one constant chain for another. QED.

These constant-chain orbits are valid controls. They are not a license
to discard the mixed returning states proved in Proposition 4.
