# Prime continued-fraction digits are an external observable, not a prime-symbolic source

**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-91`  
**Status:** `PRE-P0 STOP — PRIME-DIGIT PERIODIC WORDS BELONG TO THE GAUSS CODING, NOT TO ORDINARY PRIME DISTRIBUTION`  
**Route state:** no classical P0 or formal Route-A coordinate; Route B `NOT INVOKED`.

A Gauss-map orbit can be coded by continued-fraction digits, and its periodic
digit words genuinely give periodic dynamics.  Restricting those digits to
primes, however, only filters the already available countable alphabet.  In
particular, every finite word of prime digits is admitted by the full
continued-fraction coding and yields a periodic symbolic word.  This makes the
construction a useful `PROVES_TOO_MUCH` external symbolic control, rather than
an endogenous model of the ordinary prime/composite sequence.
