# Evidence and source lock — `ATF-20260914-WET01`

Primary source: Baptiste Morin,
[*Sur l'analogie entre le système dynamique de Deninger et le topos Weil-étale*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.0527).

Frozen source assertions:

- the Weil–étale topos of a number ring has a canonical morphism to `B_R`;
- a finite place `v` has a closed embedding from the classifying topos of its
  Weil group, and the associated homomorphism into `R` has period `log N(v)`;
- for `Spec Z`, finite closed points are `(p)`, so the time is `log p`.

Boundary sources: Morin presents the construction as a topos
formulation/analogy for a conjectural dynamical system, and the carrier is not
a point-set flow. Flach and Morin's Weil–étale cohomology work relates
cohomology to zeta special values under stated analytic hypotheses, but that
does not supply this card with a dynamical trace determinant.

Supporting source: Matthias Flach and Baptiste Morin,
[*On the Weil-étale topos of regular arithmetic schemes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3833).
