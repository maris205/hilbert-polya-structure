**Scope:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260919-PSR01`. **Date:** 2026-09-19.

## Primary source

Greg Back and Mihai Caragiu, *The Greatest Prime Factor and Recurrent
Sequences*, Fibonacci Quarterly 48(4) (2010), 358–362.
[Publisher's complete PDF](https://www.fq.math.ca/Papers1/48-4/Back_Caragiu.pdf).
The journal's [issue contents](https://www.fq.math.ca/48-4.html) confirms
the bibliographic identity. Retrieval used the publisher, not a secondary
summary or a prime-sequence database as authority.

The first non-www PDF open failed. Search recovered the www publisher URL;
the complete five-page text was then accessible. Theorem 3 on printed
page 359 explicitly requires unequal initial values in the prime-sequence
setting; its proof continues on page 360. The lemma on page 359 also
recognizes idempotence of the prime binary operation. The broad wording
in the abstract is not a substitute for the theorem's hypotheses.

No quotation longer than a phrase is needed. The present audit applies the
theorem only to distinct PRIME coordinates already on a periodic orbit.
It does not assert that every unequal pair of positive integers enters the
four-cycle. The local identity (2,6) -> (6,2) -> (2,2) is an adverse test
against that stronger claim, not a new statement attributed to the source.
