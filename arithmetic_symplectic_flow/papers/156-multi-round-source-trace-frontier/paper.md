## Abstract

This paper integrates three authorized breadth rounds without constructing
a composite mathematical candidate. Seven changed-map/carrier constructions,
two separately frozen analytic-owner contracts and one changed-roof contract
were audited. Four new geometric constructions have complete prime-exclusive
hyperbolic packet ledgers and owned ordinary zetas. A specified full-map
differential-form pullback contract additionally has legitimate distributional
flat traces and a graded identity with its own orbit zeta. The five scoped
stops identify dense nonprimitive return groups, mixed or continuous packet
multiplicity, failed particular trace prescriptions, and incomplete variable
roof time. The surviving analytic statement is materially stronger than an
ordinary orbit product, but remains distinct from a trace-class Fredholm
realization, exact prime-log timing, analytic continuation, or a target-divisor
identity. No positive component is transferred between candidate IDs.

## 2. Preserved source lineage and what changed

All classical constructions begin from the prior-work prime/composite
divisor-exclusion symbol, not generic geometry decorated with a prime label.
Their common elementary distinction is

\[
a(n)=\sum_{2\le d<n}1_{\{d\mid n\}}=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n\text{ is prime},\qquad n\ge2.
\]

The candidate maps actually execute the divisor witnesses and use their
nonnegative integer gap in full periodic equations. Every integer-labelled
component is present before that equation is solved. A fixed all-integer
carrier is not a separately chosen prime-sized object, and no centres or
zero sections are selected to eliminate unwanted periodic states.

There are nevertheless important non-equivalences. These objects retain n
along a trajectory and test all integer fibres in parallel. They do not
construct a chronological infinite sieve orbit, prove a source conjugacy,
or establish a uniquely privileged arithmetic geometry. Their linkage to
the [prior-work programme](../../docs/prior_work/README.md) is an explicit
constraint deformation and conservative realization, with that limitation.

The all-integer binary phase convention is

\[
K_n=\max(1,\lfloor\log_2(n-1)\rfloor).
\]

For 147 and 149 the phases execute local dyadic blocks of divisor tests.
For 152 and 154 the complete count a(n) is executed at every macrostep;
one full phase cycle therefore costs K_n(n-2) direct tests under the stated
implementation, rather than one scan partitioned into K_n steps. The
retained unit roofs genuinely own macrotime, but that time is not elementary
runtime. This distinction prevents an algorithmic speed claim from being
smuggled into a geometric clock claim.

The broadened paths deform different source features. 146 replaces local
divisibility by power-saturated denominator admissibility and actual
localization equality. 148 derives prime ratios from covers in the
divisibility order before forming normalized chains. Neither starts with
an externally supplied prime alphabet or clock, but neither preserves the
classical geometric carrier contract.

## 3. Round 1: separate clock, geometry and multiplicity tests

### 3.1 A genuine exact clock that does not close the full primitive ledger

[146's complete proof](../146-localization-scaling-groupoid/paper.md) uses all
actual rings A=Z[1/n], identifying equal rings rather than selecting an integer
representative. Its multiplication-unit group acts by u to u+log q, and the
same real-coordinate translation supplies time. The time-return subgroup is

\[
\Lambda_A=\log U_A=\sum_{p\in S(A)}\mathbb Z\log p.
\]

Rank-one components are genuinely single circles with primitive time log p.
This is an owned logarithmic action, not a manually inserted prime roof.
But every higher-rank component has a dense proper return subgroup with no
least positive return. Infinitely many distinct higher-rank components also
return at each fixed time log p. Their existence survives actual-localization
equality and circle-basepoint identification.

The decisive stop concerns the full primitive/repetition convention and its
ordinary product. It does not say that every conceivable groupoid trace is
impossible. Conversely, discarding all higher-rank components would change
the owner. In particular, 146 cannot donate its log p times to 153: neither
its carrier, action nor full return convention is that of the latter's
unit-roof symplectic map.

### 3.2 Two cotangent constructions with full isolated prime packets

[147](../147-saturated-drift-cotangent-sieve/paper.md) places local witnesses
inside saturated configuration drift and then takes the exact cotangent
lift. A full composite scan has more positive witness displacement than
the bounded negative drift can cancel. On a prime fibre the only periodic
configuration is zero, and the full momentum return equation forces zero
momentum. The complete intrinsic packet therefore has one cyclic phase
orbit per prime, with monodromy diag((3/2)^K_p,(2/3)^K_p).

[149](../149-coupled-convex-cotangent-sieve/paper.md) uses two genuinely coupled
configuration variables and their full cotangent geometry. Global
invertibility, composite exclusion, prime configuration recurrence and
momentum exclusion are each proved for the new map. Its four primitive
multipliers are (1+1/(4K_p))^K_p, (1+3/(4K_p))^K_p and their reciprocals.
This is not a passive hyperbolic factor attached after selecting a packet.

Both own a complete unit suspension, primitive lengths K_p, repetitions
r K_p and their own ordinary product. Nonzero monodromy denominators alone
do not provide either package with an operator trace.

### 3.3 Two exact source positives stopped by their full multiplicity

[148](../148-saturated-divisibility-chain-shift/paper.md) has a full-shift
ratio coding derived from divisibility covers. All primitive finite ratio
words occur, not only constant prime words. The mixed word (2,3) is a
primitive length-two packet with product 6. Constant-prime packets already
give infinitely many length-one packets, so its ordinary unit-roof
logarithmic series has no absolute convergence half-plane. The valid
multiplicative observation is not a logarithmic time coordinate.

[150](../150-witness-kicked-cylinder/paper.md) successfully excludes all
composite periodic support using momentum-kick sums. However, every rational
momentum over a prime admits arbitrary circular positions. Quotienting by
the finite phase rotation still leaves continuum many distinct primitive
packets. Already n=2 and zero momentum force ordinary-product failure.
The stop is full intrinsic multiplicity, not absence of an arithmetic
selection mechanism.

### 4.2 A direct coupled Hénon route is also geometrically positive

[152](../152-coupled-witness-henon-escape/paper.md) is a new four-dimensional

composite integer count a>=1, one component of the periodic second-difference

power has only the origin as a fixed vector. The retained phase gives exactly
one prime packet, not one packet per phase point.

Its same-map hyperbolicity and ordinary zeta are established, but its chosen
macroclock and general finite-constraint interpolation remain explicit.
No analytical owner is transferred from a cotangent comparator.

### 4.3 The strongest current analytic result has an actual full-map owner

[153](../153-saturated-sieve-flat-trace/paper.md) explicitly freezes the full
saturated-drift cotangent map, its unit suspension and the pullbacks

\[
U_j=F^*:C_c^\infty(M;\Lambda^jT^*M)
\longrightarrow C_c^\infty(M;\Lambda^jT^*M),\qquad j=0,1,2.
\]

All integer components, all positions and all momenta belong to these
domains. For every positive m, the full fixed set is finite, with

\[
N_m=\sum_{K_p\mid m}K_p,\qquad
J_m=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda^m,\lambda^{-m}),\quad\lambda=3/2.
\]

Every graph/diagonal intersection is transverse. Hence the actual full
distributional graph kernel has a well-defined diagonal restriction,
supported on that finite fixed set; its integral is the flat trace.
No operator was first cropped to selected periodic points. A cutoff used
to integrate an already compactly supported restricted distribution does
not change the full operator domain.

Writing B_m=lambda^m+lambda^(-m)-2, the paper derives

\[
T_0(m)=T_2(m)=\frac{N_m}{B_m},\qquad
T_1(m)=N_m+2\frac{N_m}{B_m},\qquad
T_0-T_1+T_2=-N_m.
\]

The numerator weights are the traces of the actual exterior powers of
the derivative, and the denominator is the absolute transverse Jacobian.
The minus sign is not a manually prescribed prime weight. With the frozen
normalization

\[
D_j(s)=\exp\left(-\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{e^{-sm}}mT_j(m)\right),
\]

the exact identity on the common absolute-convergence half-plane is

\[
\frac{D_0(s)D_2(s)}{D_1(s)}=Z(s),\qquad \Re s>\log2,
\qquad Z(s)=\prod_p(1-e^{-sK_p})^{-1}.
\]

The scalar D_0=D_2 logarithmic series has exact absolute-convergence
abscissa log(4/3); D_1 and Z have abscissa log 2. These are statements
about their defining series, not natural-boundary or continuation claims.

This advances beyond a formal orbit product to an actual distributional
trace of specified full operators. It still does not construct a
trace-class Fredholm determinant on a Banach or Hilbert space. The scalar
area-L2 pullback is a noncompact multiple of a unitary; the one-form
operators here are test-section operators, without an asserted default
L2 boundedness theorem. A nuclear or anisotropic realization remains OPEN.

## 5. Round 3: a simpler direct force and a decisive completeness stop

[154](../154-algebraic-witness-henon-sieve/paper.md) uses the full real-algebraic
force g_a(t)=(1-a)t+a sqrt(1+t^2) in a planar Hénon map. For integer a>=1
the force is strictly positive on all real t, so a periodic second-difference
sum is impossible. At a=0 the exact step matrix has trace 3 and determinant
one, and no positive power has a nonzero fixed vector. This gives the
complete one-packet-per-prime hyperbolic ledger inside the same map.
It is a fixed direct force, not a cotangent map or borrowed transverse
stability. The ordinary zeta is owned, but clock/trace obligations remain.

[155](../155-derivative-roof-completeness-test/paper.md) instead changes the
saturated cotangent map's roof to log(1+(1/2)sech^2 q). The formula is smooth
and positive at every finite state but has infimum zero. Already on the
allowed n=2 fibre, each q_0>0 grows at least linearly, and the full forward
roof sum is bounded by a convergent geometric series. A continuous observable
on the actual glued space tends to infinity along this finite-time path,
so no endpoint exists in the frozen carrier. Thus the complete-flow P0
condition fails before any flow A1/A2 advancement. Positive roof sums on
the central prime base cycles do not repair omitted escaping states.

## 6. Controls and surviving limitations

Three recurring controls distinguish constructive achievement from a stronger
target interpretation. Removing divisor witnesses changes recurrence to all
integer labels; replacing them by block cardinalities or shifted divisibility
changes the selected zero set; retaining every continuous coordinate tests
whether packet finiteness is intrinsic. The last test separates the positive
maps from 150's continuum. All comparator maps remain separate owners.

The PROVES_TOO_MUCH finding also survives: related finite nonnegative integer
constraint counts can be realized by similar mechanisms. Thus exact prime
selection alone does not establish privileged Riemann naturalness. This is a
limitation of interpretation, not a reason to deny the proved full-state
construction or impose an unwritten uniqueness requirement on every model.

All surviving classical lengths satisfy K_p=log(p)/log(2)+O(1), but K_5=K_7=2.
No constant rescaling separates those coincident lengths into log 5 and log 7.
The rounded clock loses exact prime information at the length level even
though the full primitive packets retain their different integer labels.
Neither 146's exact clock nor a changed derivative roof can be substituted
without a new candidate and a new complete-state audit.

The geometric results concern transverse monodromy of classified periodic
points, not global uniform hyperbolicity or a compact Anosov realization.
The mapping tori are odd-dimensional and are not automatically Hamiltonian,
contact or quantum systems. No prime-power/von-Mangoldt trace, completed
Riemann divisor, zero match or spectral realization is claimed.
