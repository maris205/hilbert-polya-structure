We prove their complete branch inverses, positive finite pointwise IMAGE densities, every-Borel change of variables, and full signed-lag groupoid clocks. MAIN therefore stops on a necessary arithmetic condition. The two controls have their own adverse fixed packets. No larger periodic census or global absence claim is made.

## 2. Complete cells, self-maps and actual inverses

Write \(j=\lfloor au\rfloor\). Since \(0\le u<1\),
\[
b=a^2+j,\quad 0\le j<a,\qquad g=\gcd(a,j).
\]
Conversely every such pair has the nonempty actual cell
\[
I_{ab}=\left(\frac a{b+1},\frac ab\right].
\]
Indeed \(a/x=b+v\) with \(0\le v<1\) gives \(u=(j+v)/a\in[0,1)\), recovering both floor labels. These cells partition \((0,1]\); all other \(a,b\ge1\) cells in the card are empty. This is a classification of the entire infinite label set, not a cutoff. For \(j=0\), \(g=a\); units and exact divisibility are not exceptional deletions.

For M set \(h=g\), and for C set \(h=1\). On each cell define
\[
\delta=h-a,\qquad K=b+a\delta=j+ah>0.
\]
Then, independently for these two owners,
\[
T_h(x)=\frac{a-bx}{1+\delta x},\qquad
T_U(x)=\frac{a-bx}{gx}.
\]
Here \(1+\delta x=x(h+u)>0\). The original formulas give \(0\le T_O(x)<1\) for every legal source and every owner. Hence all three are Borel partial self-maps; no source \(x>0\) becomes terminal by a hidden denominator restriction.

The complete inverses and their exact target domains are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{h,ab}(t)&=\frac{a-t}{b+\delta t},
&\Omega_{h,ab}&=\left[0,\frac a{K+1}\right),\\
\theta_{U,ab}(t)&=\frac a{b+gt},
&\Omega_{U,ab}&=[0,1/g).
\end{aligned} \tag{1}
\]
For proof, parametrize the whole source cell by \(x=a/(b+v)\), \(0\le v<1\). Its own outputs are \(av/(K+v)\) and \(v/g\), strictly increasing in \(v\), with exactly the ranges in (1). Solving these equations gives the displayed inverses and restores \(u=(j+v)/a\), both floor labels, and the own forward equality. Thus (1) is necessary and sufficient for every frozen source check. Empty cells have no inverse.

All denominators in (1) are positive on their actual domains: \(b+\delta t\ge b+h-a>0\) for \(0\le t\le1\), and \(b+gt>0\). At target \(0\), every nonempty branch gives the actual source \(a/b\). No branch has target \(1\); this is absence of a predecessor, not deletion of that object. The legal source \(1\) maps to \(0\). Every source cut uses its actual right-closed cell; an excluded left limit is never assigned the preceding cell's germ.

## 4. Full histories, all kernels, terminal states and time

Fix one owner O throughout this section. A legal \(m\)-iterate requires exactly its \(m\) steps; its final state may be \(0\). Write
\[
W_m(z)=\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}\rho_O(T_O^iz),\quad S_m(z)=\log W_m(z),\quad

\]

\[
G_O=\{(z,m-n,w):T_O^mz=T_O^nw\text{ legally}\},\qquad
c(z,m-n,w)=\log\frac{W_m(z)}{W_n(w)}. \tag{5}
\]
Source is \(w\), range is \(z\), inverse negates the lag, and multiplication adds lags. Equal actual triples alone are identified. Countably many inverse labels give countable fibres; Borel legality and equality give a Borel subset of \(X\times\mathbb Z\times X\).

To check (5), two witnesses of the same triple have lengths differing by a common integer. From the shorter common meeting, any additional legal steps have identical values and clocks on both sides, so they cancel. Composition is also legitimate at terminals: align the two middle histories at their larger already-legal length; the matching opposite history can follow the same available suffix. The resulting clock is the sum. No argument extends a terminal beyond its legal history.

Source isotropy is trivial precisely when the source is not eventually periodic.
