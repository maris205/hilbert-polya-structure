## Abstract

This breadth screen compares a proposed projective residue action
with one source-defined multidimensional continued-fraction flow.
Neither is admitted as a new main candidate. The first enlarges a
residue source but does not yet explain prime-selective admissibility
or returns. The second genuinely owns a geometric clock; its missing
item is the project's arithmetic lineage interface, together with any
needed pointwise boundary completion. These are bounded admission
findings, not new obstruction theorems. No Jacobian or periodic census
is computed and no earlier negative result is transferred.

## 1. Frozen question and owner separation

The [version-1 screen](candidate-card.md) precommits two distinct lanes.
The question is whether either supplies enough of the requested
prime/composite symbolic source AND its own time mechanism to warrant
a new full candidate audit. The [prior-work guide](../../docs/prior_work/README.md)
requires a specific preserved, deformed or replaced admissibility rule,
not just arithmetic terminology or a higher-dimensional carrier.

|---|---|---|
| P | All projective residue lines, all rational projective transformations, proposed finite-level measure and image-clock extension | Connection from prime/composite admissibility to this action's actual return structure; clock derivation not undertaken |
| B | The source-defined unsorted three-dimensional Brun natural extension and its flow | Exact project-lineage map and any pointwise completion needed to retain that arithmetic source |

These rows are not combined. Lane B's actual time is not the proposed

## 2. Lane P — an enlarged source is not yet a prime-return mechanism

The scout's concrete proposal is X=lim P¹(Z/mZ), acted on by all
Gamma=PGL₂(Q), retaining every compatible local projective line,
including infinity. Finite residue-level uniform measures are proposed
as a compatible source law. A full IMAGE Jacobian, its continuous
logarithmic cocycle and its real extension would need an owner audit.
No value for that cocycle is asserted in this record.

Its stated source interface is meaningful but incomplete for admission:
numerator/denominator residue observations extend divisibility symbols,
and reciprocal transformations exchange their roles. This is more
specific than saying that a geometric space is arithmetic. It still
does not specify how prime/composite admissibility constrains actual
arrow compositions or selects the intended intrinsic return structure.

The full transformation group is an exact action proposal; the lack of
a preferred single generator does NOT make it mathematically undefined
or violate the broadened groupoid track. Nor is a state-dependent rule
a universal necessary condition for every successful candidate. The
deficiency here is the missing proposed arithmetic-to-return mechanism,
not a categorical prohibition on group actions. Merely adding inversion
to a previous source does not supply that evidence.

The closest comparison, [275](../275-rational-affine-residue-flow/candidate-card.md),
has an affine residue carrier with its own proved clock and returns.
Those conclusions do not extend automatically to X. In particular,
neither a dense time stabilizer at infinity nor absence of primitive
packets anywhere is proved for lane P. Looking at the FULL stabilizer,
without selecting a prime dilation, is an appropriate future test only
after a justified admission and fresh full-owner contract.

**Decision:** NO MAIN ADMISSION / FORK at the present definition stage.
This is a search-priority decision under the breadth mandate, not a
failed theorem or proof that the proposal can never work. No new long
projective-groupoid calculation is launched merely to repeat 275.

## 3. Lane B — a real geometric clock, with a separate lineage gap

### Verified source facts

Arnoux–Labbé's section 6 defines Brun on strict ordering cones:
B_pi=I+E_(pi(3),pi(2)), F(x)=B_pi^(-1)x and
Ftilde(x,alpha)=(B_pi^(-1)x,B_pi^T alpha). The domain is
D=union_pi Lambda_pi×{alpha>0:alpha_pi(1)<alpha_pi(3)}.
Proposition 26 supplies branch inverses; its preceding Lemma 25
identifies the domain unions almost everywhere.

Section 3 defines D1 by x·alpha=1, Omega=D1/Ftilde, and the flow
phi_t(x,alpha)=(exp(t)x,exp(−t)alpha). On the unit-sum section,
Remark 10 gives return time −log||F(x)||_1. Remark 9 qualifies the
section identification almost everywhere. Section 2 Remark 1 explains
the omitted equal-coordinate convention and its use in later examples.
[Primary source, exact v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.07814v3).

### Project admission inference — not a source theorem

This supplies genuine state feedback and a time belonging to its own
geometry. Calling that clock absent would misrepresent the source.
However, neither the supplied proposal nor the checked passages gives
an exact map from this project's prime/composite divisor witnesses to
Brun admissibility. That is the decisive lineage gate, before P0.

Additionally, an application that retains arithmetic boundary states
must specify how they belong to a complete pointwise action. The
source's measure-theoretic convention alone is not that specification.
This is NOT a claim that all arithmetic information is located on
those boundaries, or that a satisfactory completion is impossible.
No Brun integer/rational orbit theorem is inferred from the source's
separate Farey example, and no periodic classification is attempted.

**Decision:** EXTERNAL CONTROL / NO MAIN ADMISSION. Keep the positive
source-owned clock as a design reference; do not promote it through
terminology, label prime branches, or attach it to lane P.

The concrete distinction from [168-E](../168-source-return-breadth-frontier/paper.md)
is that this source does supply an extension/time construction, whereas
that record tested a terminating integer execution. The warning in
[127](../127-prime-continued-fraction-digit-boundary/candidate-card.md)
about prime labels is still relevant, without identifying the two maps.

## 5. Controls, assessment and next decision

The strongest counterarguments are retained. A full group action can
be a legitimate broadened owner without a singled-out map; an
almost-everywhere geometric model can be a valid research result;
and the source really has a return clock. None by itself supplies
the required project-specific arithmetic interface. Conversely, a
missing interface does not invalidate the source's mathematics.

Ownership controls prohibit importing 275's stabilizer theorem,
280's product-clock theorem, or lane B's time into lane P. Source
controls retain the a.e. qualifications without turning them into
a universal no-go. PROVES_TOO_MUCH is a risk of prime relabelling,
not an established universality theorem for either lane. No chosen
subgroup, boundary deletion, prime table, manual prime roof, fitted
parameter or selected periodic sector is used.

on an unchanged full owner, not more matrices or a borrowed roof.
