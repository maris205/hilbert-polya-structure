## Lineage and A0 audit

The present programme requires a documented chain from prime distribution / prime-composite observables through sieve-symbolic admissibility and justified sequential deformation, then through the Logistic/Hénon conservative bridge. Coprimality of arbitrary adjacent labels is an arithmetic condition, but it is not the Eratosthenes survivor update: it neither reads the next sieve prime from its own state nor marks, toggles, or evolves a prime/composite word.

The distinction is decisive. A graph realization of this countable shift could preserve the allowed-edge relation while arbitrarily relabelling the vertices. The integer names and their gcd relation would then be presentation data unless the map itself supplies a canonical arithmetic interpretation. Selecting a countable horseshoe or inserting the coprime graph into a Hénon coding would therefore repeat the external-language failure of 012 and 046. Conversely, interpreting gcd labels as visible lattice strata repeats the static-decoration failure of 056--060.
