## Boundary

For a family ((X_p,F_p)), suppose prime (p) is chosen first and then
determines a finite carrier, modulus, network size, or map instance. Exact
cycles of (F_p) belong to that one selected object. Their numerical relation
to (p) does not derive (p) from the action.

| Control | Prime-selected datum | Retained feature | Missing owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 088 finite-field Hénon | (X_p=\mathbb F_p^2) | reversible finite permutation | internal prime selection |
| Q2R network | (2p) vertices | limit cycle of length (p) | internal prime selection |
| Arnold cat map on prime lattices | prime lattice L_p | common nonzero orbit period for each selected lattice | internal prime selection |

A disjoint union over primes would be a new carrier. It requires its own
action, arithmetic mechanism, packet convention, and recurrence audit. It
cannot inherit the separate finite cycles as one prime-orbit ledger.

## Decision

**Portfolio position: fork.** Reject prime-modulus, prime-size, and
prime-indexed finite systems at pre-P0 unless one fixed carrier/action creates
its own arithmetic selection and return data.
