## Frozen broadened object

Deninger attaches to an integral normal arithmetic scheme an infinite-dimensional continuous-time dynamical system \(X_0\). Closed points \(x_0\) correspond bijectively to compact packets \(\Gamma_{x_0}\) of periodic orbits, with every orbit in the packet having length \(\log N x_0\). Each periodic orbit belongs to one packet, and the packet fibres over a compact group; this multiplicity is part of the frozen orbit convention.

For \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\), the closed points are the prime ideals \((p)\). Hence the construction gives a genuine prime-labelled packet family with source-derived length \(\log p\), rather than a manually imposed prime clock. The source also states that the constructed space is infinite dimensional and leaves the existence of a suitable lower-dimensional subsystem as a question.
