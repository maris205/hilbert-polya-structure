## 2. SC20-IDX: a genuine rank-one size invariant, not a naturalness theorem

Freeze E=End_+(Z), the injective additive endomorphisms taking positive
integers to positive integers, and N(alpha)=card(Z/alpha(Z)).

**Lemma 1.** Every element is uniquely alpha_n(k)=nk for n>=1, and
N(alpha_n)=n. The map n->alpha_n is a multiplicative monoid isomorphism.
The cardinal is independent of a chosen cyclic generator.

**Proof.** Put n=alpha(1). Positivity makes n a positive integer.
Additivity gives alpha(k)=nk for all positive, zero and negative k.
Conversely multiplication by any n>=1 is positive and injective.
Its image is nZ; the n residues 0,...,n-1 are distinct and exhaustive.
Composition multiplies n and m. If g is either generator of an
infinite cyclic group, alpha_n(g)=ng; replacing g by -g changes
neither n nor this identity. Both automorphisms of Z, multiplication
by 1 or -1, conjugate alpha_n to itself. An intertwining group
isomorphism also induces a cokernel isomorphism. QED.

Reversing the chosen order leaves the same positive multiplication
maps. Removing positivity altogether would admit multipliers -n with
the same cokernel size n: they are not conjugate to +n. Thus cardinal
recovers the signed multiplier only in the declared positive category.

The induced monoid-algebra identification with the existing A sends
nonunits exactly to n>=2 and preserves factorization into two nonunits.
It identifies the proposed source grading
D[e_alpha]=N(alpha)[e_alpha] with the existing D. This is a
generator-free finite-cokernel interpretation of integer size; it does
not derive the decision to use I/I^2 or create a new prime orbit.

**Higher-rank control.** On Z^2 the injective maps 2I_2 and
diag(1,4) both have index 4. Their cokernels are respectively
(Z/2Z)^2 and Z/4Z. They are not conjugate, since every conjugate
of 2I_2 equals 2I_2. Extending by identity gives the same failure of
recovery for every higher rank. A scalar nI_r has n^r residue classes,
not n. Index is nevertheless multiplicative: for finite-index injections
alpha,beta on G=Z^r,

\[
 [G:\alpha\beta G]
 =[G:\alpha G]\,[\alpha G:\alpha\beta G]
 =[G:\alpha G]\,[G:\beta G].
 \tag{2}
\]

Multiply coset representatives for the first equality and use the
isomorphism alpha:G->alpha G for the second. No determinant
classification theorem is needed.

**Real-line control.** A positive real-linear dilation x->lambda x
is onto R for every lambda>0. Its additive cokernel is trivial,
of cardinal 1 independently of lambda. Recovering a dilation size by
adding a lattice would add data.

**Character control.** Any positive multiplicative character chi(n)
is also a generator-independent composition weight. Already
chi_c(n)=n^c, c>0, supplies distinct strictly increasing examples.
For c not equal to 1 these are not the cardinal of Z/nZ. They
therefore do not refute Lemma 1; they refute the stronger inference
that multiplicativity, positivity and presentation invariance alone
select the literal cardinal rule uniquely.

**Time control.** Keep the old D but use the separately declared
different radial speed exp(kappa t), kappa>0. The singleton equation
exp(kappa t)=a^j gives least time (log a)/kappa while index stays a.
No clock of 193 or 194 is actually modified in this control.

Decision: retain the rank-one size lemma; STOP promotion of this
clarification to complete arithmetic naturalness or natural A0.
Its canonical character is relative to the specified infinite-cyclic
category and literal finite cardinality. I/I^2, positivity, topology,
completion and physical speed are not forced by the lemma. Naturalness
remains OPEN, and reservation 200 does not become a new paper.

## 3. SC20-FM: an actual fixed cover group with no scalar quotient descent

This screen freezes the full original finite cone P, sigma=2,
H=ell^2(A times Z), and

\[
 (Jb)(v)=\sum_{a,k}b_{a,k}e^{-k^2}v_a^{2+ik},
 \qquad 0^{2+ik}=0,
 \tag{3}
\]

using the real logarithm on positive coordinates. No mode or mixed
state is removed. Put C_0=sum_k exp(-2k^2)<infinity and m(v)=sum_a v_a.

**Lemma 2 (whole-cover observable space).** J is well defined,
injective and continuous as a map to continuous cover functions with
uniform control on each mass-bounded set. Its image separates all
cover states and becomes a Hilbert space under the transported norm.

**Proof.** Cauchy--Schwarz gives the absolute-series estimate

\[
 \sum_{a,k}|b_{a,k}|e^{-k^2}v_a^2
 \le\sqrt{C_0}\|b\|_2\left(\sum_a v_a^4\right)^{1/2}
 \le\sqrt{C_0}\|b\|_2 m(v)^2.
 \tag{4}
\]

For arbitrary finite coefficient truncations the same bound on the
omitted ell^2 tail is uniform whenever m(v)<=R. Individual coordinate
powers are continuous also at zero, because their modulus is v_a^2.
The mass is continuous in the original topology, so the local uniform
limit is continuous even across support boundaries.

If Jb=0, evaluate the full function at e^x q_a, for any fixed a.
Dividing by e^(2x) gives sum_k b_(a,k)e^(-k^2)e^(ikx)=0.
Its coefficients are absolutely summable. Integrating against
e^(-i ell x) on [0,2pi] term by term forces
b_(a,ell)e^(-ell^2)=0 for every a,ell. This proves injection
without restricting the carrier to axes. Completeness follows by
transporting the ell^2 norm. Bounded point evaluations follow from
(4). The observables Je_(a,0)(v)=v_a^2 distinguish any two different
positive states, including mixed states. QED.

Write this complete image as mathscr H. The actual radial pullback is

\[
 R_t(Jb)=J(M_t b),\qquad
 (M_t b)_{a,k}=e^{(2+ik)t}b_{a,k}.
 \tag{5}
\]

It is a fixed strongly continuous group with inverse R_(-t) and norm
e^(2t). Strong continuity follows first for finitely many coefficients,
then for all b by a uniformly bounded tail estimate on compact time
intervals. This is a real positive result, not a quotient representation.
It is not unitary under this norm and does not embed in global C_b(P):
R_(2pi)=e^(4pi)I, so every nonzero image function is unbounded along
the sequence e^(2pi n)v at a point where its value is nonzero.

The actual inverse-deck pullback is

\[
 B(Jb)=J(Tb),\qquad
 (Tb)_{a,k}=a^{-(2+ik)}b_{a,k},\qquad \|B\|=1/4.
 \tag{6}
\]

These equalities hold on every full state by the absolutely convergent

norm(f)<=norm(f)/4, so f=0. The entire image contains no nonzero

Nor is (6) a representation of the full integer deck group on this

**Ordinary trace obstruction.** The functions Je_(a,k) form an

also noncompact. Gaussian decay ensures observable-series convergence

Decision: STOP the scalar-quotient and ordinary-trace aspiration,

## 4. The boundary-jet obstruction is a representation stop, not a flow stop

\[
 h(t)=\frac{3t}{t+2},\qquad w_s(t)=\left(\frac{t+2}{6}\right)^s,
 \qquad g_{\mathcal L_s f}(t)=w_s(t)g_f(h(t)).
 \tag{7}
\]

\[
 \mathcal L_s'\delta_j
 =3^{-s}(3/2)^j\delta_j+\sum_{i<j}A_{ji}\delta_i .
 \tag{8}
\]

Finite-jet surjectivity makes the first m+1 functionals independent.

norm(L_s)>=3^(-Re(s))(3/2)^m for every m, which is impossible.

These are dual-jet eigenvalues, not additional physical orbits.

The controls sharply limit the stop. Finitely many jets give only a

The original carrier, prime packets and actual clock remain intact.
The result stops only the specified jet-rich representation before
