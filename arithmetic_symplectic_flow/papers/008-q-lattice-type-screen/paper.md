# Q-lattices: arithmetic geometry is not yet a classical symplectic candidate

**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260913-07`  
**Status:** `P0 TYPE REJECTED; Route B NOT INVOKED`

The Bost–Connes and \(\mathrm{GL}_2\) Q-lattice programmes encode substantial arithmetic through noncommutative spaces of commensurability classes, groupoids, and \(C^*\)-dynamical systems. Their two-dimensional Q-lattice geometry and Shimura connections make them useful arithmetic controls.

They nevertheless do not provide the required P0 carrier: no cited result identifies the groupoid quotient/time evolution with a finite-dimensional smooth symplectic manifold, one single-valued \(C^r\) symplectomorphism, positive roof, and suspension flow. A groupoid correspondence is not made deterministic by selecting representatives. `fork`: retain only as a type-control against false semiclassical claims.
