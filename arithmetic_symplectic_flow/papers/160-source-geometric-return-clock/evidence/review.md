## Strongest counterargument

The sharp objection is not that the constructed flow fails to exist. It is
that the exact prime-log period can arise from two deliberately separated
engineering choices: witnesses determine which integer fibres recur, while
the scan interval from 1 to n, dilation velocity and reset identification
determine their elapsed times. Equation (14) identifies the latter input
precisely. The roof is cohomologous to a reset-edge contribution log n, and
the witness values do not appear in this identity. The same event map with
constant velocity has period n-1; translating every transit mark gives
another explicit period function. Therefore the equality of the resulting
ordinary product with the prime Euler product cannot, by itself, establish
that the sieve-symbolic source explains arithmetic logarithmic time.

This objection does not amount to a theorem that all such geometric
realizations are forbidden or that a clock must be uniquely determined.
The manuscript does not place one externally supplied prime in each
preselected carrier: it keeps all integers and proves the periodic
classification on full positive-dimensional planes. Nor does the
cohomological rewrite invalidate its positive roof or complete flow. The
supported distinction is between an exact engineered realization and the
programme's stronger source-clock claim. The paper explicitly preserves
that distinction and stops promotion. A future argument for the latter
would have to justify the clock mechanism from retained source structure,
not merely re-express the same total time in another coordinate.

1. **Inverse and symplectic form.** In Proposition 1, f'>1 and
   f(q)-q bounded on each fixed component give an onto diffeomorphism.
   Recovering q=f^{-1}(Q) and p=P f'(q) at the preceding phase is a
   full inverse. The identity P dQ=p dq verifies the claimed cotangent
   lift without selecting an invariant subset.
2. **Seam and completeness.** Proposition 2 uses the actual transition
   through F. The final collar r_new=r_old/n pushes r partial_r to the
   same vector field. For each orbit, the fixed positive number
   log(n/(n-1)) bounds all its roofs below. This remains true for inverse
   iterates. Infimum zero over other disconnected n fibres is not the
   sequential shrinking-roof mechanism in 155.
3. **No extra periodic states.** For m=r(n-1), the composite displacement
   in (9) is strictly greater than m(a(n)-1/2)>0. For a prime,
   every nonzero q escapes monotonically from zero in its sign direction;
   at q=0, momentum after m steps is (2/3)^m times its initial value,
   so only zero momentum returns. The derived orbit has exactly p-1
   phase points, not p-1 different primitive flow orbits.
4. **Orientation and stability.** Positive flow traversal is the
   orientation convention; cyclic section representatives identify
   one orbit and do not add an opposite-flow copy. The momentum
   cross derivative vanishes at the derived periodic states.
   The monodromy eigenvalues and determinant in (10)--(11) then exclude
   every positive repetition degeneracy.
5. **Scalar analytic scope.** Proposition 5 obtains the ordinary
   unweighted product from this full ledger. The majorant (13) gives
   absolute and locally uniform convergence for Re s>1. The finite Euler
   product/harmonic-sum argument proves divergence of the r=1 terms at
   the boundary, hence the stated exact absolute abscissa. This check
   supplies no trace identity or operator owner.
6. **Cohomology including n=2.** For an internal edge, h(Fz)-h(z) is
   log((d+1)/d); for the final edge it is -log(n-1), leaving precisely
   the log n reset term. At n=2 the difference is zero and the entire
   positive time is log2. There is no hidden negative-time reset.
