# Evidence — `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-36`

Ingram and Silverman's Theorem 1 proves the needed primitive-divisor statement
for a rational `phi` of degree at least two with `phi(0)=0`, not vanishing to
full degree there, and a rational point `alpha` of infinite orbit. Writing
`phi^n(alpha)=A_n/B_n` in lowest terms, every sufficiently large `A_n` has a
prime divisor absent from all earlier numerator terms.

Primary source: [Ingram--Silverman, *Primitive Divisors in Arithmetic
Dynamics*, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2505). This record uses only
the theorem's stated hypotheses and conclusion; it introduces no numerical
orbit, prime table, roof, or operator.
