# Prime-gap graph growth is input-driven, not an endogenous sieve flow

**Paper ID:** `037-prime-gap-graph-screen`  
**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-26`  
**Status:** `EXTERNAL CONTROL — PRE-P0 STOP`

The graph construction realizes a degree sequence whose entries are already the
consecutive prime gaps. Its recursive step attaches a new vertex with degree
equal to the next prime gap. Thus it is valuable combinatorics, but at the
object boundary the arithmetic sequence is input rather than read from a
sieve-symbolic state. It has no roof, closed-orbit convention, or symplectic
owner.

**Decision: stop.** It cannot enter the main portfolio without a new direct
sieve-lineage mechanism.
