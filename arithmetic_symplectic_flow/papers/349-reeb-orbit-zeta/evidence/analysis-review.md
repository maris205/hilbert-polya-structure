### J1a — convergence, Euler identity and sharpness

```text

```

logarithm is holomorphic by local uniform convergence. Grouping by p and
exponentiating yields the ordinary Euler product. Each finite product

prime set, by unique factorization. The absolute Dirichlet majorant permits
passage to the increasing prime-set limit. This proves `Z_orb=zeta` on

The argument for divergence of sum_p 1/p is complete: under the contrary
assumption, the k>=2 part of sum_p sum_k p^(−k)/k is bounded by

bounded. The product over primes<=N contains all n<=N with positive
coefficient 1 and dominates the divergent harmonic partial sum. For
sigma<=1, the absolute k=1 terms p^(−sigma) dominate 1/p. Hence the
claimed absolute-convergence half-plane is sharp without a prime-number
asymptotic or any zero theorem.

The classical continuation verified above then supplies the unique

## 3. J2: full scalar kernel and analytic function

### J2a — joint diagonal transversality and noncompact pushforward

The inverse physical flow on Q_p is
`([u−t],e^(−t)v,e^t z)`. Relative to du dv dz, the local kernel is the
product of its three point-evaluation Dirac distributions. Near a positive
return t=kL, restricting the joint kernel to the spatial diagonal imposes

```text
F(t,u,v,z)=(t−kL,(1−e^(−t))v,(1−e^t)z)=0.
```

At that zero set v=z=0, and the derivative in (t,v,z) is diagonal with
entries `1,1−e^(−kL),1−e^(kL)`, all nonzero. The map is a submersion
there. The elementary delta pullback/change-of-variables calculation is
therefore defined and gives

```text
delta(t−kL) delta(v) delta(z) / |(1−e^(−kL))(1−e^(kL))|.
```

Including t is essential: at a fixed singular return time the neutral u
direction alone would not justify an ordinary diagonal trace. The actual
joint map's time derivative supplies the missing rank. No fixed-time
trace is manufactured by this calculation.

The circle delta relative to du is the periodized real delta, so its
time restriction has coefficient 1, not 1/L. Integration over the whole
fixed circle then supplies exactly L. It does not supply kL for a repeated
return. This independently checks both the transverse denominator and
the primitive-length numerator.

On the real-u component there is no positive-time zero of the diagonal
constraint. For a compact positive time set, only finitely many (p,k)
occur, and the full restricted support lies on finitely many compact
central circles. Projection to time is thus proper on the restricted
support. This justifies integration over the entire noncompact Q and
the countable union of components. It neither imposes a spatial cutoff
nor changes the state space to the periodic locus.

The resulting scalar distribution is exactly

```text
Theta_0 = sum_(p,k>=1) (log p)/a(p,k) delta_(k log p),
a(p,k)=p^k+p^(−k)−2=p^k(1−p^(−k))^2.
```

It is a positive locally finite measure for positive time. The excluded
time zero is not silently regularized. **Disposition: PASS.**

### J2b — trace class and coefficient mismatch

Volume preservation and invertibility give an isometry with inverse U_−t
on the dense compactly supported smooth subspace, hence a unitary on all
of H. The Hilbert space has an infinite orthonormal sequence, obtained
by normalizing indicators of disjoint finite-positive-volume boxes.
Its images under any U_t remain orthonormal, so U_t is noncompact; also
`|U_t|=I` has infinite trace. This excludes trace class for every real t,
including 0, and excludes the ordinary trace-class prescription for

At log 2, the single contributing repetition has a(2,1)=1/2, so the

There is no constant repair: at log 3, a(3,1)=4/3, giving ratio 3/4
instead of 2. Both computations concern the same full flow and its actual
scalar kernel. The STOP is therefore correctly specific to the frozen
scalar identification. **Disposition: PASS.**

### J2c — sharp domain and shifted-product continuation


