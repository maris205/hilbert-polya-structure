## 2. C1 EXACT-REUSE: an existing lattice/digit specification

[301's original card](../301-sublattice-intersection-digit-flow/candidate-card.md),
Sections 1–3, specifies all actual finite-index sublattice pairs \((L,M)\),
\(K=\widehat{\mathbb Z}\), full \(K^2\) seeds, and root mass one with joint
normalized additive Haar measure. Its proposed column Hermite coordinate is
\(A_L=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
\(a,c\ge1,\ 0\le b<a\); its justification remains that original card's obligation.
Its exact digit/quotient prescription is
\[
j_2=x_2\bmod c,\quad y_2=(x_2-j_2)/c,\quad
j_1=(x_1-by_2)\bmod a,\quad y_1=(x_1-by_2-j_1)/a,
\]
followed by \(j=(j_1,j_2)\), \(N=(L\cap M)+\mathbb Zj\), and
\(T(L,M,x)=(M,N,y)\). These are the same clauses specified by C1.
Its inverse prescription retains every \((L,j)\) satisfying that actual
lattice equality and maps \((M,N,y)\) to \((L,M,j+A_Ly)\) on the full target seed.
Unit roots and all non-diagonal lattices are retained, not selected out.
The source-lineage interface specified there, for positive integers, is
\(n\mapsto L_n=n\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z\), with divisibility read through
reverse lattice inclusion. This is a specific arithmetic interface, not the
unsupported assertion that every lattice construction inherits prime dynamics.
C1 therefore specifies literal reuse, not an additional autonomous law.
301's proposed groupoid/IMAGE obligations remain with 301; this audit neither
re-proves them nor transfers a result, source lock, clock or Route status.
