## Construction screen

Let `A=[[2,1],[1,1]]`, with determinant one, and let `V` be the primitive vectors of `Z^2`. Because both `A` and `A^{-1}` are integral, `gcd(Av)=gcd(v)`; thus `A` preserves `V`. This is the direct spatial-sieve to conservative-geometry attempt after 056.

## Incompatibility

On `(R^2,dx wedge dy)`, `A` is symplectic and retains `V`. Its eigenvalues are `(3+sqrt(5))/2` and `(3-sqrt(5))/2`; neither positive power is one. Hence `A^n-I` is invertible over `R` for every `n>0`. The only periodic point in `R^2` is zero, which is not visible. Thus this same arithmetic carrier has no primitive visible orbit.

On the quotient torus, the induced cat map is symplectic and has ordinary periodic points. But the quotient map sends every integer vector, including every element of `V`, to `[0]`. The visible-point symbolic condition therefore has no nontrivial image from which a prime-divisibility packet can be recovered. Replacing it by a chosen torus subset would be a new external decoration, exactly the failure audited in 001 and 012.
