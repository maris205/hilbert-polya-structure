## Consequence for the live search

Neither an invertible action alone nor an arithmetic label alone meets the
target. A new screen records the following obligations for the same frozen
construction, marking unproved items OPEN. Under plan.md a precise source
deformation can be screened before its geometry is solved; completed A1/A2
results are not prerequisites for freezing a scope card.

1. a state-internal rule producing or testing the required prime-side symbolic observable, without a complete input word, prime-indexed schedule, selected modulus, or finite cutoff;
2. an intrinsically reversible/recurrent action with a natural primitive-packet and repetition convention; and
3. a prime-carrying state branch that itself belongs to a natural primitive packet, rather than being either an indiscriminate family of prime fixed points or a terminating branch disjoint from unrelated periodic packets; and
4. identity of the arithmetic-generating action and the packet action: a completed output permutation, lookup function, or functional graph may not supply cycles unless its cyclic state itself retains the state data that make the arithmetic update well-defined; and
5. a documented deformation or realization path from that action to a specified Hénon/conservative or symplectic owner.

Record 119 confirms that the first four conditions are not vacuous: a fixed
factorisation rule can carry a prime state through an actual same-action cycle.
It does not weaken the fifth condition or make a discrete arithmetic map a
classical carrier.  The live gap is now sharply geometric and lineage-specific,
not merely the absence of any arithmetic recurrence.

The fourth condition also applies when the apparent cycle belongs to a frozen
finite subsystem rather than to a post hoc function.  If the arithmetic rule
can append coordinates, pivots, clocks, history, or a new period before the
next update is defined, then fixing the currently installed finite subsystem
changes the owner.  Its finite cycle is not a packet of the growing action
unless the full arithmetic-update state itself returns.

The p-adic/profinite literature scan does not change this rule. Standard local dynamics starts after one prime \(p\) has fixed its base field/carrier; profinite wheel completions and the nonstationary S-adic source already have their own stopped records. Calling the collection of these selected local systems one action would pool different carriers and violate the one-object ledger.

The reversible-CA screen sharpens rather than reopens the computational branch.

An admissible reopening must therefore change the *arithmetic representation*
from temporal prime output to a source-defined spatial or packet-valued

The third condition is a same-action compatibility check, not an assertion that every admissible construction must make a prime itself a phase-space point. A documented prime-side observable may be carried by a packet through an explicit state factor; it may not be inferred from an unrelated cycle elsewhere in the carrier. Conversely, the SOPFR and GPF-Ducci controls show that merely fixing every prime also does not yield a selective packet.

The fourth condition addresses a distinct time-level error. A recursive procedure can define an eventual function or permutation only after a growing construction history has been formed. Cycles of that derived function do not return the construction state unless an explicit conjugacy preserves the full arithmetic selection data. Yellowstone is the current control; the condition applies equally to any proposed self-enumeration or post hoc lookup action.

The same ownership rule covers a finite wheel, residue array, or clock array
whose internal state cycles only after the prime-producing update has been
disabled.  The LCM/SSM generation screens and Prime Clockwork control show the
three forms of the error: a strictly advancing index, an expanding pivot/period
record, and an expanding coordinate list.  These are not variants to repair by
adding a roof or a natural extension.

The continued-fraction screen adds a complementary label-source control.  A
fixed chaotic or symbolic carrier may have a natural alphabet consisting of

prime table.  That fact is still insufficient: if the carrier admits every
chosen finite prime-digit word as a periodic word, the asserted prime packet
comes from the carrier's unrestricted coding rather than from the ordinary
prime/composite mechanism.  No induced map, roof, transfer operator, or
geometric realization changes that source ownership.

The modular-geodesic control makes the same point at the opposite end of the
pipeline.  A rich Hamiltonian/geometric closed-orbit theory and an analytic

the standard word for a primitive orbit is “prime.”  The primitive convention
is required for any orbit product, but it is not a map from ordinary primes to
packets.  Thus a future construction must preserve the source-level sieve
observable *before* it receives credit for a geometric primitive-orbit ledger.
