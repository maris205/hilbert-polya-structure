# Standard Hénon horseshoes cannot intrinsically realize the prime-gap language

The symbolic control `X_P` from 041 allows exactly prime-length zero gaps. A standard hyperbolic Hénon horseshoe is conjugate to a full finite-alphabet shift; more generally a finite Markov partition produces an SFT. These are complete intrinsic codes for that geometric basic set.

`X_P` is not SFT. If finitely many forbidden words described it, let `N` bound their lengths. Take a composite gap length `c>N` and take a sufficiently larger prime gap `p>c`. Every local word of length at most `N` in `10^c1` occurs in the permitted word `10^p1`: a short window cannot see both endpoint `1`s because the gaps exceed `N`; windows with one or no `1` occur in both. Thus a finite local rule cannot reject `10^c1` while allowing prime gaps. Contradiction.

Therefore no standard finite-Markov Hénon horseshoe has `X_P` as its complete intrinsic symbolic code. A full horseshoe can contain sequences satisfying the prime-gap rule, but selecting that invariant subset supplies an external infinite language, precisely the failure of 012 and 041. This result does not rule out a new nonuniform/countable-coded geometric construction with its own all-orbit ledger; none is supplied here.
