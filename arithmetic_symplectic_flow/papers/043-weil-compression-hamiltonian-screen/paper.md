## Finding

Paper 6 is a valuable reverse-direction benchmark: prime-power data enter Weil's explicit formula and evaluate a finite Hermitian compression. A Hermitian matrix can be realified as a quadratic linear Hamiltonian system, but that operation occurs after the matrix and its finite test family have been supplied.

The proposed linear flow has eigenmode trajectories determined by the already formed matrix. These trajectories are not the prime-power terms used to form its entries. Identifying an eigenfrequency with a prime/power would therefore be an external label assignment. Changing the finite test family changes the matrix and the putative flow. No symbolic coding, sequential deformation, Hénon lift, specific symplectic return map, roof, primitive arithmetic orbit ledger, or dynamical zeta is present.
