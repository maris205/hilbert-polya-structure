## Abstract

The entire completed arithmetic quotient now has a fixed Hilbert
space of bounded continuous scalar observations that contains constants,
separates all states and carries actual time as a strongly continuous
bounded group. Its designed norm is nonunitary and its time operators
are noncompact. A separate theorem classifies every invariant Borel

singleton circles; an explicit nonzero continuous mixed-state
observation is lost in every corresponding L2 space. Two short screens
stop a continuous separating flow-invariant kernel and a prescribed
constant-fiber deck gluing. These are compatible results for different
observation contracts, not a universal Hilbert obstruction or a
combined trace/spectral construction. Every mixed finite and infinite
state, the original topology and the actual clock remain intact.
Arithmetic and representation naturalness remain OPEN.

## 2. SC21-UK: asymptotic states prohibit a separating invariant kernel

The frozen contract asks for a finite-valued, jointly continuous,
Hermitian positive-definite kernel K on the whole X_hat times X_hat,
invariant under simultaneous actual flow, and with distinct kernel
sections at different states. No uniform bound on K(x,x) is assumed.

**Proposition UK.** No kernel satisfies this entire contract.

**Proof.** The full carrier contains the two different points

\[
 x=[q_2+q_3],\qquad y=[q_2],\qquad T=\log2.
 \tag{3}
\]

They differ because integer deck action preserves support. For every
integer n>=0, the actual quotient flow satisfies

\[
 \phi_{nT}x=[q_2+(2/3)^n q_3]\longrightarrow[q_2]=y,
 \qquad \phi_{nT}y=y.
 \tag{4}
\]

Indeed 2^n D^(-n)(q_2+q_3) is the representative in (4); its
difference from q_2 has k-th norm 3^k(2/3)^n, tending to zero for
every fixed k. Quotient continuity proves the displayed convergence.
This is a full-space test at retained states, not a new two-atom
carrier or a finite-support extrapolation.

For a positive-definite kernel set

\[
 d_K(x,y)^2=K(x,x)+K(y,y)-2\operatorname{Re}K(x,y)\ge0.
 \tag{5}
\]

Simultaneous flow invariance makes (5) unchanged when both arguments
are advanced by nT. Joint continuity and (4) make those advanced
values tend to d_K(y,y)^2=0. Hence d_K(x,y)=0.

Positivity on finite formal linear combinations gives the
Cauchy--Schwarz inequality for the combination of kernel sections
at x minus y and the section at any z:

\[
 |K(x,z)-K(y,z)|^2\le d_K(x,y)^2 K(z,z)=0.
 \tag{6}
\]

For completeness, this inequality follows by applying nonnegativity
to the quadratic expression in a complex coefficient multiplying
one of the two combinations; when one squared norm is zero, varying
that coefficient forces the cross term to vanish. Thus the sections
at distinct x,y are identical, contradicting point separation. QED.

Decision: STOP only the jointly continuous, separating, invariant
kernel contract. In its reproducing-kernel interpretation, simultaneous
invariance is the unitary observation requirement. The contradiction
uses no probability measure, invariant volume, trace or local
compactness. It persists without a bounded diagonal hypothesis.
All mixed and infinite states of (1) remain present.

The constant kernel K=1 is a legitimate continuous invariant positive
kernel but does not separate states. Thus the result is not a claim
that invariant kernels do not exist. Conversely the kernel equal to
1 when its two arguments coincide and 0 otherwise is positive
definite, invariant and separating, but (4) shows that it is not
jointly continuous. The same proof works in any flow
with distinct states that approach one another in this manner while
the second is fixed at the chosen sampled time. It is a dynamical
obstruction, not evidence of special arithmetic uniqueness. Removing
invariance permits a different contract, such as 204's proposed
bounded nonunitary group; this proof does not decide its validity.
Reservation 206 remains uncreated.

## 3. SC21-BD: one-way deck contraction does not define the proposed bundle

Keep the whole old finite cover P and the constant complete fiber
H=ell2(A times Z). The
[203 Fourier--Mellin convention](../203-completed-owner-analytic-frontier/paper.md)
uses all atoms and all integer Fourier modes, with

\[
 Jb(v)=\sum_{a,k}b_{a,k}e^{-k^2}v_a^{2+ik},\qquad
 (Gb)_{a,k}=a^{-2-ik}b_{a,k},
 \tag{7}
\]

zero powers set to zero and the real logarithm used on positive
coordinates. The new proposed bundle gluing is the integer-deck
generator (v,b)->(D^(-1)v,Gb). A flat constant-fiber Hilbert-bundle
transition in this contract must be a bounded linear automorphism,
so that forward and inverse changes of trivialization exist on every
fiber vector. The sign of the chosen generator does not remove that
requirement.

**Proposition BD.** G is bounded and injective with dense range, but
is not onto and has no bounded inverse. The proposed map therefore
does not define the claimed full integer-group fiber action or the
prescribed flat Hilbert-bundle gluing.

**Proof.** Since the modulus of each multiplier is a^(-2),

\[
 \|Gb\|_2\le\tfrac14\|b\|_2,
 \qquad \|G\|=\tfrac14.
 \tag{8}
\]

Every multiplier is nonzero; G is injective. Every finitely supported
fiber vector has a finitely supported preimage, giving dense range.
However the full vector h with h_(a,0)=1/a and all other coordinates
zero is in H because sum_a a^(-2)<=sum_(n>=2)n^(-2)<infinity.
Its only possible preimage has b_(a,0)=a, which is not in H. There
are infinitely many derived atoms by the elementary integer
infinitude argument. Thus G is not onto. Also, for unit vectors
e_(a,0) as a tends to infinity,

\[
 \|G e_{a,0}\|_2=a^{-2}\longrightarrow0,
 \tag{9}
\]

which rules out any bounded inverse even on its range with the
inherited norm.

For a proposed target (w,h) in P times H, a preimage under the
frozen generator would have base v=Dw and require Gb=h. The vector
above has no such preimage. Hence the full product map is not an
automorphism and cannot generate a Z action. A one-direction
contraction does not supply inverse bundle transitions. QED.

Decision: STOP this precise constant-fiber bounded-automorphism
contract before a trace question. An abstract equivalence relation
generated by a non-surjective map is not thereby the specified
Hilbert bundle with those transition maps. No mode, atom, norm,
carrier or clock is changed to repair it; reservation 207 remains
uncreated.

As a control, on one fixed atom and all its Fourier modes, the same
diagonal map has a bounded inverse of norm a^2. The obstruction to
the full automorphism is the unbounded set of atom sizes, not infinite
Fourier multiplicity by itself. The latter causes the different
noncompactness result in 203, and is not needed here. The Gaussian
in J helps observable-series convergence but is not an extra weight
in the frozen coefficient norm; it cannot repair (8)--(9). Different
varying-fiber bundles or different norms are not excluded, but are
not this frozen contract.

In particular, a scalar contraction cI with 0<abs(c)<1 is a bounded
automorphism. It is failure of onto-ness and of a positive lower
multiplier bound, not contraction alone, that decides this screen.

## 4. 204: a fixed scalar Hilbert group on the actual full quotient

\[
 m(D^{-\theta(v)}v)=1,\qquad w(v)=D^{-\theta(v)}v
 \tag{10}
\]

has a unique solution by strict monotonicity and the limits zero and

existing X_hat onto S_hat times R/Z. These are auxiliary deck

The bounded continuous scalar functions g_0=1,
g_*=exp(2 pi i theta) and g_a=w_a separate all quotient states.
With beta_0=beta_*=1/4, beta_a=2^(-a), and

\[
 \mathcal K=\bigoplus_j L^2(\mathbb R,\rho(t)dt),\qquad
 Jb(x)=\sum_j\sqrt{\beta_j}\int b_j(t)g_j(\phi_t x)\rho(t)dt.
 \tag{11}
\]

The estimate norm(Jb)_infinity<=sqrt(sum beta_j) norm(b) and

Constants belong to H. Distinct states are separated by integrating

Actual time pullback, not a new coefficient evolution, satisfies

\[

The density ratio comes from changing the integration variable.

## 5. 205: all invariant probabilities and what their L2 spaces lose

[205 / AQC-20260916-IMS01](../205-invariant-measure-support/paper.md)
has status ADVANCE — ALL INVARIANT PROBABILITIES CLASSIFIED; FULL-STATE
L2 FAITHFULNESS FAILS; NATURALNESS OPEN. Its geometric owner is still
the entire (1), not the periodic locus. Its measures are countably

For any two atoms a<b the set U_ab where both coordinates are
positive is an invariant open set of the full quotient. On it

\[
 h_{ab}([v])=\frac{\log v_a}{\log a}-\frac{\log v_b}{\log b},
 \qquad h_{ab}(\phi_t x)=h_{ab}(x)+t c_{ab},
 \qquad c_{ab}=\frac1{\log a}-\frac1{\log b}>0.
 \tag{13}
\]

Deck multiplication by D^j adds the same integer j to the two
terms, so this is a genuine continuous quotient function. The Borel
strip 0<=h_ab<c_ab and all its integer-time translates are disjoint,
have equal measure and cover U_ab. Finite invariant measure therefore
forces each strip, and hence U_ab, to have measure zero. Countably
many pairs cover every mixed support, including every infinite support.
No recurrence theorem, local compactness or prior regularity of the
measure is needed.

Let Gamma be the union of singleton circles gamma_a. Its complement
is precisely the union of those open pair sets, so Gamma is closed.
Each gamma_a is the actual time circle R/(log(a) Z), compact and
closed in the Hausdorff quotient. If lambda_a denotes its normalized
time measure, the exact classification is

\[
 \mu=\sum_a w_a\lambda_a,\qquad w_a\ge0,\quad\sum_a w_a=1.
 \tag{14}
\]

Indeed the pair argument puts all finite mass on Gamma; restricting
to any circle with positive mass gives a rotation-invariant
probability. Equal finite arc partitions and rational approximation
show that this restriction is normalized length. Conversely every
countable mixture (14) is invariant, and its circle masses recover
the weights uniquely. Thus concentration on the union does not mean
all probabilities are supported on one common circle. No preferred
weights are selected.

The natural map C_b(X_hat)->L2(X_hat,mu) loses the same explicit
nonzero continuous function for every such mu. On the full cover put

\[
 \eta(r)=\max(0,1-4|r-1|),\qquad
 \psi(v)=\eta(m(v))\frac{v_2v_3}{m(v)^2},\qquad
 \widetilde f(v)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}\psi(D^j v).
 \tag{15}
\]

The active mass interval is (3/4,5/4). Nontrivial deck mass ratios
are at least two, so at most one term is nonzero at any point.
On a neighborhood with mass between two positive constants, only
finitely many integer powers enter the closed active interval. Thus
the sum is locally finite, continuous, bounded by 1/4 and deck
invariant. It descends to f in C_b(X_hat). It is zero on Gamma
but equals 1/4 at [(q_2+q_3)/2]. Classification (14) therefore
makes its L2 class zero for every permitted probability.

Actual time pullback does give a unitary representation on each such
L2 space. What fails is faithfulness of the natural map from all
continuous full-state observations, not existence of that measure
representation or of other fixed Hilbert models. Mixed states remain
actual nonempty open parts of the carrier, not nonexistent states
because their probability is zero.

Finiteness matters. Push Lebesgue time measure forward along one
actual mixed orbit. The orbit map is injective by the geometric
period equation; its compact finite-time images are closed Borel
sets with finite measure, and their union has full measure. This
gives an infinite sigma-finite invariant Borel measure concentrated
on mixed states. It is not a probability or a newly selected
preferred observation owner. The pair proof also works for generic
distinct multipliers greater than one when their quotient is valid;
repeated multipliers can instead give mixed periodic rays. Thus the
measure theorem does not establish arithmetic naturalness.
