## 3. SC18-G: the full divisor-port metric flow

The source is the entire cover graph of the positive divisors of each
n>=2. A vertex v has its neighbors ordered increasingly; let rho_v
be their cyclic successor permutation. Every oriented edge (u,v) is
a phase state. The frozen routing and actual travel time are

\[
 F(n,u,v)=(n,v,\rho_v(u)),\qquad
 \tau(n,u,v)=|v-u|.
\tag{G1}
\]

The prime-symbolic arrow is divisor admissibility -> deterministic
routing -> geometric travel. The graph is a static arithmetic input;
the flow is not claimed to execute a trial-division algorithm. Ports
are not identified merely because their projections meet a graph vertex.

**Complete owner and full cycle rule.** On the entire discrete port
space the inverse is

\[
 F^{-1}(n,u,v)=(n,\rho_u^{-1}(v),u).
\tag{G2}
\]

Each finite component is therefore a permutation of all its ports.
For a minimal cycle C=(x0,...,x_(ell-1)), put
T_C=sum_j tau(x_j) and S_j=sum_(i<j) tau(x_i). Its endpoint suspension
is exactly the circle of length T_C, through

\[
 [x_j,t]\longmapsto S_j+t\pmod {T_C}.
\tag{G3}
\]

This map agrees at every glued endpoint and is a homeomorphism: the
finitely many consecutive closed edge intervals tile the circle, with
only their prescribed successive endpoints identified. The whole
suspension is the countable disjoint union of these circles, hence
Hausdorff and second countable. Arc-length translation gives the full
two-sided flow. In particular tau>=1 also prevents infinitely many
edge crossings in bounded time. Every minimal routing cycle gives
exactly one primitive time orbit of least period T_C and repetitions
rT_C. Distinct starting points on one circle are not different packets;
different routing cycles remain different packets even at equal periods.

**Decisive full-state controls.** For n=2 the whole port set is the
single cycle (1,2)->(2,1), with T=2. For n=4 it is the single cycle

\[
 (1,2)\longmapsto(2,4)\longmapsto(4,2)
 \longmapsto(2,1)\longmapsto(1,2),\qquad T=6.
\tag{G4}
\]

The latter is a composite primitive packet, not the second repetition
of the n=2 circle, whose time would be 4; moreover the preserved n
labels belong to different flow components. Generally a prime p has
one edge 1--p and one routing cycle, with T_p=2(p-1). This is not
the logarithmic clock. Every integer component has a nonempty finite
port set, so the routing actually makes all its states periodic.

prime-exclusive packet and logarithmic-time target fails. Changing to
unit edge lengths would change the n=4 time from 6 to 4; deleting reverse
ports or composite cycles also changes the frozen object. This is not

## 4. SC18-F: exact fragmentation, no positive-energy periodic point

The source sends each d to all unordered proper factor pairs r<=s,
rs=d, rather than to a homology class. In the real coordinate basis
e2,...,en freeze

\[
 A_ne_d=\sum_{2\le r\le s,\;rs=d}(e_r+e_s-e_d),
 \quad H_n(x,\xi)=\xi^T A_nx,\quad
 \omega_n=\sum_{d=2}^n dx_d\wedge d\xi_d.
\tag{F1}
\]

Every real coordinate is retained, and the flow owner is the whole
H_n=1 over all n. Because r,s<d, A_n is upper triangular in this
ordered basis, with real diagonal entries minus the number of such
factor pairs of d. A prime cutoff n does not make the whole A_n zero:
for example A_5e4=2e2-e4. Component dimensions vary, so this is a
declared non-pure-dimensional Hamiltonian carrier, not one fixed-
dimensional ASFS return base.

With i_X omega=dH the full equations and flow are

\[
 \dot x=A_nx,\qquad\dot\xi=-A_n^T\xi,\qquad
 \Phi_n^t(x,\xi)=(e^{tA_n}x,e^{-tA_n^T}\xi).
\tag{F2}
\]

They exist for every real time, preserve the canonical form by the
dual inverse factors, and preserve H because A_n commutes with its
exponential. Thus every nonempty frozen energy component is genuinely
complete. The nonnegative population cone is not substituted for this
two-sided real owner.

Already A_2=0 makes H_2=1 empty, excluding the least prime packet.
There is also a short all-state closure: suppose a positive-energy
trajectory is periodic. Its x coordinates are periodic. Starting with
x_n and proceeding downwards, the upper-triangular system makes each
coordinate solve u'=a u+c with real constant coefficients once the
higher coordinates have been shown constant. Such an equation has
only constant periodic solutions: any nonconstant part is a real
exponential or a linear drift. Hence x is constant, A_nx=0, and
H_n=xi^T A_nx=0, a contradiction. No frozen positive-energy point
is periodic for any n, including equilibria.

This is not a claim that every energy component is empty. For every
n>=4 the point x=e4, xi=-e4 has H_n=1. As a precise reverse-time
control, in the n=4 component its trajectory has
x=2(1-e^(-t))e2+e^(-t)e4 and xi=-e^t e4, so x2<0 at t<0.
That explicit covector formula is only asserted for n=4; higher
cutoffs may create additional covector components.

**STOP:** factor exclusion -> fragmentation evolution -> cotangent

positive-energy clock has no primitive packets at all. No rotor,
energy change, positive-cone deletion, global section, operator or

## 5. SC18-C: local binary constraints remember every factor packet

Use the frozen width L with 2^(L-1)<=n<2^L, all factor bits a_i,b_i,
and all carries c0,...,c_(2L) specified in the card. The local equations
are

\[
 c_k+s_k=n_k+2c_{k+1},\qquad
 s_k=\sum_{i+j=k}a_i b_j,\qquad c_0=c_{2L}=0.
\tag{C1}
\]

All bits outside the declared factor width are absent; n_k=0 for
k>=L. The original carry bound is 0<=c_k<=2L, and the factor orientation
is 1<=A<=B. The source arrow is multiplication/carry admissibility ->
all accepted tableaus -> marked cyclic symbolic evolution. The shift
does not claim to compute or validate its own tableau.

**Complete admissibility classification.** Multiplying (C1) by 2^k
and summing over k=0,...,2L-1 cancels the interior carries and yields
AB=n. Conversely, given any positive factor pair A<=B, AB=n, both
factors fit width L. Starting with c0=0, perform the uniquely determined
ordinary binary carry recursion: divide c_k+s_k by 2, taking its
remainder as the output digit and quotient as c_(k+1). Since s_k<=L,
induction gives 0<=c_k<=L-1: from c_k<=L-1 the next quotient is at
most floor((2L-1)/2)=L-1. The usual weighted recurrence gives the
digits of AB, hence those of n and zero remaining carry at 2L.
This proves existence within the frozen, weaker 2L bound. Given A,B,
the local equation and c0=0 force every carry, proving uniqueness.

Thus the full tableau set is in bijection with all unordered positive
factor pairs, not just the trivial factorization or a chosen witness.
If d(n) counts positive divisors, the number is

\[
 M(n)=\frac{d(n)+\mathbf1_{n\text{ is a square}}}{2}.
\tag{C2}
\]

Indeed nonsquare factor pairs pair distinct complementary divisors;
a square additionally has its unique equal-factor pair. Consequently
M(n)=1 exactly when n is prime. This is an exact prime/composite
observable of the full source, not a prime-only orbit set.

Each tableau retains all phases k modulo 2L, with the mark part of
the state. Incrementing this mark gives exactly one primitive cycle
of length 2L per tableau, even if some columns agree. The full unit
suspension is a disjoint union of these circles, with actual repetitions
2Lr. For both n=4 and n=6, L=3 and the two respective factor pairs
are (1,4),(2,2) and (1,6),(2,3). Each pair gives a distinct primitive
period-6 circle. The time is genuinely logarithmic in scale,

\[
 T(n)=2\bigl(1+\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\bigr),
\tag{C3}
\]

but not the exact prime-log clock: T(2)=T(3)=4. In particular a
period-6 n=4 circle is not a repeated n=2 circle, whose second repeat
has time 8 and a different component identity.

**STOP:** the complete packet-count observable detects primes and
the physical marked clock has logarithmic scale, but composite
primitive packets and bit-length plateaus defeat the target. Removing
tableaus, erasing marks or changing padding/time would change the
owner. This is local arithmetic admissibility, not a symplectic lift
or proof of dynamic trial division. It does not borrow the sequential
counter or geometric conclusions of
[140](../140-cyclic-divisor-counter/candidate-card.md) or
[141](../141-divisor-counter-henon-lift/candidate-card.md).

## 6. SC18-P: completion changes the full periodic ledger

At each phase d=1,...,n-1, the frozen proper-divisor symbol chooses
I, or A=((1,2),(0,1)) for an even witness, or B=((1,0),(2,1)) for

their full cotangent lifts C_M act on the entire T*RP1. The base is
the countable disjoint union of these two-dimensional phase components,
not an affine chart or a preferred covector. The source arrow is
individual divisibility symbols -> ordered matrices -> projective
carrier -> full cotangent geometry. No matrix-group freeness theorem
or prior free-register quotient is used.

**Complete symplectic owner.** Every f_M is a global smooth projective
diffeomorphism, even where one affine formula changes charts. The
cotangent lift preserves the canonical one-form lambda: its inverse-
derivative covector update cancels Df_M when applied to the projected
tangent vector. Therefore C_M^*lambda=lambda, so it preserves
omega=-d lambda and has inverse C_(M^(-1)). In particular

\[
 F^{-1}(n,d,z)=(n,d^-,C_{M(n,d^-)}^{-1}z).
\tag{P1}
\]

The frozen unit suspension is the quotient of M times R by
(X,t)~(FX,t-1). Its integer action is free and proper because of
the time translation, so it has a smooth Hausdorff quotient and a
complete two-sided translation flow. This three-dimensional suspension
is not thereby itself symplectic or Hamiltonian.

**First prime control.** For every prime p all phase matrices are I.
Each z in the whole T*RP1 therefore yields its own primitive suspension
orbit of time p-1. The retained phase returns only after p-1 steps;
different z are not related by time translation. Already n=2 gives
an entire two-dimensional continuum of primitive period-1 packets,
not finite prime multiplicity.

**Complete n=4 control.** The scan is I,A,I and every three-step
phase return is C_A. On the finite affine chart q=u/v it is
(q,xi)->(q+2,xi), so no positive iterate has a periodic point there.
Near the projective endpoint infinity=[1:0], write x=v/u and eta dx
for a covector. The full lift is

\[
 x'=\frac{x}{1+2x},\qquad \eta'=(1+2x)^2\eta.
\tag{P2}
\]

Thus x=0 fixes every eta. For the mth iterate replace 2 by 2m;
the same finite-chart exclusion and endpoint calculation prove
Per(C_A)=Fix(C_A)=T*_(infinity)RP1. The full n=4 periodic set is
precisely these fibers at the three phases. Every eta gives a
different composite primitive time-3 circle, with repeats 3r.
It is not just a zero-section fixed-point example.

## 7. SC18-R: singular reduction really leaves a symplectic plane

\[
 \mu_n=a(n)+\frac{x^2+y^2}{2}.
\tag{R1}
\]

The complete zero level is empty for every composite n. For prime
n it is exactly {(0,0,Q,P):Q,P real}; all of S1 fixes every point
of this zero level. It is a critical moment level, not a regular
level with a free action. No regular reduction theorem is invoked.

The quotient map in a prime component is explicitly a homeomorphism
to the entire (Q,P) plane, because the orbits on the zero level are
singletons and its topology is already the subspace R2 topology.

under this explicit quotient it supplies the reduced form. Thus the
complete reduced base is a countable disjoint union of genuine
two-dimensional symplectic planes. It is not a zero-dimensional

real transverse state has been kept.

The ambient frozen map (x,y,Q,P)->(x,y,2Q,P/2) preserves Omega,
commutes with the action and preserves the whole zero level. Hence

\[
 F_n(Q,P)=(2Q,P/2),\qquad F_n^{-1}(Q,P)=(Q/2,2P).
\tag{R2}
\]

It is symplectic. Its complete unit suspension exists by the same
free proper time-shift construction used above. For every m>=1,

are no other periodic points. Therefore the full suspension contains
