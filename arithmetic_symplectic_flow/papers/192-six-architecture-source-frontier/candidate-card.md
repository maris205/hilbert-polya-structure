## SC18-G — exact divisor-port metric flow

by one undirected edge exactly when u divides v and no divisor w of n
satisfies u<w<v with u dividing w and w dividing v. No prime list is
an input. A port is an oriented edge (u,v). At a vertex v, order its
neighbors increasingly; next_v(u) is the cyclic successor of the incoming
neighbor u, including itself when the vertex has degree one. Freeze

F(n,u,v)=(n,v,next_v(u)),   tau(n,u,v)=abs(v-u).

Retain every port. Freeze the endpoint suspension
(x,tau(x))~(F(x),0), with motion at unit speed along each directed edge
and the specified routing at the endpoint. The phase distinguishes ports
meeting the same graph vertex; do not identify them merely because their
graph projections agree. This is a proposed metric/port carrier, not an
asserted planar billiard or classical symplectic manifold. All quotient,
invertibility, full-time and periodic statements are to be proved.

Packets mean every oriented primitive suspension orbit, modulo time
translation, retaining all distinct routing cycles. Repetition uses the
same edge times. First inspect n=2 and n=4 before any general enumeration.
Degree-one reflection is part of the initial definition, not a later
repair. Controls: unit rather than metric edge lengths is a different
clock; deleting reverse ports or selected composite cycles changes the
carrier. No operator or trace is supplied. This realizes divisor

## SC18-C — exact binary factor-tableau suspension

c_k (0<=k<=2L), with 0<=c_k<=2L, c_0=c_(2L)=0, satisfying

c_k + sum_(i+j=k, 0<=i,j<L) a_i b_j = n_k + 2c_(k+1)
