## Finding

But the Phase-I symplectic record already identifies the decisive missing P0/A0 owners: an endogenous varying-prime selector, a natural \(\log p\) clock and repetition weight, and an intrinsic treatment of continuous periodic fibres. Converting finite-field reductions at selected primes into the desired mechanism would select those primes externally.

## Decision
