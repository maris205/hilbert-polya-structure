## Only admissible next evidence

Do not open a P0 card unless one source defines, for the **same object**:

1. a finite-dimensional smooth \((M,\omega)\) and one \(C^r\) symplectomorphism \(F\);
2. a positive roof and suspension whose period convention is explicit;
3. an internal bijection or rigorously delimited correspondence between rational primes and primitive closed flow orbits with time \(\log p\); and
4. packet multiplicity and prime-power repetition without selecting a prime, roof, or map parameter externally.

This frontier prevents both false positives: claiming that arithmetic/topos flow is classical symplectic, and claiming that a local symplectic Frobenius construction is globally prime-varying. It is a `fork` record, not a failure theorem.
