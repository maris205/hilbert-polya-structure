## Abstract

The current real coordinates determine an integer gcd, which both permits the next
step and divides actual complex squaring. We prove the complete inverse relation,
every-Borel IMAGE identity and the prescribed analytic point version on the entire
carrier, including null boundaries. The clock is integer lag times \(\log4\) plus
an endpoint difference. Every integer \(q\ge2\) is a distinct fixed packet with
full primitive time \(\log4\), not \(\log2\). This fails the frozen necessary
prime-time target, with infinite equal-time multiplicity. Three own controls and all source/extension
isotropy, kernels, incoming histories and phases are retained. No higher-period census is made.

## 1. Frozen source and exact lineage

the point, its identity and all actual incoming arrows remain.

All iterates below are partial iterates; \(T^0\) exists at every point.

For integers \(n\ge2,\ 1<q<n\), the entire cell
\([n,n+1)\times[q,q+1)\) records \(\gcd(n,q)\); the original witness \(q\mid n\)
is exactly the readout \(d=q\). The permission \(d\ge2\) is a common-factor
deformation, not that original witness test or a prime detector.
The transported point supplies the current and next digits, with no independent integer root or passive fibre.
Floor cells, complex squaring and normalization are declared designs; strong naturalness remains OPEN.

## 2. All inverse domains and IMAGE

For the three square owners write \(Tz=z^2/k(z)\), with \(k=d,\max(1,d),1\),
respectively, and with the domains in the table. On every integer cell \(k\) is constant.
Partition nonzero points by \(H_+=\{x>0\}\cup\{x=0,y>0\}\) and \(H_-=-H_+\).
For every allowed cell and sign, retain precisely that Borel source piece \(B\).
Square is injective on \(B\): opposite roots cannot both belong to the same \(H_\epsilon\).
The inverse on \(E=T(B)\) is the unique root of \(z^2=kw\) lying in \(B\).

These are explicit Borel domains. Indeed, choose the Borel square root in \(H_+\)
at each \(w\ne0\), choosing positive imaginary root on the negative real axis;
its negative lies in \(H_-\). Then \(E\) is the Borel condition that the
corresponding root of \(kw\) belongs to the specified half-open cell and half-plane.
Every actual predecessor has exactly one assigned cell and sign; equal sources are not extra labelled arrows.
No digit bound, quadrant restriction or continuity across a square-root cut is imposed.

Differentiating \(z^2=kw\) locally at its nonzero root gives
\[
J_\theta(w)=\left|{k\over2z}\right|^2
={k^2\over4|z|^2}={k\over4|w|}>0.                 \tag{1}
\]
This finite positive value is exactly the frozen local analytic prescription at all assigned boundaries.
A root germ is analytic near each such point even if the global Borel branch jumps there.
Cover by countably many such open inverse charts and disjointify their restrictions;
ordinary real change of variables on each chart proves, for EVERY Borel \(A\subseteq E\),
\(\mu(\theta A)=\int_AJ_\theta\,d\mu\).
Null cell/half-plane pieces also satisfy this identity and remain present.
Lebesgue measure alone does not determine their point values: the frozen analytic
prescription does. Formula (1) is continuous on each fixed-\(k\) target chart;
no global continuity across changing integer cells is claimed.

For M, enumerate every integer \(d\ge2\), put \(\theta_d(w)=dw\), and retain
\[
E_d=\{w:d(dw)=d\}.
\]
These Borel domains are the full source checks; every predecessor occurs, and integer-cell partitions add no copies.
The linear inverse gives \(J_d=d^2\), finite and positive on all of \(E_d\),
and the same every-Borel IMAGE identity. No aggregate preimage count replaces either density.

## 3. Actual groupoids, global clocks and kernels

Use a separate copy of the following construction for each owner:
\[
G=\{(z,\ell,w):T^mz=T^nw,\ \ell=m-n,\ m,n\ge0,
       \text{ both histories legal}\}.
\]
The source is \(w\), range \(z\); identical triples have one arrow, all lag integers remain.
It is a Borel subset of \(Y\times\mathbb Z\times Y\): the legal-iterate equality
sets are Borel and \(m,n\) are countable. Fibres are countable by the full inverse enumeration.
Inversion changes \((z,\ell,w)\) to \((w,-\ell,z)\).
For composition, align two meeting histories along their shared middle orbit to
the larger of their two meeting times; that time exists in one of the given histories.
This proves closure with added lags without extending a terminal past its lifetime.

Put \(\rho(z)=\log|z|\) for \(z\ne0\), and \(\rho(0)=0\) only as notation
at the isolated identity. From the owned IMAGE densities, not a separate roof,
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{square owners:}&
\kappa(z)=\log(4|z|^2/k(z)^2)=\log4+2(\rho(Tz)-\rho(z)),\\
\text{M:}&\kappa(z)=-2\log d(z)=2(\rho(Tz)-\rho(z)).
\end{array}                                                   \tag{2}
\]
Let \(\delta=1\) for MAIN/G/C and \(\delta=0\) for M. Every legal finite history has
\[
S_0=0,\quad S_m=m\delta\log4+2(\rho(T^mz)-\rho(z)),\qquad
c(z,\ell,w)=\ell\delta\log4+2(\rho(w)-\rho(z)).                  \tag{3}
\]
Thus \(S_m-S_n\) is presentation-independent and additive. For finite inverse branches \(\alpha,\beta\) meeting at \(v\),
their densities are \(e^{-S_m(\alpha v)}\) and \(e^{-S_n(\beta v)}\).
The actual branch-pair map \(\beta v\mapsto\alpha v\) therefore has IMAGE density
\(e^{-c}\), for every Borel set in its domain. Countable chart restrictions cover
all arrows; the single-step and finite-history owners agree.

Write \(K_\ell=\{(z,0,w)\in G\}\), the exact same-time meeting relation.
For MAIN/G the complete clock kernel is
\[
K_c=\{(z,\ell,w)\in G:|z|=2^\ell|w|\},
\quad K_\ell\cap K_c=\{(z,0,w)\in G:|z|=|w|\}.                \tag{4}
\]
The isolated zero identity is included; it produces no extra arrows.
For C this simplifies below to \(K_c=K_\ell\); M's equal-radius clock kernel simplifies to the identities.
These formulas are exact over actual arrows, not an enlarged radius relation.

## 4. Full isotropy, incoming histories and phases

The real extension has all \((z,h)\), \(h\in\mathbb R\), and arrows \((w,h)\to(z,h+c(z,\ell,w))\).
Its lag and clock kernels are the lifts of
\(K_\ell,K_c\) with all real \(h\); their intersection is the corresponding full lift.
The forward map step is \((Tz,-1,z)\), with clock \(-\kappa(z)\);
the inverse step \((z,1,Tz)\) has clock \(+\kappa(z)\).
Height translation is a well-defined action on the orbit SET, without a claimed smooth quotient.
In any source class choose a base object \(u\) and transport heights to it along actual arrows.
Two such choices differ exactly by \(H_u=c(G_u^u)\); hence that full class has phase set
\(\mathbb R/H_u\), and its time stabilizer is exactly \(H_u\).

For any partial deterministic map here, nonzero isotropy implies an actual eventual
cycle: from \(T^mz=T^nz\), \(m>n\), the repeated segment can be continued indefinitely.
Conversely an eventual cycle of least source period \(r\) gives exactly \(r\mathbb Z\)
as the lag isotropy. Dividing differences of meeting times by the least cycle period
proves there are no additional lags. Terminals and non-eventually-periodic objects
have trivial isotropy. For every eventual cycle of a square owner, (3) gives
\[
G_z^z=r\mathbb Z,\qquad H_z=c(G_z^z)=r\log4\,\mathbb Z,
\qquad \operatorname{Iso}_{G^c}(z,h)=\{1\}.                    \tag{5}
\]
At all other objects \(H_z=0\) and extension isotropy is again trivial.
Consequently each eventual-cycle class has one full phase circle
\(\mathbb R/(r\log4\,\mathbb Z)\), primitive time \(r\log4\) and its integer repetitions;
every other source class has a free time line. This conditional identity does not classify higher cycles.

All incoming histories are untruncated finite compositions of Section 2's inverse domains:
null branches, alternative histories, terminal targets and finite approaches to cycles all remain.
No infinite-history completion adds objects.
For a fixed point \(q\), its entire source class is exactly
\(\mathcal B_q=\bigcup_{N\ge0}T^{-N}\{q\}\).
For \(T^Nz=q\), the extension phase is \(h-S_N(z)\) modulo \(H_q\).
Choosing another legal meeting time changes it only by \(H_q\). Thus all incoming
points and heights occur in the same packet without creating a smaller period.
Distinct fixed points have disjoint classes: their forward tails can never meet.

## 5. Complete fixed sets and the necessary target

For MAIN, any fixed point is active and nonzero, so \(z^2=d(z)z\) forces
\(z=d(z)\), a positive integer at least two. Conversely every such integer has
\(\gcd(\lfloor q\rfloor,0)=q\) and is fixed. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Fix}(T)=\{2,3,4,\ldots\},\qquad
H_q=\log4\,\mathbb Z\quad(q\ge2).                            \tag{6}
\]
Each basin \(\mathcal B_q\), with ALL its incoming histories and phases, is one
distinct primitive packet of time \(\log4\). Formula (5) excludes a hidden
\(\log2\) period supplied by an incoming branch or an alternate lag presentation.
There are countably infinitely many such fixed packets. The time is the logarithm
of composite \(4\), and equal-time packets cannot be merged.
This fails the card's necessary prime-time condition already at every fixed packet;
it also exhibits infinite multiplicity at that prohibited time.

The control fixed sets are equally complete. For G, nonzero \(z^2=e(z)z\)
forces \(z=e(z)\); exactly the positive integers satisfy this, including \(1\).
Thus G has countably infinitely many distinct fixed packets with \(H=\log4\,\mathbb Z\).
For C, the only algebraic roots \(0,1\) of \(z^2=z\) are both retained terminals,
not fixed points. For M, \(z/d(z)=z\) with \(d\ge2\) would force inactive zero.
Hence \(\operatorname{Fix}(T_C)=\operatorname{Fix}(T_M)=\varnothing\).

The global control isotropy can also be settled without a high-period census.
Every point of \(D\) has \(|z|>1\), since radius at most one gives floor digits
of absolute value at most one. Along C's actual steps the radius strictly squares,
so no eventual cycle exists. For a nonidentity C arrow all radii exceed one and
\(\rho(w)=2^\ell\rho(z)\); (3) then has the sign of \(\ell\), and vanishes
exactly for \(\ell=0\). Thus \(K_c=K_\ell\), their intersection is \(K_\ell\),
all \(H_z=0\), and all source/extension isotropy is trivial.
Each M step divides radius by \(d\ge2\), reaching a terminal in finite time.
A nonzero common future preserves the complex argument.
Equal radii on an actual M arrow therefore force the same point, whose isotropy
is trivial. Thus \(K_c\) and \(K_\ell\cap K_c\) are exactly the identities;
\(K_\ell\) remains the actual same-time meeting relation. All \(H_z=0\),
all source/extension isotropy is trivial, and every full source class has a free time line.
These are consequences of the controls' own actions and IMAGE, not transferred MAIN results.
