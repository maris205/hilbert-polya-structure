## 2. Complete inverse atlas and all-point IMAGE

For a target \((m,w)\), M/L/V enumerate every positive \(d,q\) with \(d+q=m\). H instead enumerates \(d\mid m,\ q=m/d\) for \(m\ge1\). Thus each target has finitely many candidate discrete branches, with no imposed cutoff. At \(m=0\) no owner has a predecessor; at \(m=1\), M/L/V have none.

\[
 z_\sigma=\frac{dw+\sigma\sqrt{\Delta}}2,\quad \sigma\in\{+1,-1\}.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
V uses \(\Delta=w^2-4q\) and \(z_\sigma=(w+\sigma\sqrt{\Delta})/2\). L uses \(z=dw\).
The selector is \(\sqrt{\Delta}=\sqrt{|\Delta|}\exp(i\Arg(\Delta)/2)\), with \(\Arg(\Delta)\in[0,2\pi)\). Each actual inverse \(I_\alpha(m,w)=(dq,z)\), with \(\alpha=(d,q,\sigma)\) or \((d,q)\), retains exactly its roots passing the owner's source condition, sector digit, permission, and forward check. For H the register \(dq\) equals \(m\).
These are all the inverses. An actual fold source satisfies \(z^2-dwz+q=0\), or \(z^2-wz+q=0\) in V, and its discriminant is
\[
 (z-q/z)^2\ne0.
\]
Thus it occurs as one of the two roots, with its unique actual digit. Conversely each retained root satisfies the appropriate forward equation by substitution. L follows from its linear equation.
A zero discriminant rejects only that candidate discrete branch: it corresponds to its excluded critical source. It does not delete the target, rule out other branches, or impose target forward legality on incoming. A label does not create an extra copy of an actual source.
Write \(A_q(z)=1-q/z^2\). On an actual source branch, the complex forward derivatives are
\[
 b_M=b_H=A_q(z)/d,\qquad b_V=A_q(z),\qquad b_L=1/d.
\]
They are nonzero. The inverse germ taking the assigned root value has derivative \(1/b_O\); the real determinant of multiplication by a complex number is its squared modulus. Therefore
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 O&J_O(F_O(n,z))&\kappa_O(n,z)=-\log J_O(F_O(n,z))\\ \hline

\end{array}                                                    \tag{4}
\]
Every entry is finite and strictly positive in the Jacobian column on its own legal domain. L keeps its constant value at states satisfying the removed equation \(z^2=q\).
Here \(J_O(F_O(n,z))\) means the inverse-root version selected by that actual source, not a value assigned to a target independently of its incoming branch.
For rigor at selector cuts and sector endpoints, fix a discrete branch and cover its regular complex source by countably many inverse-function neighborhoods \(U_j\) on which the rational map is injective. Such a cover exists by the inverse function theorem and a countable plane basis.
Intersect with the actual half-open sector and assigned root-sheet sets, then subtract preceding neighborhoods. These are disjoint Borel source pieces covering all legal points, including seams. Their images are Borel because the neighborhood restrictions are homeomorphisms. On each piece the inverse is a restriction of a holomorphic inverse.
If two charts represent the same actual root, their local inverse germs agree by local uniqueness, so (4) gives the same value. Neither the angular representative nor the jumping square-root selector is differentiated. Their cuts only determine Borel membership.
Each full retained inverse \(I_\alpha\) is injective, since \(F_O(I_\alpha y)=y\). Partitioning any Borel \(E\) in its actual target domain by the preceding charts and applying ordinary change of variables gives
\[
 \mu(I_\alpha E)=\int_E J_\alpha\,d\mu                         \tag{5}
\]
for every such \(E\), not merely away from null cuts. The images of the partition pieces are disjoint; countable additivity permits infinite measures. Counting registers each contribute one under their actual branch correspondence, not another degree factor.
For an arbitrary Borel legal source set \(B\), define

       \mathbf1_{\operatorname{dom} I_\alpha}(y)\mathbf1_B(I_\alpha y).

In particular, actual target images are unions, not sums that count overlapping points repeatedly.

## 3. Zero step clocks and the full history ledger

All statements in this section belong separately to each owner. The exact zero-step conditions, still subject to its actual legality and digit, are
\[

\end{array}                                                    \tag{7}
\]
For M/H the equivalent equation is \((d^2-1)|z|^4+2q\operatorname{Re}(z^2)-q^2=0\); V follows by expanding \(|z^2-q|^2=|z|^4\). These are genuine legal zero clocks, not surrogate clocks for excluded critical points. A zero cycle sum need not make every step zero.
Define \(\rho_O=e^{\kappa_O}=|b_O|^2>0\). For every actual legal history of length \(a\), put
\[
 R_a(\xi)=\prod_{j<a}\rho_O(F_O^j\xi),\quad
 S_a(\xi)=\log R_a(\xi),\quad R_0=1,\ S_0=0.                    \tag{8}
\]
Positive histories require all preceding steps, while their endpoint may be terminal. The actual groupoid is
\[
 G_O=\{(\xi,a-b,\eta):a,b\ge0,\ F_O^a\xi=F_O^b\eta\},\qquad
 c=S_a(\xi)-S_b(\eta).                                        \tag{9}
\]
Source is \(\eta\), range is \(\xi\), and equal actual triples are identified with their integer lag retained. The forward arrow \((F_O\xi,-1,\xi)\) has clock \(-\kappa_O(\xi)\), as specified by the card.
Two witnesses of the same triple differ by a common increment in both lengths. Along their equal future that increment adds equal sums, so \(c\) descends. For composition, extend the shorter middle history to the longer one; its required extension exists because its endpoint equals the available common future. The middle sums cancel. This proves additivity and the inverse sign.
Each equality locus of Borel partial iterates is Borel. The finite inverse atlas makes every finite-depth predecessor set finite, hence source fibers are countable. Thus (9) is a countable Borel groupoid. Composed inverse-chart IMAGE factors multiply to \(1/R_a\); a branch-pair arrow from \(\eta\) to \(\xi\) has factor \(e^{-c}=R_b(\eta)/R_a(\xi)\). These are the same measured owner and pointwise versions, without a second clock or measure.
For all four owners the full kernels, over every actual witness, are
\[
\begin{split}

\end{split}                                                    \tag{10}
\]
No fold is presumed globally injective. In L, \(R_a=(\prod_{j<a}d_j)^{-2}\), so the clock equalities in (10) are equalities of those positive integer digit products. All other owners use their own factors in (4).
Source isotropy is nontrivial exactly when the full forward state is eventually periodic: a nonzero isotropy witness is a repeated state, and a repeated state supplies such a witness.                                                                \tag{11}
\]

## 6. Complete MAIN \(5\to6\to5\) word check

Write \(z=z_0\), \(w=z_1\). An actual word in the frozen window must satisfy
\[
 w=z+5/z,\qquad z=(w+3/w)/2,\qquad z\ne0,\ w\ne0,                \tag{16}
\]
along with the first actual digit 1 at register 5, second digit 2 at register 6, and both own critical/source checks.
Eliminating \(w\) from (16), without dividing by a possibly zero intermediate because an actual word already requires \(w\ne0\), yields
\[
 2z^2(z^2+5)=(z^2+5)^2+3z^2,\qquad z^4-3z^2-25=0.             \tag{17}
\]
Put \(t_\pm=(3\pm\sqrt{109})/2\), with \(t_+>0>t_-\). All four possible roots are \(+\sqrt{t_+},-\sqrt{t_+},+i\sqrt{-t_-},-i\sqrt{-t_-}\).
They are nonzero, and none has \(z^2=-5\), since \((-5)^2-3(-5)-25=15\ne0\). Thus the intermediate in (16) is not zero for these formal roots; the elimination has not hidden an admissible zero case.
Their arguments give the complete source rejection:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 z& a(z)&\text{actual digit at }n=5\\ \hline
 +\sqrt{t_+}&0&1\\
 -\sqrt{t_+}&1/2&3\\
 +i\sqrt{-t_-}&1/4&2\\
 -i\sqrt{-t_-}&3/4&4 .
\end{array}                                                    \tag{18}
\]
The last three fail the prescribed first sector; in fact their digits do not divide 5. For the remaining positive real root, \(w=z+5/z>0\), hence at register 6 its actual digit is 1, not 2. It is not an actual word solution.
The first remaining source is noncritical because \(t_+\ne5\), as \(5^2-3\cdot5-25=-15\ne0\). No further formal check can repair the failed next digit. All four roots have therefore been rejected by actual owner conditions, not by selecting a preferred sign or rounding a boundary.
There is no core in this word window and thus no packet, incoming basin, or nonzero isotropy subgroup produced by it. The reverse cyclic starting phase cannot create one: any genuine \(6\to5\to6\) phase of this same prescribed word would rotate to an actual solution of (16). If a solution had existed, the distinct registers would have made its least full-state period exactly two, and the two phases would represent one packet, not two.
