## Abstract

For a frozen piecewise rational real system with its own inverse-IMAGE clock,
an actual rational least-q cycle has signed multiplier D and clock log|D|.
At an audit prime ell, explicitly compatible integral reduction makes the
cycle reduce to a least-s orbit. If t reduced phases are critical, then
v_ell(D)>=(q/s)t. A primitive log ell therefore requires q=s, exactly one
critical reduced phase and valuation exactly one there. In particular
q<=|R|<=ell^d. At other audit places, a prime primitive requires no critical
phase. These are necessary conditions only, with no replacement of the real
carrier by a finite residue system. Three complete real controls distinguish
period collapse, insufficiency of the local condition and failure of integral
reduction. All incoming histories, real phases and zero-clock arrows remain.

## 3. Actual rational cycles and compatible reduction

Let x_j be the card's actual rational least-q core. Every delta_j is a
nonzero rational number; D=product_j delta_j is rational and nonzero.
The signed cycle sum is C=log|D| by Section2, and H=log|D| Z.
Thus a primitive log p is equivalent to D in {p,-p,1/p,-1/p}.
The reciprocal possibility cannot be discarded before the extra assumptions.

Fix ell and the admitted R, selector b and integral unit-denominator rational
presentations from the card. Evaluation and differentiation of a quotient
with unit denominator commute with reduction modulo ell: the derivative
formula has only powers of that same unit in the denominator. Therefore
delta_j belongs Z_(ell), and its reduction is bar delta(bar x_j).
This conclusion also shows presentation independence wherever the two
presentations have unit denominators: their rational identity and derivative
identity reduce to the same value. Nonzero real delta may reduce to zero.

Because i_j=b(bar x_j), the reduced points follow ONE deterministic bar F.
Let s be the least period of bar x_0. It divides q: divide q by s and use
minimality of s on this actual reduced cycle. Let t count the s phases where
bar delta=0. Each occurs q/s times along the q real steps, with possibly
DIFFERENT positive valuations at their different rational lifts. Precisely,

    v_ell(D)=sum_j v_ell(delta_j) >= (q/s)*t,
    v_ell(D)=0 iff t=0.

Here every summand is a nonnegative integer; it is zero exactly at a
noncritical reduced phase and at least one at a critical phase. We do NOT
claim equal valuations at different lifts or equality in the lower bound.

## 4. Prime-place consequences and their limits

Suppose the actual primitive is log p. At ell!=p, v_ell(D)=0 for all four
possible signed/reciprocal values, so t=0 under the admitted hypotheses.
At ell=p, nonnegative valuations rule out D=+/-1/p, leaving D=+/-p and
v_p(D)=1. Thus t>=1 and (q/s)t<=1 force q=s and t=1. The sole critical
step has valuation1 and all other steps are units. In particular

    q=s <= |R| <= p^d.

These are conditional NECESSARY restrictions at admitted places, not a
classification of all real cores. A compatible reduction at another prime

Conversely v_p(D)=1 does not imply |D|=p: other numerator or denominator
primes remain possible. No packet multiplicity or existence follows from
