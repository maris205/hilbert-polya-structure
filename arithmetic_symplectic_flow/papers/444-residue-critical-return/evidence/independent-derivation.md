## 1. Full real inverse and measure owner

Write V_i for the frozen open rational branch domain. The inverse function
theorem at every point supplies an open neighborhood on which f_i is a
diffeomorphism. There is a rational ball containing the point whose closure
lies inside that neighborhood and V_i. Thus the frozen enumeration covers
V_i. If B_in is its nth eligible ball, set

    A_in = E_i intersect (B_in minus union_{k<n} B_ik).

These are disjoint Borel pieces covering E_i. The inverse of f_i on B_in
is a smooth rational-map inverse germ; its restriction to f_i(A_in) is the
actual inverse branch. The image is Borel because the open-chart map is a
homeomorphism. Every actual preimage is in one such piece, including every
null face or cut. The construction never substitutes a selected real root.

For a target u, the exact unrestricted inverse set is

    Pre(u) = union_i {v in E_i : f_i(v)=u}.

It includes all real rational-equation solutions satisfying their own
denominator, branch, and forward checks. Each local chart contributes at
most one preimage. Consequently the complete set is countable, not asserted
finite. A target may be terminal and still have incoming arrows.

At EVERY actual inverse point u=f_i(v),

    J_theta(u)=|det Dtheta(u)|=1/|det Df_i(v)|,
    kappa(v)=log|det Df_i(v)|.

For every Borel B subset f_i(A_in), change of variables in the open chart
gives Leb(theta(B))=integral_B J_theta(u) du. Restriction to a Borel piece
preserves this identity, also for null B. Values at null points are fixed
by the assigned local germ, not inferred from an almost-everywhere density.
The sum of these actual branches retains the full real owner.

## 2. Equal triples, histories, whole stabilizers and phases

For every legal path let S_n(v)=sum_{j=0}^{n-1} kappa(T^j v), S_0=0.
An arrow (z,k,w), from w to z, exists exactly when there are m,n>=0
with legal T^m z=T^n w and k=m-n. Define c=S_m(z)-S_n(w).
Two representations of the same triple differ by equal increments of m
and n; those increments follow the same meeting point and have equal
clock contributions. This proves well-definedness even with terminal
objects. To compose two triples, advance their representations to a common
legal meeting of the shared trajectory. Clock sums then telescope, proving
cocycle additivity. In particular a forward arrow (Tv,-1,v) has clock
-kappa(v); there is no sign reversal in the definition of c itself.

The exact full class is

    O(w) = union_{n>=0, T^n w legal} union_{m>=0} Pre^m(T^n w),

where Pre^0(u)={u}, and every further Pre uses all actual branches. This
parametrizes every depth with no cutoff. If w has terminal endpoint a,
its class is union_m Pre^m(a). If its trajectory never terminates and is
not eventually periodic, its class is given by the displayed full union.
If it is eventually periodic, its class is precisely all incoming points
of its unique least-period finite core.

The lag kernel consists of actual triples with k=0. The clock kernel
consists of actual triples with S_m(z)=S_n(w), and their intersection is
the joint kernel. Equality of clock values never identifies source packets.
At a terminal or non-eventually-periodic point, a nonzero-lag isotropy
triple would force two different iterates of its trajectory to agree,
contradiction. Source isotropy and H are therefore both zero there.

For an eventual core a_j=T^j a_0 of least period q, put
K_j=kappa(a_j), C=sum_{j=0}^{q-1}K_j, and A_0=0,
A_j=sum_{i=0}^{j-1}K_i for 0<=j<=q. Extend A to all integers by
A_{j+q}=A_j+C. At ANY point of its full incoming class, source isotropy
is q Z, and the lag rq isotropy arrow has clock r C. Thus

    H = C Z;
    extension isotropy = {0} if C!=0, and q Z if C=0.

This is the ENTIRE H, not the subgroup generated by a selected return.
Incoming histories cannot shorten its positive generator. When C!=0
the primitive physical length is |C| and its repetitions are n|C|,
n positive integer. C=0 gives no positive physical period even though
source and extension isotropy remain nontrivial.

Here is an explicit description of ALL eventual-class arrows and phases,
including nonconstant core step clocks. Let d(z) be the first core arrival,
with T^{d(z)}z=a_{e(z)}, 0<=e(z)<q. Set

    ell=C/q, v_e=A_e-e ell,
    B_z=S_{d(z)}(z)-d(z) ell-v_{e(z)}, chi_z=e(z)-d(z).

For any z,w in this class, its arrows are exactly

    k congruent chi_w-chi_z modulo q,
    c(z,k,w)=k ell+B_z-B_w.

One obtains a sufficiently large legal meeting for every such integer k.
The expression follows by expanding both clock sums past first arrival
and cancelling the same terminal core phase. The lag kernel imposes k=0
and that congruence; the clock kernel imposes k ell=B_w-B_z and that
congruence; the joint kernel imposes both. The extension orbit phase is

    h-S_{d(z)}(z)+A_{e(z)} modulo C Z.

For C=0 this is an ordinary real number, not a collapsed quotient.
For other source classes choose, only set-theoretically and class by class,
an anchor a and one actual arrow g_z:a->z with clock b_z. The phase is
h-b_z modulo H_a. Changing the choice adds an element of H_a. No measurable
global selector, Hausdorff quotient, or manifold structure is asserted.
Physical translation h->h+t stabilizes an extension orbit exactly for
t in H_a: this is equivalent to an isotropy arrow with clock t.

## 3. Reduction and the exact valuation restriction

Take the frozen ACTUAL rational least-q core and the audit prime ell.
By the branch selector hypothesis every r_j=bar x_j is in R and obeys
r_{j+1}=bar F(r_j). Its reduced least period s divides q; writing q=k s,
its s distinct reduced phases repeat exactly k times. This fact uses
the actual cycle, not a claim that arbitrary residue cycles lift.

For a coordinate f=P/Q with coefficients in Z_(ell), a unit denominator
at r_j remains a unit when evaluated at x_j. Its derivative entries are
(Q partial P-P partial Q)/Q^2 at x_j. They therefore lie in Z_(ell),
and reduce to the derivatives of the reduced rational coordinate. Taking
determinants commutes with reduction. Hence each nonzero actual delta_j
is ell-integral, and

    v_ell(delta_j)>=0,
    v_ell(delta_j)=0 iff bar delta(r_j)!=0,
    v_ell(delta_j)>=1 iff bar delta(r_j)=0.

The derivative statement requires the frozen unit-denominator branch
presentation; it does not follow from a rational determinant alone.
Let t be the number of critical phases among the s distinct reduced
phases, and let D=product_{j=0}^{q-1} delta_j. Then

    V=v_ell(D)=sum_j v_ell(delta_j)>=k t=(q/s)t.
    V=0 iff t=0.

When t>0, this also gives q<=s floor(V/t)<=|R| floor(V/t).
Critical lifts with the same residue need not have equal positive
valuations; the proof uses only their individual lower bounds.
There is no bound on q from this inequality when t=0.

The signed cycle clock of the REAL object is C=log|D|, and the complete
stabilizer is C Z by Section 2. Its primitive length is log p for an
ordinary prime p exactly when

    D belongs {p,-p,1/p,-1/p}.

At ell=p, the admitted integral-denominator hypotheses make V>=0,
excluding both reciprocal possibilities. Thus D=+p or -p, V=1,
k=t=1, q=s<=|R|<=p^d. There is exactly one reduced critical phase;
its single actual occurrence has valuation exactly one, and every other
actual delta_j is a p-unit. The signed cycle clock is then +log p.

At an admitted place ell!=p, both p and 1/p are ell-units. Therefore a
prime-length return requires V=0 and t=0 at that place. The sign of D
is never detectable from the valuation, and is not discarded from D.

These are NECESSARY local restrictions. Even q=s, t=1 and V=1 at p
do not exclude another prime dividing the rational product; Control B

There is no sufficient condition here for lifting residue cycles, realizing
