## Object and purpose

Let `F:X->X`, `x in X`, and a time-independent observable `h:X->{0,1}` obey
`h(F^n x)=1` exactly when positive integer `n` is prime. This records a narrow
prime-symbolic obstruction before any sequential, Henon, or symplectic lift.

## Theorem

**Theorem.** `x` is not periodic under `F`.

**Proof.** Suppose `F^q x=x` for some `q>0`. Then `h(F^n x)` has period `q`.
For any prime `p`, its value at `p+pq` must therefore also be one. But
`p+pq=p(1+q)` is composite. Contradiction. ∎

The assertion concerns only the point carrying the stated prime-time
observation. It does not classify other periodic points of `F`, which cannot
be borrowed as its arithmetic orbit ledger.
