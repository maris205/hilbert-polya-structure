## 2. Full partial action and every inverse

remainder 1/x-j lies in [0,1). If a divides b+j, c
is a positive integer and the next seed belongs to [0,1/c),

    U_(a,b,j)={(a,b)} x (1/(j+1),1/j],

For every B,C there are infinitely many such A, so the
full image is exactly

    T(domain T)=coproduct_(B,C>=1) {(B,C)} x [0,1/C).

## 4. ALL fixed states and their actual primitive packets

### 4.1 Exhaustive return equation

Root equality in T(a,b,x)=(a,b,x) forces a=b=c=n and

    j=n^2-n,     n*x^2+j*x-1=0.

For n=1 this would require digit zero, which is not allowed.
For each n>=2 the quadratic is strictly increasing at x>=0,
negative at zero and positive at 1/n, since its value there
is n-2+1/n>0. There is exactly one positive solution x_n
in (0,1/n). Its equation gives

    1/x_n=j+n*x_n,     0<n*x_n<1,

so floor(1/x_n)=j exactly and the source divisibility condition
holds. Thus this algebraic solution really belongs to its digit
branch, not a formal root outside the domain. The COMPLETE
fixed locus is one state z_n=(n,n,x_n) for every n>=2.
Neither terminal x=0 nor endpoint x=1 is fixed.

### 4.2 Isotropy, minimal time and nonmerging

At z_n, source isotropy is exactly the retained-lag group Z.
Its positive-lag generator has clock

    L_n=-log(n*x_n^2)>log n>0.

Hence H_(z_n)=L_n Z, with least positive generator L_n, and
extension fixed-object isotropy is trivial. This is an actual
primitive time packet, not a sampled length or a repeated word.
All its r-fold repetitions have time r*L_n on the SAME packet.

Different fixed states have different constant T-tails, so no
arrow relates them. Arbitrarily long inverse excursions cannot merge
two such cores: groupoid composition would then give precisely
the forbidden equal-tail arrow. Transient preimages attached to a
core do not change its least time or create another core packet.
The proof classifies the fixed-family packets, not all higher returns.

### 4.3 Exact non-prime times, without relying on root labels

Put lambda_n=exp(L_n)=1/(n*x_n^2)>1. Squaring
j*x_n=1-n*x_n^2 and dividing by n*x_n^2 gives

    lambda_n + 1/lambda_n = n*(n-1)^2+2 = K_n,
    lambda_n^2-K_n*lambda_n+1=0.

Here K_n is an integer >=4. A rational root of this monic
integer polynomial with constant term one must be +1 or -1;
neither is lambda_n>1 satisfying K_n>=4. Thus lambda_n is
an irrational quadratic unit. In particular L_n is NOT log
of any integer, independent of any proposed root-to-prime label.

Already n=2 gives

    x_2=(sqrt(3)-1)/2,
    n*x_2^2=2-sqrt(3),
    L_2=log(2+sqrt(3)).

The composite root n=4 also gives a distinct genuine primitive,
with L_4=log(19+6*sqrt(10)), not an automatic repetition of
the n=2 packet. The direct prime/log-prime target therefore stops
at the fixed-family gate. This is not a proof against every
approximate statistical correspondence, alternative future model or
separately constructed arithmetic interpretation of quadratic units.
No relabelling or global retiming of this frozen owner is performed.

j=floor(1/t); an inverse exists precisely when A=(B+j)/C

### FACTOR-OFF

ALL A>=1 and t in [0,1/C), with predecessor

Along EVERY defined forward step its second root strictly increases
