# Arithmetic composition is not yet a primitive-return mechanism

Screen ID: `ASFS-SCOUT-20260920-CAF01`.
Paper ID: `290-composition-admission-frontier`. Date: 2026-09-20.
Main candidate: `NONE — PRE-P0 COMPARISON`.
Status: `NO NEW ADMISSION; COMPOSITION-RETURN INTERFACE OPEN — STOP / FORK`.
Result type: bounded definition comparison and search-admission inference,
not a new dynamical theorem or a general no-go.

## Abstract

After the complete packet and topology results for APR01, we compare
three proposed ways of linking arithmetic composition to time returns.
Finite adjacent factor exchange is an exact collision with an already
audited owner. All-scale residue embeddings specify a genuine composition
relation, but no additional relevant implication for prime primitives
or same-packet repetition has been identified. A full rational-matrix
residue action has a different stated carrier, but the arithmetic return
constraint is likewise missing. None is admitted as a new main candidate.
The unresolved generated-domain and matrix-owner questions are not
silently converted into negative mathematical results. The outcome
sharpens the next proposal requirement without discarding earlier
positive engineering or declaring the wider search impossible.

## 2. Lane E: exact collision with finite factor exchange

The submitted source is the entire space of normalized two-sided
divisor-cover chains y, y_0=1. If a_i=y_(i+1)/y_i and S_i exchanges
adjacent ratios, the proposed generators on X x R_(>0) are

    G(y,r)=(Fy,r/y_1), F(y)_j=y_(j+1)/y_1,
    H_i(y,r)=(S_i y,r).

The quotient is by every finite word in G, all H_i and their inverses;
time is multiplication of r by exp(t). The relation ab=ba is realized
by actual finite exchanges, not a selected prime-sector simplification.

This definition is exactly the [165 owner](../165-factor-exchange-scale-quotient/candidate-card.md):
same full chains, generators, scale convention, finite-word quotient
and time. Changing its descriptive motivation does not make a new owner.

Root read [165's proofs](../165-factor-exchange-scale-quotient/paper.md),
Propositions 1–5. Its exchange holonomy is

    G^(-1) H_(-1) G H_0(a,r)=(a,r*a_0/a_1).

For the retained alternating 2,3 chain, the two-step scale return
and this finite loop give log 6 and log(2/3). Proposition 4 proves
that the full return group is countable, proper and dense, with no
least positive time. It does NOT classify every state's full return
group. Proposition 5 separately preserves constant-prime discrete
return groups; it is not cited as an ambient circle-embedding proof.
These are cited OLD results under ANG-20260915-FES01, not a new
290 theorem or a result for lanes R/M.

**Admission decision: exact collision; no new admission.** Factor
exchange is a real arithmetic operation, but the already retained
mixed returns cannot be renamed as ordinary same-packet repetitions.
No new run or repaired exchange-scale rule is justified by this proposal.

## 3. Lane R: all-scale residue composition, missing return constraint

The complete proposed residue source is K=Z_hat with additive Haar.
The generating branches and their strict inverses are

    theta_(n,j):K -> j+nK, x -> j+nx,
    n>=1, 0<=j<n.

All finite generated arrows use their actual affine labels (q,r,x),
not an added word lag or a germ quotient. The submitted arithmetic
relation is theta_(m,i) theta_(n,j)=theta_(mn,i+mj). Its lineage
is specific: residue/divisibility symbols -> factor-dependent residue
composition on the same complete seed source.

The proposed clock would be minus the logarithm of the generated
owner's Borel IMAGE derivative, with a continuous full-point version
and full real extension. All of those are proposal obligations here.
No clock or stabilizer calculation is performed in this screen.

The closest comparison [275](../275-rational-affine-residue-flow/candidate-card.md)
instead freezes every rational-affine label on its entire maximal
admissible domain. The present proposal specifies a generated owner.
Whether it equals that full action is OPEN / NOT AUDITED. Neither
exact equality nor proper inclusion is asserted, so 275's return
classification is not transferred. [284](../284-least-divisor-borel-flow/candidate-card.md)
also uses strict-inverse finite generation, but has a different
state-selected rule; [286](../286-radix-return-rigidity/candidate-card.md)
fixes separate deterministic radix owners and retains lag. Those
definitions supply boundaries, not this proposal's theorem.

What is supplied is a relation between composition and integer index.
What is NOT supplied is an additional arithmetic admissibility/return
law relating irreducible factors to distinct primitive packets while
distinguishing genuine same-packet repeats from general mixed-factor
composition. Not every composite product must or can be a repetition
of a single prime packet.
An arrow product and a repeat of a closed time orbit are different
claims; the former alone proves neither existence nor primitivity
of the latter.

The digit i+mj is genuine residue arithmetic, not just the scalar
product mn. The generated branch domains may themselves have further
consequences for returns. This screen does not prove otherwise and
does not require an added predicate if the existing rules already
support a concrete new structural argument.

**Admission decision: no new admission on the submitted mechanism.**
This is a missing-interface judgement, not a proof that this generated
owner has no packets or could never support useful additional structure.
If an extra rule is later supplied, its first check must use the full
zero-seed time group, not only one chosen prime multiplier. That check
is named but NOT executed here.

## 4. Lane M: rational matrix mixing, not yet arithmetic return selection

Root's submitted definition uses X=K² with joint additive Haar,
the localization B=S^(-1)K, and every A in GL_2(Q) with det A>0,
on D_A={v in K²:Av in K²}. It retains matrix labels, all valid
compositions and complete seed pairs. Its IMAGE clock, full real
extension and return ledger remain proposed and unproved here.

The intended source replacement is simultaneous divisor/residue
symbols -> two-register completion -> rational linear mixing. This
is not the projective line of [281-P](../281-source-clock-admission-frontier/candidate-card.md),
nor 275's one-register affine action. A group action is an admissible
type in the broadened track: a single deterministic base map is NOT
required just to make such a proposal meaningful.

Nevertheless, supplying all rational matrices does not itself specify
the missing prime-admissibility mechanism or a relation controlling
primitive return/index products. This judgement is about the submitted
fields, not an inferred matrix-stabilizer obstruction. No joint-Haar
determinant formula, rational-rank classification or packet theorem
is supplied as evidence here; a hypothetical rank argument does not
replace the absent admission mechanism.

In particular D_A is a real integrality restriction, not absence of
all admissibility. A precise action already determines its returns;
the issue is that the proposal has not identified the relevant new
arithmetic-return implication, not that such an implication is proved
absent or must require another transition rule.

**Admission decision: no new admission.** The change of carrier is
explicit but insufficient for the claimed search objective at this
stage. Owner/clock/returns stay OPEN / NOT AUDITED; no result from
275, 281 or 288 applies by analogy. Selecting a rational line, a
prime subgroup or a restricted returning sector would be a new rule
requiring its own candidacy decision.

## 6. Decision and next concrete admission requirement

Portfolio: **stop these submitted admissions / fork**. No new main
candidate or dynamical coordinates are assigned. E collides exactly
with 165; R and M do not yet supply the additional composition-to-return
mechanism. Missing structure is not mathematical impossibility.

The next proposal should write ONE actual new transition, admissibility
relation or composition constraint, OR identify a concrete structural
implication of an existing rule, and explain its bearing on full
return/index structure while retaining mixed states. Its first frozen
tests should distinguish a 2*3 versus 3*2 composition loop from a
purported 2*2 same-packet repetition. Even the product 2*2 alone
does not prove that one primitive loop was traversed twice; the full
state, path and primitive convention must establish that. If it uses
generated residue arrows, keep full zero-state stabilizers in that test.
These are conditional design
requirements for the next proposal, not results about an uncreated owner.

Do not reopen 165 unchanged, run a generic matrix audit merely to add
a negative paper, or choose one prime subgroup from a larger stabilizer.
A genuinely different complete rule warrants a fresh card. The present
screen does not provide one and does not hide that limitation.
