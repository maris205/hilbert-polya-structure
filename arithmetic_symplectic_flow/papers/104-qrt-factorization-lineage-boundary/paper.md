## Screen

The source studies a symmetric QRT map as a second-order rational recurrence,
and recursively factorises its iterates into coupled Laurent recurrences.  A
QRT update is a genuine two-dimensional birational/reversible geometric
object; that makes it a useful comparator for the desired conservative-lift
arrow.  The word *factorisation* here denotes an algebraic treatment of the
iterates, however.  The cited construction supplies no prime/composite state,
sieve admissibility, prime-derived substitution, or deformation from the
project's Logistic/MSS source.

## Decisive controls

1. **Label-blind control.** The source definitions and factorisation theorem
   are unchanged if every reference to primes is removed: they are statements
   about rational-map iterates and Laurent recurrences.
2. **Lineage-direction control.** A reversible recurrence cannot be used as a
   realization of the sieve word unless an explicit coding/intertwiner is
   supplied.  None is supplied here; importing one from the primorial or GPF
   records would violate the one-object ledger.
3. **Parameter-provenance control.** Selecting QRT coefficients or initial
   values after inspecting primes would add an external fitting/input channel,
   rather than turn algebraic factorisation into endogenous arithmetic.

Thus this is not a P0 candidate.  It stops before A0: it confirms that the
geometric target class can be reversible, but gives no first lineage arrow to
which an A0 mechanism could belong.
