# A one-sided sieve chronology has no endogenous natural extension

**Paper ID:** `026-sieve-natural-extension-obstruction`  
**Record ID:** `ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-20`  
**Date / status:** `2026-09-14; METHOD STOP RULE — NO ENDOGENOUS BI-INFINITE SIEVE HISTORY`  
**Route state:** `No frozen candidate; Route A coordinates not evaluated; Route B NOT INVOKED`

## Question

Can the strictly advancing sieve-stage obstruction be avoided merely by placing
the sequential system in a formal natural extension or inverse limit?

## Exact obstruction

Consider a faithful sequential carrier

```text
Y = {(s,k): k in N, k >= 1},       U(s,k) = (u_k(s),k+1).
```

If `(s_n,k_n)_(n in Z)` were a bi-infinite `U`-orbit in `Y`, then
`k_(n+1)=k_n+1`, hence `k_n=k_0+n`.  Taking `n<-k_0+1` contradicts `k_n>=1`.
Thus `U` has no bi-infinite orbit on its own stated carrier.

For the primorial recursion this is not a bookkeeping artifact.  The forward
law is `P_(k+1)=p_(k+1)P_k`, with the sieve beginning at its first prime stage.
At that boundary the proposed predecessor is not supplied by the same
arithmetic process.  Declaring a new negative stage, a fractional primorial,
or an arbitrary predecessor does not recover the original one-sided sieve;
it defines another object that needs its own P0 card and lineage proof.

## Consequence for breadth screening

An inverse-limit construction may be meaningful only after it gives actual
compatible preimages for every time and proves that the prime-symbolic rule
descends to the resulting recurrent carrier.  The bare phrase “natural
extension” is therefore not an A1 repair for records 019 or 025.  It neither
creates primitive closed orbits nor provides a symplectic map, roof, or trace
owner.

**Portfolio decision: fork.** Search only for a new, explicitly two-sided or
recurrent arithmetic law; do not invest in a completion that obtains recurrence
by deleting the sole coordinate that owns the sieve chronology.
