\(2\varphi^9=76+34\sqrt5\) is not an integer. This is an adverse primitive

## 2. Full derivative, all inverse branches and IMAGE

### 2.1 The nine-dimensional guard

For invertible \(A\), differentiation of
\(\operatorname{adj}(A)=sA^{-1}\) gives
\[
D\operatorname{adj}_A[H]
=s\{\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}H)A^{-1}-A^{-1}HA^{-1}\}.       \tag{3}
\]
Write \(H=AK\). The input change has determinant \(s^3\) on \(M_3\).
The output is \(sR(K)A^{-1}\), with \(R(K)=\operatorname{tr}(K)I_3-K\).
The scalar direction has multiplier \(2\), and all eight traceless
directions multiplier \(-1\), so \(\det_{\mathbb R^9}R=2\).
Output multiplication by \(s\) has determinant \(s^9\), and right
multiplication by \(A^{-1}\) has determinant \(s^{-3}\). Consequently
\[
\det_{\mathbb R^9}D\operatorname{adj}_A=2s^3.             \tag{4}
\]
Both sides of (4) are polynomials in the entries; equality on the dense
invertible set proves equality also at singular matrices. Thus the
adjugate guard is exactly \(s\ne0\), for M, G and Q independently.
The fixed-label translations in M/G do not change this derivative.
L has identity derivative on all nine entries, with determinant \(1\).
This proves the domain table and the full guard at every integer seed.

### 2.2 Exhaustive inverse formulas

\[
\Theta_\epsilon(C)=\epsilon\sqrt D\,C^{-1},
\qquad \epsilon\in\{+1,-1\}.                            \tag{5}
\]

## 3. Whole history owner, incoming and clock groups

Fix one owner \(U\), never mixing its branches with another row.
Let \(D_r\) be the set of sources with \(r\) legal steps, \(D_0=X\), and
\[
S_r(A)=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\kappa_U(U^jA),\quad S_0=0,\qquad
\Lambda_r(A)=e^{S_r(A)},\quad\Lambda_0=1.
\]
For M/G/Q this gives \(2^r\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}|\det U^jA|^3\);
for L it is \(1\). No product includes an undefined terminal step.

Use the Borel groupoid
\[
G_U=\{(A,r-s,B):A\in D_r,\ B\in D_s,\ U^rA=U^sB\},\qquad
c(A,r-s,B)=S_r(A)-S_s(B).                              \tag{10}
\]
Source is \(B\), range \(A\), and only equal actual triples are identified.
The inherited Borel structure is that of \(X\times\mathbb Z\times X\).
For equal lag, any two witnesses differ by a common forward advance;
the added clock sums along their common tail cancel. Thus \(c\) descends.
To compose two arrows, advance the witness with the smaller middle
iterate to the larger one. That advance is legal along the other witness,
and determinism transports its legality to the matching endpoint.
The middle clock sums then cancel, proving additivity. Inversion changes
the sign. In particular the actual forward arrow is
\[
(UA,-1,A),\qquad c(UA,-1,A)=-\kappa_U(A).               \tag{11}
\]
This proof is valid for partial maps and does not extend terminals.

Finite compositions of the actual inverse atlas give countable Borel
arrow pieces. A piece from \(B\) to \(A\) has the local form
\((U^r)^{-1}\circ U^s\) with its full actual source checks.

\(\Lambda_s(B)/\Lambda_r(A)=e^{-c}\); repeated change of variables proves
the every-Borel identity for these pieces too. The global kernels are

\begin{split}

K_c&=\{(A,r-s,B)\in G_U:\Lambda_r(A)=\Lambda_s(B)\},\\

\end{split}                                                   \tag{12}

\(K_{\rm joint}=K_{\rm lag}\); this does not classify its source cycles.

For completeness, source isotropy is entirely determined as follows.

forward iterates, hence to an eventual periodic cycle. If its least
source period is \(p\), all isotropy lags and only those are \(p\mathbb Z\):
advance any witness into the cycle for necessity, and use sufficiently

Changing the arrow changes this by a loop clock in \(H_B\), so it is
