## Abstract

A partial measured Borel evolution is extended by a full circle whose angle
is translated by a fixed Borel function of the current source. We prove the
extension's own every-Borel inverse IMAGE identity and its prescribed
every-point clock, without assuming differentiability of that function.
The entire history groupoid is determined by the parent's actual arrows
and their accumulated angle. Above a least-q parent cycle with signed clock
C and circle return angle A of finite order d, each fibre cycle gives a
least-qd lifted cycle with entire time group dC Z. There are continuum many
distinct such full source packets, including their unrestricted incoming.
An infinite-order return angle gives no lifted periodic core, even over a
periodic parent. Consequently this whole class cannot have a nonempty,
prime-only, prime-unique positive ledger. The obstruction
uses no rational-exponential hypothesis. Four full controls expose zero,
rational and irrational rotation as well as partial terminal histories.
The theorem is a filter for a proposed symbolic angular-memory lift, not
an endogenous arithmetic source or an obstruction to all other carriers.

## 2. Complete inverse branches and every-Borel IMAGE

For each parent branch the full inverse is

    Theta_i(y,t)=(theta_i(y),t−alpha(theta_i(y))), (y,t)∈B_i×S.   (2)

It maps bijectively onto D_i×S. The source pieces D_i×S cover every legal source
exactly once. All maps in (2) are Borel, and the forward restriction is
Borel as well. Distinct parent predecessors give distinct full predecessors;
there is no root cutoff, extra label multiplicity or target-permission test.

**Theorem 1 (owned product IMAGE).** Prescribe J_F,i(y,t)=J_i(y) at every
actual inverse point. Then for every Borel E⊂B_i×S,

    nu(Theta_i(E)) = integral_E J_i(y) dmu(y) dlambda(t).        (3)

Consequently the actual all-point clock of F is kappa_F(x,s)=kappa(x).

**Proof.** A Borel bijection here has the displayed Borel inverse, so
Theta_i(E) is Borel. Its section at x∈D_i is the circle translation by
−alpha(x) of E_{Tx}. Haar length is unchanged by this translation. Tonelli
therefore gives nu(Theta_i(E))=integral_{D_i} lambda(E_{Tx}) dmu(x).
The parent IMAGE identity, first for indicators and then nonnegative simple
functions and monotone limits, changes this integral to
integral_{B_i} lambda(E_y) J_i(y) dmu(y), proving (3). This applies to every
Borel E, not merely rectangles, and allows infinite integrals. The prescribed
pointwise J is positive finite and yields the stated logarithm at every
legal source, including null strata. No derivative of Borel alpha is used. □

For all n≥0 let P_n(y) be the set of every legal parent n-step predecessor,
P_0(y)={y}, obtained recursively with all theta_i. Put
A_n(x)=sum_{j<n} alpha(T^j x) in S and S_n(x)=sum_{j<n} kappa(T^j x),
with zero sums at n=0. Induction gives

    F^n(x,s)=(T^n x,s+A_n(x)),
    P_n^F(y,t)={(x,t−A_n(x)):x∈P_n(y)}.                       (4)

Thus all finite incoming depths, terminal targets and compatible infinite
predecessor sequences are retained. Formula (4) is exact at every depth,
not a finite experimental census or a replacement by an inverse-limit space.

## 3. Actual angle and clock cocycles

For U=T,F retain all actual triples (z,m−n,w) with legal U^m z=U^n w,
m,n≥0; equal triples are identified and the integer lag remains. Source is
w, range is z. Units exist at every object. Define c_U=S_m^U(z)−S_n^U(w).
For a parent arrow g=(x,k,y) with witness m,n define also

    a(g)=A_m(x)−A_n(y) in S, c_T(g)=S_m(x)−S_n(y).             (5)

These functions are independent of the witness. Indeed two witnesses for
the same lag differ by equal increments at both ends. Their added angle
and clock sums are taken on the same legal future and cancel. Composition
is legal by aligning the two histories at their common middle object: extend
the shorter one using steps already present in the longer one, not fictitious
terminal steps. The same cancellation proves additivity of a and c.
The arrow (Tx,−1,x) has a=−alpha(x) and c=−kappa(x), fixing both signs.
All actual-arrow sets are Borel countable unions of legal equality sets.

**Theorem 2 (exact lifted arrows).** A triple ((x,s),k,(y,t)) is in G_F iff
g=(x,k,y)∈G_T and s=t−a(g). Its clock is exactly c_T(g).

**Proof.** A legal F meeting is precisely a legal T meeting together with
s+A_m(x)=t+A_n(y). This is the displayed condition and does not depend on
the chosen parent witness by (5). Conversely any such witness lifts to the
required equality via (4). The clock conclusion follows from Theorem1 and
the same witness. Every parent arrow has one lift for each specified source
angle, but the projection need not retain its isotropy at that angle. □

The full kernels, not merely their isotropy restrictions, are therefore

    K_l={lifted g:k=0}, K_c={lifted g:c_T(g)=0},
    K_l,c={lifted g:k=0 and c_T(g)=0}.                         (6)

Here every lift satisfies Theorem2's angle condition. All incoming arrows
to z are obtained by choosing any legal F^r z and all P_n^F(F^r z), with
lag r−n; (4) supplies every root. This includes a terminal z with r=0.
No complete groupoid is replaced by a cycle, source slice or orbit closure.

The height extension is the whole Y×R with arrows (w,h)→(z,h+c_F).
For a full source orbit choose an anchor only to describe coordinates.
If b(z) is the clock of any arrow from the anchor to z, two such choices
differ by the entire isotropy clock image H. Thus h−b(z) modulo H is the
complete phase; all real phases occur. The orbit SET has translation
stabilizer exactly H. This argument asserts no global Borel selector or

## 4. Every history type and the complete periodic ledger

A deterministic partial parent has nonzero isotropy precisely at eventually
periodic objects. If its eventual core has least period q, its isotropy is
qZ: equality of unequal legal iterates gives a cycle, and thereafter the
depth differences are exactly multiples of its least period. Initial tails
cancel in (5). Write C for the signed sum of kappa and A for the sum of
alpha around this core. At any parent ancestor, the isotropy characters are

    c_T(lq)=lC, a(lq)=lA, l∈Z.                               (7)

Terminal-ending and infinite non-eventual parents have zero isotropy, so
their lifts do also by Theorem2. This excludes new cycles from such parents.

**Theorem 3 (full circle return classification).** Let d be the additive
order of A∈S, a positive integer or infinity. If d<infinity, every point
above the parent core belongs to a lifted core of least period qd. If
d=infinity there is no lifted periodic core above it. For every ancestor
and every angle the entire lifted isotropy and time image are

    d<infinity: I_F=qdZ, c_F(lqd)=ldC, H_F=dC Z;
    d=infinity: I_F={0}, H_F={0}.                            (8)

When d<infinity and C≠0, the primitive is d abs(C), all integer repeats;
extension isotropy is trivial. If C=0, the full qdZ source isotropy remains
as extension isotropy but there is no positive primitive. Infinite-order
angles give zero isotropy even if C≠0.

**Proof.** A return in the product must first return its base point, so its
length is lq, l>0. At that length its angle has moved by lA; equality holds
exactly when lA=0. The least such l is d if finite. Equation(7) and the
exact angle condition give every isotropy element in (8), not just a
subgroup. The clock and primitive assertions follow from the entire image.
All ancestors have the same test because their common transient cancels. □

For explicit complete packet assignment write the parent core x_j=T^j x_0,
0≤j<q, and rho_j=A_j(x_0). A lifted core corresponding to a coset
theta+<A> in S/<A> consists of all

    (x_j,theta+rho_j+lA), 0≤j<q, l∈Z.                        (9)

For finite d use 0≤l<d; these are qd distinct points. For infinite d this
is an infinite source orbit on the parent core, not a periodic core.
Distinct cosets cannot merge: any meeting would differ by a core loop
and hence by a multiple of A. Conversely equal cosets meet along the core.

If x first enters x_j at depth n, its full lifted source class is assigned
by the coset of s+A_n(x)−rho_j modulo <A>. This uses all x in every
P_n(x_j), including all legal branch choices and cut faces. Every ancestor
is assigned, and no incoming can join two different core classes. Moving
the reference from x_0 to another phase translates the coset labels by the
appropriate rho_j; the total A, C, orders, whole groups and counts stay.
Equation(6) and the phase construction after it apply on each whole class,
including infinite-order rotation and zero C; no phase is selected away.

For clarity, the nonperiodic history types also have full coordinate tests.
If a parent class ends at terminal b, write n_x for the unique depth from
x to b. Lifted terminal classes are indexed by the actual angle
sigma=s+A_{n_x}(x) at b. The phase is h−S_{n_x}(x)∈R, source/extension
isotropy zero. Meetings have lag n_x−n_y and clock S_{n_x}(x)−S_{n_y}(y).
This includes every terminal and every incoming branch, not just terminal
points with positive measure.
For an infinite non-eventual parent class fix any anchor b. Between b and
x there is exactly one retained-lag arrow: two would give nonzero isotropy.
Call its angle and clock a_x,b_x. Then theta=s+a_x labels the complete
lifted source classes, and h−b_x∈R labels their full phases. All meetings
and kernels are still exactly Theorem2 and (6). These orbitwise labels need
not vary measurably between different parent classes.

## 5. Multiplicity obstruction, without a rational-clock premise

**Corollary 4.** No owner in this conditional class satisfies both nonempty
positive ledger and at-most-one full packet for each ordinary prime while
all its primitives are logarithms of such primes.

**Proof.** A positive primitive can only arise from a parent core with
C≠0 and a finite-order A, by (8). For such a core, S/<A> has continuum
many elements: when A has order d, each coset has d points and may be
represented by a half-open interval of length1/d after the finite rotation
subgroup is identified with {j/d}. These are distinct full source classes
by (9), all with the same primitive d abs(C). If that value is not a
prime logarithm, purity fails. If it is, uniqueness fails. If no such
parent core exists, the positive ledger is empty. These exhaust the class. □

No rational condition on exp(abs C) was needed. Infinite-order angular
escape from periodicity is real but gives no positive packet to replace
the removed ones. Changing a dense orbit to its closure, retaining one
angle, dividing a period by d or discarding a null base core would change
the frozen owner. The result does not rule out countable fibres, nonfree
angular actions, different measured lifts or independently frozen objects.
