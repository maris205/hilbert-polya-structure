## 2. Direction test

The located countable-Markov surface result describes the symbolic dynamics
of an already given `C1+` surface diffeomorphism, subject to its stated
almost-Borel scope.  In logical form, it supplies information of the form

```text
specified surface diffeomorphism  ->  countable Markov symbolic data.
```

The needed arrow is materially stronger and reverse-directed:

```text
this B1 path shift  ->  one specified conservative/symplectic surface map
                         with an arithmetic-preserving orbit relation.
```

The first statement neither creates the second nor selects the missing map.
Moreover, it does not make the selected surface action area-preserving, does
not carry the `B1` factor rule, and does not identify the prime two-packet
with an actual primitive surface orbit.  A generic natural extension or a
surface merely having some countable coding would still leave every required
owner unspecified.

shift`, `realization`, and `area-preserving surface diffeomorphism`) located
only the same surface-to-shift coding results and purely symbolic
compactification/factor literature.  It did not locate an explicit
area-preserving realization theorem applicable to the specified `B1` graph.
This is `NOT LOCATED`, not a claim that no such realization can exist.
