This new owner completes 193's integer indecomposable vector space in
every polynomially weighted coefficient norm. It adds genuine infinite-
support positive states while keeping a full continuous integer-size
map and inverse. The entire nonzero positive cone has a Hausdorff
quotient with complete radial flow, one log-prime circle per prime,
all repetitions, and no mixed-support periodic state. The old finite-
support flow embeds densely and time-preservingly in the new topology.

[claim ledger](claim-ledger.md) and [evidence/review index](evidence/README.md).

is complete in its inherited metric. The lone unweighted completion

non-Hausdorff. Naturalness remains OPEN. No symplectic, Hilbert,
operator, zeta or formal Route result is supplied by this package.
