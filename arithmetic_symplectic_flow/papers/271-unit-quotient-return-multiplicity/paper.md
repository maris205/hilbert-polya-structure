## Abstract

We audit the entire compact-unit quotient of the compatible integer
residue-word carrier, with all admissible positive-rational scaling
arrows. Its pushed-forward additive Haar measure gives the uniform
image Jacobian 1/q and hence the same-owner cocycle log q. The complete
time-return subgroup at a state is generated by log p for precisely
its infinitely divisible prime coordinates. Exactly one such coordinate
therefore gives least return log p and repetitions r log p. However,
for each p there are continuum many distinct cyclic-return packets:
their remaining finite valuations are identified only under finite
modification. States with at least two infinitely divisible coordinates
have dense returns; all return-bearing states have measure zero but
remain in the frozen carrier. The coarse extension quotient is not T1.
Thus exact prime lengths coexist with a decisive full-multiplicity
failure. This is a bounded reaudit of a known scaling construction,
not new adelic geometry, a classical symplectic lift or a trace result.

## 3. The complete unit quotient and symbolic interface

Write Nbar_0=N_0 union {infinity}, topologized by isolated finite
integers and neighborhoods {n>=N} union {infinity} at infinity.
Chinese remaindering of the inverse limit gives

    X = product over primes p of Z_p,
    U = product over primes p of Z_p^×.

These products have no restriction to finite-support coordinates.
Set v_p(0)=infinity in Z_p, and use its ordinary valuation otherwise.

### Proposition 1 — complete quotient, not a selected prime alphabet

The map v:X→product_p Nbar_0 induces a homeomorphism

    Y = X/U  ≅  product_p Nbar_0.

Under it, e_d is the indicator of alpha_p>=v_p(d) for every p dividing
d. All e_d descend, and together they separate every pair of unit
classes. For n,d>=1 the integer-action interface is

    e_d(thetabar_n y) = e_(d/gcd(d,n))(y).

**Proof.** Exact finite valuation shells and valuation tails in Z_p
are clopen, so every coordinate valuation is continuous into Nbar_0.
Every profile alpha has a representative x_p=p^(alpha_p) at finite
coordinates and x_p=0 at infinite ones. Two representatives have equal
valuations precisely when at every nonzero coordinate their ratio is
a unit; at coordinates where both vanish choose unit 1. These choices
assemble to an element of the entire product U. Thus valuation fibers
are exactly U-orbits. The induced continuous bijection from the compact
quotient to the Hausdorff product is a homeomorphism.

Membership in dX is exactly the displayed valuation inequality, hence
is U-invariant. The truth values of all e_(p^k), k>=1, recover each
finite valuation or distinguish infinity, proving separation. Adding
v_p(n) changes the condition alpha_p>=v_p(d) to
alpha_p>=max(v_p(d)−v_p(n),0). These are the exponents of d/gcd(d,n),
which proves the identity, including infinite coordinates. ∎

This quotient preserves finite divisibility data exactly but is not
lossless for the original residue words: for example, unit residues
are identified. It does not claim to preserve signed integer identity,
a shift chronology or a dynamical prime-generating theorem.

## 4. All rational arrows and the quotient-owned clock

For q=a/b>0 in lowest terms put k_p=v_p(q). Only finitely many k_p
are nonzero. In valuation coordinates the source domain and map are

    D_q = {alpha: alpha_p+k_p>=0 at every finite alpha_p},
    thetabar_q(alpha)_p = alpha_p+k_p,  infinity+k_p=infinity.

D_q is pi(bX), and the range is pi(aX)=D_(q^−1). These are clopen.
Multiplication by a nonzero integer is injective on every Z_p, hence
on X. Therefore theta_q is well-defined, and it commutes with U. Its
domains are U-saturated, so it descends to precisely this homeomorphism.

The inverse is thetabar_(q^−1). The composable domain of first r and
then q is D_r intersect D_(qr): the intermediate and final valuations
must both be nonnegative. On that domain composition adds k(r)+k(q)
and is thetabar_(qr). It need not have the entire domain of the product
map if an intermediate state is excluded. These formulas give all
partial-action and groupoid composition laws without extending an
arrow to an invalid domain.

### Proposition 2 — Borel-image Jacobian on this quotient

For every Borel E contained in D_q,

    nu(thetabar_q E) = (1/q) nu(E),
    Jbar_q = 1/q,       cbar(q,y)=log q.

The constant is determined by the full map, not by selecting a prime
return state or using an almost-everywhere orbit count.

**Proof.** A residue cylinder r+mX has mu-measure 1/m. Its image under
integer multiplication n is nr+nmX, with measure 1/(nm). The finite
measure B↦n mu(nB) is therefore a probability agreeing with mu on
the generating clopen residue-cylinder algebra. Measure uniqueness
gives mu(nB)=mu(B)/n for all Borel B. Images are Borel because
multiplication is a homeomorphism onto nX. For B contained in bX,
write B=bC; this gives mu(theta_q B)=(b/a)mu(B).

For the quotient there is the exact saturated-preimage identity

    pi^−1(thetabar_q E) = theta_q(pi^−1 E).

Indeed U-commutation gives both inclusions; any unit relating a point
to theta_q x can be moved to x in the saturated source. The set pi^−1 E
lies in bX. Taking mu on both sides proves the claim for all Borel E,
not just coordinate cylinders. In particular nu(D_q)=1/b and the
range has measure 1/a, fixing the uniform constant b/a. ∎

The pushforward nu has full support: the preimage of a nonempty open
subset of Y is nonempty open in X and has positive Haar measure.
Consequently a continuous Jacobian on a clopen q-domain agreeing
almost everywhere with this constant cannot differ on its null strata.
The frozen continuous, uniform clock is thus well-defined there also.
This does not make the choice of measure and cocycle-extension design
a proved natural physical mechanism; that further naturalness claim
remains OPEN. An unspecified measurable derivative alone would not
have fixed arbitrary null-set values.

The q-chart of the groupoid is D_q, with the disjoint-union topology
over the discrete countable group Q_(>0). Each chart is compact open;
the arrow space is locally compact Hausdorff and source and range are
local homeomorphisms. The cocycle is continuous chartwise and additive
on composable arrows. Its extension has objects Y×R and arrows

    (q,y,u): (y,u) -> (thetabar_q y, u+log q).

Give each q-chart the topology D_q×R. Source and range remain local
homeomorphisms; the extension is again a locally compact Hausdorff

translation on arrow coordinates, is a continuous automorphism for

## 5. Complete return classification and repetitions

For a profile alpha define I(alpha)={p:alpha_p=infinity}. Time returns
mean that Phi_t(alpha,u) is isomorphic to (alpha,u) in the extension,
exactly as in the card. Set

    H_alpha = {log q: alpha in D_q, thetabar_q alpha=alpha}.

### Proposition 3 — all states, not only canonical profiles

The entire time-return group is

    H_alpha = sum over p in I(alpha) of Z log p,

where every sum is finite. Consequently:

| Infinite-coordinate set | Time-return group | Primitive convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty | {0} | No positive return |
| Exactly {p} | (log p) Z | Least time log p; repeats r log p, r>=1 |
| At least two primes | Countable dense subgroup of R | No least positive time; no chosen primitive |

**Proof.** At a finite alpha_p the equation alpha_p+k_p=alpha_p forces
k_p=0. At infinity any integer k_p preserves the coordinate and stays
in the domain. Thus admissible fixing rationals are precisely the
finite products of integer powers of primes in I(alpha), giving the
formula. If two distinct primes p and l occur, log p/log l is
irrational: a rational equality would imply p^a=l^b for positive
integers a,b, contradicting unique factorization. Pigeonholing the
fractional parts of j log p/log l gives nonzero group elements
arbitrarily close to zero. Integer multiples of such elements
approximate any real number, so the subgroup is dense and has no
least positive element. This also covers infinite I(alpha). ∎

Base-groupoid isotropy is the multiplicative rational subgroup just
described. In contrast, fixed-object isotropy of the extension is
trivial: it also requires log q=0, hence q=1. The table gives time
stabilizers on extension isomorphism classes, not fixed-object
isotropy. For a cyclic case the R-orbit is abstractly the homogeneous
R-set R/((log p)Z). We do not identify its subspace topology in the
coarse quotient with a Hausdorff circle.

Every state having any positive return is nu-null in aggregate.
Indeed nu{alpha_p>=k}=p^(−k), whence
nu{alpha_p=infinity}=0 by continuity from above. The return-bearing
set is the countable union of these null sets over all primes. This
does not delete it: the frozen packet count is full-state, not an
almost-everywhere count.

## 6. The decisive multiplicity obstruction

Fix any prime p. The profiles with I(alpha)={p} have arbitrary finite
coordinates in N_0 at all other primes, not necessarily zero outside
a finite set. All have least return log p by Proposition 3.

### Proposition 4 — continuum distinct packets at every prime length

Under the actual rational arrows and physical time translations, two
such profiles give the same packet if and only if they differ at only
finitely many other prime coordinates. Hence the set of packets with
I={p} is

    N_0^(Primes minus {p}) / finite modification,

and its cardinality is 2^(aleph_0).

**Proof.** A rational has nonzero valuations at only finitely many
primes. It therefore changes only finitely many finite coordinates.
Conversely, if beta−alpha has finite support away from p, take
q=product_(l!=p) l^(beta_l−alpha_l). Its negative valuations require
only the denominator divisibility already supplied by alpha, because
beta_l>=0. Thus alpha is in D_q and thetabar_q alpha=beta. Any two
u-values can then be connected by an actual time translation. A finite
chain of arrows and times gives nothing larger: translations commute
with arrows and a composable chain multiplies to one rational arrow.
This proves the exact equivalence, including the time coordinate.

There are at most continuum profiles, hence at most continuum packets.
For the reverse bound, partition the countably infinite primes other
than p into countably many disjoint infinite blocks. To every binary
sequence assign a profile constant at its j-th bit throughout block j.
Distinct sequences differ on an infinite block and so are not finitely
equivalent. This embeds the set of all binary sequences into the
packet quotient, proving the lower bound. ∎

The canonical profile alpha_p=infinity, alpha_l=0 for l!=p represents
only one packet: its class includes all finite-support finite tails.
The profile with alpha_l=1 at every l!=p is already a different packet
with exactly the same return group and least time. Proposition 4 proves
the much stronger full cardinality; it does not mistakenly count each
coordinate representative as a separate orbit.

Thus the clock has the desired prime values but the full packet ledger
does not have finite prime multiplicity. Equality of a stabilizer or
length does not identify different packets. Quotienting all these
tails away, selecting the canonical packet, or deleting null states
would change the frozen owner and is not performed.

### Coarse-topology control

The zero class is the all-infinity profile. Its extension orbit through
(zero,0) is exactly {zero}×log Q_(>0). This subset is not closed in
Y×R because log Q_(>0) is a proper dense subgroup of R. If the coarse
orbit space were T1, the inverse image of each of its singleton points
would be closed. This orbit contradicts that requirement. The coarse
space is therefore not T1 and not Hausdorff, despite the Hausdorff
groupoid object and arrow spaces. No assertion about embedded
Hausdorff circles or ordinary manifold geometry follows from the
cyclic stabilizers in Proposition 3.
