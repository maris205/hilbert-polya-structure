## Checkpoint 1 — raw-card scope and independent derivation: PASS

**Complete quotient.** Chinese remaindering gives X=product_p Z_p and
U=product_p Z_p^×. The valuation map into product_p(N_0 union {infinity})
is continuous, surjective, and has exactly U-orbits as fibers: matching
nonzero coordinates differ by units, while zero coordinates permit unit
1. Compactness and the Hausdorff product topology give the stated
quotient homeomorphism. Every profile occurs, including infinitely many
nonzero but finite valuations. The tests e_(p^k) distinguish every
coordinate, so all finite divisibility tests separate unit classes.
This does not recover residue phases, ordering or sieve chronology.

**Partial action.** For q=a/b reduced, write k_p=v_p(q). The exact domain
requires alpha_p+k_p>=0 at every finite coordinate; infinity is unchanged.
This is pi(bX), not an unrestricted global rational action. Addition of
k gives the quotient arrow, with inverse q^(-1). The domain for first r
then q is D_r intersect D_(qr). Keeping this intermediate-state condition
avoids extending a composable arrow to all of D_(qr).

**Same-owner Borel Jacobian.** The residue-cylinder measure proof gives
mu(nB)=mu(B)/n for every Borel B. For a Borel E in D_q its inverse image
under pi is U-saturated and lies in bX. Commutation with units gives the
exact identity pi^(-1)(thetabar_q E)=theta_q(pi^(-1)E), hence the quotient
image measure is nu(E)/q. Domain and range masses 1/b and 1/a also fix
the uniform constant. Thus cbar=log q is proved for this quotient;
it is neither borrowed from an unquotiented owner nor a prime roof.

**All return groups.** Put I={p:alpha_p=infinity}. A fixing rational can
have nonzero prime valuations only in I, and every such finite-support
valuation choice is admissible. Base isotropy is the resulting rational
subgroup; its logarithm is H_alpha=sum_(p in I) Z log p, with finite sums.
Extension fixed-object isotropy is trivial because log q=0 forces q=1.
Time stabilizers of extension isomorphism classes are H_alpha, not that
trivial isotropy. Empty I gives no return; singleton I={p} gives least
positive time log p; two or more primes give a dense group with no least
positive time, by irrationality of log p/log l and arbitrarily small
nonzero integer combinations. This also covers infinite I and zero.

**Complete cyclic multiplicity.** For fixed singleton I={p}, rational
arrows change only finitely many other valuations. Conversely every
finite difference is implemented by the rational product of the required
prime powers; its denominator is admissible because the target profile
remains nonnegative. Physical translation aligns any time coordinates.
Thus packets are exactly finite-modification classes of arbitrary
N_0-valued tails, not classes determined merely by p or the return group.
There are continuum many: repeat each bit of a binary sequence over its
own infinite block of primes to obtain pairwise inequivalent tails.
The canonical zero tail covers only finite-support tails. The all-one
tail is already a different packet of the same least period.

**Topology and measure controls.** All rational charts are clopen
homeomorphism charts; the resulting groupoid and its real-cocycle
extension have Hausdorff object/arrow spaces and complete real-time
translation. The zero extension orbit is {zero} times log Q_(>0), which
is nonclosed; the coarse orbit space is therefore not T1. Meanwhile
nu{alpha_p=infinity}=lim_k p^(-k)=0. The countable union of all such
strata is null but remains part of the full frozen packet owner.

These derivations already trigger the precommitted full-multiplicity
stop. They do not authorize an Euler product, trace or T3 repair.
