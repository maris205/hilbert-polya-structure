## Abstract

We audit the full subgroupoid generated by proper-divisor affine branches on
the nonnegative real line, with its original Lebesgue measure and actual image
derivative clock. All composite integers form one source orbit, whose entire
clock isotropy is exactly log(2)Z. Noninteger composite-floor orbits are classified
by a dyadic invariant and have trivial isotropy. Every other MAIN object is
isolated. Thus the complete positive ledger has exactly one log(2) packet and
fails the required coverage of every ordinary prime. Three separately owned
controls exhibit duplication, dense return clocks, and complete zero isotropy.
No selected section, prime roof, probability, or operator is used.

Every source orbit is the exact set O(x)={g x: x in D_g}; it is countable.
Equivalently start with x, repeatedly add every applicable generator and actual

history recursion, not a finite cutoff. It retains outgoing dead ends and every
incoming branch. Direct inverse tests are, respectively,

    MAIN: n=d*m, d,m>=2,  y in [2m,2m+1/d), predecessor d*y-n;

Always take the union of ALL these tests, not one inverse chosen by a floor.
The clock kernel is exactly the actual translation arrows (a=1), which may
join different objects. Source isotropy is G_x^x={(g,x): g x=x}; its entire
clock image is H_x={-log a: (g,x) in G_x^x}. Clock-zero source isotropy is
trivial: a=1 and ax+b=x force b=0. Consequently every extension object (x,h)
has trivial fixed-object isotropy, even when the clock kernel is nontrivial.
Conjugating a source loop by an actual arrow preserves its clock, by the
cocycle identity; therefore H is identical throughout each source orbit.

Lift an arrow to (x,h)->(gx,h+c(g,x)) on the full X times R. Fix x0 in a

    [h-c_x] in R/H_x0.

Two reference choices differ by an actual source loop, hence by H_x0; this

equality of these cosets gives a source isotropy arrow and therefore an actual

## 5. MAIN: complete integer and noninteger classification

### 5.1 Integer core and the complete orbit through 4

Let C be the set of composite positive integers. Integers and nonintegers
cannot mix: in each MAIN branch, r becomes r/d, and in each actual inverse
it becomes d*r while remaining below 1. Every generator has a composite floor
at both endpoints. Hence every point whose floor is not composite is isolated,
including all prime integers, 0, 1, and their noninteger cells.

At an integer n in C the target is 2n/d. If n is even, choose d=n/2 to reach 4
in one step. If n is odd composite, any proper divisor first gives an even
composite, then the preceding step reaches 4. Conversely no edge leaves C.
Therefore O(4)=C, with ALL composite integers as incoming objects.

For a legal integer-to-integer word, let ell be its number of forward letters
minus inverse letters. This is initially a property of a presentation only.
Since each forward slope is n'/(2n), its total slope from n to m is

    a=2^(-ell)*m/n,       c=ell*log 2+log(n/m).                 (1)

At 4 the legal chart f_(4,2) fixes 4 with slope 1/2. Join any n,m in C through
4 and insert arbitrary positive or negative powers of this actual loop.
Thus every integer ell in (1) occurs, and there are no other slopes.
In particular the full arrow set n->m is

    g_(n,m,ell)(t)=2^(-ell)*(m/n)*t + m*(1-2^(-ell)), ell in Z.

These are distinct affine labels. The entire source isotropy at n consists
of g_(n,n,ell), and H_n=log(2)Z. This is a full word-closure result, not the
inference that one exhibited fixed branch must generate all returns.
The integer clock kernel contains precisely the unique translation n->m
when m/n is an integer power of 2. It is not just the units.
Choosing a reference arrow 4->n with ell=0 gives the full phase coordinate
[h+log(n/4)] modulo log 2. There is one positive packet, not one per integer.

### 5.2 Every noninteger orbit and its whole return group

For x=n+r with n in C and 0<r<1 define t(x)=n/r. On every forward branch,

    n'=2n/d, r'=r/d, t'=2t.

On every inverse t is divided by 2. Hence the dyadic class [t] in
(0,infinity)/2^Z is invariant. It is also COMPLETE. If n is even, the step
d=n/2 sends x to 4+2r/n; if n is odd composite, the two-step route above
sends x to 4+r/n. At floor 4 the branch d=2 halves the fraction. Two points
with the same dyadic t-class therefore reach floor-4 points whose fractions
differ by a power of 2; iterating toward the smaller fraction connects them.
All these steps respect 0<r<1. Necessity and sufficiency are proved.

For any representative t>0 the entire source orbit is consequently

    O_[t]={N+N/(2^k*t): k in Z, N in C, N<2^k*t}.             (2)

Every dyadic class is realized by taking N=4 and k sufficiently large.
There are continuum many such countable orbits, with no selected phase.
If x=n+r and y=m+s lie in the same orbit, every legal word has slope s/r;
therefore there is exactly ONE arrow x->y, with label

    g(t0)=(s/r)*t0 + y-(s/r)*x,       c=log r-log s.            (3)

Existence follows from (2), so the displayed coefficients are rational even
if x,y themselves are irrational. Conversely a loop forces a=1 and then b=0.
Thus all noninteger source isotropy is trivial, H_x=0, and the full phase
coordinate is h+log r in R. The clock kernel consists of connected points
with equal fractions: n+r -> m+r precisely when m/n is a power of 2.
These arrows are translations, not source or extension isotropy.
For example the word f_(8,4)^(-1) f_(4,2)^2 is the actual translation x->x+4
on all I_4, demonstrating the nontrivial kernel without inventing a relation.

## 6. Three separately owned full controls

### 6.1 L: least-divisor permission duplicates the log(2) packet

For composite n, its least proper divisor p(n) is prime: otherwise a proper
factor of p(n) would be a smaller divisor of n. Put e(n)=2n/p(n), always even
and at least 4. Every even n has p(n)=2 and e(n)=n; every odd composite maps
in one step to its even e(n), which thereafter remains the floor.
No noncomposite-floor point has an incoming or outgoing nonunit arrow.

For each even e>=4 the full integer source orbit is

    B_e={n in C: e(n)=e}.

It includes e and all its actual odd incoming integers. Writing m=e/2,
the latter are p*m where m is odd and p is an odd prime not exceeding the
least prime divisor of m; for even m there are none. This follows exactly
from p(n)=p and n/p=m, and proves that different elements of B_e have no
power-of-2 ratio. All B_e are retained, not just B_4={4}.

The own integer slope calculation (1) holds. Every ell occurs within B_e
by inserting the fixed d=2 loop at e. Thus source isotropy is Z with
H=log(2)Z, and phases are [h+log(n/e)] modulo log 2. There are countably
infinitely many positive log(2) packets, indexed by e, so uniqueness fails.

For n+r noninteger with n in C define v=r for even n, and v=r/p(n) for
odd n. The exact source orbit criterion is equality of e(n) and equality
of the dyadic class of v: first move the odd point to its even floor, then
use halving there. Every incoming odd branch is included by this criterion.
The own fraction calculation gives (3), trivial source isotropy, H=0,
and phases h+log r. Formula (1) also holds for the floors of noninteger
words, since n'=2n/d independently of r. L's full clock kernel is only units: zero clock
forces equal fractions and, by (1), a power-of-2 ratio of floors in B_e;
the preceding characterization of B_e forces equal floors and equal points.
All extension isotropy is trivial. The geometric law survives, but the
arithmetic permission change fragments the positive packet ledger.

### 6.2 A: divisibility OFF produces dense return clocks

Every nonunit A edge has both endpoints above 2. Rationality and irrationality
are separately preserved by rational affine labels, and all x<=2 are isolated.
The full rational source orbit is exactly

    O_A(4)=Q intersect (2,infinity).

Indeed, for every integer m>=3 the legal path
4 -> 4m -> m uses f_(4m,2m)^(-1) then f_(4m,8). For any rational p/q>2
with positive integers p,q, f_(p,2q) sends p to p/q and is permitted because
2q<p. Conversely no word escapes the rational points above 2.

Here is one fully legal additional return, with every intermediate point:

    4 --f_(8,4)^(-1)--> 8 --f_(8,3)--> 16/3 --f_(5,2)--> 31/6
      --f_(31,12)^(-1)--> 31 --f_(62,4)^(-1)--> 62 --f_(62,31)--> 4.

The middle noninteger 16/3 has floor 5; the inverse at 31/6 begins at
the left endpoint 62/12, and the other displayed inverse tests also hold.
Every index satisfies 2<=d<n. Multiplying the actual slopes gives 32/31,
so this loop has clock log31-5log2. The fixed f_(4,2) supplies log2. Thus

    Z log2 + Z log31  is contained in H_4, which is contained in log Q_(>0).

The ratio log31/log2 is irrational, since a rational ratio would imply
31^u=2^v for positive integers u,v. Integer approximation by pigeonhole
then gives arbitrarily small nonzero elements of the displayed subgroup;
multiples of these approximate any real number. That subgroup, and hence
the ENTIRE H_4, is dense. This does not assert H_4 equals that subgroup,
equals log Q_(>0), or equals R. Conjugation gives exactly the same H_x
throughout this one rational source orbit. There is no least positive period.
For rational x>2 the source isotropy consists exactly of the affine labels
t->exp(-v)*t+(1-exp(-v))*x for v in this actual H_4, not a larger chosen group.

At every irrational x an isotropy label would satisfy (a-1)x=-b with a,b
rational. Unless a=1,b=0 this makes x rational, so H_x=0 and source isotropy
is trivial. Irrational source orbits and every incoming object are retained
by the exact full-word recursion in Section 4; no equality with all affine
orbits is asserted. Their phases are R via the general reference formula.
The rational packet retains ALL R/H_4 phases, not a point obtained by closing H.
The clock kernel is exactly the legal rational translations; extension
isotropy is trivial. Simplified irrational orbit classification, translation
membership, and an algebraic equality for H_4 remain OPEN, as permitted for A.
Nevertheless its full positive discrete ledger is empty: every rational
nontrivial orbit has dense H, and every other point has H=0.

### 6.3 Z: carry OFF has zero isotropy everywhere

All own Z labels are positive rational dilations, so between any connected
x,y>0 the unique label is t->(y/x)t. A loop forces a=1. The point 0 is isolated.
Thus source and extension isotropy are trivial everywhere, H_x=0, and the
clock kernel consists of units. The complete phase coordinate is h+log x
for x>0 and h at 0. There are no positive packets, not zero-length primitives.

Integers and nonintegers stay separate. All integers n>=2 form one source
orbit: 2n has legal Z arrows to n and to 2. Integers 0,1 are isolated.
Every point below 2 has no incoming or outgoing nonunit arrow. For a
noninteger x=n+r with n>=2, t=n/r>n is preserved, since both n and r divide
by d. For each fixed t>2 use the finite graph with vertices

    {n integer: 2<=n<t}, and edges n->m whenever m divides n and 2<=m<n.

Its connected components correspond EXACTLY to the complete source orbits
of points n+n/t. Each graph edge is the actual Z chart d=n/m; conversely
every actual chart gives such an edge. This includes prime-floor terminal
points, incoming branches, strict fractional endpoints, and singleton
components. No finite numerical enumeration or omitted tail is involved.
