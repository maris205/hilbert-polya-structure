# GPF-Ducci dynamics has an infinite prime-diagonal fixed family

**Paper ID:** 085-gpf-ducci-diagonal-rigidity

## Abstract

The prime GPF-Ducci rule maps a cyclic tuple to the greatest prime factors of
its adjacent sums.  It is a natural multidimensional deformation of the
prime-factor recurrences already examined in this portfolio.  To avoid treating
the paper's prime-tuple domain as an externally preselected carrier, this
screen freezes its total extension to positive integer tuples.  The extension
has an elementary adverse invariant: for every prime (p), the constant tuple
((p,\ldots,p)) is fixed.  Thus the construction has infinitely many
prime-labelled period-one orbits but no internally selected nontrivial return
packet.  It is stopped before any roof, zeta, or geometric lift is proposed.

## 1. Frozen map and lineage position

For fixed (d\ge2), let

\[
D_d(x_1,\ldots,x_d)=
\bigl(\operatorname{gpf}(x_1+x_2),\ldots,
\operatorname{gpf}(x_d+x_1)\bigr).
\]

This extends the published GPF-Ducci formula from prime tuples to all positive
integer tuples.  It preserves the legitimate direct lineage arrow from a
prime/composite observable to an autonomous local vector recurrence.  It does
not supply any named Logistic, Hénon, conservative, or symplectic realization.

## 2. Diagonal rigidity

For every coordinate, (D_d(x)_i) is prime.  Hence after one iterate the
trajectory lies in the prime-tuple subspace, without defining that subspace as
the initial carrier.  If (p) is prime, then

\[
D_d(p,\ldots,p)_i=\operatorname{gpf}(p+p)
=\operatorname{gpf}(2p)=p.
\]

Consequently every prime (p) gives a distinct fixed point.  This is a full
infinite family, not an artifact of a finite enumeration.  It blocks the hoped
for inference that a higher-dimensional local rule has selected a distinctive
prime-return packet: the map retains every constant prime label equally.

The cited Ducci source proves eventual entrance into a limit cycle and reports
additional nontrivial-cycle restrictions.  None of that supplies an internally
privileged prime packet here; nor is its prime-tuple carrier permitted to be
treated as a generated prime dynamics.
