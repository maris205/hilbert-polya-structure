# 083 — Prime-Fibonacci termination screen

**Record:** ASFS-SCOUT-20260914-56  
**Status:** A0 SYMBOLIC CONTROL; A1 SCOPED FAIL — THE PARTIAL ACTION TERMINATES AT POWERS OF TWO; PRE-P0 STOP

The prime-Fibonacci recurrence uses the smallest odd prime divisor of the sum
of the preceding two positive terms.  It is a direct prime/composite observable
inside one second-order recurrence, hence a useful direct-lineage control
along the prime-symbolic to autonomous-deformation arrow.  However, its
published termination theorem says every forward positive sequence reaches a
power of two, where the rule is undefined.  Therefore the faithful partial
action has no forward closed orbit or repetition ledger.  It is also a discrete
partial noninvertible rule, with no Hénon or symplectic owner.
