# Paper plan — P188

## Reader takeaway

A nonlinear set-valued iteration is completely controlled by a one-dimensional
rank recursion, but its inverse fibres retain labelled target information.

## Claim order

1. Literal map, rank function, and contribution boundary.
2. Pointwise all-time iterate and deterministic endpoint.
3. Fixed/recurrent states, terminal fibres, and unique sharp deepest state.
4. Every-time every-target rank-chain fibres; specialize to the one-step image.
5. Fibonacci image and empty-fibre corollaries.

## Required boundaries

`n=0,1`; empty and full targets; initial segments; empty binomial sums; the
unique deepest state; mass conservation. No search non-hit becomes novelty.
