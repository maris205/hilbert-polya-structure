## Structure and proof placement

Six modular section files, including the abstract; five numbered sections.

1. `0_abstract.tex`: specific recomputed-map problem, anchor mechanism,
   target-gap inverse, exact $\lceil n/2\rceil$ bound. Self-contained,
   about 170 words; no citation or new numerical-run claim.
2. `1_introduction.tex`: carrier, right-factor-first convention, complete
   support coordinates, explicit published-credit paragraph (m01),
   separately versioned static neighbors (m02), and a compact three-row
   background/residual comparison. Preview the two claims and roadmap.
3. `2_image.tex`: projection-product block calculation, exact image
   condition, common-anchor identity, constructive image converse.
   Full proof; no claim that this elementary static calculation is an
   independent contribution.
4. `3_clock.tex`: anchored strict lists, exact two-ended endpoint update,
   pointwise entrance time, fixed/recurrent classification and terminal
   projection, sharp full-carrier height and both parity witnesses.
   Full proofs, including fixed sources, $n=1$, and even $n=2$.
5. `4_inverse.tex`: labelled-target gaps, forced/forbidden/free partition,
   necessity and sufficiency of ordered colours, published zero/one
   support-rank reconstruction credited again, unique full-source decoder,
   finite Laurent/binomial count and zero fibres. Full proof and an
   explanatory formula, not an unproved extremal add-on.
6. `5_scope.tex`: exact verifier design and current execution-pending
   qualification, finite checks versus all-size deduction, old pilot
   provenance limitation, and the excluded stronger claims.

All theorem proofs remain in the main text. No appendix is needed.
All six files are included by `main.tex`; no unused section stubs.
