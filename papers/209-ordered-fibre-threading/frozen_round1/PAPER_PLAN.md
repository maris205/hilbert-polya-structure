# P209 paper plan

2026-09-07 UTC. Author-level working outline; not manuscript review or acceptance.

**Title:** Recurrent functions and inverse fibres of ordered fibre threading.

**One-sentence contribution:** For the simultaneous ordered threading of the
old fibres of a labelled endofunction, we characterize exactly the recurrent
functions and their labelled periods, and independently decode every one-step
inverse fibre and identify its unique global maximizer.

**Format:** Anonymous, self-contained short theorem note, approximately five
to six pages including references as needed; no conference submission target.
The generic venue, one-page related-work minimum, decorative hero figure and
external-model defaults of the writing skills do not control this scope.

## Section plan

0. Abstract (~150 words): define the operation in plain terms, announce both
   proof axes and the unique maximum, distinguish vertex cycles from periods
   of whole functions. No citation, entrance-time claim or novelty adjective.
1. Introduction and setup (~one page): give the exact old-snapshot update,
   label and empty-set conventions, nontrivial three-label two-cycle, and
   restrained source positioning. Separate graph-linearization primitives
   from this full endofunction carrier. Cite only the four primary sources
   whose relevant bodies and metadata are verified in SOURCE_AUDIT.md.
2. Recurrent geometry (~two pages): state the exact theorem first; define
   all vertex-image sets and finite/infinite backward height. Prove walk
   expansion, periodic equality before height comparison, unbranched paths,
   necessary label bound, sufficiency and labelled LCM. Retain loops and
   numerically unchanged selected arrows in the proof.
3. All inverse fibres (~one to one-and-a-half pages): state the increasing
   path-cover test, prove both directions and exact recovery. Prove the
   unique maximizer, with empty and singleton cases explicit.
4. Finite checks and limits (~half a page): a small exact summary table is
   optional only after actual canonical output. Explain that exhaustive
   n=0,...,5 checks pressure, not prove, the all-size statements. Exclude a
   sharp entrance clock, general-time inverse, recurrent EGF, arbitrary-size
   period census and global priority. Keep external release held in package
   lifecycle documents without leaking author identities into the PDF.

Every proof is in the main paper. The separate PROOF_PACKAGE.md expands
dependency and edge-case explanations for audit, not a missing appendix.
