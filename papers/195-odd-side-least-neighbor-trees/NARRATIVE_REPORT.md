# Narrative report — P195

## Question

How much dynamics can a static labelled tree support when the only state is a
moving root and eligibility is determined by the parity of the far side of
an incident cut?

## Answer

Odd and even tree orders produce different recurrent geometries but the same
sharp tail scale. At odd order each edge has one eligible direction and the
walk ends at a fixed sink. At even order eligibility is symmetric; the
odd-cut subforest has odd degree at every vertex, and every orbit enters a
mutual-minimum 2-cycle. A nested-side argument and an off-path-witness
argument give the two tail bounds. Labelled species then count recurrent
states, while a direct reverse-neighbour criterion solves every fibre.

## Critical repair

An early draft incorrectly assumed one mutual-minimum edge per connected
component of the odd-cut forest. A six-vertex double star disproves this.
The final proof and EGF count oriented recurrent root states and do not use
that false uniqueness. Only functional-graph components have one eventual
cycle.
