\section{Scope and finite verification}
The results concern the full ternary carrier at every $n\ge1$. No
all-time fibre formula, transient-layer census or larger-alphabet inverse
or maximum is asserted. The source-run decoder and exact feedback images
are the two descriptions; matrix spectra and integer products are their
elementary supporting calculations.

The standalone \texttt{verify.py} exhausts $X_n$ for $1\le n\le10$,
checking images, literal arrows, recurrent cores, every target fibre and
all equality targets. Complete source sets are compared through $n=7$;
an independent part-choice dynamic program checks \eqref{eq:J} through
$n=40$. The actual \texttt{CANONICAL.json} records $655{,}256$ assertions.
These are finite falsification checks; the proofs establish the all-length
statements without extrapolation.
