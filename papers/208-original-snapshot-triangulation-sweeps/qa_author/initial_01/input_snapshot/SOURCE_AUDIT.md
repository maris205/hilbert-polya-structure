## Complete old-mechanism deductions

1. The exact edge-labelled lift exists. Give original diagonals their
   lexicographic labels using R, flip persistent labels 1,...,n-3 via tau_i,
   and erase them with E. Then F = E tau_(n-3)...tau_1 R. Other labels do not
   alter edge i before its turn. Each labelled flip is an involution. Thus
   noninjectivity of F does not exclude its elementary larger-carrier lift;
   resetting, deterministic scheduling and composition receive no separate
   contribution credit.
2. Pallo supplies the selected-rotation/fan and permanently fixed coordinate
   primitives. Generic prefix freezing is fully deducted. The surviving
   temporal content is the proved geometric-to-K identities, stronger closed
   inclusion, commuting odd phase and both all-size sharp witness parities.
3. The binary cuts/products evaluating h are old. The retained inverse is
   the disjoint operational identification of every labelled OFS source,
   including seed/later restrictions and every zero/comb/left-length boundary,
   together with the strict exponent budget establishing its unique fan
   maximizer. A mere power-of-two count would not be another axis.
4. The static image series is old and is omitted from the manuscript's
   headline results. The proof package derives
   I = 1 + z/(1-z)^2 C(z^2/(1-z)^3), where C=1+wC^2.
   It satisfies I = 1 + z^2 I + z(1-z) I^2. Mansour's displayed UUDU series
   A = C(z/((1-z)(1+z)^2))/(1-z^2) satisfies the same equation:
   (1-z^2)A = 1 + z(1-z)A^2. Both have constant coefficient one, so formal
   recursion proves equality. This deducts the full series, not only its
   first coefficients. No literal standard-Dyck-word image identification
   is claimed; that historical guess was refuted and remains preserved.
5. The quadrilateral is already a two-element Tamari chain. Descending Pop
   sends both states to its minimum, while F exchanges them, so they are
   not directly conjugate there. The broader finite same-carrier rank
   obstruction in the admitted gate is not recast as an all-parameter
   exclusion or a ban on every composite/enlarged-carrier encoding.
