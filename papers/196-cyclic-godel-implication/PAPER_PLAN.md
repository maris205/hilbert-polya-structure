# Paper plan — P196

1. Freeze the synchronous finite-chain rule and its ownership subtraction.
2. Prove the exact image language and the shift restriction.
3. Count the core and all periods with the adjacency matrix.
4. Derive the characteristic polynomial by row operations.
5. Cut a target at nontop sites and prove the gap-product fibre formula.
6. State exact controls, exclusions, and `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

The proof engines are deliberately separated: local inequalities prove the
image; closed walks prove the spectrum; weak-chain enumeration proves the
inverse product.
