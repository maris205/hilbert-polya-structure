# P197 narrative report

The cyclic comparison map has nontrivial recurrent waves despite a very
small ordered alphabet. Its fourth iterate becomes a two-step shift only
after an exactly bounded transient. This sharply separates it from a
generic one-step projection/rotation rule and from P164's binary equality
tail, while conceding their common equality front exactly.

The forward proof is local: long equal runs shorten, strict runs are at
most two, and two finite window identities enter the core. The explicit
zero-run witnesses determine first entrance and the parity split. The
inverse proof is different: a target prescribes comparisons in a closed
three-level walk, and doubled sign runs split its trace into Fibonacci
scalars. A global product bound identifies every extremal target.

This is a finite-system theorem note, not an application paper or a claimed
new class of cellular automata. Exact trace enumerators are allowed to
grow exponentially with time. External ownership remains unresolved.
