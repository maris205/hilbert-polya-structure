# P204 research narrative

The nearest-smaller operation is old; its recomputed distance array is the
state of this autonomous finite system. Starting at an inversion sequence
$x_0=0$, $0\le x_i\le i$, the output keeps exactly the zero positions. Each
zero cuts off influence from the preceding blocks. In a positive block, a
first-image distance is either one or greater than its preceding distance.
This simple order fact makes the second update binary at local coordinate
$j$: its value is $j$ when the original adjacent pair rises, and one when
it does not. The next step exchanges these two choices.

Consequently every orbit enters an explicit recurrent involution in at most
two updates, sharply once $n\ge4$. Its recurrent and fixed counts are
$F_{2n-1}$ and $F_{n+1}$, with standard Fibonacci conventions. These counts
follow from the core and are not separate new counting techniques.

To count sources of one eventual phase, fix its zero set and read its
required internal ascent sets. A positive block after global position $r$
has source bounds $1\le x_{r+j}\le r+j$. Inclusion–exclusion reduces its
exact ascent set to weakly decreasing cut segments; a segment $[a,b]$ has
$\binom{r+b}{b-a+1}$ choices. The product of the resulting alternating sums
over blocks is every target fibre at every $t\ge2$. This is an evaluated
formula, not a request to iterate or enumerate the original factorial box.

The independent candidate gate reconstructed complete graphs and checked
the count with a last-value DP. Root replayed that verifier successfully
against its actual canonical bytes. Static nearest-smaller algorithms,
Cartesian-tree codes, ascent/descent and zero/record statistics,
inclusion–exclusion and Fibonacci arithmetic are owned background. The
bounded gate found no concrete adapter removing the full literal
endpoint/fibre conjunction, but it did not certify global novelty.

The author checker has 485,578 finite assertions through $E_8$ plus bounded
flagged blocks. The candidate checker has 681,499 assertions and also tests
classical code/tie adapters. Paper-local author replay, two formal manuscript
reviews, frozen revisions and terminal builds/views are separate obligations.
The paper excludes one-step fibre counts, first-image enumeration, largest
fibre classification and arbitrary-word extensions. All external release is
held; this narrative is not a completed-paper handoff.
