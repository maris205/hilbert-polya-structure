\begin{abstract}
Recent advances in AI-assisted mathematical discovery, program search, and formally verified reasoning motivate a practical question: how can a long-horizon open problem be reorganized into a search process that is broad, falsifiable, and accountable to mathematical evidence? This paper presents a source-bound Phase-I case study around the search for arithmetic dynamical structures relevant to the Hilbert--Pólya programme. It does not claim a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, a completed Hilbert--Pólya realization, or a spectral identification of Riemann zeros. Instead, it treats the task as candidate engineering. A candidate must retain one fixed object identity while connecting an endogenous arithmetic source, primitive closed orbits and their repetitions, a dynamical zeta or determinant, and, only later, an operator-theoretic realization. The paper formalizes this discipline through an obligation roadmap, a six-direction evidence portfolio, a same-object non-transfer rule, and a human--AI workflow with explicit stop and fork conditions. Phase I preserved useful local structures---including local trace/operator constructions, a same-object Fredholm chain, periodic-orbit classifications, finite symbolic results, and continuous-time ownership controls---but no documented top-level direction supplied an integrated A0+A1+A2 chain in one natural candidate. The resulting methodological recommendation is not to combine those partial successes retrospectively. It is to widen the next candidate-architecture search while applying early, source-bound tests for arithmetic origin, orbit/repetition ownership, and a same-object determinant. Detailed research summaries and provenance boundaries are retained in the appendices and the versioned repository.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Guided search-space constraint.} The proposed Round-2 candidate space is lineage-constrained rather than a random collection of dynamical systems: each proposed object must document its prime-symbolic ancestor, its deformation/lift/geometric-realization relation, and the arithmetic mechanism that is preserved. A generic map or operator to which primes are attached only afterward is not admitted as a main candidate.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} arithmetic dynamics; human--AI collaboration; Hilbert--Pólya programme; periodic orbits; \mbox{dynamical determinants}; research methodology; reproducibility
\end{abstract}
