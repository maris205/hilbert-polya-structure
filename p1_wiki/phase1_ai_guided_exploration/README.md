# AI-Guided Exploration of Arithmetic Dynamical Systems

## Constraints, Failure Modes, and Search Strategies Toward Hilbert--Pólya Structures

**Internal, source-bound working paper · Phase-I evidence-corpus snapshot · draft v0.1**

This package turns the P1 knowledge base into a research-method paper. Its subject is not a claimed proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (RH). It is the design and stress test of a human-directed, AI-assisted workflow for exploring arithmetic dynamical systems without treating attractive local phenomena, completed pipelines, or cross-object analogies as a Hilbert--Pólya realization.
