# Paper Outline and Evidence Map

**Structure pattern:** theoretical/methodological case study

**Review-target status:** `criteria_binding_unavailable` (no journal, track, or article type was supplied; the draft makes no venue-fit claim).

## Overview

The paper begins by defining the methodological problem: a Hilbert--Pólya programme can be derailed when local structure, workflow completion, or similarities between systems are mistaken for a continuous proof chain. It then treats the P1 corpus as a case study of a human-directed, AI-assisted evidence architecture. The roadmap image supplies the common obligation map; six source-bound lines supply the empirical record of what was obtained, rejected, or left unassigned. The conclusion converts those lessons into a reusable candidate-admission protocol without authorizing a new research phase.

### 4. Phase-I case record: free exploration and five directed Sessions (~1,800 words)

**Purpose:** Summarize the preserved results and boundaries of each line.

**Content:**

1. `zeta_mvp0`: Q/W/\(S_{\rm op}\) and \(P^*_{\rm loc}\) versus the still-open \(P_0\) bridge.
2. Logistic: a same-object analytic determinant chain whose target-Riemann tuple remains negative.
3. Hénon: low/high-dimensional classification and periodic structure without an A2--A4 promotion.
4. Symplectic: constrained structural interfaces and `NOT_APPLICABLE` Batch07 status.
5. Symbolic: exact-five finite-system internal completion without an A0--A4 advance.
6. Flow: object ownership, physical-roof non-transfer, nonfactorization, no-go records, and positive controls, with positive arithmetic A2 `0/5` for P24--P28 and Route-B invocation `0/5`.

**Sources:** `Z0-C`, `Z0-A`, `S1-C`, `S1-R`, `S2-E`, `S2-HOLD`, `S3-D`, `S3-E`, `S4-P`, `S4-ST`, `S5-P`, `S5-END`.

**Transition:** The case record repeats several failure modes, which are more useful when stated as a cross-stream methodology.

### 5. Constraints and failure modes as research outputs (~900 words)

**Purpose:** Extract the recurring constraints that a good AI--human workflow must surface.

**Content:** Analyze object-transfer, evidence-transfer, and process-transfer errors; distinguish proxy/calibrator claims from owner claims; show why a determinant, self-adjointness, or a finished PDF is not portable credit. Treat negative results as scoped eliminators and diagnostic assets, not evidence that all future approaches fail.

**Sources:** `P1-X`, `S1-R`, `S3-E`, `S5-P`, `S5-END`.

**Transition:** These failure modes motivate an operational admission protocol for later exploration.

### 6. Search strategies for a next candidate, not an automatic next experiment (~850 words)

**Purpose:** State a reusable screening and evidence-preservation protocol.

**Content:** Require a candidate card with object, arithmetic origin, clock/roof, primitive/repetition law, determinant/function-space convention, controls, operator owner, and stop condition. Specify human and AI responsibilities. Require Route B only after a strong, same-object Route-A chain exists.

**Sources:** `Z0-R`, `S1-R`, `S3-E`, `S4-ST`, `S5-R`.

**Transition:** The protocol improves research governance but does not remove the paper’s source and validation limits.

### 7. Limitations, governance, and conclusion (~450 words)

**Purpose:** State the exact limits of the study and close with transferable lessons.

**Content:** Explain that the paper is not an independent proof audit, external literature review, AI benchmark, peer review, or publication claim.
