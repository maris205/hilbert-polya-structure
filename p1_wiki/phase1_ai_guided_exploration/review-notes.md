# Bounded Internal Review Notes

## Review purpose and limit

Two read-only internal reviews were performed for this working-paper draft:

1. An editorial review assessed title/abstract alignment, structure, readability, and whether the human-directed AI framework was intelligible.
2. A source-bound claim-integrity review compared potentially strong wording with the linked project records and the P1 non-transfer/status boundaries.

Neither review was external peer review, an independent proof audit, a replication of component mathematics, a Route-A/Route-B evaluation, or evidence that any cited result is scientifically correct beyond its source's own stated status.

## Changes incorporated from the editorial review

- The evidence snapshot is explicitly distinguished from the newly added manuscript package.
- The abstract and method state that AI-guided refers to this synthesis and the proposed future workflow; it does not retroactively attribute one uniform AI procedure to all historical research lines.
- A synthesis-procedure and audit-trail subsection identifies the six selected lines, the roles of AI assistance and human checking, and the inspectable supporting artefacts.
- Figure 1 has concise alternative text and a visible caption that identifies it as an obligation map rather than a progress chart.
- Internal labels that could confuse a non-internal reader, including the relationship between Paper-02 A4.15 and P1 Route-A A4, are explicitly separated.
- A six-line overview table was added before the detailed case record.

## Changes incorporated from the source-bound claim review

- Hénon's detailed result description is now controlled by its evaluation-scope record, while Route status remains controlled by the adjudication record.
- Symplectic wording was narrowed to source-recorded selector/weighted-degree and non-transfer boundaries; NOT_APPLICABLE is not treated as a generic arithmetic failure.
- Symbolic statements are limited to what the final QA/state records support; individual theorem detail is not inferred from batch completion.
- Flow statements distinguish the proposal's research questions from executed P24--P28 records, and leave magnetic/arithmetic transfer unclaimed where the source does.
- Same-object, source-status, and Route-B wording was narrowed so that a candidate-card design or a local operator/determinant result is not presented as Route closure.
- The zeta_mvp0 zero-correspondence status uses the claim ledger's unauthorized/not-evaluated boundary and distinguishes an internal Paper-02 label from the P1 Route-A gate.

A final read-only post-revision spot check found no remaining blocking source-scope issue and confirmed that the 21 unique project-record keys used by the manuscript all appear in its local record table. This confirmation remains an internal wording/source-scope check, not a proof validation or peer-review result.

## Remaining limitations

- The paper is a source-bound synthesis over an explicitly named historical evidence corpus.
- It makes no RH, completed Hilbert--Pólya, prime-trace, completed-\(\Xi\), zero-identification, or other major-open-problem claim.
- Author identities, affiliations, funding, competing interests, and any external circulation decision remain the responsibility of human authors.
- An external release would require a new release-specific source snapshot, a fresh link/citation audit, and whatever mathematical/editorial review the responsible human authors judge appropriate.
