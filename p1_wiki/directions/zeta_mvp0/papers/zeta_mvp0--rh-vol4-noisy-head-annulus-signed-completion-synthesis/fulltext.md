# Series position and rules of synthesis

The four-volume partition is functional rather than chronological rhetoric: Volume I covers RH-1--RH-160, Volume II covers RH-161--RH-241, Volume III covers RH-242--RH-281, and this volume covers RH-282--RH-361. The original numbered papers remain the atomic sources. A review anchor records status and dependency information, but it does not replace the proof in the source paper it reviews.

We use five noninterchangeable labels.

[proved]{.smallcaps}

:   an analytic theorem in the exact scope of its source;

[certified]{.smallcaps}

:   a finite exact, stored, or outward computation;

[conditional]{.smallcaps}

:   a valid implication with unproved physical inputs;

[scoped negative]{.smallcaps}

:   a theorem rejecting one named information class, model, or method, not every physical realization;

[open/not testable]{.smallcaps}

:   a required source, map, clock, uniformity, or observation is absent.

Three firewalls govern every cross-batch statement. First, actual noisy, synthetic spectral, deterministic combinatorial, and abstract coefficient objects retain their types. Second, fixed order, a selected slow diagonal, and the physical logarithmic first-alias clock are not interchangeable. Third, normalized or selected-window control is not an unnormalized complete prefix theorem.

# The common coefficient dictionary

On the source-locked Hardy clock $$k=\frac{\log(1/\sigma)}{2\log\lambda}+O(1),\qquad 2\le n<4k,$$ let $p_{\sigma,n}$ denote the actual direct coefficient, $q_{\sigma,n}$ the actual full-trace coefficient, $h_{\sigma,n}$ the actual modulus-complete Hardy head, and $s_{k,n}$ the deterministic graded counterloop. Write $d_{\sigma,k,n}=h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}$. In the typed source formulas, $$\label{eq:dictionary}
 p=\tau-a=q-d,\qquad d=h-s,qquad q=p+d,qquad h=s+d.$$

Equation [\[eq:dictionary\]](#eq:dictionary){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:dictionary"} is algebra, not a spectral identification. In particular, it does not say that the roots or moments defining $s$ occur inside the noisy operator, that the rank of the deterministic model is a physical rank, or that the off-cloud coefficient $q$ is controlled by $p$ without a bound for $d$.

The natural unnormalized defect budget is $$\label{eq:defect}
 D_{4k}(R)=\sum_{2\le n<4k}
 |h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}|\frac{R^n}{n}.$$ It is distinct from the normalized upper-band error, from coordinatewise relative matching, and from any maximum over a selected window.

# Typed nonpromotion theorem

The synthesis can now state the cross-batch inference exactly.

[\[thm:nonpromotion\]]{#thm:nonpromotion label="thm:nonpromotion"} Fix the common clock and orders $2\le n<4k$. The proved Volume IV inputs consisting of

1.  selected or normalized actual statements for $p$ and related signed quantities, and

2.  unconditional deterministic statements for $s$

do not, by the identities [\[eq:dictionary\]](#eq:dictionary){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:dictionary"} alone, imply an unnormalized weighted bound for the complete actual $q$ or $h$. Such an implication requires an independent same-clock estimate for $d=h-s$ on the complete order range.

Both target identities contain the unpaid term: $q=p+d$ and $h=s+d$. Neither a selected/normalized estimate for $p$ nor a deterministic estimate for $s$ controls the weighted norm of $d$. Moreover, normalization cannot be removed without its scale, and a moving upper window does not include the omitted low orders. Hence the claimed full budgets do not follow from the stated premises. This is a theorem about implication and types; it makes no assertion about which defects are physically realizable.

For completeness, on a fixed finite set $I$ and positive weights, hold signed arrays $p,s$ fixed and let $e$ range over all signed arrays. Define $$d[e]=e,\qquad q[e]=p+e,\qquad h[e]=s+e.$$ Along $e=tv$ with $v\ne0$, the weighted norms of $q[e]$ and $h[e]$ are unbounded as $|t|\to\infty$. Choosing $e=-p$ or $e=-s$ makes the respective norm zero. This verifies information-class nonidentifiability, but the two zero choices need not coincide and arbitrary $e$ is not declared physical.

# Frontier theorem and reopening tests

[\[thm:frontier\]]{#thm:frontier label="thm:frontier"} Relative to the canonical RH-282--RH-361 sources, no proved result supplies [\[eq:defect\]](#eq:defect){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:defect"}, a genuine physical obstruction to it, a complete actual $q/E_{\mathrm{off}}$ theorem on the same clock, or the RH-241 moving noisy all-order envelope with its coefficient bridge. Therefore the source-relative verdict is `NOT_TESTABLE`, and RH-362 is not activated by this synthesis.

Each ten-layer review records its first missing typed leaf. RH-291 and RH-301 stop at a rate-free or inactive prefix criterion; RH-311 lacks cross-branch glue; RH-321 is sharp only for a synthetic class; RH-331 and RH-341 lack all-leg actual trace replacement; RH-351 lacks the actual growing signed remainder; and RH-361 explicitly records [\[eq:defect\]](#eq:defect){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:defect"} as an assumption of every actual-head inheritance statement. The executable audit binds these reviews and their source ledgers. Absence of a source is not a physical impossibility theorem, so the conclusion is `NOT_TESTABLE` rather than a global negative.

One new direction survives the scoped raw-forward obstruction. For full-line affine Gaussian rows whose composition has slope $A$, shift $B$, and variance $V$, the cyclic diagonal integral is $$\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi V}}
 \exp\!\left(-\frac{((1-A)x-B)^2}{2V}\right)dx
 =\frac1{|1-A|},\qquad A\ne1.$$ Raw forward variance growth therefore does not by itself obstruct a cyclic diagonal functional. However the full-line kernel is not Hilbert--Schmidt on $L^2(\mathbb R)$, and compact truncation and inverse row normalizers act in opposite directions. A physical reopening must first define the complete signed/folded compact reference in the RH-334 observation type and test $$C_{\sigma,k}=1+o\bigl((\beta R)^{-2k}\bigr).$$ Only after this correction-factor test may all-leg curvature Duhamel and observation-weight estimates be attempted.
