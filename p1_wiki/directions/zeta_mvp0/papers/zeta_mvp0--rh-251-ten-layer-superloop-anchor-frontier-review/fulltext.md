# Exact progress that survives

#### Periodic superloops.

RH-242 proves for every fixed noisy operator and selected algebraic multiset that $$\tau_n=\operatorname{Tr}A_\sigma^n-\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_\sigma}s^n
 =\operatorname{Str}((A_\sigma\oplus S_\sigma)^n),\qquad n\ge2,$$ and realizes the physical term as a cyclic Gaussian loop integral [@WangRH242]. The identity is exact; the continuum selector is still an assumption.

#### Independent coefficient anchor.

RH-243 separates the all-order size problem from the numerator identity. If $P_n$ is the deterministic flat trace, then $$a_n=r_H^{-n}\left[P_n-1-(-1)^n
 +2\mathbf{1}_{\{2\mid n\}}\lambda^{-n/2}\right],$$ and the symmetric two-step coefficient is $b_k=a_{2k}$ [@WangRH243]. This defines the target but does not put it in the cloud.

#### Cancellation-preserving quotient.

RH-245 replaces the ill-conditioned Riesz estimate by an orthogonal quotient. For the invariant generalized root space $E$, $Q=I-\Pi_E$, and $C=QAQ|_{E^\perp}$, $$\tau_n=\operatorname{Tr}C^n.$$ The projection has norm one; $E^\perp$ need not reduce $A$ [@WangRH245]. RH-246 then proves that one uniformly contractive power $C^m$ gives the geometric trace envelope and an explicit logarithmic tail [@WangRH246]. These theorems survive even though their uniform hypotheses are not yet certified.

#### Determinant interface.

RH-250 proves that, on $|z|\le R$, the logarithmic determinant error is bounded by the anchored finite-head error plus the quotient tail plus the target tail [@WangRH250]. This is the exact interface to a future RH-240 application; the present three certificates are incomplete.

# Obstructions and their scope

The zero-target and anchored problems cannot be conflated. The anchor norm through order 12 is $0.4945054357$, whereas all archived tolerances are at most $0.04$; the two equal-radius balls are disjoint. Exhausting the frozen prefix class gives 0/32 passes [@WangRH244].

RH-248 removes the radial-order excuse. It places every complete shell in a box $0\le w_j\le1$ and dualizes the anchored weighted $\ell^1$ distance. The box itself has distance $0.146498$--$0.424018$, at least 10.17 times the local tolerance, so every prefix, interval, and binary subset fails [@WangRH248]. This is a frozen-window statement.

Allowing $w_j\ge0$ without an upper bound does not produce a credible rescue. Six endpoints remain outside the cone; the other 26 need minimum maximum weights between 40.584 and $5.8018\times10^{10}$ [@WangRH249]. These are arbitrary real repetitions, not algebraic spectral multiplicities.

The long-loop estimate also has a strict firewall. Any majorant that takes absolute values of physical and atomic sectors before cancellation contains $|p_\sigma|^n=r_H^{-n}$, so its root rate is superunit. All 352 archived order cases confirm rates 1.2532--3.5568 [@WangRH247]. A viable proof must work after signed/complex grouping.

# Next-batch reopening triggers

The same frozen shell window should not be reweighted again. A new batch is justified by at least one genuinely new input:

1.  a larger resolved spectral window, followed immediately by an anchored zonotope/cone audit rather than a zero-target fit;

2.  a signed or complex selector derived from invariant quotient structure, not arbitrary real shell weights;

3.  a noise-uniform theorem for one quotient block power, including the finest discretizations and a continuum bridge; or

4.  an analytic all-order tail estimate for the deterministic numerator target.

Until then, the honest endpoint is a scoped route stop with exact fixed-noise progress preserved.
