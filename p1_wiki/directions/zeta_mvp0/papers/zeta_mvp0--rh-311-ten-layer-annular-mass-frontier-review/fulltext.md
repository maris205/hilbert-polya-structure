# Direct annular route

RH-302 proves that for every fixed $1.4<\rho<\rho_*$, $$\|g_\sigma-g_\sigma^{<m_\sigma}\|_{H^\infty(\rho)}
 +\|g_\sigma-g_\sigma^{<m_\sigma}\|_{H^2(\rho)}
 \longrightarrow0,$$ where $m_\sigma=\lceil4\log(1/\sigma)\rceil$. Thus the full norm converges if and only if the moving polynomial head does. RH-303 shows that such an aggregate theorem would bypass root pairing but would still force every fixed noisy-head moment to follow the counterloop, using the archived fixed-order total-trace and shell limits.

RH-304 and RH-305 quantify the mass cost of the exact odd anchors. Relative matching at the minimal bridge clock requires the mass exponent $$0.9468615163684616\ldots,$$ and every actual modulus complement satisfies, on a strict annulus, $$\|g_\sigma\|_{H^\infty/H^2(\rho)}
 \ge C_\rho
 \frac{M_\sigma^{-\kappa(\rho)}}{\log(e+M_\sigma)}
 \ge C'_\rho\frac{\sigma^{\kappa(\rho)}}
 {\log(e+1/\sigma)},\qquad
 \kappa(\rho)=
 \frac{\log(1/(q_*\rho))}{\log(q_*/q)}.$$ At $\rho=1.41$, $\kappa=0.0350457053\ldots$. RH-306 proves that the power and logarithmic factor are sharp for the coefficient-envelope information class, while explicitly declining a spectral power-sum realization.

# Shrinking gap and endpoint

RH-307 studies $\rho_\sigma=Re^{\eta_\sigma}$ at the minimal clock. If $\eta_\sigma=c\log L_\sigma/L_\sigma$, the mass-and-cap tail threshold is $$c_*=\log(10/7)=0.3566749439\ldots.$$ RH-308 then replaces the Cauchy conversion loss $\Theta(\eta^{-1})$ by the optimal Hardy loss $\Theta(\eta^{-1/2})$.

At $\rho_*=1.426787483864073\ldots$, RH-309 proves the exact membership split $$g_\sigma\in H^2(\rho_*),\qquad
 g_\sigma\notin H^\infty(\rho_*).$$ Endpoint $H^2$ convergence would close the full $R=1.4$ coefficient budget with constant $4.9921110686\ldots$, but it must obey $$\|g_\sigma\|_{H^2(\rho_*)}
 \ge C\{\log(e+M_\sigma)\}^{-1/2}
 \ge C'\{\log(e+1/\sigma)\}^{-1/2}.$$ This tends to zero and therefore leaves endpoint convergence open.

# Typed first-alias route

 =(2k-2)\beta_k^{2k}+2\beta^{2k}+o(kR^{-2k}).$$ No archived theorem supplies this joint moving-order boundary-layer law.
