# Invariant-factor fixed counts

Let $A\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_q}(V)$ have invariant factors $f_1\mid\cdots\mid f_r$, so that $V\simeq\bigoplus_i\mathbb F_q[X]/(f_i)$ and $A$ acts as multiplication by $X$. Put $$F_n=q^{\sum_i\deg\gcd(f_i,X^n-1)}.$$

[\[thm:main\]]{#thm:main label="thm:main"} For every $n\ge1$, $|\operatorname{Fix}(A^n)|=F_n$. Write $f_i=X^{e_i}g_i$ with $g_i(0)\ne0$. Then $\operatorname{Per}(A)$ is a vector subspace of dimension $\sum_i\deg g_i$, and the largest preperiod is $\max_i e_i$. The numbers of least-period points and cycles are $$P_n=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)F_d,\qquad C_n=P_n/n,$$ and $\zeta_A(z)=\prod_{n\ge1}(1-z^n)^{-C_n}$. \>1 For $U_A\phi=\phi\circ A$ on $\mathbb C^V$, $$\chi_{U_A}(T)=T^{|V|-|\operatorname{Per}(A)|}
                 \prod_{n\ge1}(T^n-1)^{C_n}.$$

On $\mathbb F_q[X]/(f_i)$, the kernel of multiplication by $X^n-1$ has dimension $\deg\gcd(f_i,X^n-1)$. Direct sums prove the fixed formula; no square-free hypothesis on $X^n-1$ is used. Primary decomposition separates the nilpotent $X$-part, of height $e_i$, from the invertible $g_i$-part. This proves the periodic-subspace and tail assertions. Möbius inversion gives $P_n$, orbit division gives $C_n$, and exponentiating $\sum_{n\ge1}F_nz^n/n$ gives the zeta product. \>1 Each functional-graph component consists of a directed cycle and incoming trees. Ordering the cycle before tree vertices makes the composition matrix block triangular. A $d$-cycle contributes $T^d-1$ and every noncycle vertex contributes $T$, proving the last formula.

# Transient and inseparable structure

The theorem distinguishes two phenomena sometimes obscured by fixed-count tables. A factor $X^e$ contributes no nonzero periodic coordinate but fixes the exact maximal tail $e$. Conversely, repeated factors coprime to $X$ can produce nonsemisimple periodic dynamics. For example, $(X+1)^4$ over $\mathbb F_2$ is inseparable and has restriction order four, while $(X+\alpha)^2$ over $\mathbb F_4$, with $\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0$, has order six. The latter uses field arithmetic, not arithmetic in $\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$.

The theorem remains valid when the characteristic divides $n$ and $X^n-1$ is inseparable.

The cyclic-module kernel calculation uses the ideal generated by $\gcd(f_i,X^n-1)$ with its full multiplicity. It never replaces the gcd by a set of distinct roots.

# Exact audit

Eight controls range from $8$ to $625$ states. All $144$ formula cells agree with exhaustive graphs and independent nullities of $A^n-I$. SymPy checks $198$ polynomial gcd cells and six full-function characteristic polynomials. The largest transient control has $625$ states, five periodic states, tail height three, and therefore Koopman zero multiplicity $620$. A byte replay is exact.
