# The corrected phase space

The frozen release obstruction identifier is `HEN-O280`. Write $\mathbb T_r=\mathbb R/r\mathbb Z$ and orient it clockwise. A rod is represented by its left endpoint. Choose a cyclic lift $x_1,\ldots,x_N\in\mathbb R$ satisfying $$\label{eq:chamber}
 x_{i+1}-x_i\geq a\quad(1\leq i<N),
 \qquad x_1+\ell-x_N\geq a .$$ The free gaps are the left sides of [\[eq:chamber\]](#eq:chamber){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:chamber"} minus $a$; they are nonnegative and sum to $$L=\ell-Na>0.$$ We quotient global constant rotations $x_i\mapsto x_i+r$ and cyclic changes of the first rod. On a contact face, incoming and outgoing velocity labels are glued by the equal-mass elastic reflection. At an intersection of faces, the gluing is generated by the adjacent transpositions in the maximal contact block. The resulting rotation-reduced, unlabeled, collision-glued phase space is denoted by $\mathcal R_{N,\ell,a}$.

The free target is $$\label{eq:freequotient}
 \mathcal F_{N,L}=\bigl((\mathbb T_L\times\mathbb R)^N\bigr)
 \big/\bigl(S_N\times\mathbb T_L\bigr),$$ where $S_N$ permutes position--velocity pairs and $c\in\mathbb T_L$ sends every $y_i$ to $y_i+c$ but leaves every velocity unchanged. Thus common drift is retained as phase data even though its spatial motion disappears in shape space. No time-dependent Galilean quotient is taken.

# Compression and complete-flow conjugacy

For a representative satisfying [\[eq:chamber\]](#eq:chamber){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:chamber"}, define $$\label{eq:compression}
 y_i=x_i-(i-1)a,\qquad 1\leq i\leq N,$$ and reduce the $y_i$ modulo $L$ in [\[eq:freequotient\]](#eq:freequotient){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:freequotient"}.

[\[lem:cyclic\]]{#lem:cyclic label="lem:cyclic"} If the next rod is chosen as the first rod, then the compressed lifted list is $$(y_2+a,\ldots,y_N+a,y_1+L+a).$$ Consequently its class in [\[eq:freequotient\]](#eq:freequotient){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:freequotient"} is unchanged.

The new lifted endpoint list is $(x_2,\ldots,x_N,x_1+\ell)$. Subtracting $(i-1)a$ gives the displayed list, because $\ell-(N-1)a=L+a$. Modulo $L$ this is a cyclic permutation followed by one common translation by $a$. A global rod rotation similarly becomes a common translation. Hence [\[eq:compression\]](#eq:compression){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:compression"} is well defined precisely on the reduced source.

[\[thm:conjugacy\]]{#thm:conjugacy label="thm:conjugacy"} Compression induces a bijection $$C:\mathcal R_{N,\ell,a}\longrightarrow\mathcal F_{N,L}.$$ For every $t\in\mathbb R$, it conjugates the complete hard-rod flow $R_t$ to the free flow $$\label{eq:freeflow}
 F_t[(y_i,v_i)_{i=1}^N]=[(y_i+t v_i,v_i)_{i=1}^N],
 \qquad C R_t=F_t C.$$

In cyclic order, [\[eq:compression\]](#eq:compression){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:compression"} converts the free rod gaps exactly into the cyclic gaps between the $y_i$; both are nonnegative and sum to $L$. Conversely, cyclically order any representative of the free points, choose lifts $y_1\leq\cdots\leq y_N\leq y_1+L$, and put $x_i=y_i+(i-1)a$. Then [\[eq:chamber\]](#eq:chamber){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:chamber"} holds. Common translation changes only the removed rod rotation, a different first point gives the cyclic relation in Lemma [\[lem:cyclic\]](#lem:cyclic){reference-type="ref" reference="lem:cyclic"}, and a permutation changes no unlabeled state. Ties are exactly contact faces, where the collision gluing removes the remaining velocity-order ambiguity. This proves bijectivity.

Between contacts, subtracting constants in [\[eq:compression\]](#eq:compression){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:compression"} does not change velocities, so both sides follow [\[eq:freeflow\]](#eq:freeflow){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:freeflow"}. At an ordinary equal-mass collision, the rods exchange velocities; letting the two free phase points cross produces the same unlabeled pair. Adjacent transpositions generate every permutation within a simultaneous contact block. Therefore the identity persists through every collision, and the free group defines both forward and backward hard-rod evolution without an event-order convention.

Jepsen's equal-mass hard-rod dynamics and free-crossing mechanism are classical [@Jepsen1965], as is the excluded-volume setting of the Tonks gas [@Tonks1936]. Our result is a normalized source-model closure and not a claim of literature priority.

# All events, conservation, and no Zeno

Fix a free representative. For $i\ne j$ with $v_i\ne v_j$, its pair coincidences are exactly $$\label{eq:eventset}
 E_{ij}=\{t\in\mathbb R:(y_i-y_j)+t(v_i-v_j)\in L\mathbb Z\}.$$ At one event time, group all indices with the same compressed position. A group with at least two distinct velocities is a maximal collision block; different positions give simultaneous disjoint blocks.

[\[thm:events\]]{#thm:events label="thm:events"} Immediately before a collision block, its spatial velocity list is decreasing up to ties; immediately after, it is increasing. Thus the event replaces the incoming list by the same multiset in sorted order. Binary collision is velocity exchange, and a genuine multiple collision is independent of any binary resolution order in the collision-glued quotient.

Every event conserves $$P=\sum_{i=1}^Nv_i,
 \qquad
 E=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^Nv_i^2.$$ For a horizon $H\geq0$, each unequal-velocity pair has at most $$\label{eq:bound}
 \left\lfloor\frac{H|v_i-v_j|}{L}\right\rfloor+2$$ coincidences in $[0,H]$. Hence only finitely many aggregated events occur on every compact interval: the flow has no Zeno accumulation.

Near a common free position at time $t_0$, the local positions are $y_0+(t-t_0)v_i$. For $t<t_0$, increasing spatial order therefore reads the velocities from large to small; for $t>t_0$, it reads them from small to large. Sorting is a permutation, so its first and second power sums, hence $P$ and $E$, are unchanged.

Equation [\[eq:eventset\]](#eq:eventset){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:eventset"} is one affine congruence. As $t$ ranges over $[0,H]$, its left side crosses an interval of length $H|v_i-v_j|$; the number of multiples of $L$ in that interval is bounded by [\[eq:bound\]](#eq:bound){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:bound"}. Summing over the finitely many pairs proves local finiteness. If $v_i=v_j$, the two free points either never coincide or remain coincident; the latter is a persistent contact and not an isolated event.

Initial contacts are allowed because the phase space glues both one-sided velocity assignments. The construction is reversible for all real time. The strict inequality $L>0$ is indispensable; at close packing the target circle collapses.

# Complete return criterion

A return in [\[eq:freequotient\]](#eq:freequotient){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:freequotient"} may use both defining symmetries. Consequently a state returns at $T>0$ if and only if there exist $\sigma\in S_N$ and $c\in\mathbb T_L$ such that $$\label{eq:return}
 y_i+Tv_i\equiv y_{\sigma(i)}+c\pmod L,
 \qquad v_i=v_{\sigma(i)},\quad 1\leq i\leq N.$$ The common $c$ is mandatory: deleting it would restore the angle that was removed from the source.

For a velocity value $u$, let $$Y_u=\{y_i:v_i=u\}$$ be a multiset in $\mathbb T_L$ and define $$H_u=\operatorname{Stab}(Y_u)=\{h\in\mathbb T_L:Y_u+h=Y_u\}.$$ It is a finite cyclic subgroup. Write its order as $d_u$; then $$\label{eq:Hd}
 H_u=\left\{\frac{kL}{d_u}:0\leq k<d_u\right\},
 \qquad d_u\mid |Y_u|.$$

[\[thm:return\]]{#thm:return label="thm:return"} For $T>0$, the following are equivalent:

1.  the reduced hard-rod state returns at $T$;

2.  the equation [\[eq:return\]](#eq:return){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:return"} holds for some $\sigma,c$;

3.  the cosets $Tu+H_u$, over all distinct velocities $u$, have nonempty common intersection;

4.  for every pair of distinct velocity values $u,w$, $$\label{eq:lcmcriterion}
     \operatorname{lcm}(d_u,d_w)T(u-w)\in L\mathbb Z.$$

Let $$\mathcal W=\{\operatorname{lcm}(d_u,d_w)(u-w):u<w\}.$$ If there is only one velocity value, the shape state is fixed and has no least positive period. Otherwise a positive return exists exactly when the nonzero elements of $\mathcal W$ are commensurable. If $\mathcal W\subset\omega\mathbb Z$ with primitive integer coefficients, then $$\label{eq:minperiod}
 T_{\min}=\frac L\omega.$$

The equivalence of the first two statements is the conjugacy and the definition of the quotient. Since $\sigma$ must preserve velocity values, equation [\[eq:return\]](#eq:return){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:return"} restricted to one class is the multiset identity $Y_u+Tu=Y_u+c$, or $c\in Tu+H_u$. This proves the third statement.

Two such cosets intersect precisely when $T(u-w)\in H_u+H_w$. By [\[eq:Hd\]](#eq:Hd){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:Hd"}, their sum is $H_{\operatorname{lcm}(d_u,d_w)}$, giving [\[eq:lcmcriterion\]](#eq:lcmcriterion){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:lcmcriterion"}. Pairwise compatibility is sufficient: choose a common multiple of all $d_u$ and identify the finite subgroups with subgroups of one finite cyclic group; the assertion becomes the generalized Chinese remainder theorem for congruence classes. This proves the fourth equivalence.

Condition [\[eq:lcmcriterion\]](#eq:lcmcriterion){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:lcmcriterion"} is $Tz/L\in\mathbb Z$ for every $z\in\mathcal W$. Such a $T$ exists exactly when the finitely many nonzero $z$ lie in one discrete cyclic subgroup of $\mathbb R$. Taking its primitive positive generator $\omega$ gives [\[eq:minperiod\]](#eq:minperiod){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:minperiod"}; Bezout's identity for the primitive integer coefficients proves that no smaller positive $T$ works.

If all velocities are pairwise distinct, every $Y_u$ is a singleton and $d_u=1$. Hence return reduces to $$\label{eq:distinct}
 T(v_i-v_j)\in L\mathbb Z\quad\hbox{for all }i,j.$$ In particular, $(0,1,\sqrt2)$ has no positive return: [\[eq:distinct\]](#eq:distinct){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:distinct"} would make both $T/L$ and $\sqrt2T/L$ integers. With repeated velocities, the factors $d_u$ can shorten a period. The exact certificate includes two velocity classes on $L=12$, each with order-two stabilizer; velocities $\pm1$ then have reduced minimal period $3$, rather than the generic value $6$.
