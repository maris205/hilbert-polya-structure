# Classical source and reversal

On $\mathbb T^2$, let $$H(x,y)=(3x-y,x),\qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 R=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$ Then $\det A=1$ and $RAR=A^{-1}$. Writing $a_n=\operatorname{Tr}A^n$, one has $a_0=2$, $a_1=3$, and $a_n=3a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}$. Hence $$\label{eq:fix}
 \#\operatorname{Fix}(H^n)=|\det(A^n-I)|=a_n-2.$$ The primitive-cycle counts follow by Möbius inversion. This real-torus clock is kept separate from the finite quantum clock below.

# Frozen Weyl convention

Let $N=7$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/7}$, and interpret $1/2$ as $4$ in $\mathbb F_7$. On the basis $|x\rangle$, define $$Q|x\rangle=\omega^x|x\rangle,\qquad
 P|x\rangle=|x+1\rangle,$$ $$W(q,p)=\omega^{-qp/2}Q^qP^p,\qquad
 \mathcal F_{xy}=7^{-1/2}\omega^{xy},$$ $$C_{xx}=\omega^{(3/2)x^2},\qquad U=C\mathcal F^{-1}.$$ Freezing these signs is essential; changing a Fourier sign changes the classical matrix implemented by conjugation.

The half-phase convention also fixes an odd-level boundary. At the control modulus $N=8$, the congruence $2h=1\pmod 8$ has no solution, so the literal formula $\omega^{-qp/2}$ cannot be copied unchanged to even level. This does not exclude doubled-phase or other separately defined even-level Weil/metaplectic conventions.

[\[thm:egorov\]]{#thm:egorov label="thm:egorov"} The matrix $U$ is unitary and, for every $(q,p)\in\mathbb F_7^2$, $$\label{eq:egorov}
 U W(q,p)U^{-1}=W(3q-p,q).$$ If $K$ denotes coefficientwise complex conjugation and $\Theta=\mathcal F K$, then $$\Theta^2=I,\qquad \Theta U\Theta^{-1}=U^{-1}.$$

The finite Fourier orthogonality sum proves unitarity. Direct use of $PQ=\omega^{-1}QP$, including the half-phase in $W$, gives [\[eq:egorov\]](#eq:egorov){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:egorov"} first on $Q$ and $P$ and then on all Weyl operators. The same orthogonality sum gives $\mathcal F\overline{\mathcal F}=I$ and the two antiunitary identities. Iteration of [\[eq:egorov\]](#eq:egorov){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:egorov"} implements $A^n$ after exactly $n$ powers of $U$; no time rescaling occurs. This is the clock-preservation statement used here.

# Action sum and stationary recurrence

Since $$U_{xy}=7^{-1/2}\omega^{(3/2)x^2-xy},$$ closing the matrix indices gives, for every $n\ge1$, $$\label{eq:action}
 \operatorname{Tr}U^n=7^{-n/2}
 \sum_{x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1}\in\mathbb F_7}
 \omega^{\sum_j((3/2)x_j^2-x_jx_{j-1})}.$$ The stationary equations are $$3x_j-x_{j-1}-x_{j+1}=0\pmod7,$$ which are exactly the coordinate recurrence of $H$. Equation [\[eq:action\]](#eq:action){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:action"} is an exact finite action sum, not an asserted semiclassical correspondence with the real periodic points in [\[eq:fix\]](#eq:fix){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:fix"}.

# Exact aliasing obstruction

[\[thm:alias\]]{#thm:alias label="thm:alias"} For the frozen convention above, $$U^8=I,$$ and the seven eigenvalues are all eighth roots of unity except $-i$. Consequently $$\begin{aligned}
 \det(I-zU)
 &=\frac{1-z^8}{1+iz}\label{eq:det}\\
 &=1-iz-z^2+iz^3+z^4-iz^5-z^6+iz^7.\end{aligned}$$ Moreover $\operatorname{Tr}U^n=7$ if $8\mid n$ and $\operatorname{Tr}U^n=-(-i)^n$ otherwise.

Cyclotomic matrix multiplication gives $U^8=I$ and the first eight traces $i,1,-i,-1,i,1,-i,7$. Newton identities reconstruct the characteristic polynomial $(t^8-1)/(t+i)$, proving the spectral and determinant statements.

The classical count in [\[eq:fix\]](#eq:fix){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:fix"} grows like the expanding eigenvalue of $A$, whereas the fixed-level quantum trace is 8-periodic. Therefore this single finite level cannot carry the classical periodic growth for all $n$. The mismatch is an exact obstruction, not a failed numerical fit.
