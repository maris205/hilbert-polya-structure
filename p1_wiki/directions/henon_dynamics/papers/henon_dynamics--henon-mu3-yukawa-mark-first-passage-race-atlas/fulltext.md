# Frozen finite model

Let $L$ be the sixteen named C75 labels and let $A_k$ denote the support of the first $k$ labels in a uniformly random permutation. For each frozen C88 subgroup target $H_i$ define $$T_i=\min\{k:H_i\leq\Phi(A_k)\}.$$ The C88 receipt supplies the hit indicator of every one of the $2^{16}$ supports. We restrict to pairs $(i,j)$ for which neither subgroup is contained in the other; the frozen inclusion matrix has exactly 108 such unordered pairs.

# Race theorem

For every unordered incomparable pair $(i,j)$, the C91 receipt contains exact counts $R_{i,j}^{<}$, $R_{i,j}^{=}$, and $R_{i,j}^{>}$ for $T_i<T_j$, $T_i=T_j$, and $T_j<T_i$, respectively. They satisfy $$R_{i,j}^{<}+R_{i,j}^{=}+R_{i,j}^{>}=16!
 =20{,}922{,}789{,}888{,}000.$$ The receipt also contains the same three counts for each rank $k=0,\ldots,16$.

For a support $S$ of size $k$ and a label $x\in S$, consider the boundary edge $S\setminus\{x\}\to S$. If a target is not hit below the edge and is hit on $S$, the edge is pivotal. Every rank-$k$ edge represents exactly $(k-1)!(16-k)!$ permutations: the first $k-1$ positions are an ordering of $S\setminus\{x\}$ and the remaining positions are an ordering of its complement. For pair $(i,j)$, a left-only pivotal edge contributes to $R_{i,j}^{<}$ precisely when $i$ is first hit on the edge and $j$ is not hit on $S$; the symmetric condition gives $R_{i,j}^{>}$, while simultaneous pivotality gives $R_{i,j}^{=}$. These three disjoint classes partition every permutation by its first rank at which either target is hit, proving the identity and the rank decomposition.

# Certificate inventory

There are 108 pair rows, each with three exact rational probabilities, 17 rank cells, and the corresponding boundary-edge counts. Ninety-nine rows have a nonzero tie class. The independent checker rebuilds all bitset boundary edges directly from C88 and verifies every partition identity. SymPy checks all 324 outcome probability-generating functions and the exact factorial edge weights. Clean replay preserves the evidence digest $$\texttt{36b0fffda585ea483ba5603101c83c361b85ca4ba9a49c878f1e366d3c13ff0f}.$$
