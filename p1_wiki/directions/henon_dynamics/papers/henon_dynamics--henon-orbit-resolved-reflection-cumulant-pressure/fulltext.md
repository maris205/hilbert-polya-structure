# The missing distribution

For the area-preserving map $H(x,y)=(6-x^2-y,x)$, the real horseshoe is conjugate to the full two-shift and the reversor acts as sequence reversal [@DevaneyNitecki1979; @Arai2007]; see @LindMarcus1995 for the symbolic framework. At odd period, write $A_n$ for the primitive marked reflection packet. The canonical packet-mean Euler product assigns to all elements of $A_n$ the common weight generated by their average. That construction is intrinsic under coboundaries, but it does not retain within-period fluctuations.

We use the minimal cross-axis separator $$\label{eq:chi}
 \chi_{\rm R}(s)=\mathbf1\{s_{-1}=s_1\},\qquad
 S_n\chi_{\rm R}(s)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\chi_{\rm R}(\sigma^js).$$ The complete and primitive moment polynomials are $$\begin{aligned}
 F_n(q)&=\sum_{\substack{s:\,\sigma^ns=s\\s_{-j}=s_j}}
 q^{S_n\chi_{\rm R}(s)},\label{eq:Fdef}\\
 E_n(q)&=\sum_{s\in A_n}q^{S_n\chi_{\rm R}(s)}.\label{eq:Edef}\end{aligned}$$

# A reflected rank-two transfer matrix

[\[prop:decimation\]]{#prop:decimation label="prop:decimation"} Let $n=2m+1$. A reflection-fixed word is determined by $a_j=s_j$, $0\le j\le m$, and $$\label{eq:energy}
 S_n\chi_{\rm R}=1+2\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\mathbf1\{a_j=a_{j+1}\}.$$ Consequently $$\label{eq:Fclosed}
 F_{2m+1}(q)=2q(1+q^2)^m.$$

Multiplication by $2$ permutes $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, commutes with $j\mapsto-j$, and sends the distance-two comparisons in [\[eq:chi\]](#eq:chi){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:chi"} to nearest-neighbor comparisons. Under reflection, each path edge occurs twice and the edge joining the two terminal reflected sites is always equal. This gives [\[eq:energy\]](#eq:energy){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:energy"}. Equivalently, with $$T(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q^2&1\\1&q^2\end{pmatrix},$$ one has $F_{2m+1}(q)=q(1,1)T(q)^m(1,1)^{\mathsf T}$. The vector $(1,1)$ has eigenvalue $1+q^2$, proving [\[eq:Fclosed\]](#eq:Fclosed){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:Fclosed"}.

There are $2\binom mk$ reflected words of energy $1+2k$. Thus the equality increments form an exact binomial ledger; no asymptotic independence is assumed.

# Primitive subtraction with energy dilation

Ordinary Möbius subtraction would be wrong because repetition multiplies both length and energy.

[\[thm:primitive\]]{#thm:primitive label="thm:primitive"} For odd $n$, $$\begin{aligned}
 F_n(q)&=\sum_{d\mid n}E_d(q^{n/d}),\label{eq:dilation}\\
 E_n(q)&=\sum_{k\mid n}\mu(k)F_{n/k}(q^k).\label{eq:mobius}\end{aligned}$$

A word of least period $d\mid n$ repeats $n/d$ times, so its $n$-step energy is $(n/d)S_d\chi_{\rm R}$. Partitioning [\[eq:Fdef\]](#eq:Fdef){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:Fdef"} by least period gives [\[eq:dilation\]](#eq:dilation){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:dilation"}; Möbius inversion in the semigroup of the dilations $q\mapsto q^k$ gives [\[eq:mobius\]](#eq:mobius){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mobius"}.

At $q=1$, [\[eq:mobius\]](#eq:mobius){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mobius"} recovers the primitive count $D_n=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)2^{(d+1)/2}$. Coefficientwise nonnegativity is also immediate from the defining sum [\[eq:Edef\]](#eq:Edef){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:Edef"}.

# Nonlinear pressure and the exact gap

[\[thm:pressure\]]{#thm:pressure label="thm:pressure"} For each fixed real $s$, $$\label{eq:pressure}
 P_{\rm orb}(s):=
 \lim_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\ {\rm odd}}}
 \frac1n\log E_n(e^{-s})
 =\frac12\log(1+e^{-2s}).$$

The $k=1$ term in [\[eq:mobius\]](#eq:mobius){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mobius"}, using [\[eq:Fclosed\]](#eq:Fclosed){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:Fclosed"}, has the displayed rate. A proper divisor has odd $k\ge3$ and rate $$\frac1{2k}\log(1+e^{-2ks})
 <\frac12\log(1+e^{-2s}).$$ For $s\ge0$ this follows from monotonicity. For $s<0$, put $x=e^{-2s}>1$ and use $1+x^k<(1+x)^k$. Proper-divisor terms are therefore exponentially smaller; the divisor count is subexponential. Since $E_n(e^{-s})$ is a positive defining sum, the leading asymptotic follows.

The fair Bernoulli expectation of $\chi_{\rm R}$ is $1/2$. The aggregate-mean packet pressure is consequently $$\label{eq:mean}
 P_{\rm mf}(s)=\frac12\log2-\frac s2.$$

[\[cor:gap\]]{#cor:gap label="cor:gap"} For every real $s$, $$\label{eq:gap}
 P_{\rm orb}(s)-P_{\rm mf}(s)=\frac12\log\cosh s.$$ The gap is zero only at $s=0$, and $P_{\rm orb}''(0)=1/2$.

Use $1+e^{-2s}=2e^{-s}\cosh s$ in [\[eq:pressure\]](#eq:pressure){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:pressure"}. Strictness and the second derivative are immediate.

This is not merely a limiting Jensen inequality. For every odd $n\ge5$, the primitive reflected word with one centered $1$ has energy $n-2$, while the centered block $111$ has energy $n-4$. Thus finite primitive variance is already positive.

# Audit and route boundary

The certificate constructs exact integer polynomials through $n=31$. An independent implementation enumerates every reflected word through $n=17$ and builds the binomial rows by Pascal recurrence. Eight tests pass in normal and optimized modes, six dependencies are hash locked, and 25 claim mutations are rejected.

The result upgrades the packet pressure from a first moment to a full cumulant generating function for one natural potential. It does not attach rational primes, von Mangoldt amplitudes, a Fredholm operator, or a self-adjoint spectrum. Route A remains exploratory and Route B is not authorized. The exact polynomials [\[eq:mobius\]](#eq:mobius){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mobius"} are now the natural input for a genuinely orbit-resolved Euler product and its analytic boundary.
