# Question and scope

Let $G=W(E_6)$ be the group of order $51840$ supplied by the preceding exact group package. The C62 atlas supplies sixteen ambient-conjugacy classes of core-free subgroups, denoted $S_1,\ldots,S_{16}$, and C63 studies the permutation characters of the transitive $G$-sets $X_i=G/S_i$. Its exterior square shadow gives a rational character relation $R_4$ as displayed above. The present question is whether this relation is a genuine equality in the Burnside ring, or only after applying the linearization map to characters. The distinction is the classical mark-versus-character distinction [@dress1991marks; @serre1977linear; @gassmann1926bemerkungen].

We restrict throughout to the submodule generated by these sixteen types. The literal scope firewall is $$\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER}.$$ In particular, no decomposition groups, resolvents, local fields, bad Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, or Hilbert--Polya operator are inferred.

# Marks and exact reconstruction

For a subgroup $H$ and a transitive set $G/K$, the mark is $$\Phi_H(G/K)=|(G/K)^H|
 =\frac{\#\{g\in G:g^{-1}Hg\subseteq K\}}{|K|}.$$ The quotient is integral because the numerator is a union of right $K$-cosets. Define the restricted mark matrix $M$ by $$M_{ij}=\Phi_{S_i}(G/S_j), \qquad 1\leq i,j\leq16.$$

The producer first reads the C61 generator arrays and closes them under composition, obtaining exactly $51840$ permutations of 27 points. It then reads every C62 atlas stabilizer, verifies its digest, order, and core order, and selects the dictionary representative for each $S_i$. For every ordered pair $(i,j)$ it tests containment after conjugating a deterministic generating set of $S_i$ by each of the $51840$ group elements. This is a direct count, not a lookup of a table-of-marks package.

 \qquad \det M=226492416=2^{23}3^3.$$ Consequently the mark map is injective on $\mathbb Q\{X_1,\ldots,X_{16}\}$.

The distinction is conceptually useful. C63's character map forgets exactly the three displayed rational directions on this support, while the mark map retains stabilizer-fixed-point information. The determinant gives a compact integral certificate that no additional relation occurs among these sixteen transitive sets.

# Limitations and successor boundary

The full table of marks of $W(E_6)$ contains more subgroup classes than the sixteen types used here. Injectivity of this restricted matrix therefore does not imply a classification of the full Burnside ring. Nor does a mark distinction by itself distinguish arithmetic fields at bad primes or produce an Euler product. Those questions are deliberately left outside the scope of this paper.

# Conclusion

The C63 four-versus-four equality is a character-level shadow, not a Burnside equality: its exact mark witness is already visible at the $S_2$ row. The 16-type mark matrix is nonsingular, providing a short, reproducible separation certificate and a controlled starting point for any later integral question.
