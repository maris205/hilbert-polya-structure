# Frozen model and hitting event

Let $L=\{S_1,\ldots,S_{16}\}$ be the source-bound labels and let $$\Phi(A)\leq Q=\mathbb Z/9\oplus\mathbb Z/3\oplus\mathbb Z/2$$ be the subgroup generated by $A\subseteq L$. The frozen C75 order lists the twenty actual subgroups as $H_0,\ldots,H_{19}$, with $H_0=\{0\}$ and $H_{19}=Q$. If $\pi$ is uniform among the $16!$ label permutations, let $A_k$ be its first $k$ labels, including $A_0=\varnothing$, and define $$T_H(\pi)=\min\{k:H\leq\Phi(A_k)\}.$$ This is first passage into the containment up-set above $H$. It is not the event $\Phi(A_k)=H$; exact-closure hits and skips are not part of this atlas.

For $0\leq k\leq16$, put $$F_H(k)=\#\{A\subseteq L:|A|=k,\ H\leq\Phi(A)\}.$$ For a hitting support $S$, let $$p_H(S)=\#\{\ell\in S:H\not\leq\Phi(S\setminus\{\ell\})\}$$ be its number of pivotal labels.

# Exact law and stochastic order

For every actual target $H$ and $0\leq k\leq16$, $$\begin{aligned}
 \Pr(T_H\leq k)&=\frac{F_H(k)}{\binom{16}{k}},\label{eq:cdf}\\
 \Pr(T_H>k)&=1-\frac{F_H(k)}{\binom{16}{k}}.\label{eq:survival}\end{aligned}$$ For $k\geq1$, the exact number $N_H(k)$ of permutations first hitting at time $k$ is $$N_H(k)=\sum_{\substack{|S|=k\\H\leq\Phi(S)}}
 p_H(S)(k-1)!(16-k)!.\label{eq:pivotal}$$ Moreover, $$\mathbb E[T_H]=\sum_{k=0}^{15}\left(1-\frac{F_H(k)}{\binom{16}{k}}\right).
 \label{eq:mean}$$ If $H\leq K$, then $T_H(\pi)\leq T_K(\pi)$ for every permutation $\pi$. Thus their CDFs, survival functions, and expectations obey the corresponding stochastic order.

The unordered size-$k$ prefix of a uniform permutation is uniform among the $\binom{16}{k}$ subsets, proving [\[eq:cdf\]](#eq:cdf){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:cdf"}; complementing gives [\[eq:survival\]](#eq:survival){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:survival"}. A permutation first hits at $k\geq1$ exactly when its prefix support $S$ hits and its last prefix label is pivotal. For each such pair $(S,\ell)$, the preceding members have $(k-1)!$ orders and the remaining labels have $(16-k)!$ orders, proving [\[eq:pivotal\]](#eq:pivotal){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:pivotal"}. Summing the integer tail gives [\[eq:mean\]](#eq:mean){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mean"}. Finally, any prefix closure containing $K$ also contains $H\leq K$, so the pointwise order follows.

For $H_0$, all $16!$ permutations have $T_{H_0}=0$. For $H_{19}=Q$, the complete row of subset counts, pivotal data, first-passage masses, reduced probabilities, survival counts, and expectation agrees entrywise with the final frozen full-assembly certificate.
