## Independent proof plausibility audit

I rederived the theorem-critical identities rather than treating the
max-plus matrices as certificates.

1. For a selected exponent,
   \(A_\alpha=\mathbf1\alpha^{\mathsf T}-I\) and
   \(B_\beta=\mathbf1\beta^{\mathsf T}-I\).  Literal multiplication gives
   \[
   B_\beta A_\alpha
   =I+\mathbf1\bigl((B-1)\alpha-\beta\bigr)^{\mathsf T},
   \]
   so the permutation step is exactly
   \(C_{\alpha,\beta}=P+\mathbf1c_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathsf T}\) and
   \(c^{\mathsf T}\mathbf1=\lambda-1\).

2. Equitotality removes the diagonal translate from V score differences.
   With \(v=A_\alpha u\), equal W totals give
   \((\beta-\eta)\cdot v=-(\beta-\eta)\cdot x\).  Thus V is a unique
   maximizer and W a unique minimizer on the same residual phase, proving both
   directions of the cone criterion and its sign.

3. In the incidence construction all supports have total
   \(D=b_0(\ell+1)+K\ell\).  At phase \(j\), the moving spike contributes
   exactly \(K(H-1)\) to the requested V label and to every nonrequested W
   label.  Therefore the required V maximizer and W minimizer are unique with
   the same positive gap, including for repeated labels.

4. For \(r\ge2\),
   \[
   (A_\alpha u)_i-u_i
   =(\alpha_i-2)u_i+\sum_{k\ne i}\alpha_k u_k>0,
   \]
   and the same holds for \(B_\beta\).  The phase-zero gate makes the first
   source carry strict; thereafter \(u_n>w_n\) propagates both source and
   target strictness.  This closes the V1 dimension and momentum-seed gaps.

5. Prefix multiplication gives
   \(R_{s+1}^{\mathsf T}=c_s^{\mathsf T}P^s+\lambda R_s^{\mathsf T}\) and
   hence
   \[
   R_\ell^{\mathsf T}
   =\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}\lambda^{\ell-1-j}c_j^{\mathsf T}P^j.
   \]
   Since \(P^\ell=I\), the stated rank-one monodromy, characteristic
   polynomial, powers, and phase-resolved formulas follow directly.

6. The construction has \(0<(c_{a,b})_i<\lambda\).  If two pair digits
   agreed, a moving coordinate in the symmetric difference of two V
   occurrence sets would force magnitude \(K(D-1)\) on one side and at most
   \(K\) on the other; hence the V labels agree, and then the W labels agree.
   Ordinary coordinatewise base-\(\lambda\) expansion has no carry and
   recovers the rotated digits in their marked order.  The dictionary and
   relabeling qualifications are therefore exact.

7. Modulo \(\mathbb R\mathbf1\), every step is \([u]\mapsto[P u]\).  For
   the spike seed, the fixed star coordinate forces a diagonal displacement
   to vanish, while the unique H-spike rules out every \(1\le d<\ell\).
   This proves quotient least period independently of primitive selector
   period.  The exact recurrences then follow from the two eigenvalues of the
   phase monodromy or, for the full sequence, from periodic forcing and the
   multiplier \(\lambda\).
