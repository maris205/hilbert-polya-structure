## Paper-12 Candidate Gate

The shortlist was evaluated before any source lock was created:

1. **Frozen PCF quadratic, all-period equality tail.**  Decide whether every
   exact period `n >= 4` excludes the rational multiplier values `+2^n` and
   `-2^n`, equivalently whether every primitive cycle product differs from
   `+1` and `-1`.  This is the sole explicit theorem-level open tail retained
   by Paper 7.
2. **Matrix/numerator/alternating Fredholm escape.**  Determine whether a
   rigorously defined non-scalar local determinant can evade Paper 9's pure
   nonzero-scalar degree obstruction without importing a prime label.
3. **Fixed global generator-sensitive mechanism.**  Seek one group, cocycle,
   or extension fixed across all moduli whose native return law retains the
   distinguished generator without the `q`-dependent pseudo-symmetries and
   external labels ruled out by Papers 10--11.

The first candidate failed its all-period proof gate.  The second admits a
degree obstruction for fixed finite virtual rank but also a canonical global
augmentation-complex escape that works for every finite permutation and hence
fails A0.  The third was not realized by any fixed generator-sensitive object.

A broader reset then assessed an integer-sampled self-adjoint Fredholm
rigidity theorem and a cat-map fixed-line Artin factor.  Both are mathematically
valid, but primary-source collision audits left them below the conservative
standalone novelty threshold for the Batch-03 flagship.  The exact candidate
audit is frozen in `BATCH_03_IDEA_REPORT.md`, SHA-256
`d25bbc066487ba5e9da967eacc33a0fb6a5042a125763b4a79dfb277821d31d4`.

No extra-period computation, new prime scan, or exploratory candidate run was
used to choose among the candidates.
