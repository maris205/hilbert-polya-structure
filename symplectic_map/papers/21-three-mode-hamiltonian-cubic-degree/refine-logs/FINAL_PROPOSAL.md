# Final proposal and source boundary

## Title

**Three-Mode Hamiltonian Shears in A6: Exact Degree Growth and Cubic Perron Subfamilies**

## Research question

For the fixed family (F_g=T\circ S) defined by
\(V=q_1^2q_2^2q_3^2+q_1^g) and
\(W=p_1^2p_2^2p_3^2+p_3^g), can one prove an exact degree recurrence and its
first dynamical degree from finite exponent data, without cancellation or
genericity assumptions?

## Theorem target

Let (K\) be algebraically closed of characteristic zero and (g\ge8).  Put
\[
A_g=\begin{pmatrix}g-1&0&0\\2&1&2\\2&2&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
B_g=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&2\\2&1&2\\0&0&g-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
C_g=B_gA_g.
\]
For the cone (x=U_2/U_1\ge1, y=U_3/U_1\ge1, x+y<(g-3)/2), the leading
selector is unique and (C_g)-invariant.  The exponent support is governed by
the corresponding positive recurrence, no cancellation occurs in the
characteristic-zero ℕ-semiring, and
\[
\deg(F_g^n)=e_3^T C_g^n\mathbf 1\quad(n\ge0),\qquad
\lambda_1(F_g)=\rho(C_g)<(g-1)^2.
\]
The characteristic polynomial is recorded in the proof package; for
\(g\equiv2,3,4\pmod5\) its reduction modulo 5 is irreducible, giving a
contained algebraic-degree corollary only.

For every (g\ge8) with (g\equiv2,3,4\pmod5), the mod-five no-root table
and Gauss's lemma make \chi_g irreducible over \mathbb Q.  Thus the Perron
root is degree-exactly-three in these infinite subfamilies; “cubic” in the
title refers to this characteristic-polynomial degree.

## Hard exclusions

The source must not claim generic behavior, entropy or topological degree,
periodic-point classifications, torus/conjugacy statements, positive-
characteristic results, or priority over uncited literature.  “Canonical shear”
means the displayed exact-gradient map; “affine-triangular” is a larger class
and is not identified with it.

## Feasibility

Target 24–29 substantive pages, with proof-led sections, a collision table for
Papers 12–20, and a bounded bibliography.  There are no experiments, CAS runs,
network calls, or external data dependencies.
