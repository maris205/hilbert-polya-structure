# Research question and scope lock

Can the exact algebraic degree of the fixed three-mode composition
\(F_g=T\circ S\), with
\(V=q_1^2q_2^2q_3^2+q_1^g\) and
\(W=p_1^2p_2^2p_3^2+p_3^g\), be obtained from a positive exponent recurrence
for every algebraically closed characteristic-zero field (K) and integer
\(g\ge8)?

The answer sought is family-specific and deterministic.  It consists of an
explicit selector, invariant cone, support recurrence, no-cancellation proof,
exact degree formula, and Perron–Frobenius limit.  “Degree” means polynomial
degree of the coordinate tuple of (F_g^n); “first dynamical degree” means the
limit of its (n)-th roots.  No generic, probabilistic, topological,
arithmetic, periodic, entropy, conjugacy, torus, positive-characteristic, or
priority assertion is in scope.

## Formal input/output contract

**Input:** an algebraically closed field (K) of characteristic zero, an
integer (g\ge8), and six coordinate variables in \(\mathbb A^6\).

**Output:** the polynomial automorphism (F_g), its exact coordinate degree at
every iterate, and the root-growth limit.  The output must be a theorem with
all selectors and support rows visible; a plot, numerical fit, or CAS transcript
is not a substitute.

## What would falsify the proposal

The proposal fails if any selector is non-strict in the stated cone, if an old
support row reaches the selected weight, if a coefficient can vanish in
characteristic zero, if (U_3) does not dominate both other coordinates, or if
the row-sum inequality is misstated.  It also fails if the source broadens the
claim to affine-triangular maps or silently treats (g=7) as interior.

## Success criteria

Success requires the exact coordinate formulas, inverse and symplectic block
calculation, two selector gaps, cone case split, induction, semiring lemma,
visibility identity (e_3^TC_g^n\mathbf1), PF limit, characteristic polynomial,
mod-five table, boundary statement, collision ledger, and citation ledger.  The
planned manuscript occupies 24–29 substantive pages and contains no numerical
experiment or external-data dependency.

## Lock sentence

All numerical-looking quantities in the paper are symbolic integers or matrix
entries; none is an empirical measurement.  The source lock freezes this
interpretation together with the exact title and six-coordinate ambient space.
