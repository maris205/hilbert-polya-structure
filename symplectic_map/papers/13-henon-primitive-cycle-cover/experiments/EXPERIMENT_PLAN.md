## Direct-Prior and Adjacency Disclosure

The mathematics of PC1/PC2 is unchanged, but its novelty boundary is narrower
than the v1 summaries stated.

- Morton (1996), *On certain algebraic curves related to polynomial maps*,
  proves on p. 336, on the scalar fiber \(a=0\), that the orbit-shift fixed
  field for \(f_c(z)=z^d+c\) is generated by the multiplier
  \[
  w=d^n\prod_i z_i^{d-1}=\rho|_{a=0}
  \]
  for every \(d\ge2\).  For \(d=2\), Corollary 3 and p. 336 also prove that
  the orbit sum \(t=\sum_i f_c^i(z)=\tau|_{a=0}\) generates that fixed field.
  The 2011 corrigendum repairs a finite-characteristic lifting argument and
  does not retract these characteristic-zero statements.
- Consequently, scalar \(\rho\)-primitivity for all \(d\), and scalar
  \(\tau\)-primitivity for \(d=2\), are direct imported results rather than
  Paper 13 novelty.  The residual PC2 contribution is the passage through
  PC1's two-parameter normalization, exact fiber, and global \(S_r\) cover;
  uniform all-\(d\) \(\tau\)-generation, especially the separate \(d\ge3\)
  word-asymptotic input; and integral basis-free characteristic polynomials
  in \(A[T]\).
- Cantat--Dujardin (2026), Section 3.2 and Theorem A/Theorem 3.7, directly use
  formal-period derivative-trace multisets and prove finite determination of
  a Hénon map from finitely many trace spectra.  This is adjacent to \(\rho\),
  but it does not prove PC2: their object is a several-period formal trace
  spectrum used for finite map recovery, whereas PC2 concerns one fixed
  actual-period cycle cover and primitive generation of its degree-\(r\)
  function field.

The historical majority gate \(6.8/10\) novelty, \(7.4/10\) size, and \(0.74\)
proof confidence, and the historical dissent \(4.5/10\) novelty and \(3.9/10\)
size, remain distinct records.  After the direct Morton comparison, the
independent R1 conservative provisional ranges are \(4.8\)--\(5.5/10\)
novelty and \(4.5\)--\(5.5/10\) standalone size, leaning toward the dissent.
They are not averaged with or silently substituted for the historical scores;

## Frozen Mathematical Objects

Fix \(d,n\ge2\) as symbolic integers and define

\[
A=\mathbb Q[a,c],\qquad K=\mathbb Q(a,c),
\]

\[
H_{a,c}(x,y)=(ay+x^d+c,x),
\qquad
z_{i+1}=z_i^d+a z_{i-1}+c,
\]

\[
B_n=A[z_0,\ldots,z_{n-1}]/
(z_i^d+a z_{i-1}+c-z_{i+1})_i,
\]

\[
\nu=\sum_{e\mid n}\mu(n/e)d^e,
\qquad r=\nu/n.
\]

- \(E_n\): the generic reduced étale idempotent block of actual
  exact-period-\(n\) points in \(B_n\otimes_AK\).
- \(S=\operatorname{Norm}_A(E_n)\): the relative normalization.
- \(D_n=\mathbb Q[c,z]/(\Phi_{d,n}(z,c))\): the scalar affine dynatomic
  algebra.
- \(\sigma(z_i)=z_{i+1}\): time shift.
- \(S_0=S^{\langle\sigma\rangle}\) and
  \(F=E_n^{\langle\sigma\rangle}=\operatorname{Frac}(S_0)\).
- \(X_0^{\mathrm{aff}}(n)=\operatorname{Spec}(D_n^{C_n})\).
- \(\tau=\sum_i z_i\).
- \(\rho\): the trace of the pointwise derivative return product,
  \[
  \rho=\operatorname{tr}
  \prod_{i=n-1}^{0}
  \begin{pmatrix}d z_i^{d-1}&a\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
  \]
  with the displayed order matching the cyclic-coordinate convention.
- \(\chi_s(T)=\det(T\operatorname{id}_{S_0}-m_s)\in A[T]\), for
  \(s\in\{\tau,\rho\}\), defined on \(\bigwedge_A^rS_0\); over \(K\) this
  equals \(N_{F/K}(T-s)\).

No numeric instance other than the theorem's explicit \((2,2)\) boundary is a
positive fixture.  The small counterexamples below are logical falsifiers,
not exploratory data.
