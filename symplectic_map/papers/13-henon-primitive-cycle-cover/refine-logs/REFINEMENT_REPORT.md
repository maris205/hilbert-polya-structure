## R1 Direct-Prior Correction

Morton (1996), p. 336, directly identifies the scalar orbit-shift fixed field
for \(z^d+c\) as generated by the multiplier
\[
d^n\prod_i z_i^{d-1}=\rho|_{a=0}
\]
for every \(d\ge2\).  Corollary 3 and p. 336 give the scalar orbit-sum
generator \(\tau|_{a=0}\) for \(d=2\).  These are imported results, not Paper
13 novelty.

The residual PC2 delta is narrower: the two-parameter lift through PC1's
relative normalization, exact scalar fiber, and global \(S_r\) cover; uniform
\(\tau\)-generation for all \(d\), especially the separate \(d\ge3\)
asymptotic; and the integral, basis-free multiplication characteristic
polynomials in \(A[T]\).

Cantat--Dujardin (2026), Section 3.2 and Theorem A/Theorem 3.7, directly study
formal-period derivative-trace multisets across finitely many periods and use
them for finite parameter reconstruction.  This is adjacent to \(\rho\), but
distinct from fixed actual-\(n\) primitive generation of a single cycle
field.

## Final Proposal Snapshot

1. The full cyclic algebra
   \[
   B_n=A[z_0,\ldots,z_{n-1}]/(z_i^d+a z_{i-1}+c-z_{i+1})_i
   \]
   is \(A\)-free of rank \(d^n\) by a monic Gröbner basis.
2. Its generic actual-exact-\(n\) étale idempotent block \(E_n\) is one
   degree-\(\nu\) field.  The relative normalization \(S\) is geometrically
   integral and finite locally free, and its exact reduced scalar special
   fiber is
   \(D_n=\mathbb Q[c,z]/(\Phi_{d,n})\).
3. The invariant cover \(S_0=S^{C_n}\) has rank \(r=\nu/n\), special fiber
   \(D_n^{C_n}\), and full \(S_r\) geometric monodromy on cycles.
4. \(\tau=\sum z_i\) and pointwise derivative trace
   \(\rho=\operatorname{tr}(DH^n)\), both in \(S_0\), separately generate
   \(F=\operatorname{Frac}(S_0)\); their basis-free multiplication
   characteristic polynomials lie in \(A[T]\) and are irreducible of degree
   \(r\) over \(K\).
5. For \((d,n)=(2,2)\), \(r=1\),
   \(\tau=a-1\), and \(\rho=4a^2-6a+4+4c\).  This is a linear boundary, not
   evidence for nontrivial monodromy.

## Method Evolution Highlights

### 1. Generic actual-period block replaced embedded formal subtraction

The initial construction failed at parabolic/formal lower-period points.  The
repair defines exact period where the algebra is generically étale and then
uses relative normalization.  This preserves actual-period semantics without
pretending the exact locus is everywhere an embedded flat subscheme.

### 2. Special-fiber equality became a proof chain

The repaired proposal no longer says that normalization commutes with
\(a=0\) by definition.  It requires excellence/finite normalization,
Cohen--Macaulay miracle flatness, a constants argument, the unique height-one
prime above \((a)\) with \(e=1\), a finite birational comparison, and explicit
nilpotent exclusion before writing \(S/aS\cong D_n\).

### 3. Monodromy now uses two correctly directed bounds

The scalar special-line action injects into the global permutation image on a
good locus.  The time-shift centralizer bounds the global action from above.
Their equality gives the wreath point action and full \(S_r\) cycle action.

### 4. Observable primitivity has a narrower residual delta

Morton already gives scalar \(\rho\)-generation for all \(d\) and scalar
\(\tau\)-generation for \(d=2\).  Paper 13 retains the two-parameter lift,
the separate \(d\ge3\) \(\tau\) argument, the full-\(S_r\)
\(S_{r-1}\)-maximality step, and integral basis-free characteristic
polynomials.  The derivative trace remains distinct from field trace and
determinant throughout.

### 5. Validation was made smaller

The plan contains exactly three proof audits.  It rejects grids, scans,
exploratory tables, numerical roots, and machine theorem verdicts.  Any later
two-engine implementation is an independent falsifier/certificate layer only.
