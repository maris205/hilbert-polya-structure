## Proof-Critical Items for the Next Gate

1. The Henselian idempotent argument must prove that the generic exact block is
   one field; scalar irreducibility may not be cited as a slogan.
2. The proof of geometric integrality and \(S/aS\cong D_n\) must show the
   constants argument, the unique height-one prime over \((a)\), \(e=1\), the
   finite birational comparison, and exclusion of a nilpotent kernel.  Generic
   flatness alone is not enough.
3. Reynolds invariants must be shown to commute with the stated base change,
   and \(X_0^{\mathrm{aff}}(n)\) must remain explicitly affine.
4. The special-line monodromy theorem and the inclusion
   \(G_{\mathrm{special}}\subseteq G_{\mathrm{global}}\) must be invoked on a
   good unbranched locus.
5. The infinity-word comparisons must prove separately that \(\tau\) and
   \(\rho\) are non-base for every \(r>1\) case, with \((2,2)\) removed.
6. The source must use the full \(S_r\) stabilizer theorem after, not before,
   those non-base lemmas.
7. Characteristic-polynomial irreducibility must use the basis-free
   determinant on \(\bigwedge_A^rS_0\), membership in \(A[T]\), and the generic
   norm/minimal-polynomial identification; it must not be inferred from a
   field trace or derivative determinant computation.
