9. proof from CAS, floating-point spectra, finite iterates, parameter scans,

10. absolute literature priority from the bounded R1 screen.

## STOP conditions

Source progression must stop if:

- the corrected positive-sign family or $T_g^+\circ S_g^+$ order drifts;
- any of the four selectors ties on the declared cone;
- any one of the six target cone faces fails;
- a carried coordinate or coefficient cancellation defeats the recurrence;
- $q_4$ loses strict visibility at a positive iterate;
- the matrix, quartic, or $R_g(1)$ identity changes;
- the uniform mod-five proof fails;
- a direct public or internal theorem collision is verified;
- the paper cannot remain standalone inside the 22--30 content-page band.

The appropriate response to a STOP condition is correction, narrowing, or
blocking—not replacement by experimental evidence.
