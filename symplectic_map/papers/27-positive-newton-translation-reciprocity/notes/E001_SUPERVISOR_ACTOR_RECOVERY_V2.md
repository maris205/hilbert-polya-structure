## 12. Total direct-result transitions

The actor classifies every direct outer outcome into exactly one of these
branches, in order. Once selected, no later observation promotes it.

1. DIRECT_SUCCESS_CANDIDATE: exact BEGIN, exact full-layout watchdog RESULT,
   two direct stdout lines plus EOF within cap, empty stderr plus EOF, zero
   direct status, all deadlines timely, and all section 13 success predicates.
   After containment and terminal revalidation it appends FINAL_SUCCESS(S).
2. DIRECT_FULL_PERMANENT_FAILURE: exact BEGIN, one exact full-layout watchdog
   RESULT with disposition=PERMANENT_FAILURE and payload_success=0, two stdout
   lines plus EOF within cap, empty stderr plus EOF, zero direct status, and
   exact group absence. It is already the V8 terminal binder result and
   appends FINAL_FAILURE(F2a). No recovery binder is run.
3. DIRECT_SCHEMA_OVERFLOW: exact BEGIN, one exact seven-pair watchdog
   schema-layout=overflow RESULT with attempt_consumed=1, payload_success=0,

   FINAL_FAILURE(F2b), with journal x=slo and actor-result detail=slo, and never dispatches a
   recovery binder. This is the previously missing total transition.
4. DIRECT_INVALID_OR_INCOMPLETE: every other spawned-direct outcome,
   including missing or invalid BEGIN/RESULT, wrong mode, nonzero/signaled or

A syntactically valid short overflow with a missing outer EOF, nonempty

permanent failure but lacks the full recovery absence/evidence layout; it
therefore leads to F5b recovery-invalid and is never rerun.

## 14. Containment, recovery intent, and exactly-once limits

The actor alone waits the direct child P. It retains raw status and time.
ECHILD, another PID, unsafe interruption, wait error, late status, signaled or
nonzero status is invalid, but draining continues. On candidate success it
requires killpg(P,0) to return ESRCH, closes the never-written payload-stdin
writer, and requires a second ESRCH. Any live or uncertain group enters
cleanup.

Cleanup sends at most one SIGKILL to an affirmatively owned exact P group,
never a bare or unbound PID. It drains both streams and directly reaps P before
KEND when ownership remains in the live actor. It then closes payload stdin
and requires two independent ESRCH observations. If ownership or no-reuse is
uncertain, it sends no signal and cannot claim cleanup.

For branch 4, RECOVERY_INTENT is appended only after P is absent, direct
observations are frozen, CAUSE is selected, the ledger remains exactly the
expected append chain, and all recovery prerequisites are affirmative. Its
durability precedes the recovery posix_spawn call. Once durable, recovery
dispatch is consumed even if the call was never entered or never returned.
The same live actor with its nonreconstructible one-call witness may call
posix_spawn once. A synchronous error produces F4. A returned R is verified
as R>1 and getsid(R)=getpgid(R)=R and then RECOVERY_SPAWNED is durably appended
before report acceptance.

The recovery process uses QEND, independent stdout/stderr drains, direct wait,
and exact R-group containment. A valid recovery requires one full-layout
RESULT plus EOF, zero stderr plus EOF, WIFEXITED/WEXITSTATUS zero, R-group
absence, original P-group absence, and:

- mode=recovery-binder, recovery_pgid=P, attempt_consumed=1,
  disposition=PERMANENT_FAILURE, payload_success=0;
- exact V8 unavailable values for every payload, wait, stream, EOF, deadline,
  counter, hash, and hex capture field, including the two separately retained
  payload_wait_timely fields at ordinals 20 and 49;
- supervisor_lost=1, wait_provenance=unavailable-recovery,
  stream_provenance=unavailable-recovery, wait_available=0,

  recovery_esrch_terminal=1;
