### B. Translation and finite transience

\]

### C. Exact pair count, equality, and global-origin tail

representatives, \(S_V,S_W,q_V,q_W\), strict activity intervals, and the

\[
N_{\rm pair}=|I_V\cup I_W|,
\]

\[

\le(q_V-1)+(q_W-1)

equality characterization for an existing branch, not a realization
