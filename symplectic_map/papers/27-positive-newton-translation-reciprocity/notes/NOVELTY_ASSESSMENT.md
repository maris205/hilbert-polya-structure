## Decision at source-design entry

The candidate-level dual review is a PASS (E0052): R1 reported novelty 8.0/10,
standalone value 8.0/10, and proof readiness 9.1/10; the fresh R2 correction
reported novelty 8.0/10, standalone value 7.8/10, and proof readiness 9.2/10.

are conservative inherited scores, not a new priority claim.  Source design
retains the 24–28-page proof-only target and the E0051 one-edge/one-step
narrowing.

The distinguishing conjunction is the theorem-level package:

1. finite nonempty collected \(E_V,E_W\subset\mathbb Z_{\ge2}^r\), \(r\ge3\);
2. exact all-ones translation of the two-phase weighted degree state;
3. global \(d_V+d_W-2\) selector-change bound from monotone wall crossings;
4. stationary affine phase tail with explicit \((\lambda,\mu)\), including the
   inverse phase order;
5. grouped positive-face Hessian/Jacobian survival uniform in nonzero
   coefficients;
6. reflected phase reciprocity only on declared observable seed spans; and
7. a local four-section, one-edge lower-ideal margin lemma plus an asymmetric
   \(r=3\) fixture.

No one item is advertised as independently new; the assessment is for the
scoped conjunction and its proof boundaries.

## Anti-claims and novelty limits

Paper 27 does not claim arbitrary supports, zero-coordinate extensions, a
global map-level reversor, global conjugacy/classification, entropy, higher
dynamical degrees, a minimal scalar recurrence, a Perron algebraic degree,
same-seed reciprocity for a non-\(R_{\rm state}\)-fixed pair, or priority.  The
lower-ideal result is not multi-edge, target-core, C1→C2 perturbation, or
all-iterate stability.  The fixture is an exact example, not universal
evidence.  A later search finding an exact conjunction is a hard collision and
requires stopping the manuscript gate.

Safe wording for the manuscript is:

> Our search covered publicly indexed first-party sources through 2026-08-29
> UTC and found no paper matching this conjunction of hypotheses and
> conclusions.  This bounded observation is not a priority or exhaustiveness
> claim.
