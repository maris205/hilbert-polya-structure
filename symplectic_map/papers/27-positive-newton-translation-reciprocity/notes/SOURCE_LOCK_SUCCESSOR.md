### Clause B: translation and finite transience

For every certified step,
\[
u'=u+\delta_{\alpha,\beta}(u)\mathbf1,\qquad\delta_{\alpha,\beta}(u)>0.
\]
For an infinite certified strict branch, use
\[
u_n=u_0+t_n\mathbf1,\qquad t_{n+1}>t_n.
\]
Finite branches may stop at a tie, carry failure, empty cell, or missing
certificate.

### Clause C: equality and tail

\(S_V,S_W,q_V,q_W\), strict activity intervals, switch-index sets, and
\[
N_{\rm pair}=|I_V\cup I_W|.
\]

in \(d_V+d_W-2\) requires and is implied by:

1. every total class is uniquely available;
2. every \(V\)-class has a sampled nonempty strict interval;
3. every \(W\)-class has a sampled nonempty strict interval; and
4. the two component switch-index sets are disjoint.

A line touching only at a tie is not visited.  The closed stationary formula
must retain the original \(u_0\).

### Clause D: literal word reciprocity

the scaling-plus-coordinate-perturbation proof must establish

U_e=\mathbb R^r,\qquad V_e=A_\alpha U_e=\mathbb R^r.

\[

availability, unique selection, carries, and target certificates, and then

concrete row family and state.  The theorem stops after one forward and one
