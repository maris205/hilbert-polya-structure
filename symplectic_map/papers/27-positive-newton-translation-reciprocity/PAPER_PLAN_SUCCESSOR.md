\(r\ge3\), with finite collected supports contained in
\(\mathbb Z_{\ge2}^r\), the paper studies certified strict degree branches.
Its contribution is the conjunction of:

1. a fully typed source/target/reflected cell formalism and an actual
   leading-degree transition
   \[
   (u,w)\mapsto(B_\beta A_\alpha u,A_\alpha u);

   \(d_V+d_W-2\), its iff equality criterion, and a closed stationary affine
   tail with global origin;

## 2. Theorem and proof order

The manuscript will state one main theorem with five separately quantified
clauses:

- **A. Typed survival.** For one certified arrow, the displayed source,
  reflected, target, and transformed families give exact leading degrees and
  literal target membership.
- **B. Translation and finite transience.** Every certified step translates
  \(u\) by a positive integer multiple of \(\mathbf1\); every infinite
  strict branch has the ordered-pair selector bound and a stationary tail.
- **C. Equality and closed tail.** Equality in the bound has the four exact
  activity/sampling/non-simultaneity conditions, and the tail has the
  global-origin closed form.
- **D. Literal word reciprocity.** Every nonempty strict homogeneous integer
  arrow domain has \(U_e=V_e=\mathbb R^r\); finite-word reciprocity is
  equivalent to literal reflected labels and all reflected certificates.
- **E. Local radius.** For one fixed normalized state and one predeclared
  finite row family, the explicit half-margin radius preserves one forward
  and one reflected inverse step.

The proof dependency order is fixed:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{typed rows and seed realization}\\
&\quad\to\text{grouped face Hessian}\\
&\quad\to\text{Jacobian independence}\\
&\quad\to\text{associated-graded substitution}\\
&\quad\to\text{fresh/old separation and phase induction}\\
&\quad\to\text{diagonal translation}\\
&\quad\to\text{envelope ordering, pair count, equality, tail}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The full-span lemma then feeds label injectivity and word reciprocity.  The
finite composed row families feed the dual-norm radius independently.  The
fixture follows every universal proof and is never a premise of one.
