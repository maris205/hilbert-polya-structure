# Citation Verification

## Scope and cutoff

Verification cutoff: **2026-08-15 UTC**.

This record verifies bibliographic identity and the exact use boundary for
Paper 11.  It is not a claim of exhaustive coverage or historical priority.
Primary/official records were preferred; arXiv records were used to inspect
definitions and equations.  No literature source is treated as evidence for
the Paper-10 arithmetic theorem itself.

## Locked primary records

### `GuseinZadeLuengoMelle2008`

- S. M. Gusein-Zade, I. Luengo, and A. Melle-Hernández,
  “An equivariant version of the monodromy zeta function.”
- In *Geometry, Topology, and Mathematical Physics*, AMS Translations Series
  2, volume 224, pp. 139--146, American Mathematical Society (2008).
- arXiv: `0803.3708`.
- Verified links:
  - <https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3708>
  - <https://www.mat.ucm.es/~amelle/Equivariant-monodromy-GLM.pdf>
- Verified use: rational Burnside-ring power series based on equivariant
  Lefschetz numbers and the Burnside $\lambda$/power structure.
- Scope guard: later scalar ledgers apply additive maps to the exact-period
  exponent classes before forming the scalar product.  They do not assume
  that arbitrary Burnside marks or the additive orbifold homomorphism
  preserve Burnside multiplication or the symmetric-power structure.
- Boundary: this is the direct predecessor of the point-order series; no
  Paper-11 originality claim is permitted.

### `GuseinZade2013`

- S. M. Gusein-Zade, “On an equivariant analogue of the monodromy zeta
  function.”
- *Functional Analysis and Its Applications* **47**(1), 14--20 (2013);
  Russian original pp. 17--25.
- arXiv: `1207.2282`.
- Verified link: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.2282>.
- Verified use: the invariant is a virtual finite/locally finite
  $G$-permutation, equivalently a $(\mathbb Z\times G)$-set, determined by
  all $L^G(g\phi^m)$.  Finite-index stabilizers are parametrized by
  $(H,m,\alpha)$ with $\alpha\in C_G(H)/H$.
- Boundary: this is strictly stronger than the untwisted point-order series
  for the current regular translation.  No DOI is frozen because the author
  verified the arXiv full text and journal metadata but did not obtain an
  unambiguous DOI from the consulted primary records.

### `GuseinZadeLuengoMelle2015`

- S. M. Gusein-Zade, I. Luengo, and A. Melle-Hernández,
  “On an equivariant version of the zeta function of a transformation.”
- *Arnold Mathematical Journal* **1**(2), 127--140 (2015).
- DOI: `10.1007/s40598-015-0012-8`.
- arXiv: `1203.3344`.
- Verified links:
  - <https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-015-0012-8>
  - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3344>
- Exact verified content:
  - equations (4)--(7) distinguish equivariant Lefschetz classes counting
    fixed points from those counting fixed $G$-orbits;
  - the example $X=(G/H)\times\mathbb Z_k$ separates order in $X$ from order
    in $X/G$;
  - equations (10)--(11) define exact-period Burnside classes and the
    integral orbit-order zeta;
  - Proposition 2 supplies the divisibility needed for the integral version;
  - equation (13) gives the two orbifold reductions by applying the
    additive orbifold map to the two Lefschetz sequences and then forming
    their exact-period scalar zetas; and
  - the example where monodromy belongs to $G$ has trivial quotient action
    and a period-one orbit-order factor.
- Boundary: Paper 11 specializes these definitions; it does not create or
  unify them.

### `EbelingGuseinZade2018`

- Wolfgang Ebeling and Sabir M. Gusein-Zade,
  “Enhanced equivariant Saito duality.”
- *Journal of Algebra and Its Applications* **17**(10), 1850181 (2018).
- DOI: `10.1142/S0219498818501815`.
- arXiv: `1506.05604`.
- Verified links:
  - <https://doi.org/10.1142/S0219498818501815>
  - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05604>
- Exact verified content:
  - Definitions 1--2 define finite enhanced $G$-sets and their Grothendieck
    ring;
  - irreducibles carry $(H,k,\bar h,\bar\alpha)$;
  - Definitions 5--6 define the orbifold zeta through fixed sectors and
    centralizer quotients.
- Boundary: on a free regular set the character is trivial; the carrier may
  retain the translating element while the orbifold image has only the
  identity sector.

### `Zegowitz2017`

- Stefanie Zegowitz, “Closed orbits in quotient systems.”
- *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* **37**(7), 2337--2352 (2017).
- DOI: `10.1017/etds.2016.3`.
- arXiv: `1502.02693`.
- Verified links:
  - <https://doi.org/10.1017/etds.2016.3>
  - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.02693>
- Exact verified content: the three quotient phenomena are surviving,

### `Miles2017`

- Richard Miles, “A dynamical zeta function for group actions.”
- *Monatshefte für Mathematik* **182**(3), 683--708 (2017).
- DOI: `10.1007/s00605-016-0909-x`.
- arXiv: `1506.08555`.
- Verified links:
  - <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00605-016-0909-x>
  - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08555>

### `Walton2018`

- *Journal of Number Theory* **189**, 202--223 (2018).
- DOI: `10.1016/j.jnt.2018.03.023`.
- arXiv: `1705.09034` (preprint title: “Counting periodic points over finite

- Verified links:

No 2024--2026 source located supplied a closer finite-set
retention--compression theorem.  This bounded non-detection is not evidence
of novelty or priority.

Terms explicitly excluded as false friends include arithmetic Hasse--Weil
zeta functions of stacks, Selberg zetas of orbifolds, representation zeta
functions, motivic zetas, subgroup zeta functions, and Ruelle zetas of smooth
flows.  They cannot be cited as if they were the fixed finite-set
transformation definitions audited here.

## Citation-use locks

1. Every occurrence of “equivariant zeta” in later work must name the exact
   construction.
2. The 2008 rational, 2013 $G$-permutation, and 2015 integral orbit-order
   constructions are separate primary records.
3. The point-order formula may retain exact period/subgroup information but
   not a distinguished generator in the current untwisted specialization.
4. Exact generator recovery belongs to the full twisted $G$-permutation or
   enhanced carrier and requires a fixed effective labelled action.
5. Orbifold/inertia sector counts are stabilizer data, not return times.
6. Miles and Walton are boundaries, not implementation authority.
7. Current Ruelle/Fried sources do not authorize a transfer/Fredholm or
   analytic continuation claim.
