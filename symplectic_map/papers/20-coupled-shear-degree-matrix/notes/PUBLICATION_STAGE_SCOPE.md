## Frozen theorem and proof contract

The field is algebraically closed of characteristic zero, (g\in\mathbb Z),
and (g\geq5).  In coordinates
((q_1,q_2,p_1,p_2)\in\mathbb A^4_K),

\[
V=q_1^2q_2^2+q_1^g,\qquad W=p_1^2p_2^2+p_2^g,
\]
\[
S(q,p)=(q,p+\nabla V(q)),\qquad
T(q,p)=(q+\nabla W(p),p),\qquad F_g=T\circ S.
\]

The selected phase rows and complete-step matrix are exactly

\[
A_g=\begin{pmatrix}g-1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
B_g=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&g-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
C_g=B_gA_g=\begin{pmatrix}g+3&2\\2(g-1)&g-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The (q)-phase cone is

\[
\mathcal C_g=\{u\in\mathbb R_{>0}^2:
1\le u_2/u_1<(g-2)/2\},
\]

(u_{n+1}=C_gu_n).  The exact headline theorem is:

* (F_g) is a symplectic polynomial automorphism of \(\mathbb A^4_K\);
* for every (n\ge1),
  \(\deg(F_g^n)=e_2^\mathsf TC_g^n(1,1)^\mathsf T\);
* \(\lambda_1(F_g)=\rho(C_g)=(\sqrt g+1)^2\);
* \(\deg(S)=\deg(T)=g-1\) and
  \((\sqrt g+1)^2<(g-1)^2\) for (g\ge5);
