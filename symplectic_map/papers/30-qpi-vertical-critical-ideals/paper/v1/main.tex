\begin{abstract}
We determine the complete first-layer critical ideal and the higher
cyclotomic first jets of the divided state differential of the original
q-Painlev\'e I invariant. Reduction of this differential is a Hasse
power times the small-order invariant differential, but that residue
does not determine its tangent component on a supersingular level.
We identify this component through the actual Bockstein of the
anticanonical line bundle, an integer prime-trace congruence, and the
connecting class of locally exact differentials on the complete fiber.
For every prime and every tame block size, the full first-layer
coefficient ideal is generated by the cyclotomic parameter and the
Hasse polynomial. At higher odd-prime levels we obtain a complete
first-jet factorization and its exact truncated ideal, retaining
multiple Hasse roots and arbitrary unit-time jets. In characteristic
two the comparison starts at height two: a mixed cubic relation gives
a truncated transverse quotient of length five with a specified action
of the base parameter. We prove the actual spectral Jacobian and
closed-fiber Hasse identifications needed to interpret these statements
on the original eight-center surface, including its four terminal
affine lines. Higher full thickness and singular-level critical ideals
are not determined.
\end{abstract}
