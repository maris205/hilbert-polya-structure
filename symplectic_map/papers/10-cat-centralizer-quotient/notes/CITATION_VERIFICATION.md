   site does not transfer authority to a stronger adjacent statement.
2. BNR 2013, KR 2000, KRR 2007, and Gusein-Zade et al. 2015 are dominant
   collisions, not generic background.
3. Zegowitz 2017 and Miles 2017 delimit quotient-period and group-action-zeta
   semantics.  They do not prove the fixed Paper-10 torsor theorem.

   explicitly listed.  No arXiv-issued DataCite DOI is presented as a journal
   DOI.

6. The allowed novelty statement is only a **2.5--3/10 scoped synthesis and

### `BaakeNeumaerkerRoberts2013` — dominant finite-centralizer collision

- Michael Baake, Natascha Neumärker, and John A. G. Roberts, “Orbit
  structure and (reversing) symmetries of toral endomorphisms on rational
  lattices,” *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems* **33**(2),
  527--553 (2013).
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.3934/dcds.2013.33.527>.
- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.1003>.
- **Verified role:** rational lattices, cyclic-matrix commutants, full unit
  centralizers, determinant-one subgroups, finite-field scalar/Jordan/split/
  irreducible types, reversing symmetries, and prime-power cat-map context.
- **Prohibited use:** no claim that Paper 10 discovers these symmetry groups,
  finite-field cases, or cycle classifications.

### `KurlbergRudnick2000` — norm-one/Hecke collision

- Pär Kurlberg and Zeév Rudnick, “Hecke theory and equidistribution for the
  quantization of linear maps of the torus,” *Duke Mathematical Journal*
  **103**(1), 47--77 (2000).
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1215/S0012-7094-00-10314-6>.
- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9901031>.

### `KurlbergRosenzweigRudnick2007` — invariant-form/symplectic collision

- Pär Kurlberg, Lior Rosenzweig, and Zeév Rudnick, “Matrix elements for the
  quantum cat map: Fluctuations in short windows,” *Nonlinearity* **20**(10),
  2289--2304 (2007).
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/20/10/001>.
- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701685>.
- **Verified role:** the invariant quadratic form

- **Prohibited use:** no transfer of its quantum hypotheses or fluctuation
  results to Paper 10.  The fixed $\Delta_q$ identities are reproved.

### `GuseinZadeLuengoMelle2015` — decisive quotient-clock collision

- S. M. Gusein-Zade, I. Luengo, and A. Melle-Hernández, “On an equivariant

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-015-0012-8>.
