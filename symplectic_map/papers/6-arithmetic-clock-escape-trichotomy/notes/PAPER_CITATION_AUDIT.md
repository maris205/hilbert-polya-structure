## Corrections applied during verification

- The identifier `10.24033/ast.28` previously associated in a research note
  with Parry--Pollicott does not resolve in the DOI system and is not present
  in the official Numdam record.  It is omitted; the canonical Numdam record
  is used instead.
- Deninger's *Dynamical Systems for Arithmetic Schemes* is now cited as the
  formal article in *Indagationes Mathematicae* 37(1) (2026), 25--136, rather
  than only as an older preprint.
- The 2022 Cambridge edition of Baker's book lists Alan Baker as author and
  David Masser as writer of the foreword; the BibTeX preserves that distinction.
- Ingram's journal volume is 108 (2014), pages 780--808.  The DOI registration
  year can appear as 2013 because of online publication; the issue year used
  in the bibliography is 2014.
- The scaling-site article has formal issue year 2016 despite online
  availability in November 2015.
