## Deliberate omissions

- A Crossref result with an ``open problems'' title exposed internally
  inconsistent author/title metadata and was therefore excluded.
- No citation was admitted solely because a search snippet looked relevant.
- No external table of primes, spectral targets, or zero ordinates was opened or
  used.  The paper's numerical content is confined to the source-locked exact
  audit.

## Claim boundary enforced during verification

The literature establishes substantial geometric, arithmetic, height, and
rigidity theory for Hénon maps.  None of the records above was treated as a
source for the paper's precise elementary implication

\[
  |\lambda|\in\mathbf Q_{>0}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  |\lambda|\text{ is supported on the predeclared rational bad primes}.
\]

That implication is proved in the manuscript from the cyclic recurrence,
integral monodromy, and a conjugation-stable Galois closure.  The bibliography
therefore supplies context and comparison rather than surrogate evidence.
