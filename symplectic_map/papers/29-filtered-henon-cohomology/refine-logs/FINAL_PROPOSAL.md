Title: Filtered polynomial cohomology and finite periodic tests for symplectic Hénon maps.

## Problem anchor

polynomial additive coboundaries with their ordinary-degree filtration and prove
an effective exact test on a complete fixed-point scheme. Use the same equation
to classify rational invariants of the specified parameter-preserving symplectic lift.

## Thesis and dominant contribution

The mixed-radix orbit basis is compatible with ordinary degree, and degree along
each macro-shift orbit is convex. This yields degree-preserving primitives and
an exact filtered obstruction count. The same orbit structure, separated by a
unique circular gap, gives a degree-controlled test on one complete periodic scheme.
The fixed-field classification is a supporting application of this same equation.
