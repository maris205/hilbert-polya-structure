## Bibliography admission rule

Before a source appears in a manuscript bibliography:

1. its title, authors, year, venue, pagination or article number, DOI or arXiv identifier must be checked against a primary record;
2. the cited sentence must not attribute a Paper 26 proof step to it;
3. the comparison must respect the hypotheses of both results;
4. no phrase implying global priority may be inferred from absence in the local corpus.

Until those checks occur, this ledger is a source-design boundary, not a final bibliography.
