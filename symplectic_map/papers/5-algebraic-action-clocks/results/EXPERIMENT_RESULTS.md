## Exact static findings

1. **Algebraic-value certificate.**  Every declared algebraic evaluation,
   finite summation, algebraic scale, repetition, average, real part,
   imaginary part, and modulus remains algebraic under the frozen
   hypotheses.  Hence none can equal a positive prime logarithm.
2. **Gauge boundary.**  The stepwise shift is exactly
   `chi_n(P_n)-chi_0(P_0)+sum_j C_j`.  Endpoint compatibility removes only
   the endpoint difference.  A defined algebraic mismatch preserves
   algebraicity; a pole, unfrozen branch contribution, or transcendental
   normalization stops the certificate.
3. **Sharp normalization counterexample.**  The symbolic identity-map
   control with a transcendental constant shows that a map-only claim is
   false.  The conclusion therefore belongs to the frozen algebraic
   normalization, not to exact symplecticity alone.
4. **Hénon specialization.**  For
   `H_a(q,p)=(q^2-a-p,q)`, the audit verifies
   `G=2q^3/3-pq`, the opposite-sign type-1 action, determinant one, the
   low-period indexing convention, and the projective/valuation proof
   ledgers without solving a periodic equation.
5. **Integrality boundary.**  The justified refinement is only that
   `3*A_G` is S-integral in the orbit field.  The exact fixed-point control
   has action `-1/3`, so integrality of `A_G` itself must not be claimed at
   places above 3.

## Route boundary

The normalized algebraic periodic-action route is rejected as an exact
prime-logarithm clock for every period.  This does **not** exclude:

- transcendental or target-independent nonalgebraic normalizations;
- multivalued potentials with a separately frozen and proved monodromy
  ledger;
- closed but non-exact primitive changes;
- `arg(A_G)` or `log|A_G|` observables;
- multiplier, return-time, logarithmic-observable, infinite-place, or
  adelic clocks;
- approximate matching, which is outside this exact certificate.

Passing this audit does not authorize a prime/zero match, zeta or Fredholm
construction, quantization stage, or post-hoc parameter/normalization
change.  The plan's correct disposition is `GO as certificate`; because the
standalone novelty audit is only 3/10, the contribution should be merged into
a broader obstruction synthesis if a venue requires deeper standalone
novelty.
