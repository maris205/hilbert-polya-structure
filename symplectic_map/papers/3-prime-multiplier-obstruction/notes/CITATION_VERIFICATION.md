## Manuscript-level boundary

The literature already contains multiplier-polynomial integrality,
global-spectrum rigidity, dynatomic machinery, and singular symplectic
extensions.  The defensible contribution is the audited chain for one frozen
candidate: an elementary all-period divisibility obstruction, exact low-period
implementation certificates with adversarial controls, exact coordinate
duplication, and a strictly branchwise cotangent bridge.  No absolute priority
claim is permitted for any component in isolation.
