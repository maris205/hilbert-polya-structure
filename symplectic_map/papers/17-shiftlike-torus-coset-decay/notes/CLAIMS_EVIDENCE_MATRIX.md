## Forbidden claim combinations

The following combinations fail the evidence matrix and must be stopped:

- “`T_m` is positive-dimensional” instead of “`V_m` contains a torus coset”;
- “resonance implies infinite `T_2` for every `Gamma`”;
- “`a=1,P(1)=0` characterize equality”;
- any `gcd(k,nu)` dependence or iterated-rotation bookkeeping;
- “nonlinear monomials have no finite window”;
- an effective count, enumeration, or algorithm attributed to Laurent;
- finite generation or bounded torsion substituted for arbitrary finite rank;
- a positive-characteristic, `a=0`, Laurent/rational-map, or arbitrary-
  automorphism extension;
- affine-conjugacy invariance of actual support;
- improvement or absorption of Paper16;
- global priority, all-maximal-coset classification, heights, or periodic-point
  claims.

failure mode after the constant anchor is removed. Laurent converts the
geometric no-coset statements to qualitative arithmetic finiteness; it does
not participate in the internal classification. Paper16 is a portfolio
