### Assumptions, maps, inverses, and symplecticity

The source fixes a field \(K\) of characteristic zero, integers \(r\ge4\) and
\(g\ge2r+1\), and

\[
V(q)=\prod_{i=1}^r q_i^2+q_1^g,\qquad
W(p)=\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^2+p_r^g.
\]

It defines

\[
S(q,p)=(q,p+\nabla V(q)),\quad
T(q,p)=(q+\nabla W(p),p),\quad F=T\circ S.
\]

The subtraction formulas

\[
S^{-1}(q,p)=(q,p-\nabla V(q)),\qquad
T^{-1}(q,p)=(q-\nabla W(p),p)
\]

are exact polynomial inverses. With symmetric Hessians \(H_V,H_W\),

\[
J_S=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\H_V&I\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_T=\begin{pmatrix}I&H_W\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For \(\Omega=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\-I&0\end{pmatrix}\), multiplication gives

\[
J_S^{\mathsf T}\Omega J_S
=\begin{pmatrix}H_V-H_V^{\mathsf T}&I\\-I&0\end{pmatrix}
=\Omega
\]

and the analogous identity with lower-right block
\(H_W^{\mathsf T}-H_W=0\) for \(T\). Thus \(S,T,F\) are polynomial
automorphisms preserving \(\sum_i dq_i\wedge dp_i\). No algebraic-closedness
assumption is used.
