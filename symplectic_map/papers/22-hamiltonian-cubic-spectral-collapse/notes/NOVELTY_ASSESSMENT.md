## Differentiating signature

Removing any one item below weakens the novelty case:

1. arbitrary public rank $r\ge4$, rather than one extra fixed rank;
2. exact-gradient product shears with pure spikes at opposite endpoints;
3. a single explicit sufficient cone uniform in $(r,g)$;
4. strict selection, carried-coordinate control, and sign-safe leading forms;
5. exact last-coordinate visibility;
6. an $(r-3)$-dimensional identity space plus one fixed-dimensional
   complementary restriction;
7. exact factorization and multiplicity, not sampled characteristic
   polynomials; and
8. a sharp seed/selected-face tie at $g=2r$.

The literal four-mode proposal was rejected as a quartic-Perron story because
the nontrivial factor remains cubic. That failure motivates the spectral
collapse theorem; it does not create a new quartic result.

## Mandatory anti-claims

- an exact, maximal, necessary, or classified selector cone;
- arbitrary finite-support, Hamiltonian, symplectic, or affine-triangular
  generality;
- first use of a matrix, Perron root, gradient shear, or cubic in polynomial
  degree growth;

- irreducibility or minimality of every cubic;
- a global optimal threshold;
- new rank-two or rank-three results;
- genericity, entropy, integrability, non-conjugacy, periodic-point
  classification, or positive-characteristic validity;

- external release, submission, upload, identity, or messaging authority.
