## Assessed theorem boundary

For every rooted primitive selector-pair word of length \(\ell\ge3\),
including repeated labels and a singleton component alphabet when the other
component carries the primitive period, an explicit incidence construction
produces one fixed autonomous polynomial symplectomorphism
\(F_{\mathsf w}\) on \(2(\ell+1)\) affine coordinates.  Its positive-support,
equal-total Hamiltonian shears realize the word as a strict endogenous
Newton-selector cycle.  A rational-polyhedral V-max/W-min normal-fan iff
classifies strict selector feasibility; the exact momentum gate gives a
coefficient-uniform actual polynomial weighted-degree lift; selector and
quotient orbits separately have least period \(\ell\); and marked monodromy
recovers the rooted support-pair word, with literal names requiring the
labelled dictionary.

The quantifier is one map per word.  It is neither one universal map nor one
map per length, and the dimension grows with \(\ell\).

## Exact novelty-bearing conjunction

No primary theorem was located that combines all of:

1. every rooted primitive selector-pair word of length \(\ell\ge3\),
   including period three;
2. one autonomous polynomial symplectomorphism per word;
3. a rational-polyhedral necessary-and-sufficient strict-selector chamber;
4. coefficient-uniform survival of actual polynomial weighted degrees;
5. separate least selector and quotient periods; and
6. exact marked recovery of the rooted support-pair word with declared side
   information.

This is the proper unit of novelty.  Removing any clause produces substantial
prior art.

## Approved and forbidden novelty wording

Approved:

> Weighted-degree methods, polynomial symplectomorphisms, max-plus state
> cycles, switched schedules, tropical cluster recurrences, and ordered
> monodromy products are established individually.  After a bounded search
> through 29 August 2026, we are unaware of a prior theorem combining every-
> word autonomous realization, an exact strict fan criterion,
> coefficient-uniform actual-degree survival, separate least periods, and
> marked support-word decoding.

Forbidden: “the first arbitrary selector cycle,” “first long max-plus
cycle,” “only autonomous realization,” “no previous work,” “search proves
novelty,” “one map realizes every word,” “fixed dimension,” “ordinary total
degree,” “minimal scalar recurrence,” “entropy formula,” “integrability
theorem,” or any decoder wording that omits dictionary and phase assumptions.

## Remaining novelty risks

1. Terminology may conceal a close result under itinerary, active-face,
   branch, sign, or mode language.
2. Meunier is geometrically close and must be cited with preliminary status.
3. Cluster comparisons must contrast structured mutation loops with every
   prescribed primitive word and birational maps with positive-support polynomial
   Hamiltonian shears.
4. The switched-system separation must explicitly distinguish endogenous
   selection from an externally supplied schedule.
5. Algebraic digits recover vectors; literal names require the dictionary,
   and an unmarked phase yields only a cyclic class.
6. Base expansion, rank-one products, monodromy, normal cones, and gradient
   shears are ingredients rather than separate novelty claims.
7. Scalar forcing is constant across words with fixed \((\ell,D,H)\), so no
   novelty claim may rely on scalar trajectory separation.
8. Post-cutoff literature must be reassessed at publication lock.
