## Mandatory abort conditions

Authoring or review must stop without manuscript or build authority if any
of the following occurs:

1. **Literature collision:** the bounded publication-lock refresh finds a
   primary source that materially overlaps a headline clause or defeats the
   qualified six-property positioning.
2. **Scope drift:** a title, quantifier, hypothesis, map order, fan sign,
   carry gate, monodromy order, decoder input/output, period statement,
   recurrence domain, anti-claim, source path, table count, or section order
   differs from this envelope and the passed locks.
3. **Missing proof or boundary:** any theorem-critical derivation,
   counterexample, equality wall, support boundary, or information-loss case
   is absent from the main body, deferred externally, or replaced by a
   citation, fixture, or computation.
4. **Citation failure:** an intended record lacks authoritative metadata,
   has unresolved DOI/status/correction ambiguity, is uncited, is used beyond
   its verified role, or cannot be made compatible with priority-safe
   wording.
5. **Page infeasibility:** the complete proof cannot fit the inclusive
   22--30 rendered content-page interval, references cannot be separated onto
   a fresh page, or compliance would require shrinking, omitting, or moving
   theorem-critical content.
6. **Anonymity leak:** visible text, source comments, bibliography fields,
   PDF metadata, bookmarks, paths, URLs, or provenance reveal identity or any
   forbidden governance datum.
7. **Packaging or portability failure:** the article requires a fourth
   source, external asset, venue-specific dependency, generated input,
   network action, shell escape, or unpinned machine-specific behavior.

An abort preserves the local-only, no-release state.  It does not authorize a
silent repair, alternate theorem, substitute source, extra file, venue
selection, upload, message, or build.
