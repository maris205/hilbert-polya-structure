### Headline Theorem H

For every integer `d >= 2`, parameters must be chosen only in the order

`d -> p,c -> a_1 < ... < a_d -> b,R_0`

with all of the following conditions:

1. `p` is prime and `p` is congruent to `1` modulo `d`;
2. `c` has order exactly `p-1` in the multiplicative group of the finite
   field with `p` elements;
3. the residues of `a_1,...,a_d` are exactly all `d`-th roots of unity;
4. `a_1+1 > 4d`;
5. with `S_a=sum_i a_i` and `M=a_d`,
   `bd congruent 1-(-1)^d c (mod p)`;
6. `R_0=1+2M/(bS_a)`;
7. `b >= 2` and `R_0^2 < 2`; and
8. for every `i=2,...,d`,
   `b(a_i-a_1)S_a > a_i^2 R_0-a_1^2`.

The literal potentials and shears are exactly

\[
V(q)=\prod_{j=1}^{d}q_j^2+\sum_{i=1}^{d}q_i^{a_i+1},
\qquad
W(p)=\prod_{j=1}^{d}p_j^b,
\]

\[
S_V(q,p)=(q,p+\nabla V(q)),\qquad
T_W(q,p)=(q+\nabla W(p),p),\qquad
F=T_W\circ S_V.
\]

The selected matrices are exactly

\[
D=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_d),\qquad
B_0=bJ-I_d,\qquad
C=B_0D=b\mathbf1a^{\mathsf T}-D.
\]

The theorem must preserve all six conjuncts:

1. `F` is a positive integer-coefficient polynomial symplectomorphism;

   \deg(F^n)=e_1^{\mathsf T}C^n\mathbf1;

   all coordinates tie at `n=0`, and `q_1` is the unique degree-maximal
   coordinate only for `n>=1`;

   \chi_C(t)=t^d+\sum_{k=1}^{d}(1-bk)e_k(a_1,\ldots,a_d)t^{d-k}

### Structural Theorem S

Let

\[
\mathsf P\in K^{N\times\alpha},\quad
\mathsf Q\in K^{\alpha\times N},\quad
\mathsf R\in K^{N\times\beta},\quad
\mathsf S\in K^{\beta\times N},
\]

\[
A=-I_N+\mathsf P\mathsf Q,\qquad
B=-I_N+\mathsf R\mathsf S,\qquad C=BA,
\]

and

\[
r=\operatorname{rank}
\begin{bmatrix}\mathsf Q\\\mathsf S\end{bmatrix}.
\]

Choose a full row basis `T_0` of the stacked row space and factor the stacked
matrix through `T_0`. With the resulting explicit matrix `X`, the exact
identity is

\[
\chi_C(t)=(t-1)^{N-r}
\det\bigl((t-1)I_r-T_0X\bigr).
\]

The reduced determinant is monic of degree `r`. The theorem concludes only:

- unit algebraic multiplicity at least `N-r`; and
- nonunit characteristic degree at most `r`.

The reduced determinant may contain additional factors `t-1`. Rank alone
does not determine exact unit multiplicity, the complete spectral profile,
or a unique presentation.

### Corollary C

For the family in Theorem H, use the exact selected presentations

\[
D=-I_d+I_d(D+I_d),\qquad
B_0=-I_d+(b\mathbf1)\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}.
\]

Because `D+I_d` is invertible, the stacked selected row rank is exactly `d`.
Because the degree-`d` characteristic polynomial is irreducible and `d>=2`,
it has no factor `t-1`; its whole degree is nonunit. The structural bound is
therefore attained existentially for every constructed rank `r=d>=2`, not
for every rank presentation.
