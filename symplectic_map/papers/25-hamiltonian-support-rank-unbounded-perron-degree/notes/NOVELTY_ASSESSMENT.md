## Contribution unit

The proposed contribution is the following conjunction:

1. a selected support-row rank upper bound on nonunit characteristic degree;
2. an explicit positive Hamiltonian gradient-compatible family attaining
   the bound for each $d\ge2$;
3. a strict two-level cone and carry proof identifying the literal ordinary
   degree of every iterate;
4. a finite-field construction producing irreducible characteristic
   polynomials of unbounded degree;
5. exact algebraic degree $d$ of the Perron dynamical degree; and
6. exact minimal rational scalar recurrence order $d$ by
   reachability-observability.

None of the six ingredients is advertised as novel in isolation. The paper
stands or falls on the coherent all-$d$ package.

## What is established infrastructure

The following must be cited or proved as standard tools rather than presented
as contribution claims:

- Hamiltonian position and momentum gradient shears;
- the rectangular Sylvester determinant identity and matrix determinant
  lemma;
- common-kernel unit eigenspaces;
- Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions;
- cyclicity of finite-field multiplicative groups;
- the exact finite-field irreducible-binomial criterion;
- Gauss's lemma;
- Perron--Frobenius and Cayley--Hamilton;
- ordinary reachability, observability, and Hankel-rank facts.

The source-design contribution is the way these tools are made compatible
with one literal Hamiltonian support family and an exact ordinary-degree
induction.

The strongest admissible result wording is:

> For every integer $d\ge2$, the construction gives a positive Hamiltonian
> product shear on $2d$ affine coordinates whose ordinary iterate degrees
> are exactly visible through a positive $d\times d$ matrix, whose Perron
> dynamical degree has algebraic degree $d$, and whose scalar degree sequence
> has minimal rational recurrence order $d$. A selected support-row rank
> factorization bounds the nonunit characteristic degree, and the family
> attains that bound for every constructed rank $d\ge2$.

The strongest admissible literature qualifier is:

> No direct collision with the complete support-rank, sharp positive
> Hamiltonian, exact-visibility, and scalar-minimality conjunction was found
> in the bounded primary-source screen through 2026-08-26 UTC.

## Forbidden novelty wording

The project must not use first, only, unprecedented, unique, definitive, or
absolute-priority language. It must not claim that:

- arbitrary signs, supports, exponents, coefficients, or shear words work;
- every weak Perron number is realized;
- the dimension or support is minimal;
- inverse or higher dynamical degrees are controlled;
- entropy, compactification, or integrability follows;
- selector periodicity or automata are classified;
- the family is generic or classifies polynomial symplectomorphisms;
- nonconjugacy is proved;
- the theorem holds in positive characteristic;
- the general rank factor has exact unit multiplicity;
- $d=5$ alone distinguishes the paper.
