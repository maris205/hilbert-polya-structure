### L1--L2: structural factorization and rank boundaries

For abstract ambient dimension N,

A = -I_N + P Q,  B = -I_N + R S,  and C = BA

give directly

C - I_N = (R S P - P)Q - R S
          = [R S P - P, -R] [Q; S].

Let Y = [Q;S] have rank r, choose a full row basis T_0, and write Y = L T_0.
With U = [R S P - P,-R] and X = U L, one obtains
C = I_N + X T_0. Applying the rectangular Sylvester determinant identity
with the scalar t-1 and then using polynomial identity, rather than division
at t=1, gives

chi_C(t) = (t-1)^(N-r) det((t-1)I_r - T_0 X).

The reduced determinant is monic of degree r. Also
ker(T_0) = ker(Q) intersection ker(S) is fixed pointwise by C. This proves
unit algebraic multiplicity at least N-r and nonunit characteristic degree
at most r, not equality of unit multiplicity. At r=0, Y=0, C=I_N, and the
empty determinant is one. At r=N, the forced exponent is zero. Additional
t-1 factors in the reduced determinant remain allowed.

### L3: noncircular parameters for every d

For each d at least 2, Dirichlet supplies a prime p congruent to 1 modulo d.
The multiplicative group of the finite field is cyclic, so c can be chosen
with exact order p-1. The d roots of unity are distinct because d divides
p-1. Sorting positive representatives and adding one common multiple of p
preserves their residues and strict order while making a_1+1 greater than
4d.

Because d is invertible modulo p, the congruence
