## Exact claim boundaries

### BvS and SS

These records establish that affine-triangular automorphisms have a developed
dynamical-degree and realization literature. They justify positioning this
paper as a structured Hamiltonian construction rather than a general Perron
realization theorem. They do not justify saying that no related realization
exists elsewhere.

### DF

This record may motivate the spectral interpretation of dynamical degrees.
The paper's equality $\lambda_1(F)=\rho(C)$ is not delegated to DF; it follows
from the exact visible degree sequence and Perron--Frobenius.

### BT and KL

These records may establish that Hamiltonian shear maps are established
objects. The project must prove polynomial invertibility and preservation of
the standard symplectic form directly for its displayed gradients.

### Des and AX

These records locate the construction among broader degree-growth and current
algebraicity examples. They are object-distinct neighbors, not evidence for
priority or exhaustive absence.

### HS

This record is the closest frozen source for the finite-field binomial
criterion. The article must state the criterion precisely, verify
$\operatorname{ord}(c)=p-1$, verify the quotient condition, and separately
verify $p\equiv1\pmod4$ when $4\mid d$. A citation cannot replace those
checks.
