\begin{figure*}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.96\textwidth]{figures/fig1_carrier_obstruction.pdf}
  \caption{A finite branch-history carrier can pass exact symplectic checks
  while failing the frozen arithmetic clock gate. (a) The three-state Markov
  factor has four directed edges; solid blue and dashed orange edges record
  the inherited one-dimensional factor-branch signs, not symplectic
  orientation. (b) Perron--Frobenius areas \(1/4,1/4,1/2\) tile a labeled
  disjoint union of three rectangles. On every branch interior,
  \(J_{ij}=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_{ij}\sqrt2,
  \sigma_{ij}/\sqrt2)\), hence \(J_{ij}^{\mathsf{T}}\Omega J_{ij}=\Omega\)
  and \(\det J_{ij}=1\). (c) Every primitive period-\(2k\) orbit of this
  unquotiented constant-slope carrier has
  \(|\Lambda_u|=2^k\) and length \(k\log2\). More generally, a finite-memory
  locally constant scalar multiplicative clock has finite rational rank, so
  it cannot contain \(\log p\) for every rational prime. Thus the carrier is
  a structural pass but the candidate fails Route-A A0.}
  \label{fig:carrier-obstruction}
\end{figure*}
