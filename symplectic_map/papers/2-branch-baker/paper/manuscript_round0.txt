Abstract
We prove an exact obstruction for multiplier clocks on finite symbolic models. For one fixed
finite-state system with a finite-memory, locally constant, nonzero scalar multiplicative cocycle,
every periodic instability length lies in a finite-dimensional rational vector space. Since logarithms
of distinct rational primes are rationally linearly independent, such a clock cannot realize all prime
logarithms term by term. We certify the obstruction on a three-state post-critically finite Markov
factor and its compact labeled Markov–baker realization. The carrier is almost everywhere
invertible and exact symplectic on every affine branch, while two independent primitive-cycle
enumerations, an explicit boundary quotient, six matched controls, and an independent 100-digit
parent audit verify the construction. There are 226 primitive symbolic cycles through period
20, but every period- 2k orbit has unstable multiplier modulus 2k and length k log 2; hence the
rational-prime intersection is only log 2, and the multiplier product is (1 − 21−s )−1 . The carrier
therefore passes its structural checks while the arithmetic gate fails; the intrinsic orbit ledger
is formally weak because it has no arithmetic labels. The theorem concerns exact termwise
containment for locally constant scalar clocks, not variable roofs, countable-state models, matrix
spectral radii, or approximate prime resemblance.

Introduction

A finite symbolic model may carry every orbit exactly and still carry too little metric information
to support an arithmetic correspondence. This distinction matters whenever primitive periodic
orbits are compared with the rational primes. A symbolic language supplies primitive objects and
their repetitions; an arithmetic model also needs an intrinsic clock whose primitive lengths can
support the proposed labels. Neither a rational dynamical zeta nor reciprocal symplectic multipliers
establish that second property by themselves.
Arithmetic–spectral analogies motivate exact tests of this separation [Berry and Keating, 1999].
Here the requested clock is the instability length L(C) = log |Λu (C)| of a periodic orbit. The
question is deliberately termwise: can one fixed dynamical model contain log p for every rational
prime p, without inserting a prime table into local weights? This paper does not compare a fitted
spectrum with Riemann zeros. Instead, it identifies an assumption class for which the termwise
question is settled before any target data are opened.
Our main observation is elementary but restrictive. A finite graph with a finite-memory locally
constant scalar cocycle has only finitely many local log-multipliers. All closed-walk lengths therefore

elementary design-class certificate, not a no-go theorem for arbitrary smooth symplectic dynamics
and not a finite-rank assertion about the nonlinear quadratic parent below.

Certified verification and matched controls

6.1

Frozen protocol

the implemented object is the declared one. The candidate, matrices, boundary convention, scales,
controls, stopping rule, and forbidden data were source-locked before execution. Source-lock version
1 contained a mechanical transcription error in the development seed: its number did not equal its
own SHA-256 derivation rule. Version 2 corrected only that number, before exact sanity checks or
any split was opened; validation and test seeds and every scientific choice were unchanged.

manifest was frozen before the test was sampled. No external prime table, Riemann-zero table, target

6.2

Independent parent and floating audits

The parent audit intentionally did not import the exact cycle-ledger generator. It enumerated
closed words locally and recovered parent periodic points through monotone inverse branches at
100 decimal digits. Through period 20 it reproduced the parent counts and exactly the declared
boundary duplicate. Its maximum periodic residual was 9.706 × 10−98 < 10−75 , and the largest
inverse solve took 293 iterations. This is an independent high-precision consistency audit, not an
interval-arithmetic certificate.
Each floating split sampled 65,536 interior points and performed 256 steps. At every step, the
executed forward branch was immediately composed with its identified inverse before the state
advanced. Thus each split completed 65,536 × 256 = 16,777,216 per-step checks. The maximum
roundtrip error was 1.3877787807814457 × 10−16 in all three splits, below the frozen 2 × 10−13
threshold; every split had zero edge mismatch and zero boundary failure. This is deterministic
software stress, not 16,777,216 independent observations and not a claim of reversing one 256-step
chaotic trajectory. Consequently no p-value, confidence interval, effect size, or binomial success
statement is appropriate.

6.3

Matched controls

The controls alter one structural feature at a time. A dyadic baker is the positive enumeration/inverse
control and has exactly 747 primitive binary necklaces through period 12. A folded-tent baker
verifies that decreasing branches reverse stable and unstable coordinates together while retaining
determinant +1. A matched dissipative map keeps the future graph but uses stable factor ρ = 1/2;
all its branch determinants are 1/2, and its image is not onto. Erasing rectangle labels merges
distinct pasts and breaks unique reconstruction. A single-coordinate reversal produces determinant
−1 on the decreasing edges and is rejected. Finally, replacing all factor signs by +1 preserves the
unsigned carrier while changing W to A and removing the nilpotent cancellation. Together these
controls separate symbolic coding, invertibility, symplecticity, and sign convention.

Discussion and conclusion

The case study separates two questions that are easy to conflate. The three-rectangle carrier
is compact, almost everywhere invertible, and exact symplectic on branch interiors; its intrinsic
symbolic and parent ledgers are fully auditable. Yet its frozen multiplier clock has rank one. It
therefore cannot provide an exact termwise all-prime ledger, regardless of further sampling or
determinant manipulation.
The formal assessment is
PRE_A0_STRUCTURAL_PASS,

A0_FAIL / STRUCTURAL_ONLY,

A1_WEAK.

Here A1_WEAK means that the intrinsic orbit ledger is exact but contains no A0 arithmetic labels; it is
not an A1_PASS. Stages A2–A4 are STOP_SCOPED, Route B is FORBIDDEN, and the overall candidate
decision is ROUTE_A_REJECTED. The model remains useful as a structural positive control and an
arithmetic negative control. Classical baker quantizations [Balazs and Voros, 1989, Saraceno, 1990]
do not change this gate: generic quantizability supplies neither missing arithmetic origin nor a
canonical quantization of this clock.

11

The obstruction is exact but narrow. It does not cover point-dependent or Hölder roofs, countablestate or infinite-memory systems, growing sequences of finite models, nonmultiplicative matrix
spectral radii or singular values, approximate/density matching, or signed analytic cancellation
under additional convergence hypotheses. It also does not apply to the nonlinear parent derivative;
indeed, the infinite-rank theorem for characteristic exponents of non-exceptional rational maps
[Ji et al., 2026] shows why finite coding and finite derivative rank must not be identified. Any
independently motivated variable-roof, smoothed, countable-state, coupled, or higher-dimensional
construction is a new candidate requiring a new source lock and an arithmetic-origin audit, not a
post-hoc modification of this one.
The parent determinant, monotonicity-boundary discrepancy, generalized-baker platform, naturalextension strategy, and generic baker quantization all have direct prior context; the contribution
here is the elementary finite-locally-constant design certificate and its convention-safe certified
worked example. A valid carrier answers a geometric question; only an intrinsic clock can answer
the arithmetic one.
