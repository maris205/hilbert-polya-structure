## Batch-04 Reset Boundary

Batch 03 remains a preserved candidate-audit failure and does not consume the
Paper-12 identifier.  Batch 04 does not reopen its exhausted flagship routes by
renaming them.  In particular, the following are excluded as Paper-12 headline
claims unless a materially new theorem changes their status:

- further finite shell, prime-torsion, centralizer, quotient, or equivariant
  repackaging of the standard cat map;
- a diagonal reciprocal-prime operator or Euler determinant whose prime labels
  were inserted by construction;
- the standard projective fixed-line Artin/Hasse--Weil factor, or any argument
  that confuses Frobenius extension degree with iteration time;
- finite-period, finite-prime, or finite-modulus ledgers promoted to universal
  theorems;
- target fitting, shell normalization, selector rules, modulus-dependent local
  symmetries, or a numerator/augmentation that works for every permutation;
- the Batch-03 Fredholm tail-rigidity package as a flagship without a genuinely
  new stability, sampling, or structural theorem.

The search axis is reset toward intrinsic and generator-sensitive arithmetic
dynamics, stable inverse spectral questions with explicit priors, algebraic
height or finiteness theorems for non-exceptional maps, and other mechanisms
whose arithmetic content is not supplied by the desired Euler target.

## Paper-12 Entry Gate

A Paper-12 directory may be created only after one candidate simultaneously
satisfies all of the following:

1. a precise standalone theorem or falsifiable obstruction with a
   degree-independent proof strategy;
2. an independent primary-source novelty assessment of at least `5/10`, with
   no direct collision that reduces the result to a standard formalism;
3. a clear answer to why the arithmetic structure is intrinsic rather than
   injected through labels, spectral entries, or the target identity;
4. a bounded proof/counterexample pilot that can succeed or fail without new
   prime tables, Riemann-zero data, or exploratory parameter scans;
5. a credible paper-sized package rather than a one-line lemma, finite ledger,
   or open conjecture with no closing mechanism;
6. an explicit escape and nonclaim taxonomy consistent with the accumulated
   Papers 2--11 obstruction registry.

If no candidate clears every item, Batch 04 pauses again before Paper 12.  A
lower standard is not used merely to fill the five-paper queue.

  filtering, deep novelty checks, independent critical review, and only then a
  claim-driven plan.
- No source lock is created before the Paper-12 entry gate passes.
- No implementation begins before a fresh independent source-lock review
  returns PASS.
- No registered execution begins before a fresh independent deployment review
  returns PASS.  Registered execution is one-shot with durable
  claim/result/terminal evidence.
- Every result package requires independent integrity review and strict
  read-only final-manifest closure.
- Every manuscript requires independently reviewed figures and citations, two
  manuscript review rounds, final integrity closure, and byte-identical final
  PDF promotion.
- Failed review history is immutable.  Analyzer repairs use a separately
  reviewed tree when changing the execution tree would invalidate completed
  evidence.
- No later paper opens while an earlier paper has an unresolved integrity or
  manuscript-state blocker.
