### Singleton alphabets and margins

## Independent proof audit

### Symplecticity, inverse, and dimension

For
\[
S_V(q,p)=(q,p+\nabla V(q)),\qquad
T_W(q,p)=(q+\nabla W(p),p),
\]
the off-diagonal Jacobian blocks are Hessians and hence symmetric. Each shear
preserves \(\sum_i dq_i\wedge dp_i\); the simultaneous permutation
\((q,p)\mapsto(Pq,Pp)\) also preserves it. Reversing the permutation and
subtracting the gradients in reverse composition order gives a polynomial
inverse. The word construction has \(r=\ell+1\) symplectic coordinate pairs,
so the ambient affine dimension is exactly \(2(\ell+1)\), not \(\ell+1\).

### Selected matrices and normal-fan iff

Literal differentiation gives
\[
A_\alpha=\mathbf1\alpha^{\mathsf T}-I,\qquad
B_\beta=\mathbf1\beta^{\mathsf T}-I.
\]
Multiplication gives
\[
B_\beta A_\alpha
=I+\mathbf1c_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathsf T},\qquad
c_{\alpha,\beta}=(B-1)\alpha-\beta,
\]
and the post-permutation q-degree step is
\[
C_{\alpha,\beta}=P+\mathbf1c_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Equal totals yield
\[
c_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf1
=(A-1)(B-1)-1=\lambda-1.
\]

Writing \(u_n=x_n+t_n\mathbf1\), \(x_n=P^nu_0\), makes V score differences
independent of \(t_n\). After selecting \(\alpha_n\),
\[
A_{\alpha_n}u_n
=\bigl(\alpha_n^{\mathsf T}x_n+(A-1)t_n\bigr)\mathbf1-x_n.
\]
For equal-total \(\beta,\eta\), their score difference on this vector is
\(-(\beta-\eta)^{\mathsf T}x_n\). Hence V is the strict maximizer fan and W
is the strict minimizer fan. Intersecting their pulled-back cones over all
phases is both necessary and sufficient, and a nonempty rational open cone
contains a positive integer seed after scaling.

### Incidence supports, equitotality, and selector table

With \(r=\ell+1\), spike seed
\(u_0=\mathbf1+(H-1)e_0\), \(H\ge2\), and baseline \(b_0\ge2\), put
\[
S_a=\{j:a_j=a\},\qquad T_{\mathfrak b}=\{j:b_j=\mathfrak b\}.
\]
The supports are
\[
\alpha_a=b_0\mathbf1+
K\sum_{j\in S_a}e_j+K(\ell-|S_a|)e_\star,
\]
\[
\beta_{\mathfrak b}=b_0\mathbf1+
K\sum_{j\notin T_{\mathfrak b}}e_j+K|T_{\mathfrak b}|e_\star.
\]
The moving-coordinate incidence and complementary star term sum to
\(K\ell\) in every exponent, even when label multiplicities are unequal.
Thus all exponents have total
\[
D=b_0r+K\ell,\qquad \lambda=(D-1)^2.
\]

At \(x_j=P^ju_0\), with
\[
C_0=b_0(H+\ell)+K\ell,\qquad g=K(H-1),
\]
the selected V score is \(C_0+g\), each existing V competitor scores
\(C_0\), the selected W score is \(C_0\), and each existing W competitor
scores \(C_0+g\). This proves arbitrary repeated-label words, both singleton
conventions, and nonemptiness of the explicit selector chamber.

### Exact carries and leading survival

For \(r\ge2\), \(\alpha_i\ge2\), and \(u>0\),
\[
(A_\alpha u)_i-u_i
=(\alpha_i-2)u_i+\sum_{k\ne i}\alpha_k u_k>0.
\]
The identical argument applies to \(B_\beta\). The exact initial strict
source carry is \(0<w_0<A_{\alpha_{a_0}}u_0\). It yields
\(u_1>w_1\), and induction gives
\[
A_{\alpha_{a_n}}u_n>u_n>w_n,\qquad
B_{\beta_{b_n}}A_{\alpha_{a_n}}u_n>A_{\alpha_{a_n}}u_n.
\]
Permutation preserves the coordinatewise inequalities. This checks every
source and target carry and explains exactly why \(r=1\) is excluded.

Every selected exponent is unique. Since all exponent coordinates are
positive and the field has characteristic zero, every selected derivative
coefficient is nonzero. Products of nonzero leading forms remain nonzero in
the polynomial domain, while the carried source terms have strictly smaller
weight. Consequently the formal selected matrices are the actual polynomial
weighted-degree matrices for every nonzero coefficient tuple; there is no
generic-coefficient, sign, or hidden cancellation proviso.

### Scalar forcing and ordered monodromy

The exact q-degree recurrence is
\[
u_{n+1}=Pu_n+\mathbf1c_n^{\mathsf T}u_n.
\]
The incidence score table makes
\[
t_{n+1}=\lambda t_n+\mu,\qquad
\mu=(D-1)(C_0+g)-C_0>0.
\]
This constant forcing does not encode the word, and the candidate correctly
does not claim otherwise.

For \(C_j=P+\mathbf1c_j^{\mathsf T}\) and
\(Q_s=C_{s-1}\cdots C_0\), direct multiplication gives
\[
Q_s=P^s+\mathbf1R_s^{\mathsf T},\qquad
R_{s+1}^{\mathsf T}=c_s^{\mathsf T}P^s+\lambda R_s^{\mathsf T},
\]
so
\[
R_s^{\mathsf T}
=\sum_{j=0}^{s-1}\lambda^{s-1-j}c_j^{\mathsf T}P^j.
\]
Because \(P^\ell=I\),
\[
M_{\mathsf w}=Q_\ell=I+\mathbf1R_\ell^{\mathsf T},\qquad
R_\ell^{\mathsf T}\mathbf1=\lambda^\ell-1.
\]
The rank-one determinant lemma then gives
\[
\chi_{M_{\mathsf w}}(z)
=(z-1)^{r-1}(z-\lambda^\ell),
\]
and direct rank-one powering gives
\[
M_{\mathsf w}^k
=I+\frac{\lambda^{k\ell}-1}{\lambda^\ell-1}
\mathbf1R_\ell^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Multiplying by each prefix \(Q_s\) reproduces the candidate's exact
phase-resolved formula, including the factor \(\lambda^s\). No commutation or
asymptotic spectral substitution is used.

### Digit bounds and decoder

Every coordinate of every pair digit
\[
c_{a,\mathfrak b}=(D-1)\alpha_a-\beta_{\mathfrak b}
\]
satisfies \(0<c_i<\lambda\). The lower estimate
\((D-1)b_0-(b_0+K\ell)>0\) is strict, and
\(b_0+K\ell<D-1\) gives the strict upper estimate.

If
\[
(D-1)(\alpha_a-\alpha_{a'})
=\beta_{\mathfrak b}-\beta_{\mathfrak b'},
\]
a moving coordinate in \(S_a\triangle S_{a'}\), when \(a\ne a'\), has
left magnitude \(K(D-1)\) and right magnitude at most \(K\), a contradiction.
Then equality of \(\alpha\)'s forces equality of \(\beta\)'s. This argument
also covers either singleton alphabet.

Thus each coordinate of
\[
R_\ell^{\mathsf T}
=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}\lambda^{\ell-1-j}
c_j^{\mathsf T}P^j
\]
is an ordinary carry-free base-\(\lambda\) integer. From
\(M_{\mathsf w}-I=\mathbf1R_\ell^{\mathsf T}\), the marked matrix and known
\(P,\lambda,\ell\) recover the rotated digit vectors, hence the ordered
\(c_j\)'s. The injective labelled dictionary then recovers the literal
label-pair word. The V2 side-information and unmarked-phase limitations are
necessary and are stated exactly.

### Least periods and scalar indices

Modulo \(L=\mathbb R\mathbf1\), every selected step is \([u]\mapsto[Pu]\).
For \(0<d<\ell\), an equality
\(P^du_0-u_0=c\mathbf1\) forces \(c=0\) from the fixed star coordinate, while
the moving spike rules out \(P^du_0=u_0\). Hence the quotient orbit has least
period \(\ell\). The selected pair word has least period \(\ell\) by its
primitive hypothesis. These are separate statements, both proved.

With \(q_n=\max_i(u_n)_i=H+t_n\),
\[
q_{n+2}-(1+\lambda)q_{n+1}+\lambda q_n=0\qquad(n\ge0),
\]
and therefore \((E^\ell-1)(E-\lambda)q=0\) from \(n=0\).
The first-carry induction gives \(u_n>w_n\) for \(n\ge1\), so the
complete-state maximum \(d_n=q_n\) exactly for \(n\ge1\). Its longer
recurrence therefore starts at \(n=1\). At \(n=0\) the residual is exactly
\[
\lambda(d_0-q_0),
\]
so the same instance holds if and only if
\(\max_i(w_0)_i\le\max_i(u_0)_i\). The canonical seed meets that optional
condition; the actual-degree theorem does not silently require it.
