## Known content and precise increment

Gorbovickis already supplies arbitrary-period multiplier independence at
one common scalar polynomial base point. Sterling–Meiss already provide
all-symbolic-sequence continuation in one uniform anti-integrable region.
Cantat–Dujardin's complete-spectrum rigidity does not select arbitrary
prescribed periods, but is a strong adjacent result. Periodic multiplier
cocycles, normalized logarithmic differentials and the incidence descent
are established frameworks, the last of which also overlaps Paper18.

The actual increment is the combination of a period-uniform trace
differential estimate with the primitive-word/root-permutation determinant
certificate. The common-region theorem is genuinely stronger than a
separate finite-period implicit-function argument. Neither fact justifies
counting each supporting standard lemma as an additional innovation.
