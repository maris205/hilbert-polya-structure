# Internal arithmetic package: frozen common review-input manifest V2

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: FROZEN_COMMON_INPUT_IDENTITY_ONLY_V2.

This manifest defines the identical scientific input for two new, mutually blind
candidate reviews of B00. It does not contain a candidate assessment, author
page allocation or outline diagnostic. It is not a manuscript source/publication
lock or permission to write, measure, compile, submit or publish a paper.

## Reading rules and exact scope

Read B00 and C02 first in full, then the actual necessary proofs and their
mathematical checks selected by C02. The 96 named files comprise the two current
documents, 24 original author sources, nine replacement author sources,
18 historical and nine new mathematical reports, three conditional interfaces,
12 historical and three replacement acceptance records, five unchanged context
files, and 11 correction/historical-locator files. A listed file is not thereby
a separate theorem or an additional necessary body-proof block.

The allowed-line column is an **access ceiling**, not a demand to count or read
every superseded evaluation as current core. All genuinely required proof
paragraphs, definitions, finite boundaries and error arguments selected by C02
must be read. Read the corresponding allowed mathematical report portions and
the actual condition/correction boundaries, not just reported PASS labels.
Historical R04/R15 and superseded author calculations are available for
provenance or a specific dependency question; they are not additional core
obligations. All needed current mathematical paragraphs remain available.

Every line count and hash identifies the original complete file, even when only
specified excerpts may be read. A full-file byte hash does not authorize
displaying its excluded lines. Source line counts are not estimates of English
body length. Interval endpoints are inclusive. No underlying file has been
redacted or rewritten for these reviews.

Read only the allowed intervals. Do not open README, BATCH_07_CONTEXT, author
capacity/value preflights, scope dispositions, nonblind outline reports, old
formal candidate evaluations, the other new review, or communications describing
their estimates or scores. This is a bounded independent review, not the primary
agent's batch-continuation task. An outgoing link or historical instruction
inside an allowed source does not expand this manifest.

The few gaps in F2, R, N, RR, DPLUS and F1 are administrative sentences about
prior candidate evaluations. DPOS/DBLOCK/DADJ are restricted to mathematical
selection, version binding, accepted readings and condition closures. Those gaps
do not remove mathematical proofs, actual mathematical failures, corrections or
conditional statements. The unchanged 22–30-page format/threshold contract is
fully stated in B00.

C03/C04 remain complete: their nonnumeric provisional novelty labels, strongest
prior-art deductions, stand-alone significance risks, source-access/OCR/version
limits and GLOBAL_NOVELTY_UNCERTAIN are scientific evidence, not old formal
candidate votes. They must not be hidden, neutralized or converted into numerical
scores. The reviewers may disagree after examining that evidence.

XBRIEF and XMAP are historical provenance, not a second scientific target or a
current procedural instruction. Their old proof-selection requirements yield to
C02 only where an exact replacement is identified. Their presence preserves
cross-references; reading them does not require restoring superseded proofs.
F1/O1 preserve the exact inputs actually audited by RF/RO; corrected F2/O2 are
connected through the allowed DPOS correction section. RO's literal-format
failures and all original mathematical-failure boundaries remain visible.

The two reviewers own disjoint output files and must not communicate with or
read one another. Each gives its own complete four-gate decision and natural
low/central/high assessment under B00. No prior assessment is inherited, averaged
or combined. Progress updates may report read scope and factual questions, not
provisional scores or estimates.

A necessary dependency missing from this input must be reported to the primary
agent. Any substantive factual clarification is supplied identically to both
reviewers and recorded with the fixed input. A targeted primary-source check
to resolve a concrete scientific uncertainty is permitted, but is disclosed as
an additional reviewer check, not silently added to this frozen common manifest.
No broad filesystem or historical-ledger search is authorized by this input.
