## Two-route assessment

Route I uses a fixed-point subgroup, left cosets, and a regular centralizer.
Route II uses an explicit nonlinear matrix equation and a triangular family
of additive difference maps. They independently count the finite-field
multiplicity, not the already-owned existence theorem. Subsequent layer
formulas deliberately use the one-step count once rather than pretending to
have two independent temporal derivations.
