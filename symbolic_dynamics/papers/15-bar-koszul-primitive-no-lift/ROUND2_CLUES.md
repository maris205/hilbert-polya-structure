geometrically derived phase rather than an ordinary scalar sign.  Realizing
such a line would require a geometric return map or flow and therefore lies
outside Session 4.

specified Hilbert space, closed domain, self-adjointness, and exact trace
formula.  SD-C17 supplies none of these.  Route B remains locked.
