# LITERATURE AUDIT — Paper 15

## Search boundary

The audit was intentionally narrow.  It covered the classical bar/Koszul
comparison, algebraic and equivariant discrete Morse theory, Hochschild and
Harrison decompositions, André--Quillen smoothness, necklace algebras, and
symbolic zeta determinants.  It did not search for a general Hilbert--Pólya
model or leave Symbolic Dynamics.

Publisher or DOI metadata was checked for every cited item for which a DOI is
available.  The bibliography contains only works cited by the manuscript.

## Classical facts used without novelty claim

1. **Bar-to-Koszul reduction.**  Koszul resolutions and their relation to bar
   constructions are classical.  Algebraic discrete Morse theory provides
   systematic chain reductions, and equivariant Morse theory states the
   additional symmetry obligations.  The paper relies on Priddy, Sköldberg,
   Jöllenbeck--Welker, and Freij for this boundary.
2. **Mixed homology survives.**  For a polynomial algebra,
   \(\operatorname{Tor}(\Bbbk,\Bbbk)\) is exterior.  HKR identifies
   Hochschild homology of a smooth polynomial algebra with differential
   forms.  Neither statement leaves only degree-one atom classes.
3. **Harrison/AQ is a different component.**  Barr, Quillen,
   Gerstenhaber--Schack, and Loday explain how commutative indecomposable
   information is isolated by a quotient or Hodge component.  That operation
   is not an equivalence preserving all Hochschild/bar cyclic recurrence.
4. **Necklace enumeration is classical.**  Primitive-word and necklace
   algebras long predate this project.  The manuscript proves its small
   enumerations directly and cites Metropolis--Rota only for context.
5. **The scalar determinant is classical algebra.**  Inclusion--exclusion and
   the weighted one-vertex determinant are not claimed as new.

## Bounded project contribution

The paper contributes a source-locked obstruction package for the specific
SD-C17 symbolic candidate:

- the \(p^2q^2\) proof that scalar cancellation crosses primitive and power
  layers;
- the \(pqr\) fixed-character certificate
  \(\mathbf1+\mathrm{sgn}-\mathrm{Std}\);
- the explicit scalar-sign/supertrace firewall at even powers;
- the combined no-lift theorem under content, cyclic, power, and
  atom-permutation naturality.

The audit did not locate this exact combined certificate in the classical
sources, but the search is not exhaustive.  Accordingly the manuscript makes
no "first" or priority claim.  Its novelty statement is deliberately limited
to the analysis of SD-C17 under the frozen Route-A obligations.

## Positioning consequence

The manuscript should be read as a structural negative result with one exact
positive determinant theorem.  It does not present a new Koszul resolution,
new HKR theorem, new necklace formula, new Euler product, or RH mechanism.
