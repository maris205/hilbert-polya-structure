# FIGURE SPECIFICATION

## Figure 1 — Scalar determinant versus primitive/homological lifts

The pure-TikZ figure in `figures/no_lift_map.tex` has three horizontal levels.

1. **Source level:** tensor atoms, the polynomial/Koszul subset inventory, and
   the one-vertex scalar edge shift.
2. **Valid output:** the exact determinant
   \(D_A=\prod_a(1-x_a)\), including the countable
   \(1/\zeta(s)\) specialization in \(\operatorname{Re}s>1\).
3. **Blocked promotions:**
   \(p^2q^2\) primitive/power mismatch, the \(S_3\) virtual-character
   residual, and scalar sign versus supertrace parity.

Solid arrows denote proved constructions.  Dashed red arrows denote invalid
promotions.  A final box states the exact scope: scalar determinant yes;
primitive-natural atom selector no; Route B locked.

The figure uses no raster asset, no decorative title, and remains legible in
grayscale because line style and labels carry the distinction.
