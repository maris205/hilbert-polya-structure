  with `S_q`; there are exactly two continuations.
- The two generator loops have coprime lengths `q-1` and `q`, so strong
  connectivity implies aperiodicity and mixing.
- For periods at most `q-2`, a forbidden gap is zero modulo the period. At
  `q-1` and `q`, the constraint graph is complete and has exactly `q!`
  colorings. For `q+1 <= n <= 2q-3`, it is again complete but needs more than
  `q` colors.
- Conditioning on `x_0=c`, the first possible return is at `q-1`. Thereafter
  exactly one fair label returns `c`, giving `(q-2)+Geom(1/2)`.
- Hence `f(z)=z^(q-1)/(2-z)` and
  `u(z)=1/(1-f(z))=(2-z)/(2-z-z^(q-1))`; stationarity gives the covariance
  formula.

left Cayley convention, whereas P95 fixes a right action. The text now calls

registered count is **5,031 exact assertions**, plus **99,058 literal cyclic
words**. The control and four-stage build pass without warnings.
