## 3. Closest collisions

### 3.1 Caravelli--Delvenne 2026

Francesco Caravelli and Jean-Charles Delvenne, “Analog and symbolic
computation through the Koopman framework,” *Journal of Physics: Complexity*
7 (2026), 025012, DOI `10.1088/2632-072X/ae6f5f`.

This is the closest conceptual collision. It connects symbolic/Cantor-space
reachability and halting to Koopman resolvents, absorbing states, invariant
observables, and cyclic spectral modes. It prevents any claim that Paper 34
first links recognition, recurrence, and operators. It does not prove the
positive first-return/SCC, finite-code clock, free-marker, and natural
whole-adjacency conjunction studied here.

### 3.2 de Jong 2026

Huub de Jong, “On sets of periodic orbit lengths in finitely presented
dynamical systems,” *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* (2026), 1--33,
DOI `10.1017/etds.2026.10313`.

This paper classifies period sets under rational logarithmic derivatives and
least-period sets for finitely presented systems, including realization
results. It blocks any general “new period obstruction” claim. Its compact
finitely presented setting does not supply the terminal-ordering or natural
countable whole-operator theorem of Paper 34.

### 3.3 Computation and period realization

- Kůrka, “On topological dynamics of Turing machines,” *TCS* 174 (1997),
  DOI `10.1016/S0304-3975(96)00025-4`, realizes Turing dynamics through
  generalized shifts.
- Delvenne, Kůrka, and Blondel, “Decidability and Universality in Symbolic
  Dynamical Systems,” *Fundamenta Informaticae* 74 (2006), 463--490,
  publisher-reported DOI `10.3233/FUN-2006-74406`, develops universality and
  model checking for symbolic systems. The DOI resolver returned 404 on the
  audit date; the official SAGE record and primary arXiv version are used.
- Jeandel and Vanier, “Characterizations of periods of multi-dimensional
  shifts,” *ETDS* 35 (2015), DOI `10.1017/etds.2013.60`, gives complexity
  characterizations for selected multidimensional period notions.
- Doering and Pavlov, “A characterization of the sets of periods within
  shifts of finite type,” *Involve* 12 (2019), DOI
  `10.2140/involve.2019.12.203`, classifies least-period sets for finite SFTs.

These works establish that computation and period support are already deeply
connected. Their period notions and compactness assumptions must not be
silently transferred to the countable positive compiler.

### 3.4 Loop and renewal flexibility

Boyle, Buzzi, and Gómez, “Almost isomorphism for countable state Markov
shifts,” *J. Reine Angew. Math.* 592 (2006), DOI
`10.1515/CRELLE.2006.021`, uses loop graphs with prescribed first-return
series and zeta `1/(1-f)`. Sarig, “Zeta Functions for the Renewal Shift,”
RIMS Kôkyûroku 1404 (2004), shows that complex local weights on a one-sided
renewal shift can realize an arbitrary normalized holomorphic germ.

These are decisive counterexamples to broad countable-zeta rigidity. Paper
34 therefore requires positivity, source locking, and literal primitive
semantics. Shared first-return loops producing mixed words is a classical code
consequence, not a standalone novelty claim.

### 3.5 Coding and operator ownership

- McMillan, “Two inequalities implied by unique decipherability,” *IRE
  Transactions on Information Theory* 2 (1956), DOI
  `10.1109/TIT.1956.1056818`, supplies the coding-capacity baseline.
- Béal and Perrin, “Codes and sofic constraints,” *TCS* 340 (2005), DOI
  `10.1016/j.tcs.2005.03.033`, extends Kraft-type coding to sofic constraints.
- Simon, *Trace Ideals and Their Applications*, second edition, AMS (2005),
  DOI `10.1090/surv/120`, is the ordinary trace-ideal/Fredholm reference.
- Deitmar, “A determinant formula for infinite graphs,” *SIAM J. Discrete
  Math.* 29 (2015), DOI `10.1137/140957925`, is an alternative infinite-graph
  determinant framework and prevents treating the natural vertex adjacency as
  the only legitimate operator object.
- Parry and Sullivan, “A topological invariant of flows on 1-dimensional
  spaces,” *Topology* 14 (1975), DOI `10.1016/0040-9383(75)90012-9`, supplies
  the state-expansion/time-change lineage.

Kraft alone does not imply noncompactness. Paper 34 additionally freezes
orbit separation, polynomial atom growth, total log clock, positive edge
weights, private recurrent cycles, and the natural vertex space.
