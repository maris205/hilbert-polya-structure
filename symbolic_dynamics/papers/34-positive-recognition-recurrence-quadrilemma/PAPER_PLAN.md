**Working title:** Recognition Before Recurrence: A Positive Compiler
Quadrilemma for Arithmetic Markov Shifts

**Type:** theory plus exact adversarial audit

**Format:** anonymous A4 research paper; modular LaTeX; full proofs in the
appendix; no manuscript review loop.

## Claims-evidence matrix

| Claim | Formal evidence | Exact evidence | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Literal atom recurrence forces private simple cycles | word-root and multiplicative-freeness lemmas | exhaustive shared-state census | 3 |

## Structure

1. Abstract: state the quadrilemma, strongest quantitative bound, exact audit,
   and negative route result.
2. Introduction: explain why recognition must own recurrence; cite the two
   2026 closest collisions on page one; state narrow contribution.
3. Literature and source boundary: computation, period realization, renewal,
   coding, and operator ownership.
4. Frozen compiler class: phase space, atom norms, visible code, roof, literal
   ledger, original marker, and whole operator.
5. Recurrent rigidity: simple cycles, shared SCC mixed root, hypothesis repair.
6. Pruning and compiler ownership: conditional expectation, power traces,
   determinant, terminal controls.
7. Finite-code clock obstruction: word count, logarithmic subsequence,
   noncompactness.
8. Marker firewall and sharp boundaries: first return, signed/matrix controls.
9. Exact audit: canonical counts, source separation, double-run, integrity.
10. Route closure and conclusion: strict tuple, limitations, Paper 35 gates.
11. Appendices: complete proofs, experiment semantics, scope declarations.
