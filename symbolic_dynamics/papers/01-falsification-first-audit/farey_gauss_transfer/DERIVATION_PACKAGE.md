# Derivation Package: Gauss Words and Mayer Determinants

## 1. Problem Setup and Target Quantity

Let

\[
A_n=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&n\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \phi_n(z)=\frac1{n+z}.
\]

The experiment stores instead

\[
\widehat A_n=\begin{pmatrix}n&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

With \(J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), one has
\(A_n=J\widehat A_nJ\), and therefore the ordered word products are conjugate
by \(J\).  Trace, determinant, eigenvalues, roof, cyclic invariance, and
repetition tests are identical in the two conventions.

For a word \(w=(n_1,\ldots,n_m)\), use the ordered product \(M_w\) associated
with \(\phi_{n_1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n_m}\).  The target is the same-object
chain

\[
\text{word}\longrightarrow M_w\longrightarrow T_w
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal L_s^m
\longrightarrow D_{\rm MG}(s).
\]

## 2. Assumptions and Modeling Choices

1. Digits range over all positive integers; digit cutoffs are numerical
   approximations only.
2. The roof is derived from the branch derivative/eigenvalue and is not
   replaced by \(\log p\).
3. The function space and nuclearity domain are those in Mayer's primary
   construction; no stronger continuation claim is inferred from a finite
   matrix truncation.
4. The \(+\) and \(-\) sectors are fixed by the transfer-operator
   factorization, not selected from a zero comparison.

## 3. Notation

Let \(x_w\) be the attracting fixed point of \(\phi_w\).  Let
\(\lambda_+(M_w)>1\) denote the expanding eigenvalue in the hyperbolic
normalization.  A word is primitive if it is not a proper power; cyclic
rotations describe the same periodic shift orbit.

## 4. Derivation

The chain rule gives

\[
|(\phi_w)'(x_w)|=
\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}|(\phi_{n_{j+1}})'(x_j)|.
\]

For the Möbius matrix \(M_w\), the fixed-point eigenvector relation identifies
the denominator multiplier with \(\lambda_+(M_w)\), hence

\[
-\log|(\phi_w)'(x_w)|
=2\log\lambda_+(M_w)=T_w.
\]

If \(w=v^r\), then \(M_w=M_v^r\) and

\[
T_w=rT_v,
\]

so the symbolic and matrix repetition ledgers agree.

The transfer operator iterates over digit words:

\[
(\mathcal L_s^mf)(z)=
\sum_{|w|=m}|(\phi_w)'(z)|^s f(\phi_w(z)),
\]

with the holomorphic branch convention implicit in the primary source.  In
the nuclearity domain, the Fredholm expansion is

\[
\det(I-\mathcal L_s)
=\exp\left(
-\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal L_s^m}{m}
\right).
\]

The parity factorization gives

\[
\det(I-\mathcal L_s^2)
=\det(I-\mathcal L_s)\det(I+\mathcal L_s).
\]

This is a genuine infinite-dimensional determinant, so the finite-memory
\(O(T)\) theorem does not apply.

## 5. Main Result

**PROVED:** the continued-fraction grammar has a coherent primitive/repetition
ledger and a source-supported Fredholm determinant for its own modular
periodic objects.

**REFUTED for the Route-A target:** those primitive objects are periodic
continued fractions/quadratic irrationals, not rational primes.  Matrix trace,
discriminant, norm, or parity collisions do not create a canonical
rational-prime ledger.

## 6. Interpretation

The candidate demonstrates that symbolic dynamics can supply infinite
analytic complexity without free per-orbit weights.  It also isolates the
arithmetic mismatch sharply: the determinant belongs to the modular
hyperbolic species generated by the grammar.
