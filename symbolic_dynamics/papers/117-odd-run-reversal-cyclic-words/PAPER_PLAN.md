# P117 paper plan

Status: PROVABLE AS STATED / ANONYMOUS INTERNAL MANUSCRIPT / EXTERNAL HOLD.

## One-sentence contribution

For parallel odd-run reversal on labelled cyclic binary words, a
run-boundary survival law and a distinct parity-eroder cost prove the whole
recurrent census and the sharp parity-dependent maximum preperiod.

## Frozen claim boundary

The paper may claim only:

1. a boundary survives exactly when its two incident run lengths have equal
   parity, and no boundary is created;
2. recurrence is equivalent to all cyclic run lengths having one parity;
3. all eventual periods are one or two, with the exact fixed-state and
   two-cycle formulas in Theorem 3.1;
4. the maximum preperiod is \((n-1)/2\) for odd \(n\) and
   \(\lfloor(n-2)/4\rfloor\) for even \(n\);
5. the displayed extremal families attain both bounds; and
6. the finite zeta function follows from the exact recurrent census.

The paper must not claim novelty, priority, an unlabelled-necklace census,
a full basin-size distribution, or equivalence with a published shrinking
cellular automaton.

## Proof routes

Route I uses cyclic run compositions.  It proves monotone boundary loss,
recurrence, enumeration, the odd clock, and an extremal odd family.

Route II labels each surviving boundary by its position parity.  The induced
shrinking word deletes a symbol when its two neighbours differ.  The cost
\(C(q)=|q|+e(q)\) drops by at least four at every mixed step; minimal
realizations and the family \(0^{2t+1}1\) prove the sharp even clock.

The routes share only the elementary boundary-survival lemma.  Exhaustive
enumeration through order sixteen is an independent finite control.
