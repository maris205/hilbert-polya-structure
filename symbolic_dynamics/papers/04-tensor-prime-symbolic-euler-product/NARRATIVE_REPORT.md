# Narrative Report

## The bet

The previous three papers asked whether the wheel sieve could be made
periodic without losing its prime clock.  That branch ended in a theorem
stop.  Paper 04 changes the intrinsic invariant rather than weakening the
clock: use the tensor factorization of finite full shifts itself.

The bold hypothesis is that rational primes should appear as **symbolic
objects that cannot be decomposed under a natural symbolic product**, not as
labels attached to already existing cycles.  Finite full shifts provide the
minimal test.  Their product is multiplication and their entropy is a
logarithmic norm:

\[
 F_m\otimes F_n\cong F_{mn},\qquad h(F_n)=\log n.
\]

It follows immediately, but nontrivially for the Route-A bookkeeping, that
the tensor atoms are exactly \(F_p\).  A universal atom-loop functor then
turns those categorical atoms into genuine primitive orbits of one
countable Markov suspension.  No prime table or log-prime roof is supplied.

## What works

The same frozen object supplies all of the following:

1. tensor atoms \(F_p\) as rational primes;
2. the entropy roof \(h(F_p)=\log p\);
3. one primitive loop per atom and its repetitions;
4. a trace-class diagonal Ruelle operator for \(\Re s>1\);
5. \(\det(I-\mathcal L_s)=1/\zeta(s)\);
6. the exact \(1\), \(\mu\), and \(\Lambda\) Dirichlet ledgers.

The finite experiment is an exact prefix certificate rather than a zero fit.
At every cutoff through 256, opaque tensor-table factorization recovers all
atoms and every coefficient.  Random, additive, and shifted-law controls
show why unique factorization alone proves too much; the full-shift entropy
norm must remain locked to the same tensor operation.

## Why the trivial-looking dynamics is informative

The atom-loop shift is a countable disjoint union of fixed symbolic points.
That is a weakness for chaos and a strength for the exact Euler ledger.
Whenever two distinct positive atom symbols recur in one strongly connected
component, a mixed primitive cycle has length
\(a\log p+b\log q+\cdots\).  Its logarithmic-derivative coefficient is
positive at an integer containing distinct prime factors, but the target
von Mangoldt coefficient there is zero.  Exact positive realizations are
therefore forced to split the recurrent core by tensor atom.

## Where the road now breaks

The ordinary determinant is oriented as \(1/\zeta\), is trace class only in
the Euler half-plane, and contains no archimedean completion.  A holomorphic
trace-class continuation through a nontrivial zeta zero would make its
Fredholm determinant holomorphic there, contradicting the pole of
\(1/\zeta\).  Thus analytic continuation cannot come from simply extending
this ungraded diagonal operator.

This sharpens the next research problem.  Within Symbolic Dynamics, seek a
canonical **graded transfer complex** whose superdeterminant or determinant
quotient is generated by the same tensor/entropy data and whose duality
implements the completed functional equation.  Its grading must be derived,
not assigned.  This is the smallest plausible bridge from exact Euler
arithmetic to a critical-strip determinant.

## Research status

The result is deliberately bolder than a negative audit and more modest than
an RH claim.  It is the first candidate in this project to close A0, A1, and
A2 on one source-locked symbolic object.  A3 is partial and exposes a precise
operator obstruction; A4 and Route B remain closed.
