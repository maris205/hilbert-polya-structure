# Paper Plan

## Working title

**Tensor Primes of Full Shifts and a Symbolic Fredholm Realization of the
Riemann Euler Product**

## One-sentence contribution

The tensor atoms of finite full shifts, equipped with their intrinsic entropy
roof and a canonical diagonal orbitification, form a single countable
symbolic suspension whose zeta is exactly \(\zeta(s)\) in \(\Re s>1\), while
positive mixing and ungraded trace-class continuation are both sharply
obstructed.

## Claim order

1. Freeze the full-shift monoidal skeleton and distinguish categorical atoms
   from temporal primitive orbits.
2. Prove tensor atoms are \(F_p\), with entropy \(\log p\).
3. Define the diagonal atom-loop shift and its suspension.
4. Prove the trace/Fredholm/Euler identities.
5. Prove positive no-mixing necessity.
6. Report exact finite prefix certification and adversarial controls.
7. Apply Route A layer by layer.
8. Prove the trace-class continuation obstruction and state the graded
   symbolic-completion conjecture.

## Paper structure

- Abstract and status box.
- Motivation and relationship to the wheel-sieve theorem stop.
- The monoidal full-shift source.
- Canonical atom orbitification.
- Exact determinant and prime-power ledger.
- Positive no-mixing theorem.
- Exact computation and controls.
- Route-A evaluation and continuation obstruction.
- Bold next conjecture and conclusion.
- Appendices: proof details and reproducibility record.

## Writing policy

The paper says exactly where the construction is elementary, where the
synthesis appears new, and where it fails.  It does not run a review loop:
per the project directive, formula checks, exact code tests, source checks,
and a clean PDF build are sufficient for this exploratory stage.
