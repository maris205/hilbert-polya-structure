# Literature and Novelty Audit

## Closest symbolic-dynamics precedents

- Lind proved foundational entropy results for topological Markov shifts and
  noted direct-product indecomposability consequences for prime full shifts.
- Meyerovitch and Kopra study direct factorization and direct-prime subshifts.
- Delvenne treats Bernoulli shifts and entropy in a monoidal categorical
  framework.
- Pakapongpun and Ward give functorial orbit-counting formulas under products.
- Metropolis–Rota and Dress–Siebeneicher identify necklace/Euler-transform
  algebra with Witt-vector structures.
- Artin–Mazur and Bowen–Lanford supply the periodic-orbit zeta and finite-shift
  determinant foundations.

These sources mean that none of the following is claimed as new in isolation:
prime full shifts, entropy additivity, full-shift product factorization,
necklace/Witt identities, or finite-shift determinant formulas.

## Terminology firewall

A tensor atom \(F_p\) is an object indecomposable under Cartesian product.  It
is not a temporal primitive orbit inside \(F_p\).  The ordinary Artin–Mazur
zeta of \(F_n\) has its own necklace orbit counts.  Paper 04 first extracts
the tensor atoms and then applies a separately stated atom-loop functor; only
the resulting loop is a temporal primitive orbit.

## Defensible contribution

We did not locate in the cited primary literature the exact synthesis used
here: the symmetric monoidal skeleton of all finite full shifts, the entropy
norm, a canonical countable atom-loop orbitification, the resulting Riemann
and von Mangoldt ledgers, a positive no-mixing necessity theorem, and a
layer-by-layer RH Route-A audit.  The contribution is this construction and
diagnostic package, not the elementary integer identities by themselves.

## Novelty and naturality risks

1. The full-shift skeleton is equivalent to \((\mathbb N_{\ge1},\times)\), so
   the arithmetic origin is transparent and may be called a repackaging.
2. A generic free commutative monoid has an atom Euler product; unique
   factorization alone therefore proves too much.
3. The diagonal atom-loop functor is canonical but dynamically reducible.
4. No prior-art claim is made for a completed Riemann determinant or RH
   mechanism; neither is constructed here.

The adversarial controls address item 2 by locking the same full-shift tensor,
entropy, fixed-point, and Artin–Mazur data.  Items 3–4 remain explicit open
boundaries.
