# Obstruction Registry

## SD-O04 — positive mixed-cycle obstruction

For a positive weighted symbolic grammar with atom roofs \(\log p\), any
recurrent component containing two distinct tensor atoms creates a mixed
primitive cycle.  Its mass has at least two distinct prime divisors and its
logarithmic-derivative coefficient is positive, contradicting the support of
\(\Lambda\).  Exact positive Euler ledgers therefore require atom-separated
recurrent components.

## SD-O05 — ungraded Fredholm-continuation obstruction

The SD-C07 operator satisfies
\(\det(I-\mathcal L_s)=1/\zeta(s)\) for \(\Re s>1\).  A holomorphic
trace-class continuation through a nontrivial zeta zero would have a
holomorphic Fredholm determinant, whereas \(1/\zeta\) has a pole there.
Thus the ungraded family cannot provide such a continuation.  Determinant
quotients or transfer complexes are outside this theorem and remain open.
