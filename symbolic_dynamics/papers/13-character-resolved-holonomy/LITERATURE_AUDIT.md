# Paper13 Literature and Novelty Audit

## Direct prior art

1. Parry and Pollicott, *Zeta Functions and the Periodic Orbit Structure of
   Hyperbolic Dynamics*, Astérisque 187--188 (1990), especially Chapters 8
   and 12: compact-group and `Z^d` symbolic extensions, dual characters,
   twisted transfer operators, and character `L`-functions.  Official record:
   <https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_1990__187-188__1_0/>.
2. Sharp, “Convergence of Zeta Functions for Amenable Group Extensions of
   Shifts,” Contemporary Mathematics 744 (2020), 245--257,
   <https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/744/14927>: the identity-periodic sector and
   group-trace zeta are the closest direct precedent for the neutral
   coefficient.
3. Clair, “Zeta Functions of Graphs with `Z` Actions,” JCTB 99 (2009),
   48--61, <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2008.04.002>: Fourier/Bloch
   decomposition and group determinants for periodic graphs.
4. Adachi--Sunada (1987), Stark--Terras (1996/2000), Katsuda--Sunada (1990),
   and Guido--Isola--Lapidus (2008) establish twisted Ruelle, graph-cover,
   homology-character, and periodic determinant frameworks.  These broad
   mechanisms are not novel here.
5. Livšic/coboundary rigidity is classical; the present graph gauge identity
   is only its elementary finite-range specialization.

## Terminology and claim boundary

- The primary `+1/+1` labeling is a positive-cone directed cocycle, not a
  standard magnetic connection; reverse magnetic edges would carry inverse
  phases and trigger the power-two leak.
- The neutral coefficient `[w^0]` is not a character fiber.  The trivial
  character is `w=1` and sees mixed cycles.  The neutral target is the
  conditional expectation/group trace, also recoverable as Haar Fourier
  coefficient or analytic evaluation at noncharacter `w=0`.
- The parent lifted operator is semifinite-trace-class, not ordinary trace
  class on the full deck Hilbert space.  Individual Bloch fibers are ordinary
  trace class in `Re(s)>1`.
- Character twists, Bloch theory, group-trace zeta, and coboundary invariance
  are prior art.  The contribution is the tensor-prime pointed-cone
  specialization and its proved no-unified-fiber/selectivity/reversal stops.
