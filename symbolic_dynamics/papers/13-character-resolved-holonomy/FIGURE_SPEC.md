# Figure specification

`figures/character_holonomy_map.tex` is a pure TikZ research map embedded by
the manuscript.  Blue boxes are source/construction, green boxes are exact
analytic outputs, orange is unresolved character motion, and gray is the
holonomy trilemma.  Solid versus dashed arrows and explicit text make the
figure readable without color.  No raster or external generated asset is
used.
