\section{Proof details and scope ledger}

\paragraph{Trace-norm holomorphy.}
On every compact subset of $\Re s>1$, the series
$\sum_np_n^{-\Re s}$ is uniformly convergent.  The same majorant controls the
diagonal and both weighted shifts, including all local $s$ derivatives after
the usual logarithmic factor.  This supplies the trace-norm holomorphic family
used in \cref{thm:trace-class}.

\paragraph{Coefficient extraction.}
At finite cutoff the determinant is a polynomial in $w$.  At infinite cutoff,
the trace-log is a normally convergent power series for sufficiently small
$z$, and coefficient extraction commutes with the trace and repetition sums.
The scalar Euler identity then extends in its own honest half-plane.  We do
not assert that a single logarithm branch survives every resolved fiber zero.

\paragraph{Periodicity convention.}
The one-sided skew product is autonomous but not an invertible covering.
Primitive lifted paths are counted modulo deck translation using the canonical
semifinite trace per cell.  The lifted operator is not ordinary trace class.
Calling the strongly connected base ``recurrent'' is a graph
statement; no positive-recurrence theorem for the countable lifted shift is
claimed.

\paragraph{Evidence labels.}
The lifted ledger, trace-class theorem, zero-mode identity, two-atom formula,
gauge lemma, and reversal trilemma are proved analytically.  Finite
computations are labeled \texttt{NUMERICAL\_OBSERVATION} or exact
implementation certificates.  Arithmetic specificity is
\texttt{REFUTED} for the frozen statistic and controls.  Analytic
continuation, a target divisor, and a Hilbert--P\'olya operator remain
\texttt{OPEN} or absent, not numerically supported.

\paragraph{Round-two boundary.}
Any geometric magnetic flux, scattering phase, or Hamiltonian carrier is
recorded only as a \texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE}.  No such system is used in the
candidate, proof, experiment, or Route-A tuple.
