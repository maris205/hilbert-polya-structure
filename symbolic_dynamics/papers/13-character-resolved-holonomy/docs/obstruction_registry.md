# SD-C15 Obstruction Registry

## Character-holonomy trilemma

For the frozen adjacent symbolic graph:

1. **Positive charges:** charge \(+1\) in both directions protects the zero
   Fourier Euler ledger, but the nonzero character response is generic across
   prime and nonprime inventories.
2. **Inverse charges:** charges \((+1,-1)\) restore reversed-edge holonomy but
   make the adjacent two-cycle charge zero, contaminating the ledger at
   power two.
3. **Coboundaries:** rank or entropy vertex-potential phases are unitarily
   gauge-conjugate to the untwisted transfer and cannot provide transverse
   information.
4. **Roof entropy:** twisting atom loops by entropy is exactly
   \(s\mapsto s-i\theta\), not a new transverse invariant.
5. **Forward DAG:** deleting returns restores an all-order Euler determinant,
   but all character information moves into a determinant-invisible
   triangular sector.

## Reusable obstruction

Any positive adjacent charge field with nonzero cross amplitudes has a
nonconstant two-atom continuant coefficient. Therefore the frozen
prime/nonprime control-collapse criterion cannot hold for a mechanism that
depends only on positivity and adjacency. A future symbolic candidate needs
an intrinsic arithmetic selection rule in the nonzero character modes, not
merely a different charge magnitude or inventory ordering.

This is a `STOP_SCOPED / PROVES_TOO_MUCH` result, not an RH or target-divisor
statement.
