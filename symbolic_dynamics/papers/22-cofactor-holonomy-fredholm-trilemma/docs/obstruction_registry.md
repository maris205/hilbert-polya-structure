# Obstruction registry

## SD-C24 closed branch

The abelian product-holonomy branch on the successor-divisor shift is closed
under scalar unitary characters and arbitrary positive inventory changes.

1. `Q(C_k)=2` for every `k>=2`, so holonomy two contains an all-length
   primitive spine.
2. More generally, each atomic holonomy `p` has infinitely many primitive
   representatives `C_(k,p)`.
3. Pure cofactor weighting produces the desired label scale but is never
   trace class; whenever bounded it is noncompact because of the unit-weight
   successor spine.
4. Endpoint regularization restores a Fredholm domain but gives factorial
   damping rather than a prime Euler roof.
5. Scalar unitary phases depend only on total holonomy, so they cannot select
   among cycles in the same class.
6. The canonical neutral group trace sees no recurrent neutral cycle, making
   its local determinant exactly one.
7. Positive inventories and transported presentations preserve the entire
   support obstruction, hence the selectivity margin is zero and the mechanism
   `PROVES_TOO_MUCH`.

The minimal next in-family obligation is ordered finite memory or a
noncommutative cocycle that distinguishes cycles with equal product holonomy,
without importing a prime-length mask. Cross-family carriers remain only a
`ROUND2_CLUE` and are not tested here.
