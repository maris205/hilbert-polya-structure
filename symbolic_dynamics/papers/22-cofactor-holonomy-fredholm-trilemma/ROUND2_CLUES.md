\[

difference between an abelianized path invariant and a noncommutative path
signature.  Any cross-family analogy is admissible only after an intrinsic
symbolic representation and a trace-class function space are independently
constructed.

## 3. Semifinite versus ordinary spectral objects

separation may be compared conceptually with density-per-fundamental-domain
traces in periodic systems.  It must not be used to infer an ordinary
Hilbert–Pólya spectrum.

hidden multiplicity.  No scattering, quantum, or geometric interpretation
