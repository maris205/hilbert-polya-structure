# Claims-to-evidence map

| Claim | Analytic evidence | Independent exact control |
|---|---|---|
| `F^3=F` | antitonicity, extensivity of `F^2`, and symmetry of adjacency | every state of every simple graph through six vertices |
| fixed points are maximal independent sets | independence plus domination in both directions | literal set implementation versus bitset MIS predicate |
| image equals the `F^2`-closed set and zeta has only 1/2-cycle factors | cubic identity and finite-map cycle factorization | image/closure equality and odd/even iterate counts |
| bipartite periodic square law | independent one-sided Galois closures and their anti-isomorphism | every bipartite graph through `3+3` vertices |
| path recurrence and family zeta | first-vertex decomposition of maximal independent sets | every path state through 17 vertices |

Owner boundary: the MIS-network fixed-point correspondence is attributed to
Aracena--Demongeot--Goles, Aracena--Richard--Salinas, and the recent
Gadouleau--Kutner treatment; general formal-concept closure theory is
attributed to Ganter--Wille.  Those ingredients receive zero novelty credit.
The frozen residual claim is their synchronous temporal conjunction with the
bipartite square law and zeta reduction.
