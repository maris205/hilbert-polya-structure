## 4. Attribution and language firewall

Do not claim a new twisted Ihara theory, local-system theory, matrix-weighted
determinant, or Newton/Cayley--Hamilton criterion. Do not call the non-flat
edge connection a local system on the filled `2`-complex. Do not infer a
complete factor deletion from one vanishing trace, or call the graded ratio an
ordinary positive determinant.

zero matches, analytic continuation, functional equations, critical-line
carriers, RH consequences, or a universal no-go for all cocycles,
representations, groupoids, or infinite-dimensional coefficients.
