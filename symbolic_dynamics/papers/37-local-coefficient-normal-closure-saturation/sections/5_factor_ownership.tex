\section{Ordinary factors and determinant ownership}
\label{sec:factor}

\subsection{Trace class on the full edge space}

At most four oriented edges originate at any affine vertex.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
  \Tr(\Dtheta)
  \le 4\sum_{b,k\ge0}\theta^{1+b+k}
  =\frac{4\theta}{(1-\theta)^2}<\infty.
  \label{eq:damping-trace}
\end{equation}
Tensoring with a finite-dimensional identity preserves trace class.  With
$M_P$ from \cref{eq:uniform-transport}, every row and column of $H_P$
contains at most three nonzero blocks.  The block Schur test gives
\begin{equation}
  \lVert H_P\rVert\le3M_P.
  \label{eq:block-schur}
\end{equation}
The two-sided trace-ideal property now makes $\TP$ trace class.  This is the
weighted infinite-graph Fredholm setting, not a determinant assigned to a
finite quotient \citep{Deitmar2015}.

For $|z|<\lVert\TP\rVert^{-1}$,
\begin{equation}
  -\log\det(I-z\TP)
  =\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{z^n}{n}\Tr(\TP^n).
  \label{eq:fredholm-log}
\end{equation}
No quotient, support projection, or boundary representation appears in this
ownership statement.

\subsection{Primitive grouping}

Expanding a diagonal block in \cref{eq:fredholm-log} gives one based
cyclically nonbacktracking path.  A primitive orbit $\gamma$ repeated $m$
times contributes $|\gamma|$ cyclic base states; this cancels the factor
$m|\gamma|$ coming from the logarithm at length $m|\gamma|$.  Absolute
convergence near zero permits grouping into
\begin{equation}
  -\log\det(I-z\TP)
  =\sum_{[\gamma]\ \mathrm{primitive}}\sum_{m\ge1}
  \frac{q_\theta(\gamma)^m z^{m|\gamma|}}{m}
  \Tr(W_\gamma^m).
  \label{eq:primitive-expansion}
\end{equation}
Equation \eqref{eq:ordinary-factor} follows by the finite-dimensional
determinant logarithm.  This matrix-holonomy organization is established in
twisted and matrix-weighted graph zeta theory
\citep{AdachiSunada1987,Goda2020,MatsuuraOhta2024}; the source-locked question
is what factorwise cancellation can leave.

For two parities, subtraction is allowed only after each determinant has been
owned:
\begin{equation}
  \log\Zgr(z)
  =\sum_{[\gamma]}\sum_{m\ge1}
  \frac{q_\theta(\gamma)^m z^{m|\gamma|}}{m}
  \left[\Tr((W_\gamma^+)^m)-\Tr((W_\gamma^-)^m)\right].
  \label{eq:graded-expansion}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Why ordinary deletion is impossible}

Let $W$ be $d\times d$.  Its primitive factor is trivial precisely when
\begin{equation}
  \det(I-tW)=1.
  \label{eq:factor-one}
\end{equation}
If \cref{eq:factor-one} holds, every nonconstant characteristic coefficient
vanishes.  Equivalently, Newton identities make every power trace vanish and
the characteristic polynomial is $x^d$.  Cayley--Hamilton gives $W^d=0$.
The converse is immediate for nilpotent $W$.  Thus
\begin{equation}
  \det(I-tW)=1
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \Tr(W^m)=0\ (m\ge1)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  W\ \text{nilpotent}.
  \label{eq:nilpotence}
\end{equation}

Every holonomy formed from \cref{eq:inverse-transport} is invertible.  The
ordinary branch therefore cannot delete one affine relation factor, much less
the complete relation ledger.  Allowing the nilpotent control from
\cref{cor:nilpotent} changes the coefficient category rather than repairing
local transport.

The free marker survives this negative result.  Each term of
\cref{eq:primitive-expansion} carries the actual edge length
$z^{m|\gamma|}$, and no cell comparison changes it.  The Route-A analytic
gate therefore passes even though the recurrence-selection gates fail.
