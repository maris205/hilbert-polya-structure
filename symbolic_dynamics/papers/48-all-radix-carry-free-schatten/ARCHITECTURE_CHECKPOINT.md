## Scientific separation

decides carry freedom by repeated quotient/remainder on positive integers,
and constructs direct finite positive-prefix or shell matrices. Evaluator B
has a separate parser and expansion, constructs digit compatibility tables,

independently derived sine formula). Shell records additionally contain A's
direct weighted norm and B's rigorous endpoint envelope. The exact trace
record is the unweighted finite closed-walk support ledger; weighted traces
remain labeled diagnostics in the native records and never certify an
infinite domain.

Auditor P alone emits the four `INFINITE_THEOREM_CERTIFICATE` records. It
parses the proof headings, quantifiers, strict inequalities, the binary
adjacent-pair exception, positive-vertex trace deletion, determinant domains,
and period construction without importing either evaluator.

## Adversarial and governance separation

implemented semantic consumers. Consumer programs never read mutation IDs,
designated-consumer lists, or expected codes from the registry. The harness
passes a code-free semantic attack envelope. A kill requires every and only
the frozen designated consumers to reject with exact code and exit 2; all
non-designated consumers must accept. Physical full-object attacks separately
exercise scalar bool/int confusion, coordinated scientific fields, both full
Route objects, absolute/parent paths, symlinks, missing/extra/renamed nodes,
forbidden caches, forced late failure, and pre-existing-target idempotence.
