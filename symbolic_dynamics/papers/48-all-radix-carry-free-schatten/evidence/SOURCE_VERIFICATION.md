## Claim-level citation firewall

- Kummer is cited only for prime-base carry valuations; the phrase “by
  Kummer” never appears in a composite-radix definition.
- Lucas, Christopher–Kennedy, Bacher–Chapman, LaGrange, Chistikov et al.,
  Linial–Shraibman, and Alman–Guan–Padaki are credited for finite arithmetic,
  matrix, graph, covering, or algorithmic controls.  None is presented as an
  exact collision with the infinite weighted theorem.
- Simon supports standard definitions and general trace-ideal machinery, not
  the paper’s critical surface.
- The only admissible search wording is: “the bounded directed search did
  not find the exact same-quantifier infinite weighted theorem.”
- No entry in `references.bib` retains an unresolved verification marker, and
  no uncited entry may survive the final bibliography filter.
