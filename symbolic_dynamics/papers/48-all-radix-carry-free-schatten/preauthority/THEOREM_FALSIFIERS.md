## Exact outcome and stop rule

A mutation is killed only if every and only its designated consumers return
REJECT with its exact registry code and exit 2. Missing/extra/duplicate
consumers, ACCEPT/zero exit, wrong codes, HARNESS_ERROR, parse/schema
exceptions, timeouts, malformed payloads, and unclassified nonzero exits are
survivors and force package HOLD. A source or independence failure cannot be
waived by numerical agreement.

An exact same-quantifier primary theorem causes package-level HOLD and sets
the external claim disposition `STOP_DUPLICATE=LIVE_CONDITIONAL`. That token
is never a Route tuple member, overall verdict, or Route terminal code.
