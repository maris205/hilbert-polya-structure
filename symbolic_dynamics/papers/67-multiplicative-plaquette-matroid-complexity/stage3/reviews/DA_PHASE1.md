## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor requires the methodology seat to test whether design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support scrutiny at the relevant field standard; from the DA perspective, any later challenge here must remain unscored by this seat and must not substitute for that owner’s assessment.

D2 domain_accuracy requires the domain seat to test claims, representations of prior work, terminology, and reported results against current domain evidence; from the DA perspective, possible factual vulnerabilities may inform stress-testing but this seat must not score this dimension.

D3 argumentative_coherence requires adversarial examination of whether the central thesis is internally consistent, whether each inferential step is supported by the stated evidence, and whether any fallacy or unsupported premise breaks the core argument; this mandatory dimension is the DA seat’s sole scoring responsibility.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance requires the perspective seat to test accessibility of framing and definitions for adjacent fields and the substantiation of interdisciplinary implications; from the DA perspective, any challenge touching these matters remains outside this seat’s scoring authority.

D5 writing_and_structure requires the EIC seat to assess organisation, expository clarity, visual presentation, and applicable conventions; from the DA perspective, presentation concerns may matter only when they create an identifiable break in the argument, without producing a D5 score.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution assigns the EIC seat responsibility for evaluating readership suitability, originality, and significance; from the DA perspective, this dimension is acknowledged but neither scored nor used for any alignment, track, tier, or placement judgement.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A complete and internally consistent chain from premises through evidence to the core thesis, with assumptions exposed and serious counterarguments addressed.
what_triggers_block: A repairable but central inferential break leaves the main thesis unsupported unless substantial argument reconstruction is supplied.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, omitted qualification, or underdeveloped counterargument weakens the reasoning without severing the central chain.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational contradiction, circular dependency, or evidence-conclusion mismatch makes the core thesis logically untenable even if peripheral exposition is revised.
