## Review Body

The manuscript gives adjacent dynamics, graph-theory, matroid, and information-theory readers a coherent common object: a finite coordinate set becomes a root-wise bipartite graph, and its graph rank controls both extension counts and dependence. The arithmetic-prefix versus exponent-box comparison is especially useful because it prevents readers from treating two sampling geometries as interchangeable.

### S1: A concrete graph dictionary for arbitrary finite shapes

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §1 "allowed labels are exactly those whose alternating sum vanishes on every graph cycle."

The potential-map formulation makes the transfer from multiplicative coordinates to graphic matroids explicit rather than metaphorical. Forests, cycles, bridges, and edge additions all receive operational meanings for finite projections, so a combinatorics reader can reuse the result without first adopting the full symbolic-dynamics vocabulary.

### S2: Dependence is translated into a standard information quantity

**Evidence Anchor**: equation: §3, Eq. (haar-formulas)

The entropy and total-correlation identities connect matroid rank and cycle defect to multivariate dependence with exact units of log q. The four-corner example then makes pairwise independence versus joint dependence legible outside symbolic dynamics.

### W1: The adjacent-domain transfer stops one step before an application

**Severity**: Minor

**Evidence Anchor**: absence: §6 Comparison, scope, and controls — expected a worked translation of the graphic-matroid result into one adjacent application domain; checked matroid, information-theory, and deterministic-control subsections

**Confidence**: 4 — cross-disciplinary mathematical assessment

The paper carefully identifies matrix completion, rigidity, and entropic matroids as neighboring frameworks, but it does not show how a user in any one of them would instantiate the arithmetic graph construction. Add one compact worked example framed as a linear-code parity check, a completion pattern, or an entropic-matroid query, explicitly marking which conclusions transfer and which rely on the multiplicative root decomposition. This would strengthen reach without enlarging the theorem.
