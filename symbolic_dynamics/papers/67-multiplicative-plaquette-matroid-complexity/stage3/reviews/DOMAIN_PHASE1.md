## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor is a mandatory criterion about study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances; it is owned and scored only by the methodology seat, while the domain seat does not score it.

D2 domain_accuracy is a mandatory criterion owned by the domain seat; it requires domain claims to agree with current evidence, prior work to be represented correctly, and specialized terminology and results to be factually accurate.

D3 argumentative_coherence is a mandatory criterion about internal consistency, evidentiary support for the central thesis, and the absence of thesis-undermining fallacies; it is assigned to the devil's-advocate and methodology seats rather than the domain seat.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance is a high-priority criterion about accessible framing and definitions for adjacent-field readers and substantiated interdisciplinary implications; it is owned and scored only by the perspective seat.

D5 writing_and_structure is a normal-priority criterion about organization, expository clarity, figures and tables, and applicable presentation conventions; it is owned and scored only by the eic seat.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution is a mandatory criterion assigned only to the eic seat and concerns fit with an explicitly configured venue together with originality and significance for its readership; the domain seat neither scores it nor infers a target venue.

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Check the accuracy and consistent use of symbolic-dynamics, multiplicative-semigroup-shift, plaquette-constraint, cycle-matroid, and binary-matroid concepts; verify theorem statements and domain-specific deductions against their definitions; and assess whether relevant prior results are represented and attributed correctly.
what_triggers_block: A repairable but central domain defect, such as a materially false theorem statement, an invalid claimed correspondence between plaquette constraints and cycle-matroid dependence, or a major misrepresentation of prior work, that requires substantial correction before the domain claims are reliable.
what_triggers_warn: A localized domain imprecision, incomplete qualification, terminology inconsistency, or non-central literature omission that weakens accuracy or positioning but leaves the central domain claims potentially intact.
what_triggers_fatal: A decisive domain contradiction or foundational falsehood that by itself invalidates the paper's central mathematical claim, such as the main result relying on an impossible object, a false defining identity, or an irreparable misstatement of established theory.
