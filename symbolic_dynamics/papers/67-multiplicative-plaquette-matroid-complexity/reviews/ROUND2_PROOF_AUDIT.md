## Reconstructed proof audit

### Global algebra

- Maximal whole-power divisibility is valid for composite coprime `a,b` and
  yields a unique `r a^i b^j` representation.
- The mixed-difference equation telescopes on every exponent rectangle, and
  the three-coordinate inverse uses only axis indices.
- Each output coordinate of the inverse depends on finitely many inputs, so
  the topological inverse argument is complete.

### Finite-shape theorem

- Used row/column potentials extend to unused vertices, so the finite
  potential image is exactly the global projection rather than merely an
  upper bound.
- The kernel of the potential map is one-dimensional on each connected
  component, including in characteristic two.
- The rows of the edge-by-vertex matrix represent the coordinate evaluation
  maps.  Transposition and a vertex-class sign change therefore give the
  correct graphic, not cographic, matroid.
- Forest integration establishes that fundamental-cycle equations generate
  all compatibility conditions.

### Haar and special shapes

- Compact-group Haar measure pushes uniformly to each finite image.
- All singleton marginals are surjective and uniform, so total correlation is
  exactly the matroid nullity times `log q`.
- A two-edge simple graph is a forest, proving pairwise independence.
- Prefix extension uses only axis indices no larger than the target; the
  prefix formula is exact.
- `K_{M,N}` has rank `M+N-1` and nullity `(M-1)(N-1)`; the boundary-order and
  area-normalized statements follow.

## Remaining MAJOR issues

### R2-M1. Replace the cycle-sign ellipsis by an indexed identity

The theorem says the alternating cycle equations are the complete finite
obstruction, so their displayed general form should be unambiguous.  For a
cycle

```text
(i_1,j_1),(i_2,j_1),(i_2,j_2),...,(i_k,j_k),(i_1,j_k),
```

write `i_{k+1}=i_1` and use

```text
sum_{ell=1}^k (z_{i_ell,j_ell}-z_{i_{ell+1},j_ell})=0.
```

Make the same replacement in `PROOF_PACKAGE.md`.  This also makes the
characteristic-two reading immediate.

generated by 2 and 3.  Although their dimension problem differs from P67's

two-generator exponent-coordinate decomposition and should be present in the
scope boundary.
