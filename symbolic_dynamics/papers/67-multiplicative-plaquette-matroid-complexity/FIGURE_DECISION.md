# Figure and table decision

## Decision

No graphical figure is included.

## Rationale

The central correspondence is exact and already compact:

```text
coordinate r a^i b^j  <->  edge joining row i to column j,
allowed value          <->  u_i+v_j.
```

A drawn lattice or Cayley diagram would repeat this definition without adding
evidence.  The manuscript instead uses:

1. a geometry table comparing arbitrary finite sets, arithmetic prefixes, and
   exponent rectangles; and
2. a claim-boundary table separating proved finite-shape identities from
   uncomputed dynamical entropy and prohibited priority claims.

The four-corner cycle is displayed as an equation in the text.  That form is
more precise than a decorative plaquette drawing and remains accessible in
grayscale.

## Reconsideration trigger

Add a figure only if a reviewer requests a visual explanation of the
finite-set-to-bipartite-graph construction.  Such a figure would be
explanatory, not evidentiary.
