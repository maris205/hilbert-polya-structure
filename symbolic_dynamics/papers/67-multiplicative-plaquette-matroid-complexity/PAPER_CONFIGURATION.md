## Theorem contract

Let `a,b >= 2` be coprime, let `F_q` be a finite field, and put

```text
X_{a,b}(F_q)
 = {x in F_q^N : x_n-x_{an}-x_{bn}+x_{abn}=0 for every n>=1}.
```

The package must prove, rather than assume:

1. every positive integer has a unique representation `r a^i b^j` with
   `a` and `b` not dividing `r`;
2. on each root component, every solution is exactly `y_ij=u_i+v_j`;
3. restriction to `B={n:ab does not divide n}` is a topological-group
   isomorphism `X_{a,b}(F_q) -> F_q^B`, with an explicit inverse;
4. for every finite coordinate set `F`, the projection dimension is the sum
   of `|V|-components` over explicit root-wise bipartite incidence graphs;
5. the linear-dependence matroid on the coordinates in `F` is the direct sum
   of the graphic matroids of those bipartite graphs;
6. the only finite compatibility conditions are alternating cycle sums;
7. normalized Haar measure projects uniformly, and a finite coordinate set
   is jointly independent exactly when every incidence graph is a forest;
8. the exact arithmetic-prefix count is
   `q^(N-floor(N/(ab)))`;
9. an `M x N` exponent rectangle on one root has exactly `q^(M+N-1)`
   patterns and cycle defect `(M-1)(N-1)`; and
10. deletion and addition of one coordinate obey the exact graphic-matroid
    rank/nullity dichotomy; and
11. prefix complexity, exponent-box boundary complexity, and any dynamical
    entropy along a multiplicative Foelner sequence remain terminologically
    separate.
