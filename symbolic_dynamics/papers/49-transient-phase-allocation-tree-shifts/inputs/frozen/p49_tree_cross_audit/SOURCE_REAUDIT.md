## Reducible topological-entropy owners

The publisher and arXiv records confirm Ban, Chang, Hu, and Wu,
“On structure of topological entropy for tree-shift of finite type,”
*Journal of Differential Equations* 292 (2021), 325--353, DOI
[`10.1016/j.jde.2021.05.016`](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022039621003004),
and
[`arXiv:2105.05406`](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.05406).  Its abstract states
that, for reducible adjacency matrices, equality with the maximum entropy of
the irreducible components fails in general.

The same authors' “Topological entropy for shifts of finite type over
`Z` and trees,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 930 (2022), 24--32, DOI
[`10.1016/j.tcs.2022.07.007`](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030439752200439X),
and [`arXiv:2006.13415`](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.13415), states that a
reducible hom tree-shift can have topological entropy strictly larger than
that of its maximal irreducible component.

These two papers therefore own the conceptual reducible/topological
max-component-failure phenomenon.  They do not state the frozen Hausdorff
metric formula, the transient phase-composition integer optimizer, its
constant-convolution saturation law, or the `d^L` approximation bound.
