## Search-bounded verdict

The primary-source screen found no exact predecessor for the combined result pursued here: a classification of counterterms for the Paper 28 incidence-derived critical quadratic cutoff form under a declared decorated-poset/cutoff category, with diagonal divergence removal, mixed-phase preservation, and exact mutated/composite/generic controls without a label oracle.

This is not a global priority claim.  Every broad regularization ingredient is classical.  The defensible contribution is the scoped classification and local selectivity no-go, not the invention of a regularized trace or the act of subtracting a diagonal term.

## Closest collisions

### Prime-harmonic and prime-zeta finite parts

Mertens' prime-harmonic theorem gives

\[
\sum_{p\le X}\frac1p=\log\log X+\mathfrak B_1+o(1).
\]

The prime zeta function satisfies, as
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\),

\[
P(1+\varepsilon)
=\log\frac1\varepsilon+\mathfrak B_1-\gamma+o(1).
\]

Consequently the compatible Abel/prime-zeta finite part differs from the sharp-
\(X\) finite part by
\(-2C_\eta\gamma\).  This is an exact classical scheme collision.  It prevents any regulator-independent use of “canonical” without another normalization axiom.  The recent Kawalec preprint states the expansion explicitly, but the manuscript bases the comparison on the classical Mertens and prime-zeta relation and treats the preprint only as a recency boundary.

### Modified and relative determinants

For
\(K\in\mathcal S_m\),
\(\det_m\) removes the first
\(m-1\) logarithmic powers.  Thus
\(\det_3\) deletes the complete quadratic coefficient; it cannot selectively retain the mixed quadratic phase.  Relative determinants begin with a pair and trace-class heat differences.  They are a useful model for reference subtraction, but the reference is data and independence is a theorem, not a consequence of the word “relative.”

### Pseudodifferential, heat, and singular traces

Kontsevich--Vishik canonical traces, weighted/zeta finite parts, Wodzicki residues, equivariant residues, and heat counterterms live in symbol or heat calculi with precise continuation and defect theorems.  The current incidence family has no such calculus.  The correct current term is “sharp-cutoff finite part of the source-diagonal quadratic ledger.”

At the Paper 28 critical line, singular values of
\(\mathcal B^2\) have partial sums of order
\(\log\log N\).  The usual Dixmier
\(1/\log N\) normalization therefore gives zero.  A custom
\(\log\log\)-scale singular trace would add a new ideal and generalized limit and would extract a leading coefficient, not the finite mixed functional.

### Hadamard finite part

Primary treatments define a Hadamard finite part only after specifying a singular/cutoff expansion and may retain scale or derivative ambiguities.  The manuscript may say “Hadamard-type sharp finite part” because it displays the
\(X\)-asymptotic.  The phrase does not grant trace properties or regulator independence.

### Incidence functoriality

Incidence algebras are canonical on a fixed locally finite poset, but functoriality is restricted.  Classical Möbius functors and modern CULF/ULF maps impose interval/finiteness conditions; arbitrary monotone maps do not preserve the required algebraic structures.  This audit directly motivates the paper's order-ideal embedding class and its explicit distinction between finite-source recompilation and ambient active restriction.

### Ihara--Hashimoto--Bass

The closest structural precedent is the non-backtracking graph determinant.  It removes immediate inverse-edge reversals by a graph-isomorphism-natural local rule and admits Bass's degree correction.  It does so by changing the admissible word system and transfer operator.  It is an ordinary finite determinant, the divisibility DAG has no native edge reversal, and it does not preserve the weighted mixed Gram phase.  This is a Round-2 clue, not an identity with the present counterterm.

### Symbolic-dynamical zeta determinants

Bowen--Lanford finite-type zeta functions use finite determinants.  Ruelle and modern hyperbolic constructions establish nuclearity, anisotropic trace formulas, or cohomological/distributional traces before introducing a determinant.  These are adjacent symbolic-dynamics precedents but not examples of selectively subtracting a non-trace-class quadratic Gram ledger.

## Safe claims

1. Relative to the frozen marked active-cutoff tower, the exact diagonal ledger and its leading harmonic sub-ledger are natural under transported isomorphisms.
2. Full-diagonal, atomwise leading, sharp
   \(\log\log X\), and Abel/prime-zeta schemes are distinct finite-part conventions.
3. The paper classifies the quadratic/reference-independent class and proves a no-go for the additional additive pair-local linear-Gram class.
4. The bounded primary-source audit found no exact collision for that combined scoped result.
5. Ihara non-backtracking is the closest source-local structural collision, while Mertens versus Abel is the closest scalar scheme collision.

## Claims barred by the audit

- “first canonical regularized trace for symbolic dynamics”;
- “the Wodzicki/Dixmier trace” of the critical quadratic ledger;
- “zeta regularization” for a sharp subtraction without a meromorphic regulator;
- “functorial for all poset maps”;
- “reference independent” when a comparison object has merely been chosen;
- “arithmetic-selective” for a rule reproduced by generic decorated controls;
- “Ihara-like” without constructing a reversal word system and proving equivalence;
- a global novelty claim beyond the stated search neighborhoods.
