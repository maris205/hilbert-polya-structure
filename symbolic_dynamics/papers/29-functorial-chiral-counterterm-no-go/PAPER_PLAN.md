# Paper plan — Paper 29 / SD-C31

## Title

Functorial Counterterms at the Hilbert--Schmidt Boundary:
Finite-Scheme Ambiguity and Generic Mixed-Gram Residues

## Central thesis

For the marked divisibility incidence realization, the critical finite-cutoff quadratic ledger has a unique divergent diagonal germ and an absolutely summable mixed Gram phase.  Natural cutoff convergence leaves an infinite-dimensional finite-scheme ambiguity.  In particular, leading-only and full-diagonal subtraction are equally natural but differ by a nonzero summable atom-local shift.  The remaining mixed phase is generic: diagonal schemes cannot change it, and quadratic additive pair-local linear-Gram schemes cannot preserve it on the arithmetic source while cancelling matched controls.

The theorem is restricted to the quadratic/reference-independent and pair-local linear-Gram classes.  Bare isomorphism naturality still admits nonlocal filtered-tower invariants, whose existence or exclusion is open.
