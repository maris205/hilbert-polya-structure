# Round-2 clues and next-paper obligations

No manuscript review loop is requested.  This file records only research obligations exposed by the completed scoped theorem.

## Stop decision

Do not write another same-object completion paper by changing a local coefficient, a positive metric, or a block regularization.  The diagonal divergent germ is understood, the finite part is nonunique, and pair-local mixed Gram motion is generic.

## Minimum obligation for any successor

A successor must be preregistered before control outcomes and must provide one of two results:

1. a nonlocal divisibility-coherence functor, independent of printed numerical labels, with a proved comparison map for every declared cutoff embedding and exact failure on mutated, composite-only, generic-DAG, and random-inventory controls; or
2. a universal theorem excluding that nonlocal class under independently motivated locality, continuity, additivity, or descent axioms.

In either case it must also prove exhaustion/reference independence of every finite part, preserve the inherited marker ownership, and keep any renormalized functional separate from ordinary trace and modified Fredholm determinants.

## Open structural problems

### Embedding cocycle

When incidence idempotents are recompiled in a larger finite source, new upper-tail terms change the Gram kernel.  Classify this defect as a cocycle, or prove a source condition under which it vanishes.  The present active-cutoff theorem does not solve it.

### Nonlocal tower naturality

Bare isomorphism naturality allows an invariant to inspect the entire filtered source.  Find a non-oracular axiom that distinguishes coherent global arithmetic structure from a hard-coded tower inventory.

### Normalization principle

Naturality and convergence leave arbitrary summable scheme shifts.  A canonical finite part would require a normalization axiom with an independent source interpretation.  Reflection symmetry alone is insufficient.
