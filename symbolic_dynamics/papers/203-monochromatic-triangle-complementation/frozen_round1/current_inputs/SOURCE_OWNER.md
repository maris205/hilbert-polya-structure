## Exact literal and credited residual

MCT acts on every simple loopless undirected labelled graph on $[n]$.
It complements the three edges of the lexicographically first triple
whose edges are all 0 or all 1, holding if none exists. The carrier is
not restricted to forests, tournaments, triangle-free graphs, or a fixed
parity/degree class.

Generic subgraph complementation, minimum-involution selection, the
fixed/two-cycle consequence, finite-state enumeration and the static

also receive zero credit.

## Primary sources actually accessed

### Static inverse-family owner: Johnson cliques

Pavel Shuldiner and R. Wayne Oldford, *The Clique Structure of Johnson
Graphs*, 2022 preprint,
[primary HTML v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.12710v1).
Read the introduction, §3, Propositions 3.1–3.2, Theorem 3.4 and its full
proof, and the stated star/top cardinalities. This directly owns the
static classification used after MCT inverse triples are shown to form
a clique of $J(n,3)$: a common-edge star or the four triples of a four-set.
Its capacities give the familiar Johnson clique number. No novelty credit
is assigned to either classification or bound. The MCT-specific assertions
are that actual inverse triples form such a family, both capacities are
attainable by literal graph targets, and the stated ordered-colour
certificates characterize all equality targets.

### Operation owner: induced subgraph complementation

Fedor V. Fomin, Petr A. Golovach, Torstein J. F. Strømme and Dimitrios M.
Thilikos, *Subgraph Complementation*, Algorithmica 82 (2020), 1859–1880,
[primary full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-020-00677-8).
Read the abstract and §1 definition, including the displayed adjacency
rule for $G\oplus S$. The operation complements exactly the induced
subgraph on a chosen set, so MCT's three-edge operation is an instance
and receives zero credit. The paper studies whether some chosen set
reaches a graph class. Its reviewed definition does not prescribe the
repeated least-monochromatic-triple selector or supply MCT's temporal
trace and target certificates. No assertion about an unread theorem in
its later sections is needed for this bounded boundary.

### Keyword false positive: colourful triangulations

Rohan Acharya, Torsten Mütze and Francesco Verciani, *Flips in colorful
triangulations*,
[primary arXiv record, v3 revised 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03783).
Read its abstract and version record. Vertices of a convex polygon carry
colours, and a flip changes the diagonal in a quadrilateral while the
triangulation remains colourful. It is not MCT's edge colouring of every
pair or simultaneous reversal of three equal edges. This source is a
rejected keyword match, not an owner claim or an exhaustive full-text audit.
