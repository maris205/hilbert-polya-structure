# Figure specification — Paper 35 / SD-C37

All figures are pure TikZ and contain no raster data.

## Figure 1 — four-object firewall

Show the positive affine graph, formal symmetrization, Hashimoto edge graph,
and Bost--Connes diagonal as four distinct objects. Label their outputs:
acyclic, backtracks, reduced relation cycles, and partition trace.

## Figure 2 — affine relation polygon

Draw the two paths `vu` and `u^r v` with common endpoints. Reverse the second
path to form `vuv^{-1}u^{-r}`. Mark that the cyclic word is reduced and has
length `r+3`.

## Figure 3 — trace/determinant coefficient firewall

Display `Tr(D_beta)=zeta(beta)` as the `z^1` coefficient of
`-log det(I-zD_beta)=sum z^m zeta(m beta)/m`. Keep the prime-seeded bosonic
determinant in a separate comparison box with a `z=1` specialization arrow.

Every label must remain legible at 100 percent zoom; no node or fill may cover
text or arrows.
