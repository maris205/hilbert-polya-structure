# Round-2 clues — Paper 35 / SD-C37

## Eligible Paper 36 direction

Test a source-natural chain-level or quotient-aware cancellation of the
affine relation ledger on one uninduced symbolic object. It must:

1. preserve the original generator-step marker;
2. descend the positive source before any KMS/boundary projection;
3. kill two-step backtracks and every affine/commutation relation cycle;
4. retain a nonzero recurrent arithmetic sector;
5. have a bounded trace-class or otherwise honestly owned operator;
6. fail matched generic semigroup presentations;
7. distinguish a homological quotient from a scalar character;
8. preserve power/repetition compatibility;
9. use no prime basis, terminal support, target-zero data, or Route B.

Immediate stop conditions are a disguised normal-form tree, a boundary/GNS
support projector, a quotient that changes the marker, or cancellation that
works for arbitrary one-relator monoids.
