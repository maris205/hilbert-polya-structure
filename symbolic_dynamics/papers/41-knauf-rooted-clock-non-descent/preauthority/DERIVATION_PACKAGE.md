# Derivation package

## 1. Problem setup and target quantity

The frozen object is a rooted binary word, not a cyclic word.  For

\[
 L=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\]

write `M_w=M_{w_1}...M_{w_k}` and

\[
 h(w)=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\end{pmatrix}M_w
      \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The target question is whether the same label supplies a function on cyclic
primitive classes with the repetition law needed by a trace-log/Fredholm
ledger.  The derivation keeps separate the finite-depth partition sum, the
direct-limit state inventory, a proposed necklace ledger, and the marked
diagonal determinant.

## 2. Assumptions and modeling choices

1. Matrix products use the exact `SD-C06` convention.
2. The direct system embeds `w` as `w0`; no other limiting dynamics is
   assumed.
3. A scalar cyclic orbit weight must be independent of the chosen rooted
   representative.
4. Clock repetition means `T(w^r)=rT(w)`, hence for `T=log h`,
   `h(w^r)=h(w)^r`.
5. A scalar orbit phase must satisfy `chi(w^r)=chi(w)^r`.
6. The diagonal determinant is computed only on `Re(s)>2`, its trace-class
   domain, and its logarithmic expansion only on `|u|<1`.

## 3. Notation and type checks

Let `1=(1,1)^T`, `e_1=(1,0)^T`, and

\[
 M_u=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then `h(u)=a+c`.  Let `J=[[0,1],[1,0]]`.  Since `JLJ=R` and `JRJ=L`,

\[
 M_{\bar u}=JM_uJ,
 \qquad h(\bar u)=\mathbf 1^TM_ue_2=b+d.
\]

All matrix-vector products have dimension `2 x 1`; every `h(w)` is a positive
integer; `log h(w)` is real and nonnegative.

## 4. Derivation

### 4.1 Recovery of the source recursion

Right multiplication by `L` preserves the first column:

\[
 M_uL=\begin{pmatrix}a&a+b\\c&c+d\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad h(u0)=a+c=h(u).
\]

Right multiplication by `R` replaces the first column by the sum of the two
columns:

\[
 M_uR=\begin{pmatrix}a+b&b\\c+d&d\end{pmatrix},
\]

so

\[
 h(u1)=a+b+c+d=h(u)+h(\bar u).
\]

This verifies that the matrix calculations below concern the frozen
recursion, rather than a conjugate trace model.

### 4.2 Direct-limit non-autonomy

Let `~_0` be the equivalence relation generated by `w ~_0 w0`.  Because
`h(w0)=h(w)`, `h` descends to the colimit.

For a right append-one map to descend, `w ~_0 v` must imply
`w1 ~_0 v1`.  But

\[
 \epsilon\sim_0 0,
 \qquad h(\epsilon 1)=h(1)=2,
 \qquad h(01)=3.
\]

Since `h` is constant on every `~_0` class, `1` and `01` cannot be
`~_0`-equivalent.  Thus right append-one is not a well-defined self-map on
the source-owned stable states.  The missing information is visible in the
second column: `M_w` and `M_wL` have the same first column but append-one
turns that hidden second column into state data.

### 4.3 Cyclic-clock non-descent

The two cyclic rotations of the length-two mixed word give

\[
 M_{01}=LR=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad h(01)=3,
\]

whereas

\[
 M_{10}=RL=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad h(10)=2.
\]

Therefore neither `h` nor `log h` descends through the cyclic quotient
`W_n -> W_n/C_n`.

### 4.4 Temporal-power failure

For `w=1`,

\[
 h(1)=2,
 \qquad
 R^2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad h(11)=3.
\]

Thus

\[
 h(1^2)=3\ne 4=h(1)^2,
 \qquad
 \log h(1^2)\ne2\log h(1).
\]

Even if a root were chosen for every putative cycle, the source clock would
not assign repeated traversal the repeated primitive time.

### 4.5 Liouville phase failures

The words `001` and `010` are cyclic rotations.  Direct calculation gives

\[
 M_{001}=L^2R=\begin{pmatrix}3&2\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
 \quad h(001)=4,
\]

and

\[
 M_{010}=LRL=\begin{pmatrix}2&3\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
 \quad h(010)=3.
\]

Therefore

\[
 \lambda(h(001))=\lambda(4)=+1,
 \qquad
 \lambda(h(010))=\lambda(3)=-1.
\]

The sign does not descend to necklaces.  It also fails the scalar
repetition law:

\[
 \lambda(h(11))=\lambda(3)=-1
 \ne +1=\lambda(h(1))^2.
\]

As a particularly small local-character corollary, suppose
`chi(w)=alpha^(#0(w)) beta^(#1(w))` equaled `lambda(h(w))`.  The words `0`
and `1` force `alpha=1`, `beta=-1`, while `11` forces both `beta^2=1` and
`lambda(h(11))=-1`, a contradiction.

### 4.6 State-inventory trace and its actual determinant

Let `S_K` be the trailing-zero colimit.  The source multiplicity theorem
states that the number of stable states of label `n` is `phi(n)`.  On
`ell^2(S_K)` set

\[
 Q_se_x=h(x)^{-s}e_x.
\]

For `sigma=Re(s)>2`,

\[
 \sum_x|h(x)^{-s}|=\sum_{n\ge1}\varphi(n)n^{-\sigma}<\infty,
\]

so `Q_s` is trace class and

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}Q_s
 =\sum_{n\ge1}\varphi(n)n^{-s}
 =\frac{\zeta(s-1)}{\zeta(s)}.
\]

The determinant owned by this diagonal operator is

\[
 \Delta_K(s,u)=\det(I-uQ_s)
 =\prod_{n\ge1}(1-u n^{-s})^{\varphi(n)}.
\]

For `|u|<1`, absolute convergence permits the standard expansion

\[
 -\log\Delta_K(s,u)
 =\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{u^r}{r}
   \sum_{n\ge1}\varphi(n)n^{-rs}
 =\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{u^r}{r}
   \frac{\zeta(rs-1)}{\zeta(rs)}.
\]

The source quotient is exactly the first `u` coefficient.  It is not the
whole determinant, and the `n=1` factor makes `Delta_K(s,1)=0`.

### 4.7 Declared repair audit

- **Keep rooted words:** the partition clock survives, but there is no cyclic
  primitive class.
- **Quotient by rotations:** `h` and the Liouville phase fail to descend.
- **Use word powers:** the clock and phase fail their temporal laws.
- **Use `tr(M_w)` or a matrix eigenvalue:** cyclicity/powers can be recovered
  in a matrix model, but the clock and finite partition function change;
  this is Farey/Gauss trace-model territory.
- **Use `Q_s`:** a genuine determinant exists, but `u` counts powers of a
  diagonal state inventory rather than returns of the binary recursion.
- **Retain the full matrix as state:** append actions become definable, but
  this is a larger state space and does not make the rooted `h` cyclic.

This list is exhaustive only for the declared repair family.  It is not a
universal classification of all extensions.

## 5. Main result

The exact rooted label `h` and the appended Liouville observable do not
descend to a scalar primitive-necklace ledger with temporal powers.  The
source-owned stable-state quotient also does not carry the full right binary
append action.  Hence the literal `SD-C06` object fails the historical
canonical primitive-cycle/sign test before analytic target comparison.

## 6. Interpretation

The zeta quotient remains a rigorous arithmetic partition trace.  The
obstruction is an ownership result: arithmetic multiplicities and a
meromorphic quotient do not by themselves supply cyclic orientation,
repetition, or an endogenous orbit phase.

## 7. Scope and limitations

The result does not deny the existence of Farey/Gauss transfer operators,
trace-based spin chains, Selberg determinants, or enlarged-state dynamical
models.  It says that such constructions change at least one of object,
clock, marker, phase, or operator and therefore cannot be credited as an
in-place A1/A2 repair for the frozen rooted `SD-C06` contract.
