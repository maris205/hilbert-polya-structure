# Methodology blueprint

## Research architecture

The list below is the logical audit order of the final package, not its
historical chronology:

1. freeze terminal Paper 39, the final Paper-40 research seal, all six
   Session-4 Route cards, and the Route-A schema by SHA-256;
2. apply a deterministic retrospective selection rule to the six cards
   without treating Paper 39 as a ranking or Paper 40 as an authorization;

4. derive the smallest exact witnesses before any numerical experiment;
5. test logical scope against prior internal Route records and primary

Historically, the six cards, their results, and the exact witnesses were known
before the final Boolean rule and package were sealed.  The rule therefore has

restricted to independence from P39 ranking and P40 authorization.  Only the
final corrected input bytes are frozen before independent DA.

## Evidence hierarchy

Evidence is graded in this order:

1. exact definitions in the SHA-locked Session-4 preregistration and `SD-C06`
   card;
2. source-proved identities cited by the original audit;
3. exact integer matrix calculations reproduced in this package;
4. general trace-class/Fredholm identities with explicit domains;
5. literature metadata and abstracts from primary or official sources;
6. bounded absence statements from the documented search, never universal
   literature-exhaustion claims.

Finite witnesses prove universal failure only because the proposed descent
properties are universal identities.  No finite census is used to infer an
asymptotic or spectral result.

## Mathematical method

Let `W={0,1}*`, `M_0=L`, `M_1=R`, and let matrix products follow the frozen
left-to-right word convention.  The method checks four necessary maps:

```text
rooted word --trailing-zero colimit--> stable state
rooted word --cyclic quotient--------> binary necklace
primitive word --power--------------> repeated word
stable-state inventory --diagonal----> trace-class operator
```

Each arrow has its own marker and ownership obligation.  A failed arrow is
not repaired by moving a theorem across types.

## Adversarial controls

- **Convention control:** verify the recurrence and matrix formula on every
  word through length three by hand.
- **Cyclic positive control:** `tr(M_w)` satisfies
  `tr(M_uv)=tr(M_vu)`; this confirms that the cyclic test is capable of
  passing, while showing that trace is a changed clock.
- **Power positive control:** a matrix eigenvalue/spectral radius satisfies
  the matrix-power law for a fixed matrix; again this is a changed clock.
- **Determinant positive control:** the diagonal inventory operator has a
  valid ordinary Fredholm determinant for `Re(s)>2`; its existence must not be
  promoted to a primitive orbit determinant.
- **Sign negative control:** test both cyclic rotation and word powers for the
  exact observable `lambda(h(w))`, not for an invented cocycle.

## Synthesis rule

The proposed contribution is the conjunction of T1--T3: exact rooted-state,
cyclic-clock, and Liouville-character non-descent for `SD-C06`.  T4 (diagonal
trace versus determinant) is a consistency lemma and prior-art control.  It
receives no novelty credit because Paper 35 already establishes the general
partition-trace firewall in another source family.

No novelty or priority credit is inferred from the retrospective selector or
the ordering of this final exposition.
