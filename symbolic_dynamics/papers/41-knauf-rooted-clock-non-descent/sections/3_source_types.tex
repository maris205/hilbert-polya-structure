\section{Frozen rooted object and typed quotients}
\label{sec:source-types}

We fix the source convention of the Knauf chain
\citep{knauf1998spin,knauf1999erratum}.  Let $W=\{0,1\}^{*}$, with empty
word $\eps$, and set
\[
 L=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 M_w=M_{w_1}\cdots M_{w_k},
\]
where $M_0=L$ and $M_1=R$.  The rooted label is
\[
 h(w)=\onevec^{\mathsf T}M_we_1,
 \qquad \onevec=(1,1)^{\mathsf T},\quad e_1=(1,0)^{\mathsf T}.
\]
The citation fixes source ownership and convention; the calculation below is
an explicit derivation rather than an appeal to authority.

\begin{lemma}[Recurrence fidelity]
For a binary word $u$, let $\bar u$ denote its bitwise complement.  Then
\[
 h(u0)=h(u),\qquad h(u1)=h(u)+h(\bar u).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $M_u=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
Then $h(u)=a+c$.  Right multiplication gives
\[
 M_uL=\begin{pmatrix}a&a+b\\c&c+d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 M_uR=\begin{pmatrix}a+b&b\\c+d&d\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus $h(u0)=a+c$ and $h(u1)=a+b+c+d$.  With
$J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ one has
$JLJ=R$, $JRJ=L$, and hence $M_{\bar u}=JM_uJ$.  It follows that
$h(\bar u)=b+d$, proving the recurrence.
\end{proof}

This identity matters because $h$ is rooted.  It reads the first column of
$M_w$, whereas $\Tr(M_w)$ is invariant under cyclic permutation of factors.
The latter property underlies trace-energy Farey models
\citep{prellbergFialaKleban2006cluster}; it is a positive control, not an
identity for the rooted label.

a rooted word with forgotten notation, and interpreting a free Fredholm
