# Bilingual abstract

## English

We determine the congruence-fixed spaces of a weighted three-term family of
linear shifts over the discrete Heisenberg group.  For a prime finite
Heisenberg quotient of order `ell^3` in characteristic `p != ell`, the fixed
dimension separates into a cyclotomic torsion-intersection degree and a
noncommutative contribution.  The nonlinear contribution is either zero or
the full jump `ell(ell-1)`, and it appears exactly on the coefficient-space
Fermat locus `alpha^ell+beta^ell+gamma^ell=0`.  The proof combines the regular
representation decomposition with a clock--shift determinant and an exact
cyclic-recurrence corank bound.  Specializing the weighted formula at the
previously studied integer element `1+a+b` gives a nonlinear jump exactly in
characteristic three; prior work owns the element, not this mod-`p`
congruence calculation.

**Keywords:** symbolic dynamics; linear group shift; discrete Heisenberg
group; congruence quotient; finite-field convolution; Fermat locus.

## 中文

本文计算离散 Heisenberg 群上一族带权三项线性移位的同余不动空间。对阶为
`ell^3` 的素数阶有限 Heisenberg 商，并假设特征 `p != ell`，不动空间维数
精确分解为两个来源：一维表示给出的分圆扭点相交次数，以及非交换表示给出的
整体跳跃。后一项只能取零或 `ell(ell-1)`，并且恰在系数空间的 Fermat 轨迹
`alpha^ell+beta^ell+gamma^ell=0` 上出现。证明将正则表示分解、钟移矩阵行列式
和循环一阶递推的余核维数估计结合起来。将本文带权公式特化到已有文献研究的
整数元素 `1+a+b` 后，非线性项恰在特征三跳跃；已有工作拥有该元素与其整数
动力学背景，而本文才主张这里证明的模 `p` 同余计算。

**关键词：** 符号动力学；线性群移位；离散 Heisenberg 群；同余商；有限域卷积；Fermat 轨迹。
