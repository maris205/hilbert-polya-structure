# Narrative report

The paper turns a finite-quotient calculation for one Heisenberg group-ring
element into a coefficient-space theorem.  On the congruence quotient, the
right-regular representation separates into `ell^2` characters and
`ell-1` Schrödinger representations.  The character blocks see a torsion
intersection on a plane curve.  Every nonlinear block, by contrast, has the
same determinant

```text
alpha^ell + beta^ell + gamma^ell.
```

This split is the story: commutative arithmetic controls a small, varying
term, while noncommutative representation theory creates a simultaneous
macroscopic jump of size `ell(ell-1)` on a Fermat curve in coefficient space.
The familiar `1+a+b` case becomes one point in this phase diagram and gives
the characteristic-three corollary.

The theorem is proved over the algebraic closure and descended by preservation
of nullity.  A standalone finite-matrix control checks ten parameter tuples,
including singular and nonsingular nonlinear blocks.  External release remains
held because the exact finite-quotient formula has received only a bounded
specialist-source audit.
