D5 writing_and_structure is a normal-priority criterion about organization, expository clarity, figures and tables, and applicable presentation conventions; it is owned and scored only by the eic seat.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution is a mandatory criterion assigned only to the eic seat and concerns fit with an explicitly configured venue together with originality and significance for its readership; the domain seat neither scores it nor infers a target venue.
