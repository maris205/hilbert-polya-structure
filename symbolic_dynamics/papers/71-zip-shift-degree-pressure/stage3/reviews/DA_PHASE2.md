## Review Body

**Genuine strength.** The manuscript does not infer profile rigidity from ordinary entropy or from the natural extension, both of which forget the fibre partition. It instead uses the conjugacy-invariant preimage degree at fixed points and independently shows that the full pressure curve is a finite exponential sum that recovers every fibre multiplicity.

**Strongest counter-argument.** The strongest skeptical case is that the degree-pressure curve and multifractal spectrum are elaborate transforms of a finite fibre histogram that is already visible without thermodynamic formalism. A conjugacy preserves pointwise preimage degree, and fixed points immediately give (N_k=k m_k); this alone recovers the profile and proves necessity for the classification. Sufficiency is then a coordinatewise bijection between equal fibres. Likewise, the natural extension is the ordinary full shift on (S), so pressure for the one-coordinate degree potential is the elementary log-sum-exp function, its derivatives are a mean and variance, and the level-set spectrum is the standard constrained digit-frequency entropy. Weighted periodic sums merely raise the same finite exponential sum to the (n)th power. From this perspective, the pressure curve is not a deeper invariant but redundant packaging of the fixed-point degree histogram, and the multifractal theorem is a full-shift types calculation transferred through a bounded zipper term. A more economical account could prove classification from degree-labelled fixed points first and present pressure and spectrum as corollaries. The so-what challenge is sharpened by the full-system assumption: no transition constraints interact with degree, so none of the hard thermodynamic phenomena of a proper zip subshift appears. Even so, this counterargument does not reveal a logical gap. The paper explicitly confines classification to full one-block systems, proves the natural-extension correspondence and entropy preservation, handles noncompact Bowen level sets through Carathéodory covers rather than capacity counts alone, and keeps endpoint and uniform-profile cases inside the formulas.

**Logic-chain stress test.** The vulnerable steps were whether degree is intrinsic under conjugacy, whether fixed-point degree histograms recover multiplicities rather than only weighted totals, whether equality of pressure curves uniquely determines a finite profile, and whether negative coordinates alter Bowen entropy. Preimage bijections, division by (k), finite exponential-sum peeling, and the bounded Bowen-cylinder refinement close these steps.

**Alternative path.** A direct classification via degree-labelled fixed points is shorter, while a large-deviation proof on the natural extension gives the spectrum. These alternatives confirm the conclusions but make clear that pressure-profile recovery is a thermodynamic representation of the invariant, not the only route to it.

**So-what and frame-lock conclusion.** The package usefully unifies classification, equilibrium averages, periodic weights, and orbit-exponent entropy in one finite transform. The critical hidden premise is fullness of the future shift; it is stated repeatedly and the proper-subshift extension is expressly left open. No isospectral unequal profile survives the finite exponential-sum inversion, and no broader local-homeomorphism classification is asserted.
