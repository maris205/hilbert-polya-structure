# Narrative report

P71 began as a fibre-profile conjugacy lemma supported by a natural-extension observation. After subtracting the zip-shift definitions and the existing uniform-fibre entropy literature, that package was too small. The Stage-2 strengthening uses the topologically intrinsic local degree as a potential. The resulting log-sum pressure is not an ornamental calculation: its derivatives give degree averages and fluctuations, its Legendre transform gives the entire orbit-exponent spectrum, its periodic partition sums factor exactly, and its complete curve recovers the conjugacy profile.

The same system is already studied under the name extended shift by Martins--Mattos--Varão. Their Theorems A--B own the Bernoulli metric- and folding-entropy formulae. The new manuscript keeps that ownership explicit and only inserts the pressure-equilibrium weights to obtain `h=P-tP'` and folding entropy `P'`.

The result is one coherent short-paper mechanism: local information loss becomes an additive potential, and the finite exponential sum `sum k_z^(t+1)` links thermodynamics, multifractal level sets, periodic data, and topological profile rigidity.
