# Paper plan — P180

1. Define the literal map for an arbitrary nondegenerate bilinear form and
   subtract monomial, polynomial-functional-graph, power-map, and level-count
   background.
2. Count every bilinear level and the null cone.
3. Prove the radial iterate and scalar-cube semiconjugacy.
4. Use exponent differences to classify every tail and eventual period.
5. Close the time-zero identity boundary, then invert the radial formula
   target by target and reduce every positive-time fibre to a scalar root
   equation.
6. Count `3`-primary scalar layers, lift by the common level size, and add the
   null-cone boundary.
7. Specialize the fibre theorem at one step for image size, uniform fibres,
   and the unique maximum.

The proof has two independent axes after the iterate lemma: temporal order
classification and target-local inverse classification.
