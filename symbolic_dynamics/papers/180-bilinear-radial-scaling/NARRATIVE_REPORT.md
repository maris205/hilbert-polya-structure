# Narrative report — P180

## Problem

On ordered pairs in `V = F_q^m`, multiply both vectors by their current value
under a fixed nondegenerate bilinear form.  Determine not only the induced
scalar power dynamics, but the complete lifted pair dynamics: every orbit,
every target fibre at every time, every tail population, and the image.

## Mechanism

Bilinearity turns a common radial scale `lambda` into a squared scale of the
form value.  Consequently the value cubes each epoch and the pair accumulates
exponent `(3^t-1)/2`.  Nonzero targets then have exactly one forced pair
predecessor for each scalar root; the null cone is the sole exceptional
fibre.

## Theorem-level progress

- Closed iterate and induced scalar cube dynamics for every `t`.
- Exact tail `a` and period `ord_(2s)(3)` when the initial value has order
  `3^a s`.
- Every-time, every-target fibre formula, including zero and nonzero null
  targets.
- Exact nonzero-dot tail populations, recurrent population, null-cone layer,
  and sharp maximum tail when `q-1=3^A h`.
- Exact image size, uniform nonzero image fibres, and proof that zero is the
  unique maximum-fibre target.

## Limits and status

Power-map functional graphs, cyclic finite-field groups, polynomial/monomial
dynamics, and static bilinear level counts receive zero credit.  Internally,
P102/P103 scalar reductions, the P125 formed-space shear, and the P171 Boolean
Gram system are explicitly subtracted.  The residual is their complete lift
to the literal radial pair map.  Owner status remains amber and external
release is forbidden.
