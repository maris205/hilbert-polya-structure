# SD-C11 Source Lock

- Primary family: countable Symbolic Dynamics with positive group cocycles.
- Atoms: entropy-ordered tensor-prime full shifts.
- Base: recurrent nearest-neighbor grammar inherited from SD-C10.
- Channels: two states, plus and minus; every step switches channel.
- Plus block: `T_s^+`; minus block: `T_(1-s)^-`.
- Cocycles: disjoint universal directed-positive alphabets; loops identity.
  The lower block uses the same positive-monoid representation at `1-s`;
  group inversion, the adjoint, and the full group-ring transpose are
  explicitly excluded from the frozen object.
- Roofs/potentials: entropy endpoint roofs and equal `-log 2` cross potentials.
- Function algebra: two-channel matrix algebra over atom and group von Neumann
  factors.
- Trace: channel matrix trace times both canonical group traces.
- Reflection: fixed channel swap plus canonical alphabet exchange. This is a
  block identity, not by itself an arithmetic functional equation.
- Determinant: trace-series `det_q`, first available integer `q=3`, on the
  common strip.
- Forbidden data: Riemann zeros, fitted phases/scales, target-root loss,
  hand-inserted von Mangoldt weights, and another primary system family.

Claim boundary: exact reflection rigidity theorem and scoped finite-channel
no-go. No moving pure-prime divisor, analytic completion, or Route B.
