# Paper09 Preregistration

Hypothesis: a holomorphic `s`/`1-s` double without adjoint may avoid positive
backtracking while retaining a determinant-visible mixed coefficient.

Go tests:

1. nonempty common Schatten strip;
2. exact reflection symmetry;
3. pure prime-power identity trace;
4. nonzero critical frequency in a retained coefficient;
5. no mixed atom ledger.

Stop tests:

- pure reflected words reduce to `p^-r` and are vertically constant;
- a moving word has unequal atom labels or unbalanced channels;
- regularization deletes the only nonzero low-order term;
- finite phases only cancel the moving term rather than retain it.

Controls: shared positive labels, inverse labels, two-atom cross blocks,
random balanced finite channels, unbalanced channels, composite/random
inventories, and zero-data firewall.
