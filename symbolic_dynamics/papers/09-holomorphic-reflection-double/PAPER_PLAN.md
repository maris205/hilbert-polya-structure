1. **Abstract.** State the holomorphic reflection construction and the
   vertical-sterility theorem.
2. **Introduction.** Motivate removal of the adjoint and list exact advances
   and stops.
3. **Related work.** Restrict to primary symbolic-dynamical, formal-language,
   and group-algebra zeta sources.
4. **Source lock.** Freeze atoms, recurrent grammar, two channels, cocycles,
   trace, ideal domain, determinant, and reflection.
5. **Rigidity theorem.** Derive all power traces and the `det_3` product;
   generalize to finite reflected channel grammars.
6. **Escape controls.** Compare independent positive, shared positive, and
   inverse-identified labels; prove the motion/ledger dichotomy.
7. **Finite audit design.** Give the exact two-atom matrices and adversarial
   symbolic controls without target-zero fitting.
8. **Route evaluation.** Record the Route-A tuple, the A4 failure, and the
   Route-B lock.
9. **Conclusion and appendix.** State the next same-family obligation and
   isolate ideal/word/frequency proof details.

Target length: a compact theorem paper of roughly 10--14 pages. No review
loop is part of this stage.
