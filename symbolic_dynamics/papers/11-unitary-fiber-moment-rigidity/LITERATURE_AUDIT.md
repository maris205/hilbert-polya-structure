# Paper11 Literature Audit

## Verified primary sources

- Adachi and Sunada (1987), DOI `10.1016/0022-1236(87)90014-0`:
  twisted Perron--Frobenius/Ruelle operators and dynamical `L`-functions on
  topological graphs.
- Parry and Pollicott (1990), DOI `10.24033/ast.28`: Chapter 8 treats an SFT,
  a compact unitary group, a finite-dimensional representation, and its
  character in a dynamical `L`-function.  This is the direct precedent for
  `Tr(U_gamma^r)` on repeated orbits.
- Stark and Terras (1996), DOI `10.1006/aima.1996.0050`: multivariable
  edge/path zetas and determinant formulas, used here to motivate the formal
  mixed-monomial audit.
- Boyle and Schmieding (2017), DOI `10.1017/etds.2015.87`: finite group
  extensions of SFTs and the warning that zeta data need not classify an
  extension.
- Ward (1999), DOI `10.1006/ffta.1999.0250`: isometric extensions of full
  shifts can substantially change zeta functions.
- Bowen and Lanford (1970), DOI `10.1090/pspum/014/9985`: untwisted SFT
  determinant baseline.
- Simon (1977), DOI `10.1016/0001-8708(77)90057-3`: infinite Fredholm
  determinant background.

## Claim boundary

The manuscript does not claim the first twisted symbolic zeta, the first
unitary-character periodic weight, the first Bloch/covering `L`-function, or
the first finite unitary moment uniqueness result.  Its scoped contribution
is a tensor-prime-specific synthesis: finite positive fibers cannot generate
visible phase motion while preserving the exact Euler repetition ledger, and
finite recurrent flux cannot erase every mixed repetition.

## Citation audit

The bibliography is cited-only.  No secondary web source and no unverified
metadata are used to support a theorem.
