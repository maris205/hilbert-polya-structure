# ROUND2 Clues

The main paper remains entirely inside Symbolic Dynamics.  The following are
recorded without development:

- `ROUND2_CLUE`: a genuinely geometric holonomy could make phase an action or
  orientation rather than an arbitrary finite fiber label; any such carrier
  must still reproduce the exact repetition character.
- `ROUND2_CLUE`: a fixed self-adjoint or scattering realization would need a
  single operator whose trace and determinant retain the same prime-power
  ledger.  SD-C13 supplies no such operator.

Infinite diffuse fibers and rank-dependent virtual traces remain same-family
mathematical possibilities, but they are not promoted here: the former can be
trace-invisible and the latter changes the frozen data type.
