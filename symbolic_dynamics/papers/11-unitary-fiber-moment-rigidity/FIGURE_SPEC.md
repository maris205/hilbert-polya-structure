# Figure Specification

The pure TikZ figure `figures/fiber_rigidity_map.tex` shows one tensor-prime
atom loop with a finite unitary fiber and the three theorem-controlled
branches:

- positive exact ledger implies `U=I` and no motion;
- nontrivial positive fiber implies a wrong repetition coefficient or a
  surviving mixed cycle;
- matched grading hides an arbitrary `V`, but `V` cancels from the
  superdeterminant.

The graphic uses shape and line style in addition to color and remains legible
in grayscale.
