# SD-C13 Preregistration

## Frozen hypothesis

Attach a finite-dimensional unitary/Bloch fiber to each tensor-prime atom and
ask whether its positive tracial moments can create visible phase motion
without changing any coefficient of the prime/repetition ledger.

## Pass condition

A nontrivial state-visible family must satisfy `tau(U_p^r)=1` for every atom
and every repetition while changing the determinant as the Bloch parameter
moves.

## Stop conditions

- `U_p=I` follows from positive moment exactness.
- A recurrent cross-atom cycle leaves a mixed monomial at some repetition.
- A graded matched sector preserves the ledger only by cancelling out of the
  superdeterminant.
- The same phase response survives matched nonprime or random-clock controls.

## Frozen controls

1. identity fiber;
2. scalar phase and conjugate phase pair;
3. cyclic root-of-unity fibers of dimensions `2,...,8`;
4. ordinary-trace Newton identities through dimension `8`;
5. nonfaithful state and matched graded sector;
6. a directed three-atom cycle in independent formal variables;
7. two parallel primitive returns with opposite scalar phases;
8. prime, composite, and random increasing clocks with matched block sizes;
9. cross-edge-off reduction to the atomwise theorem.

No Riemann zero comparison, root fitting, or crossing census is authorized.
