# Obstruction Registry

## SD-O38 — faithful first-moment rigidity

For a unitary \(U\), the faithful normalized trace identity

\[
\tau((U-I)^*(U-I))=2-2\Re\tau(U)
\]

implies \(\tau(U)=1\Rightarrow U=I\). There is no nontrivial positive
state-visible Bloch fiber preserving even the first ledger coefficient.

## SD-O39 — ordinary all-moment rigidity

If \(\operatorname{Tr}(U^r)=1\) for \(r=1,\ldots,d\), Newton identities
force \(e_1=1\) and \(e_2=\cdots=e_d=0\). Unitarity then forces \(d=1\).

## SD-O40 — nonfaithful determinant mismatch

A state supported on the trivial summand of \(1\oplus V\) sees all moments
as one, while the ordinary determinant retains \(\det(I-zV)\). The proposed
ledger and determinant are not controlled by the same invariant.

## SD-O41 — graded cancellation removes motion

For even \(=1\oplus V\) and odd \(=V\), every supertrace is one, but the
Berezinian equals \(1-z\). The moving \(V\)-sector cancels from the
determinant with the same multiplicity that cancels its moments.

## SD-O42 — roots of unity only delay leakage

An \(m\)-cycle fiber has normalized moment zero until repetition \(m\), then
moment one. A triangle mixed cycle first leaks at transfer power \(3m\).
Two independent return paths also leave a nonzero formal polynomial at every
repetition.

## SD-O43 — Bloch motion proves too much

Fiber dimensions 2, 3, 4 show determinant motion for all 32 seeds on tensor
primes, composites, and random increasing clocks. Motion is generic, while
the positive ledger fails at the first dimension-dependent repetition.
