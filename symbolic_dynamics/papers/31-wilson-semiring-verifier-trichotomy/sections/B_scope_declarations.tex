\subsection{Limitations}

The branch theorem covers the frozen Wilson grammar, feed-forward terminal
verifiers with trace-class regulators, and vertex-disjoint recurrent cycles
with nonnegative exact roofs.  It does not classify overlapping recurrent
grammars, signed or complex roof cancellation, supertraces, homological
weights, or arbitrary semiring enrichments.  No determinant of the primary
operator, analytic continuation to the critical line, Gamma factor,
functional equation, Weil compression, fixed self-adjoint carrier, target-zero
correspondence, or implication for the Riemann hypothesis is proved.
