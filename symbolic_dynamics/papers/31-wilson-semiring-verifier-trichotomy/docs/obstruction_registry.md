# Obstruction registry — SD-C33

## O33.1 — Matched semiring clone

Alphabet addition genuinely breaks Paper30's bare polynomial-UFD monomial
clone, but a relabeled copy with transported addition and multiplication is a
semiring isomorphism. It transports successor, remainder, Wilson paths,
cycles, roofs, and markers term by term. This blocks any claim that addition
selects the literal integer presentation rather than its isomorphic copy.

## O33.2 — Terminal verifier pruning

A feed-forward verifier followed by an accept loop has no periodic data in
its acyclic computation. Its determinant is exactly the diagonal product over
accepted support. The same wrapper works for squares, powers of two,
Fibonacci numbers, and seeded computable predicates; Wilson is not a special
dynamical cancellation mechanism in this architecture.

## O33.3 — Entropy-budget noncompactness

The recurrent prime cycle has `p-1` edges and total nonnegative roof `log p`.
At least one edge therefore has weight modulus at least
`p^(-sigma/(p-1))`, which tends to one. Across disjoint prime cycles this
produces an orthonormal sequence whose images do not tend to zero, so the
primary adjacency is noncompact and belongs to no finite Schatten class.

## O33.4 — First-return marker change

First return contracts a full prime cycle to one diagonal step. It owns the
honest factor `1-z p^(-s)` for `Re(s)>1`, while the primary graph owns the
formal factor `1-z^(p-1) p^(-s)`. They agree at `z=1` and differ for a free
marker. The induced determinant is not the determinant of the primary graph.

## Paper32 gate

Do not test another terminal primality verifier. A successor must derive a
nonterminal, overlapping recurrent interaction before acceptance is known;
prove compactness or an honest determinant for the uninduced whole operator,
preserve the original graph-step marker, and retain matched-clone controls.
