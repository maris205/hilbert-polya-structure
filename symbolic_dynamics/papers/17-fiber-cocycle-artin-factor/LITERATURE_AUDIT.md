# LITERATURE AUDIT — SD-C19

**Search freeze:** 2026-08-14
**Scope:** finite-group extensions of symbolic systems, twisted transfer
operators, dynamical Artin factors, cocycle cohomology, and graph/hypergraph
cover zeta functions.

## Search boundary

The literature search used variants of:

- finite-group extensions of shifts of finite type;
- dynamical Artin \(L\)-functions and twisted Perron–Frobenius operators;
- Frobenius classes and Chebotarev theorems for symbolic or hyperbolic systems;
- Livšic periodic obstructions and compact/matrix cocycles;
- graph and hypergraph Artin–Ihara factors;
- subset alphabets, cardinality cocycles, and factor-count cocycles.

The audit is search-bounded rather than exhaustive.  It does not claim that no
adjacent result exists in every language or database.  No target-zero source
was used to define, select, or tune SD-C19.

## Novelty assessment

| Claim | Assessment | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| A finite-group symbolic cover admits character factors whose product is the regular determinant. | Classical | Cite Parry–Pollicott, Adachi–Sunada, and graph-cover analogues. |
| The intrinsic \(C_2\) subset-degree cocycle gives \(D_+\), \(D_-\), and \(D_{\rm reg}\) as exact atom products in one tensor-subset transfer. | Scoped synthesis | Elementary algebra, but a lawful repair of the preceding relabeling failure. |
| Relabeling-natural, inclusion-compatible, operator-coherent atom locality forces \(\alpha(S)=a^{|S|}\), hence cyclic image. | Main search-bounded theorem claim | Assumptions must appear in the theorem title or immediately before it. |
| The sign-block zeta divisor cancels in the whole cover while mixed lifted primitives remain. | Candidate-specific obstruction | Standard cover-lifting principles applied to the frozen tensor-subset model. |
| The mechanism selects primes. | Refuted | Arbitrary-variable and matched-inventory controls give exact zero pass-rate margin. |

The honest headline is therefore not “new dynamical Artin \(L\)-functions.”  It
is an exact tensor-subset degree factor accompanied by a functorial cyclic
rigidity theorem and a primitive/selectivity obstruction.

## Cross-paper consistency

- Paper 16 rejected atom permutations because distinct arithmetic roofs destroy
  commutation.  SD-C19 acts only on a new fiber and therefore does not reuse the
  illegal symmetry.
- Livšic-type rigidity is not contradicted: the parity cocycle has nontrivial
  singleton periodic data and is not in the trivial-periodic-data case.
- Rich Frobenius variation in graph and hypergraph covers does not transfer to
  SD-C19.  Its label records only total subset degree, which explains its
  arithmetic nonselectivity.
- Identical zeta data need not classify finite-group extensions.  Accordingly,
  no conjugacy or completeness claim is inferred from the determinant.
