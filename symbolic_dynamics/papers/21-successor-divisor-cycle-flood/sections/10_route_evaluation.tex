\subsection{Minimal Paper22 obligation}

The unique quotient \(q=(n+1)/d\) should be exposed on edge symbols.  The next
symbolic task is to classify relabel-natural finite-state quotient filters or
finite-dimensional same-object cocycles.  \Cref{prop:qfamily} already blocks
memoryless positive filtering: retaining \(q=1\) and any \(q\ge2\) creates
infinitely many simple primitive cycles.

Any cancellation proposal must therefore satisfy three locks:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item prove cancellation coefficientwise at the trace level;
  \item report the whole determinant and every relevant isotypic block;
  \item show that unwanted cycles are canceled, not merely moved into an
  unreported block.
\end{enumerate}

This obligation remains inside Symbolic Dynamics.  Geometric, scattering, or
self-adjoint carriers are recorded only as out-of-scope round-two clues and
do not affect the present verdict.  No Paper22 construction is attempted
here.
