# SD-C16 Obstruction Registry

## O16.1 — Prime-exclusive abelian-character obstruction

For any abelian monoidal charge, vanishing on both `p^2` and `p^3` forces
vanishing on `p`. A character cannot be nontrivial on tensor atoms while
vanishing on every composite without destroying the required repetition
ledger.

**Type:** proved obstruction.

**Decision:** `STOP_PRIME_EXCLUSIVE_ABELIAN_CHARACTER`.

## O16.2 — Coboundary and finite-local obstruction

Thin tensor-divisor cocycles are coboundaries. In the path control, the only
determinant-relevant integer data are positive round-trip charges; the first
Fourier coefficient has a fixed sign. A shuffled inventory preserving one
finite witness block preserves that response.

**Evidence:** 18 named rules, 256 radius-one truth tables, and 260 labelled
small transducers; zero robust selector survives.

**Type:** proved obstruction plus exhaustive finite certificate.

**Decision:** `STOP_FINITE_LOCAL_SELECTOR`.

## O16.3 — Raw bar convergence barrier

The individual ordered-word absolute sum is
`sum_(k>=1)(zeta(Re(s))-1)^k`. It converges exactly for
`Re(s)>sigma_bar`, `zeta(sigma_bar)=2`. Endpoint-first coefficient grouping
extends the incidence readout to `Re(s)>1` but is not raw edge-alphabet
convergence in the intervening strip.

**Type:** proved analytic-domain boundary.

**Decision:** `STOP_RAW_COMPLETION_BELOW_SIGMA_BAR`.

## O16.4 — Universal inversion obstruction

The reduced-word calculation uses only a geometric-series inverse. Every
weighted inventory satisfies `F_X=B_X/(1+B_X)` and `D_X=1/(1+B_X)` wherever
the declared sums exist. Composite, random-support, random-weight, signed,
shuffled, and scalar controls all pass exactly.

**Type:** proved adversarial obstruction.

**Decision:** `STOP_ARITHMETIC_SELECTIVITY / PROVES_TOO_MUCH`.

## O16.5 — Primitive mismatch

The trace-log primitives are cyclic necklaces of bar-code edges. Tensor atoms
and their powers emerge only after signed endpoint aggregation, not as an
orbitwise bijection `p <-> gamma_p`.

**Type:** open theorem obligation / current failure.

**Decision:** `A1_WEAK`; `STOP_ORBITWISE_PRIME_CORRESPONDENCE`.

## O16.6 — Global Route-A structure absent

SD-C16 has no completed functional equation, Gamma factor, trivial-zero
treatment, Riemann--von Mangoldt law, intrinsic Weil compression, or natural
unitary/operator lift.

**Type:** open theorem obligations.

**Decision:** `A3_FAIL`, `A4_FAIL`, `ROUTE_B_LOCKED`.
