## Figure 1 — Same-object tensor bar pipeline and universal obstruction

**Purpose.** Show the one relationship for which prose is easy to misread:
the same frozen symbolic object produces the determinant and entropy
innovation, while the generic-inventory branch proves that the inversion is
not arithmetically selective.

**Required nodes.**

1. Full-shift tensor inventory `F_m tensor F_n = F_mn`, entropy `log n`.
2. Nonempty ordered tensor words, roof sum, bar parity.
3. One-vertex countable signed edge shift.
4. Raw sum `F=B/(1+B)` in `Re(s)>sigma_bar`.
5. Determinant `D=1-zF`, with `D(s,1)=1/zeta(s)`.
6. Endpoint-first incidence completion `c(n)=-mu_tensor(n)` in `Re(s)>1`.
7. Roof derivative `mu_tensor*h=Lambda_tensor`.
8. Generic weighted inventory branch `D_X=1/(1+B_X)`, labeled
   `PROVES TOO MUCH`.
9. Bottom boundary: primitive cycles are factorization necklaces, not primes.

**Visual grammar.** Use only TikZ vectors, text, and fills.  Blue boxes denote
source-locked symbolic data, green boxes denote proved analytic outputs,
orange denotes the endpoint grouping boundary, and red denotes the route
obstruction.  Solid arrows are operations inside the same object; a dashed
arrow is the adversarial generic-inventory substitution.

**Caption boundary.** The caption must say that the wider half-plane is a
grouped incidence completion, not raw edge convergence, and that the generic
branch is evidence against arithmetic selectivity.
