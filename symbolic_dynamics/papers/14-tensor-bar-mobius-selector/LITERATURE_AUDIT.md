# LITERATURE AUDIT

## Audit boundary

The search was restricted to primary or authoritative sources relevant to:

- Möbius inversion in incidence algebras, monoids, and categories;
- normed arithmetic semigroups and transported Mangoldt-type functions;
- symbolic/dynamical zeta functions and entropy of finite full shifts;
- temporal orbit Möbius inversion;
- bar/Koszul language needed only to state the next obligation.

No literature result is presented as evidence for RH.  The audit supports a
bounded synthesis claim only.

## Prior art that must be acknowledged

### Incidence and monoid Möbius inversion

- Rota's incidence-algebra framework establishes the general poset Möbius
  calculus: G.-C. Rota, *On the foundations of combinatorial theory I.
  Theory of Möbius functions*, 1964,
  DOI `10.1007/BF00531932`.
- Cartier and Foata develop monoid Möbius functions: P. Cartier and D. Foata,
  *Fonction de Möbius d'un Monoïde*, 1969,
  DOI `10.1007/BFb0079471`.
- Content, Lemay, and Leroux treat Möbius categories and functoriality:
  DOI `10.1016/0097-3165(80)90083-7`.

Paper 14 therefore does not claim the first incidence, monoid, categorical,
or functorial Möbius construction.

### Arithmetic semigroups

- Knopfmacher's *Abstract Analytic Number Theory*, Chapters 1 and 2, develops
  arithmetical semigroups and their arithmetic functions:
  DOI `10.1016/S0924-6509(08)70317-9` and
  DOI `10.1016/S0924-6509(08)70318-0`.
- Benito, Navas, and Varona study Möbius inversion formulas for flows of
  arithmetic semigroups: DOI `10.1016/j.jnt.2007.04.009`.

Thus transporting zeta, Möbius, logarithm, and Mangoldt-type functions to a
normed unique-factorization monoid is established prior art.

### Classical arithmetic identity

NIST DLMF equation 27.5.5 records the classical identity
`Lambda = mu * log`, and equation 27.4.12 records the logarithmic derivative
`-zeta'/zeta`.  Paper 14's tensor notation is an intrinsic realization of
this identity on the full-shift tensor monoid, not a new von Mangoldt
formula.

### Symbolic zeta and entropy

Bowen and Lanford's shift zeta functions supply the classical finite-state
symbolic determinant background (DOI `10.1090/pspum/014/9985`).  Lind's work
on entropies of topological Markov shifts supplies the symbolic entropy
context (DOI `10.1017/S0143385700002443`).  The present one-vertex signed
countable edge shift is a deliberately elementary weighted-adjacency object;
no general countable thermodynamic-formalism theorem is claimed.

### Temporal dynamical Möbius inversion

Pakapongpun and Ward's *Functorial orbit counting* and Byszewski, Graff, and
Ward's work on Dold sequences concern the Möbius transforms that recover
least-period orbit counts from fixed-point counts.  This temporal-period
Möbius axis is distinct from the tensor-divisor Möbius function used here.
The notation and manuscript keep the two axes separate.

### Bar and Koszul language

Priddy's *Koszul resolutions* (DOI
`10.1090/S0002-9947-1970-0265437-8`) is cited only as background for the
Paper 15 question.  Paper 14 proves no Koszul-resolution theorem and does not
claim that its reduced-word cancellation is already a homological primitive-
cycle reduction.

## Bounded synthesis claim

Classical Möbius inversion gives `Lambda=mu*log`, and abstract arithmetic-
semigroup theory transports Möbius, zeta, and Mangoldt-type functions to
normed unique-factorization monoids.  We claim neither a new von Mangoldt
identity nor the first incidence-algebra, categorical, or dynamical Möbius
construction.

Within the primary sources audited, we did not locate the exact source-locked
combination of:

1. the Cartesian-product monoid of finite full shifts;
2. its intrinsic topological-entropy norm;
3. the rigidity classification of standard functorial abelian characters;
4. a tensor-divisor Möbius fiber represented by a single recurrent signed
   symbolic edge shift; and
5. an adversarial Route-A audit that explicitly marks the same construction
   `PROVES_TOO_MUCH`.

The contribution claim is limited to that symbolic synthesis, its local
character obstruction, and its route boundary.  Absence from this bounded
audit is not a global novelty claim.
