## Argument blueprint

### Core claim

The desired cyclic selector exists exactly, but stationarity makes its memory
observable.  A support-dependent exterior rule can cancel mixed words after
seeing their support.  A stationary finite realization must instead retain one
net simple character for each supplied color.  The analytic tensor product
then reproduces disjoint atom blocks.

### Dependency chain

1. Freeze the nonempty wordwise coefficient and both empty-word conventions.
2. Exhibit the orbitwise exterior and stationary projector constructions.
3. compute the exact Hankel rank and syntactic algebra.
4. Apply finite-image character rigidity to ordinary and graded realizations.
5. Quarantine radicals and matched parity sectors as trace-invisible rather
   than claiming literal diagonalization.
6. Pass from every word trace to the full multivariable determinant.
7. Audit canonical bar/Hochschild candidates and identify the successful
   separable algebra with the color inventory.
8. Tensor with the inherited holomorphic de Rham sector, retain the digit
   marker, and prove the countable trace-class statement.
9. Run wordwise, aggregate-adversary, empty-word, arbitrary-inventory, and
   integrity controls.
10. Award scoped A2 credit and reject Route A at the source-selection gate.
