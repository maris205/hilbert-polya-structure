\section{Ordinary and graded character rigidity}
\label{sec:character}

For \(0\le i\le m\), define one-dimensional \(R_m\)-modules by
\begin{equation}
 a_j\big|_{L_i}=\delta_{ij}\quad(i\ge1),
 \qquad
 a_j\big|_{L_0}=0.
 \label{eq:color-characters}
\end{equation}
Put \(\Lambda_m=\bigoplus_{i=1}^mL_i\).  On every positive word,
\begin{equation}
 \Tr(w\mid\Lambda_m)=\chi_m(w),
 \label{eq:target-character}
\end{equation}
while the identity trace is \(m\).

\begin{theorem}[ordinary semisimple rigidity]
\label{thm:ordinary-rigidity}
Let \(\rho:R_m\to\operatorname{End}(V)\) be finite dimensional over \(\C\).
If
\begin{equation}
 \Tr\rho(w)=\chi_m(w)\qquad(w\in A_m^+),
 \label{eq:ordinary-word-hypothesis}
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}
 V^{\ssim}\cong
 \Lambda_m\oplus L_0^{\oplus(\dim V-m)}.
 \label{eq:ordinary-semisimplification}
\end{equation}
In particular, \(\dim V\ge m\), and every active color character occurs once.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(d=\dim V-m\).  The trace difference between \(V\) and \(\Lambda_m\)
vanishes on the augmentation ideal spanned by positive words.  If \(d\ge0\),
the characters of \(V\) and \(\Lambda_m\oplus L_0^{\oplus d}\) agree also at
the identity.  If \(d<0\), compare instead
\(V\oplus L_0^{\oplus(-d)}\) with \(\Lambda_m\).  In either case the two
representations factor through their finite-dimensional combined image
algebra.

Quotient that image algebra by its Jacobson radical.  The resulting complex
semisimple algebra is a finite product of matrix algebras, and its irreducible
trace functionals are linearly independent.  Equality of characters therefore
gives equality of semisimplifications.  The case \(d<0\) would put a positive
\(L_0\)-multiplicity on the first side and no \(L_0\) on the second, impossible
because simple-module classes form a free basis of the Grothendieck group.
Thus \(d\ge0\), and \eqref{eq:ordinary-semisimplification} follows.
\end{proof}

The proof is a finite-image form of the Brauer--Nesbitt principle.  It uses
the trace of every algebra element, obtained by linearity from every word; it
does not infer a common eigenbasis for the original generators.

Now let \(V=V_+\oplus V_-\) be \(\mathbb Z/2\)-graded and require each letter
to act evenly.

\begin{theorem}[graded virtual rigidity]
\label{thm:graded-rigidity}
If
\begin{equation}
 \Str\rho(w)=\chi_m(w)\qquad(w\in A_m^+),
 \label{eq:graded-word-hypothesis}
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}
 [V_+^{\ssim}]-[V_-^{\ssim}]
 =\sum_{i=1}^m[L_i]+d[L_0],
 \qquad d=\dim V_+-\dim V_- -m.
 \label{eq:virtual-class}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, there are a semisimple module \(W\) and integers \(a,b\ge0\),
\(a-b=d\), such that
\begin{equation}
 V_+^{\ssim}\cong W\oplus\Lambda_m\oplus L_0^{\oplus a},
 \qquad
 V_-^{\ssim}\cong W\oplus L_0^{\oplus b}.
 \label{eq:virtual-decomposition}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Add \(|d|\) dormant modules to the side required to match the identity trace.
Dormant modules vanish on every positive word, so
\eqref{eq:graded-word-hypothesis} and \eqref{eq:target-character} give equality
of the full ordinary characters of the two enlarged sides.  Apply the same
finite combined-image argument as in \Cref{thm:ordinary-rigidity}, then cancel
common simple multiplicities.  This yields
\eqref{eq:virtual-class} and \eqref{eq:virtual-decomposition}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[literal splitting is false]
\label{prop:radical-counterexample}
There are noncommuting, nonsplit realizations satisfying every word trace in
\eqref{eq:ordinary-word-hypothesis}.  Moreover, arbitrary common
representations may be added in even and odd degree without changing a single
supertrace.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Choose strictly upper-triangular matrices \(N_i\) in a basis compatible with
the projector diagonal and set \(A_i=P_i+N_i\).  Every product \(A_w\) is upper
triangular with diagonal equal to that of \(P_w\).  Hence
\(\Tr A_w=\Tr P_w=\chi_m(w)\), while the \(A_i\) need not commute and the
module need not split.  The second statement follows because identical even
and odd traces cancel word by word.
\end{proof}

\Cref{fig:visibility-layers} is the operative interpretation of the collapse.
An implementation can look connected through its upper-triangular blocks,
but no such connection changes the observable color character.  Conversely,
the theorem does not label radical geometry meaningless for every purpose; it
says only that cyclic traces and the determinant considered here cannot use it
to select arithmetic atoms.
