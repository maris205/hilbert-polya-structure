# Artifact Schema — SD-C28

All CSV files are UTF-8, LF-terminated, headered, and deterministically
ordered. JSON uses sorted keys and a terminal newline.

## Finite word and memory ledgers

- `projector_word_ledger.csv`: every frozen projector product and trace.
- `radical_word_ledger.csv`: noncommuting upper-triangular extensions.
- `graded_word_ledger.csv`: common even/odd modules and block couplings.
- `hankel_syntactic_ledger.csv`: both empty-word conventions, exact ranks,
  syntactic dimensions, and finite trace lower bounds.
- `aggregate_adversary.csv`: 32 exact scalar-pencil powers and two oriented
  mixed-word witnesses.

## Complex and analytic ledgers

- `support_incidence_ledger.csv`: reduced-support exterior dimensions and
  Euler coefficient, with the nonstationary ownership field.
- `bar_hochschild_controls.csv`: color idempotents, separability checks, and
  the atomic `HH_0` survivor.
- `de_rham_local_controls.csv`: chain, power-supertrace, and characteristic
  quotient checks.
- `de_rham_tensor_word_ledger.csv`: individual pure and mixed word
  supertraces after tensoring color and analytic sectors.

## Controls and reproducibility

- `arbitrary_inventory_controls.csv`: full finite inventory sums and z=1
  products for seven families at three cutoffs.
- `marker_ownership_controls.csv`: digit and return marker firewall.
- `route_gate_summary.csv`: five frozen Route layers.
- JSON metadata, analysis, tests, double-run, integrity, and
  `SHA256SUMS.txt` complete the deterministic audit.

The six target-zero/root fields in Route YAML start with `not_applicable;`.
Target-zero use and Route-B permission are false. Provenance is paired and
two-stage pending until a later metadata-only commit-binding pass.
