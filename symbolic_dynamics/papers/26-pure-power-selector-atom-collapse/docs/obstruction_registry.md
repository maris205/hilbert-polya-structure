# Obstruction Registry — SD-C28

1. **Linear memory growth.** The trace completion has Hankel rank `m`; the
   literal nonempty language has rank `m+1`.
2. **Virtual character collapse.** Every finite even graded word-trace
   realization has one net one-dimensional character per color, modulo
   common sectors and dormant zero action.
3. **Radical invisibility.** Noncommuting triangular extensions alter matrix
   geometry but not word traces or determinants.
4. **Aggregate blindness.** Scalar commuting pencils cancel the transpose
   adversary while oriented mixed words retain supertraces `+1` and `-1`.
5. **Support ownership.** The exterior Euler selector is chosen after global
   word support is known and is not a stationary fixed fiber.
6. **Homological atomization.** The canonical separable color algebra has
   `HH_0=C^m` and zero positive Hochschild homology.
7. **Countable direct sum.** Coordinate projectors on `ell2(I)` are visibly
   the supplied disjoint inventory.
8. **Analytic ceiling.** The operator family is honest on its l1 domain but
   no same-object continuation, functional equation, or spectral carrier is
   constructed.

The finite theorem does not classify arbitrary infinite-dimensional traces.
