## 1. Executive boundary

The language `L_m={a_i^r}` is a regular cyclic language.  Classical results
already imply recognizability, virtual-character realization, semisimple
syntactic algebra, and rational zeta data.  Exterior-power automata and
alternating determinant cancellation are also prior art.  SD-C28 therefore
does not claim any of these mechanisms in general.

The bounded contribution is the explicit source-locked classification of
this coefficient: exact empty-word ranks, the color algebra, ordinary and
graded semisimplification, radical invisibility, the aggregate-word
counterexample, and the collapse of the Paper25 holomorphic tensor to the
disjoint supplied inventory.  No searched source states that complete chain
as one theorem.  It is nevertheless built from classical ingredients, so the
standalone algebraic novelty is moderate and the main value is programmatic.

## 2. Verified classical sources

### Recognizable series and minimal memory

- M.-P. Schützenberger, “On the Definition of a Family of Automata,”
  *Information and Control* 4 (1961), 245–270,
  DOI `10.1016/S0019-9958(61)80020-X`, supplies the foundational finite
  linear/weighted realization framework.
- J. W. Carlyle and A. Paz, “Realizations by Stochastic Finite Automata,”
  *Journal of Computer and System Sciences* 5 (1971), 26–40,
  DOI `10.1016/S0022-0000(71)80005-3`, and Berstel–Reutenauer's
  *Noncommutative Rational Series with Applications* (Cambridge, 2010,
  book DOI `10.1017/CBO9780511760860`) give the Hankel/minimal-realization
  boundary.
- Christophe Reutenauer, “Séries formelles et algèbres syntactiques,”
  *Journal of Algebra* 66 (1980), 448–483,
  DOI `10.1016/0021-8693(80)90097-6`, links rationality, syntactic algebras,
  central series, and semisimple character descriptions.

SD-C28's ranks `m` and `m+1` and its explicit diagonal syntactic algebra are
elementary specializations, not a new realization theory.

### Cyclic languages and virtual characters

- Jean Berstel and Christophe Reutenauer, “Zeta Functions of Formal
  Languages,” *Transactions of the AMS* 321 (1990), 533–546,
  DOI `10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0998123-X`, prove that a regular cyclic
  language has a characteristic series expressible as an integer linear
  combination of trace series and derive rational zeta consequences.
- Ariane M. Masuda, Luciane Quoos, and Benjamin Steinberg, “Character Theory
  of Monoids over an Arbitrary Field,” *Journal of Algebra* 431 (2015),
  107–126, DOI `10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.02.017`, give a monoid-character
  proof of the Berstel–Reutenauer virtual-character result.

Publisher metadata and abstracts were rechecked on 2026-08-14.  The broad
claim “a cyclic pure-power selector is a virtual trace” is therefore
classical.

### Exterior automata

Marie-Pierre Béal, “Puissance extérieure d’un automate déterministe,
application au calcul de la fonction zêta d’un système sofique,” *RAIRO.
Informatique Théorique et Applications* 29 (1995), 85–103, Numdam record
`ITA_1995__29_2_85_0`, constructs exterior automata and alternating
determinant formulas for sofic zeta functions.  No DOI was located.  SD-C28's
support-Euler coefficient is an exact special rule, but signed exterior
cancellation itself is not new.

### Character rigidity and radical invisibility

The finite-dimensional Brauer–Nesbitt principle is standard; Curtis and
Reiner, *Representation Theory of Finite Groups and Associative Algebras*,
Theorem 30.16 (1962; AMS Chelsea reprint 2006), is the authoritative book
boundary.  SD-C28 proves its required specialization directly by passing to
the finite combined image algebra and its semisimple quotient.  The paper
does not cite the principle as if it forced literal splitting: extension
blocks remain invisible to characters.

### Separable Hochschild homology

- Gerhard Hochschild, “On the Cohomology Groups of an Associative Algebra,”
  *Annals of Mathematics* 46 (1945), 58–67, DOI `10.2307/1969145`, is the
  foundational source for separability and Hochschild (co)homology.
- Jean-Louis Loday, *Cyclic Homology*, second edition, Springer, 1998,
  DOI `10.1007/978-3-662-11389-9`, supplies the standard bar/cyclic
  framework.
- Gonçalo Tabuada and Michel Van den Bergh, “Noncommutative Motives of
  Separable Algebras,” *Advances in Mathematics* 303 (2016), 1122–1161,
  DOI `10.1016/j.aim.2016.08.031`, records the relevant additive-invariant
  computations for separable algebras.

Over `C`, the application to `C^m` is exact: degree-zero Hochschild homology is
`C^m`, and positive degrees vanish.  The claim is not extended to arbitrary
semisimple algebras over imperfect fields.

## 3. Current categorical neighbors

- Paul Gustafson, Mee Seong Im, Remy Kaldawy, Mikhail Khovanov, and Zachary

  Defects,” *Journal of Geometry and Physics* 222 (2026), 105771,
  DOI `10.1016/j.geomphys.2026.105771`, is a current formal-language/TQFT
  neighbor.

These sources prevent a broad categorical-priority claim.  SD-C28's claim is
the explicit color-selector collapse after imposing the frozen arithmetic and
analytic ownership rules.
