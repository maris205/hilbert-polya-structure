# Round-2 Clues — Quarantined after SD-C28

These are research prompts, not claims of the present paper.

## 1. Source-derived infinite incidence

Replace supplied coordinate projectors by a countable complex generated from
divisibility or factorization incidence.  The admission test is stringent:
the complex must produce necklace-resolved pure-word coefficients before
abelianization and must not contain one primitive idempotent per target atom
at input.

## 2. Nonrecognizable controlled memory

The finite Hankel theorem forces memory growth with colors.  A genuine escape
must therefore justify infinite memory and its operator ideal, not merely take
larger finite cutoffs.  Candidate notions include nuclear countable
representations, relative determinants, or derived completions, but none is
licensed without a source-derived domain and exact cyclic trace theorem.

## 3. Divisor-poset Möbius complexes

A Möbius/incidence differential might cancel composite assemblies while
retaining atomic classes.  The danger is tautology: if the differential is
defined using primality or a precomputed Möbius table, A1 has already failed.
The differential must arise functorially from the full-shift semiring or an
equivalent source object.

## 4. Chain-level continuation

SD-C28's de Rham/color totalization is trace class only on the absolute
summability half-plane.  A future relative determinant must show why its
renormalization is owned by the same chain complex and why radicals or
contractible pairs do not smuggle in target coefficients.

## 5. Marker preservation

Any countable compiler must retain `u^ell(n)` at digit scale.  A one-step
Euler product in a return marker is allowed only after explicit first-return
induction.  Incidence cancellation may change coefficients; it may not erase
symbolic duration silently.

## 6. Forbidden shortcuts

- no target-zero or prime-table input;
- no claim from aggregate commuting traces alone;
- no word-dependent support fiber called stationary;
- no graded ratio called an ordinary determinant;
- no finite-cutoff compactness promoted to a countable operator theorem;
- no categorical packaging counted as a selector unless its observable
  algebra is computed.

## Paper27 admission statement

Produce a source-derived countable factorization/divisibility incidence
compiler with exact necklace and repetition control, an honest analytic
operator domain, and an explicit proof that it either avoids or necessarily
recreates the atom idempotents of `C^m`.
