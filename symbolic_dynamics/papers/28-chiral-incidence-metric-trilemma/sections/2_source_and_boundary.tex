\section{Source compiler and prior boundary}
\label{sec:source}

\subsection{Weighted divisibility realization}

Fix $\eta>1$ and let
\[
 \cH_\eta=
 \left\{x=(x_n)_{n\ge1}:
 \sum_{n\ge1}n^{2\eta}|x_n|^2<\infty\right\}.
\]
The divisibility zeta operator and its M\"obius inverse are denoted by
$Z$ and $M=Z^{-1}$.  In this range both are bounded and boundedly
invertible.  Let $E_n$ be the coordinate projection and define
\[
 q_n=ZE_nM.
\]
Then
\[
 q_nq_m=\delta_{nm}q_n,\qquad
 \sum_nq_n=I
\]
in finite cutoffs, and the individual $q_n$ extend as bounded
rank-one idempotents in the countable realization.

For the atom set $A$ of covers of the bottom element, define
\[
 D_s^A=\sum_{p\in A}p^{-s}E_p,
 \qquad
 T_s=ZD_s^AM=\sum_{p\in A}p^{-s}q_p.
\]
In the unmutated divisibility relation, $A=\Pp$.

\subsection{Holomorphic reflection}

Let $J$ be coordinate conjugation after normalizing the weighted
coordinate basis.  For source-real operators put
\[
 X^{\sharpop}=JX^*J.
\]
This reflection is complex-linear, reverses products, and preserves
all Schatten norms.  In unnormalized coordinates it is
\[
 X^{\sharpop}=W_\eta^{-1}X^{\mathsf T}W_\eta,
 \qquad W_\eta=\diag(n^{2\eta}).
\]
The reflected block
\[
 \cB_s=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 0&T_s\\T_{1-s}^{\sharpop}&0
 \end{pmatrix}
\]
is therefore holomorphic on every common Schatten domain.  When
$s=1/2+it$, the reflected leg equals $T_s^*$.

\subsection{Marker ownership}
\label{subsec:marker}

The critical theorem in this paper is the arithmetic specialization
$u=1$.  The inherited digit marker could be retained formally as
\[
 T_s(u)=\sum_pu^{\ell(p)}p^{-s}q_p.
\]
Two firewalls are essential.  First, the $r$-fold atom contribution is
$u^{r\ell(p)}$, not $u^{\ell(p)}$.  Second, when $|u|<1$ the Schatten
sum becomes
\[
 \sum_p|u|^{q\ell(p)}p^{-q\Re s},
\]
so the threshold changes.  This is a different regularized family,
not analytic continuation of the $u=1$ critical object.

\subsection{Literature boundary}

Rota's incidence-algebra framework supplies the M\"obius source
\citep{Rota1964}.  Oblique projections and biorthogonal Riesz systems
are classical operator geometry \citep{Tang2000}, and compatibility
with positive diagonal weights has been studied in the weighted
projection setting \citep{AntezanaEtAl2004}.  The square-root metric
transfer is the operator form of symmetric orthogonalization
\citep{Lowdin1950}.

Schatten ideals and modified determinants are standard
\citep{Simon2005}.  Higher determinant product formulas require
explicit correction terms \citep{BritzEtAl2020,KoutsonikosLesch2022};
we therefore keep a power-by-power deletion ledger rather than treating
$\detthree$ as an ordinary determinant.  Recent work on M\"obius
inversion over locally finite posets remains combinatorial rather than
chiral-operator theoretic \citep{Goh2026}.

The individual tools are classical.  The new content is the exact
source-locked synthesis: the minimal common strip, the divisibility
Gram formula, the isolated fourth frequency, the non-arithmetic
adversaries, and the full positive-metric collapse theorem.
