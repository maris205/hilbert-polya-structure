# Paper plan — Paper 28 / SD-C30

## Title

The Chiral Completion Trilemma for Möbius-Incidence Atoms:
Schatten-3 Motion versus Metric Collapse

## Central thesis

The source-derived reflected double has an exact \(\mathcal S_3\)
critical-line theory with genuine fourth-order spectral motion, but
the motion is a generic oblique-incidence effect; every positive metric
that removes the mixed Gram defect collapses the active sector to
independent coordinate atoms.

## Section architecture

1. **Introduction.** State the positive theorem and the genericity
   obstruction together. Declare the strict route failure.
2. **Prior boundary and source compiler.** Position incidence
   inversion, oblique projections, orthogonalization, and modified
   determinants. Define \(q_n,T_s,\sharp,\mathcal B_s\).
3. **Schatten-3 threshold.** Prove the common strip and critical-line
   self-adjointness; separate an operator family from a fixed operator.
4. **Native mixed-Gram geometry.** Derive \(G_{pp},G_{pq}\) and exact
   two/three-atom cutoff motion; state the \(B^2\) trace firewall.
5. **Third regularization and fourth motion.** Record deleted powers,
   prove the isolated positive frequency, and establish genuine
   spectral motion.
6. **Positive metric rigidity.** Classify common positive metrics,
   derive unitary atom coordinates, and compute the phase-free
   regularized product.
7. **Exact adversarial audit.** Present four fixtures, exact rational
   witnesses, deterministic hashes, and inference boundaries.
8. **Chiral completion trilemma and route evaluation.** Explain which
   pair of properties each completion retains and why the strict tuple
   is rejected.
9. **Conclusion.** Preserve the strongest theorem and identify the
   next discriminating problem.
10. **Appendices.** Supply continuity, Fourier-isolation, metric, and
    scope details.

## Writing constraints

- Never call the finite \(B^2\) cutoff the trace of the countable
  operator.
- Always say that \(B_t\) is a \(t\)-dependent self-adjoint family.
- Distinguish the original Euler determinant on \(\Re s>1\) from the
  chiral \(\det_3\) on \(1/3<\Re s<2/3\).
- Treat auxiliary \(z\)-zeros only as zeros of the displayed product.
- Keep Route B false and all target-zero fields absent or NA.
- Use exact controls as rejection evidence, not as RH evidence.
- State that the critical theorem is the \(u=1\) specialization. If
  \(T_s(u)\) is displayed, preserve \(u^{r\ell(p)}\) under repetition
  and do not use the altered \(|u|<1\) threshold as continuation.
