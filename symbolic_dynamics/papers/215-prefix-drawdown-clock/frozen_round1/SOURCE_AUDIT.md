## Specific collision checks and limitations

The author desk records exact original paths/read spans and source
failures. Root then read the concrete formulas in those originals before
admission. The conclusions are narrow:

- P93's running-maximum/drawdown expression is a statistic of a driven
  push/pop cocycle; S2's clipping is driven interval synchronization.
  Neither supplied the autonomous full-vector clock in the material read.
- P117's odd cyclic-run rule has strict two-cycles; same-length full
  binary drawdown has only a fixed recurrent state. This excludes that
  conjugacy, not all possible nonlinear factors.
- P132's prefix-majority map has n+1 fixed points, not one.
- P138's normalized length-three quotient is constant; binary drawdown
  sends 010 to 001. The specific quotient is not this map.
- P185's same-carrier height is n-1 for n>=3, versus drawdown's n.
- UPC is complement of an upper closure and satisfies F^4=F^2. The binary
  drawdown support map is upper closure minus the source, not that
  complement. A surjective factor preserves the iterate identity, whereas
  drawdown with height at least three violates it; this excludes that
  particular factor transport, not arbitrary encodings.
- Parking's monotone record heights warn against crediting static
  enumeration as novel. Its first-deficiency update is not the drawdown
  rule. Zigzag factor reversal and parallel-run erasure were also read at
  the gate; no admitted clock transport from them was established.

The binary restriction is prefix OR XOR the current bit. Its exact
ownership was expressly considered at the gate. Similar vocabulary,
triangularity or run erasure alone is not a proved collision. Conversely,
failure to locate an exact owner is not global novelty. A later direct
owner/factor discovery reopens admission under the received contract.
