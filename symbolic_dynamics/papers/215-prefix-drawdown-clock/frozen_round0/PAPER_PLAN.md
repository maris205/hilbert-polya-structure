## Narrative and sections

1. Abstract: exact clock, its sign-run mechanism, all-target reconstruction,
   sharp height and fibre extremum, with primitive attribution.
2. Setup: define the finite carrier/update, distinguish whole-vector
   autonomous feedback from the classical one-step drawdown statistic,
   state the initial-zero sign-run statistic and scope.
3. Clock: prove the local reflection recurrence; account for each input
   sign run, plateaus and saturation; deduce exact depth, recurrence,
   sharpness and all deepest states. One hand orbit illustrates the proof.
4. Inverse: reconstruct record heights at all target zeros; turn lower
   barriers into upper barriers; prove the binomial first-violation
   recurrence; deduce image and unique maximum fibre.
5. Scope/protocol: classical deductions and bounded ownership limits,
   fixed 24 boxes, complete future output, no finite execution claimed.

The main article includes all proofs; there is no deferred appendix lemma.
The two primary references are cited in the setup, and only those entries
belong in references.bib. No numerical table is warranted before execution.
