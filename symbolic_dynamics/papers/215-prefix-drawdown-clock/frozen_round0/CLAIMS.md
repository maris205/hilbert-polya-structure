# P215 admitted mathematical scope

Source-stage restatement of the received theorem contract, not a new
admission or immutable Round0. For n,q>=0 take X={0,...,q}^n and
F(x)_i=max_(j<=i)x_j-x_i, with the empty word fixed.

- C1: R is the number of nonzero sign runs of the differences of (0,x).
  R(Fx)=R(x)-1 for x!=0. Zero is uniquely recurrent and h(x)=R(x).
- C2: For n,q>0 the sharp height is n; equality means all n differences
  are nonzero and alternate signs. Singleton boundaries have height zero.
- C3: A nonempty-length target is in the image iff y_1=0. Its zero blocks
  have b_j=blockmax(y), B_j=prefixmax(b). Every predecessor is uniquely
  x_i=L_j-y_i with nondecreasing 0<=L_j<=q and L_j>=B_j.
- C4: For c_i=q-B_(k+1-i), A_0=1 and
  A_m=binom(c_m+m,m)-sum_(i=1)^(m-1)
  A_(i-1)binom(c_m-c_i+m-i,m-i+1), the fibre is A_k.
- C5: For n>0 the image size is (q+1)^(n-1). For n,q>0 the unique
  maximum fibre is zero's, of size binom(q+n,n). Singleton boundaries
  have their unique fibre of size one.

No all-time inverse atlas, arbitrary-poset extension, stochastic theorem,
global priority or universal no-factor theorem is claimed. The one-step
drawdown statistic and static barrier enumeration are explicitly owned
background. Every finite validation result remains pending execution.
