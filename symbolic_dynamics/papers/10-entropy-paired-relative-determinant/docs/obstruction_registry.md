# Obstruction Registry

## SD-O33 — fixed parity is not a repetition phase

The relative supertrace gives a^r-b^r at every repetition. A primitive
holonomy -1 gives a^r+(-1)^r b^r. They disagree at every even repetition,
so the negative rank sector cannot be reinterpreted as an orbit cocycle.

## SD-O34 — zero-sum versus positive orientation

For one fixed bounded local block pattern, extending the aggregate
difference to trace class on every Re(s)>0 requires coefficient sum zero.
An all-positive pattern has strictly positive sum and cannot satisfy the
constraint.

## SD-O35 — reflected motion with an empty divisor

The center curvature of log H is strictly positive, so H moves on the
critical line. Nevertheless, the relative quotient is invertible and
H(s,1) is zero-free throughout 0<Re(s)<1. Motion alone does not create a
spectral divisor.

## SD-O36 — bounded-local pairing proves too much

Offsets, orientation changes, random width-eight block pairings, and
nonprime increasing inventories all inherit paired trace class and
reflected motion. The mechanism uses bounded overlap rather than arithmetic.

## SD-O37 — relative quotient is not two absolute determinants

The quotient differs from identity by trace class on Re(s)>0, but its two
individual diagonal factors are not separately trace class on that entire
half-plane. Splitting R into independently asserted Fredholm determinants
would be invalid.
