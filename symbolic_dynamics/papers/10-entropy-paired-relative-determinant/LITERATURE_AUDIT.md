# Literature Audit

The manuscript uses verified primary sources from the Session-4 source base
plus one explicitly labeled MathOverflow collision. The bibliography is
cited-only.

| Key | Role |
|---|---|
| `bowenlanford1970` | Finite symbolic zeta/determinant background |
| `ruelle1976` | Transfer-operator and dynamical-zeta framework |
| `beal1995` | Exterior/alternating automata determinants |
| `deeley2018` | Signed symbolic Lefschetz and zeta structures |
| `kimleepark2003` | Flip-system zeta and symbolic involution context |
| `simon1977` | Infinite Fredholm determinants and trace ideals |
| `bayartkouroupis2024` | Primary collision: universality of the alternating prime Dirichlet series |
| `agno2014mathoverflow` | Web collision only: the scalar alternating prime Euler product |

The MathOverflow item is not used as theorem-level evidence. The paper does
not claim priority for alternating prime-rank coefficients, graded traces,
signed zeta functions, or relative Fredholm determinants. Its scoped result is
the tensor-atom relative-operator proof and the resulting Route-A obstruction
for SD-C12; it makes no unbounded novelty claim for that combination.
