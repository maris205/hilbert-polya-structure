\section{The relative half-plane theorem}
\label{sec:relative}

The analytic gain comes from estimating each entropy pair before summing over
the inventory.  For $a<b$ and $\RePart s>0$,
\[
 a^{-s}-b^{-s}=s\int_a^b x^{-s-1}\dd x.        \tag{4.1}
\]
The prime intervals $[p_{2n-1},p_{2n}]$ are disjoint.  No estimate of the odd
or even sector alone has this property.

\begin{theorem}[Relative trace-class half-plane]
\label{thm:trace-class}
Let $\sigma=\RePart s>0$.  The map
$s\mapsto K_s=D_s^+-B_s$ is holomorphic from the right half-plane to the trace
class $\cS_1(\cH_+)$.  More precisely,
\[
 \|K_s\|_1
 \le \frac{|s|}{\sigma}2^{-\sigma}.           \tag{4.2}
\]
For every integer $r\ge1$,
\[
 K_{r,s}=(D_s^+)^r-B_s^r\in\cS_1,
 \qquad
 \|K_{r,s}\|_1
 \le \frac{|s|}{\sigma}2^{-r\sigma}.          \tag{4.3}
 \label{eq:power-bound}
\]
Consequently the relative trace
\[
 \cS_r(s)=\Tr K_{r,s}
 =\sum_{n\ge1}\left(p_{2n-1}^{-rs}-p_{2n}^{-rs}\right)             \tag{4.4}
\]
exists for every repetition $r$, including $r=1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{eq:pair-integral} entrywise and sum absolute values:
\begin{align}
 \|K_s\|_1
 &\le |s|\sum_{n\ge1}\int_{p_{2n-1}}^{p_{2n}}
                       x^{-\sigma-1}\dd x \notag\\
 &\le |s|\int_2^\infty x^{-\sigma-1}\dd x
  =\frac{|s|}{\sigma}2^{-\sigma}.             \tag{4.5}
 \label{eq:pair-integral}
\end{align}
The bound is locally uniform in $s$.  Uniform limits in $\cS_1$ of the finite
diagonal analytic sums prove trace-norm holomorphy.  Since
$(D_s^+)^r=D_{rs}^+$ and $B_s^r=B_{rs}$, applying the same calculation to
$rs$ yields \cref{eq:power-bound}; the factor $r$ cancels in the integral.
The trace formula follows from the diagonal entries and trace-norm convergence.
\end{proof}

Equation \eqref{eq:pair-integral} also shows why the cancellation is local.
The proof uses the gaps inside the selected pairs but ignores every gap between
pairs.  Primality is not needed for the estimate once an increasing inventory
has been supplied.

\begin{theorem}[Exact relative Fredholm determinant]
\label{thm:relative-determinant}
On $\Omega_+$, $Q(s,z)-I$ is trace class and $R$ is holomorphic and nonzero.
It has the locally convergent product
\[
 R(s,z)=\prod_{n\ge1}
 \frac{1-zp_{2n-1}^{-s}}{1-zp_{2n}^{-s}},       \tag{4.6}
 \label{eq:relative-product}
\]
and the all-order trace expansion
\[
 \log R(s,z)=-\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{z^r}{r}\cS_r(s).                  \tag{4.7}
 \label{eq:relative-log}
\]
At $z=1$, these statements hold for every $\RePart s>0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The domain condition gives $\|zB_s\|<1$.  Algebraically,
\[
 Q(s,z)-I=-z(D_s^+-B_s)(I-zB_s)^{-1}.           \tag{4.8}
\]
The first factor is trace class by \cref{thm:trace-class}, and the second is
bounded and holomorphic.  Hence $Q-I\in\cS_1$.  Both $I-zD_s^+$ and $I-zB_s$
are invertible on $\Omega_+$, so $Q$ and its Fredholm determinant are
nonzero.  Finite diagonal truncations give the product; the trace-norm limit
passes their determinants to \eqref{eq:relative-product}.

For the logarithm, \cref{eq:power-bound} gives
\[
 \sum_{r\ge1}\frac{|z|^r}{r}|\cS_r(s)|
 \le \frac{|s|}{\sigma}
       \sum_{r\ge1}\frac{(|z|2^{-\sigma})^r}{r}<\infty.           \tag{4.9}
\]
The scalar logarithm series may therefore be summed in trace norm, producing
\eqref{eq:relative-log}.  Since $2^{-\sigma}<1$, $z=1$ is safe throughout the
right half-plane.
\end{proof}

The logarithmic derivative displays the arithmetic ledger without changing
clocks:
\[
 \partial_s\log R(s,1)
 =\sum_{p}\eps_p\sum_{r\ge1}(\log p)p^{-rs},
 \qquad
 \eps_{p_k}=(-1)^{k+1}.                         \tag{4.10}
\]
This series is understood through the entropy pairs in the proved half-plane.
It contains the correct primitive lengths and repetitions, but its orientation
already differs from the positive prime-power side.
