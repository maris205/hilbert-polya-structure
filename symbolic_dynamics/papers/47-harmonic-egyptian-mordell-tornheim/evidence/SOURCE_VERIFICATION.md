# P47 primary-source verification — writer preflight

Verification date: 2026-08-18 UTC.

This is a bibliography and ownership check for the manuscript.  It does not
repeat the frozen collision search, and it cannot turn a bounded search
absence into a priority result.  Publisher records, DOI metadata, and primary
repository records were checked before any citation was admitted.

## Verified records and permitted uses

1. **Leonard Tornheim**, “Harmonic Double Series,” *American Journal of
   Mathematics* 72(2) (1950), 303--314, DOI `10.2307/2372034`.
   The DOI record verifies the author, title, journal, volume, issue, and
   year; the frozen literature audit supplies the complete page range.
   Manuscript use: historical ownership of the depth-two harmonic sums now
   included in the Mordell--Tornheim family.  It is not cited as a source for
   the graph operator or its trace identity.

2. **L. J. Mordell**, “On the Evaluation of Some Multiple Series,”
   *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* s1-33(3) (1958), 368--371,
   DOI `10.1112/jlms/s1-33.3.368`.  The Oxford Academic article record and
   DOI metadata agree on the title, author, issue, date, and pages.
   Manuscript use: foundational ownership of the multiple sums bearing the
   Mordell--Tornheim name.

3. **David M. Bradley and Xia Zhou**, “On Mordell-Tornheim Sums and
   Multiple Zeta Values,” arXiv:1205.0037 (2012); journal reference
   *Annales des sciences mathématiques du Québec* 34(1) (2010), 15--23.
   The primary arXiv record verifies the authors, title, abstract, date, and
   journal reference.  The final bibliography deliberately cites this
   primary arXiv record; the journal reference is contextual metadata rather
   than the bibliographic form used by the manuscript.  Manuscript use:
   modern identities relating positive integer Mordell--Tornheim values to
   multiple zeta values; no ownership is transferred to the present operator
   realization.

4. **Hirofumi Tsumura**, “On Functional Relations between the
   Mordell--Tornheim Double Zeta Functions and the Riemann Zeta Function,”
   *Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society* 142(3)
   (2007), 395--405, DOI `10.1017/S0305004107000059`.  Cambridge Core and
   DOI metadata agree.  Manuscript use: analytic and functional-relation
   background for classical Mordell--Tornheim functions, not a source for
   the graph's phase diagram.

5. **Nikita Kalinin, Ernesto Lupercio, and Mikhail Shkolnikov**, “Residues
   of a Tropical Zeta Function for Convex Domains,” arXiv:2604.21709v2
   (2026).  The primary arXiv record verifies the title, authors, version,
   and scope.  The frozen audit identifies its Section 5.2 primitive
   boundary series with exponent pattern `(s,s;s)`.  Manuscript use: a
   distinct, prior-owned primitive Mordell--Tornheim realization; it is not
   the `(s,s;2s)` trace of the frozen harmonic graph.

6. **Barry Simon**, *Trace Ideals and Their Applications*, 2nd ed.,
   Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 120, American Mathematical Society,
   Providence, RI (2005), DOI `10.1090/surv/120`.  The official AMS record
   verifies the edition, series, volume, year, and the chapter on regularized
   determinants.  Manuscript use: standard Schatten, trace, Fredholm, and
   Hilbert--Carleman determinant machinery only.  Every specialized estimate
   and every graph-specific trace identity is proved in the manuscript.

## Claim-level firewall

- Egyptian-fraction reduction, the coprime-scale coordinates, and gcd
  extraction receive zero novelty credit.
- Classical Mordell--Tornheim identities and every primitive realization in
  general remain externally owned.
- The admissible contribution is the exact theorem package for one frozen
  looped graph: its two edge coordinates, strict ideal domains, loop-owned
  first trace, `(s,s;2s)` coprime second trace, and mixed cycles.
- Search-bounded absence is described only as such.  The manuscript never
  uses “first,” “novel,” or another priority formulation.
- Simon supports standard operator-ideal definitions.  The manuscript does
  not cite a general reference as proof of the exact walls `0,1/2,1`.

## Bibliography gate

The filtered `references.bib` may contain only the six records above unless
a later source is independently verified.  Every entry must be cited, every
source claim must fit its permitted use, and no `[VERIFY]` marker may survive
the final build.
