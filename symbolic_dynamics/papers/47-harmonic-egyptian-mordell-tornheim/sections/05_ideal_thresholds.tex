\section{Sharp Hilbert--Schmidt and trace-class thresholds}
\label{sec:ideal-thresholds}

The global edge coordinate now separates the scale $t$ from the coprime
shape $(a,b)$.  Since
\[
 mn=t^2ab(a+b)^2,
\]
the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm, whenever finite, is
\begin{equation}
 \|E_s\|_2^2
 =\zeta(2\sigma)
  \sum_{\substack{a,b\geq1\\(a,b)=1}}
  [ab(a+b)^2]^{-\sigma}.
 \label{eq:hs-exact}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Sharp Hilbert--Schmidt wall]
\label{prop:hs-wall}
The operator $E_s$ belongs to $\Schatten{2}$ if and only if
$\sigma>1/2$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
If $\sigma\leq1/2$, the diagonal terms from the loops $m=n=2t$ already
give the divergent subseries
$\sum_{t\geq1}(2t)^{-2\sigma}$ in the squared Hilbert--Schmidt sum.

If $\sigma>1/2$, the arithmetic--geometric mean inequality gives
$(a+b)^2\geq4ab$, and consequently
\begin{equation}
 [ab(a+b)^2]^{-\sigma}
 \leq4^{-\sigma}a^{-2\sigma}b^{-2\sigma}.
 \label{eq:hs-majorant}
\end{equation}
After dropping the coprimality restriction, the double majorant is
$4^{-\sigma}\zeta(2\sigma)^2<\infty$.  The scale factor in
\eqref{eq:hs-exact} is finite as well.  This proves sufficiency and the
strict endpoint.
\end{proof}

Trace class requires a different power of the scale variable.  Absolute
summability of the entries is sufficient, and the coprime coordinate gives
\begin{align}
 \sum_{m,n\geq1}|e_s(m,n)|
 &=\zeta(\sigma)
  \sum_{\substack{a,b\geq1\\(a,b)=1}}
  [ab(a+b)^2]^{-\sigma/2} \\
 &\leq 2^{-\sigma}\zeta(\sigma)^3,
 \label{eq:absolute-entry-bound}
\end{align}
where the last line again uses $(a+b)^2\geq4ab$ and drops coprimality.

\begin{proposition}[Sharp trace-class wall]
\label{prop:trace-wall}
The operator $E_s$ belongs to $\Schatten{1}$ if and only if $\sigma>1$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $\sigma>1$, \eqref{eq:absolute-entry-bound} is finite.  Expanding the
matrix as a sum of rank-one matrix units shows
$\|E_s\|_1\leq\sum_{m,n}|e_s(m,n)|$, so $E_s$ is trace class.

Conversely, for every trace-class operator $T$ and every orthonormal basis,
$\sum_m|\langle Te_m,e_m\rangle|\leq\|T\|_1$.  The loops of
$\mathcal G$ are exactly the even vertices, hence a hypothetical
trace-class $E_s$ would satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{m\geq1}|\langle E_se_m,e_m\rangle|
 =\sum_{t\geq1}(2t)^{-\sigma}<\infty.
 \label{eq:diagonal-obstruction}
\end{equation}
The series diverges for $\sigma\leq1$.  Together with the boundedness wall
from \cref{sec:bounded-compact}, this proves necessity for every parameter.
\end{proof}

The proofs also explain why the walls cannot be interchanged.  At
$\sigma=0$ the obstruction is unbounded row degree; at $\sigma=1/2$ it is
the squared loop scale; and at $\sigma=1$ it is the absolute diagonal.
Hilbert--Schmidt membership in the strip $1/2<\sigma\leq1$ never licenses an
ordinary Fredholm determinant.
