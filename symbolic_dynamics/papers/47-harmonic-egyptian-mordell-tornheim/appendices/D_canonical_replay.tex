\section{Canonical replay and provenance boundary}
\label{app:canonical-replay}

The finite results in \cref{sec:replay} were not transcribed from a plot.
A candidate-local extractor checks canonical JSON encoding, rejects
duplicate keys, verifies the State-A result ledger and all referenced
SHA-256 values, requires the exact twelve-field comparison schema, and
rejects any mutation survivor.  A second candidate-local generator renders
\cref{tab:canonical-replay} from that checked summary.  Both scripts have a
read-only check mode used after the final build.

The replay has three logically distinct layers:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item a direct evaluator tests $m+n\mid mn$ and constructs literal finite
  matrices;
  \item an independent parameter evaluator constructs coprime triples and
  divisor rows;
  \item an exact comparator checks supports, quotients, rows, finite trace
  powers, the negative minor, and the finite evidence type.
\end{enumerate}
The complete State-A output tree has SHA-256
\texttt{32852768\allowbreak{}0d533e34\allowbreak{}ce3aabc1\allowbreak{}7f2cf568\allowbreak{}8759b067\allowbreak{}4b7fc874\allowbreak{}0d0c2df3\allowbreak{}32b64c42};
the result ledger has SHA-256
\texttt{dba16171\allowbreak{}9ef85dee\allowbreak{}433a13aa\allowbreak{}14505ab6\allowbreak{}b0f5ff0f\allowbreak{}ef8c627e\allowbreak{}a39ddb4b\allowbreak{}f81bfe47}.
These identifiers bind the report to exact bytes.  They do not turn a
finite equality into a proof of a limit.

In particular, the phrase ``termwise finite scale cutoff'' means that, for
each fixed coprime pair, only scales whose two vertices lie below $N$ are
included.  Since that maximum scale depends on $(a,b)$, the finite sum must
not be rewritten with a common infinite $\zeta(2s)$ factor.  The latter
appears only after absolute convergence has been proved for the full
ordered-edge sum.

Finally, the protected writer intake records 91 authority/state nodes, 67
regular files and 24 directories.  The paper artifact is built in a
separate candidate tree.  Integrity, route, and mutation records are
reported as provenance; none is cited as a mathematical premise for
\cref{thm:main}.
