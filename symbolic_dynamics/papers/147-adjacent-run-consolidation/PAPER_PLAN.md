## Claim architecture

   identify its fixed class without claiming the classical Carlitz census.
2. Prove termination and the logarithmic upper bound by a backwards chain of
   newly produced equalities whose weights double at every generation.
3. Give the explicit binary cascade, with a remainder placed on the safe side,
   to attain the bound for every carrier size.
4. Prove the every-target inverse by the bijection between source maximal runs
   and adjacent-unequal target divisors; refine by source length.
5. Record exact falsification, internal/external collision boundaries, and the
   mandatory availability/ethics/contribution/conflict/funding/limitation
   statements.
