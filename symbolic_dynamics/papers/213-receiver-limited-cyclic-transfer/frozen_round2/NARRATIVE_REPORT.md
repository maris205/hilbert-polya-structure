# Narrative report — author source milestone

## Technical story

The receiver-limited current permits transfer only where both neighboring
masses are positive. After subtracting the invariant global minimum, zeros
therefore preserve the original run boundaries. Each nontrivial run loses
sites only from its left end and increases the mass at its original right
endpoint. This fixes the endpoint of every trajectory and gives a sharp
linear worst time, except on three sites where the surviving endpoint
doubles until saturation.

That temporal argument does not describe the predecessors of an arbitrary
target. For the inverse problem, weak-ascent/strict-descent words partition
sources uniquely. Valleys and strict descents are forced; a single ascent
edge also forces its peak. A longer ascent leaves one prepeak integer
interval, with no remaining global mass condition because the local
equations telescope. The interval product is thus an evaluated source
description, not an unevaluated current-feasibility predicate.

Each free interval needs at least three cyclic edges. This makes the
largest one-step fibre grow with degree $\lfloor n/3\rfloor$ at fixed $n$.
Isolated fixed-target spikes with two preceding zeros give a matching
construction. The argument proves the degree and explicit uniform bounds,
not the exact maximum for every finite mass or all maximizing targets.

## Retained claims

C1: every terminal state, fixed/recurrent classification and exact worst
clock, including all short-cycle and small-mass branches.
C2: all-target one-step predecessor bijection, disjoint interval-product
formula, and $O(n2^n)$ arithmetic-operation evaluation.
C3: exact fixed-target specialization and sharp largest-fibre growth degree.

The full proof is the evidence for these all-parameter statements.
Conservation, zero barriers, linear chamber solving, elementary dyadic
arithmetic, product counting and packing receive no independent novelty
credit. The bounded contribution is their exact resolution for this literal.

## Positioning and exclusions

Published conservative cellular-automaton and Burgers constructions are
background. The relevant Burgers current uses receiver vacancy, whereas the
present rule uses receiver mass. This literal distinction does not exclude
indirect encodings or claim global ownership. The correlated Burgers
neighbor is cited at the limited scope actually verified.

Historical UUC and MNA originals are explicitly deducted in SOURCE_AUDIT:
a binary uphill current with generic flux reconstruction, and changing-length
mergers of weakly increasing positive runs. P211's corrected carrier is
nondecreasing selfmaps of a finite chain, not a finite field. Neither P211
nor P212 is reviewed or changed here.

No all-time inverse, complete basin census, closed general pointwise clock,
exact finite-mass extremal classification or global novelty theorem is added.
