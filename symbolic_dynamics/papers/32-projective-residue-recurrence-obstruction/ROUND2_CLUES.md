# Round-two clues and Paper 33 obligation — Paper 32 / SD-C34

## What the second pass resolved

1. **The graph is not terminal.**  Every state keeps two outgoing modular
   transitions, and the cusp graph is recurrent across moduli.  The Paper 31
   prohibition is genuinely respected.
2. **Overlap alone is insufficient.**  Shared states do not create arithmetic
   selectivity when the return words come from universal presentation
   relations.
3. **A2 can be repaired independently of A1.**  The original uninduced
   graph-step operator is trace class on \(\operatorname{Re}s>2\), yet its
   determinant owns the wrong primitive ledger.
4. **Static source expressivity is not periodic emergence.**  The exact field
   defect detects primes but becomes a terminal projector when used to remove
   blocks.
5. **Cross-modulus recurrence has a sharp orientation dichotomy.**  Natural
   downward maps are transient; adding their reverses creates an infinite
   overlapping family of composite diamonds.

## Closed retries

The following moves do not reopen SD-C34:

- replacing \(S,R\) by another fixed finite-order modular presentation while
  retaining universal relation cycles;
- increasing the cutoff or trace order;
- changing positive roofs after seeing prime/composite labels;
- keeping only blocks with \(|X_n|=n+1\);
- orienting all residue maps downward and then crediting their transient
  computation as periodic structure;
- inducing on cusps or first return if the original edge marker changes;
- adding signs that cancel the audit range but lack a source-derived
  chain-level identity;
- moving to Route B or comparing target zeros.

## Paper 33 minimum obligation

Paper 33 must not switch to another residue map, terminal verifier, or static
field projector.  It may continue this family only through a **cycle-level,
source-natural quotient or twist of the same recurrent object**.

Required gates, in order:

1. **Same source and ownership map.**  Start from \(X_n\), the \(S,R\) edges,
   and cusp correspondences, or give an explicit functorial quotient map from
   that object.  Selected blocks are not a quotient.
2. **Universal-cycle annihilation before labels.**  Prove at chain level that
   the construction kills the presentation cycles \(S^2\), \(R^3\), and cusp
   diamonds for every modulus.  No coefficient may consult primality,
   factorization, \(|X_n|=n+1\), or an Euler table.
3. **Signs fixed before controls.**  A character, reduced homology,
   Steinberg-type module, Kac--Ward sign, or supertrace must arise from a
   frozen source symmetry.
4. **Complete surviving ledger.**  Before roofs, prove all surviving primitive
   or cycle-homology classes.  A positive candidate must show an infinite
   source-linked prime family and theorem-visible failure on prime powers,
   mixed composites, random actions, and matched finite rings.
5. **Matched-clone firewall.**  Isomorphic relabels must transport the
   quotient exactly.  Non-isomorphic finite rings and generic actions remain
   selectivity controls.
6. **Same-object analytic ownership.**  The uninduced quotient/twisted
   operator with the original marker must remain in a proved determinant
   class on an explicit half-plane.
7. **Immediate stop.**  If the quotient inserts the static field projector,
   leaves universal relation cycles, or cancels random actions equally, close
   the entire semiring-residue family.
