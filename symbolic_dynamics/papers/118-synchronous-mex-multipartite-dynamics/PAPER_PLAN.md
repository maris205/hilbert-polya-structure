# P118 paper plan

Status: PROVABLE AS STATED / ANONYMOUS INTERNAL MANUSCRIPT / EXTERNAL HOLD.

## One-sentence contribution

For unconditional synchronous open-neighbourhood mex on
\(K_{a_1,\ldots,a_k}\), exact labelled fibre weights connect the
part-size vector to a fully classified fixed/two-cycle quotient and yield
all global and orbitwise depth-zero, one, and two layers.

## Frozen claim boundary

The paper may claim:

1. one-round collapse to the part quotient
   \(T(y)_i=\operatorname{mex}\{y_j:j\ne i\}\);
2. two exact one-step fibre formulas, by inclusion–exclusion and by colour
   supports/labelled EGFs;
3. the exact quotient recurrent list: \(k!\) fixed permutations and the
   injection-indexed two-cycles;
4. every original colouring is recurrent after two rounds;
5. closed recurrent-target fibres and exact global/basin depth formulas; and
6. the routine finite zeta product after the cycle classification.

It must not claim a new Grundy colouring rule, asynchronous convergence,
arbitrary-graph results, polynomial-time evaluation, or external novelty.

## Proof routes

Route I translates mex into required absences and presences, then applies
bad-event inclusion–exclusion.  Route II fixes the part support of each named
colour and counts onto assignments by multivariate labelled EGFs.  These are
genuinely different exact derivations of the same fibre.

The temporal route uses the repeated-maximum image lemma and an explicit
support-reduction identity for \(T\).  A deterministic verifier separately
enumerates every labelled colouring on fifteen parameter lanes.

## Presentation

Lead with synchronous cycles and part-sensitive fibres, not with a colouring
algorithm.  Keep the \(q\ge\Delta+1\) extension because it clarifies surplus
colours; identify \(q=\Delta+1\) as canonical.  Use one compact
\(K_{1,2}\) table and no decorative figure.
