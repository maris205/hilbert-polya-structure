# P118 — synchronous mex dynamics on complete multipartite graphs

Status: anonymous internal manuscript; external release, novelty, priority,
and submission remain HOLD.

Every vertex simultaneously takes the least colour absent from its open
neighbourhood.  On a complete multipartite graph, one round collapses the
state to a part vector, but the labelled one-step fibre weights retain every
part size.  The paper proves two independent exact fibre formulas, the full
fixed/two-cycle quotient, two-round global recurrence, and every basin and
depth layer.

Artifacts include the manuscript, claim and narrative contracts, exact
control report, canonical verifier and transcript, and one compact asymmetric
table.  The direct 2003 serial-daemon mex rule and the 2018 synthesis of
distributed Grundy protocols receive full prior credit; the residual is only
the unconditional synchronous multipartite functional graph.
