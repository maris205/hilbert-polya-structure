## Closest external primary sources

### Luan--Khoi: generic weighted composition

D. M. Luan and L. H. Khoi, Weighted composition operators on weighted
sequence spaces, Contemporary Mathematics 645 (2015), 199--215,
DOI 10.1090/conm/645/12907.

The source owns the generic weighted-composition setting and its
boundedness/compactness questions on weighted sequence spaces. Under the
usual convention \(W_{\psi,\varphi}f=\psi(f\circ\varphi)\), the present
basis operator is naturally read on the adjoint side. Consequently none of
the following is credited as new: partition into fibers, square-summing
weights on a fiber, taking a supremum for boundedness, or sending fiber
norms to zero for compactness.

### Carlson: discrete spectrum and commutant context

J. W. Carlson, The spectra and commutants of some weighted composition
operators, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 317 (1990),
631--654, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0979958-6.

This source owns generic discrete weighted-composition spectral and
commutant context. The Phase-2 primary-source audit did not find in it the
specific infinite-branch saturated/modulo arithmetic pair, the two
constants \(C_{h,\sigma}\) and \(D_{h,\sigma}\), the primorial maximal-order
law, or the commutator phase diagram. The absence statement is limited to
the audited scope.

### Abanin--Mannanikov: quasi-Banach weighted composition

A. V. Abanin and R. S. Mannanikov, Weighted Composition Operators on
Quasi-Banach Weighted Sequence Spaces, Vladikavkaz Mathematical Journal 25
(2023), no. 4, 5--19, DOI 10.46698/x5057-2500-3053-t.

This source extends the generic boundedness and compactness setting for
weighted composition operators to quasi-Banach weighted sequence spaces.
It receives zero novelty credit. It does not supply the present paired
arithmetic retractions, primorial projection law, two Weyl constants,
normal-similarity split, or self-commutator wall.

### de Weger--van de Woestijne: power-free parts

B. M. M. de Weger and C. E. van de Woestijne, On the power-free parts of
consecutive integers, Acta Arithmetica 90 (1999), 387--395,
DOI 10.4064/aa-90-4-387-395.

This is a primary arithmetic source for power-free-part terminology and
nearby Diophantine uses. The \(h\)-free set, radical at \(h=2\), and the
idea of removing \(h\)th powers receive zero object novelty.

## Delete-shared-method test

Delete every paragraph whose only content is weighted composition, rank-one
fiber algebra, generic Schatten theory, generic regularized determinants,
oblique-projection philosophy, or free-UFD methodology. What remains is a
coherent arithmetic theorem:

- exact paired fiber masses and different bounded domains for every \(h\);
- a common \(h\)-free cyclic ledger;
- saturated similarity iff \(\sigma>1\) versus modulo similarity iff
  \(\sigma>1/h\);
- an exact primorial maximizing family and three asymptotic regimes;
- a positive generalized Dirichlet series with explicit holomorphic strip
  and residue, paired with the modulo and eigenvalue Weyl laws;
- strict commutator walls, including a distinct \(h=2\) necessity witness.

The \(h=2\) saturated operator by itself fails this deletion test and remains
STOP_GENERIC_SPECIALIZATION. The all-\(h\) pair passes at preauthority stage.
