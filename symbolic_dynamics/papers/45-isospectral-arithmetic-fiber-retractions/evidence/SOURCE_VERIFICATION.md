# Primary-source verification for the writer stage

Verification date: 2026-08-18 UTC.

This writer-stage check revalidated bibliographic identity and the narrow
ownership statements needed by the manuscript. It did not rerun the earlier

## Verified records

1. **D. M. Luan and L. H. Khoi**, “Weighted composition operators on
   weighted sequence spaces,” *Contemporary Mathematics* 645 (2015),
   199--215, DOI `10.1090/conm/645/12907`.
   - Primary/publisher boundary checked through the AMS volume record; an
     author-hosted publisher offprint was used to verify the abstract.
   - Verified scope: invariance, boundedness, compactness, compact
     difference, closed range, and essential norm on weighted complex
     sequence spaces.
   - Manuscript use: zero-credit generic weighted-composition context.

2. **J. W. Carlson**, “The spectra and commutants of some weighted
   composition operators,” *Transactions of the American Mathematical
   Society* 317 (1990), 631--654, DOI
   `10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0979958-6`.
   - AMS journal identity and the original-paper abstract/offprint were
     checked.
   - Verified scope: weighted composition on discrete `L^2` spaces, with
     spectrum and commutant results under hypotheses on the symbol.
   - Manuscript use: zero-credit discrete spectral context, not an owner of
     the paired arithmetic theorem.

3. **A. V. Abanin and R. S. Mannanikov**, “Weighted composition operators
   on quasi-Banach weighted sequence spaces,” *Vladikavkaz Mathematical
   Journal* 25(4) (2023), 5--19, DOI
   `10.46698/x5057-2500-3053-t`.
   - Primary MathNet/journal record checked at
     `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/vmj880`.
   - Verified scope: boundedness, compactness, compact differences, closed
     range, and essential-norm questions for weighted `l^p`, `0<p<infinity`.
   - Manuscript use: zero-credit quasi-Banach extension context.

4. **B. M. M. de Weger and C. E. van de Woestijne**, “On the power-free
   parts of consecutive integers,” *Acta Arithmetica* 90 (1999), 387--395,
   DOI `10.4064/aa-90-4-387-395`.
   - Primary IMPAN journal record checked at

     the original paper was checked for its definition of the `k`th
     power-free part.
   - Manuscript use: zero-credit terminology and arithmetic context only.

## Standard-tool location checks

These are source-location checks, not related-work ownership claims.

- The official Springer chapter record for Korevaar identifies Chapter III,
  “Complex Tauberian Theorems,” as pp. 117--176; its contents place
  “The Wiener--Ikehara Theorem” in Section III.4 beginning on p. 124.  The
  manuscript cites the supporting pp. 124--127 and states the exact
  Mellin--Stieltjes hypotheses it uses.
- The official AMS record and endmatter for Simon's second edition identify
  Chapter 9, “Regularized Determinants and Renormalization in Quantum Field
  Theory,” as pp. 75--80.  The manuscript reproduces the precise product it
  uses and separately proves membership in its legal Schatten class.
- Author-hosted Cambridge chapter PDFs for Montgomery--Vaughan were checked
  directly.  Theorem 2.7(e), p. 50 is the Mertens product estimate, and
  Theorem 6.9, p. 179 is the quantitative prime number theorem; the
  subcritical prime sum follows from the latter by partial summation.
