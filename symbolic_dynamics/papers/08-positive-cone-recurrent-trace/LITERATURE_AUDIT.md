# Paper08 Literature Audit

Only primary sources used in the manuscript are included.

- Bowen--Lanford (1970): determinant zeta for symbolic shifts.
- Ruelle (1976): transfer-operator zeta framework.
- Parry--Pollicott (1990): symbolic periodic-orbit zeta and group twists.
- Kassel--Reutenauer (2014): identity-word coefficients and zeta functions of
  matrices over free-group algebras.
- Clair--Mokhtari-Sharghi (2002): convergence of graph zeta functions.
- Guido--Isola--Lapidus (2009): traced graph zeta/Fuglede--Kadison setting.
- Fuglede--Kadison (1952): positive determinant in finite factors.
- Brown (1986): spectral distribution of nonnormal operators in the type-II
  setting.

These sources establish the component theories. They do not provide the
SD-C10 combination of tensor-prime atoms, a recurrent entropy-neighbor
grammar, a directed-positive cocycle, exact tau-Euler extraction, and the
quadratic chiral backtracking obstruction.

The paper therefore claims a source-locked synthesis and its theorems, not
the invention of group-cocycle zeta functions, canonical group traces,
Fuglede--Kadison determinants, or Brown measure.
