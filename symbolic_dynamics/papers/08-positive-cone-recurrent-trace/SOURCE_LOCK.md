# SD-C10 Source Lock

- **Primary family:** countable-state Symbolic Dynamics with a group cocycle.
- **Atoms:** tensor indecomposable full shifts, entropy ordered.
- **Base grammar:** a loop at every atom and both arrows between adjacent atoms.
- **Loop roof:** `log p_n`; loop label: identity.
- **Cross roofs:** source and target endpoint roofs, each potential `-log 2`.
- **Cocycle:** each directed cross edge has its own positive free generator;
  reverse directions are independent, not inverses.
- **Function algebra:** `B(ell^2(At)) bar-tensor L(F_infinity)`.
- **Trace:** matrix trace tensor canonical group trace.
- **Transfer:** diagonal loop weight plus both adjacent endpoint-average weights.
- **Euler determinant:** normalized analytic trace-series dynamical
  determinant in `Re(s)>1`; it agrees with the local operator determinant on
  the small-norm identity component, with no global invertibility claim.
- **Chiral object:** `B_t=[[0,T_t],[T_t*,0]]`, `s=1/2+it`.
- **Regularization:** noncommutative `det_3` where defined.

Allowed data are tensor multiplication, entropy, exact word reduction,
noncommutative trace/Schatten calculations, and verified primary literature.
Riemann zeros, fitted scales, hand-inserted von Mangoldt weights, fitted
counterterms, and another primary system family are forbidden.

Claim boundary: exact recurrent-base tau-Euler ledger on the Euler half-plane;
no analytic continuation, unified divisor, fixed Hilbert--Polya generator, or
Route-B promotion.
