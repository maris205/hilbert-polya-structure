# Paper08 Round-2 Clues

## RC-08-01 — inverse phases with local sign reversal

A self-adjoint carrier naturally makes reverse transport the inverse of
forward transport, while SD-C10 proves that this restores a positive
quadratic backtrack. A future external carrier would need an additional
intrinsic sign-reversing rule without deleting the target divisor. Record only;
do not construct it in Session 4.

## Same-family Paper09 branch

A holomorphic doubled free-semigroup transfer with separate positive
alphabets for the `s` and `1-s` channels remains Symbolic Dynamics and is not
a round-two clue. Its first task is a common Schatten-domain theorem for a
relative analytic tau determinant.
