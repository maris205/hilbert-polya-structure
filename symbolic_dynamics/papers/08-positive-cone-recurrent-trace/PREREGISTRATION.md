# Paper08 Preregistration

## Hypothesis

A positive-cone cocycle can retain recurrence in the tensor-prime base while
the canonical trace deletes every mixed periodic lift. Self-adjointization is
expected to restore a positive inverse-backtracking obstruction.

## Go gates

1. Base grammar is irreducible and aperiodic.
2. Mixed base cycles exist but have nonidentity positive monodromy.
3. Every traced power equals the prime-power ledger.
4. The analytic traced determinant equals the Euler product in a rigorous
   nonempty domain.
5. Noncommutative Schatten domains are explicit.
6. Chiral motion or a sharp chiral obstruction is derived from the same object.

## Stop gates

- the Euler determinant has no continuation beyond its honest half-plane;
- inverse closure leaks a positive mixed term;
- regularization deletes the first leaked term;
- analytic, Fuglede--Kadison, Brown, or chiral determinant coordinates are
  spliced as if they were one object;
- no fixed generator or target counting law exists.

## Controls

- shared abelian-positive label;
- randomized directed-positive labels;
- inverse/time-reversal labels;
- trivial labels;
- composites, random masses, and shuffled atom order;
- exact two-atom word and trace algebra.
