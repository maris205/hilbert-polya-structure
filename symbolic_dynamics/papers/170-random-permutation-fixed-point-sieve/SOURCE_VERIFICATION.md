## Method

Each retained bibliography record was checked against its DOI resolver
metadata and an independent primary preprint record.  Searches targeted the
literal repeated fixed-set intersection, exact labelled endpoints, and total
generator-cycle marks conditioned on an exact common-fixed endpoint.  A
search non-hit is recorded only as `owner-thin`; it is not affirmative
evidence of priority.
