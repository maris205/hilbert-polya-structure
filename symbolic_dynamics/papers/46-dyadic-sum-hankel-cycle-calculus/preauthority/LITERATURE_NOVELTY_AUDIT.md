## Frozen search object

The exact object searched was the infinite Dirichlet-weighted matrix

$$
H_s(m,n)=\mathbf 1_{\{m+n=2^a,\ a\ge1\}}(mn)^{-s/2}
$$

on \(\ell^2(\mathbb N)\), with loops retained, together with:

- the sharp bounded/compact, \(S_2\), and \(S_1\) thresholds;
- the exact \(v_2\)-orthogonal direct sum;
- legal \(\det_2\) and trace-power factorization;
- the complete odd/even solution theorem for cyclic equations
  \(n_i+n_{i+1}=2^{a_i}\).

Searching for any one ingredient alone was not treated as evidence that the
combined package is new.

## Publication firewall

Admissible contribution:

1. the exact arithmetic phase diagram with independent endpoint proofs;
2. the \(v_2\)-orthogonal self-similarity;
3. the legal trace/\(\det_2\) block product;
4. the complete odd/even cyclic solver.

Forbidden contribution:

- generic Peller theory or Schur tests;
- Guo's finite determinant regularity;
- Alekseyev's finite distinct-label optimization;
- an unproved all-\(S_p\) theorem;
- a second paper obtained by changing the additive support;
- rational-prime selectivity or any Hilbert–Pólya claim.
