# Figure plan — P190 Round 0

No figure is used.  The local filter, cyclic good-run condition, target-gap
product, and two-dimensional spectral block are more legible as adjacent
equations.  A decorative cellular-automaton diagram would add no evidence and
would consume space needed for the `n=1`, `m=1,2`, and matrix-direction
boundaries.

show one inverse-compatible coloured run eroding from its right boundary and
the corresponding nonzero-anchor/zero-gap decomposition.  It must not replace
the quantified normal form or the labelled fibre formula.
