# Literature and Novelty Audit — SD-C26

**Search date:** 2026-08-14  
**Scope:** finite and countable symbolic coding, renewal and coded shifts,
Kraft--McMillan constraints, graph/Fredholm zeta functions, prime-language
recognition, and analytic transfer-space boundaries.

## Closest primary collisions

1. Keller, “Circular Codes, Loop Counting, and Zeta-Functions,” *JCTA*
   56 (1991), DOI 10.1016/0097-3165(91)90023-A, directly joins circular
   codes, loop ledgers, and zeta functions.
2. Béal and Perrin, “Codes and Sofic Constraints,” *TCS* 340 (2005),
   DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2005.03.033, already develops Kraft--McMillan theory
   under sofic constraints. Paper24 therefore claims no first symbolic
   Kraft theorem.
3. Hong, “Zeta Functions and Periodic Orbit Structure of Renewal Systems,”
   *ETDS* 31 (2011), DOI 10.1017/S0143385710000015, is the closest renewal
   determinant collision.
4. Béal, Perrin, and Restivo, “Unambiguously Coded Shifts,” *EJC* 119
   (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.ejc.2023.103812, sharpens the coded-shift boundary.
5. Bowen--Lanford (1970), DOI 10.1090/pspum/014/9985, and
   Berstel--Reutenauer (1990), DOI
   10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0998123-X, provide the standard graph and formal
   language determinant setting.
6. de Jong, *ETDS* (2026), DOI 10.1017/etds.2026.10313, treats least-period
   realization and zeta classification. No broad priority claim about
   prime period sets is made here.

## Analytic and computational boundaries

Sarig’s countable Markov thermodynamic formalism (DOI
10.1017/S0143385799146820), Deitmar’s determinant formula for infinite
graphs (DOI 10.1137/140957925), and Simon’s trace-ideal theory delimit the
whole-operator language. Mayer’s nuclear holomorphic transfer operator
(DOI 10.1007/BF02473355) is the decisive warning: noncompactness on the
natural graph-coordinate space is not a universal function-space no-go.

Hartmanis--Shank (DOI 10.1145/321466.321470) rules out finite-automaton
recognition of binary primes, while Pratt (DOI 10.1137/0204018) supplies
succinct non-finite-state prime certificates. Thus Paper24 does not infer
an arithmetic impossibility from code length alone.

## Claim boundary

Fully classical and not claimed as new:

- Kraft--McMillan inequalities and their sofic extensions;
- circular/renewal code zeta functions;
- graph determinant and formal-language zeta expansions;
- existence and nonexistence results for various period sets;
- trace-ideal compactness criteria;
- finite-automaton limitations for binary prime recognition.

The only claimed contribution is the source-locked conjunction:

> finite visible separation + positive literal prime-only recurrence +
> total roof \(\log p\) + compact whole natural graph adjacency.

Within that class, shared recurrence produces mixed primitive roots, while
separated logarithmic codes force noncompactness. The countable length-one
escape is explicitly classified as an arbitrary-inventory control. No
searched primary source was located that states this exact trilemma, but no
first-ever or universal impossibility claim is made.

## Excluded successor classes

Signed, graded, matrix-valued, nonlocal, infinite-alphabet, nonstationary,
and anisotropic analytic mechanisms are outside the theorem. The most
important immediate successor is a holomorphic de Rham graded transfer:
it can repair local nuclearity, but must be tested for all-repetition
ownership, mixed-word flooding, and cohomological collapse.
