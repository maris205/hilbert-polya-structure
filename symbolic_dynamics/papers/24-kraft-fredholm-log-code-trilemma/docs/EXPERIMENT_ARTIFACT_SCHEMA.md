# Artifact Schema — SD-C26

All CSV files are UTF-8, LF-terminated, headered, and deterministically
ordered. JSON files have sorted keys and a terminal newline.

## Finite-code and operator artifacts

- `finite_code_counting.csv`: finite alphabet, marker, capacity, cyclic
  separation, record length, prefix/Kraft controls, and target-call fields.
- `disjoint_cycle_witnesses.csv`: exact positive roof shares, rendered
  singular values, universal lower bounds, block-(S_1) bounds, and theorem
  labels.
- `finite_roof_inventory.csv`: formal rational rank of audited prime-log
  generators against a smaller finite roof inventory.
- `marker_firewall.csv`: original graph degree versus standard degree one,
  induction flag, and same-object flag.

## Shared-recurrence artifacts

- `shared_prime_pair_firewall.csv`: distinct-prime products and exact
  unique-factorization contradictions to a shared positive ledger.
- `shared_trie_closure.csv`: node/edge census, exact loop clock, positive
  return roof, and concrete noncompactness witness.
- `mixed_primitive_ledger.csv`: primitive necklaces of two through five
  returns and the connected/disconnected gate.
- `finite_trie_determinant_checks.json`: exact SymPy determinant and `1-F`
  comparison for every frozen encoder.
- `factorization_renewal_controls.csv`: mixed returns, product collisions,
  and renewal determinant form.
- `finite_prefix_stationarization.csv`: finite-prefix versus stationary-union
  compactness and mixed-level-state controls.

## Selectivity controls

- `diagonal_escape_controls.csv`: exact rational (S_1) prefixes and
  determinants for every inventory; all rows are selector-tautological.
- `arbitrary_inventory_controls.csv`: disjoint, shared, and diagonal
  architectures on prime, composite, square, Fibonacci, matched random,
  matched hash, and arbitrary decidable inventories.
- `source_oracle_certificate.json`: candidate/evaluator separation and static
  forbidden-call audit.

## Reproducibility and Route artifacts

- `run_parameters.json`, `environment_lock.json`, `theorem_ledger.json`,
  `summary.json`, `analysis_summary.json`, `test_summary.json`,
  `double_run_certificate.json`, `integrity_audit.json`, and
  `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- `route_gate_summary.csv` and
  `evaluations/route_a/SD-C26/2026-08-14.yaml` use the strict frozen tuple.

The six target-zero/root fields in the YAML are strings beginning with
`not_applicable;`; `target_zero_data_used` and
`route_b_invocation_allowed` are false. Provenance uses the documented
pending-or-sealed two-stage schema.
