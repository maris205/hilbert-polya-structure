\section{Prior work and claim boundary}

Kraft--McMillan theory constrains uniquely decipherable codes
\citep{mcmillan1956}; its extension inside sofic constraints is already
known \citep{bealperrin2005}.  We therefore use Kraft theory as an ingredient,
not as novelty.  Circular codes and renewal systems already provide
determinant and loop-counting formulas
\citep{keller1991,hong2011,bealperrinrestivo2024}.  Countable Markov
thermodynamic formalism \citep{sarig1999} and weighted infinite-graph
determinants \citep{deitmar2015} likewise delimit the analytic terminology.

Nor do we claim that symbolic systems cannot realize prime period sets:
modern realization results are substantially broader
\citep{dejong2026}.  Direct prime in symbolic dynamics refers to
topological product indecomposability, a different notion
\citep{kopra2023}.  Automata do not recognize binary primes
\citep{hartmanisshank1968}, but short non-finite-state certificates exist
\citep{pratt1975}; hence code length alone is not an arithmetic no-go.

The defensible statement is narrower: no searched primary source combines
finite visible separation, a positive literal prime-only orbit ledger, total
roof $\log p$, and the whole natural graph-coordinate Fredholm gate.  We
make no priority claim beyond that conjunction.  We also do not infer a
universal operator-space obstruction from the natural $\ell^2(V)$ result.
