### Abstract

State the construction obligation, the four-hypothesis incompatibility,
the shared-trie alternative, and the only surviving countable diagonal
control.  Include the exact 35/35 audit result and the scope boundary.

### 1. Introduction

Motivate the move from filtering unary/factorial cycles to constructing a
new logarithmic recurrent grammar.  State the trilemma early.  Place the
hero figure after the contributions and distinguish connected primitive
orbits from disconnected determinant selections.

### 2. Prior boundary and literature

Position finite coding/Kraft--McMillan, graph zeta and Fredholm trace
logarithms, countable Markov/renewal systems, commuting words, and S-adic
stationarization.  Make the novelty claim model-specific and explicitly
exclude universal symbolic no-go language.

### 3. Source lock and coding lemma

Define the full-shift semiring skeleton, stationary graph, finite local code,
positive roof, connected prime ledger, and arbitrary-inventory firewall.
Prove the finite-code counting lemma.

### 4. Connected primitive purity

Prove vertex-disjointness, the no-shared-core corollary, and simplicity of
prime cycles.  Explain exactly where positivity rules out cancellation.

### 5. Kraft--Fredholm incompatibility

Prove noncompactness via a weakly null basis sequence.  State the main
trilemma, finite-roof-inventory corollary, product-weight form, and exact
graph-marker corollary.  Include the private-cycle side of Figure 1.

### 6. Renewal and factorization obstruction

Prove that commutative multiplication cannot inject into temporal free-word
concatenation.  Derive the prefix-trie determinant (1-F), explain mixed
primitive necklaces, and contrast connected cycles with disconnected Euler
factor selections.

### 7. Three candidate-family audits

Audit binary/Euclidean tableaux, factorization renewals, and the frozen
S-adic stationary union.  Separate what each family achieves from the exact
gate at which it stops.  Use Figure 2 as the decision tree.

### 8. Exact implementation audit and controls

Describe target-blind encoders, positive roof allocations, exact symbolic
trie determinants, primitive-necklace census, factorization collisions,
finite-prefix stationarization, and diagonal matched inventories.  Report
sealed counts only.  State that finite computation audits formulas and does
not prove the infinite theorem.

### 9. Route-A evaluation and conclusion

Give the strict tuple, explain A0 through A4, preserve Route-B lock, and
identify signed/matrix connected cancellation as Paper25's smallest
admissible obligation.

### Appendices

Give detailed proofs, finite-closure identities, sharp countercontrols,
source-integrity rules, and a full claims/boundaries ledger.
