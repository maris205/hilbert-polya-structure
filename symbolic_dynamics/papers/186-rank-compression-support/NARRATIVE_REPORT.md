**Lifecycle:** `OWNER_AMBER / HOLD_EXTERNAL`

The paper starts from one literal surprise: subtracting the position rank
from a sorted subset makes its consecutive gaps decrease by one, and taking
support deletes exactly the gaps that reach zero.  That converts an opaque
set map into independent erosion of an ordered positive gap word.

The story then advances in three theorem steps:

1. the gap erosion is pointwise and valid at every time, yielding the exact
   maximum-gap clock, all singleton basins, and the unique deepest state;
2. every time-target has a unique long-gap skeleton, while arbitrary short
   gap words fill `r+1` slots, yielding a complete coefficient-sum fibre;
3. bounded-gap words enumerate every depth shell, with the Fibonacci first
   image as the `t=1` shadow of the all-time image formula.

Strict/weak sequence shifts, stars and bars, beta sets, Fibonacci summation,
and generic finite-map spectral bookkeeping are explicitly subtracted.  The
residual is the conjunction created by feeding the support back into the
same rank subtraction.  A bounded search found no direct iterative owner;
that is recorded only as a non-hit.  The manuscript makes no novelty,
priority, or release claim.
