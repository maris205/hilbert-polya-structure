# Narrative report — P114

The early anomaly is that a globally nonuniform absorber has a two-parameter
local indegree law.  Once the target forest has `m` vertices and `s` non-root
leaves, all other shape information disappears from its one-step fibre.
This local collapse coexists with strongly root-set-dependent global basins.

The paper resolves that tension in two stages.  The metric route proves that
parallel deletion is exactly a height clock and isolates the Hamilton paths
as the deepest shell.  The enumerative route first counts whole basins by a
Cayley determinant, then keeps time with nested labelled-tree EGFs, and
finally derives every local fibre by inclusion–exclusion.

The defensible residual package beyond the individually standard ingredients
is only their endpoint-indexed assembly: exact basins, all temporal CDFs and
shells, and the elementary two-parameter local-fibre collapse, together with
the consequent periodic census.  Parallel `RAKE`, height pruning,
labelled-height EGFs, Cayley/all-minors counts, inclusion–exclusion,
absorption/zeta conversion, and Hamilton-path extremality receive zero
priority credit.  Owner and priority claims remain externally held.
