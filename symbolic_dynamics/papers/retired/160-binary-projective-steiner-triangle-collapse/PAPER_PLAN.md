# Paper plan — P160

## Claim spine

1. Define the classical binary-projective Steiner quasigroup and subtract its
   ownership explicitly.
2. Partition ordered triples into diagonal, two-equal, block, and nonblock
   strata.
3. Prove the exact orbit behavior and count all four strata.
4. Solve the block inverse equations with one free vector and three forbidden
   values; derive the codomain-wide fibre law.
5. Derive symmetry orbits, weak components, fibre moments, and rank recovery.
6. Record stable images, iterate-fixed counts, and zeta as corollaries.
7. State computational and ownership limitations.

## Claim ceiling

The contribution candidate is only the complete functional graph plus the
explicit target-fibre atlas for this literal map.  The carrier, operation,
projective lines, Steiner identities, quasigroup CA, and generic zeta
conversion are zero-credit background.

## Mandatory boundaries

- `r=2`: no depth-one states and block fibre `N-2=1`.
- `r=3`: first nonblocks and first nontrivial block fibres.
- “`N(N-1)` three-cycles” counts cycles, not states.
- the fibre formula holds for every positive iterate because nonblock sources
  themselves have no predecessors.
