# Narrative report — P160

The state is an ordered triple of nonzero vectors in `F_2^r`.  Each new
coordinate is the Steiner product of the other two.  The idempotent branch
acts only when two coordinates agree; otherwise the rule is ordinary vector
addition.

That small case distinction completely organizes the dynamics.  Diagonals
are fixed.  If two entries agree, the singleton and repeated value transform
around a strict three-cycle.  If all entries differ, one step replaces the
triple by its three pairwise sums, whose total is zero.  It is therefore a
projective-line block and is fixed.  The first rank has no nonblocks; from the
Fano plane onward a depth-one shell appears.

The stronger result is target-resolved.  A block `(x,y,z)` has source family
`(t,t+z,t+y)`, where exactly `0,y,z` are forbidden.  Thus it has `N-2`
sources: itself and `N-3` Garden-of-Eden nonblocks.  Diagonal and two-equal
targets have one source and nonblocks have none.  This inverse calculation,
not the elementary collapse alone, is the main residual after the classical
Steiner carrier and operation are subtracted.

The same census decomposes the graph into isolated diagonals, isolated
three-cycles, and block-centered stars.  It yields every fibre moment and a
small identifiability corollary: the maximum fibre is `N-2`, so the unlabelled
fibre multiset recovers `r=log_2(max fibre+3)`.

The manuscript is anonymous and internal.  It claims neither a new Steiner
system nor a general quasigroup theorem, and a bounded exact-map search is not
treated as novelty or priority evidence.
