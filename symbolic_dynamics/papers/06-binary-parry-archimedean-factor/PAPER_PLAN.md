# Paper 06 Plan

## Central claim

The minimal binary full shift supplies, through its trivial and sign
representations, an exact Euler determinant and an internally generated
Archimedean Gamma factor. Their product advances Route A to a partial A3
symbolic completion, while a general chiral no-motion theorem blocks the
naive route to a vertical spectrum.

## Section map

1. **Introduction:** state SD-C08 and the exact claim boundary.
2. **Prior symbolic ingredients:** position the construction relative to
   transfer determinants, Parry measures, and Paper05.
3. **Minimal binary source:** prove the intrinsic selection of \(F_2\) and
   the multiplicity-free trivial/sign decomposition.
4. **Two-sector factorization:** derive the Euler Fredholm determinant, the
   Gamma Mellin limit, and the \(q\)-dimensional controls.
5. **Chiral no-motion theorem:** prove critical-axis unitary gauge
   equivalence and the \(\det_3\) obstruction.
6. **Exact numerical audit:** report algebraic residuals, convergence, slow
   corners, and all proves-too-much controls.
7. **Route-A decision:** assign SD-C08, score A0--A4, and lock Route B.
8. **Conclusion:** isolate the required single-trace multi-phase architecture.
9. **Appendix:** proofs, domains, and scope discipline.

## Claim ladder

- Theorem: \(K_2\) preserves the complete tensor-prime trace ledger.
- Theorem: binary sign Mellin moments converge locally uniformly on
  \(\Re s>0\) to the Archimedean Gamma factor.
- Proposition: canonical \(q\)-symbol radial sectors have a shifted Gamma
  law and select \(q=2\).
- Theorem: every bounded one-sided chiral Hellinger completion is
  critical-axis isospectral, independently of mass commutation.
- Corollary: the natural \(\det_3\) is \(s\)-independent and loses the
  divergent quadratic trace.
- Decision: A3 advances; global completion stops.

## Nonclaims

No single dynamical determinant, analytic continuation, functional equation,
pole removal, Riemann-zero computation, or Route-B operator is claimed.
