## Hypothesis

The unique least tensor atom \(F_2\) may supply the missing Archimedean
structure of SD-C07 without leaving symbolic dynamics. Its two canonical
\(S_2\)-sectors should separate into a stationary Euler channel and a
one-dimensional fluctuation channel.

## Frozen positive gates

- **P0 — source lock:** every credited construction is functorial in the
  full-shift tensor source and the internally distinguished \(F_2\).
- **P1 — exact Euler ledger:** \(\operatorname{tr}K_2^r=1\) and the
  matrix-weighted transfer preserves every prime-power trace coefficient.
- **P2 — intrinsic scalar fluctuation:** the mean-zero sector of \(F_2\) is
  a multiplicity-one sign representation; no scalar label is fitted.
- **P3 — Archimedean limit:** odd binary Birkhoff sums, normalized by the
  self-dual Gaussian scale, converge in absolute Mellin transform to
  \(\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\) locally uniformly for \(\Re s>0\).
- **P4 — specificity:** the canonical radial standard sectors of \(F_q\),
  \(q>2\), have dimension-shifted Mellin limits rather than the binary one.
- **P5 — same-source assembly:** the Euler and Gamma channels must be the
  trivial and sign sectors of the same frozen \(S_2\)-equivariant object.

Passing P0--P5 authorizes SD-C08 and the status
`GO_A3_ARCHIMEDEAN_FACTOR`.

## Frozen stop gates

- **S0 — single determinant:** if the two transforms are not one proved
  trace/determinant, call the result a Mellin–Fredholm factorization only.
- **S1 — global completion:** without internal meromorphic continuation,
  pole removal, and a functional equation, stop before a completed divisor.
- **S2 — spectral motion:** if critical-axis dependence is a unitary gauge,
  do not interpret it as a zero-generating spectral parameter.
- **S3 — regularization loss:** if the first critical-strip determinant
  deletes the divergent low trace, give it no Euler or zero-divisor credit.
- **S4 — mixed-cycle audit:** do not introduce cross-atom transitions unless
  their unwanted mixed primitive cycles cancel by an intrinsic theorem.

Any of S0--S4 blocks a global RH claim. Route B stays locked regardless of
the positive gates.

## Decision rule

Promotion is not a claim that RH, analytic continuation, or the functional
equation has been proved. It records a source-specific A3 advance: the
Archimedean Gamma factor has been generated by symbolic fluctuations while
the Euler channel is simultaneously preserved. Failure of the global stop
gates remains part of the candidate definition.
