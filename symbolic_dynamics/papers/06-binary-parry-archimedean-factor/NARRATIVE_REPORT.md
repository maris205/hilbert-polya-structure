# Paper 06 Narrative Report

## The step forward

Paper05 found an intrinsic grading but no symbolic half-density or Gamma
factor. Paper06 asks whether the tensor source itself already contains a
distinguished fluctuation object. It does: \(F_2\) is the unique least
nonunit tensor atom, and its two-symbol permutation representation splits
canonically into a stationary line and a sign line.

The stationary line changes nothing that should not change. Attaching its
Parry projection \(K_2\) to every tensor atom preserves every prime-power
trace and hence the Euler determinant. The sign line, however, is the unique
one-dimensional centered standard sector among finite full shifts. Its
Birkhoff fluctuations converge to the self-dual Gaussian, whose absolute
Mellin transform is exactly \(\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\).

The two statements meet before either final transform. The tilted Parry
matrix \(H(z)=e^{zQ/2}K_2e^{zQ/2}\) obeys
\(\operatorname{tr}H(z)^r=(\cosh z)^r\): \(z=0\) is the Euler ledger and
\(z=iu/\sqrt r\) is the Gaussian characteristic-function limit.

This produces a genuine A3 advance: both the finite-prime Euler factor and
the Archimedean factor come from two canonical sectors of one source-locked
binary object. We name the extension SD-C08.

## The hard boundary

The two factors are still obtained by two transforms: a Fredholm/Berezinian
in the stationary sector and a fluctuation Mellin limit in the sign sector.
Their product is legitimate as a same-source factorization, but it is not one
dynamical determinant. The paper therefore does not claim analytic
continuation, a functional equation, or a Riemann-zero divisor.

The most tempting attempt to fuse the sectors is a Hellinger chiral double.
It fixes the center exactly because the paired weights multiply to \(p^{-1}\).
But every block-preserving version is unitarily gauge-equivalent along the
critical axis. Its spectrum cannot move with height. A regularized
determinant exists only after deleting the divergent quadratic trace and is
independent of \(s\).

## What the controls teach

Rank-one kernels on larger or biased alphabets preserve the Euler ledger, so
that identity alone proves too much. Canonical radial standard sectors for
\(F_3\) and \(F_4\) instead produce dimension-shifted Gamma functions and
therefore distinguish the binary representation. Biased binary and arbitrary
scalar-observable controls still expose the universality of one-dimensional
CLT limits. This limitation is kept in the candidate rather than hidden.

## Next live theorem

The next same-family target is an intrinsic noncommuting coupling between
tensor atoms whose single trace formula contains both channels while
cancelling all mixed primitive cycles. The chiral no-motion theorem says
exactly why such a coupling is needed. Any geometric analogy belongs only in
`ROUND2_CLUE`; no other system family is developed here.
