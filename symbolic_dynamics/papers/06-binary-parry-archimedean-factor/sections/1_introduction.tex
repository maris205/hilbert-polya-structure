\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

The arithmetic symbolic-dynamics program studied here begins from a rigid
source rather than a target spectrum. Finite full shifts satisfy
\[
 F_m\boxtimes F_n\cong F_{mn},\qquad h_{\rm top}(F_n)=\log n.
\]
Tensor atoms therefore recover the rational primes as internal
indecomposables, their entropy is the desired logarithmic clock, and loop
repetition supplies prime powers. The resulting diagonal atom transfer gives
the Euler product in its honest half-plane. Paper05 added an intrinsic
exterior grading, but its strongest same-family completion attempts failed:
they generated neither a half-density nor an Archimedean factor.

The present note takes a deliberately narrow next step. The tensor source has
a unique least nonunit atom, $F_2$. Its maximal-entropy Markov kernel is
\[
 K_2=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
More importantly, symbol exchange decomposes its permutation representation
into two multiplicity-one lines. The trivial line is stationary, while the
sign line is centered. These two sectors furnish an exact finite-prime
channel and a fluctuation channel without adding a second system family.

Our key organizing identity uses the sign operator
$Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and the tilted Parry matrix
\[
 H(z)=e^{zQ/2}K_2e^{zQ/2}
 =\frac12\begin{pmatrix}e^z&1\\1&e^{-z}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It has rank one and nonzero eigenvalue $\cosh z$, so
\[
 \tr H(z)^r=(\cosh z)^r.                     \tag{1.1}
\]
At $z=0$, this is the unit power trace needed for every prime-power Euler
coefficient. At $z=iu/\sqrt r$, it is the exact characteristic function of a
normalized binary Birkhoff sum and tends to $e^{-u^2/2}$. The Mellin transform
of the resulting self-dual Gaussian is $\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)$. Thus the two
channels are not coordinates copied from unrelated candidates: they are two
specializations of one source-internal trace family.

This produces the new symbolic candidate SD-C08. In its initial common domain
$\RePart s>1$, its two canonical transforms give
\[
 \mathfrak Z_{\rm SD}(s)
 =\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s).
\]
The formula is a \emph{Mellin--Fredholm factorization}. We do not call it a
single dynamical determinant. We prove neither meromorphic continuation nor
the functional equation; we do not insert $s(s-1)/2$; and we do not score
zeros. This claim boundary is the difference between an A3 mechanism and a
purported solution of RH.

The negative theorem is as useful as the positive construction. A natural
Hellinger pairing replaces $p^{-s}$ and $p^{-(1-s)}$ by a chiral block. On
$s=1/2+it$ the paired weights have modulus $p^{-1/2}$, suggesting a centered
self-adjoint family. We show that every block-preserving implementation
commuting with tensor mass is unitarily conjugate to its value at $t=0$.
The entire vertical parameter is a phase gauge. Spectral motion therefore
requires cross-atom coupling, precisely the move that risks manufacturing
mixed primitive cycles and corrupting the exact Euler ledger.

The contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item an intrinsic selection theorem for the binary scalar fluctuation
        sector inside the full-shift tensor source;
  \item a single tilted-trace identity generating both the Euler and Gaussian
        channels;
  \item locally uniform Mellin convergence to the Archimedean Gamma factor;
  \item dimension-shifted $q$-symbol controls and explicit universality
        limitations;
  \item a general critical-axis chiral no-motion theorem and an exact
        $\det_3$ loss formula;
  \item a preregistered Route-A decision: A3 advances, global completion
        stops, and Route B remains locked.
\end{enumerate}

All primary constructions remain in Symbolic Dynamics. Analogies with other
system families are outside the scope of this note and are recorded, if at
all, only as future clues.
