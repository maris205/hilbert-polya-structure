\subsection{Why the arithmetic gate remains selective}

Some local ingredients are universal: every rank-one kernel preserves unit
power traces, and every suitable scalar mixing process has a Gaussian limit.
SD-C08 receives A0 credit from their constrained conjunction inside the
tensor source, not from either ingredient alone. The tensor monoid selects
the atom loops and clock; its unique minimal atom selects $F_2$; the
multiplicity-free $S_2$ decomposition supplies both channels; and the full
$q$-symbol radial controls change the Gamma law. Shifted, additive, random,
biased, reversible, and higher-dimensional controls reveal exactly which
parts are and are not selective.
