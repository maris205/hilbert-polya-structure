\section{Conclusion: the next phase architecture must change}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The minimal binary full shift closes one gap left by the preceding symbolic
stage. Its stationary and sign representations are two intrinsic channels of
one Parry trace family. The first preserves the exact tensor-prime Euler
determinant; the second generates the self-dual Gaussian and hence
$\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)$. This justifies SD-C08 and a bold but bounded A3
claim: Symbolic Dynamics now produces both the finite-prime and Archimedean
factors on a common honest half-plane.

The construction also identifies its own failure mode. Every one-sided
Hellinger double $A_t=G^{1/2+it}K$, including mass-noncommuting choices of
$K$, makes $1/2$ exact but makes the vertical parameter a gauge.
Regularization reaches the critical line only after deleting the first
nonzero trace. Thus neither exact centering nor self-adjoint block form is
enough.

The next same-family hypothesis can now be stated sharply.

\begin{conjecture}[Cycle-cancelling multi-phase symbolic coupling]
\label{conj:coupling}
There exists a functorially defined chiral extension of the tensor-atom fibers
with at least two inequivalent $t$-phase channels such that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item one trace/determinant family specializes to the SD-C08 tilted Parry
        trace and retains every prime-power coefficient;
  \item cross-atom mixed primitive cycles cancel by an intrinsic grading or
        cohomological identity, not by fitted counterterms;
  \item its critical-axis dependence cannot be removed by a single
        block-diagonal unitary and is not unitarily equivalent to a constant
        operator;
  \item its determinant has a nonempty analytic domain containing the
        symbolic Archimedean and Euler channels.
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjecture}

This is a walkable next step: search within finite symbolic extensions for
inequivalent phase channels, and demand exact cycle cancellation before
looking at any target zeros. Merely imposing $[G,K]\ne0$ on the present
one-sided ansatz cannot work. Until the stronger theorem exists, analytic
continuation, the functional equation, RH, and Route B remain outside the
claim boundary.
