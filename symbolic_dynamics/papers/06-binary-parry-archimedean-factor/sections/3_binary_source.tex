\section{The minimal binary source and one tilted trace}
\label{sec:binary}

Let $F_n=\{1,\ldots,n\}^{\ZZ}$ with the shift map. We work with the symmetric
monoidal skeleton generated by Cartesian product.

\begin{proposition}[Intrinsic binary selection]
\label{prop:binary-selection}
The unique nonunit tensor atom of minimum topological entropy is $F_2$.
Moreover, among the permutation representations of the full shifts, $F_2$ is
the unique one whose centered standard representation is a canonical scalar
line.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The tensor-isomorphism classes form $(\NN_{\ge1},\times)$ and entropy is
$\log n$. Nonunit indecomposables are therefore $F_p$, and strict
monotonicity of $\log n$ selects $F_2$. For $F_q$, the symbol-permutation
representation decomposes as
\[
 \CC^q=\CC\Omega_q\oplus E_q,
 \qquad \Omega_q=q^{-1/2}(1,\ldots,1),
\]
where $E_q=\Omega_q^\perp$ is the standard representation of dimension
$q-1$. It is one-dimensional exactly when $q=2$. Its two unit generators
differ only by sign, which disappears under an absolute moment.
\end{proof}

The unique positive $S_q$-invariant unit vector is $\Omega_q$, and its
projection is
\[
 K_q=|\Omega_q\rangle\langle\Omega_q|=J_q/q.
\]
For $q=2$, introduce the sign involution $Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$.
Relabeling the two symbols sends $Q$ to $-Q$ but leaves every even or absolute
quantity below unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Tilted Parry trace]
\label{thm:tilted-trace}
For
\[
 H(z)=e^{zQ/2}K_2e^{zQ/2}
 =\frac12\begin{pmatrix}e^z&1\\1&e^{-z}\end{pmatrix},
\]
one has, for every integer $r\ge1$,
\[
 \tr H(z)^r=(\cosh z)^r.
\]
In particular, $z=0$ gives $\tr K_2^r=1$, while
\[
 \tr H(iu/\sqrt r)^r
 =\cos(u/\sqrt r)^r\longrightarrow e^{-u^2/2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The matrix $H(z)$ has rank one. Its trace, hence its only nonzero eigenvalue,
is $(e^z+e^{-z})/2=\cosh z$. The power-trace formula follows. The last limit
is obtained from
$r\log\cos(u/\sqrt r)=-u^2/2+O(r^{-1})$ locally uniformly in $u$.
\end{proof}

By cyclicity, these are also the power traces of the cyclic representative
$H_{\rm cyc}(z)=K_2e^{zQ}$ (up to the harmless convention
$Q\mapsto-Q$). On the active rank-one sector it has the same nonzero
eigenvalue and Fredholm block determinant as the symmetric representative;
the numerical implementation uses $H_{\rm cyc}(iu)$.

The final expression is also exactly the characteristic function of
$r^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^rX_j$ for independent $X_j\in\{-1,1\}$. Thus a single
finite symbolic trace family contains both channels before any Gamma function
is mentioned.

\subsection{Euler transfer as the untwisted channel}

Let $\At$ denote the nonunit tensor atoms and set
\[
 \cH=\ell^2(\At)\otimes\CC^2,
 \qquad
 \cA_{s,z}=\bigoplus_{F_p\in\At}p^{-s}H(z).
\]
This is a matrix-weighted transfer on the atom-loop symbolic suspension. It is
not a group cocycle and we do not claim an independently constructed skew
shift.

\begin{theorem}[Exact stationary ledger]
\label{thm:euler}
For $\RePart s>1$,
\[
 \Tr\cA_{s,0}^r=\sum_p p^{-rs},
 \qquad
 \det(I-\cA_{s,0})=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})=\zeta(s)^{-1}.
\]
With the fixed purely odd one-particle convention,
$\Ber(I-\cA_{s,0})=\zeta(s)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since $K_2^r=K_2$ and $\tr K_2=1$, each atom block contributes $p^{-rs}$.
Trace-class convergence holds for $\RePart s>1$, where the logarithmic
Fredholm expansion is absolutely convergent:
\[
 \log\det(I-\cA_{s,0})
 =-\sum_{r\ge1}\frac1r\sum_pp^{-rs}
 =\sum_p\log(1-p^{-s}).
\]
The Berezinian is the reciprocal determinant on a purely odd space.
\end{proof}

The exact ledger selects rank one, not binary fairness. Uniform $K_q$ and
biased rank-one kernels have the same power traces. Conversely, for a finite
reversible stochastic kernel, the identities $\tr K^r=1$ for all $r$ force
all nonstationary eigenvalues to vanish; reversibility then gives a rank-one
orthogonal projection. Binary selection enters through the tensor minimum and
the one-dimensional centered representation, not through the Euler identity
alone.
