## Paper-06 obstructions

### SD-O10 — rank-one ledger proves too much

For every finite rank-one projection \(K\) with trace one,

\[
K^r=K,\qquad \operatorname{tr}K^r=1.
\]

Consequently, replacing each tensor-atom scalar block by \(p^{-s}K\)
preserves the Euler ledger independently of alphabet size or stationary
weights. The ledger alone cannot select \(F_2\). Minimality of \(F_2\) and
the dimension-one canonical sign sector are additional necessary source
conditions.

The reversible control with eigenvalues \(1,\lambda\), \(\lambda\neq0\),
gives \(\operatorname{tr}K^r=1+\lambda^r\) and fails exactly.

### SD-O11 — one-dimensional CLT universality

After centering and variance normalization, biased binary kernels and many
scalar observables on larger alphabets have the same Gaussian absolute
Mellin limit. Therefore \(\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\) by itself does not
identify the fair binary process.

Canonical radial \(K_q\) controls partially resolve this ambiguity: their
standard representations have dimension \(d=q-1\) and yield a shifted
Gamma factor. The ambiguity remains within arbitrary one-dimensional
projections and biased binary processes.

### SD-O12 — Mellin--Fredholm transform separation

The tilted family

\[
H_{\rm cyc}(u)=K_2\operatorname{diag}(e^{-iu},e^{iu})
\]

does give the exact same-trace identities

\[
\operatorname{tr}H_{\rm cyc}(0)^r=1,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}H_{\rm cyc}(t/\sqrt N)^N=\cos(t/\sqrt N)^N.
\]

However, the Euler object is a Fredholm determinant over tensor atoms,
whereas the Archimedean object is an absolute Mellin limit of binary
fluctuations. Their same-source product is not yet one operator determinant.
Declaring it unified without a coupling theorem would be coordinate
splicing.

### SD-O13 — chiral phase-gauge no-motion theorem

For block-preserving \(K\) commuting with
\(G=\bigoplus_p p^{-1}I\), the critical-axis chiral family

\[
B_t=\begin{pmatrix}0&G^{1/2+it}K\\G^{1/2-it}K&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

is unitarily conjugate to \(B_0\). For the frozen rank-one atom block its
active spectrum is \(\{\pm p^{-1/2}\}\), independent of \(t\). The
Hellinger half-density centers the block but cannot generate vertical
spectral motion.

### SD-O14 — chiral regularization deletes the divergent quadratic trace

The active chiral eigenvalues force
\(\operatorname{Tr}B_s^{2r}=2\sum_p p^{-r}\). The \(r=1\) term diverges;
the first usable regularized determinant cancels precisely this term and is
independent of \(s\). It has no claim to the target moving divisor.

### SD-O15 — global atom-inventory Parry normalization is non-specific

Solving \(\sum_a a^{-h}=1\) makes Hellinger product and critical conjugacy
identities hold for shifted and matched-random positive inventories as
well. Moreover, the SD-C07 atom-loop adjacency is the identity, so it has no
canonical mixing Parry probability across atoms. This global-inventory
branch is an obstruction control and must not be confused with the
canonical Parry kernel internal to \(F_2\).
