## Seven sections and proof coverage

1. Setup and results (about 0.8 page): literal reset schedule, rooted tree
   dictionary, N leaves versus m internal vertices, exactly two main theorems.
2. Protected cells (about 1.2 pages): schedule well-definedness, non-ear fan
   cells, ear single-fan branch and longer-fan branch, unchanged internal
   root edges and increasing relabelling, well-founded F/G/P recursion.
3. Complete source sets (about 1.3 pages): simultaneous inverse induction,
   all zero/comb/l=1/l=2/l>2 branches, operational disjoint seed/later parser.
4. Fibre evaluation and extremum (about 0.8 page): all gap factors, zero
   branches, strict decoration exponent loss and unique fan equality case.
5. The K map (about 1 page): defining factorization, KG=GK, frozen cherry,
   full image class and stronger closure with N=3,4 boundaries, K clock.
6. The sharp F clock (about 1.2 pages): both square identities, unique
   recurrent pair, commuting odd phase, exact minimum bound, both witness
   parities and triangle/quadrilateral exceptions.
7. Scope and bounded validation (about 0.8 page): exact edge-labelled lift,
   close Pop/rotation precedents, deductions, bounded complete checks,
   no all-time/basin/global-priority conclusion.
