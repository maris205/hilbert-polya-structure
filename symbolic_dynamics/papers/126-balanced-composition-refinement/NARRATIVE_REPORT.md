# Narrative report

## One-sentence story

Balanced refinement looks like a routine terminating split, but every iterate
has a canonical kernel: small source parts become indistinguishable one-runs,
large parts survive as uniquely suffix-decodable codewords, and that single
normal form simultaneously gives every fibre and the complete image tower.

## The actual advance

The logarithmic clock was visible in the first proof spike and is generic
divide-and-conquer background.  The independent owner/value gate forced a
rewrite and found the stronger invariant.  For `K=2^t`, replace every source
part `<=K` by that many ones.  Equality of these canonical forms is equivalent
to equality after `t` rounds.  This turns a target-local factorization DP into
a complete congruence theorem and a unique right decoder.

Once decoded, each maximal one-run can be regrouped independently into parts
at most `K`; this yields the pointwise fibre product.  The canonical alphabet
also puts the entire image in bijection with restricted compositions.  These
are complementary consequences of the same kernel theorem, not independent
proofs of it.
