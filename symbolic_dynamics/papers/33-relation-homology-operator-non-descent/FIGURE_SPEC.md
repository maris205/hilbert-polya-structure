# Figure Spec — Paper 33 / SD-C35

All figures are pure TikZ and compile inside `main.tex`.

## Figure 1: relation dessin

File: `figures/relation_dessin.tex`

Purpose: show that the Manin relation quotient is the cycle space of a
bipartite incidence graph and that the cusp state and its `R` image form two
parallel state edges.

## Figure 2: cross grid

File: `figures/cross_grid.tex`

Purpose: show `n=m2^a3^b` components and that the inherited cusp diamonds are
unit squares.  Filling all unit squares contracts the cross component.

## Figure 3: operator firewall

File: `figures/operator_firewall.tex`

Purpose: separate the relation quotient from determinant ownership.  The
original `S+R` adjacency does not preserve `W_n`; a scalar homology action is a
replacement clock.
