## One-sentence contribution

For the graded wheel-sieve path system whose endogenous clock is the rational
prime sequence, the note proves that exact single-valued clock decoding
excludes periodic semiconjugate images, extends the exclusion to continuous
orbit-closure recodings under an explicit lag-pair condition, and identifies by
counterexample the principal ways periodicity returns when inheritance is
weakened.

## Novelty and prior-art boundary

The core cycle contradiction is classical in mechanism. A coboundary
\(d\circ S-d\) telescopes to zero around a periodic orbit. This is the easy
necessary direction of the Livšic periodic-orbit obstruction and belongs to
the ordered-cohomology viewpoint for integer-valued observables. The note
will say this explicitly and will not claim a new universal theorem about
cocycles, factors, or periodic points.

The contribution defended here is the wheel-sieve-specific package:

- the already proved endogenous prime multiplier clock is made the frozen
  observable;
- exact decoding is characterized by fiber consistency;
- direct-image and orbit-closure cases are separated;
- the closure result is stated through lag-pair separation, covering both
  \(q_{k+1}\) and \(\log q_{k+1}\);
- compact target and compactified clock-codomain cases are distinguished;
- hypothesis-deletion controls expose clock erasure, level wrapping,
  discontinuity, partiality, and post-hoc orbit labels; and
- the result is converted into a Route-A decision without inventing a
  determinant.

Heeren's 2026 SSRN working paper is direct topical prior art for an
endogenous, deterministic, non-stationary S-adic symbolic sieve. The paper
therefore will not claim the first symbolic realization of prime sieving.
The present claim concerns stationarization of the specific frozen wheel
tail-path clock, not priority for symbolic sieves in general.

## Problem anchor

Let

\[
X=\bigsqcup_{k\ge0}X_k,
\qquad
\sigma(X_k)\subseteq X_{k+1},
\]

be the one-sided tail-path space generated by the wheel recurrence

\[
Q_1=q_1=2,
\qquad
q_{k+1}=\min\{n>q_k:\gcd(n,Q_k)=1\},
\qquad
Q_{k+1}=Q_kq_{k+1}.
\]

Paper 01 proves that \(q_k\) is the \(k\)-th rational prime. If
\(\ell(x)=k\) on \(X_k\), define

\[
\kappa(x)=q_{\ell(x)+1},
\qquad
\tau(x)=\log\kappa(x).
\]

Strict grading gives, for every \(m\ge1\),

\[
\kappa(\sigma^m x)
=q_{\ell(x)+m+1}
\ne q_{\ell(x)+1}
=\kappa(x).
\]

The question is precise: can a shift-compatible image or closure of this
source have periodic points while inheriting that exact clock through one
autonomous, single-valued decoder?

## Mathematical story

### Exact decoding is a fiber condition

For any map \(\pi:X\to Y\), the proposed rule
\(d(\pi(x))=\kappa(x)\) is well defined exactly when

\[
\pi(x)=\pi(x')
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\kappa(x)=\kappa(x').
\]

A quotient therefore cannot choose different clock values for different
lifts of the same target state. State, edge, finite-window, variable-window,
or infinite-memory rules are all covered once they define one total function
of the target path state.

### A periodic image point violates fiber consistency

Suppose \(y=\pi(x)\) and \(S^my=y\). Semiconjugacy gives

\[
\pi(\sigma^m x)=S^m\pi(x)=\pi(x).
\]

The two source points are in one fiber. Exact decoding therefore demands
\(\kappa(\sigma^m x)=\kappa(x)\), contradicting strict prime-clock drift.
This proves the direct-image result without compactness, continuity,
finite-memory, or a topology on \(X\) or \(Y\).

In additive notation, the same calculation says that \(d\circ S-d\) has
zero sum on a periodic target orbit, while its exact source lift has sum
\(\kappa(\sigma^m x)-\kappa(x)\ne0\). This acknowledges the
Livšic/ordered-cohomology lineage without importing converse or regularity
results.

### Boundary cycles require a separate closure theorem

A periodic point can be added in
\(Y_0\setminus\pi(X)\) even when the image itself is aperiodic. Exactness on
the dense image is not enough. For each lag \(m\), set

\[
E_m
=\{(\kappa(x),\kappa(\sigma^m x)):x\in X\}
\subset C\times C.
\]

If the decoder extends continuously and totally to \(Y_0\), then

\[
F_m=(d,d\circ S^m):Y_0\to C\times C
\]

is continuous and sends the dense image into \(E_m\). Hence
\(F_m(Y_0)\subseteq\overline{E_m}\). An \(m\)-periodic point maps to
\((d(y),d(y))\in\Delta_C\). The hypothesis
\(\overline{E_m}\cap\Delta_C=\varnothing\) excludes it.

The exact integer clock with \(C=\mathbb N_{\rm disc}\) satisfies the
hypothesis. So do the ordinary real clocks \(q_{k+1}\) and
\(\log q_{k+1}\), because their lag pairs are locally finite and escape every
compact subset of \(\mathbb R^2\). Metrizability and sequential arguments
are not required.

### Compactness has two compatible effects

If the target phase space \(Y_0\) is compact and the exact decoder is
continuous into \(\mathbb N_{\rm disc}\), its image is finite. It cannot
recover all wheel multipliers. This is a stronger feasibility stop for a
compact exact-discrete-clock recoding, independent of periodicity.

On the other hand, compactifying the clock codomain to
\(C=\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}\) lets the boundary clock converge to \(\infty\).
Consider

\[
Y=\{0\}\cup\{(k+1)^{-1}:k\ge0\},
\qquad
S((k+1)^{-1})=(k+2)^{-1},
\quad
S(0)=0.
\]

The rule

\[
d((k+1)^{-1})=q_{k+1},
\qquad
d(0)=\infty
\]

is continuous into the compactified clock space, while \(0\) is fixed. The
lag pairs converge to
\((\infty,\infty)\in\Delta_C\). This demonstrates why decoder continuity
cannot replace lag-pair separation.

### Clock erasure makes cycles easy

The constant one-point factor has a fixed point and no exact decoder. The map

\[
\pi_m(x)=\ell(x)\bmod m
\]

has an \(m\)-cycle, but a state revisited after one lap would need to decode
both \(q_{k+1}\) and \(q_{k+m+1}\). A boundary fixed point may likewise be
added if the decoder is partial or extended discontinuously. These are valid
dynamical factors or closures but invalid exact-clock inheritance mechanisms.

Sharpness is checked by replacing the prime clock with a genuinely periodic
control clock \(c_{k+m}=c_k\). The level-mod-\(m\) factor then has an exact
decoder. Thus the abstract obstruction depends on nonrecurrence of the
inherited clock, not on primality. The wheel-specific content is that this
nonrecurrent clock is generated endogenously and has already been proved to
equal the prime sequence.

### A roof is not automatically an absolute clock

The derived quantity \(\tau=\log\kappa\) remains an absolute source-level
label in the frozen mechanism and inherits the same obstruction. This is not
an ordinary suspension roof \(r(y)>0\), whose values are increments accumulated
each time a periodic base orbit is traversed. A periodic symbolic base can
carry such a roof without contradiction.

Assigning \(\log p\) independently to a new target cycle would create a new
orbit-level arithmetic rule. It is not a decoder of the frozen wheel clock,
does not inherit the source's A0 status, and must pass the arithmetic-origin
gate on its own.

## Literature narrative

- Hedlund (1969) supplies the standard local-code setting for continuous
  shift-commuting maps on finite-alphabet shifts. The direct-image theorem is
  more set-theoretic because it needs no topology.
- Livšic (1972) supplies the classical periodic-orbit/coboundary context. We
  use only telescoping necessity, not the Livšic converse.
- Parry and Pollicott (1990) supply the distinction between periodic bases,
  suspension roofs, and accumulated orbit weights.
- Boyle and Handelman (1996) supply ordered-cohomology language in which
  finite-orbit sums kill coboundaries.
- Heeren (2026, SSRN working paper) is direct prior art for endogenous,
  non-stationary symbolic prime sieving. It changes the priority language but,
  from its public description, does not settle the exact-clock
  stationarization result proved here.

The conclusion is deliberately scoped:

> Exact single-valued clock plus shift-compatible semiconjugacy, on either

It is not a theorem that every symbolic system, every measurable decoder, or
every independently designed arithmetic grammar is aperiodic.

## Nonclaims and guardrails

The paper does not claim that:

- the abstract periodic-clock or coboundary obstruction is new;
- Heeren's S-adic automaton and the wheel tail-path system are equivalent;
- every factor of an aperiodic system is aperiodic;
- every continuous decoder excludes boundary cycles without a clock-topology
  condition;
- a suspension roof must return to its initial numerical value after one
  periodic base traversal;
- a discontinuous or partial boundary label is inherited arithmetic data;
- a candidate, determinant, zeta identity, spectral realization, or RH-like
  conclusion has been obtained; or
- Route B may rescue the theorem-stopped mechanism.

## Reproducibility and experiment policy

All evidence is finite proof text or an explicit counterexample. No random
seed, dataset, fitting procedure, or numerical tolerance exists. A numerical
experiment would not strengthen or falsify a universal theorem whose
hypotheses are checked symbolically, so no numerical run is preregistered.
