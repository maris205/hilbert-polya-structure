# Narrative report — Paper 30 / SD-C32

## Executive interpretation

Paper29 deliberately left one opening: perhaps a global invariant of many atoms, their lcms, and the entire filtered source could succeed where local quadratic counterterms failed.  Paper30 tests the most direct Boolean-join and connected-cumulant realizations, then proves that the opening is illusory at the present information boundary.

The decisive fact is not a bad coefficient choice.  Positive-integer divisibility is itself a free commutative monoid.  Valuation vectors identify it with formal monomials, preserving the pointed order, atoms, lcms, intervals, and Möbius values.  Once the admitted roof, Gram, cutoff, and compiler data are transported, the identification is an isomorphism of the whole object visible to an SD-C32 construction.  An invariant natural under such isomorphisms cannot tell the two presentations apart.

This yields a sharp specification diagnosis.  Demanding a nonzero value on the integer source and zero on **every** UFD control quantifies over an isomorphic clone of the baseline.  The two requirements contradict one another.  The proof applies to local and nonlocal constructions of arbitrary arity, including complete-tower predicates, as long as they are honest natural invariants of the declared source data.

The two frozen candidates show the obstruction from opposite sides.  The Boolean weight is one on every squarefree tuple interval, both for integer primes and formal generators, so it is nonzero but nonselective.  The connected cumulant removes factorized contributions and is therefore zero on the free baseline itself.  A different moment may change the value, but it is new scheme data and is transported to the clone.

The final executable refinement adds unique-join, Boolean-interval, Möbius-sign, roof-multiplicativity, and associative-ownership predicates.  Its triple contraction is nonzero at all three integer cutoffs and exactly zero on the four finite non-UFD fixtures.  This is a meaningful finite-fixture GO.  It is not arithmetic specificity: the free and polynomial-UFD clones reproduce every pair, triple, coefficient, marker, and predicate-mask row.  The final package passes 28/28 tests and 1616/1616 independent checks, with two byte-identical 17-artifact runs.

The Paper28/29 analytic stack remains useful for bookkeeping.  Weighting its mixed Gram series by the Boolean pair kernel gives a normally convergent holomorphic function and an exact ratio-frequency ledger.  That series is a new functional, not an ordinary trace or a modified Fredholm determinant.  A separately defined absolutely summable zero-diagonal Gram matrix is trace class and owns an honest auxiliary ordinary Fredholm determinant.  Its phase cancels, its coefficients are cloned, and it does not upgrade the original chiral determinant.

## English abstract record

The local counterterm analysis of incidence-based symbolic dynamics leaves a possible nonlocal escape: a source-natural invariant built from several atoms, their joins, incidence Möbius values, or connected cumulants.  We show that this escape is unavailable for the admitted multiplicative source.  Unique factorization identifies positive-integer divisibility with the free commutative monoid of finitely supported valuation vectors.  After compatible cutoffs and all roof, Gram, and compiler decorations are transported, this map is an isomorphism of decorated sources.  Hence every isomorphism-natural local or nonlocal invariant agrees on the integer source and its formal UFD clone.  It is impossible to be nonzero on the former while vanishing on every UFD control.  The Boolean weight is one on both sources; its partition-lattice cumulant is zero on the factorizing baseline.  A stronger triple filter is nonzero at three integer cutoffs and zero on four finite non-UFD fixtures, but free and polynomial-UFD clones copy its full ledger.  The final audit passes 28/28 tests and 1616/1616 checks.  The mixed Gram series is holomorphic for \(1-2\eta<\operatorname{Re}s<2\eta\) but remains a new functional.  A separate trace-class Gram matrix owns an auxiliary ordinary determinant; neither object upgrades the original chiral determinant, and both are cloned.  The multiplicative incidence/counterterm branch is therefore closed.

**Keywords:** symbolic dynamics; incidence algebra; free commutative monoid; Möbius inversion; cumulants; naturality; no-go theorem
