# Round-two clues and branch closure — Paper 30 / SD-C32

## Status

`CLOSE_CHIRAL_INCIDENCE_COUNTERTERM_BRANCH`

The loophole left by Paper29 was the possibility of a genuinely nonlocal, source-natural divisibility-coherence statistic.  Paper30 closes that loophole for every invariant of the admissible multiplicative decorated source, not merely for the frozen pair/triple candidate.  Locality, arity, linearity, and additivity are not assumptions of the clone theorem.

## Machines that remain clues only

- graded/subset-lcm Möbius transforms in trace and Artin–Tits monoids;
- incidence-Hopf primitive and logarithmic/connected extraction;
- partition-lattice cumulants;
- CULF/decomposition-space functoriality;
- Ihara/Bass connected-cycle determinant expansions; and
- dynamical determinant formulas from transfer operators, flows, and cohomology.

These mechanisms organize incidence or connected data across broad families.  Their functoriality does not distinguish the integer free monoid from a transported formal clone.  Graph or dynamical determinant results use additional operator, inverse-edge, periodic-orbit, transfer, or cohomological structure absent from SD-C32.  They are literature clues, not imported machinery and not novelty claims.

## Paper31 gate

Paper31 is permitted only if it introduces an independently motivated, source-derived **nonmultiplicative** operation and proves that the new decorated object is not transported by the valuation isomorphism.  A valid proposal must:

1. define the operation without printed labels, primality lookup, target zeros, or post-control fitting;
2. prove why the formal-UFD clone cannot carry it by transport;
3. rebuild naturality and compatible-cutoff behavior in the enlarged category;
4. prove summability, holomorphy, and marker ownership for any analytic functional;
5. rerun mutated-cover, composite-only, generic-DAG, random-inventory, and free/UFD controls; and
6. keep Route B locked unless a separate theorem first satisfies its invocation gate.

Potentially legitimate new data include addition–multiplication interaction, congruence correspondences, or a genuine symbolic-dynamical transfer operator derived from a new source.  These examples are permissions to formulate a new problem, not evidence that any candidate will work.

## Changes that do not reopen the branch

Changing coefficients, tuple arity, the moment convention, a cumulant basis, cutoff order, Abel versus sharp regularization, a finite-part constant, or the contraction used for (K_3) does not break the transported-clone isomorphism.  Nor does hard-coding the isomorphism class of the integer tower: that merely turns the full inventory into an oracle.

No additional round within the old chiral-incidence/counterterm branch is scientifically obligated.
