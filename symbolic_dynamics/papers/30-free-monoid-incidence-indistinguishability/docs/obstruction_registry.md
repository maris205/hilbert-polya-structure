# Obstruction registry — SD-C32

## O32.1 — Pair-local interval blindness

Promoting `6` to a bottom cover leaves the generated Boolean intervals of the
pairs `(2,5)`, `(2,7)`, and `(3,5)` intact.  All five frozen predicates hold
on those pairs.  Therefore the preregistered pair separator fails, and none of
the 31 nonempty predicate masks repairs it.

## O32.2 — Free-monoid clone indistinguishability

Unique factorization sends each integer to its finite exponent vector.  This
is a pointed-order and join isomorphism preserving bottom covers, generated
intervals, Möbius values, compatible cutoffs, and—by required transport—roof,
Gram, marker, and compiled-operator decorations.  Hence every natural local or
nonlocal invariant of the frozen decorated source has exactly the same value
on integers and on the formal free-commutative/UFD clone.

This is a theorem-level `PROVES_TOO_MUCH` certificate.  It cannot be repaired
by changing pair/triple arity, Möbius coefficients, cumulant convention,
cutoff, or a source-natural finite-part scheme.

## O32.3 — Determinant ownership

The filtered Gram matrix has absolutely summable entries, so its auxiliary
ordinary Fredholm determinant is legitimate.  It is phase-free and is copied
by the UFD clone.  It is not the original chiral transfer determinant.
Exponentiating the pair or connected-triple series defines a new functional,
not an inherited ordinary determinant or modified determinant identity.

## Escape boundary

The obstruction does not cover a newly justified source object containing a
nonmultiplicative operation absent from the clone.  Paper31 must first derive
such an operation—plausibly addition, congruence, or an Archimedean–multiplicative
coupling—and prove exact clone separation without a numeric-prime oracle.
