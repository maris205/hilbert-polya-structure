## Closest collision

The closest mechanical collision is the graded Möbius transform of Abbes, Gouëzel, Jugé, and Mairesse, because it explicitly uses subsets of generators and their lcms in broad monoids.  The closest conceptual collision is the conjunction of Speed’s partition-lattice cumulants with Schmitt’s incidence-Hopf primitive/logarithmic organization.  These sources do not produce a baseline-nonzero/UFD-zero selector.  Their cross-family scope instead makes the clone obstruction natural.

In strict symbolic dynamics, Ruelle's determinant construction and the recent flow/cohomology formula of Álvarez López, Kim, and Morishita are adjacent but not collisions.  Both begin with dynamics or operator/cohomological data absent from the multiplicative incidence object.  They cannot be invoked to upgrade the new mixed series to a determinant.
