Paper27 asks whether the integer divisibility grammar can compile its own
prime-loop selector without receiving a prime list, color inventory, prime
mask, or prime-power coefficient table. It can: covers of \(1\) identify the
source atoms, and

\[
q_n=\zeta * \varepsilon_n * \mu
\]

forms a complete system of oblique primitive idempotents. The resulting
letter actions select exactly the monochromatic temporal repetitions of
source atoms, before cyclic trace and with the Elias-gamma digit marker
retained.

The same construction proves its limitation. At every finite cutoff the
compiled idempotents are unitriangularly conjugate to coordinate projectors,
and on the canonical countable weighted space they are boundedly similar to
those projectors for \(\eta>1\). The honest graded Fredholm ratio is therefore
the usual atom Euler product on its proved trace-class domain, with no
same-object continuation or critical-line mechanism.
