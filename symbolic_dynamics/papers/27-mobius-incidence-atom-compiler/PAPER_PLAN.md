# Paper Plan — Paper27 / SD-C29

## Narrative spine

The paper has a deliberately asymmetric result.

1. **Positive:** the fixed divisibility grammar identifies its own atoms as
   covers of \(1\), so no prime inventory is supplied.
2. **Compiler:** incidence conjugation turns every source coordinate into a
   primitive idempotent and makes the atom actions exact on every word and
   every repetition.
3. **Analytic realization:** the idempotents admit an honest countable
   rank-one realization and couple to the inherited holomorphic de Rham
   complex with a trace-class graded determinant.
4. **Negative:** finite radical conjugacy and countable bounded similarity
   show that the compiled geometry is an oblique coordinate table. It earns
   A1 but moves neither A3 nor A4.

The manuscript must lead with this positive/negative pair. It must not market
the Euler product itself as new.

### 7. Exact controls and ownership firewalls

### 8. Strict route evaluation

### 9. Conclusion

## Nonnegotiable terminology

- Say **source atom** or **cover of \(1\)** before identifying it with a prime.
- Say **temporal repetition** for repeated traversal of one atom loop.
- Say **composite source letter** for \(p^r\) when it is one return label.
- Say **graded ratio of honest determinants**, never ordinary determinant of
  the ungraded block sum.
- Say **bounded similarity for \(\eta>1\)**, not for all \(\eta>1/2\).
- Say **same-object continuation absent**, even though the scalar Euler product
  has familiar continuation elsewhere.
