recorded but not developed.

## ROUND2_CLUE 1 — geometric carrier for the \(C_2\) sign line

A geometric orientation or holonomy line could realize the formal
\(C_2\)-color as an actual bundle over periodic orbits.  Such a carrier may
distinguish scalar edge signs from supertrace parity while retaining temporal
powers.

**Why stopped:** constructing the carrier requires a geometric flow or
return map and leaves Symbolic Dynamics.

## ROUND2_CLUE 2 — unitary/scattering realization of resolved modes

A unitary or scattering system with a genuine finite-group fiber action
could supply character factors that commute with one operator independently
of the arithmetic roof.

**Why stopped:** no Hilbert space, domain, boundary conditions, or unitary
operator is defined.  Route B remains locked.

## ROUND2_CLUE 3 — regularized equivariant determinant

A nonstandard regularized determinant might be constructed from a completed
representation-valued ghost sequence.

**Why stopped:** regularization belongs to operator analysis and cannot be
used to alter the frozen finite power traces.  Any such object must first
define a new same-object trace ledger.
