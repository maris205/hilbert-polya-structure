# Citation metadata lock for drafting

Only the entries below may enter the initial bibliography.  Each metadata
record is tied to a primary publisher, DOI, journal, or arXiv page; the cited
claim must remain within the recorded scope.  This ledger is not a
literature-exhaustiveness or priority statement.

## Required owners and dependencies

1. **Downarowicz--Kwiatkowski--Lacroix (1995).** T. Downarowicz,
   J. Kwiatkowski, and Y. Lacroix, “A criterion for Toeplitz flows to be
   topologically isomorphic and applications,” *Colloquium Mathematicum*
   68 (1995), 219--228. DOI `10.4064/cm-68-2-219-228`.
   Primary record: <https://www.impan.pl/get/doi/10.4064/cm-68-2-219-228>.
   Permitted use: standard position-period/Toeplitz terminology and the
   general same-period, over-zero aligned-symbol factor criterion.

2. **Hosseini--Yassawi (2026).** M. Hosseini and R. Yassawi, “Obstacles to
   topological factoring of Toeplitz shifts,” *Discrete and Continuous
   Dynamical Systems* 46 (2026), 413--432. DOI
   `10.3934/dcds.2025105`; arXiv `2412.04422v3`.
   Primary record:
   <https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2025105>.
   Permitted use: constructive-period terminology and the pure-power
   cross-base necessary obstruction.  The paper's all-coordinate
   finite-block convention is stated explicitly rather than silently
   attributing the source's displayed zero-index inconsistency.

3. **Hedlund (1969).** G. A. Hedlund, “Endomorphisms and automorphisms of
   the shift dynamical system,” *Mathematical Systems Theory* 3 (1969),
   320--375. DOI `10.1007/BF01691062`; DBLP key
   `journals/mst/Hedlund69`.
   Primary DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01691062>.
   Permitted use: the Curtis--Hedlund--Lyndon finite-local-rule theorem.

4. **Gjerde--Johansen (2000).** R. Gjerde and Ø. Johansen,
   “Bratteli--Vershik models for Cantor minimal systems: applications to
   Toeplitz flows,” *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 20(6) (2000),
   1687--1710. DOI `10.1017/S0143385700000948`.
   Primary record: <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0143385700000948>.
   Permitted use: Toeplitz period structures and Bratteli--Vershik context.

5. **Downarowicz--Durand (2002).** T. Downarowicz and F. Durand, “Factors
   of Toeplitz flows and other almost 1-1 extensions over group rotations,”
   *Mathematica Scandinavica* 90(1) (2002), 57--72. DOI
   `10.7146/math.scand.a-14361`.
   Primary record: <https://www.mscand.dk/article/view/14361>.
   Permitted use: general factor context for Toeplitz/almost-automorphic
   systems, not the present letter-quotient theorem.

## Optional simple-Toeplitz context

6. **Sell (2020).** D. Sell, “Simple Toeplitz subshifts: combinatorial
   properties and uniformity of cocycles,” arXiv `2006.15348` (2020).
   Primary record: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15348>.
   Permitted use: combinatorial context for aperiodic simple Toeplitz
   subshifts.  Omit it if the final sentence does not need that context.

## Drafting rules

- Fetch BibTeX through DBLP or DOI content negotiation; do not transcribe
  an entry from memory.
- Prefer the published version whenever listed above.
- Do not cite an entry for a stronger claim than its permitted-use line.
- Keep only cited keys in `references.bib`.
- Any unresolved metadata blocks compilation readiness; no `[VERIFY]`
  marker may survive into a compiled round.
