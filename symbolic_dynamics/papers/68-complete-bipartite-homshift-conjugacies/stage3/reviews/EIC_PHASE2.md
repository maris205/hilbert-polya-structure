## Review Body

The paper has a concise and intelligible organizing idea: the global checkerboard phase both enables an intrinsic dimer recoding and controls the finite-dependence, thermodynamic, and periodic consequences. The explicit two-sided conjugacy classification is a substantial field-general result within the stated complete-bipartite family, and the manuscript carefully excludes one-sided and general-target categories. The writing is economical, with hypotheses and category boundaries repeated where they matter. Because no target criteria or venue authority is bound, this assessment makes no venue-specific fit, track, tier, or alignment determination.

### S1: The classification mechanism is explicit rather than invariant-only
**Evidence Anchor**: text: Introduction "We prove that this extra memory collapses the classification precisely to \(mn\)"

The radius-one dimer construction gives the classification a concrete coding mechanism, while entropy supplies necessity. This combination is stronger and more informative than merely observing agreement of standard invariants.

### S2: One phase variable links otherwise different results
**Evidence Anchor**: text: Introduction "The same global phase has a different effect on stochastic dependence"

The manuscript uses the checkerboard phase to connect conjugacy, finite dependence, equilibrium structure, and periodic data without blurring their distinct conclusions. This creates a recognizable contribution package rather than an assortment of calculations.

### W1: The hierarchy among four result contracts should be made explicit
**Severity**: Minor
**Evidence Anchor**: text: Introduction "The paper establishes four linked contracts"
**Confidence**: 4 — editorial assessment of contribution hierarchy and narrative load

The introduction lists four substantial contracts but does not immediately state which is the headline contribution and which are consequences or complementary applications of the phase decomposition. Add a short dependency sentence or compact roadmap identifying the product conjugacy classification as the central theorem and showing how the phase lemma feeds the other three packages. This would sharpen the paper's identity without removing any result.
