## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires the methodology seat to determine whether the study design, handling of evidence, reporting practices, and reproducibility support satisfy the relevant peer-review standard; editorial oversight will treat this as mandatory and will not pre-judge it without paper-visible evidence.

D2 requires the domain seat to determine whether the claims, treatment of prior work, terminology, and reported results are accurate in the relevant field; editorial oversight will treat domain accuracy as mandatory and await the specialist assessment.

D3 requires the eligible methodology and devil's-advocate seats to test whether the central thesis is internally consistent and adequately supported, without argument-breaking fallacies; editorial oversight will treat argumentative coherence as mandatory.

D4 requires the perspective seat to examine whether framing, definitions, implications, and any interdisciplinary claims are usable and substantiated for adjacent-field readers; editorial oversight will treat this cross-disciplinary relevance dimension as high priority.

D5 assigns the EIC seat responsibility for judging whether organisation, exposition, visual presentation, and structural conventions make the manuscript clear and professionally presented; this is a normal-priority, non-mandatory dimension.

D6 assigns the EIC seat mandatory responsibility for judging, once paper-visible evidence is available, the originality, significance, readership relevance, and appropriate scope of the contribution; no fit or alignment conclusion is made during this blind pre-commitment.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Clear organisation, coherent exposition, readable and informative figures or tables where present, and consistent professional presentation appropriate to the manuscript form.
what_triggers_block: Structural or presentation defects are pervasive enough that the manuscript's central contribution cannot be reliably followed or evaluated without extensive reconstruction.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity, organisation, visual-presentation, or convention problems materially impede reading but are repairable through focused revision.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Paper-visible evidence of a clearly identified, original, significant contribution with a defensible scope and meaningful relevance to the intended scholarly readership, without presuming any venue alignment in advance.
what_triggers_block: The contribution appears insufficiently original, significant, scoped, or relevant to the intended readership, but substantial reframing or strengthening could make the central contribution suitable for scholarly consideration.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution appears potentially meaningful, yet its originality, significance, scope, or readership relevance is not established clearly enough and needs targeted clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: Paper-visible evidence shows that the central contribution is wholly non-original or fundamentally outside the contemplated scholarly readership, with no credible revision path that preserves the paper's core contribution.
