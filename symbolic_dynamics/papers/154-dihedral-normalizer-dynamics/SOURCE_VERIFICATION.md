## Direct-owner name resolution

The authoritative citation form retained in `references.bib` is **Hader
Baqer Shelash**, matching the author line of the article PDF and the surname
printed in the official journal volume index.  The underlying records are:

- article DOI `10.47974/JDMSC-1553`, whose PDF author line gives Hader Baqer
  Shelash and whose running header gives H. B. Shelash;
- the official JDMSC volume-26 index, which lists H. B. Shelash for the same
  article;
- the article contact address `hayder.ameen@uokufa.edu.iq`.

The spellings **Hayder** and the contact-address surname **Ameen** therefore
remain recorded as identity/metadata variants, not as a second author and not
as grounds for changing the printed citation.  This metadata repair does not
alter the zero-credit assignment to the direct one-step owner.
