# Methodology blueprint

## Research paradigm

**Selected:** exact deductive mathematical audit.

**Justification:** the question is a compatibility question among finite
cyclic words, clocks, monomials, multiplicities, and determinant coefficients.
Exact counterexamples and formal coefficient comparison answer it; statistical
inference does not.

## Method

**Type:** theorem proof plus source-ownership and literature-collision audit.

**Specific method:**

1. freeze the Session-4 `SD-C01` object and all type conventions;
2. derive its marked primitive Euler product from periodic-point counts;
3. state necessary rational-prime factor conditions before inspecting
   witnesses;
4. use independent length-two, degree-one multiplicity, and first-coefficient
   witnesses;
5. run positive controls showing the same ledger is correct for finite-field
   prime polynomials;
6. classify every declared repair by object, clock, marker, and operator owner;
7. apply strict Route-A v0.2 coordinates without inheriting or combining
   coordinates from other candidates.

## Data strategy

**Data type:** frozen mathematical source bytes and primary bibliographic
records.

**Sources:** the six immutable Session-4 cards, the `SD-C01` derivation/proof,
the terminal Paper-39 seal, the final Paper-40 research seal, the frozen
Paper-41 preauthority seal, the Route-A v0.2 criterion, Bowen-Lanford, current
necklace-polynomial literature, and an authoritative rational Euler-product
reference.

**Sampling:** complete over all six Session-4 cards; theorem witnesses are
exact, not sampled. The literature search is bounded and non-exhaustive.

**Time frame:** source and web audit through 2026-08-17 UTC.

## Analytical framework

### Exact layers

1. **Combinatorial:** primitive necklaces and ordinary word powers.
2. **Clock:** `T_q(gamma)=|gamma| log q` and target `T_p=log p`.
3. **Marker:** `z` counts one original shift symbol; a length-`n` primitive
   factor carries `z^n`.
4. **Weight:** source `q^{-ns}` versus target `p^{-s}`.
5. **Multiplicity:** `N_q(n)` source factors versus one Euler factor per
   rational prime.
6. **Operator:** the scalar weighted adjacency for the full shift versus the
   separate diagonal rational-prime comparison operator.

### Validation criteria

| Criterion | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Convention fidelity | Re-derive `#Fix(sigma^r)=q^r` and `D_q=1-zq^(1-s)`. |
| Quantifier validity | Every no-go states the exact map class and preserved fields. |
| Independent witnesses | Clock/support, multiplicity, and analytic coefficient failures do not depend on one another. |
| Positive controls | Function-field prime-polynomial ledger remains exact. |
| Ownership | A repair receives no credit after object, marker, clock, or operator changes. |
| Reproducibility | Portable source IDs and sorted self-excluding manifests. |

## Limitations by design

- The theorem is source-specific and does not classify all arithmetic
  symbolic systems.
- The selection rule is retrospective over known outcomes and witnesses.
- The literature search does not establish nonexistence of an equivalent
  theorem under all terminology.
- No numerical experiment, target-zero comparison, or Route-B work is part of
  Phase 1.

## Ethics and integrity

No human subjects or personal data are involved. The main integrity risk is
novelty inflation: classical source identities must not be presented as new,
and an unsuccessful recent-window search must not be presented as a theorem of
literature absence.

## Reporting and preregistration

- Reporting standard: theorem/proof package with explicit falsifiers and
  source-to-claim matrix.
- Preregistration: not applicable to the already-known retrospective theorem
  witnesses. Only final corrected input bytes are frozen before independent
  DA; this is not called prospective preregistration.
- Cross-model checkpoint: unavailable to this Phase-1 worker. Independent DA
  is therefore a mandatory downstream gate, not simulated here.
