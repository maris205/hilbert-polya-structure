# Research question brief

## Question

For the exact full-`q` shift of historical `SD-C01`, can its primitive
necklaces be identified factor by factor with rational primes while preserving
all of the following on the same object?

1. the source clock `T_q(gamma)=|gamma| log q`;
2. the free source-symbol marker `z`, so a length-`n` primitive factor carries
   `z^n`;
3. temporal repetition;
4. one source factor per target rational prime with correct multiplicity;
5. the Bowen-Lanford determinant owner and its normalization.

If not, what is the smallest exact witness, and which repairs leave the frozen
object or marker?

## Exact claim boundary

The package proves nonexistence of a total map

\[
 \pi:\operatorname{Prim}_q\longrightarrow\mathbb P
\]

that preserves the exact clock

\[
 \log\pi(\gamma)=|\gamma|\log q
\]

for every primitive necklace. It separately proves that no factorwise
identification preserves source marker, analytic weight, and multiplicity, and
that the two marked determinants disagree already in their first `z`
coefficient.

The theorem does not rule out:

- the exact function-field Euler ledger;
- an arbitrary relabeling that abandons the source clock;
- an induced, projected, or re-marked changed object;
- a new countable rational-prime inventory;
- infinite-memory or infinite-dimensional systems;
- all maps between function-field and number-field arithmetic.

## Subquestions

1. Does the norm-compatible map `gamma -> q^|gamma|` ever have rational-prime
   support beyond length one?
2. Can length-one factors satisfy target multiplicity even though their clocks
   agree with the prime `q`?
3. Can equality of marked determinants survive the first `z` coefficient?
4. Which proposed repairs change the marker, object, or determinant owner?

## Decision criterion

Proceed to independent DA only if all exact statements are provable without a
prime table, zero table, numerical fitting, asymptotics, or a post-hoc
projection. Stop or reframe if the claim requires a universal naturality
classification or if primary literature already states the exact typed
closure theorem.
