Abstract. On a loopless labelled directed graph, choose a uniform vertex and replace its outgoing
star by a copy of its incoming star. We determine the resulting absorbing chain at every time. The
unordered pairs carrying unequal opposite arcs form a conflict graph, and one update deletes precisely
the conflict star at the chosen vertex. Hence the length-t absorption probability is an exact sum over
independent vertex sets of the initial conflict graph. The endpoint law is finer: the local idempotents
do not commute, and each resolved conflict is oriented by which endpoint appears first in the history.
We turn this first-occurrence order into a complete source–target kernel using Stirling numbers. Finally,
for every target we count both labelled predecessor/action pairs and distinct predecessor states; the
two counts differ because all admissible predecessor families meet at the target. Exhaustion through
four vertices supplies 47,033 author-side regression assertions, not proof or novelty evidence. External
ownership remains unresolved, so the manuscript status is HOLD_EXTERNAL.

Proof. If Cv A = B, all pairs incident with v are symmetric in B, so v must be isolated in H(B). Conversely,
fix such a vertex. Every arc not leaving v is forced to equal its target bit, while the n − 1 arcs leaving v
are overwritten and may be chosen freely. This gives 2n−1 sources for each of the k(B) labelled actions,
proving (8).
Each admissible-vertex source family contains B. Suppose a source belongs to the families for two
distinct vertices v and w. Membership in the w-family forces every arc leaving v except possibly v → w
to its target value; the v-family gives the analogous statement for arcs leaving w. On the remaining pair,
the v-family fixes w → v and the w-family fixes v → w. Thus the source is B. The families have no other
intersections, so their union contains one shared state plus 2n−1 − 1 new states per isolated vertex. If
there is no isolated vertex, there is no admissible action. This proves (9).
□
For n = 1, the unique empty digraph is fixed, has one labelled predecessor pair and one distinct source;
all formulas include this boundary.
