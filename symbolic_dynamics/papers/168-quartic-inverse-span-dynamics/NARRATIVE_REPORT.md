# P168 narrative report

## Literal object

Fix a prime `p` and view `K=F_{p^4}` as a four-dimensional vector space over
`F_p`.  The state space contains every linear subspace of `K`.  The update
inverts each nonzero point and then repairs the nonlinear image by taking its
`F_p`-linear span:

```text
J(A)=span_Fp(A^(-1)),   J(0)=0.
```

The span makes this a self-map on the full subspace lattice.  No basis,
coordinate support, rank-dependent switch, or chosen linear operator appears
in the definition.

## Story

The map has only one transient basin, but its depth changes sharply with the
base prime.  A non-subfield plane is a projective line generated by `1` and an
element `alpha` of degree four.  Its inverse projective points have
representatives

```text
1 and (alpha-t)^(-1),  t in F_p.
```

Clearing denominators shows that any at most four of these points are
independent.  There are only three at `p=2`, so the plane first becomes a
hyperplane and only then the full field.  At every odd prime there are at
least four, so the plane reaches the full field in one update.  This gives the
sharp depth jump `2 versus 1` without a parameter search.

Rank cannot decrease because inversion preserves the number of nonzero
points.  Equality means that the inverse set already fills a subspace, so two
updates return to the original state.  The published classification of such
subspaces reduces the recurrent core to zero, the full field, all lines, and
the scaled quadratic subfields.  On the latter two strata the dynamics is
plain inversion on cyclic scalar quotients.  Gaussian coefficients then give
all recurrent and periodic counts and the finite-map zeta function.

The inverse axis is target-local.  Rank growth rules out incoming edges to
non-subfield planes and, away from the binary case, to hyperplanes.  At
`p=2`, all 30 non-subfield planes land on the 15 hyperplanes.  The twisted
scalar relation `J(lambda A)=lambda^(-1)J(A)` and the Singer action on trace
hyperplanes force equal fibres, hence exactly two plane predecessors per
hyperplane.  These exclusions and one uniform count determine every target's
fibre at every positive time.

## Formula capsule

Set

```text
L=p^3+p^2+p+1,
P=(p^2+1)(p^2+p+1),
Q=p^2+1,
S=2+2L+P,
R=2+L+Q,
F=2+gcd(2,L)+gcd(2,Q).
```

Then `S` is the total number of states, `R` the recurrent count, and `F` the
fixed count.  There are `(R-F)/2` two-cycles.  The depth enumerator is

```text
D_2(u)=R+Lu+(P-Q)u^2,
D_p(u)=R+(S-R)u for odd p.
```

The zeta function is

```text
zeta_J(z)=(1-z)^(-F)(1-z^2)^(-(R-F)/2).
```

At `p=2`, the one-step image has `R+L=37` states and the stable image after
two steps has `R=22`.  At odd primes, the first image is already the recurrent
core.  The full-field fibre is `1+L` at binary time one and
`1+L+P-Q` thereafter; at odd primes it is `1+L+P-Q` at every positive time.
