## Exact owner translation used in the revision

For a connected component `C` of order `s`, choose a pivot `*` and use

```text
theta_*([a]) = (a_v + a_*)_(v != *).
```

Then the non-pivot labelled pushes map to the coordinate generators of
`F_2^(s-1)`, and the pivot push maps to the all-ones generator.  This is the
standard Xu--Meng Cayley presentation of `FQ_(s-1)`.  The manuscript exposes
the coordinate bridge but assigns the identification no residual credit.

The low-dimensional conventions are not hidden:

- `s=1`: the quotient is trivial and the sole labelled push is an identity
  move;
- `s=2`: the two labelled vertices map to the same nonzero generator in
  `F_2`; the normalised component kernel is the usual `FQ_1` walk, while the
  unnormalised labelled operator contains the generator twice;
- `s>=3`: the `s` generators are distinct and give the standard simple folded
  hypercube.

The disconnected kernel is a degree-weighted random-scan tensor sum, not an
unweighted simple-graph Cartesian product.  Accordingly, the manuscript
distinguishes Markov transition eigenvalues from unnormalised simple-graph
adjacency eigenvalues.

The revision does not retain the carrier, quotient identification,
single-component spectrum, bipartiteness, return-as-spectral-moment fact, or
folded-hypercube random-walk setting as contributions.  Its residual is
limited to:

1. the explicit labelled multi-component weighted-product factorisation; and
