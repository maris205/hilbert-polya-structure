## Problem and exact scope

Classify and count the cycles of the established pointer-reversal update
R(u,v,f)=(v,f(v),f[v:=u]) on the full labelled carrier [n]^2 × [n]^[n].
All right-hand sides use the old state. No nil, stopping rule, distinct-register
assumption, auxiliary clock, hidden edge identity or quotient of labels is added.
The interest is the arbitrary-register closed completion, not invention of the
reversal kernel or a claim of global novelty.

## Exactly two axes

1. The seven-row exact-period theorem, including every loop/parallel-edge
   degeneration, the complete attained period set and sharp maximum.
2. Fixed-labelled-core orbit decorations and arbitrary frozen-complement maps,
   yielding the explicitly evaluated bivariate rational core EGF and full orbit
   census. Its q=1 specialization is evaluated, not left as an unnamed series.

These axes share the core and return-state analysis. Their information differs:
equal periods need not imply equal orbit counts. They are not logically disjoint.
Inverse, fixed states, zero preperiod, unit fibres, the augmented-edge invariant,
list reversal, rotor theorems and generic labelled-counting operations receive
zero independent contribution credit.
