# Paper07 Preregistration

## Frozen hypothesis

An entropy-oriented successor coupling can violate atom-mass commutation without creating mixed periodic words. Endpoint-symmetric weighting may produce critical-axis chiral spectral motion while retaining the exact Euler ledger.

## Positive gates

1. Tensor atoms and their order are intrinsic.
2. Every periodic word is a pure atom loop.
3. Every prime-power trace is exact.
4. The Fredholm determinant equals the Euler product on its honest half-plane.
5. The chiral double has a nonempty Schatten strip and exact `s -> 1-s` symmetry.
6. A preregistered unitary invariant changes with height.

## Stop gates

- Euler and chiral determinants are not one analytic object.
- Chiral regularization deletes the divergent quadratic trace.
- Critical-axis crossings have no target counting law.
- A fixed self-adjoint generator is absent.
- Arbitrary DAG controls share the mechanism.

## Frozen controls

- one-sided random noncommuting couplings;
- reverse successor edges;
- shuffled mass order;
- matched composite/random masses;
- arbitrary forward-edge phases and random DAGs: must preserve the all-order
  ledger by triangularity and test whether motion is nonselective;
- exact two-atom prefixes.
