\section{Proof details, determinant conventions, and scope}
\label{app:proofs}

\subsection{Operator-ideal estimates}

The singular values of $D_s$ are $p_n^{-\RePart s}$.  For a weighted
unilateral shift $W_se_n=w_n(s)e_{n+1}$, the singular values are
$|w_n(s)|$.  Here
\[
 |w_n(s)|\le\tfrac12
 (p_n^{-\RePart s}+p_{n+1}^{-\RePart s}).
\]
Consequently $L_s\in S_q$ whenever
$\sum_np_n^{-q\RePart s}<\infty$, namely when $q\RePart s>1$.  Applying the
same argument to $L_{1-s}^{\mathsf T}$ gives the common $S_q$ strip
\[
 \frac1q<\RePart s<1-\frac1q.
\]
The first integer $q$ with a nonempty strip is $q=3$.

\subsection{Regularized determinant convention}

For $A\in S_3$ we use
\[
 \det{}_3(I-A)
 =\det\bigl((I-A)e^{A+A^2/2}\bigr),
\]
so near the origin
\[
 \log\det{}_3(I-zA)
 =-\sum_{r\ge3}\frac{z^r}{r}\Tr A^r.
\]
For the chiral $\cB_s$, every odd trace vanishes.  The first visible term is
$r=4$.  This is a genuine determinant theorem in the $S_3$ strip, but the
subtracted $r=2$ term is the divergent Hellinger trace.  We do not restore it
by an ad hoc counterterm.

\subsection{Finite-prefix determinant}

Let $P_N$ project onto the first $N$ entropy-ordered atoms.  Then
$P_NL_sP_N$ is triangular and
\[
 \det(I-zP_NL_sP_N)=\prod_{n\le N}(1-zp_n^{-s}).
\]
For $\RePart s>1$, $P_NL_sP_N\to L_s$ in trace norm.  Continuity of the
Fredholm determinant proves the infinite product identity independently of
the logarithmic trace expansion.

\subsection{Source and data firewall}

The construction uses the multiplication law of full shifts, topological
entropy, the induced order on tensor atoms, and endpoint roof evaluation.
It loads no Riemann-zero table.  Finite computations may generate
indecomposables of the multiplication table algorithmically; no list of
primes is an input to the candidate.

The following implications are explicitly forbidden:
\begin{align*}
 \Delta_3(1-s,z)=\Delta_3(s,z)
   &\not\Rightarrow \text{Riemann functional equation},\\
 \cB_{1/2+it}=\cB_{1/2+it}^*
   &\not\Rightarrow \text{Hilbert--P\'olya operator},\\
 \text{critical-axis crossings}
   &\not\Rightarrow \text{Riemann zeros},\\
 \text{moving singular spectrum}
   &\not\Rightarrow \text{moving Euler divisor}.
\end{align*}

Any plan requiring a separate geometric, graph-theoretic physical, or
Hamiltonian system is outside the primary family and may appear only as a
\texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE}.
