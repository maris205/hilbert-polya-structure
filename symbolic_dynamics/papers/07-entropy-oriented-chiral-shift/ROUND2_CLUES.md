# Paper07 Round-2 Clues

## RC-07-01 — fixed generator may require a carrier

SD-C09 supplies a parameter-dependent self-adjoint symbolic pencil, not a
fixed operator whose eigenvalue is the critical height. A carrier that turns
endpoint phase interference into a fixed generator may be required. Do not
construct it in Session 4.

## RC-07-02 — local turning phases

Kac--Ward-type planar turning phases implement an all-order sign-reversing
loop involution. Their phase is geometric rather than intrinsic to the
present tensor-prime grammar. Record only; do not import it into SD-C09.

## Same-family branch, not ROUND2

The free-group canonical-trace extension remains Symbolic Dynamics and is a
candidate for Paper08. It should be tested first for the `g g^{-1}` chiral
positivity obstruction and a nonempty infinite-atom determinant domain.
