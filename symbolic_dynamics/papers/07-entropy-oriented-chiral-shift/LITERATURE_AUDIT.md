# Paper07 Literature Audit

## Primary conclusions

1. Formal-language zeta theory already provides virtual-character and
   triangular realizations of cyclic languages. Broad novelty claims about
   signed cancellation are forbidden.
2. Finite-monoid character theory supports the semisimple/radical reading:
   word traces determine semisimplification while nonsemisimple triangular
   couplings may remain trace-invisible.
3. Exterior automata and homological alternating determinants can cancel
   redundant symbolic sectors, but canceled contractible sectors cannot move
   the corresponding divisor.
4. Finite or compact group cocycles cannot delete all repetitions of a mixed
   invertible monodromy while retaining the identity sector.
5. Free-group canonical traces are a genuine infinite-dimensional symbolic
   escape; their chiral positive trace and analytic domains remain open.

- Clair--Mokhtari-Sharghi (2002) and Guido--Isola--Lapidus (2009), analytic
  graph/von-Neumann determinant precedents.

## Legal novelty statement

Within the finite primary-source search, no prior work was found combining
tensor-prime full-shift atoms, entropy-oriented triangular radical coupling,
an exact Riemann Euler ledger, and a moving critical-axis chiral singular
spectrum in one Route-A audit. This is a source-locked synthesis claim, not a
claim of first-ever signed or graded zeta theory.
