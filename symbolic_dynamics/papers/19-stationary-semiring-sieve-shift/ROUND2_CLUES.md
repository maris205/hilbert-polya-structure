# ROUND2 CLUES — SD-C21

These notes leave the current Symbolic Dynamics-only scope.  They are
recorded but not used in the manuscript or Route score.

## ROUND2_CLUE 1 — scattering or self-adjoint realization

One could ask whether a graph with arithmetic recurrent interactions admits
a canonical unitary, scattering, or self-adjoint realization whose spectral
determinant continues beyond the trace-class half-plane.

**Why stopped:** SD-C21 defines no boundary conditions, physical carrier,
domain, self-adjointness theorem, scattering matrix, or completed
determinant.  Route B remains locked.

## ROUND2_CLUE 2 — geometric realization of semiring witnesses

The quotient witness (F_d\boxtimes F_q=F_n) might be realized by a geometric
cover, correspondence, or moduli object so that successful factorization
changes recurrent topology rather than selecting an accept loop.

**Why stopped:** such a carrier is not a symbolic shift and would require an
independent source/naturality audit.

## ROUND2_CLUE 3 — cohomological cancellation of extra cycles

Signed chain complexes or supertraces might retain recurrent computation
while cancelling unwanted primitive classes.

**Why stopped:** the positive exact-ledger theorem intentionally excludes
cancellation.  A cohomological lift changes the data type and must re-audit
temporal powers, chain maps, and determinant compatibility from scratch.

## In-family continuation, not ROUND2

Remain in Symbolic Dynamics and remove accepted-object loops.  Build a local
countable grammar on all nonunit full shifts, for example an expanded witness
presentation of

\[
F_m\longrightarrow F_d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d>1\text{ and }F_d\boxtimes F_c=F_m\boxplus F_1
\text{ for some exposed }c.
\]

Before choosing any weights, enumerate primitive SCCs and compare them under
multiplication-shuffled, addition-shuffled, composite, random-divisibility,
and factorial-monoid controls.  The object advances only if arithmetic is a
cycle invariant rather than an accept label.
