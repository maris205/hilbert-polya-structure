## Argument blueprint

### Core claim

Order information repairs Paper22's abelian blindness only enough to expose a
unary memory problem.  Every fixed finite or linear reader is too periodic;
larger readers become target compilers; and none repairs the original
factorial orbit weight.

### Reasoning chain

1. Mark the unique minimum of the exact \(Q=2\) family and derive
   \(1^{k-1}2\).
2. Reduce all fixed finite fibers to powers in one finite semigroup.
3. Reduce all fixed weighted fibers to one characteristic recurrence.
4. Contrast the infinite no-go with explicit arbitrary finite-prefix
   realizations.
5. Attach the linear fiber to the same trace-class vertex adjacency.
6. Show that coefficient selection leaves \(z^kM_k^{-2s}\).
7. Compare the two licensed countable exact repairs from Papers19--20:
   transient pruning, recurrent clock dilution, and their inducing
   consequence.
8. Close Route A before any zero comparison.

## Section plan

### Abstract

State the exact word, finite-semigroup theorem, LRS/SML theorem, nilpotent
control, countable wrapper alternative, factorial roof, and strict route
verdict.  No citations.

### 1. Introduction

Open with the exact ordered word, not broad RH motivation.  Explain why it is
the smallest unresolved object after Paper22.  State four falsifiable
contributions and show Figure 1.  Surface the wrong-roof conclusion before
technical detail.

### 2. Prior boundary and novelty

Synthesize unary automata, prime recognition, weighted automata/LRS, SML, and
de Jong's direct Symbolic-Dynamics collision.  State precisely what is
model-specific.

### 3. Frozen symbolic source

Define the full-shift operations, successor–divisor graph, quotient word,
unique-minimum marking, endpoint roof, graph marker, complex block
adjacency, and determinant convention.

### 4. Finite-fiber periodicity

Prove the prime-only periodic-set lemma, finite-semigroup power lemma,
finite-group corollary, and DFA/NFA no-go.  State quantifiers carefully.

### 5. Linear-recurrence support rigidity

Derive the Cayley–Hamilton recurrence and rational generating functions.
Invoke SML for zero/nonzero and fixed-level supports.  State the
sign/cutpoint firewall.

### 6. Growing finite memory

Construct bilinear and trace nilpotent memorizers.  Compare primes with
matched arbitrary targets and label every finite fit PROVES_TOO_MUCH.

### 7. Same-object Fredholm and roof ledger

Prove the sharp \(\mathcal S_1\) threshold over \(\mathbb C\), distinguish
the complete block factor from its first trace-log term and marked response,
derive the factorial roof and Stirling scale, and
show that even oracle filtering misses both the marker and roof.

### 8. Countable wrappers

Restate the universal transient total-decider theorem and recurrent
clock-dilution theorem.  Prove the \(C_k\) corollary and show that first
return changes \(z^k\) to \(z\).  Include Figure 2.

### 9. Exact audit protocol

Present E1–E10 as preregistered theorem/implementation audits.  Do not report
unexecuted counts.  Separate candidate source from post-freeze evaluator.

### 10. Route evaluation

Give the claims table, tuple, overall rejection, and Paper24 admission test.
Route B remains locked.

### 11. Conclusion

Restate the hierarchy without repeating the introduction.  Name the exact
remaining open grammar obligation.

### Appendix A

Full proof details for finite packages, operator compressions, transient
majorant, recurrent noncompactness, first return, and factorial asymptotic.

### Appendix B
