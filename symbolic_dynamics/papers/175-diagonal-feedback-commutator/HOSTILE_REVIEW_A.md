## Adversarial claim audit

### HR-A1 — Does `Phi^2=0` depend on characteristic or diagonalizability?

No.  Every first image has zero diagonal entrywise.  The feedback diagonal
of that image is therefore the zero matrix over every finite field, so the
second commutator is identically zero.  The proof covers prime powers and
does not invoke spectral diagonalization.

### HR-A2 — Is undirected support sufficient when `B` is directed?

Yes.  For fixed prospective diagonal colours `c_i`, the scalar equation
`(c_i-c_j)a_ij=b_ij` is independently solvable for each ordered pair.  An
edge is forbidden from being monochromatic if either direction of `B` is
nonzero.  If both directions vanish and the colours agree, both ordered
source entries are free.  Thus the simple union support records exactly the
proper-colouring constraint, while the exponent counts ordered free slots.

### HR-A3 — Does the occupation polynomial overcount diagonals or omit
off-diagonal degrees of freedom?

No.  A colouring is exactly one input diagonal.  For each admissible
colouring, unequal-colour ordered pairs have one forced entry and equal-colour
ordered pairs have `q` choices.  Their number is
`sum_alpha n_alpha(n_alpha-1)`.  Grouping these terms by the colour-class
sizes gives the stated marked coefficients and zero-fibre weak-composition
formula.

### HR-A4 — Is zero really the unique maximum target?

Yes.  The empty support of zero admits every colouring with its positive
weight.  Every nonzero zero-diagonal target has a nonempty support graph and
removes at least one positive-weight colouring; any target with nonzero
diagonal has empty fibre.  The comparison is termwise, so varying occupation
weights cannot reverse it.

### HR-A5 — Does the tree census confuse kernel states with image states?

No.  `im Phi` is contained in the set-theoretic zero fibre because
`Phi^2=0`.  Nonzero elements of the zero fibre are precisely depth-one
vertices.  Among them, exactly the nonzero image points have predecessors;
every predecessor of such a point lies outside the zero fibre and is a
depth-two leaf.  This yields the branch/leaf and mass formulas without an
unstated linear-kernel assumption.

### HR-A6 — Is classical commutator or Potts ownership being claimed?

No.  The manuscript assigns fixed/two-input commutator theory, P119's
centralizer-coset machinery, ordinary graph colouring, Potts/Tutte
partition functions, and Artin–Mazur zeta bookkeeping zero contribution
credit.  The residual is explicitly the conjunction generated by the
state-extracted diagonal.  A bounded search non-hit is not called novelty.
