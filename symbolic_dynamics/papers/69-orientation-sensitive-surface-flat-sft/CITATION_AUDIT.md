# Citation audit

## Verified sources

### Klug (modern account of the surface homomorphism formulas)

- Michael R. Klug, “Counting homomorphisms from surface groups to finite
  groups,” *Canadian Mathematical Bulletin* **68** (2025), no. 1, 141--153.
- DOI: `10.4153/S0008439524000420`.
- arXiv: `2106.11089`.
- Source role: a modern source and normalization check, not the original owner
  of the classical formulas.  Verified content: Definition of the
  Frobenius--Schur indicator; Theorem 1
  and Corollary 1 give the Frobenius/Frobenius--Schur word-count formulas; the
  closed-surface displays following Theorem 3 give the orientable and
  nonorientable homomorphism normalizations used here.
- Bibliographic note: the article was published online in 2024 and assigned to
  the March 2025 issue.  The bibliography uses the issue year 2025.

### Carroll--Penland (periodic points for group SFTs)

- David Carroll and Andrew Penland, “Periodic points on shifts of finite type
  and commensurability invariants of groups,” *New York Journal of
  Mathematics* **21** (2015), 811--822.
- arXiv: `1502.03195`.
- Verified content: periodic points of SFTs over groups and their relation to
  finite-index subgroup data.  Cited only for context.

### Cohen--Goodman-Strauss (surface-group SFT context)

- David Bruce Cohen and Chaim Goodman-Strauss, “Strongly aperiodic subshifts
  on surface groups,” *Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics* **11** (2017), no. 3,
  1041--1059.
- DOI: `10.4171/GGD/421`; arXiv: `1510.06439`.
- Verified content: finite-type symbolic systems over surface groups.  Cited
  only for context, not as a source for the flat-connection count.
