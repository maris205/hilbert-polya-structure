## Contract Paraphrase

D1 concerns whether the study design, treatment of data, statistical reporting, and support for reproducibility satisfy the field's peer-review standard; the methodology role alone is eligible to assess this mandatory dimension.

D2 concerns whether claims agree with current domain evidence, prior work is represented accurately, and domain-specific terminology and results are factually sound; the domain role alone is eligible to assess this mandatory dimension.

D3 concerns whether the central thesis is internally consistent, the offered evidence supports the claims, and no fallacy undermines the core argument; the devil's-advocate and methodology roles are eligible to assess this mandatory dimension.

D4 concerns whether framing, definitions, and implications can be understood by readers in adjacent fields and whether any interdisciplinary claims receive adequate support; the perspective role alone is eligible to assess this high-priority dimension.

D5 concerns manuscript organization, expository clarity, the quality of figures and tables, and conformity with relevant venue conventions; the editor-in-chief role alone is eligible to assess this normal-priority dimension.

D6 concerns whether the contribution is original and significant for the configured readership and appropriate to the configured venue; the editor-in-chief role alone is eligible to assess this mandatory dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Determine whether adjacent-field readers can follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and whether interdisciplinary claims are supported rather than merely asserted.
what_triggers_block: Essential framing or definitions are inaccessible to adjacent-field readers, or central interdisciplinary claims lack substantiation to an extent that defeats the claimed cross-disciplinary relevance.
what_triggers_warn: The cross-disciplinary framing is broadly intelligible but contains localized unexplained concepts, weak bridges between fields, or implications that need additional support.
