## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) asks whether the theoretical design, definitions, assumptions, derivations, proof procedures, and reproducibility affordances meet the methodological standard required for peer review; this is a methodology-eligible mandatory dimension.

D2 (domain_accuracy) asks whether field-specific claims, terminology, results, and representations of prior evidence are accurate; it is mandatory but assigned to the domain seat rather than the methodology seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) asks whether the central thesis is internally consistent and whether each material conclusion follows from adequate mathematical support without a core-invalidating fallacy; methodology shares eligibility for this mandatory dimension.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) asks whether framing, definitions, implications, and interdisciplinary claims are usable and substantiated for adjacent-field readers; it is high priority and reserved for the perspective seat.

D5 (writing_and_structure) asks whether organisation, exposition, figures or tables, and relevant formal conventions support a clear manuscript; it is a normal-priority dimension owned by the eic seat.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) assigns the eic seat the mandatory task of assessing contribution and configured-target suitability; the methodology seat does not score this dimension, and absent criteria authority it makes no target-alignment determination.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit definitions and assumptions, fit between stated aims and proof strategy, valid derivations, treatment of edge cases, and enough procedural detail to independently audit or reproduce the theoretical analysis.
what_triggers_block: A material methodological gap, invalid derivation, or missing justification leaves a principal result unsupported but potentially repairable through substantial correction or reconstruction.
what_triggers_warn: A localized omission, ambiguous assumption, or incomplete reproducibility detail limits auditability while the main methodological chain remains evaluable.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational inconsistency or invalid method defeats the core results and cannot be repaired without replacing the central theoretical framework.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Traceability from premises and definitions through intermediate claims to the central conclusions, with consistent quantifiers, scopes, dependencies, and handling of logical alternatives.
what_triggers_block: A central inference is invalid or materially incomplete and requires substantial reconstruction, although a coherent core argument could still survive.
what_triggers_warn: A bounded ambiguity or localized logical gap weakens one part of the exposition without undermining the central thesis.
what_triggers_fatal: A contradiction, circular dependency, or decisive counterexample collapses the central thesis rather than merely exposing a repairable gap.
