# Bilingual abstract

## English

Let `Lambda` be the fundamental group of the closed nonorientable surface of
genus three and let `K` be a finite group.  We define a finite-type
`Lambda`-shift whose symbols are `K`-labels on generator edges and whose local
rule imposes trivial holonomy around the surface relator.  Two explicit
finite-index subgroup families produce orientable and nonorientable covers.  We
compute their fixed-point counts as

```text
|K|^(4m) sum_chi d_chi^(-2m),
|K|^(2n) sum_chi nu_chi^(n+2)d_chi^(-n),
```

where `d_chi` and `nu_chi` are the degree and Frobenius--Schur indicator of an
irreducible complex character.  Finite moment inversion shows that the two
spectra jointly determine `|K|` and the multiset of pairs `(d_chi,nu_chi)`.
In particular, the order-eight dihedral and quaternion groups have identical
orientable spectra but are separated at every odd level of the nonorientable
family.  The surface homomorphism formulas used in the proof are classical;
no priority claim is made.

## 中文

设 `Lambda` 为闭合三亏格非定向曲面的基本群，`K` 为有限群。我们构造一个
有限型 `Lambda`-子移位：符号给曲面 Cayley 复形的生成元边赋 `K`-标号，局部
规则要求曲面关系词周围的有序 holonomy 为单位元。两个显式有限指数子群族分别
给出定向与非定向覆盖，其固定点数精确为

```text
|K|^(4m) sum_chi d_chi^(-2m),
|K|^(2n) sum_chi nu_chi^(n+2)d_chi^(-n).
```

其中 `d_chi` 与 `nu_chi` 分别是不可约复特征标的次数和 Frobenius--Schur
指示子。通过有限指数矩的 Vandermonde 反演，这两列谱联合确定 `|K|` 以及
所有 `(d_chi,nu_chi)` 的多重集。特别地，八阶二面体群与四元数群的定向谱
完全相同，但在非定向族的每个奇数层都被区分。证明所用曲面群同态计数公式为
经典结果；本文不作优先权声明。
