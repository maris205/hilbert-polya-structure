# ROUND2 Clues — SD-C27

**Primary-family rule:** every item below is quarantined from the Symbolic
Dynamics proofs and Route-A score.  These are analogy prompts, not imported
evidence.

## 1. Cyclic bar and Hochschild selectors

The tangent exterior complex cancels local stability but preserves branch
words.  A reduced cyclic bar or Hochschild complex might instead attach
signs to support changes inside a cyclic word.  It earns credit only if the
complex is functorially derived from the frozen multiplication/factorization
source and its full cyclic supertrace kills all mixed primitive necklaces at
every repetition.

## 2. Divisor-support incidence complexes

A simplex whose vertices are distinct atom colors has reduced Euler
characteristic behavior suggestive of a mixed-support cancellation.  The
danger is that naming the colors already supplies the atom inventory.  A
valid construction must produce colors and incidence maps from source
relations and survive arbitrary-inventory controls.

## 3. Möbius and inclusion–exclusion analogies

Möbius inversion can isolate primitive or square-free structure in a
commutative divisor lattice.  Temporal primitive words live in a
noncommutative cyclic ledger.  A Möbius analogy is inadmissible until an
operator complex realizes its signs and trace class in every degree.

## 4. Trace monoids and partial commutation

Partially commutative languages may represent multiplication more faithfully
than a free renewal monoid.  They still need a stationary recurrent source,
an intrinsic roof, a visible marker, and a theorem identifying primitive
trace classes without listing primes.

## 5. Relative determinants beyond finite grading

An infinite complex may admit a relative determinant even when its degrees
are not separately trace class.  This would be a materially new analytic
object.  It must prove convergence, cyclicity, homotopy invariance, and
marker preservation; formal cancellation of divergent traces is not enough.

## 6. Torsion and non-acyclic survivors

The de Rham fiber here is contractible except for constants.  Reidemeister,
analytic, or categorical torsion suggests asking whether a source-derived
branch complex can retain a nontrivial global survivor.  Any such analogy is
Round2-only until it produces a same-object determinant theorem.

## 7. Paper26 decisive target

Freeze a shared prefix-renewal/factorization system and seek a cyclic
incidence complex with

\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(\alpha)=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&\alpha\text{ is monochromatic},\\
 0,&\alpha\text{ uses at least two atom colors}.
 \end{cases}
\]

The identity must hold at the full cyclic trace level, preserve pure
repetitions, retain the original digit marker, and use trace-class degrees
or a justified relative determinant.  The competing collapse theorem is
that the surviving cohomology splits into one monochromatic sector per
supplied label and is therefore equivalent to the disjoint atom inventory.
