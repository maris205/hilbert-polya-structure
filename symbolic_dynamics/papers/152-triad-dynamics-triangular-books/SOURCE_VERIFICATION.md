## Terminology firewall

The triangular book B(3,r)=K_{1,1,r} has triangles sharing one **edge**. A
friendship or Dutch-windmill graph has triangles sharing one **vertex**. The
latter has deterministic count descent under the update and is not the
paper's carrier. Some graph sources also use “book graph” for quadrilateral
pages; the manuscript always includes “triangular” and the explicit
K_{1,1,r} identification.
