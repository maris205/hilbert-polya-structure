# Figure plan — P194 Round 0

## Decision

No figure is included in the Round-0 manuscript.

The paper's two central objects are already exact and compact in symbolic
form: the seven-state example orbit in equation (1.2) and the targetwise
predecessor set in equation (4.1).  A generic crystal drawing would mainly
illustrate classical background and could visually over-credit the carrier
rather than the least-colour scheduler.

## Optional post-gate figure

If external owner clearance is later obtained, one figure may be useful:

- **Panel A:** the full `[3]^3` crystal, with all crystal edges light grey and
  only least-colour scheduler edges dark;
- **Panel B:** the resulting rooted functional forest, with nodes arranged by
  the exact clock;
- **Panel C:** the fibre of the staircase target `112`, displaying the self
  source and its two admitted lowerings.

The caption would have to say explicitly that the crystal graph and its
highest-weight decomposition are classical and that only the dark edge
selection and labelled fibres are under study.

## Production constraints

- Build from exact verifier output, not by hand.
- Preserve word labels and colour labels on every scheduler edge.
- Use a colour-blind-safe palette and distinguish edges by line weight as
  well as hue.
- Do not display an incomplete induced subgraph as if it were the whole
  carrier.
- Do not add the figure before the owner gate changes from `OWNER_AMBER`.

Round-0 figure count: **zero**.
