# Paper 36 literature audit — SD-C38

Research cutoff: 2026-08-15.

## 1. Question and source policy

The audit asks whether primary literature already proves the exact three-way
compatibility tested here: complete cancellation of affine relation cycles,
preservation of the unequal unit generator-step germ, and an ordinary
same-object primitive Fredholm determinant with nonzero recurrence.

The search was local-first through Papers 34 and 35 and then web-verified
against author, publisher, DOI, journal, and arXiv records. Technical claims
below are assigned only to primary papers or official records. No review,
encyclopedia, or search-result snippet is used as theorem authority.

## 4. Forbidden attributions and phrases

Do not say that the paper invents Cayley-complex contractibility, Fox
derivatives, path homology, Ihara determinants, or finite-factor determinant
theory. Do not say that homology retains arithmetic cycles, that the quotient
produces a Bost--Connes determinant, or that the scalar superdeterminant
discovers primes. Equality only after `z=1` is not a same-marker result.

No functional equation, critical-line divisor, target-zero calculation, RH
implication, or universal no-go for coefficient systems is within scope.
