# Paper 36 paper plan — SD-C38

Working title: **Fill the Relations, Lose the Clock: Chain Quotients of an
Affine Symbolic Shift**.

## 1. Central claim

For the frozen affine one-relator source, complete Cayley-cell cancellation
cannot coexist with nonzero primitive recurrence or the original unit
generator-step germ; an honestly owned trace-class prequotient Hashimoto
operator still sees the relation cycle, while the naive chain superdeterminant
cancels every two-generator one-relator presentation.

## 2. Section architecture

1. **Introduction.** State the Paper 35 obligation, object firewall, theorem,
   route verdict, and non-claims.
2. **Primary-source boundary.** Position Gray--Steinberg, Fox, Ihara/Fredholm,
   path-homology, TR-trace, and `L^2`-torsion sources claim by claim.
3. **Frozen affine object.** Define `M_r`, formal reverses, Hashimoto shift,
   cell complex, marker, damping, and source/evaluator firewall.
4. **Main theorem.** Present the four exact claims and two controls.
5. **Fill and lose.** Prove contractibility and marker non-descent; display the
   explicit cellular boundary.
6. **The prequotient still sees the relation.** Prove trace-class ownership,
   the positive coefficient, and the separate finite-factor control.
7. **Generic cancellation is empty.** Derive `1-2+1`, explain the generic
   control, and separate homology, Fredholm, and finite traces.
8. **Exact audit.** Report the frozen metrics, finite controls, data split, and
   reproducibility without making enumeration prove the theorem.
9. **Route conclusion.** Resolve `A0`--`A4`, close the branch, and state the
   Paper 37 minimum obligation.
10. **Appendix A.** Complete proofs and detailed exponent calculation.
11. **Appendix B.** Scope, forbidden substitutions, and claim firewall.
