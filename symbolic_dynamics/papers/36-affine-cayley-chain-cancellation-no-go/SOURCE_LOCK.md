# Paper 36 source lock — SD-C38

## 1. Exact question

On one source-natural affine presentation object, can cellular cancellation
simultaneously remove immediate backtracks and every affine relation cycle,
retain a nonzero primitive recurrent sector, preserve the original free
generator-step marker, descend to an honestly owned same-object determinant,
and fail matched generic presentations?

The answer is negative for the frozen Cayley-chain mechanism below. This is a
scoped incompatibility theorem, not a no-go for all homological, groupoid,
higher-rank, matrix-valued, or derived symbolic constructions.

## 2. Positive affine source

For `r>=2`, freeze

```text
M_r = N_0 semidirect_r N_0 = <u,v | vu=u^r v>+,
(b,k)(d,l) = (b+r^k d,k+l),
u=(1,0), v=(0,1).
```

Every element has unique normal form `u^b v^k`. The positive right actions are

```text
U(b,k)=(b+r^k,k),
V(b,k)=(b,k+1).
```

The baseline is the composite exponent `r=4`; `r=2,3,5` and balanced `r=1`
are controls. No generator or basis vector is prime-labelled. The positive
graph is acyclic because `b+k` strictly increases on every positive edge.

## 3. One recurrent symbolic object

Adjoin a distinct formal reverse to every positive edge. On the resulting
oriented-edge set `E_r^or`, the Hashimoto state shift allows

```text
e -> f  iff  t(e)=o(f) and f is not the formal reverse of e,
```

including the same rule at the cyclic join. The free marker `z` counts one
original oriented graph edge per transition. This explicit line-graph change
is not first return, inducing, or an accelerated clock.

At every `x in M_r`, the word

```text
C_(r,x) = v u bar(v) bar(u)^r
```

is a cyclically nonbacktracking primitive relation polygon of length
`L_r=r+3`.

## 4. Source-derived Cayley cells

At each vertex `x`, attach one `2`-cell comparing the positive paths `xvu` and
`xu^r v`. Call the resulting Cayley complex `K_r`. The cells come from the
presentation before any target or accepted support is known.

The tested cancellation is either path homotopy in `K_r` or the cellular
quotient

```text
H_1(K_r)=ker(partial_1)/im(partial_2).
```

Neither quotient is silently identified with ordinary positive path
multiplicity or with an operator trace.

## 5. Whole prequotient operator

For `0<theta<1` and an oriented edge `e` with origin
`o(e)=(b(e),k(e))`, freeze

```text
d_theta(e)=theta^(1+b(e)+k(e)),
D_theta=diag(d_theta(e)),
```

on the full uninduced space `ell^2(E_r^or)`, where `H_r` is the unweighted

## 6. Frozen theorem

The paper must prove all four conclusions.

1. **Total chain cancellation.** `K_r` is contractible; hence
   `pi_1(K_r)=0` and `H_j(K_r;Z)=0` for every `j>=1`. Complete source-relation
   filling removes every primitive recurrent class, not only the displayed
   relation polygon.
2. **Marker non-descent.** Any cell-invariant additive degree into a
   torsion-free abelian group satisfies `(r-1)deg(u)=0`, hence `deg(u)=0`.
   The unit assignment `deg(u)=deg(v)=1` cannot descend because the cell
   identifies paths of lengths `2` and `r+1`.
3. **Prequotient relation visibility.** `T_(r,theta)` is trace class and, for
   sufficiently small `z`,

   ```text
   -log det(I-zT) = sum_(n>=1) z^n Tr(T^n)/n.
   ```

   With `S_r=r(r+1)/2+2r+5`, nonnegative path weights and the relation polygon
   give `Tr(T^(r+3)) >= (r+3) theta^(2S_r)>0`. The empty quotient ledger is
   therefore not the determinant of this same prequotient operator.
4. **Generic graded cancellation.** For the diagonal scalar lift on the
   enveloping-group Cayley chain complex, cell-orbit multiplicities `(1,2,1)`
   give `Str(A_tilde^n)=(1-2+1)tau(A^n)=0` for every `n>=1`; its graded
   determinant is `1`. This depends only on two generators and one relator,
   works for matched generic presentations, and retains no arithmetic sector.

## 7. Finite and balanced boundaries

Finite semidirect quotients are diagnostic controls, not the infinite source.
Affine cells alone may leave classes generated by finite relations such as
`u^q` and `v^t`; complete finite-presentation filling kills them. Such residue
does not prove descent from the infinite source.

For the balanced control `r=1`, both relation sides have length two and the
unit marker descends. Its filled square-grid Cayley complex is nevertheless
contractible. Marker homogeneity is necessary for descent but insufficient for
retained recurrence.
