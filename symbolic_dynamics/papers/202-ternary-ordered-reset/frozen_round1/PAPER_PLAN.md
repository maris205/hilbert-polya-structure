# P202 paper plan

Title: Two Recurrent Languages and Sharp Tails for a Ternary Reset Rule.
One-sentence contribution: for one synchronous local map on all cyclic
ternary words, identify its full colour/rotation recurrent union and sharp
all-length entrance time, separately from its complete local source atlas.

Format: anonymous project-standard A4 10pt amsart theorem note, approximately
five pages including references. No ML venue is targeted. Full proofs stay
in the main text; no external proof supplement is needed to read the paper.

## Section outline

1. Map and source boundary: define update, tail, colour advance and rotation;
   credit pulse-coupled and particle-parking owners immediately.
2. Every target and all largest fibres: prove the entire decoder, forbidden
   image, boundary n1, and complete even/odd maximum classes.
3. Run coordinates and finite parking: derive the factor with tracked cyclic
   order and prove the sharp finite clearance lemma, including zero mass.
4. Recurrent languages and sharp tails: prove both directions of the union,
   original-coordinate actions, overlap, point clock and global witnesses.
5. Census and exact controls: derive the finite matrices, epsilon/recurrence,
   all-time fixed counts, and state computational and originality limits.

No figures or decorative plots. The local map equation, two recurrent
languages and short run equations explain the relevant structure directly.

the source differences are therefore stated in precise prose instead.

Lind--Marcus 1995 for finite adjacency enumeration. Every entry uses
verified primary metadata and an actual source read or stated metadata-only
scope. No citation is invented from memory.
