# Paper plan — P149

## Working title

Iterated Endpoint-Peak Extraction on Permutations: All-Rank Images, Sections,
and a Sharp Clock

## One-sentence contribution

For the variable-rank self-map that standardizes the endpoint-inclusive peak
values of a permutation, prove every iterate image with explicit right
sections, a sharp logarithmic absorption clock, and a secondary
target-resolved one-step multiplicity formula.

## Frozen claim ceiling and lead axis

The manuscript follows the P149 ceiling in

```text
all-rank iterate images + constructive right sections + sharp clock.
```

The comparison-word/linear-extension fibre is secondary.  Ji's static
two-zero-boundary peak distribution, Fu's one-sided exterior-peak

fixed-set enumeration, run-sorting equidistribution, and generic zigzag-poset
technology receive zero contribution credit.

## Structure

1. Define the finite disjoint-union carrier, endpoint convention, map, and
   convention-aware owner subtraction, including the ordinary-pinnacle
   padding bridge.
2. Prove one-step packing and the explicit high/valley/decreasing-tail right
   section.
3. Compose minimal odd lifts to obtain every iterate image, then derive the
   sharp clock and recursive witness for every source rank.
4. Prove the complete one-step target multiplicity through comparison words
   and linear extensions.
5. Report exact counterexample pressure, limitations, conclusion, and required
   declarations.

## Claims–evidence map

- Nonempty output and packing: the global maximum is a peak and peak positions
  are nonadjacent.
- One-step reverse inclusion: explicit top-value peaks, small valleys, and a
  decreasing unused tail.
- All-iterate reverse inclusion: a feasible backward chain of minimal odd
  source lengths followed by the available outer length.
- Sharp clock: recursive lifting of a deepest witness at rank `ceil(n/2)`.
- Target multiplicity: bijection between permutations with a fixed comparison
  word and linear extensions of the corresponding augmented zigzag poset.
- Exact evidence: exhaustive source census through rank 9 and fibre checks
  through rank 8, used only as a falsifier.
