# ROUND2 CLUES — SD-C20

These clues leave the current Symbolic Dynamics-only session.  They are
recorded but not developed.

## ROUND2_CLUE 1 — geometric connection carrying nonuniform holonomy

A flat bundle or geometric connection over a return map could turn symbolic
transition words into geometric parallel transport.  Nonuniform curvature or
Frobenius-type data might then distinguish atom identities rather than only
their incidence types.

**Why stopped:** constructing the bundle, connection, and geometric return
map leaves Symbolic Dynamics.  No such carrier is used in SD-C20.

## ROUND2_CLUE 2 — unitary or scattering carrier

A quantum-graph, unitary, or scattering realization could supply a
representation-valued orbit amplitude and possibly a stronger determinant
continuation.

**Why stopped:** no Hilbert space, operator domain, boundary conditions,
self-adjointness theorem, or scattering matrix is defined.  Route B remains
locked.

## ROUND2_CLUE 3 — operator lift of the group-ring ledger

The edge-separated group-ring periodic ledger might admit an operator-valued
completion whose faithful blocks retain commutator information without
commutative cycle collisions.

**Why stopped:** an operator completion is a new data type unless its
function space, traces, and determinant are derived from one carrier.  SD-C20
proves only the symbolic finite-group and trace-class half-plane statements.

## In-family continuation, not ROUND2

Replacing the complete subset full shift by a constrained
tensor-factorization, renewal, coded, or countable Markov grammar remains
inside Symbolic Dynamics.  The allowed transitions, rather than an added
fiber label, must generate the arithmetic selectivity.  The first obligation
is separation from matched nonprime inventories.
