# SD-C20 Implementation Notes

The implementation uses exact integer group tables and representation
matrices.  `S3` is generated by the frozen transpositions `(12)` and `(23)`;
`D4` uses its integral two-dimensional geometric representation; `Q8` uses
the four-dimensional real left-quaternion block, which is two copies of its
complex two-dimensional irrep and is a faithful final audit.

One-dimensional determinants are expanded directly as sparse bivariate
polynomials.  Higher-dimensional tables are rejected by an explicit modular
witness.  Final survivors pass full interpolation grids over three primes,
with a CRT modulus larger than the stated determinant coefficient bound.
Thus no floating tolerance enters the clean/nonclean classification.

The arrival convention is

`B_rho(S,T)=epsilon(T) x_T rho(alpha(S,T))`.

The regular determinant belongs to the whole skew extension; irreducible
determinants are isotypic factors.  Primitive cycles are quotiented only by
rotation.  The isolated edge-marker coefficient for the four-cycle is kept
separate from the aggregated unmarked `x^3 y^3` coefficient.

All randomized inventories use seeds `18001..18005`.  No time stamp, elapsed
time, target-zero datum, fitted parameter, prime-indexed group label, or
cross-family object is present in code or results.
