# Obstruction registry

## SD-O14.1 — Positive Fourier-null classification

If a finite positive circle measure has every positive moment equal to one,
then it is uniquely \(\delta_1+c m_{\rm Haar}\), \(c\geq0\). Normalized-state
and finite-support versions are therefore trivial.

## SD-O14.2 — Analytic determinant invisibility

The Haar mass has zero nonzero Fourier coefficients, so it contributes no
term to the analytic trace-log: \(D_c(q)=1-q\) for every \(c\). This is exact,
not a finite-cutoff observation.

## SD-O14.3 — Approximation and perturbation leaks

An order-\(N\) cyclic approximation first leaks at repetition \(N\). A density
perturbation with first Fourier mode \(k\) first leaks at repetition \(k\).

## SD-O14.4 — Adjoint and recurrence restore the identity sector

Self-adjointization yields \(H^2=I\). Recurrent inverse-labelled paths create
balanced words such as \(uu^{-1}=1\). Both expose total Haar mass rather than
its vanished nonzero modes.

## SD-O14.5 — Inventory blindness

Prime, composite, and random positive inventories share the same determinant
invisibility. Thus the mechanism is not an arithmetic discriminator and is
`PROVES_TOO_MUCH`.
