# Paper12 Narrative Report

## One-sentence contribution

SD-C14 classifies the unique positive diffuse escape from finite unitary
moment rigidity and proves that the escape is invisible to the scalar
holomorphic determinant that preserves the Euler ledger.

## Mechanism

The fiber `W=1 direct-sum u` contains a canonical Haar unitary.  Its nonzero
moments vanish, so the nonnormalized trace `Phi_c` reports exactly one at
every nonzero repetition.  The classification theorem shows that every
finite positive spectral measure with this property is exactly
`delta_1+c m_Haar`.

## Payoff and price

The infinite diffuse sector genuinely escapes Paper11's finite-support
rigidity.  Yet the analytic trace-log uses precisely the moments that vanish,
so its determinant is `1-q`, independent of the Haar mass.  Normalizing the
trace destroys exactness.  Magnitude determinants see the sector only beyond
the Euler disk and without holomorphic phase.  Self-adjointization collapses
the spectrum to two points, while recurrent inverse coupling creates extra
balanced closed paths.

## Controls

Finite cyclic approximants postpone leakage to their dimension but cannot
remove it.  Perturbing Haar density restores a nonzero Fourier coefficient.
Composite and random increasing atom inventories inherit exactly the same
ghost determinant.  These controls establish `PROVES_TOO_MUCH` rather than
arithmetic selectivity.
