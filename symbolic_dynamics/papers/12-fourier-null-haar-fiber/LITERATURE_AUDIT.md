# Paper12 Literature Audit

## Verified primary sources

- Adachi and Sunada (1987), DOI `10.1016/0022-1236(87)90014-0`:
  twisted transfer and dynamical `L`-function precedent.
- Parry and Pollicott (1990), DOI `10.24033/ast.28`: compact-group/unitary
  representation extensions of symbolic dynamics.
- Ward (1999), DOI `10.1006/ffta.1999.0250`: zeta behavior of isometric full
  shift extensions.
- Sharp (2020), AMS Contemporary Mathematics 744: amenable group extensions
  and zeta approximation.
- Clair (2009), DOI `10.1016/j.jctb.2008.04.002`: graph zeta functions with
  `Z` actions; Fourier/Haar group trace is the nearest mechanism collision.
- Guido, Isola, and Lapidus (2008), DOI `10.1016/j.jfa.2008.07.011`:
  periodic graph zeta and analytic determinant framework.
- Fuglede and Kadison (1952), DOI `10.2307/1969645`: finite-factor
  determinant theory.
- de la Harpe and Skandalis (1984), DOI `10.5802/aif.958`: trace-associated
  Banach-algebra determinant boundary.
- Thiel (2024), DOI `10.1353/ajm.2024.a937944`: diffuse traces and Haar
  unitaries.

## Claim boundary

Haar unitaries, group von Neumann traces, twisted symbolic zetas, and infinite
periodic-graph determinants are not new.  This manuscript claims only that it
isolates and proves the exact positive-measure classification and its
determinant-invisibility consequence inside the frozen tensor-prime symbolic
ledger.  It makes no priority claim and no RH spectral claim.
