# Figure Specification

The pure TikZ figure `figures/haar_escape_map.tex` shows the classification
`mu=delta_1+c m_Haar` and its three observable projections:

- positive trace moments and analytic trace-log: exact but ghost-blind;
- normalized spectral distribution: ghost-visible but ledger-diluted;
- Fuglede--Kadison/chiral/coupled data: radial, phase-erasing, or mixed-word
  contaminated.

Color is supplemented by labels and line styles for grayscale readability.
