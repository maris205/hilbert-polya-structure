# Research question brief -- P127--P131

## Primary question

Among at least 88 newly specified finite or locally finite dynamical systems,
which five have mutually distinct carriers, literal updates, and proof engines,
and admit all-parameter temporal theorems after subtracting both their direct
mathematical owners and the P1--P126 internal portfolio?

## Operational subquestions

1. Does an exact small-range pilot show a stable law before extensive casework?
2. Can the observed law be strengthened to at least two nontrivial outputs such
   as a sharp clock, complete recurrent classification, fibre census, image
   characterization, zeta/PGF formula, or hitting-time law?
3. Is there a proof route independent of the pilot implementation?
4. After zero-credit subtraction, is enough temporal content left for one
   short paper without borrowing a second paper from the same system?

## Scope

- Included: explicit deterministic or stochastic maps on finite graded
  carriers, and locally finite maps with a controlled size/weight slice.
- Excluded at intake: unnamed arbitrary functional graphs, parameter retunings
  of occupied updates, endpoint-only enumeration, simulation-only phenomena,
  and claims resting on an unverified literature non-hit.
- Unit of selection: one literal carrier/update pair, not a topic label.
- Required intake: at least 88 systems in four independent lanes.
- Required output: exactly five internally validated short theorem papers.

## Success and failure

Success means five contracts survive proof, source, collision, verifier, and
hostile-review gates.  A mathematically correct but owner-consumed or
theorem-thin map is a documented kill.  A bounded search non-hit is never a
novelty or priority conclusion.
