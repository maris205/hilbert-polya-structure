## Freeze test

update, temporal silhouette, primary proof engine, and theorem outputs against
both the other four finalists and its closest historical papers.  Shared nouns
do not alone establish a collision; a cosmetic carrier change does not cure
one.  Any direct conjugacy, same-map owner, or transfer of a core theorem
reopens the gate immediately.
