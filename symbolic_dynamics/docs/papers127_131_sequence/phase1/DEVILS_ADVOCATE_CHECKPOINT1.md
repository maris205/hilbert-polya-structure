# Devil's advocate checkpoint 1 -- scope and design

## Verdict: PASS WITH ENFORCED GUARDS

No critical issue blocks scouting.  Three major failure modes are present but
are controlled prospectively.

## Major issues

1. **Theorem fitting after inspection.**  A crisp small case can tempt the
   search to redefine the map around the pattern.  The fix is to freeze each
   literal update and its falsification target before deeper enumeration.
2. **Carrier relabelling.**  A new object can conceal an occupied update or
   proof engine.  Every promotion therefore needs both a literal-map comparison
   and a proof-mechanism comparison against P1--P126.
3. **Ownership by subtraction.**  Direct literature can consume the apparent
   advance even when all computed formulas are correct.  Owner machinery gets
   zero credit, and a bounded search non-hit cannot be called novelty.

## Minor issues

- Four lanes do not automatically ensure diversity if each favors terminating
  refinements.  The five-paper freeze requires different temporal silhouettes
  as well as different carrier nouns.
- Assertion volume can masquerade as theorem strength.  Reports must keep
  scouting, paper-local, and independent-control assertions separate.

## Strongest counterargument

Five selected systems from a broad computational funnel may be artefacts of the
enumeration ranges rather than mathematically natural objects.  The answer is
not a larger pilot alone: each finalist must have an intrinsic definition, an
all-parameter proof, a direct-owner subtraction, and a second derivation or
control that does not reuse the discovery code.

## Stress tests

| test | result |
|---|---|
| Remove the strongest pilot: can the contract rest on proof? | required: yes |
| Flip from success to collision: are correct negative results retained? | yes, permanent kill ledger |
| Change carrier while keeping the update: is it detected? | yes, mechanism firewall |
| Ask “so what?” after owner subtraction: are two outputs left? | required before freeze |

The design may proceed, but any violation reopens this checkpoint.
