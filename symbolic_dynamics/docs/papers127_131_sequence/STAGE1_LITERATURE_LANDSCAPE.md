# Stage 1 literature landscape — P127–P131

This landscape is an owner-interface map, not a conventional claim of an
exhaustive literature review.  Searches were performed on 2026-08-31 against
primary technical sources and current 2025–2026 neighbors.  Non-hits are
bounded.

## Five owner neighborhoods

1. **Binary graph operations and transpose algebra.**  Bouchet, Traldi, and
   Brijder–Hoogeboom own local/pivot/loop complementation interfaces; current
   subgraph-complementation work owns the static induced-toggle operation.
   P127 claims only the exact recomputed odd-outdegree iteration.
2. **Translation invariants of polynomials.**  Garefalakis and Reis own the
   translation-fixed irreducible classification and count.  Cyclic
   semilattice orbit folding is also internally occupied.  P128 retains only
   the all-depth product and target-wise terminal fibres.
3. **Coalescing processes.**  Kanade–Mallmann-Trenn–Sauerwald,
   Cooper–Elsässer–Ono–Radzik, Ermakov, and related work own broad graphical,
   meeting, and coalescence machinery.  P129's owner-risk remains medium to
   high; the literal one-way active-pile kernel was not found in the bounded
   screen.
4. **Chord diagrams and noncrossing decompositions.**  Flajolet–Noy,
   Nabergall, Acan, Callan, Lam, Igusa, and Alman–Lian–Tran consume component,
   uncrossing, parallel-parts, transform, and sequence claims.  P130 is
   restricted to its cut-dependent section and pointwise inverse geometry.
5. **Finite continued fractions and cyclic compositions.**  Minelli–
   Sourmelidis–Technau own subtractive digit-sum cost; Reutenauer owns the
   Stern–Brocot/run interface; Kan and Jones cover current finite/periodic
   continuants; Gibson–Just–Wang and Hadjicostas own cyclic-composition
   enumeration.  P131's necklace consequences are therefore zero credit.
