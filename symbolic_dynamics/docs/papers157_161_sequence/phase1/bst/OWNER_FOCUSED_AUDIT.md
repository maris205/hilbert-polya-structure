# Focused owner and collision audit — BST

Decision: **NARROW PASS / HOLD_EXTERNAL**.  This bounded search does not
establish novelty, absence, priority, or permission to release.

## External subtraction

- Drápal, Kozlik, and Griggs, *Latin directed triple systems*, Discrete
  Mathematics 312 (2012), 597–607,
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2011.04.025>, explicitly records the
  standard equivalence between Steiner triple systems and Steiner
  quasigroups.  That operation and all of its defining identities are owned
  background.
- Falcone, *Extensions of Steiner Triple Systems*, Journal of Combinatorial
  Designs (2025), <https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.21964>, records projective
  point-line systems `PG(n,2)` among the classical examples.  The carrier is
  therefore not a contribution.
- Kettinger and Peterson, *Oriented Steiner Triple Systems, Steiner Products,
  and Dynamics*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09396>, studies dynamics of a
  different object: an orientation-dependent anticommutative bilinear
  product on a real vector space, iterated with a fixed vector.  It does not
  state the coordinatewise self-map here or its fibres.  It is cited because
  the title is an especially close false positive, not as absence evidence.
- Generic quasigroup cellular automata are prior art and receive zero credit;
  their usual one-dimensional local-rule setting does not supply this finite
  ordered-triple functional graph.

Exact-map phrases, the six coordinate permutations, characteristic-two
forms `(b+c,c+a,a+b)`, and the inverse parametrization were searched.  No
inspected primary record stated the literal map together with its
four-stratum graph or the `N-2` block fibre.  This remains only a bounded
non-hit.

## Internal firewall through P156

P152 uses triad language but a stochastic sign process on triangular books.
P153 has a shallow collapse over a finite plane but a different carrier,
update, image, and inverse mechanism.  P125 uses characteristic-two
polynomial dynamics but not a Steiner quasigroup or ordered-block collapse.
P130 and P144 have retraction/fibre silhouettes on Catalan carriers; their
primitive-component inverse cannot prove the affine `t`-parametrization
above.  No occupied proof transfers.

## Claim ceiling

Retain only the four-stratum functional graph, sharp counts, explicit
block-source parametrization, and codomain-wide fibre law as one package.
Do not claim the Steiner system, quasigroup operation, projective carrier,
characteristic-two identities, or zeta conversion.  An exact-map owner found
in either hostile review triggers kill or further narrowing.
