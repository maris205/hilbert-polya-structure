# Focused owner and collision audit — Newton--Hensel finite atlas

## Decision

**DIRECT FOUNDATION RECORD / NARROW RESIDUAL PASS / HOLD_EXTERNAL.**

The literal cubic and its convergence mechanism are known prior art. A
bounded search did not locate the complete one-step residue-class image and
every-target fibre atlas,
but that non-hit is not a novelty or priority certificate.

## Direct external prior/foundation record

I. Burban and Y. Drozd, *Derived categories of nodal algebras*, Journal of
Algebra 272 (2004), 46--94, Appendix A,
<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2003.07.025>, explicitly uses

\[
G_1(x)=3x^2-2x^3
\]

as an idempotent-lifting polynomial and records improvement of an approximate
idempotent modulo successive powers of an ideal.  The authors present the
result as known and point to classical background, so this is not evidence
that they originated the cubic.

This source controls the literal polynomial, the idempotent-lifting role, and
the quadratic error mechanism. Those facts are background only. The
real-variable name smoothstep and its interpolation properties are likewise
irrelevant and receive zero credit.

## Bounded query frontier

The search combined the exact polynomial with Newton iteration, Hensel
lifting, idempotents, modular iteration, powers of two, functional graphs,
preimage counts, smoothstep, and finite-ring dynamics. The direct source above
was followed through its stated appendix context.

No inspected record supplied the conjunction of:

1. the parity-resolved pointwise clock and exact shell polynomial on every
   residue class modulo $2^n$;
2. normalized-unit image strata with the distinct $v=1$ and $v\ge2$
   congruences;
3. nonuniform every-target fibres, including the $N=1,2$ truncations;
4. the closed image-size formula.

This is a bounded non-hit only. Different $2$-adic or numerical-analysis
terminology may conceal an earlier source.

## Focused exact-control evidence

The dedicated verifier separates two interfaces:

- the odd-unit map is exhaustively checked for $v=1,\ldots,6$ and
  $N=1,\ldots,11$;
- the full map, every orbit, every target fibre, and image size are checked
  through modulus $2^{17}$.

Its 2,563,880 assertions are counterexample pressure, not proof or ownership
evidence.

## Gate conditions

3. The $N=1,2$ branches must remain explicit.
4. A hostile reviewer must search numerical-analysis, ring-theoretic, and
   $2$-adic terminology for the full inverse atlas.
5. Any direct owner of (B1)--(B4) triggers kill or further narrowing.

External status remains **HOLD_EXTERNAL**.
