## Permanent intake kills

- Generic powers, closure operators, graph/relation powers, comparator sorting,
  or generic finite-state spectra without a sharp temporal and second theorem.
- Another split/join/shift/saturation/pruning process whose main proof transfers
  from an occupied partition, subspace, relation, ideal, word, or tree paper.
- Another record/border/run/palindrome/Lyndon/peak/excedance selector obtained
  only by changing parity, direction, standardization, or endpoint convention.
- Another graph complement, greedy independent-set/matching process, random
  forest/ear deletion, generic random walk, or first-passage wrapper whose
  principal law is already carrier-independent.
- A triangular-polynomial affine conjugate, subgroup normalizer/centralizer
  relabeling, signed-book specialization, ordered cycle-support extractor, or
  weak-excedance/drop compression with the same theorem silhouette as P152–P156.
- Generic hook-length, linear-extension, matroid-basis, Boolean-lattice,
  Fourier-diagonalization, or classical-algorithm formulas renamed as dynamics.
