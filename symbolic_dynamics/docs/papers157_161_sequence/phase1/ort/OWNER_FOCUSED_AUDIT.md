# Focused owner and collision audit — ORT

Decision: **AMBER PASS / HOLD_EXTERNAL**.  The temporal four-cycle engine is
classical; the paper is viable only if the singular/fibre residual remains
unowned.  This bounded audit is not novelty or priority clearance.

## External subtraction

- Kocik and Solecki, *Disentangling a Triangle*, American Mathematical
  Monthly 116 (2009), 228–237,
  <https://doi.org/10.4169/193009709X470065>, records the classical
  orthocentric-system fact: after
  adjoining the orthocenter, any point is the orthocenter of the other three.
  The four-window recurrence receives zero credit.
- Wildberger, *Neuberg cubics over finite fields*,
  <https://doi.org/10.1142/9789812793430_0027> and
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2495>, places metrical triangle geometry and
  orthocenter constructions over finite fields.  The finite-field setting is
  not claimed.
- Guy, *The Triangle*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.03379>, explicitly treats
  a triangle as an orthocentric quadrangle and discusses a different
  iterative construction.  It does not state the sliding-window map with a
  singular sink or the target-fibre law.

Queries covered `orthotetrad`, `orthocentric quadrangle`, finite-field
orthocenters, right-triangle counting, triangle-center iteration, the exact
window `(B,C,H)`, and singular/degenerate totalizations.  No inspected
primary record supplied the conjunction of the three oriented right-angle
depths, the `p=3` empty-core boundary, the stable image, and every-target
fibres.  This is only a bounded non-hit.

## Internal firewall through P156

P150 is the closest silhouette: it also totalizes a finite-field map by a
sink and resolves a singular in-tree.  Its Lyness rational map, carrier,
periods, and inverse equations are different; no P150 proof yields the
orthocenter reverse window.  P153 has a finite-plane collapse but no metric
orthogonality or four-cycle core.  P81 studies a spherical orthogonality
shift, not a finite affine triangle map.  No occupied proof transfers.

## Claim ceiling and kill switch

Do not claim the orthocentric identity, the finite-field orthocenter,
right-triangle basics, or periodicity by itself.  Retain only the singular
totalization, orientation-sensitive depth split, `p=3` boundary, and
codomain-wide fibre/image package.  If either hostile review locates a prior
source for that conjunction, kill or radically narrow the paper.
