# Problem anchor — P157–P161

**Route:** A.  **Batch:** exactly five anonymous theorem notes.  **External
status:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Anchored research question

Outside the literal systems and proof mechanisms already occupied by P1–P156,
which five genuinely different finite, absorbing, rank-changing, stochastic,
or exactly soluble dynamical systems display an early exact signal that can be
promoted to both (i) a clean all-parameter temporal theorem and (ii) an
independent structural, inverse, fibre, extremal, identifiability, or
distributional theorem after direct-owner subtraction?

The governing objective is breadth across dynamical subclasses.  No single
system receives unlimited local patching: a candidate without a crisp small-
instance silhouette is killed and replaced by a different literal update.

## Operational subquestions

1. Which literal updates exhibit a stable, non-generic clock, image, orbit,
   basin, endpoint, fibre, or law profile in exact small cases?
2. Which profiles admit an all-parameter deductive proof plus a paper-local
   exact falsifier whose role is explicitly limited to regression pressure?
3. Which theorem conjunctions remain substantive after direct owners and all
   carrier/update/proof-engine collisions with P1–P156 receive zero credit?

## Progress threshold

Every selected paper must contain all of the following:

1. a mathematically natural literal dynamical system, not a cosmetic carrier
   change for an occupied map;
2. an all-parameter theorem proved deductively rather than extrapolated from
   enumeration;
3. a sharp temporal, recurrent, terminal, image, orbit, or endpoint result;
4. a second independent inverse, fibre, structural, extremal,
   identifiability, or distributional result;
5. an exact deterministic verifier with a frozen transcript; and
6. a self-contained anonymous short manuscript with owner subtraction,
   limitations, review evidence, and external-hold boundaries.

A one-step formula, generic finite-map spectrum, parameter sweep, renamed
classical algorithm, or isolated numerical pattern is below threshold.

## Design and evidence boundary

This is exploratory pure-mathematics research.  Exact computation supplies
counterexample pressure and checks consequences of proofs; it never replaces a
proof.  Source search uses primary or authoritative records.  A bounded search
non-hit is a bounded result only, never a novelty, priority, ownership,
freedom-to-operate, or release certificate.

## Batch separation rule

Freeze exactly five systems whose carrier, literal update, temporal
silhouette, second theorem axis, and dominant proof engine are pairwise
separated.  Automatically replace every weak, owner-saturated, directly
conjugate, or mechanism-colliding candidate.
