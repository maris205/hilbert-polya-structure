## Literal system

Fix `n>=2`.  Let `B_n=2^[n]` and let the phase space be the set of all set
families `X_n=2^{B_n}`.  For `A subset [n]`, write `bar(A)=[n]\A`.  Define

```text
S_n(F)={ bar(A\{a}) : A in F, a in A }
      ={ bar(A) union {a} : A in F, a in A }.
```

Thus `S_n` takes the ordinary lower shadow and complements every member.
The empty set is a silent atom: `S_n({empty})=empty`.  The map distributes
over unions.

For `A!=empty`, put `k=|A|` and let `K_t(A)=S_n^t({A})`.  Let
`phi(k)=n-k+1` on `{1,...,n}`.

## Contract A -- atomic kernels and exact forward clock

For every `s>=0` and nonempty `A`,

```text
K_(2s)(A)   ={B in C([n],k)       : k-|A intersect B| <= s},
K_(2s+1)(A) ={C in C([n],phi(k)) : |A intersect C| <= s+1}.
```

The even formula includes `s=0`.  For the empty atom, `K_0(empty)` is the
singleton family `{empty}`, whereas `K_t(empty)=empty` for `t>=1`.  For an
arbitrary family,

```text
S_n^t(F)= union_(A in F) K_t(A).
```

For every nonempty rank slice `F_k=F intersect C([n],k)`, define

```text
e_k(F)=max_(B in C([n],k)) min_(A in F_k) (k-|A intersect B|),
o_k(F)=max_(C in C([n],phi(k))) min_(A in F_k) (|A intersect C|-1).
```

Let `e(F)` and `o(F)` be the maxima over the nonempty rank slices, and let
`R(F)={k>=1:F_k!=empty}`.  If `R(F)` is nonempty, the exact tail is

```text
mu(F)=max( 1_(empty in F), min(2e(F),2o(F)+1) ).
```

If `R(F)` is empty, `mu(empty family)=0` and `mu({empty})=1`.  The eventual
period is one when `R(F)=phi(R(F))` and two otherwise.  The global height is

```text
H_n=n-1.
```

For `n=2m`, a singleton family generated by an `m`-set realizes `2m-1`.
For `n=2m+1`, a singleton family generated by an `(m+1)`-set realizes `2m`.

## Contract B -- recurrent core, fixed counts, and zeta function

For `R subset {1,...,n}`, write

```text
U_R=union_(k in R) C([n],k).
```

The recurrent states are exactly the `2^n` families `U_R`, and
`S_n(U_R)=U_(phi(R))`.  Hence the number of fixed states is
`2^ceil(n/2)`, and the remaining recurrent states form strict two-cycles.
For every `j>=1`,

```text
|Fix(S_n^j)| = 2^ceil(n/2),  j odd,
             = 2^n,          j even,
```

and

```text
zeta_(S_n)(z)
 =(1-z)^(-2^ceil(n/2))
  (1-z^2)^(-(2^n-2^ceil(n/2))/2).
```

## Contract C -- every-time, every-target inverse atlas

For `t>=1` and a target family `G`, define

```text
c_t(G)=#{A in B_n\{empty}: K_t(A) subset G}.
```

Then

```text
|S_n^(-t)(G)|
 =2 sum_(J subset G) (-1)^|J| 2^(c_t(G\J)).
```

The leading factor two is the optional silent atom `empty`.  Moreover `G`
is a rank-`t` image exactly when

```text
G = union_{A!=empty: K_t(A) subset G} K_t(A).
```

For `t>=n-1`, only recurrent targets have nonempty fibres.  If `G=U_R`,
then the stable formula simplifies to

```text
|S_n^(-t)(U_R)|
 =2 product_(k in phi^t(R)) (2^C(n,k)-1).
```

In particular, the empty target has exactly two rank-`t` preimages for every
`t>=1`, and the recurrent-state count recovers the ambient parameter as
`n=log_2 |Rec(S_n)|`.

## Proof engine

1. For `B=bar(A) union {a}`, a second update has the form
   `(A\{a}) union {b}`.  It is either `A` or one Johnson-graph exchange.
   Therefore `S_n^2` is closed-neighbourhood dilation independently on every
   nonzero rank.  Induction gives the even Johnson balls.
2. A `phi(k)`-set `C` has a one-step predecessor
   `bar(C) union {c}` for `c in C`.  Minimizing its Johnson distance from
   `A` gives `|A intersect C|-1`, proving the odd kernel.
3. A rank slice is full at even time `2s` exactly when `s>=e_k`, and its odd
   image is full exactly when `s>=o_k`.  Taking the earlier common parity over
   all occupied ranks proves the tail formula.  Johnson diameters and the
   displayed singleton witnesses prove `H_n=n-1`.
4. A periodic family is fixed by `S_n^2`.  Each nonempty rank slice is then
   invariant under closed-neighbourhood expansion in the connected Johnson
   graph, so it is empty or the whole rank.  This proves the recurrent core,
   fixed counts, and zeta formula.
5. A source is a subset of the admissible atoms whose kernels lie in `G` and
   whose kernel union covers `G`.  Inclusion--exclusion over uncovered target
   members gives Contract C; the silent atom supplies the factor two.

## Mandatory boundaries and owner ceiling

- `n>=2` is part of the theorem.  The small systems `n=0,1` have exceptional
  height caused by the silent empty atom.
- `t=0` is excluded from the inverse formula; its fibres are singletons.
- The target `G=empty` is not dropped from inclusion--exclusion.
- Ordinary shadows, iterated shadows, Johnson graphs, their diameters, and
  the identity `B(S)=nabla(Delta(S))=Delta(nabla(S))` receive zero credit.
  The last identity is explicit in Diego--Serra--Vena (2018), DOI
  `10.1007/s00373-018-1923-7`.
- The allowed residual claim is only the integrated mixed-rank dynamics:
  the parity-sensitive exact clock, recurrent/fixed atlas, and every-time
  target-resolved fibres.  No statement may use `new`, `first`, `novel`, or
  infer priority from the bounded search.
- Because the forward engine is a directly owned Johnson dilation and the
  inverse engine is a general cover inclusion--exclusion, this contract is
