## 1. Scope and source discipline

The claims audited were the literal maps and the all-parameter theorem spines,
not broad keywords such as "trace" or "exterior power."  Sources retained in
the decision are author-hosted papers/notes, arXiv originals, or DOI landing
pages for the original article.  Search-aggregation pages were used, when
necessary, only to discover a primary copy and receive no evidentiary credit.

The unavailable independent-review endpoint required by the local novelty
workflow could not be used in this run.  The replacement was a claim-by-claim
subtraction against primary sources, with direct and nearest hits kept
separate.  This is a limitation of the audit, not a reason to promote any of
the audited candidates.

### Claims put under audit

For TMD the audited conjunction was:

1. the literal self-map `T(x)=x Tr_{F_{p^n}/F_p}(x)` for odd primes `p` and
   `n>=2`;
2. the closed iterate and exact tail/period law;
3. the full temporal polynomial, recurrent and fixed-point counts; and
4. every-target one-step fibre sizes.

For XPF the audited conjunction was:

1. the literal fixed-rank type map induced by
   `G -> exterior^(r-1) G`, uniformly for `r>=3`;
2. the sharp depth-0/1/2 classification;
3. exact depth strata and image size; and
4. every-target fibres and every-terminal basins, uniformly in `(r,e)`.

The supplementary QAR audit tested:

1. the literal restriction of `F(x)=x(x+p)` to `p Z/p^e Z` for odd `p`;
2. the cancellation-sensitive pointwise and sharp absorption clock;
3. its temporal polynomial; and
4. the image and every-target fibre atlas obtained from a quadratic
   discriminant over `Z/p^(e-2)Z`.

## 4. QAR supplementary subtraction in detail

### 4.1 Affine normalization and external owner boundary

On `X_{p,e}=p Z/p^e Z`, with odd `p`, let

`F(x)=x(x+p)=x^2+px`.

Translation by `p/2` preserves the maximal ideal and conjugates this map to

`z -> z^2 + p/2 - p^2/4` on `p Z/p^e Z`.

Thus the assertion "this is a quadratic polynomial dynamical system" and the
reduction of a general monic quadratic to `z^2+c` are zero credit.  General
cycle-lifting modulo prime powers and general local-field quadratic dynamics
are also established lanes.  On the other hand, the screened sources do not
state the literal invariant-ball graph or its complete finite-level census.

The exact occurrence of `x^2+px (mod p^2)` in Dwivedi--Mittal--Saxena is a
different problem: they count factors/roots, not iterates.  It is therefore an
owner warning, not a direct functional-graph hit.

### 4.2 What remains after the generic quadratic subtraction

For `x=p^a u`, `u` a unit, the trajectory separates into two genuinely
different valuation branches:

- if `a>=2`, then `v_p(F(x))=a+1` until absorption;
- if `a=1`, then `v_p(F(x))=2+v_p(u+1)`, capped at `e`, so cancellation at
  `u=-1` determines the first jump.

This gives the pointwise clock

`tau(0)=0`, `tau(x)=e-a` for `a>=2`, and
`tau(pu)=max(1,e-1-v_p(u+1))` with the natural cap.

It also gives, for `e>=3`, the exact temporal polynomial

`1+(2p-1)z + sum_{d=2}^{e-2} 2(p-1)p^(d-1)z^d
 +(p-2)p^(e-2)z^(e-1)`.

For inverse structure, an allowed target has the form `y=p^2w`.  Writing
`x=pu` reduces `F(x)=y` to `u(u+1)=w (mod p^(e-2))`, hence

`(2u+1)^2 = 1+4w`.

If `rho_{p^n}(Delta)` denotes the number of square roots of `Delta` modulo
`p^n`, then

`|F^(-1)(p^2w)| = p rho_{p^(e-2)}(1+4w)`.

The valuation and quadratic character of the discriminant give the complete
fibre stratification and the corresponding image-size sum.  This cancellation
branch plus discriminant atlas is not supplied by the general cycle-lifting or
local Julia-set sources above.

### 4.3 External zero-credit and surviving conjunction

External zero-credit items are:

- affine conjugacy to a standard monic quadratic;
- generic polynomial cycle lifting modulo `p^e`;
- generic local-field quadratic classification;
- standard counts of square roots modulo odd prime powers; and
- mere occurrence of the polynomial `x^2+px` in factor/root-counting work.

The honest external-owner residual is:

> For one literal maximal-ideal polynomial family, a simultaneous exact
> cancellation-sensitive absorption clock and temporal polynomial, together
> with a discriminant-stratified every-target fibre law and image count.

Unlike TMD and XPF, this residual is not eliminated by a located direct owner
or by a one-line standard type identity.  It is enough for a rigorous short
**internal note** if the project wants to retain the computation and proof.

### 4.4 Internal collision with P142

QAR is nevertheless too close to the already completed P142 paper for the
current five-paper intake.  Both packages are indexed by an odd prime and an
exponent ceiling; both reduce the temporal theory to `p`-adic valuation
branches; and both culminate in a pointwise/sharp clock, a temporal polynomial,
an image theorem, and every-target fibres.  QAR's element carrier,
`v_p(u+1)` cancellation boundary, and quadratic-discriminant fibres are real
differences, but they change the local formula rather than the portfolio-level
research question.  P100's valuation erasure and P107's ideal-power lane add
secondary proximity.

**QAR verdict:** `OWNER_REPAIRED_INTERNAL_NOTE_ONLY` externally, but
`KILL_FOR_P147_P151_PAPER_INTAKE` internally.  This is **not** a permanent
mathematical kill: it may be kept as a proof-complete note or revisited in a
future, deliberately quadratic/local-ring project.  It must not receive a
P147--P151 paper number unless a new theorem moves beyond the P142-shaped
clock/image/fibre atlas.

## 5. TMD subtraction in detail

### 5.1 Literal direct collision

For `n=2`, put `q=p` (indeed the owner allows any odd prime power `q`).  Then

`x Tr_{F_{q^2}/F_q}(x) = x(x+x^q) = x^2+x^{q+1}`.

Thus TMD is exactly the `a=1,c=1` map of Brochero Martínez--Teixeira, not
merely conjugate to or inspired by it.  Their Section 3 writes it in quadratic
extension coordinates as `(x,y)->(2x^2,2xy)`.  Their results already include:

- the fixed points and trace-zero/coordinate-axis component;
- the zero fibre and the `0/2` law away from that component;
- a closed iterate;
- cycle lengths and counts from the odd part of `q-1` under doubling; and
- the complete binary preperiodic trees controlled by `v_2(q-1)`.

These are the same mechanisms and essentially the same graph data as the TMD
spine on `n=2`.  Repackaging them as a depth polynomial does not create an
independent theorem on this slice.

### 5.2 Why the all-degree residue is only a lift

Choose `w in F_{p^n}` with `Tr(w)=1` and write uniquely

`x = a w + v`, where `a=Tr(x) in F_p` and `v in ker(Tr)`.

Trace linearity gives the exact conjugate normal form

`(a,v) -> (a^2,a v)`.

Consequently

`T^t(x)=x a^(2^t-1)` and `Tr(T^t(x))=a^(2^t)`.

All extension-degree dependence is now the cardinality
`|ker(Tr)|=p^(n-1)`.  The period and tail are inherited from squaring on the
cyclic group `F_p^*`; the trace-zero hyperplane is a one-step absorber; and a
nonzero fibre reduces to solving a scalar square equation.  If
`p-1=2^s m`, `m` odd, the proposed temporal polynomial

`1+(p^(n-1)-1)z + p^(n-1)(m + sum_{j=1}^s 2^(j-1)m z^j)`

is therefore the already-owned scalar depth distribution multiplied by a
constant hyperplane size, plus the trace-zero boundary.  Likewise the proposed
all-target fibre law is `p^(n-1)` over zero, `2` over a nonzero target with
nonzero square trace, and `0` otherwise.

This derivation is valuable as an exact check, but it also performs the owner
subtraction: no new higher-dimensional interaction survives.

### 5.3 TMD zero-credit list

The following cannot support a novelty or value claim for TMD:

- the literal map and complete graph when `n=2`;
- the coordinate skew product and closed iterate on that slice;
- the odd-order/2-primary tail and cycle mechanism of scalar squaring;
- the `x h(Tr x)` construction or the idea of projecting through trace;
- the zero fibre and `0/2` nonzero fibre pattern on `n=2`; and
- mere presentation of the same counts as a generating polynomial.

### 5.4 TMD surviving conjunction

After subtraction, the honest residual is:

> A single statement, uniform in all extension degrees `n>=2`, that lifts the
> quadratic-extension graph to `F_{p^n}`, records the temporal polynomial and
> recurrent/fixed census, and states every-target fibre sizes.

No source in the bounded audit was found stating that exact all-`n`
conjunction.  However, the normal form `(a,v)->(a^2,av)` proves that the
residual is a dimension-multiplicity lift of the direct `n=2` mechanism.  It
does not clear the portfolio's paper-sized-progress threshold.

**TMD verdict: `PERMANENT_KILL_AS_LITERAL_FAMILY / DO_NOT_ASSIGN`.**  The
restriction `n>=3` is **not** an owner-repaired internal note: the same normal
form proves it by replacing the owner's one-dimensional transverse coordinate
with an `(n-1)`-dimensional kernel of identical scalar copies.  Nothing in the
proof or graph changes except multiplicity.  A future class
`x h(Tr x)` whose iterates do not close on an owned scalar map would be a new
system, not a repair of TMD.  Merely changing `n`, `p`, or presentation cannot
reopen this candidate.

## 6. XPF subtraction in detail

### 6.1 The structural identity already contains the map

Let

`G_lambda = direct_sum_{i=1}^r C_{p^(lambda_i)}`,
with `lambda_1>=...>=lambda_r>=1`.

The exterior-power direct-sum formula and
`C_{p^a} tensor C_{p^b} isomorphic to C_{p^min(a,b)}` give, for every `k`,

`exterior^k G_lambda isomorphic to direct_sum_{|I|=k}
 C_{p^min_{i in I}(lambda_i)}`.

For `k=r-1`, exactly one subset omits the smallest invariant factor and all
other subsets contain it.  Hence the induced type map is immediately

`W_r(lambda)=(lambda_(r-1),lambda_r,...,lambda_r)`.

This is the whole algebraic engine.  Applying it once more gives

`W_r^2(lambda)=(lambda_r,...,lambda_r)` and `W_r^3=W_r^2`.

There is no hidden group-theoretic dynamics after this identity: the map
forgets all but the two smallest invariant factors at the first step and all
but the smallest at the second.

### 6.2 The census is stars-and-bars after the collapse

Fix `1<=lambda_r<=...<=lambda_1<=e`.  From the displayed type map:

- the fixed types are the `e` homocyclic types;
- tail at most one is equivalent to `lambda_(r-1)=lambda_r`, yielding
  `binom(e+r-2,r-1)` types including the fixed ones;
- the remaining `binom(e+r-2,r)` types have tail two;
- the image consists of `(b,a,...,a)` with `1<=a<=b<=e`, hence has
  `e(e+1)/2` types;
- the fibre of `(b,a,...,a)` has
  `binom(e-b+r-2,r-2)` elements, and every other fibre is empty; and
- the basin of `(a,...,a)` is the set `lambda_r=a`, of size
  `binom(e-a+r-1,r-1)`.

These are clean formulas, but once `W_r` is written down they are elementary
multiset counts.  Exact enumeration can falsify transcription errors; it does
not supply a separate proof engine or novelty increment.

### 6.3 Internal and external collision at rank three

At `r=3`, `exterior^(r-1)=exterior^2`, so XPF is exactly the earlier M01
exterior-square system.  The P132--P136 hostile gate rejected M01 because its
type formula and dynamics were immediate and its chosen rank-three closure was
artificial.  XPF improves one aspect: `binom(r,r-1)=r`, so codimension-one
exterior power preserves the number of cyclic summands for every fixed rank.

That repair does not create a new dynamical mechanism.  Frei--Loughran--Newton
state the exterior-square invariant factors explicitly, and the Schur
multiplier literature both identifies the same invariant-factor transform for
abelian groups and records its second iterate.  Thus the `r=3` slice cannot be
used as fresh evidence; arbitrary `r` merely repeats the same two-coordinate
collapse.

### 6.4 XPF zero-credit list

The following receive zero credit toward a new paper:

- the exterior-power Künneth/direct-sum formula;
- the invariant factors of `exterior^2 G` and the abelian Schur multiplier;
- the rank-three update, already present as internal M01;
- the type formula `W_r`, since it is a one-line specialization of the
  standard direct-sum identity;
- depth at most two, since it is immediate from `W_r`;
- stars-and-bars counts once the two retained coordinates are known; and
- the fact that unrelated exterior-power operations have also been iterated
  on representation rings.

### 6.5 XPF surviving conjunction

After subtraction, the honest residual is:

> A uniform packaging, for every fixed rank `r>=3` and exponent ceiling `e`,
> of the three tail strata, image size, all one-step fibres, and all terminal
> basins of the codimension-one exterior-power type map.

No direct all-`r` functional-graph owner for this conjunction was found.  The
residual is nonetheless below the paper threshold: every component follows
without additional group theory from one standard invariant-factor identity
and elementary counting.  The chosen degree `r-1` also changes with the rank;
it is a coherent family, but not a fixed functor iterated across varying
ranks.
