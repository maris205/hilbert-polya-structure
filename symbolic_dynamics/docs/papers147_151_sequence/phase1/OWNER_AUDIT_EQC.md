# Owner audit — EQC equal-cardinality coarsening

**Decision:** `KILL_UNRESOLVED_DIRECT_OWNER`.  **External status:**
`HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Literal candidate

For a labelled set partition of `[n]`, EQC simultaneously unions all blocks
of each repeated cardinality.  Passing to the multiset of block sizes sends
an integer partition

`lambda=(1^m_1 2^m_2 ...)`

to the partition whose parts are the nonzero products `s m_s`.  Thus the
entire temporal silhouette of the labelled map factors through an integer-
partition self-map; labels only refine its inverse fibres.

## Direct-owner risk

The primary publisher record for Shalom Eliahou and Martin J. Erickson,
"Mutually describing multisets and integer partitions," *Discrete
Mathematics* 313 (2013), 422--433,
<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2012.11.014>, says that the paper introduces
and studies a discrete dynamical system on the integer partitions of each
`n`.  Its multiset construction is explicitly driven by multiplicities, so
this is not a merely thematic neighbour: it is a plausible direct owner for
the displayed shape map.

The publisher exposes the article as open archive in metadata, but the full
text could not be recovered reproducibly in this environment: the public
PDF endpoint returned an access challenge, and the Elsevier text-mining API
returned only metadata without an API key.  Consequently we cannot certify
which orbit, clock, or inverse statements are already present.

Static set-partition shape multiplicities are also standard background; see
Dougherty and McCammond's partition-shape formalism.  They receive zero
credit regardless of the direct-owner outcome.

## Subtraction test

If the integer-partition shape map is assigned to the direct owner, the
candidate retains a labelled every-target divisor-injection fibre formula
and a Hall-type image test.  Those are useful, but they do not supply an
independent temporal axis: the proposed logarithmic clock and recurrent
classification are inherited from the owner-risk shape dynamics.  The
residual therefore fails the two-axis paper gate.

## Intake consequence

EQC is excluded from P147--P151.  Re-entry would require inspection of the
full primary source followed by a theorem-by-theorem subtraction showing a
paper-sized temporal result independent of the labelled inverse lift.  No
novelty or non-hit statement is authorized from the present search.
