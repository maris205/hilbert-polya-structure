# Owner and claim-subtraction audit: replacement algebraic lane

**Scope:** `ZTL`, `QNC`, six new negative controls, and `DNT` re-entry.  
**Source policy:** primary papers, arXiv author manuscripts, author-hosted PDFs,
publisher DOI pages, and institutional author repositories only.  
**External status:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

| handle | owner relation | owner-subtracted value | gate |

anonymous internal contract after explicit subtraction.  `QNC` is not declared
novel: it is retained only because it passes the internal two-axis value gate
and no exact owner appeared in a bounded search.

## Primary and author-hosted source ledger

### Lyness and finite-field birational dynamics

1. A. N. W. Hone and T. E. Kouloukas,
   [*Deformations of cluster mutations and invariant presymplectic
   forms*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.11866), arXiv:2107.11866; published DOI
   `10.1007/s10801-022-01203-5`.  This is a direct owner for the Lyness
   five-cycle as the type-`A_2` cluster example and for its rational
   period-five mechanism.
2. A. N. W. Hone,
   [*Efficient ECM Factorization in Parallel with the Lyness
   Map*](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/81862/1/Hone_ISSAC_final.pdf), author accepted
   manuscript; DOI `10.1145/3373207.3404044`.  This is direct for the rational
   map `(x,y)->(y,(y+1)/x)`, its five-cycle specialization, finite-ring/field
   use, and projective-coordinate treatment of denominators.  It does not use
   the convention `inv0(0)=0` or give the affine functional graph claimed here.
3. D. Jogia, J. A. G. Roberts, and F. Vivaldi,
   [*An algebraic geometric approach to integrable maps of the
   plane*](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~jagr/IntegrabilityRS.pdf),
   author-hosted manuscript; DOI `10.1088/0305-4470/39/5/008`.  This is nearest
   background for birational integrable maps over finite fields and periodic
   orbit distributions.  It works through invariant curves/projective
   geometry, not the exact zero-totalized affine boundary.

### Quadratic and p-adic polynomial dynamics

   [*On Functional Graphs of Quadratic

   [*Ergodic Transformations of the Space of p-adic

   polynomial dynamics on `Z_p`, and reductions modulo `p^k`.  Its focus is
   ergodic/measure-preserving behavior, whereas `QNC` is a contracting map on
   the maximal ideal.  No exact-map theorem was located there.
7. S. Wright,
   [*On the Quadratic Formula Modulo

## `ZTL`: direct versus nearest and claim subtraction

### Directly owned; zero credit

The following cannot appear as contributions:

* the recurrence `x_{n+2}x_n=x_{n+1}+1`;
* the rational map `(x,y)->(y,(1+y)/x)`;
* its five-period identity where the rational iterates are defined;
* its type-`A_2` cluster/associahedron interpretation;
* the generic language of integrable birational maps, invariant curves,
  elliptic curves, finite-field reduction, or projective denominator handling;
* generic finite-map zeta identities once the cycles are known.

Hone--Kouloukas and Hone are **direct**, not merely related, for this core.
Jogia--Roberts--Vivaldi is **nearest** for the finite-field birational setting.

### No direct owner located for the surviving conjunction

The bounded search did not locate a paper treating exactly

```text
L_q(x,y)=(y,(1+y)inv0(x)) on every point of F_q^2
```

and proving all of:

1. the five-stratum affine decomposition;
2. the sharp depth-three exceptional tree;
3. the complete `1/2/4/5` cycle census with the quadratic root correction;
4. the resulting zeta function;
5. the codomain-wide `0/1/q` fibre law, image size, and unique maximum fibre.

This conjunction is the only proposed credit.  It is not obtained by merely
quoting rational five-periodicity: all transient arrows and the 2/4-cycle axis
graph live precisely where the rational map is singular.

### Freeze recommendation

**Recommend internal freeze**, with mandatory language such as “after
zero-credit use of the classical Lyness identity.”  Do not claim the first
finite-field study of Lyness maps, the first analysis of its periodicity, or
external novelty.  A later direct owner of the all-affine conjunction reopens
the slot.

## `QNC`: direct versus nearest and claim subtraction

### Zero-credit inputs

The following are standard and must be subtracted:

* valuation arithmetic in `Z/p^eZ`;
* completing the square in a quadratic congruence;
* Hensel lifting and the standard root-count formula for
  `z^2=Delta mod p^k`;
* generic functional-graph terminology for polynomial maps;
* general p-adic compatible-map and polynomial-dynamics frameworks.

Wright is direct for the discriminant reduction in general.  Anashin and the
finite-field functional-graph papers are nearest, not exact-map owners.

### Surviving conjunction

What remains is map-specific:

1. the two-regime valuation identity for `x(x+p)` on the maximal ideal;
2. the sharp pointwise clock and complete temporal polynomial for every
   `(p,e)`;
3. the target-resolved specialization of square-root counts with the extra
   `p` source lifts;
4. the exact image size and the even/odd-precision switch in the maximum fibre
   and its multiplicity.

No source found in the bounded search owned this exact conjunction or even the
literal self-map on `pZ/p^eZ`.

### Gate

**Retain as a paper-sized internal reserve, not a cleared finalist.**  The
local/p-adic polynomial-dynamics literature is too broad for a search-string
miss to support stronger language.  A specialist search by polynomial
conjugacy class and minimal-decomposition literature is mandatory before
freeze.  If an exact owner is found, kill rather than narrowing to the standard
quadratic-congruence formula.

## Decisive internal reductions

### `OTL`

Regardless of external ownership, the identity

```text
T^3(a,b,0)=(L^2(a,b),0)
```

identifies its period-15 boundary with the selected `ZTL` root.  This is a
stronger collision than shared vocabulary or proof style.  Its attractive
depth and inverse results do not justify two papers in one batch.  Permanent
kill unless `ZTL` itself is abandoned.

### `GAE`

Negation modulo `p^e` is an explicit conjugacy to P100.  No owner search can
repair an internal exact conjugacy.  Permanent kill.

## Bounded-non-hit limitation

The search was finite, English-heavy, and indexed by literal formulas and
common terminology.  It did not exhaust older recurrence literature,
non-English journals, theses, books, all citation descendants, or every
equivalent polynomial/birational conjugacy.  Therefore:

* “no direct owner located” means only “none in this bounded audit”;
* it does not establish novelty, priority, authorship, or freedom to publish;
* exact enumeration establishes neither a proof nor a literature claim;
* `ZTL` remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`, and `QNC` remains below the freeze line until
  its specialist audit is complete.

## Final recommendation

Freeze **`ZTL` only** from this lane.  Keep **`QNC`** as a genuine two-axis
replacement reserve.  Permanently kill `RIC`, `NID`, `RDC`, `DLV`, `OTL`,
`GAE`, and `DNT`.  Do not use `OTL` as a second Lyness paper and do not revive
`GAE` under its gcd presentation.
