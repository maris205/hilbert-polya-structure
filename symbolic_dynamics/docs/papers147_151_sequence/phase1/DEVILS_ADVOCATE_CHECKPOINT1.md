# Devil's-advocate checkpoint 1 — before numbering

## Hostile questions

1. Is the candidate a complement, quotient, scheduler variation, carrier
   encoding, or same-root extension of P1–P146 or a standard map?
2. Does it have a genuine temporal/endpoint theorem beyond a static class or
   one-step closure?
3. Does the proposed main conjunction collapse after the closest primary
   owner is written in the same coordinates?
4. Is each sharp bound supported by an all-parameter proof route and equality
   witness?
5. Does every inverse use only the advertised public data and distinguish
   exact-size layers from finite self-map carriers?
6. Is finite computation used only as falsification rather than proof or
   novelty evidence?

## Findings

- `S09`, `XPF`, `TMD`, `DNT`, `FPD`, rowmotion, chip firing, PEO fill,
  reverse Kruskal, linear extensions, hook deletion, Bernoulli--Laplace, and
  Kauffman smoothing fail questions 1–3 and are permanent kills.
- `EQC` had a strong raw clock/fibre package but failed question 3 because a
  plausible direct partition-multiplicity owner could not be resolved.
- `OTL` failed question 1 by containing ZTL on a zero slice; `GAE` failed by
  exact conjugacy to P100.  These are not reserves.
- P147 passes questions 2 and 4 through a dependency-doubling proof and an
  every-size cascade, while static Carlitz results are removed.
- P148 passes question 5 only on the finite union `PT_{<=N}`; its manuscript
  must separately state exact-size source fibre counts.
- P149 passes question 2 only when led by all-rank images, sections, and the
  sharp clock.  Pinnacle admissibility cannot be advertised as residual.
- P150 passes after the classical period-five locus is assigned zero credit;
  the residual must cover the singular affine boundary completely.
- P151 required the strongest repair: general-tree endpoint and mean formulas,
  unequal-arm endpoint probability, equal-arm distributions, and generic
  tomography were all subtracted before retaining the joint transform,
  variance, extremizers, and narrow geometry inverse.
- The second stochastic pool returned no select.  The value threshold was not
  relaxed to create a sixth option.

## Decision

Five systems may be numbered internally under the frozen theorem ceilings.
Every paper requires a second checkpoint after proof formalization, two
independent hostile reviews, cold verifier replay, and visual PDF inspection.
External status remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
