## Consequential direct-owner and transfer kills

- `S09` triangle-book edge deletion is exactly the P136 sunflower process
  under a line-graph incidence encoding and also lies inside the hypergraph
  Pitt process.
- `TMD` is already the standard trace/norm finite-field polynomial mechanism;
  `XPF` is a direct exterior-power type formula; `DNT` is the classical
  successive-normalizer problem.  None may re-enter by adding a scheduler.
- `EQC` may match the Eliahou--Erickson integer-partition multiplicity
  dynamics at the shape level.  Full direct-owner resolution was unavailable,
  so it was killed rather than promoted from a bounded miss.
- `QAR` lies too near the occupied valuation tent.  `RIC`, `NID`, `GND`, and
  Chebyshev/Artin--Schreier controls have direct algebraic owners.  `RDC` and
  `DLV` are theorem-thin or generic quadratic foliations.
- `OTL` contains P150 literally on its zero slice; `GAE` is exactly conjugate
  to P100.  Both are permanent same-root kills.
- The strongest first combinatorial signal, lexicographic chord uncrossing,
  reduces after source subtraction to constrained bubble sorting and collides
  with P130.  Peak-free rowmotion, PEO fill, shifting, closure, parent motion,
  contour successor, and chip firing are direct/classical kills.
- `FPD` merely serializes the directly owned derangement reduction and also
  meets P105.  It was removed even though its exact basin formula is correct.
- Random path deletion becomes graph peaks; nonbridge deletion becomes
  reverse Kruskal/random MST; induced-`P3` deletion becomes the OK-Corral urn;
  uniform DAG-source deletion is linear extensions; Young-corner deletion is
  the hook formula; random inversion swaps and Bernoulli--Laplace exchange are
  directly owned processes.
- The second stochastic pool was deliberately allowed to return no select:
  fair halving is an occupied digit contraction, totient--radical descent has
  infinite law collisions, randomized squarefree reduction sees only maximum
  multiplicity, and two-braid smoothing is a Kauffman state sum.

## Replacement reserve

`QNC`, the map `x -> x(x+p)` on `pZ/p^eZ`, has a sharp depth `e-1`, a full
temporal polynomial, and a singular square-root every-target fibre atlas.  It
is genuinely independent and paper-sized, but remains reserve-only because
the local/p-adic polynomial-dynamics owner neighbourhood is not yet narrow
enough for freeze.

## Freeze rule

The five numbered systems may proceed to anonymous internal Stage 2.  A later
direct owner, transferred proof engine, false all-parameter statement, or
Critical hostile-review finding reopens the affected slot.  A bounded non-hit
never supports novelty, priority, authorship, submission, publicity, or
external release.  All such actions remain `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
