## P147 — adjacent-run consolidation

- **Carrier/update:** positive compositions of fixed total; every maximal
  equal adjacent run is replaced simultaneously by its sum.
- **Nearest occupied systems:** P126 composition refinement and P144 Dyck
  component reassociation.
- **Separation:** P147 coarsens equal runs and uses weight-doubling ancestry;
  its inverse is a path through target divisor sets.  P126 splits blocks and
  decodes suffixes; P144 performs one leftmost Catalan reassociation.
- **External subtraction:** Carlitz fixed states, static run statistics, and
  ordinary run-length encoding are zero credit.

## P148 — even-level plane-tree contraction

- **Carrier/update:** the finite union of plane rooted trees up to a size
  bound; delete all odd-depth vertices and promote ordered grandchildren,
  then reset parity.
- **Nearest occupied systems:** P114 leaf peeling, P115 coefficient
  decimation, P120 fringe mirroring, and P144 Dyck reassociation.
- **Separation:** height is divided by two rather than decremented; target
  fibres are independent ordered block-and-gap insertions with generating

- **External subtraction:** generic ordered-tree contraction, parity
  statistics, Catalan counts, and Horton/leaf pruning are zero credit.
- **Post-drafting direct-owner repair:** forgetting plane order makes one step
  exactly Soo--Khoussainov--Linz outward-contraction.  The unordered rule,
  partition-tree interpretation, and its cheap iterated unordered depth/clock
  consequences are therefore direct-owned.  Independent Review B accepted
  only the ordered every-target fibre, exact-layer image, and algebraic-series
  conjunction as the eligible residual.

- **Separation:** P149 is a size-decreasing ordered-subsequence map with

## P150 — zero-totalized finite-field Lyness map

- **Carrier/update:** the entire affine plane over every odd finite field;
  `(x,y)->(y,(1+y)inv0(x))` with `inv0(0)=0`.
- **Nearest occupied systems:** P103 double adjugation and the algebraic
  singular-map controls in the current scout.
- **Separation:** P150 has one explicit depth-three singular in-tree, periods

- **External subtraction:** Lyness's original cycle observation, classical
  period five, QRT/cluster interpretations, Kanki's distinct extension/almost-
