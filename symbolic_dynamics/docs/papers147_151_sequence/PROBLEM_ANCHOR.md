## Anchored research question

Among many genuinely different literal finite, absorbing, or exactly soluble
random dynamical systems, find five whose first exact profiles expose a clean
all-parameter signal and whose residual theorem package remains substantive
after direct-owner subtraction and the full P1–P146 portfolio firewall.

Every selected system must admit:

1. a deductive all-parameter proof rather than an enumerative extrapolation;
2. a paper-local exact counterexample-pressure verifier;
3. at least one sharp temporal or endpoint theorem and one independent
   structural, inverse, or distributional result; and
4. a self-contained anonymous short manuscript with explicit zero-credit and
   external-hold boundaries.

## Progress threshold

A candidate is paper-scale only if its owner-subtracted residual contains a
clear conjunction drawn from the following axes:

- pointwise and sharp entrance/absorption clock with equality witnesses;
- complete recurrent, periodic, terminal, or image classification;
- every-target fibres, basin polynomials, or an input-only inverse theorem;
- exact temporal-layer, history, endpoint, or continuous-time law;
- a sharp extremal mass/count theorem with a characterized equality class;
- a precise identifiability/nonidentifiability boundary.

A new carrier alone, a one-step closure, a generic finite-map spectrum, or a
classical algorithm restated dynamically is below threshold.

## Breadth and replacement rules

- Search algebraic/arithmetic, combinatorial/word/relation, stochastic/graph,
  and a root cross-class control lane in parallel.
- Count literal updates, not parameter sweeps.  Preserve every kill, owner hit,
  false conjecture, transferred proof, and owner-thin reserve.
- Do not revive any P1–P146 mechanism by complement, parity, relabelling,
  carrier encoding, scheduler change, rate change, or state quotient.
- In particular, do not recycle valuation–gcd tent dynamics, Boolean
  row-inclusion residuals, leftmost Dyck reassociation, folded-hypercube vertex
  pushing, or convex-polygon ear deletion from P142–P146.
- If the first profiles show only generic closure, free-monoid transport,
  classical sorting, linear extensions, matroid bases, or known graph powers,
  kill the candidate and change system form.
- Freeze exactly five finalists whose carrier, update, temporal silhouette,
  inverse/endpoint object, and main proof engine are pairwise separated.

Finite computation supplies falsification pressure only.  A bounded owner
search non-hit is never novelty, priority, release, or authorship clearance.
