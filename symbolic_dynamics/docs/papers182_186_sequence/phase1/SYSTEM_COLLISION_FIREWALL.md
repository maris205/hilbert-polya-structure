# System collision firewall — frozen P182–P186 contracts

Passing this firewall means that no inspected P1–P181 theorem package supplies
the selected paper by a routine relabelling or proof transfer.  It is not an
external novelty or ownership determination.  All five papers remain
`HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## P182 — cyclic subspace-lattice comparator

The carrier is `L(V)^3` and

```text
F(A,B,C)=(C,A meet B,A join B).
```

The pair comparator itself is zero credit.  P109 acts on one subspace by a
fixed nilpotent image map; P173 samples a random quotient/image mechanism;
neither has a cyclic register or a recurrent swap locus.  The cross-domain
`SMJ` scout is the one-step pair comparator and was killed.  P182 survives only
on `F^4=F^2`, the exact depth criterion, the fixed/two-cycle census, and the
quotient-complement every-target fibre formula.

## P183 — random incoming-copy symmetrization

For a chosen vertex `v`, every outgoing bit `v->u` is replaced by the incoming
bit `u->v`.  P145 and P159 alter undirected graph bits through cuts or degree
parity.  P177 is an additive Cayley walk.  P179 is the closest internal system:
its local idempotents commute and its endpoint depends only on support.  P183's
maps do not commute on a conflicting pair; the first selected endpoint chooses
the final reciprocal bit.  The retained residual is the conflict-graph
deletion law together with the independent-set absorption and order-sensitive
endpoint kernels.

## P184 — co-gcd translation

On `Z/p^a Z`,

```text
T(x)=x+p^a/gcd(x,p^a) mod p^a.
```

P128 uses polynomial gcd data, P142 maps a divisor/valuation chain, and P166
selects a cyclic-cube translation by Hamming weight.  None acts on every
residue by its own additive-order step.  The paper must include `p=2`, the
even-exponent equality stratum, the uniform tail ladder, and the exact
double-target atlas.  Removing these items would collapse the residual toward
the occupied valuation shell.

## P185 — prefix-diversity delay

For `w in [n]^n`, coordinate `i` becomes the number of distinct letters in the
strict prefix ending before `i`.  Restricted-growth words and set-partition
encodings are background only.  The earlier word/automaton papers do not use
this autonomous prefix-diversity self-map.  The retained residual is the
closed iterate plus the simultaneous all-time image, every-target fibre, and
depth-CDF formulas; a paper about restricted-growth enumeration alone fails
the firewall.

## P186 — rank-compression support

For `A={a_0<...<a_{k-1}}`,

```text
R(A)=support{a_j-j}.
```

The weak/strict stretching bijection, stars and bars, and Fibonacci counts are
zero credit.  P97 uses additive sumset growth, P158/P162 use different subset
closures, and P179 randomly erases singleton labels.  P186 is retained only on
the exact gap evolution under iteration, the maximum-gap pointwise clock, and
the all-time inverse generating function.

## Reopen conditions

A selected slot reopens if a direct owner states the same literal update with
the same theorem conjunction, if an uninspected P1–P181 proof transfers
mechanically, if a theorem fails at a boundary parameter, or if the independent
verifier/rebuild controls cannot reproduce.  Search non-hits do not prevent a
reopen.
