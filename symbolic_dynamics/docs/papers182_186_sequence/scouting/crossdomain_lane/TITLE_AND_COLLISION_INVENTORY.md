## Hard firewalls adopted before ideation

- Generic exclusion/TASEP/Rule-184 dynamics, plain graph powering, canonical
  retractions, local complementation, and textbook lattice sorting are killed.
- A new carrier with the same clock and proof as an old paper is a transfer,
  not breadth.
- Parameter changes, relabellings, complements, duals, and conjugates do not
  count as distinct candidates.
- A stochastic choice is insufficient by itself: the local maps, sufficient
  statistic, and theorem package must differ.
- Search non-hits never support novelty.  Every survivor remains
  `HOLD_EXTERNAL` until an independent owner audit is complete.
