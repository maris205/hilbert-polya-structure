## Decisive negative controls

- `MCJ` has a substantial theorem package, but its inverse mechanism reduces
  to Foata/Renyi--Joyal record cuts plus star transpositions and collides with
  P105/P122/P155.  It is killed, not counted as a paper.
- `FDF` has a sharp binary clock and target fibres, but its literal update is
  Project Euler's *First Sort* (Problems 523/524).  The direct owner kills it.
- `SST` has a complete stopping theorem, but central-line stopping transfers
  from P166/P153 and its enumerator lies in established matrix cycle-index
  theory.  It remains reserve-only.
- `FAR` is value-complement conjugate to `FDR` and is excluded from fresh
  breadth.
- Linear cellular automata, static canonical repairs, transparent rotations,
  and irregular rules without a second stable axis were killed even when the
  first boxes looked attractive.
