# Frozen theorem contracts — P177--P181

These contracts fix the all-parameter work that each anonymous short paper
must contain.  Exhaustive programs are required as counterexample pressure,
but no finite run proves a contract.  Every paper remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## P177 — Random projective-hyperplane toggling

On subsets of `F_2^d \ {0}`, toggle the zero set of a freshly sampled
nonzero linear form.

1. Identify every closed communicating class as a coset of the augmented
   simplex code and prove that its support graph is exactly the crown graph
   `K_{2^d,2^d}` minus a perfect matching.
2. Count every ordered history at every positive time and hence give every
   labelled source--target transition probability.
3. Give the exact parity-class total-variation distance.
4. Determine the complete four-point spectrum, including multiplicities on
   the full `2^(2^d-1)`-state carrier.
5. Recover `d` and the number of components from an unlabelled component and
   the total carrier size, within the stated family.

## P178 — State-selected finite differences

On all functions `f:F_p -> F_p`, iterate
`(Tf)(x)=f(x+f(0))-f(x)`.

1. Prove the sharp nilpotence `T^p=0` and identify every image in the tower
   with the corresponding augmentation-ideal power.
2. Give every-time fibres, separating zero, nonzero reachable targets, and
   unreachable targets.
3. Determine every exact depth layer and exhibit a depth-`p` witness.
4. Compute the deterministic transition operator's characteristic
   polynomial.
5. Determine the complete zero-Jordan inventory, including exactly `p-1`
   top blocks of size `p`.

## P179 — Random singleton isolation on set partitions

Choose a uniform label of a partition of `[n]` and split it from its block as
a singleton.

1. Prove that the label-isolation maps are commuting idempotents and derive
   the complete diagonal spectrum, with multiplicities counted by
   singleton-free set partitions.
2. For arbitrary initial block sizes, give the all-time absorption CDF as an
   all-but-one coupon formula.
3. Give every labelled target probability as a finite exact-support sum and
   state its reachability condition.
4. For every target, count both distinct one-step predecessors and labelled
   predecessor/action pairs.
5. Separate all small boundaries, including the missing `n-1` singleton
   layer and `n=1`.

## P180 — Bilinear radial scaling

On pairs in `F_q^m`, iterate
`Phi(u,v)=(<u,v>u,<u,v>v)` for a fixed nondegenerate bilinear form.

1. Prove the closed iterate formula and the induced scalar cube dynamics.
2. Classify every tail and eventual period from the `3`-primary part of the
   initial bilinear value.
3. Give all-time fibres for every target, including the null cone.
4. Count every tail layer and the recurrent population when
   `q-1=3^A h`.
5. Determine the image size, the uniform nonzero image fibres, and the
   unique maximum fibre at zero.

## P181 — First-descent prefix reversal

On `S_n`, fix the increasing word and otherwise reverse the prefix ending
immediately after the first descent.

1. Characterize the image exactly and prove it has size `n!/2` for `n>=3`.
2. Identify the recurrent core as the identity plus peak-at-two two-cycles.
3. Give the exact depth-0/1/2 census.
4. Express every target fibre through the decreasing run beginning in
   position two.
5. Determine the sharp maximum fibre and every maximizing target, with the
   `n=2,3` boundary cases stated separately.

## Freeze rule

An exact external owner or an internal proof transfer can still kill or
shrink a paper.  It cannot silently change a contract or promote a reserve;
any replacement must be documented before manuscript work continues.
