# Problem anchor — P177–P181

**Route:** A.  **Batch:** exactly five anonymous theorem notes.  **External
status:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Anchored research question

Beyond the literal systems and proof mechanisms occupied or killed by
P1–P176, which five genuinely different finite dynamics show an early exact
signal strong enough to support both (i) a sharp all-parameter temporal,
recurrent, terminal, image, spectral, or distribution theorem and (ii) an
independent inverse, fibre, extremal, structural, or identifiability theorem
after owner subtraction?

The governing objective is breadth across dynamical subclasses.  A difficult
candidate receives no loyalty: without a stable small-case silhouette, a
natural literal update, an independent second theorem axis, and a credible
uniform proof route, it is killed and replaced.

## Promotion threshold

Each selected paper must contain:

1. one natural, completely specified finite autonomous map or Markov kernel;
2. at least one deductive all-parameter theorem;
3. a sharp temporal, recurrent, terminal, image, spectral, or distributional
   conclusion;
4. a genuinely separate inverse, fibre, structural, extremal, or
   identifiability conclusion;
5. a deterministic exact verifier and frozen transcript used as bounded
   counterexample pressure, never as a substitute for proof;
6. explicit P1–P176 mechanism subtraction and bounded direct-owner checks;
7. an anonymous short manuscript, two hostile review records, repaired
   theorem deltas, cold builds, manifests, integrity/failure-mode audit, and a
   visible external-hold statement.

Renamed standard algorithms, parameter variants, generic finite-map
bookkeeping, unstable enumerations, and search non-hits are below threshold.
Search breadth is never reported as a count of proved or validated subclasses.

## Selection boundary

Independent lanes cover algebra/finite fields/number theory,
combinatorics/automata/words, and stochastic/graph/matrix/lattice systems.
Exact enumeration may falsify a conjecture, locate exceptional parameters,
and suggest an invariant; it does not prove a uniform result or establish
novelty.  Only primary or authoritative records support ownership statements.
Exactly five pairwise separated systems are numbered only after proof-route,
collision, and owner pressure.  A later Critical defect reopens the slot.
