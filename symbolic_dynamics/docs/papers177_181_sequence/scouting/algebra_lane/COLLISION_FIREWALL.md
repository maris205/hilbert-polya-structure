## Hostile decision on the two spikes

\`SFD\` survives because its second axis is not a uniform kernel-coset count:
the state-selected nonzero direction word is recovered from the orbit, and
each word has a unique anchored lift. This is precisely what creates the
zero/nonzero fibre split and the top-block multiplicity.

\`SST\` does not receive the same recommendation. Once a central line is
chosen, its update is the simplest stopped clockwise walk. P166 already owns
the reduction of a statistic-controlled global translation to cyclic phase
orbits, while Kung--Stong--Morrison own the determinant-root enumerator. The
gap-marked conjunction is worth preserving as a reserve, but treating it as a
clean new mechanism would overstate the residual.
