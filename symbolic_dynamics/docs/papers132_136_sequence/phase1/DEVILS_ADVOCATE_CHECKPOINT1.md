## Strongest objections

1. **R02 is an owned physical rule.**  The all-`k` extension, sector
   language, and generic abelianness add no value.  The manuscript is viable
   only if the open-line terminal projection and every-target Gaussian depth
   fibre are absent from direct owners and proved without importing periodic
   ASEP conclusions.
2. **A02 may be cosmetic arithmetic.**  After prime support, the map is a
   small signed feed-forward Boolean network.  Its arithmetic presentation
   earns nothing unless the recurrent census and target-wise fibre formula
   form a useful unified theorem.
3. **PM1 may be known flattening plus a scheduler.**  Allowing block arity
   `b` merely changes the logarithm base.  Foata/cycle-composition and
   cut–join background could leave only an artificial deterministic wrapper.
4. **M01 is nearly a corollary.**  Once the classical exterior-square type
   formula is written, the whole depth-two atlas fits in a few lines.  Brevity
   is not a defect, but lack of an independent question may be.
5. **X01 is finite linear dynamics.**  The Frobenius map is `F_p`-linear and
   nilpotent, so `1+F` is a standard unipotent operator.  The truncated-ideal
   formula must contribute more than a basis-dependent restatement of Jordan
   theory.
6. **GR1 is a closure repackaging.**  Graph powers, diameter contraction,
   cluster endpoints, and connected-graph exponential formulae are classical.
   It should replace a lead only if an independent review values the complete
   pointwise basin/fibre conjunction.
