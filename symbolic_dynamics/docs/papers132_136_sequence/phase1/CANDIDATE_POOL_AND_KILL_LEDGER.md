## Consequential permanent kills

- `R02` open directed `k`-mer transport was not promoted: the literal dimer
  rule, irreducible strings, and ASEP sector structure are directly owned, and
  the residual open-boundary atlas did not clear the five-way value gate.
- `PM1` cycle merge and `GR1` graph squaring were killed as classical
  flattening/coarsening and graph-power wrappers.  A changed schedule or
  logarithm base does not create a new system.
- `M01` exterior-square types and `X01` Frobenius shear were killed after
  subtracting the exact invariant-factor formula and generic finite-linear or
  additive-polynomial dynamics.
- Complementary power–GCD (`CPG`/`D01`) collapsed to an occupied conjugacy.
  The two Frobenius candidates (`FM1`, `FM2`) likewise reduced to standard
  finite-linear structure after the owner gate.
- The attractive `BR1`, `SR1`, and `FG1` replacement signals did not retain a
  theorem-scale residual once classical border, scheduler, or functional-graph
  machinery was removed.
- Inversion-rank refinement (`PR1`) is permanently killed by the exact
  Allagan–Gao–Testart operator, theorem package, and witness; it may not be
  revived by renaming its ranks or carrier.
- The nondivisor algebra controls `CT2` and the remaining 19 systems failed
  recurrence, inverse, or owner-value gates.  Only the derived-centralizer map
  survives.
- Thirty-nine stochastic replacement systems were killed by direct random
  greedy owners, occupied stochastic wrappers, thin martingales, or
  unstructured terminal laws.  Only `SF1` survives, and its process itself is
  zero credit.

The detailed negative rows remain in their lane ledgers.  Replacement never
erases a failed system, and no killed candidate can re-enter this batch without
a genuinely different literal update and a new hostile gate.

## Freeze rule

The five systems are frozen only for internal Stage 2 drafting.  A manuscript
may narrow a contract in response to review, but it may not broaden it or
convert a bounded owner non-hit into novelty, priority, or publication
language.  Any direct owner of a residual conjunction reopens the gate and can
still kill the corresponding paper.
