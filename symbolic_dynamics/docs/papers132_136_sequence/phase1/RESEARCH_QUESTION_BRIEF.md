# Research question brief — P132–P136

## Question

Which underexplored literal finite dynamics, outside the mechanisms already
occupied by P1–P131, exhibit a simple early anomaly that can be upgraded to a
complete all-size theorem package?

## Required answer form

The batch answer is not a ranked idea list.  It is five frozen contracts, each
specifying:

- carrier and literal update;
- exact temporal invariant;
- deductive proof route and independent control route;
- direct-owner zero-credit boundary;
- closest internal collisions and a separating invariant;
- finite falsification range and excluded claims.

## Search emphasis

Prefer systems whose first small sizes already show a stable arithmetic or
combinatorial discontinuity: powers-of-a-prime periods, unexpectedly tiny
fibres, an exact support interval, a factorized inverse, a sharp extremizer,
or a clock determined by one canonical statistic.  Kill unstable spectra and
parameter-specific coincidences early.
