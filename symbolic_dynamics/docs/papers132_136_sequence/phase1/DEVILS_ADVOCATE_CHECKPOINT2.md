## Strongest objections and resolutions

1. **P132's literal coordinate predicate is already owned.**  Husfeldt and
   Rauhe study exactly the query “is the first `i` bits' sum at least
   `ceil(i/2)`?”  The paper therefore gives zero credit to the predicate and
   its batched one-step evaluation.  What survives is the repeated
   full-answer-vector feedback, the fixed-prefix amplifier, and its sharp
   logarithmic convergence theorem.  Classical excursion and meander counts
   remain supporting inverse geometry, not the novelty pitch.
2. **P133 could be cosmetic Boolean dynamics in arithmetic clothing.**  The
   support conjugacy alone is thin.  The paper survives only as the conjunction
   of an explicit source-phase recurrent decoder, a proved `h+1` entry bound,
   and an every-target compatibility-sensitive fibre formula.  No claim is
   made that Pratt chains or feed-forward Boolean propagation are new.
3. **P134 risks confusing a standard failure-link operation with iteration.**
   KMP, border tables, validation, realization, and census are directly owned.
   The manuscript must distinguish following `beta^j[i]` in a fixed word from
   recomputing the whole table `beta(beta(w))`.  It must also include the full
   indexed mismatch lemma and the corrected suffix proof for both factorial
   maximizers.  Failure of either kills the paper.
4. **P135 starts from owner-supplied group structure.**  Correct: centralizer
   orbit partitions and wreath-product commutators are zero credit.  A
   three-case local rule is not enough.  The residual must include the tagged
   all-weight temporal proof, complete disjoint recurrent decoder with OGFs,
   and every-target coefficient inverse.  Its `2n` clock is explicitly
   nonsharp and must not be advertised as an extremal theorem.
5. **P136 is literally a known random greedy cover process.**  Bar-Yehuda's
   hypergraph Pitt process owns the algorithm.  Exponential clocks and
   size-biased orders are also generic.  Only the exact heterogeneous
   sunflower endpoint law, actual-vertex resolution, full unit-rate discrete
   selection-count distribution, and disjoint-forest endpoint/count
   tensor/convolution package survive.  The
   endpoint is the full recorded set and may be nonminimal; silently reducing
   it would change the process and invalidate the formula.
