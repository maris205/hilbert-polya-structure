# Problem anchor — P132–P136

**Route:** A.  **Batch:** five theorem papers.  **External status:**
`HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Anchored research question

Across genuinely different finite or locally finite dynamical carriers, find
five literal update rules for which early exact computation exposes an
all-parameter temporal law that survives direct-owner subtraction and the
P1–P131 portfolio firewall.  Each surviving law must support a deductive
proof, an independent exact falsifier, and a self-contained anonymous short
paper.

## Nonnegotiable contribution test

A candidate survives only if its residual theorem describes at least one
substantive dynamical invariant: a sharp clock, full recurrent structure,
all-depth kernel/image law, target-wise fibres, exact hitting statistic,
cycle/zeta census, or an extremal theorem with a complete inverse.  A renamed
involution, closure, rotation, sorting rule, generic finite-map computation,
or carrier-only variation is killed.

## Breadth and diversity constraint

- Audit at least 100 literal systems across four independently recorded
  lanes before freezing five contracts.
- Preserve every negative result in a permanent kill ledger.
- Promote at most three systems per lane before the global gates.
- The five finalists must not share carrier, literal update, temporal
  silhouette, and proof engine.
- When an early signal dissolves into casework or a direct owner theorem,
  change system form rather than over-optimizing the failed candidate.

Finite enumeration is counterexample pressure, never proof or novelty
evidence.  No public release, priority, authorship, posting, submission, or
specialist contact is authorized by this anchor.
