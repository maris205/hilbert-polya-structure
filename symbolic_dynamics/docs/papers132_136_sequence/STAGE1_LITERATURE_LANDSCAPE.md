## Prefix-majority feedback

- T. Husfeldt and T. Rauhe, *New Lower Bound Techniques for Dynamic Partial
  Sums and Related Problems*, SIAM J. Comput. 32(3) (2003), 736–753,
  [DOI 10.1137/S0097539701391592](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539701391592).
  Their dynamic prefix query asks exactly whether a bit prefix has weak
  majority.  The coordinate predicate and batched one-step evaluation are
  direct background.
- E. Sparre Andersen, *On the fluctuations of sums of random variables II*,
  Math. Scand. 2 (1954), 194–222,
  [DOI 10.7146/math.scand.a-10407](https://doi.org/10.7146/math.scand.a-10407),
  and G. A. Hunt, Jr. with P. Erdős, *Changes of sign of sums of random
  variables*, Pacific J. Math. 3 (1953), 673–687,
  [primary article](https://msp.org/pjm/1953/3-4/pjm-v3-n4-p01-p.pdf).
  Persistence, sign changes, reflection, excursions, and meanders are
  classical zero-credit tools.
- E. Goles, P. Montealegre, M. Salo, and I. Törmä, *PSPACE-completeness of
  majority automata networks*, Theoret. Comput. Sci. 609 (2016), 118–128,
  [DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2015.09.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2015.09.014).
  General synchronous majority-network language is background; it does not
  supply the nested-prefix feedback theorem.

## Totient complements and Pratt DAGs

- K. Ford, S. Konyagin, and F. Luca, *Prime Chains and Pratt Trees*, Geom.
  Funct. Anal. 20 (2010),
  [DOI 10.1007/s00039-010-0089-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-010-0089-0),
  [arXiv:0904.0473](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0473).  Relations `p|q-1`,
  prime-chain depth, and Pratt height are owned.
- J. Aracena, L. Cabrera-Crot, and L. Salinas, *Finding the fixed points of a
  Boolean network from a positive feedback vertex set*, Bioinformatics 37
  (2021), [DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa922](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa922).
  Signed interaction-graph and Boolean-network machinery are background.

No primary source was located in the bounded search that prints the literal
map `d -> gcd(n,(n/d)phi(d))` together with the source-phase recurrent decoder,
the `h+1` entry bound, and every-target fibre formula.  That non-hit is not
novelty or priority evidence.

## Recomputed whole border arrays

- D. E. Knuth, J. H. Morris, Jr., and V. R. Pratt, *Fast Pattern Matching in
  Strings*, SIAM J. Comput. 6 (1977),
  [DOI 10.1137/0206024](https://doi.org/10.1137/0206024).  Prefix/failure
  functions and their linear computation are directly owned.
- F. Franek, S. Gao, W. Lu, P. J. Ryan, W. F. Smyth, Y. Sun, and L. Yang,
  *Verifying a Border Array in Linear Time*,
  [primary PDF](https://www.cas.mcmaster.ca/~franek/proceedings/border.pdf),
  and J.-P. Duval, T. Lecroq, and A. Lefebvre, *Efficient Validation and
  Construction of Border Arrays and Validation of String Matching Automata*,
  RAIRO Theor. Inform. Appl. 43 (2009),
  [DOI 10.1051/ita:2008030](https://doi.org/10.1051/ita:2008030).  Validation,
  realization, generation, and census of border arrays are zero credit.
- P. Gawrychowski, A. Jeż, and Ł. Jeż, *Validating the Knuth–Morris–Pratt
  failure function, fast and online*, Theory Comput. Syst. 54 (2014),
  [DOI 10.1007/s00224-013-9522-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00224-013-9522-8).
  Failure-function validity and realization on a minimal alphabet are owned.

Those sources concern a word's table and operations within it.  The residual
contract iterates by treating the complete table as a new integer word and
recomputing its complete table; a direct owner of that literal iteration was
not found in the bounded search.

## Derived centralizers and partition iteration

- J. R. Britnell and M. Wildon, *Orbit coherence in permutation groups*, J.
  Group Theory 17 (2014),
  [DOI 10.1515/jgt-2013-0029](https://doi.org/10.1515/jgt-2013-0029),
  [arXiv:1205.4960](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4960).  Orbit partitions of
  permutation centralizers and the wreath decomposition are direct
  structural background.
- R. Skuratovskii, *Commutators of Sylow subgroups of symmetric groups and
  commutator width in the wreath product of groups*,
  [arXiv:1812.10481](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.10481).  Commutator subgroups
  in permutational wreath products are owned background.
- S. Eliahou and J. Erickson, *Mutually describing multisets and integer
  partitions*, Discrete Math. 313 (2013),
  [DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2012.11.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2012.11.014),
  and A. Baalbaki et al., *On integer partitions and continued fraction type
  algorithms*, Ramanujan J. (2024),
  [DOI 10.1007/s11139-023-00791-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-023-00791-5).
  Iterated multiplicity descriptions and weight-preserving partition maps are
  zero-credit context.

The bounded search did not find the complete derived-centralizer self-map,
tagged temporal theorem, recurrent decoder, and target-fibre coefficient as a
single printed package.  The structural ingredients above remain expressly
owner credited.

## Random sunflower transversals

- P. Erdős and R. Rado, *Intersection theorems for systems of sets*, J.
  London Math. Soc. 35 (1960),
  [DOI 10.1112/jlms/s1-35.1.85](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-35.1.85).
  Sunflower/Delta-system geometry is owned.
- R. Bar-Yehuda, *One for the Price of Two: A Unified Approach for
  Approximating Covering Problems*, Algorithmica 27 (2000),
  [DOI 10.1007/s004530010009](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004530010009).
  Section 5.1 and Theorem 6 directly own the general hypergraph Pitt process:
  select an unhit edge, select a random vertex in it, record the vertex, and
  delete hit edges.  P136 makes no algorithm-invention or approximation claim.
- R. L. Plackett, *The Analysis of Permutations*, J. R. Stat. Soc. Ser. C 24
  (1975), [DOI 10.2307/2346567](https://doi.org/10.2307/2346567), and
  A. Gnedin, *Infinite Size-Biased Orders*,
  [arXiv:2309.15799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15799).  Weighted rankings,
  exponential representations, and dissociation of restrictions are generic
  zero-credit probability machinery.

The bounded search found no primary source printing the exact heterogeneous
sunflower endpoint integral, actual-vertex resolution, complete unit-rate
discrete selection-count law, and disjoint-forest endpoint/count
tensor/convolution result together.  This is
only a search boundary, not a novelty certificate.
