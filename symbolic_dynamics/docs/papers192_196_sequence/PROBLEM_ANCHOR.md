# Problem anchor — P192–P196

**Route:** A. **Batch:** exactly five anonymous theorem notes. **External
status:** `OWNER_AMBER / HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Anchored research question

Beyond the literal systems and proof mechanisms already occupied, killed, or
reserved by P1–P191, which five genuinely different finite deterministic maps
show an early exact silhouette strong enough to support both:

1. a sharp all-parameter theorem about temporal structure, recurrence,
   transients, image, or a structural quotient; and
2. a separate inverse, fibre, extremal, enumeration, or identifiability theorem
   proved by a second mechanism?

The governing objective is breadth across dynamical subclasses. A candidate is
killed quickly if its first boxes show only generic convergence, if its update
is a relabelled earlier map, if a clean sufficient statistic does not emerge,
or if the inverse problem has no tractable exact structure. Increasing a cutoff
does not rescue a weak literal system.

## Promotion threshold

Every selected paper must provide:

1. one natural and completely specified finite autonomous deterministic map;
2. at least one deductive all-parameter temporal or recurrent theorem;
3. a sharp clock, complete recurrent classification, image law, or comparably
   rigid structural conclusion;
4. a genuinely separate fibre, inverse, enumeration, or extremal conclusion;
5. a paper-local exact verifier and frozen transcript used only as bounded
   counterexample pressure;
6. explicit subtraction against P1–P191 and bounded direct-owner checking;
7. an anonymous short manuscript, two process-separated hostile reviews,
   accepted deltas, two physical cold builds, page-level visual QA, manifests,
   and a visible external hold.

Parameter variants, complements, conjugates, renamed classical algorithms,
generic finite-map bookkeeping, and search non-hits are below threshold.

## Selection boundary

Scouting spans algebraic and arithmetic carriers, words and compositions,
graphs and relations, matrices and set systems, and finite posets or lattices.
Exactly five pairwise-separated systems receive P192–P196 only after their
proof routes, collision boundaries, and source-owner checks survive. A later
Critical defect reopens the slot. No bounded search result is a novelty,
priority, ownership, or freedom-to-operate certificate.
