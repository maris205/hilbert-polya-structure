\begin{theorem}[Ordered-spine closure]
\label{thm:routeclosure}
For the frozen canonical family and its fixed finite-dimensional fibers,
H1--H9 imply that A3 fails.  The full local determinant is
\(
\det_{\C^d}(I-w_kBA^{k-1})
\), its coefficient supports are finite unions of ultimately periodic
sets, and its natural monomial remains $z^kM_k^{-2s}$.  The separate
one-dimensional oracle deletion control can keep prime indices but still
retains that marker and roof.  Within the licensed Paper19 transient and
Paper20 recurrent wrappers, exact countable selection has the failures
proved in \cref{sec:countable}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Finite-semigroup and automaton responses are covered by
\cref{thm:finite-semigroup,thm:automata}.  Fixed characteristic-zero linear
responses are covered by \cref{thm:smlsupport}; the complete block factor,
rather than merely its first trace-log term, is covered by
\cref{thm:exteriorlrs}.  Growing finite responses satisfy
\cref{cor:provestoomuch}.  The two licensed infinite-memory alternatives
are \cref{thm:transient,thm:clock,prop:inducing}.  Finally,
\cref{prop:monomial,thm:factorial,prop:stirling} fix the marker and roof.
\end{proof}

Thus the frozen tuple is
\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
(\mathrm{A0\_STRUCTURAL\_ARITHMETIC\_RELATION},\\
 \mathrm{A1\_WEAK},
 \mathrm{A2\_ANALYTIC\_DETERMINANT},\\
 \mathrm{A3\_FAIL},
 \mathrm{A4\_FAIL})
\end{gathered}}
\]
and the overall decision is
\[
        \boxed{\mathrm{ROUTE\_A\_REJECTED}}.
\]
Route B remains locked.  H1--H9 mathematically certify the A3 failure;
\texttt{A4\_FAIL} records only that this candidate constructs no qualifying
spectral mechanism.  It does not assert that such a mechanism cannot exist.

\subsection{Paper24 admission test}

A successor is worth opening only after it presents, before any
prime-prefix experiment, a source-derived recurrent grammar satisfying all
of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
\item prime selection is intrinsic rather than an attached scalar oracle;
\item accepted symbolic length and intrinsic roof are both $O(\log n)$;
\item the whole, uninduced vertex operator is compact or trace class on an
explicit half-plane;
\item the same object preserves the intended orbit marker and produces
$n^{-s}$ rather than the factorial monomial;
\item matched nonprime decidable controls do not pass for the same reason.
\end{enumerate}
These conditions are an admission test, not a claim that no grammar can
meet them.
