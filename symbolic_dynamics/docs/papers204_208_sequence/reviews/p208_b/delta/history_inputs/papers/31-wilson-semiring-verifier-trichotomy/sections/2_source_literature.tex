\section{Source and literature boundary}
\label{sec:source-literature}

\subsection{Finite-full-shift semiring skeleton}

Let $A_n$ be an alphabet with $n$ elements and set
\[
  F_n=(A_n^{\mathbb Z},\sigma_n),\qquad n\geq0,
\]
with $F_0$ the empty system.  We work with conjugacy classes.  Alphabet sum
and alphabet product are
\[
  F_m\boxplus F_n:=F_{A_m\sqcup A_n}\cong F_{m+n},
  \qquad
  F_m\boxtimes F_n:=F_{A_m\times A_n}\cong F_{mn}.
\]
The word \emph{alphabet-sum} matters.  The operation is not asserted to be a
categorical coproduct in the category of subshifts and block maps; categorical
structures in symbolic dynamics require more careful choices
\citep{SaloTorma2015}.  The source norm and roof are
\[
  \mathcal N(F_n)=e^{h(F_n)}=n,
  \qquad h(F_n)=\log n\quad(n\geq1).
\]
The additive successor is $S(F_n)=F_n\boxplus F_1$, and its iterates generate
the source order used for quotient and remainder.

Full shifts also have a separate direct-product primeness theory
\citep{Kopra2023}.  Paper 31 does not identify prime alphabet cardinality with
a newly discovered symbolic notion.  The semiring skeleton is used only to
derive integer arithmetic from source operations and to test the naturality
of a stationary verifier.

\subsection{Four neighboring literatures}

\paragraph{Symbolic zeta functions.}
Finite-state shift zeta functions and determinant identities go back to
\citet{BowenLanford1970}.  Countable-state thermodynamic formalism requires
recurrence and summability hypotheses that are absent from a bare finite-block
calculation \citep{GurevichSavchenko1998,Sarig1999}.  Renewal systems further
show how flexible code-based zeta functions can be \citep{Hong2011}.  These
sources support the formal periodic context; none licenses an ordinary
determinant for the noncompact Wilson adjacency.

\paragraph{Computation and prime recognition.}
Prime recognition by automata is classical \citep{HartmanisShank1968}, and
register machines already realize broad classes of recursive computations
\citep{ShepherdsonSturgis1963}.  Topological and symbolic dynamical systems can
encode computation and universality \citep{Kurka1997,DelvenneKurkaBlondel2006}.
Consequently, a graph that computes a prime predicate is not by itself an
arithmetic-dynamical contribution.  The relevant question is whether the
computation changes primitive recurrence and operator ownership.

\paragraph{Semirings of dynamics.}
Addition and product of finite dynamical systems support a nontrivial
factorization theory \citep{NaquinGadouleau2024}.  Recent work extends this
language to formal sums and injective partial transformations
\citep{GadouleauJohnson2026}.  Paper 31 uses a narrower full-shift source and
does not claim the semiring viewpoint as new.  Its contribution is the exact
comparison between the bare multiplicative clone, the matched semiring clone,
and the resulting analytic failure modes.

\paragraph{State expansion and operator determinants.}
State expansion changes discrete graph time even when it preserves an
unmarked flow invariant \citep{ParrySullivan1975}.  Weighted shifts on
directed trees provide a general operator-theoretic setting for graph weights
\citep{JablonskiJungStochel2012}, while infinite weighted-graph zeta functions
require their own hypotheses \citep{Deitmar2015}.  Ordinary Hilbert-space
Fredholm determinants belong to trace-class perturbations
\citep{Simon1977}.  We therefore track the free marker $z$, state the operator
space explicitly, and distinguish raw adjacency, first return, and transient
replacement.

\subsection{Nearest collisions and bounded novelty}

The nearest prior constructions are internal.  Paper 19 proves that a
trace-class semiring verifier can carry the Euler determinant while all
transient arithmetic prunes to the atom diagonal.  Paper 20 proves the exact
compactness criterion for disjoint weighted cycles, entropy-clock dilution,
first-return marker change, and a universal total-decider control.  Those
results are inherited, not renamed.

To our knowledge, based on the primary-source searches recorded in
\texttt{LITERATURE\_AUDIT.md} and completed on 2026-08-14, no external paper
found in that search combines the specific Paper-30 bare-clone separation,
matched-semiring transport, Wilson graph-step product, and whole-operator
compactness trichotomy.  This is a search-bounded positioning statement.  It
does not assert the absence of every equivalent formulation.

\subsection{Functional and data conventions}

The candidate may use the source semiring, equality, quotient/remainder,
congruence, entropy, exact arithmetic, and deterministic cutoffs.  It may not
read a prime, prime-power, factor, atom-color, orbit-projector, or target-zero
table.  Target-zero ordinates, root matching, and post-control fitting are
excluded.  A matched isomorphic relabeling is a mandatory control.  Formal
periodic products, ordinary traces, Fredholm determinants, and induced return
operators remain different functional types throughout.
