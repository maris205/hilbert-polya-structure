\section{Raw orbit product and first-return collapse}
\label{sec:return}

Noncompactness does not erase the finite-block cycle census.  It changes what
can legitimately be called an operator determinant.

\begin{theorem}[Raw graph-step ledger]
\label{thm:raw-product}
For $\RePart s>1$ and $|z|\le1$, the product
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{orb}}^{\mathrm{raw}}(s,z)
:=\prod_p\left(1-z^{\ell(p)}p^{-s}\right)
\label{eq:raw-product}
\end{equation}
converges normally on compact subsets of that domain.  At $z=1$,
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{orb}}^{\mathrm{raw}}(s,1)
=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})=\zeta(s)^{-1}.
\label{eq:raw-zeta}
\end{equation}
It is not the ordinary Fredholm determinant of the whole $L_s$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $|z|\le1$ and $\sigma>1$,
\[
\sum_p\left|z^{\ell(p)}p^{-s}\right|
\le\sum_pp^{-\sigma}<\infty.
\]
The same majorant is locally uniform after fixing a compact lower bound for
$\sigma$, so the canonical product converges normally.  Equation
\eqref{eq:raw-zeta} is Euler's absolutely convergent product.  On every
finite prime cutoff,
\eqref{eq:prime-block} shows that
\eqref{eq:raw-product} is the determinant of the finite direct sum.  But
\cref{thm:noncompact,thm:no-schatten} show that the infinite whole adjacency
is not trace class.  Thus no ordinary Hilbert-space Fredholm determinant of
$L_s$ has been produced.
\end{proof}

This theorem gives full graph-step information, not merely the specialization
$z=1$.  Formally differentiating its logarithm near $z=0$ recovers the block
trace rule
\eqref{eq:block-traces}; nonzero terms occur only at multiples of the
expanding cycle lengths.

\subsection{Poincar\'e section}

Let
\[
\Sigma=\{I_p:p\text{ prime}\}
\]
be the section of accepted input states.  One full return from $I_p$ traverses
$\Gamma_p$ and accumulates weight $p^{-s}$.

\begin{theorem}[First-return diagonal]
\label{thm:first-return}
The induced weighted return operator on $\ell^2(\Sigma)$ is
\begin{equation}
R_s\delta_{I_p}=p^{-s}\delta_{I_p}.
\label{eq:return-diagonal}
\end{equation}
For $\RePart s>1$, $R_s\in\Sone$ and
\begin{equation}
\det(I-zR_s)=\prod_p(1-zp^{-s}).
\label{eq:return-det}
\end{equation}
As an operator, $R_s$ is unitarily equivalent to the diagonal prime-loop
core obtained by replacing each entire verification cycle with one loop.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The product of the edge weights around $\Gamma_p$ is $p^{-s}$, proving
\eqref{eq:return-diagonal}.  Since
$\sum_p|p^{-s}|<\infty$ for $\sigma>1$, the diagonal operator is trace class.
Its Fredholm determinant is the product of its diagonal factors.  Sending
$\delta_{I_p}$ to the standard prime basis gives the asserted unitary
equivalence.
\end{proof}

First return is a legitimate dynamical operation; the point is that it is not
the same discrete-time object.  The original block and its induced loop give
the respective factors
\begin{equation}
\underbrace{1-z^{\ell(p)}p^{-s}}_{\text{original graph steps}}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\underbrace{1-zp^{-s}}_{\text{return steps}}.
\label{eq:marker-firewall}
\end{equation}
They coincide at $z=1$.  They also agree if induction explicitly carries the
composite marker $z^{\ell(p)}$ as part of the return weight.  Without that
transport, equality at $z=1$ cannot be promoted to equality of marked
determinants.  This is the marker firewall in
\cref{fig:overview}.

\subsection{The opposite roof choice}

There is an instructive trace-class control.  Retain the summable source roofs
from the transient SD-C21 verifier and redirect the old terminal successful
edge directly to $I_p$.  In particular, retain its roof
$\log(p(m+1))$; do not add a new $\log p$ return edge.  The edge-by-edge
summability majorant then survives on $\RePart s>1$.  However, every verifier
edge already carries a clock comparable to at least $\log p$, so the total
around $\Gamma_p$ is at least $\ell(p)\log p$, not $\log p$.

Thus the two natural objectives point in opposite directions:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{ccl}
\text{exact total clock }\log p
&\Longrightarrow&\text{noncompact whole adjacency},\\[2pt]
\text{summable source edge clocks}
&\Longrightarrow&\text{trace class but wrong orbit clock}.
\end{array}
\label{eq:clock-dichotomy}
\end{equation}
The source roofs are themselves a frozen modeling choice and are invisible to
the desired Euler total once first return is imposed.
