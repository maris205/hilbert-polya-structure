\section{Strict route evaluation and limitations}
\label{sec:route}

The construction was designed to test one specific Route-A loophole, so we
evaluate the frozen object rather than combining certificates from different
candidates.  The strict tuple is
\[
\begin{split}
(&\texttt{A0\_ANALYTIC\_ARITHMETIC\_ORIGIN},\
  \texttt{A1\_WEAK},\\
 &\texttt{A2\_ANALYTIC\_DETERMINANT},\
  \texttt{A3\_FAIL},\
  \texttt{A4\_FAIL}).
\end{split}
\tag{9.1}
\]

\paragraph{A0: credited with a restriction.}
The tensor factorization and logarithmic roof supply a noncircular
arithmetic origin.  The incidence cocycle itself is inventory-blind, so A0
does not certify arithmetic selectivity.

\paragraph{A1: weak only.}
Primitive words and holonomies are exact, including the commutator witness.
There is no bijection between symbolic primitives and primes or prime
powers; mixed subset primitives persist.

\paragraph{A2: analytic determinant.}
The finite same-object character determinants are genuine, and the
nontrivial blocks have a trace-class Fredholm realization on
$\RePart s>2$.  This is enough for the stated A2 label, but not for critical
strip access.

\paragraph{A3 and A4: failed.}
The standard block leaks mixed products, matched inventories reproduce the
same result, and the analytic domain supplies neither robustness near the
critical line nor the missing arithmetic primitive correspondence.

Thus the frozen decision is
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c}
\texttt{GO\_GENUINE\_TRANSITION\_HOLONOMY}\\
\texttt{GO\_SAME\_OBJECT\_ARTIN\_BLOCKS}\\
\texttt{STOP\_NONABELIAN\_CLEAN\_FACTOR}\\
\texttt{STOP\_ARITHMETIC\_SELECTIVITY}\\
\texttt{PROVES\_TOO\_MUCH}\\
\texttt{ROUTE\_A\_REJECTED}\qquad
\texttt{ROUTE\_B\_LOCKED}.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The limitations are equally concrete.  We have no general classification of
all-irrep-clean cocycles, no theorem beyond the frozen local grammar, no
analytic continuation of the nontrivial block, and no zero correspondence.
These are not deferred details of a positive RH argument; they explain the
negative route decision.
