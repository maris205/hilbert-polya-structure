Let $\alpha$ be a quadratic Sturmian slope and let a legal ultimately
periodic Ostrowski digit sequence encode a formal intercept $\rho$.  Using the
complete finite repetition table for the word $T^\rho(c_\alpha)$, we give an
exact all-boundary computation of its Diophantine exponent.  If $L$ is a common
phase-aligned period of the slope and intercept tails, the exponent is one plus
the maximum of at most $4L$ explicit elements of the quadratic field generated
by the Perron root of the continuant period matrix.  An exact three-scale sign
test decides empty plateaus, including equality cases, and a terminating
algorithm returns a primitive minimal polynomial and a rational isolating
interval and compares two outputs.  The reduction also exposes a subscript
error in case~7 of the available v1 repetition theorem: $\rho_{n-1}$ must be
replaced by $\rho_n$.  The correction follows from the preceding plateau
formula and the Ostrowski carry rule; on the finite data
$a_i=3$, $(b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(0,1,2,0)$, the printed row gives the impossible
value $r(9)=13>10$, whereas the corrected row gives $r(9)=10$.
