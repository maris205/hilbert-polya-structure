\section{Source-derived graph, cocycle, and operators}
\label{sec:source}

\subsection{Full-shift skeleton}

Let \(F_n=A_n^{\mathbb Z}\) denote the full shift on an \(n\)-letter
alphabet, up to topological conjugacy.  We freeze

\[
 F_m\boxtimesf F_n:=F_{A_m\times A_n}\cong F_{mn},
 \qquad
 F_m\boxplus F_n:=F_{A_m\sqcup A_n}\cong F_{m+n}.
\]

Here \(\boxplus\) means alphabet disjoint union followed by the full-shift
functor; it is not the categorical coproduct in a category of subshifts.
The successor and entropy are

\[
 S(F_n)=F_n\boxplus F_1\cong F_{n+1},
 \qquad h(F_n)=\log n.
\]

The vertex set is \(V=\{2,3,\ldots\}\), with \(n\) representing \(F_n\).
An edge is an exposed tensor factorization:

\begin{equation}
 n\to d
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 d\ge2,\quad d\mid n+1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 S(F_n)\cong F_d\boxtimesf F_q.
\label{eq:edge-rule}
\end{equation}

The factor witness is unique and defines the cofactor label

\begin{equation}
 q(n,d)=\frac{n+1}{d}\in\N_{\ge1}.
\label{eq:cofactor}
\end{equation}

The one-sided countable Markov shift is

\[
 X_G^+=\{(n_0,n_1,\ldots)\in V^{\N}:n_j\to n_{j+1}\}.
\]

No primality predicate occurs in \cref{eq:edge-rule,eq:cofactor}.  Rational
primes will enter only after freezing, as the atoms of the coefficient
monoid used to evaluate the resulting ledger.

\subsection{Monoid, group completion, and periodic conventions}

Let

\[
 \mathsf M=(\N_{\ge1},\cdot),
 \qquad
 \Gamma=\gp(\mathsf M)=\Q_{>0}^{\times}.
\]

Every edge voltage \(q(n,d)\) lies in \(\mathsf M\subset\Gamma\).  For a
rooted closed path
\(\gamma=(n_0,\ldots,n_{r-1})\), with \(n_r=n_0\), define

\[
 Q(\gamma)=\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}q(n_j,n_{j+1}),
 \qquad
 N(\gamma)=\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}n_j.
\]

Directed paths are identified under cyclic rotation when called orbits;
reflection is not quotiented.  A primitive orbit is not a positive temporal
power of a shorter orbit.

The compact dual

\[
 \widehat\Gamma\cong\prod_{p\ \mathrm{prime}}\T
\]

has normalized Haar measure \(\dd\chi\), with convention

\begin{equation}
 \int_{\widehat\Gamma}\overline{\chi(m)}\chi(g)\,\dd\chi
 =\id_{\{g=m\}}.
\label{eq:haar}
\end{equation}

This is coefficient extraction in a source-label group, not a spectral
transform of the Riemann zeta function.

\subsection{Roofs and scalar fibers}

The endpoint and cofactor roofs are

\[
 \tau(n,d)=\log n+\log d,
 \qquad
 \rho(n,d)=\log q(n,d).
\]

On \(\cH=\ell^2(V)\), with standard basis \(e_n\), use the column-source
weighted adjacency

\begin{equation}
 L_{s,u}e_n=
 \sum_{\substack{d\ge2\\d\mid n+1}}
 (nd)^{-s}q(n,d)^{-u}e_d.
\label{eq:Lsu}
\end{equation}

For a unitary character \(\chi\in\widehat\Gamma\), set

\begin{equation}
 L_{s,\chi}e_n=
 \sum_{\substack{d\ge2\\d\mid n+1}}
 (nd)^{-s}\chi(q(n,d))e_d.
\label{eq:Lschi}
\end{equation}

Around a closed path, endpoint weights telescope by cyclicity to

\begin{equation}
 \prod_j(n_jn_{j+1})^{-s}=N(\gamma)^{-2s}.
\label{eq:endpoint-cycle-weight}
\end{equation}

Thus the full power-twist weight is

\[
 w_{s,u}(\gamma)=N(\gamma)^{-2s}Q(\gamma)^{-u}.
\]

The factor two in the endpoint exponent is part of the source roof and will
not be changed after target comparison.

\subsection{Scalar and lifted determinant conventions}

Whenever \(L_{s,u}\in\Sone\), define

\[
 D(s,u;z)=\det_{\cH}(I-zL_{s,u}).
\]

For a character fiber, write

\[
 D_\chi(s,z)=\det_{\cH}(I-zL_{s,\chi}).
\]

These are entire in \(z\), and near \(z=0\),

\begin{equation}
 -\log D_\chi(s,z)=
 \sum_{r\ge1}\frac{z^r}{r}\Tr L_{s,\chi}^r.
\label{eq:trace-log}
\end{equation}

The logarithm is the normalized local germ.  No global branch through
determinant zeros is asserted.

For the regular representation \(\lambda\) of \(\Gamma\), define on
\(\cH\otimes\ell^2(\Gamma)\)

\begin{equation}
 \mathbb L_s(e_n\otimes\delta_g)=
 \sum_{\substack{d\ge2\\d\mid n+1}}
 (nd)^{-s}e_d\otimes\delta_{q(n,d)g}.
\label{eq:regular-lift}
\end{equation}

Its natural semifinite algebra and trace are

\[
 \cN=B(\cH)\bar\otimes L(\Gamma),
 \qquad
 \Phi=\Tr\bar\otimes\tau_\Gamma.
\]

When its trace series is defined, the normalized local semifinite determinant
means

\begin{equation}
 \det_\Phi(I-z\mathbb L_s)=
 \exp\left[-\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{z^r}{r}
 \Phi(\mathbb L_s^r)\right].
\label{eq:phi-det}
\end{equation}

It is not an ordinary Fredholm determinant of
\(\mathbb L_s\) on the lifted Hilbert space.
