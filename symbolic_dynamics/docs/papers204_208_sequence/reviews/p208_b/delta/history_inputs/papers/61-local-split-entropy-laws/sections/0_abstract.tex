\begin{abstract}
We study a presentation-local family of nontrivial split factor maps between
mixing shifts of finite type.  Starting from a primitive directed multigraph,
we duplicate a length-$N$ based return walk by an internally new $N$-edge
return chain whose internal vertices are all new, and map that chain edgewise
onto the old walk.  The map is
a nonconjugate one-block factor with a one-block section.  If
$D(z)=\det(I-zA)$ and $C_v(z)$ is the principal cofactor at the base vertex,
the enlarged graph satisfies
$D_N(z)=D(z)-z^NC_v(z)$, equivalently
$F_v(r_N)+r_N^N=1$.  At positive entropy, left and right Perron eigenvectors
give a vertex weight $\pi_v$, and the entropy excess is
$\pi_v\lambda^{-N}\{1+O(N\lambda^{-N})\}$.  At the mixing zero-entropy
boundary, the law changes to $W(N)/N$, with an explicit first correction.
We separate these residual quantitative statements from standard retract,
renewal, and Perron mechanisms and retain a specialist priority hold.
\end{abstract}
