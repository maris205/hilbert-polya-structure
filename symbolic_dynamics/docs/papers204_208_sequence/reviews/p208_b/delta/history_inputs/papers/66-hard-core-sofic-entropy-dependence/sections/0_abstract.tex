\begin{abstract}
Let the free group $\F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ act by shifts on the
four-symbol nearest-neighbour hard-core subshift: adjacent vertices cannot
both carry one of the three active symbols, while zero is safe.  Motivated
directly by the continuous-alphabet hard-core construction of Li and Liu, we
show that this elementary mixing subshift of finite type has two homomorphic
sofic approximations with different positive topological sofic entropies.
For every homomorphic approximation we first identify entropy with the
exponential growth rate of the activity-three independence polynomial.  A
quantitative safe-symbol repair is the step that passes from approximate
microstates to exact graph models.  A bipartite residual-chain approximation
has entropy at least $\log 2$.  A deterministic approximation selected from
two independent uniform permutations has entropy between
$\frac15\log4$ and
\[
 -\log\bigl((1+y)(1-y)^2\bigr)
 =0.6770873658955\ldots,
 \qquad y=3(1-y)^4,
\]
where $y\in(0,1)$ is unique.  The displayed upper endpoint is strictly below
$\log2$.  Thus positive approximation dependence already occurs in a natural
four-symbol hard-core model.  This is not a priority claim for positive sofic
entropy dependence in general, which was previously established for a mixing
SFT by Airey, Bowen, and Lin.
\end{abstract}
