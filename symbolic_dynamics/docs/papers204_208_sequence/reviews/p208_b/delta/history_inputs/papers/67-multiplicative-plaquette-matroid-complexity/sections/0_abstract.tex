\begin{abstract}
Let $a,b\geq2$ be coprime and let $\F_q$ be a finite field.  We study the
compact linear multiplicative constraint space
\[
 X_{a,b}=\{x\in\F_q^{\N}:x_n-x_{an}-x_{bn}+x_{abn}=0
 \text{ for every }n\geq1\}.
\]
Every integer has unique coordinates $r a^i b^j$, and on each root component
the rule integrates to $x_{r a^i b^j}=u_i+v_j$.  This yields an explicit
topological-group isomorphism between $X_{a,b}$ and the unrestricted
coordinates whose indices are not divisible by $ab$.  We then calculate every
finite projection.  A coordinate $r a^i b^j$ is viewed as an edge from row
vertex $i$ to column vertex $j$; the projection dimension is the sum of
the number of vertices minus the number of connected components over the
resulting root-wise bipartite graphs.  Thus the
coordinate-dependence matroid is a direct sum of graphic matroids, and the
only finite compatibility conditions are alternating cycle sums.  Under Haar
measure, forests are exactly the jointly independent coordinate families and
each cycle-rank unit contributes $\log q$ of total correlation.  Arithmetic
prefixes have $q^{N-\lfloor N/(ab)\rfloor}$ patterns, whereas an $M\times N$
exponent rectangle has $q^{M+N-1}$ patterns.  These are two normalizations of
one finite-shape rank formula, not interchangeable entropy statements.
\end{abstract}
