\section{From tensor atoms to temporal primitive orbits}

Let $\cA=\At(\FSh)$.  We now apply a fixed construction that depends only on
the atom set.

\begin{definition}[Atom-loop shift]
\label{def:atom-shift}
Define the one-sided countable Markov shift
\[
  Y_\otimes=\{y=(a_j)_{j\geq0}\in\cA^{\mathbb N}:a_{j+1}=a_j
  \text{ for every }j\},
\]
with left shift $S$.  Its adjacency matrix is the identity on $\cA$.  Equip
it with the roof
\[
  \tau(y)=\htop(a_0).
\]
The suspension is denoted $(Y_\otimes^\tau,\phi_t)$.
\end{definition}

The phase space is a countable union of fixed symbolic points.  It is neither
compact nor mixing, but it is one autonomous symbolic system.  Atom
extraction and orbitification are both universal rules and do not change with
a cutoff.

\begin{theorem}[Exact primitive/repetition ledger]
\label{thm:ledger}
For every rational prime $p$, $Y_\otimes$ has exactly one primitive
period-one orbit $\gamma_p$, represented by the constant symbol $[F_p]$.
The suspension length of its $r$-fold repetition is
\[
  T_{p,r}=r\tau([F_p])=r\log p=\log(p^r).
\]
There are no mixed-symbol primitive cycles.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Identity adjacency forces every admissible sequence to be constant, hence
each symbol supplies one primitive fixed point and no orbit uses two symbols.
The roof statement follows from \cref{thm:tensor-primes}; suspension
repetition adds roof lengths.
\end{proof}

The diagonal adjacency is a modeling choice, but not an arbitrary
prime-indexed table: its alphabet is selected by tensor indecomposability and
its roof is selected by entropy.  In \cref{sec:no-mixing} we show that, under
positive weights, exact von Mangoldt support forces this separation.

Because $Y_\otimes$ has countably many fixed points, its unweighted
Artin--Mazur fixed-point count is infinite.  The zeta used below is the
\emph{entropy-weighted Ruelle/suspension zeta}; it is not an ordinary
unweighted Artin--Mazur zeta.  Likewise, the monoidal object inventory and the
atom-loop system are two successive constructions, not isomorphic dynamical
systems.

Functorial orbit counting under products is classical
\citep{pakapongpunward2009}.  Here the order of operations matters: first
factor the symbolic objects in the monoidal category, then make the atoms
temporal loops.  We do not identify the temporal orbit monoid of $F_n$ with
the tensor monoid of full-shift objects.
