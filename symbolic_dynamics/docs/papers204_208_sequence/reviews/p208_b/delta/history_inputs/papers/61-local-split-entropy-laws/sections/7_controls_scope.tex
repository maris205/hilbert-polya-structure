\section{Controls, ownership, and scope}
\label{sec:controls}

The proofs above are symbolic.  A deterministic companion script protects
the graph orientation, determinant sign, edge-count exponent, Perron
normalization, and regime split against implementation and transcription
errors.  Its frozen coverage is summarized in \cref{tab:controls}.

The exact layer constructs $A_N$ and verifies
\[
 \det(I-zA_N)-\{D(z)-z^NC_v(z)\}=0
\]
as a polynomial for five matrices and $N=1,\ldots,6$.  The three
positive-entropy examples verify $F_v(r)=1$ and both identities in
\cref{eq:derivative}; one example is the nonsymmetric multigraph
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
for which $\pi_v=1/2$.  This example detects the omission of a left
eigenvector.  High-precision roots then compare $\Gap_N$ with $\pi_vr^N$.
The ratios for the deliberately wrong normalizations $r^{N-1}$ and
$r^{N+1}$ converge to $r$ and $r^{-1}$ rather than one.  Finally, six
zero-entropy sizes compare the exact scalar root with both terms of
\cref{eq:lambert}.  The run terminates with \texttt{ALL CHECKS PASS}.

The script \texttt{code/verify\_split\_gap.py} uses SymPy for exact
polynomials and fixes mpmath precision at 90 decimal digits for regression
tables.  It has no random input.  The stored output and environment receipt
are included in the internal artifact.  Numerical convergence is
falsification evidence only: it neither proves an asymptotic theorem nor
establishes priority.

\paragraph{What is standard.}
The image of a continuous section is a conjugate subshift, entropy minimality
of irreducible SFTs is standard, and the first-return determinant ratio is a
classical renewal mechanism \cite{LindMarcus2021,Gomez2010,BlockEtAl1980}.
Perron spectral projectors likewise own the residue calculation.  The paper
does not advertise any of these ingredients separately as a new theorem.

\paragraph{What is not addressed.}
We do not characterize which prescribed factor maps split, extend the
construction canonically across graph presentations, or assert that $\pi_v$
is a conjugacy invariant.  The old image return walk need not avoid $v$
internally.  Primitivity is needed for every sufficiently large $N$, and
irreducibility is essential in \cref{prop:rigidity}.  Broad small-gap
existence is already available by a product construction and is not the
headline.

\paragraph{Release rule.}
The source audit found no exact owner for the combined edgewise split
construction, determinant/radius equation, normalized constant, and Lambert
boundary correction.  This is negative evidence, not a novelty proof.
An exact prior source or a specialist assessment that the package is routine
forces publication HOLD.  Until that review occurs, this document is an
internal mathematically closed candidate only.
