Sharp Span Clocks and Stabilizer-Weighted Fibres}

\begin{abstract}
Let $V=\F_2^d$ and repeatedly replace a subset $A\subseteq V$ by
$A\cap(A+v)$, where $v$ is sampled uniformly from $V$.  A length-$t$
history acts exactly as intersection by every translate in the span of its
sampled vectors.  We use this sufficient statistic to give the complete rank
law and a sharp worst-non-full-source emptying clock, witnessed by $V\setminus
\{0\}$.  For every target $B$, every time, and every source size, we then
enumerate source--history pairs by a polynomial determined by the translation
stabilizer of $B$.  Its one-step boundary separates targets with trivial
stabilizer and recovers the stabilizer dimension once the phase dimension and
target size are known.  The forward erosion algebra and finite-field rank law
are classical inputs; the contribution assessed here is the exact conjunction
with the target-dependent inverse atlas.  All release claims remain under
\texttt{HOLD\_EXTERNAL}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Process and theorem}

Write $A+v=\{a+v:a\in A\}$.  Starting from $A_0\in\cP(V)$, sample independent
uniform vectors $v_1,v_2,\ldots$ and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:update}
 A_i=A_{i-1}\cap(A_{i-1}+v_i).
\end{equation}
For a subspace $H\leq V$, define
\[
 \E_H(A)=\bigcap_{h\in H}(A+h),
 \qquad H_t=\langle v_1,\ldots,v_t\rangle.
\]
Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:S}
 S(t,r)=\prod_{i=0}^{r-1}(2^t-2^i),
\end{equation}
with the empty product equal to one, and put $S(t,r)=0$ for $r>t$.  We write
$\genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{m}{r}_2$ for the Gaussian binomial coefficient.

For a target $B\subseteq V$, set $b=|B|$ and
\[
 \Stab(B)=\{v\in V:B+v=B\},\qquad s=\dim\Stab(B).
\]
The joint source-size/history polynomial is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Fdef}
 F_t(B;z)=\sum_{A\subseteq V}\ \sum_{(v_1,\ldots,v_t)\in V^t}
 \mathbf 1\{A_t=B\}z^{|A|}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Span clock and every-target inverse atlas]\label{thm:main}
For every $d,t\geq0$ the following statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every history satisfies the pointwise identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:span}
 A_t=\E_{H_t}(A_0).
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rank}
 \Pr(\dim H_t=r)=
 \genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{d}{r}_2\frac{S(t,r)}{2^{dt}}.
\end{equation}
\item The only states fixed by every possible sampled update are $\varnothing$
and $V$.  Let $\sigma=\min\{t:H_t=V\}$.  Every $A_0\neq V$ is empty by
time $\sigma$, and this bound is attained by $A_\star=V\setminus\{0\}$.
Thus the sharp worst-source emptying distribution is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cdf}
 \Pr(\sigma\leq t)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&t<d,\\[2pt]
 \displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{d-1}(1-2^{i-t}),&t\geq d,
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mean}
 \mathbb E\sigma=\sum_{r=0}^{d-1}\frac{1}{1-2^{r-d}}.
\end{equation}
\item For every target $B$ and indeterminate $z$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fibre}
 F_t(B;z)=z^b\sum_{r=0}^{s}
 \genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{s}{r}_2 S(t,r)
 \left((1+z)^{2^r}-z^{2^r}\right)^{2^{d-r}-b/2^r}.
\end{equation}
For $r\leq s$, the exponent is a nonnegative integer.  Formula
\eqref{eq:fibre} includes $t=0$, $d=0$, and the empty and full targets.
At one step its unweighted specialization is the boundary-safe formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:one-step}
 F_1(B;1)=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&s=0,\\[2pt]
 1+(2^s-1)3^{2^{d-1}-b/2},&s\geq1.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\item The phase cardinality $|\cP(V)|=2^{2^d}$ determines $d$.  At fixed
$(d,b)$, the value in \eqref{eq:one-step} is strictly increasing over the
feasible values of $s$, with value one occurring exactly at $s=0$.
Consequently phase size, target size, and one-step inverse mass recover the
target stabilizer dimension.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The clock in part~(2) is a sharp universal bound, not an assertion that every
non-full source empties exactly at $\sigma$.  This distinction is important:
individual sources may disappear earlier.

\section{History spans and the sharp clock}

The algebra of erosions by translates is standard in mathematical morphology
\citep{heijmans_ronse_1990,heijmans_serra_1992}; stochastic morphological
sampling is also established background \citep{sivakumar_goutsias_1996}.
We record the short reduction needed for this particular process.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:semigroup}
For subspaces $H,K\leq V$,
$\E_K(\E_H(A))=\E_{H+K}(A)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Distributing intersections over translation gives
\[
 \E_K(\E_H(A))
 =\bigcap_{k\in K}\bigcap_{h\in H}(A+h+k)
 =\bigcap_{u\in H+K}(A+u).
\]
Repeated factors do not affect an intersection.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(1)]
Because $\langle v\rangle=\{0,v\}$ in characteristic two, one update is
$\E_{\langle v\rangle}$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:semigroup} iterated over the history
proves \eqref{eq:span}.

For a fixed $r$-subspace $H$, histories spanning $H$ are surjective linear
maps $\F_2^t\to H$.  Choosing the images dually as an ordered independent
$r$-tuple in $\F_2^t$ gives \eqref{eq:S}.  There are
$\genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{d}{r}_2$ choices of $H$, which proves
\eqref{eq:rank}.  This finite-field rank enumeration is classical; we do not
claim it as a standalone increment \citep{balakin_1968}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(2)]
If $A$ is fixed by every update, then $A\subseteq A+v$ for every $v$.
Cardinalities force equality, so $A$ is invariant under all translations and
hence is either $\varnothing$ or $V$.

When $H_t=V$, the set $\E_V(A)$ is $V$ for $A=V$ and is empty otherwise.
Sharpness follows from the stronger identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:witness}
 \E_H(V\setminus\{0\})=V\setminus H:
\end{equation}
$x$ survives exactly when $x+h\neq0$ for every $h\in H$, equivalently when
$x\notin H$.  Therefore the witness empties exactly at full span.

Equation \eqref{eq:cdf} is the full-rank probability in \eqref{eq:rank}.
At rank $r<d$, a fresh uniform vector increases the rank with probability
$1-2^{r-d}$.  The independent geometric waiting times for the successive
rank increments have means $(1-2^{r-d})^{-1}$, proving \eqref{eq:mean}.
\end{proof}

\section{Stabilizer-weighted inverse fibres}

The inverse enumeration uses information absent from the rank marginal: the
specific target controls which history spans are possible.

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-span fibre]\label{lem:fixed}
Let $H\leq V$ have dimension $r$.  There exists a source $A$ with
$\E_H(A)=B$ if and only if $H\leq\Stab(B)$.  In that case the source-size
polynomial is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fixed}
 \sum_{A:\,\E_H(A)=B}z^{|A|}
 =z^b\left((1+z)^{2^r}-z^{2^r}\right)^{2^{d-r}-b/2^r}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The set $\E_H(A)$ is the union of precisely those $H$-cosets wholly contained
in $A$.  Hence it is $H$-invariant, proving necessity.  Conversely, if
$H\leq\Stab(B)$, then $B$ is a union of $b/2^r$ full $H$-cosets.  A source
with erosion $B$ must contain all of them.  On each of the remaining
$2^{d-r}-b/2^r$ cosets it may choose any proper subset, independently.  One
coset contributes $(1+z)^{2^r}-z^{2^r}$, which proves \eqref{eq:fixed}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(3)]
For a rank-$r$ history, Lemma~\ref{lem:fixed} permits exactly the subspaces
$H\leq\Stab(B)$.  Their number is
$\genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{s}{r}_2$, and each is generated by $S(t,r)$ ordered
histories.  Summing \eqref{eq:fixed} proves \eqref{eq:fibre}.

When $t=0$, only $r=0$ remains and the expression reduces to $z^b$, as it
must.  If $B=V$, the only source is $V$ and the rank sum gives
$F_t(V;z)=2^{dt}z^{2^d}$.  The empty target and $d=0$ require no exceptional
convention beyond empty products.

At $t=1$, the zero history has rank zero and contributes one at $z=1$.
Every nonzero vector of $\Stab(B)$ spans a rank-one subspace, and each outside
two-point coset has three proper subsets.  There are $2^s-1$ such histories.
If $s\geq1$, then $B$ is a union of two-point orbits, so $b$ is even and the
second line of \eqref{eq:one-step} follows.  If $s=0$, there is no nonzero
stabilizing history and the value is one.  Keeping this branch separate is
essential for odd $b$, where the other displayed exponent would not be an
integer.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(4)]
The map $d\mapsto2^{2^d}$ is injective.  At fixed $(d,b)$ the power of three
in the second line of \eqref{eq:one-step} is constant, while $2^s-1$ is
strictly increasing.  Its positive contribution also separates every
$s\geq1$ from the value one at $s=0$.
\end{proof}

The polynomial contains more than unweighted fibre sizes: its coefficient of
$z^k$ counts all length-$t$ histories and $k$-point sources reaching the
prescribed target.  Thus target shape enters through $\Stab(B)$ even when
target cardinality is held fixed.

\section*{Limitations}
The process uses independent uniform translations of a binary vector space
and begins from an arbitrary labelled subset.  We do not treat biased or
dependent histories, non-elementary finite groups, noisy observations,
unlabelled targets, or asymptotic distributional limits.  The source search
was bounded, so a direct owner under different terminology would reopen the
claim boundary.
