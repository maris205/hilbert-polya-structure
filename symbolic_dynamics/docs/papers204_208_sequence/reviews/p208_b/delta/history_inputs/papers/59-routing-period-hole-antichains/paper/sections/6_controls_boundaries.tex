\section{Exact controls, ownership, and boundaries}
\label{sec:controls}

The proofs above are symbolic.  We supplement them with a deterministic
integer harness designed to falsify their finite consequences.  The harness
performs four groups of checks:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item it exhausts every relation containing the diagonal on at most four
  component labels and every route map allowed by that relation;
  \item it computes exact Boolean powers for the renewal graphs with
  $s=1,\ldots,8$ and verifies primitivity;
  \item it computes integer traces through the Frobenius number plus $2m$ and
  compares their support with $\langle m,m+1\rangle$; and
  \item it checks the hole count, conductor, divisor-ideal condition, selected
  antichain, and three forbidden predicates.
\end{enumerate}

The relation census covers $4{,}165$ diagonal-containing relations and
$160{,}522$ allowed route maps, with no failure of the finite statement that
every allowed route map is onto exactly when the relation has no
off-diagonal arrow.  The renewal checks have zero trace-support mismatch for
all eight sampled parameters.

The frozen reproduction command is
\begin{verbatim}
PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python3 controls/exact_control.py
\end{verbatim}
and its terminal line is \texttt{STATUS PASS}.  The package records the full
integer output and the script hash.

The three hostile mutations are intentionally modest predicate checks.  They
verify that the loop length $m$ is realized by the core, that the hole labels
$1$ and $2$ are comparable, and that duplicating a label creates equality and
hence divisibility between distinct copies.  The program does not construct
mutated subshifts and send them through an independent end-to-end classifier.
It also does not test compact separation, clopen pasting, or the imported
entropy theorem.  Its role is exact finite falsification and reproducibility.

The ownership boundary in \cref{tab:boundary} is part of the theorem's
interpretation.  The 2026 component paper treats general one-dimensional SFT
component posets, including configurations outside the nonwandering pieces
\cite{ceccheriniCoornaertSalo2026}.  Our finite-clopen hypothesis is narrower:
the irreducible pieces exhaust the entire subshift.  That restriction is what
makes every homoclinicity class component-local and every redirect definable
on the whole space.

Because the adjacent component action, the two mixing-core theorems, and the
semigroup arithmetic are all subtracted, the residual package should be
assessed only after specialist checking under alternate routing and coproduct
terminology.

The scope firewall can be summarized as follows.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item No assertion is made for infinitely many accumulating pieces.
  \item No assertion is made for a reducible SFT with heteroclinic or wandering
  configurations outside the displayed irreducible pieces.
  \item The positive-entropy assumption cannot be removed from the entropy
  obstruction $M\not\pito O_p$.  A zero-entropy mixing SFT is a finite orbit
  case and requires a separate finite-system analysis.
  \item The antichain theorem classifies only disjoint periodic-orbit
  extensions of one fixed core, not arbitrary finite factors and not all
  Myhill SFTs.
  \item Search misses do not establish priority.  The finite-clopen iff and
  its antichain specialization remain subject to specialist checking under
  terminology such as coproducts, factor preorders, and Garden-of-Eden
  properties of disjoint unions.
\end{enumerate}

These boundaries also explain why the route theorem does not answer the open
general characterization problem in
\cite[Section~9]{ceccheriniCoornaertSalo2026}.
