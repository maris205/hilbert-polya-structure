\section{Related work and claim boundary}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Symbolic zeta functions and transfer determinants.}
Periodic-point zeta functions for shifts of finite type admit finite
determinant formulas, and transfer operators organize primitive cycles and
their repetitions \citep{bowenlanford1970,ruelle1976}.  SD-C12 retains that
periodic-word logic after reducing each tensor-prime full shift to its atom
loop.  Its new object is not another finite-state zeta formula.  It is a
relative determinant for a countable entropy-ordered atom inventory in a
domain where the two sector determinants do not separately exist.

\paragraph{Signed and graded symbolic constructions.}
Alternating determinants arise from exterior automata in sofic dynamics
\citep{beal1995}.  Signed Lefschetz and zeta structures also occur in the
homological treatment of symbolic systems \citep{deeley2018}, while flip
systems provide zeta functions adapted to a symbolic involution
\citep{kimleepark2003}.  These works establish that signs and gradings are
natural symbolic data.  They do not imply that a sector sign behaves as an
orbit holonomy.  The distinction between a fixed supertrace sign and a phase
raised to the repetition number is central here.

\paragraph{Relative Fredholm determinants.}
For operators differing by trace class, an $I+\cS_1$ quotient has an ordinary
Fredholm determinant with a trace expansion; infinite determinant theory
provides the analytic framework \citep{simon1977}.  The novelty claimed here
is narrower than that general theory.  The entropy-adjacent symbolic pairing
supplies an exact trace-class difference on $\RePart s>0$, and its arithmetic
meaning can be audited orbit by orbit.  No new theorem about arbitrary
Fredholm pairs is asserted.

\paragraph{Direct collision audit.}
Bayart and Kouroupis prove strong universality on
$2/3<\RePart s<1$ for the alternating prime Dirichlet series
$\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^kp_k^{-s}$ \citep{bayartkouroupis2024}.  A 2014
MathOverflow question also writes an Euler product whose prime values are
chosen by even/odd prime index \citep{agno2014mathoverflow}.  The latter
is cited only as evidence that the rank-parity product has appeared in public
discussion, not as a refereed theorem.  These collisions rule out any novelty
claim for alternating prime-rank coefficients themselves.  SD-C12 instead
proves a tensor-atom source theorem, an $I+\cS_1$ relative quotient, a
reflected zero-free completion with strict center curvature, and the scoped
finite-block rigidity result.  No claim is made that this combination is the
first in the literature.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The paper proves a source-specific relative determinant theorem and a general
finite-block cancellation criterion under explicit locality hypotheses.  It
does not claim priority for graded traces, signed symbolic zeta functions, or
relative determinants.  Nor does it identify the resulting product with the
Riemann zeta function.  The prime factors with even entropy rank occur in the
denominator, the reflected product is zero-free in its proved strip, and no
operator lift is supplied.  Those failures are part of the theorem package,
not qualifications deferred to future work.
