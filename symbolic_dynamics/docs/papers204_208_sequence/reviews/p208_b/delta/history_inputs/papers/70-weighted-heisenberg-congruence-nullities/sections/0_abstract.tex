\begin{abstract}
We compute the congruence-fixed spaces of a weighted three-term family of
linear shifts over the discrete Heisenberg group.  Let $\ell$ be an odd prime,
let $p\ne\ell$ be prime, and reduce the group modulo $\ell$.  For nonzero
coefficients $\alpha,\beta,\gamma\in\mathbb F_p$, the fixed-space dimension is
the sum of a cyclotomic torsion-intersection degree and a nonlinear term.  The
nonlinear term is either zero or the full jump $\ell(\ell-1)$, and it occurs
exactly on the Fermat locus
$\alpha^\ell+\beta^\ell+\gamma^\ell=0$.  The proof decomposes the finite
Heisenberg regular representation, evaluates a clock--shift determinant, and
uses a cyclic recurrence to show that every singular nonlinear block has
nullity one.  Unit coefficients recover a characteristic-three jump for the
$1+a+b$ specialization.  Full quotient matrices provide independent finite
checks but are not used in the proof.
\end{abstract}
