\section{Introduction}

Sofic entropy extends entropy theory beyond amenable groups, but its value is
defined relative to a sequence of finite models
\citep{KerrLi2011,KerrLi2013}.  Dependence on that sequence can therefore
encode genuine finite-model geometry.  The strongest qualitative version of
this phenomenon is not new: Airey, Bowen, and Lin constructed a mixing
subshift of finite type with two different positive sofic entropies
\citep{AireyBowenLin2022}.  Their mechanism compares planted and uniform
random hypergraph colourings.

Our starting point is a recent and much more elementary model.  Li and Liu
considered the continuous-alphabet hard-core subshift over the free group and
showed that its sofic mean dimension changes between a parity-compatible
finite-quotient approximation and an approximation selected from uniform
random permutations \citep{LiLiu2026}.  We retain both approximation
templates, but discretize the active coordinate into three colours.  The
finite statistic is no longer the dimension of a free coordinate set.  It is
the entire activity-three independence polynomial, and its exponential rate
is topological sofic entropy.

Write $\F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ and $A=\{0,1,2,3\}$.  With the left shift
$(g x)_h=x_{g^{-1}h}$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:X3}
 X_3=\left\{x\in A^{\F_2}:
 x_g\ne0\Longrightarrow x_{gs}=0
 \text{ for every }g\in\F_2,
 \ s\in\{a,b\}\right\}.
\end{equation}
Since $g$ ranges over the whole group, these rules also impose exclusion in
the $a^{-1}$ and $b^{-1}$ directions.  The symbol $0$ is safe.

For a homomorphism $\sigma:\F_2\to\Sym(V)$, let $G_\sigma$ be the undirected
multigraph with edges
\[
 \{v,\sigma(s)v\},\qquad v\in V,\quad s\in\{a,b\}.
\]
Loops and multiple edges are retained.  If $\Ind(G_\sigma)$ denotes the
independent sets, with looped vertices excluded, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:partition}
 Z_{G_\sigma}(3)
 =\sum_{I\in\Ind(G_\sigma)}3^{|I|}.
\end{equation}
The activity is $3$, one for each nonzero colour.

The main result is the following.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
There are two homomorphic sofic approximations
$\Sigma_{\bip}$ and $\Sigma_{\unif}$ of $\F_2$ for which
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-chain}
 0<\frac15\log4
 \le h_{\Sigma_{\unif}}(X_3)
 \le C_3
 <\log2
 \le h_{\Sigma_{\bip}}(X_3),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:C3}
 C_3=-\log\bigl((1+y_3)(1-y_3)^2\bigr),
 \qquad y_3=3(1-y_3)^4,\qquad y_3\in(0,1).
\end{equation}
Numerically,
\[
 y_3=0.3968993633863\ldots,
 \qquad C_3=0.6770873658955\ldots
 <\log2=0.6931471805599\ldots.
\]
In particular, both entropies are positive and they are different.
\end{theorem}

There are four proof layers.  First, exact $X_3$-models on $G_\sigma$ are
counted by \eqref{eq:partition}.  Topological sofic entropy counts approximate
microstates, however, so this observation alone is insufficient.  We prove a
uniform repair estimate: zeroing at most a $\delta$ fraction of coordinates
repairs every labelling with at most $\delta|V|$ bad generator incidences, and
the Hamming fibres of this repair have size
\[
 \exp\!\left(|V|[H(\delta)+\delta\log3+o(1)]\right).
\]
Consequently, for every homomorphic sofic approximation $\Sigma=(\sigma_n)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:model-formula-intro}
 h_\Sigma(X_3)
 =\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{|V_n|}
  \log Z_{G_{\sigma_n}}(3).
\end{equation}

Second, finite quotients inside the kernel of the parity map
$\F_2\to\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$ yield bipartite action graphs.  One side has
half the vertices and each of its vertices can independently carry zero or
one of the three active colours, giving $4^{|V_n|/2}$ models.

Third, if $\sigma(a)$ and $\sigma(b)$ are independent uniform permutations of
$[n]$, then an exact first-moment calculation and Stirling's formula give the
annealed exponent
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Psi-intro}
 \Psi_3(t)=H(t)+2\left[
 2(1-t)\log(1-t)-(1-2t)\log(1-2t)\right]+t\log3
\end{equation}
on $0\le t\le1/2$.  Its unique maximum is $C_3$.  Finally, Markov's inequality
and asymptotic freeness permit a deterministic diagonal selection.  Deleting
the $o(n)$ looped vertices leaves maximum degree at most four, so a greedy
independent set gives the positive lower bound.

The inequalities in \eqref{eq:main-chain} are deliberately one-sided.  We do
not identify either entropy exactly and do not assert convergence of the
normalized partition functions.  More importantly, the theorem is not
presented as the first mixing SFT with two positive values.  Its residual
contribution is the natural four-symbol nearest-neighbour system, the exact
safe-symbol bridge, and the explicit elementary interval.
