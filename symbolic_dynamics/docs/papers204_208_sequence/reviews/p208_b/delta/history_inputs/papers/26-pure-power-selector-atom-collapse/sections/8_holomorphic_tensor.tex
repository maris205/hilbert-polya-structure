\section{Holomorphic tensor and countable limit}
\label{sec:analytic}

We now attach the selector to the analytic object left by the preceding
paper.  The construction remains inside Symbolic Dynamics and retains the
source code and marker.

\subsection{Inherited logarithmic returns}

For \(n\ge1\), let \(c(n)\) be its Elias gamma code and
\begin{equation}
 \ell(n)=2\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1.
 \label{eq:gamma-length}
\end{equation}
Compose the two affine disk maps

\[
 \psi_0(z)=\frac z2-\frac14,
 \qquad
 \psi_1(z)=\frac z2+\frac14
\]

along \(c(n)\), obtaining a branch \(\phi_n\) with derivative
\(q_n=2^{-\ell(n)}\).  On the Bergman zero-/one-form pair use

\[
 U_{n,0}f=f\circ\phi_n,
 \qquad
 U_{n,1}(g\,dz)=q_n(g\circ\phi_n)\,dz.
\]

The inherited local theorem says that for every completed branch word
\(\alpha\), after its scalar weights are removed,
\begin{equation}
 \Str_{\mathrm{dR}}U_\alpha=1.
 \label{eq:inherited-derham}
\end{equation}
This is a graded ratio of separately trace-class degreewise operators; the
ordinary block determinant remains their product.

\subsection{Finite total grading}

Give the tensor of a color complex and the de Rham pair total parity.  For an
ordered branch word,
\begin{equation}
 \Str_{\mathrm{color}\widehattensor\mathrm{dR}}
 (A_\alpha\otimes U_\alpha)
 =\Str_{\mathrm{color}}(A_\alpha)
  \Str_{\mathrm{dR}}(U_\alpha)
 =\chi_m(\alpha).
 \label{eq:total-supertrace}
\end{equation}
Thus the local tangent denominator and the mixed label coefficient are both
cancelled at every repetition.  Before totalization the complex has four
bidegrees; its determinant is the quotient of honest total-even and total-odd
Fredholm determinants.

\subsection{Countable projector fiber}

Let \(S\subseteq\{2,3,\ldots\}\) be a countable supplied inventory and
\(\cK_S=\ell^2(S)\).  Write \(P_n=|e_n\rangle\langle e_n|\) for the coordinate
projector.  The digit-resolved weight is
\begin{equation}
 b_n(s,u)=u^{\ell(n)}n^{-s},
 \label{eq:digit-weight}
\end{equation}
where \(u\) counts original binary digit steps.  Define degreewise
\begin{equation}
 \cT^k_{S,s,u}
 =\sum_{n\in S}b_n(s,u)P_n\otimes U_{n,k},
 \qquad k=0,1.
 \label{eq:countable-operator}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[countable trace-class selector]
\label{thm:countable-selector}
If
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{n\in S}|u|^{\ell(n)}n^{-\Re s}<\infty,
 \label{eq:l1-domain}
\end{equation}
then both operators in \eqref{eq:countable-operator} are trace class.  For
every \(r\ge1\),
\begin{equation}
 \Str(\cT_{S,s,u})^r
 =\sum_{n\in S}u^{r\ell(n)}n^{-rs},
 \label{eq:countable-power-trace}
\end{equation}
and their graded determinant is
\begin{equation}
 \Dgr(S;s,u,z)=
 \prod_{n\in S}\left(1-zu^{\ell(n)}n^{-s}\right).
 \label{eq:countable-product}
\end{equation}
For \(|u|\le1\), \(\Re s>1\) is a uniform domain for every such inventory.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Common compact containment of the affine branches gives a uniform constant
\(C\) with \(\|U_{n,k}\|_1\le C\).  Because the coordinate ranges are
orthogonal,
\[
 \|\cT^k_{S,s,u}\|_1
 =\sum_{n\in S}|b_n(s,u)|\,\|U_{n,k}\|_1
 \le C\sum_{n\in S}|u|^{\ell(n)}n^{-\Re s}.
\]
This proves degreewise trace class.  Also \(P_nP_j=0\) for \(n\ne j\), so every
mixed word vanishes before analytic tracing.  The surviving pure powers and
\eqref{eq:inherited-derham} give \eqref{eq:countable-power-trace}.  The
trace-log identity yields \eqref{eq:countable-product}.  Finally,
\(|u|^{\ell(n)}\le1\) and the \(p\)-series bound give convergence on
\(\Re s>1\).
\end{proof}

The construction is analytically honest and structurally disjoint.  The
unitary identification
\begin{equation}
 \ell^2(S)\otimes\cH^k
 \cong\bigoplus_{n\in S}\cH^k
 \label{eq:unitary-disjoint}
\end{equation}
turns \eqref{eq:countable-operator} into one private holomorphic block per
supplied label.  Its cohomological survivor is the diagonal space
\(\bigoplus_n\C e_n\) with eigenvalues \(b_n(s,u)\).  The local nonconstant
holomorphic modes and the color-complex invisible sectors have both
retracted.

\subsection{Marker and continuation firewalls}

At digit scale, an \(r\)-fold pure return contributes
\(u^{r\ell(n)}n^{-rs}\), not \(u^rn^{-rs}\).  The variable \(z\) in
\eqref{eq:countable-product} counts completed returns.  Setting \(u=1\) is
legal after first-return induction, or after declaring whole codewords to be
the return alphabet; it is not an identity at the original digit scale.

At \(u=1\), the constant zero-form mode has absolute eigenvalue \(n^{-\Re s}\).
For the full integers, and for primes at the corresponding boundary, this
prevents continuation of the same trace-class family through the absolute
summability line.  Meromorphic continuation of a scalar product, if obtained
elsewhere, would not by itself continue the operator family in
\eqref{eq:countable-operator}.  SD-C28 claims only the honest A2 domain.
