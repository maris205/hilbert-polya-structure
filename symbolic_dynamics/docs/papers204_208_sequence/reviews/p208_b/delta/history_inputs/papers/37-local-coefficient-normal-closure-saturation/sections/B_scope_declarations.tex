The no-go applies only to finite-rank inverse-edge mechanisms under the
complete mixed-relation obligation on this affine Cayley shift.  It does not
rule out matrix-weighted graph zeta in general, an infinite-dimensional trace,
a groupoid cocycle, or an independently defined loop class on another object.

Six substitutions receive no credit: a first trace for a complete factor; a
nilpotent endomorphism for invertible transport; a graded ratio for a positive
determinant; $z=1$ for equality of free germs; a quotient, first return, or
boundary support for the full source; or a representation-discovered residue
for a source-canonical arithmetic class.
