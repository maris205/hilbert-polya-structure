\section{Conclusion}

Weighted three-term Heisenberg shifts have an exact finite-quotient phase
diagram.  Character blocks contribute the cyclotomic intersection degree
$\Dcycl$, while every nonlinear block is controlled by the same Fermat
polynomial $\alpha^\ell+\beta^\ell+\gamma^\ell$.  The regular multiplicities
turn its vanishing into a jump of $\ell(\ell-1)$.  The unit-coefficient
characteristic-three phenomenon is therefore one specialization of a
coefficient-space theorem rather than an isolated determinant coincidence.

The excluded modular case $p=\ell$ is the next concrete problem.  There the
failure of semisimplicity should replace the two-stratum ledger by a radical
filtration, so the present formula identifies both the baseline and the exact
point at which new representation-theoretic behavior must enter.
