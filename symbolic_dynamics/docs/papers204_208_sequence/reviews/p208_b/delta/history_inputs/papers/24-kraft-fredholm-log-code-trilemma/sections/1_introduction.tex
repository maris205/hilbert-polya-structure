\section{Introduction}

The tensor monoid of finite full shifts contains a canonical arithmetic
skeleton:
\[
  \F_m\boxtimes\F_n\cong\F_{mn},
  \qquad h_{\mathrm{top}}(\F_n)=\log n.
\]
Its tensor-indecomposable objects are the $\F_p$.  Earlier papers in this
program used those atoms as countably many isolated primitive loops and
obtained
\[
  \det(I-zD_s)=\prod_{p\in\Pset}(1-zp^{-s}),
  \qquad \Ree s>1.
\]
That identity is exact but structurally sparse: the recurrent core already
contains one state per atom.  The present question is whether the same
ledger can be compressed into a finite visible symbolic grammar of
$O(\log p)$ code length without forfeiting the whole Fredholm operator.

Coding and zeta-function interactions are classical
\citep{keller1991,bealperrin2005,hong2011,bealperrinrestivo2024}, as are
finite-state graph determinants \citep{bowenlanford1970} and zeta functions
of formal languages \citep{berstelreutenauer1990}.  Our contribution is not
a new Kraft inequality or a general classification of possible period sets.
It is the following source-locked incompatibility.

\begin{theorem}[Kraft--Fredholm trilemma]\label{thm:trilemma}
On the natural vertex space $\ell^2(V)$, the following four requirements
cannot hold simultaneously:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item a stationary directed graph with a finite local code separating the
        intended rational-prime cycles;
  \item a positive scalar primitive ledger containing exactly one primitive
        orbit $\gamma_p$ for every prime and no other primitive orbit;
  \item the additive total roof $\roof(\gamma_p)=\log p$;
  \item compactness of the whole one-step weighted adjacency for some real
        $\sigma>0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The proof has two independent pieces.  Literal positivity forces different
prime cycles into disjoint recurrent components.  Finite visible separation
then forces $\ell(p)\ge c\log p$ along infinitely many primes.  Since the
total roof of such a cycle is only $\log p$, one edge retains a uniformly
large weight; distinct cycle vertices give a weakly null sequence on which
the adjacency does not tend to zero.

The theorem is intentionally attached to the uninduced graph-coordinate
operator.  Holomorphic transfer spaces can be nuclear even when a vertex
adjacency is not \citep{mayer1990}; signed or graded cancellation can remove
cycles that positivity cannot.  Those are successor obligations, not
counterexamples to the theorem.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
We prove cycle-separation, coding, compactness, roof-rank, and marker
firewalls; audit prefix tries, renewal and factorization grammars, and
finite-prefix S-adic controls; and identify the only clean positive escape
as the countable atom diagonal.  The exact suite contains 35 tests and no
zero data or fitted spectral targets.
