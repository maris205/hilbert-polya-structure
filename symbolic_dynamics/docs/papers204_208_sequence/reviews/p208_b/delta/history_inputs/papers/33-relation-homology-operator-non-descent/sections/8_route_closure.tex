The adversarial stop is stronger than a single failed experiment.  Four
independent blocking conditions occur:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item relation homology is nonzero on every tested prime power and mixed
  composite;
  \item the universal cusp word survives all frozen character and
  supercharacter controls;
  \item diamond filling erases cross linkage rather than selecting a prime
  family;
  \item the original graph-step operator fails quotient invariance.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore the semiring-residue family is not merely paused.  It is closed for
this research program: continuing with $P^1(\ZZ/n\ZZ)$ blocks, fixed
$C_2*C_3$ presentations, static field criteria, or Manin-style quotients would
replay one of the same obstructions.
