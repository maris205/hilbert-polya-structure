\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Uniform strong finite energy converts a whole-site pivot bijection into one
factor of $\eta$ per requested output site.  Nonzero scalar affine rules
provide those pivots through Laurent-domain row independence, while a
uniquely exposed permutive corner provides them through a finite triangular
order.  Both mechanisms work at every time and for arbitrary finite shapes,
and the identity rule shows that the resulting bound is numerically exact.

The two firewalls identify where this proof architecture stops.  Scalar
coordinate pivots distributed across several input sites do not support a
whole-site vector estimate with exponent $|S|$, even for a reversible rule.
Surjectivity also does not by itself supply an exposed permutive coordinate.
Consequently, neither vector alphabets under the same normalization nor
arbitrary surjective cellular automata are covered by the theorems above.
