\section{Digit covariance details}
\label{app:digit}

We record the elementary inverse and boundary calculation behind
\cref{prop:digit-spectrum}.  Write \(K_{ij}=\min(i,j)\) and let \(L\) have
diagonal \(2,\ldots,2,1\) and first off-diagonals \(-1\).  For an interior
column \(2\le j\le b-1\),
\[
  (KL)_{ij}
  =
  -K_{i,j-1}+2K_{ij}-K_{i,j+1}
  =
  \one_{\{i=j\}}.
\]
The same direct difference at \(j=1\) uses \(K_{i,0}=0\), and the terminal
column gives \(-K_{i,b-1}+K_{i,b}=\one_{\{i=b\}}\).  Thus \(L=K^{-1}\).

For \(Lv=\lambda v\), the interior recurrence and \(v_0=0\) give
\[
  v_i=\sin(i\theta),
  \qquad
  \lambda=2-2\cos\theta.
\]
The final equation \(-v_{b-1}+v_b=\lambda v_b\) agrees with the interior
recurrence exactly when \(v_{b+1}=v_b\).  Therefore
\[
  0=\sin((b+1)\theta)-\sin(b\theta)
   =2\cos(\frac{(2b+1)\theta}{2})
      \sin(\frac{\theta}{2}).
\]
The nonzero modes are
\(\theta_j=(2j-1)\pi/(2b+1)\), \(1\le j\le b\).
Since the eigenvalues of \(K\) are
\([4\sin^2(\theta_j/2)]^{-1}\), their positive square roots give
\cref{eq:digit-spectrum}.

Finally, column reversal changes \(C_b\) into a triangular matrix with
ones on its diagonal.  Hence
\[
  \prod_{j=1}^{b}s_j(C_b)=|\det C_b|=1.
\]
The strict inequality \(\sum_js_j(C_b)>b\) follows from strict
arithmetic--geometric mean because
\(\sum_js_j(C_b)^2=b(b+1)/2>b\), so the singular values cannot all equal
one.
