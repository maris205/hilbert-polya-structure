\begin{abstract}
Let $E=\F_2^d\setminus\{0\}$, and repeatedly toggle the points in the
kernel of a uniformly sampled nonzero linear form.  We determine this chain
on the full $2^{2^d-1}$-state subset carrier.  Its communicating classes are
$2^{2^d-d-2}$ equal cosets, and every class is simple random walk on the
crown graph $K_{2^d,2^d}$ with one perfect matching removed.  Fourier
inversion gives the exact number of ordered histories between every pair of
states at every time.  The resulting law converges within its
parity-compatible half at exact total-variation distance
$1/(2^d(2^d-1)^{t-1})$; it does not mix in ordinary total variation because
the chain has period two.  On the whole carrier the only eigenvalues are
$1,-1,\pm(2^d-1)^{-1}$, with all multiplicities explicit and no Jordan
blocks.  Simplex-code, incidence-design, Cayley/Fourier, and crown-graph
facts are treated as background.  The residual claim remains
\textsc{owner\_amber}; external circulation is \textsc{hold\_external}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model, background boundary, and theorem}

Fix $d\ge2$, put $V=\F_2^d$, $E=V\setminus\{0\}$, and write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:parameters}
             q=2^d,\qquad N=q-1,\qquad m=|E|=N.
\end{equation}
We identify a subset of $E$ with its incidence word in $\F_2^E$; addition
of words is symmetric difference.  At each epoch choose, independently and
uniformly, a nonzero form $\ell\in V^*\setminus\{0\}$ and update
\begin{equation}\label{eq:update}
 A\longmapsto A+ h_\ell,
 \qquad h_\ell=\mathbf 1_{H_\ell},\qquad
 H_\ell=\{x\in E:\ell(x)=0\}.
\end{equation}
Over $\F_2$, the nonzero forms index the projective hyperplanes without a
scalar ambiguity.  The zero form is not sampled.  In particular,
$|H_\ell|=2^{d-1}-1$ is odd.

Projective systems and simplex codes are standard coding-theoretic objects
\cite{KwiatkowskiPankovPasini2018}; binary codes, symmetric-difference
designs, and projective hyperplane sections also have a direct established
connection \cite{Tonchev1993}.  Walks involving generating sets of Abelian
groups provide broader Markov-chain context \cite{DiaconisSaloffCoste1996}.
Brown's hyperplane-chamber walks \cite{Brown2000} are a useful terminology
control but have a different state space and update from \eqref{eq:update}.
We assign simplex-code and incidence language, symmetric difference,
Cayley-graph reduction, finite-abelian character calculus, the named crown
graph, and its elementary spectrum no contribution credit.  The calculations
below are included to make the literal conjunction self-contained.

For $a\in V^*$ define the evaluation word
\begin{equation}\label{eq:code}
 c_a=(a(x))_{x\in E},\qquad
 \cC=\{c_a:a\in V^*\},\qquad
 \cW=\langle\one,\cC\rangle\le \F_2^E,
\end{equation}
where $\one$ is the all-one word.  Let $P$ be the transition operator of
\eqref{eq:update} and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:K}
                  K=2^{m-d-1}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[complete finite atlas]\label{thm:main}
The following statements hold for every $d\ge2$.
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item The closed communicating classes are precisely the $K$ cosets of
$\cW$.  Each has $2q$ states, all states are recurrent, and the support on
each class is the crown graph $K_{q,q}$ with one perfect matching deleted.
The chain is simple random walk on that graph and has period two.

\item For $t\ge0$ and $A,B\subseteq E$, the endpoint lies in the
parity-compatible phase exactly when there is a unique $L\in V^*$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:endpoint-condition}
             A+B=(t\bmod2)\one+c_L.
\end{equation}
When this condition holds, the number of ordered form histories is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:history-count}
 a_t(L)=\begin{cases}
 q^{-1}\bigl(N^t+N(-1)^t\bigr),&L=0,\\
 q^{-1}\bigl(N^t-(-1)^t\bigr),&L\ne0,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad P^t(A,B)=\dfrac{a_t(L)}{N^t}.
\end{equation}
A history exists exactly when \eqref{eq:endpoint-condition} holds and
$a_t(L)>0$: this requires $L=0$ at $t=0$, $L\ne0$ at $t=1$, and permits
every $L$ at $t\ge2$.  Outside \eqref{eq:endpoint-condition},
$P^t(A,B)=0$.

\item Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phase}
 \Phi_t(A)=A+(t\bmod2)\one+\cC
\end{equation}
and let $U_{\Phi_t(A)}$ be uniform on these $q$ states.  For $t\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phase-tv}
 \left\|P^t(A,\cdot)-U_{\Phi_t(A)}\right\|_{\TV}
       =\frac{1}{qN^{t-1}}.
\end{equation}
If $\pi_{A+\cW}$ is uniform on the full communicating class, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ordinary-tv}
 \left\|P^t(A,\cdot)-\pi_{A+\cW}\right\|_{\TV}
 =\begin{cases}
 \frac12+\frac{1}{2q},&t=1,\\[1mm]
 \frac12,&t\ge2.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Thus \eqref{eq:phase-tv} is parity-phase convergence, not ordinary mixing.

\item On the entire $2^m$-state carrier, the eigenvalues and algebraic
multiplicities are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:spectrum-table}
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\lambda&1&-1&1/N&-1/N\\ \hline
\operatorname{mult}(\lambda)&K&K&NK&NK.
\end{array}
\end{equation}
Boolean characters give a complete eigenbasis.  Hence there are no other
eigenvalues and no nontrivial Jordan blocks.

\item Within this family, the degree $N$ of one unlabelled component
recovers $d=\log_2(N+1)$.  If the total state count is also supplied, then
it recovers $K$ as the number of components.  This is not a characterization
among arbitrary regular bipartite graphs.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\section{Code coordinates and crown components}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:code}
The map $a\mapsto c_a$ is injective and linear, $\one\notin\cC$, and
$\dim\cW=d+1$.  Moreover, the masks $h_\ell$, $\ell\ne0$, generate $\cW$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Linearity is coordinatewise.  If $a\ne0$, some nonzero $x$ satisfies
$a(x)=1$, so $c_a\ne0$ and the evaluation map is injective.  A nonzero form
has a kernel of dimension $d-1\ge1$; hence it cannot equal one on every
nonzero vector.  Thus $\one\notin\cC$, proving $\dim\cW=d+1$.

For every nonzero $\ell$ and $x\in E$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mask-code}
          h_\ell(x)=1+\ell(x),\qquad h_\ell=\one+c_\ell.
\end{equation}
Given $u\ne0$, choose $a\notin\{0,u\}$, which is possible because $d\ge2$,
and put $b=a+u$.  Then $a,b$ are nonzero and
$h_a+h_b=c_{a+b}=c_u$.  Finally $\one=h_u+c_u$, so the masks generate both
$\cC$ and $\one$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(i)]
A walk on an additive group remains in the coset of the subgroup generated
by its increments.  Lemma~\ref{lem:code} identifies that subgroup as
$\cW$, so its cosets are exactly the closed irreducible classes.  Their size
is $2^{d+1}=2q$, and \eqref{eq:K} gives their number.  Finite closed
irreducible classes contain only recurrent states.

Fix a coset representative $R$.  The unique code coordinates give a
bijection
\begin{equation}\label{eq:crown-coordinates}
 R+\epsilon\one+c_a\longleftrightarrow(\epsilon,a),
 \qquad (\epsilon,a)\in\F_2\times V^*.
\end{equation}
By \eqref{eq:mask-code}, a step indexed by $\ell\ne0$ sends
$(\epsilon,a)$ to $(\epsilon+1,a+\ell)$.  It therefore joins
$(0,a)$ to every $(1,b)$ except $b=a$, exactly the crown graph
$K_{q,q}$ minus its diagonal matching.  Every edge changes subset parity
because every mask has odd weight.  Thus all returns have even length, while
repeating any mask gives a two-step return.  The period is exactly two.
\end{proof}

\section{Ordered histories and the two TV comparisons}

After the ordered choices $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_t$, linearity in
\eqref{eq:mask-code} gives the total increment
\begin{equation}\label{eq:increment}
 \sum_{r=1}^t h_{\ell_r}=(t\bmod2)\one+c_L,
 \qquad L=\sum_{r=1}^t\ell_r.
\end{equation}
Injectivity of $a\mapsto c_a$ proves the endpoint condition and uniqueness
in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(ii).

\begin{proof}[Proof of \eqref{eq:history-count}]
The additive characters of $V^*$ give the exact number of ordered nonzero
summands with sum $L$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fourier-count}
 a_t(L)=\frac1q\sum_{y\in V^{**}}(-1)^{y(L)}
       \left(\sum_{\ell\ne0}(-1)^{y(\ell)}\right)^t.
\end{equation}
The inner sum is $N$ for the trivial character and $-1$ otherwise.  The sum
of all nontrivial characters at $L$ is $N$ when $L=0$ and $-1$ otherwise.
This proves \eqref{eq:history-count}, including $t=0$.  Division by the
$N^t$ equiprobable histories proves the transition formula.  Directly in
\eqref{eq:history-count}, $a_0(L)$ is positive only for $L=0$,
$a_1(L)$ is positive only for $L\ne0$, and both displayed values are positive
for every $L$ when $t\ge2$.  These are precisely the asserted support cases.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(iii)]
On the $q$-point set \eqref{eq:phase}, the probability deviations from
$1/q$ are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deviations}
 p_t(0)-\frac1q=\frac{(-1)^t}{qN^{t-1}},\qquad
 p_t(L)-\frac1q=-\frac{(-1)^t}{qN^t}\quad(L\ne0).
\end{equation}
Half the sum of their absolute values is $1/(qN^{t-1})$, proving
\eqref{eq:phase-tv}.

The connected regular crown walk has the uniform stationary law on all
$2q$ component vertices.  At $t=1$, one point in the occupied parity half
has mass zero and the other $N$ points have mass $1/N$; direct subtraction
from $1/(2q)$ gives the first line of \eqref{eq:ordinary-tv}.  For $t\ge2$,
\eqref{eq:deviations} and $N\ge3$ show that every probability in the
occupied half exceeds $1/(2q)$.  That half contributes total absolute
deviation $1/2$, and the empty half contributes another $1/2$.  Dividing by
two proves the second line.  In particular, the phase limit must not be
reported as ordinary mixing.
\end{proof}

\section{Full-carrier spectrum and reconstruction}

For $S\subseteq E$, define the Boolean character
\begin{equation}\label{eq:boolean-character}
 \chi_S(A)=(-1)^{|S\cap A|},\qquad
 \sigma(S)=\sum_{x\in S}x\in V.
\end{equation}
These $2^m$ characters form an orthogonal basis on $\F_2^E$.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(iv)]
Convolution by the uniform mask measure makes $\chi_S$ an eigenvector with
eigenvalue
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eigen-character}
 \lambda_S=\frac1N\sum_{\ell\ne0}(-1)^{|S\cap H_\ell|}.
\end{equation}
Modulo two,
$|S\cap H_\ell|=|S|+\ell(\sigma(S))$.  Character orthogonality therefore
gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eigen-cases}
 \lambda_S=\begin{cases}
 (-1)^{|S|},&\sigma(S)=0,\\
 (-1)^{|S|+1}/N,&\sigma(S)\ne0.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}

It remains to count the four cases without hiding a rank assumption.  The
linear map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:parity-sum-map}
 \F_2^E\longrightarrow\F_2\oplus V,\qquad
 S\longmapsto (|S|\bmod2,\sigma(S))
\end{equation}
has columns $(1,x)$ for $x\in E$.  For any $u\ne0$, choose
$x\notin\{0,u\}$; then $(1,x)+(1,x+u)=(0,u)$.  These vectors span
$\{0\}\oplus V$, and $(1,x)+(0,x)=(1,0)$ supplies the remaining direction.
The map is surjective, so every fibre has size
$2^{m-d-1}=K$.  Sorting \eqref{eq:eigen-cases} by parity and by whether
$\sigma(S)$ vanishes proves \eqref{eq:spectrum-table}.  The full character
basis also proves diagonalizability, so no Jordan block is concealed by the
repeated multiplicities.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(v)]
The crown support has degree $N$ because the $N$ nonzero forms give distinct
masks.  Hence $N+1=2^d$ recovers $d$.  A component has $2(N+1)=2q$ states;
dividing the supplied total state count by this number recovers $K$.  The
argument uses the promised family and makes no inverse claim for an arbitrary
graph with the same degree and order.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact controls, exclusions, and status}

The paper-local author-side verifier uses only integer and rational arithmetic
and imports no scouting or prior-paper code.  It checks $1{,}095{,}999$ exact
assertions: literal carriers, cosets, neighborhoods, and Boolean characters
through $d=4$; ordered histories through five steps in those boxes; and the
algebraic formulas through $d=8$ and sixteen steps.  This is falsification
pressure, not a proof or an owner-search instrument.

At $d=1$, the sole projective hyperplane is empty, so the chain is the
identity rather than a crown walk.  Sampling the zero form for $d\ge2$ would
add an empty mask; toggling hyperplane complements would replace the
generators $\one+c_\ell$ by $c_\ell$.  Neither variant is covered.  No claim
is made for nonbinary spaces.

The remaining claim is deliberately owner-thin: the literal process plus
the disjoint-crown conjugacy, exact labelled history kernel, parity-phase TV,
and full-carrier multiplicity lift.  Generic Fourier and the finite-group
proof shell already appear in the internal P145 line and receive zero
separation credit.  A bounded literature non-hit is not a novelty result.
The author-side gate is \textsc{owner\_amber}, and the external lifecycle is
\textsc{hold\_external}.
