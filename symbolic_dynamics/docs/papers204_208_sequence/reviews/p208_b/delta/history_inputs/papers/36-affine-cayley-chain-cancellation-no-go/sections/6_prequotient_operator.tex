\section{The prequotient determinant still sees the relation}
\label{sec:operator}

The formal symmetrization has vertex degree at most four.  Each oriented edge
has at most three nonbacktracking successors and predecessors, so the Schur
test gives
\begin{equation}
\|H_r\|\le3.
\label{eq:H-bound}
\end{equation}
At most four oriented edges originate at a vertex.  Hence
\begin{equation}
\Tr(\Dtheta)
\le4\sum_{b,k\ge0}\theta^{1+b+k}
=\frac{4\theta}{(1-\theta)^2}<\infty.
\label{eq:D-trace}
\end{equation}
Thus $\Dtheta$ is trace class.  The trace class is a two-sided ideal, so
$\Ttheta=\Dtheta H_r\Dtheta$ is trace class and owns the ordinary Fredholm
expansion in \cref{eq:fredholm-log}.  This is the weighted infinite-graph
ownership regime, not a formal determinant assigned to a noncompact adjacency
\citep{Deitmar2015}.

Follow the relation polygon based at $(0,0)$:
\[
(0,0)\xrightarrow{v}(0,1)\xrightarrow{u}(r,1)
\xrightarrow{\bar v}(r,0)\xrightarrow{\bar u^r}(0,0).
\]
The sum of $1+b+k$ over its oriented-edge origins is
\begin{equation}
1+2+(r+2)+\sum_{j=1}^r(j+1)
=\frac{r(r+1)}2+2r+5=S_r.
\label{eq:cycle-exponent}
\end{equation}
A closed cycle in $DHD$ has the square of the product of its diagonal edge
weights.  Its weight is therefore $\theta^{2S_r}$, and each of its $r+3$
cyclic starts supplies one diagonal term.  All entries are nonnegative, which
proves \cref{eq:positive-trace}.

For the composite baseline $r=4$ and $\theta=1/2$,
\[
S_4=23,
\qquad
\theta^{2S_4}=2^{-46}
=\frac{1}{70368744177664},
\qquad
\Tr(T_{4,1/2}^7)\ge7\cdot2^{-46}>0.
\]
Immediate backtracks have already been forbidden.  The surviving coefficient
is the affine relation recurrence that the cell quotient removes.

\paragraph{Finite-trace group control.}
The enveloping group
\[
G_r=\Z[1/r]\rtimes_r\Z
=\langle u,v\mid vuv^{-1}=u^r\rangle
\]
belongs to the Baumslag--Solitar family \citep{BaumslagSolitar1962}.  With
\[
A_r=\tfrac14(\lambda_u+\lambda_{u^{-1}}+
\lambda_v+\lambda_{v^{-1}})
\]
and the canonical group von Neumann trace $\tau$, one has
\begin{equation}
\tau(A_r^n)=\frac{c_r(n)}{4^n},
\label{eq:group-count}
\end{equation}
where $c_r(n)$ counts identity words.  For real $|z|<1$,
\begin{equation}
\log\Delta_r(z)
=-\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{c_r(n)}{n4^n}z^n
\label{eq:FK-log}
\end{equation}
is a Fuglede--Kadison determinant logarithm
\citep{FugledeKadison1952}.  This honest finite-trace control also counts the
relator; it is not called the ordinary Fredholm determinant above and is not
substituted for the semigroup source.
