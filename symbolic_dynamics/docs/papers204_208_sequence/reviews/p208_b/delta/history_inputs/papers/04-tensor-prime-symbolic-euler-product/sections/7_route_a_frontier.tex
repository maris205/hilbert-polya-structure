\section{Route-A result and the critical-strip frontier}

The frozen evaluator returns
\[
\begin{split}

\texttt{A1\_PASS\_ANALYTIC},\\
&\texttt{A2\_ANALYTIC\_DETERMINANT},
\texttt{A3\_PARTIAL\_ANALYTIC\_STRUCTURE},

The overall status is \texttt{ROUTE\_A\_ANALYTIC\_CANDIDATE}.  A0 is carried
by tensor indecomposability and entropy, A1 by the atom-loop suspension, and
A2 by \cref{thm:fredholm}.  No coordinates are borrowed from another
candidate.

A3 is only partial.  The scalar identity $Z_\otimes=\zeta$ has a unique known
meromorphic continuation, but the present symbolic operator does not produce
that continuation.  It also lacks the archimedean Gamma factor, pole
removal, $s\leftrightarrow1-s$ duality, Riemann--von Mangoldt zero count, and
a natural Weil Hermitian compression.

\begin{theorem}[Ungraded trace-class continuation obstruction]
\label{thm:continuation-obstruction}
Let $U\subset\mathbb C$ be a connected domain containing a nonempty open set
in $\RePart s>1$ and a nontrivial zero $\rho$ of $\zeta$.  There is no
holomorphic trace-class family $\widetilde{\cL}_s$ on a fixed Hilbert space
such that $\widetilde{\cL}_s=\cL_s$ on that open set and
$\det(I-\widetilde{\cL}_s)$ continues the same determinant along $U$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Fredholm determinant of a holomorphic trace-class family is holomorphic.
On the initial open set it equals $1/\zeta(s)$ by
\cref{thm:fredholm}.  Uniqueness of analytic continuation forces equality
with the meromorphic continuation of $1/\zeta$ wherever both are defined in
$U$.  The latter has a pole at $\rho$, contradicting holomorphy.
\end{proof}

This theorem does not rule out a quotient of determinants, a meromorphic
operator family, or a graded transfer complex.  It says that the current
ungraded trace-class family cannot simply be pushed across the critical
strip.  Nor does the naive double $\cL_s\oplus\cL_{1-s}$ help: the first block
is trace class for $\RePart s>1$, the second for $\RePart s<0$, so there is no
common nuclear strip.

\begin{conjecture}[Symbolic graded completion target]
\label{conj:graded-completion}
There exists a functorially defined $\mathbb Z/2$-graded symbolic transfer
complex built from the full-shift tensor product, entropy norm, and an
intrinsic past--future duality, such that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item its positive prime-power trace reduces to the SD-C07 ledger in the
  Euler half-plane;
  \item mixed-atom traces cancel by a derived, pre-registered grading;
  \item its superdeterminant or determinant quotient has a controlled
  meromorphic continuation and a dynamical $s\leftrightarrow1-s$ duality;
  \item the archimedean normalization is generated by the same symbolic
  construction rather than multiplied in afterward.
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjecture}

This is a research target, not evidence for RH.  Random parity, a copied
Gamma factor, or signs chosen after inspecting zeros would falsify its source
lock.  Any operator-algebraic or Hamiltonian realization remains a
\texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE}; Route B is not invoked.
