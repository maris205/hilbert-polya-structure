\section{The Bost--Connes trace/determinant firewall}
\label{sec:bc-firewall}

In the standard Bost--Connes representation on $\ell^2(\N^{\times})$,
\[
H\varepsilon_n=(\log n)\varepsilon_n,
\qquad \Dbeta=e^{-\beta H}.
\]
For $\beta>1$, $\Dbeta$ is positive trace class and
\begin{equation}
\Tr(\Dbeta)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-\beta}=\zeta(\beta).
\label{eq:partition-trace}
\end{equation}
This celebrated identity is genuine; the firewall concerns its ownership.

\begin{theorem}[Trace coefficient versus determinant]
\label{thm:bc-determinant}
For $\beta>1$ and $|z|<1$,
\begin{align}
\det(I-z\Dbeta)
 &=\prod_{n\ge1}(1-zn^{-\beta}),
\label{eq:diagonal-det}\\
-\log\det(I-z\Dbeta)
 &=\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{z^m}{m}\zeta(m\beta).
\label{eq:diagonal-log}
\end{align}
Thus $\zeta(\beta)$ is the coefficient of $z$ in the connected logarithm,
not the determinant or its reciprocal.  At $z=1$ the determinant vanishes
because $1$ is an eigenvalue, whereas $\zeta(\beta)$ is finite.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The eigenvalues $n^{-\beta}$ are summable.  The trace-class spectral product
gives \cref{eq:diagonal-det}; absolute expansion of
$-\log(1-zn^{-\beta})$ and Tonelli summation give
\cref{eq:diagonal-log}.
\end{proof}

The comparison with the graph-step operators is now exact.  $\Dbeta$ is
compact and trace class; $\Aplus$, $\Asym$, and $B$ are noncompact.  Compactness
is invariant under unitary equivalence and bounded invertible similarity, so
these cannot be the same operator in disguise.

The original bosonic construction gives another honest determinant.  On the
prime-indexed one-particle space $\ell^2(\Primes)$, put
\[
\Kbeta\varepsilon_p=p^{-\beta}\varepsilon_p.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
\det(I-z\Kbeta)^{-1}
=\prod_{p\in\Primes}(1-zp^{-\beta})^{-1}
=\sum_{n\ge1}z^{\Omega(n)}n^{-\beta}.
\label{eq:bosonic-det}
\end{equation}
At $z=1$, this is $\zeta(\beta)$.  \Cref{eq:bosonic-det} simultaneously
shows why it is not a discovery
mechanism here: the basis was already indexed by primes, and $z$ counts
boson number $\Omega(n)$ rather than one original affine generator step.

On the full affine space $\ell^2(P)$, the apparently analogous Hamiltonian
$H\delta_{(b,a)}=(\log a)\delta_{(b,a)}$ has infinite multiplicity in the
additive coordinate $b$.  Hence $e^{-\beta H}$ is never compact or trace
class there.  The finite multiplicities in KMS/GNS constructions are a
representation change, not a same-space trace theorem.
