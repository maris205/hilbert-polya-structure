\section{Scope, comparison, and computational controls}

\subsection{What the theorem does and does not say}
The exact finite-group statement, \cref{thm:finite-group-intro}, converts a
geometric separation problem into a local inverse-window formula.  For the
rank-one family, the spacer/defect coding solves that separation problem
exactly and gives \eqref{eq:main-bound-intro}.  The argument does not say that every
radius-one conjugacy on every bounded rank-one family has a large inverse.
It gives one fixed radius-one rule and one explicit family for which no
coarse uniform bound is possible.

The factor theorem of Gao and Ziegler~\cite{GaoZiegler2020} supplies important
qualitative context: subshift factors of rank-one subshifts are finite or
isomorphic to the source.  Our injectivity proof is independent and specific
to the derivative; its residual contribution is the exact inverse-window
criterion and the quantitative family.  The construction is also compatible
with the finite-spacer-rank framework of Gao et al.~\cite{GaoEtAl2025}.
Durand--Leroy's computable bounds~\cite{DurandLeroy2022} concern a fixed pair
of minimal substitution systems and allow a shift power; they do not provide
the coarse-parameter uniformity ruled out here.

\subsection{Deterministic control}
The companion script \texttt{code/verify\_rank\_one\_radius.py} performs the
following finite checks.
\begin{itemize}
\item For cyclic groups of orders $2$ through $5$, and separately for the
  noncommutative group $S_3$, it enumerates short source words and verifies
  that every derivative fibre is exactly a left-translation orbit.
\item For $1\leq N\leq8$, it constructs $v_N,v_{N+1},v_{N+2}$, checks the
  alternating formula, locates $W$ and $\comp(W)$ at the positions used in
  \cref{lem:witness}, verifies the spacer recursion and $101$ avoidance, and
  checks the exact separation/radius arithmetic.
\item For $1\leq N\leq8$, a collision-free integer-window enumeration at
  finite tail depth verifies that the longest complementary overlap is
  $3|v_N|+1$ and that the next length is disjoint.
\item It checks the length, one-count, and discrepancy recurrences as exact
  integer or rational identities and reproduces the density and exact-radius
  formulas.
\end{itemize}
The frozen output terminates in \texttt{ALL CHECKS PASS}.  These enumerations
are regression evidence only.  Compactness, minimality, unique ergodicity,
and all asymptotic statements are proved in the text and do not depend on the
finite cutoff.
