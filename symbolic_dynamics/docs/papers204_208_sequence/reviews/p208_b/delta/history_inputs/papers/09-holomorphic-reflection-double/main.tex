\textbf{Research status.} This note defines SD-C11 and tests the most direct
holomorphic reflected double of SD-C10 without using an adjoint.  The
construction has a common Schatten strip and exact $s\leftrightarrow1-s$
symmetry, but every identity-visible closed word balances the two layers and
therefore loses the vertical parameter.  Couplings that restore height
dependence either remain trace-invisible or introduce mixed arithmetic
lengths.  No unified divisor or Route-B candidate is obtained.
