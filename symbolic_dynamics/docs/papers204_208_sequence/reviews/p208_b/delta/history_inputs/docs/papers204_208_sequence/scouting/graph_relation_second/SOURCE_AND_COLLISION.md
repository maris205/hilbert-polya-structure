## Internal collision scope

- `papers/112-tournament-score-upset-reversal/main.tex`: actual definition,
  quadratic energy proof and start of score-block factorization were read.
  P112 reorients from higher to lower outdegree; DGO does the opposite.
  Its two-vertex action is already different. All score-feedback terminology
  is deducted; the symmetric threshold adapter, not a false P112 identity,
  closes DGO.
- `papers/118-synchronous-mex-multipartite-dynamics/main.tex` and this batch's
  `word_local/GM_GATE/`: actual mex and avoidance-palette proofs had been read
  during the prior independent assignment. CCI is not a mex rule. Its period
  $q\ge3$ precludes a surjective autonomous factor from a period-at-most-two
  mex system onto the whole corresponding CCI system. This is a concrete
  recurrence obstruction, not a proof that every possible embedding fails.
- `papers/202-ternary-ordered-reset/main.tex`: the actual map, inverse theorem
  and initial run factor were read. It uses an ordered right-neighbour
  increment/reset rule, not equality-triggered graph activation; it has no
  fixed state, whereas CCI on many graphs has proper fixed colourings.
  Its target-local independent $01$ choices do not give CCI's coupled
  total-cover/predecessor constraints. Oscillator and finite-clock language
  are still deducted.
- `docs/papers197_201_sequence/scouting/root_graph_bellman/THEOREM_SPIKE.md`:
  the complete original contract was read. It is the literal graph Bellman
  minimum envelope on bounded heights, with a graph-Lipschitz core. It is
  an old scout, not P200 or an accepted paper. CCI's colour-dependent seed
  distances use the same classical shortest-path primitive, receiving zero
  credit; the original state evolves by persistent local clocks and has
  period $q$, so it is not that fixed-only height self-map.
- `docs/papers182_186_sequence/scouting/combinatorial_lane/SCOUT_AND_KILL_LEDGER.md`:
  the full twelve-rule ledger was read. Its FPT leftmost transfer already
  kills the generic load-balancing/sorting energy silhouette. MGE is a
  different scheduler, not a recovered inverse theorem. RMA's independent
  support census is standard support/composition bookkeeping and is below
  the two-mechanism threshold independently of any literal owner.
- `docs/papers162_166_sequence/scouting/open_fresh_p166_round8/SCOUT.md` and
  the P167 owner-gate locators exposed the exact minimum-preimage feedback
  rule. It was desk-excluded and was not counted among the seven new pilots.

## Reproducible search subjects and access ceiling

Local text queries included equal/same-colour neighbour counts, lower-neighbour
ranks, conflict increments, richest/steepest/mutual resource transfer, score
reversal, first passage/Bellman, and total vertex covers. External queries
included these exact subjects with synchronous, cyclic, graph and counting
qualifiers, plus the named primary papers above and arXiv records. Result
snippets about generic dynamic colouring, unrelated majority models and
ordinary CCA are not exact-rule ownership evidence.

An indexed question about counting vertex covers with no isolated vertices
and an additional even-edge parity condition is not used as a primary
theorem source. Search results for independent-set bounds were not silently
credited as read proofs. No external full text was uploaded or saved into a
paper package, and no specialist review was requested.
