# Frozen theorem spikes — P192–P196

The spikes below record what had to be visible before manuscript construction.
The finite data are falsification pressure and convention checks, not proofs.

## HRF / P192

- The first-collision index moves strictly right after one Hurwitz update.
- Exhaustion through `n=8` produced maximum tail `n-2`, fixed count
  `(n-1)^(n-2)`, and unique maximum fibre `n-1` at the canonical chain.
- Every predecessor could be indexed by a single inverse Hurwitz move before
  the target's first collision.
- The stronger history-set product survived a separate `n=9` stream but no
  compatible Prüfer-position bijection was found; it was frozen as a
  conjecture rather than a theorem.

## MBR / P193

- Literal mutual nominations agreed source-by-source with one first/minimum
  exchange in each current indecomposable direct-sum block.
- Block count increased off identity; measured tails matched the recursive
  selection-decomposition height.
- Exact depth layers matched
  `A_t=(1-B_t)^(-1)` and `B_(t+1)=x+x^2 A_t B_t'`.
- Labelled target indegrees factored using optional boundaries before
  singleton target components.

## LRCW / P194

- Every move lowered letter sum by one while preserving reverse-RSK shape.
- Endpoint energy equalled `sum_i i lambda_i`; all pointwise depths were the
  energy difference.
- Independently enumerated semistandard tableaux matched the normalized Schur
  specialization, not merely the total component size.
- Direct predecessor sets matched admissible `f_i` candidates, and staircase
  highest words achieved the `k`-fibre threshold.

## OLW / P195

- Odd order oriented each tree edge one way; even order made eligibility
  symmetric on the odd/odd-cut forest.
- The maximum tails appeared as `floor((n-1)/2)` in every complete box.
- A double-star killed the false idea that an odd-edge component has only one
  reciprocal attractor; the theorem was repaired to functional components.
- Fixed/recurrent counts matched the two rooted-tree EGF constructions, and
  direct fibres matched the local incident-edge test.

## CGI / P196

- One epoch landed exactly in the language
  `y_(i+1)<M => y_i>y_(i+1)`; another epoch was left rotation.
- Closed-word counts matched `tr(A^m)` and iterate-fixed counts matched
  `tr(A^gcd(m,r))`.
- The guessed q-bonacci polynomial failed at `q=3`; exact determinants gave
  `lambda^q-(lambda+1)^(q-1)`.
- Direct target fibres split into independent weak-chain gaps and matched a
  product of binomial differences.
