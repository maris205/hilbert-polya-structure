## Direct-owner controls

- **`SYT`.**  Rhoades's [Cyclic sieving, promotion, and representation
  theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.2568) explicitly treats jeu-de-taquin
  promotion on rectangular tableaux and its fixed points.  The 2025 preprint
  [Orbit lengths for promotion on 2-row and near-hook
  tableaux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03671) makes the two-row owner risk even
  more direct.  `SYT` is killed.
- **`CUB`.**  Stanley's author-hosted paper [Symmetries of Plane
  Partitions](https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/pubs/pubfiles/65.pdf) introduces
  complementation in a box; Kuperberg's [Self-complementary plane partitions
  by Proctor's minuscule method](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9411239) explicitly
  enumerates self-complementary classes.  `CUB` is killed.
- **`HAR`.**  Florez--Zaslavsky's [Projective Rectangles: Harmonic
  Conjugation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12248) confirms that harmonic
  conjugation is a standard projective operation over finite
  characteristic.  Since the proposed dynamics is just that involution with
  anchors retained, `HAR` is killed.
- **`PDU`.**  No further external search can cure the internal identity:
  writing the ordered triangle as a nonsingular `3 x 3` matrix turns the
  opposite-side-polar update into its adjugate, so P103's double-adjugate
  proof transfers.  `PDU` is killed internally.
