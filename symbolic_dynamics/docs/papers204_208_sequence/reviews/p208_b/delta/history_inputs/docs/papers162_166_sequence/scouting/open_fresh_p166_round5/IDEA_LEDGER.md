## Ranking before subtraction

1. `XCT` is the only paper-sized-looking raw package.  It fails because both
   its large target fibres and its exceptional fixed states are controlled by
   translation stabilizers, precisely the inverse language already made a
   central theorem axis in P162.  Its weight refinement is static zero-sum
   subset enumeration and receives zero contribution credit.
2. `GSR` is a smaller sign analogue with closed formulas, but every statement
   is an orbit calculation for one central involution.
3. `BND` has a universal clock identity, but it is a standard boundary
   operator relation and does not produce a second uniform axis.

No candidate survives at least two independent axes *after* subtraction.
There is therefore no GREEN theorem contract from Round 5.
