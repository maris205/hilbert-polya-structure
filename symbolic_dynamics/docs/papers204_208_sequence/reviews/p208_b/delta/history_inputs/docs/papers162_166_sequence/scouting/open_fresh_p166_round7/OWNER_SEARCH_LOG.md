Queries included:

```text

```

Konyagin, Luca, Mans, Mathieson, Sha, and Shparlinski,
[*Functional graphs of polynomials over finite fields*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2015.07.003),
explicitly studies polynomial functional graphs and quadratic cases.  Mans,
Sha, Shparlinski, and Sutantyo,
[*On functional graphs of quadratic polynomials*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04734),
is a focused primary study over prime fields.

RPF's invariant-line identity is exact, so its temporal problem is not merely
near this literature: it is the family `x -> x^2+(1-d)x`.  The source paper
does not need to own the two-coordinate notation for the temporal axis to be
fully reduced.  The quadratic-congruence fibre formula remains elementary
and alone.  P125 and P150 are additional internal warnings.
