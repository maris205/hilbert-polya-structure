## FDR — first-descent prefix reversal

Queries included `"first descent" "prefix reversal" permutation map`,
`permutation map reverse prefix first descent functional graph`, and the
longest-increasing-prefix vocabulary.

- Hurkens, van Iersel, Keijsper, Kelk, Stougie, and Tromp, *Prefix Reversals
  on Binary and Ternary Strings*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 21 (2007), 592--611,
  DOI [10.1137/060664252](https://doi.org/10.1137/060664252), owns generic
  prefix-reversal and pancake-sorting background, not the autonomous update.
- Pudwell and Smith, *Sorting via shuffles with a cut after the longest
  increasing prefix*, Theoret. Comput. Sci. 1008 (2024), 114679, DOI
  [10.1016/j.tcs.2024.114679](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2024.114679),
  studies four cut-and-shuffle procedures, two with a reversal of the second
  part before a riffle.  This is a nearby state-selected-prefix vocabulary,
  but it is not reversal of the prefix ending immediately after the first
  descent.

No inspected record states the literal map together with its half-sized
image, depth-0/1/2 census, peak two-cycle core, and decreasing-run target
fibres.  Status is therefore `OWNER_AMBER`, not owner-cleared.

## RSI — random singleton isolation

Queries included `set partition isolate selected label singleton Markov
chain`, `remove marked element make singleton partition`, and finite
fragmentation/coupon-collector variants.

- Knopfmacher, Mansour, and Wagner, *Records in Set Partitions*, Electron. J.
  Combin. 17 (2010), R109, DOI
  [10.37236/381](https://doi.org/10.37236/381), uses the one-step bijection
  “remove a marked element from its block and make it a singleton.”  That
  local operation and its marked-partition count receive zero credit.
- Stark, *Markov chains generating random permutations and set partitions*,
  Stochastic Processes Appl. 178 (2024), 104483, DOI
  [10.1016/j.spa.2024.104483](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spa.2024.104483),
  constructs partition-generating arborescence chains and variants excluding
  singleton blocks.  It does not iterate uniform-label singleton isolation on
  a fixed completed partition.
- Brown, *Semigroups, rings, and Markov chains*, J. Theoret. Probab. 13
  (2000), 871--938; preprint
  [arXiv:math/0006145](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0006145), owns generic
  diagonalization technology for left-regular-band/semigroup walks.

The local split move, partition lattice, associated Bell numbers, commuting
idempotents, and coupon coverage are all background.  The retained residual
is the conjunction of the literal uniform-label chain, every-initial-block
absorption law, complete singleton spectrum, every-target support sum, and
target-local predecessor/action-pair atlas.  Status: `OWNER_AMBER`.
