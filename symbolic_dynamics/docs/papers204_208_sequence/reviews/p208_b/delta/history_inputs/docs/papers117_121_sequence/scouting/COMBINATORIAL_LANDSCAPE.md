# Combinatorial landscape audit

The lane closed graph powers, transitive closure, rowmotion/promotion,
ordinary leaf/core stripping, corner rank, blocker/Glaisher/KMP/eroder
variants, and any graph-height reformulation of P114.  It also marked the
classical content of local complementation, rotor routing, strong collapse,
zero forcing, GYO reduction, graph-convexity iteration, and parallel
chip-firing as zero-credit background.

Conditional Phase-2 entrances, in decreasing priority, are: an intrinsically
selected synchronous local-complement map; the full transient/basin/fibre
functional graph of rotor routing on a rigid non-Eulerian family; canonical
parallel strong collapse with genuinely high-dimensional incidence; an
all-subset synchronous zero-forcing map; and, as backups, simultaneous GYO or
beat-point reductions whose chronology is not a renamed height.  Convexity
and chip-firing remain fragile controls.  No bounded-search gap is a novelty
claim.
