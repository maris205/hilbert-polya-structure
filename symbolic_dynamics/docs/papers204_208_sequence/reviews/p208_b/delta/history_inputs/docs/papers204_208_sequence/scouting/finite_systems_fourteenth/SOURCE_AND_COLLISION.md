1. Hamsa Balakrishnan, Inseok Hwang and Claire J. Tomlin,
   [*Polynomial Approximation Algorithms for Belief Matrix Maintenance in

   Author-hosted PDF title/abstract, introduction and Section II.A,
   Algorithm 1 equations (2)–(3), plus adjacent Theorem 1 were actually read.
   The row-then-column scaling operation is classical. The source has
   nonnegative real matrices; its convergence theorem is not a theorem

   not audited and are not used.

   [*Alternate minimization and doubly stochastic matrices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.11930),
   arXiv 1812.11930v3 (2019), journal record Integers 20A (2020), A10.
   Actual arXiv metadata and abstract only: positive-real two-by-two finite
   convergence is treated there. No finite-field two-by-two theorem is
   attributed to it; the intake's scalar adapter is an elementary local
   calculation. Its full proof was not read.
3. Eric Rowland and Jason Wu,
   [*The entries of the Sinkhorn limit of an m by n matrix*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02789),
   arXiv 2409.02789v2, revised 25 May 2025. Actual metadata and abstract
   read. It concerns algebraic equations for positive-real limits, not
   finite-field recurrent sets. Its methods/ancillaries were not read or
   run. An author-hosted PDF link was a lead, not another full read.

   its stated degree result is context, not a transfer of dynamics over
   finite fields. The body and Mathematica ancillary were not inspected.

   [*Locally-zero Groupoids and the Center of Bin(X)*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.09220),

   [*Groupoid Factorizations in the Semigroup of Binary Systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09229),

   [*A property of the inverse of a subspace of a finite field*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.1293),
