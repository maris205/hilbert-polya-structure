**Frozen decision:** `KILL_ALL`  
**Lifecycle:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`  
**Date:** 2026-09-03 UTC

This was a strict Route-A breadth round.  Six new literal systems were built
from their carrier definitions and exhaustively tested; none is promoted to a
paper contract.  In particular, neither a large exact-control count nor a
clean formula overcomes a permanent owner or an internal proof-silhouette
collision.

## Intake firewall

- Required: at least five literal systems, at least four carrier classes, and
  an early exact functional-graph or kernel pilot.
- Required for `GREEN`: a natural map/kernel and two independent theorem axes
  selected from all-time dynamics, target-resolved inverse/image data, sharp
  extrema, and parameter recovery.
- Permanent exclusions applied before scoring: generic closure or projection,
  linear/Jordan dynamics, ordinary valuations and powering, standard sorting,
  coupon-collector/exposure wrappers, generic random deletion, standard
  Glauber or random-transposition chains, and literal/proof-engine collisions
  with P1--P165.
- A bounded owner-search non-hit has zero positive evidential weight.

## Why the strongest two still die

`RMR` is not rejected for lack of formulas.  If the reference matching is
contracted, every alternating component becomes a signed cycle; repairing a
chosen reference edge removes that selected cycle element and makes it a
singleton.  Conditional on an accepted move this is exactly the old `S06`
cycle-erosion rule.  Uniform selection from all reference edges merely adds
self-loops.  Thus its temporal law and inverse switch count are a decorated
matching presentation of a permanent kill.

`OST` is not the same selector as P148: it tests current fringe-subtree parity,
where P148 tests depth parity.  Nevertheless the portfolio firewall is at the
proof-silhouette level, not literal equality.  Both act on `PT_{<=N}`, contract
selected vertices with ordered child promotion, have the singleton as unique
core, attain `ceil(log2 N)`, and obtain their scored inverse theorem by a local
outdegree product followed by Lagrange extraction.  The new minimal-fibre
formula is real mathematics, but it is not a separated P166 paper in this
portfolio.

## Frozen handoff

No candidate is reserved, drafted, posted, or released.  The six systems and
their formulas remain negative controls only.  Reopening either `RMR` or
`OST` would require a new theorem axis not inherited from, respectively,
cycle erosion or ordered plane-tree contraction, followed by a new owner and
internal-collision gate.
