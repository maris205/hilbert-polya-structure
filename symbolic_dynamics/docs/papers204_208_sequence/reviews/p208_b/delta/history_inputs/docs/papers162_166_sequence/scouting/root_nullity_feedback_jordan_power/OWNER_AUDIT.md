## Decisive interpretation

The target-resolved fixed-`r` flow is correct, but it is a repackaging of the
nilpotent root/power multiplicity problem.  Öztürk's abstract is unusually
direct: it advertises both the Jordan form of a nilpotent power and the converse
nonnegative-integer criterion for roots.  Our flow coordinates make the
criterion explicit for enumeration but do not create a new theorem axis after
subtraction.

The feedback recursion is not erased by those sources.  Nevertheless, novelty
is conjunctive here: one clean temporal axis without an independent inverse or
structural axis does not pass the P166 gate.  The internal P137 collision below
removes the package architecture as well.
