## Primary near-owner inspected

Henryk Fukś, *Ternary cellular automata induced by semigroups of order 3
are solvable*, [arXiv:2601.00486v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00486v1).
The root read the abstract, definitions and local-rule table on 2026-09-05.
It studies two-input ternary rules induced by associative binary operations,
organized into 18 isomorphism/anti-isomorphism classes. This is a relevant
solvable-ternary-CA comparison and deserves explicit background credit.

Our comparison, not a claim from that paper: d(a,b)=sgn(b−a) is not
associative. Indeed

$$d(d(-1,-1),0)=0\ne1=d(-1,d(-1,0)).$$

Associativity is invariant under alphabet isomorphism and passage to the
opposite operation. Thus TCSD is not one of those semigroup rules under a
direct alphabet renaming or reflection. This small obstruction does NOT
exclude higher-block encodings, factors, time iterates, isotopies, or other
published ternary rules. Such relations remain an owner-review obligation.
