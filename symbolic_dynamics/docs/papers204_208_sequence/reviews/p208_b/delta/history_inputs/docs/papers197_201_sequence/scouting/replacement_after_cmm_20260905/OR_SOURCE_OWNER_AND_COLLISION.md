```text

```

where C is global colour advance and A changes1 to2 precisely before0.

description of the rule, not a claim that this family is unprecedented.

with t. Therefore this substitution gives a periodic colour schedule,
not by itself a conjugacy to the usual autonomous cyclic cellular
