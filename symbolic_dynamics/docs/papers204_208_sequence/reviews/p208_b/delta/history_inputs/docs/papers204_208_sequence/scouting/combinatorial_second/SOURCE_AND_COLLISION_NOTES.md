# Bounded source and collision deductions

2026-09-05 UTC. These are direct source checks and explicit mathematical
adapters, not global novelty certificates. No external upload, contact or
specialist review occurred. The installed literature workflow's unavailable
external bibliographic/review providers were not simulated; local evidence
and the available primary-source web interface were used. `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## PR

The nearby internal [P195 manuscript](../../../../papers/195-odd-side-least-neighbor-trees/main.tex),
Sections 1–2, was read: its underlying tree never changes and a single
marker follows a parity-eligible least-labelled neighbour. PR instead
updates the whole function and its predecessor relation. Thus there is no
established literal conjugacy or fixed-carrier factor. A least-neighbour
name is not a valid collision proof. PR is rejected for unclosed all-size
temporal and inverse structure, not for a falsely asserted P195 equivalence.
No decisive literal external owner is claimed.

## ZR

Shallit and Shan, *A General Approach to Proving Properties of Fibonacci
Representations via Automata Theory*, EPTCS 386 (2023), 228–242:
[primary arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02765), Proposition 2,
PDF p.4, was read. It uses successive left-to-right 011→100 replacements
for linear Zeckendorf normalization, with a digit-count termination proof.
Complementation and spatial reversal turn our local 001→110 into 011→100.
This deducts the local carry identity and monotone count mechanism; it does
**not** identify cyclic simultaneous evolution with that linear algorithm.
The sharp cyclic clock is unproved here. The inverse hitting-set fragment
is valid but does not by itself provide the missing independent temporal
axis. No claim is made that the source contains our exact target formula.

## BS

Guo, *The q-Binomial Theorem and two Symmetric q-Identities*, Electronic
Journal of Combinatorics 10 (2003), R34:
[primary journal PDF](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v10i1r34/pdf/),
Section 2, especially the proof of Theorem 2.1 on PDF pp.3–4, was read.
It explicitly defines the 2-modular diagram and its involutive conjugation
on partitions without repeated odd parts, attributing that construction
to Chapman. Our proof notes give the exact adapter T=σC and T²=C on the
full partition carrier. This is stronger collision evidence than shared
partition terminology.

The nearby [P126 manuscript](../../../../papers/126-balanced-composition-refinement/main.tex),
Section 1 and its subtraction boundary, was also read. It preserves ordered
balanced-split compositions and studies all-iterate codeword fibres; BS
forgets order and adds transpose, so no literal P126 equivalence is asserted.
The external modular involution, not a superficial P126 title match, kills
BS's proposed temporal contribution.

## BA

Belitsky and Ferrari, *Ballistic annihilation and deterministic surface
growth*, Journal of Statistical Physics 80 (1995), 517–543:
[primary publisher page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02178546),
the complete accessible abstract was read; full text is subscription-only
and was **not** inspected. The source defines particles retaining velocities
±1 and annihilating on collision. Our ring rule is exactly the integer-time
sampling of this mechanism with periodic finite data and initially vacant
sites, as proved by the crossing/landing case split. The source studies
random fully occupied infinite initial data, so its distribution theorem
is not asserted to be our finite-ring theorem. The carrier adaptation does
not make ballistic motion or the elementary collision-time bound new, and
there is no separate developed inverse axis.

## AZ

Haviv and Levy, *Symmetric Complete Sum-free Sets in Cyclic Groups*:
[primary arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04118), title, version
metadata and abstract were read. It constructs the exact static class
identified by our fixed-point equation and records nontrivial abundance
and size results. Its full 20-page proofs were not relied on. The fixed-set
identification follows independently in our proof notes. We do not claim
the source solves the feedback dynamics. Lack of an all-parameter orbit
theorem, not a supposed static-to-dynamic transfer, blocks AZ.

## MA

Ayad, Badkobeh, Fici, Héliou and Pissis, *Constructing Antidictionaries of
Long Texts in Output-Sensitive Space*:
[primary publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00224-020-10018-5),
abstract, Section 1 Problem 1, and Section 2 factor definitions were read.
These explicitly characterize minimal absent words of a collection by
absence of the whole word and presence of its maximal proper prefix and
suffix. This owns the language primitive, not our iteration theorem.
The generic closure/involution adapter and the support-indexed inverse
formula are proved explicitly in our proof notes and apply to any fixed
directed graph. This exact transfer is the value deduction.

Internal [W04 in the P122–P126 replacement scout](../../../papers122_126_sequence/scouting/replacement/SCOUT.md)
was read. W04 changes one cyclic word to repair a chosen absent factor;
MA changes an entire language by taking its absent-edge boundary. They are
not asserted to be conjugate. The decision is generic-closure value failure,
not a false exact collision with W04.

## Decision boundary

Six actual maps were tested; there are no additions based only on source
desk search. BS and BA have explicit owned-mechanism reductions; MA has
an explicit generic closure transfer on both axes. PR and AZ lack proof
closure. ZR retains a genuine exact inverse fragment, but its proved
temporal axis is only the deducted monotone carry mechanism and the stronger
clock is still open. All six are not admitted. This is bounded intake
disposition, not a claim of six globally unoriginal systems.
