## Search discipline

This is an early owner-risk screen, not a systematic novelty review.  Queries
were issued against publisher pages, author copies, arXiv, MathNet, AMS, and
primary journal records.  Secondary snippets were used only to locate a
primary record and do not carry a claim.  `BOUNDED_NO_DIRECT_HIT` means only
that the stated searches did not retrieve the literal conjunction; it is not
evidence of novelty, priority, or freedom to publish.

The internal gate preceded web search.  Directory names P1--P161 and the most
recent `HISTORICAL_OCCUPANCY`, `SYSTEM_COLLISION_FIREWALL`, theorem-contract,
and kill-ledger files were read.  An internal exact conjugacy or proof-engine
transfer kills a candidate even if web search is empty.

## Why `AA01` is not cleared

The exact searches did not retrieve the finite-local-ring statement

```text
number of t-step sources of B in M_k(R)
  = |R^x|^t |R|^(2kt+t(t-1)).
```

That absence has three possible explanations: the result is unstated because
it is routine, it is stated under different elimination terminology, or it is
genuinely absent.  This scout does not distinguish those possibilities.  A
specialist owner review must search LU/LDU big cells over finite local rings,
Bruhat cells, Gaussian elimination over rings, and finite-ring flag counts.
Until then the only authorized label is `TOP_1_SPECIALIST_OWNER_GATE` under
`HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
