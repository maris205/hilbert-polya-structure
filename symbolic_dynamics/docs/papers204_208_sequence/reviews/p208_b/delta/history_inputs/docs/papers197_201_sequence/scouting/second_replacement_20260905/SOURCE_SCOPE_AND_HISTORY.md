## Primary records inspected

### D2G — exact operator owner

William Q. Erickson, Daniel Herden, Jonathan Meddaugh, Mark R. Sepanski,
Mitchell Minyard and Kyle Rosengartner, *Limits and Periodicity of Metamour
2-Distance Graphs*, arXiv:2409.02306 (2024).

- [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02306).
- [Primary full text, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.02306v1).
- Inspected introduction, definitions 2.1–2.3 and start of Section 3.
  Definition 2.1 is the literal D2G rule on the same vertex set. The authors
  distinguish equality of labelled graphs from graph isomorphism, removing
  a possible labelled/unlabelled escape. The paper investigates iteration,
  periods and which graphs occur as images; it is not merely a static
  two-hop analogy.
- **Verdict:** verified exact external operator collision. No field,
  parameter or conjugacy subtlety is needed. No temporal theorem or empty
  fibre count in this scout is offered as new-system credit.
- Source type: author preprint. Peer-review, venue rankings, retraction
  screening and personal conflicts were not independently certified here;
  none is needed to identify the publicly documented literal owner.

### CCW — classical window; real/finite-field boundary retained

arXiv:2407.19767 (record submitted 2024).

- [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19767).
- [Primary full text, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.19767v1).
- Inspected introduction, Definition 2.2, Remark 2.3, Lemma 2.7, and
  Section 4's scale/similarity statements. Their dimension-two rule is

- **Scope limit:** their setting is Euclidean real space, not the finite

  arXiv identifier/version, not an inferred publication event. We do not
  import their positivity arguments or period-eight conclusion into

- **Verdict:** classical moving operator; changing the coefficient field
  and adding held singular states do not satisfy this batch's fresh-system
  threshold. The finite-field proof in CONTRACTS_AND_PROOF.md is preserved,
  but neither global conjugacy nor whole-theorem transfer is claimed.
- Source type: author preprint; existence and the cited definition were
  verified, but no independent bibliometric, COI or retraction certificate
  is asserted.
