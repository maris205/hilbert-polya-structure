# Title inventory and recovered gaps

`PAPER_DIRECTORY_TITLES.txt` is a mechanically sorted snapshot of all numbered
directories under `papers/` at the audit point.  It has 171 lines and SHA-256

```text
48ecb8bb39517531b94cc7174760f27910a8a4157a6ce140473fb5fef83f0d0f
```

The count is explained by 170 distinct paper numbers on disk plus two P96
directories: numbered directories run through P1--P50 and P57--P176, while
P51--P56 are absent.  Sequence records recover the missing historical topics:

- P51: countable finite-memory shadowing;
- P52: one-forbidden-word SFTs;
- P53: synchronized spoke codes;
- P54: uniform word morphisms and Diophantine repetition transfer;
- P55: probabilistic finite automata; and
- P56: irreducible-SFT finite covers and a Galois invariant.

The scan was followed by mechanism-level reading of the collision seeds and
the closest full manuscripts/ledgers, especially P105, P118, P122, P132,
P154--P156, P164--P167, P172--P176, and the current sibling cross-domain
ledger.  A title list is a reproducibility artifact, not a collision proof.
