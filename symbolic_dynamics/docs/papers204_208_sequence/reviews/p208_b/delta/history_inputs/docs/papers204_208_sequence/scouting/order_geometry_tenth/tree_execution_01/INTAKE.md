- QAS: the three-point area-weighted Laplacian variant is an immediate

- OFS: hand flips at n=5 give two distinct inputs {02,03} and {02,24}
  mapping to {13,14}; it is not conjugate to bijective polygon rotation or
  promotion. The n=4 two-cycle excludes a direct descending pop-stack
  identification. This does not exclude a composed known adapter. Old Q09
  least-ear, C14 parity-selected, fan/Lawson flip rules are not repeated.
- SBF is the susceptible/infected part of explicit-Euler SIR with
  beta=gamma=step=1. The removed coordinate is omitted and is not a new
  integral or theorem. For a target (a,b), a+b determines the old x; if
  nonzero the old y is b/(a+b), while (0,0) has p predecessors and every
  other zero-sum target has none. This rational inverse is elementary and
  deducted before any observed clock. Real SIR convergence does not
  transfer to finite fields. Old RPF (x+xy,y+xy) has an invariant difference
  and is not asserted conjugate to this rule.
- HGF is one quarter of the gradient of the classical quartic
  H=2a^2b^2+2b^2c^2+2c^2a^2-a^4-b^4-c^4 when 4 is invertible. The classical
  Heron identity, polynomial polar maps and homogeneous scaling carry no
  novelty credit. This is not the old binary-quartic Hessian determinant
  feedback or the ninth-lane additive Euler-top QEF.
- JCA has the old adjugate as its literal p=2,3 controls because inv0 is
  then the identity. Over larger fields its ordinary nonzero/invertible
  domain lies in the usual/Hadamard-inverse dynamics studied by Korepanov
  and in the Kontsevich periodicity neighborhood. Classical generic
  inversion, diagonal-gauge reductions, adjugate singular-rank strata and
  rank-one reciprocal factorization are deducted. No generic birational
  statement is silently extended across zero entries or singular matrices.
