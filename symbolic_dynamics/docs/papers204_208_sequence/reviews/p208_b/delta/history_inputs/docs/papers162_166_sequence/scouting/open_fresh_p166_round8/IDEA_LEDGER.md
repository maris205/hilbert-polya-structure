## Ranking after subtraction

`MIP` is the only survivor.  Its literal rule is a canonical operation on a
finite function, its temporal axis is a path-reversal/endpoint-peeling law
with a sharp linear clock, and its inverse axis resolves every target rather
than only an aggregate.  Its recurrent species and zeta form a third check.

`BFR` and `QDP` have attractive closed formulas but die because those formulas
are the direct algebraic/algorithmic content of their classical primitives.
`QTF` is bijective and therefore has no nontrivial fibre axis.  `BLM` never
produced an all-parameter clock, while `DWS` stops after one step for a formal
reason.

No paper has been drafted or assigned.  `MIP` is a theorem-contract candidate
only, and remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL` pending an independent hostile gate.
