The lane closed reset/synchronization as a headline, left-regular-band and
\(\mathscr R\)-trivial specializations, finite memory and transfer matrices as
claimed advances, ordinary random matrix growth, max-plus switching without a
new cancellation phenomenon, push–pop, coupon/rank loss, and termination-only
probabilistic rewriting.

Conditional Phase-2 entrances are: a finite confluent two-rule string rewrite
with a complete absorption-time and normal-form fibre law; a non-
\(\mathscr R\)-trivial Rees/Green family coupling minimal-ideal entrance to
maximal-subgroup phase; a one-local-defect abelian network; a rank-greater-than-
one non-synchronizing permutation-plus-defect automaton; and an inverse-
semigroup pair whose rank loss does not reduce to coupon collection.  A finite
kernel, pressure, LLN/CLT/LDP, or stationary law is supporting machinery only.
