\section{Frozen compiler class}\label{sec:lock}

\begin{definition}[phase space and marker]
Let $G=(V,E)$ be one fixed countable loop-allowed directed graph with no
parallel edges, generated by a
cutoff-independent rule.  Its phase space is the one-sided edge shift
\[
X_G^+=\{e_0e_1\ldots:t(e_j)=o(e_{j+1})\}.
\]
A primitive orbit is a nonempty directed closed edge word modulo cyclic
rotation that is not a proper temporal power.  Reflection is not identified.
The free variable $z$ records one edge of this original graph.
\end{definition}

Let $\Aset$ be a countably infinite atom set with norm
$N:\Aset\to(1,\infty)$.  We require multiplicative freeness:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:free}
\prod_{a\in\Aset}N(a)^{m_a}=1,\quad m_a\in\mathbb Z
\text{ finitely supported}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m_a=0\ \forall a.
\end{equation}
The prime specialization is $\Aset=\mathbb P$ and $N(p)=p$.  Enumerate the
atoms in nondecreasing norm and assume eventually
\begin{equation}\label{eq:growth}
N(a_j)\le j^\kappa.
\end{equation}
For rational primes one may take $\kappa=2$.

Fix a finite visible alphabet $\Bset$, $b=|\Bset|\ge2$, and after a
higher-block recoding a one-edge label map $\lambda:E\to\Bset$.  If
$\Lambda(\gamma)$ is the cyclic label word of an orbit, visibility means
\begin{equation}\label{eq:separation}
\Lambda(\gamma_a)=\Lambda(\gamma_c)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=c.
\end{equation}
Without \eqref{eq:separation}, an arbitrary inventory may be hidden in
countable vertex names.

The nonnegative edge roof $\tau:E\to[0,\infty)$ is frozen before evaluation.
For real $\sigma>0$ the whole weighted adjacency on $\Hh=\ell^2(V)$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:whole}
L_\sigma\delta_u=\sum_{e:u\to v}e^{-\sigma\tau(e)}\delta_v.
\end{equation}
If it is unbounded, the whole-operator gate has already failed.  A weighted
anisotropic space or an induced return space is a different owned object.

\begin{definition}[literal atomic ledger]
There is exactly one primitive orbit $\gamma_a$ for every $a\in\Aset$, there
are no other primitive orbits, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:clock}
T(\gamma_a):=\sum_{e\in\gamma_a}\tau(e)=\log N(a).
\end{equation}
An extra mixed primitive word is a failure before any cancellation is
considered.  Temporal repetitions of $\gamma_a$ are allowed and are not new
primitive orbits.
\end{definition}

The central connected ledger is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ledger}
\mathcal L_G(s,z)=
\sum_{[\gamma]\ \mathrm{primitive}}\sum_{r\ge1}
\frac{z^{r|\gamma|}}{r}e^{-srT(\gamma)}.
\end{equation}
We first read \eqref{eq:ledger} literally.  Only after trace-class ownership
is proved may it be identified with $-\log\det(I-zL_s)$.

Recognition edges may be transient, periodically active, or shared recurrent.
A terminal support projector, a supplied prime table, a cutoff-dependent
alphabet, target-zero data, and an after-the-fact orbit weight are forbidden.
Ordinary nondeterministic paths are counted with multiplicity; Boolean path
existence is not the scalar semantics of \eqref{eq:whole}.
