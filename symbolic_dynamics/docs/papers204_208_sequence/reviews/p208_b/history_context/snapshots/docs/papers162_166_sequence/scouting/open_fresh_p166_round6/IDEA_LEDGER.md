## Ranking after exact enumeration

1. `MPS` is the only raw signal meeting both mathematical axes.  It has a
   pointwise all-time law and a genuinely target-resolved inverse product.
   The collision is nevertheless decisive: changing scalar truncation to a
   parity cap and a transfer matrix to a product over top bands does not make
   a new proof architecture.
2. `MEG` has a clean inverse description and a uniform eventual two-cycle
   law, but both are immediate after the universal-vertex rewrite and its
   atomic predicate is directly established terminology.
3. `GGT` has the cleanest arithmetic fibre formula, but its complete dynamics
   last only two steps and split into coordinatewise minimum counts.

The other three fail before a paper-sized theorem contract can be stated.
Accordingly this lane has **no GREEN candidate and no P166 theorem contract**.
