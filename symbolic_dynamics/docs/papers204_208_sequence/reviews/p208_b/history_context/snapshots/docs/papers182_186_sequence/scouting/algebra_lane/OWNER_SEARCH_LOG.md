## A01 / cyclic lattice comparator

Targeted query families included the literal coordinate law
`(c, a meet b, a join b)`, cyclic meet/join triple dynamics, subspace-lattice
sorting operators, and lattice-map image/orbit enumeration.

Primary adjacent sources checked:

1. O. Gasanova and L. Nicklasson, [“Chain algebras of finite distributive
   lattices”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-023-01294-8), *Journal of
   Algebraic Combinatorics* 59 (2024), 473--494.  It uses Hibi meet/join
   binomials and sortable monomials; it does not study the three-register
   self-map or functional graph here.
2. L. Hong, [“The Pop-stack-sorting Operator on Tamari
   Lattices”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10030), arXiv:2201.10030.  Its map
   sends one lattice element to a meet of covered elements.  It owns nearby
   lattice-dynamics language, but not the cyclic comparator.
3. M. Püschel, B. Seifert, and C. Wendler, [“Discrete Signal Processing on
   Meet/Join Lattices”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.04358), arXiv:2012.04358.
   It develops meet/join shifts for lattice signal processing, not this finite
   triple functional graph or complement-pair fibres.

Result: **OWNER_THIN / HOLD FOR DEEP SEARCH**.  Exact-phrase and structural
queries returned no exact owner in the bounded box.  This is only a non-hit.
A serious paper would still require MathSciNet/Zentralblatt/Crossref citation
chasing around lattice polynomial dynamics and comparator/sorting networks.

## A02 / Lie-derived subspaces

Targeted query families included `[U,U]` for arbitrary matrix subspaces,
derived-subspace iteration, commutator spans in `gl_2/sl_2`, and finite-field
commuting-subspace classifications.

Primary adjacent sources checked:

1. A. Baranov, [“Lie structure of associative algebras containing matrix
   subalgebras”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09805), arXiv:1805.09805.  It
   proves perfectness results for Lie commutator subalgebras; this owns the
   ambient perfect-derived-algebra background, not iteration over every vector
   subspace.
2. M. Homolya et al., [“Lie properties in associative
   algebras”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02534), arXiv:2110.02534.  It treats
   Lie generation, centralizers, and Lie-nilpotent subspaces, but no full
   functional/fibre atlas of `U -> [U,U]` on `Z+sl_2` was located.
3. M. Brešar, [“Commutators and images of noncommutative
   polynomials”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2020.107346), *Advances in
   Mathematics* 374 (2020), 107346.  It studies spans of commutators and
   polynomial images in associative algebras, an important adjacent owner
   domain but a different carrier and question.

Result: **OWNER_THIN / HOLD FOR DEEP SEARCH**.  No exact dynamic atlas was
found in the bounded query set.  This is not a novelty claim.  A release path
must search finite Lie algebra subgroup/subspace classifications, derived
subspace operators, and low-dimensional commutator geometry more deeply.
