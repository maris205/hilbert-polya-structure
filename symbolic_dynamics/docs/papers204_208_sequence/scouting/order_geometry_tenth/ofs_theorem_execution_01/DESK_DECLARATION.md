## QAS: area-weighted quadrilateral Laplacian

The carrier is every labelled quadruple $v=(v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3)$ of points of
$\mathbf F_p^2$, with repeated and collinear points included. Indices are
cyclic modulo four. Define

$$A_i=\det(v_{i+1}-v_i,v_{i-1}-v_i),\qquad
T(v)_i=v_i+A_i(v_{i+1}+v_{i-1}-2v_i).$$

All arithmetic is in $\mathbf F_p$. There are no division guards, new sink
states or degenerate exclusions. Fixed complete pilot boxes, if this row
survives the desk: $p=2,3$ only, respectively $2^8$ and $3^8$ states.

## DTC: dot-product triangle circulation

Every labelled triple $(v_0,v_1,v_2)\in(\mathbf F_p^2)^3$, with all
degeneracies included. Indices are cyclic modulo three. With the standard
bilinear dot product, define

$$B_i=\langle v_{i+1}-v_i,v_{i-1}-v_i\rangle,\qquad
T(v)_i=v_i+B_i(v_{i+1}-v_{i-1}).$$

This is a polynomial feedback on the full finite carrier, not an orthic
projection, normalized Euclidean reflection, differential-equation flow or
totalized orthocenter map. Fixed complete boxes if not desk-excluded:
$p=2,3,5$, with $p^6$ states each. No other primes are preauthorized.

## OFS: original-diagonal flip sweep

Every triangulation of a convex polygon with cyclic labels
$0,\ldots,n-1$, $n\ge3$. Record the **original** internal diagonals in
lexicographic order by their sorted endpoint pair. Starting with the
original triangulation, visit this recorded list once; when a recorded
diagonal is present, flip it to the other diagonal of its current
quadrilateral. New diagonals are not added to the list. The triangle is
fixed. Every individual flip preserves the carrier.

Fixed complete boxes if not desk-excluded: every triangulation for
$3\le n\le10$. This is neither first-ear nor first-illegal-edge flipping,
nor a repeated-to-completion fan normalization. Questions: Does the entire
