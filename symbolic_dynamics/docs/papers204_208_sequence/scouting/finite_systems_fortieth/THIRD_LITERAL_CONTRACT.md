## Exact autonomous closed carrier

For each integer `N >= 1`, the carrier is all positive compositions
`a=(a_1,...,a_k)` of `N`, equivalently all interval partitions of `[N]`.
Every old cut between `a_i` and `a_(i+1)` is deleted exactly when
`a_i <= a_(i+1)`. Delete all such cuts simultaneously. Each maximal weakly
increasing run of OLD part lengths is replaced by its sum; other cuts stay.
The carrier does not contain a clock, scheduler, history or external input.

In particular, the test uses adjacent OLD parts, not a growing partial sum.
The outputs of `(1,2,3)`, `(3,1,2)`, `(2,2,1)` are respectively `(6)`,
`(3,3)`, `(4,1)`. The rule never changes during a pilot or proof revision.
