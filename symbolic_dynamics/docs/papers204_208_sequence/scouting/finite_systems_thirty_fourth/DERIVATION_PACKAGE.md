## Notation

## Derivation Strategy

The classical Lipschitz lemma places $M(x)$ in $\mathcal W_n$.
The endpoints of each palindromic block of a numerical path are equal;
crossing a block boundary increases height by at most one. Consequently
$M(q)\ge q$ for $q\in\mathcal W_n$. Finite integer potential then gives
convergence. This last potential argument is generic and receives no
independent contribution credit.
