  with the arithmetic derivative defined by p'=1 on primes and Leibniz.

empty family and the family containing the empty set remain distinct.

## Original formulas and mechanism boundaries

### Arithmetic originals

P133's full manuscript defines

    F_N(d)=gcd(N,(N/d) phi(d)),
    q -> p iff p divides q-1,
    F(S)=(P minus S) union N(S).

It uses phi(d)=product_(q in S)(q-1), so the prime-support rule is a
negative-self/feed-forward Boolean map on the induced Pratt DAG. Its old
decoder, nonsharp h+1 entry bound and inclusion-exclusion fibres belong to
that displayed formula. The earlier A01 control is gcd(N,phi(d)). EGD is
gcd(d,phi(d)) and explicitly uses p dividing r-1. None of those originals
defines d' by p'=1 and Leibniz.

The old C08/APD formula is

    x -> ((x-x^p)/p) mod p,  x in Z/p^2 Z,

with the output returned to the embedded prime field. Its ledger expands
x=a+pb to b+(a-a^p)/p modulo p. Its owner log names Buium--Ralph--Simanca
and calls that Fermat quotient operator an arithmetic derivative. This desk
read that historical attribution, not the cited external article. The name
collision cannot identify it with the integer Leibniz arithmetic derivative.

### Set-family originals

The current graph lane's originals explicitly allow every family, including
the empty member, with no multiplicity, and define

    DI(H)={A intersection B:A,B in H, A!=B},
    SX(H)={A symmetric-difference B:A,B in H, A!=B}.

DI's full archived proof compares the downward closure I(DI(H)) with
I(H) minus Max(I(H)); its sharp n+1 witness is the full family 2^[n], not
an antichain except at the degenerate ground set. The old proof itself
assigns zero credit to this maximal-layer erosion. This is the relevant
deduction boundary for FID; it does not constitute a new candidate proof.

The archived SX proof, for at least three old members, uses a third member c
to represent a+b as (a+c)+(b+c), obtaining inclusion after the first image
and punctured additive closure. After Max, those two witness outputs need
not be retained. Consequently this desk supplies no FSD time conjugacy or
unqualified transfer of SX's recurrence proof.

Older H02 adjoins all nonempty pairwise intersections; H04 adjoins all
nonzero pairwise symmetric differences to a family of nonempty subsets.
Those are inflationary closure variants, not the new antichain maps. P97
itself is A -> A+A on nonempty subsets of the additive group F_p, p odd;
its 2^t-fold sumset identity, Cauchy--Davenport growth and singleton doubling
are not an exact FSD temporal theorem.

The old C04/MPU row says only “clutter to inclusion-minimal pairwise unions.”
It is a historical desk kill, not one of that package's four fully enumerated
maps. Searching the package found no additional MPU formula/implementation.
Ground complement exchanges intersections/maximal with unions/minimal, but
an exact FID conjugacy needs the old rule to exclude diagonal pairs and to
match empty conventions. The located original does not establish those
conditions; this desk therefore does not label MPU an exact conjugate.

### Formal-series originals

Old P04 acts on h=x+a_2 x^2+...+a_D x^D modulo x^(D+1) over F_p, taking
the compositional inverse h^(-1). The inspected implementation enumerates
that carrier and solves h composed with g = x, checks g composed with h = x,
and uses the inverse dictionary as the update. Its involution is the old
reversion primitive. It is not a multiplicative-unit reciprocal, USS or WCF.

The ninth lane ELD acts on f=1+sum_(k=1)^(m-1) a_k t^k modulo t^m by
T(f)=1+t f'/f. Its archived proof gives

    b_k=k a_k-sum_(i=1)^(k-1) b_i a_(k-i),

and a classical ghost-coordinate image/fibre adapter. Its triangular
permutation conclusion is on that explicitly defined ghost image. This
does not by itself establish the dynamics of arbitrary USS/WCF units.

AA08/FCG has finite continued-fraction words over a fixed finite alphabet,
plus the empty expansion, and deletes the first quotient. Its old exact
length clock and prefix-word inverse fibres are a shift-register mechanism.
The current WCF formula was not located in that original; describing it as
an exact prior “continued-fraction reset” would overstate this desk's hit.
