## Exact attempted literals, including discarded alternatives

1. **SRT, score-ranking with tie reversal.** On every labelled tournament
   on $[n]$, orient each pair from higher old outdegree to lower; if their
   old outdegrees are equal, reverse its old arc. All old scores are from
   the unchanged snapshot, including at $n=0,1$. This is not literally
   P112's tie-retaining rule. It nevertheless inherits the full permanent
   score-block/refinement mechanism, with reversal signs, so it is rejected
   at the author mechanism gate before any pilot.
2. **GCF0, conservative guarded gcd current.** For $n\ge1$, $N\ge0$,
   the carrier is $X_{n,N}=\{x\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^n:\sum_i x_i=N\}$,
   with indices modulo $n$. Put
   $$g_i(x)=\begin{cases}\gcd(x_i,x_{i+1}),&x_i x_{i+1}>0,\\0,&x_i x_{i+1}=0,\end{cases}
   \qquad T(x)_i=x_i-g_i(x)+g_{i-1}(x).$$
   All currents use the original snapshot. There is no clipping, sequential
   update or time-dependent schedule. At $n=1$ the two currents cancel.
3. **GCF+, positive-carrier restriction considered and deducted.** On
   $X^+_{n,N}=\{x\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^n:\sum_i x_i=N\}$ for $N\ge n$,
   use the same formula with $g_i=\gcd(x_i,x_{i+1})$. This is exactly the
   invariant positive stratum of GCF0, not a third independent direction
   or a separate promotion candidate. It counts as an attempted literal
   because this alternative carrier was explicitly considered.
