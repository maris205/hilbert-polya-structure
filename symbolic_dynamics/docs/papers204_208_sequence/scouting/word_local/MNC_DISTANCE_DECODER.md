# MNC distance-word decoder — root proof contribution

2026-09-06 UTC. Status: **PROVABLE AS STATED**. Root is a mathematical
contributor if this lemma is used, not an independent manuscript reviewer.
This is a static evaluated decoder with zero separate novelty credit.
It is not a candidate admission or a substitute for an extremal theorem.

## Claim, assumptions and notation

Let $n\ge3$ and keep the labelled cyclic positions. For
$x\in\{0,1,2\}^n$, put $d_i=|x_i-x_{i+1}|$ and
$F(x)_i=\min(d_{i-1},d_i)$. For an arbitrary cyclic distance word $d$,
delete its zero letters to obtain the cyclic nonzero word $w$. This is
edge contraction for counting only; the original labelled coordinates
are retained when reconstructing a source. Let $a$ and $b$ count the
ones and twos of $w$.

Write $N(d)$ for the number of labelled sources realizing $d$. Then:

1. If $w$ is empty, $N(d)=3$.
2. If $b=0<a$, $N(d)=2^{a/2+1}$ for even $a$, and zero for odd $a$.
3. If $a=0<b$, $N(d)=2$ for even $b$, and zero for odd $b$.
4. If $a,b>0$, $N(d)=2^{a/2}$ when every cyclic run of ones between
   successive twos has even length (zero allowed), and zero otherwise.

Consequently every MNC target has the exact evaluated finite sum
$$
|F^{-1}(y)|=\sum_{\substack{d\in\{0,1,2\}^n\\
                 \min(d_{i-1},d_i)=y_i\ (\forall i)}}N(d).
$$
No claim that this sum is an optimized closed product is made.

## Strategy and dependency map

The distance conditions are coloured closed walks on the three source
heights. Their two nontrivial edge matrices split into a common symmetric
two-dimensional space and an antisymmetric line. Matrix multiplication
evaluates the four cases. Recovering the distance word from each source
then proves disjointness of the target sum. No temporal theorem is used.

## Proof

**Step 1: exact trace.** In coordinate order $0,1,2$, the three adjacency
matrices for a distance zero, one and two are
$$
P_0=I_3,\qquad
P_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\0&0&0\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Expanding $\operatorname{tr}(P_{d_0}\cdots P_{d_{n-1}})$ sums one
product of edge indicators for every ordered source tuple. The trace
enforces the last edge back to the original first vertex. Thus the
trace equals $N(d)$ without division by rotations. Removing $P_0=I_3$
factors changes no count, including the empty product whose trace is
three. A contracted self-loop with a nonzero distance has trace zero,
as required; short contracted cycles do not require a separate convention.

**Step 2: invariant spaces.** On the orthonormal symmetric basis
$(e_0+e_2)/\sqrt2,e_1$, the two matrices are
$$
R=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt2\\\sqrt2&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
On the orthogonal antisymmetric line generated by $e_0-e_2$, their
actions are respectively zero and minus one. We have $R^2=2I_2$,
$E^2=E$, and
$$
ER^kE=\begin{cases}2^{k/2}E,&k\text{ even},\\0,&k\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

**Step 3: evaluate.** For ones only, the symmetric trace of $R^a$
is $2^{a/2+1}$ for even $a$ and zero for odd $a$; the antisymmetric
part is zero. For twos only, the trace is $1+(-1)^b$. For mixed words,
the antisymmetric contribution vanishes. Cyclically start at a two and
write the symmetric product as $ER^{k_1}ER^{k_2}\cdots ER^{k_b}$.
The displayed rank-one identity makes its trace zero if any $k_j$ is
odd, and otherwise gives $2^{(k_1+\cdots+k_b)/2}=2^{a/2}$.
This proves all four evaluations.

**Step 4: full inverse interpretation.** For each admissible $d$, choose
$x_0\in\{0,1,2\}$, then successively choose each next height with the
prescribed distance, keeping only walks whose last edge returns to
$x_0$. This reconstructs every original labelled source once. Its MNC
target is the stated adjacent minimum. Conversely a source determines
one and only one distance word, so the admissible walk sets are disjoint.
Their union is precisely $F^{-1}(y)$, proving the sum. $\square$
