## Exact admitted theorem ceiling

For every integer total mass $N\ge1$, the carrier is all ordered positive
compositions of $N$. One synchronous update $F$ replaces each maximal
weakly increasing run of **old** parts by that run's total. All boundaries
are selected from the same old composition; no newly formed sum is scanned
again within that update.

1. **Sharp global stabilization clock.** Fixed points are exactly the
   strictly decreasing compositions, and every orbit reaches one. With
   $\tau(a)$ the number of nonfixed updates before first fixation,
   $$\max_{a\vDash N}\tau(a)=H(N)
     =\max\{h\ge0:1+h(h+1)/2\le N\}.$$
   The linked inductions say that a cut deleted in round $t$ has old left
   mass at least $t$, and every block born in that round has mass at least
   $1+t(t+1)/2$. The witness
   $(h,h-1,\ldots,1,1+r)$ works for every $h\ge1$, $r\ge0$, and total
   $N=1+h(h+1)/2+r$; $N=1$ has time zero. Generic coarsening, decreasing
   length, genealogy and fixed-point recognition receive no separate credit.
2. **Full image threshold and explicit coding.** For a target
   $s=(s_1,\ldots,s_m)$, initialize $r_m=1$ and scan right to left. Given
   $r=r_{i+1}$, fail if $s_i\le r$, set $r_i=s_i$ if $s_i=r+1$, and set
   $r_i=1$ if $s_i\ge r+2$. The target is in $F(\mathcal C_N)$ exactly
   when the scan never fails. Here $r_i$ is the attained minimum first
   part of a feasible refinement of the entire suffix; no interval
   assumption about all feasible first parts is used. The explicit
   weight-preserving map and inverse in the addendum identify the image
   with nonempty compositions whose parts are positive triangular numbers.
   The threshold, bijection and consequent image series are **one**
   structural axis, separate from the temporal induction.
3. **Supporting inverse bookkeeping, zero separate novelty credit.**
   Every preimage is uniquely segmented into weakly increasing refinements
   of the target parts with strict descents between refinements. The
   endpoint-refined partition coefficient formula and its finite transfer
   evaluation are valid on every target, including empty fibres. They may
   appear as self-contained supporting propositions, not a third advance.

Writing $T_k=k(k+1)/2$ and $\Theta(z)=\sum_{k\ge1}z^{T_k}$, the image
series is $\Theta(z)/(1-\Theta(z))$. The triangular composition class,
count, reciprocal series, recurrence and reset/increment code are prior
knowledge. The retained image contribution is the proved identification
of this particular image class, not a new integer sequence or generating
function technique. The auxiliary empty count is not an added $N=0$ carrier.

No maximum-fibre theorem, pointwise closed time formula, all-time inverse,
