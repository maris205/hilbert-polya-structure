## Unchanged theorem scope

fibre into a path, retaining its old destination at its largest member.

1. Recurrent components are directed cycles with disjoint unbranched
   feeding paths, at most one per cycle vertex, whose final path labels
   lie below every cycle label. Attachments rotate one cycle predecessor
   per update. The whole-function period is the least common multiple
   of the lengths of cycles carrying paths; pure cycles are fixed.
2. Admissible increasing path covers of an arbitrary target give a
   nonredundant decoder of every one-step predecessor, including empty
   fibres and the empty-label case.
3. For nonempty label sets, the unique maximum fibre is $2^{n-1}$,
   attained by the cyclic successor permutation. Empty/singleton cases
   are explicit.
