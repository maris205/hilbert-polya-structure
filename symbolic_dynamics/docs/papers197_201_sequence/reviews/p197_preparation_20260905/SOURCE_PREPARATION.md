## Fukś, arXiv:2601.00486v1

[Primary HTML, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00486v1), dated
1 January 2026: *Ternary cellular automata induced by semigroups of order 3
are solvable*, Henryk Fukś. Read scope: abstract, introduction, definitions,
Tables 1–2 and the beginning of Section 3, including its first two basic
propositions in the initial read. No claim of auditing every later solution.

The paper studies two-input ternary local operations that are associative:
113 labelled semigroups, grouped into 18 isomorphism/anti-isomorphism
classes. This is a genuine nearby solvability source, not the literal TCSD
rule. For d(a,b)=sgn(b-a), d(d(-1,-1),0)=0 whereas
d(-1,d(-1,0))=1. The local table therefore fails associativity.
An independent finite test strengthens this limited comparison: none of
the 216 separate input/output symbol permutations of d is associative.
This does not exclude higher-block encodings, spacetime factors or other
prior exact owners. It supplies no priority claim.
