additional systems. The induced complementation operation, generic
least-involution descent, all fixed/two-cycle consequences, generic

After that subtraction, the candidate's usable conjunction is:

1. the all-parameter no-return trace and uniformly attained clock

The value judgement is not based on the formula $\max\{4,n-2\}$ alone.

### Exact Q01 separation control, not a noun comparison

Under the standard upper-triangle tournament encoding, a cyclic triple
$a<b<c$ satisfies $x_{ab}=x_{bc}\ne x_{ac}$; MCT instead requires all
three bits equal. A fixed edgewise XOR change cannot make these predicates
agree on every triple at $n=4$. It would require
$d_{ab}=d_{bc}\ne d_{ac}$ for each triple. The triples $012$ and $013$
force $d_{02}=d_{03}$, contradicting the constraint from $023$.

More strongly, the gate independently computes both complete functional
graphs at $n=4$: MCT has 18 fixed states and maximum tail 1; Q01 has
24 fixed states and maximum tail 2. Thus no arbitrary bijective conjugacy
exists between these two full carriers at that same parameter. This does
not exclude a changed carrier, embedding, power, or quotient; no such
transport supporting a whole-owner kill has been demonstrated. The
control is a separation check, not an additional candidate or theorem axis.

selector lemma: that lemma only bounds by the number of possible labels
and gives the recurrent periods. The colour/no-return geometry replaces
that coarse bound by a sharp vertex budget. Separately, Johnson cliques
alone do not identify which graph targets realize a full star or top;
the literal colour/order tests and both realizations are needed. With all
owned components visibly deducted, this conjunction meets the internal
two-axis scouting threshold. This is an **internal amber selection**
recommendation, conditional on the provenance delta documented separately,
