## Primary source actually read

Florent Foucaud, Narges Ghareghani, Aida Roshany-Tabrizi and Pouyeh Sharifani,
*Characterizing extremal graphs for open neighbourhood location-domination*,
[primary arXiv HTML, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.05322), dated
17 June 2021; [publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2021.06.006).

I opened and read the complete rendered mathematical text, including the
abstract, introduction, Definition2, Propositions3/5, Corollary7 and the
proof of Theorem1. Definition2 calls a vertex location-forced when it is
the unique member of the symmetric difference of two open neighborhoods.
This is precisely ND1's edge-witness motif. The paper characterizes
locatable graphs whose only open-neighborhood locating-dominating set is
the full vertex set; its theorem gives half-graphs. It does not define
the autonomous map that replaces all graph edges by singleton-difference
pairs or supply its iteration/fibre atlas. Its locatable assumption also
excludes isolates and open twins, which cannot be discarded from ND1's
full carrier. The half-graph theorem is not an ND1 recurrence theorem.
Bondy's separating-system result is stated there as background; I did
not separately read Bondy's original paper and claim no such read.

The singleton-witness motif and generic cube/XOR methods receive no
fresh-method credit. No further source is needed for the elementary
proofs in PROOF_PACKAGE.md, but that does not establish their novelty.
