new name. All six colour permutations and both exchanged-input tables

chosen neighbor but does not exchange current/neighbor roles. The extra
argument-exchange control tests a shifted local presentation; it is not
misrepresented as a proof about arbitrary spatial conjugacies.

More decisively, on length two P202 has a 3-cycle and a 2-cycle, hence five
recurrent states. FCA has only its three constant recurrent states; CCA
has three fixed states, and GHM one fixed state. These are actual full
graph controls and disprove a same-length family conjugacy, even by an
arbitrary state permutation, with those displayed models. P196 has a
fixed state and two 2-cycles at n=2, not P202's cycle census. P164 has a
single fixed recurrent state. These elementary invariants do NOT exclude
a different carrier, state extension, factor, iterate, or block coding.
The P164 length-two control evaluates its literal equality map outside
that paper's n>=4 dyadic theorem scope; no theorem extension is asserted.

In particular, the FCA source explicitly relates a time-rescaled particle
factor to CCA/GHM. It would be invalid to treat the lack of a local colour
conjugacy as excluding that broader owner surface. For P202, subtracting
time from the colour makes the exceptional pair (1-t,-t) modulo 3, which
depends on t. That calculation by itself supplies no autonomous CCA
conjugacy, but does not rule out a more elaborate autonomous lift.

The P202 surplus equations are already permanent parking with slots every
third bin. This entire factor, the two possible clearance regimes, basic
particle/hole reasoning, and the easy spacing-three clock receive zero
independent-mechanism credit. The externally owned factor does not retain

claims, not three new general theories.
