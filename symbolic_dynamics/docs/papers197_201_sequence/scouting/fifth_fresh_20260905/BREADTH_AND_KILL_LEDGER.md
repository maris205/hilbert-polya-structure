## Parking owner subtraction: all-target, not a terminology collision

The retained historical source is
`docs/papers172_176_sequence/scouting/fresh_geometry_automata/SCOUT_AND_KILL_LEDGER.md`,
row D05_CPA. That earlier map outputs the assignment permutation, so neither
CPD nor CSPD is a literal repeat of D05_CPA. However it already records the
prescribed-outcome displacement-marked product
$\prod_i(1+z+\cdots+z^{r_i})$. Marking each car separately and summing over
outcomes yields the CPD joint-displacement fibres. Reorganizing that marginal
as a Cartesian-tree recursion does not create a separate inverse mechanism.

The binding external source for CSPD is Lucas Chaves Meyles, Richter Jordaan,
Sam Sehayek and Ethan Spingarn, *Parking Functions of Fixed Displacement*,
Summer@ICERM, 3 August 2022,
[primary slides](https://app.icerm.brown.edu/assets/372/4323/4323_3429_ChavesMeyles-Spingarn-Jordaan_080320221400_Slides.pdf).
Its site-ordered preference normalization and precedence-poset class counts
own the underlying static inverse classes. The independent gate proves a
source-set bijection from **every** circular target fibre to one of those
classes, after a cyclic cut. The forest hook specialization is standard
enumeration on that owned poset. This is an owner/mechanism transfer, not a
claim that the exact iterated circular map was previously published.

Nearby [Kang–Selig–Yang–Zhang–Zhu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06560) and
[Kenyon–Yin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.17180) were checked as parking sources.
The former's term *cyclic parking functions* is an outcome-constrained
classical line-parking class, not this all-words circular carrier. It must
not be used to fabricate a literal collision.
