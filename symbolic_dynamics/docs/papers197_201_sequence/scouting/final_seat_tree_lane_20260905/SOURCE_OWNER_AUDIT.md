## Primary external tree neighbour: actually read

Péter Csikvári, *On a Poset of Trees*, author-hosted 11-page primary PDF:
[author manuscript](https://csikvarip.web.elte.hu/csikvarin2002uj.pdf).
The browser delivered the PDF text; the comparison used §2, Definition 2.1
and Corollary 2.5, plus §3. The generalized shift moves **all** off-path
neighbours of one endpoint to the other when interior path vertices have
degree two. Its induced order has path/star extremal trees. LGB moves one
specified leaf. For an adjacent parent/grandparent this agrees with that
shift only when the parent has no other child. If the parent has several
children, the prescribed generalized shift moves all of them, not just the
least leaf. Thus this is a direct surgery/extremal neighbour, **not** an
established complete owner of LGB's selector or its fibres. Its static
path/star conclusions do not certify LGB's contribution.

The publisher abstract for *On a Poset of Trees II*, J. Graph Theory
74 (2013), 81–103, DOI
[10.1002/jgt.21693](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.21693), was also returned.
It corroborates the generalized-shift setting and spectral applications;
we did not treat that abstract as a complete full-text audit of the second
paper.
