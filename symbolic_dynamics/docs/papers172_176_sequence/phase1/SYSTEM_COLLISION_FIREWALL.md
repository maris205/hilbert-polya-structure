# Cross-paper collision firewall — P172–P176

The five literals live on different carriers and their decisive proofs do
not reduce to one shared normal form.  Shared elementary ingredients receive
zero credit.

| pair/family | superficial overlap | enforced separation |
|---|---|---|
| P172 vs P173 | fresh random maps, nested erosion, small quotients, Jordan obstruction | P172 uses set-image occupancy and Stirling surjections; P173 uses a state-dependent linear quotient and injective maps. | P176 vs P166 | cyclic phase coordinate and an `n-2` clock | P166's cyclic-phase architecture and target indicator are zero credit.  P176 retains only its constrained `+/-k` component theorem, proper-divisor periods, two-branch fibres, and Möbius census. |
| P172/P173/P175 | all have height at most two or absorption language somewhere | height, triangularity, Jordan algebra, and generic finite-map bookkeeping earn no separation credit.  The target-fibre mechanism must remain literal-specific. |

## Hard kill rules during review

1. An exact external owner for a literal theorem conjunction changes the row
   to `KILL` or forces theorem subtraction; a bounded non-hit cannot prevent
   this.
2. If a proof can be obtained from P1–P171 by carrier renaming plus routine
   coefficient substitution, the paper is killed even if the literal map is
   syntactically new.
3. P174 is killed if its pivot interval is shown to be a routine consequence
   of a known adaptive-normalization theorem.
4. P176 is killed if its component/fibre package is conjugate to P166 or a
   known variable-shift map.
5. No reserve may silently replace a failed paper; a replacement requires a
   new frozen contract and an explicit audit trail.
