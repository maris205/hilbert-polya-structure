## Strongest internally nonduplicative theorem windows

### Window A — finite-`P0` exact periodic census

For a finite prime set `P0`, let `X_P0` impose only the missing-residue
constraints modulo `p^2` for `p in P0`. A nonzero point of least period `n`
exists exactly when

```text
rad(P0) = product_{p in P0} p  divides n.
```

Necessity follows because one nonzero `n`-periodic coordinate fills every
residue modulo `p^2` when `p` does not divide `n`. Sufficiency is witnessed by
one residue class modulo `n`. This strictly strengthens P43's single
`Q=product p^2` witness.

With `d_p(n)=gcd(n,p^2)` and `D(n)=product_p d_p(n)`, the proposed exact Fix
census is the finite CRT inclusion–exclusion sum

```text
F_P0(n) = sum_{1 <= b_p <= d_p(n)}
          (-1)^(sum_p (b_p+1))
          product_p binom(d_p(n),b_p)
          2^((n/D(n)) product_p (d_p(n)-b_p)).
```

Möbius inversion then gives exact least-period point and primitive-orbit
counts. The iff theorem, Fix census, primitive census, and AM zeta belong in
one unit; separating them is `STOP_SALAMI`.

### Window B — primorial odometer ownership theorem

Generate the primorial tower by a frozen low-description recursion, form the
profinite adding machine, and give its compact-infinite-alphabet orbit-name
embedding. The paper-sized theorem package must combine:

- absence of positive-period source points;
- classification of finite cyclic factors by divisors of a finite primorial;
- character/pure-point Haar–Koopman spectrum;
- finite-stage identities `det(I-z U_k)=1-z^(Q_k)`;
- proof that the infinite-dimensional Koopman operator is unitary but not
  compact or trace class, so no finite-stage determinant inherits full-object
  Fredholm ownership.

It must not claim conjugacy to a fixed finite-alphabet subshift. A Toeplitz
realization would be another extension/source lock.

### Window C — squarefree-period compact bouquet

A separately locked compact shift may place one primitive cycle at every
squarefree period and include the required limit points. Its expected ledger
is

```text
#Fix(T^n) = product_{p | n} (1+p),
zeta_AM(z) = product_{m squarefree} (1-z^m)^(-1).
```

The compact phase space, continuity, complete primitive census, AM product,
and analytic boundary form one package. It is an assembly derived from a
factor-order catalog, not a factor of the all-prime-square P43 source.

### Window D — Gauss finite-holonomy selector obstruction

Freeze a finite-group/local-system twist of the P40 Gauss-pair object before
any prime/composite comparison. For the one-parameter primitive family

```text
w_t = (1,t-2),
M_t = [[t-1,1],[t-2,1]],
trace(M_t)=t,
```

the image of the relevant parabolic element has finite order `h`. Parameters
congruent modulo `h` have the same finite holonomy. A sufficiently large
prime `ell` and the composite `ell(1+h)` therefore give identical
finite-holonomy data but different trace primality. The eligible theorem is
only a finite-holonomy-only selector obstruction; it cannot quantify over
arbitrary infinite or digit-reading twists.

### Window E — finite-fiber positive-tower calculus (conditional)

This unit survives only if it proves a complete operation calculus rather
than restating P43. For a finite fiber permutation and positive integer roof,
it must classify all lifted cycles, their total lengths, the weighted
permutation determinant, and the exact effect of clopen first return. If the
main proof reduces to “periodic points project to the sole source fixed
point,” it is a corollary and must merge or stop.
