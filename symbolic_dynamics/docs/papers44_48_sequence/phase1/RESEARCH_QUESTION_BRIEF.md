# Research Question Brief — Papers 44–48

## Topic Area

Post-Paper-43 arithmetic symbolic dynamics: selecting a nonduplicative,
source-locked five-paper sequence without treating the historical Session-4
registry as an automatic successor list.

## Primary Research Question

Which source-locked sequence, if any, can support five independently
publishable same-object closure theorems beyond Paper 43 without reusing an
object, claim, clock, marker, operator owner, or determinant owner already
closed in Papers 1–43?

## Scope Boundaries

**In Scope**

- Git authority baseline `6e5658649d2eab0fce077cbcdcc00070dd54095f`
  and its byte-identical plain mirror as the internal evidence cutoff.
- The six immutable Session-4 cards, the P43 commissioned working remainder,
  and every explicit open/terminal boundary in Papers 1–43.
- Symbolic systems only, including finite/countable/compact-alphabet shifts,
  coded systems, symbolic inverse limits, renewal systems, and their natural
  transfer or Koopman objects when ownership is proved.
- Exact theorem statements, primitive/repetition ledgers, clocks, markers,
  determinants, operator domains, adversarial controls, and Route-A v0.2.
- Primary literature available through 2026-08-17, searched in Phase 2.
- A five-paper batch only if all five units survive the independence,
  nonduplication, and source-ownership gates.

**Out of Scope**

- Treating P39 or P43 as a ranking or successor authorization.
- Reusing C03 or C05 unchanged, or inheriting either card's prior A0 credit.
- Riemann-zero fitting, prime tables in the candidate definition, fitted
  coefficients, or Route-B rescue of a weak Route-A candidate.
- A geometric, quantum-graph, flow, general operator-algebra, or unrelated
  Hilbert–Pólya model as the primary object.
- Splitting one theorem into multiple papers merely to reach five.
- Claiming novelty from absence of a search hit.

**Key Assumptions**

- P1–P43 terminal artifacts are accepted as the historical baseline, not as
  transferable proof credit for a new object.
- A new universe may be opened only by a new source lock that names the
  object, arithmetic rule, primitive types, clock, marker, operator owner,
  determinant convention, and forbidden data before canonical results.
- “Five” is a requested upper target, not a license to lower the publishable
  unit: if only `k<5` units survive, the correct result is `STOP_AT_k`.
- Preliminary mathematical sketches made during scoping are retrospective
  context, not blind or outcome-independent evidence.
