# Devil's Advocate Report — Checkpoint 1

## Verdict: PASS

This is a pass to Phase 2 investigation only. It does not authorize P44,
freeze five candidate IDs, or imply that five publishable units exist.

## Critical Issues (Blocks Progression)

No critical issues remain after the scope was revised to permit
`STOP_AT_k` and to distinguish the historical registry, the commissioned
remainder, and a new source universe.

## Major Issues

1. **The requested number can manufacture papers**
   - **Type:** Scope / publication bias.
   - **Location:** Primary RQ and candidate-sequence gate.
   - **Problem:** Local theorem audit supports three robust packages and at
     most one additional plausible package. Roof-only, presentation-only,
     spectrum-only, and typed-closure units would inflate the count without
     independent principal theorems.
   - **Impact:** A forced five-paper outcome would invalidate the sequence
     even if each fragment were mathematically correct.
   - **Recommendation:** Preserve the theorem-independence test and
     `STOP_AT_k`; commission a fifth source only if it has a distinct object,
     arithmetic source, primitive ledger, and owner contract.

2. **New-universe work can be falsely narrated as consuming C03/C05**
   - **Type:** Chronology / source ownership.
   - **Location:** Candidate-universe protocol.
   - **Problem:** `{C03,C05}` is a workflow remainder, not an authorization.
     C05's same-clock branch is closed; a profinite, bouquet, reset, or
     target-intrinsic construction is a new object.
   - **Impact:** Mislabeling would transfer A0 and priority credit across an
     object reset.
   - **Recommendation:** Every new direction must carry the literal status
     `NEW_SOURCE_UNIVERSE`; no C05 A0 or P43 factor credit may transfer.

3. **Internal noncollision is not novelty**
   - **Type:** Evidence / confirmation bias.
   - **Location:** Windows A–E.
   - **Problem:** Odometer finite-factor theory, twisted Gauss operators,
     B-free approximants, and periodic-orbit zeta products have substantial
     established literatures. Local absence in P1–P43 is weak evidence.
   - **Impact:** The strongest-looking Phase-1 units may be exact published
     duplicates.
   - **Recommendation:** Phase 2 must search theorem signatures and primary
     citations, not merely titles; exact collisions trigger
     `STOP_DUPLICATE`.

4. **The conditional fifth unit is especially vulnerable**
   - **Type:** Logic / duplication.
   - **Location:** Window E.
   - **Problem:** A finite-fiber/roof/first-return theorem may collapse to the
     one-line fact that periodic points project to P43's sole fixed point,
     while finite cocycle and first-return ownership were already treated in
     P17–P18, P20, P24, and P34.
   - **Impact:** It cannot currently count as an independent paper.
   - **Recommendation:** Keep it outside the sequence unless literature and a
     full operation-calculus proof establish a distinct principal theorem.

## Minor Issues

- “Same-object closure theorem” must be interpreted per paper, not as one
  object shared across all five papers.
- Compact-alphabet orbit naming is a presentation of an odometer, not by
  itself a symbolic-discovery theorem.
- A finite-prime-square approximant is a supersystem/source reset, not a
  dynamical factor of the P43 source.
- Preliminary theorem sketches already exist, so future selection can be
  content-addressed but cannot honestly be called blind.

## Observations

- Window A has the cleanest internal novelty delta: P43 owns a single
  period-`Q` witness, whereas the proposed unit owns the full least-period,
  Fix, primitive, and AM ledger.
- Window D is potentially strong because the indistinguishability witness is
  explicit and does not require a prime table in the dynamics, but twisted
  transfer ownership must be reconstructed from primary sources.
- A negative Phase-2 result is useful: proving that the program has fewer
  than five legal successors is better than producing five weak manuscripts.

## Strongest Counter-Argument

> The sequence is being designed backward from a requested paper count and
> from known negative conclusions; the proposed “new source locks” merely
> rename standard odometer, B-free, and twisted modular constructions, while
> the final units subdivide routine corollaries.

The only adequate response is not rhetorical. It is the Phase-2
primary-source collision audit plus the independent-theorem and merge/stop
gates already frozen in the blueprint.
