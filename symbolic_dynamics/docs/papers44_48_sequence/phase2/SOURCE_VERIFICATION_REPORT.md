# Source Verification Report — P44–P48 Phase 2

## Verification protocol

For every source used to support a technical or ownership statement, this
review checked the title, author list, year, venue, and persistent identifier
against a DOI/publisher page, arXiv author manuscript, or institutional
repository. Sources found only through snippets were used for discovery and
then either upgraded or excluded. “No exact hit” means only that the recorded
queries and citation chains did not locate the exact theorem package by the
search cutoff; it never means “proved novel.”

## Candidate-specific verification

### q-adic finite-size spectrum of multiplicative SFTs

S28 and S36 own the multiplicative SFT/golden-mean object, its orbit-chain
product, Fibonacci counts, leading entropy, and leading dimension. S37 owns
the closest named boundary-complexity and surface-entropy framework, while
S38 extends leading fractal dimensions to affine multiplicative shifts.
Full-text searches of these sources found no `q`-adic continuous extension
of the exact order-one remainder, no equality identifying its complete
accumulation set, and no Cantor dimension for that boundary image.

The eligible Paper 44 delta is therefore only

```text
log Z(N)-log Z(N-1)=c_(nu_q(N)),
log Z(N)=hN+E(N),  E continuous on Z_q,
Acc(log Z(N)-hN)=E(Z_q),
```

together with the golden strong-separation theorem and its scoped natural-
boundary corollary. Object definition, chain product, leading entropy, and
leading Hausdorff/Minkowski dimension are explicitly excluded from novelty.
Two independent proof/source passes returned `GO`; this remains an absence
search, not a priority claim.

### Isospectral arithmetic fiber retractions

S39–S40 own generic discrete weighted-composition boundedness, compactness,
spectrum, and commutant questions.  S42 supplies classical power-free-part
terminology; the arithmetic maps themselves are not novelty. Internally,
Papers 27–29 already own oblique similarity, adjoint/Gram/Schatten, and
counterterm mechanisms, while Paper 30 owns the free-UFD firewall and Paper
43 uses an `h`-free inventory in another object. The single radical
operator's rank-one blocks, ideal criterion, and determinant ledger are
therefore not a paper.

The strengthened all-`h` candidate compares two fixed arithmetic maps,

```text
tau_h(n)=product p^min(v_p(n),h-1),
omega_h(n)=product p^(v_p(n) mod h).
```

They have the same `h`-free eigenvalues and power traces but different
bounded domains, Riesz-projection maxima, normal-similarity domains, singular
Weyl constants, and self-commutator ideal walls. Directed searches across
S39–S40, weighted-composition citation chains, `rad(n)`/squarefree-kernel
operators, Riesz projection growth, and arithmetic Weyl laws found no exact
paired classification. The eligible contribution is this exact arithmetic
comparison and its Tauberian/maximal-order laws, not the generic fiber method.
A separately commissioned proof/ownership audit reproduced the exact
Tauberian strip, residue, similarity iff, maximal-order coefficient, and all
strict ideal endpoints after the `C_(h,1)=D_(h,1)=1` correction.  Its final
decision is `GO_WITH_FIREWALL`.

### Withdrawn finite-B and perfect-power proposals

S21–S22 and S34 reduce the finite-prime-square proposal to a short CRT
specialization plus standard inversion. S33 reduces the all-`k`
perfect-power kernel to a short local residue computation inside a generic
prime tensor theorem. Both formulas are correct, but the standalone verdicts
are respectively `STOP_SALAMI / MERGE_CONTROL` and
`STOP_SALAMI / MERGE_WORKED_EXAMPLE`. S41 additionally removes any novelty
credit for the finite-stage Perron pole necklace in the backup nested-PFT
candidate.

### Withdrawn power-free-gcd control

S04–S06 own finite gcd/meet factorization and finite coprimality interactions.
The tested object was

```text
G_{h,s}(m,n) = 1_{gcd(m,n) is h-free} (mn)^(-s/2), h >= 1.
```

Its proposed delta combined:

- finite exact factorization
  `1_{gcd h-free}=sum_{d^h|m,n} mu(d)` and resulting inertia;
- a prime-local rank-two operator at every prime;
- exact local two-eigenvalue recurrences for every power trace;
- the global Fredholm determinant in the common trace-class half-plane.

No exact full-package source was located, but after subtracting the
meet-matrix factorization the remaining local recurrences and Euler products
were too mechanical for a standalone unit.  The final verdict is
`MERGE_CONTROL / STOP_SALAMI`; it is not Paper 45.  “Coprimality is new” is
forbidden.

### Dyadic-sum Hankel kernel

S07–S10 and S18 were checked separately. S18 studies finite Hankel
determinants of the unweighted characteristic sequence of powers of two.
That is a real proximity hit but not the same operator as

```text
H_s(m,n)=1_{m+n is a power of two}(mn)^(-s/2)
```

on `ell^2(N)`. S07–S09 own generic analytic methods; S10 owns finite graph
label equations. No source located the combined sharp `S_2/S_1` transition,
`v_2` direct-sum self-similarity, odd/even closed-cycle solution theorem, and
`det_2` trace product. The honest novelty wording is “exact arithmetic
specialization and closure,” not “first lacunary Hankel operator.”

### Harmonic/Egyptian adjacency kernel

The candidate relation

```text
m+n divides mn  <=>  1/m+1/n=1/k for some integer k
```

has the unique coprime-scale parameterization

```text
m=t a(a+b), n=t b(a+b), k=t ab, gcd(a,b)=1.
```

Searches for integer harmonic-mean graphs, reciprocal-sum graphs, the above
factorization, and weighted Schatten operators found unrelated “harmonic
mean graph labeling” but no exact infinite operator package.  The coprime-
scale parametrization is an elementary derivation and receives zero novelty
credit; no external ownership is asserted for that derivation. The proposed
second trace is a primitive
Mordell–Tornheim type sum; that series family is classical and cannot be
claimed as new. S27 owns that series family but not its realization as an
exact second trace. Independent proof and literature passes classify the
operator package as `GO`, with classical-series ownership subtracted.

### Radix carry-free weighted kernel

S23–S24 own the exact arithmetic predicate

```text
m & n = 0  iff  binom(m+n,m) is odd.
```

S25–S26 and S30–S32 own finite disjointness/binomial/Pascal matrix
structure, including the binary Lucas closed-walk census. The candidate does
not claim any of these components. Its eligible delta is the all-radix
Dirichlet-weighted infinite adjacency

```text
B_{b,s}(m,n)=1_{m+n has no carry in base b}(mn)^(-s/2),
```

the full theorem `B_{b,s} in S_q` iff
`Re(s)>max(1,log_b ||C_b||_{S_q})`, the endpoint proof by orthogonal shell
pinching, the `det_2` closed-walk ledger, and the countable positive-vertex
trace/least-period ledger. Prime bases recover Kummer's binomial criterion.
The binary case has `alpha_2=log_2(sqrt(5))` and trace zero; bases above two
have loops and a nonzero digit-restricted trace.  Every finite positive-prefix
or zero-completed census is a zero-credit validation control.
Searches around S23–S26 and S30–S32 did not
locate this weighted operator or critical exponent family. This supports
`NO_EXACT_HIT_FOUND`; it does not establish priority. Composite-base,
randomized-digit-mask, and unweighted finite-matrix controls are required to
prevent the Kummer predicate from being mistaken for operator novelty.

### Rejected and merged directions

- Primorial odometer: measure isomorphism/pure-point spectrum and finite
  factors are classical; non-trace-class Koopman is immediate.
- Finite-holonomy Gauss selector: exact finite-holonomy no-go is too narrow
  and generic twisted Mayer/Fredholm theory is established.
- Finite fiber plus positive roof: a short corollary of P43 and standard
  finite-group extension/suspension zeta theory.
- Beta `3/2`: exact least-period set may be a new short corollary, but S15 and
  Parry/Sigmund sources own the framework; `STOP_STANDALONE`.
- Finite-prime visible-lattice census: exact formula not found, but S13–S14
  plus P43 make it a high-risk same-mechanism extension; `MERGE` unless a new
  principal theorem is supplied.
- Finite-field simplex: special formulas are standard local group-algebra
  consequences inside S11–S12; two selected sequences are insufficient.
- Carry shift: S16–S17 own the matrix and spectrum; standard Möbius orbit
  conversion is not a new paper.
- Cyclic multiplicative SFT: S28–S29 plus standard closed-walk trace and
  Möbius formulas own its two main components. Its combined formula is a
  useful verified control but is `STOP_CLASSICAL_COMPONENT_ASSEMBLY`.

## Search limitations

1. Citation databases incompletely index formulas and theses.
2. Exact absence cannot be established by keyword search.
3. Several proposed formulas are elementary consequences of known general
   frameworks even without a literal published match; this is handled by the
   separate salami/technical-distance gate.
4. Sources published after 2026-08-18 are outside this freeze.
