## Quantifier and ownership firewalls

### P44 — q-adic finite-size laws

Admissible: the exact one-step valuation law, bounded remainder on `Z_q`,
complete accumulation image, golden strong-separation Cantor dimension, and
the scoped lacunary natural-boundary corollary.  Forbidden: novelty for the
multiplicative SFT, chain product, Fibonacci counts, entropy, Hausdorff or
Minkowski dimension of the underlying shift, or the general concept of
boundary complexity.  Continuous Minkowski-content nonexistence is excluded
unless proved separately from the exact dyadic-scale content spectrum.

### P45 — paired arithmetic retractions

Admissible: the all-`h` saturated/modulo comparison, exact bounded domains,
two explicit singular Weyl laws (including their `sigma=1` coincidence),
common eigenvalue ledger, maximal primorial
Riesz growth, normal-similarity iff classification, and self-commutator
ideal law.  Forbidden: novelty for weighted composition, rank-one fibers,
generic oblique projections, generic Gram/Schatten geometry, regularized
determinants, or the free-UFD method.  The paper must not claim completion of
an open P27 task: P28 already studies the adjoint route.  The free-UFD clone
must reproduce all structural thresholds.

### P46 — dyadic-sum

Admissible: exact weighted power-of-two-sum phase diagram, `v_2` orthogonal
decomposition, and odd/even cycle/determinant calculus.  Forbidden: generic
Hankel theory, finite determinants/label optimization, an unproved all-`S_p`
threshold, or a sequel split by another additive support.

### P47 — harmonic/Egyptian

Admissible: unique coprime-scale edge decomposition, exact phase diagram,
zeta trace, primitive Mordell–Tornheim second trace, and mixed-cycle ledger.
Forbidden: novelty for Egyptian parametrization or multiple zeta series;
deleting loops; replacing endpoints by numerics; or counting another conic
kernel with the same ideal proof as a separate paper.

### P48 — radix carry-free

Admissible: the all-radix infinite weighted shell theorem, exact equality
endpoints, weighted trace/`det_2`, and corrected infinite least-period set.
Forbidden: novelty for Kummer/Lucas, finite Boolean/binomial/Pascal or
disjointness matrices, finite tensor norms/spectra, or either finite census.
Binary trace zero cannot be extended to odd radices.  All finite-prefix
formulas are zero-credit controls.
