# Historical collision seed and pre-batch exclusions — P167–P171

This is the discovery-stage firewall, not the final five-way collision matrix.
It records what a candidate must survive before a paper number is allocated.

## Comparison coordinates

Every live candidate is compared on all of the following coordinates:

- carrier and literal update;
- canonical reduction or sufficient statistic;
- forward invariant and temporal proof engine;
- recurrent or terminal classification;
- inverse, fibre, reconstruction, or extremal proof engine; and
- direct literature owner of the retained theorem conjunction.

Shared generic outputs such as fixed points, tails, fibres, image towers, or
zeta functions earn no separation credit.  A new name, application story, or
carrier repaint also earns no credit if the operative reduction and proof
transfer from an occupied paper.

## Permanent pre-batch kills

Three systems explored before this batch remain excluded:

- **GCM:** exact but its traffic-transducer mechanism collides with P90;
- **SRW:** directly owned by star-transposition/perfect-matching dynamics;
- **OFP:** directly collapses to a polarity involution.

They may be used only as negative controls.  Rephrasing or parameter changes
do not reopen them.

## Conditional reserve

The minimum-inverse endofunction map (MIP) enters this batch only as a
conditional reserve.  Its proposed sharp height, recurrent species, fixed and
iterate counts, target-fibre formula, Bell extremum, boundary cases, and owner
neighbourhood must all be independently rederived and hostile-tested.  It
receives no paper number merely because an earlier scout produced a coherent
contract.

## Kill switch

Any literal duplicate, direct conjugacy, direct owner, or proof transfer that
covers the retained two-axis theorem package kills or reopens the candidate.
Internal collision clearance is not an external novelty or freedom-to-operate
certificate.
