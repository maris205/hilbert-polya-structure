## P167 / MIP — minimum inverse-position feedback

at which the value `i` occurs, with identity default `i` when `i` is absent.

cycles and loop-rooted paths, with cycles inverted and paths reversed or
split according to the root comparison.  It may prove sharp full-carrier and
first-image heights `2n-2` and `2n-3`, the recurrent connected-species EGF,
fixed involutions, fixed-iterate counts, and the finite-map zeta function.

including zero fibres, and prove the sharp maximum fibre `B_n`, attained by
the identity.  Functional-digraph decomposition, first-occurrence/RGF
language, transformation-semigroup inverse conventions, Bell enumeration,
and zeta conversion receive zero contribution credit.

## P168 / QIS — quartic inverse-span dynamics

uniform permutation and replace `A` by `A intersect Fix(pi)`.  For every


```text

R_(n,s)(u) = u^s product_(q=0)^(n-s-1)(u+q).
```

## P171 / BGM — Boolean Gram dynamics
