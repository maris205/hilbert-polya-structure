## Intake-level hard exclusions

- Generic powers or roots in finite groups, semigroups, matrices, or functional
  digraphs without a map-specific inverse/clock theorem.
- Another split/join/shift closure on partitions, subspaces, relations, or
  ideals whose proof is transferred from P109, P110, P126, P135, or a standard
  closure operator.
- Another record, border, run, palindrome, Lyndon, prefix-majority, or
  continued-fraction queue transform obtained by changing parity or direction.
- Another graph component complement, MIS/cover greedy process, relation
  cubing, tournament score correction, or rooted-forest pruning under a new
  schedule.
- Another adjacent contraction/coalescence, quota/exposure stopping rule, or
  exponential-race wrapper whose only residual is a classical waiting-time
  identity.
- Any static support/census theorem re-presented as dynamical progress after a
  one-step retraction.

## Required finalist comparison

Every finalist must identify its closest occupied paper and compare:

1. carrier and literal update;
2. temporal silhouette and sharp witness;
3. inverse/fibre or endpoint object;
4. primary proof engine;
5. externally owned inputs receiving zero credit.

A direct conjugacy, same-map owner, or transferred main theorem kills the
candidate immediately.  A bounded source non-hit records only unresolved owner
risk and never clears novelty or priority.
