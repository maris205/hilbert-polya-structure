# Devil's-advocate checkpoint 1 — before numbering

## Hostile questions

1. Is the candidate a complement, quotient, orientation change, scheduler
   variation, or carrier encoding of P1–P141 or a standard map?
2. Does it have a temporal theorem beyond a static support/counting result?
3. Does the apparent main theorem collapse after the closest primary owner is
   written in the same coordinates?
4. Is every sharp bound backed by an all-parameter witness and proof route?
5. Does the inverse theorem consume only the advertised data, without an
   oracle for the hidden object?
6. Is computation being used as falsification rather than proof or novelty
   evidence?

## Findings

- Stack sorting, numerical-semigroup trees, Meir--Moon cuts, relation powers,
  generic Cayley walks, Cremona inversion, and several one-step closures fail
  questions 1--3 and were permanently killed.
- The initially strong sibling-min tree cascade failed question 3 after a
  direct priority-forest transfer was located.
- P142 exposes a full temporal and inverse atlas immediately, but its finite
  tent normalization is explicitly subtracted.
- P143 and P144 survive only under owner-thin contracts; their static
  preorder/Catalan ingredients receive no residual credit.
- P145 initially passed the computational test but question 3 remained open
  and was deliberately carried into hostile review; it later triggered the
  folded-hypercube subtraction.
- P146 has a fixed pathwise clock, so its temporal value must come from the
  endpoint/history distribution and sharp mass theorem, not from absorption
  time alone.

## Decision

Five systems may be numbered internally, subject to theorem ceilings and a
second checkpoint after every inverse object and ownership boundary is
formalized.  External status remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
