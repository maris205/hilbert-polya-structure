## Cross-paper stress test

The systems use five different carriers: divisors, Boolean relations, Dyck
paths, graph-orientation push orbits, and shrinking convex polygons.  The
proof engines are valuation branches, relational residuals/poset embeddings,
first-return reassociation, factor-polynomial root peeling, and weak-dual leaf
orders.  No selected pair shares both an update mechanism and a theorem
spine.  The Boolean Gram and dihedral normalizer reserves remain excluded to
avoid a second Boolean-matrix or algebraic-tower paper.

## Freeze decision

All five narrowed contracts have an all-parameter deductive route and an
exact falsifier.  P143, P144, and P146 are owner-thin; P145 is retained only
after a direct-owner repair; P142 retains a cosmetic-lift warning.  Stage 2
may continue internally.  Novelty, priority, authorship, public release,
submission, and external contact remain forbidden.
