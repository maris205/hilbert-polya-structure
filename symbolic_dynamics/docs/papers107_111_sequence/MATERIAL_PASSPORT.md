statements; those rest on the written arguments.  No GPU, sampling, or

negative evidence is retained rather than erased:

  because of the direct lattice-sorting neighbor and the generic sorting/
  0-Hecke firewall.

  occupied all-matrix/collapse classes.
- Cyclic gcd erosion, degree-parity switching, clipped random paths, paired
  ideals, and a `UT_4` word cocycle remain owner-heavy or collision-heavy
