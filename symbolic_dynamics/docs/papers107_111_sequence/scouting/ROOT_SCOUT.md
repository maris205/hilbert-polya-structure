# Root candidate notes: P107--P111

Status: discovery only.  No candidate below owns a paper number until its
direct-owner and internal-collision gates pass.

## R1: paired-ideal sum--product dynamics

- Phase space: ordered pairs of ideals of `Z/NZ`.
- Update: `(I,J) -> (I+J,IJ)`.
- First signal: in a prime-exponent coordinate `0<=e,f<=a`, the first step
  gives `(m,min(a,e+f))`, where `m=min(e,f)`, and thereafter the first
  coordinate freezes while the second rises by `m` until saturation.
- Possible package: exact iterate, fixed-set product, complete transient CDF,
  sharp depth and finite zeta.
- Independent routes: literal ideal arithmetic using gcds; CRT exponent
  coordinates and a finite-sum CDF.
- Main risks: another arithmetic valuation slot; possible semiring-dynamics
  owner.  Search absence will not count as novelty evidence.

## R2: regular-nilpotent subspace saturation -- killed

- Phase space: the full subspace lattice of `F_q^d`.
- Update: `U -> U+NU`, with `N` one regular nilpotent Jordan block.
- Proposed signal: the absorption depth would depend only on the gaps of the
  Schubert pivots.
- Falsification: already over `F_2^4`, the subspace generated by `e_3` and
  `e_4+e_2` saturates in one step although its pivot-gap prediction is two.
  Coefficients therefore split every relevant Schubert cell.
- Independent routes: Krylov-span leading pivots; exhaustive finite-field
  RREF enumeration.
- Decision: `KILL_NO_CLOSED_SECOND_PROBE`.  The update is also the standard
  reachable-subspace closure and was previously held as an owner-risk reserve;
  coefficient-sensitive controllability indices would consume a paper slot
  without a clean new invariant.

## R3: signed escort dynamics (uncommitted)

- Phase space: the positive probability simplex.
- Update: normalize coordinatewise powers, `p_i -> p_i^alpha/sum_j p_j^alpha`.
- First signal: centered log ratios are multiplied exactly by `alpha`.
- Possible package: complete parameter-regime classification, fixed and
  periodic sets, Hilbert-metric scaling, boundary limits and random-exponent
  extension.
- Main risks: escort distributions and replicator maps are mature; the exact
  conjugacy may leave no residual owner-subtracted contribution.

## R4: annihilator-power ideal dynamics

- Phase space: the ideals of `Z/NZ`.
- Update: `I -> Ann(I)^r`, for an integer `r>=2`.
- First signal: on a `p^a` coordinate the map is
  `e -> min(a,r(a-e))`.  Its only recurrent states are the endpoint two-cycle
  `{0,a}` and, exactly when `(r+1)|a`, one interior fixed exponent.
- Possible package: an exact signed-deviation iterate until saturation,
  perfect-power fixed-point anomaly, full one/two-cycle census and zeta,
  coordinatewise transient-depth law, and CRT product profiles.
- Independent routes: literal annihilator/power arithmetic on divisor ideals;
  prime-exponent dynamics with the deviation
  `D=(r+1)e-ra`, which obeys `D -> -rD` before saturation.
- Main risks: annihilator ideals are classical and their graphs have a large
  literature.  The temporal self-map must survive a direct-owner search, and
  selecting it would exclude the paired-ideal candidate R1 on diversity
  grounds.

## R5: capped Fibonacci dynamics

- Phase space: the finite square `{0,...,a}^2`.
- Update: `(x,y) -> (y,min(a,x+y))`.
- First signal: the exact iterate is the coordinatewise cap of the Fibonacci
  matrix iterate.  Except for `(0,0)`, every state reaches `(a,a)`.
- Possible package: exact iterate, two fixed points and no other cycles,
  Fibonacci-threshold sharp depth, every transient CDF as a lattice-point
  count under `F_{t-1}x+F_t y<a`, finite zeta, and cap recovery.
- Independent routes: monotonicity plus the capped-addition identity; literal
  functional graphs and an independent half-plane counter.
- Main risks: saturated linear recurrences may have a direct digital-systems
  owner; Fibonacci mechanisms occur in P83/P89, while cap language occurs in
  P101.  It advances only if the combined object/action remains distinct.

## Current decision

R1 and R4 proceed to bounded owner checks but compete for at most one
arithmetic-ideal slot.  R2 is killed by its independent coefficient-sensitive
probe.  R3 is only a diversity fallback and should be killed immediately if
the temporal classification is directly owned.
