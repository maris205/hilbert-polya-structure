# Problem anchor — P107–P111

## Objective

Continue Route A by finding many genuinely different dynamical systems with
an early exact signal that expands into a short, auditable theorem package.
Breadth of phase spaces and update mechanisms is a hard objective.  A
candidate is rejected when its residual result is directly owned,
internally duplicated, or too thin after subtraction.

## Closure rule

A provisional slot must have:

1. a phase space and update rule separated from P1–P106 and the other four
   slots;
2. a theorem contract stronger than an example or computational pattern;
3. two proof/control routes with materially different failure modes;
4. an explicit background-owner subtraction and bounded-search limitation;
5. a paper-local exact verifier with stored output;
6. two retained nonauthor hostile reviews, a clean compiled PDF, final QA,
   and exact hashes before batch freeze.

No GPU experiment is relevant.  These are finite algebraic, combinatorial,
and probabilistic systems; exhaustive exact arithmetic is the appropriate
falsification layer, while the manuscripts must prove the infinite-family
claims.
