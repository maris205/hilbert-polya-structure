The closest object is siteswap: `j+a_j mod n` being a permutation directly
owns the recurrent occupancy slice and its gap language.  It does not iterate
the Hamming-weight state map, count its transient shells, or provide its
arbitrary-target inverse formula.  Mapping parking begins with an externally
chosen functional digraph; it does not derive `g_m(j)=j+m_j` from the target's
own multiplicities.  Unit-interval parking owns an ordered-Bell occurrence,
not the dynamical conjunction.  These distinctions leave a residual theorem
package but not a novelty claim.
