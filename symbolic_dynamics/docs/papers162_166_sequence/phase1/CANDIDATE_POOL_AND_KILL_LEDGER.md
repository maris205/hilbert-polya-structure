## Counting rules

1. Different parameter values, primes, ranks, carrier caps, and exhaustive
   boxes of one formula count once.
2. A stronger contract, owner dossier, hostile gate, repair, or independent
   verifier of the same map adds zero.
3. Two presentations related by an explicit state-space bijection and the
   same update count once.  A merely shared proof engine does **not** merge two
   different literal maps; both are counted and the later one is killed for
   collision.
4. An exact P1--P161 re-entry is not a new P162--P166 system.  It is shown as a
   zero-addition duplicate lane.
5. A candidate mentioned only as a lead or permanent-kill sentinel is not
   counted unless a new literal system was actually admitted and tested.

“Admitted” below therefore means admitted to exact or theorem-level probing
after the local desk screen.  It does not mean promoted, novel, proved in full,
or publishable.

## What 308 does and does not mean

The phrase **308 distinct literal systems** is a breadth accounting statement.
It means that 308 different update rules or Markov kernels were admitted to a
documented small-box or theorem-level probe after the deductions above.

It does **not** mean that 308 dynamical subclasses were validated.  In
particular:

- many rows were screened only on bounded carriers before an early KILL;
- a deterministic verifier supplies counterexample pressure, not an
  all-parameter proof;
- several proved formulas are direct consequences of classical owners and
  receive zero contribution credit;
- many distinct literals share one already occupied proof engine; and
- bounded owner-search non-hits are not novelty, priority, freedom-to-operate,
  or release evidence.

The defensible progress statement is therefore: **308 distinct literal
systems were admitted and screened/tested; five in-scope systems remain GREEN
or retained, one remains nonselectable amber, and 302 are killed.**  The live
P162--P166 paper set contains five papers only because P163/CSD was imported
from the earlier reserve lane.  Everything remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
