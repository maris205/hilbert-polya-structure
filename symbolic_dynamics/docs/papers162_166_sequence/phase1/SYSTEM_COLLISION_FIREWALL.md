**Decision:** five literal systems are pairwise separated for internal paper
allocation.  **External state:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

Generic finite-map outputs—fixed points, tails, fibres, image towers, and
zeta functions—do not establish separation.  The gate compares carrier,
literal update, invariant/reduction, forward proof engine, and inverse proof

## Nearest historical silhouettes

- P128 has a translation–GCD all-depth/fibre package, but acts on monic
  polynomials through semilattice erosion; it transfers no selected proof.
- P137 uses a current-statistic feedback on integer partitions.  That
  resemblance killed the nullity-feedback Jordan candidate, but does not
  transfer HWT's diagonal histogram phase map or marked occupancy inverse.
- P138 is a binary palindromic-prefix XOR feedback map and also has a sharp
  `n-2` tail plus a target decoder.  Its prefix-palindrome recursion is not a
  quotient, conjugacy, or proof of HWT; the matching numerical height is
  explicitly treated as a generic silhouette.
- P145 and P158 use random graph deletion/intersection; only P162 is random
  in the live batch, and its sufficient statistic and inverse grammar are
  different from those earlier systems.
- P63/P78/P96/P126 and the killed `HWR`, `AQN`, and `XCT` lanes were checked
  explicitly at the HWT gate.  Diagonal group action, cyclic action, ordered
  compositions, and translation language are zero-credit background, not a
  reason to conflate the literal maps.

## Negative controls that enforced the gate

Mathematically correct candidates were killed when a proof engine did
transfer.  Examples include reciprocal Fibonacci windows after linear
conjugacy, cyclic neighbour-GCD after literal semilattice-erosion detection,
nilpotent nullity-feedback powers after the P137 partition-feedback match,
traceless determinant shear after Artin–Schreier product conjugacy, and
odd-subtree contraction after the P148 pruning silhouette.  These records
show that the five live allocations were not selected from title-level
differences.

## Kill switch

A future direct conjugacy, literal duplicate, or proof transfer covering a
retained conjunction reopens that paper.  Pairwise internal separation is
not an external novelty or freedom-to-operate certificate.
