## Proof of Contract D

For a singleton source of rank `k`, the even covering radius is the Johnson
diameter `min(k,n-k)`.  The largest odd defect is

```text
max_C(|A intersect C|-1)=min(k-1,n-k),
```

which proves (D1).  Separating the parity of `d` in (D1) yields (D2), and
choosing the atom yields (D3).

For the arbitrary-family shell, write `n=2m` first.  Every occupied slice
satisfies `e_k<=min(k,n-k)<=m` and
`o_k<=min(k-1,n-k)<=m-1`.  Tail `2m-1` therefore requires simultaneously
`e(F)=m` and `o(F)=m-1`.  Only rank `m` can attain the first equality.  In
`J(2m,m)`, a vertex has a unique point at distance `m`, namely its
complement.  Thus `e_m(F_m)=m` exactly when `F_m` is a singleton; such a
singleton also has `o_m=m-1`.

Now let `n=2m+1`.  The even bound is `e(F)<=m`, while `o(F)<=m`.
Tail `2m` requires both equalities.  Only rank `m+1` can attain the odd
equality.  If `o_(m+1)(F_(m+1))=m`, some `(m+1)`-set `C` has
`|A intersect C|-1=m` for every `A` in the slice.  Hence every such `A`
equals `C`, so the slice is a singleton.  Conversely that singleton attains
both radii.  This proves (D4), and free choice outside the middle layer gives
(D5).

For fixed support `R`, the middle singleton has `M_n` choices, each other
occupied rank slice has `2^C(n,k)-1` choices, absent slices are forced, and
the silent atom is optional.  This proves (D6).  The recurrent atlas already
shows that eventual period one is equivalent to involution-invariant support.
Within a `phi`-orbit away from the middle rank, invariant support is either
empty on the entire orbit or nonempty on every rank; the orbit containing
`k_star` is forced nonempty.  Multiplication proves (D7)--(D8).
