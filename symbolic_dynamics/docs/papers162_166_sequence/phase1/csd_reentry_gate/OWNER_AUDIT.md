### Inverse counting

The formula

```text
2 sum_(J subseteq G)(-1)^|J| 2^c_t(G\J)
```

is the ordinary inclusion--exclusion count of admissible atomic kernels whose
union covers `G`, times the optional silent atom.  Its correctness is useful
for completeness, but the mechanism is generic and cannot be the residual
inverse axis.  No source search is needed to manufacture contribution credit
for classical inclusion--exclusion.

## Why Contract D survives the subtraction

The strongest hostile reduction is:

1. the square is owned Johnson dilation;
2. the powerset lift is a generic finite-relation image;
3. the inverse atlas is generic cover inclusion--exclusion; and
4. recurrent/zeta bookkeeping is automatic after the base relation is
   decomposed.

Those reductions do **not** directly give the complete deepest shell.  One
must combine both parity clocks across every occupied rank, determine which
of the two global bounds is decisive for even and odd `n`, prove equality
rigidity at the central rank, and then exploit the fact that all noncentral
slices are genuinely free.  That yields the exact full-phase count

```text
C(n,ceil(n/2)) 2^(2^n-C(n,ceil(n/2)))
```

and the support/period factorization (D6)--(D8).  Neither a Johnson ball
volume nor a generic target-cover formula contains that free-layer census.
The ingredients remain standard; the residual credit belongs only to their
specific extremal dynamical conjunction.

## Co-batch audit against RTI and CEF

- **RTI:** stochastic intersections of translates in `F_2^d`; its surviving
  axis is a target-stabilizer/source-history polynomial.  CSD is deterministic,
  uses a shadow-incidence Boolean lift, and its surviving axis is a complete
  extremal transient shell.  The two share a broad powerset vocabulary but no
  exact scheduler, sufficient statistic, or fibre engine.
- **CEF:** a `q`-ary cyclic word map enters a nilpotent binary difference
  system; its prospective residual is the nonlinear change-mask/affine-code
  interface and a full depth CDF.  CSD has neither a linear tail nor an affine
  fibre.  The fact that both have depth enumeration is architectural only.

CSD can therefore sit in the same batch without literal or proof-engine
duplication.  It should rank behind RTI and behind CEF if CEF passes its own
independent hostile gate, because CSD's owner subtraction is heavier.
