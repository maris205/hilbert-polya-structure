## P162 / RTI — random translation intersection

uniform `v in V` and replace `A` by `A intersect (A+v)`.  The paper may prove
the exact history-span identity, the Gaussian rank law, and the sharp
worst-source emptying clock witnessed by `V\{0}`.  For every prescribed target
`B`, it may prove the stabilizer-weighted joint source-size/history polynomial

```text
z^|B| sum_(r=0)^s [s choose r]_2 S(t,r)
 ((1+z)^(2^r)-z^(2^r))^(2^(d-r)-|B|/2^r),
```

where `s=dim Stab(B)`, together with the boundary-safe one-step specialization
and recovery of `s`.  Erosion algebra, generic stochastic morphology, and
finite-field random-rank laws are mandatory zero-credit inputs.




