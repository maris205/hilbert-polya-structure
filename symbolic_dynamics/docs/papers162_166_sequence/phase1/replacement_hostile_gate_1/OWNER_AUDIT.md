## 2. BQC source subtraction

### 2.1 Direct and near-direct owners

1. **Liu, Li, You, Hua, and Chen, _Enumeration of spanning trees and
   resistance distances of generalized blow-up graphs_, arXiv:2504.15314,
   DOI `10.1016/j.dam.2025.11.014`.**  The primary
   [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15314) explicitly enumerates
   spanning trees of generalized blow-up graphs and extends multiple
   2021--2025 blow-up results.  It directly occupies the determinant/counting
   layer represented by BQC equation (B3).  BQC receives no credit for a
   blow-up spanning-tree formula as such.

2. **_Localizing spanning trees and arbors to vertex-induced partitions_,
   JCMCC 131 (2026), 429--440, DOI `10.61091/jcmcc131-20`.**  The primary
   [journal page](https://combinatorialpress.com/jcmcc-articles/volume-131/localizing-spanning-trees-and-arbors-to-vertex-induced-partitions/)
   states edge-variable spanning-tree/arbor generating functions relative to
   a vertex partition, factorization through a contracted multigraph, and an
   exact partition identity.  It was published online on 2026-08-12.  Its
   local condition is not BQC's: it requires the restriction in every part
   to be a spanning tree, while a BQC source tree may induce a forest or a
   disconnected subgraph inside a block.  It is therefore not a literal
   owner, but it is a strong and extremely current adjacent owner.

3. **Parthasarathy, _Enumeration of graphs with a prescribed partition_,
   Canadian Journal of Mathematics 20 (1968), DOI
   [`10.4153/CJM-1968-005-0`](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1968-005-0).**
   Prescribed-partition graph enumeration is prior territory; no value is
   assigned merely for stratifying source graphs by a fixed block partition.

4. Quotient graphs, existential adjacency under a partition, Kirchhoff's
   Matrix--Tree theorem, Cayley's formula, and ordinary Boolean
   inclusion--exclusion are classical inputs and receive zero contribution
   credit.

### 3.2 Residual theorem value

The bounded search found no primary source giving RTI's exact conjunction,
and in particular none giving the polynomial

```text
z^|B| sum_(H<=Stab(B))
  (# ordered histories spanning H)
  ((1+z)^|H|-z^|H|)^(# outside H-cosets).
```

This is materially more than juxtaposing morphology and random rank:

- the rank law sees only `dim H` and does not see a target at all;
- forward erosion theory determines `E_H(A)` but does not enumerate all
  sources by cardinality for every prescribed target;
- the new constraint `H<=Stab(B)` and the proper-subset condition on each
  outside affine coset are precisely what create the target-dependent
  inverse atlas; and
- same-size targets with different stabilizers have different inverse mass,
  so the statistic is not a disguised size/rank marginal.

Owner decision: bounded non-hit for the complete target-stabilizer atlas;
`GREEN_AFTER_REQUIRED_MINOR_REPAIR`, not a novelty claim.
