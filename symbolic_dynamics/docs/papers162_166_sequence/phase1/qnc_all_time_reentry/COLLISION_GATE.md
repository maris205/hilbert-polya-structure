## 3. Proof-template decomposition

The proposed package factors into three templates.

1. **Valuation clock.**  Once an orbit enters `p^2`, valuation rises by one
   per step; the outer shell supplies one cancellation.  This is the old
   QNC axis and is close to the P100/P107/P142/P157 arithmetic portfolio.
2. **Singular quadratic fibres after a target permutation.**  The
   permutation `B_t` is new bookkeeping, but its positive histogram is the
   same critical-quadratic histogram at reduced precision and is directly
   covered by desJardins--Zieve.
3. **Attracting fixed-point linearization.**  The proposed product chart is
   the iterative logarithm for `px+x^2`, and Lindahl--Zieve gives precisely
   its full-conjugacy disc.  Finite uniform fibres are multiplication-by-`p`
   fibres under that chart.

After assigning templates 1's historical material and templates 2--3's
general owners zero credit, only the target-coordinate formula

```text
a=B_t^(-1)(y/p^(t+1))
```

remains.  This is a useful exact label, but not two independent theorem axes.

## 4. Batch-separation decision

QNC would be pairwise distinguishable at the literal-map level from P162--P165.
That is not the failing gate.  It fails the stronger progress threshold:

```text
all-parameter temporal theorem
+ independent inverse/structural theorem
+ owner-thin residual after subtraction.
```

The first is historical and crowded; the spectrum is directly compressed;
the structural chart is directly compressed; the surviving coordinate
transport is too narrow alone.

do not interpret the bounded exact-map non-hit as novelty.  Preserve the
