## Generic proof-engine collision

For any finite endofunction `f`, the time-`t` image of pullback on partitions is
the principal filter above `ker(f^t)`, canonically the partition lattice on
`im(f^t)`.  Every target fibre is the set of extensions of its descended
partition across the complement of the image.  Therefore:

- closed iterates come from inverse-image functoriality;
- Bell image counts come from rank alone;
- support is kernel containment;
- every-target counts come from `r`-Bell extension; and
- EDP's root statistic only distinguishes one attachment color.

This is an exact engine collision, stronger than merely observing similar
integer sequences.  EDP's particular `p` supplies only the elementary schedule
`rank(p^t)=n-min(t,n-1)` and nested initial-segment kernels.

### P143: adjacent, not an exact collision

P143 acts on all Boolean relations by row-support inclusion.  Its first image
is the labelled preorders; on that image it transposes, producing fixed
equivalences and preorder two-cycles.  Its fibres are induced-order embeddings
into a Boolean lattice with inclusion--exclusion.  Thus P143 contributes a
weak relation/equivalence/Bell/every-target silhouette, but neither its literal
map nor its inverse engine is EDP's pullback-extension engine.  The kill does
not depend on calling P143 an exact collision.
