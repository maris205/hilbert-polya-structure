## System cards

### AA01 / USP — unit-pivot Schur stripping

Let `R` be a finite commutative local ring, `Q=|R|`, and `U=|R^x|`.
The carrier is the disjoint union of `M_j(R)` for `0<=j<=n` and a failure
sink `dagger`.  The empty matrix and `dagger` are fixed.  For

```text
A = [ a  b ]
    [ c  D ]
```

with `a` a unit, set `S(A)=D-c a^(-1)b`; if `a` is a nonunit, send `A` to
`dagger`.

- **Small exact signature.**  On `M_2(Z/mZ)`, the survivor counts after one
  and two steps are `(54,36)`, `(128,64)`, `(500,400)` for `m=3,4,5`.
  Every prescribed `1 x 1` target has respectively `18,32,100` one-step
  sources.
- **All-parameter temporal theorem.**  Among `M_n(R)`, the number surviving
  the first `t<=n` pivots is

  ```text
  U^t Q^(n^2-t).
  ```

  Consequently first failure at step `t+1` has size
  `U^t Q^(n^2-t)(1-U/Q)`, while `U^n Q^(n^2-n)` matrices reach the empty
  matrix.
- **Independent target/fibre theorem.**  For every prescribed
  `B in M_k(R)` and `t>=0`, its sources in `M_(k+t)(R)` are counted by

  ```text
  U^t Q^(2kt+t(t-1)).
  ```

  Reverse construction independently chooses a unit pivot, a row, and a
  column at each stage; the old trailing block is then forced.
- **Identifiability.**  The size-zero one-step fibre gives `U`; the ratio of
  the size-one and size-zero fibres gives `Q^2`.  Thus the atlas recovers
  `(Q,U)`, but not the isomorphism type of `R`.
- **Owner/collision gate.**  Recursive Schur complementation, the quotient
  property, LU/LDU coordinates, and leading-principal-minor criteria are all
  zero-credit Gaussian-elimination background.  Parker's 1998 paper is a
  direct static-mechanism owner.  Finite-chain-ring determinant enumeration
  is close additional ownership.  The bounded search found no primary source
  stating the complete finite-local-ring failure-time plus every-target fibre
  conjunction; this is only `BOUNDED_NO_DIRECT_HIT`, not novelty evidence.
- **Disposition.**  `TOP_1_SPECIALIST_OWNER_GATE`.  It is proof-closed and
  plausibly short-paper sized, but should be killed if a specialist regards
  the displayed counts as routine LDU enumeration rather than an independent
  dynamic result.

### AA02 / DMC — adjacent-minor condensation

- **Carrier/update.**  Square matrices of sizes `0,...,n` over `F_q`; replace
  `A` by the `(n-1) x (n-1)` matrix of adjacent `2 x 2` determinants.
- **Small signature.**  For `2 x 2` matrices the scalar-zero fibres are
  `10,33,145` at `q=2,3,5`; every specified nonzero scalar has
  `6,24,120` sources.
- **Temporal theorem.**  Size drops by one at every nonempty step, hence the
  exact clock is the starting size.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  At size two, zero has
  `q^3+q^2-q` sources and each nonzero target has `q(q^2-1)`.
- **Gate.**  Robbins--Rumsey directly places connected-minor condensation in
  established determinant machinery, and P103 is an internal determinant
  neighbour.  The only uniform temporal theorem is dimension bookkeeping.
  `KILL_OWNER_THIN`.

### AA03 / GIO — GCD increment

- **Carrier/update.**  `X={1,...,p^k}`, with `p^k` fixed and
  `G(x)=x+gcd(x,p^k)` otherwise.
- **Small signature.**  Heights are `5,8,12` for `(p,k)=(2,5),(3,4),(5,3)`;
  maximum one-step fibres are `6,5,4`.
- **Temporal theorem.**  Reflection `R(x)=p^k-x` gives exactly

  ```text
  R G R^(-1)(y) = y-gcd(y,p^k),
  tau(x)=s_p(p^k-x),   H=(p-1)k.
  ```

- **Independent fibre axis.**  A target `y<p^k` has one predecessor for each
  `d|gcd(y,p^k)` satisfying `gcd(y/d-1,p^k/d)=1`; the terminal also has its
  fixed self-source.
- **Gate.**  The conjugate is literally P100's least-valuation digit erasure.
  The fibre formula cannot undo exact system conjugacy.  `KILL_EXACT_P100_CONJUGATE`.

### AA04 / LDT — Lucas digit truncation

- **Carrier/update.**  At level `k`, use pairs `0<=b<=a<p^k`; send
  `(a,b)` to `(floor(a/p),floor(b/p))` at level `k-1`.
- **Small signature.**  The tested carriers have 528 states for `(2,5)` and
  3,321 for `(3,4)`.
- **Temporal theorem.**  Every level-`k` state reaches the unique level-zero
  state in exactly `k` steps.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  A time-`t` target `(A,B)` has `p^(2t)` lifts
  if `A>B`, and `p^t(p^t+1)/2` lifts if `A=B`.
- **Gate.**  The two regimes are simply the inequality boundary under a
  base-`p` coordinate shift.  Lucas/Davis--Webb owns the digit framework;
  P77/P100 are close internal digit systems.  `KILL_CLASSICAL_THIN`.

### AA05 / RGM — reciprocal-GCD meet

- **Carrier/update.**  For a reciprocal-stable squarefree product
  `G=prod_i P_i` and cap `c`, use divisors of `G^c`; set
  `T(f)=gcd(f,f*)`.  In factor coordinates, `e_i` becomes
  `min(e_i,e_(m+1-i))`.
- **Small signature.**  `(m,c)=(3,2),(4,2),(5,3)` give state/fixed counts
  `27/9`, `81/9`, `1024/64` and maximum fibres `5,25,49`.
- **Temporal theorem.**  `T^2=T`; fixed targets are exactly palindromic
  exponent vectors, counted by `(c+1)^ceil(m/2)`.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  A fixed target of paired exponent `a` gets a
  factor `2(c-a)+1` per reciprocal pair; multiply these factors.  Nonfixed
  targets have empty fibre.
- **Gate.**  Self-reciprocal factor theory is direct background, and the
  dynamics is one meet under an involution, colliding with P102/P128.
  `KILL_SHALLOW_INTERNAL`.

### AA06 / NAK — Nakayama syzygy

- **Carrier/update.**  Isomorphism classes `M_l=k[x]/(x^l)`, `0<=l<=n`, as
  modules over `k[x]/(x^n)`; set `Omega(M_n)=0`, `Omega(0)=0`, and
  `Omega(M_l)=M_(n-l)` for `0<l<n`.
- **Small signature.**  Through `n=12`, the projective has depth one; all
  nonprojectives are on 2-cycles except `M_(n/2)` when `n` is even.
- **Temporal theorem.**  The recurrent count is `n`, fixed count is
  `1+1_(2|n)`, and the unique transient nonzero class is `M_n`.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  Zero has two sources, each nonprojective has
  one, and the projective target has none.
- **Gate.**  This is the standard periodic syzygy action for a self-injective
  Nakayama algebra; general periodic-resolution literature is direct enough
  to kill the relabelling.  `KILL_DIRECT_HOMOLOGICAL_OWNER`.

### AA07 / COX — type-A Coxeter escape

- **Carrier/update.**  Positive roots of `A_n`, written `(a,b)` with
  `1<=a<b<=n+1`, plus a sink.  Apply the cycle `(1 2 ... n+1)`; a positive
  image is retained and a negative image is sent to the sink.
- **Small signature.**  At `n=8,9,10`, root counts are `36,45,55`, heights
  `8,9,10`, and sink fibres `9,10,11`.
- **Temporal theorem.**  `tau(a,b)=n+2-b`; the depth-`t` shell has
  `n+1-t` roots.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  The sink has `n+1` sources; a root has one
  source iff `a>1`, otherwise none.
- **Gate.**  The update is a Coxeter element with an artificial negative-root
  sink.  Bernstein--Gelfand--Ponomarev is a direct reflection/Coxeter owner.
  `KILL_DIRECT_OWNER`.

### AA08 / FCG — finite function-field Gauss shift

- **Carrier/update.**  Rational functions over `F_q(z)` whose unique finite
  continued fraction has at most `n` partial quotients in a fixed finite
  alphabet `A`, plus the empty expansion; delete the first quotient.
- **Small signature.**  Alphabet/cap `(2,6)` gives 127 states; `(3,5)` gives
  364.
- **Temporal theorem.**  The exact clock is continued-fraction length and the
  sharp height is `n`.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  A nonempty target of length `l` has `|A|^t`
  time-`t` sources when `l+t<=n`; the empty target has
  `sum_(j=0)^min(t,n)|A|^j`.
- **Gate.**  This is the literal Gauss/partial-quotient shift, not an unowned
  arithmetic dynamic.  P131 additionally occupies Euclidean quotient coding.
  `KILL_DIRECT_OWNER_INTERNAL`.

### AA09 / PEP — least-prime exponent peeling

- **Carrier/update.**  Divisors of `N=prod_i p_i^(a_i)` with ordered primes;
  divide by the least prime currently dividing the state.
- **Small signature.**  Caps `(2,1,3)`, `(1,2,2,1)`, `(3,2,1,2)` give
  24, 36, 72 states and heights 6, 6, 8.
- **Temporal theorem.**  `tau(d)=Omega(d)` and the depth polynomial is
  `prod_i(1+z+...+z^(a_i))`.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  If `j` is the least active coordinate of a
  nonunit target, its indegree is `j+1_(e_j<a_j)` in zero-based indexing;
  the unit has `r+1` sources including itself.
- **Gate.**  The proof is P100's least-active-coordinate erasure on a divisor
  box and also lies in P142's valuation carrier.  `KILL_PROOF_ENGINE_TRANSFER`.

### AA10 / LRD — least-rational-root deflation

- **Carrier/update.**  Monic polynomials over `F_q` of degree at most `N`,
  with a fixed ordering of `F_q`; if a rational root exists, divide by one
  copy of the least root, otherwise fix the polynomial.
- **Small signature.**  `(q,N)=(2,6),(3,4),(5,3)` give state/fixed counts
  `127/32`, `121/36`, `156/51`, with sharp heights `6,4,3`.
- **Temporal theorem.**  The clock is the total multiplicity of all rational
  roots.  The fixed degree-`d` count is

  ```text
  R_q(d)=sum_(j=0)^min(q,d) (-1)^j binom(q,j) q^(d-j).
  ```

- **Independent fibre axis.**  If `deg h<N`, a fixed target has `q+1`
  one-step sources, while a nonfixed target whose least root has rank `r`
  has `r+1`; at degree `N`, fixed targets have one and nonfixed targets zero.
  For fixed `g`, its time-`t` fibre in the cap has size

  ```text
  binom(q+min(t,N-deg g),q).
  ```

- **Gate.**  Berlekamp owns finite-field factorization input; the temporal
  scheduler transfers P100, and factor coordinates transfer P128.  The
  conjunction is exact but not portfolio-distinct.  `KILL_INTERNAL_OWNER`.

### AA11 / CRT — ordered CRT forgetting

- **Carrier/update.**  For ordered distinct primes `p_1,...,p_r`, states are
  `(S,x mod prod_(i in S)p_i)` for every subset `S`; remove the least index in
  `S` and reduce the residue.
- **Small signature.**  Prime lists `(2,3,5)` and `(2,3,5,7)` give 72 and 576
  states, with heights three and four.
- **Temporal theorem.**  The clock is `|S|`.  Total carrier size is
  `prod_i(1+p_i)`.
- **Independent fibre/identifiability axis.**  For a nonempty target `S`,
  time-`t` fibre size is the degree-`t` elementary symmetric polynomial in
  primes indexed below `min S`.  For the terminal it is the sum of degrees
  `0,...,t` in all primes.  Hence the full terminal-fibre sequence recovers
  the prime multiset.
- **Gate.**  This is labelled coordinate erasure with CRT weights; all the
  formulas are immediate.  `KILL_THEOREM_THIN`.

### AA12 / PFX — strict prefix summation

- **Carrier/update.**  On `F_q^n`, set
  `T(x)_i=sum_(j<i)x_j`.
- **Small signature.**  Exact boxes `(q,n)=(2,8),(3,6),(5,5)` have 256, 729,
  3,125 states and sharp heights 8, 6, 5.
- **Temporal theorem.**  `T` is regular nilpotent: `rank(T^t)=n-t`.  Thus
  depth-at-most-`t` has size `q^t`, and exact depth `t>=1` has size
  `q^t-q^(t-1)`.
- **Independent fibre axis.**  `im(T^t)` has `q^(n-t)` targets and every
  nonempty time-`t` fibre has size `q^t`.
- **Gate.**  This is completely explained by a single Jordan block, hence
  generic finite-linear machinery; P109/P115 and early incidence papers are
  internal owners.  `KILL_GENERIC_LINEAR`.
