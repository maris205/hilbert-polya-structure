# Problem anchor: the P122--P126 breadth round

## Shared question

Which natural finite or controlled updates, outside the mechanisms already
occupied by P1--P121, reveal an exact temporal law early enough to support a
short theorem paper?  This remains Route A: search broadly across dynamical
subclasses, then promote only literal systems whose first bounded experiments
show a crisp clock, fibre law, recurrent classification, algebraic branch, or
finite-time distribution.

## Intake and freeze rules

A retained paper must prove an all-parameter result with at least two
substantial dynamical outputs.  Endpoint-only observations, generic finite
functional-graph language, and cosmetic variants of an occupied mechanism do
not qualify.  Each finalist must have:

1. a literal update and carrier fixed before theorem fitting;
2. a bounded exact pilot with a recorded falsification target;
3. direct-owner and P1--P121 collision subtraction;
4. a proof route plus a materially different derivation or control;
5. a deterministic paper-local verifier; and
6. enough residual content for a self-contained anonymous note.

At least sixty explicit candidates across four independent lanes must enter
the ledger before exactly five paper numbers are frozen.  A candidate with no
clear early signal is killed or reserved; it is not rescued through prolonged
casework.

## Hard firewalls

Ordinary finite-linear maps, powers in finite groups or semigroups, standard
polynomial iteration, rowmotion/toggle relabellings, generic closure or
pruning, local parity variants of P106/P117/P120, fixed-regular Engel variants,
Yule/root-configuration statistics, and random waiting-time wrappers begin
behind a collision firewall.  The new batch must contain five genuinely
different carriers and proof engines rather than five deformations of the
last successful system.

External release, submission, specialist contact, novelty, priority,
authorship, and venue decisions remain **HOLD_EXTERNAL**.
