# P215 Review A page-view contract

PENDING BUILD. The same A reviewer or root must actually open every fresh page
at readable scale. Check title/abstract, map definition, clock proof, inverse
theorems, recurrence, scope, citations and bibliography for clipping, overlap,
overflow, blank content, broken formula, missing glyph or stale text. PNG
existence and hashes alone are not views. Record actual page hashes and
observations before final same-A acceptance.
