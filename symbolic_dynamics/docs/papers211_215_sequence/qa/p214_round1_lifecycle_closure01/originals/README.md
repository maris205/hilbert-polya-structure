## Content and limits

The anonymous article studies F(x,y)=(y,x(t+y)) on I², with
I=tF_q[t]/(t^m), q a prime power and m>=2. The cancellation-safe exact
clock is the temporal contribution. The depth census follows from it. Every
one-step target fibre and its complete census are evaluated, but their whole
multiplication/annihilator mechanism is deducted through F=QMP.

The linear control (y,tx) has the same clock and depth census; those shapes
are not new. At m=2 the maps are identical. At m>=3 unequal positive fibres
exclude conjugacy to every finite-group endomorphism, not arbitrary nonlinear
factors. There is no all-time inverse theorem, arbitrary-chain-ring extension,
functional-graph classification or global priority claim.
