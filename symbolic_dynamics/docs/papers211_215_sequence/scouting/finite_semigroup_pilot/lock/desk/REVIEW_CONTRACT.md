## How explicit is the inverse?

The displayed Laurent coefficient is equivalently the finite sum
$$\sum_{\substack{a_i,b_i\ge0,\ a_i+b_i\le d_i\ (1\le i\le k)\\
                    \sum_i(b_i-a_i)\in\{0,1\}}}
             \prod_{i=1}^k\binom{d_i}{a_i+b_i}.$$
All summation limits and summands are explicit functions of the target;
there is no unknown function $f$, unresolved composition equation,
linear-system consistency test or generic transfer-matrix oracle remaining.

gap positions and the two rank branches.

$$[u^s]P_d(u)=\sum_{\substack{|s|\le m\le d\\m\equiv s\pmod2}}
                               \binom dm.$$
Indeed $b-a=s$, $a+b=m$ force $a=(m-s)/2$, $b=(m+s)/2$; the inequalities
and parity condition are exactly their feasibility conditions. This is an
algebraic restatement of the same inverse theorem, not an extra axis.
