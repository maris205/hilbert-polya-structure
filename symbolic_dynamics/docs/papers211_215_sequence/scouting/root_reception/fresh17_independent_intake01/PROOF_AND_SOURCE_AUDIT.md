## Independent attack of the forward theorem

Let M have rank r on E with |E|=n. One-rank truncation fixes rank zero.
Dual rank is |A|-r+r_M(E\A), because a basis of E\A extends to a basis of E.
Truncation changes subset rank x to min(c,x); independent-set exchange
continues to hold under a cardinality cap. Thus the carrier of all matroids
on this exact ground set is finite and closed.

For n>0 and r<n, put s=n-r-1. Direct substitution, without any scientific
map execution, gives

r_F(M)(A) = min(s, |A|-r+r_M(E\A)),
r_F²(M)(A) = min(r, |A|, r_M(A)+1).

The second formula preserves total rank r. Repeating the same capped
increment therefore gives min(r, |A|, r_M(A)+k) after 2k steps. Starting
instead at F(M) proves the odd formula with rank cap s. This also confirms
that the displayed even formula is valid at k=0, since matroid rank is
already at most both r and |A|. A free initial matroid must be excluded
from s=-1: it goes to rank zero in one step. The empty ground set has its
single fixed matroid.

For d(M)=max_A[min(r,|A|)-r_M(A)], the even subsequence becomes uniform
exactly at its k=d(M) term: the maximum exists on finite 2^E, and a
maximizing subset disproves earlier uniformity. A periodic rank-r state
must equal its arbitrarily late even iterates, so it must be uniform.
Conversely F(U_r,n)=U_n-r-1,n for r<n, yielding exactly the n uniform core
states. Odd n has the single middle-rank fixed point; positive even n has
none. The free matroid cannot recur because it is never an output.

Applying the same argument to F(M), with e=d(F(M)), gives the exact first
core entrance min(2d,2e+1). This is a first-entry statement, not merely an
upper bound or a period-divisibility observation. Once a uniform state is
reached, both parity subsequences stay in the core.

The proposed rank-stratum maximum is genuinely attained, not inferred
from defect upper bounds. For W with r coloops C and n-r loops L,

d(W)=min(r,n-r),    d(F(W))=min(n-r-1,r).

For the first formula, deficiency is at most both r and |A∩L|; a subset
of L attains the displayed minimum. The dual swaps loops and coloops,
and truncating it at s gives rank min(s,|A∩L|), proving the second formula
by the same argument on C. If r≤s, entrance time is 2r; if r≥s+1, it is
2s+1. Hence H(n,r)=min(2r,2(n-r)-1) is sharp for every 0≤r<n, including
r=0. Maximization over ranks yields n-1 for n≥2. At n=1 the free state
has entrance one, while the rank-zero state is fixed; at n=0 the maximum
is zero. No hidden representability/simplicity/graphicality assumption
was introduced.

## Independent inverse and source-mechanism attack

For a positive-rank target K of rank s<n, duality is a bijection from
F-predecessors to rank-(s+1) matroids N with truncation N=K. This is
exactly the set of NONTRIVIAL erections. An erection of unchanged rank,
when included in the source's terminology, is not a predecessor here.
Rank-n targets have empty fibres. Rank zero is exceptional: intermediate
N may have rank zero or rank one. The former contributes the free source.
The latter are uniquely determined by their nonempty nonloop set,
contributing 2^n-1. Thus the fibre is 2^n, with the empty-ground case
separately giving one.

The source-owned fixed-positive-target-rank bound is p(n,s+1), and the
uniform target attains it: rank-(s+1) erections of U_s,n are precisely the
rank-(s+1) paving matroids. Truncating such a matroid makes every set of
size ≤s independent, and conversely a uniform truncation precludes any
circuit of size ≤s. This is not an evaluation of paving counts or a
classification of all maximizing targets.

The temporal subtraction has a real quotient hypothesis. Every flat of
M is a subset of E, and every subset is a flat of the free matroid, so M
is a quotient of U_n,n. The standard Higgs lift toward that free upper
matroid has subset rank min(|A|,r_M(A)+k); applying the rank-r cap gives
the exact even formula. The author explicitly avoids the generally false
claim that M is a quotient of the same-rank U_r,n. The Higgs-lift
identification is therefore a valid mechanism reduction, not a formal
similarity with an inapplicable theorem.

The two apparent axes are consequently consumed by standard rank
lifting/truncation and erection reconstruction. A residual problem asking
for more paving enumeration or all equality cases is not a proved second
contribution, a nomination, or a reason to expand a pilot.
