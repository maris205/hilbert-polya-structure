## Exact accepted contract

For every prime power q and m>=2, let R=F_q[t]/(t^m), I=tR and

    F:I² -> I²,   F(x,y)=(y,x(t+y)),   v(0)=m.

The full carrier is prescribed; no field-only, generic-unit or t²R
restriction replaces it. The substantive nonlinear family is m>=3; m=2
is retained only as the explicitly linear boundary control.

1. With a=v(x), b=v(y), the first time to (0,0) is
   max{2(m-b),2(m-a)-1}. Zero is the unique recurrent state. The sharp
   height is 2m-2, attained exactly when v(y)=1.
2. For 0<=h<=2m-2, the cumulative depth census is q^h. Exact depth h>=1
   has (q-1)q^(h-1) states; depth zero has one.
3. At a target (u,w), put d=min(v(t+u),m-1). It is reachable iff
   w belongs to t^(d+1)R. The predecessor set, when nonempty, is
   {(x_0+k,u):k in t^(m-d)R}, with q^d elements.
4. For 1<=d<=m-2, there are (q-1)q^(2(m-d-1)) targets with fibre q^d.
   There are q saturated targets with fibre q^(m-1), precisely
   (-t+c t^(m-1),0). Image size is q+(q-1) sum_(j=1)^(m-2) q^(2j);
   all other targets have zero predecessors.

These are an exact pointwise temporal result and a complete evaluated
one-step inverse result. The depth census is a corollary of the temporal
formula, not a third mechanism. The fibre extremum is a corollary of the
inverse atlas, also not a third mechanism. No all-time fibre theorem,
functional-graph isomorphism classification or new general contraction
principle is included.

The recurrence x_0=x, x_1=y, x_(n+2)=x_n(t+x_(n+1)) indeed gives the
two coordinates after n iterates. For n>=2 its terms are in t²R, so each
subsequent adjacent multiplier has valuation one. This fact is valid in

When b>=2, the two exact valuation chains reach their truncation thresholds
at the displayed parity-dependent indices. A nonzero sequence term at index
r occurs in the state at time r and disappears from both coordinates at
time r+1. This checks the minus-one in the even-chain contribution. When
a=m, that contribution is -1 but the nonnegative odd-chain contribution
makes the stated maximum correct; when a=b=m it is zero. Exactly one zero
coordinate is therefore covered, not omitted.

When b=1, the even chain can die earlier through cancellation, but its
valuation lower bound suffices. The odd chain has exact valuation 1+k at
index 2k+1 until truncation, so x_(2m-3) survives and the following pair
vanishes. This proves the full-state clock despite the failure of an exact
increment in the even chain. The submitted example (t,-t) and its stated
even/odd iterates are correct for k>=1, including characteristic two and
m=2. It is not a counterexample to the theorem.

Solving the two inequalities for depth<=h gives thresholds
a>=m-ceil(h/2), b>=m-floor(h/2). Their ideal sizes multiply to q^h;
the endpoint is the whole q^(2m-2)-state carrier. This independently checks
the census and the exact maximum equality case.
