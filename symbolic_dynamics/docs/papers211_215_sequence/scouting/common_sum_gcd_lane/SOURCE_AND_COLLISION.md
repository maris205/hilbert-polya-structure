## Direct primary definitions actually read

**Harris–Louwsma.** Zachary Harris and Joel Louwsma, *On Arithmetical
Structures on Complete Graphs*, arXiv:1909.02022v3, 22 January 2024.
The [primary HTML introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.02022v3)
defines primitive positive vectors with every coordinate dividing their
sum, and Eqs. (1)–(2) explicitly identify these vectors with unit-fraction
solutions summing to one. For $n=3$ this is exactly our normalized fixed
object. Their static definition and correspondence are deducted; the source
is not claimed to contain our gcd update, its clock or its inverse fibres.

**Corrales–Valencia.** Hugo Corrales and Carlos E. Valencia, *Arithmetical
structures on graphs*, arXiv:1604.02502v3, 13 June 2017.
The [primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1604.02502)
was read at the introduction/Definition 3.1 and at §6, Algorithm 1 and
Examples 6.2/6.7. The definition independently matches the same primitive
vertex-divisibility object. Algorithm 1 instead fills initially zero
coordinates in a supplied vertex order to construct structures on graph
subdivisions. It is not the autonomous all-positive simultaneous gcd map;
the word “iteration” there does not establish a literal collision.
