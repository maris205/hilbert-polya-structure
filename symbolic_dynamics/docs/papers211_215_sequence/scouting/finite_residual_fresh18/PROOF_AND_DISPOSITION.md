## 1. Numerical-semigroup ordinarization is already a complete source entrance

For fixed genus $g$, write $m(S)$ for the least positive element and $F(S)$
for the largest gap of a nonordinary semigroup. The source's literal map is

$$O(S)=(S\setminus\{m(S)\})\cup\{F(S)\},$$

fixing the ordinary semigroup $S_g=\{0,g+1,g+2,\ldots\}$.
The genus-zero state $\mathbb N_0$ fixes separately.

For $g>0$, the map $s\mapsto F-s$ injects $S\cap[0,F]$ into the gap set:
otherwise $s+(F-s)=F$ would be in $S$. Thus $F+1-g\le g$ and
$F\le2g-1$. Fixed-genus carriers are therefore finite, with each semigroup
determined by its gaps in $[1,2g-1]$.

A genus-$g$ semigroup is nonordinary exactly when $m\le g$, and then
$F>g$. Removing the least positive element preserves closure: no sum of
two positive elements equals $m$. Adding $F$ also preserves closure,
because every sum involving $F$ and a positive element exceeds $F$ and
cannot equal the removed $m$. Exactly one gap and one nongap are exchanged.

Consequently the statistic
$$r(S)=\#(S\cap[1,g])$$
decreases by exactly one at each nonfixed step. The sole recurrent state
is $S_g$, and its exact entrance time is $r(S)$. This is precisely
Bras-Amorós's Lemma 1, not a new clock.

The maximum $r(S)=\lfloor g/2\rfloor$ is also source-owned (Lemma 2).
For completeness, when $r>0$ let
$0<\lambda_1<\cdots<\lambda_r\le g$ be these nongaps.
The $r$ distinct sums $\lambda_r+\lambda_i$ are nongaps in $(g,2g]$:
they exceed the largest nongap at most $g$, and are at most $2g$.
There are $g$ nongaps in $[1,2g]$ by the Frobenius bound, and $r$ of them
are at most $g$. Thus there are $g-r$ nongaps in $(g,2g]$, so $r\le g-r$.
The semigroup
$$E_g=\{0,2,4,\ldots,2g\}\cup\{2g+1,2g+2,\ldots\}$$
has genus $g$ and attains the bound. The case $r=0$ needs no sum argument.

The inverse primitive is also explicit in source §2.2: delete an effective
generator above the current Frobenius number and adjoin a current gap below
the current multiplicity as a new nongap in a closure-preserving position. This is the
source's parent/child tree, not a new decoder. Source Lemma 8 supplies
an evaluated first-depth census as well. No all-target fibre maximum or
full equality classification is newly claimed here.

This is not literally old NSB, which only adjoins the Frobenius number and
decreases genus, nor old X16's blowup-multiplicity sequence. Their exact
records were read; the decisive exclusion of ordinarization is its direct
primary owner, not a false identity with either old map. Quasi-ordinarization
was encountered as an already named related source, not instantiated as
a second candidate.

## 2. Moore refinement is an exact old literal

by the partition keyed at $q$ by
$$([q]_\Pi,c(q),([\delta(q,a)]_\Pi)_{a\in\Sigma}).$$

after $t$ steps, the old proof gives
$$pE_tq\iff
\begin{cases}
\delta(p,w)E\delta(q,w)&\text{for all }|w|\le t,\\
c(\delta(p,w))=c(\delta(q,w))&\text{for all }|w|<t.
\end{cases}$$

adds the empty-word colour condition, and prepends each alphabet letter

## 3. Cycle-set retraction: named operator, non-singleton terminals

The source relation
$$x\sim y\iff \sigma_x=\sigma_y$$
is a congruence, and its quotient $\operatorname{Ret}(X)=X/{\sim}$ has
$[x]*[y]=[x*y]$. On isomorphism classes with size at most a fixed $N\ge1$, this
already defined operator has a finite closed carrier. Taking such a bound
or naming representatives adds no new literal.

Each nontrivial quotient strictly reduces cardinality, so after at most
$|X|-1$ changes an irretractable quotient is reached. Fixed points are
exactly those with pairwise distinct left translations. Calling the
time to a singleton a multipermutation level merely names an existing
statistic; it neither proves every endpoint is a singleton nor supplies
a sharp new general clock.

The stronger singleton statement is false even for square-free solutions.
Bachiller–Cedó–Jespers–Okniński's Theorem 3.3 and Remark 3.4 explicitly
give an eight-point example by taking
$A=B=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$, $I=\{1,2\}$ and
$\varphi_1=\varphi_2=\mathrm{id}$ in their construction.
Its translations, indexed by $(a,b,i)$, act by
$$\sigma_{(a,b,i)}(c,d,j)=
\begin{cases}
(c,d+a-c,j)&i=j,\\
(c+b,d,j)&i\ne j.
\end{cases}$$
All arithmetic is modulo two. The source establishes its nondegenerate
involutive Yang–Baxter law and square-freeness.

Its translations are distinct: differing $i$ are separated by a point
in block $i$ where $a-c\ne0$; with the same $i$, differing $a$ are
separated inside that block, and differing $b$ outside it. Thus its
retraction still has eight elements. This checks the source's obstruction
directly; the construction and counterexample are not credited to fresh18.

has nonzero socle (Theorem 6.2), with retraction again quasilinear and
equal to the socle quotient (Theorem 3.4). These are source-owned claims,
not new results proved here. The source explicitly does not address the
