### B. Square-complement graph iteration

Fix n>=2; the carrier is every labelled finite simple undirected graph on
[n]. Define T(G)=overline(G²), where G² retains edges and adds all pairs with
a common neighbor. Equivalently uv belongs to T(G) iff uv is not an edge
of G and no vertex is adjacent to both u and v. Intercomponent pairs are
therefore retained; no infinite-distance ambiguity or connected-carrier
failure is hidden. This is an autonomous endomorphism of the stated finite
carrier, structurally different from A.
