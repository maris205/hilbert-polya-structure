# Sources, subtraction and limits

## Direct primary sources

1. **Bors, Panario and Wang**, *Functional Graphs of Generalized Cyclotomic
   Mappings of Finite Fields*, Memoirs EMS 23.
   [Publisher record](https://ems.press/books/mems/314);
   [official PDF](https://ems.press/content/book-files/51678?nt=1);
   DOI [10.4171/MEMS/23](https://doi.org/10.4171/MEMS/23).
   Published 7 October 2025; cover year 2026. Actually read printed pp.3–9,
   Definition 1.1 and the complete p.8 coordinate derivation; then pp.45–48,
   Proposition 3.1.1 and Theorem 3.2.1 with complete proofs, Example 3.2.2,
   and the opening of §3.3. No later tree/complexity theorem was audited.

   The source converts coset-monomial branches to affine cyclic-group
   maps and reduces periodic points to return maps. It explicitly allows
   unequal branch exponents and zero coefficients. Thus merely abandoning
   the earlier common-exponent/niceness restriction is not a fresh route.
   Its maximal-index representation includes every zero-fixing function,
   but loses the small-index structural advantage; it is not a closed
   uniform solution of arbitrary nonlinear dynamics. These statements
   delimit this desk, not a universal no-enumeration-value assertion.

2. **Oliveira and Brochero Martínez**, *Dynamics of polynomial maps over
   finite fields*, [arXiv:2201.00954v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00954v1),
   submitted 4 January 2022.
   [Author HTML body](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.00954v1).
   Actually read §2's Definition 2.2 and Theorems 2.4/2.7, §3 through
   Proposition 3.6 including its proof, and both §4 proofs. The HTML header
   also displays “August 24, 2026”; that rendered date is not taken as a
   new submission or revised theorem version.

   The graph results impose injectivity of the induced roots-of-unity
   map outside the zero preimage. In the actual old QTS specialization,
   that map is inversion outside the singular root. The old n=1 quotient
   display problem is visible in the body; the finite geometric sum before
   the quotient in §4.2 remains meaningful. No blind substitution into
   0/0 or a modulus-zero order is used here. Minor inconsistent displayed
   indices in Example 2.5 are not silently repaired or treated as new
   scientific evidence. General theorem correctness outside the needed
   specialization was not independently certified by this desk.
