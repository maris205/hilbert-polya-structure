## Direct primary contexts

**SER:** Carlos E. Marciano, Abilio Lucena, Felipe M. G. França and Luidi G. Simonetti, *Minimum Concurrency for Assembling Computer Music*, INOC 2019, DOI 10.5441/002/inoc.2019.16. The [proceedings primary PDF](https://openproceedings.org/2019/conf/inoc/INOC_2019_paper_32.pdf) was read at title/introduction and Section 2, provider lines 0–174 (first two pages). It explicitly gives simultaneous old-sink edge reversal, attributes SER to Gafni/Bertsekas and Barbosa/Gafni, and reports equal within-period firing counts with concurrency m/p. Section 2's cycle formula assumes a connected non-tree; it is not applied to trees here. These are known-literal/source boundaries, not verification of the inaccessible 1989 proof or a full exact-period classification.

**Tree diameter:** Bang Ye Wu and Kun-Mao Chao, *A note on Eccentricities, diameters, and radii*, author-hosted four-page excerpt from *Spanning Trees and Optimization Problems* (2004). The [primary excerpt](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~kmchao/tree10spr/diameter.pdf) returned all 201 lines. Fact 1, Lemma 2, double-sweep and center arguments were read directly. They provide the classical farthest/diameter geometry; the exact least-label two-image refinement was checked in old LAR itself. No new leaf-surgery ownership theorem is inferred.
