## Final deduction and authorship boundary

CBF's full structural fibre is the old bridge-component decomposition with
the prescribed datum exchanged; its new time deduction is a uniformly
short complement-of-forest classification, not a substantial evolving
mechanism. ZFW has a literal-specific reciprocal-pair kernel, but every
subsequent iterate is exactly fixed Boolean erosion, and its global fibre
extrema are elementary boundary counts. MCS has only switching-class
setup. CII is an established equivalence predicate followed by a two-state
image collapse. The author recommends **NO_PROMOTION**, not a claim that
these maps cannot support future different research.

explicitly unproved. The research-lit skill led to checking actual
