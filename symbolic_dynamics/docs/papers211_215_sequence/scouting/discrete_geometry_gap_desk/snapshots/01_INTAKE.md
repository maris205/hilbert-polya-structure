## QHK — quantized bounded-confidence configurations

For integers $L,k\geq1$, the carrier is every labelled tuple
$(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\in\{0,\ldots,L-1\}^k$. Simultaneously replace
$x_i$ by the nearest integer to the average of all old $x_j$ satisfying
$|x_j-x_i|\leq1$, including $j=i$. An exact half-integer is rounded
down. Multiplicities count. The labels are particle labels, not a
spatial word whose adjacent positions define the interaction.

Fixed full boxes: $L=1,\ldots,6$ and $k=1,\ldots,5$, all 30 boxes.
Quantized consensus and ordinary Hegselmann–Krause averaging receive
zero primitive credit. A state-dependent confidence graph is not
asserted to be the old fixed-neighbour averaging literal.

## OLS — oblique-line compression

Fix $X=\{-a,\ldots,a\}\times\{-b,\ldots,b\}$ and the global point
order $(x^2+y^2,x,y)$. A line compression retains the number of old
occupied points on each line and fills that many lowest-order ambient
points of that line. One autonomous update first compresses horizontal
lines $y=c$, then diagonal lines $x-y=c$. The carrier is all subsets
of $X$; all empty and full states are included.

Fixed full boxes $(a,b)$:
$(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(2,0),(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)$.
The maximum ambient size is 15 points. No larger square is authorized.
This is not alternating time-dependent scheduling: the ordered pair
of compressions is the same complete update at every time.
Rearrangement/coordinate-compression potentials and any complete
line-occupancy inverse adapter receive zero credit.

## MER — maximal-empty-rectangle centres

For a fixed integer pixel grid $X=\{0,\ldots,a-1\}\times
\{0,\ldots,b-1\}$ and occupied $S\subseteq X$, list every nonempty
axis-aligned subrectangle of pixels disjoint from $S$ and maximal
under inclusion among such empty rectangles. Output all pixel centres
nearest the Euclidean midpoint of each maximal rectangle, with every
tie included. Centres lie in the rectangle. Thus a full source maps
to empty, and an empty source maps to the central one, two or four
pixels of the entire grid. No artificial empty-state sink is added.

Fixed full grids $(a,b)$:
$(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(2,2),(2,3),(3,3),(3,4)$.
The empty-rectangle construction and skeleton representations are
classical primitives; forgetting rectangle dimensions is not itself
a second theorem axis.

## NTL — lattice nearest-site tie locus

On the same eight fixed pixel grids and full subset carriers as MER,
output every ambient point having at least two old sites at its
minimum squared Euclidean distance. The empty source has no nearest
sites and outputs empty. At an occupied point the nearest site is
unique, so that point is not output. This is the lattice sample of
the Voronoi tie locus, not all circumcentres of triples, and no finite
field or source-dependent ambient set is used.

The Voronoi construction and its static reconstruction information
are owned primitives. A direct source-defect adapter or lack of a
uniform time/inverse contract is sufficient to reject the literal.

## NSV — nonmanifold cubical-vertex feedback

The ambient torus is $X=(\mathbb Z/a)\times(\mathbb Z/b)$, with
$a,b\geq2$. A state occupies unit square faces indexed by their
lower-left vertices. Output a vertex $q$ exactly when among the four
incident old faces $q,q-e_1,q-e_2,q-e_1-e_2$ precisely one diagonal
pair is occupied. Identify that output vertex with the unit face
whose lower-left vertex is $q$, giving a self-map of the same finite
carrier. Equivalently the four incident bits form a checkerboard;
adjacent occupied pairs do not qualify.

Fixed full tori $(a,b)$:
$(2,2),(2,3),(2,4),(3,2),(3,3),(3,4),(4,2),(4,3)$.
The local cubical-link singularity criterion is classical. The
vertex-to-face identification is stated, not hidden as topology.
This is a 2D binary geometric local map, not a 1D rank/contrast rule;
no all-torus theorem is inferred from that distinction.

## LVC — Laguerre-cell volume feedback

Fix $N\geq1$, ambient demand points $q=0,\ldots,N-1$, and $m$
distinct sites $s_i=i$ for $i=0,\ldots,m-1$, with $1\leq m\leq N$.
The state is every integer weight vector $w\in\{0,\ldots,N\}^m$.
Assign each demand point to the site minimizing $(q-i)^2-w_i$,
breaking ties by the least site index. Output the vector of cell
cardinalities. Its coordinates lie in the same weight carrier.

Fixed full boxes: $N=1,\ldots,7$ and $m=1,\ldots,\min(4,N)$,
all 22 boxes. Sites remain fixed; this is not a Lloyd centroid move.
Power diagrams, convex piecewise-linear subgradients and their
integer chamber inverses receive zero primitive credit. No geometry
claim survives merely because its weights can be called volumes.
