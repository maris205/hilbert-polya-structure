## 2. Literal identity in multiplicity coordinates

For fixed integers $n\ge0,L\ge1$, let the state be
$(b,f_1,\ldots,f_L)$ with $0\le b<L$, $f_j\ge0$ integers and
$$b+\sum_{j=1}^{L}j f_j=n.$$
Here $b$ is the bank and $f_j$ counts size-$j$ piles. The published update
is
$$
f'_j=f_{j+1}\quad(1\le j<L),\qquad
f'_L=\left\lfloor\frac{b+\sum_j f_j}{L}\right\rfloor,
\qquad b'=\left(b+\sum_j f_j\right)\bmod L.
$$
This is a coordinate description of the exact source map, not a renamed
new candidate. Adding labels or changing the representation has not been
proposed as a rescue.

## 3. Elementary evaluated inverse — correct, but not a new temporal axis

Status: **PROVABLE AS STATED** for this author's inverse calculation.
Assume the target $(c,g_1,\ldots,g_L)$ lies in the same carrier. Set
$$S=\sum_{j=1}^{L-1}g_j,\qquad A=Lg_L+c-S.$$
The claim is
$$|T^{-1}(c,g)|=\max\{0,\min\{L,A+1\}\}.$$

Strategy and dependencies: all source frequencies except $f_1$ are
forced by the shift; the bank condition leaves one integer interval;
mass conservation then verifies sufficiency. No source theorem or
scientific execution is required for this deduction.

1. Necessity gives $f_{j+1}=g_j$ for $1\le j<L$. Writing $x=f_1$, quotient
   and remainder force $b+x+S=Lg_L+c$, hence $b=A-x$.
2. The conditions $x\ge0$ and $0\le b<L$ are exactly
   $$\max(0,A-L+1)\le x\le A.$$
3. Each integer in this interval supplies a source of the correct mass:
   $$b+x+\sum_{j=1}^{L-1}(j+1)g_j
     =A+S+\sum_{j=1}^{L-1}j g_j=n.$$
   Its quotient/remainder and shifted coordinates return the target.
   Different $x$ give different sources. Counting the interval proves
   the formula, including its empty case and $L=1$.

There is also a direct sharp extremum. Since
$$A=n-\sum_{j=1}^{L-1}(j+1)g_j\le n,$$
the maximum fibre is $\min(L,n+1)$. It is attained by the target with
$g_1=\cdots=g_{L-1}=0$, $g_L=\lfloor n/L\rfloor$ and $c=n\bmod L$.
If $n<L-1$, equality requires that sole all-bank target. If $n\ge L-1$,
equality holds exactly when $A\ge L-1$. These statements include the
single-state cases $n=0$ and $L=1$.

This is an evaluated inverse, not an implicit enumeration over all
partitions. Nevertheless the complete-cycle axis is directly owned, and
the inverse is only a one-variable bank interval. No new pointwise
transient law or other material temporal residual was established here.
Consequently this control is not nominated and no pilot is requested.
