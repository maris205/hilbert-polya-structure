## Direct primary sources actually read

1. Andreievskii, Gurbatov and Sobolevskii, *Ballistic aggregation in symmetric
   and non-symmetric flows*, [author-hosted PDF](https://www-n.oca.eu/etc7/AGS.pdf),
   §2.1, physical PDF pages 2–3. The source specifies linear free trajectories
   and post-collision total mass with mass-weighted center-of-mass velocity.
   Its trajectories cannot cross without collision. These are substantive
   differences from discrete landing-only MCA with velocity equal to pile
   mass. This is a close background mechanism, **not** proof that the exact
   MCA update or its finite-ring theorem is already owned. The downloaded
   PDF and readable local extraction preserve the checked definition.

2. Knopfmacher, Odlyzko, Pittel, Richmond, Stark, Szekeres and Wormald,
   *The asymptotic number of set partitions with unequal block sizes*,
   [primary author PDF](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/unequal.block.partitions.pdf),
   introduction, Eq. (1.1), printed page 1 / physical PDF page 2. That page
   was actually visually read from `distinct_blocks_page2.png` after native
   rendering of the downloaded PDF. It defines the distinct-size set
   partition count and displays exactly the product EGF used for D(N) in
   `PROOF_NOTES.md`; the page attributes the formula to Carlitz. We deduct
   the formula on the strength of this directly checked primary display,
   without pretending to have read the earlier Carlitz source or the full
   asymptotic paper. No supermultiplicativity or MCA extremum theorem is
   attributed to these authors.

The author publication list at
[Odlyzko's combinatorial enumeration page](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/enumeration.html)
provided bibliographic identity and the direct PDF link. It lists EJC 6(1)
