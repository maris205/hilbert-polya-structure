## 1. Repeated-letter stack sorting: both axes already direct subjects

[Defant–Kravitz, *Stack-sorting for Words*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.09158)
was inspected at Definition 1.1, the fixed-multiplicity carrier, Proposition
2.2's statement, Lemma 3.1 and its tree/preimage discussion. Their operators
hare and tortoise already resolve the two equal-letter stack conventions.
For multiplicities $(c_1,\ldots,c_n)>0$, the paper states sharp maximum
sorting times $n-1$ and $c_2+\cdots+c_n$, respectively. It also supplies the
weakly decreasing tree/valid-hook approach to arbitrary-word fibres.
These are direct source boundaries, not newly proved time or inverse results.

A current source correction is important before proposing comparisons:
[Chen–Luo–Zhang, *A Disproof of Two Conjectures on Stack-Sorting Maps for
Words*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00026-025-00791-w), published
1 December 2025, says in its publisher abstract that both proposed universal
time-comparison bounds fail and the hare/tortoise sorting-time ratio is
unbounded. Only the publisher abstract/metadata were read, not the
counterexample proof. No stronger quantitative bound is inferred.

Thus the natural fixed-multiplicity sharp-time entrance and a relabelled
tree/hook inverse are unavailable as new contributions. A genuinely stronger
joint time/fibre theorem was not proved here. There is no justified small
pilot contract based on an assumed universal time comparison.

## 2. Queuesort: all-target inverse and maximum already controlled

[Cioni–Ferrari, *Preimages under the Queuesort algorithm*](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.07628)
was inspected at its queue definition, equivalent LTR-maxima update,
recursive preimage construction, Theorem 4.4 statement and Corollary 4.5.
The source gives full-target recursive preimages, not merely one sortable
pattern class. It states monotonicity of fibre size under inclusion of the
positions of left-to-right maxima; identity has all positions and Catalan
many preimages. Consequently even the maximum-size bound by the identity
is already an immediate source consequence. No complete equality-target
classification is claimed here from non-strict monotonicity alone.

No all-iterate inverse refinement and independently new sharp pointwise
clock were proved. Replacing the published recursive decoder with a program,
or citing the identity's Catalan fibre as a second theorem, would not meet
the requested residual threshold. Stop before designing an alternate queue
