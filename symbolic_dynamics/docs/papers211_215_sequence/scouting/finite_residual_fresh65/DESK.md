## 2. Different proposed literal: reverse only long descending runs

For n>=0 the carrier is S_n. Decompose w uniquely into maximal contiguous
strictly decreasing runs. Simultaneously reverse each run of length at least
three and leave runs of lengths one and two unchanged; call the result L(w).
This is a finite autonomous map without scheduler memory or changing carrier.
It is not unrestricted pop-stack: L(21)=21 while pop-stack sends 21 to 12.
It is also not the value-complemented old odd-increasing-run reversal ORR:
on 4321, L gives 1234, while odd-descending-run reversal holds 4321.
These examples prove only nonidentity, not nonconjugacy or novelty.

### What is actually proved at this desk

Let inv(w) count inverted pairs. Reversing a decreasing run of length r
removes precisely binomial(r,2) inversions. Relative order between elements
in different runs is unchanged, because their position intervals do not
interleave. Therefore

    inv(w)-inv(L(w)) = sum over selected runs R of binomial(|R|,2).

Every nonfixed update lowers inv by at least three. Every orbit reaches a
fixed point in at most floor(inv(w)/3) steps; all recurrent points are exactly
permutations with no contiguous decreasing triple. This means consecutive
321 avoidance, not classical 321 avoidance. No sharpness of this bound is
claimed. The rule is not idempotent: by direct hand substitution,

    326541 -> 321456 -> 123456.

The maximal descending runs of the first state are 32 and 6541; those of
the second are 321, 4, 5, 6. This is a symbolic example, not a pilot.

There is a complete special fibre, at the identity. A predecessor must be
a direct sum of decreasing consecutive-value blocks, each of size one or
at least three. Indeed the output intervals must individually be increasing;
a held decreasing pair is impossible. Identity output forces every selected
input block to be the reversal of its corresponding identity interval.
Conversely every such composition gives those maximal descending runs,
separated by ascents, and maps to identity. Thus the identity fibre has size

    a_n = 1 + sum_{k=1}^{floor(n/3)} sum_{s=0}^{n-3k}
                 binomial(s+k,k) binomial(n-s-2k-1,k-1).

Here k is the number of long blocks and s the number of singleton blocks.
Choose their interleaving, then distribute n-s-3k excess letters among k
long blocks by stars and bars. The initial 1 is the all-singleton composition,
including n=0. This is an evaluated special fibre, not a general inverse,
an extremum, or a substitute for either missing result.

For arbitrary targets one can guess interval cuts, reverse selected
increasing intervals and test reconstructed maximal descending runs. This
is only a generic inverse search description; no evaluated all-target
formula or sharp maximum is claimed here.
