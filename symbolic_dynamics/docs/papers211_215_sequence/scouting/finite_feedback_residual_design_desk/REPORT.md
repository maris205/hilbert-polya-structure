## Literal and obtained deductions

For $x\in\{0,\ldots,M\}^n$, let $k$ be its number of positive coordinates.
Fix zero; otherwise simultaneously replace each $x_i$ by $x_i\bmod k$.
This is F1/SCRF, support-count remainder feedback. There is no
second-positive-value selector, cyclic neighbor divisor, active-coordinate
protection, phase tag or replacement of the carrier.

The proof package establishes:

- The recurrent states are precisely zero and positive-support vectors
  whose maximum is smaller than their support count.
- The sharp global entrance time is $H=\min(n,M)$. For $M\ge1$, every
  maximizing source has support $H$ and its positive coordinates form a
  complete residue system modulo $H$, with the zero residue represented
  positively. All such sources and their exact count are supplied. At
  $M=0$ the sole source is zero; the $n=1$ boundary is also explicit.
- A disjoint all-target decoder gives the full fibre product sum and an
  exact image criterion in terms of target support and maximum.
- Zero is the unique maximum-fibre target. A constructive injection keeps
  the source quotient vector while compressing its multiplier to the
  quotient's support count. Its inverse recovery and an explicit omitted
  zero-predecessor prove strictness.

No theorem is inferred from finite data. The sole displayed comparison
box with old SPR is hand evaluated: at $n=2,M=1$, SCRF has two fixed
points whereas SPR has three. This separates those exact same-parameter
maps, not every possible old family.

## Why it still closes

The temporal proof is wholly organized by support deletion, with an
unchanged-support step producing a fixed output immediately. Its exact
attaining staircase follows by demanding equality at every support drop.
The inverse consists of one common modulus, a choice of occupied
zero-target coordinates, and independent quotient choices; its extremum
uses a quotient-preserving monotone injection.

Those mechanisms are mathematically distinct but do not automatically
supply two separately valued research contributions. The actual old
SPR gate rejects direct quotient/occupancy inverses even alongside its
stronger majorization-based clock; CPRM already supplies a
quotient-preserving lowering injection. This desk therefore declines
promotion on its current residual value, without falsely asserting an
exact SCRF-to-SPR or SCRF-to-CPRM conjugacy. More boxes would not repair
that value issue. No second literal is introduced just to fill the
two-literal allowance.
