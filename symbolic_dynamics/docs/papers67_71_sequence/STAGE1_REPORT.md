## Why the surface-group row was replaced

The first draft placed a surface-group flat-connection SFT in P69.  Hostile
review found that it and P70 both led to finite-index fixed-point data and then
to finite-group representation decompositions.  More importantly, the raw
flat-connection formula is already the classical surface state-sum after a
based-gauge factor, so the remaining symbolic wrapper did not yet demonstrate
paper mass.  It is therefore a documented reserve, not a selected row.

The replacement P69 changes all three axes: its acting object is a `Z`-skew
product, its current invariant is subexponential edge-name growth at scale
`sqrt(n)`, and
its proof engine is a Rudin--Shapiro cocycle-range recurrence.  It is also more
risky in a different sense: the mathematics closes compactly from owner
recurrences, so independent paper mass rather than proof feasibility is the
Stage-2 kill gate.
