# Provisional primary-source leads

## Status vocabulary

This is a Stage-1 scoping list, not the final annotated bibliography.
`PRIMARY_TEXT_INSPECTED_FOR_SCOPING` means that the abstract or source text was
opened and the local claim neighborhood was checked.  Stage 2 must still

## P67 -- multiplicative plaquette subshift

- Kenyon, Peres and Solomyak, [Hausdorff dimension for fractals invariant
  under the multiplicative integers](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.5136):
  foundational multiplicative symbolic/fractal setting.
- Ban, Hu, Lai and Liao, [Entropy of axial product of multiplicative
  subshifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19324): current entropy geometry for
  multiplicative/axial symbolic systems.
- [Additive and multiplicative densities, prime valuations and symbolic
  models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19525): nearest current source for finite
  prime-valuation coordinates and symbolic correspondences.

Scoping subtraction: multiplicative symbolic models and valuation coordinates
are owned.  The proposed residual is the specified linear plaquette constraint
and its two exact finite-geometry laws.  Exact-equation search remains open.

## P68 -- complete-bipartite hom-shifts

- Chandgotia and Thorat, [Finitely Dependent Processes on
  Subshifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02226): defines the current finite-
  dependence problem for hom-shifts, proves a negative result for finite
  four-cycle-free targets and asks for a natural graph characterization.
- Chandgotia and Marcus, [Mixing Properties for Hom-Shifts and the Distance
  between Walks on Associated Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.08357):
  foundational mixing/structure neighborhood that must be searched for the
  complete-bipartite phase decomposition and its consequences.
- Chandgotia, [Lecture 4: An introduction to
  hom-shifts](https://nishantchandgotia.github.io/Teaching/2019_Jagiellonian/coursekrakow/l4.pdf),
  slide 28: public teaching notes already describe the fair checkerboard phase
  and independent-colouring measure for the complete-bipartite example.  This
  is not a peer-reviewed theorem owner, but it is direct evidence that the MME
  core is publicly recorded/folklore and cannot supply paper mass by itself.

Scoping subtraction: no general first-ever finite-dependence claim is
available.  The contract is frozen to `m,n,d>=2`: then `K_(m,n)` has
four-cycles and is outside the stated four-cycle-free theorem.  When one part
has size one, that theorem already covers the `d>=2` full-action nonexistence
claim; `d=1` is also excluded as a classical period-two one-dimensional case.
The residual must be the subgroup-action finite-dependence dichotomy plus a
nontrivial upgrade beyond the recorded phase/MME description, not merely the
observation that the target is bipartite.  Exact owner/folklore search and a
paper-mass review are not complete, so the row is `REVISE_SOURCE`.

## P69 -- Rudin--Shapiro `[T,T^-1]` skew product

- Carrasco-Vargas, [Topological slow entropy, sequence entropy, and generalized
  `[T,T^-1]` systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17932): owns the general
  skew-product/slow-entropy transfer framework; its Equation (14) already uses
  the exponential sum over cocycle visited ranges that specializes to `C_q(n)`
  when `t=log q`.
- Lü, Chen, Wen and Wu, [On the abelian complexity of the Rudin--Shapiro
  sequence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06935): owns the maximal factor-sum
  recurrence and 2-regular structure used to control the cocycle.
- Lü and Han, [A Note on the Abelian Complexity of the Rudin--Shapiro
  Sequence](https://doi.org/10.3390/math10020221): gives the optimal
  `sqrt(n)` envelope and density results for the abelian-complexity sequence,
  together with the first-occurrence recurrence
  `s(2k)=4s(k)-1`, `s(2k+1)=4s(k)` used here.

Scoping subtraction: neither the general slow-entropy transfer nor the
Rudin--Shapiro recurrence/envelope is available as a new claim.  The proposed
residual is the exact relation between visited range and the running maximum
of known factor-sum complexity, followed by the resulting accumulation
interval for the frozen full-shift fibre.  The owner recurrence closes the
constants in a short argument, while the general paper already contains the
same range-sum scale.  Thus mathematics is viable but the specialization is
not yet an independent paper; owner subtraction and paper mass are hard gates.

## P70 -- discrete-Heisenberg principal linear shift

- Göll, Schmidt and Verbitskiy, [Algebraic actions of the discrete Heisenberg
  group](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.2469): owns the principal Heisenberg
  algebraic-action framework and studies the neighboring element `2-x-y`.
- Lind and Schmidt, [A Survey of Algebraic Actions of the Discrete Heisenberg
  Group](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.06243), Example 4.4(a): studies the exact
  defining element `1+x+y` and proves a mixing statement for its principal
  action.  P70 therefore claims neither a new system nor a new group-ring
  element.
- Gurevich and Hadani, [Notes on quantization of symplectic vector spaces over
  finite fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0669): finite-Heisenberg
  Stone--von Neumann/Schrödinger background.

Scoping subtraction: P70 cannot claim a first Heisenberg algebraic shift or a
new defining element.  The residual is only the mod-`p` congruence fixed-
nullity formula on finite Heisenberg quotients.  A bounded source-text search
did not expose that formula, but this is not a priority certificate.

## P71 -- full zip shifts

- Lamei and Mehdipour, [Zip shift
  space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11272): definition of zip spaces/maps,
  local-homeomorphism structure and orbit/preimage results.
- Lamei, Mehdipour and Vargas, [S-Expansiveness and Zip Shift Maps in Symbolic
  Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12980): S-expansivity, shadowing and a
  factor theorem; the source also records a periodic-point statement.
- Mehdipour and Jangjooye Shaldehi, [Square Entropy and Uniform n-to-1
  Bernoulli Transformations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24647): proves
  intrinsic ergodicity for uniform `n`-to-one full zip shifts and discusses
  invariant measures/Bernoulli transformations.

Scoping subtraction: fixed-point counts, density/mixing, local homeomorphism,
S-expansivity and shadowing are not available as new headlines.  Text searches
also show that uniform-fibre intrinsic ergodicity and invariant-measure results
must be returned to their owner.  The surviving candidate is restricted to
nonuniform fibre profiles and led by topological conjugacy classification;
natural-extension measures are supporting structure.  No fibre-profile
classification was found in the bounded scoping search, but Stage 2 must
confirm this against all versions and cited zip papers and must pass a paper-
mass gate.

## Surface-group flat-SFT reserve firewall

- Mulase and Yu, [A generating function of the number of homomorphisms from a
  surface group into a finite group](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0209008), and
  Klug, [Counting homomorphisms from surface groups to finite
  groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11089): own the surface representation-
  count formula and its modern normalization.
- Turaev, [Dijkgraaf--Witten invariants of surfaces and projective
  representations of groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.0160): explicitly
  expresses the untwisted surface state-sum as
  `|Hom(pi_1 Sigma,K)|/|K|` and develops its representation-theoretic form.

Consequently the reserve identity
`|Fix_H X|=|K|^(n-1)|Hom(pi_1 Sigma_h,K)|` is a transparent based-gauge
normalization of classical material.  It may return only if a genuinely new
dynamical theorem, not the wrapper or its immediate power-sum corollaries,
passes an owner and paper-mass audit.
