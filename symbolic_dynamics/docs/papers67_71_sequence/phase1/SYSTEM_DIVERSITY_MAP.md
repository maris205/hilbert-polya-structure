## P1--P66 occupancy firewall

The audit treated the following clusters as occupied or too recent to reuse as
the primary system:

- P4--P18: symbolic Euler/Fredholm and tensor-prime constructions;
- P19--P34: arithmetic graphs, codes, verifiers and countable Markov engines;
- P35--P39: affine, HNN and Bass--Serre constructions;
- P44--P48: weighted-operator and Schatten packages;
- P49--P53: tree shifts, divisibility Toeplitz, countable finite-memory,
  one-forbidden-word and synchronized-code systems;
- P54--P61: uniform morphisms, probabilistic automata, graph covers, cellular
  automata, generic periodic realization, renewal routing, Sturmian/Ostrowski
  and graph splitting;
- P62--P66: random substitutions, rank-one systems, beta-shifts, 2D
  IUBS/unishifts and free-group hard-core sofic entropy.

Consequently, this batch rejected generic S-adic/substitution, Toeplitz,
Sturmian/multi-hole rotation, beta, tree, CA, renewal, graph-splitting and
generic amenable-lattice entropy continuations even where a proof looked easy.

## Stage-2 hard rule

No failed row may be replaced by a second example from another selected row's
system family.  A replacement must open a new cluster.  Current ordered
reserves are the surface-group flat-connection SFT, a critical-Jordan 2D block
substitution, and a metallic-mean Wang shift, each subject to the owner,
internal-collision and proof gates recorded in the candidate ledger.
