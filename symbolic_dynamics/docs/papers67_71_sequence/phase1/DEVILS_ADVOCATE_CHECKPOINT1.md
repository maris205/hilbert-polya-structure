multiplicative action, while a single `(a,b)`-root component has a separate
geometry.
The 2026 prime-valuation paper may also contain the formula in disguised

- all `(a,b)`-exponent rectangles through `6 x 6` over three fields;
- explicit pivot check that each relation has largest new coordinate `abn`.

**Decision: `GO_MATH / HOLD_EXACT_NEIGHBOR_SEARCH`.**  Use “arithmetic prefix
rate” and “`(a,b)`-exponent-boundary law,” not two topological entropies.  Stage 2
must search the exact equation and prove the global root-component product
decomposition.  An exact owner kills or narrows the row.

## P69 adversarial review

**Issue.**  For a `+1/-1` cocycle, the maximal visited range reduces to the
running maximum of factor-sum excursions.  Existing work already owns the
Rudin--Shapiro maximal factor-sum recurrence and its optimal square-root
envelope, while a 2025 paper
owns the general `[T,T^-1]` slow-entropy framework.  The proposed result may
collapse to a short composition of those sources.

**Stress tests performed.**

- the four-letter fixed point has `8n-8` factors at every tested
  `2<=n<=256`;
- for dyadic lengths through `256`, exhaustive factor enumeration gives
  `R(2^j)=3*2^(j/2)-1` for even `j` and
  `R(2^j)=2^((j+3)/2)-1` for odd `j`;
- for each factor, the fibre-name count is exactly `q^r(w)` because a
  nearest-neighbor walk visits every integer between its extrema.

**Decision: `MATH_VALID / HOLD_OWNER_AND_PAPER_MASS`.**  With
`s(k)=min{n:rho(n)=k}`, the owner recurrence
`s(2k)=4s(k)-1`, `s(2k+1)=4s(k)` turns the finite pattern into a short proof:
`R(n)=max{k:s(k)<=n}`; `27s(k)<=5k^2+9` (equality at `k=3*2^t`) gives the
liminf; powers of four give the limsup; and vanishing adjacent increments give
the full interval.  Stage 2 must record that proof with exact citations, then
test for an additional theorem.  Carrasco--Vargas already uses essentially the
same exponential range sum, so the present specialization alone is too close
to an owner-derived corollary.  If no substantive extension survives, replace
P69.

**Issue.**  Principal Heisenberg actions are an established subject; finite

**Stress tests performed.**

  choices;

- the characteristic-three cases give nullities `21` at `ell=5`, exactly
  `1+ell(ell-1)`;

**Decision: `GO_MATH / HOLD_EXACT_ELEMENT_AND_FINITE_QUOTIENT_SEARCH`.**  The
proof engine survived hostile checks, but Lind--Schmidt already treat the exact
element `1+x+y`.  Stage 2 must formalize the dual-convolution invariance and
search specifically for the mod-`p` finite-quotient nullity formula.  No first-
action, first-element or generator claim is allowed.

natural-extension structure, not a first intrinsic-ergodicity claim.  The
decorated periodic identity contains no more profile information than its
fixed-point case.  P71 must pass a separate paper-mass gate or be replaced.
