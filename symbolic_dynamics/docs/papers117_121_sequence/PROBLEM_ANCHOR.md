# Problem anchor: the P117–P121 breadth round

## Shared question

Which natural finite or controlled updates, outside the dynamical mechanisms
already occupied by P1–P116, expose exact temporal laws quickly enough to
support a short theorem paper?  The round remains on Route A: discover many
different dynamical subclasses and promote only systems whose first finite
experiments reveal a crisp structural clock, anomaly, factorization, or law.

## Intake criterion

A paper counts as progress only if it proves an infinite-family result with at
least two substantial temporal outputs—for example recurrent structure and
fibres, pointwise depth and extremal depth, an exact finite-time law and a
limit theorem, or a generating function together with parameter recovery.
Each candidate must also have a deterministic verifier and two materially
different proof or control routes.

## Search discipline

This round is not an attempt to deepen one previously successful model.
Generic finite functional graphs, ordinary linear finite dynamical systems,
rowmotion/toggle variants, standard pruning processes, and cosmetic reset,
switching, valuation, coefficient-decimation, or closure variants begin behind
a hard collision firewall.  A system without a clear early anomaly is killed
or returned to reserve rather than rescued by prolonged calculation.

## Freeze status

No paper number is assigned during the landscape audit.  Five systems will be
frozen only after a pool of at least forty explicit candidates has passed
bounded falsification, nearest-owner subtraction, and internal collision
screening.

External posting, submission, specialist contact, novelty, priority,
authorship, and venue decisions remain **HOLD_EXTERNAL**.
