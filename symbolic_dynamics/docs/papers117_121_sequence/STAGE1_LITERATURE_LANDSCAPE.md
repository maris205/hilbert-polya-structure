## Global conclusion

Several broad labels are now too occupied to serve as contributions by
themselves.  In particular, “finite functional graph,” “linear map over a
finite field,” “rowmotion,” “reset automaton,” “finite-state reduction,”
“Perron–Frobenius pressure,” “parallel peeling,” and “closure process” are

must be a rigid update family with a pair- or family-specific temporal anomaly
and at least two exact outputs after this background is subtracted.

## Algebraic landscape

The generic finite-field and linear lanes are dense.  Linear finite dynamical
systems already have structural functional-graph theory and asymptotic
enumeration; polynomial and generalized cyclotomic maps over finite fields
likewise have direct functional-graph programs
([Reis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09814),
[Konyagin–Luca–Mans–Mathieson–Sha–Shparlinski](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2718),
[Bors–Panario–Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00181)).  Consequently a new
finite-ring, matrix, polynomial, or module candidate must expose a
structure-specific clock or resonance not supplied by rational/Jordan
decomposition, ordinary valuations, a power map, or a generic functional
digraph.

The internal firewall is also substantial: P97, P99, P100, P102, P103, P107,
P109, and P115 already occupy sumset squaring, unipotent sublattice shear,
valuation erasure, group-algebra norm, double adjugation, annihilator-power
ideals, nilpotent-image subspaces, and bounded Cartier decimation.  The only
reasonable algebraic entrances are therefore high-threshold ones such as bad-
characteristic anomalies of a rigid nonlinear transform, a coupled
semilinear coefficient operator beyond Cartier, a genuinely multidimensional
clock in a non-principal finite algebra, or a two-operator module action whose
temporal invariant does not reduce to P109/P115.

## Combinatorial landscape

Classical named actions are owner-heavy.  Poset rowmotion and promotion have
large structural theories, including toggle-group formulations and broad
families
([Dilks–Striker–Vorland](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.07179),
[Hopkins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01568),
[Bernstein–Striker–Vorland](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12219),
[Elder–Lafrenière–McNicholas–Striker–Welch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08520)).
Local complementation has Bouchet's isotropic-system and equivalence theory
([1988](https://doi.org/10.1016/0095-8956(88)90055-X),
[1993](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(93)90357-Y)); rotor routing already
has recurrent-state and Picard-group orbit theories
([Holroyd et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.3306),
[Chan–Church–Grochow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05811)); and propagation time
and forcing-set enumerators are direct zero-forcing subjects
([Hogben et al.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166218X1200162X),
[Boyer et al.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/am/pii/S0166218X18306474)).

Parallel reduction is especially risky after P105 and P114.  Strong collapse
already has core theory
([Barmak–Minian](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.2954)); GYO reduction is classical
hypergraph-acyclicity machinery
([Beeri–Fagin–Maier–Yannakakis](https://doi.org/10.1145/2402.322389),
[Fagin](https://doi.org/10.1145/2402.322390)); and graph-convexity iteration
time is itself an established parameter
([Hon–Kloks–Liu–Liu–Wang](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0096300324005459)).
Thus a simultaneous local-complement map, full rotor-router transient census,
high-dimensional collapse map, or all-seed forcing map may be spiked only for
a residual absent from those owners: complete fibres/basins, an unexpected
period law, or a new algebraic factorization.  Ordinary leaf/core stripping,
corner rank, closure depth, and a renamed graph height are excluded.

## Stochastic, semigroup, and rewrite landscape

The finite-semigroup and synchronization baselines are broad.  Random walks
on left-regular bands and \(\mathscr R\)-trivial monoids have explicit spectral
theory, while arbitrary finite semigroups have general stationary-distribution
machinery
([Brown](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0006145),
[Ayyer–Schilling–Steinberg–Thiéry](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4250),
[Rhodes–Schilling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10689)).  Reset and random
synchronization are also direct research areas
([Nicaud](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.6962),
[Gusev](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.6731),
[Berlinkov–Nicaud](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.02154)).  Probabilistic rewriting
has termination and runtime frameworks
([Avanzini–Dal Lago–Yamada](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09774),
[Kassing–Giesl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17714)); asynchronous/abelian
networks have general halting and recurrence theories
([Bond–Levine](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0169),
[Chan–Levine](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03322)).

Internally, P62, P79, P89, P93, P101, P104, P111, and P116 already occupy
random substitution, hidden finite memory, reset random SFTs, push–pop
reflection, cap/floor synchronization, monomial contraction, Heisenberg word
area, and finite-gap max-plus switching.  Hence regeneration, a finite Markov
lumping, a transfer matrix, pressure, LLN/CLT/LDP, or an expected reset time
earns no independent credit.  The most credible residual entrances are a
finite confluent two-rule rewrite with a complete absorption-time/fibre law,
a non-\(\mathscr R\)-trivial Rees/Green family coupling entrance time to group
phase, a one-defect abelian network with an exact schedule law, or a
permutation-plus-defect automaton whose minimal image has rank greater than
one and never synchronizes.

number is assigned:

4. two materially different proof or control routes are credible; and
5. any negative literature result is phrased only as “no direct owner located
   in the bounded search,” never as novelty or priority.
