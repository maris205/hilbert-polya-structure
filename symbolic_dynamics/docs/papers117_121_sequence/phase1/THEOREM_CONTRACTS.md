# Frozen theorem contracts for P117–P121

Date: 2026-08-30.  
Status: internal paper contracts; external HOLD.

## P117 — odd-run reversal

Required:

1. boundary survival iff incident run parities agree;
2. recurrent iff all run lengths share one parity;
3. exact fixed/two-periodic census and zeta;
4. sharp maximum preperiod \((n-1)/2\) for odd \(n\);
5. sharp maximum preperiod \(\lfloor(n-2)/4\rfloor\) for even \(n\);
6. explicit extremal families and exhaustive control through \(n=16\).

Forbidden: necklace quotient counts, full basin layers, priority.

## P118 — synchronous multipartite mex

Required:

1. one-round part collapse;
2. inclusion–exclusion and support/EGF formulas for every labelled fibre;
3. exact fixed and injection-indexed two-cycle quotient;
4. repeated-maximum image lemma and two-round recurrence;
5. closed recurrent fibres and every global/orbitwise depth layer;
6. explicit serial-daemon owner subtraction.

Forbidden: arbitrary graphs, asynchronous convergence, polynomial-time
evaluation, a new Grundy rule.

## P119 — regular Engel map

Required:

1. fixed \(J=I+N\) with \(N\) the regular upper shift;
2. reproduce Bier's owned \(E(\gamma_k)=\gamma_{k+1}\) only as an interface,
   and refine it by exact \(q^{n-k}\) finite-field fibres;
3. centralizer-coset and triangular-difference proofs;
4. all iterated fibres, exact layers, sharp height and deepest shell;
5. filtration-stratum indegrees, unique recurrent identity, zeta;
6. the \(U_4\) nonregular counterexample.

Forbidden: arbitrary unipotent \(J\), general Engel/Lang novelty.

## P120 — odd-fringe mirror

Required:

1. topology-preserving involution and local fixed criterion;
2. even-root fixed sequence and odd-root twisted-palindrome grammar;
3. coupled \(E/O\) generating system;
4. explicit degree-six annihilating equation with the unique
   zero-constant branch condition;
5. exact fixed/two-cycle/iterate/zeta census;
6. ordinary-mirror and current Catalan-involution firewalls.

Forbidden: asymptotics, polynomial minimality or irreducibility, general
Catalan-action novelty.

## P121 — random product-plus-one coalescence

Required:

1. prove the objectwise owner identification \(X_n=R_n+1\) under the common
   ordered-history coupling;
2. assign Disanto et al. zero credit for the split/law, unmarked antichains,
   mean, and order-two neighborhood;
3. credit the fixed-tree cardinality marker and derive only its
   Yule-averaged closed transform;
4. treat the arbitrary-order identity as mechanical zero-credit input and,
   beginning at \(r=3\), prove a strict radius continuation with unit-residue
   poles and exact limsup;
5. retain the owned mean only as normalization;
6. reproduce the owned caterpillar minimum and exact Chang--Fuchs
   probability only as controls.

Forbidden: for \(r\ge3\), uniqueness of all dominant complex singularities
or full coefficient asymptotics; novelty for the Yule statistic, split/law,
unmarked antichains, fixed-tree cardinality markers, mean, second moment,
caterpillar probability, random-BST, or forest ideals.
