## Search boundary and interpretation

The searches used exact-rule strings and several neighboring formulations:

- S01: `"BA -> AB" "AA -> A"`, `BA=AB A^2=A`, graph products of
  monoids, random redex/reduction strategy, absorption or derivation-time
  distribution, and probabilistic-rewrite runtime/complexity;
- S02/S10: one-local-defect abelian network, random legal execution, equal-rate
  tandem service, finite-customer tandem clearing, lattice-path/ballot hitting,
  and queue--RSK--tableau formulations;
- S07: `"CB -> BC" "BA -> AB"`, `CBA -> BCA/CAB`, nondeterministic
  multiway strings, random adjacent swaps, oriented swap process, and
  multispecies exclusion with pair/species-dependent rates;
- each family was rerun with `2025`, `2026`, recent-arXiv, title, DOI, and
  official-author/publisher-page variants. In particular, the search included
  2025 probabilistic-runtime work, a 2026 probabilistic-rewriting paper, the
  December-2025 exact S07 rule-pair hit, and 2026 oriented-swap work.

The evidence base is arXiv records, DOI/publisher pages, proceedings pages, and
author/institutional copies. Search snippets alone were not used to assign an
owner when the direct paper or its abstract/full text was available. This is a
**bounded owner audit, not an exhaustive equivalence-class or citation-graph
search**. Every negative statement below means only “no direct hit in these
formulations and sources as of the checked date”; it is not a novelty claim.

## Direct owner landscape

1. **Probabilistic rewriting and quantitative reduction.** Faggian develops
   probabilistic abstract rewriting, unique limit distributions, comparison of
   reduction strategies, and expected termination time; these are framework
   ownership, not S01 residuals
   ([FSCD 2019 paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2019.19)).
   Avanzini--Dal Lago--Yamada own probabilistic term-rewrite termination with
   nondeterministic redex choice
   ([FLOPS/technical version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09774)). Recent
   quantitative neighbors include Avanzini--Yamada's weighted ARSs and expected
   derivation heights
   ([FSCD 2025](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2025.6)) and
   Kassing--Spitzer--Giesl's expected-runtime dependency-pair framework
   ([2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12918)). The 2026 search also
   reached Kassing--Nagel--Schlecht--Giesl's random-walk techniques for
   disproving probabilistic termination
   ([arXiv:2602.16522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16522)); it is temporal
   coverage, not a direct owner of any candidate here.
2. **Graph-product normal forms.** Yang--Gould prove unique Foata normal forms
   and reduction results for arbitrary graph products of monoids
   ([Journal of Algebra 620 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.12.018)).
   S01's presented monoid is the direct/complete-graph product of the
   two-element monoid `E={1,A}`, `A^2=A`, and the free monogenic monoid
   generated by `B`. Consequently its algebraic normal form is owner territory.
3. **Abelian executions.** Bond--Levine prove the least-action principle and
   order-independence of final output/odometer for halting abelian networks
   ([Abelian Networks I](https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1030984),
   [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.3445)). This owns the unstarred
   S02/S10 baseline and every claim that is merely schedule independence.
4. **Tandem queues and tableaux.** Draief--Mairesse--O'Connell explicitly treat
   transient tandem behavior via RSK and semistandard Young tableaux
   ([Journal of Applied Probability 42 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1134587823),
   [author record/preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.4104)). This is a direct
   proof-engine owner for S02 and especially S10.
5. **Local adjacent-swap systems.** Angel--Holroyd--Romik's oriented swap
   process is the canonical random adjacent inversion-swap process
   ([Annals of Probability](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2222)). Cantini formulates
   multispecies TASEP by local exchange rates `r_(alpha,beta)` and derives exact
   algebraic structure for an integrable species-dependent family
   ([arXiv:1602.07921](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07921)); Lee gives transition
   probabilities for another species-rate family
   ([arXiv:2105.14612](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14612)). Kovchegov treats
   exclusion-type particle systems whose interactions are assigned rates through
   permutations
   ([SPA 115 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spa.2005.03.003)). These do not
   by themselves prove S07's block terminal law, but they remove any claim that
   a random local swap carrier is new.
6. **Exact S07 literal collision.** Dündar--Arsiwalla--Elshatlawy display a
   terminating finite multiway string system with initial word `BBBAAACC` and
   the exact two rules `BA -> AB`, `CB -> BC`
   ([arXiv:2512.20587, Section 4/Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20587)).
   Their initial word does not activate the `CBA` critical overlap and they do
   not study the uniform-redex terminal law from `C^c B^b A^a`; nevertheless,
   the **literal rule pair and its finite multiway graph are already public**.

## S01 — commutation--idempotence random rewrite

### Claims under review

1. `BA -> AB`, `AA -> A` terminates and is confluent, with normal form
   `A B^b` whenever an `A` occurs.
2. The bounded fibre is
   `#{w: |w|<=N, NF(w)=A B^b}=binom(N+1,b+1)-1`.
3. For uniform choice among current redex occurrences from `B^b A^a`, the
   support is every integer from `a+b-1` through `ab+a-1`.
4. For `b>0`, the minimum-time atom is `1/a`; a future claim would be a closed
   full PGF or coefficient formula for the absorption law.

### Subtraction and residual

- Claim 1 is a special case of graph-product/complete-rewrite normal forms.
- Claim 2 is the hockey-stick identity after choosing exactly `b` copies of
  `B` and at least one `A`; it is evidence/notation, not a contribution.
- The atom `1/a` follows from the elementary telescoping product
  `prod_(k=2)^a (k-1)/k`; by itself it is not paper-scale.
- Claim 3 appears pair-specific, and the bounded search found no direct owner
  for this exact interval of derivation lengths. Yet derivation length,
  strategy comparison, and expected runtime are already owned at framework
  level.

The only defensible residual is therefore a **closed, coefficient-level full
law** `F_(a,b)(z)=E[z^T]` (or an equivalently strong bivariate generating
function), together with a nontrivial structural consequence not implied by a
finite-state recurrence. Support plus one extreme atom does not clear the gate.

**Closest owners:** Yang--Gould for the monoid normal form; Faggian and
Avanzini--Dal Lago--Yamada for probabilistic reduction semantics/strategies;
Avanzini--Yamada and Kassing--Spitzer--Giesl for quantitative expected
derivation analysis.

**Score:** **5.5/10.**  
**Verdict:** **CAUTION** — retain only for one hard symbolic PGF pilot.  
**Strongest objection:** after normal-form, fibre, and telescoping-atom
subtraction, the manuscript does not yet possess its proposed theorem; without
the full law it is an elementary worked example inside mature probabilistic
rewriting.

**Kill trigger:** no closed full law, or a full law that is merely a restatement
of exponential-state dynamic programming.

## S02 — one-defect two-stage queue

### Claims under review

1. The unstarred transfers from `(n,0)` are abelian and always take `2n`
   updates.
2. Replacing `B(1,1)` by `B*(1,1)=(0,0)` gives the exact two-point law
   `T_n in {2n-2,2n}`.
3. The short branch has mass
   `rho_n=binom(2n-2,n-1)/4^(n-1)` and hence order `n^(-1/2)`.
4. The harmonic/frontier description, including `h(1,k)=2^(-k)`, is a second
   structural output rather than a rephrasing of the same walk.

### Subtraction and residual

Claim 1 is exactly the abelian-network baseline. The workload `2x+y` proves
Claim 2 immediately. More seriously, the uniform legal-update chain is the
embedded jump chain of two equal-rate exponential tandem servers (an inference
from the literal transition rule), and `rho_n` is exactly the time-`2n-2`
return mass of a fair one-dimensional walk. Thus Claim 3 is the classical
central-binomial/bridge event in queue notation. The displayed boundary value
in Claim 4 is the same elementary harmonic computation, not yet a distinct
network theorem.

A no-hit was obtained for the exact phrase “one-defect abelian network” and
this literal starred update, but that absence does not save the package: the
claimed residual has already collapsed internally to an owned random-walk
quantity. A materially broader defect-frontier transform would be a new project,
not evidence for the current system.

**Closest owners:** Bond--Levine for the abelian legal-execution baseline;
Draief--Mairesse--O'Connell for tandem transient/tableau methods; classical
ballot/reflection theory for the surviving coefficient.

**Score:** **3.0/10.**  
**Verdict:** **ABANDON.**  
**Strongest objection:** “one noncommuting defect” is packaging; all current
formulas are the workload identity plus one fair-walk bridge probability, so no
network-specific exact temporal delta remains.

## S07 — competing critical-pair rewrite

### Claims under review

1. For `CB -> BC`, `BA -> AB`, the potential
   `I(C before B)+I(B before A)` decreases by one per step, proving termination.
2. `CBA` has the unresolved critical branching `CBA -> BCA` or `CBA -> CAB`,
   so the system is nonconfluent.
3. Uniform redex choice from `C^c B^b A^a` induces a schedule-dependent
   terminal distribution.
4. That block-family terminal law has a closed fibre/product/lattice-path form
   beyond generic absorbing-chain DP.

### Subtraction and residual

Claims 1--2 are the elementary critical-pair analysis. Claim 3 is a standard
embedded jump chain for a local adjacent-swap process with only two positive
pair rates. General random-swap/exclusion and probabilistic-rewrite frameworks
own that carrier. Most damagingly, the 2025 Dündar--Arsiwalla--Elshatlawy
preprint already displays the **same two literal substitutions** in a finite
multiway graph. Their chosen word and quantum/path-sum observable differ, so
they do not own Claim 4; however, S07 can no longer be positioned as a new
two-rule system or a new branching mechanism.

The mathematical residual is at most the exact terminal measure for the
specific initial block `C^c B^b A^a` under uniform-redex weighting. No such
formula was found in the bounded search. That delta is too narrow and too
exposed to “analyze a probability weighting on an already published multiway
example” unless a striking product/determinant formula is already in hand. It
is not.

**Closest owner:** Dündar--Arsiwalla--Elshatlawy for the exact literal rule
pair/multiway carrier; Cantini, Lee, Angel--Holroyd--Romik, and Kovchegov for
the adjacent-swap/exclusion neighborhood.

**Score:** **2.0/10.**  
**Verdict:** **ABANDON.**  
**Strongest objection:** a referee can cite a December-2025 paper containing
the identical two rules and then describe the proposed delta as merely putting
uniform jump probabilities on its branches.

## S10 — same-engine fallback only

S10 replaces two stages by three and places the defect at `(1,1,1)`. Its three
candidate claims are: workload support `{3n-5,3n}`; an exact defect-hit mass;
and a Weyl-chamber/determinantal or tableau evaluation of that mass. The first
is immediate from `3x+2y+z`. The other two lie even closer to classical tandem
queue/RSK/nonintersecting-path technology than S02. It also repeats the same
single-defect mechanism, so it cannot supply an independent contribution.

**Closest owner:** Draief--Mairesse--O'Connell's tandem/RSK correspondence,
after Bond--Levine subtraction of the abelian baseline.  
**Residual:** none unless a genuinely new weighted chamber identity is first
proved; even then it should be presented as a combinatorial identity, not a new
stochastic-network mechanism.  
**Score:** **2.0/10.**  
**Verdict:** **ABANDON; do not use as S02 fallback.**  
**Strongest objection:** the proposed “higher-rank rescue” changes dimension
but not engine, while moving the proof directly into an owner-rich RSK/tableau
domain.
