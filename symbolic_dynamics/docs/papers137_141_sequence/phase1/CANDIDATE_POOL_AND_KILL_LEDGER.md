## Consequential permanent kills

- `GPE`, `(x,y)->(xy,yx)`, is the previously killed Thue–Morse operator on
  finite groups.  Restricting to class-two groups does not revive it.
- Even-run flipping is exactly global-complement conjugate to P117's odd-run
  flip.  This collision is algebraic, not merely visual.
- Prior-equal occurrence rank is the old S04 truth table.  Prefix-rank stable
  standardization is a weak earlier-rank operator and is held owner-adjacent
  to the strict Lehmer-code iteration rather than promoted.
- Relation cubing `R->RR^T R` has a strong exact clock and basin census but is
  a standard difunctional closure; its strongest structural claim is owned.
- Synchronous equal-size set-partition merging reduces after quotienting to an
  occupied multiplicity-coagulation mechanism.  Equal-run composition
  coagulation was likewise below the inverse/value floor.
- State-dependent permutation powers (`CCP`, `FCP`, `MCP`) and nilpotent
  Jordan powers remain too close to owned power-map and occupied
  feedback-splitting machinery.  `CCP` is retained only as a reserve.
- Critical-root derivative dynamics (`CRD`) has a clean two-cycle and small
  depth layers, but its residual intersects the split-polynomial/Fail–Rolle
  literature and lacks the value of the frozen five.
- Boolean relation switches, degree-parity cut toggles, cyclic-rotation record
  masks, bare palindromic-prefix indicators, inventory feedback, and critical
  values failed either a direct-owner, collision, or temporal-spine gate.
- Random quotient-chunk Euclid collides with P131; biased adjacent contraction
  repeats the P121 separator/Yule skeleton; quota races, birthday-repeat
  stopping, Pólya quota stopping, and hyperedge cover exposure have direct
  process owners or only a classical inclusion–exclusion residual.

## Reserve policy

`CCP`, `CRD`, prefix-rank standardization, and relation cubing remain recorded
as negative or owner-adjacent evidence, not unpublished claims awaiting a
cosmetic rewrite.  No killed system may re-enter this batch without a new
literal update, a substantively different proof engine, and a fresh owner and
portfolio gate.

## Freeze rule

The five systems are frozen only for internal Stage 2 drafting.  A manuscript
may narrow a contract when review finds a flaw; it may not broaden it or turn
a bounded source non-hit into novelty, priority, authorship, or publication
language.  A direct owner of the residual conjunction immediately reopens the
corresponding slot under `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
