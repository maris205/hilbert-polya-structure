# Devil's-advocate checkpoint 1 — before numbering

## Hostile questions

1. Are any finalists merely a complement, quotient, parity restriction, or
   renamed instance of P1–P136?
2. Does each system expose a temporal theorem, rather than only a static
   counting identity?
3. Is the strongest-looking formula already the direct output of a classical
   decomposition or process?
4. Can every proposed sharp bound be paired with an explicit all-parameter
   witness and a uniqueness proof when uniqueness is claimed?
5. Is finite enumeration being used only to search for counterexamples?

## Findings

- The even-run flip and group product-exchange maps fail question 1 exactly
  and were permanently killed.
- The set-partition merge, bare palindrome indicator, inventory map, and
  several graph switches fail question 2.
- Relation cubing and the threshold-MIS support fail question 3; the former
  was withheld, while P141 was narrowed to its weighted residual.
- P137, P138, and P139 have explicit sharp witnesses.  P137 and P139 also
  carry uniqueness claims with proof obligations frozen in the contracts.
- The reports distinguish theorem proofs from exact pilots and never infer a
  claim from a depth table alone.

## Decision

Five systems may be numbered internally, conditional on the exact contracts
and a second checkpoint after the inverse and clock statements are frozen.
External status remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
