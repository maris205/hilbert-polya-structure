# Devil's-advocate checkpoint 2 — contract freeze

## Contract stress test

| paper | easiest overclaim | enforced repair or boundary |

| P137 | call the p-group split or Gaussian-binomial count new | assign both to classical input; claim only recomputed-rank dynamics and prove every fibre |

## Cross-paper stress test

P138 and P139 both feed a word diagnostic back into the carrier, but their
predicates, recurrence, attractors, sharp witnesses, and inverse engines are
different.  P140 and P141 are both stochastic, but one is a shrinking word
process with a deterministic number of jumps and the other is graph deletion
with a random accepted set size.  P137 shares partition notation with P135 but
has strict rank growth and no nontrivial cycles, rather than tag coarsening and
several two-cycle families.

## Freeze decision

All five contracts have an all-parameter proof route, an explicit
counterexample-search boundary, and a nonoverlapping theorem core.  P141 is
marked owner-thin and all papers remain `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.  Stage 2 may begin.
