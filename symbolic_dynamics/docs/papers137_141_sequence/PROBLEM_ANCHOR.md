## Anchored research question

Find five literal finite or absorbing dynamical systems on genuinely different
carriers for which early exact computation exposes an all-parameter theorem
that remains substantive after direct-owner subtraction and the complete
P1–P136 portfolio firewall.  Each selected system must support a deductive
proof, a paper-local exact falsifier, and a self-contained short manuscript.

## Nonnegotiable contribution test

A candidate survives only if its residual package contains a real dynamical
advance: a sharp clock, a complete recurrent or endpoint law, an all-target
inverse theorem, a temporal generating function, an extremal fibre theorem,
or an exact stochastic limit law.  A changed carrier, complement, parity,
schedule, logarithm base, standard closure, or renamed known operator is not a
new system.  Once a literal or mechanism collision is proved, the candidate is
permanently killed rather than repaired cosmetically.

## Breadth and replacement rule

- Search algebraic, word/combinatorial, graph, and stochastic lanes in
  parallel, and record every tested literal update.
- Preserve negative results and owner hits in a permanent kill ledger.
- Require a theorem contract and exact bounded counterexample search before a
  paper number is frozen.
- The five finalists may share generic bookkeeping such as generating
  functions, but not carrier, literal update, temporal silhouette, and proof
  engine simultaneously.
- When a clean signal is an internal conjugate or owner-saturated corollary,
  change system form instead of deepening the same failed candidate.

Finite enumeration is counterexample pressure, never proof or novelty
evidence.  No public release, priority, authorship, posting, submission,
specialist contact, or venue claim is authorized by this anchor.
