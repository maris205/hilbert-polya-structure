## T1. Bernoulli max-plus neutral-pair resonance -- GO / HOLD

### Definition and orientation

Work over `R_max=(R union {-infinity}, max, +)`.  Set

```text
A = [[-2,-1], [ 1,-1]],
B = [[-1, 1], [-1,-2]],
P(X_t=A)=p, P(X_t=B)=q=1-p,
M_n = X_n tensor ... tensor X_1.       (X_1 acts first.)
```

Let `0=(0,0)^T` and

```text
H_n = max_i (M_n tensor 0)_i = max_(i,j) (M_n)_(i,j).
```

Both matrices have tropical spectral radius zero:

```text
rho_max(A)=max{-2,-1,(-1+1)/2}=0,
rho_max(B)=0.
```

Indeed `H_n(A...A)=H_n(B...B)=n mod 2`, so both deterministic endpoints
have zero asymptotic growth.  Neither matrix has tropical rank one: its two
diagonal-sum alternatives are unequal.  This says nothing about longer
semigroup words.  In fact no word of length one or two is a projective reset,
whereas the four shortest resets are exactly `ABA, ABB, BAA, BAB`, with
forced output gaps `-3,0,0,3`.

### Exact finite-time law

The literal projective gap takes five values `{-3,-2,0,2,3}`, but the two
negative and two positive values are strongly lumpable.  Write the lumped
state as `N,Z,P`.  The complete state/reward transition table is

| current | choose `A` (probability `p`) | choose `B` (probability `q`) |
|---|---|---|
| `N` | `(Z,-1)` | `(P,+1)` |
| `Z` | `(N,+1)` | `(P,+1)` |
| `P` | `(N,+1)` | `(Z,-1)` |

The second component is the increment of `H`.  Consequently, with `y=e^t`,

```text
Q_p(y) = [[0, p/y, q y],
          [p y, 0, q y],
          [p y, q/y, 0]],

E[y^H_n] = e_Z^T Q_p(y)^n 1.
```

This is the full finite-time law, not only a moment recursion.  Its Perron
polynomial collapses to

```text
det(r I-Q_p(y))
  = r^3 + [2a-1-a y^2] r - a y,       a=pq.
```

The stationary projective law for `0<p<1` is

```text
pi_N = p/(1+p),
pi_Z = (1-a)/(2+a),
pi_P = q/(1+q).
```

### Proposed limit and pressure package

For `0<p<1`, finite-state Markov-additive theory, with the displayed kernel
as the complete calculation rather than a black box, gives

```text
H_n/n -> mu_p = 3a/(2+a)                         a.s.,

(H_n-n mu_p)/sqrt(n) => Normal(0,sigma_p^2),

sigma_p^2 = 4a(1-a)(5-2a)/(2+a)^3.
```

The variance is strictly positive on the open interval and vanishes at the
two deterministic endpoints.  Therefore arbitrarily weak iid switching
turns two zero-growth deterministic systems into a positive-growth cocycle.

The scaled cumulant generating function is exactly

```text
Lambda_p(t)=log rho_PF(Q_p(e^t)),       0<p<1,
```

and is analytic for finite `t`.  The word extrema and both zero-temperature
edges are also exact:

```text
n mod 2 <= H_n <= n;
H_n=n is attained by exactly the two alternating words for n>=1;

Lambda_p(t)-t -> (1/2)log(pq)             as t -> +infinity,
Lambda_p(t)   -> (1/2)log(1-2pq)          as t -> -infinity.
```

At `p=0,1`, `Lambda_p(t)=0` for every fixed `t`, because `H_n` is bounded.
These endpoint statements must be kept separate from the primitive interior
kernel; silently substituting an endpoint into a stationary formula is not a
proof.

## T2. Bernoulli signed-cat toral cocycle -- retain with specialist gate

### Definition and early exact signals

Let

```text
A_+ = [[2, 1], [ 1,1]],
A_- = [[2,-1], [-1,1]],
P(X_t=A_+)=p, P(X_t=A_-)=q,
M_n=X_n ... X_1,
Phi_n(x)=M_n x mod Z^2 on T^2.
```

Both generators lie in `SL_2(Z)`, are symmetric positive definite, and are
conjugate by `diag(1,-1)`.  On projective slopes `u in [-1,1]`,

```text
A_+ : u -> (1+u)/(2+u)       maps into [0,2/3],
A_- : u -> (u-1)/(2-u)       maps into [-2/3,0].
```

This strict multicone supplies a direct route to wordwise hyperbolicity.
There is also a mandatory owner warning already at the algebraic level:

```text
A_s = B_s^2,       B_s=[[1,s],[s,0]],       s in {+1,-1}.
```

Thus the cocycle is a correlated block-square relative of signed Fibonacci
matrix products.  Its ordinary Lyapunov theory receives no originality
credit; only the exact toral periodic-count/tensor/extremal package could be
residual.

For every nonempty word tested, and in the proposed infinite theorem,

```text
det M_n=1,   tr M_n>2,
#Fix(Phi_n)=|det(M_n-I)|=tr M_n-2.
```

### Exact annealed finite-time formulas

Put `r=2p-1` and

```text
Mbar_p = E X_1 = [[2,r],[r,1]],
lambda_+/- = [3 +/- sqrt(1+4r^2)]/2.
```

Independence gives an exact formula at every positive time `n>=1`:

```text
E #Fix(Phi_n)
  = tr(Mbar_p^n)-2
  = lambda_+^n + lambda_-^n - 2.
```

In particular, at `p=1/2` and `n>=1`,

```text
E #Fix(Phi_n)=2^n-1.
```

There is an exact hierarchy, not just a first-moment coincidence.  For each
positive integer `k`, define

```text
K_(p,k)=p A_+^(tensor k)+q A_-^(tensor k).
```

Then, for `n>=0`,

```text
E[(tr M_n)^k]=tr(K_(p,k)^n).
```

For `n>=1`, binomial expansion therefore gives every positive integer moment
of `#Fix(Phi_n)=tr M_n-2`.  The exact probe independently checks `k=1,2`.

### Sharp extremal contract and growth question

Write `F_n` and `L_n` for Fibonacci and Lucas numbers with
`F_0=0,F_1=1,L_0=2,L_1=1`.  Exhaustion through length 16 supports the sharp
word theorem

```text
for n>=1:  max_w tr M(w) = L_(2n),
  attained only by the two constant words;

min_w tr M(w) = L_n       if n is even,
                3 F_n     if n is odd,
  attained by 2 words for even n and 2n words for odd n.
```

This is a proposed theorem obligation, not inferred from enumeration.  It
would imply uniform wordwise exponential growth between `phi^n` and
`phi^(2n)`.

Let

```text
gamma_p = lim n^(-1) log tr M_n
```

for the Bernoulli environment.  The intended residual asymptotic statement
is existence, `p <-> 1-p` symmetry, the deterministic endpoint value
`2 log phi`, and a strict quenched--annealed inequality

```text
log phi <= gamma_p < log lambda_+(p),       0<p<1.
```

The upper strictness needs a real proof; a numerical estimate is not an
acceptable substitute.  The fair annealed exponent is `log 2`, while rare
constant words carry the larger word exponent `2 log phi`, so the exact
tensor hierarchy supplies a natural rare-event control route.

### Two materially different proof/control routes

1. **Geometric/lattice route.**  Use the invariant projective multicone to

2. **Annealed tensor route.**  Independence gives

## T4. Affine cyclic-register fixed-point resonance -- reserve internal

On `V_m=F_2^m`, let `R` cyclically rotate the coordinates and choose iid

```text
f_0(x)=Rx,             probability 1-p,
f_1(x)=Rx+e_0,         probability p.
```

For a chronological bit word `(epsilon_1,...,epsilon_n)`,

```text
Phi_n(x)=R^n x+B_n,
B_n=sum_(j=1)^n epsilon_j R^(n-j)e_0.
```

For `n>=1`, put `d=gcd(m,n)` and `k=n/d`.  The equation
`(I+R^n)x=B_n` has either zero or `2^d` solutions.  Its `d` cycle-parity
constraints use disjoint groups of `k` Bernoulli bits, hence the full biased
fixed-count law is

```text
P(#Fix Phi_n=2^d)
  = [(1+(1-2p)^k)/2]^d,

P(#Fix Phi_n=0)=1-the above,

E #Fix Phi_n=[1+(1-2p)^k]^d.
```

At `p=1/2`, `E #Fix=1` for every `m>=1,n>=1`.  On the resonant diagonal `n=km`,
the expectation is exactly `[1+(1-2p)^k]^m`; at `p=1`, fixed points exist
iff `k` is even.  These endpoint and gcd effects are real exact signals.

`code/stochastic_affine_register.py` compares literal full maps against the
affine normal form for `m<=7,n<=10` and checks the biased two-point law for
`m<=10,n<=13`, including orientation and deterministic endpoints.  Result:
**663,325 exact assertions; PASS**.

The system is nevertheless a cyclic affine register whose randomness enters
through independent parity groups.  That is too close to the P92 register
reserve, P104 occupation parity, P98 torsion windows, and the old affine-
semigroup firewall.  Decision: `RESERVE_INTERNAL_HIGH_RISK`, not a current
recommendation.
