## Evidence provenance

Each paper retains four evidence layers:

1. `main.tex` contains the infinite-family proof with explicit boundary and
   parameter quantifiers.
2. `CLAIMS_EVIDENCE.md` and supporting control/narrative records map claims to
   analytic arguments and finite checks.
3. A deterministic exact verifier plus canonical stdout supplies
   convention-sensitive falsification evidence.
4. `HOSTILE_REVIEW_A.md`, `HOSTILE_REVIEW_B.md`, consolidated
   `HOSTILE_REVIEW.md`, `FINAL_QA.md`, build records, and `SHA256SUMS` retain
   reconstruction, repair, production, and integrity evidence.

Finite controls do not prove field-uniform, all-size, or asymptotic claims;
those rest on the manuscript arguments.  No GPU, sampling, fitted statistic,
or stochastic numerical experiment is part of this batch.

## Selection and falsification provenance

Four scouting lanes specified 48 candidate dynamics and executed 4,456,612
historical scouting assertions.  Those checks are kept separate from the
15,630,166 canonical paper assertions.  The retained negative evidence
includes:

- score-upset reversal is not idempotent; the first specified scan failure is
  the six-vertex mask 148;

  reserved because it would duplicate the tournament slot;

  polynomial maps were killed by direct owners or theorem thinness; and
- P116 is not reset-free: the hostile audit found four shortest resets and
  converted the mistake into an exact proposition and verifier lane.
