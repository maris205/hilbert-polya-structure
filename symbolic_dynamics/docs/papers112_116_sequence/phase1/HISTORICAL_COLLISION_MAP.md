# Historical collision map for P112--P116

**Provenance note:** this is a Stage-1 exclusion map.  Post-review paper-local
claim and owner ledgers are authoritative for the frozen five.

This intake map is a system-level firewall, not a keyword filter.  A new
candidate is rejected when its phase object, update rule, and main proof
engine jointly reproduce an earlier paper.  Shared vocabulary alone is not a
collision, but a candidate must say exactly what temporal invariant survives
after the nearest owner and internal mechanisms are subtracted.

## Hard exclusions carried into this intake

- finite-group or transformation-monoid power maps, ordinary graph powers,
  rowmotion/promotion/0-Hecke actions, line-graph iteration, and Euclidean
  remainder maps have direct owners or were already killed;
- centralizer/neighborhood polarities and meet/join sorting cannot reuse the
  P106 or lattice-sorting engines;
- a new subspace system must not be a cosmetic dual of P109, and a new finite
  local-ring system must not repackage P100, P102, or P107 valuations;
- another pruning paper must clear the P105 mechanism explicitly;
- cosmetic variants of random reset/reflection/synchronization, hidden
  finite-memory, monomial-matrix, or positive-Heisenberg systems are excluded
  by P79, P89, P93, P101, P104, and P111.  A survivor that also has reset
  words must subtract memory loss completely and separate its literal phase,
  update, observable, and residual theorem package; absence of reset may not
  be assumed from generator ranks;
- derivative--GCD factor removal, Glaisher splitting, permutation squaring,
  cyclic Boolean erosion, Schur-square code closure, and ordinary
  transitive/graph closure remain historical kills unless a genuinely new
  theorem contract defeats the recorded reason.

## Intake rule

A survivor needs all four of the following before a paper number is assigned:

1. a phase space and update not already present above;
2. an exact anomaly visible in a bounded proof spike;
3. a symbolic infinite-family theorem with at least two substantial temporal
   outputs (for example cycles plus fibres, or depths plus a limit law);
4. two materially different proof/control routes.

Every external novelty or priority statement remains `HOLD_EXTERNAL` pending
specialist owner search.
