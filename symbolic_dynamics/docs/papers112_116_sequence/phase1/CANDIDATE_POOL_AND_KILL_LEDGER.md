## Why the remaining candidates were not promoted

The complete, candidate-by-candidate decisions remain in the three specialist
reports and the root report.  The decisive kill classes were:

1. **direct classical owner:** graph powers, transitive closure, KMP border
   descent, blocker duality, Glaisher carry, ordinary derivative, and other
   maps whose temporal transition is already the named construction;
2. **P1--P111 internal collision:** power/closure, valuation erasure,
   deletion-standardization, polarity, random synchronization, hidden
   finite-memory and pruning variants;
3. **same-engine batch collision:** a second tournament map, a second
   tropical three-state cocycle, or a second coefficient-decimation system;
4. **theorem-thin:** projections, coordinatewise contractions, or finite
   nilpotent maps with only a one-line clock and no second temporal output;
5. **failed spike:** attractive guesses about idempotence, maximum depth, or
   transient enumeration were falsified at small size; and
6. **owner uncertainty:** differential-core and signed-cat candidates had
   strong computations but sat too close to established algebraic or random
   Fibonacci mechanisms for this batch.

## Replacement policy

The five paper numbers are frozen.  A selected system is replaced only if a
proof contract fails, a canonical verifier finds a counterexample, or a
direct temporal owner is located.  Page pressure, a difficult lemma, or a
less elegant formula is not by itself a reason to weaken the contract.
