# Problem anchor: five new finite/controlled dynamics

## Shared question

How much exact temporal structure can be recovered when a familiar finite
object is equipped with a genuinely different deterministic or random update?
The round is deliberately breadth-first: it does not optimize one model, but
tests five disjoint dynamical types for symbolic infinite-family laws.

## Success criterion

A paper counts as progress only if it determines more than an endpoint.  It
must combine at least two of: complete recurrent structure, pointwise or
global depth, exact fibres/basins/layers, finite-time distribution, a limit
law, a generating function/zeta, extremal states, or parameter recovery.
Those outputs must be proved for an infinite family and independently checked
at finite scale.

## Frozen systems

1. global score correction on labelled tournaments;
2. principal diagonal-hook iteration on integer partitions;
3. parallel leaf peeling on rooted forests;
4. bounded Cartier decimation on finite-field polynomials; and
5. Bernoulli switching between two neutral max-plus matrices.

The five systems were selected from 48 precise candidates after 4,456,612
scouting assertions.  The useful early anomalies were respectively strict
score energy, a sharp gap clock, closed Cayley fibres, separated semilinear
index/field clocks, and positive random growth created by mixing two
zero-growth generators.
