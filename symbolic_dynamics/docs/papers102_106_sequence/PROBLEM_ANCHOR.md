# Problem anchor

## Objective

Find many genuinely different dynamical systems for which a short, auditable
paper can establish a complete nontrivial temporal invariant.  Prefer an early
formula or anomaly that exposes the whole orbit mechanism; abandon a candidate
when the residual result is owned, internally duplicated, or too thin.

## Batch selection and closure rule

A candidate may occupy a provisional P102--P106 theorem slot only if it has
items 1--4 below.  The slot does not close, and the batch is not final, until
item 5 is also complete:

1. a phase space and update rule not used by P1--P101 or another paper here;
2. a theorem contract stronger than a worked example;
3. two proof/control routes with materially different failure modes;
4. an explicit owner subtraction and bounded-search limitation;
5. two retained hostile-review passes, a final-QA compiled PDF, and exact
   artifact hashes.

At the final internal Stage-2 checkpoint, all five slots satisfy the
theorem/control selection criteria, have two retained hostile-review ledgers,
and pass batch-level review acceptance, final QA, and exact hash gates.
External owner review, release, submission, contact, and priority gates remain
closed.

## Frozen outcome

The accepted systems are group-algebra involutive norms, double adjugation,
random monomial-toggle products, permutation cycle-minimum pruning, and the
synchronous MIS Boolean network.  Annihilator-power ideals, derivative--GCD
multiplicity dynamics, Durfee-hook pruning, telegraph PDMPs, and several graph
quotients remain reserves or kills; none is silently folded into the five
papers.
