The purpose of each spike was to demand a visible formula or anomaly before
spending a paper slot.  Counts below are author-stage checkpoints; hostile
review may legitimately change the final assertion ledger.

The five spikes registered **24,679,662 exact assertions** at author freeze:
116,278 for P102, 140,340 for P103, 741,486 for P104, 17,219,241 for P105,
and 6,462,317 for P106.  These are convention-sensitive falsification lanes,
not statistical evidence and not substitutes for proof.

All owner searches were bounded.  A spike could justify internal drafting,
but it could not grant novelty, priority, submission, or external-release
clearance.
