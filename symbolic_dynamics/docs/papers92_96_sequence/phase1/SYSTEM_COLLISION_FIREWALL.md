## Hard exclusions for this round

- no prime-loop, determinant, incidence-compiler, Schatten, or arithmetic
  retraction variant;
- no direct parameter lift of a tree shift, cellular automaton, renewal/Dyck
  shift, beta/IET coding, finite relation-graph SFT, or adjacent-window hidden
  process already used above;
- no generic theorem whose entire engine is an owned SFT trace formula,
  substitution incidence matrix, graph split, or standard finite-map cycle
  census;
- no candidate whose only evidence is a short integer sequence or numerical
  entropy estimate.

## Conditional openings

The following broad areas are not automatically excluded, but must carry a
new object and an owner-subtracted theorem:

- marked S-adic/fusion systems, if the result goes beyond invoking the general
  finite-rank measure-cone theorem;
- higher-form algebraic shifts, if the theorem is genuinely higher-form and
  not a relabeling of P67's plaquette rank calculation;
- symbolic natural extensions of nonlinear recurrences, if a structural
  conjugacy controls the full recurrent and transient dynamics;
- tiling, adic, configuration-space, or symbolic-flow systems with an exact
  invariant not already present in P1--P91.

## Batch diversity test

For every selected pair, record four differences: primary phase space, acting
map/group, headline invariant, and proof engine. Sharing one generic word such
as “entropy,” “periodic points,” or “spectrum” is not a collision; sharing the
same object and proof after a change of parameters is.
