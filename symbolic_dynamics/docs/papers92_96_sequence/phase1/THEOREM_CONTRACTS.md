# Frozen theorem contracts — P92–P96

Cutoff: 2026-08-28 UTC. These contracts are the maximum claim surface for the
first complete drafts. A proof or control failure narrows a contract; prose
does not enlarge one.

## P92 — primitive recurrence-avoidance shifts

1. Define the order-`r` recurrence-avoidance SFT over `F_q` from a primitive
   companion map and prove it is mixing with the uniform unique MME.
2. Prove the full characteristic polynomial
   `(lambda-(q-1))(lambda^(q^r-1)-(q-1)^(q^(r-1)-1))` by an explicit
   Fourier/Singer transfer, not by numerical interpolation.
3. Derive the rational zeta and all fixed counts.
4. Prove exact agreement with the full `(q-1)`-shift before `q^r-1`, compute
   the first anomaly, and recover `r` from that first anomaly for fixed `q`.
5. Subtract primitive-LFSR/Singer and generic SFT-zeta ownership explicitly.

## P93 — random push–pop stack cocycles

1. Prove the unique normal form `C_u D^J` and the identities
   `J_t=M_t`, `I_t=M_t-S_t`, image cylinder depth `I_t`, and constant fiber
   multiplicity `b^J_t`.
2. Derive both quenched rates and the pathwise synchronization criterion,
   including failure at `p=1/2`.
3. Give the exact finite annealed degree formula and its three regimes at
   `p=1/(b+1)`, including the correct limiting constant or linear coefficient.
4. At `p=1/2`, distinguish typical `exp(Theta(sqrt(t)))` degree from
   annealed exponential growth.
5. Subtract bicyclic-monoid normal forms and classical reflected-walk results;
   retain only their coupling to the symbolic image/fiber observables.

## P94 — marked symmetric S-adic shifts

1. Prove that `10` is an exact supertile marker and obtain existence and
   uniqueness of desubstitution, minimality, and aperiodicity.
2. Establish the affine bijection between invariant measures and compatible
   two-tower probability vectors; letter frequencies alone are not enough.
3. In the bias coordinate, identify the full simplex as `[-R,R]` with
   `R=prod a_n/(a_n+2)`; translate correctly to the `[0]`-frequency interval.
4. Prove unique ergodicity iff `sum 1/a_n` diverges and exactly two ergodic
   endpoints otherwise.
5. Include the exact products for `a_n=n` and `a_n=n^2`, while subtracting
   general S-adic recognizability and measure-transfer ownership.

## P95 — minimal-slack no-repeat shifts

1. Prove the `S_q` Cayley block presentation from the two long position
   cycles, hence mixing, entropy `log 2`, and the uniform unique MME.
2. Prove the entire initial short-period desert with the two `q!` islands at
   periods `q-1` and `q`.
3. Prove the delayed-geometric first return of a fixed color and the rational
   conditional return/correlation generating function.
4. Recover `q` from the least positive period.
5. Do not claim a full closed formula for all later cycle-power colorings.

## P96 — finite-subset circle expansions

1. For every iterate, prove that a fixed finite subset is exactly a disjoint
   union of base periodic orbits.
2. Prove the orbit-selection Euler product and its quotient
   `(1-Q u^2)/((1-Q u)(1+u))`; derive every exact-cardinality coefficient.
3. Sum through cardinality `k` to obtain the odd/even fixed-count formula,
   then derive the explicit alternating-factor Artin–Mazur zeta and Möbius
   temporal orbit census.
4. Prove `h_top(H_(d,k))=k log d` through the uniformly finite-to-one factor
   from the `k`-fold product.
5. Subtract finite-subset-space topology and generic induced-recurrence
   ownership; do not claim priority for the hyperspace itself.

## Common evidence contract

Every package must contain a deterministic exact script, stored output,
claims/evidence table, verified bibliography, four-stage PDF build, two
independent hostile-review rounds with implemented repairs, final QA, and
checksums. Numerical checks guard formulas and endpoints; the manuscript
proofs carry every infinite-family statement.
