# P92--P96 problem anchor

Cutoff: 2026-08-28 UTC. Route: **A / Symbolic Dynamics**. External release:
**HOLD**.

## Immutable bottom line

Produce exactly five theorem-bearing short papers, P92--P96, on five genuinely
different symbolic-dynamical systems. Every paper must add a concrete,
auditable result: a closed formula, a sharp transition, a rigidity or
reconstruction invariant, an exact orbit classification, or another theorem
of comparable strength. A numerical prefix without a proof mechanism is not a
result.

## Selection rule

A candidate advances only after two independent short probes:

1. a discovery probe exposes a theorem-sized pattern; and
2. a hostile probe tests a different consequence or boundary case of the same
   proposed mechanism.

The candidate is stopped if either probe fails, if the result is only a
parameter change of an existing P1--P91 system, or if direct prior ownership
leaves no independent theorem mass. Search absence is never promoted to a
worldwide novelty claim.

## Non-goals and authorization boundary

- Do not deepen one convenient system five times.
- Do not force a weak candidate into a manuscript to preserve a slot.
- Do not treat computation as a substitute for proof.
- Do not claim first discovery, global novelty, venue fit, or priority.
- Do not post, submit, circulate externally, or contact authors/editors.

Internal theorem discovery, exact controls, manuscript writing, compilation,
two bounded hostile-review rounds, checksums, and scoped synchronization to the
configured research mirror are authorized by the standing workflow.

## Success condition

The round is complete only when all five packages contain a frozen theorem
contract, complete proof, deterministic exact control and stored output,
verified references and ownership boundary, compiled PDF, two review/fix
records, final QA, and package checksums; the batch must also have a collision
ledger, kill ledger, canonical PDF manifest, and verified remote Git commit.
