# Standing workflow authorization

The author supplied the standing instruction:

> 后续就5个论文一轮，中间无需确认就行，每个论文要有明确的进展，然后及时git同步就行

Operational interpretation:

- exactly five papers per research round;
- no conversational confirmation between internal stages;
- each paper must land a specific, auditable mathematical advance;
- maximize distinct dynamical-system subclasses rather than five variants of
  one system;
- synchronize meaningful stage closures to the configured research Git
  mirror.

This authorizes internal research, drafting, proof control, review, revision,
local commits, and pushes to the configured mirror. It does not authorize
public release, journal submission, external circulation, author/editor
contact, or worldwide novelty and priority claims. Those actions remain
`HOLD`.
