# Stage 1 report — Papers 92–96

Result: **five diverse early-signal theorem contracts selected**.

The selected systems expose, respectively:

1. two-factor zeta compression and a first Singer-period anomaly in a
   primitive recurrence-avoidance SFT;
2. a pathwise image/fiber normal form with separated quenched and annealed
   thresholds in a random push–pop cocycle;
3. an exact invariant-measure simplex and summability transition in a marked
   nonstationary S-adic shift;
4. a no-repeat realization of a known symmetric-group Cayley presentation,
   with a short-period desert and rational color-return law; and
5. an Euler-quotient fixed-subset census and odd/even zeta law on a finite-
   subset hyperspace of the circle.

The fifth selection replaced an already drafted but thinner equal-window-sum
candidate because the hyperspace system is both stronger and farther from the
other four systems. Higher-form cochains, finite-field multiplicative
Fibonacci maps, and projective triple-independence were explicitly stopped
after hostile owner/internal-collision checks, despite passing their first
algebraic probes.

`phase1/CANDIDATE_POOL_AND_KILL_LEDGER.md`; the non-expanding claim surface is
