# P97–P101 exact proof-spike ledger

The GPU-pilot phase was replaced by deterministic exact theorem probes
because all five selected systems are finite or symbolically reducible.
Every selection passed two logically distinct routes before drafting.

| Paper | Probe A | Probe B | Frozen exact scale |
|---|---|---|---:|
| P97 | full nonempty-power-set functional graphs | independent iterated-sumset growth, AP extremizers, Möbius/zeta reconstruction | 91,509 assertions |
| P98 | polynomial gcd degrees and literal companion ranks | affine-residue reconstruction and full states over prime/nonprime fields | 152,266 assertions |
| P99 | canonical HNF layer enumeration | raw-basis containment and stabilizer/orbit reconstruction | 93,912 assertions |
| P100 | every arithmetic orbit and one-step digit descent | independent digit convolution, inclusion–exclusion, exact rational moments | 46,319,420 assertions |
| P101 | all update words and threshold-rank permutations | independent conditional spacing and geometric-convolution identities | 6,948,361 assertions |

The probes guard formula discovery, signs, conventions, endpoints, and small
instances. Infinite-family statements are carried by manuscript proofs.
Randomized tests, decimal fitting, and sequence interpolation are absent.

## Strong reserves that did not consume a paper slot

- uniform codimension-one projections over finite fields: exact rank
  pure-death chain;
- graph squaring: Bell attractor and diameter-controlled depth;
- projective-line unipotent action over finite local rings: full valuation
  zeta staircase;
- Jordan subspace closure: exact flag basins in the single-block case;
- Bernoulli leaky register: exact image law and geometric-maximum
  synchronization.

They remain reserves because of batch diversity, direct-owner proximity, or
internal proof-engine collisions, not because their first probes failed.
