# Frozen theorem contracts — P97–P101

Cutoff: 2026-08-29 UTC.  These contracts are the maximum claim surface for
the first complete drafts.  A failed proof or exact control narrows a
contract; prose does not enlarge one.

## P97 — sumset-squaring dynamics

1. Prove the iterated-sumset identity and use Cauchy–Davenport to classify
   every recurrent point on the nonempty power set of `F_p`.
2. Derive every fixed count, the complete recurrent cycle decomposition,
   finite Artin–Mazur zeta, and Möbius temporal-orbit census.
3. Prove the exact worst absorption depth on every cardinality layer and
   exhibit arithmetic-progression extremizers; state any Vosper rigidity only
   in the range actually justified by the cited theorem.
4. Recover `p` and `ord_p(2)` from the first fixed-count anomaly.
5. Subtract Cauchy–Davenport, Vosper, and iterated-sumset ownership; do not
   infer priority from the bounded absence of an exact dynamics source.

## P98 — equal adjacent block-sum shifts

1. Prove that the local rule gives a reversible recurrence of dimension
   `2r-1`, identify the companion module, and count the whole phase space.
2. Prove `Fix(sigma^n)=q^deg gcd(f_r,z^n-1)` and derive the complete
   repeated-root formula in characteristic `p`, including all endpoints.
3. Derive the finite zeta and the least-period orbit census by Möbius
   inversion; isolate the characteristic-torsion staircases.
4. Give an explicit recovery statement from the state count and fixed
   sequence, limited to parameters that the proof genuinely distinguishes.
5. Subtract finite algebraic dynamics, companion matrices, repeated-root
   finite-field algebra, and generic finite-system zeta ownership.

## P99 — unipotent shear on fixed-index sublattices

1. Establish unique HNF coordinates and compute the shear action exactly.
2. Classify every cycle on every divisor layer and derive all fixed counts,
   the cycle inventory, and the finite zeta.
3. Specialize to `N=p^r`, prove the valuation staircase for fixed counts and
   the displayed zeta factorization.
4. Prove a precise recovery statement from the maximal period/cycle data.
5. Subtract HNF and subgroup-zeta background; make no worldwide novelty or
   Hecke-correspondence priority claim.

## P100 — least-valuation digit erasure

1. Construct the exact digit-vector conjugacy and prove that hitting time is
   the base-`p` digit sum.
2. Derive the full transient-depth polynomial, maximum depth, symmetry,
   unimodality, exact mean and variance.
3. State and justify the CLT and lattice local limit from iid uniform digits,
   with endpoint and span conditions explicit.
4. Recover `(p,r)` from the depth profile and distinguish this finite
   absorber from the digit-weight orbit closures of P77.
5. Subtract classical digit-sum distribution and local-limit ownership; do
   not add an artificial restart map merely to manufacture periodic points.

## P101 — random floor/cap interval cocycles

1. Prove the interval-clamp/constant normal form for every environment word
   and identify synchronization with threshold-order crossing.
2. Derive the distribution-free survival law, PGF, independent-geometric
   decomposition, mean, variance, and the endpoint failures at `p=0,1`.
3. For uniform thresholds, derive the exact mean image diameter and the
   annealed exponential rate; contrast it precisely with finite-time
   pathwise collapse without abusing Lyapunov terminology.
4. Prove the critical `(t+1)2^{-t}` prefactor at `p=1/2` and the off-critical
   asymptotics.
5. Subtract general monotone random-iteration synchronization and finite
   contraction-semigroup background; retain only the special finite-time
   laws established here.

claims/evidence table, verified bibliography, four-stage PDF build, two
independent hostile-review rounds with implemented repairs, final QA, and
checksums.  The manuscripts carry every infinite-family proof; computation
