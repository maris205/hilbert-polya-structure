## Hard exclusions for this round

- No prime-loop, determinant-compiler, Schatten, arithmetic-retraction, or
  completed-zeta variant.
- No finite relation-graph paper whose entire result is a known graph spectrum
  inserted into the SFT trace formula.
- No adjacent-window hidden process, reset renewal, push–pop information-loss
  cocycle, substitution incidence product, or hyperspace orbit-union paper
  after only a change of parameters.
- No generic finite-map cycle census, standard toral/monomial functional graph,
  or owned cyclic-sieving action without a residual mechanism.
- No candidate supported only by a short integer sequence, a decimal entropy
  estimate, or one implementation of the same identity.

## Conditional openings

These broad areas remain available only with a new object and a new proof
engine:

- finite algebraic shifts when the local relation yields a nonstandard global
  normal form rather than another rank/nullity calculation;
- finite random endomorphism cocycles when the information-loss observable is
  not the reflected-walk mechanism of P93 or the reset regeneration of P89;
- configuration, tiling, adic, graph-rewrite, or symbolic-flow systems with an
  exact invariant not already carried above;
- nonlinear or continuous systems only when an intrinsic symbolic/coding
  mechanism controls the complete stated dynamics;
- cellular systems only when the theorem is not another frozen-state SFT,
  particle transport law, or standard reversible block construction.

## Prior reserves are not automatic selections

The equal-window-sum torsion shift, Bernoulli leaky cyclic register, and
two-matching Margolus update were reserved in the previous round. They must be
re-probed and owner-checked from zero. A reserve advances only if it now beats
new candidates on theorem mass and batch diversity.

## Pairwise diversity test

For every selected pair, record four differences:

1. primary phase space;
2. acting map, semigroup, or group;
3. headline invariant; and
4. proof engine.

Sharing a generic word such as entropy, periodic points, spectrum, or zeta is
not itself a collision. Sharing the same object and proof after a relabeling
is a collision and forces replacement.
