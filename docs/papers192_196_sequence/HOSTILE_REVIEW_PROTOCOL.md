# Hostile review and repair protocol — P192–P196

Each paper receives two process-separated reviews after its author verifier and
Round-0 PDF are frozen.

## Review A

- independently reconstruct the literal map and complete small functional
  graph;
- attack theorem hypotheses, boundary parameters, tail conventions, fibre
  orientation, image iff statements, and mass identities;
- audit every bibliography record and the P1–P191 subtraction boundary;
- classify findings as Critical, Major, or Minor and emit an exact delta record;
- if a repair is required, the same reviewer must accept or reject the repaired
  delta before Round 1 freezes.

## Review B

- start from frozen Round-1 inputs with exact SHA-256 pins;
- use a representation and proof route materially different from the author and
  Review-A implementations;
- rederive the central theorem package and replay at least two byte comparisons
  against its canonical transcript;
- independently inspect source/owner boundaries and PDF/build surfaces;
- emit an accepted no-change or repair delta before Round 2 freezes.

## Terminal rule

No paper reaches terminal PASS unless both review packages have one verifier,
one canonical transcript, exact zero-open-finding census, complete input pins,
proof/source/build records, accepted delta, non-self manifest, and successful
root replay. All internal passes preserve `OWNER_AMBER / HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
