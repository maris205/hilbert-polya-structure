## What changed in execution

- One active workflow records recovery, early rejection, candidate admission,
  R0 → A/accepted delta/R1 → B/accepted delta/R2, terminal evidence and Git.
  Routine PASS transitions continue under the standing five-paper request;
  report-only questions do not launch work.
- Optional template chains and older provider defaults do not create extra
  project requirements. Scientific claims still require proofs, meaningful
  separate theorem axes, source subtraction and actual independent reviews.
- Verification follows changed dependencies. A status-only edit checks links,
  manifests and scientific-input integrity; it does not trigger another full
  numerical audit. Required first-terminal checks are retained. Reuse requires
  the complete input/runtime or build dependency closure and prior evidence.
- Parallel tasks have an input, question, output owner and acceptance rule.
  Root integrates evidence; proof authors cannot serve as their own reviewers.
- Preserve failed candidates, original numbering, frozen history and external
  HOLD. Private synchronization remains scoped to documented mirror paths.

its PNG checks establish artifacts, not actual visual inspection. The accepted
