schemas, fixed narrower provenance and the whole source/receipt/input graph.
Old schema/status/key/event layouts do not enter its successful path.

complete binding is keyed outside itself, not accepted by its internal hash.

read only after direct-settlement reception. Separate source/one-call/
preflight/help/settlement references are never semantic acceptance merely
because their filenames or JSON status say accepted.

must compare its supplied data against original tool/transport evidence,
verify source/binary closure and receipt reasoning, and then issue any actual
bounded reception. Independent review must read all three complete new
sources, all disabled records, the row classification and this entire map.

syntax/AST parsed, unit-tested or run by this preparation.
