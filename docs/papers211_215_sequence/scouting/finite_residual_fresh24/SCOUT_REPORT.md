## Mechanism subtraction and non-transfer guards

1. **Words are not a new recurrence.** Starting from words `U_0=a,V_0=b`,
   the symbolic recursion `U_{t+1}=U_t V_t,V_{t+1}=V_t U_t` gives
   `T^t(A,B)=(U_t(A,B),V_t(A,B))` by induction. This is precisely the
   Thue–Morse evaluation engine. Evaluation can identify different words;
   it is not a full-state conjugacy from the free-word system to the matrix
   system, and it does not transfer every fibre count.

2. **Known group restriction.** On `GL_2(F_q)^2` the map is a literal
   closed restriction of C12. For target `(P,Q)`, setting
   `B=A^{-1}P` leaves `A^{-1}PA=Q`; a nonempty fibre is a centralizer
   coset. On an odd-order class-two group, `AB` and `BA` commute,
   so the second iterate is diagonal and later dynamics is squaring.
   These are the old proved/owned mechanisms, not Fresh24 results.
   Arbitrary matrix groups of dimension `d` give restrictions of
   `M_d(F_q)^2`, not automatically of the literal size-two C12 carrier.
   In particular, the old gate's family-level Heisenberg comparison must
   not be reused as an unproved size-two embedding claim.

3. **Trace is only a factor/invariant coordinate system.** Identical trace
   coordinates do not identify a unique matrix pair. The WIAS
   determinant-one formula cannot be called the all-`M_2` dynamics
   without accounting for determinants, singular strata and matrix-level
   fibres. No such theorem is provided here.

4. **Nilpotent filtration is background.** If `J` is an associative
   nilpotent algebra with `J^h=0`, products of length `2^t` vanish when
   `2^t>=h`. This supplies only a generic bound for the same old word
   update, not pointwise sharp clocks, the equality classification, or an
   independent all-target inverse theorem. This conditional subtraction
   instantiates no new finite carrier. The old C13/C18 sandwich lanes and
   NL11 shallow matrix-word lane already reject corresponding thin
   filtration/bilinear packages.

5. **Output recovery is not conjugacy.** The old C19 rule
   `(A,B)->(AB-BA,AB+BA)` has invertibly recoverable product data in odd
   characteristic. This is an invertible transform of the outputs of C12;
   it does not itself prove conjugacy of the iterated self-maps.
   In characteristic two the two output expressions coincide.
   C19 is old and no Fresh24 variant is being proposed.

6. **The finite free-word truncation is also old.** The complete TWC
   near-miss section (lines 64–108) was read in the original
   [replacement nonlinear-algebra scout](../../../papers162_166_sequence/scouting/replacement_nonlinear_algebra/SCOUT.md).
   Its precise capped carrier and update, overflow clock, parsing fibres
   and constant-word extremum were already written and killed.
   Fresh24 does not revive its numerical tables or transfer its fibres
   to matrix evaluation. The Fici et al. ownership statement is identified
   as that old record's assertion; its primary paper was not separately
   obtained during this desk and is not claimed as a new primary read.
