## Nearest primary bodies

1. **Colin Defant, Toric Promotion**, arXiv:2112.06843, inspected v3
   (18 September 2022). Definition and Theorem 1.3, printed pp.1–2;
   additional extracted body through p.7. Fixed sweep toggles swap labels
   only at nonadjacent vertices. For a forest with $n\ge2$ the exact period is
   $(n-1)t/\gcd(t,n)$, with $t$ the component size containing label 1.
   The full proof dependencies and remaining pages were not audited.
   [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.06843).

2. **Colin Defant, Rachana Madhukara and Hugh Thomas, Permutoric Promotion:
   Gliding Globs, Sliding Stones, and Colliding Coins**, arXiv:2305.19961v1
   (31 May 2023). Actual HTML definitions, Theorems 1.3–1.4 and Section 6.1
   were read. On a path, inverse-permutation cyclic-descent count $d$ gives
   operator order $d(n-d)$ and a full cyclic-sieving polynomial.
   Proposition 6.4 sends $\Phi_{n,d}$-orbits to rotation-orbits of positive
   $d$-part compositions of $n$, with size scaling $d/n$ and exactly
   $d!(n-d)!$ orbit preimages. This is already an enumerative mechanism.
   Its long coin-model proof was not fully audited. The PDF open failed;
   actual HTML succeeded. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19961v1).

3. **Kerry Seekamp, Orbits of toric promotion on bridge sums**,
   arXiv:2512.00692v1 (30 November 2025). Definition 1 and the statements
   of Theorems 2, 5–8 / Proposition 3, printed pp.1–3, were read.
   This is a microstep map on labeling plus phase. For $n\ge2$, tree and
   complete-graph microperiods are $n(n-1)$ and $n$. The attachment and two-piece bridge
   results are source-owned at their specific graph hypotheses; their
   complete proofs and arbitrary iterated bridges were not audited.
   [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.00692).

4. **Jamie Kimble, Bruce E. Sagan and Avery St. Dizier, K-promotion on
   m-packed labelings of posets**, DMTCS 28:2 #16 (2026), published
   11 March 2026. Definition / inverse Proposition 1.1 and selected
   pp.10–13 bodies were read. Theorem 2.9 requires a minimum, equal-length
   maximal chains and $m=h(P)+2$. Its adapter
   $\pi(L)=\{x:L(x)=\operatorname{rk}(x)+1\}$ conjugates K-promotion to
   inverse rowmotion on $J'(P)$, which excludes **all rank-prefix ideals**,
   not merely the empty/full ideals. Theorem 3.1 gives star operator order;
   Corollary 3.2 gives equal-branch orbit lengths and enumeration.
   No unequal-branch census or all-poset classification is asserted.
   The cited antecedent papers were not independently opened.
   [Published source](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/17699),
   [Primary PDF](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/17699/pdf).

5. **Bender–Knuth Billiards in Coxeter Groups**, arXiv:2401.17360v3
   (21 December 2024), published in Forum of Mathematics, Sigma (2025).
   The actual first two pages define noninvertible toggles on all poset
   bijections and the extended-promotion sweep. Equation (2) already gives
   $\operatorname{Pro}^n(S_{n+1})=\mathcal L(P)$.
   Later theorem-search excerpts were read, but a subsequent targeted
   p.12 open failed and a later title-page retry timed out; no complete
   general-Coxeter proof audit or exact universal recurrent-period claim
   is made here.
   [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.17360v3).

6. **Hugh Thomas and Nathan Williams, Sweeping up Zeta**, arXiv:1512.01483,
   inspected PDF bearing v5 (2018). Selected modular-presweep/inverse
   definitions and Section 6.1, printed pp.21–24, were read.
   Fixed-content words use prefix-sum levels, negative levels first and
   descending nonnegative levels next, breaking ties right-to-left.
   Theorem 6.3 proves a bijection; its proof supplies inversion through a
   sufficiently large content-dependent modulus and the modular inverse.
   Theorem 6.7 also states Dyck-word invariance/bijectivity. None of these
   statements is an all-parameter orbit census.
   The full equitable-partition proof was not audited.
   [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.01483).
