   Used as inventory context, not as an exact WFS owner or borrowed proof.

   title-page date October 22).
   [NIST-hosted primary PDF](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=928827).
   Read introduction and Section 2's additive-character orthogonality

   its group-algebra decoder. No main algorithm theorem is transferred.
3. Lars Hesselholt and Ib Madsen, *On the K-theory of local fields*,
   Annals of Mathematics 158(1) (2003), 1–113.
   [Publisher metadata](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2003/158-1/p01),
   [publisher PDF](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v158-n1-p01.pdf).

   This is a direct owner of FLD's ghost identity. None of the K-theory

   Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico della Università di Padova 153

5. Richard J. Nowakowski and Paul Ottaway, *Vertex Deletion games with
   Parity rules*, [author-hosted PDF](https://www.mathstat.dal.ca/~ottaway/VDel.pdf).
