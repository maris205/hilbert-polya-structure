## Primary source actually read

Brett Kolesnik, Rivka Mitchell and Tomasz Przybyłowski,
[*Tournaments on signed graphs*, arXiv:2312.04532v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04532v2),
version dated 6 June 2024. The directly opened
[author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.04532v2), §2.1 and the reversal
definition in §3.2, treats complete-graph orientations and reversal of a
single directed cyclic triangle as a score-preserving interchange.
That is not TTRS: here the qualifying triples are transitive, all their
union arcs reverse once, and the score need not be preserved. No theorem
from this source is a premise of PROOF_PACKAGE.md.

This records the actual version/title read; an old local reference to a
later “Coxeter Interchange Graphs” publication is not silently substituted.
No PDF was downloaded or visually reviewed. A separate
[Thomassen DOI request](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(88)90031-3)
returned an Internal Error, not an article body; no result from that
unread body is asserted. PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECORDS.json preserves the actual
three browser request objects, read scope and failure. Search results
served discovery only, not proof that an exact owner does not exist.
