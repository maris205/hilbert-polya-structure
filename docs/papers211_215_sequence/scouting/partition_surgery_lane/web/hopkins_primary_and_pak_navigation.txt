L7@P0: Abstract
L8@P0: Conjugation and the Bulgarian solitaire move are considered as extreme cases of
L9@P0: several column-to-row operations on integer partitions. Each operation generates
L10@P0: a state diagram on the partitions of n, which leads to the questions: How many
L11@P0: Garden of Eden states are there? How many cycle states? How many connected
L12@P0: components? All of these questions are answered for partitions of n when at least n−1
L13@P0: 2 columns are switched to rows.
