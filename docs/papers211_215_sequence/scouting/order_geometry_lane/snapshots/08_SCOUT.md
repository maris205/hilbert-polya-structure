## Strongest raw signal: `L05/NFS`

This section records why `L05` initially crossed the mathematical threshold
and why it still does not cross the portfolio threshold.

### Literal map and algebraic form

For `n>=1`, put `X_n={0,1}^n` and work over `F_2`.  Define

```text
T_n(x_0,...,x_(n-1))=(x_1,...,x_(n-1), f_n(x)),
f_n(x)=sum_(i=0)^(n-2) (1+x_i)x_(i+1).
```

Thus `f_n` is the parity of the internal `01` transitions.  Its algebraic
normal form is

```text
x_1+...+x_(n-1)+x_0x_1+...+x_(n-2)x_(n-1).
```

### Proved axis A: recurrent language and complete cycle spectrum

Let `(x_t)_(t in Z)` be the bi-infinite bit sequence associated with a
periodic orbit and set

```text
q_t=(1+x_t)x_(t+1).
```

The recurrence and its one-step difference give

```text
x_(t+n)=sum_(j=t)^(t+n-2)q_j,
x_(t+n+1)=q_t+x_(t+n-1)x_(t+n),
q_(t+n)=(1+x_(t+n))q_t.                         (1)
```

Along a periodic orbit, the last identity cannot permanently change a `1`
to a `0`, so `q_(t+n)=q_t`; moreover `q_t=1` forces `x_(t+n)=0`.  If
`Q=sum_(j=t)^(t+n-1)q_j`, then

```text
x_(t+n)=Q+q_(t-1).
```

If some `q_t=1`, then `q_(t-1)=0` and the preceding forced zero gives `Q=0`;
if every `q_t=0`, the same conclusion is immediate.  Hence
`x_(t+n)=q_(t-1)`, and the `n`-periodicity of `q` gives
`x_(t+2n)=x_(t+n)`.  Bi-infinite periodicity then gives
`x_(t+n)=x_t`.  Substitution shows

```text
q_(t-1)=x_t,
```

so no two cyclically adjacent bits are both one, and `Q=0` says the Hamming
weight is even.  Conversely every even-weight cyclic independent word has
`f_n(x)=x_0`, so `T_n` is left rotation on it.

Therefore the recurrent set is exactly

```text
R_n={x: x_i x_(i+1)=0 cyclically and |x| is even},
```

and every temporal period divides `n`.  For `d|n`, let `I_d` be the number
of cyclic independent binary words of length `d`, and `E_d` its even-weight
subcount.  With the one-letter loop convention,

```text
I_1=E_1=1,
I_d=F_(d-1)+F_(d+1)                         (d>=2),
E_d=(I_d+2 cos(pi d/3))/2                   (d>=2).
```

The number of recurrent states fixed by `T_n^k`, with `d=gcd(n,k)`, is

```text
I_d,  if n/d is even,
E_d,  if n/d is odd.
```

Ordinary divisor Möbius inversion yields the exact number of cycles of every
length.  The verifier independently checks the recurrent classification,
rotation action, every iterate-fixed count, and the Möbius cycle census for
all states through `n=14`.

### Proved axis B: every-target one-step fibres

For a target `y=(y_0,...,y_(n-1))`, a source must have the form

```text
(a,y_0,...,y_(n-2)),  a in {0,1}.
```

Put

```text
c_-(y)=sum_(i=0)^(n-3)(1+y_i)y_(i+1).
```

The final-coordinate equation is

```text
y_(n-1)=(1+a)y_0+c_-(y).                     (2)
```

Thus, for `n>=2`, every target has exactly

```text
1 predecessor, if y_0=1;
2 predecessors, if y_0=0 and y_(n-1)=c_-(y);
0 predecessors, otherwise.
```

There are respectively `2^(n-1)`, `2^(n-2)`, and `2^(n-2)` such targets, so
`|Im T_n|=3*2^(n-2)`.  At `n=1`, zero has two predecessors and one has none.

### Exact-only strengthening signals, not frozen theorems

Complete enumeration through `n=14` finds

```text
max tail(T_1)=1,
max tail(T_2)=3,
max tail(T_n)=2n                         for 3<=n<=14,

|Im T_n^t|=F_(t+3) 2^(n-t-1)            for 0<=t<=n-1,
Im T_n^(2n)=R_n, and Im T_n^(2n-1)!=R_n  for 3<=n<=14.
```

These are high-quality proof targets, but this lane does not promote them:
even a proof would sit on top of the P90/P117 collision described next.

that its recurrent action is rotation, derives all iterate-fixed polynomials
by the gcd, and performs Möbius inversion to obtain the orbit ledger and
