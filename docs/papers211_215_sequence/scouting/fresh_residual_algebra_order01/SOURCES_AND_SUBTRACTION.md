2. Victoria Lebed, *Laver Tables: from Set Theory to Braid Theory*,
   [author-hosted 11-page note](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/~lebed/Lebed_ATS14.pdf).
   Actual text of Sections 1 and 2 through equation (4) and its nearby
   explanation was read. Equation (4) is exactly the pair map used here.
   The note distinguishes shelves from racks and treats Laver tables as
   nonbijective color propagation. This is direct literal ownership, not
   a claim that the note states our finite boundary-census corollary.
   The filename suggests the 2014 symposium; no exact publication date or
   journal status is inferred solely from it. Later cocycle material was
   returned in a large browser result but is not used as a reviewed proof.

3. Patrick Dehornoy and Victoria Lebed, *Two- and three-cocycles for Laver
   tables*, [arXiv:1401.2335v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.2335v2), PDF version
   dated 3 February 2014. Read the actual 26-page PDF's Section 1 through
   Proposition 1.3 and the following explanation; this independently
   identifies the operation, periods and projection hypotheses. Table 1

   proof, not a new scientific run. Lemma 4.4 and nearby column discussion

   fibre classification. No full cocycle proof or diagram was reviewed.
