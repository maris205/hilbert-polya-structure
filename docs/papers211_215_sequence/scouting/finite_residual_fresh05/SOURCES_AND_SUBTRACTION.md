## Two-axis assessment

The inverse axis is exact for every finite simple graph and colour count:
admissible old blinking sets dominate the target's post-blinking induced
graph and avoid zero-target neighbours. Extension gives the sharp maximum
$d(G)$; all nonisolated coordinates must equal $b+1$ at equality.
On complete graphs this becomes $2^{|V|}-1$, with every equality case.

That is one useful self-contained feasibility result on an already
published literal. It is not enough for admission. On the controlled tree
regime, the available time/recurrence structure is inherited; on arbitrary
graphs, no replacement all-source exact entrance or full recurrent atlas
has been proved here. Repackaging a known temporal theorem with this inverse
support does not meet the requested residual two-axis gate. No experiments
are proposed to enlarge a weak entrance into a seat.
