## Source-boundary deductions (not new candidate theorems)

Status: **PROVABLE AS STATED**, solely for the three nonidentities below.
Assumptions: cyclic site indices, nonnegative integer entries, and the
source/historical rules exactly as stated. Strategy: explicit witnesses.
Dependency map: rule definitions -> one substitution -> differing outputs,
or different fixed-point counts. No empirical values from old pilots are used.

1. Let the historical conserving rule on three sites be
   $F(x)_i=\lfloor x_i/2\rfloor+\lceil x_{i-1}/2\rceil$.
   The Riverbend optional half-sharing rule instead keeps the extra odd
   candy: $G(x)_i=\lceil x_i/2\rceil+\lfloor x_{i-1}/2\rfloor$.
   For $x=(1,0,0)$, $F(x)=(0,1,0)$ and $G(x)=x$.
   More strongly, on the common total-one carrier, $F$ is a three-cycle
   while $G$ fixes all three states. Their fixed-point counts differ, so no
   autonomous conjugacy on this carrier exists. Reversing the chosen spatial
   orientation cannot change these fixed-point counts. This comparison
   concerns two already-sourced rules, not two fresh attempted literals.

2. Bal–DeGaetani's abstract starts with even amounts, halves them between
   each site and its neighbour, then adds a candy at every odd site.
   From $(2,0,0)$ the halving step gives a rotation of $(1,1,0)$ and the
   top-up gives a rotation of $(2,2,0)$, whose total is four.
   In contrast, $\sum_iF(x)_i=\sum_i(\lfloor x_i/2\rfloor+
   \lceil x_i/2\rceil)=\sum_ix_i$, so $F$ preserves total two.
   The top-up rule is not the same fixed-total endomorphism. This proves
   nonidentity and incompatibility of that carrier, not a universal
   nonconjugacy claim after unspecified enlargement.

3. Chamberland's integer rule is
   $D(x)_i=|x_i-x_{i+1}|$; the Lewis–Tefft abstract uses
   $A(x)_i=x_i+x_{i+1}\pmod m$.
   On the three-site all-one tuple at $m=3$, $D(x)=(0,0,0)$ whereas
   $A(x)=(2,2,2)$. They are not the same literal rule under the natural
   residue identification. No assertion about every possible conjugacy is
   needed or made. The identical word “Ducci” is insufficient to transfer
   either source's temporal theorem.

No all-parameter recurrence/fibre result for a new map has been proved.
These elementary checks prevent false source transfer; they are not
paper-sized novelty and do not contribute to the closed-literal count.
