## 1. Cyclic nonabelian difference is exact old ND

On $G^L$, the tempting rule $D(x)_i=x_i^{-1}x_{i+1}$ is exactly ND in
`docs/papers204_208_sequence/scouting/algebra_third/PROOF_AND_ADAPTER_NOTES.md`,
whose complete original was read. No smaller solvable group or different
coordinate notation makes it a new literal.

For completeness, let $y=D(x)$. Multiplication in cyclic order cancels
successive $x_i x_i^{-1}$ and gives $y_0\cdots y_{L-1}=1$. Conversely,
given this product condition and any $a\in G$, set
$x_0=a$ and $x_i=a y_0\cdots y_{i-1}$ for $1\le i<L$.
All adjacent equations then hold, including the last one. Every source
has exactly this form because its first coordinate forces all subsequent
coordinates. Thus each nonempty fibre has $|G|$ elements.

This is not a new inverse axis: writing $\sigma$ for cyclic shift gives
$D(x)=x^{-1}\sigma(x)$. Equality $D(x)=D(z)$ is equivalent to
$\sigma(zx^{-1})=zx^{-1}$, so fibres are left cosets of the fixed diagonal
copy of $G$. In abelian groups the rule is the linear difference
$\sigma-I$; at $L=2$ its first image $(h,h^{-1})$ evolves by $h\mapsto h^{-2}$.
These exact linear/power/fixed-coset deductions consume the obvious routes.
The old full-group temporal gap is not repaired here; no class-two or
nilpotent-filtered restriction was substituted.

## 2. Braid/product-exchange words are old literal maps

The rule $H(x,y)=(y,y^{-1}xy)$ is old HUR. Its inverse is
$H^{-1}(u,v)=(uvu^{-1},u)$: substitution into both compositions returns
the original pair. Hence all finite states are recurrent and every fibre
at every time has size one. Put $p=xy$, an invariant product. Directly,
$$H^2(x,y)=(p^{-1}xp,p^{-1}yp),$$
so $H^{2r}$ is simultaneous conjugation by $p^{-r}$ and the period divides
$2\operatorname{ord}(p)$. This is a generic group-action clock with a trivial
inverse, not a separate two-axis advance. It is also the inverse of the
standard Hurwitz generator displayed on arbitrary group tuples by the
primary source identified in SOURCES_AND_LIMITS.md.

The alternative $P(x,y)=(xy,yx)$ is already old PXE/TP, not a replacement
candidate. Its first fibre can be written without counting experiments:
for target $(u,v)$, $y=x^{-1}u$ and $v=x^{-1}ux$. It is empty unless
$u$ and $v$ are conjugate. If $x_0^{-1}ux_0=v$, all choices of $x$ are
$C_G(u)x_0$, so its size is $|C_G(u)|$. This is the ordinary conjugator
coset mechanism. The corresponding full-transformation-semigroup rule
was also literally piloted as TP in the old second algebra lane.
No claim is made that group and transformation-semigroup carriers have
identical functional graphs, or that the all-semigroup temporal problem
has been solved.

## 3. Polynomial differential feedback is old TD/RC/CPA

The full truncated-ring choices $f\mapsto f f'$ and $f\mapsto f'+f^2$
on $\mathbb F_p[X]/(X^p)$ are exactly old TD and RC. The ideal is stable
under differentiation because $(X^p)'=0$.

For RC, write the source as $a=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}a_jX^j$ and target as
$b=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}b_jX^j$. After choosing $a_0$, every solution must obey
$$a_{j+1}=(j+1)^{-1}\left(b_j-\sum_{k=0}^{j}a_k a_{j-k}\right),
\qquad 0\le j\le p-2,$$
and the remaining top equation is
$b_{p-1}=\sum_{k=0}^{p-1}a_k a_{p-1-k}$.
Conversely these equations verify every coefficient, so they characterize
all sources. The decoder has at most $p$ trials, but this is ordinary
triangular coefficient recursion, not an evaluated new full fibre atlas.
The old temporal gap remains; no larger prime was run.

Changing the modulus to $X^p-X$ and differentiating the canonical degree
less than $p$ representative produces old CPA, not a fresh candidate.
Bare differentiation of a residue class under that modulus is invalid:
the two representatives $0$ and $X^p-X$ have derivatives $0$ and $-1$.
This is an algebraic representative obstruction, not a new scientific
counterexample run. Initial FLD and other adic variants remain excluded.
