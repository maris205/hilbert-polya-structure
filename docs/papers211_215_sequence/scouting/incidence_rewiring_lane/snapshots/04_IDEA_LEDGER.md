## Guardrail conclusion

The survivor count is intentionally one, not three.  C01, C05, C09, C19,
and C26 all had individually attractive exact patterns, but each failed the
independence or owner gate.  C06 is retained only because its temporal theorem
uses a queue/max-plus factor while its every-target fibre theorem uses the full
interlacing of labelled block extrema through a fixed five-state transfer
matrix.  If that matrix-trace theorem is judged merely an algorithmic inverse
rather than a structural formula, C06 must be demoted and this lane becomes
**KILL ALL**; there is no fallback candidate.
