**Generic local trace.** The author's deduction is valid on a nonempty
finite alphabet and n>=3. In the expansion of tr(M_0...M_(n-1)), a surviving
labelled closed chain p_i=(u_i,v_i) has v_i=u_(i+1), including closure.
Setting x_i=v_i therefore forces p_i=(x_(i-1),x_i); conversely every labelled
word gives exactly that chain. The remaining factors are precisely the
n target equations, so the count is exact with no rotation quotient or
extra factor n. This is manual reasoning, not executed enumeration.
Substitution phi(a,b,c)=mex{a,c} with the stated palette does not use a
mex-specific inverse feature. Accepting this generic identity as a deduction
does not award a new candidate-specific inverse theorem.

**Inventory.** The old original formula T(A)_ij=[r_i=c_j] gives
r'_i=b_(r_i) and c'_j=a_(c_j). Thus the next margin histograms obey
a'_k=sum_{v:b_v=k}a_v and b'_k=sum_{v:a_v=k}b_v. This confirms a
two-population histogram factor. It does not reconstruct labelled matrix
states from their histograms, prove conjugacy or establish periods <=2
for all sizes. The original explicitly limits that observed period ceiling
to checked square orders.

[Cain–Enin's definition](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.00209), lines 82–116,
uses multisets of positive integers and f(S)=[S]+mu(S), where the sum
concatenates multisets. It preserves distinct values as well as counts:
its example maps {1,1,3,8} to {1,1,1,2,3,8}; multiplicities alone are
{1,1,2}. This differs from simply taking a histogram, and no full RCE
conjugacy is established. The source supports related inventory context,
not an imported all-size RCE temporal theorem. The desk's explicit boundary
is narrower than an unqualified “same known rule” claim and is accepted.

**Kaprekar.** The inspected
[primary v3, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20439v3) concerns four
digits, odd B>3 and difference states 1<=d1<B, 0<=d2<=d1.
The stable core requires d1>d2>0 both odd; K_B^3 sends the difference
space into it. The restricted core is conjugate, via half-sum/half-difference,
to doubling unordered pairs of distinct nonzero residues modulo signs.
Those are nonzero residue classes, not only units in composite bases.
This is an explicitly stated existing temporal mechanism. No conjugacy
of the whole labelled digit carrier, constant-zero extension, B=3,
even-base extrapolation or new parameter scan is claimed. I checked the
definition and statement, not all proof cases or the Lean artifact.
ArXiv's reported journal acceptance remains source metadata, not an
independent journal-verification result.

**Word cancellation.** The old C01 range specifies leftmost adjacent-aa
deletion, not arbitrary parallel cancellation. Its endpoint is the reduced
normal form in a free product of C2 copies; its number of serial deletions
is half the length loss, and its endpoint-fibre recurrence is a regular-tree
walk recurrence. [Bell–Liu–Mishna's primary introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.00089v1)
describes identity-word cogrowth and Cayley-walk counting. This supports
the known normal-form/counting mechanism, not a theorem about every parallel
scheduler. No particular new parallel word map was specified, hence no
claim of exact literal identity to such a map or a new sharp parallel clock
is available. Normal-form endpoint fibres are not mislabeled as one-step
preimages of an unspecified deletion schedule.
