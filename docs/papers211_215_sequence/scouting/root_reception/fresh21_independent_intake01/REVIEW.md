## 2. Generic period bound and exact fixed locus

The triangular induction is valid over every specified field F_q.
Suppose the first k-1 coordinates have returned after M=p^(k-2) updates.
Their entire M-step lower-coordinate sequence then repeats independently
of the starting kth coordinate. The kth coordinate receives the same total
increment C in each block. After p blocks it receives pC=0, proving that
the first k coordinates return after p^(k-1) steps.

At k=N this gives T_N^(p^(N-1))=id for N≥1. Each strict period divides an
identity exponent and therefore is a power of p. N=1 is identity; N=0 is
handled separately. This induction gives a coarse general triangular bound,
not its attainability, the exact order, individual cycles or their census.

For a nonzero tuple let m be its least nonzero index. If 2m≤N, the degree
2m coefficient can only receive a_(2m) and a_m^2: any smaller nonzero

Hence no characteristic-two cancellation invalidates the argument.
A field has no nonzero square-zero element, so this coordinate changes.

Conversely, when a_1 through a_floor(N/2) vanish, every product or repeated
selection of two remaining positive degrees exceeds the truncation degree.
Only the single-index linear terms remain, and the tuple is fixed.
Consequently the fixed locus and count q^ceil(N/2) are correct, including
N=0 and N=1. The stated field assumption cannot silently be replaced by an
