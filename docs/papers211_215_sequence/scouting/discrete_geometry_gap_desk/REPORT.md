## 1. Checkerboard surgery with moving boundary: directly owned

Beil–Fischer–Nadeau define triangular fully packed loops with precise degree
and boundary restrictions. Their left-Wieland operation adds the left
boundary data, applies odd-cell gyration, deletes the right boundary, and
translates back. The choice of unchanged left word is already their
autonomous `WL`. Theorem 3 gives stabilization by $2N-1$; Theorem 4 identifies
stability by absence of drifters, and Theorem 2 supplies boundary-aware
inverse identities. These are direct owner claims, not new deductions.
[Primary v1, Definitions 1.3/2.1 and Theorems 2–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.1657v1).

Old PP2 is a **different** plane-partition map: use current volume parity
to select a checkerboard class and replace each selected entry by its
old upper bound plus old lower bound minus itself. The actual original
`pp_toggle` and caller were read. Old NSV is also different: arbitrary
binary faces on a torus output precisely diagonal occupied pairs. Neither
identity is asserted with TFPL gyration. The direct primary owner, not
the shared word “checkerboard,” excludes re-entering standard TFPL drift.
No replacement local/boundary rule with two credible residual axes emerged.

## 2. Plane-tree ornament surgery: exact old OMP

Ajran–Defant's carrier assigns connected, rooted, pairwise nested-or-disjoint
vertex sets to a fixed plane tree. Its order is coordinate inclusion,
meet is coordinate intersection, and Pop takes the meet of the current
element and its lower covers. This is exactly old OMP's carrier and update
under the identity map, not just a Tamari analogy. The source already
studies maximum forward-orbit size and image structure.
[Primary v1, §§1.1–1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.10311v1).

P120's old-fringe-parity child reversal and P148's deletion of odd levels
were checked at their actual definitions and adjacent proofs. Their
invariant-trigger involution and divisible-depth contraction are separate
occupied mechanisms. Merely retaining plane order, reversing a local
child list, or replacing a tree by its ornamentation does not provide a
new temporal axis. No new plane-tree update is instantiated here.

## 3. Triangular-grid billiards: the attractive gap is already closed

Defant–Jiradilok define a permutation of boundary panes by midpoint-to-
midpoint $60^\circ$ billiards, with the table fixed. They also identify it
with a plabic trip permutation and prove area/cycle extremal results.
[Primary v2, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.06943v2).

Their Conjecture 1.4 must **not** be carried forward as an open perimeter
problem: Zhu's Theorem 1.2 proves
$\mathrm{cyc}(P)\le(\mathrm{perim}(P)+2)/4$ and gives the primitive
equality criterion. Zhu also explicitly constructs removal of a selected
billiard cycle using a tree of horizontal strips (Theorem 2.5). Thus the
obvious cycle-removal geometric surgery is not an unowned primitive.
[Primary v3, §§1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.00100v3).

A fixed table plus a moving boundary index just iterates the existing
permutation, so its one-step fibres are singleton. Zhu's cycle-removal
construction is not silently converted into a new canonical autonomous
map: his existence statement and construction require geometric data;
choosing a schedule would also need new value after cycle-extraction
subtraction. No new bounded table-changing rule, all-parameter orbit law,
or independent target reconstruction contract was obtained.
