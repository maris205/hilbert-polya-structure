## Three no-pilot deduction details

**OSC.** In an inversion graph, an edge from position $i$ to $k>i$ connects
every intermediate position $j$ to one endpoint: either $w_j<w_i$ or
$w_j>w_k$. Therefore connected components are intervals of positions.
No inversions between successive components means their value sets are
successive intervals too; these are precisely direct-sum blocks. Value
complementation within a block toggles every inversion edge inside it,
retaining all vertex labels. Odd blocks therefore obey exactly P123's
odd-component complement, at every epoch. **KILL_EXACT_INTERNAL_FACTOR.**

**RCS.** After row sorting, the entry vector in column $j$ is coordinatewise
less than the vector in column $j+1$. Increasing order statistics preserve
these inequalities: if $k$ entries of the right vector are at most a
threshold, the corresponding $k$ left entries are strictly smaller.
Thus column sorting preserves row order while making columns increasing.
The result is fixed by both sorts. Conversely every row/column-increasing
filling is fixed. The entire dynamics is a one-step retraction, irrespective
of how difficult the static inverse count might be.
**KILL_ONE_STEP_RETRACTION.**

**OIH.** Let $G$ be the old interval-intersection graph, including loops.
All intervals meeting $I_i$ have connected union because they meet $I_i$;
the new hull is exactly that union. Two such unions meet exactly when
some old interval in one neighbourhood meets one in the other, equivalent
to distance at most three in $G$. Hence the new graph is $G^3$. Inductively
at time $t$ the graph is $G^{3^t}$ and interval $i$ is the union of the
original intervals at distance at most $s_t=(3^t-1)/2$: the radius changes
by $s_{t+1}=s_t+3^t$. This is a Boolean-power/distance expansion plus union,
the same old temporal proof mechanism as P171. No inverse theorem or global
sharp clock is sought after this deduction. **KILL_GRAPH_POWER_LIFT.**
