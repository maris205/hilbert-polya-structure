run-end absorption and sharp finite-carrier clock; evaluated one-step

## Direct primary sources actually inspected

[Nishinari–Takahashi, Analytical Properties of Ultradiscrete Burgers Equation
and Rule–184 Cellular Automaton](https://hakotama.jp/laboratory/works/public/98nt.pdf).
Front matter and the equation (16)/(21)/(24) contexts were inspected, not
the entire paper. Its flow caps incoming mass by receiver vacancy L-U,
not current receiver mass U. The displayed ordinary Cole–Hopf mechanism
is not imported. PDF screenshot attempts failed, so no visual-page audit is
claimed; equation text and the independent HTML comparison below support
only this literal distinction. The opened front date is September 11, 2001;
a filename is not publication metadata.

[Fukuda–Segawa–Watanabe, A variant of the discrete Burgers equation derived
from the correlated random walk and its ultradiscretization, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.14009v2).
The actual equations (15)–(24), surrounding bounds and traffic interpretation
were inspected. The conventional reduction still uses L-U; the correlated
model introduces an additional field/history, not the fresh07 carrier.
The authors' concluding scope is also visible. No arbitrary encoding or
nonconjugacy theorem is inferred from unequal local formulas.

[Riverbend Community Math Center, Candy Sharing Game](https://riverbendmath.org/modules/Candy_Sharing_Game/).
The actual activity body has symmetric threshold chip sharing and its
alternative passes a rounded-down half while retaining the odd extra.
This confirms only the old half-sharing entrance; neither update is the
fresh07 min-current rule. No claim to have freshly read all Bal–DeGaetani
arguments is made.

[Boccara–Fukś, Number-conserving cellular automaton rules](https://arxiv.org/pdf/adap-org/9905004).
The finite-alphabet cyclic definitions, Theorem 2.1 and its proof were read:
number conservation has an established telescoping characterization.
Generic conservation and flow machinery therefore carry no new credit.
This source's finite-alphabet full product carrier should not be conflated
with a fixed-total nonnegative-integer sector. The local fresh07 rule can
send (C,C,0) to middle value 2C and is not a selfmap of the entire alphabet
{0,...,C} product when C>0. This is a scope check, not novelty proof.

[Biham–Hertzberg Rabinovich–Katzav, First-passage processes in a deterministic
one-dimensional cellular automaton model of traffic flow, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19023v2).
The actual abstract and Section II model were read. It is binary rule
184: a car advances into an empty receiver. Its random-initial-condition
first/last-stopping questions do not identify fresh07's rule or fixed-mass
inverse atlas. The [publisher record](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/5w9x-spn4)
gives publication on June 23, 2026, so this is a genuinely recent neighbour.

[Redeker, Counting number-conserving cellular automata with radius 1,
v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31157v2).
Actual introduction and Sections 2.1–2.5 were inspected. It counts rules
on a fixed capacity alphabet via flow functions/half-flows, not source
fibres of the candidate map. Its June 1, 2026 revision supplies a recent
source-family check. The generic flow/counting machinery is background.
No Haskell program or returned counts were executed or adopted here.

[Fukś–Jin, Approximating dynamics of a number-conserving cellular automaton
by a finite-dimensional dynamical system](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00479v1).
Introduction, literal truth table and basic inverse definitions were read:
the subject is binary four-input rule 56528 and block probabilities,
not the min-current integer rule. The arXiv posting is January 2026, but
the primary record reports a 2020 journal article and the text's history
also shows 2020. It is not falsely labelled a newly published 2026 theorem.
