## Direct current primary access

Sources were browsed on 2026-09-08. Exact query groups and access outcomes
are recorded in [evidence/SOURCE_ACCESS.json](evidence/SOURCE_ACCESS.json).
Searches including 2025/2026 terms did not establish a latest-literature
clearance; recent indexing dates are not publication dates.

1. C. Duffy, T. F. Lidbetter, M. E. Messinger and R. J. Nowakowski,
   *A Variation on Chip-Firing: the diffusion game*, DMTCS 20:1 (2018), #4.
   The [publisher PDF](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/4214/pdf) was accessed
   directly; definition (1) and the surrounding first two pages were read.
   This is simultaneous one-chip flow to each strictly less loaded
   neighbour, potentially allowing negative loads. UUC instead has one
   clockwise, positivity-guarded, weakly uphill current. This comparison
   does not transfer their periodicity results or identify an exact UUC owner.

2. David Ji, Michael Li and Daniel Wang,
   *Periods and Atomic Firing Sequences of Parallel Chip-Firing Games on
   Directed Graphs*, arXiv:2407.15889v3, 8 April 2025.
   The [author primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.15889) was accessed;
   the definition/preliminaries and directed-cycle Lemma 3.4/Theorem 3.5
   passages were read. They describe degree-threshold firing and the
   directed-cycle period classification, not a recipient-load comparison.
   The HTML's wording in the preliminaries says “exceeds” while its cycle
   argument uses threshold-one firing; no theorem here depends on resolving
   that source-convention inconsistency. UUC's proofs are direct.

3. Rei Inoue, Atsuo Kuniba and Taichiro Takagi,
   *Integrable structure of box-ball systems: crystal, Bethe ansatz,
   ultradiscretization and tropical geometry*, arXiv:1109.5349v4,
   7 February 2012.
   The [author primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.5349) was accessed;
   Section 1.1 on printed pages 2–3 was read. Its basic rule moves each
   ball once to the next available hole; the stated low-density periodic
   version is invertible. UUC is simultaneous local load flow with permanent
   holes, so neither this rule nor its carrier/soliton structure supplies
   UUC's missing theorem axes. No full 73-page read is claimed.
