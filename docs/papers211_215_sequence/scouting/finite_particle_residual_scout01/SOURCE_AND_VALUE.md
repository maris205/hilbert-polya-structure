# Source ownership and value boundary

This is a bounded desk check, not a novelty certificate or a research paper.
The checked published model is background, not a new literal counted by
this lane. No all-parameter residual proof is asserted.

## Direct primary sources

### S1 — original charged lattice-gas entrance

Marko Medenjak, Katja Klobas and Tomaž Prosen, *Diffusion in deterministic
interacting lattice systems*, [author preprint arXiv:1705.04636](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.04636),
v1, 12 May 2017. The model passage, equations (1)–(2), was read directly.
It uses an even periodic chain with vacancy and two charge states and
alternating nearest-neighbour layers. Vacancy/particle pairs exchange;
pairs of particles do not. The text identifies free vacancy propagation.
An APS table-of-contents result supplied the journal pointer Phys. Rev.
Lett. 119, 110603 (2017), but no publisher-body audit is claimed.
Later correlation/transport formulae were not independently verified or
used as a new theorem here.

### S2 — direct confirmation of the charge-order mechanism

Žiga Krajnik, Johannes Schmidt, Vincent Pasquier, Enej Ilievski and
Tomaž Prosen, *Exact anomalous current fluctuations in a deterministic
interacting model*, [author preprint arXiv:2201.05126](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.05126).
The local update/layer passage and supplementary discussion of
noncrossing particle worldlines and current were read, including the
neighbourhood of equations (10)–(11) and (21). The order of particle
charges and its relation to vacancy crossings are already part of that
analysis. The full large-deviation and fluctuation proofs were not
audited. No finite-ring exact-period formula is attributed to this
source or inferred as proved from these passages.

### S3 — finite-orbit mechanism is explicitly discussed

Tamás Gombor and Balázs Pozsgay, *Superintegrable cellular automata and
dual unitary gates from Yang-Baxter maps*, [arXiv:2112.01854v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.01854v2),
13 January 2022; [SciPost Phys. 12, 102 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.21468/SciPostPhys.12.3.102).
Sections 2–2.1 and 5.2 were directly read. On an even ring, the two-layer
map uses
`U(a,b)=(b,a)` if either entry is the vacuum 1, and
`U(a,b)=(a,b)` otherwise. This is the same three-state law after
`1,2,3 -> 0,+,-` relabelling. Equation (3) reverses the layer order for
the inverse. Forgetting colours gives the permutation model; colour
order is conserved. Section 5.2 already discusses occupancy return
followed by colour-arrangement shifts and reports quadratic orbit
growth. This desk does not validate every stated return multiple,
sharpen the period formula, or import the four-state orbit assertions.
Publisher search exposed publication metadata, but direct publisher
PDF access returned an HTML denial; the successfully read body was
the author preprint.

## Why no proposal

The directly checked XXC law is already owned. Reversing known
involution layers supplies singleton fibres, not an independent inverse
research axis. Known free occupancy tracks together with known preserved
cyclic charge order supply the obvious finite-ring decomposition. A
possible finer period/enumeration question would still need a separate
proved residual theorem and further exact ownership checks. Neither was
obtained, so no such question is promoted as a candidate or reserve.

Invertibility alone does not disqualify a system; it merely makes a
routine singleton-fibre observation insufficient for the second axis.
This lane did not prove that all possible XXC statistics are already
owned or that every lattice-gas system reduces to shifts. HPP,
annihilation and staggered Rule54 stayed at discovery level. Ordinary
elementary Rule54 and staggered reversible Rule54 were not identified
with one another.

Only three primary bodies above ground the external mechanism comparison.
Secondary search hits were retrieval leads, not proof evidence; search
non-hits do not establish novelty. No complete database, retraction,
author-identity, conflict-of-interest or latest-literature clearance was
performed. No source figure was inspected. These unassessed fields remain
unassessed rather than clean. External status remains HOLD_EXTERNAL.
