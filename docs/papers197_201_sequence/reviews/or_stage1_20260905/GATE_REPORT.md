## Decision and its contribution boundary

The frozen all-length contract is mathematically sound on the stated full
carrier `{0,1,2}^n`, `n>=1`. The independent proof audit accepts:

- the complete recurrent union `A_n union B_n`, with global colour advance
  on `A_n`, spatial left rotation on `B_n`, and the exact overlap;
- the pointwise first-entry parking criterion after two steps, including
  zero mass, and sharp maximum tail `H(1)=0`, `H(2)=2`,
  `H(n)=3 floor(n/3)+1` for every `n>=3`;
- the full targetwise source-set bijection, image iff no cyclic `21`,
  fibre `2^occ01`, and all maximum-fibre targets, not just an upper bound;
- the stated image and recurrent counts, fixed-iterate counts and weighted
  fibre polynomial, as standard consequences rather than extra axes.

The parking interpretation is **not** a new mechanism. The finite transport
lemma is an elementary specialization of established particle parking;
the two-phase traffic strategy, moving-frame idea, and finite traces receive
zero originality credit. P90, P169 and primary parking/oscillator owners
were read, not dismissed by different carrier names. The retained residual
is the literal original-word identification and exact sharp prehistory,
together with its separate local target decoder and equality atlas. The
bounded audit did not establish a whole-map conjugacy or a complete
two-axis proof transfer consuming that residual.

This conclusion is narrower than a claim that OR is an unprecedented
oscillator class. It does not exclude arbitrary block codes, rescaled-time
embeddings, or every published parking specialization. The current central
anchor is applied without relaxation: a future exact old owner or a full
joint transfer would reopen this candidate, as happened to CMM and EPF.
Different cycle lengths or non-hit searches alone did not cause selection.
