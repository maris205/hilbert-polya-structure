## Strongest counterargument

The generic template is heavily occupied: cellular maps often enter local
languages, shift constraints have transfer traces, and comparison posets
often have Fibonacci counts. P164 already has exactly the first equality
front; P190 has run erosion and pinned-gap inverses; P196 has a shift core
and every-target gap products. A fourth-root identity observed in small
graphs or a fitted characteristic recurrence would not create a sufficient
new theorem package. Nor would changing a known operator's coding be enough,
as the separate P201 failure demonstrates.

This pinned manuscript supplies more than that generic shell. The finite
identities hold on complete open-word domains and yield an all-length
attraction proof. Its explicit discrepancy coordinates show first entry,
not simply eventual arrival. The inverse retains comparison orientation,
which the P164 front discards, and identifies unsupported targets and every
maximizer. An independent zero-extremum/path-independent-set derivation
recovers its Fibonacci product. The full polynomial is proved independently
by a determinant identity of known degree, rather than agreeing with seven
initial counts. No inspected earlier literal map or complete mechanism
transports this joint package. This justifies internal continuation under
the existing gate, not external priority or immunity to a later exact hit.
