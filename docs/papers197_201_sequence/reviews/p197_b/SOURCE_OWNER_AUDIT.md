Fukś's work takes an associative binary operation and applies it at adjacent
sites. The manuscript's explicit associativity counterexample evaluates to
zero on one bracketing and one on the other. Associativity is invariant under
a common alphabet relabelling and under taking the opposite operation, so
those direct identifications are excluded. This reasoning does not exclude
isotopies, higher-block maps, factors or time iterates, and the manuscript
does not say otherwise. The books credit general machinery; the paper's
actual finite-window, trace and inversion steps are derived explicitly,
not delegated to an unlocated theorem in either book.

Additional current searches used cyclic/sign-difference/ternary comparator,
signum/cellular automata, and signed Ducci terms. A primary Springer hit
[on quasi-multiautomata](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00500-022-07747-9)
was inspected at its displayed local rule: it uses a hyperstructure input
and the binary-state expression sgn(s+i+|s+i|), not the ternary adjacent
difference map. That limited definition comparison is not a full-paper
owner clearance. Other broad signum hits were unrelated thresholds or
non-mathematical uses and were not used as evidence. No exact external
owner was found in the inspected sources; this is a bounded negative only.

## Residual and review decision

The residual is modest and joint: this literal ternary comparator has an
all-length fourth-root-of-shift core with parity-sharp first entry, and its
entire oriented target inverse/equality atlas has an explicit path/cycle
independent-set interpretation. Standard CA language, erosion, traces,
Fibonacci identities and generic comparison counting are not added to the
contribution tally. No inspected historical map or owner gives an exact
conjugate/factor transfer of the whole residual. Internal continuation is
therefore defensible, while higher-block/time-iterate owner risk remains
open and external dissemination stays on hold.
