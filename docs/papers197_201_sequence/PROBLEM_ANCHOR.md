# Problem anchor — candidate batch after P196

**Route:** A. **Batch:** exactly five anonymous theorem notes, numbered only
after the Stage-1 gate. **External status:** `OWNER_AMBER / HOLD_EXTERNAL`.

## Anchored research question

Beyond every literal system and proof mechanism occupied, killed, or reserved
through P196, which five genuinely different finite autonomous deterministic
maps display an early exact silhouette strong enough to support both:

1. a sharp all-parameter theorem about temporal structure, recurrence,
   transients, images, or a structural quotient; and
2. a separate inverse, fibre, extremal, enumeration, or identifiability theorem
   obtained by a materially different mechanism?

The objective remains breadth across dynamical subclasses.  A candidate is
killed when its first exact boxes show only generic convergence, when it is a
relabelled or factored earlier map, when its theorem axes collapse to one
formula, or when a clean inverse problem does not emerge.  Larger cutoffs do
not rescue a weak literal system.

## Promotion threshold

Every selected note must provide:

1. one natural and completely specified finite deterministic map;
2. an all-parameter temporal or recurrent theorem proved deductively;
3. a sharp clock, complete recurrent classification, image law, or comparably
   rigid structural conclusion;
4. a separate fibre, inverse, enumeration, or extremal conclusion;
5. a paper-local exact verifier and frozen canonical transcript used only as
   bounded counterexample pressure;
6. explicit subtraction against the P1--P196 internal surface and bounded
   direct-owner checking;
7. an anonymous short manuscript, two process-separated hostile reviews,
   accepted deltas, two physical cold builds, page-level visual QA, manifests,
   and a visible external hold.

Parameter variants, complements, conjugates, renamed classical algorithms,
generic finite-map bookkeeping, and search non-hits are below threshold.

## Selection boundary

Scouting deliberately crosses cellular words, graph colourings and matchings,
finite algebra and geometry, incidence structures, codes, tableaux, tilings,
relations, and set systems.  Exactly five pairwise-separated systems receive
P197--P201 only after their proof routes, collision boundaries, and
source-owner checks survive.  A later Critical defect reopens its slot.  No
bounded search result is a novelty, priority, ownership, or freedom-to-operate
certificate.
