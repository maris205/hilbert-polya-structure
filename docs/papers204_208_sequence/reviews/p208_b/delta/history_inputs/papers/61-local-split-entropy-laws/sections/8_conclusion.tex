\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Duplicating one return walk by an internally new equal-length chain gives a
fully explicit split extension of a prescribed primitive graph.  The graph
map supplies the section and nonconjugacy, path elimination gives the exact
finite determinant, and first-return renewal gives the entropy-radius
equation.  At positive entropy, the diagonal Perron weight controls the
leading gap with a relative error; at zero entropy, a distinct Lambert law
takes over.

The main value of the package is its normalization discipline.  It fixes
which length $N$ counts, separates the new first-return chain from its
possibly recurrent image, retains both Perron eigenvectors, and refuses to
continue an exponential argument through its zero-entropy singularity.
Whether this combined local package has sufficient priority and standalone
mass is intentionally left to specialist review.
