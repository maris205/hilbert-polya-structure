\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We isolated two finite losses that obscure periodic realization and bounded
each explicitly.  A common boundary shell converts independent local blocks
into a recoverable point of prescribed multidimensional period, yielding the
entropy sandwich in \cref{thm:fixed-period}.  Proper-divisor fixed points are
the corresponding loss for exact periods; Perron separation controls them
and yields \cref{thm:least-period} and an effective conductor.  The resulting
certificates are transparent enough to evaluate at a chosen size while
remaining grounded in symbolic proofs.

The note also records where this viewpoint stops.  Reflection introduces a
self-copying obstruction already at range two, and the spectral tail depends
essentially on primitivity and positive entropy.  Natural next questions are
whether alternate, non-reflection gluing schemes give explicit finite-range
certificates and whether arithmetic information on a matrix can sharpen the
least-period conductor without losing effectivity.  Those questions are not
resolved here.
