\section{Computational controls, ownership, and scope}
\label{sec:controls}

The proof is symbolic; the companion computation is a deterministic
falsifier.  The script \texttt{code/verify\_projective\_entropy.py} performs
four independent checks:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item it explicitly constructs level sets for several small parameters and
 verifies the disjoint-union identities before comparing their cardinalities
 with the polynomial recurrence;
 \item it iterates exact integers for the binary example through level $14$
 and evaluates both endpoints of \cref{eq:tail-certificate};
 \item it constructs an outward rational/fixed-point entropy interval using
 the atanh series for logarithms, with $80$ correction terms (hence partial
 sum $H_{81}$), then independently evaluates through $H_{261}$ at
 90-decimal precision;
 \item it checks strict parameter ordering for $2\le\ell\le8$ and reports
 scaled parabolic and superattracting tail ratios.  These finite tests do not
 prove \cref{prop:monotone,thm:parabolic,thm:super}.
\end{enumerate}

The stored output ends with \texttt{ALL CHECKS PASS}.  There is no random
seed and no external data.  Python's arbitrary-precision integers protect the
word-count recurrence; the scale $10^{80}$, directed integer division, and a
rational atanh remainder give the certified interval, while the
standard-library \texttt{decimal} module is fixed at 90 digits for an
independent evaluation.  The latter is a regression check, not part of the
outward proof.

\paragraph{Owned background.}
Equality of topological and inflation-word entropy in the primitive
constant-length setting is due to Mitchell \cite{Mitchell2024}.  Gohlke's
semi-compatible framework owns broad inflation-word bounds
\cite{Gohlke2020}, and ratio recurrences also occur in recent intrinsic
ergodicity work \cite{GohlkeMitchell2026}.  We claim neither mechanism in
isolation.  Our candidate residual is the exact two-parameter cocycle together
with its parabolic/superattracting remainder classification, strict parameter
order, and reusable a posteriori enclosure.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The substitution is compatible in length but not deterministic.  We do not
classify invariant measures, recognizability, or intrinsic ergodicity.  The
general polynomial result begins only after exact distinct-word recurrences
have been proved.  Constants in the $O$-terms of \cref{thm:parabolic,thm:super}
may depend on the fixed pair $(\ell,p)$.  The displayed decimal is not a
machine-assisted proof of the universal theorems.
