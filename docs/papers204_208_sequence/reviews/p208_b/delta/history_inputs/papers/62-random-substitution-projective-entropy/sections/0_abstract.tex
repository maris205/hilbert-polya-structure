\begin{abstract}
For integers $\ell\ge2$ and $1\le p\le\ell-1$, put $q=\ell-p$ and
consider the primitive constant-length random substitution
\[
 a\mapsto\{a^\ell,b^\ell\},\qquad b\mapsto\{a^pb^q\}.
\]
Exact level-block decoding reduces its inflation-word counts to a scalar
projective recurrence
\[
 r_{m+1}=\frac{r_m^q}{1+r_m^\ell},\qquad r_1=\frac12.
\]
We derive an exact discounted-cocycle formula for topological entropy and a
finite-level certified remainder.  The entropy tails have two genuinely
different regimes: when $q=1$ the fixed point is parabolic and the remainder
has an additional $M^{-1}$ factor, whereas for $q\ge2$ it is
superattracting and the projective coordinate decays doubly exponentially.
For fixed length, entropy is strictly increasing in $p$.  We also give a
general interval-enclosure principle for disjointly decodable two-letter
systems whose counts obey a homogeneous polynomial map.  As a numerical
consequence, the substitution $a\mapsto\{aa,bb\}$, $b\mapsto\{ab\}$ has
entropy $0.4328862961227668\ldots$; the value $0.4115$ displayed in the
cited source's arXiv v2 is excluded already by a level-$14$ exact lower bound.
The correction is presented as a corollary, not as the paper's principal
contribution.
\end{abstract}
