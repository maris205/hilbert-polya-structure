\section{Fixed arithmetic symmetry obstruction}
\label{sec:symmetry}

The Burnside class of \Cref{thm:pqr} is functorial under relabeling.  A
character Fredholm factor needs more: the same group must commute with one
fixed transfer operator.  This section tests that requirement directly.

\subsection{Semilinear covariance}

For $g\in G_P=\SP(P)$, let $\rho(g)e_S=e_{gS}$.  Define the relabeled
variables by $(g\cdot x)_S=x_{g^{-1}S}$.

\begin{theorem}[Covariance and commutator criterion]
\label{thm:covariance}
The rank-one family satisfies
\[
  \rho(g)A_x\rho(g)^{-1}=A_{g\cdot x}.
  \tag{5.1}
\]
For a fixed specialization $x$, the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $[A_x,\rho(g)]=0$;
\item $x_{gS}=x_S$ for every nonempty $S\subseteq P$;
\item $x_{gp}=x_p$ for every $p\in P$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The vector $u_P$ is invariant.  On a basis vector,
\[
 (\ell_x\rho(g)^{-1})(e_S)
 =\eps(S)x_{g^{-1}S}=\ell_{g\cdot x}(e_S),
 \tag{5.2}
\]
which proves (5.1).  Commutation is equivalent to invariance of the covector
$\ell_x$, giving the equivalence of the first two statements.  Singleton
subsets show that the second statement implies the third; multiplicativity
of $x_S$ gives the converse.
\end{proof}

Equation (5.1) is a semilinear symmetry of a family: the permutation changes
both the subset basis and the coefficient point.  It is not a decomposition
of $A_x$ into invariant character fibers unless the equivalent conditions of
\Cref{thm:covariance} hold.

\begin{corollary}[Arithmetic stabilizer]
\label{cor:arithmetic-stabilizer}
Under $x_{F_p}=p^{-s}$ with $\Ree s>0$, the fixed operator $A_x$ has trivial
atom-permutation stabilizer.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
If $p\ne q$, then
$|p^{-s}|=p^{-\Ree s}\ne q^{-\Ree s}=|q^{-s}|$.  A stabilizing permutation
must fix every singleton weight and hence every atom.
\end{proof}

Central idempotents of $\C[S_n]$ therefore do not project the fixed arithmetic
operator onto invariant character fibers.  They project the unweighted
permutation representation, but $A_x$ moves those subspaces when the weights
are distinct.

\subsection{Equal weights and rank-one collapse}

One can restore commutation by setting $x_p=t$ for all labels.  This removes
the arithmetic distinctions and exposes a second obstruction.

\begin{theorem}[Nontrivial isotypes are determinant-invisible]
\label{thm:equal-weight}
At equal weights, $A_t$ is $S_n$-equivariant,
\[
  \operatorname{im}A_t=\C u_P,
  \tag{5.3}
\]
and its restriction to every nontrivial isotype is zero.  Hence
\[
  \det(I-A_t\mid V_\lambda)=1
  \tag{5.4}
\]
for every nontrivial irreducible type $\lambda$.  The only nonzero eigenvalue
is
\[
  b_P(t)=1-(1-t)^n.
  \tag{5.5}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equal weights make $\ell_t$ invariant, so \Cref{thm:covariance} gives
equivariance.  The rank-one formula (3.5) gives (5.3), and $u_P$ spans a
trivial representation.  An equivariant map from a nontrivial irreducible
summand to the trivial line is zero.  The determinant on every such summand
is therefore one.  Finally, the rank-one eigenvalue is
$\ell_t(u_P)=b_P(t)$.
\end{proof}

The full subset representation has one trivial basis vector for each subset
cardinality, so its trivial isotypic component has dimension $n$.  The
operator on that component is still rank one.  Its determinant at $z=1$ is
\[
  1-b_P(t)=(1-t)^n.
  \tag{5.6}
\]
All resolved nontrivial modes are identically one.  Equalization thus restores
the group only after deleting the label scale that made the specialization
arithmetic and the character motion that motivated SD-C18.

\subsection{What the finite character still means}

The obstruction does not make $R_3$ spurious.  It means that $R_3$ belongs to
the formal primitive-cycle functor, not to invariant fibers of the fixed
prime-weighted rank-one operator.  Three statements must be kept separate:

\begin{itemize}
\item the formal variable family is equivariant under simultaneous relabeling;
\item the squarefree primitive coefficient is a nonzero virtual $S_3$ class;
\item the arithmetically specialized transfer has no nontrivial $S_3$ symmetry.
\end{itemize}

The first two earn \texttt{GO\_FORMAL\_EQUIVARIANT\_LEDGER}.  The third
blocks a character Fredholm interpretation of the canonical rank-one
specialization.
