\section{Conclusion}

The finite-group derivative forgets one global left translation and nothing
else.  On a subshift, excluding translated copies turns that fibre statement
into a conjugacy, while finite-language separation determines the inverse
window exactly.  The bounded rank-one family shows that compact separation
may occur only at increasingly long scales even when every visible
construction parameter and the forward local rule remain fixed.

The proof keeps the global and local mechanisms separate.  Exact frequency
imbalance makes the source disjoint from its complement; an explicit
length-$3|v_N|+1$ complementary pair shows that short observations still
cannot distinguish the two.  A typed-defect coding of the tail proves that
this pair is maximal, yielding exact formulas for both the separating length
and the inverse radius.  The latter grows exponentially in the construction
parameter.

A natural next problem is to determine what additional quantitative
hypotheses---for example a fixed recurrence constant or a uniform lower bound
on separation of invariant measures---do control inverse radii.  The present
family shows that bounded cutting and bounded spacers alone do not.
