\begin{abstract}
We quantify the canonical simple-Parry approximation of a $\beta$-shift when the greedy expansion of one is binary and its zero gaps increase strictly.  The existence of such expansions and the qualitative truncation mechanism are already present in the $\beta$-expansion literature; our object is the entropy error.  If the ones occur at $n_1=1<n_2<\cdots$, if $\beta_j$ is defined by $\sum_{k\leq j}\beta_j^{-n_k}=1$, and if $L_j=\log\beta-\log\beta_j$, we prove a non-asymptotic two-sided certificate for $L_j$ and a fully finite rational root-enclosure version.  They yield the sharp law
\[
 L_j=\frac{\beta^{-n_{j+1}}}{\sum_{k\geq1}n_k\beta^{-n_k}}
 \left(1+\bigO_\beta\!\left(\beta^{-(n_{j+2}-n_{j+1})}
 +n_{j+1}\beta^{-n_{j+1}}\right)\right).
\]
Consequently, the first omitted one fixes the logarithmic scale, strictly increasing gaps force faster-than-exponential convergence in the truncation index, and arbitrary prescribed upper envelopes can still be realized by choosing later gaps; no arbitrary exact-schedule claim is made.  A rational-arithmetic certificate and a dependency-free executable check accompany the proof.
\end{abstract}
