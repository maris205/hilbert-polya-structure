\begin{abstract}
Let $\LambdaSurf$ be the fundamental group of the closed nonorientable surface
of genus three and let $\K$ be a finite group.  We define a
$\LambdaSurf$-subshift of finite type whose symbols label generator edges by
elements of $\K$ and whose local rule imposes trivial holonomy around the
surface relator.  Two explicit finite-index subgroup families give orientable
and nonorientable covers.  If $d_\chi$ and $\nu_\chi$ are the degree and the
Frobenius--Schur indicator of $\chi\in\Irr(\K)$, their fixed-point counts are
\[
 |\K|^{4m}\sum_{\chi\in\Irr(\K)}d_\chi^{-2m}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 |\K|^{2n}\sum_{\chi\in\Irr(\K)}
       \nu_\chi^{n+2}d_\chi^{-n}.
\]
Finite moment inversion shows that the two spectra jointly determine $|\K|$
and the multiset of pairs $(d_\chi,\nu_\chi)$.  In particular, the order-eight
dihedral and quaternion groups have identical spectra on the orientable family
but are separated at every odd level of the nonorientable family.  The surface
homomorphism formulas used in the proof are classical; no priority claim is
made.
\end{abstract}
