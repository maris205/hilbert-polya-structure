\title[Even-level plane-tree contraction]{Even-Level Contraction of Plane Rooted Trees:\\
Divisibility Iterates and Complete Size-Refined Fibres}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Delete every odd generation of a plane rooted tree, promote each deleted
vertex's ordered child block to its parent, reset parity, and repeat.  We
analyze this literal map on the finite carrier of trees with at most $N$
vertices while keeping its exact-size source layers distinct.  After $k$
steps, precisely the original depths divisible by $2^k$ survive.  Hence a
tree of height $h$ reaches the singleton after
$\lceil\log_2(h+1)\rceil$ steps, and the sharp maximum on $n$ vertices is
$\lceil\log_2n\rceil$.  For every target $U$ with $m$ vertices and $I(U)$
internal vertices, a reversible ordered block-and-gap construction gives
\[
 \sum_{E(T)=U}y^{|T|-m}=\frac{y^{I(U)}}{(1-y)^{2m-1}}.
\]
Thus $U$ occurs from the exact $n$-vertex layer exactly when
$m+I(U)\le n$, with an explicit binomial fibre.  Weighting a target by its
minimum predecessor size yields the algebraic series
$H=z+z^2H/(1-H)$, and $H/(1-z)$ counts exact-layer images.  An exact audit of
all $23{,}714$ plane trees through $11$ vertices supplies counterexample
pressure but is not used as proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{The finite map and the exact-layer question}

Let $\PT_n$ be the set of plane rooted trees with $n$ vertices, and fix
$N\ge1$.  The literal dynamical carrier is the finite disjoint union
\[
                  \PT_{\le N}:=\bigsqcup_{1\le n\le N}\PT_n.
\]
A tree is an ordered list of its child trees.  If
$T=(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$ and
$T_i=(T_{i1},\ldots,T_{ir_i})$, define recursively
\begin{equation}\label{eq:E-recursive}
 E(T):=\bigl(E(T_{11}),\ldots,E(T_{1r_1}),
             \ldots,E(T_{d1}),\ldots,E(T_{dr_d})\bigr).
\end{equation}
Geometrically, the root and every even-depth vertex are retained.  Each
odd-depth child is deleted, its child block is promoted, and the blocks are
concatenated in the original plane order.  Equation~\eqref{eq:E-recursive}
then resets parity within every retained grandchild subtree.  The singleton
$\one$ is fixed.

The output uses a subset of the input vertices, so $E$ is a self-map of
$\PT_{\le N}$.  It is generally not a self-map of $\PT_n$.  We therefore use
$\PT_n$ only as an \emph{exact source layer}: expressions such as
$E(\PT_n)$ always mean the image of that subset inside the finite carrier.
The formal fibre series below ranges coefficientwise over all finite source
layers; for fixed $N$, it is truncated after source size $N$.
Because $N$ is arbitrary, we state exact-layer results for every $n$; inside
a fixed carrier they are read with $1\le n\le N$.

Plane-tree decompositions and parity-sensitive statistics have a mature
enumerative literature~\citep{ChenLiShapiro2007}.  More directly,
outward-contraction groups every even-level vertex with all its odd-level
children and takes the resulting partition-tree~\citep[Definition
6.6]{SooKhoussainovLinz2022}.  If $\operatorname{For}$ forgets plane order,
then, with the same designated root,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:forgetful-owner}
 \operatorname{For}(E(T))\cong
 \operatorname{OutContr}(\operatorname{For}(T),\operatorname{root}(T)).
\end{equation}
Indeed, the quotient vertices are indexed by the original even-depth
vertices, and each quotient edge crosses an odd vertex to an original
grandchild.  Thus the unordered one-step rule, its partition-tree
interpretation, and bare height compression receive zero contribution credit.
Ordered child promotion also occurs inside generic forest
contractions~\citep{BerkemerSiederdissenStadler2021}, while simultaneous
plane-tree transitions~\citep{NicholsEtAl2020} and Horton-style leaf
pruning~\citep{KovchegovZaliapin2016} give nearby but different dynamics.
Catalan enumeration, parity statistics, generic contraction, transition
vocabulary, and pruning clocks likewise receive zero credit.  The residual
claims concern the plane-order lift under repeated parity reset, its all-rank
divisibility and sharp pointwise clock, and its complete ordered target
inverse and exact-layer image series.

\section{Divisible-depth skeletons and the sharp clock}

Write $h(T)$ for the maximum root depth and let $\tau(T)$ be the least
$k\ge0$ such that $E^k(T)=\one$.

\begin{theorem}[iterate skeleton and clock]\label{thm:clock}
For every plane rooted tree $T$ and $k\ge0$, the vertices of $E^k(T)$ are
exactly the original vertices whose depths are divisible by $2^k$.  Each is
joined to its nearest retained ancestor, and the plane order is induced by
the original contour order.  Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:height-clock}
 h(E(T))=\left\lfloor\frac{h(T)}2\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 \tau(T)=\left\lceil\log_2(h(T)+1)\right\rceil.
\end{equation}
The singleton is the unique recurrent state, and for every $n\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:max-clock}
       \max_{T\in\PT_n}\tau(T)=\lceil\log_2n\rceil.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Track original vertex identities.  The assertion at $k=0$ is immediate.
Assume that at rank $k$ an original vertex of depth $d$ survives precisely
when $2^k\mid d$, in which case its current depth is $d/2^k$.  The next
contraction retains it precisely when this current depth is even, or
equivalently when $2^{k+1}\mid d$.  Promoting across the deleted current
level joins nearest retained ancestors.  Since concatenation of ordered
blocks is associative, the resulting order is the one induced from the
original contour.  This proves the iterate assertion by induction.

A deepest root path contains a vertex at every depth from $0$ through $h(T)$.
It follows that
$h(E^k(T))=\lfloor h(T)/2^k\rfloor$.  Thus $E^k(T)$ is the singleton exactly
when $2^k>h(T)$, whose least solution is the second formula in
\eqref{eq:height-clock}.  Every nonsingleton loses at least one depth-one
vertex, so it cannot be recurrent.  Finally $h(T)\le n-1$, and the
$n$-vertex path has height $n-1$; this proves~\eqref{eq:max-clock}, including
non-powers of two.
\end{proof}

\section{A complete ordered block-and-gap inverse}

For a target tree $U$, let $m=|U|$, let $d_U(v)$ be the outdegree of $v$, and
let
\[
           I(U):=|\{v\in U:d_U(v)>0\}|.
\]
Weight every source vertex deleted in one contraction by $y$.

\begin{lemma}[local inverse factor]\label{lem:local}
At a target vertex of outdegree $d$, the generating function for its inserted
odd children is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local-factor}
 A_0(y)=\frac1{1-y},
 \qquad
 A_d(y)=\frac{y}{(1-y)^{d+1}}\quad(d>0).
\end{equation}
These choices form a reversible local construction.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For $d=0$, every odd child must be a leaf; an arbitrary number gives
$A_0(y)$.  Suppose $d>0$ and exactly $r$ odd children are productive.  Their
grandchildren split the ordered target child list into $r$ nonempty
consecutive blocks, in $\binom{d-1}{r-1}$ ways.  Any number of empty odd
leaves may occupy each of the $r+1$ gaps before, between, and after those
productive children.  Hence
\begin{align*}
 A_d(y)
 &=\sum_{r=1}^d\binom{d-1}{r-1}\frac{y^r}{(1-y)^{r+1}}\\
 &=\frac{y}{(1-y)^2}
   \left(1+\frac{y}{1-y}\right)^{d-1}
 =\frac{y}{(1-y)^{d+1}}.
\end{align*}
The predecessor child list uniquely recovers which odd children are empty
and which consecutive block each productive child carries.  Thus the
construction is reversible.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[every-target fibre]\label{thm:fibre}
For every target $U$ with $m$ vertices,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fibre-series}
 \sum_{E(T)=U}y^{|T|-m}
       =\frac{y^{I(U)}}{(1-y)^{2m-1}}.
\end{equation}
Consequently, among sources in $\PT_n$ the fibre size is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fibre-coefficient}
 |E^{-1}(U)\cap\PT_n|=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\binom{n-m-I(U)+2m-2}{2m-2},&n-m\ge I(U),\\[6pt]
 0,&n-m<I(U).
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a target $U$, write
\[
 F_U(y):=\sum_{E(T)=U}y^{|T|-|U|}.
\]
If $U=(U_1,\ldots,U_d)$, the reversible construction in
Lemma~\ref{lem:local} gives the coefficientwise formal-series recursion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fibre-recursion}
                  F_U(y)=A_d(y)\prod_{j=1}^dF_{U_j}(y).
\end{equation}
Indeed, each productive inserted odd child carries predecessor subtrees for
one nonempty consecutive block of the ordered list $(U_1,\ldots,U_d)$;
empty inserted odd children occupy the intervening gaps.  The predecessor
child list recovers both the block partition and every subtree predecessor,
so the construction is injective and surjective with no double counting.
Induction on $|U|$ proves \eqref{eq:fibre-recursion}; for each coefficient
only finitely many inserted vertices occur.

Now apply Lemma~\ref{lem:local} through this recursion.  Internal vertices
contribute one numerator factor $y$ and leaves contribute none.  Since a
rooted tree satisfies
$\sum_vd_U(v)=m-1$, the total denominator exponent is
\[
              \sum_{v\in U}(d_U(v)+1)=(m-1)+m=2m-1.
\]
This proves~\eqref{eq:fibre-series}.  Extracting the coefficient of
$y^{n-m}$ gives~\eqref{eq:fibre-coefficient} by the negative-binomial
expansion.  For the singleton target, the formula correctly gives one source
of every size: the corresponding star.
\end{proof}

The temporal and inverse proofs use different structures.  The first tracks
depth divisibility, whereas the second reconstructs ordered child lists; the
fibre is not inferred from the clock or a state census.

\section{Exact-size images and an algebraic series}

\begin{corollary}[image criterion]\label{cor:image}
A target $U$ lies in $E(\PT_n)$ if and only if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:image-condition}
                     |U|+I(U)\le n.
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The coefficient in~\eqref{eq:fibre-coefficient} is positive exactly under
condition~\eqref{eq:image-condition}.
\end{proof}

Define the minimum-source-weight series
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H-def}
                    H(z):=\sum_Uz^{|U|+I(U)},
\end{equation}
where the sum is over all finite plane rooted trees.  A leaf contributes $z$.
An internal root contributes $z^2$ and has a nonempty ordered sequence of
child trees.  Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H-equation}
          H=z+z^2\sum_{d\ge1}H^d
           =z+\frac{z^2H}{1-H}.
\end{equation}
Equivalently, $H$ is the root with zero constant term of
\[
             H^2-(1+z-z^2)H+z=0.
\]
Explicitly,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H-closed}
 H(z)=\frac{1+z-z^2-
 \sqrt{(1+z-z^2)^2-4z}}{2},
\end{equation}
where the minus branch is forced by $H(0)=0$.
Corollary~\ref{cor:image} now gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:image-gf}
 \sum_{n\ge1}|E(\PT_n)|z^n=\frac{H(z)}{1-z}.
\end{equation}
The factor $(1-z)^{-1}$ takes cumulative sums of the minimum source weights;
it does not turn $E$ into a size-preserving map.

\section{Exact audit and conclusion}

An independent standard-library verifier generates every plane tree through
$11$ vertices.  For every state it labels original vertices, compares each
iterate with the predicted divisible-depth skeleton, and checks the clock.
For every target and source size it compares observed fibres with
\eqref{eq:fibre-coefficient}; it also checks the local factors
coefficientwise and solves~\eqref{eq:H-equation} as a formal series.  The
$216{,}905$ exact assertions all pass.  Table~\ref{tab:audit} records the
main profile; it is falsification pressure, not proof evidence.

dynamics remains on $\PT_{\le N}$.

\section*{Limitations}
The theorem is specific to finite plane rooted trees and the simultaneous
deletion of every odd generation.  It does not cover unordered trees,
arbitrary selected levels, weighted contractions, or asymptotic random-tree
questions.  The unordered one-step shadow is the directly owned
outward-contraction in~\cite{SooKhoussainovLinz2022}; only the ordered
iterate/fibre/image conjunction is retained.  The reopened literature audit
was bounded; no novelty or priority claim is made.  Enumeration through $11$
vertices cannot replace the proofs.  The manuscript remains under
\texttt{HOLD\_EXTERNAL}.
