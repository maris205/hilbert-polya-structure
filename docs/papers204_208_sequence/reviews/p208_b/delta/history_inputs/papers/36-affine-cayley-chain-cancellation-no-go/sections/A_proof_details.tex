\section{Proof details and boundary calculations}
\label{app:proofs}

\subsection{Primitivity of the relation polygon}

The word $vu\bar v\bar u^r$ is freely reduced: its adjacent generator types
are distinct, and the cyclic join from $\bar u$ to $v$ is not an inverse pair.
It is therefore cyclically nonbacktracking.  If it were a $d$-fold temporal
power for $d\ge2$, every oriented-letter count would be divisible by $d$.
The word contains exactly one positive $v$, a contradiction.  Translation by
any $x\in\Mr$ preserves these properties.

\subsection{Exact chain line}

The path-chain of $vu$ is $e_v+ve_u$.  The path-chain of $u^rv$ is
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}u^je_u+u^re_v.
\]
Their difference is \cref{eq:cell-boundary}.  Since $\Kr$ is contractible,
the augmented cellular sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow\Z\Mr\xrightarrow{\partial_2}(\Z\Mr)^2
\xrightarrow{\partial_1}\Z\Mr\longrightarrow\Z\longrightarrow0
\]
is exact.  Thus every cellular one-cycle is a relation boundary.  Simple
connectivity separately gives the path-homotopy statement for every reduced
closed word.

\subsection{Damping exponent}

On the based polygon, the oriented-edge origins are
\[
(0,0),\ (0,1),\ (r,1),\ (r,0),(r-1,0),\ldots,(1,0).
\]
Their $1+b+k$ values are
\[
1,\ 2,\ r+2,\ r+1,r,\ldots,2.
\]
The sum is \cref{eq:cycle-exponent}.  In a product
$DHD\cdots DHD$, every visited oriented-edge state receives one diagonal
factor from the left and one from the right.  Hence a closed cycle has the
square of the product of its $D$-weights.  This supplies the exponent $2S_r$
in \cref{eq:positive-trace}.

\subsection{Finite quotient interpretation}

For $q$ coprime to $r$, multiplication by $r$ is an automorphism of
$\Z/q\Z$ and has order $t$.  The quotient adds the closed words $u^q$ and
$v^t$.  Filling only the affine cell cannot make these new presentation
relations into boundaries in general.  Adding their cell orbits completes the
finite presentation; the exact rank audit in \cref{tab:finite-audit} then
gives $H_1=0$.  This explains the finite residue without promoting it to an
infinite-source class.

\subsection{All-orders superdeterminant}

For trace-class or finite-trace blocks, define the connected graded logarithm
by
\[
-\log\SDet(I-z\widetilde A)
=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{z^n}{n}\Str(\widetilde A^n).
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:supertrace} makes every coefficient zero, so the germ is
identically one.  This conclusion uses no property of the affine relation
word and is therefore a generic-control failure, not evidence of selective
relation removal.
