\subsection{Explicit nonclaims}

Nothing in this paper proves or assumes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \(D_{\SD}(s,1)=1/\zeta(s)\);
  \item convergence of the primitive product at \(z=1\);
  \item analytic continuation of \(D_{\SD}\) to or across
  \(\RePart s=1/2\);
  \item a functional equation or Gamma factor;
  \item a completed Riemann divisor or Riemann--von Mangoldt law;
  \item a Weil Hermitian compression;
  \item RH or a Hilbert--Pólya operator;
  \item absolute priority over all arithmetic graph constructions.
\end{itemize}

The conjugation identity in \cref{cor:conjugation} is not a functional
equation.  The exact fixed-order trace expressions in
\cref{eq:finite-trace-app} are not a continuation of the infinite Fredholm
determinant.  The half-plane in \cref{thm:traceclass} is not credited as A3.

\subsection{Whole-object and comparison locks}

The only operator determinant is the whole Fredholm determinant
\[
 D_{\SD}(s,z)=\det(I-zL_s).
\]
There is no regular-representation determinant, character factor, or
unreported isotypic block.  The target comparison is performed after the
source graph, roof, and operator are frozen.  It uses marked Taylor
coefficients and no target zeros.

\subsection{Control interpretation}

The quotient spine is a graph-selectivity control.  Positive changes of the
roof inventory are weight controls only.  The observed zero margin means
that all quotient edges beyond \(q=2\) can be deleted without changing the
properties that reject the target.  It does not mean that the full graph and
spine have identical trace coefficients at every order.

\subsection{Round-two clue firewall}

symbolic holonomy into a unitary object.  That thought crosses to another
system family and is recorded only as a round-two clue.  No carrier,
Hamiltonian, boundary condition, or self-adjoint operator enters the
definitions, proofs, or Route-A score of SD-C23.
