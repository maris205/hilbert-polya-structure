## 5. Why the pool is empty

The forced sliding commutator candidate fails exactly as anticipated: class-two
centralization makes its depth at most three, while direct primary work already
owns commutator-word fibres.  The only candidate that initially met the three
formal axes, `NL07`, then received a stronger mechanism hit: its update is the
standard Thue–Morse morphism acting on the two letter images.  Once that action
and ordinary word conjugacy are assigned zero credit, the overflow clock and
cut counts are too thin and too closely coupled to constitute three residual
axes.  The other 22 candidates fail temporally, inversely, structurally, or by
an internal proof-engine collision before any novelty inference is relevant.

Therefore this lane freezes **`EMPTY_POOL / HOLD_EXTERNAL`**.  Reopening a slot
requires a different mechanism, not a larger parameter box for one of these
maps.
