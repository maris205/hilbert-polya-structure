## Ranking before collision subtraction

1. **POC** had the most complete theorem package, including an independent
   parameter-recovery statistic.  It is nevertheless not admissible because
   its entire proof skeleton is already occupied by P127.
2. **GDI** had the cleanest arithmetic clock plus a genuine target-dependent
   inverse condition.  A direct public literal owner appeared before this
   scout and is decisive.
3. **CTC** has unusually clean odd/even characteristic formulae, but all
   dynamics end after two steps; the remaining work is classical quadratic
   counting on a self-commutator image.

No candidate survives both theorem and owner/collision gates.  There is no
P166 theorem contract from this lane.
