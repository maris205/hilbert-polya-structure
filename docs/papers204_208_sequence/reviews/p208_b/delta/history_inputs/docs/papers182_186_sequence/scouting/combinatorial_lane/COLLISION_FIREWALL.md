## Reserve and killed controls

`DSR` is not promoted.  Although its fixed points have a composition
parametrisation and all permutations through `S_9` converge, the lane has no
general pointwise clock or complete inverse.  Its stable-ranking interface is
also adjacent to P137/P156/P167 and to the external displacement/rank
literature.  This is an auditable `RESERVE_AMBER_NOT_PROMOTABLE`, not an
observed theorem.

The other nine controls remain killed:

- `BDS` is close to the occurrence-rank/tableau transform directly owned by
  Prasad--Ram, while its own period-two signal lacks a general clock and
  inverse;
- `CAD`, `CGS`, and `FCR` have rank-dependent, fragmented period sets;
- `IHM`, `MEP`, and `HLC` are idempotent or static canonical closures whose
  clock and image arise from one mechanism; and
- `FPT` and `DTR` enter owner-dense load-balancing/threshold territory without
  a second closed axis.

No killed system is rescued by a large tail, a surprising period, or the
absence of a literal title match.
