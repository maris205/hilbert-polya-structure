## RICS: random incoming-copy symmetrization

| Nearest occupied system | Shared surface | Decisive separation |
|---|---|---|
| P145 vertex-push cut walk | random vertex actions on graph bits | P145 toggles an undirected cut and supports a walk; RICS copies a directed incoming star, deletes incident conflict edges monotonically, and absorbs. |
| P159 odd-degree pruning | graph statistic decreases | P159 selects through degree parity; RICS has an arbitrary initial conflict graph and the selected vertex deletes its entire conflict star. |
| P177 projective-hyperplane toggling | random local involutive-looking bit update | P177 is additive Cayley motion governed by a code; RICS local maps are idempotent, noncommuting, and have an order-sensitive absorbing endpoint. |
| P179 random singleton isolation | coupon-like random labels and a support clock | P179's idempotents commute and the endpoint depends only on selected support.  In RICS, on a conflicting pair `C_u C_v != C_v C_u`; the earlier endpoint permanently chooses the resolved bit. |
| directed-network reciprocity literature | opposite arcs and reciprocal pairs | The nearest bounded source studies static/exponential random graph ensembles or growing preferential-attachment models, not the finite vertex-copy kernel or its independent-set absorption polynomial. |

**Non-negotiable paper identity.**  A RICS paper must center the exact
conflict-graph/first-order kernel, not generic coupon collection.  It must prove
the independent-set absorption polynomial and the two different one-step fibre
counts.  Losing order sensitivity collapses it toward P179 and is a kill.

## CGT: co-gcd translation

| Nearest occupied system | Shared surface | Decisive separation |
|---|---|---|
| P128 translated-polynomial gcd | translation and gcd in the formula | P128 takes polynomial gcd data; CGT translates the residue itself by the additive order `p^a/gcd(x,p^a)`. |
| P142 valuation--gcd prime-power divisors | `p`-adic strata, tails, fibres | P142's carrier is the divisor chain and its literal output is a gcd.  CGT acts on all `p^a` residues; within a low stratum it is a permutation, and an even-exponent middle stratum forms a finite conveyor before falling low. |
| P166 Hamming-weight translation | state-dependent translation | P166 translates coordinates by a Hamming-weight-selected phase on a cyclic cube.  CGT is a one-coordinate nonlinear residue map selected by additive order and has a `0/1/2` fibre atlas. |
| finite-ring dynamics literature | functional graphs over finite rings | The bounded primary source located concerns linear maps over finite commutative rings.  CGT is nonlinear and valuation-switched; this difference is not a novelty claim. |

**Non-negotiable paper identity.**  The paper must prove all prime-power
strata, including `p=2` and the even-exponent middle layer; state only an odd
prime or only generic valuations and the candidate is too close to a P142
transfer.  The exact double-target atlas is required.
