## Primary external sources actually opened

### Permanent cofactors and unique matching

- Marvin Marcus and Russell Merris, [A relation between the permanental
  and determinantal adjoints](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/relation-between-the-permanental-and-determinantal-adjoints/0B5E73B5FE0F76C416C2782E18B0D00B),
  DOI `10.1017/S1446788700013173`. The original publisher PDF's first-page
  definition was read: the permanental adjoint transposes the deleted
  row/column positions. Its positive-Hermitian theorem is not used to
  infer Boolean iteration. The static adjoint construction is owned.
- Zur Izhakian and John Rhodes, [New Representations of Matroids and
  Generalizations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.0503), arXiv:1103.0503.
  Read Section 3 through the full proof of Lemma 3.2 (PDF printed pages
  7–9, file pages 6–8). The three-element superboolean permanent records
  whether a Boolean matrix has exactly one perfect matching, and the
  lemma gives permutation triangularization. This supports the static
  deduction in the UPA branch, not a claimed all-matrix iterate theorem.
  Unrelated later matroid results were not needed or invoked.

### Partition solitaire and regrouping

- Brian Hopkins, [Column-to-row operations on partitions: The envelopes](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/j6proc/j6proc.pdf),
  *Integers* 9 Supplement (2009), Article 6. Read title/abstract and
  Sections 1–2 definitions, including the fixed-k column-to-row rule.
  This owns the general conjugation/column-repacking primitive. WGP's
  equal-column area grouping and DSR's state-dependent square removal
  are not identified with that literal; no later theorem is transferred.
- Brian Hopkins, [Henrik Eriksson's Bulgarian Solitaire Variant](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/a9Proc23/a9Proc23.pdf),
  *Integers* 24A (2024), #A9, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.11352790`.
  Read the original title/metadata and Section 2 definitions of H_k,
  L-solitaire and σ-solitaire, plus the displayed H_k cyclic-state
  theorem and part of its proof. H_k removes up to a fixed k from every pile;
  DSR instead removes d from exactly the first d piles with endogenous d.
  The H_k theorem is therefore not used as a DSR convergence proof.

### First fit

- György Dósa and Jiří Sgall, [First Fit bin packing: A tight analysis](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.538),
  STACS 2013, DOI `10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.538`.
  Read the official landing metadata and original PDF first-page
  first-fit definition; also inspected the early bin-fit inequalities.
  This establishes the ordinary packing primitive and its ownership.
  The approximation-ratio theorem concerns packing quality, not iterated
  residual feedback, and is not claimed as a temporal result here.
