### H01 — P113 principal-hook regrouping

Source: papers/113-principal-hook-partition-dynamics/main.tex, Sections 2–3.
For Durfee size d, the map on partitions of fixed mass N is

    H(lambda)=(lambda_i+lambda'_i-2i+1 : 1<=i<=d).

The complete old one-step inverse chooses Frobenius arms and legs with
alpha_i+beta_i=h_i-1. Its bottom arm choice and independent adjacent
arm/leg gap splits give image h_i-h_(i+1)>=2 and fibre
h_r product_(i<r)(h_i-h_(i+1)-1). Both map and inverse product are
explicitly owned background in the manuscript.

The separate clock uses first gap g=lambda_1-lambda_2:
g(H lambda)-g(lambda)=2+m_1(lambda) for d>=2; at d=1 a nonterminal
(a,1^b) goes directly to (N), with increment b+1. Summing increments
gives tau<=floor((N-g)/2). The sharp floor(N/2) is attained on two rows,
which evolve by (a,b)->(a+1,b-1). First-hook absorption is already
low-credit. Neither this gap clock nor the Frobenius inverse transfers to
HMP merely by sharing hook terminology.

### H07 — P181 first-descent prefix reversal

Source: papers/181-first-descent-prefix-reversal/main.tex, Sections 1–4.

    d=min{i:pi_i>pi_(i+1)}; F(pi)=rho_(d+1)(pi); F(id)=id.
