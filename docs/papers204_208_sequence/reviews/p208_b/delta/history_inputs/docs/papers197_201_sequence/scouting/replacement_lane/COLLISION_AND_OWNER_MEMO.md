## 1. Binding mechanism firewall

The replacement search applied the following hard rules before interpreting
small-box data.

- Ordinary powers, pointer jumping, and a first-image compression followed by
  an action are killed.
- Standard sorting/0-Hecke, closure/Bellman, canonical pruning, and a clock
  inserted directly by the definition are killed unless a genuinely
  independent inverse/census axis survives.
- A new scheduler on an old local move is not enough when the earlier ledger
  already killed that move or carrier family.
- An old killed or reserved literal is not revived by a larger cutoff,
  decoration, stronger computation, or a new acronym.
- Generic functional-graph language, transfer matrices, chromatic
  polynomials, Stirling/onto counts, and zeta bookkeeping receive zero credit.

These rules kill LSPO even though its current theorem package is correct, and
they kill six of the nine new literals in the breadth ledger.

## 3. LZK claim boundaries that must not drift

1. Main sharpness assumes `r,s>=1` and `q>=3`.  At `q=2` the carrier has two
   states in one two-cycle and maximum tail zero.
2. Formula (1) in the contract counts two-cycle **points** and has a leading
   factor `2`; divide once, and only once, to count cycles.
3. Every-time fibre formulas include `t=0`.  In particular, the zero-free
   target sum contains the `j=0` self-source.
4. A transient target has `binom(a(y),t)` sources, not a cumulative binomial
   sum.  A zero-free target has the cumulative sum because a source may reach
   it early and then hold.
5. No mixing, Kempe-connectivity, chromatic-polynomial novelty, or general
   multipartite extension is claimed.
