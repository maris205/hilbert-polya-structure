## 2. External owner subtraction

### 2.1 Common fixed points: direct object ownership

Hanany and Puder,
[*Word Measures on Symmetric Groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00897),
IMRN 2023, DOI
[`10.1093/imrn/rnac084`](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnac084), starts from
independent uniform images of free generators.  Section 2 explicitly passes
from fixed points of one word to common fixed points of several words, or
equivalently common fixed points of the image of a subgroup.  For a subgroup
generated by basis letters this is exactly the common-fixed-point object of
the RPS history.  The current arXiv record is version 5, revised 2 February
2026.

Consequences for the claim ceiling:

- equation (2) of the gate is not sold as a new group-action object;
- the expected number of common fixed points is zero credit;
- free-group, word-measure, and random-homomorphism framing is zero credit.

The inspected paper studies fixed-point and stable-character statistics of
word images.  It does not mark the total cycle counts of the independent
generator images conditional on their exact labelled common fixed set, and it
does not state the sharp endpoint-conditioned range in gate equation (17).

### 2.2 One-permutation fixed-set law

Diaconis, Evans, and Graham,
[*Unseparated Pairs and Fixed Points in Random Permutations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.5459),
Advances in Applied Mathematics 61 (2014), 102--124, DOI
[`10.1016/j.aam.2014.05.006`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2014.05.006),
treats the random fixed-point set itself and uses inclusion--exclusion and
Markov constructions.  Diaconis, Fulman, and Guralnick,
[*On Fixed Points of Permutations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2643),
treats fixed-point/derangement laws in symmetric-group actions.

Consequences:

- `(n-s)!` for permutations fixing prescribed labels is zero credit;
- rencontres, derangements, exact one-epoch fixed-set probabilities, and
  their inclusion--exclusion are zero credit;
- Poisson fixed-point asymptotics are outside the retained theorem.

Neither inspected source defines the repeated intersection chain with its
endpoint-conditioned generator-cycle history polynomial.

### 2.3 Semigroup-walk spectrum

Brown,
[*Semigroups, Rings, and Markov Chains*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0006145),
Journal of Theoretical Probability 13 (2000), 871--938, DOI
[`10.1023/A:1007822931408`](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1007822931408),
develops eigenvalues, multiplicities, diagonalization, and projections for
left-regular-band walks.  Ayyer, Schilling, Steinberg, and Thiery,
[*Markov Chains, R-Trivial Monoids and Representation Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4250),
International Journal of Algebra and Computation 25 (2015), 169--231, DOI
[`10.1142/S0218196715400081`](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218196715400081),
gives lattice-Möbius spectral formulas, diagonalizability criteria, and
absorption/mixing tools for a wider monoid class.

RPS is multiplication by an i.i.d. random element of the commutative
idempotent semigroup `(2^[n], intersection)`.  Its Boolean-zeta eigenbasis,
Möbius inverse, eigenvalues, and generic hitting-transform manipulations
therefore receive no independent contribution credit.

### 2.4 Cycle polynomials

Cameron and Semeraro,
[*The Cycle Polynomial of a Permutation Group*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06954),
Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 25(1) (2018), P1.14, DOI
[`10.37236/7299`](https://doi.org/10.37236/7299), defines the cycle
polynomial `sum_g u^cyc(g)` and records its relationship to the multivariate
cycle index.  The symmetric-group identity

```text
sum_(pi in S_m) u^cyc(pi)=u(u+1)...(u+m-1)
```

and the prescribed-fixed-label factor in gate equation (15) are zero credit.
Recent cycle-type-restricted random-generation work also makes generic
interactions between fixed points and cycle type background rather than a
claim.

The bounded searches did not locate a source that conditions the total cycle
count of all independent sampled generators on an exact endpoint of the RPS
intersection, nor one stating equations (16)--(19).  That non-hit only
supports the internal owner-thin gate; it does not establish priority.
