## Literal identity found

For a relation (R\subseteq X\times Y), the row-containment relation used by the candidate is the standard relational residual

\[
 R\backslash R=\overline{R^{\mathsf T}\,\overline R}.
\]

Schmidt's *Relational Mathematics* calls the corresponding construction a column-containment preorder (transpose conventions exchange rows and columns).  Thus “an arbitrary relation induces a preorder by self-residuation” is fully owned background and receives zero contribution credit.

Primary bibliographic anchor:

- Gunther Schmidt, *Relational Mathematics*, Cambridge University Press, 2011, ISBN 978-0-521-76268-7.
