## 3. Sole survivor: exact theorem signal

The full derivation is in
[`QUADRATIC_STATE_SHEAR_REPORT.md`](QUADRATIC_STATE_SHEAR_REPORT.md).  In its
notation `N=2^(2m)` and `S=epsilon 2^m`.  The proved contract is:

1. `B(x,y)` is invariant and
   `(Q(x),Q(y),B(x,y)) -> (b,a(1+b+c),c)`;
2. every transient has depth at most `2`, and every eventual period belongs to
   `{1,2,3,4}`, with a pointwise classification including all short-period
   exceptions;
3. every target `(u,v)` has the exact fibre
   `{(v,u):Q(v)=0} union {(u+v,u):Q(u+v)=1}`;
4. the numbers of indegree `0,1,2` targets are respectively
   `N(N-1)/4`, `N(N+1)/2`, `N(N-1)/4`;
5. the depth layers are
   `N(5N-S+4)/8`, `N(N-1)/4`, `N(N+S-2)/8`;
6. `|im Phi|=N(3N+1)/4` and `im Phi^2` is exactly the recurrent set;
7. every component is one of six explicit decorated cycles, yielding exact
   counts of 1-, 2-, 3-, and 4-cycles and the finite-map zeta function.

Two proof routes are available.  The forward route uses the invariant polar
bit, the eight-state quotient, and short matrix words over `F_2`; a Walsh
transform counts every pair type.  The reverse route solves every preimage,
uses quadratic-hyperplane counts, and rebuilds all six component shapes from
the fibre tree.  These are not restatements of one induction.
