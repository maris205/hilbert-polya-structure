## 3. Strongest residual: D01 leftmost Dyck reassociation

### 3.1 Literal definition and exact profile

Write the unique prime decomposition of a Dyck path of semilength `n` as

```text
P=C_1 C_2 ... C_k,       C_1=U A D.
```

If `k=1`, fix `P`.  If `k>=2`, define

```text
Phi(P)=U A C_2 D C_3 ... C_k.                         (D01.1)
```

Thus the closing down-step of the first prime component crosses exactly the
next prime component.  This is one Tamari cover, always selected at the
leftmost ground return.

The verifier exhausts all **82,499** Dyck paths through semilength eleven.
Every orbit is absorbing; the observed maximum tail is ten, the fixed count
at `n=11` is `Cat_10=16,796`, and the following all-parameter formulas agree
at every tested state and every terminal target.

### 3.2 Proof skeleton and sharp witness

One induction gives, for `0<=t<k`,

```text
Phi^t(P)=U A C_2 ... C_(t+1) D C_(t+2) ... C_k.
```

Hence

```text
depth(P)=k-1.
```

The recurrent set is exactly the prime Dyck paths, all fixed, and therefore
has size `Cat_(n-1)`.  The unique path with `n` prime factors is

```text
(UD)^n,
```

so it is the unique deepest source and has depth `n-1`.

The number of Dyck paths of semilength `n` with exactly `k` prime factors is

```text
k/(2n-k) * binom(2n-k,n).
```

Consequently the full temporal polynomial is

```text
D_n(u)=sum_(k=1)^n [k/(2n-k) binom(2n-k,n)] u^(k-1).   (D01.2)
```

The formula follows either from the standard sequence construction of prime
Dyck paths or by Lagrange inversion.  Both are owner background.

### 3.3 Every-terminal inverse and extremum

Fix a recurrent target

```text
T=U Q D,
```

and let `Q=D_1...D_r` be the prime decomposition of its interior.  For each
`j=0,...,r`, there is exactly one source terminating at `T`:

```text
P_j=U(D_1...D_j)D (D_(j+1)...D_r).
```

It has depth `r-j`.  Conversely, (D01.1) shows that every source of `T` has
this form.  Therefore the complete terminal depth-fibre polynomial is

```text
B_T(u)=sum_(E(P)=T) u^depth(P)=1+u+...+u^r.            (D01.3)
```

Since `r<=n-1`, every terminal fibre has size at most `n`.  Equality uniquely
forces `Q=(UD)^(n-1)`, so

```text
T_max=U(UD)^(n-1)D
```

is the unique largest target and has `n` sources.

## 4. Raw second signal but permanent kill: T01 sibling-min cascade

### 4.1 Literal map and stepwise rank

For a rooted labelled tree `T` on `{0,...,n-1}`, root `0`, let `C_T(v)` be
the children of `v`.  If `C_T(v)` is nonempty, put `m_T(v)=min C_T(v)`.
Simultaneously set

```text
p'(x)=p(x)                    if x=m_T(p(x)),
p'(x)=m_T(p(x))               otherwise.                 (T01.1)
```

The moved vertex and the new parent were siblings in disjoint subtrees, so no
cycle is created and (T01.1) is again a rooted tree.

Let `rho(T)` be the length of the initial unary trunk: the largest `r` such
that the root begins a path of `r` edges and each of the first `r` vertices
has exactly one child along that path.  If `T` is not a path, the first
branching vertex on this trunk has all but its least child moved under that
least child.  Hence

```text
rho(Psi(T)) >= rho(T)+1
```

at every nonfixed step.  Fixed trees are exactly rooted paths, so all
recurrence is fixed and every orbit has depth at most `n-2`.  The rooted star

```text
p(1)=...=p(n-1)=0
```

gains exactly one unary-trunk edge per round and proves sharpness.

### 4.2 Endpoint and fibre derivation

Priority-first traversal starts at `0`, keeps the children of visited
vertices in a frontier, and repeatedly visits the least-labelled available
vertex.  Reparenting nonminimum siblings under their least sibling does not
change this order: that least sibling was necessarily visited before any of
the moved siblings, and immediately unblocks them again.  Thus the priority
order is invariant under every update.  As the limit is a path, its vertex
order is exactly this traversal.

Fix a target order

```text
v_0=0,v_1,...,v_(n-1).
```

For `k>=1`, define

```text
L_k=max({j:1<=j<k and v_j>v_k} union {0}).
```

If the parent of `v_k` is at order-position `p_k`, then `p_k<k`.  The vertex
`v_k` is exposed from step `p_k+1` onward; priority traversal visits all
intermediate vertices first exactly when they are smaller than `v_k`.
Equivalently,

```text
L_k <= p_k < k.
```

These parent choices are independent and always produce a rooted tree,
because parent positions strictly decrease.  Therefore

```text
|E^(-1)(v_0,...,v_(n-1))|=product_(k=1)^(n-1)(k-L_k).  (T01.2)
```

Each factor is at most `k`, with equality for every `k` exactly for the
increasing order.  Hence the increasing terminal path is the unique largest
target and has `(n-1)!` sources.  The verifier checks (T01.1)--(T01.2) for all
280,393 rooted trees through `n=8`.

### 4.3 Decisive owner transfer

This pointwise product is not an admissible residual.

- Priority-first traversal is classical algorithmic background; Prim's
  original paper is
  [*Shortest connection networks and some generalizations*](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1957.tb01515.x).
- Lillo and Rosas explicitly develop priority forests, priority traversal,
  parent maps, and a bijection to parking functions that preserves traversal
  as the parking outcome:
  [*The Priority Lattice*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28905).
- Pinsky gives the explicit product for **every** parking outcome and its
  extrema:
  [*The Distribution on Permutations Induced by a Random Parking Function*](https://doi.org/10.37236/13842).

Indeed, Pinsky's factor `l_i`, the longest consecutive block ending at
position `i` on which the last value is the maximum, is exactly `i-L_i`.
The Lillo--Rosas bijection transports his product to (T01.2), and both totals
are the same Cayley/parking number.  This is a proved theorem transfer, not a
keyword resemblance.  Searches for the literal simultaneous sibling
reparenting did not locate the repeated map, but after (T01.2) is subtracted
only the elementary unary-trunk clock remains.  Under the intake rule, T01 is
therefore a **permanent kill**.

The internal comparison reaches the same conservative result.  T01 is not
literally P114 leaf peeling, P120 fringe mirroring, or the killed grandparent
pointer jump: it keeps all vertices, ends at `(n-1)!` paths rather than one
star, and has a linear rather than logarithmic clock.  Those distinctions
prove nonidentity, not sufficient residual value.

## 5. Raw third signal but exact conjugacy kill: P01 Cartesian preorder

### 5.1 Literal map, clock, and inverse recursion

For a permutation word `w=L m R`, where `m=min(w)`, define

```text
C(empty)=empty,
C(w)=m C(L) C(R).                                      (P01.1)
```

This is preorder traversal of the min-Cartesian tree.  Every update grows the
initial identity prefix by at least one, so the unique recurrent state is
`12...n` and the depth is at most `n-1`.  Equality is attained exactly by

```text
n,1,sigma,       sigma in S_{2,...,n-1};
```

there are `(n-2)!` sharp sources for `n>=2`.

For a target word `y`, let `f(empty)=1`.  Its complete one-step fibre obeys

```text
f(y)=0,                                                   y_1!=min(y),
f(y)=sum_(c=1)^|y| f(y_2...y_c) f(y_(c+1)...y_|y|),      otherwise. (P01.2)
```

The cut chooses the left and right subwords around the minimum in a unique
source.  The increasing target has the Catalan fibre `Cat_n` and is the
unique maximum.  All claims replay through `S_9`.

### 5.2 Exact conjugacy

Let `J` reverse the word and complement every value, and let `s` be West's
stack-sorting map

```text
s(L M R)=s(L)s(R)M,        M=max(L M R).
```

Direct recursion gives

```text
s=J C J.                                                (P01.3)
```

The verifier checks (P01.3) on every one of the 409,113 permutations through
`S_9`.  West's map, its `n-1` iteration ceiling, sorted images, and preimage
theory are mature; see
[Bousquet-Melou, *Sorted and/or sortable permutations*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00146-1).
Vuillemin's primary Cartesian-tree source is
[*A unifying look at data structures*](https://doi.org/10.1145/358841.358852).

P01 is therefore **permanently killed by exact conjugacy**.  Formula (P01.2)
cannot be repackaged as a new Cartesian-tree inverse theorem.
