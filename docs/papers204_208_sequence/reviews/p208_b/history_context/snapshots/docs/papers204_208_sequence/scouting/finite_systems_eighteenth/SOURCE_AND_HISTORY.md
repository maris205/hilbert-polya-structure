## Procedure and scope

The project workflow controls this source-first desk. `research-lit` was
used for local-first discovery and web fallback; the ARS source-verification
route was used only for bounded source checking, not an external-model or
new research-to-paper pipeline. `proof-writer` shaped the exact deduction/
gap separation. No external review API, Zotero mutation, upload, manuscript
submission or contact was performed. Ordinary web bibliographic retrieval
sent only public mechanism/title queries, not private manuscript text.
Programmatic S2/OpenAlex/Crossref/arXiv resolver clients were not invoked;
the ARS router defaults to browser lookup for ordinary discovery. The arXiv
author record for the Moore paper was inspected through that fallback.

The scope was three preferred carrier areas: automata and partitions,
incidence and set systems, and terminating/commutation rewriting. We
screened six **comparison groups**, not six newly discovered maps. Immediate
old-mechanism hits were not renamed or executed. Recent algebra-derivative,
rank/count, polygon flip, and local-priority families were not explored.
Lanes16 CNL/D2LC/LRG/LIR/LMH/KFR and lane17 Viète/Newton/Hessian,
permanental-gradient transpose, depth-to-Prüfer and LZ78 exclusions were
accepted as exclusions, not reintroduced as controls.
