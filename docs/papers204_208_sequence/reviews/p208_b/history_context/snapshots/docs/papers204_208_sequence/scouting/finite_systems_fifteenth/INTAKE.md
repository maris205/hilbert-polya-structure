## Mechanisms deducted before code

**ADG/LPG.** For squarefree $d$ and $p\mid d$, the sum
$d'=\sum_{q\mid d}d/q$ has exactly one term not divisible by $p$.
Thus $\gcd(d,d')=1$. LPG is constant one. For ADG adjacent divisor
supports are disjoint, $T_N(1)=N$ and $T_N(N)=1$. For $N>1$ this gives
a two-cycle and rules out fixed points, but it does not prove that every
orbit joins that cycle or has period two. If $p\mid N$ but $p\nmid d$,
then

$$p\mid T_N(d)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \sum_{q\mid d}q^{-1}=0\quad\text{in }\mathbf F_p.$$

This exact primewise predicate and the input-support exclusion are
generic arithmetic/CRT background. A product of such predicates is only
an inverse encoding, not an evaluated target-fibre or extremal formula.
The Fermat-quotient operator from old APD is explicitly different from
the Leibniz arithmetic derivative.

**FID.** Distinct incomparable sets have intersection strictly smaller
than each. Every nonempty output therefore has smaller maximum member
size. For $n\ge2$ any antichain with at least two members has largest
size at most $n-1$, giving termination within $n$ arrows; the level
family of all $(n-1)$-subsets attains that bound through successive lower
levels and then the empty family. At $n=0,1$ the maximum depth is one.
This is exactly the old DI maximal-layer proof, with the antichain
boundary tightened; it is not a new time mechanism or a valued second axis.

**FSD.** Before maximalization, the operation is literally old SX.
Its usual first-image inclusion proof uses the two witnesses $a+c,b+c$
for a difference $a+b$; maximalization can discard those witnesses, so
the old increasing-span theorem is not automatically transferred. Every
output is still a binary sum of old members and its ground support is
contained in the old support. For at least two old members that ground
support is exactly the union minus the intersection of the old members.
These elementary shadows receive no new time credit. No generic full
temporal identity or all-target inverse/extremum is asserted before pilot.

**USS.** Its coefficient at degree $k$ is $a_k$ plus a polynomial in
$a_1,\ldots,a_{k-1}$: the term indexed by $k$ contributes $a_k$,
and every smaller index uses only smaller coefficients. Successive
coefficient recovery proves bijection, zero tails and singleton fibres.
The resulting finite triangular permutation action is already a complete
low-credit inverse mechanism, irrespective of its cycle census.

**WCF.** For two units,

$$T(f)-T(g)=\frac{-x(f-g)}{(1+xf)(1+xg)}.$$

The denominator is a unit, so the order of a nonzero difference increases
by exactly one until it is truncated away. There is a unique fixed unit
$h$ determined by $h+xh^2=1$, and its coefficients are signed Catalan
numbers reduced modulo $p$. The exact precision clock and complete
one-step inverse (recover $f$ below degree $m$ from $(y^{-1}-1)/x$,
leave its degree-$m$ coefficient free) are generic contraction/forgetful
shift mechanisms. The $m=0$ singleton boundary is separate. No pilot.
