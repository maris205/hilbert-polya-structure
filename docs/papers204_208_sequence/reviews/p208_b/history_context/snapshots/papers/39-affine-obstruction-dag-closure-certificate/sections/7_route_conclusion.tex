\subsection{Registry handoff}

The historical registry predicate has six source-locked witnesses,
\code{SD-C01} through \code{SD-C06}.  These entries were already evaluated.
Their role in Paper 39 is only to establish that the historically existing
registry is nonempty.  Consequently, the realized terminal is
\begin{center}
\code{RETURN\_CONTROL\_TO\_PREEXISTING\_GLOBAL\_CANDIDATE\_REGISTRY}.
\end{center}
No ordering, score, proposal, or reclassification is attached to the handoff.
It does not assert that one of the six entries is an unevaluated live candidate.

The mutually exclusive empty-registry guard carries the conditional code
\begin{center}
\code{STOP\_NO\_SOURCE\_LOCKED\_NON\_AFFINE\_SUCCESSOR}.
\end{center}
That guard is inactive in the frozen snapshot.  The predecessor text does not
define the phrase “unspent successor,” so Paper 39 does not manufacture such a
predicate.

\subsection{Limitations}

The closure theorem should be read with six limitations.
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Retrospective universe.}  The encoding was assembled after the
  predecessor outcomes; checker freeze proves reproducibility, not
  outcome-independent selection.
  \item \textbf{Finite request scope.}  The 16 tokens exclude arbitrary
  instances, unlisted compounds, and other readings of the 14 class labels.
  \item \textbf{Inherited mathematics.}  Source-owned predecessor theorems keep
  their own hypotheses, which Paper 39 does not re-prove.
  \item \textbf{Category exits.}  Changed objects may be productive; EXIT says
  only that they are not the frozen candidate.
  \item \textbf{Snapshot governance.}  Historical registry nonemptiness and its
  handoff authorize nothing.
  \item \textbf{Qualified novelty.}  The bounded search found no exact
  combination, while every generic ingredient has prior art.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conclusion}

Four heterogeneous negative studies support a branch decision only after their
objects, markers, owners, and exits are typed separately.  The retrospective
14-class/16-token encoding, 22-node/28-edge proof graph, six-node spine, and
endpoint classifier close that encoded branch without treating EXIT as
failure.  Nothing larger follows: Paper 40 requires a newly source-locked
non-affine object, which neither the DAG nor the registry handoff selects.
