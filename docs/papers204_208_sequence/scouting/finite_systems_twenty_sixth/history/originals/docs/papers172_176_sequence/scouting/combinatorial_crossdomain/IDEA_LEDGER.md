image/fixed/fibre statistics, and map-specific identities.  Focused programs
then independently check the three most tempting signals:

  image DP, every-target fibre formula, and strict convergence potential;
- adjacent-sum reranking: complete functional graphs through `S_9` and the
  explicit principal `2n` orbit through rank 100.
