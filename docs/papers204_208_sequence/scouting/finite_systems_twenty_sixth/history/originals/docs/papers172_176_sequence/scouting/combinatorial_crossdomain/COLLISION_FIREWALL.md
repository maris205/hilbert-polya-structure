## Within-lane distinctness checks

The 22 entries are different literal maps, not parameter variants counted
multiple times.  In particular:

- graph and 3-uniform-hypergraph states use different carriers even when the
  `n=5` signatures of `G03_DEC` and `H02_PCP` happen to coincide;
- the two tournament maps reverse different, state-defined arc sets;
- the three set-family maps respectively discard to a dual antichain, close
  Boolean intervals, and generate left exchanges;
- the two permutation maps use unrelated adjacent-score and Cartesian-tree
  sufficient statistics; and
- the three word maps respectively use bracket cancellation, LZ parsing, and
  a dynamic alphabet list.

An equal finite signature is not treated as a conjugacy, while a different
signature is not treated as proof of meaningful novelty.

## Strongest false-positive controls

1. **Cartesian BFS:** complete sharp clock and fibre theorem, but killed by
   P142 proof transfer.
2. **Bracket support:** exact fibre and convergence theorems, but the static
   axis is P74-owned and the residual clock is not sharp.
3. **Adjacent-sum reranking:** a genuinely different exact `2n` orbit, but
   `S_9` refutes the simple recurrent classification and no inverse axis
   exists.

`T01_UTR` is the most lively unanalysed raw literal update, but it is not
promoted into this top three because no all-rank theorem has been proved.

## Kill switch

No row may be assigned a paper number from this package.  Re-entry requires a
new theorem conjunction that survives the specific collision above, followed
by a fresh primary-owner search.  A bounded source non-hit, a renamed carrier,
or a larger exhaustive box does not satisfy that condition.
