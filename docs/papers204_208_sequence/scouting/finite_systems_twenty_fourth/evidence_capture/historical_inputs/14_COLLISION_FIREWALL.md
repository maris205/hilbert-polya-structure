statistic and inverse/counting engine.  A shared carrier or generic trace

quotient, or labelled lift does not create a new system.

## Why the composition candidate is killed

`C03_PDM` is not literally P147 or P191, but its residual package is consumed
by the forbidden standard shell:

- delete old cuts monotonically;
- merge adjacent violators in parallel;
- stop exactly at a sorted/nondecreasing composition;
- count predecessors by refinements of target parts.

P147 already occupies adjacent-run coalescence and target-refinement fibres;
P191 already occupies monotone composition-cut deletion, a sharp nearly
linear clock, a unique travelling defect, and target path inversion.  Changing
the cut predicate to `a_i>a_(i+1)` produces a clean theorem but not enough
proof separation.  It is therefore killed rather than used to fill a paper
slot.
