## 5. JCA: ordinary/Hadamard inversion and full static adjugate adapter

Korepanov's
[*Exact solution for a matrix dynamical system with usual and Hadamard inverses*](https://arxiv.org/html/nlin/0303007v2)
was actually read through Sections 1--4, including the 21-variable
multiplicative basis and explicit three-step-multiple formula for
three-by-three matrices. It studies $E\circ I$ on its regular complex
domain. Our direct identity
$E(\operatorname{adj}A)=\det(A)^{-1}E(A^{-1})$ identifies JCA's
projective regular action. Generic inverse/gauge dynamics receives no
novelty credit. This is not a claim that the source solves every
zero-containing finite matrix.

Iyudu and Shkarin's
[*The proof of the Kontsevich periodicity conjecture on noncommutative birational transformations*](https://preprints.ihes.fr/storage/KonjArxiv_.pdf)
was read in its introduction, domain definitions, Theorem 1.1 and
Proposition 1.2 (PDF pages 1--4); no full 28-page proof read is claimed.
The action is ordinary inverse followed by transposed entrywise inverse;
on symmetric matrices the transpose has no effect. Its third iterate
is identity only modulo left/right invertible-diagonal action on the
specified natural domain. The stabilized domain requires all square
submatrices of the matrix and its entrywise-transposed inverse to be
invertible. This does not extend through our zero-preserving totalization.
The source is therefore deducted on its actual domain, not used to
invent an all-prime full-carrier clock.

The separate exact identity
$T^{-1}(B)=\operatorname{adj}^{-1}(E(B))$ consumes the entire
one-step inverse axis even outside that regular domain. Its full
rank/determinant count is derived in the proof notes. It is a target
relabelling, not an independent inverse mechanism.
