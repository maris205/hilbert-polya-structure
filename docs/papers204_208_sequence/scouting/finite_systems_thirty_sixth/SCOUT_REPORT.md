code. If $R(x)$ is its ordered run-length composition and
$\Psi(b_1,\ldots,b_k)=b_1^{b_1}\cdots b_k^{b_k}$, then

$$F=\Psi R,\qquad R\Psi=M,\qquad F\Psi=\Psi M,$$

where $M$ merges every maximal adjacent group of equal masses. $\Psi$ is
injective. Every merger at epoch $t\geq2$ has an input created at epoch
$t-1$, so the resulting mass is at least $2^t$. Geometric compositions,
with a separate placement of the residual $2^{k-1}$, make the resulting
word-time bound sharp at every size.

the $n=1,2$ exceptions are included. This is fully evaluated, but it has
