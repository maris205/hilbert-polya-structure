## One literal: mutual transport of two partial matchings (MRT)

identity. Identify $a$ with its partial matching on the labelled set $[n]$:
transpositions are matched pairs, fixed points are unmatched vertices. The
total finite carrier is $X_n=I_n\times I_n$. Use permutation composition
right to left. All updates use the same old pair:

$$T_n(a,b)=(bab,aba).$$

involution, and transport the second matching's edges by the old first.
Conjugation preserves involutions, so the carrier is invariant, including
