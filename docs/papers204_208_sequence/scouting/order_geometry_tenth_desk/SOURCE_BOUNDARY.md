   74(3), 1319–1363 (2024), DOI
   [10.5802/aif.3641](https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.3641), with primary
   [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08124).
   Actual read: introduction and relation (1.2), Section 2.1's regular
   domain, Section 3.2's recutting definitions, and the quadrilateral
   Theorem 5.5 statement and proof context in Section 5.2. These specify
   a side-determinant-preserving correspondence over real/complex regular

   synchronous QAS map. The QAS diamond in the proof changes side
   determinants from 1 to 9 and collapses in characteristic three, so
   direct transfer fails. This excludes that literal identification, not
   every possible nonlinear factor or birational relation.

   [author PDF](https://encov.ip.uca.fr/publications/pubfiles/2021_Arango_etal_GMOD_area.pdf)
   and [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.12074).
   Actual read: introduction, problem definition and Sections 2.1–2.2,

## OFS primary sources and exact deductions

   [arXiv:2501.10311v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.10311v1), 2025;

   A composition with rotation or another relabelling remains a distinct,

2. Mansour, *Statistics on Dyck paths*, Journal of Integer Sequences 9
   (2006), primary [journal PDF](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL9/Mansour/mansour86.pdf).
   Actual read: Section 2.1 definitions and Proposition 2.1's first-return
   proof, the Theorem 2.5 statement/proof context, and Corollary 2.1 with
   its explicit UUDU specialization. The displayed specialization counts
   UUDU-avoiding Dyck paths by
   `C(z/((1-z)(1+z)^2))/(1-z^2)`. The desk independently derives the same
   series for a specified forbidden-subtree class. This is old static
   enumeration; it is not an OFS temporal or inverse theorem.

   Source inconsistency: Table 1 prints 10 and 26 in its alpha_2 zero
   column at semilengths four and five, while the displayed formula gives
   9 and 22. The discrepancy is visible in the actual rendered PDF page
   retained as `sources/mansour_table.png`; that page was actually viewed,
   so this is not merely text-extraction damage. The table is excluded
   from all deductions. Only the explicitly reconstructed class equation
   is used. No other PDF page is labelled visually reviewed here.

4. Sapounakis, Tasoulas and Tsikouras, *Ordered trees and the inorder

   [10.1016/j.disc.2006.03.044](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2006.03.044),
