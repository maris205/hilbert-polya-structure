opinions; finite/discrete Steiner compression; maximal empty rectangles;
iterated Voronoi or lattice nearest-site tie loci; well-composed cubical

The search is bounded author scouting, not an exhaustive latest-literature
survey. It finds no basis for admitting any of the six.
