# Fresh nonlinear algebra scout and kill ledger

**Scope:** eighteen deterministic finite systems, not eighteen parameter values.
Sixteen pass the intake exclusions; two idempotent retractions are retained
only as explicit negative controls and are not counted toward the required
non-closure breadth.  
**Exclusions enforced:** no valuation/multiplicity erosion, pure power map,
incidence-linear map, or order-theoretic closure candidate is eligible to
survive.  
**External state:** `HOLD_EXTERNAL`.  A bounded owner-search miss is never a
novelty statement.  
**Allocation:** no paper number is assigned here.

Throughout, (\langle-,-\rangle) is the standard dot product, (D(A)) is
the diagonal matrix with the diagonal of (A), (U(A),L(A)) are its strict
upper and lower parts, and (C_A(t)) is the (2\times2) companion matrix of
the characteristic polynomial of (A).

## Gate result

- Systems tested: **18 genuinely different literal updates**, of which **16**
  qualify after excluding the two retraction negative controls.
- Exact assertions in the canonical run: **517,353**.
- Theorem-complete but internally killed: **V02**.
- Sole internal survivor: **M01**.
- External release/novelty state for every item: **HOLD**.

The one-survivor outcome is intentional.  V02 is not promoted merely to fill
a second slot, and none of the sixteen other pilots is converted into a
paper-shaped claim by parameter extension.
