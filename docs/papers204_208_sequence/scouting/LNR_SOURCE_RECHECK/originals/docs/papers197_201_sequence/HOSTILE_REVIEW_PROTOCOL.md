# Hostile review and repair protocol — candidate batch after P196

Each selected paper receives two process-separated reviews after its author
verifier and Round-0 PDF are frozen.

## Review A

- independently reconstruct the literal map and complete small functional
  graphs;
- attack theorem hypotheses, boundary parameters, tail conventions, fibre
  orientation, image iff statements, and mass identities;
- audit every bibliography record and the P1--P196 subtraction boundary;
- classify findings as Critical, Major, or Minor and emit an exact delta
  record;
- if a repair is required, the same reviewer must accept or reject the repaired
  delta before Round 1 freezes.

## Review B

- start from frozen Round-1 inputs with exact SHA-256 pins;
- use a representation and proof route materially different from the author
  and Review-A implementations;
- rederive the central theorem package and replay at least two byte
  comparisons against its canonical transcript;
- independently inspect source/owner boundaries and PDF/build surfaces;
- emit an accepted no-change or repair delta before Round 2 freezes.

## Terminal rule

No paper reaches terminal PASS unless both review packages contain one
verifier, one canonical transcript, an exact zero-open-finding census, complete
input pins, proof/source/build records, an accepted delta, a non-self manifest,
and successful root replay.  All internal passes preserve
`OWNER_AMBER / HOLD_EXTERNAL`.
