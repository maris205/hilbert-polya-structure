## Tentative slate, not a precommitted execution contract

1. DGR: integer partitions of N. Replace each positive adjacent gap
   `lambda_i-lambda_(i+1)` by i copies of that gap and sort. The intended
   mass identity is `sum i*(lambda_i-lambda_(i+1))=N`. This factors through
   conjugation followed by part/multiplicity exchange with merging, so
   those standard primitives require deduction. No sharp clock or
   independent extremal contract has been proved or tested.
2. DTD: upper-triangular relations on `[n]`, `R -> R^2 \ R` for Boolean
   composition. This is a directed distance-two/metamour-type restriction;
   minimum edge span doubles, exposing the old filtration clock. It is not
   a proven fresh mechanism merely because its literal was not located.
3. BPA: full Boolean n-by-n matrices; transposed support of permanental
   `(n-1)`-minors. The n=1 empty minor convention would be one. Ordinary
   field adjugation (P103), matching/alternating paths and Boolean closure
   must be deducted; no complete candidate proof has been written here.
4. BRF: all self-maps of `{1,...,n}`; send vertex i to the cardinality of
   `{j: f^k(j)=i for some k>=0}`. Count includes i and is in `[1,n]`.
   Rename from tentative BRC, since the current graph lane already uses
   BRC for `R compose complement(R)`. The old OS forward-orbit-cardinality
   map is not this update, but related statistic-feedback ownership remains.
5. FFR: ordered item sizes in `[0,M]^n`; first-fit in capacity M, then
   replace each item by the final unused capacity of its assigned bin.
   Bins open in order, zero items fit the first existing bin, and a first
   zero item opens a bin. No literal pilot or recurrence proof exists.
6. GFC: graphs on `[n]`; retain a lexicographically greedily selected forest,
   then take its complement in `E(K_n)`. The greedy-basis inverse has a
   standard external-activity/Boolean-interval description. Earlier GCM
   complementary path matchings are closely related but not this literal.
