## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Fully ordered gates with typed inputs, outputs, and stop conditions; separate orbit-tail, rank, quadrature, roundoff, and geometry or roof uncertainties; a justified common norm and stability map; determinant conditioning; and independently replayable enclosure evidence.
what_triggers_block: A central determinant or validation claim would rely on materially invalid error aggregation, an unjustified operator-to-determinant stability step, or a missing gate that requires substantial reconstruction of the rigorous-numerics design.
what_triggers_warn: The validation strategy would remain viable, but one or more uncertainty dependencies, norm conversions, conditioning assumptions, stopping rules, or replay obligations would be incompletely specified.
what_triggers_fatal: A single unrecoverable defect in the core operator definition or error-to-determinant map would make the claimed validated object mathematically undefined or incapable of supporting the central conclusion.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A noncircular argument that preserves the distinction between roof-agnostic internal agreement, physical-model identification, validated enclosure, and the precise scope of any determinant-based conclusion.
what_triggers_block: A central conclusion would be supported only by internal agreement that cannot establish physical identity, or by a serious inferential conflation among calibration, validation, and model adequacy requiring substantial reframing.
what_triggers_warn: The main inferential chain would survive, but the scope of a calibration result, a conditional dependency, or the boundary between numerical evidence and physical interpretation would need sharper qualification.
what_triggers_fatal: A single irreparable circular identification of the constructed calibration object with the physical target would invalidate the central thesis or make acceptance impossible.
