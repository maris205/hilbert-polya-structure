## Overall assessment

The report develops a disciplined type system for an equilateral three-disk transfer-determinant program. Its strongest editorial contribution is the separation of roof-internal Euler/trace/determinant calibration from independent physical-roof fidelity and theorem-licensed cross-roof nontransfer. The exact four-part error budget and the explicit statement that a common cutoff label cannot create an orbit-to-projection coefficient map are also valuable safeguards.

The current manuscript nevertheless remains a programmatic evidence synthesis rather than an established determinant result or a complete methods paper. The central roof-agnostic calibration statement is not formulated with enough mathematical hypotheses to be evaluated as a general proposition; the exact `d=6a` object-to-operator chain is unbound; originality has not been assessed; and all theorem-adjacent source use lacks stable passage locators. The appropriate editorial disposition is major revision, not rejection, because the report consistently discloses these limits and does not misreport an executed physical determinant.

## Findings and required revisions

### Critical findings

None. The report clearly labels every physical, operator, determinant, numerical, and cohomological endpoint as unexecuted.

### Major findings

#### `P30-EIC-001` — The roof-agnostic calibration claim needs an exact theorem surface

**Exact section evidence:** Section 2.3 states that Euler, trace-exponential, and determinant expressions organize the same roof and repetition weights “when the operator theorem applies”; Sections 4.2 and 8 elevate this to the clearest synthesis finding.

**Editorial consequence:** Without a declared base system, roof regularity, potential, operator space, determinant convention, and convergence domain, the statement ranges from a formal identity to a nontrivial analytic theorem. The present wording does not identify which level is claimed.

**Required revision:** State a precise proposition or comparison schema with all hypotheses, object types, and domains. Separate purely formal coefficient identities from analytic Fredholm-determinant identities and from finite numerical calibration tests. If no theorem is proved, recast the statement explicitly as a design principle.

#### `P30-EIC-002` — The six gates are not yet connected to the exact `d=6a` object

**Exact section evidence:** Sections 2.2 and 4.1 state that the exact collision section, branches, pointwise roof, regularity, normalization, function space, and serialization path remain unnamed; Section 7 confirms that none of these objects was constructed or selected.

**Editorial consequence:** A list of appropriate components is not yet a reproducible method for the frozen billiard. The article cannot currently support an implementation-readiness or determinant-availability claim.

**Required revision:** Supply a self-contained theorem-to-object map for the precise three-disk geometry, including every hypothesis and explicit stop condition. For a methods-paper presentation, add a separately authorized worked certificate or minimal non-target demonstrator that exercises the interfaces without claiming the final scientific endpoint.

#### `P30-EIC-003` — The evidence-synthesis protocol is not independently reproducible

**Exact section evidence:** Section 3 reports 68 manifestations, 52 unique screened records, and 26 admissions, but does not include query strings, interface-level dates, complete eligibility decisions, or the source-effect coding form in the report.

**Editorial consequence:** Readers cannot reproduce the corpus or assess whether the claimed gap in roundoff or object-specific theorem coverage is a search artifact.

**Required revision:** Provide an immutable methods supplement with the complete search, deduplication, screening, verification, correction-binding, and matrix-coding records. State how the bounded search limits each gap claim.

#### `P30-EIC-004` — Originality and article type are unresolved

**Exact section evidence:** The abstract calls the six-gate synthesis the central finding, while Section 7 and the ledger state that novelty was not assessed and that no scientific endpoint was evaluated.

**Editorial consequence:** The draft cannot yet be judged as an original research article, a methods protocol, or a perspective because its claimed contribution is not positioned against the nearest prior workflows.

**Required revision:** Conduct a separately authorized contribution comparison and choose one article type. Align the title, abstract, claims, and evaluation standard with that type; do not imply a determinant result if the contribution remains a fail-closed protocol.

#### `P30-EIC-005` — Passage-level theorem applicability remains uncleared

**Exact section evidence:** Sections 2.2–2.5 rely on open-billiard, Fredholm, spectral-approximation, and Livšic sources while repeatedly conditioning their use on exact hypotheses; Section 7 and the verification limitation state that all citations are `anchor:none` and full applicability proofs were unavailable.

**Editorial consequence:** The sources are relevant, but the report cannot yet support the specific transfers on which the six-gate design rests.

**Required revision:** Bind each gate to exact source passages and record the required hypotheses, the exact P30 object field satisfying each hypothesis, and any unclosed condition. Narrow any claim whose passage-level support cannot be secured.
