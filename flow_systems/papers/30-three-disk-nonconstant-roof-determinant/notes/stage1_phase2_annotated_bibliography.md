### Interfaces and authoritative endpoints

1. Crossref REST metadata and DOI resolver for exact title/author/venue/DOI resolution.
2. Publisher and journal interfaces: AIP Publishing, American Physical Society, IOPscience, Elsevier/ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, American Mathematical Society, Johns Hopkins University Press, Annals of Mathematics, Wiley, and Centre Mersenne/Numdam.
3. Primary repositories: Project Euclid, EuDML, arXiv, institutional repositories, and author-hosted primary manuscripts when a journal or DOI record was identifiable.
4. A general web index was used only as a discovery layer with domain restrictions. Search results, blogs, encyclopedias, commercial summaries, and citation aggregators were excluded from the source corpus.

Scopus and Web of Science were not available as auditable result sets. Semantic Scholar identifier reconciliation was not used to make a source-verification verdict in this pass; exact DOI and normalized title/author/year deduplication was used instead. This is a retrieval limitation, not a novelty inference.

### Search limits

- Date coverage: database inception through **2026-09-02**.
- Language: English.
- Eligible document types: original peer-reviewed articles, formal/primary corrections, primary preprints when necessary to bind a correction, and one directly relevant research proceedings article.
- Currency: 2021–2026 work was queried explicitly. Older sources were retained when they originated the three-disk construction, symbolic/suspension formalism, nuclear/Fredholm framework, numerical bound, or Livšic obstruction used by the frozen interface.
- Stopping rule: stop after every required coverage cell had at least two plausible sources where available, at least 15 unique records remained after screening, the peer-reviewed share exceeded 60%, and two successive refinements produced only duplicates, secondary material, or results not applicable to the frozen typed interface.

### A priori inclusion and exclusion criteria

Include a record if it supplied a primary or authoritative basis for at least one of:

1. the three-disk/n-disk scattering geometry, collision coding, periodic orbit, Poincaré/return map, or geometrical flight length/time;
2. suspension/Axiom-A/open-billiard transfer operators with nonconstant weights/roofs, nuclear or trace-class machinery, dynamical zeta functions, or Fredholm determinants;
3. periodic-orbit/cycle expansions and their algebraic relation to traces/determinants;
4. rigorous finite-rank, spectral, coefficient, truncation, or determinant-evaluation error control; or
5. Livšic periodic-orbit obstructions and cohomology/nontransfer criteria for roof functions or cocycles.

Exclude a record if it concerned unrelated astronomical “scattered disks,” a closed billiard with no transferable interface, a secondary tutorial/blog, purely empirical resonance fitting without a defined roof/operator, a quantum determinant presented as interchangeable with the frozen classical typed determinant, or any source use that would require computing a target, tuning a cutoff on the target, or treating internal control inconsistency as the physical test.

- Coverage: connects the same three-disk periodic orbits and their physical actions/lengths to semiclassical spectral constructions.
- Claim-support boundary: a semiclassical spectral function is not automatically the frozen classical nonconstant-roof transfer determinant.
- Mandatory correction companion: formula- or quotation-level use must also bind https://doi.org/10.1063/1.457669; the notice separately corrects P30-S02. It does not change the classical-versus-semiclassical type boundary.

### Theme T: nonconstant-roof transfer operators, nuclearity, and determinants

**P30-S11.** Stoyanov, L. (2001). Spectrum of the Ruelle operator and exponential decay of correlations for open billiard flows. *American Journal of Mathematics, 123*(4), 715–759. https://doi.org/10.1353/ajm.2001.0029

- Coverage: the most direct rigorous open-billiard transfer-operator source in the corpus, with symbolic coding and return-time weights for obstacle flows.
- Claim-support boundary: later verification must check geometric hypotheses, normalization, roof convention, and function space against the exact three-disk object before transfer.

**P30-S12.** Stoyanov, L. (2011). Spectra of Ruelle transfer operators for Axiom A flows. *Nonlinearity, 24*(4), 1089–1120. https://doi.org/10.1088/0951-7715/24/4/005

- Coverage: supplies spectral estimates for transfer operators associated with Axiom-A flows and nontrivial return-time weights.
- Claim-support boundary: estimates are hypothesis-dependent and do not choose Paper 30's discretization, rank cutoff, orbit cutoff, or coefficient window.

**P30-S13.** Ruelle, D. (1976). Zeta-functions for expanding maps and Anosov flows. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 34*(3), 231–242. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01403069

- Coverage: foundational periodic-orbit zeta and transfer-operator relation for expanding maps/Anosov flows.
- Claim-support boundary: the algebra applies to consistently defined roofs/weights generally; therefore unit and shuffled controls may also be internally consistent. It is not physical-specificity evidence.

**P30-S14.** Ruelle, D. (1990). An extension of the theory of Fredholm determinants. *Publications Mathématiques de l’IHÉS, 72*, 175–193. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02699133

**P30-S15.** Pollicott, M. (1986). Meromorphic extensions of generalised zeta functions. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 85*(1), 147–164. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01388795

**P30-S16.** Fried, D. (1995). Meromorphic zeta functions for analytic flows. *Communications in Mathematical Physics, 174*(1), 161–190. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02099469

- Coverage: constructs trace-class/nuclear operator machinery and finite-rank approximation ideas for analytic hyperbolic flows and their zeta functions.
- Claim-support boundary: analyticity and model hypotheses must be matched before use; norm approximability alone does not instantiate the four frozen error terms.

**P30-S17.** Giulietti, P., Liverani, C., & Pollicott, M. (2013). Anosov flows and dynamical zeta functions. *Annals of Mathematics, 178*(2), 687–773. https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2013.178.2.6

- Coverage: modern functional-analytic treatment of transfer operators and meromorphic dynamical zeta functions for smooth Anosov flows.

**P30-S18.** Giulietti, P., Pollicott, M., & Liverani, C. (2022). *Anosov flows and dynamical zeta functions (Errata)* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04917

### Theme N: finite-rank, coefficient, and determinant error control

**P30-S19.** Bandtlow, O. F., & Jenkinson, O. (2007). Explicit a priori bounds on transfer operator eigenvalues. *Communications in Mathematical Physics, 276*(3), 901–905. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-007-0355-7

- Coverage: explicit eigenvalue bounds for transfer operators, relevant to tail and truncation control on appropriate holomorphic spaces.
- Claim-support boundary: the required space and constants must be instantiated before the bound can enter Paper 30's numerical contract.

**P30-S20.** Bandtlow, O. F., & Jenkinson, O. (2008). Explicit eigenvalue estimates for transfer operators acting on spaces of holomorphic functions. *Advances in Mathematics, 218*(3), 902–925. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2008.02.005

- Claim-support boundary: it does not guarantee that the three-disk coded operator acts on the assumed holomorphic domain or that the chosen basis realizes the bound.

**P30-S21.** Wormell, C. (2019). Spectral Galerkin methods for transfer operators in uniformly expanding dynamics. *Numerische Mathematik, 142*(2), 421–463. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00211-019-01031-z

- Coverage: rigorous spectral-Galerkin approximation framework with convergence/error analysis for transfer operators.
- Claim-support boundary: the map class, basis, projection, and norm must be fixed prospectively; results cannot be transplanted to an open-billiard operator without a legal comparison map.

**P30-S22.** Bornemann, F. (2010). On the numerical evaluation of Fredholm determinants. *Mathematics of Computation, 79*(270), 871–915. https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-09-02280-7

### Theme L: Livšic cohomology and nontransfer tests

**P30-S23.** Livšic, A. N. (1972). Cohomology of dynamical systems. *Mathematics of the USSR-Izvestiya, 6*(6), 1278–1301. https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1972v006n06ABEH001919
