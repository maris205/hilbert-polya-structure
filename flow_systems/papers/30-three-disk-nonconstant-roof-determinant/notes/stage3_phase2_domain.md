## Review Body

The paper maintains a strong type firewall among the physical billiard flow, symbolic suspension, semiclassical orbit constructions, exact-quantum determinants, and a prospective classical transfer determinant. It also keeps all operator, enclosure, fidelity, cohomology, and novelty conclusions explicitly unexecuted. The remaining D2 concerns are localized: the primitive-cycle equivalence convention is not fully declared, and two correction companions are cited only by DOI rather than as independently resolvable bibliography records.

### S1: Shared orbit vocabulary is not treated as object identity

The manuscript consistently resists transferring formulas or conclusions merely because several constructions use the same three-disk geometry or periodic rays.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §1 Typed claim graph "Similar notation and a shared orbit vocabulary do not erase these differences."

### S2: Error channels are kept mathematically typed

The separation of geometry and roof-input uncertainty from four numerical approximation channels is domain-appropriate, and the paper refuses to add heterogeneous bounds without connecting norms and stability maps.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §2 Error components "Without these links, even individually small terms cannot be called additive, exhaustive, or complete."

### S3: Livšic-type inference is correctly directional

The manuscript limits mismatch reasoning to a predeclared relation after hypothesis matching and does not promote finite periodic agreement into a global cohomology statement.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §2 Directional Livšic reasoning "Finite agreement does not establish the equality for all required periodic orbits."

### W1: Primitive cycle ownership needs a complete equivalence convention

**Problem**: The paper calls realized primitive cyclic collision words the prospective owners, but it does not state whether equality quotients only cyclic rotation or also time reversal, disk-label symmetries, or a symmetry-reduced coding. Those choices affect orbit multiplicities and determinant coefficients.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §1 Introduction "Prospective owners are realized primitive cyclic collision words; traversal powers are repetitions."

**Why it matters**: A pointwise physical roof can be correct while the periodic-orbit ledger still double-counts or merges cycles under an unstated equivalence policy.

**Suggestion**: Freeze labeled versus symmetry-reduced coding, cyclic-shift equivalence, time-reversal treatment, and the map from each accepted code class to one realized physical periodic orbit.
