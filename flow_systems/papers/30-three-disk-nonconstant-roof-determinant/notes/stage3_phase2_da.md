## Review Body

### Genuine Strengths

- **S1 — Strong firewall between internal agreement and physical identity.** The manuscript correctly refuses to let consistent Euler, trace, and determinant expressions identify an unconstructed roof as physical. Evidence Anchor: text: §Typed claim graph and falsifiability "Each level can reveal an implementation inconsistency, but none by itself identifies the roof as the Euclidean-flight roof"
- **S2 — Dependency-aware uncertainty framing.** Geometry uncertainty, truncation, projection, evaluation, and roundoff are not declared independent or combined before a common norm and propagation argument exist. Evidence Anchor: text: §Prospective composition of the five error channels "The architecture prescribes neither numerical independence nor quadrature of errors."

### Strongest Counter-Argument

The strongest counter-position is that the six-gate program remains a carefully worded requirements checklist for an object that has not yet been specified closely enough to test. The paper fixes the geometry and physical clock, but it supplies no collision section, pointwise roof, regularity class, operator space, coefficient map, complex domain, norm, or conditioning quantity. Consequently, every downstream gate is populated by fields that a future project should provide, not by a present theorem showing that the proposed dependency chain is attainable or distinctive for the three-disk flow. The error ledger is sensible, yet listing five channels and forbidding premature addition does not establish a composition theorem; many validated-computation pipelines would already require stability, dependence control, and conditioning. Likewise, Euler–trace–Fredholm agreement is correctly demoted to internal calibration, but the manuscript gives no roof-specific calculation showing what diagnostic value that calibration adds. Gate 6 is optional, while the article still advertises a six-gate program and later a complete decision structure, so the completion criterion is not fully closed. An opponent could therefore argue that the physical-roof title lends specificity to an architecture that is presently portable to almost any suspension-flow determinant problem. A decision-bearing advance would need at least a formally specified roof-to-operator interface, a synthetic coefficient-map counterexample, or one bounded proof showing how the proposed states distinguish failures that a conventional verification plan would conflate. Until then, the paper is admirably cautious but remains a prospective design rather than evidence about the physical determinant.

### Minor Findings

- **N1.** Severity: Minor. Issue: Calling every gate “open” collapses prerequisite-blocked, not-started, and genuinely evaluable states even though the paper's diagnostic value depends on locating the first failed dependency. Evidence Anchor: text: §Gate dependencies "all six gates remain open because none has been executed". Confidence: 5/5 — workflow-logic analysis within core expertise. Minimum Remedy: Define a per-gate state machine that distinguishes not started, blocked by prerequisite, evaluable, passed, failed, and not evaluable.
- **N2.** Severity: Minor. Issue: Gate 6 is described as optional, but the manuscript does not say when the overall six-gate certificate is complete without it. Evidence Anchor: text: §Gate dependencies and Conclusion "Gate 6 is optional and directional" and "complete prospective decision structure". Confidence: 5/5 — specification-consistency analysis within core expertise. Minimum Remedy: State whether Gate 6 is outside the minimal determinant certificate, and define the exact condition that activates it.

### Alternative Paths

- Publish a smaller roof-to-operator interface note first, with one exact collision-section convention and a typed synthetic roof, before claiming completeness for the six-gate structure.
- Separate the mandatory physical-determinant certificate from an optional cohomological nontransfer module so that completion and stop states are mechanically unambiguous.

### Boundary Preservation

This review does not convert the architecture into a roof, operator, determinant, enclosure, fidelity result, arithmetic A2 evidence, Route-A promotion, or Route-B entry; the declared `A0_FAIL / A2_NOT_ELIGIBLE / NO_ROUTE_PROMOTION` state remains untouched.
