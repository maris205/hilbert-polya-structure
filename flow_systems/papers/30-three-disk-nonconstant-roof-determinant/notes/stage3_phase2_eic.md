### Summary Assessment

The manuscript develops a careful certificate architecture for determining when a transfer determinant can be interpreted as belonging to the specified physical three-disk flow. It consistently separates physical dynamics, symbolic suspensions, semiclassical orbit constructions, exact-quantum determinants, and classical transfer determinants. Its five-channel error contract and six-gate dependency graph are concrete enough to guide later proof and implementation work, while all scientific outputs remain explicitly unexecuted. The route boundary is preserved and no arithmetic or scientific credit is inferred from the nonarithmetic architecture. The article is generally readable, although internal workflow history and evidence-status tokens interrupt the mathematical-physics narrative, and two correction companions acknowledged as mandatory are absent as bibliography entries. Most importantly, the manuscript concedes that the corpus is not a novelty review, leaving the originality and broader significance of the proposed architecture materially under-positioned. The D6 assessment is therefore field-general and repairable; it does not concern alignment with a named venue, track, or submission-readiness standard.

### S1: Strong determinant-type firewall

The manuscript clearly explains why common geometry and periodic-orbit vocabulary do not make physical, symbolic, semiclassical, quantum, and classical determinant objects interchangeable.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: Introduction, research-question paragraph — "physical, semiclassical, exact-quantum, and classical transfer objects can be discussed with similar symbols while carrying different maps, weights, domains, and spectral meanings."

### S2: Error channels are not falsely collapsed

The five-entry error ledger is paired with the important requirement that stability, propagation, dependence, norm, and conditioning arguments precede any total enclosure claim.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: Review-Adjudicated Method Architecture, Gate 4 — "These bounds cannot simply be summed."

### S3: Gate ordering prevents downstream repair of upstream type errors

The six-gate dependency structure gives the prospective program a clear logic and makes stop states interpretable rather than cosmetic.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: Review-Adjudicated Method Architecture, Gate dependencies, artifacts, and independent replay — "A later gate cannot retroactively validate an earlier one."

### W1: Originality and field-level significance remain under-positioned

**Problem**: The manuscript offers a potentially useful synthesis, but its own corpus was not designed to establish whether the type firewall, five-channel proof contract, or six-gate ordering advances beyond the closest validated-computation and dynamical-systems methods literature.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: Acknowledged Limitations, opening paragraph — "The source corpus is bounded and not a novelty review."

**Why it matters**: The article's value rests on the architecture itself because no roof, operator, determinant, enclosure, or fidelity result is produced. The field-general originality and significance of that architecture must therefore be established rather than assumed.

**Suggestion**: Compare the proposed architecture with the closest prior error-budget, transfer-operator validation, proof-carrying numerics, and reproducible-computation frameworks; state precisely which integrations or constraints are new and which are established practice.
