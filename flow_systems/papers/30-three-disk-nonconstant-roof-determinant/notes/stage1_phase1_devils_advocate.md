## Verdict: REVISE

The frozen physical clock, owner/repetition bookkeeping, data firewall, and
explicit functional-analytic kill gate are strong. The current primary test is
nevertheless not methodologically identified: Euler–trace–determinant
compatibility can be a formal identity for any consistently supplied roof, so
the unit-roof and shuffled-roof controls may pass rather than fail. In addition,
the finite-rank determinant is not yet placed under the same orbit cutoff as
the two cycle channels, and the inherited orbit totals do not by themselves
define the pointwise roof/operator.

## Critical issues

### DA30-1 — The primary “physical-roof compatibility” test is roof-agnostic

- **Severity:** Critical
- **Type:** Method / proves-too-much / construct validity
- **Location:** Primary RQ; Method justification; three comparison channels;
  Frozen controls 1 and 3; Sub-question 3.
- **Problem:** If the primitive Euler product, trace exponential, and operator
  determinant are three correct representations of one weighted symbolic
  object, their internal identity is expected for any admissible roof. Replacing
  `tau` by the unit roof or consistently shuffling all `T_p` values does not, by
  itself, break Euler–trace algebra. Those controls can preserve the primary
  compatibility endpoint exactly. A failure would more naturally indicate
  inconsistent bookkeeping or an operator mismatch, not physical-roof
  specificity.
- **Impact:** The method can “validate” arbitrary roofs and therefore cannot
  support the proposed physical-specific interpretation or control margin. As
  written, it risks testing implementation self-consistency while naming the
  result physical A1–A2 calibration.
- **Recommendation:** Before progression, choose and freeze one of two honest
  studies: (a) a pure algebra/implementation consistency study, in which
  self-consistent unit and shuffled roofs are expected to pass and no
  physical-specific/A2 inference is made; or (b) a physical-model validation
  study with an independent, prospectively defined physical criterion that is
  not generated by the same three representations. State each control's
  predicted direction and why the endpoint can distinguish it. Do not repair
  this by adding a target after seeing the finite ledger.

## Major issues

### DA30-2 — The finite-rank channel has no common orbit-cutoff semantics

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Method alignment / truncation
- **Location:** Three comparison channels; Frozen corpus and cutoffs;
  Target-blind computational branch steps 3–5.
- **Problem:** The Euler and trace channels are explicitly truncated by
  `r n_p<=N`. A finite-rank approximation to `det(I-L_s)` normally approximates
  an operator determinant and is not automatically restricted to precisely the
  same primitive/repetition set. “Same cutoff label” does not define how longer
  cycles are removed from channel 3 or how projection/rank error is separated
  from orbit-truncation error.
- **Impact:** Function and zero discrepancies may be caused by comparing
  different mathematical objects, making validation/test verdicts
  uninterpretable.
- **Recommendation:** Define one exact common comparison object. For example,
  compare coefficients/traces only through a frozen order and treat the
  full finite-rank determinant as a separately bounded approximation, or give
  a source-supported projection whose periodic content matches the stated
  cutoff. Freeze the decomposition of orbit-tail, rank, quadrature, and
  precision errors before validation.

### DA30-3 — Orbit totals do not define the pointwise roof operator

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Feasibility / circularity
- **Location:** Frozen operator formula; Data strategy; Formal branch step 1;
  Computational branch steps 2–3.
- **Problem:** The inherited ledger provides finite primitive itineraries and
  total periods `T_p`. A transfer operator requires a roof function `tau(y)` on
  a defined return-map/coding domain, not merely finitely many orbit sums. Many
  roof functions can share those finite sums. If channel 3 is reconstructed
  from the same totals solely to force agreement, it is not an independent
  operator construction.
- **Impact:** The RQ may be aspirational rather than executable, and the
  “independent finite determinant” may be circular.
- **Recommendation:** Freeze the return section, symbolic state space,
  pointwise/geometric roof evaluation, admissible domain, and independent data
  path used to build `L_s`. If Phase 2 cannot verify those objects without
  fitting the orbit totals, stop with an operator-construction obstruction.

### DA30-4 — “Compatible” and the error contract are deferred past Phase 1

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** RQ clarity / outcome flexibility
- **Location:** Primary RQ; Cutoff paragraph on rank/grid/precision; Kill gate
  9; Required result fields.
- **Problem:** No primary complex domain, contour, norm, rank/precision rule,
  absolute/relative tolerance, root-matching rule, or acceptable cutoff drift
  is frozen. The blueprint says these will be selected from Phase-2 theory and
  construction convergence, leaving substantial discretion before validation.
- **Impact:** The answer to “compatible” is not reproducible from the Phase-1
  contract; favorable domains and tolerances can be chosen after construction
  behavior is visible.
- **Recommendation:** Require a second explicit design-freeze checkpoint after
  source verification and before any construction-result inspection. It must
  fix one primary endpoint and the complete error budget; otherwise report
  `NOT_TESTABLE` rather than calibration.

### DA30-5 — The “sealed test” is not demonstrated to be sealed

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Leakage / provenance
- **Location:** `n_p=11,12` sealed-test cutoff and Leakage resistance row.
- **Problem:** The complete inherited ledger already exists as a readable
  project artifact, and no access-control, prior-exposure declaration, or
  sealed-byte receipt is supplied. Naming its last rows “sealed” does not show
  that designers or prior rounds have not inspected them.
- **Impact:** The split cannot carry prospective holdout evidentiary weight and
  may overstate independence from method selection.
- **Recommendation:** Either supply an honest provenance record establishing
  non-exposure and mechanical access control, or rename the partition a
  historical temporal/complexity holdout and downgrade its inference. Do not
  infer unread status from file labels.

### DA30-6 — A0 absence precludes Route-A A2 credit

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Route semantics / scope
- **Location:** Scope “physical A1–A2 calibration”; Phase-1 disposition;
  Methodology conclusion after kill gates.
- **Problem:** Route A defines A0 as the mandatory arithmetic entry gate. The
  artifacts correctly state that A0 is absent, but still say a passed study can
  support a “physical A1–A2 record.” A mathematical determinant subproblem may
  be worth studying, but it is not eligible for positive Route-A A2 credit
  while A0 fails or is absent.
- **Impact:** The route label can be mistaken for advancement of the main
  Hilbert–Pólya program despite an explicit arithmetic-gate failure.
- **Recommendation:** Recast the outcome as a separately typed
  physical-determinant infrastructure/calibration result with
  `A0_FAIL`/`A2_NOT_ELIGIBLE` for the full candidate. Preserve the result, but
  do not promote the Route-A layer.

## Minor issues

### DA30-7 — Independence is asserted more strongly than specified

- **Severity:** Minor
- **Type:** Reporting
- **Location:** “independently implemented” finite-rank channel.
- **Problem:** Separate code does not establish independent evidence if all
  channels share the same owner ledger, conventions, and potential
  transformation errors.
- **Recommendation:** Report exact shared inputs, shared assumptions, code-path
  separation, and correlated failure modes rather than using “independent” as
  an unqualified evidentiary label.

## Strongest counter-argument

> Once one supplies a weight for every primitive cycle and uses that same
> weight consistently, Euler-product and trace-exponential agreement is an
> algebraic bookkeeping identity, not evidence that the roof is physically
> correct. A unit roof, a shuffled roof, and many artificial roofs can pass the
> same test. The finite physical ledger also does not identify the pointwise
> transfer-operator roof. Therefore the proposed experiment can at most check
> internal implementation consistency unless it introduces an independent,
> prospectively frozen physical criterion.

## What's missing

- A primary endpoint that is not automatically preserved under arbitrary
  self-consistent roof substitution.
- Directional hypotheses for every control.
- A shared-cutoff definition for the finite-rank channel.
- An explicit return map, symbolic domain, and pointwise physical roof.
- A complete prospective error budget and resource/rank stop rule.
- Honest holdout exposure provenance.
- Route labeling that respects the mandatory A0 gate.
