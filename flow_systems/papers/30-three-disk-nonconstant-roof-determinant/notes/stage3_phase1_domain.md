## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — methodology_rigor: This dimension asks whether research design, evidence handling, quantitative analysis, and reproducibility provisions satisfy the field's review threshold. The methodology seat owns that judgement, so the domain seat records the criterion without planning a score for it.

D2 — domain_accuracy: This mandatory domain-owned dimension asks whether the physical and symbolic objects, domain terminology, uses of prior work, and substantive claims are faithful to established evidence. For the three-disk configuration, the review will distinguish the classical physical flow from symbolic, transfer-operator, semiclassical, and quantum objects without inferring manuscript content in advance.

D3 — argumentative_coherence: This dimension tests the consistency of the main reasoning and the adequacy of support for its conclusions, including any logical defect capable of defeating the core thesis. It is eligible for the Devil's Advocate and methodology seats, not the domain seat.

D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance: This dimension concerns intelligibility to neighboring disciplines and evidentiary support for cross-disciplinary framing or implications. The perspective seat alone plans and records that score.

D5 — writing_and_structure: This dimension concerns organization, clarity, presentation of figures and tables, and compliance with applicable exposition conventions. It is assigned to the Journal-Fit Reviewer rather than to the domain seat.

D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution: This dimension asks the Journal-Fit Reviewer to judge the scale of contribution and fit for an authorized readership and venue. With no criteria binding available, the domain seat makes no venue-alignment commitment or claim.
