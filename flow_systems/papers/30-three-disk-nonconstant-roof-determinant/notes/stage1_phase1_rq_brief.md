## Three non-interchangeable endpoints

1. **Internal calibration identity:** for each admissible typed roof `rho`, test
   whether its own Euler, trace-exponential, and operator-determinant
   coefficients agree under one frozen convention. Unit and admissible shuffled
   controls are expected to pass internally; their success is not physical
   evidence.
2. **Pointwise physical fidelity:** construct `tau_phys` from a frozen return
   section, coding, and geometric flight evaluator independent of the inherited
   total periods, then test whether its periodic sums reproduce the held-out
   physical orbit data and exact period-two/period-three witnesses.
3. **Cross-roof nontransfer:** under the frozen equivalence relation
   `rho_1 = c rho_2 + h - h o sigma`, test whether `tau_phys` is non-equivalent
   to unit, admissible shuffled, and neighboring-geometry roofs. Each control
   has its own internally calibrated determinant; cross-roof non-equivalence,
   not internal failure, is the directional endpoint.
