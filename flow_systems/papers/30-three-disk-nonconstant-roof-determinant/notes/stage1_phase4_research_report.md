## 1. Introduction and Research Question

The fixed physical object is a no-eclipse equilateral three-disk billiard with equal disk radius `a`, center separation `d=6a`, and unit particle speed. The clock is actual Euclidean flight length. Primitive owners are realized oriented cyclic no-repeat itineraries: cyclic rotations identify an owner, time reversal remains distinct, disk labels are not silently quotiented, and repeated traversal does not create a new primitive owner. A unit roof, a fitted owner scale, stability weights, semiclassical actions, and quantum weights are differently typed objects.

The physical origin of the model is well represented in the frozen corpus. Gaspard and Rice (1989b)<!--ref:P30-S01--><!--anchor:none:--> supports the classical three-hard-disk repellor, its collision organization, and geometrical orbit lengths, subject to the formal correction companion DOI `10.1063/1.457669` for affected formula-level use. Cvitanović and Eckhardt (1989)<!--ref:P30-S04--><!--anchor:none:--> and Cvitanović and Eckhardt (1991)<!--ref:P30-S05--><!--anchor:none:--> support periodic-orbit and cycle-expansion organization. Ikawa (1988)<!--ref:P30-S09--><!--anchor:none:--> supplies rigorous several-convex-obstacle trajectory geometry, while Ikawa (1994)<!--ref:P30-S10--><!--anchor:none:--> supplies a primary zeta/scattering bridge but is conservatively outside the peer-reviewed numerator.

The primary research question asks what typed calibration and nontransfer disposition a source-verified transfer operator built from the independently defined physical flight roof should receive under frozen internal-identity, pointwise-fidelity, and cross-roof-equivalence contracts. The question separates three endpoints. Internal calibration asks whether each typed roof's Euler, periodic-trace, and determinant channels agree under one convention. Physical fidelity asks whether a geometry-derived pointwise roof reproduces independently held physical period data within predeclared bounds. Cross-roof nontransfer asks whether the physical roof is incompatible with a fixed global scale plus coding coboundary relative to unit, admissible shuffled, and neighboring-geometry roofs.

This separation prevents a tempting but invalid inference. A unit or shuffled roof may be internally consistent because calibration concerns algebraic organization of one typed object. Such success cannot establish or refute physical Euclidean-flight fidelity. Conversely, a control's internal failure indicates a construction or bookkeeping problem, not evidence that the physical roof is special.

## 2. Literature and Theoretical Framework

The physical endpoint has two additional demands. ### 2.4 Numerical approximation and the four-error contract

Bandtlow and Jenkinson (2007)<!--ref:P30-S19--><!--anchor:none:--> supports explicit a priori eigenvalue bounds on eligible transfer-operator spaces. Bandtlow and Jenkinson (2008)<!--ref:P30-S20--><!--anchor:none:--> supplies more detailed spectral estimates on holomorphic or Bergman spaces. Their constants and rates cannot be used until the exact three-disk operator, domain, and function space satisfy the source hypotheses.

Wormell (2019)<!--ref:P30-S21--><!--anchor:none:--> supports spectral-Galerkin approximation and error analysis for one-dimensional full-branch uniformly expanding Markov maps. Bornemann (2010)<!--ref:P30-S22--><!--anchor:none:--> supports numerical evaluation and quadrature-related error analysis for eligible trace-class integral-operator Fredholm determinants. The two methods concern different numerical representations. A common rank label or cutoff integer does not create a coefficient correspondence between an orbit expansion and a projected trace.

The frozen design therefore requires two distinct objects. The first is a lawful coefficient/projection map that compares complete orbit-and-repetition coefficients through order `N` with the corresponding operator-trace and projected-trace coefficients. The second is a full-function approximation budget

```text
E_total = E_orbit_tail(N) + E_rank(R) + E_quadrature + E_roundoff.
```

All terms must be bounded on one declared complex domain under prospectively frozen rank, quadrature, precision, and stopping rules. The corpus supplies conditional tail, spectral, projection, and determinant-evaluation components. It does **not** identify a dedicated frozen source for roundoff. Roundoff is therefore a separate unsourced or locator-unresolved obligation, and a three-term bound cannot be described as a complete four-error certificate.

## 4. Evidence-Synthesis Findings

### 4.1 Finding 1: The corpus supports categories, not one executable chain

The literature synthesis finds at least one relevant source category for physical geometry, coding, open-billiard operators, determinant algebra, numerical approximation, and cohomological obstruction. It does not find a single instantiated chain that maps the exact `d=6a` geometry to a pointwise roof on an eligible operator space and then to a certified determinant and nontransfer endpoint. This is an object-composition gap, not a claim that the chain is impossible.

The distinction between category support and project applicability is essential. A general theorem may be valid and a source may be verified while the project-specific hypotheses remain unproved. The correct response is an explicit theorem-hypothesis map and `NOT_EVALUABLE` when the map cannot be completed.

### 4.2 Finding 2: Calibration and physical specificity are independent

The evidence synthesis supports internal zeta/trace/determinant calibration as a roof-agnostic algebraic endpoint. A physical roof and every lawful control roof should be able to pass their own identity. Consequently, control success is expected and control failure is diagnostic of implementation inconsistency. Neither outcome determines whether the physical roof is geometrically correct.

Physical specificity requires a separately defined roof whose input is collision geometry rather than inherited total periods. Its periodic sums must pass exact and interval-valued witnesses under a priori errors. The same physical roof must then be compared with unit, pointwise shuffled, and neighboring-geometry roofs under one frozen equivalence relation. A shuffle of finite labels is not a pointwise roof and cannot produce an operator determinant.

### 4.3 Finding 3: Six object-specific obligations remain

The integrated evidence produces six ordered obligations:

1. **Pointwise physical roof:** freeze the section, symbolic states, inverse branches, collision evaluator, regularity, and geometry-derived error independently of periodic totals.
2. **Operator eligibility:** freeze one function space, normalization, and nuclear or trace hypothesis through an exact object map.
3. **Common coefficient map:** prove which complete orbit/repetition coefficient corresponds to which operator and projected-trace coefficient.
4. **Four-part error contract:** bound orbit tail, rank/projection, quadrature/evaluation, and roundoff on one domain under frozen stop rules.
5. **Physical fidelity and typed controls:** validate physical periodic sums and instantiate unit, pointwise-shuffled, and neighboring-geometry roofs without fitting.
6. **Theorem-licensed nontransfer:** bind scalar cohomology hypotheses and use only predeclared certified mismatches for a negative obstruction.

These are six obligations, not five. A separate author freeze is required before scientific output access, but authorization is a governance gate rather than a seventh object-specific bridge.

## 5. Discussion

The report converts a broad multidisciplinary corpus into a falsification-first program. The first two gates protect object validity: a finite orbit ledger cannot be promoted into a pointwise roof, and a general transfer theorem cannot be promoted into an exact operator without a hypothesis map. The next two gates protect comparison and numerical validity: orbit and rank channels need a common coefficient object before error budgets can be combined, and every error component must remain separately visible. The final two gates protect physical interpretation: internal calibration does not establish the clock, and a nontransfer claim needs the correct directional theorem logic.

Several anticipated outcomes are intentionally unglamorous. `OPERATOR_NOT_EVALUABLE` is correct if the frozen project artifacts lack pointwise collision inputs. `CALIBRATION_NOT_EVALUABLE` is correct if no lawful coefficient map exists. `ERROR_CONTRACT_NOT_EVALUABLE` is correct if any one of the four terms, including roundoff, lacks a bound. A pointwise shuffled control can be `NOT_EVALUABLE` even when a finite-label shuffle is easy. `NONTRANSFER_NOT_PROVED` is correct when finite witnesses do not satisfy the licensed obstruction conditions.

The type firewall also constrains future interpretation of zeros or resonances. A zero of a quantum multiscattering determinant, a semiclassical zeta function, and a classical roof determinant is not automatically the same object. No exact-quantum identification, arithmetic target comparison, or Riemann-zero fit is in scope. Target-driven tuning would invalidate the pre-validation design.

## 6. Theoretical and Practical Implications

The principal theoretical implication is that physical time enters the determinant through a pointwise roof, not through an after-the-fact scalar adjustment of symbolic length. A suspension model can be internally coherent for many roofs. The physical question is therefore a model-identification problem constrained by geometry and cohomology, not merely an algebraic determinant-identity problem.

The second implication concerns numerical proof. Determinant evaluation is compositional: an accurate quadrature routine cannot prove operator eligibility, and a spectral-tail theorem cannot create an orbit-to-projection coefficient map. The four-error decomposition becomes meaningful only after all components refer to one declared object and domain. The absence of a frozen roundoff source is thus substantive, even though it does not invalidate the literature synthesis itself.

Practically, the next design should start from the closest object chain rather than from numerical convenience. The physical three-disk geometry, symbolic coding, rigorous open-billiard operator, and chosen determinant theorem should be mapped hypothesis by hypothesis before basis or rank selection. A geometry-only roof algorithm should then be frozen, followed by coefficient and error contracts, typed controls, and negative nontransfer witnesses. If any interface fails, its typed stop state should be reported rather than repaired by fitting.
