## 4. Mandatory control families

The design must cover all of the following surfaces, while keeping symbolic
theorems distinct from finite diagnostics.

### C17-1 — range-first groupoid and handedness

Use explicit finite carrier/action tables to check source, range, unit,
inverse, composability, multiplication, and the residual **left** sheet
action.  Include a wrong-order/opposite-action negative that is constructed
semantically and then detected, rather than labeled by an expected token.

### C17-2 — action-blind actual open records

On the same nonempty indiscrete finite carrier, compare at least trivial,
transitive, and nontransitive actions.  The recorded arrow-open descriptors,
product/involution formulas, and two-element base must agree.  This is a
finite consistency model for Theorem 5.1, not a proof about `R`.

### C17-3 — connected/disconnected firewall

Encode the connected-real conclusion as a locked symbolic receipt, and use
a genuinely nontrivial finite cyclic quotient of the regular discrete
`Z`-action as the executable falsifier.  The design must forbid inferring
`B(G)=Set` for arbitrary disconnected time from a finite sample.

### C17-4 — non-etale/nonunital domain guards

Separate the usual nondiscrete-real owner from discrete-time controls.  A
discrete proxy must not be permitted to certify real-time non-etaleness or
nonunitality.  Include explicit wrong-domain negatives.

### C17-5 — bare quantale versus localic reconstruction

Rows must distinguish the direct `O(G) ~= O(H)` calculation from the
additional `q_H` and local-compactness receipts.  Any row that promotes the
bare quantale alone to localic reconstruction must fail.

### C17-6 — actual versus standard owner

Record the exact comparison

```text
actual:   Set / O(R) / base 2,
standard: BZ  / O(S_L x R) / base O(S_L),
```

with separate owner and topology columns.  Owner-splicing, importing a
standard topology into the actual packet, or identifying the two base
frames must be isolated negatives.

### C17-7 — unmarked dilation and strict marker

Check the algebraic dilation formulas and their inverse on a finite rational
fixture.  A strict-marker row must reject every nonunit scale.  These rows
must not claim that a plain topos or quantale recovers numerical `L`.

### C17-8 — fixed-prime and provenance firewall

The fixed-prime rows may substitute only Paper-9 indiscreteness and the
literal `(log p)Z` stabilizer after the generic result.  Controls must reject
new C-star, Haar, measure, trace, determinant, Route-B, or priority claims.
