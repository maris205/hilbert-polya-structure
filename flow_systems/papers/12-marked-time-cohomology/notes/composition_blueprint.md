### Central thesis

For a nonempty globally indiscrete right real action, global continuity makes
the author-defined unnormalized nerve complex factor through real time; with
real coefficients the actual degree-one cohomology is the marked line
`R[c]`.  If every stabilizer is the same cocompact lattice `H=LZ`, the same
carrier admits a section-free orbitwise standard coproduct topology whose
degree-one cohomology is the full algebraic product `R^Q`.  The continuous
identity functor has the forced direction

```text
J:G_std -> G_actual,
```

and its contravariant pullback is

```text
H_cnv^1(G_actual;R)=R[c]
  --J^*--> H_cnv^1(G_std;R)=R^Q,

image(J^*)=(R^Q)^(Aut_R(G_std))
           ={constant functions Q->R}.
```

This same-carrier change of continuity, not the ordinary quotient circle,
the wreath mechanism, the inherited actual collapse, or Deninger's
source-owned stabilizer alone, is the manuscript's nonredundant centre.
