## 2. V3 direct-precedent rule

A record counts as a **direct v3 package precedent** only when one source or
one explicitly connected theorem package satisfies all of the following:

1. **V1 — same topological owner:** a nontrivial indiscrete unit space `X`
   with a transitive right action of the usual topological group `R`, lattice
   stabilizer `H=LZ`, action-groupoid arrow topology `X_indisc x R`, and the
   strict coordinate-time mark `c(x,t)=t`;
2. **V2 — canonical same-set retopologization:** the quotient topology placed
   on the same underlying set through `q_x(t)=x dot t`, including the
   calculation `q_(x dot u)=q_x o T_u` and hence independence of the chosen
   unit;
3. **V3 — the asserted categorical strength:** a full-and-faithful `Std`
   functor and an `Indisc` inverse/equivalence with unpointed standard
   Hausdorff transitive `R`-homogeneous spaces, not merely the set-level
   identification of one orbit with `R/H`;
4. **V4 — the marked morphism mechanism:** strict groupoid maps over the
   one-object groupoid `B R` are exactly the uniquely lifted equivariant unit
   homeomorphisms; and
5. **V5 — automorphisms and topology boundary:** the strict automorphisms are
   classified as the ordinary group `R/H`, while the standard topology is
   kept distinct from the actual indiscrete topology and from a Hausdorff or
   completely-regular reflection.

The test is conjunctive: `V1 AND V2 AND V3 AND V4 AND V5`. A source for
`G/H`, a source for action groupoids over `BG`, and a source for
`N_G(H)/H` do not become an exact direct precedent merely because their
standard conclusions can be assembled. Conversely, failure to locate a
single exact package does not by itself show that the assembled result is a
substantive standalone increment.

## 4. Included sources and exact domain ceilings

### S1 — standard homogeneous quotient topology

The Encyclopedia of Mathematics entries
[*Homogeneous space*](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Homogeneous_space)
and [*Topological group*](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Topological_group)
state the standard ingredients already retained as Phase-2 comparator I12:
a transitive `G`-space is identified setwise with `G/H`; `G/H` receives the
quotient topology through the canonical map; and the quotient is Hausdorff
exactly when `H` is closed. For `G=R` and `H=LZ`, this gives the standard
Hausdorff circle quotient.

Andreas Čap's official University of Vienna
[*Lie Groups* notes](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~cap/files/LieGroups.pdf),
Theorem 1.16 and the surrounding quotient-topology discussion, provide a
second authoritative statement in the Lie-group/closed-subgroup domain.

**Ceiling:** these sources make the `R/H` quotient topology, closed-lattice
Hausdorffness, and orbit-map transport generic background. They do not start
with a different indiscrete topology on the same unit set, do not use the
time mark, and do not state `Std`/`Indisc`.

### S2 — action groupoids over `BG` are fully faithful

David Gepner and Lennart Meier,
[*On equivariant topological modular forms*](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X23007509),
*Compositio Mathematica* **159** (2023), 2638--2693, arXiv
[`2004.10254`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10254), Proposition 2.15, is the
load-bearing nearest precedent found by this audit.

The proposition states that for any topological group `G`, the action-
groupoid functor factors through topological groupoids over the one-object
groupoid `{G => pt}` and is fully faithful there, even as a topologically
enriched functor. Its proof says exactly that compatibility over `{G => pt}`
forces the arrow's `G`-coordinate to be the projection, while groupoid
compatibility forces the object map to be `G`-equivariant; the arrow map is
therefore determined by the object map.

For Paper 12, the mark `c(x,t)=t` is precisely the projection of the action
groupoid to `B R`. Thus the formal mechanism behind

```text
F(x,t)=(F_0(x),t)
and
F_0(x dot t)=F_0(x) dot t
```

is a specialization of Proposition 2.15, not a literature-free categorical
phenomenon.

**Exact-domain stop:** Gepner--Meier explicitly assume that topological
spaces are compactly generated and weak Hausdorff. A nontrivial indiscrete
space is not even `T1`, hence is not weak Hausdorff. Proposition 2.15 is
therefore not an exact imported theorem for the Paper-12 unit space. It also
does not retopologize an existing unit set, identify the standard quotient
topology, construct `Indisc`, or state the v3 equivalence. Its proof is,
however, formal enough to establish a strong routine-reduction ceiling for
the proposed strict lift/descent step.

### S3 — equivariant automorphisms of `G/H`

The standard orbit-category formula is

```text
Aut_G(G/H) ~= N_G(H)/H
```

as an ordinary group. It appears, for example, in Tammo tom Dieck's official
Göttingen [*Representation Theory* notes](https://www.uni-math.gwdg.de/tammo/rep.pdf),
§1.3.2, in the finite-group orbit category, and in the usual algebraic
homogeneous-space literature. Those domains are comparators rather than an
imported topological theorem here. The direct set-level proof is one line:
an equivariant map is fixed by the image of one coset, admissible images are
the normalizer cosets, and the kernel is `H`. Since `R` is abelian,
`N_R(H)=R`, giving `R/H`; translations are homeomorphisms of the standard
quotient.

**Ceiling:** `R/H` is not an owner-specific automorphism surprise. The cited
comparison and the elementary proof support an ordinary-group
classification only; no topology on `Aut_Cstr` or `Aut_R` is supplied by the
current v3 category definitions.

### S4 — the 2026 categorical “indiscrete groupoid” false near-match

Sophie Marques,
[*Galois descent theorem in general categories*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40687-026-00629-8),
*Research in the Mathematical Sciences* **13**, article 49 (2026), places a
`G`-object, its action groupoid, and the kernel-pair “indiscrete groupoid”
inside an internal-category descent diagram and proves a torsor/effective-
descent category equivalence.

This record is not a direct precedent. Its “indiscrete groupoid” is the
categorical chaotic/kernel-pair groupoid `E x E`, not a topological space
with the indiscrete topology. Its pseudotorsor condition is free and
transitive, whereas Paper 12 has nontrivial stabilizer `H=LZ`. It contains no
coordinate-time mark, no same-set Hausdorff retopologization, and no
`R/H` automorphism statement. It is retained in the exclusion ledger because
VQ24--VQ27 can otherwise create a terminology-driven false match.

## 7. Standalone substantive-increment audit

The exact-package search and the standalone-strength test have different
outcomes.

The proposed v3 centre decomposes source-side as follows:

1. `q_x:R -> X`, its kernel `H`, the quotient topology, and Hausdorffness for
   closed `H` are standard homogeneous-space facts. Basepoint independence
   is the immediate identity `q_(x dot u)=q_x o T_u` with `T_u` a
   homeomorphism.
2. Preservation of `c=t` is compatibility over `B R`. Gepner--Meier
   Proposition 2.15 already isolates the general principle that this makes
   the action-groupoid functor fully faithful and forces the arrow map to be
   `(id_R,F_0)`.
3. `Indisc` and the displayed `Std`/`Indisc` inverse laws package the same
   underlying transitive action with two topologies. No exact same-set
   package was found, but on the locked object class the inverse laws are an
   elementary combination of items 1 and 2, not a separately sourced deep
   equivalence theorem.
4. `Aut ~= R/H` is the abelian specialization of the standard homogeneous-
   orbit normalizer formula and also follows directly from transitivity.

The Gepner--Meier weak-Hausdorff convention prevents a false statement that
the entire v3 theorem is already published in its exact domain. It does not
remove the routine-reduction problem: the proof mechanism is formal and the
remaining new element located by this search is the exact packaging of an
indiscrete presentation and a standard quotient presentation on the same
set.

The v3 amendment itself requires `NOTE_OR_MERGE` if the new theorem collapses
to cited standard formalism with no substantive owner-specific categorical
content. On the source evidence now available, this audit cannot close the
prior standalone M1. A negative exact-package search cannot substitute for
that substantive-increment showing.
