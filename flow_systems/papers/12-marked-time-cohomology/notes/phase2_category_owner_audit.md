### 3.1 Answer to the exact-match question

There are three different questions which must not be conflated.

1. **Does a primary source publish the same all-face continuous cochain
   formula?** Yes. Blanco--Uribe--Waldorf's `Map(X_bullet,A)` complex is the
   closest exact primary formula, and for `A=R` the coefficient conditions
   match.
2. **Has that source already been shown here to cover the particular
   non-Hausdorff Paper-12 nerve in every degree?** No. Its
   simplicial-paracompact hypothesis and the exact face/sign identification
   are Phase-3 obligations, not assumptions that this audit may import.
3. **Does a primary source match the entire locked theory for arbitrary `T0`
   coefficients on this owner?** No source in the preregistered comparator
   set does. In particular, `T0` does not imply the Hausdorff, locally
   contractible, or compactly generated coefficient hypotheses in the named
   theories.

Thus `C_cnv` is not an invented differential in the `A=R` case, but neither
is the full locked object safely renamed as an off-the-shelf
topological-groupoid cohomology theory. A manuscript citation may say that
the real-coefficient complex agrees, after the stated convention and
hypothesis checks, with continuous cochains on the nerve in the sense of
Blanco--Uribe--Waldorf. It may not cite their Segal--Mitchison comparison as a
proof of `P12-1`, `P12-2`, or `P12-3`.

## 5. Constant bundle, covariance, and category terminology

### 5.1 Coefficients and constant bundle

The frozen `X x A -> X` bundle with identity arrow action is an
author-specified trivial coefficient system. After the frozen
trivialization, cochains are scalar continuous maps on the nerve. This is
compatible with the primary continuous-cochain formula, but Mackenzie's
continuous vector-bundle module cannot be cited as if it covered arbitrary
`T0` groups or the non-Hausdorff base.

The manuscript should keep these distinctions explicit:

- `C_cnv^n` is used only as an abelian group; no compact-open cochain topology
  or quotient topology on `H_cnv^n` is inherited.
- “Unnormalized” means all continuous maps on all composable tuples are
  retained; no vanishing-on-degeneracies condition is imposed.
- The coface differential is contravariant pullback along nerve faces.
  Agreement with a cited source requires an explicit face order and sign
  comparison, not merely the same number of summands.
- A source theorem for `A=R` cannot be generalized by citation to every
  `T0` topological abelian group.

### 5.2 The three categories

The following vocabulary is accurate if each author label is immediately
defined.

| Locked category | Safe terminology | Exact relation | What must not be said |
|---|---|---|---|
| `C_str` | **strictly cocycle-preserving topological-groupoid isomorphism**; “graded isomorphism” only after saying the grade group is `R` and its map is the identity | `c' o F=c` | Do not use bare “isomorphism” when the marking matters. |
| `C_scale` | **positive cocycle-scaling isomorphism**, equivalently cocycle intertwining relative to the value-group automorphism `t |-> alpha t` | pair `(F,alpha)`, `alpha>0`, with `c' o F=alpha c` | It is not strict cocycle preservation, and the period is not invariant. “Graded isomorphism” without the coefficient automorphism is ambiguous. |
| `C_un` | **unmarked underlying topological-groupoid isomorphism** | no equation involving `c` | It does not transport a preferred cohomology class and cannot carry an unqualified period invariant. |

For `(F,alpha)` in `C_scale`, the only correctly directed covariance formula
registered by the definitions is

```text
Per_(F_0(x))([c']) = alpha Per_x([c]).
```

Strict preservation is its `alpha=1` specialization. “Covariant under
scaling” is accurate; “invariant under scaling” is not. Equality of the two
subgroups does not characterize strictness, because the unmarked
orientation-reversal control preserves `LZ` while sending `c` to `-c`.
Non-descent in `C_scale` and `C_un` is existential: the categories contain
unequal-period isomorphisms. It is not a universal claim that every weaker
morphism changes the subgroup.

These three categories and this exact boundary remain author-defined in the
sources inspected for applicability. This audit makes no absence claim
beyond that comparator set; the separately bounded novelty search owns any
`SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SEARCH` conclusion.

## 6. Isotropy restriction and class notation

Farsi--Huang--Kumjian--Packer Definition 3.7 supports the degree-one
convention that a continuous real-valued groupoid 1-cocycle is a continuous
groupoid homomorphism and that a coboundary has range-minus-source form.
The following typing is therefore clean:

```text
res_x : Z_cnv^1(G;R) -> Hom_cont(G_x^x,R),
res_x(b) = b|_(G_x^x).
```

The domain is initially the cocycle group, not the cohomology group. Only
after the direct proof that every 1-coboundary vanishes on isotropy may the
paper define

```text
Per_x([b]) = image(res_x(b)) subset R.
```

That image is representative-independent only after the same descent proof.
The notation `Per_x([b])` and its use as a marked class invariant are
Paper-12 author notation/claims, not a named theorem imported from Definition
3.7. For arbitrary `[b]`, the image need not be advertised as a discrete
lattice. The lattice conclusion is restricted to the normalized transitive
objects and the proved scalar multiples of their coordinate class.

Under a groupoid isomorphism, isotropy is compared at `F_0(x)`, never at an
untransported unit. This makes the registered covariance expression typed;
its proof remains `P12-7`.

## 7. Pointed standard quotient and topology audit

The proposed target category is correctly separated from the actual owner:

```text
Hom_(R,0)^std:
  objects    (R/H,[0]) with H=LZ, L>0, usual Hausdorff quotient topology;
  morphisms  basepoint-preserving continuous strictly R-equivariant
             homeomorphisms.
```

The proposed `S` is a **normalized strict** functor only. A strict morphism
must first be shown to identify the source and target subgroups; its target
map is then `[t]_H |-> [t]_(H')`. The construction is not a functor on the
unmarked category, and the dilation

```text
D_alpha([t]_H)=[alpha t]_(alpha H)
```

is semilinear relative to `t |-> alpha t`, not strictly `R`-equivariant for
the unchanged `R`-action unless `alpha=1`. It therefore cannot be inserted as
a morphism of `Hom_(R,0)^std` in the scaled case.

For the actual nontrivial indiscrete orbit, the topology direction is exactly
one-sided. The chart

```text
theta_x : (R/H)^std -> X,  [t] |-> x dot t
```

is intended to be a continuous equivariant set bijection from the standard
Hausdorff proxy to the actual indiscrete space; its inverse is not
continuous. Paper 10 supplies the inherited actual-versus-standard direction.
Neither Deninger nor a standard homogeneous-space citation may be used to
upgrade `theta_x` to a homeomorphism. Changing `x` rotates the chart, so only
the unbased homogeneous space is canonical.

For an arbitrary class `[b]`, `R/Per_x([b])` remains a value-space quotient.
It is not an orbit parametrization and acquires no actual-orbit topology by
notation alone.

No exact direct-precedent decision for the full pointed functor is made here:
that is assigned to the bounded novelty-search lane. None of the
definition/domain comparators above supplies the complete normalized strict
functor, basepoint rotation, one-sided actual chart, and scaled semilinear
stop as the same package.

## 10. Standalone branch decision

The current implication is:

```text
packet source gate PASS
  => PACKET_COROLLARY remains an admissible Phase-3 target
  => ORBIT_ONLY is not forced
  => the automatic ORBIT_ONLY => NOTE_OR_MERGE trigger is not active
  != standalone approval
```

The final standalone test remains conjunctive. It must fail to
`NOTE_OR_MERGE` if either of these later conditions occurs:

1. the packet conclusion fails its direct proof or any same-owner input is
   displaced, producing an honest `ORBIT_ONLY` outcome; or
2. the proved package reduces to Paper 11 factorization plus routine
   all-degree nerve formalism and the already-owned Deninger stabilizer, or a
   direct same-package precedent removes the substantive category/quotient
   delta.

Conversely, this category audit alone cannot declare the package standalone.
That decision requires the separately bounded novelty report, all Phase-3
proofs and controls, and independent review.
