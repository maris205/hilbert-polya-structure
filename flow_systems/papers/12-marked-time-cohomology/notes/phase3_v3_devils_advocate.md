but it is still built from standard orbit--stabilizer formalism. On the frozen
transitive lattice objects, the orbit map has kernel `H=LZ`; giving the same

of `c(x,t)=t` forces a groupoid arrow to keep the time coordinate, and strict
equivariant maps of a transitive `R`-space are translations. Fullness,
faithfulness, the inverse indiscretization construction, and

That does not make the repair cosmetic. Unlike the v2 pointed `S`, `Std`
retains the full unit map and therefore retains every strict translation. It

## 4. Independent mathematical reconstruction

### 4.1 Quotient topology and basepoint independence

For a `C_str` object and `x in X`, transitivity makes

```text
q_x:R -> X,       q_x(t)=x dot t
```

surjective. Its fibre relation is

```text
q_x(t)=q_x(s)  iff  t-s in H_x,
```

so it factors bijectively through `R/H_x`. Because `H_x=LZ` is closed, the
usual quotient `R/H_x` is compact Hausdorff. Defining the topology on the
same set `X` by

```text
U open  iff  q_x^(-1)(U) open in R
```

makes the induced `R/H_x -> X` a homeomorphism by construction.

If `x'=x dot u`, then

```text
q_(x')(t)=x dot(u+t)=(q_x o T_u)(t).
```

Since `T_u` is a homeomorphism of `R`, it gives exactly the same collection
of open subsets of the underlying set `X`. The standardization is therefore
basepoint-independent, although a chosen identification with `(R/H,[0])` is
not. The identity `Std(G,c) -> X_actual` is continuous because the target is
indiscrete. Its inverse cannot be continuous because it would be a
nonconstant map from a nontrivial indiscrete space to a Hausdorff space.

### 4.2 Descent of strict morphisms and unique lift

Let `F:(G,c)->(G',c')` be strict. The image of `(x,t)` has range `F_0(x)` and
must have marked time `t`. In the frozen range-first coordinates there is
exactly one such arrow:

```text
F(x,t)=(F_0(x),t).
```

Comparing sources gives

```text
F_0(x dot t)=F_0(x) dot t.
```

Thus `F_0` is strictly equivariant. The commuting orbit-map square

```text
F_0 o q_x = q_(F_0(x))
```

and the quotient definitions make `F_0` continuous between
standardizations; applying the same argument to `F^(-1)` makes it a
homeomorphism.

Conversely, an equivariant standard homeomorphism `phi` lifts by

```text
(x,t) |-> (phi(x),t).
```

It preserves range, source, product, inverse, and `c`. After indiscretizing
the unit sets, `phi` and `phi^(-1)` are automatically unit homeomorphisms,
and their products with `id_R` are arrow homeomorphisms. Range and marked
time also prove uniqueness. Therefore `Std(F)=F_0` is full and faithful.

### 4.3 `Indisc`, object recovery, and automorphisms

For a target object `Y`, `Indisc(Y)` keeps the set and action, replaces the
unit topology by the indiscrete topology, and forms `Y rtimes R` with
`c(y,t)=t`; on a morphism `phi` it uses `(y,t)|->(phi(y),t)`. Standardizing
again recovers the original topology. One direct proof is that the orbit map
induces a continuous bijection `R/LZ -> Y`; its source is compact and `Y` is
Hausdorff, hence it is a homeomorphism. Conversely, `Indisc(Std(G,c))`
recovers the original indiscrete unit topology, action, arrow product, and
mark. Under the frozen same-set convention the two composites are strict
identities; under abstract representatives the corresponding identity maps
give the natural isomorphisms.

Every strictly equivariant self-map of a transitive object is determined by
the image of one unit. If that image is `x dot u`, then the map is
`y |-> y dot u`; two parameters give the same map exactly when they differ by
`H`. Hence, as ordinary groups,

```text
Aut_Cstr(G,c) ~= Aut_R(Std(G,c)) ~= R/H.
```

The v2 pointed shadow sends all these translations to its unique
basepoint-preserving arrow. It is therefore nonfaithful for exactly the
reason stated in the amendment, while `Std` retains them.

## 6. Findings

### M1 — the packet is not an object of the transitive standardization category

**Severity:** Major  
**Confidence:** 5/5 — exact category definition and inherited packet orbit
quotient were both checked  
**Evidence Anchor:** `text: notes/phase3_standalone_amendment_v3.md:85-87 — "orbit/packet application may instantiate the theorem"`

The amendment first restricts `Std` to the already frozen normalized
transitive lattice objects, then says that the rational-Witt “orbit/packet
application” may instantiate it. The orbit object can; the packet object
cannot.

Paper 9 proves that the source-flow orbit quotient

```text
Q_p=Gamma_p/(R_{>0}/p^Z) ~= U_p/H_p
```

is nontrivial. Thus `Gamma_p` has more than one source-flow orbit. The packet
groupoid `G_p^pkt=Gamma_p rtimes R` is not transitive and is not an object of
`C_str` as frozen in `research_protocol.md:326-340`. A common stabilizer at
every packet unit does not turn a nontransitive action into a transitive one.

This does not invalidate `P12-6`: the every-unit packet period remains the
stable `PACKET_COROLLARY`. It invalidates only the v3 packet-level
standardization wording.

**Mandatory repair:** state that the v3 equivalence applies to every exact
fixed-orbit object `G_(p,a)^orb`. If desired, it may be applied separately to
each orbit component lying in `Gamma_p`, with the orbit owner and quantifiers
shown. Keep `G_p^pkt` itself outside `C_str`; its only present v3-relevant
claim is the separately proved every-unit period corollary. Do not infer one
packet-level standardization object merely from common isotropy.

### M2 — the expanded Route owner conflates canonical same-set `Std` with its `p`-only standard proxy

**Severity:** Major  
**Confidence:** 5/5 — direct comparison of the construction with every frozen

**Evidence Anchor:** `text: notes/phase3_standalone_amendment_v3.md:130-133 — "record must describe both the canonical unpointed standardization and its pointed shadow"`

```text

```

It therefore belongs to the exact orbit owner and depends on `(p,a)`. Its
identification with `R/(log p)Z` requires choosing a unit and changes by a

phase_space: usual Hausdorff R/(log p)Z

Those fields correctly type the old abstract quotient proxy and its pointed

**Mandatory repair:** choose one nonconflated serialization.

1. Keep `DEN-EF-STANDARD-PERIOD-QUOTIENT-P` unchanged as the unpointed
   standard **isomorphism-class proxy** plus pointed shadow, explicitly state

No option may call the abstract `p`-only quotient a canonical identification
of the same underlying orbit set. The arithmetic ceiling remains the copied

### m1 — the automorphism isomorphism needs an explicit algebraic/topological type

**Severity:** Minor

state that no topology on the automorphism groups is claimed. Alternatively,
define the exact compact-open/enrichment topology and add a separate
topological-group proof and control; that stronger branch is not implicit in
the present theorem.
