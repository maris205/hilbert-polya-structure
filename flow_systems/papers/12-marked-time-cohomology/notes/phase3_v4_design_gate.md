## 2. V4 repair selected for exact locking

V4 replaces the transitive centre by a common-stabilizer, possibly
nontransitive theorem.  It standardizes every orbit and takes their
topological coproduct, then compares continuous cohomology before and after
that topology change.

For the bare orbit set `Q=X/R`, the new central conjunction is

```text
H_cnv^1(G_actual;R) ~= R,
H_cnv^1(G_std;R)    ~= R^Q,
J^*(lambda)         = (lambda)_(q in Q),
image(J^*)           = (R^Q)^(Aut_R(G_std)).
```

Here `R^Q` is the algebraic Cartesian product of all functions `Q->R`, and
`Aut_R` means strict time-preserving equivariant automorphisms.  The functor
`J:G_std->G_actual` is the continuous identity-on-sets map from the finer
orbitwise coproduct topology to the actual indiscrete topology, so the
cohomology arrow is contravariant in exactly the displayed direction.

The canonical automorphism statement is the abstract-group exact sequence

```text
1 -> (R/H)^Q -> Aut_R(G_std) -> Sym(Q) -> 1.
```

A wreath-product splitting requires a chosen origin in every orbit and is
noncanonical.  It is not part of the section-free construction.
