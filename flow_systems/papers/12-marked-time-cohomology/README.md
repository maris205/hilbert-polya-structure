$H=L\mathbb Z$, $L>0$, the same carrier has a canonical section-free
orbitwise-standard coproduct topology. It is the unique Hausdorff,
action-compatible topology with open orbits. Standardization is full and
faithful and has global indiscretization as a strict inverse.

```text
H_cnv^1(G_actual; R) = R[c]
          --J*--> H_cnv^1(G_std; R) = R^Q,
image(J*) = (R^Q)^(Aut_R(G_std))
```

Cartesian product. No topology is placed on cohomology, `R^Q`, or the
