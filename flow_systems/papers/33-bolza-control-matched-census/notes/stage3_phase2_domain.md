## Review Body

The paper gives a careful semantic separation between candidates, oriented classes, primitive roots, inverse-paired unoriented owners, and population completeness. It also correctly refuses to interpret the systole-confounded empty/nonempty direction as an arithmetic comparison, and it makes no novelty or executed-census claim. D2 remains at warning because the control surface is not identified self-containedly enough for an exact producer contract, the self-reciprocal owner serialization is under-specified, and correction records remain absent from the bibliography.

### S1: Owner admission is not reduced to a proxy

The owner definition correctly requires exact conjugacy, maximal-root, cutoff, inversion, and completeness evidence rather than treating a word, matrix, trace, or approximate length as an owner.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §1 Problem and scope "A valid owner record therefore needs more than a word, trace, matrix, or approximate length."

### S2: Negative evidence has the right proof burden

The common schema requires theorem-backed negative payloads and preserves unresolved states instead of converting failed searches into nonconjugacy.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §4 Common schema "Negative evidence requires a theorem-backed canonical or exhaustive payload; failed search, timeout, cap, or numerical nonmatch is not a certificate."

### S3: The target-control asymmetry is not overinterpreted

The manuscript explicitly identifies the systolic confound and restricts the two-surface pairing to an interface stress test.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §5 Frozen-cutoff asymmetry "an empty-versus-nonempty replay at a cutoff lying on opposite sides of the inherited systoles cannot distinguish arithmetic from nonarithmetic geometry."

### W1: The control surface is not self-containedly identified

**Problem**: The control is repeatedly called source-locked or transcendental, but the manuscript does not state its exact presentation, generator matrices, parameter specialization, or exact cutoff-predicate inputs. Those data are deferred to a future freeze even though they determine the object consumed by the proposed control producer.

**Evidence Anchor**: absence: Introduction and control-producer contract — expected the exact control presentation, generators, specialization, and cutoff-predicate inputs; checked §§1.1, 2.1, 4.1, and Future Work

**Why it matters**: Without a self-contained object identifier, readers cannot assess whether the cited Fuchsian algorithms apply to the same control or whether future producer outputs refer to an unchanged surface.

**Suggestion**: Add a concise frozen-object table with exact algebraic or proof-bearing generator data, presentation, parameter values, and the canonical digest that CP must consume.

**Severity**: Minor

**Confidence**: 4 — core expertise: compact Fuchsian surface and owner semantics; producer inputs remain prospective

### W2: Self-reciprocal owners need a canonical membership rule

**Problem**: The schema requires universal external inverse pairing and a separately proved self-reciprocal flag, but it does not say whether a self-reciprocal unoriented owner stores one oriented-class member or two identical links, nor how its deterministic owner ID is derived.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: §4 Common schema "A self-reciprocal flag requires its own conjugacy-to-inverse evidence, while external inverse pairing is required regardless of that flag."

**Why it matters**: Different producers could serialize the same reciprocal geodesic differently and thereby fail interoperability or double-count a singleton inverse pair.

**Suggestion**: Specify the set cardinality, deduplication rule, canonical member ordering, inverse-link self-reference, and owner-ID derivation for the self-reciprocal case.

**Severity**: Minor

**Confidence**: 4 — core expertise: primitive unoriented geodesic ownership

### W3: Bound correction records are absent from the reference list

**Problem**: The Takeuchi and Strohmaier–Uski corrections are preserved only as notes on the base entries; the correction publications themselves are not independently listed.

**Evidence Anchor**: absence: references.bib — expected standalone records for the P33-S03 and P33-S16 correction bindings; checked both base entries and Acknowledged Limitations

**Why it matters**: The exact correction identity and affected scope cannot be resolved from the bibliography alone, weakening provenance for two source-role claims.

**Suggestion**: Add complete correction entries at the next authorized bibliography update and bind every affected use to both the base and correction records.

**Severity**: Minor

**Confidence**: 4 — direct bibliography and manuscript cross-check
