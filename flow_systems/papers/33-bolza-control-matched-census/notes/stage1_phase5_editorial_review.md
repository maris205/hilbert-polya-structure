## Overall assessment

The report provides a clear certificate architecture for a difficult two-presentation primitive-owner census. It sharply distinguishes candidate generation from full-group conjugacy, primitivity, external inversion pairing, cutoff certification, completeness, deterministic serialization, and independent validation. It also handles the existing comparison responsibly: the inherited empty-versus-nonempty direction is explicitly declared systole-confounded and the control panel incomplete.

The manuscript is not yet ready as a census paper or as a complete methods paper. No census was executed, the common exact input model for the algebraic Bolza and frozen transcendental-control representations is unbound, the seven obligations are still a checklist rather than an executable certificate specification, and the between-surface design cannot support the motivating arithmetic comparison at the frozen cutoff. The report also contains an explicit material locator gap and no novelty assessment. These issues require major revision but do not justify rejection because the manuscript itself exposes them and confines its claim to component support without interface closure.

## Findings and required revisions

### Critical findings

None for the report's bounded evidence-synthesis claim. The comparative scientific question would have a fundamental design defect if the current cutoff contrast were presented as arithmetic evidence, but the report explicitly refuses that inference.

### Major findings

#### `P33-EIC-001` — The motivating between-surface comparison is not scientifically identified by the frozen design

**Exact section evidence:** The Introduction states that the common cutoff straddles the source-locked systoles; “Validity Protections” says comparative validity is limited; “Route-A Interpretation” retains `A0_INCONCLUSIVE_SYSTOLE_CONFOUNDED` and `A0_CONTROL_PANEL_INCOMPLETE`.

**Editorial consequence:** Even a perfectly executed owner census at the current cutoff would not support the motivating arithmeticity comparison. The design can answer a bounded certificate-completeness question but not the stronger between-surface inference.

**Required revision:** Choose the article's endpoint. Either restrict the paper to a representation-agnostic certificate/census method and treat the two surfaces as interface cases, or propose a separately authorized matched-threshold and qualifying-control design before making any comparative scientific claim. Do not reinterpret the existing cutoff after seeing outcomes.

#### `P33-EIC-002` — No exact common input model is bound for both surfaces

**Exact section evidence:** “Algorithmic Components and the Common-Interface Problem” states that Voight's framework does not automatically cover the frozen transcendental control and that the Dirichlet-domain route is real-RAM and leaves exact algebraic-number issues outside scope; Findings 1, 2, and 5 keep representation applicability open.

**Editorial consequence:** Conjugacy, primitivity, cutoff predicates, and deterministic bytes cannot be claimed compatible across the two presentations until a precise number and representation model is proved.

**Required revision:** Define the exact computational representation for every generator and predicate on each surface, identify the theorem and implementation version, and prove that both frozen objects satisfy its hypotheses. If one common model is impossible, specify two sound producers with one semantically identical certificate schema and independent checker.

#### `P33-EIC-003` — `P33-RC-1` is a checklist, not yet a reproducible certificate method

**Exact section evidence:** Finding 5 lists seven obligations and says all remain unimplemented; “Practical Implications” describes desired producer fields and checker behavior but supplies no formal schema, acceptance logic, fixtures, or completeness theorem.

**Editorial consequence:** The report cannot yet be evaluated as a methods contribution because no reader can implement or independently reject an artifact from the prose alone.

**Required revision:** Specify the schema, canonical ordering, evidence types, correction provenance, hash domain, verifier predicates, incompatibility rules, termination/completeness certificate, and accepted/rejected fixture contract. Keep production and independent checking implementations separate.

#### `P33-EIC-004` — The article's title and contribution overpromise relative to the executed work

**Exact section evidence:** The main title foregrounds “Checkable Primitive-Owner Censuses,” whereas the abstract, Methodology, Limitations, and Conclusion state that no owner census, pair decision, root certificate, validator test, or scientific computation was executed and novelty was not assessed.

**Editorial consequence:** Readers may expect a completed census or validated algorithm, but the actual contribution is an evidence-synthesis and preexecution contract.

**Required revision:** Complete a separately authorized novelty/contribution analysis and align the title, abstract, and article category with the achieved result. If no census or validated method is added, present the work explicitly as a certificate-design protocol or research architecture.

#### `P33-EIC-005` — The material source-locator gap blocks theorem-adjacent claims

**Exact section evidence:** “Limitations and Future Work” labels the absence of exact quotation, page, section, or paragraph contexts `[MATERIAL GAP]`; S06 remains `PLAUSIBLE` and page-unpinned; all citations retain `anchor:none`.

**Editorial consequence:** Record-level source verification cannot clear the exact arithmeticity, systolic, conjugacy, root, or validation-method claims needed by the architecture.

**Required revision:** Run a separately authorized source-finalization pass, bind every central premise to stable passages and correction records, and record exact theorem hypotheses. Remove or narrow any claim that cannot be supported. S06 must remain contextual unless its precise relevant passage is verified.
