### Prespecified inclusion criteria

A work was eligible when all of the following held:

1. it was original research or an authoritative mathematical source;
2. an original/authoritative stable locator was available (DOI, publisher,
   journal archive, arXiv, or institutional repository);
3. it addressed at least one frozen need: Bolza group/length/systole; the
   exact control octagon or its nonarithmeticity chain; conjugacy or root
   extraction; orientation/inversion/primitive owner semantics; finite exact
   enumeration; completeness; rigorous numerics; certificate serialization;
   or independent validation;
4. its abstract, record, or full text was sufficient to state a conservative
   claim-support boundary; and
5. its use did not change the surfaces, cutoff, clock, Route-A scope, or closed
   magnetic/A2 boundary.

Foundational older works were explicitly currency-exempt. Recent publications
were not preferred merely for recency when the governing theorem or primary
construction was older.

### Prespecified exclusion criteria

A work was excluded when it was only a secondary aggregator, concerned a
different metric or problem without a distinct P33 method role, duplicated a
more direct source without adding a new support surface, lacked an
authoritative locator, or would expand into magnetic flow, determinants, A2,
Route B, prime/zero targets, or scientific execution. Lack of a search result
was never treated as evidence of novelty.

A. V. Nazarenko, “Two-Parametric Hyperbolic Octagons and Reduced

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5446v1) ·
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1301.5446)

- **Candidate support surface:** the original author record gives a compact

- **Boundary:** it does not use the project ID

### Theme B — Bolza group, length spectrum, and hyperbolic systole

## Distributional-skew advisory

This advisory is descriptive and does not block the bibliography or imply
novelty.

- **Language:** 20/20 included works are available in English or English
  translation (100%); non-English research traditions may be underrepresented.
- **Document type:** 17/20 are journal articles (85%); conference,
  author-preprint-only, and book-chapter evidence is sparse by design.

  formal publication channels.

## Limitations and Phase-2 handoff

1. Several publisher records exposed only metadata/abstracts; full-text status
   is itemized in `stage1_phase2_source_inventory.tsv`. A downstream
   source-verification pass must inspect page-level statements before citing
   them for exact claims.
2. Search interfaces did not provide stable global hit totals. The reported
   flow counts are auditable unique-work counts, not claims about exhaustive
   database coverage.
3. MathSciNet and zbMATH were not used as systematic screening databases;
   official journal archives and author/publisher records were prioritized.
4. The corpus covers algorithmic solvability, but no source inspected here
   directly specifies a deterministic, independently checkable positive and
   negative conjugacy-certificate schema for the two frozen presentations.
5. The exact nonarithmeticity of the frozen control is a project derivation
   combining S01, S03, S04, and frozen formulas; it is not a quotation from a
   single source.
6. Length-spectrum and rigorous-spectral precedents do not establish P33's
   full owner census. Full-group conjugacy, inversion pairing, primitivity, and
   zero-unresolved completeness remain distinct obligations.
7. Magnetic flow, determinant/A2, later Route-A stages, Route B, synthesis,
   novelty judgment, and all scientific computation were deliberately
   excluded.

The handoff is therefore a 20-source, 90%-peer-reviewed candidate corpus with
all required topic surfaces represented and one explicit preexecution gap:
`P33-RC-1` remains open pending method selection and certificate-contract
freeze.
