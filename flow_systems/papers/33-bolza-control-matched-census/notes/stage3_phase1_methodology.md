## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): For this configured exact-certificate review, methodological rigor requires complete surface-specific producers, a semantically neutral common schema, proof-carrying positive and negative decisions, and an independently implemented validator capable of checking termination, completeness, and cross-surface conformance.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Geometric, group-theoretic, and ownership claims and terminology must align with the relevant evidence and scholarship, while the domain reviewer—not the methodology seat—provides this dimension's categorical score.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): Any inference from producer outputs and validation records to ownership, completeness, or surface comparison must respect the declared cutoff, control logic, and evidence status, without converting workload or raw counts into unsupported arithmetic conclusions.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): Definitions, schema semantics, and implications should be understandable to adjacent-field readers and interdisciplinary claims must be supported; scoring belongs to the perspective reviewer.

D5 (writing_and_structure): Organisation, exposition, figures or tables, and convention compliance should expose producer, schema, and checker responsibilities clearly, but the Journal-Fit Reviewer owns this score.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): The responsible reviewer must determine originality, significance, readership relevance, and fit against an authoritatively resolved review target; criteria are unbound here, so no venue-alignment claim is made.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: For each producer, explicit representations, candidate generation, conjugacy, maximal-root, inversion, cutoff, termination, and completeness obligations; a common schema for positive and negative certificates; and a validator that independently rejects malformed, unsupported, incomplete, or inconsistent records.
what_triggers_block: A central ownership or census conclusion would rely on materially incomplete producer obligations, a producer-specific schema, or a checker that trusts opaque assertions or recreates the same authority, requiring substantial redesign.
what_triggers_warn: The producer-checker architecture would remain viable, but one or more witness payloads, failure classifications, inverse-pairing semantics, cutoff rules, termination proofs, or conformance tests would be insufficiently precise.
what_triggers_fatal: A single unrecoverable circular dependency between producer assertions and validator acceptance would leave the core certificate without any independently checkable basis for the central claim.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A valid implication chain separating certificate validity, census completeness, comparable control conditions, raw workload or counts, ownership conclusions, and any arithmetic-versus-nonarithmetic interpretation.
what_triggers_block: A central comparison would be inferred from non-comparable enumeration conditions, unsupported counts, or incomplete certificate obligations in a way that requires substantial redesign, reanalysis, or reframing.
what_triggers_warn: The main reasoning would remain defensible, but the distinction among workload, cutoff comparability, validation status, and the scope of a comparative conclusion would need sharper qualification.
what_triggers_fatal: A single irreparable dependence of the central comparative thesis on inherently confounded or incommensurate quantities would invalidate that thesis under the declared design.
