## Shared exact census contract

The acceptance criteria are identical on both surfaces even where their
completeness proofs use different geometry.

1. **Replay.** Verify exact generators, inverses, determinant-one identities,
   polygon relator, source locks, cutoff, clock, and both known-before-P33
   invariants before owner enumeration. Any invariant violation emits
   `UPSTREAM_REPLAY_CONTRADICTION` and stops.
2. **Complete candidate universe.** For Bolza, replay the exact strict systole
   inequality and require an empty candidate universe. For the control, replay
   the frozen exact identity-connected tile component, finite-completeness
   theorem, and primitive `g0*g3` witness; require at least one primitive owner.
3. **Cutoff inclusion.** Decide `ell(g)<=21/10` from exact trace expressions
   and rational enclosures; rounded matrices and decimal tolerances are
   forbidden as keys or gates.
4. **Full quotient conjugacy.** Two rows merge only by an exact conjugator
   witness or a theorem-backed canonical conjugacy normal form. Literal matrix
   equality, trace, length, and homology are filters, not sufficient
   conjugacy evidence. Nonconjugacy also requires a complete exact certificate.
5. **Primitivity.** Admit an owner only with an exact no-proper-power proof.
   The common systolic implication `ell<2*sys` may be used only after its
   surface-specific inequalities are replayed exactly.
6. **Inversion and repetition.** Collapse `[g]` and `[g^-1]` to one owner while
   retaining orientation provenance. Powers/repetitions are recorded
   separately and never create owners.
7. **Completeness closure.** A surface manifest closes only when every class
   below the cutoff has a represented candidate, every candidate has a final
   conjugacy/inversion/primitivity disposition, and the unresolved count is
   zero. Counts alone never establish completeness.
8. **Common-contract readout.** If and only if both surface manifests are
   complete, set `scientific_result` to
   `BOTH_CENSUSES_CLOSED_UNDER_COMMON_CONTRACT` and report the two counts
   separately. Every count contrast remains
   `A0_INCONCLUSIVE_SYSTOLE_CONFOUNDED`; it cannot produce a formal A0 verdict.

The new P33 significance is exact full conjugacy/inversion/primitivity
ownership closure beyond the already-known coarse support, not discovery of
the support contrast itself.
