## Review Body

The manuscript provides the most concrete semantic schema of this five-paper set. It permits surface-specific mathematics while requiring common meanings for candidate admission, conjugacy, roots and primitivity, inversion, reciprocity, ownership, repetition, termination, completeness, and validation. Its incompatibility rules and three-axis state separation sharply distinguish design, execution status, scientific content, and Route interpretation. The only executed work is the bounded literature workflow; neither producer, schema, validator, fixture set, candidate universe, census, nor completeness proof exists. This review therefore awards no owner, census, comparative, or Route result. D1 warns because the schema remains prose rather than bytes, the independent-checker trust boundary is not defined, and surface-specific candidate completeness is only a prospective obligation. D3 passes because the manuscript consistently treats the inherited cutoff asymmetry as a confound rather than evidence for arithmeticity and never turns a well-designed interface into an executed scientific conclusion.

### W1: Validator independence lacks a concrete trust boundary
**Problem**: The manuscript calls for one semantic checker with producer-specific proof adapters but does not state whether adapters may reuse producer libraries, how their proof kernels are independently implemented, who supplies expected fixture outcomes, or which artifacts are outside producer control.
**Evidence Anchor**: absence: Independent validator contract — expected a code-reuse prohibition, adapter trust boundary, independent implementation criteria, and oracle provenance; checked §§4.3, 4.5, and 6.2
**Why it matters**: A checker that reuses the producer's normalization or decision path can reproduce the same semantic defect while appearing independent. This is especially consequential when BP and CP have heterogeneous representations.
**Suggestion**: Define the verifier's trusted computing base, prohibit reuse of producer decision code, separate proof parsing from predicate kernels, freeze independently authored expected fixtures, and record build/provenance hashes for producer, adapter, and checker components.
**Severity**: Major
**Confidence**: 5 — core expertise: independent validation and cross-implementation conformance

### W2: The common schema is not yet a serializable contract
**Problem**: The table lists semantic fields and incompatibilities, but no byte grammar, proof-type registry, canonical numeric encoding, state-transition table, digest domain, or executable validator predicate set has been frozen.
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §4.2 — "It is a design specification, not a serialized artifact already frozen for execution."
**Why it matters**: BP and CP can interpret the same field names differently, and a validator cannot deterministically reject drift or unknown evidence without a closed representation. The central interoperability claim is therefore prospective rather than testable.
**Suggestion**: Publish a versioned schema with closed enums and canonical bytes, a proof-type registry and adapter contract, exact status transitions, hash-domain rules, and positive/rejected fixture bytes before either producer sees census inputs.
**Severity**: Major
**Confidence**: 5 — core expertise: proof-carrying data and deterministic serialization

### W3: Surface-specific population completeness has no bound procedure
**Problem**: Package A names candidate bounds and termination proofs, but the paper does not define the actual BP or CP candidate-universe descriptor, exact cutoff decision procedure, exhaustive enumeration theorem, or stop proof.
**Evidence Anchor**: table: §4.4, Package A — candidate bound and termination proof are prospective and current status is unimplemented
**Why it matters**: Correct local conjugacy and root certificates cannot establish a complete census if an admissible candidate was never generated. The expected target emptiness makes a complete empty-universe proof particularly sensitive to this distinction.
**Suggestion**: For each surface, freeze the exact input representation, theorem-bounded enumeration domain, ordering, cutoff predicate, interval or exact comparison method, termination measure, coverage digest, and unresolved accounting, then require the validator to recompute the population bound independently.
**Severity**: Major
**Confidence**: 4 — core expertise: exact finite census and completeness certificates

### W4: The executed evidence workflow cannot be reconstructed or passage-audited
**Problem**: The search lacks auditable global hit totals, and all 48 literature uses lack exact passage locators despite supporting object, algorithm, correction, and asymmetry boundaries.

**Why it matters**: The 20-source corpus and its claim roles cannot be independently reproduced from retrieval through screening, and theorem applicability remains unresolved at precisely the points future producers must bind.
**Suggestion**: Add exact interfaces, dates, queries, returned identifiers or explicit unavailable-total receipts, row-level screening decisions, and passage/hypothesis maps for every decision-bearing source and correction.
**Severity**: Major
**Confidence**: 4 — adjacent expertise: reproducible evidence and correction provenance
