## Frozen Phase-2 candidate protocol

Phase 2 may populate, but may not alter, this protocol.

### Admissibility grammar

A candidate is eligible only if all of the following are true before any P29
owner-performance field is evaluated:

1. a verified source states a direct deterministic construction from exact
   loxodromic/congruence data to an ideal of `Z[i]`, with a precise formula or
   algorithm locator;
2. the construction has no learned parameter, fitted threshold, target label,
   prime/zero lookup, or outcome-selected composition;
3. its domain, codomain, conjugacy law, inversion law, and power/repetition law
   are mathematically stated rather than inferred from the finite ledger;
4. every optional branch and parameter is fixed by the source definition, not
   by `(D9,J3)` collisions; and
5. a direct formula is registered as one mechanism—post-hoc combinations of
   multiple formulas are inadmissible.

### Registry and performance-independent selection

- Phase 2 creates a registry containing at most three eligible direct
  mechanisms. Each ID is the tuple `(canonical source identifier, exact
  theorem/formula locator, SHA-256 of normalized formula bytes)`.
- Eligible rows are ordered lexicographically by that tuple. If more than three
  exist, only the first three enter this run; the remainder require a new run.
- The registry, exclusions, ordering, formulas, and control-sensitivity table
  must be frozen and author-confirmed before any mechanism is applied to a P29
  matrix row.
- Formal admissibility is tested in registry order. The primary mechanism is
  the first row whose codomain, conjugacy, inversion, repetition, and
  split-ideal compatibility proofs all pass. Selection never uses collision or
  separation performance. All tested formal outcomes are reported.
- If no row passes, the terminal disposition is
  `NO_ADMISSIBLE_IDEAL_OWNER_MECHANISM`. A newly discovered or modified
  mechanism cannot be substituted without a new author-approved Phase-1
  design checkpoint.

## Decision-bearing meaning of “refine”

Let `H` be the prospectively designated holdout subset of **certified primitive
owners**, let `B(u)=(D9(u),J3(u))`, and define

```text
C_H = {{u,v}: u<v, u and v in H, B(u)=B(v)}
S_H(M) = number of pairs {u,v} in C_H with M(u) != M(v).
```

The sole primary performance estimand is the exact integer `S_H(M)`.

- `FINITE_REFINEMENT` means `|C_H|>0`, every holdout output is a valid Gaussian
  prime ideal, and `S_H(M)>=1`.
- `FINITE_NO_REFINEMENT` means `|C_H|>0`, every output is valid, and
  `S_H(M)=0`.
- `REFINEMENT_NOT_EVALUABLE` means the quotient is not certified, the holdout
  has no baseline-collision pair, or a required output is unresolved.

The rate `S_H/|C_H|`, mapped fraction, collision-bucket sizes, and owner-type
tables are secondary diagnostics. Specificity is a later, separate gate and
never changes the value of `S_H`.

## Scope boundaries

### In scope

- The inherited unit-speed, level-(3) Gaussian Bianchi geodesic flow with
  hyperbolic arclength fixed as clock.
- Primitive loxodromic classes only after a complete quotient certificate.
- Literal nonzero Gaussian prime ideals as outputs: associates define the same
  ideal; conjugate split ideals remain distinct; inert and ramified owners keep
  their types.
- The closed candidate registry, exact formal laws, primary `S_H` estimand,
  and directionally valid mechanism controls.
- A theorem, counterexample, fail-closed obstruction, or bounded finite result.

### Out of scope

- Euler products, transfer operators, determinants, zero comparisons, analytic
  continuation, Weil criteria, operator lifts, A2–A4, or Route B.
- Riemann-prime ownership or Riemann-zeta credit; the immutable arithmetic type
  is the prime-ideal ledger of `zeta_{Q(i)}`.
- Learned/fitted maps, target labels, outcome-selected formulas, or post-hoc
  changes to the quotient, panel, controls, or endpoint.
- Calling matrix-row collisions primitive-owner collisions before quotient
  certification.

### Key assumptions to test, not promote

1. The inherited artifacts replay within their stated finite word-ball and
   matrix-row boundaries.
2. Phase 2 can produce a source-verified candidate registry without examining
   P29 mechanism performance.
3. A complete primitive-root and conjugacy procedure can close the quotient;
   otherwise performance is `NOT_EVALUABLE`.
4. An unoriented class identifies `gamma` with `gamma^-1`, while powers are
   repetitions of one primitive owner.
5. A valid mechanism can select one literal split prime ideal while remaining
   inversion-invariant; failure is the first formal obstruction, not a reason
   to weaken the codomain.
