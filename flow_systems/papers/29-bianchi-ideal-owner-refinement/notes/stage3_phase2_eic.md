The manuscript presents a coherent fail-closed architecture for separating mechanism admissibility from primitive-unoriented quotient completeness before any collision performance is exposed. Its strongest editorial feature is disciplined claim typing: definitions, bounded corpus findings, prospective proof obligations, and actual status are repeatedly distinguished. The directed certificate graph also gives a field-relevant account of how object serialization, quotient evidence, mechanism registration, performance, and replay should depend on one another. The route boundary is preserved; this card awards no scientific-execution or roadmap credit. The prose is understandable, but internal phase labels and workflow artifacts interrupt the external scholarly narrative. More importantly, the manuscript expressly leaves originality positioning undone and supplies no reader-accessible manifest for artifacts invoked as the basis of auditability. Accordingly, the writing problem is repairable through focused editorial revision, while the field-general case for an original, significant contribution to arithmetic dynamics and certificate methods requires substantial substantiation. No named-venue, track, or submission-readiness judgement is made.

The frozen bibliography is rich in domain and algorithmic components, yet it does not establish how this certificate architecture differs from existing proof-carrying, fail-closed, or computational-reproducibility frameworks.

**Evidence Anchor**: text: Acknowledged Limitations, third paragraph — "The contribution has not been positioned against the closest research-design literature through a separately authorized novelty analysis."

**Why it matters**: Without a closest-work comparison, readers cannot determine whether the two-gate architecture is an original field contribution, a significant synthesis of known safeguards, or primarily project-specific workflow documentation.

**Suggestion**: Add a bounded but explicit comparison with the closest certificate-methods and proof-carrying computational frameworks, identify the architecture's distinct unit of contribution, and state which elements are adaptations versus new synthesis.
