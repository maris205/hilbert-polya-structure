## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — methodology_rigor: This dimension asks whether the study design, handling of evidence, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility support are rigorous enough for peer review. It belongs to the methodology seat, so the domain seat recognizes the requirement but does not pre-commit a score for it.

D2 — domain_accuracy: This mandatory domain-owned dimension asks whether the mathematical objects, arithmetic terminology, prior-work representations, and substantive claims agree with the applicable evidence. For this arithmetic-geometry configuration, the domain review will test the precision of group, geodesic, ownership, and Gaussian-ideal distinctions without presuming anything about the manuscript's contents.

D3 — argumentative_coherence: This dimension concerns whether the central line of reasoning remains internally compatible and whether its support is adequate, without a reasoning failure that defeats the thesis. It is assessed by the Devil's Advocate and methodology seats rather than by the domain seat.

D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance: This dimension asks whether readers from neighboring fields can follow the framing and definitions and whether any cross-field implications have real support. It is assigned to the perspective seat, not to the domain seat.

D5 — writing_and_structure: This dimension covers the effectiveness of organization and exposition, the quality of displayed materials, and conformity with applicable presentation conventions. It is owned by the Journal-Fit Reviewer and is outside the domain seat's scoring plan.

D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution: This dimension asks the Journal-Fit Reviewer to assess contribution and readership fit under an authorized target. Because no criteria binding is supplied here, the domain seat makes no venue-alignment commitment or claim.
