## 8. Linux 5.15 P-lifetime and P_CRASH semantics

### 8.1 Exact FD12 poll predicate

G uses one direct poll target, the exact inherited and pre-bound FD12:

~~~text
pollfd.fd = 12
pollfd.events = POLLIN
poll return = 1
(pollfd.revents & POLLIN) != 0
(pollfd.revents & POLLNVAL) == 0
(pollfd.revents & POLLERR) == 0
~~~

Exact revents equality is forbidden. POLLHUP is not required. Additional
nonfatal readiness such as POLLRDNORM is not a mismatch. A timeout, EINTR,
zero return, POLLNVAL, POLLERR, failed call, or ambiguous result is not
P-exit evidence.

On Linux 5.15, pidfd poll readiness begins when the target whole thread
group has exited; it remains ready through later reap. The readiness proves
only that fact. It does not report or prove:

- signal versus ordinary exit;
- exit status;
- core status;
- which thread failed first;
- a wait/reap result; or
- why the process exited.

P must remain within the inherited single-threaded coordinator axiom, or
P_CRASH must continue to mean whole-thread-group termination. A leader exit
with a live residual thread correctly leaves FD12 nonready.

### 8.2 Forbidden substitute evidence

G is not P's child. V15 must explicitly forbid G from:

- waitid on P or FD12;
- waitpid or any attempt to reap P;
- treating ECHILD, WNOHANG, or a wait error as life/death evidence;
- pidfd_send_signal with signal 0 or another signal as a liveness probe;
- treating EINVAL, EPERM, or signal success as death;
- read on FD12;
- requiring G-side pidfd fdinfo Pid to equal the outer PID;
- reading P task content through FD13 after its pre-ready close;
- reopening /proc/PID_DEC or pidfd_open(PID_DEC) after the namespace
  transition; or
- using a pathname, status/cgroup byte replay, EOF alone, or a numeric PID
  as identity or death.

A live nonchild P can yield ECHILD. A descendant PID namespace can make a
signal-0 attempt yield EINVAL before liveness is addressed. A dead P's
already-open procdir may still fstat successfully while task files are gone.
None is an admissible substitute for the FD12 POLLIN predicate.

### 8.3 Exact P_CRASH guard and no-future-producer proof

FD12 readiness is necessary but not sufficient. G may classify P_CRASH only
when all of the following are retained:

1. the v14 carrier scope has begun;
2. the normal terminal has not completed and no already-finalized C14
   success may be revised;
3. the authenticated P--G control transport has produced in-scope EOF/loss;
4. P_LIFETIME_LEDGER is at G_PIDFD_ARMED_PROC_CLOSED;
5. the exact FD12 poll predicate above enters P_EXIT_OBSERVED;
6. G permanently stops every future in-scope enqueue;
7. the v14 profile/holder ceiling proves no alternate reader or producer,
   and the inherited LAUNCHER_REAPED proof excludes L;
8. G is the one authenticated survivor with its actual EP_G;
9. G drains P-to-G inbound records one record at a time through every queued
   record to exact EOF;
10. G reconciles all G-to-P outbound endpoint-bound full-return receipts,
    including form, direction, complete frame bytes, and one-use identity;
11. existing C14, raw17, winner, losing-bit, owner-evidence, tie, and
    tombstone rules are applied without a new coordinate; and
12. G completes the existing endpoint close/absence and holder teardown,
    then closes FD12 last and immediately proves EBADF before exit.

EOF while FD12 is nonready is EOF/transport failure or unreconciled state,
not P_CRASH. FD12 ready after the unique C14 success has already finalized
cannot turn success into P_CRASH. FD12 ready without the holder ceiling,
exact EOF drain, or outbound reconciliation proves exit only and cannot
freeze an exact failure row. P exit before the applicable v14 carrier scope
is bootstrap failure, not a forced projection into one of the fifteen C14
rows.

### 8.4 Normal terminal

The normal terminal uses the inherited v14 success and disposal order. A
later FD12 readiness observation is only the expected consequence of P exit
and has no classifier authority. G performs its existing endpoint
drain/reconcile/freeze/close duties first and closes FD12 last with immediate
EBADF. FD13 is already absent. No normal or crash terminal leaves a
P-lifetime descriptor in G.
