## 2. Exact split of the height-saturation proof

Base-protocol Section 4.4 is superseded by the following two branches.

### 2.1 Branch `r != p` — Kummer--Chebotarev

For the away-coordinate restriction `g:S_r->C_{r^infty}`, prove

```text
g(S_r[r^m])=C_{r^infty}[r^m]       for every m>=1
```

by constructing infinitely many primes `ell != p,r` satisfying

```text
v_r(ell-1)=m,
v_r(ord_ell(p))=m.
```

For odd `r`, the proof may prescribe a Frobenius element in the compositum
of the `r^{m+1}` cyclotomic tower and the degree-`r` Kummer extension of
`p`.  It must prove the required compatibility over `Q(zeta_r)`.  The
ramification-separation argument is licensed only because `r!=p`: every
nontrivial Kummer subextension is ramified at `p`, while the added
`r`-power cyclotomic layer is not.

For `r=2` and odd `p`, use the separate quadratic field `Q(sqrt(p))` and
the `2`-power cyclotomic tower.  Prescribe `ell` with exact
`v_2(ell-1)=m` and with `p` nonsquare modulo `ell`; in the cyclic residue
group this is equivalent to `v_2(ord_ell(p))=m`.  The case `m=1` remains
included.  No odd-prime Kummer formula or hidden GRH is permitted.

### 2.2 Branch `r=p` — Bang--Zsigmondy bounded surjectivity

No Kummer/cyclotomic disjointness at `p` is asserted.  Instead, for every
`m>=1`, apply the exact Bang--Zsigmondy theorem to `p^{p^m}-1` and choose a
primitive prime divisor `ell!=p` with

```text
ord_ell(p)=p^m.
```

The protocol's exception audit must explicitly show that none of the
Bang--Zsigmondy exceptional cases occurs for a rational prime base `p` and
exponent `p^m`.

The pro-`p` Sylow of the `ell` coordinate then contains the literal cyclic
subgroup generated by `p` of order `p^m`.  Restriction of its character
group to the pro-`p` exponent subgroup maps a character of order `p^m` to
an arbitrary element of `C_{p^infty}[p^m]`.  Therefore the away-coordinate
map in the `r=p` branch satisfies the same bounded-surjectivity identity

```text
g_p(S_p[p^m])=C_{p^infty}[p^m]
```

without any field-intersection claim.  This is the input for
`K_{p,p}~=S_p` and `p^omega K_{p,p}=0`.

Every later use of “height saturation” must name which of these two proofs
supplies it.
