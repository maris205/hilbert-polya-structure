`terminal_frame` on FD 4.

### 6.2 Exact prepared, grant, direct observation, finalize, and receipt

The remaining exact P--G terminal records are:

~~~text
P -> G:
SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_GRANTED requester_session=0 requester_child=DEC audit=DEC auth_serial=DEC auth=DEC session=DEC close_request=DEC outcome=OUTCOME terminal_cap=LOWERHEX64 reply_digest=LOWERHEX64 reply=LOWERHEX

P -> G:
SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZE requester_session=0 requester_child=DEC audit=DEC auth_serial=DEC auth=DEC session=DEC close_request=DEC outcome=OUTCOME terminal_cap_sha256=LOWERHEX64 reply_digest=LOWERHEX64

G -> P:
SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZED_ACK requester_session=0 requester_child=DEC audit=DEC auth_serial=DEC auth=DEC session=DEC close_request=DEC outcome=OUTCOME terminal_cap_sha256=LOWERHEX64 reply_digest=LOWERHEX64
~~~

In TERMINAL_GRANTED, `reply` is hex of exact `terminal_frame`.  P completely
sends that record once; this is the first disclosure of raw `terminal_cap`
to G.  G requires exact tuple, raw capability, reply bytes, and both
recomputed digests.  It decodes one exact frame and performs exactly one
plain `send` of that whole datagram on the registered connected Linux
`SOCK_SEQPACKET` FD-4 peer.

`terminal_frame` is at most 4096 bytes.  G sets its local
`terminal_full_send=true` only if the return equals the complete datagram
length.  A positive short return, zero, errno, ambiguous result, wrong
tuple, or wrong decoded bytes leaves the flag false.  There is no suffix
retry, second terminal send, normalization, readiness success, or EOF
success.  SOCK_SEQPACKET atomicity means a complete send enqueues exactly
one whole datagram rather than a stream prefix, but G's local flag alone is
not P's evidence.

The requester may send exactly once on its child-unique FD 5, and only after
one exact complete nontruncated FD-4 receive whose U32BE length and canonical
payload both validate:

~~~text
requester -> P:
SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_OBSERVED audit=DEC auth_serial=DEC auth=DEC session=DEC close_request=DEC outcome=OUTCOME terminal_cap=LOWERHEX64 reply_digest=LOWERHEX64 reply=LOWERHEX
~~~

The requester extracts the raw capability and `reply` hex from that same
immutable receive buffer.  It cannot use a reconstructed or second buffer.
P receives with `recvmsg` and requires the registered endpoint, exactly one
kernel-supplied `SCM_CREDENTIALS`, no other cmsg, no truncation flags, and
exact equality with P's raw secret, expected frame, both digests, close
tuple, child identity, and one open terminal attempt.  G holds no endpoint
or peer copy by the frozen D-M2 evidence; a G-local send assertion cannot
satisfy this direct record.

Only after this join may P completely send `SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZE`.  G
requires its exact local `terminal_full_send=true`, the granted raw value,
exact tuple, and recomputed digests before completely sending
`SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZED_ACK`.  A direct observation alone cannot cure a
wrong/partial G send: even if a defect exposes the raw cap and a requester
constructs expected bytes, G's full-send flag is false and ACK is forbidden.
Conversely, G's full-send flag cannot replace the direct observation because
P sends no FINALIZE without it.

Only after P validates the exact ACK does P completely send once:

~~~text
P -> requester:
SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_RECEIPT audit=DEC auth_serial=DEC auth=DEC session=DEC close_request=DEC outcome=OUTCOME terminal_cap_sha256=LOWERHEX64 reply_digest=LOWERHEX64
~~~

The requester closes original FD 5 only after receiving the complete
receipt.  P accepts clean EOF only when no auth, child-audit, or terminal
frame remains open, retains the peer/terminal identities through requester
reap, and closes its peer only after the inherited reap reconciliation.

The design proves P's complete receipt send followed by peer closure.  It
does not claim that P independently observes the requester's application-
level read of TERMINAL_RECEIPT.  If such a read becomes a success predicate,
a forbidden thirteenth FD-5 form would be needed and this design must stop.

### 6.3 Exact endpoint and terminal lifetime

FD 5 remains the child-unique P-created endpoint from v4.  It is not duped,
transferred, used as a listener, or held by G except for v6's separately
bounded audit-local P duplicate, which is absent before exit from its exact
audit interval.  The lifetime is now exactly:

~~~text
P pair creation and child installation
  -> every inherited registration/source/running/frozen audit
  -> terminal FD-4 reply receive
  -> requester SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_OBSERVED send
  -> P SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZE / G SESSION_AUTH_FINALIZED_ACK
  -> P SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_RECEIPT complete send
  -> requester closes original FD 5
  -> P accepts clean EOF and retains peer identity
  -> requester reap and CHILD_REAPED reconciliation
  -> P closes retained peer and proves the expected absence
~~~

Every inherited D-M2 audit whose unchanged registered/source/running/frozen
trigger occurs while FD 5 is live executes at that same trigger and with the
same bytes.  V7 creates no new D-M2 trigger, record, row, or invocation.
Every audit duplicate and fresh row proc FD is still closed, proved EBADF,
and absent before its own EXIT_ACK; none crosses FINALIZE, ACK, receipt,
original close, EOF, reap, or P-peer close.  FD 4 retains its inherited
terminal-reply, EOF/drain,
holder-removal, and CHILD_REAPED requirements.  After completely receiving
and validating the one terminal reply and completely sending
TERMINAL_OBSERVED, the requester closes original FD 4 before exit; G drains
and observes that EOF, closes its peer, and removes both identities before
CHILD_REAPED.  Those FD-4 close receipts are neither terminal-send success
nor FINALIZE evidence.  G retains its already-fixed full-send flag after
peer close.  Retaining immutable reply bytes for the FD-5 observation is not
an FD-4 holder.

## 9. Exact successful states, joins, and acceptance

### 9.1 Complete state paths

P's successful path is exactly:

~~~text
UNSEEN -> CHALLENGE_ISSUED -> REGISTERED -> CREATE_GRANTED
-> CREATE_ACCEPTED -> INACTIVE_COMMITTED -> ACTIVATION_JOINED
-> ACTIVE_RECEIPT_SENT -> ACTIVE_PENDING -> ACTIVE_AUTHORIZED
-> CLOSING -> TERMINAL_PREPARED -> TERMINAL_GRANTED
-> TERMINAL_OBSERVED -> FINALIZE_SENT -> FINALIZED_ACKED
-> TERMINAL_RECEIPT_SENT -> FD5_EOF_OBSERVED -> CLOSED_TOMBSTONE

any nonterminal state -> ABORTING -> FAILED_TOMBSTONE
~~~

G's successful path is exactly:

~~~text
NO_SESSION -> CREATE_ARMED -> CREATE_HELD -> INACTIVE
-> ACTIVE_ARMED -> ACTIVE_AUTHORIZED -> CLOSING
-> TERMINAL_PREPARED -> TERMINAL_GRANTED -> TERMINAL_REPLY_SENT
-> FINALIZE_RECEIVED -> CLOSED_TOMBSTONE

any nonterminal state -> ABORTING -> FAILED_TOMBSTONE
~~~

P's pre-terminal v6 `activation_hold` remains a separate one-packet bounded
slot, not a state.  It retains the exact EMPTY/HELD ordering and join rules.
The changed ACTIVE record arms G with only the commitment; the first correct
active-cap-bearing non-child packet supplies raw equality before mutation
without adding a P state or per-operation provenance claim.

There is no separate pre-PREPARED close notice.  Exactly one valid
`SESSION_AUTH_TERMINAL_PREPARED`, joined to P's already accepted ACTIVE
session, close request, inherited cleanup/object ledger, and OUTCOME, causes
P's two consecutive transitions
`ACTIVE_AUTHORIZED -> CLOSING -> TERMINAL_PREPARED`.  P takes neither
transition from G readiness, FD-4 EOF, a copied close request, or an invalid
PREPARED record.

G enters `TERMINAL_REPLY_SENT` only on exact complete terminal send.  It
enters `FINALIZE_RECEIVED` only on exact FINALIZE plus that local flag.  Its
complete FINALIZED_ACK send then permits its `CLOSED_TOMBSTONE`; that G state
does not imply P success.  P holds `FD5_EOF_OBSERVED` through the required
requester reap and P-peer close/absence proof, and only then enters
`CLOSED_TOMBSTONE`.  Thus its complete terminal receipt, actual clean EOF,
requester reap, and P-peer close are all required.

The terminal transition owners and successful edges are exactly:

| Side/from | Sole success event | To |
|---|---|---|
| G `CLOSING` | complete PREPARED send after inherited cleanup | `TERMINAL_PREPARED` |
| P `ACTIVE_AUTHORIZED` | valid complete PREPARED plus consecutive CLOSING edge | `TERMINAL_PREPARED` |
| P `TERMINAL_PREPARED` | complete GRANTED send | `TERMINAL_GRANTED` |
| G `TERMINAL_PREPARED` | validate one complete GRANTED | `TERMINAL_GRANTED` |
| G `TERMINAL_GRANTED` | one complete exact FD-4 send | `TERMINAL_REPLY_SENT` |
| P `TERMINAL_GRANTED` | validate one direct FD-5 OBSERVED | `TERMINAL_OBSERVED` |
| P `TERMINAL_OBSERVED` | complete FINALIZE send | `FINALIZE_SENT` |
| G `TERMINAL_REPLY_SENT` | validate FINALIZE with local full-send flag | `FINALIZE_RECEIVED` |
| G `FINALIZE_RECEIVED` | complete FINALIZED_ACK send | `CLOSED_TOMBSTONE` |
| P `FINALIZE_SENT` | validate complete FINALIZED_ACK | `FINALIZED_ACKED` |
| P `FINALIZED_ACKED` | complete TERMINAL_RECEIPT send | `TERMINAL_RECEIPT_SENT` |
| P `TERMINAL_RECEIPT_SENT` | identity-valid clean FD-5 EOF | `FD5_EOF_OBSERVED` |
| P `FD5_EOF_OBSERVED` | requester reap plus P-peer close/absence | `CLOSED_TOMBSTONE` |

No sender's attempted send changes state before its complete return, and no
receiver changes state before exact validation.  An unchanged D-M2 audit is
not a D-M1 state and never advances a D-M1 edge.

P--G control EOF is terminal failure in every pre-FINALIZED_ACK state.  Once
P has received and validated the complete ACK, G's later orderly control
close from its local `CLOSED_TOMBSTONE` is permitted; it neither invalidates
nor proves P's still-required FD-5 receipt/EOF/reap/peer-close path.  No
post-ACK P--G message exists.

### 9.2 Complete successful order

The exact successful causal order is:

1. direct FD-5 registration and P draw of `create_cap`;
2. complete commitment/template CREATE_GRANTED;
3. complete requester receipt, first raw create-cap disclosure;
4. G's one immutable first FD-4 receive and exact CREATE_ACCEPTED return;
5. P's independent raw/frame/commitment/identity join before `reply_nonce`;
6. unchanged COMMIT, actual SESSION_CREATED, COMMITTED, and direct
   activation join;
7. P draw of `active_cap`, complete requester receipt, then commitment-only
   ACTIVE and complete ACK;
8. ACTIVE operations, with raw active-cap commitment check before the first
   non-child mutation and no later provenance overclaim;
9. one legal SESSION_CLOSE, complete inherited cleanup, then
   TERMINAL_PREPARED;
10. P draw of fresh `terminal_cap` and complete TERMINAL_GRANTED;
11. G's one exact complete terminal FD-4 send;
12. requester direct exact TERMINAL_OBSERVED on FD 5;
13. P complete FINALIZE and G complete FINALIZED_ACK;
14. complete TERMINAL_RECEIPT, clean FD-5 EOF, requester reap, and P-peer
    close.

No receive timeout, queue readiness, copied digest, expected value, G-only
send flag, direct record alone, EOF alone, or cleanup completion alone
advances a different success edge.

## 10. Total failures, tombstones, and teardown

### 10.1 Exact failure table

Every send is attempted once and succeeds only at the complete byte count.
There is no suffix retry.  The affected total table is:

| First failing edge | Required result before any later operation |
|---|---|
| create/active/terminal entropy fill or raw collision | retain exact partial/colliding P-only receipt; no redraw, forbidden disclosure, or success; abort/contain according to whether G has matching state |
| CREATE_GRANTED incomplete | raw create cap remains undisclosed to G/requester; no requester receipt; local failed tombstone/control containment; no retry |
| grant complete but requester receipt incomplete | P aborts; G discards arm/held packet; no COMMIT or construction; raw cap remains unknown to G |
| requester remains live but sends no create | no progress; no invented timeout success |
| first create datagram wrong in any coordinate | consume attempt; no second receive, accepted construction, object, or success; abort/tombstone |
| CREATE_ACCEPTED incomplete | no object; G local failed tombstone and containment; P cannot synthesize acceptance |
| P CREATE_ACCEPTED comparison fails | no `reply_nonce`, COMMIT, private construction, or retry; exact abort/containment |
| COMMIT through direct activation join fails | retain exact v6 no-retry INACTIVE cleanup; no active cap |
| ACTIVE_RECEIPT incomplete | raw active cap is not operational; send no ACTIVE; abort |
| ACTIVE or ACTIVE_ACK incomplete/invalid | G stays nonmutating for non-child operations; abort and clean |
| first active-cap-bearing packet wrong | no requested mutation; consume operation attempt; abort session without retry |
| SESSION_CLOSE or inherited cleanup fails | no TERMINAL_PREPARED; retained containment and foreign preservation; failed tombstone |
| TERMINAL_PREPARED incomplete/invalid | P draws/discloses no terminal cap; failed tombstone/containment |
| terminal-cap fill/collision | send no TERMINAL_GRANTED; retain partial/colliding P-only bytes and failed cleanup state; abort |
| TERMINAL_GRANTED incomplete/invalid | G sends no terminal reply; failed tombstone; no retry |
| terminal FD-4 send incomplete, short, zero, errno, or ambiguous | G never sets full-send flag; no second reply; abort/contain; no terminal success |
| requester remains live but sends no TERMINAL_OBSERVED | no progress; no invented success or timeout |
| TERMINAL_OBSERVED wrong, duplicate, malformed, premature, replayed, cross-session, or cross-endpoint | consume terminal attempt; send no FINALIZE; failed tombstone |
| requester FD-5 EOF before terminal receipt | failed tombstone; never clean close |
| FINALIZE incomplete/invalid | no FINALIZED_ACK success; failed tombstone |
| FINALIZED_ACK incomplete/invalid | P sends no terminal receipt; failed tombstone |
| TERMINAL_RECEIPT incomplete | P does not enter success tombstone; requester endpoint is contained; completed G cleanup is not relabelled P-observed success |
| clean FD-5 EOF absent after complete receipt | P does not enter CLOSED_TOMBSTONE; no EOF synthesis |
| P--G control EOF/crash before final ACK | retained whole-control freeze/kill/reap containment; no terminal success or ABSENT |
| ABORT or ABORTED incomplete | each side completes locally reachable cleanup and containment; missing wire record is never success evidence |

For a P-detected error, P sends `SESSION_AUTH_ABORT` only if G has installed
the matching auth state and the P--G channel remains usable.  A pre-grant
local error or failed send on the only channel does not fabricate ABORT.
For a G-detected error, G enters ABORTING locally, performs every reachable
cleanup, and may send `SESSION_AUTH_ABORTED` without a preceding P ABORT.
Duplicate/mismatched ABORT or ABORTED is fatal.

More precisely, P-detected create-return, activation, PREPARED, terminal-
entropy, direct-observation, FINALIZED_ACK, or receipt-precondition failure
uses ABORT with the mapped phase/reason only when the corresponding G state
exists and a complete ABORT send remains possible.  G-detected first-create,
create-send, private-construction, active-operation, terminal-grant, FD-4
terminal-send, or FINALIZE failure uses ABORTED after reachable cleanup when
the control send remains possible.  Failure of the very P--G send that would
carry CREATE_GRANTED, CREATE_ACCEPTED, PREPARED, GRANTED, FINALIZE, ACK,
ABORT, or ABORTED goes directly to the printed control-failure containment;
it never retries or reports success over that failed channel.

### 10.2 Terminal-cause and retention ledger

The changed close portion retains exactly one cause from this closed
in-memory enum:

~~~text
PREPARED_SEND | PREPARED_MISMATCH | TERMINAL_ENTROPY_FILL |
TERMINAL_ENTROPY_COLLISION | TERMINAL_GRANT_SEND |
TERMINAL_GRANT_MISMATCH | TERMINAL_FD4_SEND |
TERMINAL_OBSERVATION_MISSING | TERMINAL_OBSERVATION_MISMATCH |
SESSION_CLOSE_OR_CLEANUP | REQUESTER_EARLY_EOF | FINALIZE_SEND |
FINALIZE_MISMATCH |
FINALIZED_ACK_SEND | FINALIZED_ACK_MISMATCH |
TERMINAL_RECEIPT_SEND | TERMINAL_EOF_MISSING | CONTROL_EOF |
CONTROL_CRASH
~~~

Continuation is presentational.  It is not a wire field, detector, public
exit, artifact, or OUTCOME replacement.  Each live/terminal ledger retains
the exact auth/session/request/close tuple, endpoint identities, raw-value
digests, commitments, templates, reply bytes/digest, local full-send flag,
direct-observation hash, terminal cause, and exact inherited OUTCOME through
complete teardown.  P additionally retains every raw value under Section 3.
`CONTROL_EOF` in this cause enum denotes only a pre-FINALIZED_ACK loss; the
permitted orderly post-ACK G close is already-terminal lifecycle state and
is not a failure cause.

`OUTCOME` is always the already-computed inherited value.  UNSET remains
legal where inherited state yields UNSET; ABSENT remains legal only after
the complete owned-object-gone proof; no terminal failure upgrades an
outcome or deletes/relabels a foreign object.  Physical cleanup may already
be complete on a terminal-protocol failure, but auth success remains false.

Before cleanup each side closes admission, rejects queued frames, starts no
new operation, and uses only the retained cgroup/freeze/kill/reap and
capability-relative root/lock machinery.  A control EOF seen by G still
authorizes no pathname deletion; a control EOF seen by P uses its retained
cgroup containment.  Neither path emits PASS, successful CLOSED_TOMBSTONE,
or false ABSENT.

## 11. D-M2 and prior closures: exact non-regression
