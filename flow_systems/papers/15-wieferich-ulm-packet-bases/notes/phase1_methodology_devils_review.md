## 4. Exact theorem signature and typing

### 4.1 Objects and maps

For a rational prime p, define the profinite abelian group

    U_p = ∏ over primes ℓ≠p of Z_ℓ^×.

The exponent map is a continuous homomorphism

    e_p : (Z-hat,+) → (U_p,×),    a ↦ p^a.

The proof must establish that e_p is injective.  Compact-to-Hausdorff
injectivity then makes

    H_p = e_p(Z-hat)

a closed copy of Z-hat.  Only after that step is the quotient

    B_p = U_p/H_p

the intended bare compact profinite group with the required exact dual
sequence.

For every rational prime r:

- B_{p,(r)} is the characteristic pro-r Sylow subgroup of B_p;
- K_{p,r} = (B_{p,(r)})* is its discrete Pontryagin dual;
- S_r = ⊕ over n≥1 of (C_{r^n})^(aleph-zero); and
- P_r = S_r* = ∏ over n≥1 of (C_{r^n})^(aleph-zero).

The typing guards are:

- B_{p,(r)} is compact, countably based, abelian, and pro-r;
- K_{p,r} is discrete, countable, torsion, and r-primary;
- Pontryagin dual arrows reverse direction; and
- all quotients on the compact side are by closed subgroups.

### 4.2 Exact dual presentations

The exceptional branch r=p has no local ℓ=r unit coordinate.  Its active
dual presentation is

    0 → K_{p,p} → S_p → C_{p^∞} → 0.

For r≠p the local ℓ=r unit coordinate contributes a Prüfer summand on the
dual side, and the presentation has the form

    0 → K_{p,r} → S_r ⊕ C_{r^∞}
      → C_{r^∞} → 0,

where, after multiplication by an r-adic unit, the last map is

    Φ_{p,r}(s,z) = f_{p,r}(s) + r^{κ_r(p)}z.

For r=2 and p odd, the finite sign coordinate is absorbed abstractly into
the countably repeated S_2, but its restriction map must remain visibly
inside f_{p,2}.  “S_2 absorbs C_2” is an abstract isomorphism observation,
not permission to delete the sign contribution before computing Φ.

### 4.3 Signature and proposed conclusions

The locked arithmetic signature is

    κ_p(p) = 0;

    κ_r(p) = v_r(p^{r−1}−1)−1
    for odd r≠p;

    κ_2(p) = v_2(p²−1)−3
    for odd p.

These values are nonnegative in their stated domains.  The proposed full
Ulm ledger is:

    u_n(K_{p,r}) = aleph-zero for every finite n;

    r^ω K_{p,r} ≅ C_{r^{κ_r(p)}};

    if κ_r(p)>0, the sole nonzero transfinite invariant is
    u_{ω+κ_r(p)−1}=1;

    if κ_r(p)=0, all transfinite invariants vanish.

Here r^ωK means the intersection of r^nK over all finite n.  The compact
translation is:

    closure(Tor(B_{p,(r)})) ≅ P_r;

    B_{p,(r)}/closure(Tor(B_{p,(r)}))
      ≅ C_{r^{κ_r(p)}}.

The global theorem is precisely:

    B_p ≅_top B_q
    if and only if
    κ_r(p)=κ_r(q) for every rational prime r.

This is well typed.  It is a theorem about unmarked topological-group
isomorphism.  It says nothing about actual Q_p, preservation of conductor
labels, flows, Haar data, traces, operators, or measured enhancements.

## 5. Dual-reduced guard and classification applicability

The general classification theorem cannot be invoked merely because one
number κ has been computed.  The following chain is mandatory.

1. K_{p,r} is countable, hence B_{p,(r)} is countably based.
2. In the r=p branch, kernel absorption gives K_{p,p}≅S_p, which is
   reduced.
3. In the r≠p branch, r^ωK is finite.  Any divisible subgroup of K lies
   inside r^ωK, while a finite group has no nonzero divisible subgroup.
   Hence K_{p,r} is reduced.
4. Therefore B_{p,(r)} is dual-reduced: it has no nontrivial continuous
   torsion-free quotient.
5. The full finite and transfinite Ulm sequence, not only its last finite
   subgroup or its order, must be computed.
6. Only then may the countably based dual-reduced pro-r classification be
   applied factorwise.

Kiehlmann's Theorems 1.1 and 1.8 support exactly this guard: torsion closure
is dual to quotienting the discrete dual by its infinite-height subgroup,
and the full torsion sequence classifies the countably based
dual-reduced factor.  They do not compute the P15R sequence for us.
See [Kiehlmann, Classifications of Countably Based Abelian Profinite
Groups](https://arxiv.org/html/1101.3005v3).

This guard is present in the locked protocol/source package.  It must not be
compressed in the eventual proof to “by Ulm” or “by Kiehlmann.”

## 6. Complete iff proof plan

### 6.1 Common entry: exponent embedding and primary decomposition

The proof first has to show e_p is a closed embedding.  Off the missing
p-coordinate, local logarithms detect the r-adic components for r≠p.
For the p-adic component, primitive-divisor input detects every finite
p-primary quotient.  This is the only active Bang-Zsigmondy role.

Pontryagin duality then supplies the exact restriction sequences, and the
standard primary decomposition of an abelian profinite group gives

    B_p ≅ ∏ over r of B_{p,(r)}.

The Sylow factors are characteristic, which will later give the necessary
direction of the global iff.

### 6.2 Exceptional branch r=p: algebraic absorption

Let g:S_p→C_{p^∞} be the dual restriction map.  For each homogeneous block

    E_n = (C_{p^n})^(aleph-zero),

choose a cyclic summand R_n carrying the image of g restricted to E_n and
an infinite-rank complement T_n killed by that restriction:

    E_n = T_n ⊕ R_n,
    T_n ≅ (C_{p^n})^(aleph-zero).

With T=⊕T_n and R=⊕R_n, one obtains

    S_p = T ⊕ R,
    T ≅ S_p,
    ker(g) = T ⊕ ker(g restricted to R).

The remainder R is a direct sum of cyclic p-groups.  Kulikov's subgroup
theorem therefore makes ker(g restricted to R) another direct sum of
cyclic p-groups.  It has at most countably many summands of each order, all
of which are absorbed by the already countably infinite multiplicities in
T.  Consequently

    K_{p,p}=ker(g)≅S_p.

Hill's published formulation of Kulikov says that a subgroup of a direct
sum of cyclic primary groups is again a direct sum of cyclic groups.
See [Hill, Corollary 2](https://msp.org/pjm/1972/42-1/pjm-v42-n1-p08-s.pdf).

This establishes all finite invariants, r^ωK=0, κ_p(p)=0, dense compact
torsion, and no transfinite invariant.  It is a complete exceptional
branch; it does not need the withdrawn bounded-surjectivity lemma.

The eventual proof must display the block automorphism/triangular
elimination that produces R_n and T_n.  Merely saying “choose a basis
adapted to g” would hide the only algebraic step on which absorption
depends.

### 6.3 Off-local branch r≠p: exact-order saturation

The arithmetic lemma needed here is stronger than merely finding primes
whose order is divisible by r.  For each m≥1 it must give infinitely many
primes ℓ such that

    v_r(ℓ−1)=m
    and
    v_r(ord_ℓ(p))=m.

For odd r≠p, set

    F=Q(ζ_r),
    L=F(p^{1/r}),
    C=Q(ζ_{r^{m+1}}).

The Kummer extension L/F is nontrivial and ramified above p, while C/F can
ramify only above r.  Because p≠r, their intersection over F is F.  A
compatible Frobenius can therefore be chosen to:

- fix ζ_{r^m} but move ζ_{r^{m+1}}, forcing v_r(ℓ−1)=m; and
- act nontrivially on p^{1/r}, making p a non-rth-power modulo ℓ.

In a cyclic residue-field unit group whose order has r-valuation m,
non-rth-power is equivalent to the element's order having r-valuation m.
Chebotarev gives infinitely many such primes.  The theorem used here is
unconditional; the required Frobenius set has positive density.  See the
statement in [MIT 18.785, Lecture 28, Theorem
28.9](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2025/LectureNotes28.pdf).

For r=2 and odd p, Q(√p) is disjoint from the relevant 2-power cyclotomic
field because the former is ramified at the odd prime p.  The same
Frobenius combination makes p a quadratic nonresidue and fixes one
2-power cyclotomic level but not the next.  Thus the exact-order conclusion
also holds at r=2 off the diagonal.

The p=2 diagonal is deliberately absent from this argument.  Indeed, for
ℓ≡1 mod 8 the element 2 is a square modulo ℓ, so one cannot demand
v₂(ord_ℓ(2))=v₂(ℓ−1) at high 2-adic levels.  Amendment v2 correctly routes
this case through exceptional-primary absorption.

### 6.4 Height calculation

For r≠p, write a kernel element as (s,z) in
S_r⊕C_{r^∞}.  If it lies in r^nK for every n, its S_r projection lies in
the intersection of r^nS_r, which is zero.  The kernel equation then gives

    r^{κ_r(p)}z=0.

This proves the upper inclusion

    r^ωK_{p,r} ⊆ 0⊕C_{r^{κ_r(p)}}.

The reverse inclusion is the load-bearing use of exact-order saturation:
for every desired finite depth one chooses off-local cyclic coordinates
whose restriction values cancel the required root.  This constructs roots
inside the kernel, not merely in the ambient group.  It yields

    r^ωK_{p,r}=0⊕C_{r^{κ_r(p)}}.

The full proof must write this cancellation uniformly in the depth and
must not replace it with ambient divisibility of C_{r^∞}; ambient roots
need not satisfy the kernel equation.

### 6.5 Finite and transfinite ledger

Each homogeneous source block contains countably many unused cyclic
summands killed by the restriction map.  They give the lower bound
u_n≥aleph-zero at every finite n; countability gives the matching upper
bound.  Hence u_n=aleph-zero for all finite n.

The finite group r^ωK has its ordinary cyclic filtration.  When κ>0 this
places one one-dimensional Ulm contribution exactly at
ω+κ−1 and nowhere else; when κ=0 it contributes nothing.  This step must
be stated as a full ledger rather than inferred from the order of r^ωK
alone.

### 6.6 Compact translation and global iff

Kiehlmann's annihilator formula gives

    (closure(Tor(B_{p,(r)})))* ≅ K_{p,r}/r^ωK_{p,r}.

The finite ledger identifies this quotient with S_r, so the closure is
P_r.  Dualizing the inclusion of the infinite-height subgroup gives the
finite cyclic quotient of order r^{κ_r(p)}.

Necessity in the global theorem follows because a topological isomorphism
preserves characteristic Sylow factors and their intrinsic
torsion-closure quotients.  Sufficiency follows from equality of every
κ-coordinate together with the common finite ledger: factorwise Ulm
classification gives B_{p,(r)}≅B_{q,(r)}, and taking the product over all
r gives B_p≅B_q.

No marked labels are used in either direction.  This is therefore a
genuine bare-group classification, provided every ledger step above is
proved.

## 7. Arithmetic boundary checks

### 7.1 The locked κ classification

The three cases are exhaustive and disjoint:

| Domain | κ-value | Proof mechanism |

| r=p, including p=r=2 | 0 | exceptional-primary kernel absorption |

not to be extended outside its stated domain.

### 7.2 Explicit separation B₂ not isomorphic to B₃

At r=11:

    2^10−1 = 3·11·31,
    so κ_11(2)=1−1=0;

    3^10−1 = 8·11²·61,
    so κ_11(3)=2−1=1.

The intrinsic 11-primary torsion-closure quotients therefore have different
orders.  Hence B₂ is not topologically isomorphic to B₃.  This is a valid
corollary of the factor theorem and supplies a nonvacuous arithmetic
separation, but it is not by itself enough publication weight.

### 7.3 Universal recovery boundary

The theorem under review does not claim that

    p ↦ (κ_r(p)) over all r

is injective.  That problem remains OPEN.  In particular:

- the missing-coordinate identity κ_p(p)=0 does not single out p, because
  many off-diagonal coordinates can also vanish;
- B_p≅B_q iff κ(p)=κ(q) does not imply p=q without a separate injectivity
  theorem; and
- no marked conductor may be smuggled into the bare group to force
  recovery.

This open boundary is correctly frozen in the protocol, candidate lock,
amendments, and final source audit.

## 9. Strongest devil's-advocate counterargument

### 9.1 Steelman

After subtracting Kiehlmann, Kulikov, Pontryagin duality, local unit
structure, Kummer theory, and Chebotarev, the manuscript may look like a
routine substitution into known machinery:

- Kiehlmann already says that the full torsion sequence classifies;
- Kulikov already controls subgroups of direct sums of cyclic groups;
- the κ-values are familiar Wieferich valuations;
- the global iff merely restates equality of the complete invariant just
  computed;
- B₂ not isomorphic to B₃ is a one-prime congruence calculation; and
- without injectivity of p↦κ(p), the result does not recover the numerical
  prime or even prove that all differently labelled packet bases differ.

On that reading, P15R is an appendix to P2 or a short arithmetic example
for a general profinite-classification theorem, not one of the batch's
full-paper centers.  Since P17 is the sole Technical Note candidate, this
would force P15R to merge or stop.

### 9.2 Adjudication

The counterargument is not sustained at the design level, but it remains
the decisive post-proof publication-weight test.

What prevents the current center from being mere substitution is the
joined load-bearing package:

1. the natural Deninger quotient has a missing local coordinate exactly at
   r=p, so the diagonal cannot be handled by the generic off-local
   argument;
2. the exceptional diagonal needs a separate countable kernel-absorption
   theorem with a visible algebraic decomposition;
3. the off-diagonal needs simultaneous exact-order primes, not just
   ordinary order divisibility or a formal Chebotarev citation;
4. the arithmetic lemma must be converted into kernel-internal roots at
   every finite depth;
5. those roots determine a transfinite Ulm tail and an intrinsic compact
   torsion-closure quotient; and
6. the all-prime assembly produces a complete isomorphism criterion for a
   natural arithmetic family.

No single cited classification or arithmetic source carries that chain.
The two-branch repair also explains a real obstruction: the tempting
uniform r=p route is false, especially at p=r=2.  A proof that makes this
dichotomy and the height cancellation explicit has plausible full-paper
weight.

The devil argument wins, however, if the eventual proof does any of the
following:

- treats exact-order saturation as an unproved “standard consequence”;
- omits the triangular block decomposition at r=p;
- records only |r^ωK| and never proves all finite Ulm invariants;
- invokes Kiehlmann without the countable dual-reduced guard;
- presents only B₂ versus B₃ rather than the full iff theorem; or
- markets a marked conductor or universal prime recovery as intrinsic.

In any such outcome the project is MERGE_OR_STOP, not a Technical Note.

## 10. Can the paper be complete without universal prime recovery?

Yes.  There are two different classification questions:

1. classify the bare compact groups B_p up to topological isomorphism; and
2. recover the external numerical label p from the unmarked group.

The proposed theorem completely answers the first by describing the
equivalence relation:

    B_p≅B_q exactly when κ(p)=κ(q).

It need not prove that every equivalence class is a singleton.  Possible
collisions of the arithmetic signatures would not invalidate the
classification; they would be part of the classification.  Universal
injectivity is a stronger arithmetic rigidity statement.

Accordingly, universal recovery is not logically required for a coherent
paper center.  The center remains a complete full-family structural
theorem if, and only if, the exact factor ledger, compact intrinsic
formula, and global iff are all proved.  The explicit B₂/B₃ result shows
that the invariant is nonconstant.  It does not replace the full-family
theorem.
