## 10. R3/P4: closed K2/H4 grammar and semantic reducer

This section is complete. Every uppercase symbol ending in _BYTES that is

### 10.1 Primitive bytes and terminal text grammars

BYTE_LENGTH(x) is the exact number of octets in x. CONCAT(x1,...,xn) is the
ordered octet concatenation with no separator. ASCII_BYTES(s) is the exact
7-bit ASCII encoding of literal s. LF=0x0A, TAB=0x09, COLON=0x3A,
COMMA=0x2C, and SLASH=0x2F.

~~~text
U16BE(x)=exactly two unsigned big-endian bytes, 0<=x<=65535
U64BE(x)=exactly eight unsigned big-endian bytes, 0<=x<=18446744073709551615
BOOL(false)=0x00
BOOL(true)=0x01
TOKEN(s)=ASCII_BYTES(s), where s is one exact closed token declared at use

FIELD(tag,value)=
  U16BE(BYTE_LENGTH(ASCII_BYTES(tag)))
  || ASCII_BYTES(tag)
  || U64BE(BYTE_LENGTH(value))
  || value

SEQ(item_tag,item_1,...,item_n)=
  U64BE(n)
  || FIELD(item_tag,item_1)
  || ...
  || FIELD(item_tag,item_n)
~~~

FIELD tag is nonempty ASCII and at most 65535 bytes. SEQ n is the exact
number of following same-tag FIELD items. Every structured production below
is its printed ordered FIELD concatenation. There is no padding, alignment,
native endian, NUL terminator, implicit delimiter, unknown/missing/
duplicate/reordered tag, extra item, or trailing byte.

~~~text
CANONICAL_DECIMAL(0)=ASCII_BYTES("0")
CANONICAL_DECIMAL(n>0)=ASCII_BYTES(first digit 1..9 followed by zero or more digits 0..9)
DECIMAL_SEMANTIC_RANGE=0..18446744073709551615

KERNEL_OCTAL(0)=ASCII_BYTES("0")
KERNEL_OCTAL(n>0)=ASCII_BYTES("0") || ASCII_BYTES(first magnitude digit 1..7 followed by zero or more digits 0..7)

PID_DIR_RELATIVE_BYTES=CANONICAL_DECIMAL(l_outer_pid)
STAT_RELATIVE_BYTES=ASCII_BYTES("stat")
FD_DIR_RELATIVE_BYTES=ASCII_BYTES("fd")
B_FDINFO_RELATIVE_BYTES=ASCII_BYTES("fdinfo/") || CANONICAL_DECIMAL(Bfd)
~~~

Decimal and octal parsers reject sign, empty input, nonminimal leading zero,
whitespace, NUL, LF, overflow, suffix, and trailing byte. They independently
re-encode and require exact equality to the source value span.

CONTROLLER_ATOM_BYTES is one or more bytes from ASCII A--Z, a--z, 0--9,
underscore, period, or hyphen. CONTROLLER_TOKEN_BYTES is either exactly one
CONTROLLER_ATOM_BYTES or ASCII_BYTES("name=") followed by exactly one
CONTROLLER_ATOM_BYTES. Equals is legal only in that name= prefix. Tokens are
strictly ascending by unsigned-byte lexicographic order and unique.

CONTROLLER_LIST_RAW_BYTES is empty for unified v2, otherwise the ordered
COMMA-joined CONTROLLER_TOKEN_BYTES values with no empty item. A unified-v2
record has hierarchy_id=0 and empty controller list; a legacy record has
nonzero hierarchy and at least one controller.

CGROUP_PATH_RAW_BYTES is at least one byte, begins with SLASH, contains no
NUL or LF, and is retained byte-for-byte without normalization, escaping,
case folding, or rewriting. A source cgroup line is exactly:

~~~text

  CANONICAL_DECIMAL(hierarchy_id)
  || COLON
  || CONTROLLER_LIST_RAW_BYTES
  || COLON
  || CGROUP_PATH_RAW_BYTES
  || LF
~~~

Entries are unique and sorted by numeric hierarchy_id, then encoded
controller sequence bytes, then raw path bytes. Source order must already
match. The complete cgroup source is the ordered concatenation of one or
more CGROUP_RAW_LINE_BYTES and ends exactly after the last LF.

The four permitted relative components are exactly the four relative byte
productions above. Leading slash, empty component, dot, dot-dot, duplicate
or trailing slash, escape, normalization, reopened root, wrong parent,
wrong PID, or wrong Bfd is forbidden. PID_DIR is relative only to the

### 10.10 Raw-to-semantic-to-rebuild reducer and direct equality
