## 3. C14 committed-carrier lifecycle

### 3.1 Exact tuple and coordinate meanings

The v14 tuple, in this exact order, is:

~~~text
C14 = (RE,YE,AE,SS,E_PG,E_GP)
~~~

All six coordinates are binary and monotone.

- RE=1 iff one exact state-qualified canonical v14
  PRIVILEGE_DROP_RELEASE frame actually completes queue insertion on the
  real P-to-G connection in its sole authorized slot.
- YE=1 iff one exact state-qualified canonical v14 GUARDIAN_READY frame
  actually completes queue insertion on the real G-to-P connection in its
  sole authorized slot.
- AE=1 iff one exact state-qualified canonical v14 GUARDIAN_READY_ACK frame
  actually completes queue insertion P-to-G in its sole authorized slot.
- SS=1 iff one exact state-qualified canonical v14 BOOTSTRAP_SEALED frame
  actually completes queue insertion G-to-P in its sole authorized slot.
- E_PG=1 iff at least one recognizable complete member of the old-boundary
  form set is actually enqueued P-to-G outside that form's sole authorized
  P-to-G slot before the AE cut, or before the reconciled terminal
  checkpoint when AE=0. P has two form-specific authorized old-form slots:
  LAUNCHER_REAPED and PRIVILEGE_DROP_RELEASE.
- E_GP=1 iff at least one recognizable complete member of the old-boundary
  form set is actually enqueued G-to-P outside that form's sole authorized
  G-to-P slot before the SS cut, or before the reconciled terminal
  checkpoint when SS=0. G's sole authorized old-form slot is
  GUARDIAN_READY.

The old-boundary form set remains exactly:

~~~text
LAUNCHER_REAPED
PRIVILEGE_DROP_RELEASE
GUARDIAN_READY
~~~

RE, YE, AE, and SS change only at their actual enqueue events and never
roll back. A committed pre-cut extra changes its direction E bit
immediately and never rolls back. E_PG closes at AE, or at the reconciled
failure checkpoint if AE never occurs. E_GP closes at SS, or at that
checkpoint if SS never occurs.

E_PG/E_GP are direction-owned enqueue facts, not receiver observations and
not owner-local aliases. A receiver's exact queued frame and a sender's
valid endpoint-bound full-return receipt may witness the same enqueue; they
never create two transitions. A local partial syscall, positive proper
prefix, errno, receive invocation, parse, validation, hash, freeze, or
staging step sets no coordinate. A complete unrecognizable malformed record
may support raw MALFORMED without setting E. A complete recognizable
duplicate, replay, wrong-direction, reordered, or wrong-state old form sets
the direction-owned E bit and every independently supported raw bit.

Only pre-directional-cut extras enter E. A post-cut old-form enqueue is a
later-protocol violation and independent containment fact. It cannot change
C14, SS, raw17, a boundary winner, or an already frozen tombstone, and it
receives no HC-backed sender-full-return enqueue inference.

probe records, and other earlier authenticated setup traffic are outside

## 10. Conditional Linux-v5.15 enqueue implication

### 10.1 Exact implication

All scoped framed payloads are far below UINT32_MAX. Under exact upstream
Linux v5.15, the one-instruction RET K 0xffffffff local cBPF returns at least
the skb length and therefore neither rejects nor trims it. Readback proves
the original cBPF fields. Lock, three EPERM negative tests, and second
readback prove that the local sk_filter cannot be unlocked, detached, or
replaced by the endpoint holder.

This mechanical receipt does not prove hidden-holder absence, future
custody, security-hook behavior, cgroup sockopt transparency, kernel
immutability, or syscall-return integrity. Those are precisely the HC model
predicates.

Only while actual HP/HG/HM predicates and every HC predicate apply to the
actual endpoints and complete scoped window may the design use:

~~~text

  => receiver-side checks do not reject or trim
  => exact skb_queue_tail of one complete same-byte record occurred
~~~

MECH is the post-RE commitment to component predicates; it is not a causal
prerequisite for LAUNCHER, RE, or a pre-MECH E witness. The implication is
not an equivalence: enqueue may occur before sender death without a retained
return, in which case the peer's queued exact frame is the witness.

The HC-backed scope includes LAUNCHER_REAPED, RE, YE, AE, SS, and every
recognizable pre-directional-cut old-form E witness. A cutless direction
continues to reconciliation. The window begins only after both role-local
filter locks and holder freezes and before LAUNCHER.

seal return and its child stub performs closure only; EP_P and hook/kernel

scoped send, and both closes.
