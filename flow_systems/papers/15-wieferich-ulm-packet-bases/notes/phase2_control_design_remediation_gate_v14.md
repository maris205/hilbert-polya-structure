### 3.1 Exact tuple and event meanings

The amendment must wholly replace C11/C13 at this boundary with:

```text
C14 = (RE,YE,AE,SS,E_PG,E_GP)
```

All six coordinates are binary and monotone. `RE/YE/AE/SS` change from zero
to one only at their actual enqueue events and never roll back. A committed
pre-cut extra changes its direction E bit to one immediately and never rolls
back; `E_PG` closes at the AE cut, or at the reconciled failure checkpoint
when `AE=0`, while `E_GP` closes at the SS cut, or at that checkpoint when
`SS=0`. A failure row, boundary raw17 receipt, and failure tombstone become
final only after Section-5 reconciliation. The sole success row becomes
final immediately at SS's bilateral clean cut; later drain/close evidence is
audit only and cannot flow back into C14. The coordinates mean exactly:

- `RE=1` iff one exact, state-qualified, canonical v14
  `PRIVILEGE_DROP_RELEASE` frame actually completes queue insertion on the
  real P-to-G connection in its sole authorized slot.
- `YE=1` iff one exact, state-qualified, canonical v14 `GUARDIAN_READY` frame
  actually completes queue insertion on the real G-to-P connection in its
  sole authorized slot.
- `AE=1` iff one exact, state-qualified, canonical v14
  `GUARDIAN_READY_ACK` frame actually completes queue insertion P-to-G in its
  sole authorized slot.
- `SS=1` iff one exact, state-qualified, canonical v14 `BOOTSTRAP_SEALED`
  frame actually completes queue insertion G-to-P in its sole authorized
  slot.
- `E_PG=1` iff at least one recognizable complete member of the old-boundary
  form set is actually enqueued P-to-G outside that form's sole authorized
  P-to-G slot before the AE cut, or before the reconciled terminal checkpoint
  when `AE=0`. P has exactly two form-specific authorized old-form slots:
  `LAUNCHER_REAPED` and `PRIVILEGE_DROP_RELEASE`; it has no single generic
  old-form slot.
- `E_GP=1` iff at least one recognizable complete member of the old-boundary
  form set is actually enqueued G-to-P outside that form's sole authorized
  G-to-P slot before the SS cut, or before the reconciled terminal checkpoint
  when `SS=0`. G's sole authorized old-form slot is `GUARDIAN_READY`.

`E_PG/E_GP` are direction-owned enqueue facts, not receiver observations and
not owner-local `X` aliases. A receiver's exact queued frame and a sender's
valid endpoint-bound full-return receipt are two possible witnesses of the
same enqueue, never two transitions. A local partial syscall, positive
proper prefix, errno, receive invocation, parse, validation, hash, freeze, or
staging event sets no C14 coordinate. A complete but unrecognizable malformed
record may support raw `MALFORMED` without setting E. A complete recognizable
duplicate/replay/wrong-direction/reordered old form sets the direction-owned
E and every independently supported raw bit.

Only committed pre-cut extras enter E: the P-to-G cut is AE and the G-to-P
cut is SS. An old-form enqueue after its direction's public irreversible cut
is an inherited later-protocol violation and independent downstream
containment fact. It does not change C14, SS, the boundary raw17 bitmap or
winner, or an already frozen boundary tombstone. Such a post-cut enqueue is
unreachable inside the trusted non-Byzantine profile. It is outside v14's
HC-backed sender-full-return inference and is retained, if at all, only by
inherited exact receive/validation and independent containment evidence.
When a direction's cut never occurs, its pre-cut interval ends only at the
reconciled failure checkpoint.

### 3.2 Exact invariants and success predicate

```text
YE <= RE
AE <= YE
SS <= AE

AE == 1  => E_PG == 0
SS == 1  => E_PG == 0 and E_GP == 0

E_PG == 1 or E_GP == 1 => FAILURE

SUCCESS iff
  (RE,YE,AE,SS,E_PG,E_GP) == (1,1,1,1,0,0)
  and the Section-7 model ceiling is the accepted claim scope
```

The ACK publishes P's clean P-to-G direction cut, so `AE=>E_PG=0`; it does
**not** publish `E_GP=0`. Under inherited v11 ordering, G completes READY,
then freezes its old-form emission ledger. A fail-closed negative trace may
enqueue READY, then enqueue a G-to-P old-form extra before G's own freeze,
while P receives READY and enqueues ACK. G later detects the prior extra and
refuses seal. Thus row 14 below is `(E_PG,E_GP)=(0,1)`. The impossible mirror
`(1,0)` would require trusted P to enqueue ACK after publishing a dirty own
direction cut. SS alone publishes both directions clean.

## 5. Terminal reconciliation, crash matrix, and tombstones

### 5.1 Deterministic terminal checkpoint

The numbered algorithm governs failure finalization. It also supplies
terminal evidence for the distinct non-live success-audit case described
after step 7; that case never becomes a failure row. Normal live success
finalizes at the actual SS enqueue and requires neither owner death nor
drain-to-EOF. A timeout, errno, peer death, local error, or missing successor
may begin containment but may not freeze a failure row. Before the one
immutable failure terminal receipt is created, the design must:

1. irreversibly stop both owners from making any further in-scope boundary
   enqueue;
2. establish the Section-7 holder/profile ceiling and no alternate reader;
3. retain exactly one authenticated owner survivor and that owner's retained
   actual endpoint and endpoint-bound evidence;
4. after peer close/death, drain the survivor's inbound actual queue one
   record at a time through every queued record and then exact EOF;
5. reconcile the survivor's outbound endpoint-bound enqueue receipts for
   records sent into the dead peer, with exact form, direction, frame bytes,
   and one-use identity;
6. count every committed pre-AE P-to-G and pre-SS G-to-P out-of-slot old-form
   record once in `E_PG/E_GP`, while retaining any post-cut record only as
   independent downstream-containment/nonconformance evidence; and
7. only then freeze the maximal carrier prefix, direction bits, boundary
   raw17 bitmap,
   winner, owner tie, Q phase, exact frames/digests, endpoint/profile hashes,
   and secondary evidence references.

If G actually enqueues the exact seal and dies before observing its exact
full return, SS changed to one at enqueue and row 15 was already the sole
success row. P's surviving exact queued seal before EOF is a non-live success
audit under the terminal branch of HC item 38. The audit retains row 15,
never routes it through failure finalization, and never delays or rolls back
SS. G observed no return and therefore exercised no governed write or normal
post-bootstrap clone authority. The same rule applies whenever an owner dies
after the enqueue but before the later non-coordinate live-cut observation.

A live-peer timeout while a producer can still enqueue is not terminal.
`MISSING` cannot conceal an unresolved in-flight record. If quiesce,
drain, and reconciliation cannot complete, no exact C14 row is claimed.
Any double-owner loss, with or without surviving endpoint descriptions,
remains outside scope as `UNIVERSAL_RECOVER_P=OPEN_NOT_AUTHORIZED`; an
undefined frozen description pair is not shared persistence or recovery
authority.
