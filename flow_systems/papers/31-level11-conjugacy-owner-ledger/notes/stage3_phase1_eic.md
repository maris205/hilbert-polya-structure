## Contract Paraphrase

D1 establishes a mandatory methodology-rigor bar: the methodology seat must determine whether study design, evidence handling, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility support permit dependable peer evaluation.

D2 makes domain accuracy mandatory: the domain seat must check that technical terminology, factual statements, reported results, and representations of prior scholarship accord with current field knowledge.

D3 requires mandatory argumentative coherence: the eligible seats must assess whether the central thesis is internally consistent and whether the reasoning and evidence adequately support the claims made.

D4 assigns high priority to cross-disciplinary relevance: the perspective seat must examine whether framing, definitions, and implications are intelligible to adjacent-field readers and whether interdisciplinary connections are substantiated.

D5 assigns the EIC seat normal-priority responsibility for writing and structure: editorial assessment will consider organization, clarity, notation, figures and tables, and consistent separation of mathematical definitions, computational representations, audit evidence, completed results, and prospective work under generally applicable scholarly conventions.

D6 assigns the EIC seat a mandatory contribution assessment; because criteria binding is unavailable, this assessment is limited to field-general originality, significance, and value for an identifiable scholarly readership and does not judge alignment with a named venue or track, or submission readiness.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A coherent arithmetic-dynamics narrative with clear definitions and notation, readable data and audit representations, and an explicit separation among mathematical objects, computational procedures, evidence, completed findings, and prospective work.
what_triggers_block: Structural or presentation defects prevent reliable comprehension of the central scholarly contribution or make essential mathematical, computational, or ledger relationships unusable.
what_triggers_warn: Localized problems in exposition, sequencing, notation, tables, data representations, or evidence-status labeling impede reading but remain repairable without fundamental reframing.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A clearly substantiated field-general contribution whose originality and significance are meaningful to readers in arithmetic dynamics, modular geodesic theory, computational algebra, exact canonicalization, or proof-carrying data methods.
what_triggers_block: The case for originality, significance, or scholarly-readership value is materially inadequate, although substantial reframing and stronger substantiation could make the contribution assessable.
what_triggers_warn: A potentially worthwhile field contribution is visible, but its novelty, importance, or value to the identified scholarly readership is incompletely articulated and needs focused strengthening.
what_triggers_fatal: The proposed contribution is demonstrably duplicative without substantive advance or wholly outside the stated scholarly field, leaving no field-general original and significant contribution for an identifiable readership.
