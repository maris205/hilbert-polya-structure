### Summary Assessment

The manuscript makes a clear design correction: a deterministic canonicalization biconditional becomes the foundational certificate, while the 9,453-pair expansion becomes a derived adversarial audit rather than thousands of independent proof obligations. The distinction among the global owner table G, occurrence-level incidence relation I, and cell-local quotient C is also carefully developed and should help readers avoid information-loss and double-credit errors. The article repeatedly and correctly states that no canonicalizer, owner partition, audit, or estimand table has been executed, preserving its A1-preparation boundary without awarding route credit. Its mathematical progression is coherent, but the reader-facing narrative is interrupted by internal revision decisions, phase labels, and project tokens. More substantively, the paper states that no novelty search was authorized and provides no reader-resolvable artifact manifest for its reproducibility claims. The architecture is potentially valuable to arithmetic dynamics and proof-carrying ledger design, but its originality and significance require substantial, repairable positioning. This assessment is field-general and makes no named-venue, track, or submission-readiness claim.
