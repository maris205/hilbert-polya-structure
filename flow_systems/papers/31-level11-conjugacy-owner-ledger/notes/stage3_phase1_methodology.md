## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: A precisely scoped canonicalization biconditional covering orientation, primitive roots, conjugacy, sign and representation choices, deterministic byte equality, positive and negative witnesses, termination, collisions, serialization stability, and an independent validator for derived ledger rows.
what_triggers_block: A central owner or ledger conclusion would depend on a materially incomplete or one-directional canonicalization criterion, unstable serialization, or a validator unable to establish the required equivalence and replay obligations without substantial redesign.
what_triggers_warn: The canonicalization method would remain viable, but important edge cases, witness formats, termination arguments, collision handling, or cross-implementation replay details would be underspecified.
what_triggers_fatal: A single unrecoverable contradiction in the core equivalence relation or canonical-byte criterion would make correct owner classification impossible under the declared method.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A sound two-way implication from the declared oriented primitive-owner equivalence to canonical-byte equality, with inputs, derived audit rows, validator evidence, and conclusion scope kept logically distinct.
what_triggers_block: A central claim would rely on an unsupported reverse implication, circularly use the ledger as proof of its own canonicalizer, or conflate design inputs with derived evidence in a way requiring substantial reanalysis or reframing.
what_triggers_warn: The central reasoning would remain defensible, but one or more implication directions, representation qualifications, evidence-status distinctions, or limitations would need explicit clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: A single irreparable inconsistency between canonical equality and the declared owner equivalence would invalidate the core thesis or make acceptance impossible.
