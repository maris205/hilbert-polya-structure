## 3. P13-8A cardinality and topology feasibility

### 3.1 Exact cardinality chain and mandatory internal prior

The proposed argument is correct and uses no continuum hypothesis, but its
lower-bound core is not new.  Sealed Paper 2, Proposition
`prop:uncountable` (lines 391--436), already takes

```text
S_p=product_(ell in I_p){+1,-1},
```

with the omitted allowed coordinate fixed at the identity, proves
`|S_p|=2^aleph_0`, and uses procyclicity to prove

```text
|S_p intersect H_p| <= 2.
```

It then proves that the quotient image of `S_p` has cardinality continuum.
This is the same sign-subgroup/procyclic lower bound proposed in P13-8A, not
merely a nearby argument.  Its mandatory status survives the fact that Paper
2 calls the quotient `B_p=G_p/H_p`: P13 must rebind the conclusion to the
exact Paper-9 actual/bare owner and must not claim independent credit.

The only cardinal increment is the converse bound.  Each `Z_ell^x` has
cardinality at most continuum and there are countably many prime coordinates,
so

```text
|U_p| <= (2^aleph_0)^aleph_0=2^aleph_0.
```

Combining that elementary upper bound with Paper 2's inherited lower bound
gives `|Q_p^bare|=|U_p/H_p|=2^aleph_0`.  No size estimate for `H_p` is needed,
and no topology or Haar measure enters.  This equality/retyping step is
source-feasible but carries no substantive or novelty weight.

### 3.2 Topology consequences

Stacks Project Section 5.29, Tag `0B1W`, explicitly gives set-indexed
topological coproducts and their componentwise topology.  Paper 12 then
proves on the exact owner that

```text
Std(Gamma_p)=coproduct_(q in Q_p^bare) O_q,
O_q ~= R/(log p)Z,
```

with every `O_q` nonempty, open, closed, compact, and Hausdorff.  The new
consequences are elementary and direct:

- a second-countable space cannot contain an uncountable disjoint family of
  nonempty open subsets;
- a compact subset of a coproduct meets only finitely many components,
  because the component opens cover it; and
- a countable union of such compact subsets meets at most countably many
  components.

Hence `Q_p^disc`, `Std(Gamma_p)`, and its arrow space are not second
countable and not sigma-compact.  The amendment correctly leaves
`Q_p^actual` indiscrete and second countable.  Stacks supplies only the
coproduct convention; the compact-intersection, second-countability, and
sigma-compactness arguments remain direct Paper-13 proofs.

## 5. Time-diagonal faithfulness and norm chain

The norm conclusion (5.1) is feasible, but its exact inequality chain is a
mandatory proof rather than a consequence of the symbol `d_(q,sigma)`.

At test level, the constant coefficient function

```text
d_(q,sigma)(f)(t)=f(t) 1_(C(O_q))
```

belongs to the component dense algebra because `C(O_q)` is unital.  Its
product and star reduce exactly to the time formulas.  Under a faithful
coefficient representation, the component regular covariant representation
restricts to `Lambda_sigma` on this subalgebra.  Therefore

```text
||d_q(f)||_r=||f||_(C*_r(R,sigma)).
```

For the maximal norm, restriction of every component representation gives

```text
||d_q(f)||_max <= ||f||_(C*max(R,sigma)).
```

The quotient from maximal to reduced supplies the opposite endpoint, and
Austad Proposition 2.4, or equivalently the proved gauge followed by
Williams Theorem 7.13, gives time full/reduced equality.  Thus

```text
||f||_time,max
  >= ||d_q(f)||_component,max
  >= ||d_q(f)||_component,r
   = ||f||_time,r
   = ||f||_time,max.
```

All inequalities are equalities.  This proves faithfulness in both norms.
It also proves that the completed image lands in `B_(q,sigma)^epsilon`, not
merely in its multiplier algebra: approximate a time completion element by
`C_c(R)`; the isometric images lie in the component algebra and converge
there.

Williams Proposition 2.34 alone would not close this gate.  It says that the
canonical **group-valued** multiplier homomorphism is injective; an
injective group representation need not induce a faithful representation of
the full group C-star algebra.  The amendment correctly requires the
regular restriction and amenability argument instead.

## 6. Arbitrary `c0` sum, multiplier product, and corona

The retained sources do not contain a frozen exact locator for the full
arbitrary-index assertion

```text
M(direct_sum_q^c0 B_q)=product_q^bounded M(B_q).
```

The amendment's “prove or source” clause is therefore resolved here as
**DIRECT PROOF REQUIRED**.  No new source is necessary.  A sufficient proof
must establish:

1. every multiplier of `A=direct_sum_q^c0 B_q` preserves each coordinate
   ideal `B_q` (use an approximate unit of `A`), yielding a uniformly bounded
   family in `product_q M(B_q)`;
2. conversely, a uniformly bounded family of coordinate multipliers acts
   coordinatewise on `A`, preserves the `c0` condition, and has the
   coordinatewise adjoint; and
3. these constructions are inverse and isometric.

Under this identification, `A` itself consists of the coordinate families
`(b_q)` with `||b_q||` vanishing at infinity.  The isometric component
embeddings give

```text
||d_q(a)||=||a||
```

for every `q`.  Hence a nonzero diagonal is in `A` exactly when `Q` is
finite.  For infinite `Q`, the quotient map

```text
M(A) -> M(A)/A
```

has kernel `A`, so its restriction to the diagonal has zero kernel.  A
faithful star homomorphism between C-star algebras is isometric, giving the
claimed corona injection.  No additional corona theorem is needed.

The component gauge isomorphisms take `c0` sums coordinatewise and extend
uniquely to multiplier algebras and their corona quotients.  The dense-level
identity

```text
U_(alpha,q) d_(q,sigma)=d_(q,tau) U_alpha
```

therefore proves every commuting square after completion.  This is a direct
functorial consequence of the proved isomorphisms, not a new global groupoid
functoriality claim.
