## 4. Algebraic cochain and gauge lock

The coefficient is the usual Hausdorff circle `T`, with trivial action.
Use the phrase "normalized continuous T-valued group 2-cocycle (projective
multiplier)" at first occurrence.  The pointwise-multiplicative groups are

    C^1_n,cont(G;T) = {a:G->T continuous : a(x,0)=1},
    Z^2_n,cont(G;T) = {normalized continuous sigma satisfying the cocycle},
    B^2_n,cont(G;T) = delta(C^1_n,cont(G;T)),
    H^2_tw(G;T) = Z^2_n,cont(G;T) / B^2_n,cont(G;T).

This is an abstract group; no quotient topology is asserted.  With the
range-first right-action convention,

    sigma(x;t,u) sigma(x;t+u,v)
      = sigma(x.t;u,v) sigma(x;t,u+v),

    (delta a)(x;t,u)
      = a(x,t) a(x.t,u) overline{a(x,t+u)}.

The protocol must verify directly that `delta a` is normalized and is a
cocycle.  For normalized multipliers `sigma,tau`, freeze

    sigma ~ tau  iff  sigma overline{tau} = delta a.

The gauge map has the oriented type

    U_a : A_sigma -> A_tau,       (U_a f)(t)=a(t)f(t).

Thus `sigma=delta alpha` means `sigma~1` and
`U_alpha:A_sigma->A_1`.  Every later product, star, representation, and norm
identity uses this direction.

## 7. New central support-transfer target

Let `X` have common cocompact stabilizer `H=L Z`, with bare nonempty orbit
set `Q=X/R`, and let `J:G_std(X)->G_actual(X)` be the continuous identity
functor from Section 3.3.  For `f in C_c(R)`, Paper 11 supplies

    Phi_actual(f)(x,t)=f(t),
    supp(Phi_actual(f)) = X x supp_R(f).

The actual support is quasi-compact by the Paper-11 projection criterion.
The pullback is the same pointwise function,

    J^*Phi_actual(f)(x,t)=f(t),

and its standard-owner support is

    Std(X) x supp_R(f).

The candidate theorem to prove is

    J^*Phi_actual(f) belongs to C_c(G_std(X))
      iff f=0 or Q is finite.

For nonzero `f`, this is the exact finite-orbit criterion.  The proof must
use that every standard orbit `R/H` is nonempty compact Hausdorff, that
`Std(X)` is their topological coproduct, and that an infinite coproduct of
nonempty compact summands is not compact.  Here `C_c(G_std(X))` means only
the ordinary space of continuous compactly supported functions on the
Hausdorff arrow space.  No standard groupoid completion or analytic theorem
is inferred.

Every allowed time gauge is circle-valued and nowhere zero, hence preserves
support exactly.  The same finite-orbit criterion must therefore hold on the
twisted author test space after any proved trivialization.  When `Q` is
finite, the proof may additionally check the resulting test-function star
map.  When `Q` is infinite, cochain pullback remains defined and continuous,
but the nonzero global-QC time functions do not land in the standard
compact-support test space.

For the fixed-prime packet, substitute `H=(log p)Z` and `Q=Q_p` only as a
bare set.  The result is conditional:

    nonzero support transfer holds iff Q_p is finite.

Paper 13 makes no claim that `Q_p` is finite or infinite and imports no
actual topology on `Q_p`.  This theorem is the candidate owner-specific
obstruction that the standalone review must assess; its truth alone does not
prejudge `STANDALONE_PASS`.
