### 4.1 Two elementary choice lemmas

Let `c=(x,iota)` and `c'=(x',iota')` be two permitted choice pairs.

**Injection lemma.**  For `K=Qbar` and `C=C`, both root-of-unity groups are
abstractly `Q/Z`.  An injective endomorphism of each Prüfer primary component
is multiplication by an `ell`-adic unit, hence is an automorphism.  Therefore
there is a unique

```text
epsilon in Zhat^x
```

such that, after using `iota` to identify the two root groups,

```text
iota' = iota o ( )^epsilon.
```

Restriction `Zhat^x -> U_p` is surjective.

**Point-change lemma.**  Choose `sigma in G=Aut_Q(Qbar)` with
`sigma x=x'`, and let `bar_sigma:kappa(x)->kappa(x')` be the induced residue
isomorphism.  Compatibility of reduction gives

```text
i_{x'}^{-1} o bar_sigma o i_x = sigma|mu_(p)(K).
```

Write `chi_c=iota o i_x^{-1}` and similarly for `c'`.  Define
`d(c,c';sigma) in U_p` by

```text
chi_{c'} o bar_sigma = chi_c o ( )^d.                     (4.1)
```

With the displayed convention,

```text
d = (epsilon * chi_cyc(sigma))|_(away from p).             (4.2)
```

Only the class of `d` in `B_p` matters.  If another `sigma'` also maps `x`
to `x'`, then `sigma^{-1}sigma'` stabilizes `x`.  Equation (34) identifies
its prime-to-`p` cyclotomic action with an element of `p^Zhat=H_p`.
Consequently

```text
bar_d(c,c') = d(c,c';sigma) mod H_p in B_p                (4.3)
```

is independent of `sigma`.  This closes the potential decomposition-group
ambiguity; no choice of a Galois transporter remains in the transition map.

### 4.2 Transition at equations (37)--(39)

The old chart represents a character by exponent `a`; the new reference
character represents the same Galois-orbit point by `a'` satisfying

```text
d a' = a.
```

Therefore the change from `c`-coordinates to `c'`-coordinates is

```text
equation (37):  (b,nu)    -> (bar_d^{-1} b,nu),
equations (38)--(39):
                 (b,[r]) -> (bar_d^{-1} b,[r]),            (4.4)
```

where `b in B_p`, `nu in N_0`, and `[r] in Q_{>0}/p^Z`.
This formula respects the fibre relation in (37) and the contracted-product
relation in (38).  In particular, changing the Galois transporter by a
stabilizer element cannot hide a `p^n` time shift: its entire effect is
already killed by the quotient by `H_p`, while equation (40) fixes `[r]`
canonically.

### 4.3 Suspension and time-coordinate law

For a suspension representative `[[b,r],u]`, the circle coordinate is

```text
[r u] in K_p=R_{>0}/p^Z.
```

It is invariant under the diagonal relation
`(r,u)->(rq,q^{-1}u)`.  Suspending (4.4) gives the complete chart transition

```text
T_(c,c'):B_p x K_p -> B_p x K_p,
T_(c,c')(b,[s]) = (tau_(c,c') b,[s]),
tau_(c,c') = bar_d(c,c')^{-1}.                             (4.5)
```

Thus:

- the transverse coordinate changes by translation;
- the time coordinate is pointwise unchanged;
- there is no time shift, dilation, inversion, or phase twist; and
- `T_(c,c')` commutes with the literal flow:

  ```text
  T_(c,c') o phi^t = phi^t o T_(c,c').                    (4.6)
  ```

The source-stated canonical map (40) is the direct time-coordinate firewall;
(4.5)--(4.6) are the derived transition and covariance laws.

### 4.4 Composition, inverse, and exhaustiveness

For three choice pairs, the comparison exponents multiply modulo `H_p`.
Equivalently, the translation labels obey

```text
tau_(c,c) = 1,
tau_(c',c) = tau_(c,c')^{-1},
tau_(c,c'') = tau_(c',c'') tau_(c,c').                    (4.7)
```

Hence

```text
T_(c',c'') o T_(c,c') = T_(c,c''),
T_(c,c')^{-1} = T_(c',c).                                 (4.8)
```

These are the complete identity/composition/inverse laws.  There are no
additional transition maps from the two choice types named by Deninger.

Moreover, every translation of `B_p` occurs.  Hold `x` fixed.  Because the
restriction map `Zhat^x -> U_p` is surjective, varying `iota` realizes every
class in `U_p/H_p`.  Therefore the image of the source-choice groupoid is
exactly

```text
{ L_g x id_(K_p) : g in B_p }.                            (4.9)
```

This is a translation group, not an uncomputed moduli space.

### 4.5 Translation versus automorphism

`L_g:b->gb` is an affine self-homeomorphism of the abstract compact group
`B_p`, with identity linear part.  It is a group automorphism only when
`g=1`, because a group homomorphism must fix the identity.  Thus the exact
classification is:

```text
TRANSITION_CLASS        = all B_p translations,
GROUP_AUTOMORPHISM_PART = identity only,
TIME_PART               = identity.
```

The unpointed transverse carrier consequently has a canonical `B_p`-torsor
interpretation under these charts, but no choice-independent identity point.
That distinction is important: the calculation gives a torsor, not a
source-canonical group structure on `Q_p_actual`.

## 5. Haar and measure-class consequence

Let `m_B` be normalized Haar probability on the abstract compact group
`B_p`.  For every transition label `g`, left invariance gives directly

```text
(L_g)_* m_B = m_B.                                        (5.1)
```

This is stronger than measure-class invariance.  It is exact equality of
normalized measures, and it requires no automorphism argument.  If `m_K` is
normalized Haar on the compact time circle `K_p`, then (4.5) gives

```text
(T_(c,c'))_*(m_B x m_K) = m_B x m_K.                      (5.2)
```

No source-permitted change of `x` or `iota` furnishes a counterexample.

Equations (4.5) and (5.2) also imply a limited positive descent statement:
transporting the compact topology, Borel sigma-algebra, and normalized Haar
from a chosen chart to the underlying orbit carrier or packet carrier gives
the same transported structure for every source choice.  This is a
**reviewer-derived chart enhancement**.  It must receive a new owner name.
It does not prove any of the following:

- that a source chart is a homeomorphism for `Gamma_p_actual`;
- that its inverse is measurable for the actual trivial Borel algebra;
- that `Q_p_actual` has the compact topology of `B_p`;
- that the actual topology selects a nontrivial transverse measure;

The P9/P10 result remains untouched: the actual packet/orbit quotient is
