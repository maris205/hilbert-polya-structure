## Contract Paraphrase

D1: From an adversarial standpoint, methodological rigor survives only if the research design, treatment of data, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility provisions can withstand field-appropriate scrutiny; weaknesses on this axis must be judged by the methodology seat rather than converted into a DA score.

D2: The domain-accuracy requirement calls for stress-testing whether substantive assertions agree with the relevant evidence base, earlier scholarship is characterized faithfully, and specialized terms and reported results are factually sound; formal scoring belongs to the domain seat.

D3: Argumentative coherence requires the central thesis to remain consistent across the full reasoning chain, with decisive claims warranted by evidence and no fallacy, hidden premise, contradiction, or stronger unaddressed counter-explanation capable of defeating the core case; this is the DA's eligible and owned scoring dimension.

D4: Cross-disciplinary relevance requires an adjacent-field reader to be able to follow the framing and definitions while finding genuine support for any interdisciplinary reach or implication; the perspective seat owns the score, while adversarial concerns outside scoring eligibility remain possible later if evidence warrants them.

D5: Writing and structure require an intelligible organization, clear exposition, usable visual materials, and conformity with the applicable presentation conventions; the Journal-Fit Reviewer, not the DA, supplies the score for this dimension.

D6: Venue fit and contribution require both an appropriate match to the configured readership and a contribution whose originality and importance justify publication there; because no target-criteria binding is supplied here, the DA makes no venue-alignment claim and leaves this score to the Journal-Fit Reviewer.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace the central thesis through each decisive premise and inference, test whether the cited evidence actually warrants the claims, probe for contradictions, hidden assumptions, fallacies, and stronger rival explanations, and assign pass only when no material break or unsupported leap remains.
what_triggers_block: A central inference, premise, or evidence-to-claim link is materially unsupported or logically invalid, substantially undermining the thesis but remaining repairable through major clarification, reanalysis, reframing, or additional support.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, incompletely defended assumption, or limited overreach weakens part of the reasoning without defeating the central thesis and can be corrected without substantial reconstruction.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis collapses because a decisive premise is contradicted or wholly unsubstantiated, the evidence cannot support the central conclusion, or a stronger counter-explanation defeats the argument in a way that revision cannot salvage.
