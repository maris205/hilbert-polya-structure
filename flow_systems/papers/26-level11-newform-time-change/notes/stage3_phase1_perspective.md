## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): The methodological bar requires a study design, data treatment, statistical presentation, and reproducibility support robust enough that readers from neighboring disciplines can understand what was done and judge whether the results can be trusted or transferred.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Domain-facing claims must accord with current evidence, represent prior scholarship faithfully, and use specialized terminology and reported results correctly so that adjacent-field readers are not asked to build connections on factual or disciplinary errors.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): The central thesis must remain consistent, its claims must be supported by the evidence offered, and no reasoning defect may undermine the core argument when it is examined from outside the primary disciplinary frame.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): The framing, definitions, and implications must be intelligible to readers in adjacent fields, while every interdisciplinary claim needs enough substantiation to make the proposed connection credible rather than merely asserted.

D5 (writing_and_structure): Organisation, exposition, figures, tables, and conformity to expected conventions must make the work navigable and clear, including for readers who do not already share the primary field's tacit knowledge.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): The review must determine whether the work belongs in the configured venue and offers an original, consequential contribution suited to that venue's readership, including the breadth of interest implied by the intended audience.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Assess whether framing and definitions are accessible to adjacent-field readers, implications are clearly developed, and interdisciplinary claims are supported with explicit reasoning or evidence.
what_triggers_block: Central framing or definitions are inaccessible to adjacent-field readers, or a central interdisciplinary claim is unsubstantiated in a way that materially defeats the work's cross-disciplinary relevance.
what_triggers_warn: Localized framing, definitions, or implications need clarification for adjacent-field readers, or a non-central interdisciplinary claim has incomplete support while the broader cross-disciplinary connection remains viable.
