## PROVED Stage-1 formula

Along an original unit-speed closed orbit, the new time element is
`dt_epsilon=rho_epsilon dt`; hence

```text
T_epsilon(gamma)
  = integral_gamma (1 + epsilon alpha_f(v)) dt
  = ell(gamma) + epsilon integral_gamma alpha_f.
```

Because `alpha_f` is bounded, `|epsilon|<||a||_infinity^(-1)` makes the
time-density strictly positive and hence makes the reciprocal speed multiplier
`1/rho_epsilon` positive.  The same integral on `gamma^r` is exactly `r` times
the primitive integral.
