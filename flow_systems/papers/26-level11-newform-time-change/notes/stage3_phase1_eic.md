## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) requires the methodology seat to judge whether the research design, treatment of data, reporting of quantitative evidence, and provisions for reproducibility reach the discipline's review standard; editorial oversight should treat this as a mandatory methodological gate owned outside the Journal-Fit Reviewer remit.

D2 (domain_accuracy) requires the domain seat to verify the manuscript's factual and terminological correctness, its fidelity to the relevant evidence base, and its representation of prior scholarship; editorial oversight should treat this as a mandatory subject-matter gate owned outside the Journal-Fit Reviewer remit.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) requires the eligible methodology and devil's-advocate seats to test whether the central position is logically consistent, adequately supported, and free of reasoning defects capable of defeating it; editorial oversight should treat this as a mandatory shared assessment led by the devil's-advocate role.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) requires the perspective seat to determine whether neighboring-field readers can understand the framing and terminology and whether claims spanning disciplines have adequate support; editorial oversight should treat this as a high-priority assessment owned outside the Journal-Fit Reviewer remit.

D5 (writing_and_structure) requires the Journal-Fit Reviewer to assess whether the submission is organized and expressed clearly, whether its visual displays communicate effectively, and whether its presentation follows applicable submission conventions; editorial oversight should distinguish pervasive barriers from repairable presentation defects.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) requires the Journal-Fit Reviewer to assess the submission's scope and reader relevance together with the originality and significance of its contribution; editorial oversight should treat this as a mandatory gate and distinguish remediable positioning problems from conditions that make the submission nonviable for the intended venue.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Clear organization, precise exposition, legible and purposeful figures or tables, and conformance with relevant submission conventions.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive structural or presentation defects that prevent readers from following the manuscript or evaluating its claims.
what_triggers_warn: Localized clarity, organization, display, or convention problems that are material but repairable without wholesale restructuring.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Evidence that the submission's scope, originality, significance, and reader value satisfy the expectations applicable to the intended venue.
what_triggers_block: A major scope or readership mismatch, or a contribution too weakly established to clear the venue bar without substantial reframing.
what_triggers_warn: A broadly plausible fit and contribution accompanied by repairable uncertainty in positioning, novelty articulation, significance, or reader relevance.
what_triggers_fatal: A definitive out-of-scope condition or absence of an identifiable original and significant contribution that revision cannot make viable for the intended venue.
