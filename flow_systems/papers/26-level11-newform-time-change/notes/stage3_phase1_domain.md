## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): The methodology seat must determine whether the study design, treatment of data, statistical reporting, and provisions for reproducibility satisfy the peer-review standard of the field; from a domain-accuracy perspective, this establishes whether the substantive claims rest on field-appropriate evidentiary procedures.

D2 (domain_accuracy): The domain seat must determine whether claims agree with current domain evidence, the prior literature is represented faithfully, and field-specific terminology and reported results contain no factual errors.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): The eligible methodology and devil's-advocate seats must determine whether the central thesis is internally consistent, adequately supported by evidence, and free of fallacies that would undermine the core argument; from a domain-accuracy perspective, this distinguishes substantively supported reasoning from unsupported domain inference.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): The perspective seat must determine whether the framing, definitions, and implications are understandable to readers in adjacent fields and whether interdisciplinary claims have adequate support; from a domain-accuracy perspective, accessibility must preserve the meaning and limits of the specialized concepts involved.

D5 (writing_and_structure): The Journal-Fit seat must determine whether the manuscript is well organized and clearly explained, whether figures and tables communicate effectively, and whether presentation follows venue conventions; from a domain-accuracy perspective, the exposition must make substantive claims and their support identifiable without distorting them.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): The Journal-Fit seat must determine whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution appropriate for that venue's readership; from a domain-accuracy perspective, the contribution claim must be grounded in an accurate account of what the field already knows.
