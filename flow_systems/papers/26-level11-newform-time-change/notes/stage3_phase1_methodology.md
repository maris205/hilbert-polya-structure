## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor commits the methodology review to determine whether the declared research design, treatment of evidence or data, analytic reasoning, reporting discipline, and reproducibility provisions provide a rigorous and auditable route to the stated research objective.

D2 domain_accuracy requires the panel to establish that specialized claims, terminology, results, and representations of prior work are factually dependable; from the methodology perspective, this defines an accuracy constraint on the evidence and inferences while substantive scoring remains with the eligible domain role.

D3 argumentative_coherence requires a logically supported chain from premises and evidence through intermediate reasoning to the central conclusions, without fallacies or gaps that defeat the main argument; the methodology review will examine the validity of that chain where research design and inference bear on it.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance asks whether readers in neighboring fields can follow the framing and definitions and whether claims spanning disciplines have adequate support; the methodology perspective treats this as a panel-level communication and substantiation requirement assigned to its eligible role.

D5 writing_and_structure requires an organized and readable presentation, usable figures or tables where applicable, and conformity with the relevant venue's presentation conventions; the methodology perspective recognizes that clear structure must make the evidentiary and analytic route inspectable while scoring remains with the eligible role.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution requires the panel to determine whether the work belongs in the configured publication setting and offers a sufficiently original and consequential contribution for that audience; the methodology perspective treats rigorous support as necessary context while leaving venue and contribution scoring to the eligible role.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: A clearly specified research design with justified assumptions, traceable evidence or data, appropriate analytic and validation procedures, and enough detail for independent reproduction.
what_triggers_block: A material design or analysis defect leaves a central conclusion unsupported but could be repaired through substantial correction, validation, or reanalysis.
what_triggers_warn: The core methodological route remains credible, but limited omissions in justification, reporting, robustness checks, or reproducibility detail require clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The research design cannot support the central conclusion, or essential evidence and procedures are absent such that the core claim cannot be validated or repaired within the reported study.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A valid and traceable inferential chain from stated premises and admissible evidence through intermediate results to the principal conclusions, including explicit handling of assumptions and countercases.
what_triggers_block: A substantial logical gap, circular dependency, or unaddressed countercase materially undermines a central argument while leaving a plausible route to reconstruction.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument is intelligible and potentially sound, but a localized inference, assumption, or evidence-to-claim transition needs sharper justification.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis depends on a contradiction, invalid inference, or unsupported premise whose failure collapses the core argument and admits no repair without replacing it.
