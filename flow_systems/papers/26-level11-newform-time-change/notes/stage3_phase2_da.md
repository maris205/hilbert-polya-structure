## Review Body

Strongest counter-argument: the exact 51-of-55 failure count does not yet discriminate a newform-specific primitive-Euler obstruction from a largely forced consequence of the chosen representation. Genus one already makes the linear Hecke period identity hold for every compactly extending closed real one-form, so that identity supplies no selective arithmetic bridge. Passing to the inverse-paired second variation then replaces signed linear sums by nonnegative degree-wise square sums. Once an output group contains any nonzero real period at branch degree greater than one, the all-parameter scalar recurrence fails regardless of the proposed scalar. The 134 nonkernels therefore make widespread failure structurally expected, while the four survivors are exactly the exceptional vanishing loci of the selected real projection: two full complex-period kernels and two nonzero compact classes killed only by real projection. Exact rational homology removes numerical doubt, but exactness alone does not add scientific discrimination. More seriously, the 138 entries are correspondence components in a non-deduplicated multiset, whereas a primitive-orbit Euler product is indexed by primitive conjugacy owners. Until cross-instance conjugacy and its intended multiplicity rule are resolved, the reported moments may measure branch-incidence multiplicity rather than a canonical primitive-owner product. The defensible result is therefore a finite, real-projected correspondence-multiset obstruction; the broader conclusion that the tested primitive-Euler interpretation fails requires either owner canonicalization or substantially narrower wording.

The branch-degree, primitive-root, and zeta-repetition concepts are correctly separated at the formal level: a degree-$d$ branch can close at a primitive owner, and product repetition begins only after that owner is chosen. The manuscript does not, however, include the completeness argument behind the finite primitive-root search. Because instance-level primitivity is load-bearing for treating $d$ as branch closure rather than hidden traversal repetition, a reference to a certificate without its exponent bound and exhaustive root-reconstruction lemma leaves a central premise unauditable within the paper.

The full-kernel versus real-projection-only distinction is sound and must remain intact. The two projection-only classes have nonzero complex periods and survive only because the observable squares the real projection. Consequently all four survivors are valid positives for the frozen real-valued identity, but they cannot support an inference about full complex newform periods, primitive-owner arithmetic, a prime-owner map, or Route-A A2 evidence.

The finite and global quantifiers are otherwise mostly handled responsibly. The exact claims cover 138 registered instances and 55 registered groups, not a global primitive census; the product manipulations are finite or conditional on convergence; and no global determinant, analytic continuation, target-zero comparison, or Hilbert--Pólya realization is established. The status must remain `(A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)` with `ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY`; Route B remains unauthorized. No target criteria binding is available, so this report makes no venue-alignment or submission-readiness claim.
