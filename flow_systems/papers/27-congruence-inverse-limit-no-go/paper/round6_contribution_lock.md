## Locked contribution statement

The article compares a cusped factorial principal-congruence tower with a
closed genus-2 residual/homology tower.  Both share the same normal-residual
owner obstruction: a common inverse-limit period would force one fixed group
power into the residual intersection, while every fixed owner's finite-
quotient order divides forward and diverges.  The closed control proves a
factorial lower bound for exact minimal lifted periods and shows that cusp,
principal congruence, and arithmetic provenance are not causal.  Exact finite
ledgers illustrate the two owner types without transferring finite-level
credit to the aperiodic limit flow.
