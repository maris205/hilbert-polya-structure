# Paper 27 research spine after Round 7

Working title: *Owner Escape in Residual Hyperbolic Towers: Compact and Cusped
No-Go Results for Same-Owner Dynamical Zeta Limits*

## New theorem-level contribution

For every fixed primitive base owner `g`, the quotient order `o_n(g)` diverges.
Consequently its finite-level Euler factor satisfies

```text
(1-x_g^(o_n(g)))^(-1) = 1 mod x_g^(N+1)
```

for every fixed `N` at all sufficiently large levels.  The same is true for
every fixed finite owner panel.  In physical time, all corresponding lifted
periods leave every bounded window.

## Comparative paper argument

1. Prove the inverse-limit periodic set is empty under normal residuality.
2. Prove quotient-order and closing-time escape for every fixed owner.
3. Give the factorial quantitative compact homology control.
4. Compare cusped principal-congruence and cocompact residual towers without
   transferring owner credit between finite levels and the limit.
5. Prove the Round-7 coefficient-stability no-go: fixed-owner Euler factors
   become trivial in every fixed formal prefix under the unchanged clock.
