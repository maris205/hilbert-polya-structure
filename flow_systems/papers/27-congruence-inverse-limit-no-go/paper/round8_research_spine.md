# P27 Round-8 paper spine

Working title: **Period Escape and the Two Renormalizations Required to Undo It**

## New paper-level contribution

The paper now contains both sides of the tower story:

1. under the original residual owner and physical clock, every fixed-owner
   Euler prefix escapes; and
2. in a separately registered homology-cover calibrator, exact recovery of a
   finite owner panel requires two simultaneous interventions—`1/N` time
   rescaling and `1/N^3` logarithmic multiplicity normalization.

The four quadrants distinguish support escape, multiplicity divergence, and
exact base-factor recovery.  This is stronger than proposing a renormalization:
it proves which components are individually insufficient.

1. State the residual inverse-limit aperiodicity and same-owner prefix-escape

2. Explain why any rescue must register a new object, clock, and normalization.
3. Define the pure genus-two homology cover and primitive-content-one panel.
4. Prove deck degree `N^4`, owner order `N`, lift count `N^3`, and period

## Claim boundary

The positive fully renormalized identity is for a fixed finite panel, not the
full primitive spectrum.  The pure homology tower is not residual, and its
intersection is the commutator subgroup.  No rational-prime owner, full
dynamical determinant, or Route-B input is claimed.
