## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) requires the study's design and inferential procedures to be appropriate for its questions, with data or formal inputs handled transparently, any quantitative reporting technically sound, and enough procedural detail or computational support for an informed reviewer to audit and reproduce the reasoning.

D2 (domain_accuracy) requires methodology-sensitive scrutiny of whether specialized factual and technical claims are stated correctly, use field terminology consistently, and represent prior results without distortions that would invalidate premises or comparisons used by the analysis.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) requires tracing the full inference from premises and evidence to the central thesis, testing internal consistency and claim scope, and identifying contradictions, unsupported steps, or fallacious reasoning capable of weakening the main conclusion.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) requires checking whether methodological framing, definitions, and inferential bridges make the work intelligible to neighboring fields and whether any interdisciplinary implication is supported rather than merely asserted.

D5 (writing_and_structure) requires presentation that lets a reviewer reconstruct the study or proof strategy efficiently, inspect figures and tables where present, and distinguish assumptions, procedures, results, and conclusions within a clear venue-appropriate organization.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) requires judging fit, originality, significance, and readership relevance as separate questions while ensuring that any contribution claim rests on a methodologically credible result rather than on technical presentation or ambition alone.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Alignment among the research question, design or proof strategy, handling of data or formal inputs, analytical procedures, reporting, and the detail needed to audit and reproduce the central result.
what_triggers_block: A substantial but potentially repairable defect in design, input handling, analytical validity, reporting, or reproducibility materially weakens a core result.
what_triggers_warn: A localized methodological omission or clarity gap reduces auditability or confidence but does not materially undermine any core result.
what_triggers_fatal: A single uncorrectable design, analysis, or evidence defect invalidates a core result or makes acceptance impossible.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Consistency of the central thesis, validity of each evidence-to-claim inference, explicit treatment of assumptions and scope, and absence of fallacies that could defeat the main argument.
what_triggers_block: A substantial but potentially repairable break in the evidence-to-claim chain, internal consistency, or inferential logic materially weakens the central argument.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity or non-central reasoning gap reduces clarity or support without materially weakening the central argument.
what_triggers_fatal: A single uncorrectable contradiction, invalid inference, or unsupported central premise collapses the core thesis or makes acceptance impossible.
