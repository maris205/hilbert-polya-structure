## Contract Paraphrase

D1 concerns whether the research design, treatment of data, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility provisions reach the methodological standard expected in peer review; from a cross-disciplinary vantage, these features establish whether readers outside the primary field could regard the study process as dependable.

D2 concerns whether the paper's claims remain consistent with current knowledge in the relevant domain, accurately characterize earlier scholarship, and avoid errors in specialized concepts or reported results; the perspective role recognizes this domain-validity requirement without taking the domain seat's scoring authority.

D3 concerns whether the central thesis forms a consistent line of reasoning in which the offered evidence genuinely supports the stated claims and no reasoning defect defeats the main case; for an outside-field reader, this is the baseline for interpreting the argument as a coherent whole.

D4 concerns whether readers from neighboring disciplines can understand the framing, terminology, and consequences, and whether assertions that cross disciplinary boundaries receive real support rather than relying on disciplinary familiarity or unsupported reach.

D5 concerns whether the organization and exposition make the work intelligible, whether tables and figures communicate effectively, and whether presentation follows the relevant submission conventions; cross-field accessibility benefits from these qualities even though this dimension belongs to the journal-fit seat.

D6 concerns whether the work belongs in the configured publication setting and offers a sufficiently original and consequential contribution for that readership; this describes the contract's venue-and-contribution standard without asserting that the paper satisfies it.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Framing, definitions, and implications that adjacent-field readers can follow, together with explicit support for claims that cross disciplinary boundaries.
what_triggers_block: Central framing or definitions are inaccessible to adjacent-field readers, or essential interdisciplinary claims lack substantiation, so the stated cross-disciplinary relevance cannot be sustained.
what_triggers_warn: The main cross-disciplinary relevance remains understandable and supported, but localized framing, definitions, implications, or interdisciplinary claim support needs clarification.
