## Landed result

The primary-source audit found direct prior work for both the broad
aperiodicity phenomenon and the structural mechanism behind the P27 theorem.
Hyperbolic laminated geodesic flows without periodic orbits already appear in
Martínez--Matsumoto--Verjovsky. Universal and punctured inverse-limit
solenoids with simply connected disk leaves already appear in the same paper
and in Penner--Šarić. Alcalde Cuesta et al. now treat noncompact finite-area
surface-covering inverse limits as hyperbolic solenoidal surfaces of finite type
and explicitly identify the regular-cover case as McCord.

No checked primary source stated the exact “Gamma(3 n!)” factorial-chain
proposition verbatim. That negative result is limited to the frozen search and
does not permit an absolute novelty statement.

## Contribution decision

The broad working claim is merged into prior work and the candidate
contribution is narrowed to:

1. the explicit principal-congruence tower Gamma(3 n!);
2. the elementary but fully exposed PSL-sign proof that its intersection is
   trivial;
3. the 3-by-8 finite reduction-order ledger, including two independent order
   algorithms, transition compatibility, and two-run identity; and
4. the finite-owner firewall: level-dependent closing periods belong to the
   finite tower plus a chosen element, whereas a periodic point of the
   inverse-limit flow requires one coherent point and one common positive time.

This is best framed as a short explicit case study and methods or claim-audit
note. The current evidence does not support marketing the no-period statement
as a new general theorem.
