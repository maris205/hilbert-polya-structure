## Contract Paraphrase

D1: The methodology dimension is mandatory and requires the panel to probe whether the research design, treatment of data, reporting of quantitative claims, and reproducibility provisions can withstand the standards of the relevant field.

D2: The domain-accuracy dimension is mandatory and requires challenges to any factual, terminological, evidentiary, or prior-work representation that could make the paper's field-specific claims unreliable.

D3: The argumentative-coherence dimension is mandatory and requires an adversarial test of whether the central thesis remains consistent, each material conclusion follows from adequate support, and no fallacy, hidden premise, or contradiction breaks the core reasoning.

D4: The cross-disciplinary-relevance dimension has high priority and asks whether neighboring-field readers can follow the framing and definitions and whether claims that cross disciplinary boundaries have real support rather than rhetorical reach.

D5: The writing-and-structure dimension has normal priority and asks whether organization, exposition, visual materials, and conformity to expected presentation practices permit the argument to be evaluated clearly.

D6: The venue-fit-and-contribution dimension is mandatory and calls for scrutiny of whether the work belongs within the configured publication context and whether its novelty and significance are sufficient for the intended readership.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace the central thesis from indispensable premises through each inferential step to the stated conclusions, testing evidentiary support, internal consistency, hidden assumptions, rival explanations, and possible fallacies.
what_triggers_block: A repairable but substantive unsupported premise, invalid implication, or unresolved contradiction materially breaks the route from evidence to the central conclusion.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, incomplete inferential bridge, or limited overstatement weakens confidence in part of the argument without severing the central logic.
what_triggers_fatal: An indispensable premise is demonstrably false, or the presented evidence contradicts the core conclusion, so the central thesis cannot be rescued without being replaced.
