## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From a domain-accuracy perspective, this dimension asks whether the study design, handling of evidence or data, statistical reporting where applicable, and reproducibility provisions are rigorous enough to make the field-specific claims dependable.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension requires the paper's substantive claims to agree with current domain evidence, its account of prior work to be accurate, and its field-specific terminology and stated results to be free of factual error.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): From the domain lens, this dimension concerns whether the central thesis remains internally consistent, whether the cited evidence actually supports the propositions drawn from it, and whether any fallacy defeats the main argument.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether the framing, definitions, and implications can be understood by readers in neighboring fields and whether any interdisciplinary assertions are supported rather than merely stated.

D5 (writing_and_structure): From a domain-accuracy standpoint, this dimension requires an organization and exposition clear enough to communicate the technical content correctly, usable figures or tables where present, and conformity with the relevant venue's presentation conventions.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension requires the manuscript to suit the configured venue and its readership while offering a contribution whose originality and significance are appropriate to that setting.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether domain-specific claims match current evidence, prior work is represented accurately, terminology and stated results are correct, theoretical framing is appropriate, and the contribution is positioned without overclaiming.
what_triggers_block: A material domain error, systematic misrepresentation of prior work, or misuse of central terminology that substantially undermines a core claim and requires major correction.
what_triggers_warn: Localized omissions, imprecisions, weak literature integration, or modest overstatement that reduce domain accuracy but leave the core claim supportable through bounded revision.
what_triggers_fatal: A central domain premise or claimed result is demonstrably false, contradicts decisive field evidence, or depends on a fundamental theoretical misrepresentation such that the core claim is invalid and cannot be repaired within the stated contribution.
