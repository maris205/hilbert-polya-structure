## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): Editorial acceptance requires the research design, treatment of data, reporting of analyses, and provisions for reproducibility to satisfy the methodological standards expected in peer review; this mandatory dimension belongs to the methodology seat.

D2 (domain_accuracy): The submission must use field-specific concepts and results correctly, represent the relevant prior work faithfully, and keep its claims consistent with current domain evidence; this is a mandatory judgement assigned to the domain seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): The central thesis must remain internally consistent, its supporting evidence must warrant the claims made, and no fallacy may compromise the main argument; this mandatory dimension is assessed by the Devil's Advocate and methodology seats under Devil's Advocate ownership.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): Readers from neighboring fields should be able to follow the framing and definitions, understand the implications, and find adequate support for any interdisciplinary assertions; this high-priority dimension is reserved for the perspective seat.

D5 (writing_and_structure): From an editorial-oversight perspective, the manuscript must be organized and explained clearly, present figures and tables effectively, and follow the applicable venue conventions; this normal-priority dimension is assigned to the Journal-Fit Reviewer.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): From an editorial-oversight perspective, the work must suit the configured venue and its readership while offering a contribution that is both original and significant at the level expected there; this is a mandatory Journal-Fit Reviewer dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Assess the logical organization, clarity and continuity of exposition, communicative quality of figures and tables, and compliance with applicable venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive organizational or expository failure, unusable figure or table presentation, or extensive convention noncompliance prevents reliable reading and requires comprehensive restructuring.
what_triggers_warn: Localized problems in clarity, sequencing, visual presentation, or convention compliance impede readers but can be corrected through targeted revision.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Assess the case for scope and readership fit together with the identifiable originality, significance, and audience-level value of the contribution under the available review context.
what_triggers_block: Substantial deficiencies in the scope, readership, originality, or significance case require major repositioning or fundamental reframing before the contribution could meet the configured venue's bar.
what_triggers_warn: A plausible fit and contribution case is present, but the positioning, demonstration of originality, significance, or relevance to the intended readership needs targeted clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: An irreconcilable scope or readership mismatch, or the absence of any identifiable original and significant contribution appropriate to that readership, makes acceptance impossible under the configured venue.
