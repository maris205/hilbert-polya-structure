## 6. Cross-prime compact-group candidates

### 6.1 S14-C1 -- global subdirect reconstruction

**Exact theorem signature sought.**  Define

```text
beta = (beta_p)_p : A -> product_p B_p.
```

The proposed theorem is:

1. `beta` is a closed topological embedding.
2. If `A^*` is written as the finite Dirichlet-character group, then

   ```text
   beta_p^*(B_p^*)
     = {chi in A^* : p does not divide cond(chi), chi(p)=1}.
   ```

3. Every finite character belongs to the right side for infinitely many
   `p`, and therefore the family of packet-base quotients separates `A`.
4. The finite-coordinate images form the compatibility system whose inverse
   limit is `beta(A)`.

**Required primary/official sources.**  Deninger v4 for `B_p` and the source
Galois action; Pontryagin duality; Dirichlet's theorem in arithmetic
progressions (the 1837 primary record, with a modern official theorem
locator).  A source-owned identification of `A` with the relevant cyclotomic
quotient of the Galois action must be quoted exactly rather than inferred from
the desired conclusion.

**Internal prior subtraction.**  P15R owns each bare `B_p`; P7 owns the
Galois-equivariance audit; P9 owns actual packet separation.  C1 may use only
the simultaneous quotient diagram, not a new one-prime invariant.

**Strongest reduction attack.**  A finite character `chi` has conductor `N`.
Any prime `p=1 mod N` lies outside the conductor and satisfies `chi(p)=1`.
Dirichlet provides infinitely many such primes.  The dual images therefore
separate `A`, and compact-to-Hausdorff gives the closed embedding.  This is a
clean arithmetic proposition, but it is not a full paper.  The inverse-limit
sentence is formal once all finite images are named.  A generic compact group
with a separating family of quotients has the same categorical statement.

**Verdict fields.**

```text
SOURCE_FEASIBLE: YES
FULL_PAPER_PLAUSIBLE: NO as a standalone center
proper use: opening theorem/lemma of S14-C2
```

### 6.2 S14-C2 -- two-prime common quotient and rank-two signature

This is the only item that survives the screen.

**Exact categorical owner.**  For `p != q`, use the *source maps* `beta_p`
and `beta_q`, not merely the abstract isomorphism types of `B_p,B_q`.  Put

```text
C_{p,q} = coker(beta_{p,q}:A -> B_p x B_q)
        = (B_p x B_q)/beta_{p,q}(A).
```

There is an elementary continuous map

```text
delta_{p,q}: B_p x B_q -> A/(K_p K_q),
delta_{p,q}(a K_p,b K_q) = (a-b)(K_p K_q)
```

in additive notation (use `ab^{-1}` multiplicatively).  It is well-defined,
onto, and has diagonal image as kernel.  Hence the mandatory first theorem is

```text
0 -> A/(K_p cap K_q)
  -> B_p x B_q
  -> C_{p,q}
  -> 0,

C_{p,q} ~= A/(K_p K_q)
          ~= U_{p,q}/H_{p,q},

beta_{p,q}(A) = B_p x_{C_{p,q}} B_q.
```

The second isomorphism follows by dropping the `p,q` coordinates and checking
that the full preimage of `H_{p,q}` is `K_pK_q`.  These identities are useful
but appendix-sized; they do not earn the slot.

**Load-bearing arithmetic theorem signature.**  The only version with
full-paper potential is the following falsifiable suite.

1. Prove that `e_{p,q}:Zhat^2 -> U_{p,q}` is a closed embedding, including the
   `r=p`, `r=q`, and `r=2` pro-primary branches.
2. For every prime `r`, dualize the `r`-Sylow quotient.  Before quotienting,

   ```text
   U_{p,q,(r)} ~= P_r                 if r in {p,q},
   U_{p,q,(r)} ~= Z_r x P_r           if r notin {p,q},

   P_r = product_{n>=1}(C_{r^n})^{aleph_0}.
   ```

   Compute every finite Ulm invariant, the infinite-height subgroup, the
   transfinite tail, and dual reducedness of `C_{p,q,(r)}^*`.
3. Test, rather than assume, the candidate simultaneous defect

   ```text
   kappa_r(p,q) = 0                         if r in {p,q},

   kappa_r(p,q)
     = min(v_r(p^(r-1)-1)-1,
           v_r(q^(r-1)-1)-1)               if r is odd and r notin {p,q},

   kappa_2(p,q)
     = min(v_2(p^2-1)-3,
           v_2(q^2-1)-3)                   if 2 notin {p,q}.
   ```

   The desired classification statement is

   ```text
   C_{p,q} ~=_top C_{p',q'}
     iff
   kappa_r(p,q)=kappa_r(p',q') for every r,
   ```

   **only if** the full torsion/Ulm computation proves that no additional
   rank-two invariant occurs.  The displayed formula is a conjectural target,
   not a conclusion of this screen.
4. Establish at least one nonformal finite-set functoriality theorem for
   `C_F=coker(A -> product_{p in F}B_p)` or an equivalent compatibility
   category.  Merely saying “repeat for `|F|>2`” adds no paper weight.

**Required primary/official sources.**

- Deninger, *Dynamical systems for arithmetic schemes*, v4, for the exact
  packet-base presentation and Galois/source action.
- Kiehlmann, *Classifications of countably-based abelian profinite groups*,
  JGT 16 (2013), Theorems 1.1, 1.4, 1.8 and Proposition 2.3, for the typed
  torsion-sequence/Ulm classification ceiling.
- Hill (1972), with Prüfer/Kulikov, only after the discrete dual is correctly
  typed as a subgroup of a direct sum of cyclic primary groups.
- Zsigmondy (1892), with its exception list, for omitted-coordinate bounded
  detection where applicable.
- Qualitative Chebotarev plus a **rank-two Kummer** calculation for prescribed
  independent reductions of `p,q`.
- Debry--Perucca (arXiv:1312.6620) and Perucca (arXiv:0909.4806) as nearby
  primary evidence that finitely generated multiplicative subgroups and
  simultaneous reduction valuations are source-feasible.  Neither paper was
  found to state the exact embedding or Ulm theorem above.
- Exact odd and `2`-adic logarithm valuation formulas for the local row
  `(log_r p,log_r q)`.

**Internal prior subtraction.**  P15R removes the complete rank-one
calculation.  The new delta is the relative position of two source-induced
copies of `Zhat` inside the global quotient diagram, the common quotient, and
the rank-two Kummer/Ulm computation.  If the proof imports P15R and needs only
one Smith-normal-form paragraph, it is not independent paper weight.

**Strongest attacks.**

1. **Minimum-collapse attack.**  When `r notin {p,q}`, the local logarithmic
   row can be put into Smith form.  Its ideal is generated by two coefficients
   and therefore has valuation equal to their minimum.  If all off-local
   height arguments are copied from P15R, the whole answer may be exactly the
   displayed `min(kappa,kappa)` formula.  That would be mathematically nice
   but likely a P15R section, not P14.
2. **Rank-two saturation attack.**  The one-generator Kummer witness in P15R
   does not automatically give independent prescribed values for two
   generators.  Intersections of
   `Q(zeta_{r^n},p^(1/r^n),q^(1/r^n))` must be computed, not waved away.
   The ramified branches `r=p` and `r=q`, and the sign/principal-unit split at
   `r=2`, are separate failure points.
3. **Hidden-tail attack.**  Equality of the finite torsion-closure index does
   not imply equality of the full transfinite Ulm sequence.  A residual
   rank-two infinite-height direction would falsify the one-number formula.
4. **Bare-owner attack.**  `C_{p,q}` depends on the maps from `A`, not on the
   bare groups alone.  The source must genuinely own those maps.  Arbitrarily
   choosing embeddings after P15R is prohibited.
5. **Recovery attack.**  Even a correct classification need not recover
   `{p,q}`.  Simultaneous Wieferich defects may all vanish for many pairs.  No
   injectivity claim belongs in the theorem absent a separate proof.
6. **Generic retelling attack.**  A generic profinite group with two chosen
   procyclic subgroups has a common quotient and Smith invariants.  Arithmetic
   ownership survives only if the proof essentially uses the rational primes,
   the omitted local coordinates, and rank-two Kummer/Chebotarev fields.

**Verdict fields.**

```text

                 embedding/saturation theorem not yet located or proved
UNDERDETERMINED: full Ulm tail and standalone delta
FULL_PAPER_PLAUSIBLE: YES only if the Section-14 gate passes
```

### 6.3 S14-C3 -- the continuous Hom category


of morphisms.  Pontryagin duality gives

```text
Hom_cts(B_p,B_q) ~= Hom(B_q^*,B_p^*)
```

with composition reversed on the dual side.

**Sources needed.**  Deninger for the owners, Kiehlmann/Ulm for the dual
groups, and primary work on Hom between countable primary groups if an exact
normal form is claimed.

**Prior subtraction.**  The entire object classification and all one-prime
Ulm data belong to P15R.  Only genuinely relative morphism data could remain.

**Strongest reduction/counterexample attack.**  Each primary dual has
countably many copies of cyclic summands of every bounded order.  Hom groups
therefore contain huge row-finite families independent of the small
Wieferich defect.  Unmarked morphisms can mix equal-order summands and do not
remember the omitted prime.  Replacing the owners by generic groups with the
same Ulm sequence reproduces the category.  Restricting to source-induced
morphisms returns to C1/C2 rather than saving C3.

```text
SOURCE_FEASIBLE: YES for a generic algebraic computation
```

```text
F(t.x)=lambda t.F(x).
```
