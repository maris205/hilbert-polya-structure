The original project, **Mixed Prime-Clock Standardization and Global Scaling
Rigidity**, is `NO_GO` as a standalone paper and is removed from the five
manuscript slots.

The binding reason is the original mathematical precheck's `C1/M1/m1`
finding.  If a positive length support `S` has a minimum and `cS=S` for
`c>0`, then

```text

```

so `c=1`.  Applied to `S={log p : p prime}`, this proves the proposed prime
scaling rigidity from the minimum `log 2`; the prime number theorem is not
load-bearing.  Consequently the declared standalone center is a routine
minimum lemma, not a substantive arithmetic rigidity theorem.
