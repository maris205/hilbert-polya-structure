## 2. Executive decision

Paper 14 does **not** pass the frozen standalone gate.

The proof is mathematically correct and owner-disciplined, and it supplies a
useful missing comparison on the actual suspension.  After subtracting the
three mandatory inherited packages, however, it does not leave a closed new
theorem of full-paper weight:

1. Deninger already owns the source topology, the prime-labelled finite-field
   strata, the Frobenius/Galois/suspension maps used in the comparison, and the
   collective equality `closure(Per)=Unit`.
2. Paper 9 already owns the actual inherited indiscreteness of every fixed
   packet.
3. Paper 10 already owns every generic consequence once those packets have
   been shown to form a topological coproduct: arbitrary component unions,
   the discrete label quotient, and the universal factorization into `T0`
   targets.
4. The remaining all-prime owner arrow is proved by the single coordinate
   test `|P(p)|<1/2`, followed by routine saturation through source maps.
5. The remaining exact ambient theorem for cofinite `S` is Deninger's
   collective approximation argument with finitely many rational primes
   excluded from an infinite eligible set.
6. For the genuinely harder infinite-coinfinite case, the ledger defines
   `Elig(a;T,epsilon)` and rewrites the neighborhood definition of closure as
   an incidence condition, but proves no new arithmetic theorem deciding that
   incidence.

Thus the remainder is an exact and worthwhile **source lemma plus companion
substitution package**, not a standalone research center.  Because Paper 17
is the batch's sole possible Technical Note, the only permitted disposition
for Paper 14 is **MERGE/REPLACE**.

## 3. Mandatory subtraction

### 3.1 Deninger collective unitary closure

Deninger Section 8, especially Claim 8.1, Theorem 8.2, and Lemma 8.3, owns the
load-bearing collective density statement.  On the frozen `Spec Z`, `E_f`
owner this yields


Paper 14 correctly credits this equality to Deninger.  It earns no new-credit

quotient, and continuous maps recording the prime stratum.  These are premises,
not Paper-14 contributions.

### 3.2 Paper 9 fixed-packet indiscreteness

Paper 9 proves that every actual `Gamma_p` is a nonempty nontrivial
indiscrete subspace.  It expressly stops short of the global suspension.
Paper 14 may own the missing global comparison, but cannot re-credit any of
the following:

```text
topology(Gamma_p) = indiscrete;
closure_Gamma_p({x}) = Gamma_p;
every continuous Gamma_p -> T0 target is constant.
```

Consequently the finite packet-union theorem is a direct consequence of
source-closed fibres plus this Paper-9 theorem and carries no standalone
weight.

### 3.3 Paper 10 generic coproduct and `T0` package

Paper 10 proves for any countable tagged coproduct of nonempty indiscrete
components that opens are precisely component unions, the Kolmogorov quotient
is the discrete label set, and continuous maps to `T0` targets are precisely
label-indexed families constant on each component.  Once Paper 14 proves that
the actual `Per` has this coproduct topology, the following are substitutions
into Paper 10's generic theorem:

```text

kappa:Per -> P_discrete is the Kolmogorov unit;
```

Actual-owner identification matters for correctness.  It does not transform
the inherited generic consequences into a second standalone contribution.

## 5. Strongest counter-argument

The strongest case against `STANDALONE_PASS` is not that any proof is wrong.
It is that the manuscript-shaped narrative would make one short comparison
lemma appear to generate several theorems by counting its inherited
consequences separately.

Once `|P(p)|<1/2` isolates `Gamma_p` inside the periodic locus, `G1`, `G2`,
and `G4` collapse into the already audited Paper-10 coproduct package.
Likewise, once Deninger's Theorem 8.2 and Lemma 8.3 are imported, the cofinite
ambient formula is obtained by excluding finitely many primes from an
infinite eligible set.  The only branch that could have supplied a genuinely
new arithmetic center—the closure for arbitrary infinite-coinfinite `S`—is
left as the equivalence

```text
x in closure(U_S)
iff every finite-evaluation neighborhood has an eligible prime in S,
```

where “eligible” is defined to mean that such an approximant exists.  That is
an exact and honest stopping rule, but it relocates the unsolved problem into
the definition instead of solving it.  A paper cannot obtain standalone
weight both from stopping before the hard incidence theorem and from the
formal consequences of the easy isolator.

This objection is singleton-decisive for the frozen publication gate.  It
does not depend on any mathematical defect or on finding a prior paper with
the identical final statement.
