## 3. Current official Deninger record: Sections 4–5

The governing primary record is Christopher Deninger, *Rational Witt vectors

### 3.1 Section 4 audit

Section 4 proves a canonical epimorphism on the stated sites

\[
\omega:\underline{\mathbb Z}(\mathcal O)^\sharp\twoheadrightarrow
W_{\mathrm{rat}}(\mathcal O)^\sharp.
\]

The exact positive results are narrower than the three retained slots:

- Corollary 4.6 identifies the two sheaves on the fp site at Dedekind rings.
- Corollary 4.7 makes \(\omega\) an isomorphism only for pretopologies finer
  than the finite (`f`) pretopology; listed examples include proper, finite,
  qfh, and h sites.
- Frobenius \(F_N\) acts canonically on the sheafified reduced monoid algebra
  and is compatible with rational-Witt Frobenius.
- The author explicitly states that it is unclear whether, for the fp or fppf
  topology, sheafified \(V_N\) on the rational-Witt sheaf lifts to an
  endomorphism of \(\underline{\mathbb Z}(\mathcal O)^\sharp\).
- On sites finer than the f-topology, the isomorphism \(\omega\) does give an
  additive \(V_N\) and an explicit splitting-cover description.  This does
  not answer the fp/fppf question.

The introductory suggestion that it may be interesting to experiment with
stronger descent conditions supplies none of \(D_{sd}\), \(W_{sd}\), a
universal law, comparison arrows, \(X_{sd}\), or a source-induced topology.

### 3.2 Section 5 audit

Theorem 5.1 gives, for **normal Noetherian affine** \(X\), a unique functorial
ring isomorphism

\[
W_{\mathrm{rat}}(\mathcal O(X))\simeq
\underline{\mathrm{Corr}}(X,\mathbb A^1).
\]

Proposition 5.2 identifies rational-Witt \(F_N,V_N\) with pushforward and
pullback along the finite flat power map on this correspondence ring.  The
source then asks whether the isomorphism extends to nonnormal schemes; it does
not state such an extension.

For an **affine** morphism \(\pi:Y\to X\) of Noetherian schemes, the source
defines the exact category \(\mathcal C_{Y/X}\) of quasicoherent
\(\mathcal O_Y\)-modules whose pushforward is a vector bundle.  It assigns an
object cycle in \(\mathrm{PropCycl}(Y/X,0)\).  In the special case
\(Y=\mathbb A^1_X\) with \(X\) as in Theorem 5.1, it relates this to
\(K_0(\mathrm{End}_A)\) and the Almkvist rational-Witt result.

The final paragraph says the author hopes for a comparison between
\(K_0(\mathcal C_{Y/X})\) and \(c_{\mathrm{equi}}(Y/X,0)\), for suitable
perfect-complex categories beyond affine \(\pi\), and for higher K-groups and
higher cycle groups.  It does **not** define the desired nonaffine category,
higher equivalence relation, K-degree, higher-cycle target, comparison map, or
the required F/V laws.

## 7. S16-R4 — fp/fppf Verschiebung lift versus packet bridge

### 7.1 Algebraic program (question 1)

This question is an exact, source-authored, well-typed open problem in A1:
for the fp or fppf site, does \(V_N\) on
\(W_{rat}(\mathcal O)^\sharp\) admit a natural additive lift to
\(\underline{\mathbb Z}(\mathcal O)^\sharp\) compatible with \(\omega\)?

It remains nontrivial after maximum subtraction:

- the explicit positive construction in A1 uses \(\omega\) as an isomorphism
  on sites finer than the f-site, which does not include an answer for fp/fppf;
- A4 studies kernels for a different monoid-algebra-to-\(S\)-Witt map, not
  this sheaf epimorphism;
- A5 gives F/V on reduced K-theory of endomorphisms, not on the sheafified
  reduced monoid algebra;
- A6–A7 give KEnd/motive/TR structures under different owners;
- a generic lifting or quotient-sheaf lemma does not compute the arithmetic
  obstruction, naturality, choice-independence, or F/V identities.

Accordingly, question 1 **passes as a feasible and nontrivial algebraic
research program**, not as a solved theorem.  The smallest missing datum is a
necessary-and-sufficient lift criterion for this particular sheaf epimorphism,
together with the computed fp/fppf obstruction for each \(N\).

The smallest credible theorem program is to fix one of the fp/fppf sites,
compute the kernel and local lifting data of \(\omega\), formulate and prove
the exact lift criterion for \(V_N\), and then prove naturality,
choice-independence, additivity, and the stated F/V relations.  Kernel
information alone is not asserted to solve the lifting problem.

### 7.2 Same-owner bridge (question 2)

No primary source or exactly canonical author construction maps a solution of
that sheaf problem functorially into the frozen arithmetic packet or the P12
actual/standard owner.  A2, A10, and A11 are arithmetic-dynamical or BC-system
frameworks, but none begins with the precise A1 fp/fppf lift and lands in the
same represented packet algebra.  A5–A7 are likewise different-owner KEnd/TR
frameworks.  Importing any of them would invent the forbidden bridge.

Thus question 1 passes and question 2 fails exactly as separated by v5 §3.2.

**Final disposition: `OUT_OF_BATCH_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_ONLY`.**

This disposition creates no sixth project, candidate, protocol, or downstream
authority and recommends no batch precheck.
