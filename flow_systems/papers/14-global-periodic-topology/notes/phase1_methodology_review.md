## 5. Major findings

### M1 — The all-prime theorem is not registered with an exact, falsifiable topology signature

**Problem.**  The batch lock requires the inherited topology on the union of
all fixed-prime packets.  The protocol registers the two-prime topology,
component status inside `Per_Ef`, and the global specialization preorder and
`T0` quotient.  Its only canonical coproduct comparison, P14-5, is against
`TAGGED-COPRODUCT-PQ`, a two-component control.  It does not register:

1. the canonical all-prime map from a countable topological coproduct to
   `Per_Ef`;
2. the exact continuity/openness/homeomorphism alternatives for that map;
3. the opens or relative closures of `union_{p in S} Gamma_p` for arbitrary
   `S subset Primes`; or
4. the separate ambient closures of those subfamilies inside `X_susp`.

P14-3 and P14-4 do not fill this gap.  Knowing that each component is open or
knowing the specialization preorder need not by itself constitute the
promised full topology classification unless the equivalence is stated and
proved.  Conversely, finite unions of closed packets do not license the
infinite conclusion.

**Impact.**  An implementer can complete P14-2, observe all finite unions are
coproducts, and then choose after the fact whether P14-4 means merely a point
preorder, a discrete label quotient, or the full all-prime topology.  The
central all-prime result and the P14-to-P18 dependency would therefore lack a
stable acceptance target.  This is a Major design defect because the
two-prime theorem is explicitly too small for standalone status.

**Minimum repair.**  Add separately numbered claims, with no proof result
preinserted:

```text
P14-G1. Define J : coproduct_{p in Primes} Gamma_p -> Per_Ef by the
component inclusions.  Decide whether J is a homeomorphism by proving or
refuting continuity and openness (equivalently, the required arbitrary-union
condition on the exact owner).

P14-G2. For every S subset Primes, compute
closure_Per_Ef(union_{p in S} Gamma_p) and state exactly which such unions
are open, closed, or clopen.

P14-G3. Separately compute or bound
closure_X_susp(union_{p in S} Gamma_p).  No relative-closure result may be
promoted to this ambient owner, and no finite-S argument may be promoted to
arbitrary S.

P14-G4. Freeze the specialization convention and the candidate quotient map
kappa:Per_Ef -> Primes; prove its quotient topology and universal T0
property only after P14-G1--G2.
```

If ambient infinite-subfamily closure is outside scope, mark P14-G3
explicitly `OUT_OF_SCOPE`; do not leave the two closure owners implicit.
Also add an all-prime tagged-coproduct control distinct from
`TAGGED-COPRODUCT-PQ`.

**Severity:** Major  
**Evidence Anchor:** `absence: research_protocol.md Sections 3-6 — expected an all-prime canonical comparison and arbitrary-subfamily closure signature; checked P14-1 through P14-7 and candidate_lock.md Sections Candidate through Pass / fail disposition`

### M2 — The standalone gate can accept a direct P9/P10 composition forbidden by the batch lock

**Problem.**  The batch publication rule requires merger, stopping, or
replacement when a project reduces to a standard lemma plus companion
substitution.  The protocol instead permits standalone status from an actual
cross-prime theorem plus an actual global `T0` quotient.  On the most likely
positive branch, however, the new work may be only a source-specific proof
that every `Gamma_p` is relatively clopen.  Paper 9 already owns that each
component is nonempty indiscrete, and Paper 10 already proves that any
countable coproduct of such components has exactly the component-union
topology and a discrete label `T0` quotient.  The quotient conclusion is then
not an independent standalone-bearing theorem; it is a generic consequence
already owned by Paper 10.

The source-topology precheck itself identifies the finite theorem as an
elementary corollary and acknowledges that Paper 10 already owns every
generic coproduct consequence.  Its suggestion that the all-prime
homeomorphism plus discrete quotient is automatically “substantive” is not an
independent nonredundancy criterion and is not adopted here.

**Impact.**  The current gate can label the same package both
`STANDALONE_CANDIDATE` under protocol Section 6 and “merge/stop/replace” under
the binding batch rule.  It also permits an internal-prior delta (“P9/P10 did
not prove this exact source comparison”) to substitute for scholarly weight.
That risks padding a short owner-identification lemma with results already
proved in Papers 9--10.

**Minimum repair.**  Freeze a claim-level delta matrix before proof:

```text
claim_id
exact premise owner
P9 inherited result
P10 inherited generic result
Deninger source result or nearest source precedent
new P14 lemma/construction
derivable by direct substitution (yes/no, with derivation)
infinity-sensitive content (yes/no)
ambient-owner content (yes/no)
standalone weight
failure disposition
```

Then replace the current standalone disjunction by a fail-closed rule:

```text
STANDALONE_PASS cannot be earned solely by
Per_Ef ~= coproduct_p Gamma_p,
K0(Per_Ef) ~= Primes_discrete,
or any continuous-map/Borel/measure consequence already covered by P10.

It requires at least one central infinity-sensitive or ambient-owner theorem
whose proof is not direct substitution of a source fibre-separation lemma,
P9 indiscreteness, and P10's coproduct theorem, followed by an independent
post-proof nonredundancy review and the bounded external precedent audit.

Otherwise the disposition is TECHNICAL_NOTE_OR_MERGE; allocation of the
batch's single possible Technical Note slot must be explicit rather than
silently counted as STANDALONE_PASS.
```

A possible qualifying target is a genuinely new classification of ambient
closures of infinite prime subfamilies, but it must be delimited from
Deninger's existing collective-closure theorem.  This is an example of the
required delta, not a demand to force a theorem that may be false.

**Severity:** Major  
**Evidence Anchor:** `text: papers14_18_batch_design_lock.md:20-25 "a project that reduces to a standard lemma plus companion substitution is merged, stopped, or replaced"`  
**Confidence:** 5/5 — direct comparison of binding publication rule, protocol acceptance rule, and P9/P10 theorem ownership
