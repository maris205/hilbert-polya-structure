## Contract Paraphrase

D1, methodology_rigor, requires the study design, treatment of data, statistical reporting, and provisions for reproducibility to satisfy the field's peer-review standard; this mandatory dimension is reserved for the methodology role.

D2, domain_accuracy, requires the claims to agree with current domain evidence, the prior literature to be represented correctly, and domain-specific terms and results to be factually accurate; this mandatory dimension is reserved for the domain role.

D3, argumentative_coherence, requires an internally consistent central thesis, evidential support for the claims, and freedom from fallacies capable of undermining the main argument; this mandatory dimension is evaluated by the Devil's Advocate and methodology roles, with the Devil's Advocate as owner.

D4, cross_disciplinary_relevance, requires framing, definitions, and implications that adjacent-field readers can understand, together with support for any interdisciplinary claims; this high-priority dimension is reserved for the perspective role.

D5, writing_and_structure, requires coherent organisation, clear exposition, effective figures and tables, and compliance with applicable venue conventions; this normal-priority dimension is the Journal-Fit Reviewer's responsibility.

D6, venue_fit_and_contribution, requires alignment with the configured venue and a contribution that is original, significant, and suitable for its readership; this mandatory dimension is the Journal-Fit Reviewer's responsibility.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A coherent manuscript progression, clear exposition, informative and legible figures and tables, and adherence to applicable venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Structural, expository, presentation, or convention defects that substantially obstruct evaluation of the main contribution and require extensive reorganisation or rewriting.
what_triggers_warn: Localised organisation, clarity, figure or table, or convention problems that reduce readability or polish but can be corrected without restructuring the core presentation.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Clear alignment with the configured venue's scope and readership, together with a credibly established original and significant contribution appropriate to that audience.
what_triggers_block: A substantial mismatch with the configured venue's scope or readership, or an insufficiently established original or significant contribution, requiring major reframing or development.
what_triggers_warn: A plausible venue fit and contribution whose originality, significance, or relevance to the intended readership needs limited clarification or sharper positioning.
what_triggers_fatal: An irreparable venue-scope mismatch or absence of a credible original contribution appropriate to the configured readership that makes acceptance at the venue impossible.
