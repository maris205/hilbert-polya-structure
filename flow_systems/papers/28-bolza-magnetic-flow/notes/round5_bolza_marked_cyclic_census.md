## 3. Exact matrix/equality audit

The published Bolza matrices are evaluated exactly in

```text
K = Q(s,t,i),
s^2 = 2,
t^2 = 1+s,
i^2 = -1,
rho = s t.
```

The eight-element basis is
`1,i,t,ti,s,si,st,sti`.  Rational coefficient vectors are reduced after every
operation.  In this model all four generator determinants are exactly one and
the length-eight polygon relator is exactly the identity.  The existing
Round-4 120-decimal representation is replayed independently for lengths,
periods, actions, and stability factors.

For every canonical word, the builder compares the complete exact matrix
coefficient vector modulo `M ~ -M` in `PSL(2,R)`, and then repeats the audit
modulo inversion `M ~ M^-1`.  Among the 390 records it finds

```text

```

This proves absence of literal represented-element collisions in the finite
table.  It is not a conjugator search and therefore is not promoted to a

There are only 35 exact trace-squared groups, each non-singleton, and the
largest contains 32 records.  Equal `tr^2` gives equal hyperbolic length but
does not prove quotient-group conjugacy; no rows are merged or credited on

## 4. Exact primitivity gate

Let `ell_B` be the published Bolza systole.  For a hyperbolic element `g`,

```text
ell(g) < 2 ell_B
  iff |tr(g)| < 2 cosh(ell_B)
  iff |tr(g)| < 10 + 8 sqrt(2).
```

The last threshold is exact because the side-pairing trace is
`2(1+sqrt(2))` and `tr(f_j^2)=tr(f_j)^2-2=10+8sqrt(2)`.

If a marked-primitive `g` satisfying the strict inequality were `h^q` in
`Gamma` for `q>=2`, translation lengths would give

```text
ell(h) = ell(g)/q < ell_B,
```

contradicting systolic minimality.  Exact sign comparison in `Q(sqrt(2))`
certifies 44 records:

| Marked length | Marked-primitive candidates | `Gamma` primitivity proved |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 | 4 |
| 2 | 12 | 12 |
| 3 | 56 | 16 |
| 4 | 294 | 12 |
| **Total** | **366** | **44** |

The remaining 322 marked-primitive records do not fail primitivity; their
`Gamma` primitivity is simply `[OPEN]` under the present certificate.

## 5. Distinct owner credit and the no-double-counting rule

The polygon relator has zero exponent sum in every generator, hence the
abelianization is `Gamma_ab = Z^4`.  Conjugate elements have the same homology
vector, while inversion negates it.  Thus inverse-paired axes with different
homology vectors modulo sign cannot be conjugate or inverse-conjugate.

The credit rule is consequently:

```text
1. require exact Gamma-primitivity proof;
2. group proved records by homology vector modulo sign;
3. credit exactly the lexicographically first record in each group;
4. withhold every same-axis extra until a stronger Gamma-conjugacy invariant
   or certified conjugator/nonconjugacy procedure is supplied.
```

The 44 proved primitives occupy 36 homology axes modulo sign.  Therefore 36
inverse-paired primitive axis owners per field receive credit, and eight
additional proved primitives are retained without owner credit.  The credited
length distribution is `4,12,12,8` for marked lengths `1,2,3,4`.

This rule preserves all four Round-4 owner IDs.  It never creates an oriented
owner field, and signed `k` never creates an owner.
