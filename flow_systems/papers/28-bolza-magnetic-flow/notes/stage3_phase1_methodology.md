## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From a methodology-rigor perspective, the research design, treatment of data, quantitative or other analytic reporting, and provisions for reproducibility must collectively satisfy the field's peer-review standard.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Methodological conclusions must remain anchored to accurate domain evidence, faithful treatment of prior work, and correct use of field-specific concepts and results, although this dimension belongs to the domain reviewer for scoring.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): The methodological evidence and inferential steps must support the central thesis consistently, without contradictions, unsupported leaps, or fallacies that compromise the main argument.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): The framing, definitions, and implications should permit scrutiny by adjacent-field readers, and any interdisciplinary extension must have adequate support, although the perspective reviewer owns this score.

D5 (writing_and_structure): The manuscript should present its material in a clear, well-organized form, with effective figures or tables and conformity to relevant venue conventions, while scoring remains with the Journal-Fit Reviewer.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): The work must suit the configured venue and establish an original, significant contribution for its intended readership, with the Journal-Fit Reviewer responsible for assessing this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: A sound research design, appropriate data handling and analysis, complete analytic reporting, and sufficient procedural detail and materials for reproducibility.
what_triggers_block: A central result depends on a materially invalid design, analysis, data-handling procedure, or non-reproducible method that requires substantial correction.
what_triggers_warn: The core method remains usable, but important reporting, justification, robustness, or reproducibility details are incomplete or unclear.
what_triggers_fatal: A single unrecoverable methodological defect invalidates the core claim or makes acceptance impossible.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Consistent reasoning in which the methods and evidence warrant the central claims, assumptions are explicit, and no fallacy undermines the thesis.
what_triggers_block: A central claim is materially unsupported by the stated evidence or rests on a serious inferential inconsistency or fallacy requiring substantial reanalysis or reframing.
what_triggers_warn: The argument generally holds, but one or more evidence-to-claim links, assumptions, or limitations need clarification or bounded correction.
what_triggers_fatal: A single irreparable contradiction, unsupported inferential leap, or fallacy invalidates the central thesis or makes acceptance impossible.
