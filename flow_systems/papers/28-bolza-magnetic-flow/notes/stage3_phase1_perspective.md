### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Clear definitions and scope boundaries across the named fields, substantiated bridges between disciplinary objects or implications, and an interpretation that adjacent-field readers can use without importing unstated equivalences.
