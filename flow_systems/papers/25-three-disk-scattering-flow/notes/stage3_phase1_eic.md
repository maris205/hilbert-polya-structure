## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires the methodology seat to determine whether the study design, handling of evidence, statistical reporting, and reproducibility provisions meet the field’s mandatory peer-review standard.

D2 requires the domain seat to determine whether the claims, use of prior work, terminology, and reported results are factually accurate and consistent with current domain evidence; this is a mandatory threshold.

D3 requires the designated argumentative and methodological seats to determine whether the central thesis is internally consistent and adequately supported, without reasoning failures that compromise the core argument; this is also mandatory.

D4 requires the perspective seat to assess whether adjacent-field readers can follow the framing, definitions, and implications and whether any interdisciplinary reach is genuinely supported; this carries high priority.

D5 requires editorial oversight of organization, expository clarity, visual presentation, and conformity with applicable publication conventions, with deficiencies calibrated at the contract’s normal priority.

D6 requires editorial oversight of scope appropriateness, originality, significance, and prospective value to the intended readership, with contribution quality treated as a mandatory acceptance dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Coherent organization, clear exposition, legible and purposeful figures or tables, and consistent observance of applicable presentation conventions.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive structural or presentational defects make the manuscript substantially unreadable or prevent reliable editorial evaluation of its argument.
what_triggers_warn: Localized clarity, organization, visual-presentation, or convention problems materially impede reading but remain remediable without rebuilding the manuscript.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Clear evidence that the topic, article form, originality, significance, and likely reader value satisfy the configured scope and contribution threshold.
what_triggers_block: A material scope or readership mismatch, or an insufficiently original or significant contribution, would require fundamental repositioning before the work could qualify.
what_triggers_warn: The prospective contribution and reader value are plausible, but their framing, differentiation from prior work, or scope justification needs substantial clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The work is categorically outside the configured remit or lacks any identifiable original contribution of publishable significance.
