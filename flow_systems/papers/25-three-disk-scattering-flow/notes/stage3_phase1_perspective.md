## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From a cross-disciplinary perspective, methodological acceptance requires study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support that give adjacent-field readers a trustworthy basis for interpreting and extending the work.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Domain claims, terminology, results, and representations of prior evidence must be accurate enough that readers outside the primary specialty can rely on them without carrying factual distortions into neighboring fields.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): The central thesis must follow consistently from the stated evidence and reasoning, with no premise shifts or inferential gaps that would undermine its interpretation across disciplinary boundaries.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): Framing, definitions, and implications must provide genuine bridges for adjacent-field readers, and any interdisciplinary reach must be supported rather than asserted or left dependent on unexplained specialist assumptions.

D5 (writing_and_structure): Organization, exposition, and visual materials must make the line of inquiry navigable, while presentation conventions should help the intended readership distinguish core claims, evidence, and limitations.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): Acceptance requires an original and significant contribution appropriately situated for its intended readership; from a cross-disciplinary standpoint, the contribution's scope, importance, and boundaries should also be legible beyond the core specialty.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether framing and definitions let adjacent-field readers understand the problem without importing unstated specialist assumptions, and whether interdisciplinary implications are supported with clear scope boundaries.
what_triggers_block: Cross-disciplinary framing is so inaccessible or unsupported that adjacent-field readers would be materially misled about the work's scope or implications.
what_triggers_warn: Cross-disciplinary relevance is generally intelligible, but important definitions, conceptual bridges, or limits remain underdeveloped.
