This venue-unbound assessment finds a clearly structured negative-result paper with a concise exact obstruction, useful symbolic and stability controls, and unusually careful object typing. Its general scholarly contribution and likely reader value are plausible. The main editorial reservation is originality positioning: the cited background explains the relevant object distinctions and the periodic-orbit obstruction principle, but it does not establish how the paper's specific nonconstant-roof result differs from the closest prior results for open billiards or suspension flows.

### S1: The thesis is explicit and correctly typed
**Evidence Anchor**: text: Abstract — "These positive results belong to typed symbolic and stability-control objects; they do not transfer determinant credit to the physical flow."

The title, abstract, introduction, and conclusion consistently identify scalar-clock nontransfer as the result under discussion and keep the symbolic determinant separate from the physical billiard flow and exact quantum scattering.

### S2: The central contribution has an economical exact witness
**Evidence Anchor**: equation: Propositions 4.1 and 4.2 and Corollary 4.1, exact period-two and period-three mean-roof formulas and their positive gap

The two symmetric owners turn a potentially diffuse modeling objection into a short exact proof, while the minimax corollary gives the obstruction a quantitative interpretation. This is the paper's strongest conceptual contribution.
