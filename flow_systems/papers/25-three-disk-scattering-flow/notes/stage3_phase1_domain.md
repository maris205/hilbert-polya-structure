## Contract Paraphrase

D1, methodology_rigor, asks whether the study design, treatment of data, statistical presentation, and support for reproducibility satisfy the relevant research standard; from a domain-accuracy perspective, these elements should enable field-specific claims to be interpreted and checked on an appropriate evidentiary basis.

D2, domain_accuracy, requires the domain seat to determine whether specialized claims agree with current knowledge, prior scholarship is represented faithfully, and field-specific terminology and reported results are factually sound; because this dimension is mandatory, defects that invalidate the core contribution must be distinguished from substantial or limited correctable problems.

D3, argumentative_coherence, concerns whether the central thesis remains internally consistent and whether the offered evidence genuinely supports the conclusions without reasoning failures that defeat the main argument; domain expertise informs whether the substantive inferential links are credible, although this seat does not score the dimension.

D4, cross_disciplinary_relevance, asks whether readers in neighboring fields can follow the framing, definitions, and implications and whether interdisciplinary assertions have adequate support; from the domain perspective, accessibility should preserve technical meaning rather than introduce misleading simplifications.

D5, writing_and_structure, covers the organization and clarity of the manuscript, the communicative quality of figures and tables, and conformity with applicable presentation conventions; domain accuracy matters insofar as exposition and visuals must communicate specialized content without distortion, while this seat does not assess the dimension.

D6, venue_fit_and_contribution, assigns assessment of suitability and of an original, significant contribution for the intended readership to the designated owner; from a domain-accuracy perspective, any contribution assessment should be grounded in an accurate account of existing knowledge, but this seat neither scores venue fit nor pre-judges it.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Alignment of domain-specific claims with current evidence, faithful representation of prior work, precise field terminology, and accurate characterization of results.
what_triggers_block: Material domain errors, serious misrepresentation of established evidence, or inaccurate terminology or result claims that substantially compromise the central conclusions.
what_triggers_warn: Localized inaccuracies, incomplete prior-work positioning, or imprecise terminology that weaken domain reliability but remain correctable without overturning the central conclusions.
what_triggers_fatal: A fundamental field-specific falsehood or pervasive evidence misrepresentation that invalidates the core contribution and makes acceptance impossible.
