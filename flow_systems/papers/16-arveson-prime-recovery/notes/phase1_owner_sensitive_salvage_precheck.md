locked Paper 16 tuple.

actual fixed-prime packet by a standard coproduct of circles, minimal invariant

   lines is not yet a well-typed mathematical object.  The elementary

   Fourier generators by phases or permute equal-length components; they do not
   change the restricted spectral lattices and yield no proved obstruction.

### P9 ceiling

For a fixed rational prime, the actual packet and its individual actual orbits

description, not a homeomorphism to a Hausdorff product.  Hence neither the

`J : G_std -> G_actual`,

not `G_actual -> G_std`.  Contravariant cohomological pullback sends the actual

arbitrary-index `c_0` lemma owned by P13 and is prime-nonselective.  Literal

## 5. Attack 1: source transitions versus Arveson subspaces

### 5.1 What the primary source actually licenses

The retained primary PDF is
`papers/12-marked-time-cohomology/notes/sources/coh-deninger-dynamical-arithmetic-schemes-v4.pdf`,
SHA-256
`edd0bc8c2efb601ed7574e8eceae40e8cde21d0e4b2bc8c4ce7e60d8e1f82a09`.

Around equations (35)--(40), the source fixes a global injection `iota` on
roots of unity and a point `x` above the closed point.  Equations (35)--(39)
give equivariant set presentations/balanced-product descriptions.  The source
then states two facts that must be kept together:

- the displayed coordinate maps and fibration map depend on `x` and `iota`;
- the resulting projection to the time quotient is canonical.

The source passage does **not** give formulas for replacing `(x,iota)` by
`(x',iota')`; it gives no combined transition group or groupoid, no
inverse/composition law, no double/triple-overlap data, and no induced action on
an operator algebra.  A statement of choice-dependence is not itself a cocycle.

There is nevertheless a concrete preliminary observation for the rational
base.  The roots of unity in an algebraic closure of `Q` form `Q/Z`, whose
automorphism group is `Zhat^times`.  Thus two global injections `iota`, after
the standard identifications, differ by a profinite-unit automorphism, and its
restriction away from `p` lands in `Zhat_(p)^times`.  For two primes, the two
restrictions agree on every prime-adic factor away from both `p` and `q`.  This
is a genuine lead for a cross-prime replacement, but it is not yet the full
source transition calculus: the independent changes of `x_p` and `x_q`, the
balanced-product equivalences, and their possible absorption of that overlap
compatibility remain unaudited.

Moreover, any transition between the displayed presentations that represents
the same source object must lie over the same canonical time quotient.  The
source therefore does not license an unproved mixing of the canonical time base
with the transverse coordinate merely to manufacture spectral monodromy.

### 5.2 What the standard circle calculation can test

Let `O_L = R/LZ` and use the Fourier generator

`e_n(s) = exp(2 pi i n s/L)`.

Changing an origin by `a` gives

`e_n(s+a) = exp(2 pi i n a/L) e_n(s)`.

Thus origin change multiplies a one-dimensional Fourier line by a scalar while
leaving its Arveson frequency `2 pi n/L` unchanged.  A strict flow-intertwining
permutation can only move a component to another component with the same
restricted lattice; a declared time scaling merely applies the already-audited
global spectral scaling.  Orientation reversal belongs to a reversed-time or
scaled convention and cannot be smuggled into the strict category.

These are the only transition types presently auditable **on the standard
owner**.  They produce phase gauges and component permutations.  They do not
produce a nontrivial obstruction class.  Calling the phases a cocycle would add
no content unless one first proves the source transition groupoid and then
proves that the resulting class is not a coboundary.

### 5.3 Cross-prime arithmetic does not enter through the direct sum

The numerical lengths `log p` are arithmetically special, and unique
factorisation controls integer relations among them.  But a `c_0` direct sum has
zero products between distinct minimal component ideals.  Its ideal lattice and
componentwise Arveson spectra contain no operation that couples a Fourier line
over `p` to one over `q`.  Reading the label `p` back from the already-supplied
number `log p` is parameter identification, not rational-Witt recovery.

Any surviving correlated action of the same global `iota` on different prime
presentations would have to be computed together with all allowed lift changes
before an associated bundle of spectral lines could even be defined.  That
full computation is absent from the locked tuple and cannot be replaced by the
embedding-only observation or by an unaudited choice transformation.

### Attack-1 result

**NO_GO.**  There is no complete source-permitted, composable transition system
on the coordinate presentations in the current evidence from which to form a
nontrivial Arveson cocycle/obstruction.  The elementary standard-owner changes
preserve the spectral lattice and amount to gauge/permutation packaging.

## 6. Attack 2: can the actual-to-standard comparison carry cross-component structure?

### 6.1 Direction is decisive

The underlying carrier identity is continuous as

`J : packet_std -> packet_actual`.

The reverse identity from the nontrivial indiscrete actual packet to the
standard coproduct is not continuous.  Hence there is no covariant transport of
actual topology, open components, or minimal ideals into the standard owner.
The standard component decomposition is deliberately created by
standardisation.

### 6.2 What contravariant pullback retains

There are two already-audited manifestations, neither of which is new P16
structure:

- In P12 cohomology, `J*` maps the actual line to the component-constant
  diagonal.  It carries no component selector and is fixed by arbitrary
  component permutations.
- At the unit-space level, continuous scalar observations of the actual
  indiscrete packet cannot separate actual orbits.  Their standard pullbacks are
  component-constant fixed-point data; they have no nonzero Arveson frequencies
  with which to encode orbit separation.

For time-supported/groupoid functions, P13 supplies the sharper obstruction:
the pullback has support on every standard component.  With an infinite
component set, no nonzero such pullback belongs to the standard
compact-support algebra.  Completion can package a selected diagonal in a
multiplier/corona algebra, but that is precisely P13's generic `c_0` mechanism.

### 6.3 Why using “the same map for every prime” is not coupling

Applying the same construction componentwise gives a family of identical
arrows.  A family is not a cross-prime relation.  To obtain coupling one would
need, for example, a proved diagonal transition group, a multiplication or
correspondence between different prime fibres, or an intrinsic compatibility
condition whose projections to the two individual primes lose information.
None is present in the standard coproduct algebra or in the audited comparison
map.

The prime tags, periods, and source packet memberships may be retained as
external marked data.  They are not recovered from the actual indiscrete
topology by `J`, and they are not reconstructed by the componentwise Arveson
lattice.  Treating copied labels as recovered invariants would reverse the owner
arrow.

### Attack-2 result

**NO_GO.**  The comparison map yields P12's constant diagonal, P13's generic
support/completion phenomenon, or zero-frequency/fixed-point data.  It carries
no presently defined cross-prime or cross-orbit structure that can serve as a
new centre.

## 9. Minimal slot-16 replacement research question

### RQ16-R (source-global two-prime transition factorisation)

For two distinct rational primes `p != q`, first derive the exact groupoid
`T_{p,q}` of source-permitted changes of the paired presentations in Deninger's
equations (35)--(39), induced by changing the **same global** root-of-unity
injection `iota` together with the independently allowed lifts `x_p` and `x_q`.
Let `T_p` and `T_q` be the corresponding audited fixed-prime transition
groupoids.  Is the natural image

`T_{p,q} -> T_p x T_q`

a proper overlap-compatible subgroupoid whose projections contain all relevant
fixed-prime arrows (in the transition-group case, a proper subdirect product)?
If it is, does the paired actual presentation admit an intrinsic invariant that:

1. survives every arrow of `T_{p,q}`;
2. becomes trivial or undetectable after projection to either prime alone; and
3. cannot be defined from an arbitrary pair of unmarked `c_0` circle systems?

This is the smallest credible replacement because its first binary test is
factorisation.  It does not assume that a coupling survives the lift changes,
does not import a topology onto a set presentation, and does not put Arveson
theory in the centre before there is something source-specific for it to
represent.

At the embedding-choice level alone the test is nonvacuous.  Writing

`B_p = product_(ell != p) Z_ell^times`,

the image of one global `Zhat^times` in `B_p x B_q` is the fibre product in
which the `ell`-coordinates agree for every `ell != p,q`; it is a proper
subdirect product.  A relative mismatch on the common factors is therefore a
candidate pair-only invariant.  The decisive unknown is whether the complete
source-permitted `x_p` and `x_q` transitions act independently enough to erase
that mismatch.  RQ16-R asks exactly that question; this precheck does not assume
the favourable answer.
