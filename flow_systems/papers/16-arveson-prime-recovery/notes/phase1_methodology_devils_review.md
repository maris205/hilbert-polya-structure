## 4. Arbitrary-index strong continuity

For `f=(f_q)` in the arbitrary-index `c0` sum and any tolerance, the `c0`
condition supplies a finite set `F` outside which all `||f_q||` are small.
On the finite set, uniform continuity on the compact circles gives one
common time neighborhood. Outside it,

```text
||alpha_t(f_q)-f_q|| <= 2 ||f_q||.
```

Taking the coordinate supremum proves norm continuity at zero and hence at
every time. No enumeration of `Q`, countable approximate unit, separability,
or sigma-unitality enters. This verifies amendment v1 Section 3 exactly on
the declared owner.

This is nevertheless not a standalone contribution. Paper 13 already
freezes the arbitrary-index `c0` definition and its finite-tail discipline;
the additional continuity estimate is the standard direct-sum action
argument. It is necessary domain hygiene, not a new analytic centre.

## 5. Intrinsic invariant-ideal reconstruction

For the commutative algebra `C_0(Std_mix X)`, closed ideals correspond to
open subsets of the spectrum. An ideal is `alpha`-invariant exactly when its
open set is invariant under the real action. Since each `O_q` is one
transitive orbit, every nonempty invariant open set that meets `O_q`
contains all of `O_q`. Therefore every nonzero invariant ideal contains a
coordinate ideal, and the minimal nonzero invariant ideals are precisely

```text
A_q = C(O_q), extended by zero.
```

This description uses the displayed components to verify the theorem, but
the result itself is the intrinsic order-theoretic set `Min_alpha(A)`.
Strict equivariant isomorphisms preserve it, and positive globally scaled
isomorphisms preserve it because `cR=R`. Repeated lengths are therefore not
collapsed: the number of ideals with a given reconstructed length is its
full cardinal multiplicity.

The hostile attack here does not find a correctness defect. It does find a
weight defect: this is the atomic invariant-open-subset calculation for a
coproduct of transitive components. Paper 12 already proves that
equivariant maps permute the open action orbits. Passing from that statement
to minimal invariant ideals of the commutative function algebra is an
intrinsic reformulation, not a difficult new reconstruction mechanism.

## 6. Arveson lattice, period recovery, and scaled variance

On a component, a Fourier mode with integer index `n` is an eigenvector for
translation with frequency `+2 pi n/L_q` or its negative, according to the
single frozen Arveson/Fourier convention. Trigonometric polynomials are dense
and the frequency lattice is closed, so the full restricted Arveson spectrum
is exactly

```text
Sp(alpha|A_q) = (2 pi/L_q) Z.
```

The lattice contains `0` through constant functions and contains nonzero
positive and negative modes. Thus the registered positive-length owner has
no unresolved zero-only branch, and

```text
L_q = 2 pi / min(Sp(alpha|A_q) intersect R_{>0}).
```

If a later theorem is broadened beyond these circle components, a
zero-spectrum ideal must be left as a separate undefined/infinite-period
branch rather than forced through this formula.

For a space map `F:X->Y` satisfying

```text
F(x.t)=F(x).(ct),
```

the pullback `Phi=F^*:A_Y->A_X` satisfies, exactly as the amendment states,

```text
alpha^X_t Phi = Phi alpha^Y_{ct}.
```

The corresponding frequency and length directions are

```text
Sp(alpha^X | Phi(I_Y)) = c Sp(alpha^Y | I_Y),
L_X(Phi(I_Y)) = L_Y(I_Y)/c,
M_X(lambda) = M_Y(c lambda).
```

Strict classification is the case `c=1`. Necessity follows from the
intrinsic ideals and their spectra; sufficiency chooses cardinal-matched
component bijections and circle torsor maps. The amendment correctly records
the ZFC-choice and origin-choice boundaries.

## 7. All-orbits prime application and the minimum control

The amended all-orbits type is correct. Intrinsic recovery returns one
minimal ideal for every orbit in every `Q_p`; equal periods remain repeated

supplied marked prime-clock family, not selection of primes from an

The global-scale minimum observation is also correctly bounded. If the


Hence `c=1`. The same statement holds for every nonempty positive set with
a minimum. It is a sharp control on the classification and contributes no
prime-specific novelty. Amendment v1 and candidate lock v2 respect the
binding old-Paper-15 adverse disposition on this point.

### Major M1 — The promised unmarked classification is not complete

**Problem.** Amendment v1 Section 6 is titled a complete
`strict/scaled/unmarked classification`, but its unmarked line says only
that the algebra does not recover numerical lengths. That negative statement
is correct but is not an existence or automorphism classification. For
example, forgetting the action makes every component algebra isomorphic to
`C(S^1)`, yet two sums with different numbers of connected components need
not be isomorphic. Unmarked component maps are arbitrary circle
homeomorphisms, not only the rotations occurring in the strict/scaled
categories.

**Impact.** The same phrase `complete classification` currently denotes an
iff theorem in two variances and only a loss example in the third. An
implementer could therefore overstate the unmarked result or silently assume
that arbitrary `Q_X,Q_Y` are isomorphic once the lengths are forgotten.

**Minimum repair.** Either rename the package to `strict/scaled
classification plus unmarked nonrecovery`, or freeze the exact unmarked
criterion. On the current all-circle owner, this should state at least that
an unmarked algebra isomorphism exists iff the component index sets have the
same cardinality, identify the contravariant component permutation and
arbitrary circle homeomorphisms, and record the relevant Choice boundary.
Keep this separate from minimal **invariant** ideals, since unmarked maps do
not preserve a forgotten action.

**Severity:** Major  
**Evidence Anchor:** `text: phase1_amendment_v1.md Section 6 "Complete strict/scaled/unmarked classification" and "unmarked algebra isomorphism does not recover the numerical lengths"`  
**Confidence:** 5/5 — commutative C-star/Gelfand and connected-component typing
