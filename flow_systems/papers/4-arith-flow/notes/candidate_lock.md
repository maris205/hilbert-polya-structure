## 3. What the lock does and does not certify

### Certified native statement

The construction is an exact continuous-time realization of the **native
finite-field** closed-point zeta.  Its arithmetic data are generated by a fixed
scheme and its Frobenius map, not by a list supplied after the fact.

### Non-certified Riemann statement

The construction is not a rational-prime candidate.  All its primitive lengths
belong to \((\log2)\mathbb N\).  If

\[
  n\log2=r\log p
\]

for a rational prime \(p\), unique factorization forces \(p=2\).  Exact
finite-field A0--A3 structure therefore does not transfer to
\(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\).

### Non-certified operator statement

The equality between orbit zeta and the etale-cohomological determinant is an
equality of functions established through their common arithmetic source.  No
operator conjugacy, trace-class transfer operator, wave trace, self-adjoint
quantization, or Hilbert--Pólya operator has been constructed.

## 7. Falsification triggers

The candidate is rejected or re-ID'd if any of the following occurs:

1. the discrete topology is omitted or called intrinsic;
2. \(F^{-1}\) versus \(F\) is used to manufacture an unsupported phase;
3. a shift \(s\mapsto s+1/2\) is inserted without a source-derived potential;
4. the cohomological determinant is called a Poincare/transfer-operator
   determinant of the circle flow without a trace bridge;
5. a second rational-prime base is claimed inside the one-clock period lattice;
6. disjoint \(\log p\) components are relabeled “emergent” merely because they
   are indexed by closed points of \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\);
7. finite-field functional equations or RH theorems are transferred to the
   Riemann target by analogy;
8. any Riemann-zero data enter parameter selection or validation.
