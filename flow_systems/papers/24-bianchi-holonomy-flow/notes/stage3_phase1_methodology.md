## Contract Paraphrase

D1, methodology_rigor, is a mandatory gate requiring the study design, handling of evidence or data, analytical reporting, and reproducibility provisions to meet the field’s peer-review standard; from the methodology seat, this means assessing whether the methods can validly support the stated research aims and be independently followed or checked.

D2, domain_accuracy, is a mandatory gate requiring claims, terminology, results, and treatment of prior evidence to remain factually correct within the domain; although the domain seat assesses it, the methodological significance is that an analysis cannot be sound when its substantive premises or representations are inaccurate.

D3, argumentative_coherence, is a mandatory gate requiring a consistent central thesis, evidential support for each material inference, and freedom from reasoning defects that undermine the argument; the methodology seat will examine the validity and traceability of the inferential chain.

D4, cross_disciplinary_relevance, is a high-priority gate requiring framing, definitions, implications, and interdisciplinary claims to be intelligible and substantiated for adjacent-field readers; it belongs to the perspective seat rather than the methodology score.

D5, writing_and_structure, is a normal-priority gate requiring clear organization and exposition, effective figures or tables where present, and compliance with relevant presentation conventions; it belongs to the editor-in-chief seat rather than the methodology score.

D6, venue_fit_and_contribution, is a mandatory gate defining the need for an original and significant contribution suited to the configured venue and its readership; assessment of that criterion belongs to the editor-in-chief seat, not the methodology seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: A clearly specified and logically appropriate research design, transparent evidence or data handling, valid analytical or proof procedures, adequate checks on assumptions and boundary conditions, and enough procedural detail for independent verification or reproduction.
what_triggers_block: A major but potentially repairable defect in the design, analytical procedure, validation strategy, or reproducibility record leaves a central result inadequately supported and requires substantial reconstruction or additional verification.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, incomplete robustness check, or reproducibility omission weakens confidence without defeating the main methodological pathway and can be addressed through bounded clarification or supplementation.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational design or inferential failure makes the central conclusion methodologically unjustifiable and cannot be corrected without replacing the core method, evidential basis, or research claim.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable progression from premises and definitions through evidence, computation, or proof to the central claims, with internal consistency and explicit control of limitations, boundary cases, and plausible counterexamples.
what_triggers_block: A substantial but repairable logical gap, circular dependency, or unsupported inference materially undermines the central argument and demands major proof, analysis, or claim revision.
what_triggers_warn: A limited inferential shortcut, unclear premise-to-claim transition, or insufficiently developed qualification creates uncertainty while leaving the central argumentative structure intact.
what_triggers_fatal: An internal contradiction, invalid decisive inference, or unaddressed counterexample directly defeats the central thesis and admits no local repair consistent with the stated argument.
