## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From an adversarial perspective, this dimension tests whether the research design, treatment of data, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility provisions can withstand scrutiny at the field's peer-review standard.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension requires a hostile check of whether substantive claims remain consistent with current knowledge, prior scholarship is represented without distortion, and specialized terms and results contain no consequential factual errors.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis survives the strongest counter-reading: its premises must be mutually consistent, every decisive inference must be supported, and no fallacy or hidden assumption may break the route from evidence to conclusion.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension tests whether readers from neighboring fields can follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and whether any interdisciplinary reach is supported rather than merely asserted.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension subjects the manuscript's organization, explanatory clarity, visual materials, and conformity to expected presentation practices to challenge for defects that impede evaluation or comprehension.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension requires the designated reviewer to test whether the work's subject, readership, originality, and significance satisfy the configured venue's contribution threshold; it precommits only to that test and makes no finding of alignment.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Internal consistency of the central thesis, explicit support for each decisive inferential step, disclosure of load-bearing assumptions, and engagement with the strongest plausible counterarguments.
what_triggers_block: A major break in the central reasoning chain, unsupported decisive premise, or evidence-to-claim gap that materially undermines the main conclusion but remains repairable through substantial revision.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, underdeveloped counterargument, or limited inferential leap that weakens confidence without compromising the central thesis.
what_triggers_fatal: A demonstrably contradictory or circular core argument, or a conclusion that cannot follow from the stated premises without replacing the paper's central thesis.
