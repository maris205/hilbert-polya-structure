The determinant calculation is correct, but the strongest counterargument concerns what it establishes at paper scale. The universal identity follows immediately from the elementary 2-by-2 determinant expansion and cancellation by a non-zero-divisor. It decisively defeats normalized level-three trace divisibility as the candidate chosen here, yet the manuscript supplies no independent scholarly or frozen antecedent showing that this candidate was a serious Gaussian owner proposal. Without that antecedent, the result is a sound obstruction to an internally selected mechanism rather than a demonstrated negative resolution of a live ownership hypothesis.

The first-jet algebra is likewise sound within its stated scope, but the finite certificate does not reach the owner domain. Its population consists of exact matrices rather than certified primitive loxodromic conjugacy classes, and the constructive collision witnesses are upper-unitriangular trace-two elements rather than primitive closed-geodesic owners. Because a conjugacy quotient can collapse matrix collisions, the reported 10,964 joint collision rows quantify matrix compression but do not by themselves prove that distinct primitive owners collide. The language of a quantified necessary owner refinement should therefore be narrowed or supported by an owner-level collision witness.

The ownership relation also needs one unambiguous group boundary. For the frozen level-three quotient, level-subgroup conjugacy and inversion are the stated equivalences, under which the signed jet is lawful. Ambient Bianchi conjugacy instead acts by a reduced adjoint action. The manuscript identifies both facts but does not settle whether ambient conjugacy is irrelevant covering symmetry or part of the intended owner quotient; the descriptor cannot be called owner-compatible beyond the former relation until that choice is fixed. The power law similarly verifies known repetitions but supplies no primitive-root decision, so primitivity remains an independent missing layer.

The incomplete canonical-control gate remains outcome-determinative for the Route-A package even though it does not weaken the universal algebraic theorem. Two of three prescribed control types are covered, while the missing type and its precommitted discriminating prediction are not specified. Accordingly the full Bianchi flow remains unassigned, `P24-BIANCHI-MARKED-WORD-PROXY` remains `(A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)` with `ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY` status, and the suggested ideal- or Hecke-valued successor remains a research direction rather than a positive successor theorem. Nothing here supports Route B, an operator construction, or a spectral promotion.
