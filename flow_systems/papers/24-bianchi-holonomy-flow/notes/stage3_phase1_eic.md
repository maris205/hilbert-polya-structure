## Contract Paraphrase

D1 places a mandatory bar on methodological rigor: the study design, handling of evidence, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility support must collectively permit dependable evaluation by the methodology reviewer.

D2 makes domain accuracy mandatory: the domain reviewer must verify that factual claims, technical language, reported results, and treatment of prior scholarship are accurate and consistent with current knowledge.

D3 requires a coherent central argument as a mandatory condition: the designated reviewers must determine whether the thesis is internally consistent and whether the presented reasoning and evidence genuinely support it.

D4 assigns high priority to cross-disciplinary relevance: the perspective reviewer must examine whether framing, definitions, and implications are intelligible beyond the immediate specialty and whether broader connections are adequately supported.

D5 assigns the EIC seat responsibility for writing and structure at normal priority: editorial assessment will consider organization, clarity, presentation of figures and tables, and compliance with applicable manuscript conventions.

D6 gives the EIC seat a mandatory contribution assessment: the work must establish originality, significance, and value for a relevant scholarly readership; without target authority, this commitment does not prejudge alignment with any particular venue.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A coherent manuscript architecture, clear exposition, legible and useful figures or tables, and consistent observance of applicable presentation conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organization or presentation defects prevent reliable comprehension of the central contribution or render essential manuscript elements unusable.
what_triggers_warn: Localized problems in clarity, sequencing, figures, tables, or conventions impede reading but remain repairable without fundamental reframing.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A clearly substantiated original and significant contribution whose importance and intended scholarly readership are explicitly established.
what_triggers_block: The case for originality, significance, or readership value is inadequate, although substantial reframing and stronger substantiation could make it assessable.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution appears potentially worthwhile, but its novelty, importance, or readership value is incompletely articulated and needs focused strengthening.
what_triggers_fatal: The contribution is demonstrably unoriginal or wholly outside the stated scholarly field, leaving no publishable contribution for the identified readership.
