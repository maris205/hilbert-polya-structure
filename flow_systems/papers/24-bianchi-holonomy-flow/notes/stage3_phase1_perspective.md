## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) requires the study design, handling of evidence, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility provisions to meet disciplinary review standards; from a cross-disciplinary perspective, those safeguards establish whether readers in neighboring fields can responsibly interpret and reuse the work, while assessment remains with the methodology seat.

D2 (domain_accuracy) requires claims, terminology, results, and treatment of prior work to remain faithful to current domain knowledge; for an outside-field reader, dependable domain grounding is the prerequisite for drawing connections without distorting the originating discipline, while assessment remains with the domain seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) requires the central thesis to be internally consistent and adequately supported, without reasoning defects that undermine it; from a cross-disciplinary perspective, a coherent evidential chain is necessary before implications can be transported across disciplinary boundaries, while assessment remains with the designated eligible seats.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) requires framing, definitions, and implications to be intelligible to adjacent-field readers and requires interdisciplinary assertions to have real support; the perspective seat will assess whether the work builds a defensible bridge beyond its home field rather than merely invoking broader relevance.

D5 (writing_and_structure) requires clear organization and exposition, effective figures and tables, and compliance with applicable presentation conventions; from a cross-disciplinary perspective, these features determine how readily non-specialists can follow the contribution, while assessment remains with the EIC seat.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) requires an original and significant contribution suitable for the intended readership; a cross-disciplinary lens recognizes that breadth alone does not establish significance or audience suitability, and assessment remains with the EIC seat without any venue-alignment claim here.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Clear framing and definitions for adjacent-field readers, supported interdisciplinary connections, and implications whose reach and limits are explicitly explained.
what_triggers_block: The framing is inaccessible to adjacent-field readers or the interdisciplinary claims lack support in a way that defeats the stated cross-disciplinary contribution.
what_triggers_warn: The cross-disciplinary bridge is credible but definitions, connections, or implications need targeted clarification or additional support for adjacent-field readers.
