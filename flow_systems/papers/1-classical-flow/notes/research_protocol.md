# Stage 1 Research Protocol

## Research Question Brief

### Topic area

Continuous-time flow systems as possible carriers of the primitive-orbit and classical-to-quantum structures required by an arithmetic Hilbert--Pólya programme.

### Primary research question

> To what extent can an explicitly defined continuous-time flow produce, without prime or zero fitting, primitive periods and repetition weights structurally compatible with the rational-prime terms \(\log p\) and \((\log p)p^{-r/2}\)?

### Scope boundaries

**In scope**

- genuine continuous-time flows or topological \(\mathbb R\)-actions;
- Deninger's arithmetic-scheme flow for \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\);
- geodesic flows on the modular surface and compact negatively curved surfaces;
- general contact Anosov flows as a structural comparator;
- canonical suspension flows of symplectic maps, especially the area-preserving H\'enon lineage;
- hyperbolic billiard/Hamiltonian and \(H=xp\) comparators;
- primitive/repeated orbit definitions, periods, monodromy, orientation, multiplicity and missed-orbit risk;
- only the A2--A4 facts required to classify whether an A0--A1 survivor has a natural continuation.

**Out of scope**

- fitting any Riemann zero, unfolded spacing, scale, offset, or Hamiltonian parameter;
- treating GUE statistics as arithmetic evidence;
- launching a symbolic-only, quantum-graph, cosmological, or operator-algebra project;
- claiming a local or semiclassical trace contribution as a global fixed-operator identity;
- full Route B evaluation before a Route-A-ready candidate exists.

**Key assumptions to be tested**

1. The time variable must be intrinsic to the flow and shared by its orbit and quantum descriptions.
2. A primitive prime must correspond to a primitive dynamical object; prime powers must arise as repetitions rather than as separately inserted labels.
3. Equal counting laws do not establish a canonical orbit--prime correspondence.
4. Arithmetic origin and strong hyperbolic trace structure may reside in different existing constructions.

### Sub-questions

1. Which candidate flows have an intrinsic, complete primitive/repetition ledger, including period, orientation, multiplicity and transverse stability?
2. In the best exact trace architecture, do the natural orbit norms equal rational primes, or merely obey the same asymptotic counting scale?
3. Does any arithmetic-native continuous flow also provide isolated primitive orbits, intrinsic repetition amplitudes and a natural classical-to-quantum continuation?

### Sub-question bindings

1. Inherits: continuous-time flows; no zero fitting; Stage-1 A0--A1; evidence through 2026-08-13. Deviations: none.
2. Inherits: modular/negative-curvature geodesic flows; intrinsic arc-length clock; rational primes only. Deviations: none.
3. Inherits: Deninger `Spec Z` flow plus surviving comparators; no manually collapsed orbit packets. Deviations: none.

## Methodology Blueprint

### Research paradigm

**Selected:** mathematical critical realism with a comparative falsification design.

The relevant objects and identities are mathematical, but proposed Hilbert--Pólya interpretations have different epistemic levels. The design therefore separates proved object-level facts from numerical observations and conjectural interpretation.

### Method

**Type:** mixed theoretical/computational comparative case study.

**Specific methods:**

1. theorem and definition audit against Route A/A0--A1;
2. elementary algebraic derivation for the modular length/norm obstruction;
3. finite symbolic enumeration of primitive modular geodesics, with an explicit incompleteness report;
4. formula-level comparison of Selberg hyperbolic amplitudes with the Riemann prime-power coefficient;
5. adversarial controls using non-arithmetic hyperbolic flows and constant-roof suspensions.

### Data strategy

**Primary sources:** original or peer-reviewed work on Selberg/Ruelle zeta, hyperbolic trace formulae, arithmetic-scheme flows, Anosov zeta continuation, Gutzwiller theory, wave trace, and the local project papers.

**Computation:** generated orbit words and exact integer matrices only. Rational-prime tables may be used solely in declared controls after all candidate definitions and clocks are frozen; Riemann-zero tables are prohibited everywhere in Stage 1.

**Temporal cutoff:** sources verified through 2026-08-13. Later changes require a new protocol version.

### Analytical framework

Each explicit candidate receives:

1. a source lock (object, phase space, dynamics, parameter provenance, clock, normalization);
2. A0 checks for arithmetic source, primitive-prime mechanism, repetition, weights, forbidden data and at least three controls;
3. A1 checks for primitive definition, enumeration, repetitions, phase/orientation, monodromy, completeness and arithmetic-label provenance;
4. evidence labels restricted to `PROVED`, `CONDITIONAL_THEOREM`, `NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED`, `NUMERICAL_OBSERVATION`, `HEURISTIC`, `MODELING_CHOICE`, `FITTED_PARAMETER`, `OPEN`, `REFUTED`, `NOT_TESTABLE`, and `STOP_SCOPED`;
5. the smallest next falsification test, never a broad rescue programme.

### Pre-registered mathematical tests

1. For \(g\in\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)\) hyperbolic with integer trace \(m\), derive \(\ell(g)=2\operatorname{arcosh}(|m|/2)\) and test whether \(e^{\ell(g)}\in\mathbb P\) is possible.
2. Compare
   \[
   A_{g,r}^{\mathrm{Selberg}}=\frac{\ell(g)}{2\sinh(r\ell(g)/2)}
   =\frac{\ell(g)e^{-r\ell(g)/2}}{1-e^{-r\ell(g)}}
   \]
   with the idealized coefficient \(\ell(g)e^{-r\ell(g)/2}\); report the correction factor, not only its large-length limit.
3. Enumerate primitive cyclic \(C_2*C_3\) codes to a frozen 16-block cutoff, retaining inversion as the oriented flow/trace convention; report the unoriented quotient as a diagnostic, duplicate handling, repetitions, and why the ledger is not complete under a geometric-length cutoff.
4. Compare sorted-period/counting summaries against shuffled labels, matched-density integers, composites, and a generic hyperbolic-flow control; do not interpret similar counting as arithmetic evidence.
5. Audit Deninger prime packets without collapsing a packet to one orbit unless explicitly labelled a modeling choice.

### Validity criteria

| Criterion | Protection |
|---|---|

| Internal validity | Freeze clocks and formula conventions before computation; retain signed/complex factors. |

| Reproducibility | Deterministic code, fixed cutoffs/seeds, machine-readable ledgers and checksums. |

### Limitations by design

- A finite word ledger cannot certify all geodesics below a geometric-length threshold unless a separate completeness theorem is supplied.
- Stage 1 can refute exact rational-prime norm equality for the modular flow without excluding all arithmetic hyperbolic flows.
- Deninger's construction is infinite-dimensional and topological; no smooth Hamiltonian/contact structure is assumed.
- A2--A4 are architectural observations only at this stage, not full verdicts.

### Ethics, reporting and preregistration

- Human subjects/IRB: not applicable.
- Responsible reporting: distinguish source facts, new derivations, numerical results and conjectures; disclose AI assistance.
- Reporting standard: no EQUATOR checklist applies to a mathematical/computational study; use theorem/algorithm/reproducibility conventions.
- Preregistration: this protocol is the local design-freeze record. Any post-result change must be logged rather than silently edited into the original test.

## Devil's Advocate Report — Checkpoint 1

### Verdict: PASS WITH MAJOR GUARDRAILS

No critical issue blocks investigation, but four risks must remain visible.

### Major issues

1. **Comparator asymmetry**
   - Problem: the Deninger object is a topological infinite-dimensional arithmetic flow, whereas the geodesic candidates are smooth finite-dimensional Anosov flows.
   - Impact: a simple score ranking could confuse complementary strengths with like-for-like superiority.
   - Fix: report an obligation matrix and the two-halves diagnosis; do not average A0 and A1 into one scalar.

2. **`Prime geodesic` equivocation**
   - Problem: “prime” means primitive in the prime-geodesic theorem, not rational prime.
   - Impact: the central arithmetic claim could be manufactured by terminology.
   - Fix: use “primitive closed geodesic” in conclusions and reserve \(p\) for rational primes.

3. **Finite-ledger completeness**
   - Problem: a code-word cutoff is not automatically a geometric-length cutoff.
   - Impact: orbit counts can be biased while appearing precise.
   - Fix: state the cutoff basis in every table and include a missed-orbit warning.

4. **Architecture-to-candidate leap**
   - Problem: exact Selberg trace and a self-adjoint Laplacian could be mistaken for arithmetic success.
   - Impact: Route B could be invoked despite A0 failure.
   - Fix: Stage 1 verdicts stop at A0--A1; exact trace architecture cannot rescue missing rational-prime origin.

### Minor issues

- The modular surface is non-compact and has continuous spectral components; compact controls must not be used to erase this distinction.
- Orientation reversal and orbifold elliptic points require explicit conventions in the orbit ledger.
- A nearby-looking coefficient is not the von Mangoldt coefficient unless the orbit label and all correction factors agree intrinsically.

### Strongest counter-argument

The research may rediscover a well-known analogy (prime geodesic theorem versus prime number theorem) without producing a new flow. The answer is to make the deliverable an exact obligation/obstruction result: identify which required equalities hold, prove which fail for the frozen candidates, and state the smallest interface that remains open.
