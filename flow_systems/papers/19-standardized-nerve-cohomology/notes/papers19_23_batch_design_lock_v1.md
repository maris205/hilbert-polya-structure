## 3. Non-overlap and owner firewalls

### Paper 19

Paper 19 may import Paper 12's exact standardized owner, common stabilizer,
cochain convention, degree-one theorem, and comparison functor. Its new work
must start in degree two. It must not relabel the existing `H^1` theorem, a
normalized complex, a twisted theory, a `C*`-algebra, a trace, or a
determinant.

### Papers 20 and 21

Both may cite Paper 15, but their increments are disjoint. Paper 20 compares
different primes through the full signature and must not repeat the existing
classification equivalence. Paper 21 quantifies the fixed-parameter
exact-order witness construction and must not claim signature injectivity.
Both remain results about bare arithmetic/compact-group data, not packet
topology, flow, trace, operator, or determinant.

### Paper 22

Paper 22 is a new pure-algebra owner. A lift on an fp/fppf site does not create
a map into the rational-Witt packet, Paper-12 groupoid, flow, operator, or
Route object. Any such bridge requires a separate theorem and is excluded.

### Paper 23

Paper 23 is confined to the standard Hausdorff circle proxy and the already
fixed regular representation. It may not transfer a trace, Haar system,
normal weight, or cancellation theorem to the actual non-Hausdorff packet.
The fixed proxy is not a Route-A or Route-B candidate merely because it has an
operator algebra.

`NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED`, `NUMERICAL_OBSERVATION`, `HEURISTIC`, `OPEN`,
`REFUTED`, `NOT_TESTABLE`, and `STOP_SCOPED`. Computation may falsify or
support a bounded theorem but may not replace proof.

exact theorem/page locators, subtract the nearest precedent, and freeze the
mathematical owner before proof. A zero search hit is not a novelty theorem.
