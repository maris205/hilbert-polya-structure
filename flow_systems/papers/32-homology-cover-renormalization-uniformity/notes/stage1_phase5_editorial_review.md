## Overall assessment

The report is conceptually strong in separating four layers that are often conflated: canonical owner enumeration, ownerwise lift algebra, formal independent-owner coefficient comparison, and compact-uniform analytic specialization. The positive-content/zero-content split, the fixed `1/N` and `1/N^3` normalizations, and the refusal to infer an infinite theorem from finite schedules are all important safeguards.

The current draft is still an architecture for a theorem rather than a theorem or a complete formal-methods paper. The central owner factors are displayed only as derivation targets, the proposed formal rings and transitions are undefined and explicitly unresolved, the owner enumerator is unbound, and the compact-uniform limit statement is not written as a self-contained theorem with a proved tail. Originality and passage-level evidence are likewise unresolved. Major revision is therefore required before the work can be presented as an original mathematical contribution.

## Findings and required revisions

### Critical findings

None. The displayed formulas and schedules are consistently labeled as proposed targets or unexecuted diagnostics rather than proved results.

### Major findings

#### `P32-EIC-001` — The primary formal endpoint is not yet mathematically defined

**Exact section evidence:** Sections 2.5 and 4.3 refer to a project-defined inverse-limit ring `R_+`, localizations and projections, and a separate rational-exponent Hahn object; both sections state that `CP-P32-004` is unresolved and the constructions cannot yet be asserted to be well formed.

**Editorial consequence:** Without explicit rings, exponent monoids, transition maps, topology, localization conditions, and coefficient projections, “ownerwise recovery” has no stable formal truth condition.

**Required revision:** Give self-contained definitions and prove that all operations used by the comparison are well defined. Resolve the positive-content and zero-content objects separately and state exactly how finite-support singleton projections interact with the inverse system. Do not rely on analogy to the background sources.

#### `P32-EIC-002` — The central owner-factor formulas remain unproved targets

**Exact section evidence:** Section 4.2 displays `order_N(g)=N/q_N(g)`, a primitive lift count, the positive-content factor, and a separate zero-content factor, while repeatedly stating that these are proof obligations and that primitive-lift status is unproved.

**Editorial consequence:** These formulas generate the rest of the proposed comparison. Until their derivation and hypotheses are established, the normalization program cannot be assessed for correctness.

**Required revision:** Provide an ownerwise derivation from the deck action, including the deck order, component count, covering degree, primitivity of lifted components, scaled period, and logarithmic multiplicity. Treat `d=0` separately and state every condition under which the formulas hold.

#### `P32-EIC-003` — The universal owner quantifier has no certified enumeration interface

**Exact section evidence:** Sections 2.1 and 4.1 require equality, oriented conjugacy, maximal roots, separate inverses, deterministic serialization, and prefix completeness; the report concludes `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1=UNBOUND`.

**Editorial consequence:** The primary research question ranges over every primitive owner, but the draft cannot yet define or certify the sequence over which its exhaustion and singleton comparisons run.

**Required revision:** Bind the exact marked presentation to a complete canonical-owner theorem, serialization contract, and verifier. Prove the prefix-completeness invariant and distinguish it from decision on a supplied finite list before using any owner schedule in a theorem statement.

#### `P32-EIC-004` — The analytic endpoint is not a self-contained theorem

**Exact section evidence:** Sections 2.4 and 4.4 require an absolute logarithmic-summand bound uniform on every `K(delta,T,R)` and compatible with all iterated and diagonal schedules; Sections 6 and 7 state that no such exact tail has been extracted.

**Editorial consequence:** Listing relevant counting and continuation literature and fixed schedules does not establish convergence, cofinality, or exchange of limits.

**Required revision:** State the exact scalar functions, quantifier order, compact domain, summation order, limiting schedules, and desired equality. Then prove an absolute compact-uniform majorant and every limit passage used, or narrow the article to the formal finite-support endpoint.

#### `P32-EIC-005` — Evidence and contribution positioning remain incomplete

**Exact section evidence:** Section 3 records a bounded source pipeline; Section 6 acknowledges absent locators, a non-peer-reviewed surface-group candidate, a `PLAUSIBLE` Selberg record, correction-bound use, background-only formal sources, and no novelty assessment.

**Editorial consequence:** Neither the theorem transfers nor the originality of the proposed normalization/formal architecture can presently be evaluated.

**Required revision:** Provide the reproducible search and screening protocol, exact source passages and hypotheses for every imported component, and a separately authorized contribution comparison. Align the article type with the strongest result actually proved after revision.
