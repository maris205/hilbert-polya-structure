## Review Body

### Summary Assessment

The manuscript reorganizes an ambitious homology-cover program around falsification-first local tests. It gives higher-content and zero-content owners priority over construction of the exceptional content-one formal and analytic branch, while consistently warning that the candidate factors, formal comparison objects, singleton projections, and compact-uniform tails remain unproved. This ordering has plausible methodological value because an exact adverse ownerwise result could stop a universal program before expensive global analysis. The manuscript also preserves the immutable normalizations and the pure-tower route boundary without converting prospective displays into execution credit. The exposition is detailed and largely coherent, but the title can imply completed tests, and internal revision-stage narration interrupts the mathematical argument. The article additionally claims artifact-level reproducibility without supplying retrievable artifact locators. Most importantly, it explicitly states that neither corpus nor article establishes novelty. The field-general originality, significance, and readership value of the ordering therefore require substantial but repairable substantiation; no named-venue, track, or submission-readiness judgement is made.
