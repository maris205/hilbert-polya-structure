## 1. Introduction, Research Question, and Contribution

Finite-cover products can agree on a selected stratum or finite panel without recovering every primitive owner. P32 fixes this distinction at the object level. The base is a marked closed hyperbolic genus-two surface with unit-speed geodesic flow and presentation

```text
Gamma=<a1,b1,a2,b2 | [a1,b1][a2,b2]=1>.
```

The cover tower is the pure homology tower `H_N=ker(Gamma -> H_1(Sigma;Z/NZ))`. It is not the residual inverse-limit object studied in the preceding branch, and a conclusion about one tower cannot repair the other. Owners are oriented primitive surface-group conjugacy classes; inverse classes remain separately oriented. For an owner with homology vector in `Z^4`, content `d` is the greatest common divisor of the absolute coordinates, with zero content treated as a separate case.

The normalization is immutable. Physical lifted time is multiplied by exactly `1/N`, and the logarithm of the raw lift-component product is multiplied by exactly `1/N^3`. Content-dependent changes, outcome-dependent weights, adaptive schedules, and favorable-panel selection are prohibited. The universal endpoint concerns all owner contents, not an average and not the content-one subproduct alone.

The revised research question is:

**Do the prospectively derived local factors for higher-content and zero-content owners already obstruct the frozen `1/N` and `1/N^3` full-owner recovery scheme; and only if those adverse tests are survived, what formal and analytic obligations remain for the content-one subproduct?**

The change is methodological. It does not assert that the candidate factors are correct or that an obstruction exists. It places the shortest discriminating theorem targets before the most expensive formal-topology and compact-uniform program. A higher- or zero-content obstruction would settle the universal endpoint negatively under the fixed scheme. Survival would justify continued work. A positive content-one statement, if eventually proved, would remain a restricted result.

The article is positioned as a closed-corpus theoretical research-program report. Its contribution is the review-adjudicated ordering of exact obligations: bind owners, derive adverse local factors, run falsification-first formal comparisons, and only then develop the contingent content-one branch. No novelty comparison was authorized, so no priority or originality claim is made.
