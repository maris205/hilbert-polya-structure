\begin{abstract}

<!--block:B0006-->
P32 studies a fixed renormalization proposal for the pure genus-two
homology-cover tower
$H_N=\ker(\Gamma\to H_1(\Sigma;\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z))$. Physical
lifted time is scaled by exactly $1/N$, and the logarithm of the
raw lift-component product by exactly $1/N^3$. The universal
endpoint asks for recovery of every oriented primitive base-owner
factor. Adversarial review found that the earlier four-package presentation
understated the adverse information already visible in its own
prospective local factors. The present article therefore adopts a
falsification-first architecture. Conditional on a future derivation of
those factors, positive content \texttt{d\textgreater{}1} and zero
content become the first theorem targets; they must be settled before
formal or compact-uniform resources are invested in the exceptional
content-one stratum. The content-one analytic program is now explicitly
contingent and secondary. The project-defined inverse-limit object
\texttt{R\_+}, its localizations and projections, and the separate
zero-content Hahn object remain unresolved constructions. Likewise, any
analytic tail theorem is conditional on survival of the local tests and
would require an absolute logarithmic-summand majorant uniform on each
frozen compact set and compatible with every specified limit order. The
executed work remains literature synthesis over 26 frozen sources and
review-informed argument design. No factor, mismatch, obstruction,
recovery statement, panel, or limit has been proved or computed. All 26
inherited citations retain \texttt{anchor:none}; P32-S13 is
bibliographically \texttt{VERIFIED} but remains background-only, so
claim-to-passage faithfulness is \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE}. Arithmetic A0 remains unavailable, the formal
Route-A tuple is unassigned, positive arithmetic A2 is absent, and Route
B remains closed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction, Research Question, and

<!--block:B0011-->
Finite-cover products can agree on a selected stratum or finite panel
without recovering every primitive owner. P32 fixes this distinction at
the object level. The base is a marked closed hyperbolic genus-two
surface with unit-speed geodesic flow and presentation

<!--block:B0012-->
\begin{verbatim}
Gamma=<a1,b1,a2,b2 | [a1,b1][a2,b2]=1>.
\end{verbatim}

<!--block:B0013-->
The cover tower is the pure homology tower
\texttt{H\_N=ker(Gamma\ -\textgreater{}\ H\_1(Sigma;Z/NZ))}. It is not
the residual inverse-limit object studied in the preceding branch, and a
conclusion about one tower cannot repair the other. Owners are oriented
primitive surface-group conjugacy classes; inverse classes remain
separately oriented. For an owner with homology vector in
\texttt{Z\^{}4}, content \texttt{d} is the greatest common divisor of
the absolute coordinates, with zero content treated as a separate case.

<!--block:B0014-->
The normalization is immutable. Physical lifted time is multiplied by
exactly \texttt{1/N}, and the logarithm of the raw lift-component
product is multiplied by exactly \texttt{1/N\^{}3}. Content-dependent
changes, outcome-dependent weights, adaptive schedules, and
favorable-panel selection are prohibited. The universal endpoint
concerns all owner contents, not an average and not the content-one
subproduct alone.

<!--block:B0015-->
The revised research question is:

<!--block:B0016-->
\textbf{Do the prospectively derived local factors for higher-content
and zero-content owners already obstruct the frozen \texttt{1/N} and
\texttt{1/N\^{}3} full-owner recovery scheme; and only if those adverse
tests are survived, what formal and analytic obligations remain for the
content-one subproduct?}

<!--block:B0017-->
The change is methodological. It does not assert that the candidate
factors are correct or that an obstruction exists. It places the
shortest discriminating theorem targets before the most expensive
formal-topology and compact-uniform program. A higher- or zero-content
obstruction would settle the universal endpoint negatively under the
fixed scheme. Survival would justify continued work. A positive
content-one statement, if eventually proved, would remain a restricted
result.

<!--block:B0018-->
The article is positioned as a closed-corpus theoretical and
certificate-methods study. Its contribution is the review-adjudicated
ordering of exact obligations: bind owners, derive adverse local
factors, run falsification-first formal comparisons, and only then
develop the contingent content-one branch. No novelty comparison was
authorized, so no priority or originality claim is made.

<!--block:B0019-->
The word ``owner'' is universal in this article. It never means only a
content-one class. The population comprises oriented primitive
surface-group conjugacy classes across positive higher content, content
one, and the separately typed zero-content case. Inverse classes remain
distinct. This corrects a potentially misleading shorthand in an older
state description and prevents a favorable exceptional stratum from
being substituted for the frozen all-owner endpoint.

<!--block:B0020-->
Three gates organize the program. The owner gate must bind equality,
oriented conjugacy, maximal roots, inverse handling, serialization, and
prefix completeness to the marked presentation. The local-factor gate
must derive cover order, component multiplicity, primitive-lift status,
rescaled period, and normalized exponent for each relevant content. The
formal/analytic gate must supply the coefficient objects and, if local
tests survive, the uniform tail theorem. A failure at an earlier gate
stops later work; it is not repaired by changing a schedule, selecting a
favorable panel, or moving to a different tower.

<!--block:B0021-->
The falsification-first order is therefore informational rather than
promotional. It asks the shortest exact questions capable of deciding
the universal proposal while leaving survival, obstruction, and
not-evaluable outcomes all open. The architecture does not infer a
mismatch from the displayed shapes of unproved formulas, and it does not
infer convergence from a finite diagnostic plan.
