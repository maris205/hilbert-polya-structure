### Prior inclusion criteria

A source was eligible when it was a primary paper, scholarly chapter/thesis, or authoritative institutional reference and addressed at least one frozen interface:

1. exact word equality, conjugacy, maximal roots/proper powers, centralizers, or complete enumeration in a closed genus-two surface group;
2. finite abelian/homology covers and exact lift order, splitting multiplicity, period, or representation-theoretic product relations;
3. primitive geodesic Euler products, Selberg/Ruelle zeta functions, or absolute convergence on Re(s) > 1;
4. prime/primitive geodesic counting and tail estimates capable of supporting compact-uniform truncation bounds;
5. formal power-series rings in infinitely many independent variables, inverse-limit topologies, Hahn series, or finite-support specialization maps;
6. the separately typed d=0 scalar asymptotic calculation.

Foundational older sources were retained under a currency exemption when they state the original construction or theorem. Recent sources were sought for algorithmic and analytic context.

### Prior exclusion criteria

- tertiary pages, software manuals, and informal expositions;
- duplicate preprint, repository, aggregator, or publisher manifestations;
- sources about branched or nonabelian covers without a usable bridge to the pure homology tower;
- numerical finite-prefix experiments presented as if they supplied infinite-schedule evidence;
- generic Fredholm-determinant literature outside the frozen reciprocal owner-product convention;
- formal-series sources that do not define a topology/restriction map relevant to independent owner variables;
- results whose only relevance was the rejected residual inverse-limit owner.

## Limitations and handoff to later phases

1. This is not a source-verification report. Abstracts, bibliographic records, and available landing-page/full-text availability were inspected; exact theorem statements, proof hypotheses, errata, and equation-level mappings remain for a separate reviewer.
2. SG2OwnerCanonical-v1 is still unbound. The bibliography does not itself close word equality, oriented conjugacy, primitive-root extraction, deterministic owner ordering, or prefix completeness.
3. A bounded enumeration or the k <= 8 prefix cannot replace a complete panel certificate and cannot supply an infinite-k theorem.
4. No included source directly proves the project-specific paired 1/N clock and 1/N^3 log normalization for every owner. Exact lift algebra must be derived ownerwise before analytic specialization.
5. The required tail is stronger than a prime-geodesic asymptotic: it must be absolute and uniform on each genuine compact K(delta,T,R), with delta > 0, finite T, and finite R, and must justify the declared order of limits.
6. The independent-owner ring R_+ and the one-owner rational-exponent Hahn object are not interchangeable. An infinite scalar map is not presumed merely because every finite specialization exists.
7. The d=0 formula remains separately typed; no source licenses plugging d=0 into a positive-content expression.
8. The 2013 Giulietti–Liverani–Pollicott paper has a 2022 erratum affecting its spectral-gap portion. Any later use must isolate an unaffected theorem or use another source.
9. The content-one analytic endpoint, even if later supported, cannot repair a full-owner mismatch, cannot be relabeled as the full primitive product, and cannot rescue P27's residual inverse-limit owner.
10. Any gap found here is a search/coverage limitation, not a novelty claim.
