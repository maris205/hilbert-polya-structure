<!--block:B0006-->
P32 studies a fixed renormalization proposal for the pure genus-two
homology-cover tower
$H_N=\ker(\Gamma\to H_1(\Sigma;\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z))$. Physical
lifted time is scaled by exactly $1/N$, and the logarithm of the
raw lift-component product by exactly $1/N^3$. The universal
endpoint asks for recovery of every oriented primitive base-owner
factor. Adversarial review found that the earlier four-package presentation
understated the adverse information already visible in its own
prospective local factors. The present article therefore adopts a
falsification-first architecture. Conditional on a future derivation of
those factors, positive content \texttt{d\textgreater{}1} and zero
content become the first theorem targets; they must be settled before
formal or compact-uniform resources are invested in the exceptional
content-one stratum. The content-one analytic program is now explicitly
contingent and secondary. The project-defined inverse-limit object
\texttt{R\_+}, its localizations and projections, and the separate
zero-content Hahn object remain unresolved constructions. Likewise, any
analytic tail theorem is conditional on survival of the local tests and
would require an absolute logarithmic-summand majorant uniform on each
frozen compact set and compatible with every specified limit order. The
executed work remains literature synthesis over 26 frozen sources and
review-informed argument design. No factor, mismatch, obstruction,
recovery statement, panel, or limit has been proved or computed. All 26
inherited citations retain \texttt{anchor:none}; P32-S13 is
bibliographically \texttt{VERIFIED} but remains background-only, so
claim-to-passage faithfulness is \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE}. Arithmetic A0 remains unavailable, the formal

 \Phi_m(s)=\bigl(1-e^{-s\ell/m}\bigr)^{-m}
 \;>\;

 (1-x)^m<(1-x)<(1-x^m).

inequalities:
\[
 (1-x)^{-m}>(1-x)^{-1}>(1-x^m)^{-1}.
