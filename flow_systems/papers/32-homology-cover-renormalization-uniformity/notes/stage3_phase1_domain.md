D3 — argumentative_coherence: This dimension concerns internal consistency and whether evidence supports the core chain of reasoning without a thesis-defeating fallacy. It is scored by the Devil's Advocate and methodology seats rather than by the domain seat.

D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance: This dimension asks whether neighboring-field readers can understand the framing and whether interdisciplinary assertions have adequate support. Its scoring authority belongs to the perspective seat.

D5 — writing_and_structure: This dimension concerns coherent organization, lucid exposition, effective figures and tables, and applicable presentation conventions. The Journal-Fit Reviewer owns the score.

D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution: This dimension asks whether the work offers an original and significant contribution appropriate to an authorized venue and audience. Since criteria binding is unavailable, the domain seat makes no venue-fit claim or commitment.
