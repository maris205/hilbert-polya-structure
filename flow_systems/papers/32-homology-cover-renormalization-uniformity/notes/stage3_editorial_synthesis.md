## Editorial decision letter

Dear Author(s),

The five role-separated seats examined the frozen P32 manuscript under the
`reviewer/reviewer_full/v2` sprint contract. The panel consistently preserved
the manuscript's nonexecution boundary: it did not treat displayed candidate
factors as derived results, finite diagnostics as uniform convergence, or the
content-one branch as universal recovery. Those genuine strengths do not
arithmetically offset the contract failures. The Journal-Fit seat found the
field-general originality and readership value materially under-substantiated,
producing a repairable D6 block. The Devil's Advocate found that unresolved
formal objects can function as a fallback that delays a weaker conditional
scalar comparison, producing a repairable D3 block; R1 separately warned on
D3 and identified incomplete truth conditions and limit-order registration.

The resulting decision is **Major Revision**. This decision is mechanical: F2
fires on mandatory blocks in D3 and D6 and outranks the also-fired F3 and F5.
It is not a vote over recommendations, and no per-seat recommendation has been
inferred from a dimension score. The repair path remains bounded and
proposal-only. It requires clearer contribution positioning, auditable current
artifacts and literature-method records, closed or explicitly unavailable
formal comparison semantics, an enumerated limit registry, consistent source
status, and resolution of the scalar-versus-formal ordering dispute. None of
these requests licenses the manuscript to report a factor derivation,
coefficient mismatch, obstruction, recovery theorem, limit, panel result,
positive arithmetic A2 evidence, Route-A promotion, or Route-B entry.
