## Abstract

P32 examines whether a fixed renormalization scheme can recover every base primitive-owner factor in a pure genus-two homology-cover tower. The candidate is restricted to `H_N=ker(Gamma -> H_1(Sigma;Z/NZ))`; it is distinct from, and cannot repair, the residual inverse-limit object studied earlier. The physical lifted period is to be scaled by exactly `1/N`, and the logarithm of the raw lift-component product by exactly `1/N^3`. A closed 26-source corpus was processed through bibliography construction, bounded source verification, a complete literature matrix, thematic synthesis, and an independent Devil's Advocate checkpoint. The evidence supports four ordered research packages: bind a complete oriented surface-group owner interface, prove ownerwise cover and lift algebra, construct the positive-content and zero-content formal comparisons self-containedly, and extract an absolute compact-uniform tail for every declared infinite limit order. No package is closed. In particular, the formal-precedent tension `CP-P32-004` remains unresolved, the exact owner factors remain derivation targets, and finite diagnostics cannot establish an infinite theorem. The report's contribution is a fail-closed separation of formal, analytic, and dynamical claims. Full-owner recovery and the secondary content-one analytic endpoint both remain unevaluated; arithmetic A0, automatic A2 credit, residual-tower rescue, and Route B remain unavailable.

**Keywords:** homology covers; primitive geodesics; ownerwise renormalization; formal power series; compact-uniform convergence; dynamical zeta functions

## 1. Introduction

Finite cover identities can look stronger than the objects they actually compare. A product may factor correctly at one modulus, a selected owner class may scale as intended, or a finite coefficient table may reproduce a known pattern. None of these observations alone establishes recovery of every primitive owner along a growing cover and owner schedule. P32 was designed to separate those levels before any global-product statement is allowed.

The candidate is the pure homology-cover tower of a marked closed hyperbolic genus-two surface. With

```text
Gamma=<a1,b1,a2,b2 | [a1,b1][a2,b2]=1>,
H_N=ker(Gamma -> H_1(Sigma;Z/NZ)),
```

the owner is an oriented primitive surface-group conjugacy class. Inversion remains separately oriented. The pure tower is nonresidual and is not the residual inverse-limit system from P27. This boundary is structural: a result for one tower cannot supply periodic owners to the other.

For an owner's homology vector, the project defines content `d` as the greatest common divisor of its four absolute coordinates. Positive content and zero content are treated separately. The proposed normalization is fixed in advance. Physical lifted time is multiplied by exactly `1/N`, and the raw lift-component log product is multiplied by exactly `1/N^3`. No content-dependent clock, exponent, owner weight, or post-outcome panel repair is permitted.

### Research question

**Does the paired frozen `1/N` clock and `1/N^3` logarithmic-normalization scheme recover the base primitive-owner factor for every owner under a certified target-blind exhaustion of the pure genus-two homology-cover calibrator?**

The universal full-owner endpoint is primary. A single certified higher-content or zero-content mismatch would obstruct it ownerwise. A content-one analytic subproduct is secondary and cannot erase that obstruction, substitute for the full primitive-owner product, or repair the residual tower. This report does not answer the scientific endpoint. It organizes the frozen evidence for the proof program and records where the evidence stops.

## 4. Evidence-Synthesis Findings

### 4.1 The owner interface is a composite proof object

The first finding is that canonical owner enumeration cannot be delegated to an opaque normal-form call. A valid `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1` binding must prove the equality and oriented-conjugacy biconditionals in the exact marked presentation, return maximal primitive roots, canonicalize inverses separately, serialize owners deterministically, and certify that every requested prefix contains exactly the first eligible owners. An enumeration frontier without a completeness theorem is not enough.

The corpus supplies plausible components but no closed composite. Consequently, `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1=UNBOUND`, and every proposed owner panel remains not evaluated. No finite population may be padded, reordered, or repaired after outcomes.

### 4.2 Ownerwise lift algebra remains a derivation target

For positive content `d`, the Phase 1 program proposes to define `q_N=gcd(N,d)` and prove the deck order, primitive lift count, physical lift period, and normalized owner factor. Its target formulas include

```text
order_N(g)=N/q_N(g),
primitive lift count=N^3 q_N(g),
F_N,g(s)=(1-exp(-s ell(g)/q_N(g)))^(-q_N(g)).
```

These are proof obligations, not findings of the literature report. The project must also prove primitive-lift status. For `d=0`, it proposes a separate derivation with order one, lift count `N^4`, rescaled period `ell(g)/N`, normalized exponent `N`, and factor

```text
F_N,g^(0)(s)=(1-exp(-s ell(g)/N))^(-N).
```

The zero-content formula cannot be obtained by substituting `d=0` into the positive-content expression. No scientific value, mismatch coefficient, or scalar limit has been computed in this phase.

### 4.3 Formal recovery and analytic specialization are distinct endpoints

The proposed positive-content comparison uses independent owner variables and finite-support coefficient projections in a project-defined inverse-limit ring. A singleton-owner projection is intended to prevent a mismatch from canceling against other owners. Zero content uses a separately typed rational-exponent Hahn object. Because `CP-P32-004` is unresolved and every formal source is background-only, the report cannot assert that these objects are well constructed or that their localizations and transition maps behave as required.

Even a completed formal comparison would not automatically define an infinite scalar map. That map requires absolute convergence on the declared compact rectangles and independence from certified cofinal exhaustion. Formal equality, finite scalar evaluation, and infinite analytic convergence therefore remain separate gates.

### 4.4 Infinite schedules cannot be inferred from finite diagnostics

The theorem-level schedules are exactly

```text
N_k=k!,
m_k=2^k,
N'_k=2*(k!),
```

for every integer `k>=1`. The registered finite execution prefix is only `1<=k<=8`, and the fixed diagnostic panel sizes are `m in {8,16,32,64,128}`. Both finite diagnostic families remain `NOT_EXECUTED`. Even if executed later, they could check serialization or coefficients only. They could not establish convergence, cofinality, a diagonal theorem, or interchange of limits.

This distinction is a central evidence finding because several source clusters concern asymptotics or analytic continuation. Their presence does not shorten the universal quantifiers in the frozen design.

### 4.5 The four proof packages are ordered and fail-closed

The synthesis yields the following research architecture:

1. bind `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1` and its independent verifier;
2. prove the ownerwise cover and lift algebra, including the separate zero-content case;
3. construct and validate the formal owner-variable objects and coefficient projections; and
4. extract the exact absolute compact-uniform tail for every schedule and limit order.

Package one controls which owners can be named. Package two controls the factor being compared. Package three controls the meaning of formal equality and mismatch. Package four controls whether any infinite scalar specialization exists. None can substitute for another, and every package remains unexecuted.

### 4.6 Pure-tower conclusions cannot migrate to the residual object or to arithmetic A0

The evidence base concerns a generic marked hyperbolic surface and its pure homology covers. It provides no intrinsic map from rational primes to owners, no `log p` clock, and no arithmetic specificity. The calibrator therefore cannot supply A0. A positive content-one result would also remain a restricted subproduct. It could not relabel full-owner recovery, rescue P27's residual inverse-limit object, earn automatic positive arithmetic A2 credit, or open Route B.

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 Interpretation

P32's main difficulty is not a shortage of adjacent theory. The corpus contains normal forms, conjugacy and root algorithms, cover structure, homology-refined orbit asymptotics, zeta and finite-cover factorization, continuation theory, error-term candidates, and formal-topology precedents. The difficulty is that the scientific endpoint crosses all of these interfaces while preserving exact owner coordinates and two fixed renormalizations.

The strongest counterargument is that a metadata- and abstract-level synthesis cannot establish the hypotheses needed for any package. That objection is decisive against a claim of feasibility or recovery. It does not defeat the actual contribution, which is the separation of obligations and stop states. The report makes it harder for a finite calculation, a favorable content class, or an imported formal analogy to be mistaken for the universal theorem.

### 5.2 Theoretical implications

The evidence architecture clarifies four distinctions. Canonical enumeration is stronger than decision on a supplied finite list. Cover-level factorization is different from equality of independent owner coordinates. Formal coefficientwise comparison is different from analytic scalar specialization. A restricted content-one endpoint is different from universal full-owner recovery.

These distinctions also explain why an obstruction could be informative. One exact higher-content or zero-content mismatch would refute the fixed full-owner recovery scheme without requiring a complete global product. Conversely, success on content one would remain secondary unless every other owner class and every formal gate were addressed.

### 5.3 Practical implications

The immediate research priority is interface binding, not numerical scale. A versioned `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1` contract should be tested against the frozen marked presentation and independently replayed before any panel request. The deck-order and primitive-lift proof should then be written ownerwise, with the zero-content case separated from the start. Only after the formal objects are self-contained should coefficient diagnostics be considered. Analytic tail extraction belongs last.

This order preserves interpretable failures. An unbound owner interface yields `PANEL_NOT_EVALUABLE`; insufficient population yields its own status; an exact owner mismatch yields `FULL_RECOVERY_OBSTRUCTED_OWNERWISE`; an absent tail yields a not-evaluable content-one analytic endpoint. These dispositions should not be collapsed into one binary success/failure label.

## 6. Limitations and Future Work

The literature report has several limitations. Claim-level locators are absent. The direct surface-group source is a non-peer-reviewed preprint whose presentation mapping remains unproved. The historical Selberg record is `PLAUSIBLE`, not `VERIFIED`. The Giulietti–Liverani–Pollicott source is correction-bound. The formal sources are background-only. General conflict-of-interest and retraction screens were not run. Most importantly, no source supplies the exact combined owner interface, formal algebra, or compact-uniform tail.

Future work should preserve the following sequence:

1. freeze the theorem, implementation, serialization, and test-vector binding for `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1`;
2. prove equality, oriented conjugacy, maximal roots, separate inverses, and prefix completeness in the exact presentation;
3. derive the positive-content and zero-content lift factors under the fixed `1/N` and `1/N^3` choices;
4. resolve `CP-P32-004` through a self-contained construction rather than a bibliographic analogy;
5. state the first valid singleton coefficient comparison before scalar length evaluation; and
6. extract a compact-uniform absolute tail and prove every iterated and diagonal limit order on the full schedules.

Finite diagnostics may be proposed only after the relevant proof packages close. Their role must remain certificate replay and stress testing, not evidence for an infinite theorem.

## 7. Conclusion and Bounded Recommendations

The closed evidence base supports a disciplined research program but no recovery theorem. General and surface-specific group algorithms do not yet form `SG2OwnerCanonical-v1`. Cover and homology literature does not validate the paired normalization. Product and continuation theory does not imply independent-owner equality. Counting results do not yet supply the exact compact-uniform tail. Formal precedents remain background, and `CP-P32-004` remains unresolved.

The recommended next package is therefore a proof-binding dossier for the owner interface and ownerwise lift algebra. It should precede any panel, coefficient table, or scalar grid. Full-owner recovery and content-one analytic convergence must stay separate. The pure tower must stay separate from the residual inverse-limit system. Under these boundaries, a proved obstruction, a not-evaluable interface, or eventual restricted positive result can each be reported without overstating its scope.
