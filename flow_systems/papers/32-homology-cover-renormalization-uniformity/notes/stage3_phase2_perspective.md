## Review Body

The manuscript constructs a cautious conceptual bridge from primitive geodesic owners and homology-cover lift data to formal coefficient objects and compact-uniform analytic claims. It is strongest when it separates positive content from zero content, refuses to infer a coefficient mismatch before the equality relation exists, and makes higher- and zero-content local tests precede the expensive content-one tail program. It also keeps coefficientwise, formal, scalar, compact-uniform, iterated, and diagonal claims distinct. D4 warrants a warning because the proposed positive-content inverse-limit object, zero-content Hahn-type object, singleton projections, and scalar specialization remain spread across prose without one provisional type/dependency surface. A reader from formal algebra or analysis can see the obligations but not yet the complete interface among them. This is a clarification need, not evidence of a block, an obstruction, a recovery result, scientific execution, or Route credit.
