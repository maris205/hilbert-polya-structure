scalar and fixed-operator observables, countably separated measurable targets,
and positive finite Borel measures. It also resolves the transported `Q_p`
group-law/continuous-character interface, the actual-orbit versus standard-
circle direction, and the exact label/mass content of a declared tagged copied
coproduct.
