## 1. Trigger and research question

Paper 9 proves that, for every rational prime `p`, the genuine finite-kernel
Deninger packet `Gamma_p`, every inherited time orbit, and the time-orbit
quotient `Q_p` are nontrivial indiscrete spaces. Its proof audit also records
the immediate facts that their topology-generated Borel sigma-algebras are
trivial and their continuous maps to `T0` targets are constant. Paper 10 does
not re-claim those facts. It asks for the still-unassembled universal,
measure-classification, fixed-operator, proxy-direction, and copied-component
package forced by that theorem:

> Which packet-internal arithmetic data survive canonical passage to
> separated topological spaces, standard measurable targets, continuous
> scalar/operator observables, and finite Borel measures?

The primary outcome is not assumed. It must distinguish:

- collapse forced by the actual inherited topology;
- information retained only after an explicit modeling-choice retopology;
- prime labels retained by a separately frozen disjoint-coproduct model; and
- analytic or measure claims that remain undefined because standard
  Hausdorff/Radon hypotheses are absent.

## 2. Frozen objects

Fix an arbitrary rational prime `p`. Every fixed-prime theorem below is
universally quantified over rational primes. Put
`U_p=prod_{ell != p} Z_ell^x` and `H_p=p^Zhat`, as in the Paper-9 set ledger.
For `a in U_p/H_p`, the actual objects are:

```text
ACT-PACKET-p = Gamma_p with its inherited quotient topology,
ACT-ORBIT-p-a = the inherited R_{>0}-orbit with normalized label a,
ACT-Q-p = Gamma_p / (R_{>0}/p^Z) with quotient topology.
```

Their indiscreteness and nontriviality are inputs owned by Paper 9, not
re-proved from a product proxy. The following derived objects are new:

```text
K0(X) = (X/~_0,tau_q), where ~_0 is equality of open-neighbourhood
        filters, tau_q is the quotient topology, and q_0:X->K0(X) is the
        quotient unit,
Sep_H(X) = the universal Hausdorff image characterized by its unit and UMP,
Sep_CRH(X) = the universal completely-regular-Hausdorff image characterized
             by its unit and UMP,
C(X) = all continuous complex-valued functions with pointwise unital
       *-algebra operations,
C_b(X) = bounded members of C(X), with the supremum norm,
B(X) = Borel sigma-algebra generated by the actual topology,
Mfin+(X) = positive countably additive mu:B(X)->[0,infinity] with
           mu(X)<infinity and no regularity axiom.
```

Two comparison objects are frozen separately:

```text
STD-CIRCLE-p = R_{>0}/p^Z with its ordinary Hausdorff circle topology,
COPROD-PACKETS = coproduct over rational primes of ACT-PACKET-p.
```

`COPROD-PACKETS` is a **MODELING_CHOICE** coproduct, not the topology inherited
by the full global Deninger suspension. `STD-CIRCLE-p` is a retopologized
proxy. Neither may borrow actual-source topology credit.

The Paper-9 normalized-representative set theorem supplies a specific set
bijection

```text

```

Paper 10 declares, as a `DERIVABLE_NEW_DEFINITION`, the law
`x *_p y=phi_p^{-1}(phi_p(x)phi_p(y))`, identity `phi_p^{-1}(H_p)`, and the
similarly transported inverse. This does not claim that Deninger defined a
topological group on `ACT-Q-p`, and it gives no topology to a different symbol

before any character theorem receives credit.

## 3. Typed target categories

The protocol distinguishes the following targets.

1. `T0` topological spaces.
2. Hausdorff and completely regular Hausdorff spaces.
3. Countably separated measurable spaces; standard Borel spaces are examples.
4. The unit-circle Hausdorff topological group
   `T={z in C:|z|=1}` for continuous characters.
5. The fixed complex Hilbert space `H_0=ell^2(N)` and the common carrier
   `B(H_0)`, separately endowed with the norm, strong-operator, or
   weak-operator topology. Only the Hausdorff property of each named target is
   used.
6. Positive finite measures `Mfin+(X)` on the actual Borel sigma-algebra.

The word **Radon** is forbidden unless a definition valid for the exact
non-Hausdorff object is frozen and checked. Standard LCH-Hausdorff Radon
theorems are `NOT_APPLICABLE`, not silently imported.

## 4. Registered theorem targets

New theorem targets use IDs `P10-1`--`P10-10`; the same-object certificate
keeps the separate names `T0`--`T7`.

### P10-1 — Kolmogorov and separated reflections

For each of `ACT-PACKET-p`, every `ACT-ORBIT-p-a`, and nontrivial `ACT-Q-p`,
compute the topological-indistinguishability relation and `K0(X)`. Prove the
exact universal property: for every continuous `f:X->Y` with `Y` `T0`, there
is a unique continuous `f_bar:K0(X)->Y` such that `f=f_bar o q_0`. Directly
determine the Hausdorff and completely-regular-Hausdorff units and prove the
analogous unique-factorization properties in the corresponding full
subcategories of `Top`, without assuming an unverified reflection theorem.

Outcomes: `SINGLETON`, `NONTRIVIAL`, or `NOT_TESTABLE` with an exact failed
premise.

### P10-2 — continuous scalar observables

Compute `C(X)` and `C_b(X)` for every actual fixed-prime object. Determine
whether either separates points and whether the algebraic evaluations
`ev_x:C(X)->C` coincide. Only after proving `C(X)=C_b(X)` may the theorem use
the supremum norm and identify the resulting normed unital `*`-algebra
isometrically. Do not call this a Gelfand duality theorem for a compact
Hausdorff space.

### P10-3 — Borel and measurable observables

Compute the Borel sigma-algebra of each actual fixed-prime object. Classify
measurable maps into countably separated targets and standard Borel spaces.
State separately whether the nontrivial actual source itself is countably
separated or standard Borel. The target separation assumption is mandatory;
non-separated measurable targets are negative controls.

### P10-4 — finite measures and Dirac collapse

Classify `Mfin+(X)`. For `x in X`, define the Dirac measure only on measurable
sets by `delta_x(A)=1` if `x in A` and `0` otherwise; then test whether
`delta_x` and `delta_y` are distinguishable. Proper singletons must not be
called measurable when the computed Borel algebra excludes them. Keep this
result separate from any signed/complex measure, locally compact Hausdorff
Radon, Haar, disintegration, state, or operator-trace theorem.

### P10-5 — continuous group characters and operator fields

Using the exact `phi_p`-transported law frozen in Section 2, check multiplication
and inversion for the actual topology and classify
`Hom_cont((ACT-Q-p,*_p),T)`. This conclusion is only about actual-topology
continuous characters; it says nothing about algebraic characters or the same
set with a different topology. For each actual object separately classify
continuous maps into `B(H_0)` with norm, SOT, and WOT. No conclusion about
measurable fields, representations, or unbounded operators may be inferred.

### P10-6 — standard-circle comparison direction

For each orbit label `a`, choose a basepoint and freeze the resulting
noncanonical flow-parameter set bijection
`beta_{p,a}:|ACT-ORBIT-p-a|->|STD-CIRCLE-p|`; its existence uses only the exact
set stabilizer `p^Z`. Test exactly `beta_{p,a}` and `beta_{p,a}^{-1}` for
continuity. Decide whether the standard circle can be a `T0`/Hausdorff
reflection, a continuous factor of the actual orbit, or only a finer
retopology mapping continuously toward the actual indiscrete owner. No
canonical-map or source-topology credit is attached to `beta_{p,a}`.

### P10-7 — copied-packet global control

Define `COPROD-PACKETS` as the tagged disjoint union
`coprod_{p in P} ({p} x ACT-PACKET-p)` with the coproduct topology. Compute its
`T0` quotient and continuous maps to `T0` targets. Test whether the quotient
retains exactly the discrete prime label while erasing every within-prime
orbit/packet coordinate. This target is a control theorem on a modeling-choice
coproduct, not a theorem about the global source suspension.

### P10-8 — component masses and scalar-ledger boundary

## 5. Main hypotheses and falsifiers

### H1 — complete separated collapse

Every continuous map from an actual fixed-prime object to a `T0` target is
constant; all separated reflections are singletons.

**Falsifier:** an explicit nonconstant continuous map into a named `T0` space.

### H2 — observable and measure collapse

Continuous complex observables are constant, the Borel sigma-algebra is
trivial, measurable maps to countably separated targets are constant, and
positive finite measures depend only on total mass.

**Falsifier:** a nontrivial actual open/Borel set, a nonconstant licensed map,
or two finite Borel measures of equal total mass that differ on an actual
Borel set.

### H3 — proxy cannot be a separated reflection

A nonconstant actual-to-standard-circle set bijection is not continuous; the
reverse set map is continuous because its codomain is indiscrete.

**Falsifier:** an exact owner-preserving nonconstant continuous factor from the
actual orbit to the standard circle.

### H4 — coproduct retains only prime labels

The `T0` quotient of the explicit coproduct is the discrete set of rational
primes, and component weights range freely over `ell^1_+(P)` rather than being
topologically selected.

**Falsifier:** a within-component point survives the `T0` quotient, two prime
components become indistinguishable, or the topology canonically determines
nontrivial component masses.
