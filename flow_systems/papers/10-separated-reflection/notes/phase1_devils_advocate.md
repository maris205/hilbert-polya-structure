## Strongest counter-argument

For any nonempty indiscrete space, every continuous map to a `T0` target is
constant. Most proposed fixed-prime conclusions are therefore elementary
general-topology corollaries, not new arithmetic structure. The copied
coproduct retains primes because the construction tags components by primes;
the same theorem holds for composite or arbitrary labels. Likewise
`p -> log p` is separately supplied arithmetic data, not selected by the
topology or measure ledger. Publication-level nonredundancy requires framing
the contribution as an exact rational-Witt **application and owner-boundary
no-go audit**, not as a new general reflection theorem or source-global
arithmetic recovery.
