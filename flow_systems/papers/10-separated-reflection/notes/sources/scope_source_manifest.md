## 1. Retention boundary

This manifest covers only the load-bearing sources retained by the independent
scope/source auditor for Kolmogorov and separated reflections, `C(X)` versus
`C_b(X)`, countably separated and standard Borel spaces, and the ambiguity of
`Radon` on non-Hausdorff spaces.  Operator-topology, topological-group, and
plain-measure sources owned by a different Phase-2 auditor are not duplicated
here.

The exact Deninger/rational-Witt source corpus is inherited from Paper 9 by
hash and is not copied into this directory.  Every locally retained PDF below
has an adjacent ARS `pdf_read_preflight/1.0.0` sidecar with `PASS`, equal
declared/enumerated/reader page counts, and no warnings.

## 3. Load-bearing physical-page locators

### `P10-SCOPE-KQ-v2`

- physical pp. 4--6, Definition in Section 3 and Lemma 3.5: topological
  indistinguishability and its open/Borel-set characterizations;
- physical p. 9: quotient carrier, quotient topology, and quotient map;
- physical pp. 12--13, Theorems 3.19 and 3.22: the quotient is `T0`, and a
  continuous map induces a continuous map between Kolmogorov quotients.

### `P10-SCOPE-REFL-v2`

- physical p. 2: reflective-subcategory unit and unique-factorization
  definition;
- physical pp. 3--4: `T0`, `T1`, `T2`, regular, and completely regular
  subcategories are listed as standard reflective examples, followed by the
  epireflection factorization contract;
- physical pp. 5--6, Theorem 2.3, Corollary 2.4, and Proposition 2.5:
  quotient-arrow and quotient-realization statements, with their exact class
  assumptions.

### `P10-SCOPE-CFUN-v2`

- physical p. 1, abstract: `C(X)`, `C_b(X)`, `C_0(X)`, and `C_{00}(X)` are
  respectively arbitrary, bounded, vanishing-at-infinity, and
  compactly-supported continuous functions; the printed domain is locally
  compact noncompact Hausdorff, so no theorem there is transported to an
  actual non-Hausdorff packet.

### `P10-SCOPE-CS-MT34`

- physical p. 25, 343D--343E: a countable family of measurable sets separates
  points, the property belongs to `(X,Sigma)` rather than the measure, and it
  is equivalent to an injective measurable map into the reals;
- physical p. 26, 343H: examples and closure properties, used only as
  vocabulary checks.

### `P10-SCOPE-SB-v1`

- physical p. 3: a standard Borel space is the Borel measurable space of some
  complete separable metric on the same carrier;
- physical p. 13, Section 3: countably separated means that a countable
  measurable family separates points; separable metric Borel spaces are
  countably generated.

### `P10-SCOPE-RAD-MT41`

- physical p. 5, 411H(b): Fremlin's `Radon measure space` requires a Hausdorff
  topology, local finiteness, compact inner regularity, completeness, and local
  determination;
- physical pp. 66--67, 416A--416E: later results repeatedly use the Hausdorff
  premise, including that compact sets are closed.

### `P10-SCOPE-RAD-NH-1972`

- physical p. 10 / printed p. 461, Section 5: Lipsman explicitly starts with a
  locally compact, not necessarily Hausdorff space, adds a compact-Borel
  neighbourhood-basis assumption, and defines a positive Radon measure using
  countable additivity, finiteness on compact Borel sets, and inner regularity
  by compact Borel sets.
