## 2. Title, thesis, and composition contract

### Recommended title

**Separated Reflections and Observable Collapse of Indiscrete Arithmetic
Prime Packets**

Short alternative: **Observable Collapse in Deninger Prime Packets**.

### Central thesis

Paper 9's exact inherited-topology theorem makes each fixed-prime packet,
inherited orbit, and time-orbit quotient a nontrivial indiscrete space. Paper
10 determines, on those same owners, the resulting separated reflections,
continuous scalar and fixed-operator observables, measurable interfaces,
positive finite measures, and continuous characters; it then proves that the
standard circle and copied-prime coproduct retain information only after an
explicit proxy or modeling-choice change of owner.

### Contribution sentence permitted in the manuscript

> Building on Paper 9's inherited-topology theorem, we prove the exact
> universal-reflection, continuous scalar/operator, positive-finite-measure,
> transported-character, direction-sensitive retopology, and copied-component
> classifications for the hash-locked rational-Witt prime-packet owners.

This sentence deliberately claims an exact typed assembly and application.
It does not claim a new general theorem about indiscrete spaces.

## 5. Theorem order and proof dependencies

Use the shortest dependency chain below.

1. **Inherited input proposition.** Cite Paper 9 for nonemptiness,
   nontriviality, and indiscreteness of `ACT-PACKET-p`, every
   `ACT-ORBIT-p-a`, and `ACT-Q-p`. Do not re-prove or re-claim it.
2. **General indiscrete-collapse lemma.** Prove constantness into `T0`
   targets, the sharp non-`T0` negative control, the one-point UMPs,
   constant scalar algebra, trivial Borel algebra, separated measurable-map
   collapse, total-mass measure classification, Dirac equality, and fixed
   Hausdorff operator-target collapse. Label this as standard reduction.
3. **Actual-owner application theorem.** Apply the lemma separately to the
   three exact actual owners, retaining the universal prime/orbit
   quantifiers and the distinction between `K0(ACT-Q-p)` and `ACT-Q-p`.
4. **Transported-group theorem.** Define `*_p`, identity, and inverse through
   the frozen `phi_p`; prove group axioms and actual-topology continuity
   before classifying continuous circle characters.
5. **Directed circle-comparison proposition.** State the exact domain and
   codomain of `beta_{p,a}` and its inverse, prove the two opposite
   continuity verdicts, and conclude only that the circle is a finer proxy.
6. **Abstract countable-label coproduct theorem.** For an arbitrary nonempty
   countable label set, compute opens, Borel sets, indistinguishability
   classes, the discrete `K0` quotient, `T0` maps, and `ell^1_+` measures.
7. **Copied-prime corollary and scalar boundary.** Specialize the abstract
   theorem to primes, then use composite/arbitrary labels to show the lack of
   arithmetic discrimination. Treat `log p` as separately attached data.
8. **Controls and Route section.** Report the deterministic regression
   receipt, then state the seven formal Route records without treating YAML
   or finite controls as proof premises.

The proof of the direct universal properties and owner distinctions is the
center of the paper. Literature history, controls, and Route discussion must
not interrupt this chain.
