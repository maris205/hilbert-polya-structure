## 4. Frozen conventions

- `T0` means Kolmogorov separation.
- `x~y` means identical open-neighbourhood filters.
- A `T0` reflection claim must state and prove its universal mapping property.
- `B(X)` is generated by the exact actual topology.
- `Mfin+(X)` means positive countably additive
  `mu:B(X)->[0,infinity]` with `mu(X)<infinity` and no regularity axiom.
- On `COPROD-PACKETS`, finite positive measures are parameterized only by
  `ell^1_+(P)`: `m_p>=0`, `sum_p m_p<infinity`, with zeros allowed.
- `Radon` is withheld unless a compatible non-Hausdorff definition is frozen.
- `C(X)` and `C_b(X)` are distinct until equality is proved; only `C_b` carries
  the frozen supremum norm a priori.
- Operator targets are norm, SOT, and WOT on
  `B(H_0)`, `H_0=ell^2(N)`, as three distinct Hausdorff codomains; no
  representation, measurable-field, or unbounded-domain statement is included.
- The unit-circle character target is
  `T={z in C:|z|=1}` with its ordinary Hausdorff group topology.
- `beta_{p,a}` and its inverse are tested in their named map directions.
- The coproduct prime set has no source-global topology credit.
- `p->log p` on the copied discrete label set is an external, unbounded scalar
  ledger only: it is not in `C_b`/`C_0`, is not topology-selected, and gives no
  separated-owner clock, measure, trace, or `A1` credit.

## 6. Frozen exclusions

The following are outside the primary candidate:

- the full global rational-Witt suspension;
- a standard product topology on packet coordinates;
- the intrinsic Connes--Consani scaling topology;
- a standard LCH groupoid completion;
- a coordinate/product Borel sigma-algebra;
- non-normal singular trace extensions;
- determinant, continuation, functional equation, zero matching;
- unbounded spectral operators or Hilbert--Pólya claims;
- target data, fitting, or Route B.

## 7. Decision rule

Primary verdicts:

- `CONFIRM_COLLAPSE`: all registered separated/observable claims close on the
  exact actual objects, with proxy/coproduct boundaries intact;
- `REFUTE_COLLAPSE`: a legal nonconstant separated or measurable observable is
  constructed on an actual fixed-prime owner;
- `SPLIT`: topological, measurable, measure, or operator targets have different
  exact outcomes;
- `NOT_TESTABLE`: an exact object/domain/universal-property gate remains open.

The decision is independent of whether the result helps a Riemann-zeta route.
