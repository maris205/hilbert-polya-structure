## 2. Exact repairs

1. `K0(X)` now has its quotient topology, unit `q_0`, and exact unique-
   factorization contract; Hausdorff and completely-regular-Hausdorff units
   must satisfy the analogous direct universal properties.
2. `C(X)` and `C_b(X)` are split. The supremum norm is attached a priori only
   to `C_b`; a normed conclusion for all continuous functions follows only
   after equality is proved. Evaluation domains are explicit.
3. The operator carrier is fixed as `B(ell^2(N))`, with norm, SOT, and WOT
   treated as three distinct Hausdorff targets.
4. The primary measure class is positive, countably additive, finite-total-
   mass, and nonregular. Coproduct masses lie in `ell^1_+(P)`, allow zeros, and
   obey the exact union formula.
5. The normalized-representative bijection
   `phi_p:ACT-Q-p->U_p/H_p` is frozen; the group law is a Paper-10
   `DERIVABLE_NEW_DEFINITION`, and continuity must be proved before character
   credit. Algebraic and alternative-topology characters are excluded.
6. A basepoint-dependent, noncanonical set bijection `beta_{p,a}` is frozen
   for every actual orbit; its two directions are named separately.
7. The copied object is an explicit tagged coproduct. Its Borel and mass
   ledgers are typed, composite/arbitrary-label controls remain mandatory, and
   `p->log p` is only an external unbounded scalar with no clock/measure/trace/
   `A1` promotion.
8. The owner record family is now an indexed schema covering every actual
   object and each reflection/scalar/Borel/measure/operator interface, plus
   separately named character, proxy, coproduct-reflection, and coproduct-
   measure records.
9. The novelty boundary now states that Paper 9 already records the immediate
   trivial-Borel and `T0`-map consequences. Paper 10 claims only the exact
   universal/measure/operator/proxy/coproduct package, and any literature
   novelty claim remains bounded-search evidence.
10. Prime and orbit-label quantifiers, unit-circle target, Dirac measurable-set
    definition, source-not-standard-Borel check, and no-Radon/no-Gelfand/no-
    global-source boundaries are explicit.
