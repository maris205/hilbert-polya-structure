## 4. Domain findings

### 4.1 Kolmogorov reflection

Pirttimäki physical pp. 4--6 identifies topological indistinguishability and
its open/Borel characterizations; p. 9 fixes the quotient carrier and quotient
topology; pp. 12--13 prove that the quotient is `T0` and describe the map
induced between Kolmogorov quotients. This is adequate authoritative support
for the construction used in `K0(X)`.

The cited induced-map result is not a substitute for writing the precise
factorization to an arbitrary `T0` target in Paper 10. Phase 3 must prove
existence, continuity, and uniqueness for the actual `q_0:X->K0(X)`, without
confusing `K0(X)` with the time-orbit quotient `ACT-Q-p`.

### 4.2 Hausdorff and completely-regular-Hausdorff reflections

Hernández-Arzusa--Hernández physical p. 2 gives the reflection UMP language;
pp. 3--4 list standard separated reflective subcategories; pp. 5--6 state
quotient-arrow and realization results with explicit class assumptions. This
is sufficient to establish that the vocabulary and category-theoretic shape
are standard and feasible.

There is a terminology hazard: authors vary over whether “completely
regular” includes `T1` or Hausdorff separation. Paper 10's object is explicitly
`Sep_CRH`, meaning **completely regular and Hausdorff**. It must retain that
expanded phrase and directly verify the one-point unit's UMP. The literature
must not be cited as if it had already computed the reflection of the actual
rational-Witt object.

### 4.3 `C(X)` and `C_b(X)`

Amini physical p. 1 explicitly separates all continuous functions, bounded
continuous functions, functions vanishing at infinity, and compactly
supported functions. It therefore supports the protocol's notation split.
Its stated domain is locally compact noncompact Hausdorff; no theorem from
that paper is transported to the non-Hausdorff packet.

Accordingly, Phase 3 must first compute `C(X)` algebraically, then its bounded
subalgebra `C_b(X)`, and attach the supremum norm only to `C_b(X)` or after an
independent proof that all members of `C(X)` are bounded. It may not promote
the result to Gelfand duality or to a compact-Hausdorff representation theorem.

### 4.4 Countably separated and standard Borel spaces

Fremlin Chapter 34 physical p. 25, 343D--343E, defines countable separation as
a property of the measurable space `(X,Sigma)` and records the injective
measurable-map characterization. Preston physical p. 3 defines a standard
Borel space through a complete separable metric generating the same Borel
structure; physical p. 13 independently fixes countable separation.

These definitions are sufficient for the typed target class. They do not
prove that a particular source is or is not countably separated. Phase 3 must
state the source question separately from the target-map question. In
particular, it must not infer “standard Borel” from cardinality alone or
replace the actual topology-generated Borel algebra by a coordinate/product
proxy sigma-algebra.

### 4.5 Positive finite measures and the `Radon` boundary

The protocol's

`Mfin+(X)={mu:B(X)->[0,infinity] : mu is countably additive and mu(X)<infinity}`

is a well-typed plain measure class and needs no regularity vocabulary.
Fremlin Chapter 41 physical p. 5, 411H(b), uses a Hausdorff, locally finite,
compact-inner-regular, complete, locally determined `Radon measure space`
convention. Lipsman physical p. 10 / printed p. 461 explicitly treats a
locally compact space that need not be Hausdorff, but adds a compact-Borel
neighbourhood-basis hypothesis and defines positive Radon measures using
finiteness and inner regularity on compact Borel sets.

These are genuinely competing domain contracts. Neither can be silently
attached to the actual packet. The correct Phase-3 vocabulary remains
“positive finite measure on the actual Borel sigma-algebra,” with **no
regularity axiom**. `Radon`, `Haar`, `state`, `trace`, and disintegration
claims remain out of scope unless a separately frozen definition and all its
hypotheses are verified.

## 6. Bounded novelty search

The search was deliberately bounded to authoritative/primary endpoints and
the exact conceptual conjunction. Query families combined the following:

- Deninger, rational Witt vectors, `E_f`, arithmetic dynamical systems, and
  the known suspension/topology papers;
- Kolmogorov quotient, Hausdorff reflection, completely regular reflection,
  Borel structure, finite measure, continuous observable, and operator field;
- actual packet/orbit quotient versus proxy/coproduct terminology;
- arXiv-restricted searches and exact-phrase combinations for the proposed
  reflection/observable/measure package.

The closest results separated into three non-equivalent classes:

1. Deninger's known rational-Witt/arithmetic-dynamical sources, already
   represented by the exact Paper-9 locks;
2. generic work on Kolmogorov or Hausdorff reflections and generic measurable
   space/measure conventions, represented by the retained scope corpus; and
3. later Deninger material concerning different algebraic or sheaf-theoretic
   constructions, without the exact packet-level separated-reflection,
   observable-collapse, finite-measure, proxy-direction, and copied-component
   conjunction.

No located item states the exact Paper-10 typed package. This negative result
is finite-query evidence only. Permitted wording is:

> No direct precedent for the exact rational-Witt packet package was found
> within the bounded Phase-2 search.

It is not permissible to write “first,” “novel,” “unprecedented,” or an
exhaustive nonexistence claim on this evidence. The ordinary indiscrete-space
lemmas and Paper-9 consequences must be identified as generic/prior facts,
while any Paper-10 contribution is confined to the exact assembled UMP,
classification, domain, proxy-direction, and copied-component package that is
actually proved.

## 7. Mandatory Phase-3 source gates

Phase 3 may proceed only with all of the following ceilings preserved:

1. Every theorem names the exact actual object (`ACT-PACKET-p`,
   `ACT-ORBIT-p-a`, or `ACT-Q-p`) or the separately tagged proxy/model; no
   source-to-proxy credit transfer.
2. Each `K0`, Hausdorff, or completely-regular-Hausdorff result writes and
   proves its exact unit, factorization, continuity, and uniqueness; a generic
   reflectivity citation is contextual support only.
3. `Sep_CRH` is always expanded as “completely regular and Hausdorff”; no
   convention-dependent abbreviation may weaken the target category.
4. `C(X)` and `C_b(X)` remain distinct until equality is proved; the supremum
   norm is not assigned early, and no Gelfand theorem is invoked.
5. Countable separation and standard Borelness of the **source** are checked
   directly from its actual Borel algebra, separately from constancy of maps
   into separated targets.
6. `Mfin+` means only positive, countably additive, finite-total-mass measures
   on `B(X)`. No regularity, `Radon`, Haar, state, trace, or disintegration
   language is imported.
7. The Paper-9 trivial-Borel and `T0`-map consequences are cited as inherited
   inputs. The new claim, if established, is the exact assembled package, not
   those generic consequences.
8. The standard circle and disjoint coproduct remain respectively a
   retopologized proxy and a modeling-choice global control. Neither becomes a
   fact about the global Deninger suspension.

Violation of any item above is a mandatory source/domain revision, not a
stylistic issue.
