## 6. Non-Hausdorff measure and operator-language safety

The negative wording is appropriately conservative:

- `Radon` is withheld rather than given a tacit Hausdorff/LCH meaning.
- Jüstel-style Radon/disintegration theorems remain `NOT_APPLICABLE` to the
  actual owners under their failed Hausdorff prerequisites.
- `Haar`, `state`, `trace`, disintegration, and operator completion are not
  inferred from a finite measure on a trivial Borel sigma-algebra.
- Operator targets are used only for their Hausdorff separation, not as an
  asserted representation of the packet or action.
- Measurable and unbounded operator fields are excluded.

The positive measure vocabulary still needs one exact convention.  The
smallest compatible choice is:

```text
Mfin+(X) = {mu : B(X) -> [0,infinity) | mu is countably additive and mu(X)<infinity},
with no inner/outer regularity, local finiteness, Radon, Haar, state, or trace
property asserted.
```

If signed or complex measures are desired, they require a separately frozen
variation norm and a different classification statement; they should not be
silently included under the current positive component-mass language.

For the countable prime coproduct, admissible component masses must be typed as

```text
(m_p) in ell^1_+(P) = {(m_p): m_p >= 0 and sum_p m_p < infinity}.
```

Zero component masses must be allowed.  “Arbitrary” may mean arbitrary within
this cone; topology selects no distinguished element of the cone.  Finite
controls on 2, 3, 5, and 8 components should record nonnegative vectors and
their total mass, while the infinite countable-additivity/summability statement
remains a symbolic theorem rather than a finite-computation result.

### M2 — freeze the finite-measure domain (**Major**)

Amend `research_protocol.md:40-46,105-110,136-149,190-197,230-231` and
`candidate_lock.md:66-75` to specify finite **positive** countably additive
measures, codomain `[0,infinity)`, finite total mass, and no regularity
property.  Replace unrestricted “arbitrary positive component masses” by
arbitrary `(m_p) in ell^1_+(P)`, allowing zeros.  Signed/complex measures are
out of scope unless separately locked.

### M3 — repair the unresolved/novelty boundary (**Major**)

Amend `research_protocol.md:10-17` and the P10-1/P10-3 ownership language to
acknowledge that Paper 9 already records trivial Borel structure and constancy
of `T0`-valued continuous maps.  Treat those statements as inherited inputs or
base corollaries.  Define the genuinely unresolved contribution as the exact
universal-reflection package, finite-measure classification, fixed-Hilbert
operator-target classification, two-direction proxy comparison, copied-prime
quotient/mass classification, and their same-object/Route adjudication.  Phase
2 must test novelty only for this exact conjunction and may use only bounded
negative-search wording.

### m1 — freeze scalar and operator target conventions (**Minor, mandatory**)

Before proof, either define `C_b(X)` with the supremum norm or state that
`Ccont(X)` is initially only a pointwise `*`-algebra and receives the supremum
norm only after boundedness is established.  Define the character target as
the unit-circle group `T`, and quantify over or name one fixed complex Hilbert
space `H`; write the operator targets separately as `B(H)` with norm, SOT, and
WOT.  No algebra homomorphism, representation, measurable field, state, trace,
or unbounded operator follows from classifying continuous maps.

These four repairs are exhaustive for this review.  They do not require a new
candidate family, a new Paper-9 topology proof, or a broader source search.
