Audit date: **2026-08-14**

Audited drafts: `research_protocol.md` and `candidate_lock.md`

## Verdict

**REVISE, then pass to Phase 2.**

The proposed proxy is a legitimate, sharply falsifiable next object.  The
drafts already separate it from `DEN-WITT-Z-FIN`, prohibit target fitting,
pre-register base-blind controls, and refuse Route B.  The Phase-1 lock is not
yet internally typed, however.  It currently conflates:

1. a locally finite positive-time **flat/distributional return functional**;
2. evaluation by a normal semifinite trace on its `L1(tau)` ideal; and
3. a distinct zero-mode `tau`-Fredholm determinant.

It also gives the primary candidate an `M1` suffix before unit component
masses have a proved source provenance, and calls the selected decomposable
type-I algebra a groupoid/flow-generated algebra without defining the
groupoid or proving that generation statement.  These are required Phase-1
repairs, not reasons to abandon the project.

## Required amendment 1 — split the candidate records

Do not use `DEN-WITT-PACKET-GPD-M1` as the primary construction while
`m_p=1` is still an open provenance question.  Freeze at least the following
records, or exact equivalents:

| Candidate ID | Frozen owner and role |
|---|---|
| `DEN-WITT-Z-FIN` | published rational-Witt flow; ownership target only |
| `DEN-WITT-PACKET-DECOMP-MASS-FAM` | explicit product proxy, chosen decomposable algebra, and all admissible positive mass sequences |
| `DEN-WITT-PACKET-DECOMP-FLAT-M` | componentwise Poisson traces assembled only as a positive-time distribution for a frozen mass sequence |
| `DEN-WITT-PACKET-DECOMP-K0-M1` | zero-circle-mode operator with unit central masses; conditional specialization whose provenance must be proved separately |

If an actual groupoid is frozen, `GPD` may replace `DECOMP`; otherwise the
candidate ID must not imply a groupoid theorem.  A change of groupoid, Haar
system, von Neumann completion, mass sequence, operator, or determinant
convention creates a new candidate/source lock.

The mass-family proxy is primary; `M1` is a downstream conditional candidate.

## Required amendment 2 — type the von Neumann algebra exactly

The current formula

```text
M_p = L-infinity(B_p, mu_p) bar-tensor B(H_p)
```

defines a selected decomposable type-I von Neumann algebra.  It is not, from
the present draft alone, the algebra generated by circle translations, nor is
it identified with the von Neumann algebra of an action groupoid.

Phase 1 must choose one of two paths:

1. **Decomposable-algebra path:** call `M_p` an explicit operator-algebra
   enrichment, specify its representation, and delete claims that it is a
   groupoid algebra or is generated by the flow; or
2. **Groupoid path:** define the unit space, arrows, source/range maps,
   composition, topology/Borel structure, Haar system, quasi-invariant or
   transverse measure, representation, and the precise theorem identifying
   its von Neumann algebra and trace with `M_p` and `tau_p`.

An action groupoid, pair groupoid, and the displayed algebra are not
interchangeable.  Any chosen groupoid remains a proxy until a transport theorem.

Fix the global algebra as the von Neumann direct sum/product of bounded
families.  Define `tau_m` on its positive cone and `L1(M,tau_m)` separately.

## Required amendment 3 — flat return distribution is not `tau_m(U(f))`

For each fixed component, smooth time convolution is trace class and the
component formula may be targeted as

```text
Tr(U_p(f)) = L_p sum_{r in Z} f(r L_p).
```

Globally, however, the block operator `U(f) = direct_sum_p U_p(f)` is in
general not in `L1(M,tau_m)`.  In particular, support of `f` away from zero
makes the **signed component traces** vanish outside finitely many return
times, but it does not make

```text
sum_p m_p ||U_p(f)||_1
```

finite.  Positive/negative Fourier cancellations cannot be used to extend a
normal semifinite trace beyond its `L1` domain.

Replace current T1--T3 language by two statements:

- `Theta_m(f) = sum_p m_p Tr(U_p(f))` is a componentwise-defined,
  locally finite flat/distributional trace on test functions supported away
  from zero, subject to a proof of the stated mass/local-finiteness condition;
- it is **not** asserted to equal `tau_m(U(f))` unless the separate criterion
  `sum_p m_p ||U_p(f)||_1 < infinity` is proved.

Accordingly, T3 must not pre-register that the global smeared operators lie in
the semifinite trace domain.  It must determine the exact `L1(tau_m)`
criterion and explicitly record failure for unit masses if that criterion
fails.  The paper title and primary question should use “return
distribution” rather than “semifinite return trace” unless this distinction is
made in the title itself.

## Required amendment 4 — freeze the genuine `tau`-ideal object separately

Add the zero-mode record

```text
P_0,p = projection onto the constant circle mode,
K_s = direct_sum_p exp(-s L_p) (1_Bp tensor P_0,p).
```

For `sigma = Re(s)`, the precise trace-ideal gate is

```text
K_s in L1(M,tau_m)  iff  sum_p m_p exp(-sigma L_p) < infinity.
```

For `m_p=1` and `L_p=log p`, this holds on `Re(s)>1`.  Only this record may
seek a genuine analytic `tau`-Fredholm/log-determinant identity.  Freeze the
direction explicitly:

```text
Det_tau(I-K_s) = product_p (1-p^(-s))^(m_p),
Z_m(s) = Det_tau(I-K_s)^(-1),
```

in its proved trace-ideal half-plane, with the logarithm branch and
normalization stated.  Do not call this the determinant of `U(f)`, an ordinary
Hilbert-space determinant, or a primitive-orbit Ruelle determinant.

This determinant is base-blind: normalized transverse mass gives
`tau_p(1 tensor P_0,p)=1` for a singleton or any probability base.  It is
therefore an exact ledger realization and a mandatory Paper-4-style
`PROVES_TOO_MUCH` control, not evidence that the geometry of `B_p` generated
the Euler coefficients.

## Required amendment 5 — pre-freeze, but do not assume, `m_p=1` provenance

Before any determinant calculation, add a typed open obligation containing:

1. the source-derived bijection between rational closed points `(p)` and the
   central proxy components;
2. counting measure on those closed points, fixed without reference to zeta;
3. an explicit transport/functor from that counting measure to the central
   trace weights of the **same** proxy algebra;
4. compatibility with packet duplication and coordinate changes; and
5. a statement of whether this is source theorem, new enrichment theorem, or
   modeling choice.

Only a proved item 3 establishes `m_p=1` provenance.  Uniqueness obtained from
agreement with `-zeta'/zeta`, convergence at `Re(s)>1`, or the desired Euler
product remains a circular diagnostic and cannot upgrade the evidence label.

Until this obligation passes, the `M1` record is `MODELING_CHOICE` for the
proxy and `NOT_TESTABLE` as a trace of `DEN-WITT-Z-FIN`.

## Typed ownership and T0--T7 boundary

| Record | Maximum permissible Phase-2 ownership statement |
|---|---|
| `DEN-WITT-Z-FIN` | source owns prime packets, repetitions, and clock only; measured T2--T6 remain `NOT_TESTABLE` |
| mass-family proxy | proxy owns its product Borel/topological structure, chosen algebra, trace family, and translation action |
| flat-return record | proxy owns a locally finite scalar return distribution, not a global `tau`-trace evaluation |
| zero-mode record | proxy owns `K_s` and its `tau`-determinant in the trace-ideal half-plane |

A choice-independent measurable, flow-equivariant map is necessary but not
sufficient to transfer the last three records to the original packet.  The
bridge must also transport measure/disintegration, algebra, representation,
trace domain, test class, coefficients, clock, and normalization.  Absent
that theorem, proxy success leaves original T2--T6 at `NOT_TESTABLE`.

Paper 6's lesson applies even inside one proxy: the translation return
distribution and the zero-mode determinant have different analytic owners.
They may be compared because they share a parent, but credits cannot be merged
unless one theorem identifies their traces and determinant within one record.

## Permissible Route-A claims after amendment

- **A1:** an exact primitive/repetition ledger may be proved for the explicit
  proxy, with its uncountable same-period orbit multiplicity disclosed.  This
  does not upgrade the original beyond its inherited weak packet-level A1.
- **A2, flat record:** a locally finite return distribution is structural
  evidence only; by itself it is not a zeta or Fredholm determinant and cannot
  receive `A2_ANALYTIC_DETERMINANT`.
- **A2, zero-mode record:** an exact right-half-plane `tau`-determinant may
  receive an object-specific analytic-determinant statement only after its
  determinant convention, `L1` theorem, and mass provenance are closed.  Its
  base-blind control must simultaneously record `STOP_SCOPED /
  PROVES_TOO_MUCH` for packet-geometry or Riemann promotion.
- **A3:** equality on `Re(s)>1` supplies no continuation, functional equation,
  Gamma factor, completed divisor, counting law, or Weil compression.  No A3
  promotion is permissible in this project unless a new source-locked theorem
  supplies those data; that would require a new candidate audit.
- **A4 / Route B:** not invoked.  No self-adjointness or spectral credit may be
  inferred from semifinite trace or determinant exactness.

## Phase-1 release conditions

Phase 1 may be marked **PASS** after the protocol and candidate lock, without
using any Phase-2 result, satisfy all of the following:

- candidate IDs distinguish original, mass family, flat distribution, and
  zero-mode determinant;
- the groupoid is fully defined or groupoid-generation claims are removed;
- the global `L1(tau_m)` issue replaces the current semifinite-smearing claim;
- `K_s` and its determinant direction are frozen as a separate object;
- `m_p=1` has a pre-result provenance obligation and is not the primary ID;
- proxy-to-original transport lists every T0--T7 field, not only a measurable
  conjugacy; and
- Route A is capped as above, with Route B still forbidden.
