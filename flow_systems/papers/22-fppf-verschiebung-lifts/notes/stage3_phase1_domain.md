## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) concerns whether the chosen investigative and inferential procedures are sufficiently disciplined for specialists to trust the resulting mathematical claims, including transparent assumptions, checkable derivations, and enough procedural detail for independent verification.

D2 (domain_accuracy) concerns the correctness of specialized definitions, terminology, cited results, and factual assertions, together with faithful treatment of established work and conclusions that do not exceed what the relevant mathematics supports.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) concerns whether the principal line of reasoning remains logically consistent, whether each substantive conclusion follows from its stated premises, and whether hidden assumptions or invalid inferences compromise the central case.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) concerns whether readers in neighboring areas can recover the intended meaning of technical concepts and whether broader connections or consequences are justified with the same precision expected of field-internal statements.

D5 (writing_and_structure) concerns whether the organization and presentation make domain-level verification feasible, with notation, proofs, displays, and supporting material arranged clearly enough to expose rather than conceal substantive issues.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) concerns whether the asserted advance is genuinely new and consequential relative to established knowledge and whether its scope would be suitable for an intended specialist readership once authoritative target criteria are available.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Correct use of arithmetic-algebraic definitions and operations, valid characteristic-dependent factorization arguments, accurate invocation of established results, faithful positioning of prior work, and claim strength proportionate to the mathematics supplied.
what_triggers_block: A central domain claim depends on a materially incorrect definition, theorem application, algebraic identity, or account of prior work and therefore requires substantive repair before it can be relied upon.
what_triggers_warn: A localized terminological imprecision, omitted qualification, or incomplete contextual comparison could mislead specialists but leaves the central domain claims potentially intact.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational contradiction with standard theory or an impossible core construction invalidates the principal claimed contribution rather than admitting correction within the existing argument.
