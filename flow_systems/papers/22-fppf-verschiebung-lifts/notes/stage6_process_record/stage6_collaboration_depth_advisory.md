## Forced-counterexample check

- High delegation is supported, but the division between AI judgment and
  retained author judgment could have been stated more explicitly at reviewer
  and content-confirmation checkpoints.
- Exact patch authorization and workflow gates demonstrate artifact vigilance;
  they do not by themselves establish independent verification of the central
  descent proof or Deninger attribution.
- The move to a five-paper, roadmap-indexed portfolio demonstrates cognitive
  reallocation at the research-governance level; no visible Paper-22 theorem
  reformulation or new counterargument supports a Zone-3 classification.

Re-reading the turns under the counter-hypothesis “this is Zone 2” did not
falsify that hypothesis. The observer therefore does not confirm Zone 3.

## Suggested next-round practice

- At each mandatory checkpoint, record three short author lines: the reason for
  acceptance, the current weakest claim, and the exact evidence location
  sampled.
- For each new paper, record which Route judgment changed and which bold
  hypothesis remains unsupported.
- Before switching dynamical subtype, state why the switch is informative and
  name one result that would reverse the current decision.
