## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1 — Section Completeness

Mandatory. I will draft every section in the Structure Architect's approved
outline and will leave no unresolved `[MATERIAL GAP]` placeholders when the
manuscript is handed to citation compliance.

### D2 — Citation Density

Mandatory. Every factual claim will receive at least one supporting citation;
otherwise, I will either source the claim or rewrite it with appropriate
qualification. Absence and novelty claims will instead carry documented-search
provenance in the required bounded form and cite the named nearest prior work
when adjacent literature exists. If no adjacent work is found, I will state
that absence explicitly.

### D3 — Argument Blueprint Fidelity

Mandatory. Each section will follow the claim--evidence--reasoning chains
specified by the runtime Argument Blueprint, which, not this contract, governs
the manuscript's substantive argument structure. Topic sentences will express
the assigned claims, evidence will use the assigned sources, and explanations
will connect the evidence to each section's purpose.

### D4 — Total Word Count

High priority. The final draft of *A Descent Obstruction to Verschiebung Lifts
on fppf and Finite-Flat Sites* will remain within 10 percent of the 5,000-word
target: 4,500--5,500 words.

### D5 — Per-Section Word Count

High priority. Each section will remain within 15 percent of its allocation in
the Paper Configuration Record.

### D6 — Paragraph Structure

Normal priority. At least 80 percent of eligible body paragraphs will follow
TEEL structure: topic, evidence, explanation, and link. Transitions will
connect successive sections. The introduction's first paragraph and the
conclusion's last paragraph are exempt from this calculation.

### D7 — Register Consistency

Normal priority. The manuscript will maintain a consistent scholarly register
appropriate to pure mathematics, using precise discipline-specific language
for rational Witt vectors, sheaves, descent, fppf sites, finite-flat sites,
and Verschiebung, without colloquial mixing.
