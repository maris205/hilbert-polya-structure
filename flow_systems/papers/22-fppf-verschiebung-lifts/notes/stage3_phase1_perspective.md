## Contract Paraphrase

D1 concerns whether the path from premises to conclusions is explicit, inspectable, and reproducible enough for researchers outside the primary specialty to understand how the work earns its results.

D2 concerns the reliability of technical language, substantive claims, and representations of earlier scholarship, especially where an adjacent-field reader must depend on the paper’s account of specialist knowledge.

D3 concerns whether the central line of reasoning remains consistent throughout and whether each important conclusion receives adequate support without inference gaps that become visible across disciplinary boundaries.

D4 concerns whether readers from neighboring fields receive the definitions and conceptual bridges needed to follow the contribution, and whether every asserted interdisciplinary connection is justified rather than merely suggested.

D5 concerns whether the sequence of exposition, clarity of presentation, and use of any supporting displays make the argument navigable for its intended and adjacent audiences while respecting applicable scholarly conventions.

D6 concerns whether the work’s stated contribution is sufficiently original and consequential for its intended readership and whether its scope is appropriately matched to the selected publication context, without presuming any particular target.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Clear definitions, explicit conceptual bridges, and adequately supported connections that let adjacent-field readers understand the scope and significance of the argument.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive dependence on unexplained specialist framing or unsupported interdisciplinary assertions prevents adjacent-field readers from evaluating the central contribution.
what_triggers_warn: Localized terminology or bridging gaps impede accessibility or overstate a connection, but focused clarification and qualification would repair them.
