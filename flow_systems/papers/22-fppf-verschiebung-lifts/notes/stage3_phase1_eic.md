## Contract Paraphrase

D1 asks editorial oversight to establish whether the chosen mode of inquiry is disciplined, any evidence or data are handled responsibly, quantitative reporting is transparent where relevant, and enough procedural detail exists for independent verification.

D2 asks the panel to verify that specialized statements accord with accepted knowledge, technical vocabulary is used correctly, and the account of earlier scholarship is accurate and fair.

D3 concerns the logical integrity of the central position: its parts should be mutually compatible, its supporting reasons should bear the asserted conclusions, and no decisive reasoning defect should compromise the main case.

D4 concerns whether readers from neighboring areas can understand the framing, terminology, and consequences, and whether claims that reach beyond the primary specialty receive adequate justification.

D5 treats presentation as an editorial criterion, covering the progression of ideas, readability, effective use of visual or tabular material where present, and conformity with applicable submission conventions.

D6 asks whether the work offers a sufficiently novel and consequential advance for an identified scholarly audience, with venue-specific compatibility assessed only when an authoritative target has been provided.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Assess whether the exposition has a navigable progression, introduces concepts at useful points, maintains readable notation and prose, and uses any display material to clarify the argument.
what_triggers_block: Block when pervasive structural or expository failures prevent a qualified reader from recovering the main line of reasoning without substantial reorganization or rewriting.
what_triggers_warn: Warn when the presentation remains broadly followable but recurring problems in sequencing, notation, prose, or display integration materially impede comprehension.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Assess the stated advance for novelty, mathematical significance, and prospective readership value, while reserving venue-specific alignment until an authoritative target is supplied.
what_triggers_block: Block when the proposed advance is too limited, insufficiently differentiated, or too weakly motivated to support publication at the eventual target level.
what_triggers_warn: Warn when a plausible contribution is identifiable but its distinctiveness, importance, or value to the intended audience is not yet convincingly established.
what_triggers_fatal: Treat as fatal when the central contribution is demonstrably non-original or fundamentally outside the scope of an authoritative target once such a target is supplied.
