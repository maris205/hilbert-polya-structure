## Scoring Plan

### D1: originality

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: The Introduction names a specific contribution and gap, the literature framing identifies what the paper adds, and the Discussion substantively distinguishes the descent obstruction and its bounded consequences from the named prior work.
what_triggers_block: The contribution statement is vague or absent, the Discussion lists prior work without engagement, or the paper restates known conclusions in a new context with no added framework, evidence, or perspective.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution exists but is weakly differentiated from cited prior work, or the Discussion engages that prior work unevenly.

### D2: methodological_rigor

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: The theoretical method is named and justified against the lifting question, all sites and algebraic objects are typed, the proof steps are detailed enough to reproduce, the fppf and finite-flat arguments are kept distinct where required, and limitations are explicit.
what_triggers_block: The method does not match the research question, a significant proof-design flaw invalidates an inference, limitations are not discussed, or the analysis cannot be replicated from the description.
what_triggers_warn: The method is fundamentally sound but insufficiently detailed, some choices or site-dependent steps lack justification, or limitations are mentioned but not substantively engaged.

### D3: evidence_sufficiency

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Every factual claim has an appropriate citation, every mathematical claim has an adequate proof or valid cited result, search-bounded absence or novelty language has the permitted provenance, and all inferences remain within the evidence.
what_triggers_block: Multiple core claims lack evidence, the paper over-relies on a single source, Discussion inferences exceed what the proofs or sources support, favorable evidence is cherry-picked, or a declared negative constraint is materially breached.
what_triggers_warn: Core claims have evidence but some secondary claims lack support, citation or proof density is uneven across sections, or a bounded-search qualification is present but incomplete.

### D4: argument_coherence

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: The Introduction's research question is answered in the Conclusion, the approved proof chain remains traceable across sections, transitions are present, and the thesis is consistent across both abstracts, the Introduction, theorem statements, and the Conclusion.
what_triggers_block: Multiple logical gaps separate sections, the Conclusion is disconnected from the Introduction's research question, a required site-specific argument is replaced by an invalid implication, or passages are self-contradictory.
what_triggers_warn: The overall logic holds but some transitions feel forced, a proof dependency is locally unclear, or minor inconsistencies appear between section claims.

### D5: writing_quality

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: The register is consistently academic and appropriate to pure mathematics, grammar errors are rare, notation and citation format are internally consistent, and any figures or tables are referenced in the text.
what_triggers_block: The manuscript is difficult to understand, contains numerous grammar or notation errors, uses a colloquial or materially inconsistent register, or applies citation format inconsistently across the manuscript.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is readable but has several grammar, notation, or word-choice issues, some paragraphs are overly long or structurally opaque, or minor citation-format inconsistencies remain.
