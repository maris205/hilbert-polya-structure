# Generated deterministic artifacts

The eight CSV files are generated by
`../code/packet_separation_controls.py`:

- `simultaneous_approximation.csv`: exact `q=m/p^k` finite-cylinder residues,
  numerator residues, real errors, nearest-congruence bounds, and explicit
  finite-witness scope.
- `finite_cyclic_characters.csv`: rational exponents on finite cyclic groups,
  exact character-value matching, cyclic/global finite-kernel ledgers, and the
  fixed initial-stage label.
- `action_sign_controls.csv`: correct inverse-time action versus a deliberately
  wrong sign, with exact times and numerical display errors.
- `pz_circle_controls.csv`: exact nonmembership in `p^Z` and positive standard
  log-circle distances for the stabilizer-only negative control.
- `unit_normalization_controls.csv`: exact finite checks of
  `[(F_nu P_a,u)]=[(P_a,nu*u)]` for unit exponents and several finite-kernel
  factors.
- `distinctness_controls.csv`: exact time distinctness, finite-quotient
  nonmembership in the image of `p^Zhat`, and a positive Galois-equivalence
  control.
- `illegal_kernel_proxy.csv`: growing finite kernel lower bounds for components
  `a_ell=ell`, compared with legal unit components `a_ell=1+ell`.
- `prime_uniformity_summary.csv`: deterministic summaries for
  `p=2,3,5,7,11`; this is neither statistical evidence nor a theorem of
  uniformity.

`packet_separation_manifest.json` records row counts, byte sizes, SHA-256
artifact hashes, exact active-tuple hashes, six implementation hashes,
parameters, maximum-error metrics, deterministic-input declarations, and
same-object/interpretation boundaries. It has no timestamp, so identical
inputs produce identical bytes.

All artifacts are finite target-free controls. They do not prove P9-1--P9-9,
the unit-exponent exhaustiveness lemma, inherited packet/orbit topology,
Morishita `C_p` topology, `Q_p` separation, a non-Hausdorff groupoid theory,
Paper-8 correction, or Route A/B status.
