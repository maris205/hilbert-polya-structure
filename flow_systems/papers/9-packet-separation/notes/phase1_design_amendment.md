## Methodology-review refinements

The constructive CRT witness must verify the residue of
`q_j=m_j p^{-k_j}` itself.  Equivalently, for a finite modulus `M_j` prime to
`p`, it selects

```text
m_j = a_j p^{k_j} (mod M_j)
```

and independently controls `|m_j/p^{k_j}-c|`; it does not infer profinite
convergence of `q_j` from convergence of `m_j`.  The set exhaustiveness proof
must also normalize every finite-kernel exponent to a unit exponent using the
diagonal action before the universal packet theorem is invoked.

Morishita's target is split into `Cp-INHERITED`, whose topology must be tested
on the actual adelic quotient, and `Cp-STD-CIRCLE-PROXY`, whose ordinary circle
topology is a separate retopology.  No “circle” label identifies them.
