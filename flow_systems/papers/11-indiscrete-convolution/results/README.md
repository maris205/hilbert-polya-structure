`../code/indiscrete_convolution_controls.py` and verified by
`../experiments/reproduce.sh`.

| `action_blind_controls.csv` | trivial/transitive/nontransitive actions with one global signature |

The CSVs are exact finite witnesses only. They do not prove an infinite or
continuous theorem, authorize a published Haar-system label, define a
standard actual-groupoid completion, establish arithmetic relevance, decide
Route A/B, or support a standalone-publication claim.
