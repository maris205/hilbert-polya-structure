   source-gated and may be `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

   reduced norm, transported group-full norm, and Fourier sign are frozen.

   set-groupoid map `J`, its two continuity directions, and contravariant

   independent controls, and trivial/nontransitive actions expose the general
