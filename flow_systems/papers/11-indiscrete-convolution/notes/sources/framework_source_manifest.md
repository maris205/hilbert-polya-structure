## 2. Exact locator index

### `FW-TU04`

- physical p. 3 / printed p. 567, Definition 1.1: `quasi-compact` is the
  open-cover property, `compact` means quasi-compact and Hausdorff, and
  `locally compact` means every point has a compact neighborhood; Tu notes
  that this forces local Hausdorffness;
- physical p. 17 / printed p. 581, Section 4.1: for a locally compact space
  `X`, `C_c(X)_0` consists of zero-extensions of `C_c(V)` from open Hausdorff
  `V`, and `C_c(X)` is their linear span; its elements need not be globally
  continuous;
- physical p. 19 / printed p. 583, Definition 4.6: the ambient groupoid is
  locally compact in Tu's sense, every source fibre is Hausdorff, the test
  functions are the preceding `C_c(G)`, and the Haar system satisfies full
  support, continuity into `C_c(G^(0))`, and invariance.

### `FW-MW08`

- physical/printed p. 3: the treatment is explicitly limited to locally
  Hausdorff, locally compact groupoids and later to second-countable ones;
- physical/printed pp. 4--5: “accepted practice” is the raw vector-space span
  `C(X)` of zero-extensions from `C_c(U)` over open Hausdorff `U`; the authors
  emphasize that these functions need be neither globally continuous nor
  compactly supported in the ambient space;
- physical/printed p. 6, G1--G4: operations continuous, unit space Hausdorff,
  every arrow has a compact Hausdorff neighborhood, and range/source open;
- physical/printed p. 7: the full Haar system lives on the Hausdorff range
  fibres, tests `C(G)`, and requires invariance plus a continuous compactly
  supported unit-space integral;
- physical/printed pp. 21--23, Proposition 4.4 and proof: under those standing
  hypotheses the Hausdorff-patch section span is closed under convolution and
  involution and has the stated universal completion.

### `FW-EX09-v3`

- physical/printed p. 1, Section 1: an étale groupoid is defined with a
  locally compact Hausdorff unit space and range/source local
  homeomorphisms, even when the arrow space is allowed to be non-Hausdorff.

### `FW-BHM18-v2`

- physical/printed pp. 1--2: the universal property is formulated for a
  locally compact Hausdorff groupoid with Haar system; p. 2 expressly says
  that the construction as written only works for Hausdorff groupoids and
  then freezes this assumption throughout;
- physical/printed p. 23, Section 7.1 and Theorem 7.1: for a locally compact
  group acting on a locally compact Hausdorff space, the full `C*`-algebra of
  the Hausdorff transformation groupoid with its canonical Haar system is
  naturally isomorphic to the corresponding full crossed product.

### `FW-WIL07-d3.1`

- physical p. 38 / printed p. 26, Example 1.80: every character of `R` has
  the form `x |-> exp(-ixy)` and the displayed Fourier transform uses the
  `exp(-ixy)` sign;
- physical p. 54 / printed p. 42, equations (2.4)--(2.5) and footnote 2: a
  left action induces `alpha_s(f)(x)=f(s^{-1} dot x)`, and a right action is
  converted to a left action by `s dot x := x dot s^{-1}`;
- physical p. 94 / printed p. 82, Proposition 3.1: Fourier transform extends
  to `C^*(G) ~= C_0(Ghat)` for every locally compact abelian group;
- physical p. 150 / printed p. 138, equation (4.63) and Theorem 4.30: for a
  closed subgroup `H` of a locally compact group `G`, the full homogeneous-
  space crossed product is isomorphic to
  `C^*(H) tensor K(L^2(G/H,mu_G/H))` for a quotient measure satisfying
  (4.63);
- physical p. 210 / printed p. 198, Definition 7.7 and Example 7.9: the
  reduced crossed product and reduced group `C*`-algebra are defined through
  the regular representation, with `||f||_r=||lambda(f)||` in the group case;
- physical p. 211 / printed p. 199, discussion before and Theorem 7.13:
  abelian groups are amenable, and an amenable locally compact group gives
  equality of universal and reduced crossed-product norms.
