## 2. Title, thesis, and composition contract

### Recommended title

**Continuous Convolution Collapse on Indiscrete Arithmetic Orbit
Groupoids**

More explicit alternative: **Global-QC Convolution and the Hausdorff-Open
Diagnostic on Indiscrete Rational-Witt Orbits**.

### Central thesis

On the actual inherited-indiscrete rational-Witt fixed-orbit transformation
groupoid, global continuity forces every separated-valued arrow observable to
factor through real time. Under the author-defined global-QC convention this
gives a nonzero convolution `*`-algebra canonically isomorphic to `C_c(R)`;
the raw Hausdorff-open diagnostic is instead zero. A standard-circle proxy
has more test functions, but the actual algebra enters only as the proper
unit-coordinate-constant subalgebra and no completion map is proved. The
generic theorem and adversarial controls show that the algebra, unit-regular
family, and transported completions do not retain the action, period, or
stabilizer and therefore receive no arithmetic promotion.

### Contribution sentence permitted in the manuscript

> For every hash-locked rational-Witt fixed orbit with its actual inherited
> topology, we prove the exact global-QC convolution collapse, the distinct
> raw Hausdorff-open zero diagnostic, the one-sided and strict
> actual-to-standard test-function comparison, and the action-blindness of
> the resulting author-defined regular and transported completion records.

This claims the exact typed package and its application. It does not claim a
new standard groupoid C*-framework, a completion comparison with the proxy,
or novelty for the generic indiscrete-product theorem.

### Intended paper type and length

The natural format is a self-contained theoretical analysis with a compact
reproducibility supplement. Target **8,300 words** in the main text,
excluding a roughly 250-word abstract, references, hash ledgers, full Route
records, and complete CSV listings. Adjust only after the author confirms a
venue and its current word limit.

## 4. Detailed section outline and argument function

### Section 1 — Introduction

1. Start from a precise problem: common non-Hausdorff `C_c` conventions are
   not interchangeable on the actual arrow topology.
2. State the three typed records before any operator-algebra language:
   global QC, raw HOpen, and standard proxy.
3. State the primary positive theorem (`C_qc^glob ~= C_c(R)`) and the paired
   negative facts (raw HOpen zero; arithmetic information erased).
4. State the strict topology/map direction and test-level proxy image.
5. Give a short contribution list keyed to `P11-1`--`P11-10`.
6. Close with exclusions: no standard actual groupoid C*-algebra, no
   determinant, no quantization, no Route B, no packet/global promotion.

The introduction must not lead with the proxy tensor model. The actual owner
and its convention split are the paper's subject; proxy theorems are
calibration results.

### Section 2 — Source input, actual owner, and convention dictionary

1. Attribute to Deninger only the fixed-prime set/action/stabilizer input.
2. Attribute to Paper 9 the nonempty, nontrivial, inherited-indiscrete orbit
   topology.
3. Define the generic domain `G(X,alpha)=X x R` with the range-first right
   action convention.
4. Specialize only afterward to `X_{p,a}` and `G_{p,a}^act`.
5. Define open-cover quasi-compactness and ambient support closure.
6. Put `C_qc^glob`, raw `C_c^HOp`, and proxy `C_c(G_std)` in three separate
   definition boxes.
7. Define `GLOB-FIBRE-FAMILY`, `lambda_x`, `vartheta_x`, `Ind_x`,
   `C^full_glob`, and `C^red_glob` explicitly as author records.
8. Define `theta`, `beta`, `J`, and contravariant `I` with their directions.

Do not use `C^*(G_act)`, `C_r^*(G_act)`, “the Haar system of the actual
groupoid,” or “the actual regular representation.”

### Section 3 — Arrow topology and factorization through time

1. Classify opens, closed sets, subspaces, and closures.
2. Prove `K` quasi-compact iff its time projection is compact.
3. Record second countability and the exact local quasi-compact variants,
   followed immediately by the non-`T0`/no-Hausdorff-open ceiling.
4. Verify the groupoid structure and composable-pair chart directly.
5. Prove unique factorization of continuous maps to `T0` targets.
6. Compute the arrow Borel sigma-algebra and prove measurable factorization
   to countably separated targets.
7. Include the nonseparated-target negative controls as sharpness, not as
   side remarks.

The section's exit fact is that globally continuous complex-valued arrow
functions depend only on time; the function-space theorem is deferred to the
next section because its support gate is additional.

### Section 4 — Global-QC functions and author fibre convolution

1. Define `Phi(g)(x,t)=g(t)`.
2. Prove the exact support identity and both directions of the quasi-compact
   support criterion.
3. State `Phi:C_c(R)->C_qc^glob(G)` as a linear bijection.
4. Verify the `GLOB-FIBRE-FAMILY` contract on the range fibres.
5. Derive convolution and involution directly from the range-first formula.
6. Prove support control, continuity, associativity, and the `*` identities
   with the displayed absolute-integrability/Fubini records.
7. State the dense-algebra `*`-isomorphism only after those checks.

The action disappearing from the formula is a conclusion to highlight, not
an assumption to insert by rewriting the groupoid as the group `R`.

### Section 5 — Unit-regular operators and transported completions

1. Parameterize the exact source fibre with `vartheta_x`.
2. derive `lambda_x` as the inversion pushforward of `lambda^x`.
3. State the dense-domain integral before extending to `L^2` equivalence
   classes.
4. Compute the kernel `g(t-u)` and prove Young boundedness.
5. Prove multiplicativity, adjoint compatibility, and faithfulness.
6. Deduce the exact reduced norm from the named unit family.
7. Separately define the full norm by transport from the group `R`.
8. Invoke Williams only after these direct steps to identify the group
   full/reduced norms and Fourier model.
9. Restate that the completions are author-defined and action-blind.

Keep the reduced proof and full transport in different subsections. Equality
of their final abstract models does not make their definitions identical.

### Section 6 — Hausdorff-open diagnostic and strict standard proxy

1. Prove that no nonempty actual arrow open is Hausdorff.
2. Compute the raw HOpen span as zero and label it `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`.
3. Contrast it with an explicit nonzero global-QC function.
4. Present the framework-applicability table with
   `NOT_APPLICABLE`/`AUTHOR_DEFINED_DIRECT`/`APPLICABLE_PROXY_ONLY` labels.
5. Define the standard circle proxy and prove `J` is a set-groupoid
   isomorphism.
6. Prove `J` is not continuous and `J^{-1}` is continuous.
7. Define `I(f)=f o J^{-1}` at the test-function level.
8. Prove exact image `A_const` and the unit-dependent strict witness.
9. End with a visually isolated completion stop.
10. If the proxy source ladder is retained, distinguish full isomorphism,
    Morita equivalence, stable isomorphism, and unstabilized tensor models.

The section must not present HOpen zero as a failure of the direct global-QC
construction. The point is convention sensitivity under exact owner typing.

### Section 7 — Action blindness, controls, and Route consequences

1. State and prove the generic theorem for every nonempty indiscrete space
   and every right `R`-action.
2. State separately that HOpen zero requires nontrivial `X`.
3. Give exact mathematical controls: trivial action, two-orbit
   nontransitive action, arbitrary-period transitive action, and independent
   label/period changes.
4. Apply the theorem to each rational-Witt fixed orbit in a distinct
   corollary.
5. Explain the host-versus-analytic distinction: the host retains its
   action/stabilizer, while the analytic output does not.
6. Report deterministic controls as finite witnesses and give the strict
   reproduction receipt.
7. Report the seven typed Route tuples without reproducing every YAML field.
8. State `PROVES_TOO_MUCH` and the no-splice rule.

Do not interleave the generic proof with prime-specific notation. That would
obscure the strongest negative result: the theorem succeeds unchanged on
arithmetically irrelevant systems.

### Section 8 — Limitations and conclusion

1. Restate the exact contribution without changing owners.
2. List the frameworks actually audited and avoid universal nonexistence
   language.
3. State that there is no determinant, global analytic object, quantization,
   or Route-B entry.
4. State that the strict proxy map has no completion extension.
5. State the fixed-orbit-only aggregation boundary.
6. Formulate the next admissible research condition generically: a successor
   owner must be source-canonical, action-sensitive, and distinguish at
   least one trivial or nontransitive control before receiving arithmetic
   credit.

The conclusion may identify an obstruction. It may not speculate that a
particular enriched/sheaf/stack construction works without a new source and
Phase-1 gate.
