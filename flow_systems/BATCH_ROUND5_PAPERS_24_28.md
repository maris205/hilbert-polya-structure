### P27 — cocompact residual/homology control

Let `Gamma` be the fundamental group of a marked closed genus-2 hyperbolic
surface.  Define

```text
R_n = intersection of all normal N with [Gamma:N]<=n,
H_n = ker(Gamma -> H_1(Sigma;Z/n!Z)),
Gamma_n = R_n intersection H_n.
```

Finite generation and residual finiteness make the `R_n` finite-index,
characteristic, descending, and residual.  The factorial homology kernels are
also normal, finite-index, and descending, so `Gamma_n` is a closed-surface
normal residual tower.

If the integral homology vector of `g` has coordinate content `d`, its image
modulo `n!` has order

```text
n!/gcd(n!,d).
```

Since `Gamma_n` lies inside the homology kernel, that integer divides the full
quotient order.  Primitive homology (`d=1`) gives

```text
n! divides o_n(g),
T_n(g)=o_n(g) ell(g) >= n! ell(g).
```

Primitive homology forbids a proper group power, and the cyclic stabilizer of
a hyperbolic axis then justifies `T_n` as the minimal lifted-orbit period.  The
three frozen owners and eight levels produce 24 exact-integer rows with lower
bound sequence

```text

```

The executable does not enumerate `R_n` or claim the full quotient orders as
computed.  The theorem is the evidence for the asymptotic conclusion.  It
removes the cusps, principal congruence, and arithmetic lattice from the
mechanism, thereby strengthening the owner method while narrowing any
arithmetic novelty claim.
