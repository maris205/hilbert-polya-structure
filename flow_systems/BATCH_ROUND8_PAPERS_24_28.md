triangle of side `d`, the symmetric two-disk and three-disk primitive orbits

```text

T_3/3=d-sqrt(3)a,
gap=(2-sqrt(3))a>0.
```

The symmetric collision points satisfy the equal-angle reflection law.  If
the physical roof were `tau=c+u-u o sigma`, every periodic average would be
