# C137 hostile audit

## Attacks rejected

- Enlarging the rectangle while retaining a false positive separation gap.
- Changing an endpoint, corner, norm bound, Lipschitz constant, word digest, primitive count, or order-gap formula after repairing the payload hash.
- Presenting the nine-point grid as the theorem domain.
- Extending the raw primitive product beyond `|z|<1/2`.
- Relabeling A4 as a formal/unitary hint without a lift.
- Enabling Route B or inserting target-divisor, Euler-factor, or Hilbert–Pólya flags.
- Renaming a false scope key to evade field-by-field checks.
- Leaving a stale payload hash.

## Residual boundary

The package establishes a uniform source family and nothing about an external arithmetic target.  The constants are rigorous but deliberately not optimized beyond what the theorem needs.  The negative rectangle proves that positive separation cannot be inferred merely from pointwise open-disk disjointness at a tangent endpoint.
