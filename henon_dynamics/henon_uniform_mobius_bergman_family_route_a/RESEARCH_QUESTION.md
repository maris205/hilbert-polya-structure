# Research question

Can the order-sensitive Möbius–Bergman construction be promoted from one digit pair to a nontrivial compact parameter family while retaining explicit, uniform control of separation, nuclearity, trace-norm continuity, all-period traces, the primitive product, and an order-sensitive gap?

The smallest frozen test is

`R*=[3,7/2]×[6,7]`.

Success requires analytic inequalities over the whole rectangle.  Grid enumeration may audit word formulas but cannot establish uniformity.  A mandatory negative control asks why the larger proposal `[3,4]×[6,7]` fails to have positive closed-image separation.

No target spectral comparison is part of the question.
