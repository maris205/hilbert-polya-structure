# Narrative report

C132 showed that one nonlinear Möbius pair can carry intrinsic order information.  C137 asks for more: the mechanism must survive a continuous family without a parameter-dependent proof or a sampled approximation.

The rectangle `R*=[3,7/2]×[6,7]` is large enough to vary both branches yet small enough to retain strict separation.  Its worst closed-image gap is exactly `1/45`.  The same endpoint analysis exposes the important boundary: extending the first interval to `4` creates tangency at `(4,6)`, so the tempting larger rectangle has no positive uniform closed gap.

Uniform sup-modulus bounds `1/2` and `1/5` give a trace-norm ceiling `89/16`.  Resolvent differences of the branches, summed through the Bergman rank-one decomposition, give trace-norm Lipschitz constants `4` and `5/32`.  Every word retains the same quadratic-surds description as the frozen pair, hence all power traces and the primitive Fredholm product remain available at every parameter.

The decisive family-level progress is quantitative order sensitivity.  The traces for `aaabb` and `aabab` differ by `a(b-a)^2`, uniformly at least `175/8`; monotonicity of the composition-trace weight turns this into an explicit positive operator-trace gap.

Nine rational parameter points and 18,414 exact word receipts make the implementation hostile-auditable.  They do not enlarge the theorem and do not create an arithmetic target.  The outcome remains Route-A exploratory with A2, A3, and A4 failed.
