# Paper plan

**Working title:** Uniform Trace-Class Möbius–Bergman Families with Quantified Order Sensitivity

**Type:** short exact theory/release paper

**One-sentence contribution:** a compact two-parameter Möbius family retains uniform separation, trace-class continuity, all-period determinant structure, and a quantitative order-sensitive gap.

## Claims–evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| uniform gap `1/45` | monotonic corner minimization and exact negative rectangle | closed images only |
| uniform nuclear/Lipschitz bounds | rank-one Bergman majorants | deliberately coarse, sufficient constants |
| all-period trace and primitive product | quadratic matrix formula and trace-class Fredholm logarithm | raw product only for `|z|<1/2` |
| uniform order sensitivity | symbolic `a(b-a)^2` identity and derivative lower bound | two explicit non-cyclic words |
| deterministic audit | 18,414 independent receipts, 18,379 SymPy checks, replay, 41 mutations | grid is replay only |

## Structure

1. State the family-level advance and strict scope.
2. Prove uniform disk geometry and its negative control.
3. Prove trace class and trace-norm Lipschitz continuity.
4. State and justify the all-word trace and primitive product.
5. Prove the uniform order-sensitive gap.
6. Describe exact receipts and Route-A boundary.

## Figure/table phase

No standalone figure is warranted: the material is exact and is clearer as equations plus one compact receipt table.  The 28-file release contract therefore needs no `figures/` payload.  This is an explicit Phase-2 decision, not an omitted phase.

## Citation plan

No external historical or novelty claim is made.  The paper is a self-contained release note, so it uses no bibliography and avoids unverifiable citations.

## Review gates

Round 1 audits operator conventions and uniform constants.  Round 2 audits convergence, negative controls, grid/theorem separation, and forbidden-claim language.
