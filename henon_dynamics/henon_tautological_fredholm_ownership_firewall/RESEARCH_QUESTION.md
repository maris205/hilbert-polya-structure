# Research question

Does the punctured weighted channel continuation acquire meaningful operator
ownership merely because it can be written as a Fredholm determinant, and
can the source-native primitive orbit factors instead be assembled into an
ordinary trace-class operator?

The decisive comparison has three exact tests:

1. construct the channel-diagonal trace-class family and its determinant;
2. test that construction against the universal rank-one realization of an
   arbitrary holomorphic scalar;
3. assemble the literal finite cyclic orbit blocks and decide compactness and
   Schatten membership of their full direct sum.

Only an operator whose structure is independently forced by the dynamics
would survive all three tests as a genuine transfer owner.
