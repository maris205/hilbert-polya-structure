# Methodology blueprint

The project keeps scalar realization and dynamical ownership separate.

- freeze P75's channel identity and P76's punctured domain;
- use the absolute channel tail, not scalar conditional convergence, to
  prove trace-norm holomorphy of the diagonal generator;
- exponentiate in the trace ideal and apply the canonical Fredholm identity;
- prove the universal rank-one lemma on an arbitrary complex domain;
- construct one finite cyclic block directly from each physical orbit
  weight, with no damping or fitted normalization;
- calculate its characteristic determinant and singular values exactly;
- prove that singleton reflection words supply infinitely many blocks with a
  uniform singular-value floor;
- distinguish a locally finite basis-diagonal power ledger from a
  basis-independent Hilbert-space trace;
- forbid promotion to a genuine transfer operator, arithmetic trace, or
  Route B.

Computation audits finite matrices and partial channel sums but is not used
to prove trace-class convergence or noncompactness.
