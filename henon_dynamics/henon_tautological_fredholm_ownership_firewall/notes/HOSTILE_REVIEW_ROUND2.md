# Hostile review, round 2

## Remaining attack: hidden damping or an implicit transfer theorem

The cyclic block uses exactly `q^(chi_j)` on each physical edge. There is no
period normalization. Consequently the noncompactness theorem is a theorem
about the literal source construction, not about every conceivable transfer
operator. The genuine transfer-owner question remains `OPEN`.

## Remaining attack: determinant terminology

`det_F(I+K_ch)` is legitimate because `K_ch` is trace class. No ordinary
Fredholm determinant is assigned to `I-zB_q`; the perturbation `zB_q` is not
trace class for `z!=0`. The paper does not deny that `I-zB_q` may be a
Fredholm operator at selected parameters; it denies the claimed determinant.

natural-boundary theorem, weighted Lind identity, arithmetic trace, or
