# Route-B scope

Route B is not authorized.

P77 has neither a self-adjoint Hilbert--Polya operator nor a rational-prime
labeling, von-Mangoldt trace, zero-spectrum bijection, or explicit formula.
The channel-diagonal family is parameter dependent and assembled from the
known scalar logarithm. The literal source-block sum is noncompact. Neither
construction supplies the prerequisites for an arithmetic spectral claim.
