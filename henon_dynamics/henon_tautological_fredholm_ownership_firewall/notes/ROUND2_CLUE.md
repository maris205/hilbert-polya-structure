# Round-2 clue

A genuine owner must introduce compactness from the source rather than from
the finished scalar continuation. Any viable next construction should answer
all three questions before a determinant is taken:

1. What dynamical state space fixes the operator independently of `Z_ch`?
2. What source-derived regularity, anisotropy, return-time weight, or quotient
   makes singular values decay with period?
3. Why do its traces reproduce the weighted channel coefficients without
   using a canonical-basis diagonal sum of a non-trace-class operator?

Period damping inserted only to force a Schatten norm would change the
physical cyclic blocks and does not answer the problem.
