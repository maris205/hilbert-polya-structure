# Failure-mode audit

- **Scalar determinant promoted to dynamical ownership:** the universal
  rank-one lemma proves that determinant existence alone is vacuous as an
  ownership test. The channel model is explicitly labeled tautological.
- **Pointwise trace class promoted to holomorphic trace class:** the proof
  establishes locally uniform convergence in trace norm on every compact
  subset of the punctured domain.
- **Exponential loses the trace ideal:** the estimate
  `|exp(h)-1| <= exp(|h|)|h|` controls the diagonal tail locally uniformly.
- **A zero of the scalar is hidden:** the channel continuation used here is
  an exponential and hence nonvanishing on its punctured domain.
- **Wrong cyclic determinant:** cyclicity of `e_0` forces characteristic
  polynomial `lambda^n-q^S`, not merely a divisor of that polynomial.
- **Singular values confused with eigenvalues:** they are computed from
  `B*B=diag(q^(2 chi_j))`; no normality claim is made.
- **One finite block promoted to an infinite determinant:** the singleton
  family supplies a uniform singular-value floor, so the full direct sum is
  noncompact and not in any finite Schatten class.
- **Formal diagonal sum called a trace:** the graded power ledger is tied to
  the canonical block basis and is expressly not a Hilbert-space trace.
- **Regularized determinant escape:** the source sum lies in no `S_p`, so no
  finite-order Schatten determinant follows from this construction.
- **Arithmetic or Route-B promotion:** no prime labeling, explicit formula,
  self-adjoint owner, or arithmetic trace is asserted.
