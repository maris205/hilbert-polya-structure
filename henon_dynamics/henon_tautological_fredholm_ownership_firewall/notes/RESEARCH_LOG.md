# Research log

## Frozen input

P75 supplies the weighted scalar channels
`h_m=c_m 2(qz)^m/[1-(1+q^(2m))z^(2m)]`; P76 supplies the punctured fiber
domain and its boundary classification. Their proof packages, certificates,
and papers are hash locked.

## Positive construction

The local geometric tail `(q r)^m` makes `diag(h_m)` trace class on compact
subsets. Exponentiating its diagonal gives an exact Fredholm determinant.

## Universality test

The rank-one identity `det_F(I+(F-1)P)=F` works for every holomorphic scalar
`F`. Thus parameter-dependent determinant existence cannot by itself certify
dynamical ownership.

## Source test

The literal cyclic weighted shift attached to an orbit gives exactly
`1-z^n q^S`. Its singular values are its edge weights. Singleton reflection
words at every odd length prevent decay in the full block sum.

## Decision

Retain the analytic determinant as a correct but tautological realization;
retain finite orbit blocks as genuine local source structure; refute ordinary
trace-class ownership for their undamped full direct sum; leave a genuinely
source-derived transfer owner open.
