# Experiment plan

1. Evaluate absolutely convergent channel diagonals at six interior points
   on the `q=1/2,1,2` fibers and compare trace-exponential and determinant
   products through 128 channels.
2. Test the universal rank-one formula on unrelated nonvanishing
   holomorphic sample functions.
3. Build exact rational matrices `I-zB_omega` for singleton reflection words
   through odd period 15 and compare their determinants with
   `1-z^n q^(n-2)`.
4. Verify reflection symmetry, primitivity, energy `n-2`, weighted cyclic
   powers, and the `{1,q}` singular-value ledger through odd period 49.
5. Reconstruct the channel and block arguments independently without
   importing the primary certificate.
6. Run ordinary and optimized tests and reject mutations that promote the
   tautological determinant, the noncompact direct sum, arithmetic advance,
   or Route-B authorization.
