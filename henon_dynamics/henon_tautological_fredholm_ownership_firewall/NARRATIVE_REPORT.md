# Narrative report

P75 and P76 completely determined the punctured scalar continuation.  It is
therefore easy to place its logarithmic channels on the diagonal of an
operator.  The resulting generator is trace class, and exponentiation
produces a legitimate Fredholm determinant.  At first sight this seems to
close the operator gap.

The rank-one test shows why it does not.  Any nonvanishing holomorphic
function is the Fredholm determinant of a holomorphic rank-one perturbation
of the identity.  A determinant assembled after the scalar function is known
does not identify transport, periodic points, or a source-native state
space.

Literal orbit blocks do contain that missing source structure.  Each edge
weight determines a cyclic weighted shift whose finite determinant is the
correct Euler denominator; the P70 factor is its reciprocal.  But the
weights are always `1` or `q`; they do not
decay as period grows.  Primitive singleton words at every odd length expose
a uniform singular-value floor.  The infinite direct sum is bounded and
invertible onto its range, hence noncompact and outside every Schatten ideal.

P77 therefore proves a two-sided firewall: analytic determinant ownership is
available but tautological, while source-native ownership is exact blockwise
but not Fredholm-summable.
