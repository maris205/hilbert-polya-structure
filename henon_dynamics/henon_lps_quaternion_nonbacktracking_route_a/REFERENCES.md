# References

- A. Lubotzky, R. Phillips, and P. Sarnak, “Ramanujan graphs,”
  *Combinatorica* 8 (1988), 261–277.
  DOI: [10.1007/BF02126799](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02126799).
  This is the primary source for the explicit quaternionic congruence family,
  connectedness, and Ramanujan adjacency bound.
- K.-i. Hashimoto, “Zeta functions of finite graphs and representations of
  p-adic groups,” in *Automorphic Forms and Geometry of Arithmetic
  Varieties*, Advanced Studies in Pure Mathematics 15 (1989), 211–280.
  DOI: [10.1016/B978-0-12-330580-0.50015-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-330580-0.50015-X).
  This is the primary oriented-edge/zeta source.
- H. Bass, “The Ihara–Selberg zeta function of a tree lattice,”
  *International Journal of Mathematics* 3 (1992), 717–797.
  DOI: [10.1142/S0129167X92000357](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129167X92000357).
  This supplies the general determinant theorem.
- M. Kotani and T. Sunada, “Zeta Functions of Finite Graphs,”
  *Journal of Mathematical Sciences, the University of Tokyo* 7 (2000),
  7–25.  [Official article page](https://www.ms.u-tokyo.ac.jp/journal/abstract_e/jms070102_e.html).
  This source independently presents elementary oriented-line-graph proofs
  and fixes standard conventions.
- H. Davenport, *Multiplicative Number Theory*, 2nd ed., Graduate Texts in
  Mathematics 74, Springer, 1980.
  DOI: [10.1007/978-1-4757-5927-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-5927-3).
  This is the authoritative reference used for the prime number theorem in
  arithmetic progressions and the resulting natural-density statement.

No priority claim is made for these source theorems.  The C375 contribution
is their exact, convention-locked synthesis on the norm-five LPS family,
including the chamber and complete spectral-dynamics deductions.
