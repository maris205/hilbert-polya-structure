# Paper plan

- **Round 0, arithmetic graph:** freeze the six norm-five quaternions, prove
  the matrix norm and gauge lemmas, identify the LPS `PSL2/PGL2` chambers,
  vertex counts, and exact bipartiteness dichotomy.
- **Round 1, primitive dynamics:** add the oriented-edge phase space,
  Bass--Hashimoto characteristic factorization, every-iterate trace,
  Möbius primitive-cycle inversion, and exact finite-graph Ihara product.
- **Round 2, spectral and route closure:** derive the complete Hashimoto
  spectral map and Ramanujan circle, prove conditional chamber density,
  report the five full finite panels and adversarial controls, and close the
  C329 ownership and target-claim firewalls with the strict
  `A1_WEAK`/`ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY` decision.

Each round is a strict extension of one paper.  The final round contains the
complete theorem and proof, not a stitched collection of mini-papers.
Each artifact also carries an English abstract and an independently written
Chinese abstract, with separate 5--7 item keyword lists and strict
round-specific conclusion gates.
