# Limitations

- Connectedness and the Ramanujan adjacency bound are cited LPS theorems;
  the finite panels audit their implementation but do not reprove the
  automorphic input.
- A prime `q` indexes a separate finite congruence quotient.  It is not the
  time variable of one global autonomous system.
- The generic Bass/Ihara/Hashimoto mechanism is inherited from HCS-C329;
  C375 owns only the LPS-specific combined atlas.
- The Hashimoto operator is generally nonnormal.  Its exact finite spectral
  circle is not a self-adjoint Hilbert--Pólya construction.
- Conditional chamber density has no effective error term here; the finite
  imbalance `555` versus `569` below 20,000 is not interpreted statistically.
- The exact primitive ledger is internal to each finite source graph.  It
  neither transfers `q`/primality into orbit labels nor supplies a
  `p <-> gamma_p`, prime-power, `log p`, or von Mangoldt correspondence.
- Mandatory Route-A shuffled-period, random-weight, random-phase, and
  same-density random-length controls are absent at A1, so A1 is weak and the
  overall status is exploratory.  The executed wrong-residue, composite, and
  chamber-label-shuffle checks are A0 controls and do not replace them.
- No target Euler product, functional equation, divisor, zero set, counting
  law, bad-prime datum, root number, or automorphy claim is made.
