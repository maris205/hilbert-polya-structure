# Research question

Can an explicit arithmetic expander family support a complete primitive
nonbacktracking dynamics whose arithmetic construction, finite determinant,
spectral placement, and chamber distribution are all exact, without
mistaking that source-local result for a target Hilbert--Pólya bridge?

For the norm-five LPS family the source-local answer is yes.  Quaternion
reduction produces the graph rather than merely labeling it by a prime.
The Legendre symbol determines the `PSL2/PGL2` chamber, and the specialized
Bass--Hashimoto closure determines every primitive oriented cycle.  The
strict Route-A answer remains exploratory: those cycles do not inherit the
modulus as an arithmetic orbit label, an intrinsic `log p` weight, or the
mandatory phase/weight controls.  The target answer remains no: separate
congruence quotients do not become one autonomous global dynamics, and no
target analytic divisor is identified.
