# Narrative report

C375 changes the arithmetic dynamical subtype from a prime-indexed root
fiber to a congruence quotient of a quaternionic tree.  The prime modulus is
intrinsic: it changes the finite group and the Cayley quotient.  The
Legendre symbol of five does more than label a row; it decides whether the
state group is `PSL2` or `PGL2` and whether the graph is nonbipartite or
bipartite.

The oriented-edge lift then closes the dynamics exactly.  The Bass
factorization converts every adjacency eigenvalue to a quadratic pair of
Hashimoto eigenvalues.  Combined with the LPS bound, all nontrivial pairs
land on one circle of radius `sqrt(5)`.  Traces of all powers count the
closed cyclically reduced walks, and Möbius inversion extracts every prime
oriented cycle.  Thus the arithmetic construction, primitive chronology,
finite zeta, and spectral placement share one fixed normalization.

This is a meaningful A0 advance and a complete source-graph theorem, but it
does not clear strict A1.  The modulus and its primality index the graph;
they are not labels derived from individual primitive cycles.  There is no
`p <-> gamma_p`, prime-power repetition law, intrinsic `log p` or von
Mangoldt orbit weight, and the mandatory shuffled-period, random-weight,
random-phase, and same-density-length controls are absent.  The separate
congruence quotients also have no proved global determinant-class assembly,
and their graph-zeta divisors are not identified with a target arithmetic
divisor.  The result is therefore released as `ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY` with
`A1_WEAK`, not promoted into Route B.
