# Scope

The theorem covers every LPS graph `X^{5,q}` with prime `q>5`,
`q = 1 mod 4`: the explicit norm-five generators, projective gauge
independence, `PSL2/PGL2` chamber, exact vertex and edge counts,
bipartiteness, the complete Bass--Hashimoto characteristic factorization,
every-iterate trace, primitive-oriented-cycle inversion, Ihara product, and
the Ramanujan-circle placement of all nontrivial quadratic roots.

Finite evidence constructs five full groups and audits iterates through 12.
It also tabulates the chamber rule for eligible primes through 20,000.  The
finite grid is a regression certificate, not the proof of the all-`q`
source theorem.

HCS-C329 retains ownership of the generic Bass/Ihara/Hashimoto mechanism.
C375 owns its exact LPS norm-five specialization and the combined arithmetic
chamber/spectral-dynamics atlas.

This source-local exactness does not make the primitive cycles arithmetic
owners under Route-A v0.2.  They carry no derived prime label, prime-power
repetition, intrinsic `log p`/von Mangoldt weight, or completed mandatory
phase/weight controls.  Accordingly A1 is weak and the package is
exploratory.

The scope literal is `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.  Target arithmetic local
data, target Euler factors, bad-prime data, root numbers, automorphy, target
functional equations, target divisors or counting laws, target zero matches,
Route-B constructions, and Hilbert--Pólya operators are excluded.
