# Research question — HCS-C366

For the exactly engineered open XX chain with (N+1) sites and couplings

\[
J_j=\frac{\Omega}{2}\sqrt{(j+1)(N-j)},\qquad 0\le j<N,
\]

can one prove, in one convention-locked theorem, the complete single-particle
Krawtchouk spectrum and propagator, perfect endpoint transfer, the induced
mirror operation in every fermion-number sector with its otherwise easy to
lose Jordan--Wigner sign, and all zero-size, zero-coupling, time-orientation,
field, and revival boundaries, including necessary-and-sufficient full-Fock
identity conditions in a uniform field?

The source-local theorem is then evaluated under the Route-A A0--A4 rubric.
No prime or target-zero table is allowed, and no finite diagonalization is
accepted as a proof of the all-$N$ statements.
