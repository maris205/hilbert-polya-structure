# Research question

Can one explicitly classify both all original-time returns and the entire Koopman spectrum of a compact two-parameter Heisenberg nilflow, including rational slopes and operator domains, and decide whether a source reversor removes the determinant obstruction?

Answer: yes to the complete source classification, no to determinant promotion for the natural owners. Rational phases produce clean fixed tori rather than isolated periodic cycles; irrational slope has no closed orbit but retains a full noncentral Lebesgue spectrum. The source-preserving reversal exists for every gamma, yet resolvent and heat operators remain noncompact.
