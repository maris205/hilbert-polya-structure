# Limitations and stopping conditions

Lattice integers and phase denominators do not supply rational-prime ownership, so A0 fails. Irrational slope has no closed orbit and rational slope has continuous families, so a finite isolated A1 census fails. Natural unitary, resolvent and heat operators are noncompact, giving an ordinary Fredholm obstruction rather than a target A2 determinant. No target analytic completion exists here, so A3 fails.

The exact Hilbert-space realization, domains and same-clock reversor justify only A4_FORMAL_HINT. Classical Koopman theory is not a Hilbert–Polya construction. The noncompactness theorem does not rule out every conceivable relative, distributional or regularized determinant; none is constructed or promoted.

The X=0 family, nontrivial time changes, quantitative correlation rates and spectral target matching are outside scope. All-parameter results come from proof, not the declared finite grid.
