# Assumptions and conventions

The group law is (x,y,z)(x',y',z')=(x+x',y+y',z+z'+xy'); the quotient is left by the integer lattice. Haar mass is one. X=partial_x, Y=partial_y+x partial_z and Z=partial_z. The X coefficient is fixed to one; no division by a potentially zero velocity is made. beta and gamma range over all real numbers.

The physical clock t is real and unchanged. The section x=0 has roof one; its return map acts on a two-torus, whereas the time-one map acts on the full three-manifold. U_t f=f composed with phi_t=exp(itA)f and A=-iW.

The initial Weil–Brezin series is defined on Schwartz functions and extended by isometry. Each noncentral domain is C_mj H1(R); the full operator uses square-summable graph norms. Auxiliary heat time is not substituted for physical time.
