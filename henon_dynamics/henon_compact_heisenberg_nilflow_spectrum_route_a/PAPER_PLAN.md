# Substantive three-round plan

Round 0 proves the quotient convention, every closed orbit, primitive rational phase denominators, connected fixed tori, clean derivatives and strobe zeta dichotomy. It already handles p=0, negative slopes and arbitrary central drift.

Round 1 adds the complete signed-mode Weil–Brezin transform, its inverse coefficient construction, chirp self-adjoint domains, full spectral multiplicities and noncentral correlation decay.

Round 2 adds the no-Diophantine unique-ergodicity proof, the section versus time-one distinction, explicit same-clock reversal, noncompactness and infinite heat trace, exact regression and the strict source/target tuple. Each round retains the previous proof, not only a renamed abstract.
