# Complete source theorem package

For every real beta,gamma in W=X+beta Y+gamma Z on the compact left integer quotient:

1. The full flow is explicit and has no stationary point. Irrational beta has no closed orbit.
2. At beta=p/q, the invariant phase theta=gamma q+p x-q y+pq/2 classifies every least period dq. Time kq fixes exactly k connected tori; phi(d) tori have least period dq. Their return derivative is rank-one clean unipotent.
3. The continuous flow is uniquely ergodic exactly for irrational beta. The section map is uniquely ergodic there, but the whole time-one map never is.
4. The complete signed-mode Weil–Brezin decomposition gives toral pure-point and countably infinite-multiplicity Lebesgue spectrum. Domains are exactly transported H1 domains plus the toral weighted Fourier domain; no singular-continuous spectrum is missing.
5. The explicit measure-preserving J_gamma reverses the original clock, and its conjugate-pullback is antiunitary.
6. Natural unitary, resolvent and heat owners are noncompact; the positive heat operator has infinite extended trace. Ordinary global Fredholm determinants are not available from these owners.

Each statement has a self-contained proof. The work is a source-local reconstruction and synthesis of classical mechanisms, not a new target Hilbert–Polya operator or a literature-priority certificate.
