# Research narrative

The decisive difference from the earlier discrete Heisenberg models is continuous translation with a central drift parameter and a fixed original clock. A rational horizontal slope does not yield a finite orbit list: one invariant return phase creates a complete atlas of clean tori, with all least periods present. Irrational slope removes all closed orbits, without removing the noncentral continuous spectrum.

The full Fourier–Schrödinger decomposition resolves this contrast. Every signed central mode becomes a chirped momentum operator with exact transported domain. Its Lebesgue spectrum coexists with toral eigenfunctions, so relative decay and whole-flow nonmixing must not be conflated. The global section is uniquely ergodic at each irrational slope; the full time-one map nevertheless fixes a nonconstant x-character.

The source reversor is genuine and preserves the clock, but even one noncentral block proves resolvent/heat noncompactness and infinite heat trace. Thus this paper makes a substantial complete source classification while honestly stopping the target route.
