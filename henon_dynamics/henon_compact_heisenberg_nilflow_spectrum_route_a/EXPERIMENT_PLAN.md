# Claim-driven exact regression

The finite grid freezes all primitive p/q with -3<=p<=3 and 1<=q<=4, including zero and negative slope; three gamma values (-2/3,0,5/7); all reduced rational phases with denominator <=6; and x=0,1/3. It yields 1368 orbit rows. The independent checker searches every integer time 1 through 12q, separately checks negative multiples and half-integer nonreturns, and compares actual integer-lattice displacements.

A second table gives fixed-torus and primitive counts through multiplier 12. Generic flow, reversal, section, lattice-phase and chirp identities are checked symbolically. Signed modes -6 through 6 excluding zero, all residues j and three beta values give 126 block rows. Irrational nonreturns use exact sqrt(2) coefficients, not rounded irrational numbers.

No training, random seeds, floating Fourier fits, target zeros or target arithmetic data are used. Replay means two independent working directories invoking the same frozen producer source, not independent implementations or copied source trees. Infinite spectral completeness and ergodicity are proved analytically.
