# Source audit

## Imported source roles

1. Zin Arai, *On Hyperbolic Plateaus of the Hénon Map*, Experimental
   Mathematics 16 (2007), Theorem 1.2: hyperbolicity and stability on the
   plateau containing `a=6` and `a=10`.
2. Robert Devaney and Zbigniew Nitecki, *Shift Automorphisms in the Hénon
   Mapping*, CMP 67 (1979): the full-shift anchor and sign itinerary in the
   large-parameter horseshoe.
3. Jungsoo Kang, *On Reversible Maps and Symmetric Periodic Points*, ETDS 38
   (2018), DOI `10.1017/etds.2016.71`: reversible-map context only.
4. Douglas Lind and Brian Marcus, *An Introduction to Symbolic Dynamics and
   Coding*, CUP (1995), DOI `10.1017/CBO9780511626302`: standard symbolic
   background only.

## New deductions here

- equivariance of the continued full-shift conjugacy with `J` and reversal;
- the reflection-boundary projective limit;
- the orbit-averaged cylinder estimate;
- the extensive packet-Mahler pressure laws.

The source literature does not identify the two H6 Mahler slopes or provide
a prime/operator bridge. Primary URLs were checked through the Arai author
preprint, Springer/Cambridge publisher records, and Cambridge's Kang record.
