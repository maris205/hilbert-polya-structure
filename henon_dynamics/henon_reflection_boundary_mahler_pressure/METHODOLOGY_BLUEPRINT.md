1. Freeze the P62 full horseshoe and P63 integral primitive divisor.
2. Make the structural-stability conjugacy equivariant with the Hénon
   reversor; the sign itinerary then intertwines `J` with sequence reversal.
3. Parameterize every odd reflection-fixed sequence by a free half-word.
4. Remove lower periods and prove their fraction is exponentially small.
5. Identify the marked-axis projective limit as a reflected one-sided fair
   Bernoulli process.
6. Average the marked process over all cyclic shifts; only `O(r)` centers of
   a radius-`r` cylinder see the reflection pairing, proving convergence to
   the invariant fair Bernoulli measure.
7. Push both limits through the H6 coding and test `log^+|x|`.
8. Convert the marked-axis integral into the Mahler average of the monic
   integral primitive polynomial.
9. Derive the two linear extensive packet pressures.
10. Independently enumerate primitive palindromes and rebuild finite root
    diagnostics; keep numerical slope separation below theorem status.
