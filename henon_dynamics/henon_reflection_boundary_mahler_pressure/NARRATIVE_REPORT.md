# Narrative report

P63 pointed toward Bernoulli equidistribution, but the marked roots live on a
reflection axis. A palindromic word observed at its symmetry center is not a
typical two-sided Bernoulli word. P64 corrects that point before building the
extensive pressure.

The marked roots converge to a boundary process made from one iid half-word
and its reflection. Averaging each selected periodic orbit over time washes
out the symmetry center and recovers maximal entropy. The two sampling
procedures therefore have different limiting measures even though they use
the same sparse family of reversible cycles.

The marked limit is exactly what the primitive coordinate polynomial sees.
Its Mahler mass is the sum of `log^+|x|` over all marked roots, so a true
period clock produces a nonconstant linear pressure. This is a substantial
upgrade over P63's flat pressure, while remaining far from a prime trace.
