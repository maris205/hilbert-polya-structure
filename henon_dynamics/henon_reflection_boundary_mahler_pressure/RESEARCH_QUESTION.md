# Research question

Does the complete primitive mixed-axis packet admit a genuinely extensive
height pressure, and which measure controls its slope?

The question has two traps. First, ordinary Weil height is uniformly bounded
by P63. Second, the roots are sampled at a reflection axis, so their marked
empirical measure need not be the invariant maximal-entropy measure even
though the ambient system is a full two-shift.

P64 therefore asks for two separate weak-star limits: marked-axis sampling
and orbit-averaged sampling. Only after identifying both measures is the
packet Mahler pressure defined and evaluated.
