1. strict schema, types, and release passport;

3. frozen C47/C48 source hashes;
4. exact phase, norm clock, chronology policy, and complete split-prime scope;
5. independent last-nonzero quadric charts and projective direction replay;
6. generic Fano invariants, reverse singular recurrence, and exact resultant
   firewall;
7. literal six-step dictionary chronology dynamic program;
8. third-moment, Fermat--Jacobi, Chevalley--Warning, and integer Weil gates;
9. the \(\Re s>1/4\) analytic upgrade;
10. the minimal semifinite \(\tau\)-Det8 factorization;

12. scoped decisions;
13. Route-A labels and overclaim firewall.

Producer and checker intentionally differ. The producer uses first-nonzero
quadric charts, dense integer chronology arrays, and a forward normalized
singularity recurrence. The checker uses last-nonzero charts, sparse Fermat
convolutions, dictionary chronology states, and the reversed recurrence. The
checker also recomputes the exact integer resultant with fraction-free
Bareiss elimination and then verifies its rational rescaling.
