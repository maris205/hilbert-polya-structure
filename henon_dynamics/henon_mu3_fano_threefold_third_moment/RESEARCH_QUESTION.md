# Research question

For the frozen C45 normalized root, does the third chronological moment

\[
c_{p,3}=\frac1{d_p}\left(2p^{-2}\#\Phi_{p,3}^{-1}(0)-2p^3\right)
\]

remain \(O(1)\), making \(\Re s=1/3\) a geometric wall, or does the
six-variable zero fibre acquire a cohomological square-root gain?

The decisive subquestions are:

1. Can the exact ordered phase be stratified projectively without losing
   the \(\rho x_5x_0\) closing edge?
2. Is the resulting quadric split, and is the \((2,3)\) intersection smooth
   outside at most finitely many primes?
3. Which middle cohomology groups survive the Tate cancellations?
4. After the mandatory division by \(d_p=(p-1)/2\), what are their true
   sizes?
5. Does the answer move the Euler and normalized-semifinite determinant
   half-plane to \(\Re s>1/4\)?

The answer is positive: the rank-40, weight-three Fano trace is the largest
survivor and yields \(c_{p,3}=O(p^{-1/2})\).  The next wall is the generic
\(n=4\) tail, not a third-moment obstruction.
