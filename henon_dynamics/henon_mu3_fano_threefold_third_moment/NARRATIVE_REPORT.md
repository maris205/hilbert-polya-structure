# Narrative report

C48 left a clean fork: either the third normalized chronological moment
remained \(O(1)\), making \(\Re s=1/3\) a geometric wall, or its six-step
phase exposed another square-root cancellation.  C49 takes the second
branch.

The key move is not a new candidate system.  It is a larger exact view of
the same source-locked Hénon kernel.  Radial directions turn the affine
zero fibre into three projective geometries: a Fermat cubic fourfold, a
split quadric fourfold, and a genus-four Fano threefold.  Their Tate pieces
cancel explicitly.  What remains is a rank-two Fermat Jacobi trace of
weight four and a rank-forty Fano trace of weight three.

Before normalization, the Fano trace permits \(C_{p,3}=O(\sqrt p)\).  The
canonical real-cyclotomic normalization then divides by \(d_p\asymp p\),
so

\[
c_{p,3}=O(p^{-1/2}).
\]

This answers the decisive gate: there is no normalized \(O(1)\) third
moment.  The Euler germ reaches \(\Re s>1/4\), and the same normalized
semifinite operator now requires eighth-order regularization.

The result remains far from RH.  Primes still index arithmetic fibres
rather than primitive orbits of one real Hénon map; no functional equation,
Gamma factor, zero law, or self-adjoint generator appears.  Nevertheless,
this is a substantive Route-A advance: a previously unresolved analytic
wall is removed by exact chronological arithmetic geometry rather than by
fitting or averaging.

The next large gate should not repeat an \(n=4\) point-count exercise unless
it has a structural target.  The more promising choice is to identify the
rank-forty Fano motive—possibly through its intermediate Jacobian or a Prym
model—and ask whether the \(b_p\) traces assemble into a compatible global
self-dual \(L\)-object.  Failure there would be an equally useful obstruction
to a global functional equation.
