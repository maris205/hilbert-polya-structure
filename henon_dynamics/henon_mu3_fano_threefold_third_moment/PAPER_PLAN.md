# Paper plan

## Proposed title

**A Fano Threefold in the Third Chronological Moment of a Cubic Hénon
Euler Germ**

## Claim architecture

1. Freeze the \(\mu_3\)-twisted six-step phase and the normalized moment.
2. Prove the projective direction decomposition.
3. Count the split quadric fourfold.
4. Derive the Fermat cubic fourfold trace by Jacobi sums.
5. Establish smooth finite-exception \((2,3)\) fibres and present the
   all-split certificate as a separately auditable strengthening.
6. Compute the Fano genus, Hodge numbers, and Frobenius weights.
7. Derive \(C_{p,3}\), \(c_{p,3}\), and the square-root gain.
8. Prove the fourth-abscissa Euler theorem and the \(\tau\)-Det8 identity.
9. Audit Route A and isolate the intermediate-Jacobian next gate.

## Proposed sections

1. Introduction and the C48 wall.
2. The frozen six-step phase.
3. Projective radial stratification.
4. Split quadric and Fermat cubic point counts.
5. Smoothness of the \((2,3)\) intersection.
6. Fano topology and Frobenius traces.
7. Exact third-moment formula.
8. Euler convergence and normalized-semifinite determinant.
9. Route-A audit and global-motive outlook.

Appendix A should contain the recurrence elimination and leading-coefficient
audit.  Appendix B should contain the exact computational certificate and
mutation ledger.  The main text must use the finite-exception theorem unless
Appendix B has passed independent replay.

## Nonclaims to state prominently

* no continuation through \(\Re s=1/4\);
* no proof that this boundary is natural or singular;
* no global functional equation;
* no Riemann-zero match;
* no primitive-prime orbit bijection;
* no ordinary Fredholm determinant for the normalized root;
* no all-split smoothness sentence before certificate replay;
* no distribution theorem for the observed integers \(b_p\).
