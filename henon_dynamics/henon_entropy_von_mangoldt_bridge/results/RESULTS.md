# C43 results

The exact census is

\[
N_n=\operatorname{tr}(A^n)
=\varphi^n+(-\varphi^{-1})^n+i^n+(-i)^n.
\]

Möbius inversion gives exact-period marked points (E_n) and primitive
orbits (P_n=E_n/n).  The proved limits are

\[
\frac{E_n}{\vartheta(\varphi^n)}\to1,
\qquad
\frac{P_n}{\pi(\varphi^n)}\to\log\varphi.
\]

At the finite endpoint (n=32), the exact marked ratio is already close to
one; the slower primitive ratio is retained without rescaling.  This is a
positive entropy--mass bridge, not a prime labeling.
