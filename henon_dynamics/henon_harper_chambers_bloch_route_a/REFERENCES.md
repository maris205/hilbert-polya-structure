# References

- P. G. Harper, "Single Band Motion of Conduction Electrons in a Uniform
  Magnetic Field," *Proceedings of the Physical Society A* **68** (1955),
  874--878. DOI: `10.1088/0370-1298/68/10/304`.
- W. G. Chambers, "Linear-Network Model for Magnetic Breakdown in Two
  Dimensions," *Physical Review* **140** (1965), A135--A143.
  DOI: `10.1103/PhysRev.140.A135`.
- D. R. Hofstadter, "Energy Levels and Wave Functions of Bloch Electrons in
  Rational and Irrational Magnetic Fields," *Physical Review B* **14**
  (1976), 2239--2249. DOI: `10.1103/PhysRevB.14.2239`.
- J. Avron, P. H. M. van Mouche, and B. Simon, "On the Measure of the
  Spectrum for the Almost Mathieu Operator," *Communications in
  Mathematical Physics* **132** (1990), 103--118.
  DOI: `10.1007/BF02278001`.
- M. P. Lamoureux and J. A. Mingo, "On the Characteristic Polynomial of
  the Almost Mathieu Operator," *Proceedings of the American Mathematical
  Society* **135** (2007), 3205--3215.
  DOI: `10.1090/S0002-9939-07-08830-2`.  Theorem 2.5 proves the relevant
  cyclic matching-sum cancellation and Corollary 2.6 gives the even-`q`
  discriminant constant term.  Their parameter is `lambda_LM=2 lambda` in
  the convention of this package.
- S. Jitomirskaya, L. Konstantinov, and I. Krasovsky, "On the Spectrum of
  Critical Almost Mathieu Operators in the Rational Case," arXiv:2007.01005.

These sources establish the Harper/Hofstadter model and Chambers-formula
lineage.  The package reproduces the convention-specific transfer proof,
maps the one non-elementary constant-term step to its direct precedent, and
does not make a novelty or priority claim for the classical theory.
