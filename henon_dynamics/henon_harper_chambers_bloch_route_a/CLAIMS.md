# Claims

1. For every reduced `p/q`, `q>=3`, and every `lambda>0`, there is a unique
   monic degree-`q` polynomial `P_(p/q,lambda)` for which
   `det(EI-H)=P(E)-2cos(q k_x)-2lambda^q cos(q k_y)`.
2. The full two-dimensional spectrum is exactly the inverse image under
   `P` of `[-2(1+lambda^q),2(1+lambda^q)]`.
3. The algebraic edge multiset is the real zero multiset of
   `P^2-4(1+lambda^q)^2`; an edge is multiple exactly when `P'=0` there.
   Reality follows from the exact endpoint identities
   `P-C=D(E;0,0)` and `P+C=D(E;pi/q,pi/q)`, whose fibers are real
   symmetric.
4. The polynomial obeys anisotropic Aubry duality, flux reversal, and
   bipartite parity:
   `P_lambda(E)=lambda^q P_(1/lambda)(E/lambda)`,
   `P_p=P_(q-p)`, and `P(-E)=(-1)^q P(E)`.
5. For even `q`, zero is a multiple central edge, with
   `P(0)=2(-1)^(q/2)(1+lambda^q)` and `P'(0)=0`.  This explicitly rules out
   an all-gaps-open statement.  The all-`q` constant value is sourced from
   Lamoureux--Mingo, Theorem 2.5 and Corollary 2.6, with
   `lambda_LM=2 lambda`; parity then gives the derivative.
6. The accumulated boundary fibers are `P=E` at `q=1` and
   `P=E^2-2(1+lambda^2)` at `q=2`.
7. The exact cyclotomic and dense numerical ledgers are implementation
   receipts.  Transfer matrices, magnetic Fourier duality, direct integral
   theory, and the sourced cyclic-continuant result prove the all-parameter
   statements.

These are source-local Harper--Chambers claims, not claims about a target
arithmetic divisor, target zero set, or Hilbert--Polya construction.
