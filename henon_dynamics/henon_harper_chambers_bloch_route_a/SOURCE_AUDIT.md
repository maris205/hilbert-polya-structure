# Source and collision audit

## Established background

Harper introduced the magnetic one-band difference equation; Chambers
introduced the rational-flux phase-reduction formula; Hofstadter developed
the rational/irrational magnetic-band picture.  Avron--van Mouche--Simon
and later rational almost-Mathieu work use Chambers-type formulas in precise
spectral analysis.  These results are classical inputs.  No object-level or
priority novelty is asserted here.

Lamoureux and Mingo, *Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.* 135 (2007), 3205--3215,
DOI `10.1090/S0002-9939-07-08830-2`, are the direct precedent for the
remaining all-denominator step.  Their Theorem 2.5 proves that the
intermediate cyclic matching sums vanish, and Corollary 2.6 evaluates the
even-`q` discriminant at zero.  Their operator
`u+u^-1+(lambda_LM/2)(v+v^-1)` matches this package at
`lambda_LM=2 lambda`.  Thus their result, not the finite `q<=10` lane,
proves `P(0)=2(-1)^(q/2)(1+lambda^q)`; even parity gives `P'(0)=0`.

## Package-owned synthesis

The package fixes one matrix convention and reproduces, in that convention:

- the transfer-determinant identity with total phase `q k_x`;
- the exact `0,+q,-q` vertical Fourier support and both `-lambda^q`
  extreme coefficients;
- the complete two-dimensional spectral preimage;
- the real endpoint-fiber identities `P-C=D(E;0,0)` and
  `P+C=D(E;pi/q,pi/q)`, hence the algebraic multiple-edge criterion;
- the convention-specific mapping of the sourced even-denominator central
  contact;
- anisotropic Aubry duality, flux reversal, and bipartite parity;
- direct accumulated-neighbor formulas at `q=1,2`;
- exact cyclotomic and independently reconstructed dense evidence.

This is a convention-locked reconstruction plus executable atlas.  It is
not an object-level, Chambers-formula, matching-identity, or central-contact
priority claim.

## Workspace collision audit

The direct term collision is HCS-C15/HEN-O30.  That package uses one
critical Weyl/Harper block `U+U*+V+V*` at flux `1/3^m` and proves its top
eigenvalue returns to four along a Heisenberg congruence tower.  It does not
own all reduced rational fluxes, the two-phase Chambers identity,
anisotropic duality, or the band-edge atlas.  Restricting C371 to small
critical denominators or to a spectral-edge limit would collide; the frozen
all-flux theorem does not.

Other method neighbors are C293 (magnetic Grushin cylinder), C340 (Lame
Floquet spectrum), and C356 (QWZ Chern pump).  Their phase spaces and theorem
owners differ.  In particular C371 contains no Chern or transport claim.

## Evidence boundary

The 74,880 finite fibers are regression evidence, not a sampling proof.
The continuum parameter theorem follows from transfer conjugacy, elementary
Fourier support, magnetic Fourier duality, the Bloch direct integral, and
the cited Lamoureux--Mingo cyclic-continuant theorem.  The exact `q<=10`
lane checks its implementation but does not replace that all-`q` proof.
