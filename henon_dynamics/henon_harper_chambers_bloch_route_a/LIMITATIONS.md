# Limitations

- Rational flux is a source parameter.  Its cyclotomic translation algebra
  is not a rational-prime carrier and does not encode prime powers.
- The paper treats the union of all two-dimensional Bloch fibers.  A fixed
  phase rational almost-Mathieu spectrum is a different set.
- The algebraic edge criterion detects coalescing edge labels.  The package
  does not classify every individual open gap beyond the forced even-`q`
  central contact.
- No irrational-flux Cantor-spectrum, Ten-Martini, spectral-measure, or
  localization theorem is claimed.
- No Chern number, gap-labeling theorem, edge-state correspondence, or
  quantized transport is inferred from the Bloch atlas.
- `lambda=0` is recorded only as a decoupled-chain limit; reciprocal Aubry
  duality is asserted for `lambda>0`.
- Harper, Chambers, and Hofstadter theory is classical.  Lamoureux--Mingo
  directly prove the cyclic-matching cancellation and even-denominator
  constant term used here.  The package claims a convention-locked
  reconstruction and auditable implementation, not priority for those
  objects or formulas.
