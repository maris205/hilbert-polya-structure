# Research question

For the anisotropic square-lattice Harper Hamiltonian at an arbitrary
reduced rational flux, can one give one convention-complete theorem that
eliminates both Bloch phases from the characteristic polynomial, recovers
the full two-dimensional spectrum and every algebraic band-edge
multiplicity, and closes duality, flux reversal, parity, and small-cell
boundaries without falsely asserting that all gaps are open?

Yes.  The total horizontal boundary phase is frozen as `q k_x`, the
vertical phase as `q k_y`, and the determinant as `det(EI-H)`.  A transfer
trace leaves only Fourier modes `0,+q,-q`; this produces the exact Chambers
identity.  Its cosine range gives the spectrum, while magnetic Fourier
duality and lattice symmetries give the remaining identities.  Even
denominators force a central multiple edge; the all-denominator constant
term is supplied by the convention-mapped Lamoureux--Mingo
cyclic-continuant theorem rather than finite extrapolation.

The resulting theorem is a substantial source-side Bloch spectral atlas.
It does not pass Route A because rational flux is not a prime-labelled
primitive dynamics and the finite characteristic polynomial is not a
target determinant.
