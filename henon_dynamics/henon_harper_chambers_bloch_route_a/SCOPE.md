# Scope

The theorem covers every reduced rational magnetic flux, every positive
anisotropy, both Bloch quasimomenta, the full Chambers phase collapse, the
two-dimensional spectral preimage, the algebraic edge multiplicities,
Aubry duality, flux reversal, parity, and the direct `q=1,2` fibers.

Finite evidence covers all 78 reduced fractions with `3<=q<=16`, five
anisotropies, 390 parameter panels, 74,880 Bloch fibers, 825,600 fiber
eigenvalues, and 224,640 characteristic-determinant probes.  A separate
exact quotient-ring lane checks every reduced flux through `q=10` with a
symbolic anisotropy exponent.

The scope literal is `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.  Target arithmetic
local data, target Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, target
functional equations or divisors, target-zero correspondences, Route B,
and Hilbert--Polya operators are outside scope.
