# Experiment plan

1. Freeze the fiber matrix by storing the total phases `X=q k_x` and
   `Y=q k_y`; accumulate periodic neighbors so the `q=1,2` faces are not
   ambiguous.
2. Reconstruct `P_(p/q,lambda)` from the transfer trace at a reference
   vertical phase for every reduced `3<=q<=16` and five rational
   anisotropies.
3. On a `12 x 16` total-phase grid, diagonalize every Hermitian fiber,
   verify the Chambers eigen-equation and spectral bound, and compare three
   characteristic-determinant probes per fiber.
4. Independently recover each polynomial from the characteristic
   polynomial of a reference fiber; the checker must not import producer
   code.
5. In `Q[zeta_q]/Phi_q`, exhaust all reduced `q<=10` with symbolic lambda
   degree.  Verify only Fourier modes `0,+q,-q`, both extreme
   `-lambda^q` coefficients, parity, Aubry coefficient reflection, flux
   reversal, and the even central edge.  This finite lane audits, but does
   not prove, the all-`q` Lamoureux--Mingo cyclic-continuant identity.
6. Run isolated replay, repaired-hash hostile mutations, strict parser
   attacks, and a unittest smoke suite.
7. Build three substantive conditional manuscripts twice under the fixed
   epoch, then verify warnings, fonts, text, rasterization, and manifest
   closure.

Finite calculations are implementation receipts.  They are not used to
infer the all-parameter theorem.
