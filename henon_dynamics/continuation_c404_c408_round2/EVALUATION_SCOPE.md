## Required routing actually performed

The complete prior-work guide was read, followed by the six PDFs in its
listed order, then the full flow obstruction and candidate registries.
The fresh PDF read was bounded: PDF pages 1--3 of papers 1--5 and 1--4
of paper 6, covering their abstracts, initial definitions and principal
claims. This is not an independent validation of every proof in those
documents. In particular the guide's characterization of paper 6 is
not adopted as a newly verified unconditional zeta-zero theorem; none
of the present papers uses that theorem as a proof input.

The reference lessons used here are the separation of symbolic clues
from arithmetic identities, chronology from averaged transfer data,
finite spectral fitting from a target divisor, and source operators
from a same-object arithmetic compression. Relevant Hénon registry
entries C400, C401 and C403 and their source/obstruction packages were
checked for the new mathematical dependencies. Earlier candidate
evaluations and all versioned authorities remain unchanged.

## Common frozen boundaries

- All parameters are theorem parameters selected before any comparison
  to target zeros. No target-zero data, prime-clock list, von Mangoldt
  roof or fitted unfolding is used. Finite prime indices in a GCD
  identity test are not a prime table embedded in a dynamical law.
- Each source object has its own clock, domain and observable. There
  is no union of the Hénon zeta, Gram form and delta-chain operator
  into one candidate. No target determinant is silently inferred.
- The mandatory three-type A0 control panel is **INCOMPLETE**. Exact
  neighboring-parameter and simpler-parent comparisons are identified
  where relevant; other named panels were not run. Mathematical source
  proofs do not fill missing empirical control categories.
- A1's source-only analytic grade, when assigned, credits the fully
  specified finite-field primitive-cycle problem. It is not an owner
  map from all rational primes or a target-arithmetic A1 pass.
- All target A2 and A3 grades remain FAIL. No fitted/training/validation
  zero regions exist, so their metrics are NOT_TESTABLE, never zero.
  A proof of a natural boundary is not analytic continuation through it.
- Source self-adjointness is not Route-B entry. No Route-B audit,
  target root number, bad Euler factor, gamma factor, automorphy or
  Hilbert--Polya realization is claimed.

## Proves-too-much adjudication

For C404, the counting theorem is genuinely sensitive to positive
characteristic resonance; but one fixed characteristic supplies only
its own Frobenius clock. Applying a scalar suspension does not create
all rational-prime lengths. For C405, the purely singular mechanism
holds for general multiplicative-coordinate kernels with divergent
squared correlations, so it is not a selector of zeta-zero data. For
the admitted critical delta source, a continuous interval of
second coefficients and a natural self-adjoint operator do not select
an individual target divisor. These are analytic limitations of the
inference, not fabricated randomized experiment results.

Current-team proof/source reviews are internal, not human peer review.
The evaluator's generic same-family instruction is superseded only
for subtype selection by the user's authorized broad five-paper
workflow; no scientific gate is relaxed. Full missing-control flags
and the absence of target promotion remain in each YAML.
