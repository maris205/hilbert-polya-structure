not a systematic review or a global-priority certificate. The proposed result

and positive-approximant resolvent collapse. It is not the GCD kernel itself,

## Classical dependencies and limits of verification

The positive-form/closed-operator representation is classical and is also
described in Yafaev's §2.1. The proof package derives its variational convergence
directly, rather than citing an unchecked Mosco-convergence theorem or graph-core
claim. The uniform convergence theorem for measurable slowly varying functions
is a classical input associated with Bingham–Goldie–Teugels, *Regular Variation*
(1987); Hilberdink's bibliography and regular-variation setup identify that
source. The book's full text was not accessed in this turn, so no book-page or
theorem-number locator is invented. The precise version used is stated in the
proof. Prime reciprocal divergence is a classical arithmetic input, not an
inferred consequence of a finite prime computation.

This pure-mathematics check uses theorem hypotheses and actual reading scopes,
not clinical-study evidence levels, citation counts, or journal quartile scores.
ArXiv versions are author preprints and are not automatically called peer-reviewed.
Journal identity was checked where stated; no blanket venue indexing, retraction,
or conflict-of-interest clearance is claimed. There was no external-model review.

## Admission question for independent review

Does the complete theorem — pure singularity, universal positive-approximant
collapse, and the critical nonmultiplicative summability dichotomy — close a
substantial independent question beyond the identified prior results? The
strongest objection is that its proof combines short general functional-analytic
arguments with standard regular variation. A reviewer should test both exact
ownership and significance, not equate a correct proof with paper admission.

Non-author review identified Simon's classical ownership above. The author
accepted that attribution correction and checked the original source. It does
not change the arithmetic theorem or its proof; it narrows how the general
form-theoretic step may be presented. The main increment remains the explicit
arithmetic prime-tail witness and the critical nonmultiplicative dichotomy.
