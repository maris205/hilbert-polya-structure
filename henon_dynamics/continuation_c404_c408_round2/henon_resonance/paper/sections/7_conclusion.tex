\section{Conclusion and reproducibility}

The all-period resonance in \eqref{eq:H-intro} is resolved by a
coefficient-uniform leading-term recurrence in a polynomial ring.
The characteristic-$p$ factorization isolates the largest $p$-power
dividing the period, and the remaining factor contributes a nonzero
scalar. Together with the coprime leading monomials and Jacobian,
this proves an exact geometric count for every period. The same count
then gives one explicit dynamical zeta function and its natural
boundary; the analytic consequence is not a separate counting theorem.

Several questions remain outside this result: a complete treatment of
$p\mid\deg g$, nonmonic Frobenius-degree terms, independently varying
clocks, and possible presentations through other groups or finite
quotients. Nonrationality excludes a finite-dimensional
characteristic-zero determinant formula for this exact zeta, since a
finite product or quotient of polynomial determinants is rational.
It does not exclude infinite-dimensional realizations. No target
arithmetic Euler factors, zero correspondence, root numbers,
automorphy statement, or Hilbert--P\'olya realization are obtained.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The accompanying package includes the full proof contract, a bounded
primary-source audit, the exact-composition script
\texttt{exact\_checks.py}, its archived output
\texttt{exact\_results.json}, and \texttt{CHECK\_RECEIPT.md}.
The recorded successful run used Python 3.12.3 and SymPy 1.14.0.
The receipt also records the initial JSON-serialization failure and
its implementation repair; the mathematical formula was not changed
to fit that failure. Preparing this manuscript did not rerun an older
sealed numerical package. The universal proofs are all included in
the present text, independently of executable availability.
