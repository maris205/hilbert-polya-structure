Let $q=p^e$, let $K=\overline{\F}_q$, and consider
$H(x,y)=(y,y^q+g(y)-ax)$ with $a\in\F_q^*$ and
$g\in\F_q[y]$ of degree $2\le m<q$, where $p\nmid m$.
For every positive integer $n$, the coincidence scheme
$H^n(P)=(x^{q^n},y^{q^n})$ is finite and reduced, with geometric point count
\[
  N_n=\frac{(m-1)q^{2n}+(q-m)q^{2n-p^{v_p(n)}}}{q-1}.
\]
The count is independent of all lower coefficients of $g$ and of its
nonzero leading coefficient. The proof computes the unique leading term
of every iterate of the difference of two commuting linear pullback
operators; a characteristic-$p$ factorization then resolves the cancellation
at every period, including all levels of the $p$-divisibility tower.
Coprime leading monomials and a nonvanishing Jacobian convert this degree
calculation into an ordinary point count. These are precisely the fixed
counts of the single map $S=H^{-1}\circ\Phi_q$. Its dynamical zeta function
has an explicit convergent product, and every positive integer power has
$|t|=q^{-2}$ as a natural boundary for meromorphic continuation.
We distinguish this nonlinear count from established algebraic-group
mechanisms, give a direct vector-group conjugacy obstruction, and exhibit
a counterexample when the coprimality hypothesis is removed.
