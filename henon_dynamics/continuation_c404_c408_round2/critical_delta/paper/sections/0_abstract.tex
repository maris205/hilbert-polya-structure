We determine the second high-energy eigenvalue-counting coefficient for
a half-line harmonic chain with positive point interactions at
$x_n=\pi\sum_{j=1}^n j^{-1}$. If the couplings are fixed independently
of the spectral parameter and satisfy $b_n/n\to\kappa\in(0,\infty)$,
then the inclusive count obeys
$N_b(k^2)=k\log k+C(\kappa)k+o(k)$ as real $k\to\infty$.
The coefficient is a convergent regularized integral of the integrated
density of states of a periodic comparison problem with cell length
$\pi$ and coupling $\kappa$. Its derivation requires control of a
growing transition region in which finite delta transmission contributes
at order $k$. We prove a uniform finite-chain counting estimate and
a local-periodic reduction using geometric blocks, an explicit
Rayleigh-quotient comparison, and a telescoping bound on the accumulated
freezing error. The function $C$ is continuous and strictly decreasing,
with range $(2\gamma-1,\infty)$, where $\gamma$ is Euler's constant.
The formula is stable under arbitrary positive $o(n)$ perturbations
of the couplings. If $b_n/n\to\infty$, the second coefficient is
$2\gamma-1$. If $b_n/n\to0$ and $n b_n\to\infty$, we prove only
that $[N_b(k^2)-k\log k]/k\to\infty$; no general soft-regime
leading asymptotic is asserted.
