# Primary-source and ownership audit: critical harmonic delta chain

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: `BOUNDED_PRIMARY_SOURCE_AUDIT_COMPLETE`.
This is a scoped ownership comparison, not a global priority certificate.
The proof package supplies the mathematical arguments; search absence is
not used as evidence for a theorem. The proposed contribution is **one**
critical-regime paper, not separate papers for its limiting corollaries.

## 1. Frozen question and proposed increment

The nodes are `x_n=pi H_n`, the left endpoint is Dirichlet, and positive
couplings satisfy `b_n/n -> kappa in (0,infinity)`. The observable is the
inclusive eigenvalue count of the closed source quadratic form. The new
claim is the two-term formula

    N_b(k^2)=k log k+C(kappa)k+o(k),

with `C` given by a convergent regularized integral of a periodic,
per-cell Kronig--Penney IDS. The principal proof obligation is the
uniform local-periodic reduction on a growing chain. Its freezing,
phase-mesh and cutting errors must all be `o(k)`. The proof also obtains
the range and monotonicity of `C`, rate-free stability for arbitrary
positive `o(n)` perturbations, and the hard/soft endpoint statements.

Self-adjoint form realization, positive-coupling compactness criteria,
periodic dispersion, finite-codimension bracketing, and the Dirichlet
divisor law are prior machinery, not separate novelty claims.

## 2. Inspected primary sources

### S1. Egger ne Endres and Steiner (2011)

Sebastian Egger ne Endres and Frank Steiner, *An exact trace formula
and zeta functions for an infinite quantum graph with a non-standard
Weyl asymptotics*, Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical
44 (2011), 185202, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/44/18/185202),
[author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1364).

Actual access: the primary PDF, relevant introduction and sections 2--4;
not a claim to have audited all 50 preprint pages. Equation (4) fixes
constant coupling on the same harmonic chain. Theorem 2.2 proves its
positive discrete spectrum. Equations (35)--(39) give the Dirichlet
divisor count and its two leading terms. The end of section 2 defers
finite-constant-coupling high-energy asymptotics; this is a statement
about that 2011 text, not a claim of an open problem in 2026. These
inspected passages do not supply the asymptotic-linear-coupling IDS
reduction. The source owns the graph model and Dirichlet benchmark.

### S2. Kostenko and Malamud (2010)

Aleksey Kostenko and Mark Malamud, *1-D Schrödinger operators with local
point interactions on a discrete set*, Journal of Differential Equations
249 (2010), 253--304, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2010.02.011),
[author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.3542),
[institutional metadata](https://researchprofiles.tudublin.ie/en/publications/1-d-schr%C3%B6dinger-operators-with-local-point-interactions-on-a-disc-3/).

Actual access: introduction, the harmonic-spacing example 5.12, section
5.4 through Proposition 5.23, and the initial semiboundedness statements
in section 5.5 of the primary PDF. HTML access was intermittent and was
not treated as full access. Section 5.4 relates discreteness to Jacobi
operators and gives sufficient criteria for shrinking gaps. Positive
couplings in the present critical regime fall within classical
discreteness machinery. The inspected results concern realization,
semiboundedness and discreteness; they do not give the present second
counting coefficient. Our elementary tail estimate is included for
self-contained quantitative bracketing, not as a new compactness theory.

## 3. Local prior-result collision

The frozen C400 constant-coupling package is prior work in this
repository and is not modified or counted again. It proves a
`2 k log k` law at fixed positive coupling, using a soft effective
potential comparison. The present regime has `k log k` as its leading
term, and at cells `n` of order `k` the rescaled delta strength has a
finite nonzero limit. Replacing the periodic IDS by its free or
Dirichlet endpoint there would change the order-`k` term. Consequently
the proposed reduction is not substitution into C400's formula.

The earlier 2009 Endres--Steiner harmonic-graph note and the C400
source ledger were checked as lineage/context. They are not needed as
additional theorem inputs. No repeated old experiment is presented as
fresh evidence.

## 4. Search boundary and remaining uncertainty

Bounded primary-source searches covered harmonic delta-chain Weyl
asymptotics, shrinking-cell Kronig--Penney models, growing/linear
couplings and infinite quantum-graph spectral comparison. No directly
matching primary theorem was located in the inspected material.
This does not rule out a broader slowly varying periodic-operator
theorem or an unlocated prior proof. A journal submission would still
benefit from specialist literature review. No citation count, source
quota, clinical evidence grade or invented review-panel verdict is
used to replace the actual hypothesis comparison.

## 5. Claims deliberately not made

- No priority claim for the harmonic chain, its compactness, or its
  fully Dirichlet divisor law.
- No general soft-regime leading coefficient under `b_n/n ->0` alone;
  the theorem asserts divergence of the centered second coefficient,
  with the separate compactness hypothesis `n b_n ->infinity`.
- No spectral-zeta meromorphic continuation follows merely from an
  `o(k)` counting remainder.
- A mean spectral law gives no target prime factors, root numbers,
  individual Riemann-zero correspondence or Hilbert--Polya realization.
