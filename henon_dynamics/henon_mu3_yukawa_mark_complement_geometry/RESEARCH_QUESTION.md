# C71 research question

For the fixed C69 decomposition `C=D direct-sum K`, determine the finite
geometry of all complements of `D`:

1. classify the possible quotient types and indices of pairwise intersections;
2. count every type exactly from any fixed complement and hence all pairs;
3. identify the intersection and generated span of the entire family;
4. describe the common intersection using the sixteen named coordinate classes.

The primary theorem target is the image-type distribution in `Hom(K,D)`, not
a stronger classification of the abstract isomorphism types of all kernels.

Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
