# Research question

For the positive Ermakov–Pinney oscillator \(x''+\omega^2x=\kappa/x^3\),
can one close the full source-local solution, turning-point, period, action,
invariant, and singular-face atlas with exact reproducible receipts while
keeping the Route-A arithmetic boundary explicit?

**Answer.** Yes.  The quadratic Gram representation gives all positive
solutions and the radial equation gives the exact period/action formulas.  The
\(\kappa=0\) collision face is intentionally not continued through \(x=0\).
No arithmetic origin exists, so the evaluator stops at A0.
