# Experiment and verification plan

1. Generate nine exact rational rows with `c250_ep_producer.py`.
2. Reconstruct every coefficient, radial solution, energy, invariant, turning
   radius, period, and action independently in `c250_ep_checker.py`.
3. Prove ten polynomial/differential identities in SymPy.
4. Replay the producer twice and compare bytes and payload hash.
5. Mutate schema, route, scope, and numerical cells; require all mutations to
   fail the quick hash gate.
6. Compile the manuscript three times in fresh trees with the fixed epoch and
   verify fonts, text, and manifest closure.

No simulation is used as a substitute for the closed formula.  Extensions to
negative kappa, zero frequency, or an arithmetic target are outside scope.
