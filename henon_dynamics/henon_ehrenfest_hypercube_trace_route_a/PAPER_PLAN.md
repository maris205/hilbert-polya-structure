# C171 paper plan

## Claim architecture

1. Freeze the coordinate-flip Markov operator and its clock.
2. Prove complete Walsh diagonalization and exact trace/determinant formulas.
3. Derive the vertex-return identity and bipartite odd-time obstruction.
4. Prove reversible Hamming-weight lumping, symmetric similarity and simple
   Krawtchouk spectrum.
5. Separate the natural finite self-adjoint operator from a same-clock
   Hamiltonian claim.
6. Apply A0--A4 and record the failed arithmetic/global gates.

## Figure decision

No figure is used.  The decisive relation is an exact mapping from the
\(2^d\)-state Walsh multiplicity spectrum to the \((d+1)\)-state simple
Krawtchouk spectrum; equations and one compact theorem communicate this more
precisely than a schematic.  There are no experimental curves to visualize.

## Manuscript rounds

- Round 0: source theorem and proof spine.
- Round 1: add edge cases, determinant interpretation and exact validation.
- Round 2: add arithmetic controls, the same-clock A4 obstruction, bilingual
  boundary language and the full declarations block.
