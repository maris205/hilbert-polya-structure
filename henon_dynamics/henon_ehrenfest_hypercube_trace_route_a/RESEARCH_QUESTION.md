# C171 research question

For the full family of Ehrenfest coordinate-flip chains, can the exponentially
large hypercube operator be diagonalized and compressed exactly, with its
trace, determinant, return law, reversibility and self-adjoint boundary all
stated without confusing the weighted Markov traces for \(d>1\) with a
uniform deterministic Artin--Mazur zeta, while retaining the isolated
deterministic two-cycle at \(d=1\)?

## Decisive progress criterion

The paper must prove, for every \(d\geq1\), the complete Walsh spectrum with
multiplicity, exact power traces and determinant, the vertex return formula
and odd-time obstruction, and a reversible \((d+1)\)-state Hamming-weight
lumping whose simple Krawtchouk spectrum contains every distinct eigenvalue of
the \(2^d\)-state operator.

## Failure and pivot rule

A finite-dimensional diagonalization at selected \(d\), an unproved
Krawtchouk analogy, or an Artin--Mazur interpretation of weighted closed walks
would fail the criterion and force a model change.  None occurs: the complete
all-parameter proof closes.  The arithmetic gate nevertheless fails, so the
result remains a source-side theorem rather than a primary HP-dynamics
candidate.
