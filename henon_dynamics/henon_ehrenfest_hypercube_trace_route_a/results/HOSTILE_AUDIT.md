1. **Literature hallucination:** no citations or priority claims; registered
   citation population is zero.
2. **Invented experiment:** no statistical or GPU experiment is represented;
   all reported checks are deterministic and exact.
3. **Overclaim from sentinels:** the finite JSON ledger is explicitly not the
   proof; the all-\(d\) derivation is separate.
4. **Fabricated review:** no external reviewer, score or acceptance prediction
   is represented.
5. **Abstraction leakage:** for \(d>1\), weighted Markov traces are not called
   Artin--Mazur fixed-point counts; the deterministic \(d=1\) two-cycle is
   recorded separately and not promoted to a family-level interpretation.
6. **Arithmetic leakage:** randomized labels, composite dimensions,
   neighboring dimensions and lazy kernels demonstrate genericity; A0 fails.
7. **Quantization leakage:** self-adjointness is recorded, but
   \(e^{-itP_d}\) is rejected as a same-clock lift.
