# C171 narrative report

The successful step is an exact all-dimensional compression theorem.  The
Ehrenfest operator has \(2^d\) Walsh eigenvectors, but only \(d+1\) distinct
eigenvalues.  Hamming-weight lumping produces a reversible birth--death chain
of dimension \(d+1\), and Krawtchouk polynomials show that it retains those
distinct eigenvalues once each.  Thus the compression is not merely numerical
or asymptotic: it is exact for every \(d\).

The trace and determinant are also exact for all parameters, and transitivity
turns the trace into every vertex return probability.  The bipartite structure
forces all odd returns to vanish.  These are meaningful dynamical and operator
advances.

The Route-A outcome is rejected at the mandatory arithmetic entry gate while
the source theorem is retained.  For every \(d>1\), the trace-log is a
weighted closed-walk identity for a genuine Markov average rather than a
deterministic Artin--Mazur zeta.  At \(d=1\), \(P_1\) is the deterministic
two-cycle, but that isolated boundary supplies no uniform family-level
primitive-orbit or arithmetic structure.  The family has no intrinsic prime
correspondence, logarithmic prime clock or arithmetic weights, so A0 and A1
fail.  Although \(P_d\) is naturally
self-adjoint, replacing the frozen walk with \(e^{-itP_d}\) changes its clock
and weights; A4 is a formal hint only.  No target/global gate is crossed and
Route B is not authorized.
