   \(\omega(p)=\det(I-DF(p))^{-1}\).

   primitive period-two points by direct substitution.

   traces, and factor its finite determinant.
4. Reproduce every field with an independent checker and a separate SymPy
   script; rerun the producer byte-for-byte and apply hostile mutations.

   and close the artifact ledger.

coding/partition and an analytic operator owner; neither is assumed here.
