# Research question

What exact information survives when a dissipative Hénon map is pushed only
through a finite, auditable Route-A pipeline?  In particular, can rational
low-period cycles and a local Jacobian potential produce a reproducible
discrete transfer prefix without silently being promoted to a global Fredholm
construction?

C109 answers the finite question for one frozen map.  The geometric coding,
complete orbit atlas, and source-native analytic operator remain open.
