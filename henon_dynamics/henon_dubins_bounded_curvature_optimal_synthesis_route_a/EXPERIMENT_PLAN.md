# C310 experiment plan

1. Freeze 30 poses with rational coordinates, rational turning radii, and
   headings that are rational multiples of pi.
2. Evaluate all 180 word cells, including infeasible cells, four square-root
   boundaries, both three-circle boundaries, zero displacement, coincident
   poses, heading wrap, radius scaling, reflection, and examples in which
   each of the six words is a minimizer.
3. Integrate every feasible `L`, `S`, and `R` segment and record its terminal
   residual before selecting all shortest ties.
4. Independently reconstruct every formula and replay; symbolically verify
   SE(2) primitives, reflection and scaling; demand byte replay; reject
   repaired-hash semantic attacks and malformed JSON/YAML.
5. Retain three manuscript rounds and close a deterministic 27-payload
   release manifest.
