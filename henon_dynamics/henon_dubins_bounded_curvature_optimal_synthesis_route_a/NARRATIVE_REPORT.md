# C310 narrative report

The six-word slogan is not by itself a usable global theorem.  Each mixed
turn word has a different tangent discriminant, the `CCC` words have a
different cosine feasibility boundary, and degeneracies make inverse-angle
functions nonunique.  C310 fixes those gaps by declaring a single normalized
coordinate chart, a boundary convention, and an all-ties minimum.

The decisive executable check does not trust the length formulas alone.
Every feasible triple is integrated through the actual `SE(2)` primitives,
then its position and heading are compared with the requested pose.  The
30-pose ledger contains 180 candidate cells and makes all six words appear
as minimizers, while still leaving the global theorem to the analytic
six-family reduction.

The result is substantial optimal-control progress but no Route-A bridge:
the endpoints are freely chosen, the curves are controlled and generally
open, and the Pontryagin Hamiltonian is not a target spectral owner.
