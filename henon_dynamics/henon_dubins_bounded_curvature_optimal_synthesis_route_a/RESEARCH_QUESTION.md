# HCS-C310 research question

For the forward-only curvature-bounded car joining two oriented planar poses,
can the classical Dubins reduction be turned into one auditable global
synthesis that gives every feasible `CSC`/`CCC` word, its exact segment
formula, all discriminant boundaries and degeneracies, all minimizer ties,
and a direct endpoint replay?  What does that complete control theorem imply
for Route A when no arithmetic payload is imported?
