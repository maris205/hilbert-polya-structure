# C310 source and claim audit

## Historical owner

L. E. Dubins proved that a shortest forward planar curve with bounded
curvature and prescribed endpoint tangents is of `CSC` or `CCC` type; the
original owner is
[`10.2307/2372560`](https://doi.org/10.2307/2372560).  This package cites
that theorem and claims no literature priority.

## Package contribution

The paper freezes one coordinate convention and closes all six word formulas,
four square-root feasibility tests, two three-circle cosine tests, radius
scaling, reflection, zero-length pieces, coincident poses, boundary
tangencies, and exact ties.  Every feasible candidate is replayed by direct
integration of the car equations before the minimum is selected.

The 30 finite poses are regression evidence.  Globality comes from the
Dubins/Pontryagin reduction plus exhaustive evaluation of the six analytic
families.

## Firewall

An endpoint-optimal controlled arc is not an intrinsic primitive periodic
orbit.  Pontryagin's Hamiltonian is not a self-adjoint target operator.  No
target arithmetic datum, Euler factor, root number, automorphy statement,
divisor/counting law, functional equation, zero match, or Hilbert--Pólya
operator is asserted; Route B is not invoked.
