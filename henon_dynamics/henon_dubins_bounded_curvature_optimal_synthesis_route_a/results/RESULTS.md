# C310 results

The archive contains 30 target poses, 180 candidate word cells, and 151
feasible candidates, for 1,978 audited leaves.  Every feasible word is
integrated back to its prescribed pose; all six word types occur among the
retained minimizers, including genuine and degenerate ties.

The independent checker performs 2,087 checks, the symbolic lane closes 21
identity groups, isolated replay is byte exact, and all 30 repaired-hash or
parser attacks are rejected.  These are regression controls; global
optimality is inherited from the proved six-family reduction and exhaustive
analytic minimization.
