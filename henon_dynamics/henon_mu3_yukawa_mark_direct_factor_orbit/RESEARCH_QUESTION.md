# C70 research question

Let `C=D direct-sum K` be the explicit C69 decomposition.  Determine the
`Aut(C)`-orbits of:

1. direct factors `D' subset C` abstractly isomorphic to `D`;
2. ordered decompositions `C=D' direct-sum K'` of type `(D,K)`.
3. split embeddings of an abstract copy of `D` into `C`.

Prove transitivity or exhibit multiple orbits, compute all stabilizer orders
and orbit sizes exactly.  Count all subgroups abstractly isomorphic to `D` so
the split/direct-factor boundary is quantitative rather than anecdotal.

Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
