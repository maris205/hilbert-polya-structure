# Paper plan — HCS-C237

1. object and explicit parameter/boundary atlas;
2. unified matrix exponential for under-, critical-, and over-damping;
3. Lyapunov-derived Gaussian Mehler transition and Gibbs invariance;
4. Kalman bracket and stationary cross-correlation formulas;
5. spectral-abscissa rate, critical polynomial prefactor, and no-mixing faces;
6. reproducibility counts, strict Route-A tuple, and explicit nonclaims.

Revision 1 expands the boundary and covariance proof.  Revision 2 clarifies
the distinction between the critical asymptotic exponent and a pure
exponential norm estimate, and records the independent audit counts.
