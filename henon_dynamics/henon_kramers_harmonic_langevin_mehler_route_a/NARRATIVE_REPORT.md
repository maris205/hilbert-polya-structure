# Narrative report — HCS-C237

The harmonic Kramers model supplies a clean change of dynamical subtype: a
degenerate-noise, hypoelliptic Gaussian flow rather than a positive-state
diffusion or a finite-state chain.  The drift matrix has one closed formula
with trigonometric, Jordan, and hyperbolic branches.  The stationary
Lyapunov equation then turns the entire finite-time noise integral into the
single difference \(\Sigma-M_t\Sigma M_t^T\), which is independently checked
for every serialized transition row.

The strongest source-local result is the all-damping atlas: exact means,
covariances, Gibbs invariance, rank-two Kalman bracket for positive damping,
and stationary correlations.  The damping-rate curve peaks at critical
damping in the spectral-abscissa sense; the Jordan factor \(t e^{-\omega t}\)
is retained explicitly.  The noiseless Hamiltonian and zero-frequency faces
show why positivity assumptions cannot be silently extended.

Route A nevertheless stops at the arithmetic gate.  There are no intrinsic
primitive periodic orbits, prime-power repetitions, arithmetic clock, target
determinant, or natural Hilbert--Pólya lift.  The exact stochastic theorem is
useful as a control and as a reproducible dynamical package, but not as an
arithmetic construction.
