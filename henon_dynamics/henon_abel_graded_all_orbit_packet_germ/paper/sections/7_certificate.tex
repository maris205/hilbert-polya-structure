\section{Executable certificate and adversarial controls}

The proof is analytic, but every inherited interface is frozen in a compact
certificate.  Table \ref{tab:certificate} summarizes the finite checks.

The producer reconstructs the exact marked census from the four-state
matrix, builds the inversion-fixed period-four packets from cyclotomic
polynomials, and records the analytic constants.  The independent checker
does not import the producer.  It recomputes the census and constants and
independently rereads all eight hash-locked dependency files.  It then
attacks four possible promotions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item deleting the $2^m$ field-degree cost;
\item replacing $|u|<1$ by $u=1$;
\item promoting norm pushforward to injective;
\item promoting an open continuation, determinant, or operator claim.
\end{enumerate}
All four are rejected.

The finite computation does not establish Theorem \ref{thm:germ} by
extrapolation.  It verifies the constants and dependency contracts used by
the proof.  The all-period conclusion comes from Lemmas
\ref{lem:height} and \ref{lem:packet-envelope} and the exact geometric
majorant.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The certificate, independent checker, unit tests, figure generator, source
audit, and compiled PDF are included in the project directory.  No table of
rational primes or zeta zeros is consumed.

\paragraph{Data availability.}
All finite data displayed in the paper are generated from
\path{results/c51_certificate.json}.  The figure carries a machine-readable
trace file recording the certificate hash and panel claims.
