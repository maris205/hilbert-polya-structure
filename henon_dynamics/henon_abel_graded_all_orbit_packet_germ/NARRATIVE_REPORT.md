# Narrative report

## Why this paper was selected

HCS-P50 ended with an exact but finite tagged prime-ideal ledger.  Its
narrowest meaningful successor was not another finite factorization.  The
missing object was a common topology in which packets from all primitive
orbits and varying trace fields could be summed while retaining their tags.

## Strongest positive result

The universal weighted tagged divisor space solves the varying-field
topology problem.  A crude but fully source-native height estimate combines
with symbolic orbit growth and pressure decay to produce a nonempty
two-variable holomorphy domain.  This is the first all-orbit arithmetic
packet object in the P43--P51 pressure lane.

## Strongest obstruction

The auxiliary variable \(u\) is not cosmetic.  On the exact period-four
orbit, a primitive-divisor theorem forces a nonzero packet of norm at least
\(\log2\) at every index beyond twelve.  Thus setting \(u=1\) makes even the
one-orbit series diverge.

## What changed relative to P50

- finite tagged group \(\longrightarrow\) complete weighted Banach space;
- finite cutoff \(\longrightarrow\) all primitive orbits and all \(n>2\);
- algebraic norm identity \(\longrightarrow\) continuous norm-one map;
- no convergence theorem \(\longrightarrow\) explicit joint holomorphy;
- vague boundary concern \(\longrightarrow\) proved radius-one obstruction.

## Next theorem

The next research target is a source-native Abel-boundary renormalization or
Tauberian theorem.  It must preserve the orbit/index/prime-ideal tags and
produce a pressure-compatible scalar law without pretending that the raw
\(u=1\) series converges.
