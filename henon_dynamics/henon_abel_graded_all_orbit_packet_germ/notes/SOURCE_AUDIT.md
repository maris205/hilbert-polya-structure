# Source audit

## Primary sources

### Cyclic resultants

Christopher J. Hillar, *Cyclic Resultants*, Journal of Symbolic Computation
39 (2005), 653--669, DOI
[10.1016/j.jsc.2005.01.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsc.2005.01.001),
arXiv [math/0401220](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401220).

Used only for the fixed-polynomial cyclic/cyclotomic factorization context.
It does not supply the all-orbit H6 convergence theorem.

### Quadratic Lehmer--Pierce primitive divisors

Anthony Flatters, *Primitive Divisors of Some Lehmer--Pierce Sequences*,
Journal of Number Theory 129 (2009), 209--219, DOI
[10.1016/j.jnt.2008.05.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2008.05.008),
arXiv [0708.2190](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2190).

Theorem 1.4 is applied only to the positive norm-one real-quadratic unit
\(L_4=289+24\sqrt{145}\).  It supplies a primitive rational divisor for
each Lehmer--Pierce term beyond index 12.  It is not applied to the quartic
period-one or period-three multiplier fields.

### Prime orbit context

William Parry and Mark Pollicott, *An analogue of the prime number theorem
for closed orbits of Axiom A flows*, Annals of Mathematics 118 (1983),
573--591, DOI [10.2307/2006982](https://doi.org/10.2307/2006982).

This is context for pressure-normalized orbit counting.  P51 uses the
repository's already certified pressure lower bound and exact symbolic
census; it does not borrow a packet theorem from Parry--Pollicott.

## Repository theorem locks

- P31: pressure root and the lower expansion constant;

- P46: finite-free rank \(2^m\) and integral monodromy;
- P49: inversion-fixed integral cyclotomic packets;
- P50: finite tagged norm pushforward and noninjectivity.

P51 all-orbit Banach-valued theorem; that theorem is derived in this paper.
