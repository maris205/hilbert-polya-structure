# Paper plan

## Working title

**Abel-Graded All-Orbit Prime-Ideal Packet Germs for a Hénon Survivor**

## Central question

Can the finite tagged packet ledger of HCS-P50 be completed over every
primitive H6 orbit in one common topology without erasing the source field,
orbit, or cyclotomic index?

## One-paper contribution

The paper gives one positive theorem and its sharp first obstruction:

1. construct a universal weighted tagged \(\ell^1\) space;
2. prove a normally convergent two-variable all-orbit packet germ;
3. extend rational norm pushforward continuously;
4. prove that the Abel variable cannot simply be set to one.

## Proof architecture

1. Source-lock the four-state symbolic census, all-period integral fixed
   algebra, pressure lower bound, and inversion-fixed cyclotomic packet.
2. Bound all conjugate coordinates by \(1+\sqrt7\), return traces by
   \(2(3+2\sqrt7)^m\), and packet norms by an explicit
   \(2^m n(a+bm)\) envelope.
3. Combine this with at most \(3\varphi^m\) marked period-\(m\) points and
   pressure length at least \(h_*m\log J_*\).
4. Apply the Weierstrass theorem for Banach-valued series.
5. Use residue-degree weights to extend rational norm pushforward.
6. Apply Flatters' Theorem 1.4 to the period-four Lehmer--Pierce sequence and
   force one fresh rational divisor in every packet after index 12.

## Claim boundary

The germ is not called a zeta function or determinant.  No boundary value,
continuation, functional equation, zero law, von-Mangoldt identity, or
operator is inferred from normal convergence.
