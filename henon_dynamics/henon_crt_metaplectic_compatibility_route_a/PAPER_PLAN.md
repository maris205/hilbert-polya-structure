## Structure

   negative control.
2. Progress over the prior gate: explain why C131's per-level theorem did not
   already imply cross-level compatibility.
3. Generalized additive-character family: freeze all signs; prove Egorov and
   exact antiunitary reversal.
4. Exact two-factor CRT theorem: derive the inverse coefficients with
   idempotents and prove all four operator identities.
5. Multi-factor coherence: prove direct and iterated coefficients agree for
   fixed ordered leaves and state the factor-permutation nonclaim.

   under the canonical identification and display `(3,5)`.
7. Exact receipt and Route-A boundary: report deterministic evidence and state
   nonclaims.
8. Conclusion: record the closed gate and the smallest remaining question.
