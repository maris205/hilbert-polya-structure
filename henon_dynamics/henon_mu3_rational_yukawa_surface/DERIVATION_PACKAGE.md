## D1. The abstract deformation problem is unobstructed

Let \(X_0\subset \mathbf P^7_{\mathbf Q}\) be the smooth complete
intersection of type \((2,3)\) descended in HCS-C53. It has dimension five
and

\[
K_{X_0}\simeq \mathcal O_{X_0}(-3).
\]

For any smooth fivefold,

\[
T_{X_0}\simeq \Omega^4_{X_0}\otimes K_{X_0}^{-1}
             =\Omega^4_{X_0}(3).
\]

Akizuki--Nakano vanishing with
\((p,q,n)=(4,2,5)\) and the ample line bundle
\(\mathcal O_{X_0}(3)\) gives

\[
H^2(X_0,T_{X_0})=0. \tag{D.1}
\]

Thus the usual obstruction space for abstract deformations vanishes and the
Kuranishi germ is smooth. This is a useful consistency check, but the
algebraic family used in the theorem will be obtained more directly from the
Hilbert scheme.

## D4. The smooth fixed Hilbert germ and the rational four-slice

The ambient action (D.8) induces an action on \(H\). Since
\(\mathscr G/\mathbf Q\) is finite etale of order \(24\), it is linearly

\[
T_{H^{\mathscr G},[X_0]}
=H^0(N_{X_0/\mathbf P^7})^{\mathscr G}. \tag{D.9}

\[

H^0(N_{X_0/\mathbf P^7})^{\mathscr G}
\twoheadrightarrow
