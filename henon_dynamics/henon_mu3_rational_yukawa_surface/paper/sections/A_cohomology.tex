\section{Cohomology and the transverse-slice lemma}
\label{app:cohomology}

\subsection{Line-bundle cohomology}

For a \((2,3)\) complete intersection in \(\PP^7\), the homogeneous
coordinate-ring Hilbert series is
\[
 \frac{(1-t^2)(1-t^3)}{(1-t)^8}.
\]
Its first values are
\[
 h^0(\mathcal O_{X_0})=1,\quad
 h^0(\mathcal O_{X_0}(1))=8,\quad
 h^0(\mathcal O_{X_0}(2))=35,\quad
 h^0(\mathcal O_{X_0}(3))=111.                    \tag{A.1}
\]
The normal bundle therefore has \(146\) global sections.

For \(k=0,1,2,3\),
\[
 \mathcal O_{X_0}(k)
 =K_{X_0}\otimes\mathcal O_{X_0}(k+3),
\]
and the second factor is ample.  Kodaira vanishing gives
\[
 H^q(X_0,\mathcal O_{X_0}(k))=0\qquad(q>0).
 \tag{A.2}
\label{eq:A.2}
\]
In particular \(H^1(N)=0\).  Restricting the Euler sequence,
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{X_0}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{X_0}(1)^{\oplus8}
 \longrightarrow T_{\PP^7}|_{X_0}
 \longrightarrow0,                                 \tag{A.3}
\]
and using \cref{eq:A.2} gives
\[
 H^1(T_{\PP^7}|_{X_0})=0,\qquad
 h^0(T_{\PP^7}|_{X_0})=8\cdot8-1=63.              \tag{A.4}
\]

The normal sequence then has the exact segment
\[
\begin{aligned}
0\to H^0(T_{X_0})\to H^0(T_{\PP^7}|_{X_0})
\to H^0(N)\to H^1(T_{X_0})\to0.
\end{aligned}                                      \tag{A.5}
\]
The exact ideal-stabilizer calculation gives \(H^0(T_{X_0})=0\);
therefore
\[
 h^1(T_{X_0})=146-63=83.                           \tag{A.6}
\]
The order is essential: the subtraction is valid only after the
vector-field kernel has been proved to vanish.

\subsection{A rational slice}

We isolate the elementary algebraic step used in
\cref{sec:deformation}.

\begin{lemma}[Transverse rational germ]
\label{lem:rational-slice}
Let \(H/\Q\) be smooth at a rational point \(h\), let \(W\) be a finite
dimensional \(\Q\)-space, and let
\[
 \kappa:T_{H,h}\twoheadrightarrow W
\]
be a rational surjection.  If \(L\subset T_{H,h}\) is a rational complement
to \(\ker\kappa\), then there is a smooth locally closed rational germ
\((B,h)\subset(H,h)\) such that \(T_{B,h}=L\) and
\(\kappa|_{T_{B,h}}\) is an isomorphism.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let \(n=\dim H\) and \(d=\dim W\).  Smoothness supplies an \'etale map
\(\psi:(H,h)\to(\A^n,0)\).  Compose with a rational linear automorphism of
\(\A^n\) so that \(d\psi_h(L)\) is the span of the first \(d\) coordinate
vectors.  Let \(A=\A^d\times\{0\}\subset\A^n\), and take
\[
 (B,h)=\bigl(\psi^{-1}(A),h\bigr).
\]
\'Etaleness makes \(B\) smooth of dimension \(d\) and gives
\(T_{B,h}=L\).  The restriction of \(\kappa\) is an isomorphism by the
choice of \(L\).
\end{proof}

Apply the lemma with \(H=\Hilb(\PP^7)^{\Gscheme}\),
\(W=H^1(T_{X_0})^{\Gscheme}\), and
\(\kappa\) the invariant embedded Kodaira--Spencer map.  This produces
\(\Bcore\) without placing equations on a guessed linear deformation.
