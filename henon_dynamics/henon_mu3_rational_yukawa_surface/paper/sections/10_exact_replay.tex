\section{Exact replay and evidence boundary}
\label{sec:exact-replay}

The deformation, correspondence, Hodge, and geometric arguments in
\cref{sec:deformation,sec:relative,sec:cubic-surface} are written proofs.
Exact computation supplies the finite, instance-specific inputs: quotient
ranks, all-\(24\) covariance, semilinear fixed spaces, top-line reduction,
the \(20\) tensor entries, and the gradient algebra.

\subsection{Independent finite gates}

The project-local replay has four layers.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The producer reconstructs the source ring over \(K=\Q(\rho)\),
enumerates the group, computes the quotient components, and emits a
canonical certificate.
\item The checker independently rebuilds the group action, tangent
quotients, infinitesimal stabilizer, top line, Yukawa reductions, and
gradient algebra.  It does not accept producer conclusion fields as
premises.
\item A complete scalar-leaf classifier separates central semantic leaves,
independently derived leaves, and explicitly nonsemantic chronology.  Every
central leaf is rebound after both enclosing digests are recomputed.
\item A rollback-atomic runner builds and checks temporary artifacts before
grouped promotion.  Failure injections verify zero residual
\texttt{.new}/\texttt{.bak} files.  This is an exception-safety claim, not a
power-loss durability claim.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Negative claim boundary}

Computer algebra does not prove the existence of the relative family, the
horizontal VHS splitting, or the motivic implications excluded in
\cref{sec:realization}.  The result makes no claim of a literal linear
family, a four-dimensional full fixed Hilbert germ, a relative
Chow--K\"unneth decomposition, an honest Calabi--Yau threefold, a
\(\Q\)-rational cubic surface, or a motive.  The comparator branch remains
at the status in \cref{sec:comparators}.
