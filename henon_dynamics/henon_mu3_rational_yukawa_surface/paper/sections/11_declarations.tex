\section{Declarations}
\label{sec:declarations}

\paragraph{Data and code availability.}
No external dataset, numerical fit, or trained parameter is used.  The
exact producer, independent checker, mutation suite, and certificates are
maintained inside the HCS-C55 project directory.
\Cref{sec:exact-replay} records the immutable scoped code/results tuple used
for the manuscript.  The implementation commit remains a later provenance
stage and must not alter the frozen mathematics or paper.

\paragraph{Primary-source audit.}
Bibliographic metadata, theorem and section locators, and local source-copy
digests are recorded in the project-local source audit.  The comparator
search is a bounded primary-literature screen, not a systematic review or
an exhaustive priority certificate.

\paragraph{Computational boundary.}
Machine calculations certify finite exact algebra and adversarial schema
behavior.  The deformation, descent, correspondence, Hodge, smoothness, and
realization implications are written mathematical arguments.

\paragraph{Author contributions.}
The work was carried out collaboratively: conceptualization, formal
analysis, exact verification design, source control, writing, and curation.

\paragraph{Competing interests.}
The authors declare no competing interests.

\paragraph{Funding.}
No external funding is declared for this project.

\paragraph{Ethics.}
The work uses no human participants, animal subjects, or personal data.
