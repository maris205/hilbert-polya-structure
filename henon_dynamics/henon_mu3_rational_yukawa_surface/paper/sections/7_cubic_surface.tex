\section{The Yukawa cubic surface}
\label{sec:cubic-surface}

Let
\[
 S=\Q[u_0,u_1,u_2,u_3],\qquad
 J_{\nabla Y_H}
 =(\partial_{u_0}Y_H,\ldots,\partial_{u_3}Y_H).
\]
The four generators are homogeneous quadrics.  Exact Gr\"obner reduction
gives
\[
 \Hilb_{S/J_{\nabla Y_H}}(t)=(1+t)^4
 =1+4t+6t^2+4t^3+t^4.
\tag{7.1}
\label{eq:gradient-hilbert}
\]
Equivalently, the Hilbert numerator over \(S\) is
\[
 1-4t^2+6t^4-4t^6+t^8,                            \tag{7.2}
\]
and
\[
 \dim_\Q S/J_{\nabla Y_H}=16.                      \tag{7.3}
\]
The producer obtains a \(12\)-element Gr\"obner basis in degree-reverse
lexicographic order.  An independent computation reverses both the variable
and Jacobian-generator orders and obtains the same length and Hilbert
numerator.

\begin{theorem}[Smoothness and geometric irreducibility]
\label{thm:cubic-surface}
The cubic surface
\[
 S_H=V(Y_H)\subset\PP^3_\Q
\]
is smooth over \(\overline{\Q}\) and geometrically irreducible.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The finite-dimensional quotient in \cref{eq:gradient-hilbert} shows that
the homogeneous partial derivatives have no common nonzero zero over an
algebraic closure.  Hence the projective singular locus is empty and
\(S_H\) is geometrically smooth.

Suppose \(Y_H\) factored over \(\overline{\Q}\) as \(GH\), with both factors
of positive degree.  The two projective hypersurfaces \(V(G)\) and \(V(H)\)
meet in \(\PP^3_{\overline{\Q}}\).  At any intersection point,
\[
 \nabla(GH)=H\nabla G+G\nabla H=0,
\]
contradicting smoothness.  Thus \(Y_H\) is geometrically irreducible.
\end{proof}

The independent exact backend also finds a single degree-\(3\) factor over
\(\Q\), but rational factorization alone is not the proof of geometric
irreducibility.  Conversely, the theorem makes no claim that the
\(\Q\)-defined surface is \(\Q\)-rational as a variety.  The intrinsic
object is the projective pair
\[
 \bigl(\PP(T_{\Bcore,0}),\,V([Y_H])\bigr);
\]
a change of rational tangent basis acts by
\(\operatorname{PGL}_4(\Q)\), and a common trace normalization leaves its
zero locus unchanged.
