# HCS-C55 research question

## Primary question

Does the rationally descended fourth Hénon dihedral core extend over a
four-dimensional algebraic equivariant deformation germ as a locally maximal
rank-$10$ CY3-type variation, and what is its exact rational projective
Yukawa cubic?

More precisely, can one:

1. algebraize the full four-dimensional invariant abstract deformation space
   without mistaking tangent representatives for a linear equation family;
2. extend the nonconstant rational group form and its Reynolds graph
   correspondence over that germ;
3. identify the projected period-map derivative and the exact one-Tate-twist
   Hodge ledger;
4. derive the Yukawa tensor from correctly bigraded Cayley multiplication and
   the top pairing;
5. prove over $\mathbf Q$ that its zero locus is a smooth geometrically
   irreducible cubic surface; and
6. turn this tensor into a rigorous necessary obstruction for an honest
   four-modulus Calabi--Yau-threefold family?

## Answer

The release-candidate theorem and exact certificate answer all six questions.

- The algebraic base is a four-dimensional rational transverse slice in the
  smooth fixed Hilbert locus, not the full fixed locus.
- The relative correspondence is the average of the universal action graph of
  the rank-$24$ nonconstant finite etale group scheme. It is not an average
  over only its two rational points.
- Its image on $R^5f_*\mathbf Q(1)$ has rank $10$, Hodge
  ledger $(1,4,4,1)$, and immersive period map.
- The cubic is computed from

  \[
  \operatorname{Tr}_{R_{5,-6}}(y^5p_ip_jp_k),
  \]

  where $yp_i\in R_{1,0}$ is the tangent operator.
- Exact gradient algebra, not numerical sampling or factorization over
  $\mathbf Q$ alone, proves geometric smoothness and irreducibility.
- A projective-cubic mismatch kills a pointed polarized-VHS identification; a
  match proves neither the VHS nor a motive.

## Why this is an independent paper

HCS-C52 constructs the central rank-$10$ Hodge core. HCS-C53 descends the
central variety and Chow projector. HCS-C54 classifies the projective
monomial symmetry and proves that $n=4$ is one of the only ordinary
split-denominator rows. None of those papers constructs an equivariant
deformation germ, a relative sub-VHS, a period immersion, or a Yukawa cubic.
HCS-C55 studies precisely these new infinitesimal and variational objects.

## Questions deliberately left open

- Is the C54 projective monomial group the full projective automorphism group?
- Is the CY3-type VHS the $H^3$ of an honest smooth projective CY3 family?
- Is either Braun--Candelas--Davies four-parameter Yukawa tensor projectively
  equivalent to the Hénon cubic?
- Do matching Yukawa jets, connections, polarizations, monodromy, and boundary
  cones exist?
- Is there an algebraic correspondence whose compositions recover the two
  relevant motivic projectors?

No positive answer to any of these questions is an input to the C55 theorem.
