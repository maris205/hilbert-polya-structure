# C188 research question

For every irreducible rational max-plus matrix, can the critical graph be used
to give one exact, source-locked dynamical classification of normalized matrix
powers, vector and projective orbits, CSR phases, attraction cones, eigenvectors
and ultimate spans, while stating the sharp transient and reducible boundaries?

The answer is yes.  The classical cyclicity theorem identifies the minimal
ultimate matrix period with the critical cyclicity `gamma`.  The package then
derives the least-equality transient characterization, exact divisor
classification of vector periods, periodic ultimate column cones, and the
fixed-support family with unbounded transient.

The Route-A question is separate: do these exact tropical periods carry
intrinsic rational-prime semantics or identify a target divisor/operator?
They do not.  The requested verdict is therefore stopping, not promotion to
an arithmetic or Hilbert--Pólya claim.
