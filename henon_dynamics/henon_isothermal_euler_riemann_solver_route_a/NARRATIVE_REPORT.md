# Narrative report — HCS-C300

C300 takes a full-system step beyond a scalar Riemann example.  Both genuinely nonlinear characteristic families of isothermal Euler are encoded by one continuous \(C^1\) piecewise function: its logarithmic side is a rarefaction curve and its algebraic side is the compressive shock curve.  The sum of left and right copies is strictly increasing from negative to positive infinity.  Its unique root therefore gives the intermediate state for every finite positive-density datum and partitions the entire chamber into four wave patterns and their zero-wave faces.

The advance is a single complete solver, not five slices.  Exact fan and shock formulas prove wave ordering.  The mechanical entropy is strictly convex, and every nonzero shock has the explicit production

\[
 a^3\rho_0\sqrt r\,[\log r-(r-r^{-1})/2]<0,
 \qquad r>1.
\]

The logarithmic wave branch diverges at zero density, proving that finite positive-density isothermal data do not create a vacuum gap.  Vacuum input is nevertheless excluded.  The pressureless boundary is also nonuniform: fixed symmetric separating data drive \(\rho_*\) exponentially to zero, while fixed symmetric compressive data drive it to infinity.

This is a source-local PDE theorem.  A finite self-similar fan is not a recurrent primitive-orbit system, physical \(x/t\) is not an arithmetic clock, and the monotone scalar equation is not a target determinant.  All five Route-A axes fail; Route B remains locked.
