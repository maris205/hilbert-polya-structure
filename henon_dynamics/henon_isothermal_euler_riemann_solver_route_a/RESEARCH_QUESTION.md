# HCS-C300 research question

For the one-dimensional isothermal Euler system

\[
 \rho_t+(\rho u)_x=0,
 \qquad
 (\rho u)_t+(\rho u^2+a^2\rho)_x=0,
\]

solve the entire Riemann problem for \(a>0\), \(\rho_L,\rho_R>0\), and finite \(u_L,u_R\).  The desired single-paper theorem must:

1. reduce every datum to one strictly monotone scalar equation;
2. prove existence and uniqueness of a positive intermediate state;
3. give all four shock/rarefaction patterns, fan profiles, shock speeds, Lax inequalities, and wave ordering;
4. verify the convex mechanical entropy and a strict closed shock-production formula;
5. include zero waves, density scaling, and finite-data no-vacuum;
6. separate vacuum inputs and the nonuniform \(a\downarrow0\) boundary;
7. evaluate Route A without treating PDE wave data as arithmetic data.

The uniqueness claim is for the standard self-similar Lax two-wave Riemann solver.  It does not cover vacuum initial traces, the pressureless system, general multiwave interactions, or non-isothermal equations of state.
