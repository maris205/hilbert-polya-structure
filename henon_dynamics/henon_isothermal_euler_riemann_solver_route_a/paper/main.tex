\section{One scalar equation for the full solver}
Let $U=(\rho,m)$, $m=\rho u$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:euler}
 U_t+F(U)_x=0,
 \qquad F(U)=\left(m,\frac{m^2}{\rho}+a^2\rho\right),
 \qquad a>0,\quad \rho>0.
\end{equation}
The characteristic speeds are $\lambda_1=u-a$ and $\lambda_2=u+a$.
For $r,r_0>0$ define the continuous $C^1$, strictly increasing function
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f}
 f(r;r_0)=
 \begin{cases}
 a\log(r/r_0),&0<r\le r_0,\\[2pt]
 a(r-r_0)/\sqrt{rr_0},&r\ge r_0.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Complete positive-density Riemann solver]\label{thm:main}
For arbitrary left and right states $(\rho_L,u_L),(\rho_R,u_R)$ with positive
density and finite velocity, there is a unique $\rho_*>0$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root}
 f(\rho_*;\rho_L)+f(\rho_*;\rho_R)+u_R-u_L=0.
\end{equation}
The unique intermediate velocity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ustar}
 u_*=u_L-f(\rho_*;\rho_L)=u_R+f(\rho_*;\rho_R).
\end{equation}
The 1-wave from $L$ to $*$ is a rarefaction, zero wave, or Lax shock as
$\rho_*<\rho_L$, $=$, or $>$; the 2-wave from $*$ to $R$ has the analogous
classification relative to $\rho_R$.  These waves and the constant
intermediate sector form the unique self-similar Lax entropy solution.
In particular, no finite datum in this chamber produces vacuum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For fixed $r_0$, the two derivatives of $f(\cdot;r_0)$ are
$a/r$ and $a(r+r_0)/(2r\sqrt{rr_0})$, respectively.  They are positive and
meet at $a/r_0$; moreover $f$ ranges from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$.
Hence the left side of~\eqref{eq:root} has exactly one positive
zero;~\eqref{eq:ustar} is then forced.  The logarithmic branch is obtained by
integrating $\dd u=\mp a\,\dd\rho/\rho$ on the two integral curves.  Eliminating
the shock speed from the Rankine--Hugoniot equations gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hugoniot}
 (u-u_0)^2=a^2\frac{(\rho-\rho_0)^2}{\rho\rho_0}.
\end{equation}
The compressive sign in family 1 is negative and that in family 2 is positive,
which gives the second branch of~\eqref{eq:f}.  The explicit formulas and
entropy check below complete the construction.  Positivity of the unique
root proves the no-vacuum assertion.
\end{proof}

\section{The four patterns in one table}
The two comparisons with the unique root give the full atlas.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ccl}
\toprule
comparison & family & wave\\
\midrule
$\rho_*<\rho_L$ & 1 & rarefaction\\
$\rho_*>\rho_L$ & 1 & shock\\
$\rho_*<\rho_R$ & 2 & rarefaction\\
$\rho_*>\rho_R$ & 2 & shock\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Equality deletes the wave continuously.  Thus the two independent signs
produce rarefaction--rarefaction, rarefaction--shock, shock--rarefaction, and
shock--shock solutions without separate existence arguments.

For a 1-rarefaction, on $u_L-a\le\xi\le u_*-a$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:r1}
 u(\xi)=\xi+a,\qquad
 \rho(\xi)=\rho_L\exp\!\left(\frac{u_L-u(\xi)}a\right).
\end{equation}
For a 2-rarefaction, on $u_*+a\le\xi\le u_R+a$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:r2}
 u(\xi)=\xi-a,\qquad
 \rho(\xi)=\rho_R\exp\!\left(\frac{u(\xi)-u_R}a\right).
\end{equation}
If $r_L=\rho_*/\rho_L>1$, the 1-shock speed is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s1}
 s_1=u_L-a\sqrt{r_L}=u_*-\frac a{\sqrt{r_L}}.
\end{equation}
If $r_R=\rho_*/\rho_R>1$, the 2-shock speed is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s2}
 s_2=u_R+a\sqrt{r_R}=u_*+\frac a{\sqrt{r_R}}.
\end{equation}

Equations~\eqref{eq:s1}--\eqref{eq:s2} give, by direct subtraction,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lax}
 u_*-a<s_1<u_L-a,\qquad
 u_R+a<s_2<u_*+a,
\end{equation}
whenever the corresponding shock exists.  The fan speeds in
\eqref{eq:r1}--\eqref{eq:r2} increase from left to right.  Every 1-wave lies

In conservative variables a strictly convex entropy pair is

 \eta(\rho,m)=\frac{m^2}{2\rho}+a^2\rho\log\rho,

\end{equation}

 [q]-s[\eta]=a^3\rho_0\sqrt r
