# Narrative report

C255 replaces the already-owned Chaplygin-sleigh model with a different
nonholonomic rigid-body owner.  The step is not a second version of planar
sleigh scattering: the configuration is compact `SO(3)`, the conserved
energy is an angular-velocity ellipse, and permanent reduced states reconstruct
as genuine periodic attitudes.

The scaled angular equation collapses every generic energy ellipse to
`phi'=kappa cos(phi-delta)`.  That one identity closes both heteroclinics,
their orientation and endpoint exponents.  It also makes the principal-axis
degeneration transparent.

The most important audit repair is conceptual.  Saying “all generic motions
are heteroclinic” is correct only after excluding reduced equilibria.  Those
equilibria are full-flow periodic rotations; their translates are clean
families.  The final theorem records them and assigns `A1_WEAK`, not A1 fail or
an isolated-orbit pass.  The singular reduced Poisson bracket remains only a
formal operator hint.
