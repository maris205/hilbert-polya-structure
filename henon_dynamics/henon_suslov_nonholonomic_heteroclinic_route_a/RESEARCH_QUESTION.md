# Research question

For a positive-definite rigid body on `SO(3)` with the nonintegrable body
constraint `omega_3=0`, can the full positive-energy reduced dynamics be
classified without treating permanent rotations as missing endpoints?

The desired theorem must simultaneously provide:

1. the exact reduced Euler–Poincaré–Suslov equations and energy;
2. both heteroclinic components on every generic energy ellipse;
3. stable/unstable endpoint exponents;
4. the complete reconstruction of every reduced equilibrium as a clean
   periodic `SO(3)` rotation;
5. the singular Poisson bracket, invariant half-plane density and reversor;
6. the principal-axis and zero-energy boundaries.

The Route-A question is whether this continuum supplies an arithmetic
primitive ledger or target determinant.  It does not; the source theorem is
retained with only `A1_WEAK` and a formal A4 hint.
