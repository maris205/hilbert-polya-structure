\begin{abstract}
We give a complete positive-energy reduced atlas for the classical Suslov
rigid body.  Every generic energy ellipse has two explicit heteroclinics and
two permanent rotations; the latter reconstruct as clean periodic $SO(3)$
families with an exact primitive attitude period.
This source-local nonholonomic theorem has no arithmetic origin.
\end{abstract}
\section{Constraint and reduced equations}
Align the forbidden body axis with $e_3$ and rotate the allowed plane so that
\[
 I=\begin{pmatrix}I_1&0&a\\0&I_2&b\\a&b&I_3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 I_1,I_2>0,\quad I_3>\frac{a^2}{I_1}+\frac{b^2}{I_2}. \tag{1}
\]
The constraint is $\omega_3=0$, and
$I\dot\omega=(I\omega)\times\omega+\lambda e_3$.  With
$\ell=a\omega_1+b\omega_2$, the first two components are
\[
 I_1\dot\omega_1=-\ell\omega_2,\qquad
 I_2\dot\omega_2=\ell\omega_1,\qquad
 H=\frac12(I_1\omega_1^2+I_2\omega_2^2). \tag{2}
\]
Direct substitution gives $\dot H=0$.
\section{Both heteroclinic components}
Set $u=\sqrt{I_1}\omega_1$, $v=\sqrt{I_2}\omega_2$ and
$(u,v)=R(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)$, $R=\sqrt{2H}$.  For
\[
 q^2=\frac{a^2}{I_1}+\frac{b^2}{I_2},\quad
 \cos\delta=\frac{a}{\sqrt{I_1}q},\quad
 \sin\delta=\frac{b}{\sqrt{I_2}q},\quad
 \kappa=\frac{Rq}{\sqrt{I_1I_2}}, \tag{3}
\]
equations (2) reduce to $\dot\phi=\kappa\cos(\phi-\delta)$.
\begin{theorem}
If $q,H>0$, the two components complementary to $\ell=0$ are
\[
 \sin(\phi-\delta)=\tanh\kappa(t-t_0),\qquad
 \cos(\phi-\delta)=\sigma\,\operatorname{sech}\kappa(t-t_0),
 \quad \sigma=\pm1. \tag{4}
\]
Both run from the reduced-unstable permanent rotation to its antipodal
reduced-stable partner, with exponents $+\kappa$ and $-\kappa$.
\end{theorem}
The identities $\tanh^2z+\operatorname{sech}^2z=1$ and differentiation of
(4) prove the theorem.  The scaled angular deflection is $\pi$.
\section{Permanent rotations and boundaries}
At $\ell=0$, angular velocity is constant.  The full attitude therefore is
\[
 Q(t)=Q_0\exp(t\widehat\omega),\qquad
 T=\frac{2\pi}{|\omega|}\quad(\omega\ne0). \tag{5}
\]
More explicitly, for $q>0$ the unstable/stable endpoints are
\[
 \omega_{u,s}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{2H}{I_1b^2+I_2a^2}}\,(b,-a,0),\qquad
 T=\frac{2\pi\sqrt{I_1b^2+I_2a^2}}
 {\sqrt{2H}\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}. \tag{6}
\]
Thus every generic energy ellipse already has periodic full-flow rotations;
they must not be erased by the heteroclinic statement.  For each endpoint,
the distinct attitude circles are the right cosets
$SO(3)/SO(2)\simeq S^2$, hence a clean two-dimensional family; energy adds a
continuum.  These analytic periodic families justify \texttt{A1\_WEAK}, not
an isolated-orbit pass.  If $a=b=0$, every reduced state is permanent and
every nonzero full state is periodic.  If only one of $a,b$ vanishes,
(3)--(4) remain generic.  At $H=0$ the attitude is fixed.
\section{Reduced Poisson structure and reversor}

\[
 \{\omega_1,\omega_2\}=-\frac{\ell}{I_1I_2}. \tag{7}

$\ell\ne0$, division by $|\ell|$ leaves a divergence-free linear rotation,

rotation the original divergence is nonzero; consequently no positive
