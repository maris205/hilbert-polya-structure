only the principal-axis boundary—reconstructs to a periodic `SO(3)` rotation.

and why this merits only `A1_WEAK`.  It also adds exact checker, SymPy and
mutation counts, source ownership, and the full scope tuple.  All three PDFs
are distinct; the final two named PDFs are byte-identical.
