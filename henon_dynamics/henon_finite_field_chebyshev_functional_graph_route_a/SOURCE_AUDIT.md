# Source audit

## Verified primary sources

1. Claudio Qureshi and Daniel Panario, “The graph structure of Chebyshev polynomials over finite fields and applications,” *Designs, Codes and Cryptography* **87** (2019), 393–416, DOI [`10.1007/s10623-018-0545-7`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-018-0545-7).  The primary paper studies Chebyshev polynomials of arbitrary degree over finite fields, gives a structural functional-graph theorem, and explicitly retains the characteristic-two distinction.  It is the main lineage for the rational, quadratic and special components.
2. T. Alden Gassert, “Chebyshev action on finite fields,” *Discrete Mathematics* **315–316** (2014), 83–94, DOI [`10.1016/j.disc.2013.10.014`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2013.10.014).  This is the earlier prime-degree finite-field graph analysis and number-field application.  It is used as historical and special-degree context, not as authority for the arbitrary-degree theorem.

The DOI metadata, titles, authors, venues, years and page ranges were checked against the publisher landing pages.  The Qureshi–Panario author manuscript also states the arbitrary-degree and binary-field scope.  No source is cited for a claim outside that context.

## Package contribution boundary

The package gives a self-contained quotient-and-gluing proof and derives a release-oriented ledger not advertised as literary novelty: all fixed and primitive counts, finite source zeta, global tail and image filtrations, full-function characteristic factors and zero Jordan-block sizes, plus independent executable checks.  The terms “new” and “owner” refer only to workspace bookkeeping.  No literature-priority claim is made.

## Forbidden transfers

Finite-field arithmetic, cyclic covers and a source dynamical zeta do not supply a rational-prime primitive-orbit dictionary, logarithmic prime clock, arithmetic local datum, bad Euler factor, root number, automorphy, target divisor, target functional equation or Hilbert–Pólya operator.  Nothing from either reference authorizes such a transfer.
