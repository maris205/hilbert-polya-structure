# Narrative report

The identity `T_d(z+z^{-1})=z^d+z^{-d}` is often summarized as saying that Chebyshev dynamics is a power map in disguise.  That summary is insufficient for a complete finite-field functional graph.  A field element can lift to the split multiplicative torus or to the norm-one nonsplit torus; inversion identifies the two roots of its quadratic lift; and the tori meet at ramification.  Forgetting any one of these operations gives wrong counts, most visibly in characteristic two or at the `±2` branch values.

HCS-C269 keeps the quotient literal.  It starts with the two cyclic graphs of orders `q-1` and `q+1`, folds each by inversion, and glues their common order-`gcd(2,q-1)` points.  The result is canonically conjugate to the labeled Chebyshev graph on `F_q`.  Thus the construction does not merely count cycles: it retains every preimage edge and every special folded in-tree.

The integer formulas are consequences of this geometry.  Fixed points come from the union of the signed congruences `d^n a=±a` on each cyclic cover.  Burnside handles inversion, and one or two fixed branch values are subtracted because they appeared on both covers.  Prime-support splitting of `q±1` isolates the periodic cover subgroups from their nilpotent factors.  The same quotient count applied to the killed subgroups gives the exact tail filtration; applied to power images, it gives every image rank.

On the full function space, composition by `T_d` is a finite zero-one operator.  Its power rank is the image size.  Stabilized ranks identify the recurrent dimension, second differences give exact zero Jordan-block sizes, and the recurrent cycles give all root-of-unity factors of the characteristic polynomial.  This is a complete source-operator statement but only `A4_FORMAL_HINT`: the matrix is generally nonnormal, and no self-adjoint or target-spectral promotion is available.

The direct corpus is deliberately hostile to prime-only shortcuts: most cases use extension fields, three field families have characteristic two, and degrees include all ramification parities and singular power supports through ten.  The checker follows points rather than trusting the cover formulas.  Independently, it verifies primality, monicity, extension degree, polynomial irreducibility over `GF(p)`, and the reuse of one identical field model across all degrees at fixed `q`.  The agreement is an exact regression receipt; the proof remains the quotient theorem.

Compared with HCS-C264, the arithmetic engine on each cover is familiar but the owner is different.  C264 classifies an abstract finite abelian power map and its uniform product tree.  C269 classifies a nonlinear polynomial on the field line whose two power covers must be folded and ramifiedly glued; special branch trees are therefore retained instead of being falsely declared uniform.
