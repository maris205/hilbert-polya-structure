# Research question

For arbitrary prime power `q` and degree `d`, can the complete functional graph of the non-normalized Chebyshev map `T_d:F_q→F_q` be recovered from its multiplicative semiconjugacy without erasing the ramified points, and can that recovery be pushed through exact fixed, primitive, tail, image, zeta and full-function Koopman Jordan formulas?

The answer proved here is yes.  The phase space is not silently replaced by either multiplicative cover.  Instead the two cyclic groups

\[
G_-=\mathbb F_q^*,\qquad
G_+=\ker\!\left(N_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}\right)
\]

are quotiented by inversion and glued exactly along `G_-∩G_+`.  This intersection has order `gcd(2,q-1)`, producing two branch values for odd `q` and one in characteristic two.  The map `z↦z+z^{-1}` then gives a labeled graph isomorphism to `T_d` on `F_q`.

Success requires all of the following in one theorem: arbitrary prime powers rather than only primes; characteristic two; all positive degrees; explicit branch corrections; complete transient ownership; all source cycle and zeta data; and exact zero-Jordan information for the composition operator.  Finite enumeration is a regression certificate, not the proof.
