# Paper plan

**Title:** *Exchangeability, exact moments, and the Dirichlet limit of a
multicolor Pólya urn*

Sections: frozen urn and notation; exchangeable-word and count theorem;
marginals and arbitrary factorial moments; Dirichlet mixture and converse;
martingale limit; zero-reinforcement/zero-mass/single-color faces; executable
certificate; Route-A boundary.

The manuscript is proof-led and uses no decorative figure.  One compact table
separates finite counts, normalized masses, and their limiting object.  Two
primary historical references are cited with verified DOI metadata.
