# Manuscript build

`main.tex` is self-contained and compiled with LuaLaTeX under
`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1788048000`, `FORCE_SOURCE_DATE=1`, and `TZ=UTC`.  Round 0,
round 1, and round 2 are substantively distinct.  The final `main.pdf` equals
`main_round2.pdf` byte-for-byte and is reproduced by two fresh builds.
