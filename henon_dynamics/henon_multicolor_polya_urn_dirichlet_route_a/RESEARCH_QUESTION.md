# Research question

Can the classical multicolor reinforced urn be closed in one theorem from
finite ordered histories through the full count law and arbitrary factorial
moments to its infinite-time directing measure, while retaining zero
reinforcement and zero-mass faces without undefined normalization?

**Answer.** Yes.  For `c>0`, division by `c` gives active Dirichlet parameters
`alpha_i=a_i/c`.  Chronological multiplication gives an exchangeable word
law, Dirichlet integration gives the same law as an iid mixture, and the
predictive rule recovers the urn.  The normalized masses are bounded
martingales and converge almost surely and in every finite `Lp` to the active
Dirichlet directing vector.  When `c=0`, no `alpha` is introduced: the draws
are iid with probabilities `a_i/sum a_j`.
