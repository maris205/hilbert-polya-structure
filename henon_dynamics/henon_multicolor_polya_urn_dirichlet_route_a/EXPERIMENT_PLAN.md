# Experiment and verification plan

1. Freeze twelve cases spanning one through five colors, positive and zero
   reinforcement, fractional normalized parameters, and zero-mass faces.
2. Enumerate every ordered word at each case's maximum clock and independently
   aggregate by its count composition.
3. Generate every composition through one-step predictive recursion and compare
   it with the closed Dirichlet--multinomial or multinomial law.
4. Aggregate every one-color marginal and all falling-factorial moments of
   total degree at most four; compare with beta--binomial and general closed
   formulas.
5. Verify the normalized-mass martingale for every enumerated conditioning
   composition and color.  Compare ordered-word probabilities with independent
   Dirichlet monomial moments.
6. Replay canonical bytes and reject repaired-hash semantic mutations plus a
   stale-hash control.
7. Compile three substantively different fixed-epoch paper revisions, then
   require two fresh final builds, embedded fonts, clean logs, and a closed
   27-payload release ledger.

Finite enumeration is a regression oracle.  The all-`K`, all-time theorem is
proved algebraically and probabilistically in the manuscript.
