# Research question

Can a genuinely three-site variational Hénon lattice provide a reproducible
finite witness that separates longitudinal and transverse coupling effects at
low period while retaining exact symplectic structure?

The question is intentionally narrower than a global dynamical-zeta theorem.
We freeze a rational three-cycle Laplacian, certify exact algebraic identities,
and compare the period-two monodromy with the uncoupled (`kappa=0`) control.
The output is evidence for a finite Fourier-mode decomposition, not a claim of
global spectral completeness.
