# Narrative report

## Claim 1 — exact three-site variational structure

The ring potential induces a polynomial map over `Q`; its Jacobian is
symplectic, has determinant one, admits the coordinate-swap reversor, and has
the stated exact one-form primitive. Producer, checker, and SymPy provide
independent evidence.

## Claim 2 — low-period witnesses

Two synchronous fixed points and one genuine synchronous period-two orbit are
closed exactly. The period-two orbit is a witness, not a complete list.

## Claim 3 — coupling resolves transverse modes

The 3-cycle Laplacian contributes eigenvalue `3` twice. The resulting mode
traces and determinant factors reconstruct the direct 6-by-6 monodromy exactly;
the `kappa=0` control changes the trace by `138/25`.

## Limitations

No complete primitive-orbit atlas, global transfer operator, Fredholm
determinant, analytic continuation, arithmetic local factor, automorphy, or
Hilbert–Pólya operator is claimed. The package remains
`ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY`.
