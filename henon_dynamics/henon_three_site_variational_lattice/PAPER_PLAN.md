# Paper plan

**Title:** A three-site variational Hénon ring: an exact Fourier-mode
period-two witness.

1. Motivation and Route-A scope boundary.
2. Frozen three-site variational model and exact symplectic identities.
3. Fixed and period-two orbit witnesses.
4. Longitudinal/transverse Laplacian decomposition.
5. Coupled versus uncoupled finite monodromy polynomial.
6. Reproducibility protocol, limitations, and nonclaims.

Every quantitative statement is linked to
`results/c111_three_site_evidence.json` and independently checked by the
checker/SymPy scripts. No figure is required: the mode-factor table is more
informative than a visual plot for this exact certificate.
