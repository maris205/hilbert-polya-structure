\section{A Chow-level middle splitting}

We now prove that the projector is algebraic before taking any
cohomological realization.  Since \(\deg X=2\cdot3=6\),
\[
 \int_Xh^5=6.                                      \tag{4.1}
\]

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:ambient-idempotents}
The correspondences \(\pi_{2i}\) in
\eqref{eq:ambient-projectors} are mutually orthogonal idempotents in
\(\CH^5(X\times_KX)_{\Q}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For decomposable correspondences on a smooth fivefold,
\[
 (a\times b)\circ(c\times d)
 =\left(\int_Xda\right)c\times b.                  \tag{4.2}
\]
Putting \(a=h^{5-i}\), \(b=h^i\), \(c=h^{5-j}\), and \(d=h^j\),
the integral vanishes unless \(i=j\) and equals \(6\) if \(i=j\).
The normalizing factors in \eqref{eq:ambient-projectors} therefore give
\[
 \pi_{2i}\circ\pi_{2j}=\delta_{ij}\pi_{2i}.
\]
\end{proof}

It follows immediately that \(\pi_5\) is an idempotent orthogonal to
the six ambient projectors.  Weak Lefschetz identifies the nonmiddle
cohomology with the ambient powers of \(h\), so \(\pi_5\) realizes
precisely the middle cohomology.  Also
\(\pi_{2i}^t=\pi_{10-2i}\), and therefore \(\pi_5^t=\pi_5\).

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:reynolds}
The graph average \(e_G\) commutes with \(\pi_5\), satisfies
\(e_G^2=e_G=e_G^t\), and \(\pi_{\core},\pi_{\lev}\) in
\eqref{eq:middle-projectors} are mutually orthogonal self-transpose
idempotents.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Every \(g\in\Gm\) is induced by a projective linear transformation, so
\(g^*h=h\).  Thus its graph commutes with each \(\pi_{2i}\) and with
\(\pi_5\).  Moreover,
\[
 [\Gamma_g][\Gamma_{g'}]=[\Gamma_{gg'}],
 \qquad
 [\Gamma_g]^t=[\Gamma_{g^{-1}}].                  \tag{4.3}
\]
In the double sum defining \(e_G^2\), each group element occurs exactly
\(24\) times.  This proves idempotence.  Inversion permutes the group,
proving self-transposition.  Commutation with \(\pi_5\) then proves all
claims by direct expansion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This argument proves ordinary Chow projector identities modulo rational
equivalence.  It does not assert compatibility with the small diagonal,
and therefore does not invoke an MCK theorem.  This distinction is
essential because the even-ambient-dimension hypothesis in
\cite[Theorem~4.1]{Laterveer2021} is not satisfied by
\(X\subset\PP^7\).
\end{remark}
