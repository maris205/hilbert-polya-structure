\section{The graph-algebra optimum}

Let
\[
 \eps:\Q[\Gm]\longrightarrow\Q,\qquad
 \eps\left(\sum_ga_gg\right)=\sum_ga_g
\]
be the augmentation.  If \(V_{\mathrm{triv}}\) is any copy of the
trivial representation, then every \(a\in\Q[\Gm]\) acts on it by the
single scalar \(\eps(a)\).

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:optimum}]
If \(q^2=q\), then
\[
 \eps(q)^2=\eps(q),
\]
so \(\eps(q)\) is either zero or one.  The line \(H^{4,1}(X)\) is
trivial.  Requiring \(q\) to act as the identity there forces
\(\eps(q)=1\).  Equation~(5.7) shows that \(H^{3,2}(X)\) contains four
more trivial copies, and \(q\) must act as the identity on all of them.
Because \(q\) is a rational graph correspondence, the conjugate pieces
are retained as well.  Thus
\[
 \rank(qH^5(X))\ge1+4+4+1=10.
\]
The Reynolds idempotent \(e_G\) acts as the identity exactly on the
invariant subspace, whose rank is \(10\), so the bound is sharp.
\end{proof}

This obstruction is representation-theoretic, not a universal
indecomposability theorem.  In particular, it does not control
correspondences outside the image of \(\Q[\Gm]\) in
\(\CH^5(X\times_KX)_{\Q}\).  It also does not control projectors after a
coefficient extension.

\subsection{What the Hodge ledgers do not imply}

After one Tate twist, the invariant core has Hodge numbers
\[
 h^{3,0}=h^{0,3}=1,\qquad h^{2,1}=h^{1,2}=4.
\]
We call this a Calabi--Yau-threefold Hodge type only.  No threefold
realizing it is constructed.

After the C51 twist by two, the complement has Hodge types
\[
 (1,0)^{79}+(0,1)^{79}.
\]
Over \(\mathbf C\), polarizable effective weight-one Hodge structures
are related to abelian varieties up to isogeny, but descent of an
arbitrary \(G_K\)-representation to an abelian variety over \(K\) is an
extra arithmetic statement; see the discussion in
\cite[Introduction, pp.~2--3]{ACMV2020}.  Theorem~A and
Theorem~6.1 of that work give sufficient constructions under additional
geometric hypotheses, such as coniveau or a full level-one
complete-intersection packet; they do not apply automatically to our
projected summand.  We have proved neither the needed geometric
realization nor coniveau one.  Thus the notation \(A_{79}/K\) would be
unjustified here.
