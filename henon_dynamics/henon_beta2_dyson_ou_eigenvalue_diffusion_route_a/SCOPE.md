# Scope

The package treats one finite-$N$ stochastic dynamical family: beta-two
Hermitian Ornstein--Uhlenbeck eigenvalues in the open ordered chamber. It
includes the matrix covariance calculation, exact transition semigroup,
noncollision, reversible density, complete radial spectrum, sharp gap, heat
trace, and source-local oscillator conjugacy.

It does not treat beta other than two, multiple-eigenvalue initial data,
infinite-dimensional limits, edge or bulk scaling limits, target arithmetic
data, or a target spectral realization.

The immutable scope literal is `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
