# Narrative report

The dangerous point in Dyson--OU is not the reciprocal-gap shape; it is the
coefficient. A hidden factor of two changes the matrix equilibrium, scalar
Mehler variance, Vandermonde energy shift, and spectral gap at once. We avoid
that ambiguity by beginning with standard Brownian motion in the trace metric
and matrix drift $-H/2$.

Second-order Hermitian perturbation then gives unit eigenvalue noise,
unit Coulomb repulsion, and drift $-x_i/2$. The same normalization appears a
second time from the scalar OU process: its contraction is $e^{-t/2}$, and
the Vandermonde is an eigenfunction with eigenvalue $-N(N-1)/4$. This forces
the energy factor in the Doob kernel. Andréief's identity proves that the
kernel has mass one, so the transformed diffusion has no killing and cannot
reach a collision wall from the interior.

The spectral closure is stronger than a list of polynomial eigenfunctions.
Antisymmetric tensor products of the complete scalar Hermite basis form a
complete Slater basis. Dividing by the Vandermonde transfers that entire
basis to the ordered GUE space. Strict Slater indices are precisely a filled
Fermi sea plus a partition; subtracting the ground energy leaves
$-|\kappa|/2$. This proves completeness, multiplicities, exact norms, the
sharp gap, the finite partition heat trace, and the source Fredholm
determinant in one chain.

The result is a complete source theorem but a negative Route-A evaluation.
Random-matrix universality is not arithmetic origin. There are no intrinsic
primes, prime powers, isolated arithmetic periodic orbits, target Euler
factors, target analytic data, or target zeros. The free-fermion conjugacy is
therefore labeled only `A4_FORMAL_HINT`.
