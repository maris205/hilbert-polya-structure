# Assumptions and frozen conventions

1. $N\in\mathbb Z_{\geq1}$ is finite and fixed.
2. Hermitian matrices use the real trace inner product
   $\langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(AB)$.
3. Matrix Brownian motion is standard in a trace-orthonormal basis.
4. The matrix drift is exactly $-H/2$ and scalar noise variance is exactly
   one per unit time.
5. Eigenvalues are increasingly ordered in the open type-$A$ chamber.
6. The initial spectrum is simple. No entrance theorem from a collision is
   needed or claimed.
7. All spectral assertions concern $L^2$ of the ordered GUE equilibrium for
   fixed finite $N$.
8. Probabilists' Hermite polynomials are monic and satisfy
   $\operatorname{He}_n''-x\operatorname{He}_n'=-n\operatorname{He}_n$.

These choices imply contraction $r=e^{-t/2}$, scalar variance $1-e^{-t}$,
Coulomb coefficient one, density $e^{-|x|^2/2}\Delta(x)^2$, and generator
gap $1/2$.
