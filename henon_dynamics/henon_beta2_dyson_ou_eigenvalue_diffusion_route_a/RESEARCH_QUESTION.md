# Research question

In one variance convention, can the beta-two Dyson--Ornstein--Uhlenbeck
eigenvalue process be closed from its Hermitian matrix origin through its
exact chamber kernel, boundary behavior, invariant measure, and complete
$L^2$ spectrum, rather than stopping at the formal reciprocal-gap SDE?

The answer proved here is yes for every fixed finite $N$. The same analysis
also asks the Route-A question honestly: does this exact source structure
produce intrinsic prime-power data or a target determinant? The answer is no.
