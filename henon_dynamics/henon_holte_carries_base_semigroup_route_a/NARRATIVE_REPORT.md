# C194 narrative report

The carries process makes the arithmetic clock unusually concrete: one step is
one column of ordinary positional addition.  Holte's theorem nevertheless
shows that the resulting family is governed by a base semigroup rather than by
prime-specific dynamics.  Mixed-radix composition gives `P_aP_b=P_ab`, all
matrices share one eigenbasis, and the eigenvalues are the geometric list
`1,b^-1,...,b^{-(n-1)}`.  The stationary row is Eulerian and independent of the
base.

That structure closes substantially more than a finite spectral example.  It
simultaneously determines every power, trace, characteristic polynomial,
finite Markov determinant and convergence mode for all `n` and `b`.  The exact
release census contains 72 full matrices, 392 base-semigroup sentinels and 96
power-identity sentinels.  Convolution, inclusion--exclusion,
Faddeev--LeVerrier and SymPy supply genuinely different finite oracles.

The same theorem also supplies the stopping result.  Positional addition is an
intrinsic arithmetic origin, so A0 receives the weak relation allowed by the
v0.2 rubric.  But prime and composite bases satisfy the same formulas; no
rational prime labels a primitive deterministic orbit, no `log p` clock or
target arithmetic weight appears, and the frozen state space carries a Markov
kernel rather than an Artin--Mazur map.  The finite determinant has no target
divisor.  Real simple spectrum gives only a formal, noncanonical
self-adjoint-similarity hint.

Accordingly the exact result is a large theorem-level progress package and a
strict Route-A rejection at
`(A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`.
