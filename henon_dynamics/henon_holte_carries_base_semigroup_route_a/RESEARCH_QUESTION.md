# C194 research question

For the single frozen clock consisting of one independent digit column in the
base-`b` addition of `n` integers, what exact all-parameter dynamics is carried
by the input/output carry process, and how far does that structure pass the
Route-A v0.2.0 gates?

The mathematical part asks, simultaneously for every `n>=1` and `b>=2`:

1. What is the exact transition coefficient `P_b(i,j)`?
2. Does mixed-radix composition give `P_a P_b=P_ab` and therefore
   `P_b^r=P_(b^r)`?
3. What are the complete spectrum, stationary law, traces, determinant and
   convergence law?
4. Do prime and composite bases behave differently in any theorem-level way?

The Route-A part asks whether the genuine arithmetic provenance of positional
addition produces a rational-prime primitive carrier, target arithmetic
weights, a target divisor, target analytic structure or a natural target
quantization.  The preregistered answer is no beyond
`A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION` and an `A4_FORMAL_HINT`.  No Route-B question is
opened.
