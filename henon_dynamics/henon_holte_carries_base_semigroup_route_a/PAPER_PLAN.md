# C194 paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

Source-lock Holte's all-base carries semigroup in one theorem certificate,
derive its complete finite operator calculus and exact convergence identity,
then show why genuine positional arithmetic stops at A0 weak.

## Section plan

1. Freeze the carry phase space, digit-column clock and transition window.
2. State the mixed-radix semigroup and Holte's common diagonalization.
3. Derive Eulerian stationarity, trace, determinant, powers and convergence.
4. Report the independent exact regression without treating enumeration as
   proof.
5. Separate classical ownership, prime/composite control and Route-A ceilings.
