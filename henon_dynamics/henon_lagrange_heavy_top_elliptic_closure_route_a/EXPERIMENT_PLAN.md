# Exact evidence plan — HCS-C354

1. Freeze \(A,C,\gamma>0\), the Euler convention, energy \(E\), spatial-vertical momentum \(L\), and body-axis momentum \(G\).
2. Expand the effective-potential equation into the cubic \(P\), independently recover \(P(1)=-(L-G)^2\) and \(P(-1)=-(L+G)^2\), and isolate every real root with rational intervals.
3. On a rational parameter grid, recompute the cubic discriminant and validate each isolator with a separate exact Sturm implementation.
4. At five rational inclinations per row, verify the effective-potential identity, both reconstruction rates, and recovery of \(L,G\).
5. On four algebraic root templates, verify coefficient-by-coefficient that the Jacobi \(\operatorname{sn}^2\) substitution solves the cubic and that the two third-kind characteristics and prefactors have the declared signs.
6. Lock an exact interior double root and both pole-compatibility factors.
7. Require canonical JSON, strict YAML, repaired-hash hostile mutation rejection, isolated byte replay, and explicit -O/-OO refusal.

The analytic proof bears global completeness, root-chamber exhaustiveness, elliptic inversion, phase reconstruction and the closure iff. No finite grid is promoted to those claims.
