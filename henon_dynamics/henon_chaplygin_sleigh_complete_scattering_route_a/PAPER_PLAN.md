# Paper plan

## Central claim

One signed-offset theorem classifies every Chaplygin-sleigh motion, from exact
nonzero-offset heteroclinic scattering and `SE(2)` reconstruction to the
singular invariant measure and recurrent zero-offset boundary.

## Structure

1. Freeze the contact-point/blade convention and derive the reduced equations.
2. Solve every nondegenerate energy orbit and compute exact scattering.
3. Reconstruct contact position and prove the two asymptotic lines.
4. Classify equilibrium stability, Poisson form, measure and reversibility.
5. Separate `a=0`, `omega=0` and zero-energy boundaries.
6. Present independent executable evidence and strict Route-A evaluation.

No figure is necessary: the exact endpoint table and formulas carry the sign
relationships more reliably than a qualitative sketch.

## Revisions

- Round 0: convention, reduction, explicit orbit and scattering theorem.
- Round 1: reconstruction, asymptotic lines, stability, Poisson measure,
  reversor and all singular boundaries.
- Round 2: independent certificate, counterclaim firewall, source ownership,
  Route-A decision and disclosures.
