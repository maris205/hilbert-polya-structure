# Research question

For the Chaplygin sleigh on `SE(2)`, with mass `m>0`, central inertia `J>0`
and a signed longitudinal centre-of-mass offset `a in R`, can one theorem
classify every reduced orbit and reconstruct every physical motion?  The
required answer includes the explicit heteroclinic solution for `a!=0`, its
energy-independent blade-angle scattering, two asymptotic contact-point lines,
equilibrium stability, half-plane Poisson form, singular invariant measure,
reversibility, and the recurrent `a=0` boundary.

The second question is deliberately stricter: does this unusually complete
nonholonomic dynamics provide the prime carrier, target determinant and
source-native operator demanded by Route A?

## Success and evidence boundary

The theorem must retain the sign of `a` and distinguish blade angle from the
actual velocity heading when `u<0`.  Finite high-precision states may audit
formulas but may not prove a continuous theorem.  All parameter statements
therefore belong to the symbolic proof; the ledger is only a convention and
implementation sentinel.
