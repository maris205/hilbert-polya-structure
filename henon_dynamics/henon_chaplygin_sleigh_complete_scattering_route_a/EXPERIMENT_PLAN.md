differentiates the closed form and reconstructs Poisson, density and reversor
identities.  Replay must be byte exact.

corruptions; one stale-hash attack must also fail.  Release additionally needs
three distinct manuscript rounds, a fixed-epoch double LuaLaTeX build, embedded
fonts, clean log, extractable text, page-level visual inspection and a 27-file
self-excluded manifest ledger.
