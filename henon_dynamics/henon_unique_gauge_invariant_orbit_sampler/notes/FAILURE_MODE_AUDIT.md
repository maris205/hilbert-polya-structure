# Failure-mode audit

- Positivity is not assumed; signed weights do not evade uniqueness.
- Primitive cycles are required so values at the `n` orbit points are freely
  testable; lower-period aliases are excluded by `A_n`.
- Gauge invariance is exact at finite `n`, not inferred from convergence.
- The pressure is not mislabeled as full topological pressure.
- The inherited packet equidistribution is hash locked rather than reproved.
- No arithmetic or operator status is promoted.
