Title: **The Full-Dimensional Linear Cahn--Hilliard Spinodal Atlas**.

1. Define the mean-zero torus problem and state a single all-parameter
   theorem.
2. Diagonalize by Fourier shells; prove self-adjoint analytic trace-class
   evolution.
3. Derive the conserved-gradient-flow energy law and exact chamber/Morse
   atlas.
4. Prove global fastest-shell exhaustion analytically, preserve ties, and
   prove actual-support asymptotics and the recurrence obstruction.
5. Separate the constant mode and the three \(\kappa=0\) faces.
6. Report finite evidence, hostile validation, collision boundaries, Route-A
   verdict, reproducibility, and AI use without overclaiming.

Revision rounds are substantive: round 0 contains the core spectral theorem;
round 1 adds energy, global fastest-shell proof, long-time behavior, and
boundary atlas; round 2 adds digital evidence, hostile audit, collision and
scope analysis, and complete release metadata.
