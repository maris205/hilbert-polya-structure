# Research question and theorem contract

For every finite \(d\ge1\), let \(L^2_0(\mathbb T^d)\) be the mean-zero
real or complex \(L^2\) space on \(\mathbb T^d=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^d\).
For \(\kappa>0\), \(\alpha\in\mathbb R\), determine the complete linear
dynamics generated by
\[
A_{\kappa,\alpha}=-\kappa\Delta^2-\alpha\Delta,
\qquad D(A)=H^4(\mathbb T^d)\cap L^2_0.
\]

The required answer is global in \((d,\kappa,\alpha)\), not a parameter
slice. It must prove:

1. self-adjointness, analyticity, compact resolvent, and trace class for
   every positive time;
2. the full shell spectrum \(\sigma_n=\alpha n-\kappa n^2\) with lattice
   multiplicities \(r_d(n)\);
3. the energy identity, stable/critical/spinodal chambers, exact Morse index
   and kernel dimension;
4. all fastest represented shells (including ties), an analytic proof that
   no unsearched shell can win, and actual-support long-time projection;
5. absence of nonstationary periodic or recurrent states;
6. the trichotomy on the singular face \(\kappa=0\).

The claim excludes nonlinear Cahn--Hilliard saturation, coarsening, and
pattern selection. The finite evidence must only test the theorem and must
not replace its arbitrary-dimensional proof.
