# C304 hostile audit

## Mathematical attacks

- **Finite-cutoff promotion:** rejected. The proof ranges over every
  represented shell. If \(\alpha/\kappa\le1\), shell one wins by an exact
  difference factorization; otherwise all \(n\ge\alpha/\kappa\) are
  nonpositive while shell one is positive. The receipt window \(n\le12\)
  has no theorem role.
- **Tie loss:** rejected. Fastest sets and limiting projections retain every
  maximizing represented shell.
- **Wrong long-time shell:** rejected. The limit uses the actual nonzero
  Fourier support of the initial datum, not the ambient spectral bound.
- **Dimension slice:** rejected. Fourier diagonalization and quartic
  domination work for every finite \(d\); dimensions 1–6 are tests only.
- **Nonlinear promotion:** rejected. No nonlinear saturation, coarsening, or
  pattern-selection conclusion appears.
- **Singular substitution:** rejected. \(\kappa=0\) is a separate three-way
  theorem, including failure of a bounded semigroup for \(\alpha>0\).
