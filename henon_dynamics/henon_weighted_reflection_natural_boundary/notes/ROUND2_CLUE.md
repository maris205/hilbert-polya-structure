# HCS-P76 round-2 clue

The natural boundary closes the unrenormalized analytic route, but it does
not decide operator provenance.  Two constructions should be compared
directly:

1. a parameter-dependent trace-class diagonal obtained by exponentiating
   the already known scalar channels; and
2. the source-native direct sum of finite cyclic blocks whose determinants
   are the primitive orbit factors.

If the first is universal and the second noncompact, mere Fredholm
representability is not the ownership criterion the programme needs.
