# HCS-P76 source audit

External background is limited to:

1. Devaney--Nitecki, *Shift automorphisms in the H\'enon mapping*,
   Communications in Mathematical Physics 67 (1979), DOI
   `10.1007/BF01221361`, for the frozen horseshoe/full-shift interface.
2. Kim--Lee--Park, *A zeta function for flip systems*, Pacific Journal of
   Mathematics 209 (2003), DOI `10.2140/pjm.2003.209.289`, for the
   unweighted source Lind context.
3. Conway, *Functions of One Complex Variable I*, for standard local facts
   about meromorphic functions.

The weighted scalar channels, exact principal coefficients, strict radius
ladder, and natural-boundary theorem are derivations from the locked P70,
P72, and P75 repository objects.  No cited source is claimed to contain these
derivations, and no literature-priority claim is made.
