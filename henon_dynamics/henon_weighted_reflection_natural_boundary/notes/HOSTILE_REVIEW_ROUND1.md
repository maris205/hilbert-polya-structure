# Hostile review round 1

## Objection

The positive ladder does not imply a natural boundary; complex poles may
collide or leave open arcs.

## Resolution

The revision lists all `2m` roots, proves strict separation of different
channel moduli, computes a nonzero principal part at every root, and uses
the exact mesh gap `pi/m`.  Hence every boundary point is approached by
genuine essential singularities.

## Remaining scope

This does not address a function modified by an all-channel counterterm.
