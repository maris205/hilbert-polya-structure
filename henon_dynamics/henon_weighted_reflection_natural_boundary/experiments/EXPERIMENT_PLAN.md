# HCS-P76 experiment plan

The computation is a finite certificate for exact all-channel theorems.

1. Cross-check the divisor-sum and Euler-product definitions of `c_m`.
2. Record 40 channels on each of `q=1/2,1,2` with high-precision radii.
3. Verify all declared complex roots and their mesh gaps.
4. Independently reconstruct 64 channels on each of three different fibers.
5. Test monotonicity on five positive weights and all local principal signs.
6. Reject mutations that promote the scoped natural boundary to a
   renormalized, operator, arithmetic, or Route-B conclusion.

The finite ledgers test the formulas; monotonicity, density, and the
natural-boundary conclusion are proved symbolically in `PROOF_PACKAGE.md`.
