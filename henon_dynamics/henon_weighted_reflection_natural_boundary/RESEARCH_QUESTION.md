# HCS-P76 research question

For a fixed positive weight `q`, does the exact scalar-channel continuation
of the P70 orbit-resolved reflection Euler product stop at a single positive
essential ladder, or do the full complex channel roots accumulate densely on
a canonical limiting circle?  Determine the circle, prove or refute
meromorphic continuation across its arcs, and keep the conclusion scoped to
the unrenormalized packet continuation.
