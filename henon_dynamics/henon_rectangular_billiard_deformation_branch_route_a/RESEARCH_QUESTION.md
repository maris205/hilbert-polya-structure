# C167 research question

For the full one-parameter family of rectangular Dirichlet billiards, can one
prove an exact renormalized Abel-trace branch coefficient at every nonzero
dual shell and classify every change of shell multiplicity as the aspect
ratio varies?

The answer must be uniform for every α>0.  It must control coincident axis
and boundary poles, distinguish rational from irrational β=α², and
identify collision transversality without inferring an all-parameter theorem
from a finite shell ledger.

The target-facing question is deliberately excluded: no target trace,
divisor law, functional equation, counting law, arithmetic local data, or
Route-B input is compared.
