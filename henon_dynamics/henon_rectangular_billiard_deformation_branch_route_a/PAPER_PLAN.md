# C167 paper plan and argument blueprint

Target: a two-page theoretical certificate with independently composed
English and Chinese abstracts.

| section | purpose | evidence | transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source family | define the full rectangular trace and exact Poisson owner | separation and Poisson formula | fixes the object whose boundary is studied |
| Branch theorem | derive the coefficient and suppress all nonmatching/boundary terms | principal-branch local expansion plus dominated convergence | turns a fixed-α result into a deformation question |
| Collision geometry | classify all multiplicity changes in β | exact linear equation in β and derivative formulas | identifies rational strata and irrational controls |
| Certificates and boundary | report sentinels, negative controls, tuple, and declarations | evidence JSON, independent checker, SymPy, replay, mutations | closes without target-facing promotion |

Central thesis: the rectangular deformation preserves a canonical full-trace
branch coefficient for every aspect.  Every non-sign collision parameter is
rational, and every pairwise crossing is transverse.

The strongest objection is that a finite fibre ledger cannot prove the
irrational or all-shell conclusions.  The response is structural: the
collision equation forces rational β, and the branch proof uses normal
convergence and domination.  The ledger is labeled only as regression
evidence.
