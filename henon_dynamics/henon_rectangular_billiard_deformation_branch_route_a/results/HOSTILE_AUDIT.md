# C167 hostile audit

- A finite shell enumeration cannot establish the all-α theorem.  The
  released proof instead uses the exact collision equation and a uniformly
  dominated Poisson tail.
- Irrational β excludes exact non-sign collisions but does not supply a
  uniform shell gap.  No gap claim is made.
- Rational fibres need not have a simple universal divisor formula.  Only
  the exact equation (vm^2+un^2=N) is asserted.
- Boundary coincidences do not create a hidden double pole: the trace contains
  a sum of two simple subtraction terms.  The β=4, (E=4) sentinel checks
  the simultaneous axis and boundary case.
- The multiplicity (R_\alpha(E)) counts the complete signed clean shell,
  not an isolated primitive orbit or stability determinant.
- All semantic claim surfaces are nested-key/value checked and mutation
  tested.  Internal review is not represented as external peer review.
