# C158 narrative report

## Outcome

C158 converts C153's full-opening time `n=k` into a complete secular theorem.
At that clock, the gate is exactly a tensor power, so every surviving
eigenvalue, algebraic multiplicity, zero dimension, and field coefficient is
known for all `k`.

## Explicit progress

The normalized surviving log modulus is an affine fair-binomial variable.
This supplies an exact mean `-log(3)/4`, variance `sigma^2/k`, exponential
concentration, weak collapse to a point mass, and a nondegenerate Gaussian
fluctuation law at scale `sqrt(k)`.  These are all-`k` theorems, not finite
table extrapolations.

## Controls and correction

The closed parent collapses log moduli to zero; projector order is exactly
isospectral.  Moving the hole preserves rank, degree, and the mean because
the product modulus remains `1/sqrt(3)`, but changes the variance.  Internal
review explicitly corrected the tempting but false statement that the moved-
hole mean also changes.

## Boundary

The measure is defined only on nonzero eigenvalue moduli, normalized by
`1/k`.  No phase distribution, inverse secular-zero convention, self-adjoint
limit, target match, arithmetic structure, or Route-B step is claimed.
