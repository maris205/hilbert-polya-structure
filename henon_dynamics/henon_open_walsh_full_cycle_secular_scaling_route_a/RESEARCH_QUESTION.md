# C158 research question

What exact secular and large-`k` information emerges at the natural full-cycle
time `n=k` of the open Walsh shift, beyond the rank-escape and fixed-period
trace results of C153?

C158 asks for the complete nonzero spectrum of `B_k^k`, its exact secular
factorization and degree, and the multiplicity-weighted distribution of the
normalized log moduli.  The desired progress is a proved concentration and
central-limit theorem derived from the source tensor structure, with controls
that separate unitary closure, projector order, and hole location.

The question explicitly excludes phase convergence, a self-adjoint limit,
and every target-facing or arithmetic claim.
