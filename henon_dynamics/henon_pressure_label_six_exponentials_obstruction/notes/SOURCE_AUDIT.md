# Source audit

## Six Exponentials

The exact theorem used is the real Six Exponentials Theorem in J. Pila,
*Geometric and arithmetic postulation of the exponential function*, Journal
of the Australian Mathematical Society (Series A) 54 (1993), Theorem 3.1,
DOI `10.1017/S1446788700037022`.

It requires three real numbers linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\) and
two real numbers linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\), and concludes that
at least one of the six corresponding exponentials is transcendental. HCS-P48
uses the three exact logarithms and the pair \((1,h)\). It does not invoke the
open Four Exponentials Conjecture.

## Gelfond--Schneider

Pila's Theorem 3.2 supplies the positive-real version used only for the
optional algebraic-irrational corollary.  The original source is A. Gelfond,
*Sur le septième problème de Hilbert* (1934),
<https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im4924>.

The pressure root, algebraic-unit theorem, scalar repetition classification,
