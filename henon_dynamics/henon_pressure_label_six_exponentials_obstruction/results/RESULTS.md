# HCS-P48 results

## Strongest positive result

Three explicit primitive H6 survivor multipliers generate a compositum of
degree \(32\). Their independent inversion automorphisms prove that their
positive logarithms are linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\).

## Strongest obstruction

For every real \(h>0\), the three common-power labels cannot all be rational
primes. Rational \(h\) is excluded by the algebraic-unit theorem; irrational
\(h\) is excluded by Six Exponentials.
