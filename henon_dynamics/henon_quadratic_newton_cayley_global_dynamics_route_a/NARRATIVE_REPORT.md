# HCS-C257 narrative report

The decisive step is not another local Newton convergence estimate.  The
Cayley coordinate turns the entire quadratic Newton map on the Riemann sphere
into squaring.  That one identity simultaneously resolves the two attracting
root basins, their exact common boundary, and an exact error formula valid at
every iterate.

The same conjugacy also closes a second layer that a basin picture alone does
not supply.  Every nontrivial preperiodic point is a root of unity.  Its even
part determines the exact transient length and the multiplicative order of
$2$ modulo its odd part determines the eventual cycle.  Fixed counts,
primitive counts, multipliers, and Artin--Mazur zeta then follow without a
period cutoff.  On the Julia line, angle doubling pushes Haar measure to a
Cauchy law for the original Newton variable.

The result is therefore a large, complete complex-dynamics theorem for its
frozen family.  It is also a clean negative Route-A result: all apparent
integer structure is inherited from the generic degree-two squaring map.  It
does not create primes, a logarithmic prime clock, a target divisor, or a
natural arithmetic determinant owner.
