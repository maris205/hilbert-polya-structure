# HCS-C257 research question

For the quadratic Newton family

\[
p_a(z)=z^2-a^2,\qquad
N_a(z)=\frac{z^2+a^2}{2z},\qquad a\in\mathbb C^*,
\]

can one give a genuinely global theorem on \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C)\), not
just a local convergence estimate: exact basins and Julia set, iterate and
root-error formulas, a complete periodic/preperiodic classification with
tails, all cycle multipliers, primitive counts, dynamical zeta, and the
natural invariant boundary measure?

The frozen clock is the Newton iterate $n\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$.  The
parameter face $a=0$ is recorded separately because the rational-map degree
drops.  The hostile Route-A question is whether the attractive language of
"Newton", exact orbit counts, and a rational zeta creates an intrinsic
arithmetic carrier.  It does not: after a Möbius change the entire ledger is
the universal degree-two squaring law.

Success is the all-state theorem above plus independent exact evidence.  It
does not mean a cubic Newton classification, a target zero match, arithmetic
local data, a target determinant, or a Hilbert--Pólya construction.
