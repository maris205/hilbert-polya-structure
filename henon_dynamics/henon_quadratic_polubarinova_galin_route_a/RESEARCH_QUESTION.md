# Research question

For a normalized quadratic conformal map

\[
f(\zeta,t)=a(t)\zeta+b(t)\zeta^2,\qquad a(t)>0,
\]

can the constant-rate Polubarinova--Galin equation be solved on its full
smooth univalent coefficient branch, with the first loss of regularity under
suction identified in physical time and in a local geometric normal form?

The answer proved here is complete within degree two.  Fourier coefficient
matching yields the coefficient ODE.  The invariant `a^2 b` and the affine
area clock reduce the dynamics to a monotone scalar equation.  Injection is
global, zero rate is stationary, and noncircular suction reaches one ordinary
semicubical cusp at an exact first time.  The circular face collapses without
a pre-collapse cusp.  Already-cusped and interior-critical starting maps are
kept outside the smooth theorem.

The question does not include surface tension, weak continuation after a
cusp, higher-degree polynomials, multiply connected domains, or any target
arithmetic interpretation.
