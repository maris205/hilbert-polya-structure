# Narrative report

The degree-two Polubarinova--Galin family looks two-dimensional in its
coefficients, but the first Fourier mode freezes the complex quantity
`kappa=a^2 b`.  The remaining constant mode is exactly the area clock.  This
turns the full complex-coefficient evolution into inversion of one function,

\[
F(u)=u+2|\kappa|^2/u^2,
\]

on its increasing branch.  Its unique minimum is simultaneously the loss of
closed-disk local univalence.  Consequently the sign of `q` gives a complete
trichotomy: injection moves away from the wall forever, zero rate fixes the
map, and suction reaches the wall at a computable finite time.

The endpoint is geometric, not merely an ODE blow-up.  At the sole critical
preimage, a rigid rotation reduces the boundary parameterization to
`-B s^2-iB s^3+O(s^4)`, which is an ordinary semicubical cusp.  Strict
quadratic injectivity excludes an earlier self-contact.  The zero-invariant
face is genuinely different: it consists of circles and suction ends in
collapse rather than a pre-collapse cusp.

This is a theorem-scale advance for the selected dynamical subtype, but it
does not advance Route A.  The free-boundary flow has no source-native prime
carrier, primitive orbit ledger, determinant bridge, target analytic object,
or natural same-clock unitary lift.  All five gates fail and Route B remains
locked.
