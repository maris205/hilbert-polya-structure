# Paper plan

**Title:** A complete Floquet--Jordan atlas for the two-step Hill oscillator

1. Freeze the coefficient order, state convention, and entire segment
   functions.
2. Derive the exact monodromy and discriminant for all signs of `k_j`.
3. Prove elliptic, hyperbolic, scalar-parabolic, and Jordan-parabolic
   dynamics without conflating the `Delta=+/-2` cases.
4. Add Chebyshev iterates, Floquet rates, order swap, and every parameter
   degeneration.
5. Report independent series, symbolic, replay, and mutation gates.
6. Close collision and Route-A boundaries explicitly.

Round 0 freezes the transfer and main classification.  Round 1 adds the
Jordan/iterate/rate theorem and executable receipt.  Round 2 adds all
degenerate faces, collision audit, and strict Route-A stop.
