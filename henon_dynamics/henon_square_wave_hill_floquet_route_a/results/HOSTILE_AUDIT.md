# C262 hostile audit

The repaired-hash suite rejects 41/41 attacks covering the equation sign,
segment determinant, discriminant sum, Floquet inequalities, automatic
periodicity at `Delta=+/-2`, Jordan witnesses, Chebyshev powers, parameter
faces, order swapping, counts, scope/evaluator/commit, Route-A promotion,
Route-B enablement, prohibited flags, citations, nonclaims, and stale hashes.

The decisive adversarial controls are the `+I`, `-I`, plus-Jordan,
minus-Jordan, and exact hyperbolic matrices.  They prevent the trace alone
from being misread as full periodicity and prevent a finite source
characteristic polynomial from being promoted to a target determinant.
