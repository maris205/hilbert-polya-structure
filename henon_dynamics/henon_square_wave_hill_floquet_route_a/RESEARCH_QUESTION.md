# Research question

For the periodic two-step Hill equation

\[
y''+k(t)y=0,
\qquad k(t)=k_1\text{ for }\tau_1,
\quad k_2\text{ for }\tau_2,
\]

can one give one all-parameter theorem that treats oscillatory, zero-
frequency, and hyperbolic segments uniformly and closes the exact monodromy,
Floquet discriminant, bounded/exponential regions, both parabolic Jordan
faces, all integer iterates, growth rates, and every zero-duration or
constant-coefficient degeneration?

Success means proof-driven `SL(2,R)` closure plus independent series,
symbolic, replay, and hostile-mutation evidence.  It does not mean a theorem
for arbitrary periodic coefficients, nonlinear perturbations, or a target
arithmetic determinant.
