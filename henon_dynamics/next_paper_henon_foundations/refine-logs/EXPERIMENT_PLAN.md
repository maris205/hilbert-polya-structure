# Claim-driven experiment plan

Status: deferred protocol draft. R000 is blocked until the expanded G0 audit
of Helleman, Fornæss--Weickert, Weickert, and Shudo--Ikeda establishes a
materially new theorem target. If authorized, R000 may only determine safe
numerical ranges and R001 freezes the main protocol before any
classical--quantum comparison is inspected.

## Claims to test

### C1. Quartic counting obstruction

The static Paper-5 quartic Hamiltonian has \(N(E)\sim CE^{3/4}\), not
\(E\log E\). The proof is primary; numerical diagonalization is only an
implementation sanity check.

### C2. Specified natural quantum object

The kernel generated by

\[
S_a(q,Q)=qQ-q+\frac a3q^3
\]

is unitary on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) with canonical relation equal to the graph of
the exact Hénon map, subject to the explicitly frozen Fourier convention.

### C3. Certified local trace correspondence

For the four-state local survivor \(\Lambda_*\) at \(a=6\), the trace of a
specified chronological localization of the exact quantum map is approximated
by the corresponding certified periodic points with action phases, Hessian
signatures, and stability amplitudes.

### C4. Distinct roles of action and instability

The discrete action controls the trace phase, while
\(T_p=\log|\Lambda_{u,p}|\) controls the stability amplitude. Removing,
scrambling, or exchanging either ingredient must degrade the trace prediction.

### C5. No Hilbert--Pólya conclusion

Even a positive C1--C4 outcome supplies a localized open quantum trace, not a
self-adjoint discrete energy spectrum or an arithmetic trace identity.

## Frozen mathematical objects

- Classical map: \(H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q)\).
- Certified object: the local four-state survivor \(\Lambda_*\) from R058/R059.
- State adjacency: the exact R059 four-state matrix; do not substitute a full
  two-shift.
- State intervals:
  \(X_-=[-5/8,-1/3]\), \(X_+=[1/3,5/8]\), with the certified history
  intervals and state labels inherited verbatim.
- Quantum phase: \(S_6(q,Q)=qQ-q+2q^3\).
- Fourier normalization:
  \((2\pi i\hbar)^{-1/2}e^{iS/\hbar}\), with
  \(\sqrt i=e^{i\pi/4}\), a frozen action gauge, subprincipal convention, and
  global phase.
- Orbit source: independently checked R059 coordinates, not roots recovered
  from the quantum matrix.
- Trace convention: fixed points of \(H_6^n\), including all points on each
  primitive orbit and all repetitions.
- Determinant convention, if reached: first prove the localized operator is
  trace class and define \(D_\hbar(z)=\det_{\rm Fr}(I-z\mathcal M_\hbar)\).
  The trace exponential is used only in its proved convergence domain. If only
  Hilbert--Schmidt control is obtained, a regularized determinant is required.

## Chronological localized operator

Index the four symbolic states by \(w=(s,t)\), where \(s\) is the current
position sign and \(t\) is the previous sign. Let \(\chi_s(q)\) be a smooth
position cutoff supported near \(X_s\). It must be certified equal to one on a
neighborhood of the projected local survivor and must exclude extra
stationary points; otherwise every theorem is explicitly limited to a frozen
maximum period.

On \(\bigoplus_w L^2(\mathbb R)\), use blocks

\[
(\mathcal M_\hbar)_{w',w}
=\mathbf 1_{\{A_{w,w'}=1\}}\,\chi_{s'}U_{6,\hbar}\chi_s,
\qquad w=(s,t),\quad w'=(s',s).
\]

Products of these blocks preserve the actual order of the itinerary. No
average of transition matrices is permitted.

The implementation may use a direct high-order quadrature of this kernel. A
periodic FFT compactification is allowed only as a labeled artifact control,
never as the main operator.

## Classical trace formula implementation

For a length-\(n\) closed itinerary, define

\[
\Phi_n(q_0,\ldots,q_{n-1})
=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}S_6(q_j,q_{j+1}),
\qquad q_n=q_0.
\]

Stationarity gives the exact recurrence

\[
q_{j-1}+q_{j+1}-1+6q_j^2=0.
\]

For each fixed point, compute independently:

- \(S_n=\Phi_n(q_0,\ldots,q_{n-1})\);
- the cyclic Hessian of \(\Phi_n\);
- its signature and determinant;
- \(DH_6^n\) and \(\det(I-DH_6^n)\);
- the unstable multiplier, sign \(\sigma_p\), and instability time \(T_p\).

Require the Hessian/stability determinant identity to agree to relative
\(10^{-11}\) in float64 and to an interval enclosure containing zero for the
difference in the proof-oriented check.

The signed identity is

\[
\det D^2\Phi_n(x)=(-1)^{n-1}\det(I-DH_6^n(x)).
\]

Periods \(n=1,2\) require dedicated construction and tests because cyclic
variables and off-diagonal contributions coincide. Repetition Maslov indices
are computed from the repeated-orbit Hessian, not assumed to equal \(r\mu_p\).

Use the Hessian signature to fix the Maslov phase; do not fit a global phase to
the quantum traces.

## Initial numerical configuration

R000 begins with, but is not bound to publish:

- \(\hbar\in\{1/16,1/24,1/32,1/48,1/64\}\);
- periods \(n=1,\ldots,8\), extended to 12 only after convergence;
- Gauss--Legendre orders \(N_q\in\{64,96,128,192,256\}\) per sign interval;
- at least three smooth cutoff shoulders chosen inside the exact R058 margins;
- adaptive reference trace obtained by order doubling until the complex trace
  changes by less than \(10^{-10}(1+|\operatorname{tr}|)\);
- float64 production plus mpmath/interval spot checks for every period.

R000 must record the minimum distance of every included orbit coordinate from
the cutoff transition zone. If no cutoff can be one on all period-12 points
while staying inside the certified geometry, reduce the main period range; do
not widen the classical claim silently.

## Metrics

For each period and \(\hbar\), let \(T_Q\) be the converged quantum trace and
\(T_{PO}\) the leading periodic-orbit prediction. Report:

\[
E_{\rm abs}=|T_Q-T_{PO}|,
\]

\[
E_{\rm norm}=
\frac{|T_Q-T_{PO}|}
{\hbar+\sum_{x\in\operatorname{Fix}}
|\det(I-DH_6^n(x))|^{-1/2}},
\]

and, away from trace cancellations, magnitude and phase errors separately.

Primary aggregate:

- median \(E_{\rm norm}\) over the preregistered periods 1--6;
- worst-case \(E_{\rm norm}\) over periods 1--8;
- log--log slope of each error against \(\hbar\) over the three smallest
  numerically resolved values.

The main positive gate is:

1. quantum quadrature error is at most 10% of the reported
   quantum--orbit discrepancy;
2. median normalized error decreases at every one of the final three
   semiclassical refinements;
3. deterministic refinement ratios and \(|R_n(\hbar)|/\hbar\) are reported;
   no bootstrap population claim is made from the finite deterministic grid;
4. ablations are reported as mechanism diagnostics, without requiring the
   correct oscillatory formula to beat each one at every finite \(\hbar\);
5. conclusions persist across all admissible cutoff families.

These thresholds test trend and mechanism rather than force an unproved exact
\(O(\hbar)\) rate.

## Controls and falsifiers

### Numerical controls

- quadrature order doubling;
- independent kernel assembly;
- smooth-cutoff family and shoulder width;
- direct dense trace versus stochastic trace only as a secondary check;
- periodic FFT box as a labeled boundary-artifact control;
- phase convention changed by inversion/reflection, with predicted change
  derived analytically.

### Dynamical controls

- replace the four-state adjacency by an incorrect full two-shift;
- keep quantum \(a=6\) but insert orbit data from a mismatched parameter;
- shuffle actions among cycles at fixed periods;
- shuffle instability times among cycles at fixed periods;
- replace the exact repeated-orbit amplitude by \(e^{-rT_p/2}\) alone;
- use unit-clock or flat-amplitude cycles.

### Target-leakage control

The code and environment must contain no import or file read for Riemann zero
tables, primes, \(\zeta\), or \(\xi\). The manifest checker will scan commands,
configs, and source dependencies. Mathematical comparison to the asymptotic
Riemann--von Mangoldt formula occurs only in the analytic Weyl theorem.

## Determinant gate

Do not publish determinant zeros merely because finite sections look stable.
R080 may enter the paper only if one of the following is obtained on a frozen
contour:

- a rigorous trace-tail estimate;
- a rigorous operator determinant and approximation theorem;
- a Rouché/argument-principle bound certifying the same zero count;
- an explicitly labeled finite-time polynomial result with no infinite
  determinant claim.

Otherwise the paper stops at the fixed-time trace theorem.
