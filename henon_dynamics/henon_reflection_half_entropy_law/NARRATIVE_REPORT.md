# Narrative report

P58 showed that the physical stable tail and the all-conjugate Galois excess
use different data.  P59 closes the first half of the missing bridge: it
counts every physical reflection-symmetric primitive cycle in the frozen H6
survivor.

The adjacency matrix has characteristic polynomial
`(z^2+1)(z^2-z-1)` and a unique reversal involution swapping symbols 1 and 2.
Half-word transfer gives Fibonacci/Lucas formulas for odd reflections and
the two even axis classes.  Möbius inversion must retain the parity of every
even divisor; ignoring it gives wrong counts.

The resulting primitive reflection count grows like `phi^(n/2)`, while all
primitive cycles grow like `phi^n/n`.  Hence the reflection subsystem has
entropy exactly half the full survivor entropy and exponentially vanishing
density.  This is a whole-period analytic theorem, not a finite trend.

The result also sharpens the remaining obstruction.  The P58 Galois excess
cannot be compiled from reflection-cycle abundance alone: its nonphysical
conjugates require a separate algebraic dynatomic count and height theorem.
