# ROUND2 clue

The symbolic reflection rate is `(1/2)log(phi)`.  The P58 mixed-axis algebraic
closure has total degree `2^((n+1)/2)` at odd period `n`, suggesting an
algebraic primitive rate `(1/2)log(2)`.  The next paper should construct the
mixed-axis reflection dynatomic quotient and prove its exact Möbius degree
law, then compare the two rates without assuming that algebraic roots are
physical survivor cycles.
