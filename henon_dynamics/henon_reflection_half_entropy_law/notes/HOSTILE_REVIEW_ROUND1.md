# Hostile review round 1

The main threat was an incorrect factor of two.  For a fixed reflection
`R_k`, a primitive odd necklace has one fixed rotated representative because
2 is invertible modulo `n`.  A compatible even necklace has two.  This proves
the odd formula without division and the two even formulas with division by
two.  Direct enumeration confirms the distinction at every period through
16.

The second threat was axis mislabelling.  The frozen P58 words give shifts 3
and 4: `A8` is the odd-shift vertex--vertex class and `B8` the even-shift
edge--edge class.  These locks are executable.

Verdict: no fatal issue.
