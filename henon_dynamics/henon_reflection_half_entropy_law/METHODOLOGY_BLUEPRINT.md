# Methodology blueprint

- Encode time reversal by the involution `rho=(0,2,1,3)`.
- Count fixed cyclic words from one half of a palindrome.
- Use the matrix characteristic polynomial to reduce the path counts to
  Fibonacci, Lucas and period-four correction terms.
- Apply Möbius inversion at fixed reflection index, not after summing the two
  even conjugacy classes.
- Divide primitive fixed-word counts by one for odd periods and by two for
  each compatible even axis class.
- Prove entropy from the dominant golden-ratio roots and bound all proper
  divisors exponentially below the main term.
- Validate with a separately written primitive-necklace enumerator.
