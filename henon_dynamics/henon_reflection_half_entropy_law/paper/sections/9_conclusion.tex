\section{Conclusion}

The H6 survivor admits an exact reflection compiler: one reversal involution,
three half-word transfer formulas and a parity-sensitive primitive inversion.
Its reversible primitive cycles have entropy $(1/2)\log\varphi$, exactly half
the ambient value, and hence exponentially vanishing density.

This closes the physical counting question exposed by the preceding Galois
interface obstruction.  It also makes the next question sharper.  The only
possible exponential compensation must come from algebraic multiplicity or
height, so the next object is the primitive reflection dynatomic quotient and
not another finite physical census.
