\section{The half-entropy law}

Put $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$.  The bounded $\pm i$ contribution in
\eqref{eq:closed} and the Binet formulas imply
$N_n=\varphi^n+O(1)$.  Every proper divisor of $n$ is at most $n/2$, so
\eqref{eq:primitive-all} yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-asymptotic}
C_n=\frac{\varphi^n}{n}+O(\varphi^{n/2}).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Half entropy]\label{thm:entropy}
The primitive reflection population satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reflection-growth}
R_n=\Theta(\varphi^{n/2}).
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy}
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log C_n=\log\varphi,
\qquad
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log R_n=\frac12\log\varphi,
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:density}
\frac{R_n}{C_n}=O\!\left(n\varphi^{-n/2}\right).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For odd $n$, the leading term in \eqref{eq:primitive-odd} is
$F_{(n+3)/2}=\Theta(\varphi^{n/2})$, while every proper odd divisor is at most
$n/3$.  For even $n$, both $G_{n/2}$ and $H_{n/2}$ have positive leading
constant times $\varphi^{n/2}$; all proper-divisor terms in
\eqref{eq:primitive-even} are $O(\varphi^{n/4})$.  This proves
\eqref{eq:reflection-growth}.  Equations \eqref{eq:entropy} and
\eqref{eq:density} follow from \eqref{eq:full-asymptotic}.
\end{proof}

The result is an all-period theorem.  Finite counts are used below only to
audit conventions, not to infer either exponential rate.
