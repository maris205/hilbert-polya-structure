# Experiment plan

1. Recover the unique symbol involution implementing time reversal on the H6
   four-state adjacency matrix.
2. Count closed words fixed by each reflection class using half-word transfer
   matrices.
3. Remove proper repetitions by Möbius inversion with the divisor-dependent
   reflection parity retained.
4. Enumerate primitive necklaces independently through a nontrivial finite
   range and compare every count.
5. Extract the exponential growth rates of the full and reflection
   ensembles.
6. Reject any promotion from physical symbolic counts to Galois conjugate
   counts or arithmetic prime counts.
