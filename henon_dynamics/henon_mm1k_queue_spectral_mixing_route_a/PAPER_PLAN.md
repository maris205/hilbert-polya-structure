# Paper plan — Exact finite-capacity queue spectrum and mixing

**Target claim.**  A convention-complete finite M/M/1/K birth–death semigroup
has a closed reversible spectral kernel, a sharp elementary gap expression,
and a three-regime capacity-limit boundary.

**Sections.** (1) model and endpoint convention; (2) detailed balance and
Jacobi conjugation; (3) Robin sine eigenbasis and full spectrum; (4) kernel and
TV/L2 bound; (5) zero-rate/equal-rate/infinite-capacity boundaries; (6)
independent evidence and Route-A boundary; (7) source note and nonclaims.

**Evidence integration.** The manuscript quotes row counts and audit outcomes
from the canonical JSON and names the exact checker/SymPy/replay/mutation
commands.  It does not present finite rows as a proof of an unqualified
infinite-dimensional spectral theorem.

**Revision protocol.** Round 0 establishes the theorem. Round 1 adds the
capacity-limit table and clarifies endpoint conventions. Round 2 tightens the
mixing inequality, source metadata, and explicit no-continuous-spectrum
boundary. Three PDFs remain content-distinct and `main.pdf` equals round 2.
