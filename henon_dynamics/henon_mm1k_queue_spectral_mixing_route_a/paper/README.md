# Paper payload

`main.tex` is compiled with LuaLaTeX under `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1787875200`.
The three revision PDFs are intentionally content-distinct: round 0 states the
finite theorem, round 1 adds the singular/capacity boundary, and round 2 adds
the independent audit and Route-A scope boundary.  `main.pdf` is a byte-for-byte
copy of `main_round2.pdf`.  Build logs and hashes are recorded in
`COMPILE_REPORT.md`; LaTeX sidecars are excluded from the release manifest.
