# C150 research question

Can the bounded two-clock Rule-90 experiment be upgraded to a genuine
one-parameter scaling theorem with an exact transient/periodic decomposition?

Freeze `L_r=2^r-1` and one cellular-automaton update as the clock.  For every
`r`, determine the image, periodic set, allowed periods, fixed counts,
exact-period points, and primitive cycles.  Then compare with the matched
power-of-two circumference family without turning the comparison into an
arithmetic-factor claim.
