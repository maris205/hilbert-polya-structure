1. Freeze Rule 90, the Mersenne circumference family, one-update clock, and
   point/cycle normalization.
2. Prove the all-`r` Frobenius identity, image rank, periodic-image equality,
   and period-divisibility result before computation.
3. Generate exact divisor-period ledgers for `1<=r<=8` from polynomial gcd
   and Möbius inversion; require the exact-period partition to sum to the
   half-size image.
4. Generate power-of-two nilpotency controls for `1<=s<=8`.
5. Validate through an independent binary-matrix implementation, separate
   SymPy quotient-ring path, byte replay, and semantic mutations.
6. Compile and repair the paper through two genuine internal rounds.
7. Close exactly 27 payload files under a self-excluded manifest.

The cutoffs are implementation sentinels; all structural claims are proved
for every positive family exponent.
