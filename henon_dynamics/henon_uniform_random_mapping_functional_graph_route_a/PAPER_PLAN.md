# Paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

One cycle–forest decomposition and one marked-prefix decomposition jointly
close exact cycle/component statistics, an unexpected finite distributional
identity, and the full square-root collision limit for uniform random maps.

## Claims–evidence map

| Claim | Analytic owner | Executable receipt |
|---|---|---|
| joint `(C_n,K_n)` count | permutation plus rooted-forest determinant | 84 exhaustive cells |
| cyclic law | sum `c(k,r)=k!` | exact atlas and normalization |
| expected `ell`-cycles | indicators over labelled directed cycles | 28 exhaustive aggregates |
| marked `(mu,lambda)` law | ordered distinct prefix and forced closure | 84 exhaustive cells |
| `C_n =_d R_n` | marginal comparison | seven full histogram comparisons |
| Rayleigh and joint limits | birthday product and conditional uniformity | 28 scaling receipts |

## Three substantive stages

1. Round 0 proves the exact cycle–component count, its cyclic marginal, and
   the expected count of every cycle length.
2. Round 1 adds the marked tail/cycle law, the finite distributional identity,
   and the one-dimensional Rayleigh limit.
3. Round 2 adds the joint quadrant limit, boundary cases, executable audit,
   source discipline, and the strict Route-A rejection.

The article keeps the analytic theorem central.  It neither inflates finite
regression into proof nor claims the classical formulas as new literature.
