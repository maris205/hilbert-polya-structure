# Hostile audit

## Model and boundary attacks

- Replacing the uniform ensemble by a conditioned or nonuniform map is outside
  the theorem.
- Treating `K_n` as a directed strongly connected component count is rejected;
  it is the weak-component/cycle count.
- Dropping the empty-minor convention at `k=n` is rejected.
- Removing `mu=0`, `lambda=1`, `mu+lambda=n`, or the `n=1` map changes exact
  normalization and is rejected.

## Formula and evidence attacks

- Joint counts, forest factors, marked counts, cyclic masses, collision tails,
  cycle expectations, scaling receipts, and count-ledger mutations are checked.
- Metadata, source locks, evaluator authority, Route-A tuple, Route-B flag, and
  scope mutations are rejected.
- All 24 mutations carry repaired payload hashes; hash mismatch is not the
  detection mechanism.

## Claim attacks

Exhaustive enumeration is not promoted to an all-size proof or an asymptotic
argument.  Classical sources are cited without a novelty claim.  Ensemble
probability transforms are not called arithmetic Euler products, target zeta
functions, functional equations, or Hilbert–Pólya operators.  The lack of a
natural same-clock self-adjoint operator forces `A4_FAIL`; Route B remains
false.
