# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — global functional-graph count

The first article stage freezes the uniform labelled mapping model and proves
the exact joint cycle–component formula with the rooted-forest determinant.  It
also derives the cyclic marginal and all cycle-length expectations, including
the `n=1` and `k=n` boundary conventions.

## Round 1 — marked-orbit bridge

The second stage adds the full `(mu,lambda)` law of a marked orbit, proves that
the collision length and the global number of cyclic vertices are identically
distributed for every `n`, and derives their common Rayleigh scaling limit.
This is a substantive theorem extension rather than a prose revision.

## Round 2 — joint limit and release boundary

The final stage proves convergence of `(mu,lambda)/sqrt(n)` to the quadrant
density `exp(-(x+y)^2/2)`, records all degenerate faces, integrates independent
enumeration/SymPy/replay/mutation receipts, and states source and Route-A
boundaries.  The three PDFs have distinct hashes; `main.pdf` is byte-equal to
`main_round2.pdf`.
