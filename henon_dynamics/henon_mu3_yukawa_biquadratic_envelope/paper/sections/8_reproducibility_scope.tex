\section{Exact computation, reproducibility, and scope}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The proof uses finite computation at four bounded points: subgroup closure and
the collision census, stabilizers and modular orbit values, permutation
characters and conductor orbits, and local double cosets.  This section
records how those premises are reconstructed and how they connect to the
mathematical arguments.  Test counts are evidence about implementation
integrity, not substitutes for the proofs in
\cref{sec:envelope,sec:primitive,sec:uniqueness-zeta,sec:global,sec:local}.

\subsection{Independent reconstruction and adversarial checks}

The paper-facing package contains $13$ source files and $8$ result files;
the complete live inventory has $21$ files, while the self-excluding scoped
manifest binds $20$.  Two complete official cycles each pass $53/53$ tests
and reconstruct all eight gates.  The canonical payload has $9310$ scalar
leaves.  The independent checker rejects $9339$ value mutations, $9339$ type
mutations, and $14$ structural mutations.  Its evidence-rebound suite rejects
$6$ group-document and $4$ resolver-document mutations; two additional
self-consistent artifact-row digest attacks give the explicit count summary
$6/4/10/2/12$.  Thirty-nine child-input snapshots are rebound before the
certificate write.

The group and primitive-resolver evidence are produced by separate lanes.
The integrated checker does not call the producer's theorem functions: it
runs the final group checker to recover the canonical projection, invokes an
independently implemented resolver reconstruction, and rebuilds all payload
leaves.  Strict parsers reject duplicate keys, booleans disguised as integers,
unknown fields, symlinks, path substitution, stale caches, and input changes
between validation and write.  The exact schema, certificate, check report,
component evidence, and self-excluding manifest are bound by the hashes in
\cref{tab:evidence-hashes}.  These controls make the finite claims
reproducible from the anonymous supplement while leaving the conceptual proof
visible in the paper.

\subsection{Scope firewall}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.92\textwidth}
\textbf{Scope.}  The exact uniqueness theorem is restricted to the eleven
collision buckets and the predicate ``normalizers conjugate in $\WE$ and
both normalizer indices equal two.''  Orbit polynomials are exact in product
form; no expanded characteristic-zero coefficient vector is claimed.  The
degree obstruction concerns commutative formal polynomials in independent
labelled variables.  The character relation is not a finite-set
isomorphism.  The zeta identity yields no new bad Euler factor, epsilon
factor, root number, holomorphy, automorphy, or analytic-continuation
statement.  Neither $D_3$
branch is selected, and local tuples are not a classification of local
fields.  No integral-equivalence, integral-basis, maximal-order, class-number,
regulator, trace-form, rational-point, motive, H\'enon, Yukawa, Calabi--Yau,
dynamical, spectral, Riemann-hypothesis, or Hilbert--P\'olya conclusion is
made.
\end{minipage}}
\end{center}

The literal scope tag in the certificate is
\[
 \codeword{NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER}.
\]
The full set of thirty false scope leaves is grouped and listed in
\cref{app:scope-leaves}.  This redundancy is intentional: a downstream use
of a discriminant or zeta identity should not be able to erase the nonclaims
that accompanied it.

\subsection{Anonymous supplementary boundary}

An anonymized supplementary archive is intended to contain canonical JSON,
strict schemas, immutable manifests, durable group arrays, and replay
instructions.  The draft contains no repository URL, host name, user name,
institutional path, commit identity, or identifying acknowledgement.  The
supplementary certificate's machine-layer field
\codeword{release_authorized=false} records that its finite evidence is not,
by itself, a promotion or publication claim.  That workflow field is not a
mathematical premise of the theorem.  Release and archival state are managed
outside the manuscript so that the mathematical source remains stable across
later packaging steps.
