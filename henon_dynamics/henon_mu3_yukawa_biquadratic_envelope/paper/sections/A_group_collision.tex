\section{Durable group data and the collision census}
\label{app:group-census}

This appendix records the finite data needed to reproduce
\cref{prop:group-lattice,prop:restricted-uniqueness,eq:G-character-relation}.
All arrays are one-line images of $1,\ldots,27$ and are one-based.  The
Table-of-Marks integers elsewhere in the paper are versioned checks only.
