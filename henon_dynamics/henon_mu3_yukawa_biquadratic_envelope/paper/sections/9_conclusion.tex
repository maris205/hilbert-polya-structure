\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The exact common normalizer upgrades one pairwise Gassmann collision into a
coherent fixed-field envelope.  Its subgroup lattice gives a biquadratic
degree-$160/320/640$ diamond; exact line-coordinate carriers make three
levels primitive; equality of point and pair partitions explains why the new
quadratic companion first appears through a cubic formal invariant; and the
$\Vfour$ character relation organizes both the zeta identity and the
relative arithmetic.  Global conductor calculations and two separate local
double-coset tables then show that all relative ramification is tame above
$3$, independently of which retained decomposition-group branch occurs.

Two natural questions remain outside the theorem.  One is to resolve which
of the two $3$-adic decomposition groups belongs to the fixed normal
extension.  The other is to study integral refinements of the five fields
beyond their discriminants.  Neither question is answered by the present
zeta or local-tuple data, and neither is needed for the envelope constructed
here.
