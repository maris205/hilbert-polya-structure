# Research question — C297

For the autonomous PT-symmetric dimer

\[
H_{\gamma,\kappa}=\begin{pmatrix}i\gamma&\kappa\\
\kappa&-i\gamma\end{pmatrix},\qquad \kappa>0,
\]

can one give a single exact dynamical theorem that remains correct on both
exceptional sheets, distinguishes vectors from projective rays, and identifies
exactly where a positive conserved metric exists?

The desired answer must cover every real `gamma`, not a finite scan.  It must
also state what fails: the standard norm is not conserved away from the
Hermitian axis, the positive metric degenerates at the exceptional point, and
the finite-dimensional model is not a target spectral construction.
