# Narrative report — C297

The decisive simplification is not diagonalization but the scalar square
identity `H^2=delta I`.  It produces one propagator formula in each sign
chamber and remains meaningful at `delta=0`, where diagonalization fails.
This closes the exceptional point rather than treating it as a removable
limit.

The second advance is projective.  In an affine chart the dimer becomes a
Riccati flow.  Generic rays rotate with least period `pi/sqrt(delta)` in the
unbroken phase, the two fixed rays merge at the exceptional point, and split
into attracting and repelling rays in the broken phase.  Vector periods are
twice projective periods.

The third advance is the metric boundary.  `sigma_x` supplies a conserved
indefinite form for every parameter.  The explicit metric
`eta=I+(gamma/kappa)sigma_y` is positive exactly for
`|gamma|<kappa`, becomes singular at equality, and is indefinite beyond it.
Thus the manuscript never transports a positive Hilbert norm through an
exceptional point.

The strict Route-A tuple is
`(A0_FAIL,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`.  The periodic signal is a
clean projective family, not an isolated arithmetic orbit atlas, and the
pseudo-Hermitian matrix is only a finite-dimensional formal hint.
