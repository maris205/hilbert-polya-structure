# Research question

What is the complete event-time atlas of a one-dimensional impact oscillator when
a restitution factor and a constant affine impulse coexist? The answer is
closed: on the outgoing section the hybrid dynamics reduce to the affine map
`P(u)=r u+J`, but the physical clock weights each iterate by `2u/g`.
This distinction makes the Zeno boundary `J=0,0<r<1` transparent and exposes a
unique positive-duration forced cycle for `J>0,r<1`.

The zero-speed section point is handled as an absorbing rest state rather than
a spurious zero-length periodic flight. Elastic identity and impulsive
translation boundaries are listed separately. The result is a source-native
hybrid theorem, not an arithmetic or target-operator construction.
