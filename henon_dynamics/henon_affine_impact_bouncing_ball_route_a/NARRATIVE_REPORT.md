# Narrative report

`P(u)=r u+J` is discrete, but the physical clock assigns the roof `2u/g`
to each positive outgoing speed. This produces a complete boundary atlas:
geometric decay and finite accumulation for `J=0,0<r<1`, one-step sticking at
`r=0,J=0`, a unique positive
forced flight for `J>0,r<1`, a continuum of elastic periods at `r=1,J=0`,
and quadratic-time acceleration at `r=1,J>0`.

The ledger contains 12 exact rational controls and 96 event-time cells, plus
36 interior impact reconstructions. The checker treats the zero-speed point as
rest and rejects any attempt to call a zero-duration event a physical cycle.
The event-map series is explicitly formal and separate from a flow zeta.
