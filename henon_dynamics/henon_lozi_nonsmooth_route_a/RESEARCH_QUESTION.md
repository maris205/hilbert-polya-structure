# Research question — C116

For a genuinely nonsmooth Lozi map, how much periodic sign geometry can be
certified directly from exact affine branch returns before any global Markov
partition is known?

C116 answers only the finite question through period eight.  It tests every
candidate word with strict inequalities, records the pruning spectrum, and
builds a finite direct sum of the surviving cycle blocks.  Global coding,
boundary dynamics beyond the prefix, and analytic operator ownership remain
open.
