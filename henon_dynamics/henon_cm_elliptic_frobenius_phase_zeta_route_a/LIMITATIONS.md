# Limitations

This package specializes classical elliptic-curve theory and asserts no literature-first result. Flow papers 4/6 already own the universal closed-point and graded-determinant dictionary; C41 owns a cubic j=0 CM bridge. The new repository owner is the complete quartic all-prime phase mechanism and its all-degree consequences.

The six mandatory A1 orbit controls are not completed. Characteristic-p differential zero and reduced scheme multiplicity one do not supply a real-flow stability atlas. A1 therefore remains weak. Native determinant/FE/RH give no target A2/A3 credit. No cross-prime global product, convergence theorem, Gamma completion, target divisor or quantum domain is supplied. A4 is a formal phase hint only.
