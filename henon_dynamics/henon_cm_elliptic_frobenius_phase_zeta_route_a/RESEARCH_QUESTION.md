# Research question

Can one determine the Frobenius eigenvalue pair of y²=x³−x at every good prime with a proved primary sign, classify its phase dynamics, and derive every degree of closed point and the complete native determinant without confusing that result with the Riemann target?

The answer is affirmative for the native system. The sign is forced by rational four-torsion. The ordinary and supersingular phases differ at every degree, and the fixed-fibre target-divisor obstruction is explicit.
