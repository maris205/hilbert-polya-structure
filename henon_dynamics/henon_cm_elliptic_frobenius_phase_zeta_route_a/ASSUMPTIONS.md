# Assumptions

The model is the smooth projective completion of y²=x³−x at an odd prime p. Characteristic two is excluded. Time is iteration of the explicitly defined p-power morphism. The cohomological operator is pullback, fixing the H2 eigenvalue p.

External inputs are the elliptic endomorphism classification and integrality/degree theorem, separable isogeny factorization, the finite-field Frobenius trace theorem, and the ordinary criterion p∤t. Gaussian unique factorization fixes norm-p representations. The Riemann--von Mangoldt counting law is used only for a negative target comparison.

No experimental tolerance, random seed, fitted phase, zero table, or prime table is part of the candidate definition.
