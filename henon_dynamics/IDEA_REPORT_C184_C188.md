## Design objective

The user requested another five-paper round in which every paper takes a visibly large step and no theorem is split into five cosmetic variants. The collision scan therefore changes the dynamical owner in every slot: fractal spectral renormalization, an isospectral gradient flow, a Hamiltonian rigid-body flow, a finite combinatorial permutation, and max-plus projective dynamics.

Every selected paper must close an all-parameter theorem, its singular or degenerate strata, exact operator/orbit consequences where they genuinely exist, and a strict Route-A stop. A finite evidence census is a regression oracle and not proof of an infinite statement. `NEW` below means only that no C1--C183 release owns the result; it is not a claim of priority in the mathematical literature.

## Collision scan and kill gates

- A general additive cellular automaton was killed because it strictly subsumes the already extensive Rule-90 line rather than changing owner.
- Open Toda/QR flow was deferred because it would collide both with C182's ultradiscrete Toda source and C185's sorting geometry in the same round.
- Rowmotion/Y-systems, power maps, and Bernoulli--Laplace walks were killed as near-neighbors of existing necklace/Lyness, expanding-map, and finite Markov-spectrum packages.
- No credible A0-strong candidate survived the arithmetic scan. A k-free arithmetic shift would import forbidden local/Euler data; finite Cayley walks repeat existing finite-group spectra; Vieta/Markoff descent has no logarithmic prime clock. The round therefore records five honest `A0_FAIL` results instead of manufacturing arithmetic semantics.
- C187 survives only if the complete rectangular cyclic-sieving fixed-point theorem is closed. C188 survives only if matrix-power periodicity, projective-orbit bounds, and reducible/transient boundaries are kept distinct. The hard fallbacks are Watanabe--Strogatz reduction and Markoff--Vieta descent.

## Frozen independent theorem increments

### C184 -- Sierpiński-gasket Dirichlet spectral decimation

Freeze the standard unnormalized graph Laplacian on every finite pre-gasket with the three boundary corners removed. Starting from the Schur-complement decimation map \(R(\lambda)=\lambda(5-\lambda)\), one paper must classify the 2-, 5-, and 6-series, their admissible inverse branches, births, forbidden values, and exact multiplicities for every level. It must close the dimension sum, characteristic polynomial recursion, determinant product, heat trace, and finite spectral zeta. The spectral inverse-branch tree is a correspondence between levels, not the time evolution of a physical point.

### C185 -- Brockett double-bracket sorting flow

Freeze every real symmetric isospectral orbit with simple spectrum and every strictly ordered diagonal target \(N\). For \(\dot H=[H,[H,N]]\), prove global existence, isospectrality, the exact Lyapunov identity, all permutation equilibria, their linearization and Morse indices, generic convergence to the sorted diagonal, and absence of nonconstant recurrence. Degenerate source spectrum or repeated entries of \(N\) form a separate Morse--Bott boundary; the Lax form does not turn the dissipative clock into a unitary one.

### C186 -- triaxial Euler-top all-energy elliptic atlas

Freeze every \(I_1<I_2<I_3\), every angular-momentum sphere, and its full energy interval. Give the Jacobi-elliptic solution and exact period on both regular regimes, the six axial equilibria and their stability, the component topology of every energy level, time reversal, and the intermediate-axis separatrix with its infinite-period limit. Then classify the fixed sets of every time-\(\tau\) map: resonant energy circles occur as clean continua, so an ordinary isolated-orbit Artin--Mazur census is not available. Spherical, symmetric, zero-momentum, and separatrix cases are explicit boundaries rather than silent continuations of the regular formula.

### C187 -- rectangular tableau promotion and cyclic sieving

Freeze all standard Young tableaux of every rectangle \(a\times b\), with \(N=ab\). Use the rectangular q-hook cyclic-sieving theorem to determine \(\operatorname{Fix}(\mathrm{pr}^d)\) for every iterate, then apply Möbius inversion to obtain exact point periods and cycle counts, the finite Artin--Mazur zeta, Koopman determinant and spectral multiplicities. Evacuation supplies an exact reversor. Promotion has order dividing \(N\); one-row and one-column rectangles are identity boundaries, so exact order \(N\) is not claimed uniformly.

### C188 -- irreducible rational max-plus projective cyclicity

Freeze every irreducible rational max-plus matrix. Compute its maximum cycle mean, critical graph, component cyclicities, and a CSR/Nachtigall expansion that certifies the eventual relation between normalized matrix powers. Determine the minimal eventual period of the matrix-power sequence from the certified tail and prove that every projective vector orbit has eventual period dividing that matrix period; also record the eigencone and the primitive/cyclicity-one boundary. Critical-graph cyclicity is an upper bound and need not be every point's minimal period. Reducible matrices may have several growth rates, and there is no weight-independent dimension-only transient claim.
