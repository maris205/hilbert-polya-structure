# Result artifacts

- `c19_certificate.json`: producer's symbolic genus proof ledger, exact
  branch-corrected point-count tables, and candidate Frobenius numerators.
- `frobenius_counts.csv`: flat point-count table for \(p=5,11,13\) and
  \(r=1,2,3\).
- `c19_independent_check.json`: independent polynomial-quotient field counts,
  schema checks, and the direct \(p=5,r=4\) test.
- `c19_neighbor_correspondence.json`: exact generic degree-two neighbor
  subresultant, graph theorem ledger, and \(\mathbb F_{43}\) split control.
- `c19_neighbor_independent_check.json`: non-importing reproduction of the
  neighbor identities and exact order-seven control.
- `RESULTS.md`: interpreted mathematical summary.

The point-count files contain raw affine counts and an explicit
characteristic-zero branch correction.  They must not be interpreted as a
proved simultaneous normalization, a good-reduction certificate, or a
complete global regular model.

The neighbor controls do not assert good reduction at a selected prime.  They
certify that the adopted septic generically carries one exact Hénon
seven-cycle and a degree-14 ordered-edge lift.  They do not claim a
publisher-issued erratum or exhaustion of the full saturated period-seven
scheme.
