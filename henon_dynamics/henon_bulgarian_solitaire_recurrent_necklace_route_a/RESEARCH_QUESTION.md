# C190 research question

For the ordinary noninvertible Bulgarian-solitaire map on every partition set
`P(N)`, can one use the Brandt recurrent classification to close, in one
all-parameter theorem package, all of the following?

1. an explicit recurrent-partition/binary-word conjugacy with a frozen
   rotation direction;
2. the fixed count of every positive iterate;
3. every least-period population and primitive cycle;
4. the full finite Artin--Mazur zeta;
5. the full Koopman algebraic spectrum, including the transient zero
   multiplicity, without claiming the nilpotent Jordan structure;
6. a reflection reversor on the recurrent core and the obstruction to a
   global reversor;
7. the triangular boundary;
8. a strict Route-A verdict without arithmetic or target imports?

The answer is yes.  Brandt supplies the all-`N` recurrent characterization;
Akin and Davis supply classical dynamical background.  Rotation counting,
divisor Möbius inversion, finite functional-graph linear algebra, and a
reflection identity prove the remaining items.  Direct enumeration of all
215,307 partitions for `N<=40` is an independent regression oracle only.
