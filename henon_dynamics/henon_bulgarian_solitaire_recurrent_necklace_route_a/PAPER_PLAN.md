# C190 paper plan

## Title

*Bulgarian Solitaire: Recurrent Necklaces, Every Fixed Iterate, and the Full
Finite Koopman Spectrum*

## One-sentence contribution

Brandt's recurrent necklace coordinates turn every Bulgarian-solitaire system
into an all-parameter fixed/cycle/zeta classification and determine the full
Koopman algebraic spectrum, while sharply isolating the unclassified
transient-tree geometry.

## Structure

1. Freeze the noninvertible partition map and source-owned Brandt coordinates.
2. Prove every fixed iterate and recover every least period, cycle, and zeta
   factor.
3. Add the full Koopman algebraic spectrum, not merely the recurrent roots.
4. Prove recurrent reflection reversal and block a global-reversor overclaim.
5. Close the triangular family and separate all-`N` proof from finite census.
6. State the Route-A stop, limitations, declarations, and verified sources.

No decorative figure is used: the fixed formula, characteristic polynomial,
and N=8 sentinel carry the technical comparison more efficiently in this
two-page theorem note.
