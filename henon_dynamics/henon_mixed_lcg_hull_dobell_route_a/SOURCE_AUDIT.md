# Source and claim audit

The classical ownership citation is:

T. E. Hull and A. R. Dobell, Random Number Generators, SIAM Review 4 (1962),
230--254, DOI 10.1137/1004061.

The DOI, authors, venue, volume, issue, year, and pages were checked on the
SIAM publisher page on 31 August 2026.  The citation supports ownership of the
mixed-congruential full-period criterion.  This package supplies its own
prime-quotient, mod-four, valuation, and CRT proof and makes no literature
priority claim.

All executable data are derived from m,a,c.  No target prime or zero table,
arithmetic local factor, Euler factor, root number, automorphy object, target
divisor, functional equation, or Hilbert--Polya operator is used.  The source
zeta and Koopman determinant are labeled source-local.
