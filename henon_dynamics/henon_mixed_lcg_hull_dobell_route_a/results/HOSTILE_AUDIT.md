prime/mod-four conditions, regression counts, fixed ledgers, the evaluator
tuple, Route-B authorization, scope flags, and the verified DOI.  Every
mutation is rejected by the quick semantic preflight (37/37).

source zeta and a unitary Koopman permutation.  Those generic facts do not
produce target ownership, a target divisor, or a Hilbert--Polya operator.
