# Research question

For the mixed congruential map

    F(x) = a x + c (mod m)

can one classify exactly, for every modulus and every affine parameter pair,
when the entire finite ring is one primitive orbit?  Can the same theorem
close the prime-power return mechanism, CRT assembly, fixed counts, source
Artin--Mazur zeta, and canonical Koopman spectrum without promoting any of
them into target arithmetic or a Hilbert--Polya claim?

The frozen answer is the Hull--Dobell criterion together with a self-contained
local-to-global proof.  The arithmetic relation is weak: primes occur as
factors of the finite modulus, not as a rational-prime primitive-orbit
dictionary, and the clock is one update rather than log p.
