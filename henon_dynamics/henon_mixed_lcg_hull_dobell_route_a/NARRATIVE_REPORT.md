# Narrative report

C258 makes one complete step rather than splitting a finite-map calculation
across several papers.  For every modulus m, it proves exactly when the affine
update x maps to a x+c consists of one cycle through all residues.  Necessity
comes from transitivity on every prime quotient and the special quotient
modulo four.  Sufficiency comes from an exact p-adic valuation of the return
gap on each prime-power factor, followed by CRT.

The same proof gives a complete primitive ledger, the fixed-count sequence,
the rational source zeta 1/(1-t^m), and the full spectrum of the canonical
counting-measure Koopman permutation.  It also counts all admissible parameter
pairs: phi(m)m/L, where L is the least common multiple of rad(m) and the
required factor four.

This is real arithmetic structure, so A0 is weak rather than absent.  It is
still far from the Route-A target: prime factors are quotient coordinates,
not globally selected primitive orbits; periods are prime powers rather than
logarithmic prime clocks; and there is no target divisor, continuation,
functional equation, or operator identification.
