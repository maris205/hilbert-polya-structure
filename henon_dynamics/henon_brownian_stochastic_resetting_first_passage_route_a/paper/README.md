# C214 manuscript build

`main.tex` is compiled with LuaLaTeX under a fixed `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`.
`main_round0_original.pdf`, `main_round1.pdf`, and `main_round2.pdf` are
substantively different revisions; `main.pdf` is byte-identical to round 2 at
release.  The paper keeps free resetting on `R` and killed search on
`(-infinity,a)` separate, states both exact moment identities, and names the
first-passage denominator a renewal resolvent rather than a dynamical zeta.
