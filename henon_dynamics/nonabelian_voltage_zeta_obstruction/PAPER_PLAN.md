# Paper plan

## Working title

**Aggregation, Zero Density, and Branch-Pole Return in Nonabelian
Artin--Ihara Models**

## One-sentence contribution

Canonical regular-minus-trivial aggregation coarsens nonabelian holonomy to
element order, every fixed finite-memory representation repair has only
linear divisor growth, and the primitive Schrödinger sectors of the natural
Heisenberg congruence tower still return to the trivial branching poles.

## Section outline

1. **Introduction.** State the three-way obstruction and its role in
   dynamical Hilbert--Pólya searches.
2. **Background and prior boundaries.** Separate standard Artin--Ihara
   factorization, Heisenberg graph spectra, and exponential-sum theory from
   the project-specific synthesis.
3. **Canonical aggregation and chronology.** Prove the order-only formula and
   present the same-bigram \(H_7\) witness.
4. **Finite-memory zero density.** Prove the exponential-polynomial theorem
   and the Riemann divisor corollary.
5. **Congruence-tower branch return.** Prove both exact-conductor results,
   emphasizing the primitive nonabelian block.
6. **Exact computational certificates.** Describe D4 factorization, the
   independent group check, and which tables are illustrative.
7. **Hilbert--Pólya assessment and limitations.** State Route-A failure and
   unclosed escape routes.
8. **Conclusion.** Record the design rule and next system-level pivot.
9. **Appendix.** Full Jensen constants, word ledgers, D4 factors, and
   reproducibility details.

## Claim language

Use “we derive,” “we exhibit,” and “we combine” for the obstruction package.
Do not say that the general Artin decomposition, Heisenberg graph zeta, or
zero theory is new. Call the \(H_7\) pair an explicit chronology witness and
the tower result a scoped primitive-sector obstruction.
