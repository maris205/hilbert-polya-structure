\section{Hilbert--Pólya assessment and limitations}
\label{sec:assessment}

The three obstructions answer different versions of the same proposed
construction:
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
\text{canonical aggregation}
&\Longrightarrow&
\text{conjugacy data coarsened to order},\\[1mm]
\text{fixed finite resolution}
&\Longrightarrow&
O(T)\text{ divisor count},\\[1mm]
\text{natural amenable tower}
&\Longrightarrow&
\text{primitive branch-pole return}.
\end{array}
\]
None of these implications substitutes an averaged matrix for chronological
dynamics. The first theorem evaluates ordered holonomy before aggregation;
the second allows arbitrary fixed finite-memory transition blocks; the third
uses exact-conductor representations at every level.

\paragraph{Route-A ruling.}
The primitive graph cycles and their representation phases are exact, but the
clock is finite word or roof length rather than an intrinsic logarithmic
prime clock. The divisor mismatch is a theorem for every fixed model in the
registered class. The canonical finite determinant has no Gamma completion,
Riemann functional equation, or Riemann--von Mangoldt law. Twisted
nonbacktracking matrices are generally nonnormal rather than self-adjoint.
The primitive Schrödinger blocks are self-adjoint, but their spectra return to
the graph branching edge and do not define one compact-resolvent operator
with the Riemann divisor. The resulting scoped score is
\[
(\mathrm{A1\_WEAK},\mathrm{A2\_FAIL},
\mathrm{A3\_FAIL},\mathrm{A4\_FORMAL\_HINT}),
\]
so this model is rejected as a Hilbert--Pólya candidate.

\paragraph{What remains open.}
The result does not exclude a nonamenable congruence tower with a uniform
new-sector gap, provided that the tower also produces one canonical infinite
determinant. It does not exclude nuclear transfer operators for
non-locally-constant roofs, countably many intrinsic lengths, or joint
archimedean/nonarchimedean flows. Those objects must still prove convergence,
operator domains, divisor completeness, and the absence of hidden target
data. A family of finite matrices indexed by a cutoff is not yet one spectral
operator.

\paragraph{Novelty boundary.}
The regular-representation factorization, finite Heisenberg graph zetas,
Heisenberg/Harper spectral blocks, and zero theory of exponential sums are
established \citep{sato2006weighted,dedeo2003spectra,dedeo2005heisenberg,
beguin1997harper,langer1931zeros}. The claims of this paper are the explicit
same-bigram chronology witness, the scoped finite-memory Hilbert--Pólya
exclusion, and an explicit conductor-resolved Rayleigh certificate used to
rule out the frozen uniform new-sector-gap proposal. This positioning is
narrower than a new graph-zeta or Harper theorem and stronger than a finite
numerical mismatch.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Deleting a trivial representation is an algebraic subtraction, not a
guarantee that trivial dynamics has disappeared. In nonabelian voltage
models, canonical subtraction coarsens same-order holonomies; finite
representation resolution cannot supply the required global zero density;
and the amenable Heisenberg tower recreates near-trivial poles in primitive
nonabelian sectors. A viable next construction must change the structure,
not the finite-group parameter. The most direct remaining tests are
nonamenable arithmetic joint flows and infinite-rank determinants whose
intrinsic roof spectrum and operator theory are defined before comparison
with \(\xi\).
