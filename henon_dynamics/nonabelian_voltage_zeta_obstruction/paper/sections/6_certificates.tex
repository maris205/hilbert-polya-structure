\section{Exact computational certificates}
\label{sec:certificates}

The computations test conventions and finite identities; none is used to
infer an asymptotic theorem. All group products and determinants are exact.
Floating-point numbers appear only in illustrative tables.

\subsection{D4 representation-factorization control}

Take the two-generator bouquet with directed edges
\((a,A,b,B)\), inverse pairs \((a,A)\), \((b,B)\), and deck group
\[
D_4=\langle r,s:r^4=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle,
\]
with voltages \(r,r^{-1},s,s\). For the two-dimensional representation
\[
\rho(r)=
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\]
the exact two-roof twisted Hashimoto determinant is
\[
\begin{split}
D_2(x,y)
={}&(y-1)(y+1)\\
&\times(3x^2y-x^2-y-1)
\times(3x^2y+x^2-y+1).
\end{split}
\]
Here \(x\) weights \(a,A\) and \(y\) weights \(b,B\). At \(x=y=u\),
\[
D_2(u)=
(u-1)^2(u+1)^2
\times(3u^2-2u+1)(3u^2+2u+1).
\]

The direct \(32\times32\) regular-cover determinant equals the product of the
four one-dimensional factors and \(D_2(u)^2\):
\[
\begin{split}
D_{\rm cover}(u)
={}&(u-1)^9(u+1)^9(3u-1)(3u+1)(3u^2+1)^2\\
&\times(3u^2-2u+1)^2(3u^2+2u+1)^2.
\end{split}
\]
This exact equality checks orientation, inverse edges, and the required
regular multiplicities.

\subsection{Heisenberg chronology and aggregation}

The producer verifies all statements in Proposition~\ref{prop:witness}. An
independent implementation does not import the producer. It enumerates all
343 elements of \(H_7\), finds that right multiplication by either
\(z^3\) or \(z^2\) has exactly 49 cycles of length seven, and hence recovers
\[
\det(I-q\Reg(z^3))
=\det(I-q\Reg(z^2))
=(1-q^7)^{49}.
\]
The resolved phase ledgers differ, as predicted.
