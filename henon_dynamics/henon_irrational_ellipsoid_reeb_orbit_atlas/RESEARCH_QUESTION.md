# Research question — HCS-C242

For the standard contact form on \(\partial E(a,b)\), can the irrational and
rational slope regimes be certified in one finite, replayable atlas that
records every coordinate iterate's action, period, transverse multiplier and
Conley--Zehnder index, while explicitly identifying the rational
Morse--Bott degeneracy?

Answer: yes for the source-local Reeb theorem (A1 analytic); no arithmetic
origin or target determinant is supplied (A0/A2 fail).
