# Research question

Can the P55 higher-block regularity gate be converted into one explicit
all-period relation family, and does its first new width-four member already
contradict the H6 Galois-excess assignment?

The scoped subquestions are:

1. Find natural primitive families whose `m`-block incidence rows obey an
   identity for every `m` rather than by isolated enumeration.
2. Derive the exact period-six orbit and multiplier field needed by the first
   new relation.
3. Decide the forced excess equality by exact inequalities.
4. Test finite sharpness one width later.
5. State, without promotion, the resulting necessary condition for a
   one-sided Hölder realization.

The project does not ask for rational primes, Riemann zeros, a completed
determinant or a self-adjoint operator.
