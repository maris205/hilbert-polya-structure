# Methodology blueprint

## Frozen input

- H6 and its four-state certified survivor are inherited unchanged.
- Galois excess uses the P54 Mahler-height normalization.
- P55's period-three through period-five algebra is hash-locked.
- No fitted potential, prime data or zero data are allowed.

## Exact symbolic lane

1. Enumerate primitive cycles through period six independently.
2. Define `A_m=0^(m-2)21` and `B_m=0^(m-3)231`.
3. Compare consecutive cyclic block rows after inserting one zero.
4. Prove the common three-atom difference and therefore the all-`m` ladder.

## Exact algebra lane

1. Solve the reflection-reduced period-six recurrence.
2. Multiply exact monodromy matrices.
3. Compute trace and multiplier minimal polynomials.
4. Isolate the conjugate half-trace by integer square comparisons.
5. Reuse the locked period-five trace intervals.

## Obstruction and sharpness lane

1. Lower-bound both period-five excesses by the same nonphysical root.
2. Upper-bound the period-four and period-six excesses.
3. Reduce strictness to an integer comparison of logarithm arguments.
4. Compute a selected width-five incidence minor and its inverse.

## Claim firewall

- A finite width-four failure is not a general Hölder no-go.
- Width-five interpolation is allowed to use signed real coefficients.
- The ladder gives a one-sided necessary condition only.
- A two-sided statement needs a future-dependent cohomological reduction.
- Route A and Route B can advance only through their evaluators.
