\section{Frozen survivor and cyclic incidence}

We retain without refitting the area-preserving map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:henon}
 H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q),\qquad \det DH_6=1,
\end{equation}
and the certified mixing four-state survivor from HCS-P31
\citep{Henon1976,WangP31}.  Its labels and adjacency matrix are
\[
 0={--},\quad1={-+},\quad2={+-},\quad3={++},
 \qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}
 1&0&1&0\\1&0&0&0\\0&1&0&1\\0&1&0&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

For a cyclic word \(\gamma=(x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1})\), let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block-count}
 \Nblk_r(\gamma;w)
 =\#\{0\leq j<n:(x_j,\ldots,x_{j+r-1})=w\},
\end{equation}
with indices modulo \(n\).  A width-\(r\) potential
\(\varphi(x)=v(x_0,\ldots,x_{r-1})\) has periodic sum
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block-pairing}
 \Birk_\gamma\varphi
 =\sum_w v(w)\Nblk_r(\gamma;w).
\end{equation}
Consequently every linear relation among cyclic incidence rows forces the
same relation among the periodic sums of every width-\(r\) potential.

For a primitive orbit, let \(M_\gamma\in\mathrm{SL}_2\) be its chronological
monodromy and \(\Lambda_\gamma>1\) its physical expanding modulus.  If
\(f_\gamma\) is the monic minimal polynomial of the signed multiplier, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:excess}
 \mathcal H_\gamma=\log M(f_\gamma),\qquad
 \ell_\gamma=\log\Lambda_\gamma,\qquad
 \Egal_\gamma=\mathcal H_\gamma-\ell_\gamma.
\end{equation}
Reciprocity pairs the roots of \(f_\gamma\); all expanding nonphysical pairs
contribute nonnegative terms to \(\Egal_\gamma\), as certified in HCS-P54.
