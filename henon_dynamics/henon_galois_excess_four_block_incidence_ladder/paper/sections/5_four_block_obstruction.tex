\section{The four-block obstruction}

The two period-five words \(A_5=00021\) and \(B_5=00231\) belong to the
same degree-six trace field certified in HCS-P55.  Its polynomial is
\begin{align}\label{eq:q5}
 Q_5(T)={}&T^6+3300T^5-34165368T^4-7291075328T^3\notag\\
 &+26529205510272T^2+3609165326736384T
 -4266315336505009664.
\end{align}
It has exactly one root in each interval
\begin{equation}\label{eq:q5-intervals}
 (-7607,-7606),\ (-711,-710),\ (-590,-589),\
 (390,391),\ (770,771),\ (4445,4446).
\end{equation}
The physical roots for \(A_5\) and \(B_5\) lie in the first and last
intervals respectively.  Therefore the root in \((-711,-710)\) is
nonphysical for both, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p5-lower}
 \Egal_{A_5}>\acosh355,
 \qquad
 \Egal_{B_5}>\acosh355.
\end{equation}

For \(A_4=0021\), the only nonphysical half-trace is
\(287-96\sqrt6\in(51,52)\), whence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:a4-upper}
 \Egal_{A_4}<\acosh52.
\end{equation}
Equation \eqref{eq:b6-isolation} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:b6-upper}
 \Egal_{B_6}<\acosh1951.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[four-block no-go]\label{thm:four-block}
No locally constant potential depending on at most four consecutive H6
states has periodic sums \(\Egal_\gamma\) on every primitive orbit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \(x>1\),
\[
 \log(2x-1)<\acosh x<\log(2x),
\]
because \(x-1<\sqrt{x^2-1}<x\).  Moreover
\[
 709^2=502681>405808=104\cdot3902.
\]
Consequently
\[
 2\acosh355>2\log709
 >\log104+\log3902
 >\acosh52+\acosh1951.
\]
Combining this with \eqref{eq:p5-lower}--\eqref{eq:b6-upper} proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:strict-obstruction}
 \Egal_{A_5}+\Egal_{B_5}
 >\Egal_{A_4}+\Egal_{B_6}.
\end{equation}
But Theorem \ref{thm:ladder} at \(m=4\) forces equality for every width-four
potential.  A smaller-width potential is also a width-four potential after
ignoring the extra coordinates, so every width at most four is excluded.
\end{proof}

For scale only, the signed ladder discrepancy with left-minus-right
convention is
\[
 \Egal_{A_4}+\Egal_{B_6}-\Egal_{A_5}-\Egal_{B_5}
 =-55.5475038022260\ldots .
\]
The theorem rests on the exact logarithmic chain, not this decimal.
