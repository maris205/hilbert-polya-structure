# ROUND2_CLUE

The insertion identity has removed the symbolic search problem.  The next
high-value target is an algebraic or asymptotic law for

```text
Delta_m = E(A_m)+E(B_(m+2))-E(A_(m+1))-E(B_(m+1)).
```

Natural attacks, in order:

1. derive exact `A6`, `A7`, `B7` trace fields and evaluate `Delta_5`;
2. search for a recurrence in the reflection-reduced coordinates or traces;
3. compare both families as periodic approximants to their limiting
   homoclinic words;
4. prove either exponential decay with a candidate rate or a lower bound
   incompatible with every Hölder rate.

This remains within the Hénon family.  Any analogous ladder in another
dynamical system is only a future cross-family clue.
