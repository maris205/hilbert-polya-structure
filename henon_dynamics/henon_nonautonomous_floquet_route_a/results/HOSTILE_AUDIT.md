# Hostile audit

Ten semantic mutations were attempted and all were rejected by the independent
checker: schema, scope literal, primitive word, chronological matrix,
reversed trace, chronological transfer trace, reversed determinant coefficient,
primitive decomposition, Route-A assessment, and the Fredholm nonclaim.

No mutation test reads external prime or zero data.  The exact original bytes
are never rewritten by the mutation harness, so the release evidence hash is
stable after the audit.
