# Paper plan

**Claim.** A period-two quadratic Hénon cocycle admits an exact finite
Floquet/monodromy prefix in which forcing order is visible.

**Evidence.** Twenty-four admissible primitive necklaces through block period
six, three independently checked 8-by-8 transfer prefixes, 17 rows with a
chronological-versus-reversed trace difference, and exact determinant/Newton
checks.

**Boundary.** The branch language and samples are frozen modeling choices;
there is no geometric coding theorem or Fredholm operator.

**Sections.** Candidate and conventions; primitive ledger; Floquet controls and
trace identity; determinant prefix; reproducibility and hostile tests;
Route-A assessment and next experiments.
