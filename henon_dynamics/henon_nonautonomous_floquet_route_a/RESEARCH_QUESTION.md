# Research question

Can a period-two Hénon cocycle retain a reproducible primitive-word and
monodromy structure when the chronological forcing order is changed?

The narrow C110 question is deliberately falsifiable: after freezing two
quadratic Hénon phases, branch samples, a four-symbol cyclic adjacency, and a
block-period cutoff of six, do the chronological, reversed, and same-phase
controls produce exact but different Floquet trace prefixes while preserving
the primitive/repetition identity?

This question does not ask whether the frozen language codes the actual real
Hénon invariant set.  That geometric statement is an explicit open item and
is not used in the paper's conclusions.
