# Paper plan

The manuscript is one closed note rather than five incremental fragments.

* Revision 0 introduces the frozen PDE, the tanh front, first integral, and
  strict equal-well speed selection.
* Revision 1 adds the gradient-flow energy identity, surface energy, and the
  translation-uniqueness proof.
* Revision 2 adds the Pöschl--Teller factorization, discrete/essential spectrum,
  singular boundaries, and the Route-A decision.

The final text distinguishes exact identities from numerical probes and states
that no primitive periodic orbit is present.
