# HCS-C231 research question

Can one close an exact, source-local dynamical result for the equal-well
Allen--Cahn reaction--diffusion equation, including its heteroclinic front,
speed selection, gradient-flow energy ledger, and one-dimensional linear
spectrum, while testing whether the object supplies a Route-A primitive
periodic-orbit owner?

The answer is deliberately split.  The front and Pöschl--Teller spectrum close
exactly.  The heteroclinic has no primitive periodic orbit or cross-period
repetition law, so the arithmetic/Route-B bridge is rejected.
