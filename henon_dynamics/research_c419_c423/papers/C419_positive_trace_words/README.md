The independent question is the full ordinary integer-periodic locus for
every positive word containing both trace-map generators, not a census for
one word or the locus of finite whole-group orbits. The answer is 39 affine
lines plus four points, an exact at-most-40-state classifier on every level,
and proper forward/backward escape for every other integer point. The bound
40 is not asserted to be an attained period or an attained periodic count.

All central proofs appear in the PDF. The source cycle determinant is not a
target Euler product, root number, automorphic or Hilbert–Pólya construction.
Internal AI-assisted review is not human peer review or worldwide-priority
certification.
