## Contribution and scope

Classify every level N for which the complete weight-zero,
trivial-character cusp scattering family of Gamma_0(N) commutes pairwise
in fixed width-one cusp coordinates at all regular complex parameters.
The result includes both nonreal-square and odd-exponent quartic-phase
obstructions, the converse, and exact elementary level conditions.

The manuscript is an anonymous English 11pt mathematical article with
one-inch margins. It is standalone relative to explicitly cited classical
Eisenstein and Dirichlet functional-equation inputs. Every new proof,
including the all-exponent principal algebra, appears in the article.
No page quota, synthetic experiment, decorative illustration or journal
acceptance claim is imposed.
