## NG1: symmetric generalized character surface

Object: for every integer a, T_a(x,y,z)=(y,z,yz+a-x) on Z^3. The invariant
is K_a=x^2+y^2+z^2-xyz-a(x+y+z); all integer levels are included. The native
clock is one T_a iterate. Observable: ordinary integral cycles, including
singular points, with no quotient by signs or reversal and no local lengths.

Question: classify every periodic point for all a, without coordinate,
period or invariant-level cutoff. Arithmetic carrier: integral character
coordinates, the integral cubic invariant and discrete escape versus a
recurrent arithmetic core. Proposed residual increment: an all-a complete
core classification, not a finite-box algorithm or a coefficient table.

Deduct C413's a=0 Fibonacci integral classification, C402's weighted complex
residue trace, C416's different discrete-sine polynomial family, Roberts'
escape theory and Cantat--Loray's character-surface dynamics. The earlier
local generalized-character scout already left the all-parameter core
unclosed. Jang's tropical model, Abboud's periodic-set rigidity and
Iwasaki--Uehara's weighted isolated indices must not be renamed as this answer.

Cheap falsifier: verify the invariant, compare the diagonal fixed-point
condition a=2t-t^2 with any parameter-independent core guess, and inspect
whether the a=0 escape argument survives the forcing term. Success requires
a new uniform exhaustive mechanism. Stop if the only output is the old
unclosed core or an elementary height bound; do not enlarge a coefficient box.

## NG2: all coefficient-free rank-two rational cluster returns

For b,c in Z_{>=1}, put u=(1+y^b)/x and
Phi_{b,c}(x,y)=(u,(1+u^c)/y). Domain U_{b,c}(Q): rational nonzero pairs
whose entire two-sided alternating exchange sequence stays defined and
nonzero. This is the maximal invariant native torus domain, not all of
(Q^*)^2. The clock is one pair of mutations; the alternating scalar clock
is twice this clock. Observable: ordinary rational periodic points.

Question: classify Per(Phi_{b,c},U_{b,c}(Q)) for every b,c, including the
finite-type and affine transition. Arithmetic carrier: valuations of the
exchange recurrence, integrality, signs and growth. Proposed increment:
complete signed rational specialization, if substantial after deductions.

Deduct Fomin--Zelevinsky's finite-type periodic cluster variables,
Blanc--Dolgachev's cluster-surface automorphisms, C408's alternating-zero
local lengths, and the prior local cluster boundary scouts. C402/C413/C416
have different objects but their finite-state/escape templates alone are
not a new contribution. This map is not the arithmetic lane's Somos-4 QRT
map; no elliptic-translation/Mazur contract is claimed here.

Cheap falsifier: symbolic finite-type return identities, valuation minima
and a maximum-coordinate argument in the diagonal b=c>=3 subfamily;
look for elementary sign reductions in mixed parity. Success requires a
substantial all-b,c rational classification, not just classical finite type.

## NG3: one fixed Coxeter return on Markoff-type K3 surfaces

For all integers k let W_k(Q) be the affine rational solutions of
x^2+y^2+z^2+x^2 y^2 z^2+kxyz=0. Define
sigma_x(x,y,z)=(-x-kyz/(1+y^2 z^2),y,z), and cyclic analogues.
The single map is F_k=sigma_z o sigma_y o sigma_x; one whole composition
is one clock step. All denominators are positive on Q^3, so the affine
rational domain is invariant. Include singular affine points, including
the origin; count ordinary points, not points or indices on a resolution.
The k=0 degenerate member is included as an arithmetic boundary, not called
a smooth K3 surface. Nonzero members have the classical K3 minimal models.

Question: classify every F_k-periodic rational point for all k. Arithmetic
carrier: rational K3 points, quadratic projection involutions, heights and
the change from conic to genus-one fibral geometry. Proposed increment:
single-word rational exhaustiveness, not the already studied finite
whole-group orbits or finite-field census.

Deduct Fuchs--Litman--Silverman--Tran's exact W_k family and orbit machinery,
Cantat--Dujardin's large-group finite-orbit theory, and Hutz/Silverman
arithmetic K3 periodicity infrastructure. C413 and the two-twist reserve
are cubic character dynamics, C408 is a different cyclic relation scheme,
C402 is weighted Hénon residue closure and C416 is discrete sine. None
of those supplies this complete K3 rational classification.

Cheap falsifier: verify each involution and invariant algebraically; use
AM--GM to classify the |k|<=4 real boundary and determine whether that
already accounts for the only provable slice. Inspect whether finite-group
source statements actually constrain this one Coxeter word. Stop if no
uniform single-word arithmetic height/core argument exists; do not replace
it by another finite-field table, a group-orbit claim or a torus quotient.
