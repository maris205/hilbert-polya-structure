## Primary sources actually read, and exact limits

### Jang, Tropical Dynamics of Markov Surfaces

Read [v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.11357v2): main statements and §3's
comparison domain, Proposition 3.1, equation (3.5), Definition 3.2 and
nearby skeleton statements. This pass did not read the full 64-page
proof. The required valuation comparison excludes cancellation ties;
our explicit bridge failure respects that exclusion. Theorem C is a
whole-group, prime-power-denominator result, not the required native
classification. The [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11357)
confirms 18 December 2025 and says v2 corrects Theorem C from v1.

### Cantat--Jang, Orbits of automorphism groups of affine surfaces over p-adic fields

Read [the primary v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08579v1), Theorem A,
§8's parameter assumptions and Theorem E; nearby §8.1 and §9.2 were also
inspected. The applications concern group actions and integral surface
parameters. They do not classify one cyclic loxodromic map at negative
valuation of `D`. No result from the proof details or the open uniformity
question is promoted to a new lemma here. The
[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08579) is dated 11 October 2024.

### Jang, Residual Transitivity implies Minimality for Markoff Surfaces over p-adic Integers, by Means of p-adic Flows

Read the [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.18976v1), surface/action
definitions and complete Theorem 1, and inspected the
[HTML introduction and §7.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18976v1).
The theorem requires an integral parameter, a nonsingular integral
locus, specified prime/congruence assumptions and whole-group
transitivity. None is an all-rational single-map classification.
Date control matters: the PDF says February 2025; the HTML prints an
August 2026 revision date, while the arXiv record lists only February
2025 v1. This discrepancy is not evidence of a new 2026 theorem.

### Cantat--Loray, Dynamics on Character Varieties and Malgrange irreducibility of Painlevé VI equation

Read [the primary 2009 PDF](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2009__59_7_2927_0.pdf),
Theorem C and the nearby bounded-orbit discussion. Its bounded infinite
orbit is a whole-group orbit over the complex/real character variety.
It does not impose that hypothesis on every finite orbit of one chosen
map. A recent crawl/upload label is not a 2026 publication date.

Roberts' author-hosted papers were rediscovered as classical leads. No
new full Roberts theorem verification is claimed in this round. No
unread paper is credited with a rational classification or a novelty
exclusion.
