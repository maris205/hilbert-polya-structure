## Unchanged complete contract

For every integer `a`, classify the whole ordinary rational periodic
locus of

\[
T_a(x,y,z)=(y,z,yz+a-x),\qquad (x,y,z)\in\mathbb Q^3,
\]

on every rational level of
`K_a = x^2+y^2+z^2-xyz-a(x+y+z)`, including zero coordinates and singular
ordinary points. One application of `T_a` is one time step. Least
periods, not periods of a group word or quotient, are the observable;
oriented cycles are identified only by cyclic phase. No fixed-denominator,
fixed-level, bounded-period, finite-prime-set or nonsingular-only question
may replace this contract.

The accepted square-denominator lemma and all integral classifications
are inputs, not work to repeat. Known short periodic curves are deducted.
No fourth-paper split of these inputs or of the present obstructions is
permitted.

## One new mechanism, with two possible bridges

The proposed mechanism is a **uniform classification of cancellation
channels**, needed between a non-Archimedean maximum/valuation restriction
and an exact all-rational periodic-word classification. It must control
the actual residues in Vieta cancellation loci, not just the valuation
vector. A successful proof must show that these channels force explicit
finite short-period families, or supply a different complete native
classification, uniformly in every prime and denominator.

This is a missing lemma mechanism, not an asserted theorem. Only these
two bridges may be examined in this pass:

1. **S: skeletal escape plus exceptional-channel rigidity.** Identify
   `T_a^3` as the correct ordered Vieta word. Use the actual scope of
   Jang's tropical/valuation comparison to exclude periodic trajectories
   where comparison really holds. Then classify *all* excluded
   cancellation-channel periodic trajectories. It is insufficient to
   cite hyperbolic dynamics on a dense open subset of the skeleton and
   silently include ordinary points outside it.
2. **D: whole-denominator synchronization.** A possible elementary
   replacement for the exceptional-channel theorem would coordinate the
   primewise large-coordinate phases and their residues into an exact,
   denominator-independent return relation. The tempting intermediate
   assertion that primewise good phases imply one phase with two rational
   integer neighbours is explicitly subjected to a counterexample gate.
   Removing that assertion requires a genuinely different synchronization
   lemma, not a larger state alphabet indexed by `q`.

No third proof route or adjacent contract is authorized by this freeze.

## Cheap falsifier gates, before any CPU

The known `a=0` family

\[
u=1+r,\qquad v=1+r^{-1},\qquad r\in\mathbb Q^*,\qquad
T_0(u,v,u)=(v,u,v)
\]

will be used as a challenge, not as a new result.

- **S gate:** take `r=1/q`, any integer `q>=2`, and a prime `p|q`.
  Compare the actual valuation of both phases with the skeleton equation
  and with the tropical Vieta formula at the first cancellation step.
  Failure of equality blocks any blanket tropical conjugacy argument;
  a repaired argument must cover the full exceptional locus, not merely
  this already-known curve.
- **D gate:** take `r=2/3`, so the two scalar coordinates are `5/3` and
  `5/2`. Compare primes `2` and `3` and ask whether any cyclic phase has
  even one rational integer coordinate. This tests exchanging
  `for every prime there exists a phase` with `there exists a phase for
  every prime`.

These checks need only exact substitutions and valuations. A small
symbolic verification could be used if it clarifies a disputed formula,
but no numerical search is needed or planned.
