Rational Map in Positive Characteristic*. Inspected
[arXiv:1306.5267v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.5267v2), revised 2014-02-24;
the [publisher record](https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/jtnb.941/)

Actual scope: §§1–2 through the definitions and classification discussion;

zeta theorems and Conjecture 1.6 were distinguished. The root-of-unity

- WHAT: the explicit $b_3$ lift is covered; the $p\geq5$ family fails

## 4. Source-substance conclusion and limitations

The original sources own the classifications and local ramification
criteria. The direct symbolic additions are a small-prime lift,
zero-residue applicability check, and explicit rotational defect family.
These identify why a plausible route fails; they do not classify the
remaining cycles or establish a zeta analytic type. No short certificate
is promoted to a fourth or fifth contract.

`DISTRIBUTIONAL_SKEW_ADVISORY`: all three included sources are theoretical
mathematics (3/3 known methods). This is intentional for an exact theorem
question. The two residue papers share an author and related machinery;
they are not independent empirical confirmations. Their complementarity
is the different theorem range. Publication-index quartiles, complete
COI/retraction checks and subscription predatory-journal databases were
not checked; no “clean source” certificate or numerical quality score is
claimed. The medical evidence-level ladder is not used to rank
mathematical proofs.

Only the named passages were read. Search-result snippets from honors
theses, unrelated recent works, unofficial book mirrors and question
sites were not used as theorem evidence. This screen neither settles
Bridy's conjecture nor proves that a relevant result is absent from all
Nottingham or rational-dynamics literature.

The lane recommends **zero new admissions**. `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`
remains in force; none of these source-system statements supplies target
Euler factors, root numbers or a Hilbert–Pólya realization.
