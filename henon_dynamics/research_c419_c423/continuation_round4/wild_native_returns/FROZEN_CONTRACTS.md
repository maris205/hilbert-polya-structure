## Common mathematical convention

For every odd prime $p$, let $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. The state space is
the entire $\mathbb P^1(k)$, including poles and infinity; an informal
description as an open-curve map does not delete points. The clock is
ordinary composition, $f^{\circ n}$ for all integers $n\geq1$.

The requested observable is the number $N_f(n)$ of **distinct geometric
fixed points** of $f^{\circ n}$, with ordinary Artin–Mazur series
$\zeta_f(t)=\exp(\sum_{n\geq1}N_f(n)t^n/n)$. Local displacement
multiplicity is an auxiliary input, not the count. At infinity it is
computed in the coordinate $u=1/x$. A weighted count, fixed finite-field
graph or Frobenius time clock is not a substitute.
