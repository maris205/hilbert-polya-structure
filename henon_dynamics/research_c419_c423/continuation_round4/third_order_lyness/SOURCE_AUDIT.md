### P2. Cima–Gasull–Mañosa, the exact 2-integral and order reduction

28 January 2008. The initial PDF metadata/find accesses were followed

Actually read the introduction/main statements through Theorem 2 in
full, the proof of Theorem 2 in §3 and the adjacent Lemmas 5–6, and

Lie-symmetry proof in §2 and later higher-dimensional results were not

we do not claim that all positive-domain geometric statements transfer.
