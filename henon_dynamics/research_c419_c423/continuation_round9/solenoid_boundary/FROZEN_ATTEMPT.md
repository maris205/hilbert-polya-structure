## New mechanism under test

allowed projective input to one residue. Thus each word operator in
$T_{\chi,k}^m$ has rank at most one. This should bound the total
algebraic multiplicity of nonzero eigenvalues by $F_{m+2}$, where
$F_1=F_2=1$ are Fibonacci numbers, despite the $2^k$ ambient dimension.

If correct, the normalized spectra define a finite positive atomic
measure with weights $2^{-k}/|U_k|$. Its Cauchy-type transform is the
actual full logarithmic-derivative series $Q(u)=uP'(u)$ inside the
unit disk. The aim is to use the exact measure to test continuation
or surviving singularities, not to substitute a new observable.

The concrete danger is cancellation: even an absolutely summable
positive atomic spectral measure might have identically zero positive
moments while its support accumulates everywhere on the unit circle.
