## Access and ownership

- [Bell–Miles–Ward, *Towards a Pólya–Carlson dichotomy for algebraic dynamics*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.2369):
  primary PDF introduction, Theorem 9, Theorem 15 and Lemma 17 were
  actually read. These results concern fixed algebraic automorphisms
  and fixed characteristic numbers with finite sets of places. The
  switching trace sum has not been put into those hypotheses; the
  theorem is not a proof of its whole secondary circle. The restriction
  about archimedean unit-modulus conjugates is not described as a
  still-open general obstruction, because the next source removes it.
- [Baril Boudreau–Holmes–Nguyen, *Adelic perturbation of rational functions and applications*](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.07910v1):
  primary record, introduction, Theorem 2.14, Theorem 4.6 and Remark 4.7
  were read. The latter resolves the extra-conjugate-condition issue
  for finite fixed characteristic data. The former requires a stable
  rational power series and an almost quasi-polynomial multiplier.
  No such all-word factorization/multiplier proof is available here.
  Thus this is an applicability boundary, not a claim that the cited
  theorem is defective or that the source's solved problem remains open.
- [Mirotin–Atvinovskii, *Multiplicative inverse for Wolff–Denjoy Series*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.08029):
  version 2, abstract, introduction and Theorem 1 were read. The
  positivity result assumes a bounded monotone sequence of **real**
  poles. It cannot supply complex spectral noncancellation. No theorem
  from it is imported into the recursive construction.
- [Rubel–Shields, *The space of bounded analytic functions on a region*](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.231/):
  primary bibliographic record and bibliography were read. PDF discovery
  returned a 44-page record, but subsequent body/word retrieval returned
  Internal Error; its mathematical contents were **not** read. The
  bibliography identifies Brown–Shields–Zeller, *On absolutely convergent
  exponential sums* (1960). That latter paper was not opened. Neither
  source is claimed to prove the exact positive construction above.

Wolff–Denjoy series and analytic representing measures are classical
contexts, not a claimed new research territory. The displayed recursive
construction has a self-contained summability proof; no global priority
is claimed for it, its existence assertion or its elementary rank bound.
Third-party snippets that suggested these historical sources were only
locators and supply no technical assertion used in the proof.
