## Complete candidate and new mechanism

[FROZEN_QUESTION.md](FROZEN_QUESTION.md) asks to classify all a,b and all
characteristic-three fields L for which

\[
 \{\lambda\in\overline L:a,b\text{ are preperiodic for }
 X^4+X^6+\lambda\}
\]

is infinite. [PROOF_DRAFT.md](PROOF_DRAFT.md) proves the candidate answer:
exactly when both starting points are constants or f(a)=f(b).

The recent source supplies the reduction to f(b)-f(a)=0,+1,-1 and an
all-parameter local-height equality. The author contribution is the
uniform parameter

\[
 \lambda_*=1-a-f(a).
\]

For the exceptional sign +1, this gives the exact orbit segments

\[
 a\longmapsto1-a\longmapsto a,
 \qquad b\longmapsto-a-1\longmapsto0\longmapsto\lambda_*.
\]

At a pole |a|=C>1, the second orbit escapes and has local height
(log C)/36, while the first has height zero. This contradicts the
imported equality. Swapping a,b handles the other sign. The proof also
justifies the arbitrary-field reduction and directly establishes
infinitely many single-point periodic parameters using prime periods.
No finite sample is used as universal evidence.

The intended exploratory route was to analyze roots of
f(t)+t=f(a)+a and possible valuation cancellations. The exact root
t=1-a makes a Newton-polygon analysis unnecessary. An unrecorded
scratch expansion near t=a+1 was not used as evidence or retained as a
separate result. The final uniform identities were checked by hand.
