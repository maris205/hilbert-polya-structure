## CQ7: quantum-cat Hecke-character multiplicities

**Exact question.** For every hyperbolic $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$,
$|\operatorname{tr}A|>2$, and every odd $N\geq3$, take the standard finite
Weil representation $U_N$ on $\mathcal H_N=\ell^2(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)$.
Let $\alpha$ be a root of the characteristic polynomial of $A$, put
$\mathcal O_A=\mathbb Z[\alpha]$, and map $u+v\alpha\mapsto uI+vA$.
Define $C_A(N)$ as the **image** of the norm-one units of
$\mathcal O_A/N\mathcal O_A$ in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)$.
This is a commutative subgroup, not an assertion that the entire matrix
centralizer is commutative at a scalar or nonprimitive reduction.

The proposed observable is every multiplicity in
$\mathcal H_N=\bigoplus_\chi\mathcal H_{N,\chi}$, together with the
native one-step spectrum of $U_N(A)$ on those spaces. The structural
question includes ramified primes, nonmaximal orders and reductions with
a nontrivial kernel in the order-to-matrix map. It does not change the
time step, choose an eigenvalue branch to match a target zeta, or infer
target root numbers from source symmetries.

**Arithmetic mechanism.** This is finite quadratic-order/Weil character
theory. Before any implementation, the decisive test is whether a complete
classification has an increment beyond standard character projectors,
prime-power restriction theory and CRT.

For any fixed input, the character projector gives

$$m_\chi=\frac1{|C_A(N)|}\sum_{g\in C_A(N)}
\overline{\chi(g)}\operatorname{Tr}U_N(g).$$

Thus mere computability or exact finite diagonalization is already a short
classical consequence. No program or new algorithm theorem is offered.
The all-input closed structural formula was not proved in this screen.

**Ownership deduction and disposition.** The local C128/C131/C136 batch
records already own exact finite quantization, the all-odd metaplectic
family and CRT compatibility. Externally, Kurlberg–Rudnick introduced the
arithmetic symmetry/eigenfunction framework, and Kelmer supplies the
split/inert prime-power multiplicity and matrix-element framework.
Maktouf–Torasso is an additional maximal-elliptic-torus restriction lead,
not an automatically verified theorem about every nonmaximal finite order.
See the source scopes below.

No new mechanism survives those deductions in the work performed here.
The ramified/nonprimitive structural cases are **not** declared solved by
reading a generic theorem or an abstract. **STOP / NO ADMISSION:** do not
