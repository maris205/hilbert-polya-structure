## Important proof boundaries

### CR2 is an infinite fibre, not an integrality theorem

The invariant fibre

`x²y²+x²+y²+5xy=4`

is nonsingular and birational over the rationals to
`Y²=X³−74X²+1625X`. Its rational points all have least period three.
The proof treats every chart exclusion and uses an infinite-order
elliptic point to prove infinitude. This rules out both rational
integrality and finite-total-point shortcuts, even for a map that is
not globally finite-order. It does not enumerate the torsion fibres
throughout the varying parameter family. The [partial proof](cubic_recurrence/PARTIAL_PROOF.md)
keeps this distinction explicit.

### Finite completeness does not cross the parameter quantifier

B1's one exact divisor-graph run covers `1 ≤ c ≤ 512` with 6,980
potential states, **407** legal edges and eight fixed cycles. B2's one
exact run covers 272 pairs in `[-8,8]²`, `b ≠ 0`, with 229,520 potential
states, 34,997 legal edges, 71 fixed cycles and 16 two-cycles. These
finite graph extractions have no period cutoff but do have the stated
coefficient bounds. Their author receipts are not independent censuses.

The B2 identities for `b=2k`, `a=−4k²+k+2`, `k≥2`, give the exact
three-cycle word `(2k−1,−4k−1,−k−1)`. For `k≥3` it also avoids all
unit denominators. This refutes a global upgrade of the observed
period-1/2 sample; it does **not** refute the frozen at-most-three
test hypothesis, which remains unproved. The [report](birational_arithmetic/SCOUT_REPORT.md)
does not claim that periods 1, 2 and 3 exhaust the whole family.

### PC3-B is closed but source-heavy

For an ordinary elliptic curve in odd characteristic and the frozen
integer matrix family, the quotient count is the mean of the two
kernel counts for `A^n−I` and `A^n+I`. If `A mod p` is nilpotent,
there is no inseparable correction and exterior powers give rationality.
Otherwise a fixed subsequence makes the plus term an undistorted
recurrence while the minus term retains nonzero abelian distortion.
The accessed FAD criterion excludes recurrence of that minus term.
This supports the [short proof's](positive_charp_new/PROOF_PACKAGE.md)
rationality criterion, without importing an inapplicable scalar
valuation-ring assumption or claiming a natural boundary. The relevant
classical theorem supplies the mechanism, so there is no paper slot.

### AS1-R3 and RT3 do not replace their original contracts

AS1-R3's finite-state Cayley–Hamilton argument promotes each checked
scalar recurrence to all lengths **at that fixed layer**. It does not
promote six layer calculations to all depths, nor matrix poles to
singularities of the infinite layer sum. RT3's p-adic maximum proves
that the reduced invariant denominator is exactly `q²`, where `q`
is the common denominator of the entire periodic word. The resulting
integral-level extension inherits IR1; it is not a second independent
classification. Unbounded rational period-two denominators remain.
