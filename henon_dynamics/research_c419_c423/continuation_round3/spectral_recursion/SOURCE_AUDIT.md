## Three primary sources and their exact boundary

### Jiang--Wu, arXiv:2508.19374v2

Read the [official metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19374) and actual
[HTML introduction, Theorems 1.1--1.2 and initial section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.19374v2).
The official revision is 24 February 2026; the aggregator's displayed
date was not adopted. Theorem 1.2 concerns a subhyperbolic rational map
on $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Q_p)$ with a bounded locally analytic
$\mathbb C_p$-valued weight and a derivative-weighted repelling-point
series, meromorphic over $\mathbb C_p$. The authors explicitly explain
why local analyticity of these weights uses the non-Archimedean value
field. Our complex-valued, ordinary based-word determinant count is
not that series. No conjugacy or compatible weighted trace identity
has been established. Their theorem therefore does not supply a
complex continuation of AS1. The whole nine-page proof was not reviewed.

### Chen--Hu, arXiv:1503.02232v3

Read the [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02232), introduction
and section 1 through Theorems 1--3 and their stated setting, not the
entire 36-page proof. Their skew product has a smooth uniformly expanding
torus base and a smooth additive real torus-rotation cocycle. Their
anisotropic-space spectral-gap dichotomy is a substantial classical
input in that setting. Our two noncommuting determinant-eight residue
matrix updates and trace-one terminal observable have not been put in
that setting, nor have the necessary high-frequency estimates been
proved. A theorem title about compact extensions is not sufficient.

### Benoist--Quint, author manuscript of Random walks on reductive groups

The [Bordeaux PDF](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~jquint/publications/walk.pdf)
initially returned document metadata but subsequent web text requests
timed out. The [Montpellier author copy](https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~quint/Publications/walk.pdf)
was then downloaded once into a temporary source directory and extracted
with `pdftotext`. Read manuscript chapter 14, sections 14.1--14.3 through
Corollary 14.10, and checked the finite-dimensional real cocycle target
definition. The published book's chapter numbering is different; these
are manuscript labels, not asserted published labels.

Lemma 14.1 requires a contracting compact action over a finite group
and suitable cocycle moments. Lemma 14.3 characterizes peripheral
eigenfunctions by a cohomological equation; Proposition 14.8 identifies
the residual cocycle image. These statements do not identify our
trace-congruence terminal sequence with their twisted operator, prove
contractivity on its required space, or supply the annular summability
of our weighted conductor tower. We have no such reduction. Neither
the downloaded book as a whole nor all its dependencies were reviewed.

## Substance decision

Finite-state rationality and matrix characteristic factorization are
classical tools. The new six-layer calculation records their exact
application and observable cancellation; it is not a complete new
theorem answering AS1. No source above closes the missing all-layer
analytic step under verified hypotheses. Conversely, this bounded
search does not show that nobody has proved the desired statement.

Whole-contract recommendation: **do not admit**. The route remains
unclosed, not refuted. The source-level helper calculation creates no
target Euler factor, root number, automorphy or zero correspondence.
