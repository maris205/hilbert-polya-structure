## New bounded mathematical test

Instead of another state-count or floating-eigenvalue census, examine
the **exact characteristic polynomials and observable generating-function
denominators** of the congruence transfer graphs. Look for an explicit
factor recursion that can actually be proved at every precision, or a
uniform spectral separation that points to continuation away from the
two real directions. Either outcome must still be connected to the full
infinite weighted sum before an admission.

At modulus $2^k$, retain matrix product and last letter, start at the
identity sentinel, and permit $A$ except after $A$, and $B$ always.
The terminal condition is $\operatorname{tr}M\equiv1\pmod{2^k}$.
For $3n\ge k$ this is exactly the needed divisibility of
$\det(I-M)=8^n-\operatorname{tr}M+1$; earlier lengths are a finite
polynomial correction and must not be silently identified with it.

First diagnostic bound: $1\le k\le6$, at most 250,000 states, and no
floating spectral extrapolation. Existing pure graph routines may be
reused to construct the matrices needed for this genuinely different
observable; do not run the old main/check merely for another PASS.
The new output must distinguish full graph factors from eigenmodes
that the initial/terminal observable cancels.

If a rational recurrence is inferred from exact finite moments, certify
it by exact transfer-matrix identities or a dimension-sufficient
Cayley–Hamilton argument. A modular guess without exact certification
is not an exact denominator. Code/runtime and actual outcomes are
