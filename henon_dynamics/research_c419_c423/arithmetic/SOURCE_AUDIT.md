## AR1: rational conservative Hénon family

1. **Patrick Ingram, *Canonical heights for Hénon maps*.** The
   [arXiv version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3609) and
   [publisher article](https://academic.oup.com/plms/article-abstract/108/3/780/1571741)
   confirm *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society* 108 (2014),
   780–808, DOI 10.1112/plms/pdt026. The actually accessed proof version is
   [arXiv:1111.3609v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.3609v1), dated by its version
   history to 15 November 2011. Read: opening definitions/theorems, Conjecture
   1.5 on printed page 4, and the returned Lemma 7.2/parameter-search passage.
   Its concrete period conjecture and quadratic bad-place height result use
   the opposite Jacobian sign. Its square-denominator argument is a close
   predecessor to the cheap local reduction, not a new exact orbit atlas.
   The PDF's internal 2017 date is not substituted for the arXiv version date.

2. **Tadeusz Pezda, *On cycles and orbits of polynomial mappings
   $\mathbb Z^2\mapsto\mathbb Z^2*.** The original-journal
   [DML-CZ record](https://dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/120574) verifies volume
   10 (2002), no. 1, 95–102 of *Acta Mathematica et Informatica Universitatis
   Ostraviensis*. The fresh browser PDF request timed out; no fresh PDF read
   is claimed. The earlier C412 citation audit explicitly records its
   original-text integral-plane period-bound reading. That inherited scope,
   not an imagined rational-denominator theorem, is used as the collision.

3. **Julia Xénelkis de Hénon, *Hénon Maps: A List of Open Problems*.**
   The [journal-hosted HTML](https://amj.math.stonybrook.edu/html-articles/Files-2015-2024/23-70/index.html)
   was opened and Conjecture 3 located. It explicitly displays the
   opposite-sign quadratic map. This is a primary community problem record,
   not evidence that our restricted conservative classification has been
   solved or that it is exactly a named conjecture in that record.

4. **Kim–Krieger–Postolache–Szeto, *Hénon maps with many rational periodic
   points*.** The accessed
   [arXiv:2412.01668v2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01668v2), version
   8 July 2025, was read through the introduction and Theorems A–B. Its
   growing odd-degree conservative constructions are relevant contrasting
   context, not a monic quadratic one-bad-prime classification. The HTML
   rendering's 2026 date is not treated as a new version.

**Disposition boundary.** The scout's exact denominator reduction is not
presented as a paper. No source here proves the requested full AR1 atlas;
that is a limitation of this bounded investigation, not a global negative
priority result. No period or parameter census was run.

## AR2: the Somos-4 quotient collision

5. **Andrew N. W. Hone, *Sigma function solution of the initial value problem
   for Somos 5 sequences*.** The
   [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0501554) gives v3,
   30 January 2006. The actual
   [v3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0501554v3) was opened. Read:
   Theorem 1.1, Proposition 2.1, equations (2.2)–(2.8), and Remarks 2.2.
   Despite its Somos-5 title, Proposition 2.1 contains precisely AR2's
   Somos-4 quotient map, conserved quantity, and elliptic solution. This is
   an exact mechanism collision, not merely a similar keyword. The PDF's
   displayed 2013 internal date does not supersede its v3 history.

6. **Hone, *Continued Fractions and Hankel Determinants from Hyperelliptic
   Curves*.** The Kent-hosted
   [publisher PDF](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/79346/11/cpa.21923%20%281%29.pdf)
   was opened; a read-only `curl`/`pdftotext` stream supplied its cover and
   first two printed pages after browser extraction was unreliable. The
   cover identifies DOI 10.1002/cpa.21923 and the publisher-PDF version.
   Theorem 1.1 and its following paragraph explicitly discuss the
   degenerating sigma formula and coefficient-field algebraic content.
   They are not claimed to enumerate every native rational orbit avoiding
   every excluded divisor in our proposed atlas.

The initial search also found Hone's original 2005 BLMS article
[*Elliptic Curves and Quadratic Recurrence Sequences*](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024609304004163).
Its publisher metadata/abstract corroborate the earlier ownership; the
exact collision above is based on the actually read later proposition.
Mazur's torsion classification remains a classical background constraint,
not a freshly audited full proof and not a new contribution in this lane.

**Disposition boundary.** No specific substantial theorem survives the exact
map/invariant ownership hit in this pass. Singular-domain bookkeeping is
not assumed to be novel merely because it has not been completed here.

## AR3: finite-field ownership versus the prime-varying corollary

7. **Bell et al., *Density of Periodic Points for Lattès maps over Finite
   Fields*.** The
   [2021 arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00074) and
   [author-hosted preprint](https://pages.vassar.edu/thyde/files/2023/08/Bell-Camero-Cho-Hyde-Lu-Miller-Thompson-Zhu-Density-of-periodic-points-for-Lattes-maps-over-finite-fields.pdf)
   were opened. Read: Theorems 1.2–1.4, related work, and Lemmas 2.2–2.3
   through printed page 6. The exact density formula is imported entirely.
   The [2022 JNT record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022314X2100367X)
   verifies volume 238, 951–966, DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2021.10.009.
   The preprint lists eight authors including Rebecca Miller; the publisher
   record lists seven without Miller. These are recorded as different
   versions, not silently merged into a fabricated author list.

8. **Juul–Kurlberg–Madhu–Tucker, *Wreath products and proportions of periodic
   points*.** The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3378) was opened,
   as was the [author-hosted paper](https://kurlberg.github.io/eprints/Paper1005.pdf).
   Example 7.3 was read at printed pages 21–22. Its displayed Lattès
   liminf argument assumes a surjective full $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z_\ell)$
   representation and notes the non-CM setting. That is not the image for
   this Gaussian CM doubling family. It owns the general strategy of using
   prime-varying torsion/Frobenius data, not an unseen assertion of our
   exact three weak laws.

9. **Panraksa–Samart–Sriwongsa, *Arithmetic exceptionality of Lattès maps*.**
   The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25014) verifies v1,
   26 March 2026. The
   [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25014v1) was read at its
   introduction, main theorem, auxiliary Propositions 3–4, and the relevant
   Gaussian-CM part of Section 3.1. Its observable is permutation behavior
   at infinitely many primes. For Gaussian CM, the rational point of order
   two already excludes an even multiplier from exceptionality. It does
   not state the displayed full distribution of a non-permutation density.
   The regenerated HTML date is not substituted for v1's actual date.

10. **Milne, *Class Field Theory*, v4.03, 6 August 2020.** The
    [author PDF](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf) was opened.
    Read: V.3.5–3.8 on reciprocity, ray fields, ramification, and conductors;
    VI.4.2's density warning; VIII.7.2–7.4's displayed density statements.
    This is an author-written mathematical exposition, not new source
    research. It supplies the standard class-field inputs. Its displayed
    Chebotarev theorem uses Dirichlet density; the proof note does not
    pretend that this alone implies the required natural-density limit.

11. **Andrew V. Sutherland, MIT 18.785, Lecture 28 (8 December 2021).**
    The [official lecture PDF](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2021fa/LectureNotes28.pdf)
    was opened at the ideal/ray class correspondence, Theorem 28.9, and
    Remark 28.12. The latter explicitly records the number-field
    natural-density version and credits Hecke. We invoke that classical
    strengthened theorem, not claim that the eight-page lecture reproves
    the analytic strengthening in full. Only fixed finite fields are used;
    the separate tail argument controls infinitely many layers.

12. **Adjacent leads checked but not imported as proof owners.**
    [Lozano-Robledo's CM Galois-image paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02584)
    was checked at its metadata/abstract (v3, 18 November 2021; published in
    *Algebra & Number Theory* 16 (2022), 777–837). No 55-page full-text read
    or particular image theorem is claimed. The eventual proof avoids that
    dependency. [Lombardo–Perucca](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.02847) concerns
    the order of the reduction of a fixed infinite-order point; only the
    landing-page/abstract scope was used, not a full proof. The fresh
    [2026 polynomial-reduction lead](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21620) was
    checked as a metadata lead and is not used to assert a Lattès theorem.

**Residual and recommended disposition.** The exact three-law formula was
not located in the bounded primary texts inspected. That is not a novelty
certificate. More importantly, after the explicit imported results are
deducted, the remaining argument is a short elementary/class-field
specialization. Source-verification success therefore does not imply that
the result deserves a new substantial-paper slot. The proof note recommends
retention as an unnumbered corollary only.
