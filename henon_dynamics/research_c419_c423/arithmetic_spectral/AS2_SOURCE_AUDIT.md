## 1. Young: exact all-level algebraic machinery

M. P. Young, *Explicit calculations with Eisenstein series*, J. Number
Theory 199 (2019), 1–48,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2018.11.007),
[arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03624v2),
[actual HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1710.03624v2).
The verified version is dated 3 November 2017; a guessed v4 URL failed and
was not treated as a version. The coordinator inspected the cusp scaling
definition, completion, Proposition 4.2, Theorems 6.1 and 7.1, equations
(7.2)–(7.5), and the fixed Fourier basis in Theorem 8.1.

These supply character functional equations and both exact cusp/oldform
basis changes for the full level, with trivial nebentypus represented by
equal characters. Their coefficients depend on the spectral parameter.
The character-weighted cusp sums have fixed coefficients. All that
machinery is imported, not a proposed discovery. Neither the inspected
theorem statements nor their immediate explanations state the present
all-level commutativity criterion. This is a bounded statement about the
locations read, not a priority conclusion.

## 2. Booker–Lee–Strömbergsson: independent full-matrix formulas

A. R. Booker, M. Lee and A. Strömbergsson, *Twist-minimal trace formulas
and the Selberg eigenvalue conjecture*,
[arXiv:1803.06016v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06016v2), dated
17 April 2020; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12349).
The title and all three authors were checked in the metadata and
[HTML title](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.06016v2), not the page's erroneous
automatic phrase “and 1 other authors.”

Actual reading covered §2.7's opening provenance, Lemma 2.19 and its proof,
Remark 2.20, the character enumeration in Lemma 2.21, the matrix
functional equation and scattering reconstruction, and the determinant
formula in Lemma 2.26, all within §2.7 of v2. The earlier §2.8 locator was
corrected after the nonauthor checked the actual section boundary; the
coordinator also reopened and confirmed that boundary. The coefficient
formula independently matches the coordinator's principal-sector incoming
matrix. The full reconstruction explicitly uses different basis matrices
at the two functional-equation parameters. This is a direct classical
owner of the matrix calculation, not proof of a fixed eigenbasis.
The Bristol-hosted published PDF returned 403; the cited reading was the
verified arXiv HTML, not an unseen published PDF.

## 4. Cakoni–Chanillo: prime and squarefree control

F. Cakoni and S. Chanillo, *Transmission Eigenvalues and the Riemann Zeta
Function in Scattering Theory for Automorphic Forms on Fuchsian Groups of

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10114-019-8128-8),
[actual author-hosted published PDF](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~chanillo/te.pdf).

already belong to C18's deductions. Its separate principal-congruence
diagonal-entry formula is not substituted for the full Hecke-congruence
matrix. No later broad divisor/target claim from the article is imported.
