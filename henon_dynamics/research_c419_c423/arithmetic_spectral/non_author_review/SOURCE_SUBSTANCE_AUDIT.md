### Andrew R. Booker, Min Lee and Andreas Strömbergsson

*Twist-minimal trace formulas and the Selberg eigenvalue conjecture*,
[arXiv:1803.06016v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06016v2), revised
17 April 2020; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12349).
Metadata and the three author names were independently checked.

Actual [§2.7](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.06016v2#S2.SS7) reading covered

matrix functional-equation/determinant discussion around Lemma 2.26 and

incoming-coefficient formula and full scattering reconstruction.
Different basis matrices occur at the two functional-equation
parameters; reconstruction is not fixed simultaneous diagonalization.
The text credits Huxley's arbitrary-level formulas and explains the
relation to Young's basis change. Its extended-group and ordinary-group
formulas are distinguished; AS2's full-cusp proof uses the latter
object through Young, not an unannounced restriction to one parity.

The relevant reconstruction is in §2.7. The author audit's earlier §2.8
pointer was corrected; this reviewer read the corrected paragraph and
closed the minor item. There was no disagreement about the formula.

## Why the remaining contract is substantial, but narrowly so

This is a structural classification of the complete matrix family, not
an enlarged level table or a change to a easier invariant subspace.
Classical reconstruction still leaves a parameter-dependent oldform
change of basis. The remaining work is to decide, with proof for every
level, when this family actually commutes in fixed coordinates.

The submitted proof resolves that obstruction in two qualitatively
different regimes. A nonreal square is detected at a prime outside the
level even after all finite oldform factors are retained. A real square
can still fail through an imaginary unramified phase, but only at odd
exponent. The fixed central-channel analysis and tensor argument supply
the exact boundary and prevent cancellation among several offending
prime factors. The resulting elementary level criterion answers the
whole frozen question, including the two previously false guesses.

On that basis, the residual meets the repository's internal substantial
source-classification gate as **one** contract. This is the reviewer's
mathematical/substantive inference after the stated deductions, not a
claim made by the classical sources. It is not an invention of new
scattering theory, a proof of international priority, or a prediction
of acceptance by a journal. If a direct predecessor containing the
equivalent full criterion is subsequently found, the increment and
admission must be reassessed.

The entire result stays on the source-system side. There is no new
target Euler/root-number dictionary, zero correspondence, native time
evolution or Hilbert–Pólya theorem. Formal Route A labels remain the
coordinator's separately governed task, not a consequence of this review.
