## 1. Bell--Miles--Ward

Jason Bell, Richard Miles and Thomas Ward,
*Towards a Pólya--Carlson dichotomy for algebraic dynamics*,
arXiv:1307.2369v1 (submitted 2013-07-09);
the arXiv record gives Indagationes Mathematicae 25(4), 652--668 (2014)
and DOI 10.1016/j.indag.2014.04.005.
The abstract record and HTML introduction, Lemma 1, higher-dimensional
setup and Theorem 15 were actually read.
[Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2369v1);
[theorem source](https://arxiv.org/html/1307.2369v1#S3).

Theorem 15 concerns one solenoidal group automorphism with a fixed
number-field/place product formula, finitely many relevant places and
no unit-modulus archimedean conjugates. It does not assert a dichotomy
for arbitrary sums over noncommuting words. Applying it to a fixed
return, when its hypotheses hold, does not settle the full switching
sum or its different secondary radius. Lemma 1's logarithmic-derivative
principle is standard background; the needed full-function boundary
singularities must still be proved.

The HTML header displays a generated 2026 date; the version metadata
was used for bibliographic dating, not that header.

## 2. Byszewski--Cornelissen--Houben

Jakub Byszewski, Gunther Cornelissen and Marc Houben,
*Dynamics of endomorphisms of algebraic groups*,
arXiv:2209.00085v2, revised 2024-04-19; v1 submitted 2022-08-31.
The current record, §7.1 Definitions 12--13 and formula (7.1),
§9.1 Proposition 14, and §11.3 Theorem 11.3.3 and its proof were
actually inspected.
[Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00085v2);
[full theorem source](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.00085v2).

FAD is a global sequence representation by one determinant-power
factor and a finite collection of periodic/gcd local exponent data;
it does not mean merely having a finite automaton at each precision.
The natural-boundary theorem assumes hyperbolic FAD data and integer
wild exponents. Proposition 14 proves membership for specified
single-multiplier S-integer systems, not for the present skew product.
No required FAD representation of the full switching count or of its
normalized secondary correction has been established. The theorem
therefore supplies a collision target, not an application.

## 3. Expanding-semigroup zeta literature

Maria Carvalho, Fagner B. Rodrigues and Paulo Varandas,
*Semigroup actions of expanding maps*,
arXiv:1601.04275v2, revised 2016-06-22; v1 submitted 2016-01-17.
The record supplies DOI 10.1007/s10955-016-1697-3.
The actual HTML reads cover Definition 2.1, the word-count convention,
the displayed zeta definition and Theorem B.
[Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04275v2);
[statement source](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.04275v2).

Their zeta uses the averaged word count \(p^{-n}\sum_{|w|=n}\#\mathrm{Fix}(w)\),
and Theorem B assumes Ruelle-expanding generators on a compact connected
metric space. The frozen AS1 clock uses the unaveraged count.
The normalization could be tracked by a variable rescaling, but that
alone does not verify the dynamical hypotheses. Archimedean expansion
of the displayed matrices does not establish Ruelle expansion on their
localized solenoid. No such applicability proof is supplied here.
This source does not prove the requested secondary-circle statement.

C409's actual manuscript sections on the
[finite radial criterion](../../../continuation_c409_c413_round2/papers/C409_wild_fad/sections/02_criterion.tex),
[absolute Fourier representation](../../../continuation_c409_c413_round2/papers/C409_wild_fad/sections/03_fourier.tex),

absolutely summable measure for their specified finite radial kernels,
and use full unit-conductor grids plus actual nonzero Fourier masses
to establish a meromorphic natural boundary.
Unbounded conductor alone is explicitly insufficient.

No identification of the present normalized switching correction with
those radial data has been proved. Consequently their full-grid
argument cannot be imported simply because every fixed congruence
layer is finite. The aggregate-residue sufficient route in
[PROOF_STATUS.md](PROOF_STATUS.md) is a familiar analytic mechanism;
the missing work is proving its hypotheses for this exact system.

Likewise, the known failure of naive factor-by-factor boundary inference
must be deducted: logarithmic singularities may exponentiate to regular
integer-order zeros, and different phase contributions can cancel.
The companion proof checks this issue at \(+R\) and \(-R\) only.
It makes no uncancelled-infinite-product assertion elsewhere.
