## Fordy extraction discrepancy and dependency boundary

In the browser-extracted equation (20), with source variables
$u_1=pq$, $v_1=ps$, $u_2=I$ and $a-b=k$, the second reduced
component appears as
$$
\widetilde v_1=
\frac{(u_1+kv_1/(1+v_1))(u_1-u_2+kv_1/(1+v_1))}{v_1}.
$$
At $k=5$, $(p,q,r,s)=(1,2,3,4)$, this expression evaluates to
$-12$, while the actual map gives $(p',q',r',s')=(2,4,1,6)$
and $p's'=12$. The source's definitions identify $v_1$ as $ps$.
A requested PDF page image failed with a connection error. Therefore
this pass records an **extracted-formula discrepancy**, not a newly
image-attested printed erratum or a claim about author intent.

Neither the proof nor its period bound copies this reduced component.

derived explicitly from the frozen map. This does not turn the already
published QRT/scaling reduction into a new ownership claim. The earlier
