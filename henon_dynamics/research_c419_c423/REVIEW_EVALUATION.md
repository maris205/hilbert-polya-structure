## Mandatory finding M1 — C420 A4 is under-credited

Affected original fields: C420 a4.verdict, a4.evidence_status,
its source/lift explanation and the A4 component of tuple; the common
scope must agree with the resulting interpretation.

The original A4_FAIL rationale says the contract supplies no
native-time source-to-quantum lift theorem. That is a valid missing
stronger bridge, but it is not sufficient to erase the lowest formal
source-scattering credit. The fixed evaluator asks whether there is
a natural non-post-hoc path to a unitary, scattering or Hamiltonian
object. It does not require that every such ingredient be a new
theorem of this paper before even A4_FORMAL_HINT is available.

C420 is expressly the complete cusp scattering matrix of Gamma_0(N),
defined by the actual weight-zero incoming Eisenstein constant term
in fixed width-one cusp coordinates. Section 2 reconstructs blocks
of that original matrix. It is not an arbitrary matrix family being
renamed scattering, a fitted spectral surrogate, or a block borrowed
from a different candidate. This is positive classical source
structure already supplied by the object's definition.

Required calibration: give **A4_FORMAL_HINT**, with PROVED referring
only to the explicit classical source-scattering definition and a
clear statement that this does not pass the full liftability gate.
Retain NOT_TESTABLE for an explicit operator-domain/closure package,
unitarity or self-adjointness verification, required symmetry tests
and same-clock orbit-phase/weight transport not supplied by this
commutator classification. The classification's spectral parameters
must not be reinterpreted as iteration time.

No upgrade to A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION,
A4_UNITARY_OR_SCATTERING_CANDIDATE or A4_ROUTE_B_READY is requested:
the article does not provide the full candidate-specific Hilbert-space,
domain, closure, symmetry and transport evidence for those stronger
judgments. The evaluator contains no A4_SOURCE_PASS enum; that
informal phrase must not become a new YAML label.

This correction does not deny that classical source Laplacian and
scattering theory exist, nor claim a new proof of their complete
operator theory. It also does not transfer their properties to the
other four source contracts. Conversely, a generic Hilbertization
of finite point sets is not an already supplied natural quantization
package in their manuscripts.

All A0–A3 labels, ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY, target NOT_TESTABLE metrics
and false target/Route-B flags must remain unchanged. In particular,
the absence of an autonomous clock still justifies C420's A1_FAIL;
