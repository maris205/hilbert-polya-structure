Screen different subtypes, not a sequence of parameter perturbations of a
single accepted result. A source-only classification can qualify only when
it closes a substantial complete question after classical ownership and
local predecessors are subtracted. A matched mean count, a native rational
zeta, or arithmetic-looking coefficients alone are not target progress.

Before a substantial computation, each candidate must state:

1. The exact map/operator, full parameter family, domain and native clock.
2. The observable: ordinary points, local scheme length, weighted trace,
   physical spectrum or another explicitly fixed convention.
3. One paper-level question, its intrinsic arithmetic carrier, and which
   claimed bridge is actually present versus still missing.
4. Closest local and primary-source predecessors, exact imported theorems,
   and the residual proposed increment.
5. A cheap decisive check, success condition and specific replacement boundary.

corollary, a renamed classical reconstruction, or a proof scaffold does not
