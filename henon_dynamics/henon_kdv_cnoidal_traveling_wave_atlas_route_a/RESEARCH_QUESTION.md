# Research question

For the fixed plus-sign Korteweg--de Vries equation

\[
u_t+6uu_x+u_{xxx}=0,
\]

can one classify **every bounded classical real traveling profile**
`u(x,t)=U(x-ct)` directly from its cubic first integral, including all root
topologies and degenerations, and close exact formulas for:

1. the cnoidal profile and its fundamental (not doubled) spatial period;
2. the first two period moments;
3. the lower-double-root soliton and upper-double-root harmonic/constant face;
4. Galilean shifts of roots, level, and speed; and
5. the physical-time return when the profile is placed on its fundamental
   circle?

Success means a proof-driven all-root classification plus independent exact,
quadrature, symbolic, replay, and hostile-mutation gates.  It does not mean a
classification of arbitrary KdV solutions, a stability theorem, a target
dynamical determinant, or an arithmetic interpretation.
