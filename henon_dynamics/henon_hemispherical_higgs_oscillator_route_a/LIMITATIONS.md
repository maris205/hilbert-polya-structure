# Limitations

- The classical action and periodicity theorem requires (omega>0). At
  (omega=0) the omitted equator destroys completeness of the open-hemisphere
  classical flow.
- The action Hamiltonian is nonsmooth across (L=0) in signed polar actions;
  the meridional orbit is handled by smooth polar-chart continuation.
- The radial action angle degenerates on circular orbits. Their angular period
  is stated directly instead.
- The quantum boundary is the Friedrichs/Dirichlet hemisphere. It cannot be
  relabeled as the unrestricted full sphere.
- Exact revivals occur only on the rational (2\nu) wall. Irrational cases may
  have approximate recurrences but no exact identity time.
- The (N\le128) enumeration and finite parameter grids test formulas and
  serialization only.
- The natural source quantization supplies no arithmetic-target operator.
