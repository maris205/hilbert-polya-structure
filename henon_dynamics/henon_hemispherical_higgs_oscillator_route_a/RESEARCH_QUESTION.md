# Research question

Can the hemispherical Higgs oscillator be closed as one theorem-scale package
that simultaneously gives its exact classical radial action and all orbit
faces, its Friedrichs Jacobi spectrum and multiplicities, its flat and
zero-coupling limits, and a necessary-and-sufficient identity-revival law?

Yes, after one essential domain split:

- the complete classical periodic-flow theorem requires (omega>0);
- the quantum Friedrichs family permits (omega=0), whose boundary is the
  Dirichlet hemisphere rather than the full sphere.

The resulting source theorem is mathematically complete but Route-A rejected
because it has no arithmetic carrier, discrete primitive ledger, target
determinant, or target analytic bridge.
