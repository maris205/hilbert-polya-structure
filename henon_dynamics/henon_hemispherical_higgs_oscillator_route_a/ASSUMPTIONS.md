# Assumptions and conventions

- The classical theorem uses (R>0) and strictly positive (omega) on the
  open northern hemisphere (0\le\theta<\pi/2). This makes the equator a
  confining barrier.
- The regular action chart has (I_r>0) and (L\ne0). Circular
  (I_r=0), meridional (L=0), and north-equilibrium faces are treated
  separately.
- The classical (omega=0) open-hemisphere flow is not included in the
  complete periodic-flow theorem because trajectories can reach the missing
  equator.
- The quantum family uses (R,\hbar>0), (omega\ge0), and the Friedrichs
  realization on one hemisphere. Its zero-coupling boundary is the Dirichlet
  hemisphere, not the full sphere.
- (m\in\mathbb Z), (n_r\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}), and
  (N=2n_r+|m|).
- Identity revival means the full propagator equals the identity, not merely
  a scalar multiple of it.
- The finite evidence has (N\le128) and selected exact parameter grids; the
  analytic theorem has no cutoff.
