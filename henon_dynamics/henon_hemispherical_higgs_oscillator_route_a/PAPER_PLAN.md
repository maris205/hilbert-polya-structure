# Paper plan

## Title

The Hemispherical Higgs Oscillator: Exact Actions, Friedrichs Spectrum, and
Identity Revivals

## Narrative

1. Freeze the positive-coupling classical Hamiltonian and derive the turning
   quadratic in (x=\sin^2\theta).
2. Evaluate the radial action with one explicit root integral; invert it and
   obtain the (2{:}1) frequency lock and periods.
3. Separate regular, circular, meridional, equilibrium, and excluded
   zero-coupling classical faces.
4. Define the hemispherical Friedrichs operator for (omega\ge0), reduce it
   to the Jacobi equation, and prove spectrum, completeness, and multiplicity.
5. Prove the flat limit and the precise Dirichlet-hemisphere zero-coupling
   limit.
6. Prove the full-propagator revival theorem from consecutive phase
   differences, including the fact that the common phase is exactly one.
7. Close evidence, collisions, limitations, and Route-A scope.

The three PDF rounds respectively add the action theorem, the full quantum
theorem, and the revival/limit/Route-A closure.
