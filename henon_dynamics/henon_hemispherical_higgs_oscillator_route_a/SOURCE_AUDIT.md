# Source and collision audit

## Source ownership

- Higgs DOI `10.1088/0305-4470/12/3/006` and Leemon DOI
  `10.1088/0305-4470/12/4/009` own the curved oscillator and dynamical-symmetry
  lineage.
- S. Bellucci, A. Nersessian, A. Saghatelian, and V. Yeghikyan,
  “Quantum ring models and action-angle variables,” arXiv `1008.3865`,
  supplies the modern spherical action-angle lineage, including the action
  formulas used as an external cross-check.
- Ye. M. Hakobyan and G. S. Pogosyan, “On Interbasis Expansion for Isotropic
  Oscillator on Two-Dimensional Sphere,” arXiv `quant-ph/9803085`, supplies
  the two-dimensional spherical separation,
  energy, and Jacobi-mode background. C373 does not import its prose
  degeneracy statement: the one-hemisphere multiplicity (N+1) is proved by
  directly counting the admissible labels (N=2n_r+|m|).

The package claims no literature priority. It fixes one-hemisphere domains and
proves its specialized action, boundary, and revival statements directly.

## Workspace collisions

- C349: Neumann oscillator on the full sphere, with Uhlenbeck integrals and
  generic invariant tori. C373 has a singular equatorial (\tan^2\theta)
  barrier, maximal (2{:}1) locking, and a Jacobi Friedrichs spectrum.
- C244: spherical-pendulum focus-focus monodromy. C373 has no focus-focus
  singularity; its regular bounded trajectories close.
- C313: free round-sphere geodesic/Laplacian dynamics. C373 has a confining
  potential and a one-hemisphere boundary domain.
- C221: nonlinear Schrödinger PDE dynamics. C373 is a linear one-particle
  quantum Hamiltonian, not NLS.

The prior idea report mentioned the Higgs oscillator only as a deferred clue;
it did not own this theorem.

## Scope firewall

Rational revival is source spectral commensurability, not arithmetic local
data. No target Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, target divisor or
functional equation, target-zero match, Hilbert--Pólya realization, or Route B
is asserted.
