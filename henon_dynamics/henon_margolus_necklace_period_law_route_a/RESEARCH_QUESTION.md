# C165 research question

Can a genuinely different, reversible partitioned cellular automaton replace
another incremental Rule-90 paper and support an all-size theorem that owns
its primitive periods and determinant without overstating dynamical
complexity?

The hard gate asks for five linked results at the full two-layer clock:

1. derive the exact site motion produced by the staggered swaps;
2. construct an explicit conjugacy, not just matching fixed counts, to a
   four-letter necklace rotation;
3. classify all fixed points, exact-period configurations, primitive cycles,
   and the complete finite zeta for every `m>=1`;
4. prove a uniform full-period concentration estimate and retain `m=1` as a
   theorem boundary rather than silently excluding it;
5. construct the source-natural finite Koopman determinant and an explicit
   antiunitary reversal at the same clock.

The finite ledger cannot answer this question by extrapolation.  Each item
must follow from the block permutation and a proof valid for arbitrary `m`.

Success remains Route-A exploratory.  It does not ask for a target divisor,
target analytic structure, arithmetic factorization, self-adjoint spectrum,
or Route-B promotion.
