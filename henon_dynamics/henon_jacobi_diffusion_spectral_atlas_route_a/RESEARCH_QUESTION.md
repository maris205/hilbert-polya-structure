# Research question

For the canonical conservative Jacobi diffusion

\[
dX_t=[\alpha-(\alpha+\beta)X_t]dt+\sqrt{2X_t(1-X_t)}dW_t,
\qquad \alpha,\beta>0,
\]

can one close, in one theorem, every endpoint regime, the reversible
self-adjoint realization, complete spectrum and heat kernel, trace-class
semigroup determinant, all polynomial moments, and the precise distinction
between probabilistic recurrence and semigroup periodicity?

The source clock is the displayed generator, twice the common one-half
Wright--Fisher convention.  The arithmetic question is evaluated only after
the source theorem is closed; no target data enter the object.
