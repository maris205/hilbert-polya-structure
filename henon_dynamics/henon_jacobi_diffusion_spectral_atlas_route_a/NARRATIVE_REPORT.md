# Narrative report

The key structural move is to treat the boundary realization as part of the
dynamical object.  Scale and speed densities then classify all four endpoint
combinations, including the equality thresholds.  The speed probability is
Beta, and its divergence form makes the Jacobi polynomials a complete
self-adjoint spectral coordinate system.  This closes the heat kernel,
trace-class determinant, sharp relaxation and every moment in the same clock.

The hostile audit changed the theorem wording materially.  “No recurrence” is
false for sample paths of this positive-recurrent diffusion.  What the
spectrum proves is the different statement that a positive-time semigroup has
no nonconstant periodic observable.  Both facts are retained explicitly.

This is a substantial source theorem but a strict Route-A rejection.  The
trace-class determinant is a source-local heat determinant, not an
arithmetic dynamical zeta, and self-adjointness does not turn the generator
into a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
