# Narrative report

## What changed at C27

C26 closed the infinite oscillator door but left a precise replacement: reduce
the same pathwise genus-two symplectic cocycle modulo an odd prime and use the
finite Weil representation.  Unlike the oscillator fibre, its dimension is
only \(p^2\).  The C26 scalar Bergman trace-class theorem therefore survives
under tensoring, and the target---not merely an auxiliary scalar model---has
an ordinary Fredholm determinant for each fixed prime.

The exact character adds arithmetic.  Thomas's formula expresses every trace
through the fixed-space dimension and a quotient-form discriminant.  At good
primes it collapses to

\[
\Theta_p(g)=\left(\frac{\det(g-I)}p\right).
\]

This is a real quadratic character, not a fitted phase.  Singular primes
retain more information: C24-P014 and P016 have the same characteristic
polynomial and \(\det(I-g)=3\), but at \(p=3\) their characters are
\(-G_3\) and \(+G_3\).

## The positive chronology result

For the frozen C26 three-return word, the forward and noncyclic reverse
characters differ at \(p=3,5,7\).  Reconstructing the complete degree-
\(p^2\) local polynomials from all power traces shows that the distinction
survives the full repetition structure at each of these primes.  The AB/BA
two-return control remains equal, as trace cyclicity requires.

Across the 576 comparisons with odd \(p\le97\) and \(1\le r\le24\), 328
characters differ and 248 agree.  The fibre is therefore sensitive to order,
but it is not a faithful chronology invariant.

## The two collapse results

At \(p=43\), the C26 forward and reverse matrices have different
characteristic polynomials modulo 43, yet both have order 925 and their Weil
characters agree for every exponent from 1 through 925.  Periodicity proves
that the entire finite-fibre power towers, hence the degree-1849 Weil
polynomials \(\det(I-T\rho_{43}(g))\), are equal.  Their scalar Perron trace
atoms remain different, so this is not a collision of the full AGY orbit
factor.

The C24 obstruction is stronger.  P076 and P082 are distinct primitive
labeled cycles with noncyclic orders of the same three first-return branches.
Their base-trivialized matrices are nevertheless conjugate in
\(\operatorname{Sp}(J_{24},\mathbb Z)\) by an explicit determinant-one
integral matrix, where \(J_{24}\) is the frozen C24 symplectic form.
Reduction modulo every prime preserves the conjugacy.  No class
character---finite Weil, parity constituent, or any other character
combination---can distinguish any repetition of the two cycles.

## Arithmetic verdict

The arithmetic scan enumerates all 150 first-return bridges of length at most
12 and inserts each between two frozen positive prefixes.  Every resulting
branch has a different integer discriminant, characteristic polynomial, and
Legendre signature across the 24 odd primes at most 97.  This finite census
looks like an orbit-dependent bundle of quadratic fields, not a small common
conductor that could be assembled into a familiar Euler product.

## Research decision

C27 is a genuine positive construction at fixed prime.  It does not yet
justify promotion to an intrinsic global Hilbert--Pólya object: the external
modulus is an exact structural gap, class-function collapse is an exact
nonseparation theorem, and the bounded conductor census supplies finite
evidence of fragmentation.  The next gate is not another small-prime scan.
It must derive a canonical adelic measure or trace from the dynamics itself;
if that cannot be done, the programme should change the dynamical form.
