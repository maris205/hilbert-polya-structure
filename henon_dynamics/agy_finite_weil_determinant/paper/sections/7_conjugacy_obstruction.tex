\section{Integral conjugacy collapse}
\label{sec:conjugacy}

The finite-field results show that one Weil fibre is not separating.  The
underlying Rauzy graph contains a stronger obstruction that no collection of
class characters can overcome.

Consider the two primitive C24 control cycles P076 and P082.  After a cyclic
move to their common central state, their first-return orders are
\[
[bbb,ttbt,tbbtt]
\quad\text{and}\quad
[bbb,tbbtt,ttbt].
\]
They use the same three labeled return branches in different noncyclic order.
The canonical cycles are distinct and their chronological matrices remain
different modulo every prime.  In the C24 certificate these are the
\texttt{base\_trivialized\_symplectic\_matrix} entries in the fixed frame rooted at
state 2:
\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&1&0&3\\
2&3&0&4\\
3&5&2&6\\
2&3&1&4
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0&2\\
1&4&0&4\\
1&6&2&5\\
1&4&1&4
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both preserve the frozen C24 form
\[
J_{24}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1&1&-1\\
1&0&-1&1\\
-1&1&0&-1\\
1&-1&1&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]
and have characteristic polynomial
\[
t^4-11t^3+18t^2-11t+1.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Integral symplectic chronology collapse]
\label{thm:integral-conjugacy}
The matrices \(A\) and \(B\) are conjugate in
\(\Sp(J_{24},\Z)\).  Explicitly,
\[
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&-1&1\\
-1&0&0&-1\\
0&-2&0&-1\\
0&-1&0&-1
\end{pmatrix}
\]
satisfies
\[
\det X=1,
\qquad
X^TJ_{24}X=J_{24},
\qquad
BX=XA.
\]
Consequently, for every prime \(p\), every integer \(r\), and every class
function \(\varphi_p\) on \(\Sp(J_{24},\F_p)\),
\[
\varphi_p(A^r)=\varphi_p(B^r).
\]
In particular,
\[
\Theta_p(A^r)=\Theta_p(B^r)
\quad\text{for every odd }p\text{ and every }r\ge1.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The three displayed integer identities are verified by direct multiplication.
Because \(\det X=1\), reduction modulo any prime leaves \(X\) invertible and
symplectic.  Thus \(B^r=XA^rX^{-1}\) over every \(\F_p\).  A class function
is constant on the resulting conjugacy class.
\end{proof}

This collapse is stronger than equality of the first Weil character.  It
identifies the full power-character towers, every parity constituent, and
every linear combination of irreducible characters.  Since \(A\) and \(B\)
also have the same homological characteristic polynomial, their homological
Perron and characteristic data agree throughout the repetition tower.
Within the C24 control census, neither those data nor any class-function
fibre recovers the symbolic order.

The scope matters.  P076 and P082 are controls from the underlying finite
Rauzy graph; they are not promoted to branches of the source-locked C26
positive-prefix AGY operator.  The theorem therefore establishes an
information-theoretic obstruction for class-function quantizations of the
underlying cocycle, not an equality between two terms already identified in
the C26 Fredholm series.  Finding a control inside the accelerated C26
alphabet would strengthen the application but would not change the mechanism.

\begin{obstruction}[Class functions cannot be chronology-complete]
Any fibre weight that depends on a chronological cocycle only through its
symplectic conjugacy class loses some labeled symbolic chronology in this
Rauzy class.  Enlarging the finite character table or taking all prime
reductions cannot distinguish P076 from P082.
\end{obstruction}

To preserve the missing information, a future construction would have to
retain a based or path-groupoid observable rather than only a conjugacy-class
trace.  Such an observable would require a new determinant theorem: ordinary
periodic-orbit traces are cyclic and naturally descend to conjugacy data.
