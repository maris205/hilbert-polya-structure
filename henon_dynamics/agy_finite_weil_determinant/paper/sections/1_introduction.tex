\section{Introduction}

A finite Weil fibre opens a determinant door that the infinite oscillator
fibre leaves closed.  For the genus-two Rauzy--AGY cocycle considered here,
the oscillator representation acts on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space
and produces a noncompact unsmoothed transfer operator.  Reducing the same
chronological cocycle modulo an odd prime replaces that fibre by a canonical
unitary representation of dimension \(p^2\).  The resulting twisted operator
is trace class on the existing common Bergman domain.

Existence of a determinant is only the first gate.  A dynamical candidate for
Hilbert--P\'olya must also retain orbit chronology, produce arithmetic
structure without reading prime or zero tables, and resist collapse under
natural equivalences.  Finite Weil characters are plausible because they
are exact Gauss-sum invariants of the pathwise symplectic product.  They are
also class functions, which makes a sharp negative outcome possible: symbolic
histories can remain distinct while their cocycles become symplectically
conjugate.

This paper evaluates both sides of that tradeoff.  We freeze the seven-state
Rauzy source, the fixed-fibre symplectic trivialization, the raw AGY roof, and
the common complex domain from the preceding scalar construction.  For each
odd prime \(p\), we use the genuine Weil representation \(\rho_p\) of
\(\Sp(4,\F_p)\), not a projective representation and not a truncation of the
real oscillator.  We evaluate a character only after forming the complete
later-on-the-left path product.  This order convention is essential: Weil
characters are not multiplicative.

Our contributions are concrete and falsifiable.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em,label=(\roman*)]
\item We prove that the finite-Weil twist is trace class for every fixed odd
  prime and derive the exact word atom
  \[
  \Theta_p(g_w)\,
  \frac{\lambda_w^{-(s+1)}}{\chi_w'(\lambda_w)}.
  \]
  Its Fredholm determinant is jointly holomorphic on the same source
  half-plane as the scalar determinant.
\item We implement Thomas's full character formula over \(\F_p\), including
  singular fixed-vector loci, and reconstruct the finite-fibre polynomial
  \(\det(I-T\rho_p(g))\) from all \(p^2\) power traces.
\item We certify genuine chronology sensitivity.  The three-return forward
  and noncyclic reverse polynomials differ at \(p=3,5,7\), while the required
  two-return \(AB/BA\) null control remains equal.
\item We prove two limits of the method.  The same three-return pair has an
  equal complete finite-fibre polynomial at \(p=43\), and an explicit
  integral symplectic conjugacy makes every class-function fibre blind to a
  distinct pair of primitive symbolic cycles for all primes and repetitions.
\end{enumerate}

The arithmetic census gives the broader verdict.  At good primes a word
trace reduces to the quadratic character associated with the squarefree part
of \(D_w=\det(g_w-I)\).  In a finite source-locked scan, all 150 branches have
different Legendre signatures across the odd primes at most 97.  The data
therefore support an orbit-dependent family of quadratic fields rather than
a small common conductor.  No prime product, adelic measure, or limit in
\(p\) is introduced to hide that fragmentation.

The paper proceeds from the source and order conventions to the finite Weil
character, the Fredholm theorem, and the exact chronology experiments.  The
integral conjugacy result then isolates a theorem-level obstruction that no
larger character table can remove.  We close by stating the precise
Route-A boundary: fixed-prime determinants are natural and rigorous, but a
global Hilbert--P\'olya object remains absent.
