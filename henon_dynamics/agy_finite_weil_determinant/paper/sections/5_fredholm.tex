\section{A fixed-prime Fredholm determinant}
\label{sec:fredholm}

For a fixed odd prime \(p\), put
\[
\cH_p=A^2(\Omega)\otimes\C^{p^2}.
\]
The branch action uses column fibres and left multiplication:
\begin{equation}
(\cL_{s,p}F)(z)
=\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma}
w_{s,\gamma}(z)\,
\rho_{p,J_0}(g_\gamma\bmod p)F(h_\gamma z).
\label{eq:twisted-operator}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Weil Fredholm theorem]
\label{thm:fredholm}
For every fixed odd prime \(p\) and every \(s\in\halfplane\), the operator
\(\cL_{s,p}\) is trace class on \(\cH_p\).  Its singular values remain in the
same exponential class as the scalar operator, with finite multiplicity
\(p^2\).  Moreover,
\[
D_p(s,u)=\det_{\cH_p}(I-u\cL_{s,p})
\]
is jointly holomorphic on \(\halfplane\times\C\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(K_{s,\gamma}\) be the scalar weighted-composition branch.  Since
\(\rho_{p,J_0}(g_\gamma)\) is unitary on a \(p^2\)-dimensional space,
\[
\|K_{s,\gamma}\otimes\rho_{p,J_0}(g_\gamma)\|_1
=p^2\|K_{s,\gamma}\|_1.
\]
The scalar branch series is locally uniformly summable in trace norm on
compact subsets of \(\halfplane\).  Multiplication by the fixed number
\(p^2\) preserves that property, so the twisted branch series converges in
trace norm.  More explicitly, the whole matrix-weighted sum factors through
\(R\otimes I_{p^2}\), where \(R\) is the common compact Bergman restriction
from C26, followed by a bounded matrix-weighted branch sum.  The singular
values of this common restriction are therefore repeated only with finite
multiplicity \(p^2\).  Trace-norm holomorphy and the standard trace-class
determinant theorem give joint holomorphy in \((s,u)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Chronological word atom]
\label{thm:wordatom}
Let \((\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n)\) be a forward periodic Rauzy word, let
\[
g_w=g_{\beta_n}\cdots g_{\beta_1},
\]
and let \(\lambda_w\) and \(\chi_w\) be the scalar Perron data from
\cref{eq:scalaratom}.  The corresponding twisted operator word has trace
\[
\boxed{
\tr K_{s,p,w}
=\Theta_p(g_w)\,
\frac{\lambda_w^{-(s+1)}}{\chi_w'(\lambda_w)}.}
\]
For its \(r\)-fold repetition, the fibre factor is
\(\Theta_p(g_w^r)\), not \(\Theta_p(g_w)^r\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The operator-factor order is
\((\beta_n,\ldots,\beta_1)\).  The representation property gives the fibre
product \(\rho_{p,J_0}(g_w)\), while the scalar fixed-point trace is
\cref{eq:scalaratom}.  The trace of a tensor product is the product of the
two traces.  Repeating the operator word replaces \(\rho_{p,J_0}(g_w)\) by
\(\rho_{p,J_0}(g_w^r)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What the theorem does not assemble]
The family \(\{D_p\}_{p\text{ odd}}\) is not a single determinant.  The
modulus \(p\) is an external parameter, and the fibre dimensions grow with
\(p\).  Neither an unregularized product over primes nor a direct sum over
all finite fibres is justified by \cref{thm:fredholm}.  A global construction
would require a measure, trace, and convergence law derived from the dynamics
rather than appended after the computation.
\end{remark}
