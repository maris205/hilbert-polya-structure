# Paper plan

## Working title

**Finite Weil Fibres for a Rauzy--AGY Transfer Operator: Fredholm
Determinants, Quadratic Traces, and Chronology Collapse**

## One-sentence contribution

For every fixed odd prime we construct a genuine finite-Weil Fredholm
determinant for the source-locked genus-two AGY operator, then prove that its
arithmetic character is chronology-sensitive but non-separating: one C26
pair has an identical complete finite-fibre polynomial at \(p=43\), while two distinct
C24 symbolic cycles collapse for every prime and every repetition by an
explicit integral symplectic conjugacy.

## Section plan

1. Introduction: open with the positive determinant and immediately state
   the two collapse results.
2. Rauzy--AGY source lock: fixed cocycle, common Bergman domain, and order
   firewall.
3. Finite Weil character: conventions, Thomas formula, exact quadratic
   basis, and local polynomial reconstruction.
4. Fredholm theorem: finite tensor extension, word trace atom, and
   holomorphy.
5. Exact experiments: small-prime local polynomials, 24-prime power scan,
   complete \(p=43\) finite-fibre collision, singular-prime refinement, and the
   150-branch arithmetic census.
6. Integral conjugacy obstruction: P076/P082 proof and its universal
   class-function consequence.
7. Route-A implications: natural quantization without a global prime
   assembly.
8. Conclusion and concrete next gate.
9. Appendix: quotient discriminant proof, Newton identities, source
   matrices, conjugator, and reproducibility details.
