# C27 research question

Can the source-locked genus-two Rauzy--AGY cocycle be reduced modulo odd
primes and quantized by the genuine finite Weil representation so that:

1. the C26 scalar Bergman determinant becomes a chronology-sensitive
   arithmetic Fredholm determinant;
2. its periodic weights retain information beyond the scalar Perron and
   characteristic-polynomial atom; and
3. the prime family exhibits a coherent arithmetic law strong enough to
   motivate a global Hilbert--Pólya assembly?

The experiment is allowed to answer negatively.  The decisive obstructions
are an external modulus with no intrinsic aggregation rule, collapse of
different symbolic histories under symplectic conjugacy, and orbit-dependent
quadratic conductors with no common organization.

The frozen forward convention is

\[
g_{\beta_1\cdots\beta_n}
=g_{\beta_n}\cdots g_{\beta_1}.
\]

For the transfer iterate, the operator factors occur in reverse forward
order, so a column-fibre left action produces exactly this product.  No
averaged transition matrix, transpose surrogate, or product of branch
characters is permitted.
