# C175 research question

For cyclic elementary cellular automaton Rule 184, can one classify the periodic points for every ring length and every conserved particle number, prove that all remaining states enter that periodic core in finite time, and derive closed formulas for every iterate, every primitive temporal cycle, the sector zeta, and the natural Koopman boundary?

The hostile Route-A question is whether exact primitive traffic cycles acquire arithmetic meaning or a full-system unitary merely because their counting formulas are closed. They do not. The cycles are intrinsic and completely reproducible, which supports `A1_WEAK`, but no prime source or target divisor comparison exists. For sectors with at least two minority symbols, transient states make the whole map nonbijective; restricting to the canonical periodic core yields a rotation unitary but changes the phase space.

Success for this paper means a complete all-`N`, all-`k` theorem with an explicit negative Route-A boundary, not a target match or an external novelty claim.
