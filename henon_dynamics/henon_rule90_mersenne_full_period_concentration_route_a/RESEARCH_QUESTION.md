# C155 research question

Does the exact periodic image of Rule 90 on Mersenne circumferences exhibit a
nontrivial large-size orbit law, rather than merely a finite-volume count?

Freeze `L=2^r-1`, `r>=2`, and sample uniformly from the periodic image of the
Rule-90 multiplier `a=x+x^{-1}`.  Determine whether exact period `L`
concentrates, whether the total primitive-cycle count is asymptotic to
`2^(L-1)/L`, and whether the mean cycle length is asymptotic to `L`.  Retain
the exact divisor-resolved Möbius ledger and the neighboring power-of-two
nilpotent control.
