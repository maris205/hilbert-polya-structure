# C155 experiment plan

1. Freeze the Mersenne family, periodic-image probability, exact-period and
   cycle normalizations, and the cycle-averaged length.
2. Reuse the all-`r` identity `a^(L+1)=a`, but independently reprove the
   periodic-image dimension and period support.
3. Prove the fixed-space gcd dependence by polynomial Bézout and bound its
   dimension by `2 gcd(j,L)`.
4. Apply a union bound to non-full-period states and Burnside to total cycles;
   derive both asymptotic limits.
5. Generate exact divisor ledgers and every proper-time fixed dimension for
   `2<=r<=8`; retain power-of-two controls through `s=8`.
6. Validate with binary matrices, a separate SymPy quotient-ring path,
   byte replay, and repaired-hash semantic mutations.
7. Compile and genuinely repair the manuscript through two internal rounds;
   close exactly 27 payload files under a self-excluded manifest.

Finite rows are implementation sentinels; the proof is uniform in `r`.
