# C155 hostile audit

The mutation harness changes semantic fields, repairs the payload hash, and
requires rejection by the independent checker.  It covers source/date/scope,
periodic-image structure, gcd dependence and Bézout proof, dimension and
concentration bounds, Möbius/Burnside ledgers, probability and mean-length
normalizations, power controls, Route-A coordinates, scope flags, and
nonclaims.  All 53 repaired-hash cases and the separate stale-hash case are
rejected.
