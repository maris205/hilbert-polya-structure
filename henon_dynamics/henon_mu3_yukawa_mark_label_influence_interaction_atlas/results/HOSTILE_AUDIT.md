# C87 hostile mutation audit

Twenty-seven semantic mutations cover schema/status/scope, predecessor hashes,
support totals, ambient-kernel and faithful-group orders, both normalization
conventions, first-order size cells/raw/rational values, label orbits,
second-order positive/negative cells and rational interactions, pair orbits,
numerical classes, exact identities (including the C82 boundary bridge), and a
scope claim flag.

All 27/27 mutations are rejected by the independent checker.  An initial
fixture audit also caught and corrected two no-op mutation definitions that
targeted already-zero fields; only actual semantic changes are counted in the
reported 27 cases.
