# C87 research question

For the frozen sixteen-label full-core predicate, what exact first-order
influence does each label exert at every coalition size, what signed
second-order interaction does each of the 120 unordered label pairs exert,
and how do these rows quotient under the faithful 1920-element label action?

The question is finite and exact.  It asks for raw swing and discrete-Hessian
counts, rational Banzhaf and Shapley normalizations, and faithful label/pair
orbits.  It does not ask for arithmetic or local data, Euler factors, root
numbers, automorphy, a full Burnside ring/table of marks, or a Hilbert--Polya
operator.
