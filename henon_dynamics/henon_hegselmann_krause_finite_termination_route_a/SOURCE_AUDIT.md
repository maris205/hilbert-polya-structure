# C312 source and claim audit

The model and one-dimensional cubic convergence theorem are attributed to
Bhattacharyya, Braverman, Chazelle, and Nguyen,
[*On the Convergence of the Hegselmann--Krause System*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1909).
The paper proves order preservation, permanent decomposition across gaps,
and the decisive two-step lemma.  C312 reproduces the argument with radius
`epsilon`, freezes a safe explicit bound, and adds an exact fixed-point/cell
atlas and a nonconserved-mean control.

The theorem is for one dimension, homogeneous radius, synchronous updates,
and the closed rule `distance <= epsilon`.  Heterogeneous/noisy radii,
asynchronous dynamics, and a sharp higher-dimensional time bound are outside
scope.  The finite rational grid is regression evidence, not the proof.

No target arithmetic datum, Euler factor, root number, automorphy, divisor
law, functional equation, target-zero match, or Hilbert--Pólya operator is
claimed.  Route B is locked.
