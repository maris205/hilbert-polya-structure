# Research question (HCS-C221)

For the one-dimensional focusing cubic NLS, can one close a single theorem
owner that simultaneously handles:

1. the exact sech standing wave for every \(\omega>0\);
2. mass, Hamiltonian, action and the positive VK slope;
3. both real Hessians with essential threshold, all discrete eigenvalues and
   explicit kernels;
4. Morse index and Pöschl–Teller factorizations; and
5. the zero-frequency, defocusing, periodic-domain and higher-dimensional
   boundaries without overclaiming nonlinear stability or Hilbert–Pólya
   relevance?

The answer is yes at the one-dimensional variational/spectral level.  It is a
non-arithmetic Route-A certificate only: no intrinsic prime carrier or target
divisor is present.
