# Narrative report

## Progress made

This paper advances the NLS line by locking the normalization and the entire
one-dimensional Hessian owner in one theorem.  The sech profile is verified
directly, and its mass, energy, action and VK derivative are all given with
their exact \(\omega\)-scaling.  The real/imaginary Hessians reduce to the
\(\ell=2\) and \(\ell=1\) Pöschl–Teller operators, so the negative mode,
translation and phase kernels, essential threshold, and Morse index are stated
without relying on a finite numerical box.  The factorization makes the
absence of additional discrete modes transparent.

The \(\omega\downarrow0\), defocusing, periodic and higher-dimensional faces
are separated explicitly.  The certificate does not promote this local
Hamiltonian spectral result to a full nonlinear stability theorem.

## Route-A interpretation

The equation has no intrinsic arithmetic carrier, prime-power clock, or target
divisor.  Relative standing-wave symmetry gives at most a weak orbit hint, so
the strict tuple is `(A0_FAIL, A1_WEAK, A2_FAIL, A3_FAIL,
A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`.  The final entry records only the ordinary natural
Hamiltonian quantization viewpoint; Route B is disabled and no Hilbert–Pólya
operator or dynamical zeta is claimed.
