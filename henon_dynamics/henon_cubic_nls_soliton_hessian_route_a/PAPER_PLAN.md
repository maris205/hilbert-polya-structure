# Paper plan

**Working title:** *The Focusing Cubic NLS Soliton: Exact Hessian Spectrum,
Morse Index, and Pöschl–Teller Factorization — a Route-A Certificate*

**Central theorem.**  For every \(\omega>0\), one convention-locked package
contains the sech standing wave, all variational scalings and VK slope, the
complete \(L_\pm\) spectrum and kernels, Morse index, and scaled
Pöschl–Teller factorizations, followed by the four explicit boundaries.

**Sections.**

1. Equation, standing wave, and variational normalization.
2. Mass/action identities and VK derivative.
3. Hessian operators and exact Pöschl–Teller spectrum.
4. Factorization, Morse index, and what the theorem does not claim.
5. Boundary ledger, executable evidence, provenance, and Route-A verdict.

**Revision contract.** Round 0 freezes the equation and profile. Round 1 adds
the variational identities, VK slope, and Hessian eigenpairs. Round 2 adds the
factorization proof, essential-spectrum/Morse wording, boundary ledger,
independent evidence counts, source links and strict scope stop.
