## Five independent mathematical increments

**C379 -- boundary-corrected reversible multibaker transport.** On the
invariant non-dyadic geometric domain, every mixed binary necklace has a
complete lifted periodic atlas. A primitive word of length d and net
displacement S has gcd(L,S) geometric cycles, each of least period
`d L/gcd(L,S)` and winding `S/gcd(L,S)`. Repetition, reversal and reciprocal
multipliers are retained. The two homogeneous corner cycles are absent from
the geometric phase space, so the geometric zeta is the finite transport
reciprocal determinant times
`(1-(z/2)^L exp(i phi))(1-(z/2)^L exp(-i phi))`.
The exact Chebyshev determinant gives every Fourier eigenvalue and the
diffusion constant 1/2. Odd-ring absolute relaxation, even-ring parity and
the L=1,2 degeneracies are stated separately. The inverse unstable weight is
not the two-dimensional flat-trace weight; the finite transport determinant
is not misidentified with a full phase-space Perron Fredholm determinant.

**C380 -- complete nonlinear Blaschke source spectrum with quantitative
faces.** For `B_a(z)=z(z-a)/(1-az)`, `0<=a<1`, every circle iterate has
`2^n-1` fixed points with a complete primitive and nonlinear stability ledger.
An explicit Hardy annulus and bounded extension prove trace class before
finite-section arguments are used. Both signed channels give
`D_a(u)=(1-u) product_(k>=1)(1-(-a)^k u)^2`.
An independent sphere-index argument closes the stability-weighted primitive
product. The package proves a uniform logarithmic determinant-tail bound and
exact inclusive source root census. At a=0 the determinant is 1-u but the
Perron operator is not rank one; at a=1 cancellation gives a noncompact
rotation and infinitely many even-iterate fixed points. The classical
finite-Blaschke spectral theorem is explicitly attributed.

**C381 -- all-branch intermittent inducing with two clocks.** The alpha-one
LSV map assigns the partition point 1/2 to its left branch. This gives an
exact `2^n` based periodic census, one neutral orbit, and a bijection between
all other primitive cycles and first-return cycles. The return branches
`h_n=(1+g^(n-1))/2`, `g(z)=(sqrt(1+8z)-1)/4`, all map
`D(1,3/4)` strictly into `D(1,1/2)`. An explicit reciprocal recurrence and
telescoping derivative identity give a uniform `O(n^-2)` derivative bound.
A Hardy rank-one expansion proves trace-norm summability of every return
branch, including the closed original-clock parameter disk. The determinant
counts returns with u and original iterates with zeta, preserving both in
the primitive product. The original defining branch series fails for
`|zeta|>1`; no prohibition of analytic continuation is inferred. The
unnormalized Lebesgue return tail is asymptotic to `1/(4n)`. On the specified
uninduced L1 space, neutral approximate eigenvectors and contraction prove
that every restricted power on zero-integral densities has norm exactly one,
in particular ruling out uniform exponential operator-norm relaxation.

**C382 -- all-good-prime CM phase rigidity and a fixed-fibre obstruction.**
For `E:y^2=x^3-x`, character cancellation handles every supersingular prime,
while rational four-torsion fixes the primary Gaussian Frobenius sign at
every ordinary prime. The normalized ordinary phase is not a root of unity;
the supersingular phase has order four. All extension degrees, Hasse
equality cases, primitive closed points and reduced fixed-point
multiplicities are determined. The complete native H0/H1/H2 ratio, functional
equation and critical circle are retained. Under `u=p^(-s)` a fixed fibre
has only linear vertical zero-count growth, excluding equality with a
Riemann-target divisor up to a zero-free entire factor. No undefined product
over characteristics is inserted to evade this fixed-owner obstruction.

**C383 -- full Friedrichs Aharonov--Bohm scattering and determinant
obstructions.** The fixed self-adjoint domain, Hankel spectral resolution,
complete wave operators and every signed angular scattering phase are
connected to the full forward delta/principal-value distribution, local heat
kernel and gauge symmetry. Position-preserving time reversal is distinguished
from reflection-composed antiunitarity. For noninteger flux, S-I is
noncompact, and two exhaustive angular cutoff windows produce different
determinant phases. The heat semigroup is noncompact with infinite extended
positive trace. These are concrete obstructions to ordinary global
determinants, not failures of the source spectral/scattering theorem.

The owners use five different phase spaces and proof mechanisms. The three
manuscript rounds within each package are revisions of ONE paper, not
additional papers. Classical formulas and external theorem owners are
explicitly credited; no five-paper literature-novelty certificate is claimed.
