# Source Audit

## Primary theorem source

P. Deligne, *La conjecture de Weil. I*, Publications Mathématiques de
l'IHÉS 43 (1974), 273--307, DOI
<https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684373>.  The smooth-leading-form exponential-sum
bound used here is the classical input.  Its use is generic and is not
claimed as novelty.

## Twisted exponential sums

L. Fu, *Twisted Exponential Sums*, Advances in Mathematics 219 (2008),
2105--2141, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2008.08.003>, preprint
<https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607164>.  This provides context for mixed
Artin--Schreier--Kummer constructions.  The scalar Kummer control in this
project is elementary and does not require a new cohomological theorem.

## Finite symmetry determinant factorization

D. Borthwick and T. Weich, *Symmetry Reduction of Holomorphic Iterated
Function Schemes and Factorization of Selberg Zeta Functions*, Journal of
Spectral Theory 6 (2016), 267--329, DOI
<https://doi.org/10.4171/JST/125>, preprint
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6134>.  This supports the general prior-art fact
that finite symmetries can factor dynamical determinants.  It does not cover
the reversing \(\mu_3\) Hénon relation or the arithmetic-fiber obstruction
proved here.

## Repository prior-work firewall after C38--C42

The rebased repository contains a companion cubic sequence that must not be
repackaged as novelty here.  HCS-C38 proves that functorial three-channel
Kummer lifts preserve the scalar coboundary.  HCS-C39 proves divisor
accumulation for a fixed three-channel prime product.  HCS-C40 shows that
prime damping gives a Schatten determinant only by inserting an external
clock.  HCS-C41 constructs the intrinsic \(j=0\) CM elliptic Frobenius factor,
and HCS-C42 proves that every finite Tate-plus-CM virtual combination matching
the Riemann local factor at \(p=5,7,11\) deletes the CM \(H^1\) term.

The present C43 result is different: it works with the full two-variable
Fourier--cubic Hénon kernel, a reversing permutation symmetry, forced
two-step chronology, growing sector determinants, and an exact \(p=7\)
same-point conjugation obstruction.  Its successor is correspondingly not a
finite CM repair.  The remaining large gate asks whether the conjugate-paired
full-kernel moment sequences descend across split and inert places to a pure
self-dual compatible system over a fixed coefficient field, with uniformly
bounded rank and conductor.  Fixed-field descent is tested first by algebraic
degree; uniform Hankel rank comes second.  C43 does not reopen C39's shifted
divisor product or C40's prime-block Fredholm determinant, and C42 closes only
constant finite Tate--CM exponents, not these source-native full-kernel
moments.
