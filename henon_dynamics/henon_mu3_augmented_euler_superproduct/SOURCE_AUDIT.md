# Source Audit

## Primary theorem source

P. Deligne, *La conjecture de Weil. I*, Publications Mathématiques de
l'IHÉS 43 (1974), 273--307, DOI
<https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684373>.  The smooth-leading-form exponential-sum
bound used here is the classical input.  Its use is generic and is not
claimed as novelty.

## Twisted exponential sums

L. Fu, *Twisted Exponential Sums*, Advances in Mathematics 219 (2008),
2105--2141, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2008.08.003>, preprint
<https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607164>.  This provides context for mixed
Artin--Schreier--Kummer constructions.  The scalar Kummer control in this
project is elementary and does not require a new cohomological theorem.

## Finite symmetry determinant factorization

D. Borthwick and T. Weich, *Symmetry Reduction of Holomorphic Iterated
Function Schemes and Factorization of Selberg Zeta Functions*, Journal of
Spectral Theory 6 (2016), 267--329, DOI
<https://doi.org/10.4171/JST/125>, preprint
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6134>.  This supports the general prior-art fact
that finite symmetries can factor dynamical determinants.  It does not cover
the reversing \(\mu_3\) Hénon relation or the arithmetic-fiber obstruction
proved here.

## Search-bounded novelty record

Queries run on 2026-08-13 included:

- `finite field quantum map cubic phase symmetry equivariant zeta determinant`;
- `equivariant dynamical zeta finite group symmetry factorization`;
- `Kummer sheaf convolution additive character cubic exponential sum`;
- `graded determinant finite group augmentation representation zeta`;
- `symmetry reduction dynamical zeta finite group`;
- `cubic exponential sums finite field Airy sheaf Fourier transform`.

No source was found that combines the exact homogeneous Hénon reversing
relation, the integral cubic augmentation Euler germ, and the first-prime
conjugation obstruction.  This is a search-bounded novelty statement, not a
proof of bibliographic uniqueness.
