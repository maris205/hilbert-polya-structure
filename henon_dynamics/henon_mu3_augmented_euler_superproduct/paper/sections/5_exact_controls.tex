\section{Exact noncancellation certificate}

The assertion ``virtual rank two'' could conceal a large common spectral
factor.  We test this without floating-point eigenvalues.  For every frozen
prime $p$, choose the smallest auxiliary prime
$\ell\equiv1\pmod{3p}$.  Then $\mathbb F_\ell$ contains exact roots of unity
of orders $p$ and three.  Because $T_p=U_p^2$, the normalization is $p^{-1}$
and no square root is required.

We construct the three sector bases, restrict $T_p$, and compute exact
characteristic polynomials over $\mathbb F_\ell$.  A coprime reduction at a
good prime proves that the characteristic-zero polynomials were coprime.
The producer uses FLINT; the checker independently uses integer matrix
arithmetic, Faddeev--LeVerrier characteristic polynomials, and Euclidean
polynomial gcds.

Each row is an exact theorem about that characteristic-zero local factor.
The table is not promoted to an all-prime coprimality theorem.  It establishes
the narrower and decisive point: order-three symmetry alone does not enforce
a bounded-rank cancellation mechanism.  Any proposed global continuation
must explain increasingly large local divisors rather than appeal to the net
degree two.

As a scalar control, the cover $u^3=2x^3$ becomes $t^3=2$ after $t=u/x$.
Its geometric scaling local system is constant; a residual constant-field
Frobenius factor carries no $x$-dependent H\'enon chronology.  Thus the full
kernel symmetry used above is essential.
