\section{Route-A decision and next gate}

The construction makes a substantial positive step: a non-scalar symmetry
of the H\'enon generating kernel forces a chronological sector determinant,
and that determinant has a proved Euler half-plane.  It also meets a clean
stopping theorem before any comparison with Riemann zeros.

The formal Route-A tuple is
\[
(\mathrm{A1\_WEAK},\mathrm{A2\_ANALYTIC\_DETERMINANT},
\mathrm{A3\_FAIL},\mathrm{A4\_NATURAL\_QUANTIZATION}),
\]
with overall status \texttt{ROUTE\_A\_REJECTED}.  A1 is weak because primes
index different arithmetic fibers rather than primitive orbits of one real
map.  A2 records the explicit rational local determinants and the proved
nonzero half-plane; it is not a divisor match.  A3 fails by
\cref{thm:p7}.  A4 is natural at every finite place, but no single global
Hilbert--P\'olya operator has been constructed.  Route B is not authorized.

The companion C41--C42 rigidity chain already closes the obvious
finite-dimensional repair: adjoining the $j=0$ CM elliptic motive and Tate
pieces matches the Riemann local factor only when the CM contribution is
deleted.  The next large gate is therefore precise.  After pairing the
additive character with its inverse and including split and inert places, do
the full-kernel moment sequences descend to a pure self-dual compatible
system over one fixed coefficient field, with rank and conductor bounded
independently of $p$?  A fixed coefficient field first bounds the algebraic
degrees of the paired first moments.  Only if that descent gate survives does
fixed rank force a uniform recurrence, equivalently bounded Hankel rank, for
all chronological moments.  The frozen coprimality ledger points toward
growth, but does not settle either paired all-prime statement.  A positive
answer must construct the compatible system and functional equation before
inspecting zeros; unbounded trace degree or Hankel rank closes the
order-three H\'enon Euler route.  Fitted conjugate factors are not admissible.
