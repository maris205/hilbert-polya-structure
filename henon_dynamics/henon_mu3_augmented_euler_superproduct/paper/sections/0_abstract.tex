We construct a genuinely non-scalar order-three symmetry reduction of the
homogeneous area-preserving H\'enon map.  The full generating kernel
$S_0(q,Q)=qQ+2q^3$ is invariant under opposite cubic scalings of its two
variables.  Over every prime $p\equiv1\pmod3$, its Fourier--cubic
quantization satisfies the reversing relation $U_pR_p=R_p^{-1}U_p$; hence
the two-step time $T_p=U_p^2$ preserves three character sectors.  The
canonical integral augmentation weights $(2,-1,-1)$ yield a rational local
superdeterminant with an exact chronological twisted-orbit trace formula.
Smooth-leading-cubic estimates imply a uniform moment bound and a nonzero
canonical Euler germ on $\RePart s>1$.  Exact modular characteristic-polynomial
calculations at all nine split primes through $73$ show that virtual rank two does not
produce bounded local spectral rank: the reduced numerator and denominator
degrees reach $50$ and $48$ at $p=73$.  More decisively, an exact residue
histogram at $p=7$ proves that the first augmentation coefficient is nonreal.
Thus the raw product fails conjugation symmetry even though every local
factor is unitary and self-inversive.  This supplies both a new H\'enon Euler
germ and a theorem-level obstruction to its promotion as a Riemann
determinant.  Companion rigidity controls close every finite Tate--CM repair.
A viable successor must instead descend the conjugate-paired full
Hénon-kernel moments to a self-dual compatible system over one fixed number
field, with uniformly bounded rank and conductor.
