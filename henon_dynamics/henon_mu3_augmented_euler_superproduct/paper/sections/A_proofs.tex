\section{Supplementary proofs and reproducibility}

\subsection{Determinant normalization}\label{app:detnorm}

Write $U=FM$, where $F$ is normalized Fourier transform and
$M(q)=e_p(2q^3)$.  On every nonzero $\mu_3$ orbit the chirp is scalar.  The
product of these scalars is one, because the orbits are indexed by the cube
subgroup and the sum of that subgroup vanishes.  The extra vector at zero in
grade zero also has eigenvalue one.  Therefore
$\det(M|\Hh_{p,k})=1$ in every grade.

Furthermore $F^2=J$, where $(Jf)(x)=f(-x)$.  Since
$p\equiv1\pmod6$, $-1$ is a cube.  Put $m=(p-1)/3$; negation pairs the $m$
nonzero cubic orbits into $m/2$ transpositions.  The zero vector is fixed in
grade zero.  Hence
\[
\det(J|\Hh_{p,k})=(-1)^{m/2}=(-1)^{(p-1)/6}
\]
for every $k$.  Block determinants for $U^2$ give
\cref{prop:detnorm}.

Complex conjugation gives
\[
\bar U=F^{-1}M^{-1}=MU^{-1}M^{-1}.
\]
Because $M$ commutes with $R$, conjugation sends grade $k$ to grade $-k$ and
gives cross-grade similarity between $\overline{T_k}$ and $T_{-k}^{-1}$.
Grade zero is self-dual.  For grades one and two, combine this statement with
the independently proved equality $D_{p,1}=D_{p,2}$.  This proves the local
self-inversive relations used in the text without identifying conjugate
grades silently.

\subsection{Exact arithmetic protocol}

The frozen producer and checker share only scalar conventions and released
JSON.  The producer uses Python--FLINT.  The checker does not import the
producer or FLINT; it independently reconstructs matrices, computes
characteristic polynomials by the Faddeev--LeVerrier recurrence, and computes
polynomial gcds by Euclid's algorithm.  All twelve semantic blocks are compared
recursively with type-strict JSON equality.  The release runner reproduces
the certificate in a temporary directory before checking the frozen copy.
