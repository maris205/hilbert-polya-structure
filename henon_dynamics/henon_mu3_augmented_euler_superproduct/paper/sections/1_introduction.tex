\section{Introduction}

Finite-field quantizations of nonlinear maps offer an unusually direct
meeting point between chronological dynamics, unitary spectra, and arithmetic
Euler products.  Their main danger is equally direct: a local unitary matrix
can place zeros on a chosen vertical line without supplying the global
symmetries of the completed Riemann zeta function.  A useful candidate must
therefore survive both construction and falsification gates.

This paper studies the homogeneous area-preserving H\'enon map
\[
H_0(q,p)=(-6q^2-p,q),
\]
generated by $S_0(q,Q)=qQ+2q^3$.  Direct cubic roots of the scalar chirp are
geometrically constant and give no H\'enon information.  The full
two-variable kernel, however, has a non-scalar order-three reversing
symmetry.  Its quantization exchanges the two nontrivial character sectors
in one step and preserves all sectors in two steps.  This forces an integral
augmentation determinant rather than an arbitrary colored trace.

Our contributions are fourfold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item We prove the exact classical and quantum reversing relations and the
      resulting sector square identity.
\item We retain chronological H\'enon time in a twisted path-sum formula and
      prove a locally uniform, zero-free canonical Euler germ on
      $\RePart s>1$.
\item We give exact modular certificates showing that the natural virtual
      rank-two factor retains linearly growing local spectral complexity.
\item We prove, without a zero table or floating-point root computation, that
      the first split-prime coefficient is nonreal.  The raw Euler product is
      therefore rejected by the conjugation-symmetry gate of Route A.
\end{enumerate}

The positive and negative conclusions are intentionally kept together.  The
order-three symmetry is genuine and reusable; the raw single-character Euler
promotion is not.  The only serious successor is a new self-dual construction
over the Eisenstein field, not a post hoc symmetrization of this product.
