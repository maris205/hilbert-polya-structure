## 2. Infinite-localization smooth-order return systems — insufficient residual

### Exact intended question

Let `K=F_q(t)`, with characteristic p, and let P be any finite set of
rational primes containing p. In `F_q[t,t^(-1)]`, retain the prime
polynomials whose nonzero residue of t has multiplicative order supported
on `P\{p}`, and invert all other prime polynomials. Multiplication by t
on this discrete ring dualizes to a compact abelian-group automorphism.
Its clock is ordinary positive iteration n; its observable is the finite
geometric fixed-point cardinality and the Artin–Mazur zeta. Localization
membership is source arithmetic, not a target-prime factor assignment.

The candidate question was whether this entire finite-P family has exact
return counts and a noncancelling zeta natural boundary despite infinitely
many retained/localized places. The proposed increment would have had to
go beyond the established S-integer construction and valuation formula.

### Cheap decisive reduction and disposition

Write `n_P` for the P-supported part of n. Factoring `t^n-1` shows that
the retained degree is `n_P`: away from p the cyclotomic degrees sum by
`sum_(d|m) phi(d)=m`, and the characteristic-p multiplicity is
`p^v_p(n)`. The elementary quotient count is therefore `q^(n_P)`.

The normalized fixed-count series has coefficients `q^(n_P-n)`.
Its P-smooth terms are one; all remaining terms decay exponentially,
because their prime-to-P part is at least the smallest prime outside P.
Consequently the analytic issue reduces to the standard integer-coefficient
series of the P-smooth indicator plus a larger-radius analytic remainder.
The intended nonrationality/boundary route is a direct Pólya–Carlson
application, with non-eventual periodicity of that sparse infinite support.
This is a short standard reduction, not an independently developed theory
of general infinite localization sets or of their arithmetic inverse problem.

**REJECT_INSUFFICIENT_POST_CLASSICAL_INCREMENT.** This scout does not claim
that the precise selected P-family has been located verbatim in a paper;
it says the remaining argument does not meet the current paper-level gate.
It does not resurrect the old chronology-solenoid candidate. That candidate
has a different switched clock and an already recorded aggregation boundary.
No large computation or manuscript was commissioned for this reduction.

## 3. Exponential-wall endpoint changes — direct collision

### Exact intended question

For every `a>0` and every fixed real Robin parameter b, consider
`-d²/dx²+a² exp(2x)` on `[0,infinity)` with
`u'(0)=b u(0)`; include the Dirichlet endpoint separately. Use energy E
and frequency `sqrt(E)` on the positive spectrum, keeping any finitely
many nonpositive levels separate. The proposed observable was the ordinary
source spectral determinant/counting function under fixed positive frequency
rescaling and fixed energy shift. The question was whether the endpoint
freedom changes the exact target-counting obstruction.
