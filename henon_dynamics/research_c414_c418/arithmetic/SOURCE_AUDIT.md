## Governing distinction

The proposed result is the equivalence between two labelled modulus-return
rows, a **single congruence-compatible nonlinear conjugacy**, and unitary
equivalence preserving every congruence projection, for every matrix in
SL(2,Z). It is not a new proof of the general rooted-tree conjugacy criterion
or the linear local-conjugacy classification. The old hyperbolic census
quotient and its trace-18 pair are explicitly deducted.

## Substantive assessment and limits

The compatible-conjugacy result is stronger than a family of unrelated
permutation matches at separate moduli. Its explicit lifting construction is
the actual extra argument. It controls the full inverse limit, the entire
congruence filtration in the Koopman model, and the failure of differentiable
local conjugacy. The all-SL2 boundary closure is included for a complete family,
not counted as a second result. These form **one candidate**, not several papers.

After subtracting GNS, BRW, matrix LTE and the old census quotient, the residual
is a short homogeneous dyadic lifting classification plus its global and
operator consequences. That may be a worthwhile note, but paper-level value
is **not established by this author audit**. An independent reviewer should
decide whether this is sufficiently substantive for the current batch. The
old BF texts could still shrink the residual further. Nothing here clears that
historical priority gate by renaming the observation.

No target Euler factors, root number, gamma factor, zero divisor, automorphy,
Weil form or Hilbert–Pólya realization is obtained. The result diagnoses an
information boundary of the declared source model. No formal Route A evaluation
or target test is represented as having run.
