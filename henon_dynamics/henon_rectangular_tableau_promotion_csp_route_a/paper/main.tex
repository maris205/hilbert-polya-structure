\begin{abstract}
For every rectangular standard Young-tableau set $X_{a,b}=\mathrm{SYT}(b^a)$,
we turn Rhoades's attributed cyclic-sieving theorem into a complete finite
dynamical classification.  Root-of-unity evaluations of the unshifted
$q$-hook polynomial give every fixed iterate; M\"obius inversion gives all
least periods and cycles, hence the Artin--Mazur zeta, finite Koopman
determinant, traces, and every spectral multiplicity.  Evacuation reverses
promotion.  The theorem says $j^{ab}=1$, not that the order always equals
$ab$.  The resulting finite unitary is natural but carries no intrinsic
rational-prime semantics or target divisor.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} promotion; standard Young tableaux; cyclic
sieving; $q$-hook formula; Koopman spectrum; evacuation.

\section{Frozen convention and source theorem}
Fix positive integers $a,b$, put $N=ab$, and let
$X_{a,b}=\mathrm{SYT}(b^a)$.  We use the promotion convention $j$ in
Rhoades~\cite{Rhoades10}: remove $N$, slide the hole northwest, increment the
retained entries, and insert $1$.  Removing $1$ and sliding southeast is the
inverse clock and is not silently substituted.

For $[r]_q=(q^r-1)/(q-1)$ define the \emph{unshifted} polynomial
\[
 F_{a,b}(q)=\frac{[N]_q!}{\prod_{c\in b^a}[h(c)]_q}.
\]
Rhoades's rectangular standard-tableau CSP states, with
$\omega_N=e^{2\pi i/N}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:csp}
 \#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d)=F_{a,b}(\omega_N^d)
 \qquad(d\in\mathbb Z).
\end{equation}
The same source records the classical consequence $j^N=1$, with order
background attributed to Haiman~\cite{Haiman92}.  It explicitly warns that
the actual order need not be $N$.  Thus the cyclic group of order $N$ may act
nonfaithfully.

The polynomial convention matters.  The neighboring column-strict result in
the source uses a shifted Schur specialization; importing that shift into
\eqref{eq:csp} would change fixed counts.  We use exactly the standard-tableau
formula displayed above.

\section{From fixed points to every cycle}
Let $P_\ell$ be the number of tableaux of least promotion period $\ell$, and
let $C_\ell=P_\ell/\ell$.  Since $j^N=1$, only $\ell\mid N$ occur.  A tableau
is fixed by $j^d$ precisely when its least period divides $d$, so divisor
M\"obius inversion gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mobius}
 P_\ell=\sum_{d\mid\ell}\mu(\ell/d)
 F_{a,b}(\omega_N^d),\qquad C_\ell=\frac{P_\ell}{\ell}.
\end{equation}
This is an all-rectangle consequence of the source theorem, not an inference
from a finite table.  It also yields the actual promotion order as
$\operatorname{lcm}\{\ell:C_\ell>0\}$, a divisor of $N$.

For the sharp boundary, $F_{2,2}(q)=1+q^2$.  Its evaluations give no fixed
tableau at one promotion step and two at the second, hence one two-cycle.  A
uniform claim of exact order $N$ is therefore false even away from the
one-dimensional singleton families.

\section{Zeta, Koopman determinant, and reversal}
The finite Artin--Mazur series and the cycle blocks give
\begin{align}
 \zeta_j(z)
 &=\exp\!\left(\sum_{d\ge1}
 \frac{\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d)}d z^d\right)
 =\prod_{\ell\mid N}(1-z^\ell)^{-C_\ell},\label{eq:zeta}\\
 \det(I-zU_{a,b})
 &=\prod_{\ell\mid N}(1-z^\ell)^{C_\ell}
 =\zeta_j(z)^{-1},\label{eq:det}
\end{align}
where $(U_{a,b}f)(T)=f(jT)$ on counting $\ell^2(X_{a,b})$.  Each cycle is a
cyclic permutation block.  Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(U_{a,b}^d)=\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d),\qquad
 \operatorname{mult}(\omega_N^k)
 =\sum_{\substack{\ell\mid N\\N\mid k\ell}}C_\ell.
\]
Summing these multiplicities over $k\bmod N$ gives
$\sum_\ell\ell C_\ell=|X_{a,b}|$, so no spectral sector is missing.

Sch\"utzenberger evacuation $e$ is an involution and satisfies
$eje=j^{-1}$ on the rectangle~\cite{Rhoades10}.  Its permutation matrix $E$
is a unitary reversor, $EUE=U^{-1}$.  Since all matrices are real, $E$ followed
by coefficientwise conjugation is an antiunitary involution with the same
reversal law.  This is a natural source operator statement; it is not a
post-hoc diagonal encoding.
