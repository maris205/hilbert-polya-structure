# C187 research question

For every rectangle `b^a` with `N=ab`, can one begin from the correctly
normalized rectangular promotion CSP and close, in one source-locked theorem
package, all of the following without mistaking finite regression for proof?

1. the fixed count of every promotion iterate;
2. every exact-period population and cycle count;
3. the finite Artin--Mazur zeta and reciprocal Koopman determinant;
4. all root-of-unity spectral multiplicities;
5. evacuation as a source-native reversor;
6. the exact boundary between `j^N=id` and the false uniform assertion that
   promotion always has order `N`;
7. a strict Route-A verdict that does not import arithmetic or target claims.

The answer is yes.  Rhoades's 2010 theorem supplies the all-rectangle CSP with
the unshifted q-hook polynomial.  Haiman supplies the classical order
background cited by Rhoades.  Möbius inversion and finite permutation theory
then prove the cycle, zeta, determinant and spectrum statements.  Small
tableau enumeration is an independent regression layer only.
