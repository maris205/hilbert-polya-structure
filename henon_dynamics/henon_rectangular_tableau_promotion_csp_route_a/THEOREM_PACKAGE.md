# C187 proof package: rectangular promotion

## Claim

Let `a,b` be positive integers, `N=ab`, `X=SYT(b^a)`, and let `j` be
Rhoades's jeu-de-taquin promotion convention.  Put

\[
F_{a,b}(q)=\frac{[N]_q!}{\prod_{c\in b^a}[h(c)]_q},
\qquad \omega_N=e^{2\pi i/N}.
\]

Then:

1. `j^N=id`, so every actual promotion period divides `N`; equality with
   `N` is not asserted.
2. For every integer `d`,
   \(\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d)=F_{a,b}(\omega_N^d)\).
3. For every `ell | N`, the exact-period population and cycle count are
   \[
   P_\ell=\sum_{d\mid\ell}\mu(\ell/d)\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d),
   \qquad C_\ell=P_\ell/\ell.
   \]
4. The finite zeta and Koopman determinant are
   \[
   \zeta_j(z)=\prod_{\ell\mid N}(1-z^\ell)^{-C_\ell},\qquad
   \det(I-zU)=\prod_{\ell\mid N}(1-z^\ell)^{C_\ell}=\zeta_j(z)^{-1}.
   \]
5. If `omega_N^k` denotes an `N`th root, its multiplicity for the finite
   Koopman permutation is
   \[
   m_k=\sum_{\substack{\ell\mid N\\N\mid k\ell}}C_\ell,
   \qquad \operatorname{Tr}(U^d)=\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d).
   \]
6. Rectangular evacuation `e` obeys `e^2=id` and `e j e=j^{-1}`.  Its linear
   permutation together with complex conjugation gives an antiunitary
   reversor of `U`.
7. If `a=1` or `b=1`, `X` is a singleton and the actual order is one.

## Status

`PROVABLE AS STATED` as a source-derived theorem package.  Items 1, 2 and the
evacuation relation are explicitly attributed classical inputs.  Items 3--6
are proved consequences.  No new-proof or priority claim is made for the
Rhoades/Haiman inputs.

## Assumptions

- `a,b` are positive integers and the shape is rectangular.
- Promotion uses the frozen remove-maximum convention.
- Rhoades's rectangular standard-tableau CSP and order corollary are imported
  with attribution.
- Evacuation is the classical Schützenberger involution on the same set.

## Notation

- `[r]_q=(q^r-1)/(q-1)` and `[N]_q!=product_[r=1]^N [r]_q`.
- `Fix(j^d)` is the full fixed set of the `d`th iterate.
- `P_ell` counts points of least period `ell`; `C_ell` counts cycles.
- `U f=f composed with j` on `ell^2(X)` with counting probability.

## Proof strategy and dependency map

1. Rhoades's CSP gives the full fixed ledger and his order corollary restricts
   all least periods to divisors of `N`.
2. Divisor inversion converts fixed counts into exact-period counts.
3. Decomposition of a finite permutation into disjoint cycles proves zeta,
   determinant, trace and spectral formulas.
4. The classical evacuation relation supplies the reversor.
5. Hook and singleton edge cases block the false exact-order statement.

## Proof

### Step 1: fixed counts and the order boundary

Rhoades's Theorem 1.3 states that the cyclic action generated by promotion on
`SYT(b^a)` exhibits cyclic sieving with the **unshifted** polynomial
`F_ab(q)`.  Therefore, for every integer `d`, the fixed count is exactly
`F_ab(omega_N^d)`.  Corollary 3.6 gives `j^N=id`.  Hence every least period
divides `N`.  This proves only an upper bound on the order of the permutation.

### Step 2: exact periods

A point is fixed by `j^d` exactly when its least period divides `d`.  Thus, for
every divisor `d` of `N`,

\[
\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d)=\sum_{\ell\mid d}P_\ell.
\]

Möbius inversion on the divisor lattice gives the displayed formula for
`P_ell`.  Every orbit of least period `ell` contains exactly `ell` points, so
`C_ell=P_ell/ell`.  This proves integrality without assuming it from a finite
table.

### Step 3: zeta, determinant and traces

An `ell`-cycle contributes `ell` fixed points to `j^d` precisely when
`ell | d`; hence

\[
\#\operatorname{Fix}(j^d)=\sum_{\ell\mid d}\ell C_\ell.
\]

Substitute this identity into
`exp(sum_[d>=1] Fix(j^d) z^d/d)` and exchange the finite divisor sums.  The
geometric logarithm gives one factor `(1-z^ell)^(-C_ell)` for each cycle
length.  On an `ell`-cycle, `U` is an `ell x ell` cyclic permutation matrix,
whose determinant factor is `1-z^ell`.  Multiplying cycle blocks proves the
determinant formula and its reciprocal relation to the zeta.  The trace of a
permutation matrix is its number of fixed basis vectors, proving
`Tr(U^d)=Fix(j^d)`.

### Step 4: spectral multiplicities

Each `ell`-cycle contributes every `ell`th root of unity once.  The root
`omega_N^k` is an `ell`th root exactly when `N` divides `k ell`.  Summing one
copy over all `C_ell` cycle blocks proves the formula for `m_k`.  Its sum over
`k mod N` equals `sum ell C_ell=|X|`, so the spectral ledger is complete.

### Step 5: reversal and boundaries

Schützenberger evacuation is an involution and, on rectangular tableaux,
obeys `e j e=j^{-1}`.  Its real permutation matrix `E` therefore satisfies
`E U E=U^{-1}`.  Since the matrices are real, `E` followed by coefficientwise
complex conjugation is an antiunitary involution with the same reversal law.

For one row or one column, there is exactly one standard tableau.  Promotion
is the identity, so its order is one even when `N>1`.  For `(2,2)`,
`F(q)=1+q^2`; the cycle ledger consists of one two-cycle, again showing that
the actual order can be strictly smaller than `N`.  This completes the proof.

## Route-A stopping theorem

The cycles, zeta and finite unitary are source-native, so A1 is weakly positive
and A4 is natural.  Nevertheless, the construction is uniform for arbitrary
rectangle dimensions and has no intrinsic rational-prime carrier, prime-power
repetition weights, or logarithmic prime clock.  Its determinant is a finite
root-of-unity polynomial with no established target divisor.  Consequently

`(A0,A1,A2,A3,A4)=(FAIL,WEAK,FAIL,FAIL,NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`, overall
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`, and Route B is false.

## Open risks

- The package does not reprove the Kazhdan--Lusztig argument underlying the
  classical CSP; it records and cites it.
- It does not claim a closed elementary formula for the actual promotion order
  of every rectangle beyond the exact cycle ledger implied by CSP.
- Finite regression cannot establish literature-wide novelty or theorem
  priority.
