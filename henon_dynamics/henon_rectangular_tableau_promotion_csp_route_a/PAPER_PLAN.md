# C187 paper plan

## Title

*Rectangular Promotion: Cyclic Sieving, the Complete Cycle Ledger, and the
Finite Koopman Spectrum*

## Narrative

1. Freeze the rectangular standard-tableau phase space and Rhoades's exact
   promotion convention.
2. State the unshifted q-hook CSP and the order-divides-`N` boundary.
3. Derive all least-period populations and cycles by Möbius inversion.
4. Convert the cycle ledger into the finite zeta, Koopman determinant, trace,
   spectral multiplicities and evacuation reversal.
5. Exhibit one-dimensional and `2x2` controls, then state the Route-A stop.
6. Separate attributed infinite theorems from finite executable regression.

## Three revision rounds

- Round 0: fixed counts, order boundary and Möbius cycle recovery.
- Round 1: finite zeta/determinant and full root-of-unity spectral
  multiplicities.
- Round 2: evacuation antiunitary reversal, explicit evidence boundary,
  mutation closure and strict Route-A verdict.

The round-focus paragraph is selected by `CRevisionRound`; all three PDFs are
