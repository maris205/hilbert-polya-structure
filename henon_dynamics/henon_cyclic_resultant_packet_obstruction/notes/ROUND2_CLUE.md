# ROUND2_CLUE

packet assembly problem.

For each primitive orbit and cyclotomic index, factor

\[
\mathfrak C_{\gamma,n}=(\Phi_n(\lambda_\gamma))
\]

in its trace/multiplier field and retain:

- prime ideal;
- underlying rational prime;
- residue degree and ramification index;
- primitive occurrence index;
- orbit sign and pressure length;
- repetition multiplicity.

The smallest useful theorem would prove that a canonical weighted pushforward
of these packets has a stable von-Mangoldt-type main term without reading a
prime table.  A finite pilot should first compare the quadratic period-four
sequence, where eventual primitive divisors are known, against the two
quartic sequences.  Failure should be recorded as a residue-degree or
cross-orbit multiplicity obstruction, not hidden by taking absolute norms.
