# Narrative Report

## Research question

After HCS-P48 rejects one rational prime per primitive H6 orbit, can the
smallest collective replacement—cyclic resultants or their cyclotomic ideal
packets—supply a repetition-compatible arithmetic carrier?

## Strongest positive result

For any reciprocal algebraic-unit multiplier, the principal ideals
`(1-lambda^r)` and `(Phi_n(lambda))` are canonical under inversion and obey
the exact cyclotomic divisor factorization.  This is a genuine collective
object that survives the termwise scalar obstruction.

## Strongest obstruction

The full multiplier-field norm of the two-dimensional cyclic determinant is
a perfect square.  More strongly, every full primitive cyclotomic norm at
index `n>2` is a perfect square because a unit-normalized cyclotomic value
lies in the fixed trace field.  Neither full-field scalar can be a rational
prime.

## Strongest survivor

The determinant itself lies in the trace field.  Its minimal rational norm is
the ordinary cyclic resultant, not the squared full-field norm.  The
corresponding primitive half norms can be prime or composite.  They survive
as repetition-indexed sequences, but the exact controls reject a one-fixed-
scalar power law.

## Exact evidence

The signed minimal polynomials for primitive H6 periods one, three, and four
are evaluated through repetition index 12.  All 36 primitive rows satisfy
the divisor factorization and all 30 rows with index greater than two are
exact squares.  The controls include nonsquare level-two values, a
nonreciprocal polynomial with primitive norm 13, and failure of a one-scalar
power law.

## Claim boundary

The paper does not prove primitive prime ideals for the quartic H6 fields,
an all-prime distribution theorem, a von Mangoldt trace, analytic
continuation, a functional equation, or a Hilbert–Pólya operator.  Those are
new theorems, not consequences of the local packet identities.

## Recommended verdict

The full-field scalarization is `ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.  The trace-field
Lehmer--Pierce sequence and ideal-packet object are retained as
`OPEN_EXACT_STRUCTURE`; Route B remains unauthorized.
