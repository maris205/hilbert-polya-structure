# HCS-P49: cyclic resultant packets and the norm trilemma

HCS-P48 proved that no common real power turns every primitive H6 multiplier
into a rational prime.  HCS-P49 tests the next collective object: the cyclic
resultant packet generated by the signed unstable eigenvalue
$\lambda_\gamma$.

For

\[
\mathfrak A_{\gamma,r}=(1-\lambda_\gamma^r),
\qquad
\mathfrak C_{\gamma,n}=(\Phi_n(\lambda_\gamma)),
\]

the project proves

\[
\mathfrak A_{\gamma,r}
=\prod_{n\mid r}\mathfrak C_{\gamma,n},
\qquad
(\det(I-M_\gamma^r))\mathcal O_K=\mathfrak A_{\gamma,r}^2.
\]

If $K=\mathbb Q(\lambda_\gamma)$ and $K^+$ is the fixed trace field, then

\[
\left|N_{K/\mathbb Q}\det(I-M_\gamma^r)\right|
=\left|N_{K^+/\mathbb Q}\det(I-M_\gamma^r)\right|^2.
\]

For every $n>2$, the unit-normalized value

\[
\beta_{\gamma,n}
=\lambda_\gamma^{-\varphi(n)/2}\Phi_n(\lambda_\gamma)
\]

lies in $\mathcal O_{K^+}$, so its full cyclotomic norm is also a square.
This rejects **full multiplier-field norm scalarization** as a rational-prime
label.

The result is a trilemma, not a blanket no-go:

1. the full $K$-norm is an exact square and stops;
2. the minimal $K^+$-norm is the Lehmer--Pierce cyclic-resultant sequence and
   survives;
3. the principal ideals retain still more splitting data and survive.

The exact certificate uses the actual signed multipliers of primitive H6
periods 1, 3, and 4.  It verifies 36 resultant rows through index 12, all 30
square-theorem rows, 10 adversarial tests, and eight inherited hash locks.
The half norms include primes such as $19$, $7451$, and $577$, as well as
composites.  They fail the one-fixed-scalar repetition law.  Flatters'
quadratic theorem further gives a fresh primitive rational divisor in every
period-four term beyond index 12.
