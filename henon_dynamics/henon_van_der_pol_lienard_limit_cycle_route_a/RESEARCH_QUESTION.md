# Research question — HCS-C249

Can a smooth nonlinear oscillator supply one theorem-scale, replayable
dynamical certificate without importing arithmetic data?  For the standard
Van der Pol family the answer is yes at the analytic level.  Writing
\(f(x)=\mu(x^2-1)\) and
\(F(x)=\int_0^x f(s)\,ds=\mu(x^3/3-x)\), the Liénard theorem closes the
positive-sign family with one attracting cycle.  Time reversal closes the
negative-sign family, while the zero face is an exactly solvable harmonic
center.  The same certificate ties the cycle to a Poincaré fixed point and a
strictly contracting transverse Floquet multiplier.

The question is intentionally source-local.  It does not ask for a prime or
zero table, an Euler factor, a root number, automorphy, a target divisor or
determinant, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.  Those objects are outside the
frozen model and remain explicit nonclaims.
