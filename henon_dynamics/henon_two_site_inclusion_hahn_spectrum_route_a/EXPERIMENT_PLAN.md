# Exact-evidence plan

The deterministic grid is alpha in {1/2,1,3/2,2} and N=0,...,8. For every coordinate the producer writes all N+1 beta-binomial masses, both boundary-aware rates, all N+1 Hahn vectors, eigenvalues, and positive squared norms. A second ledger records every alpha=0 rate, endpoint absorption probability, complete stationary endpoint-mixture family, and limiting stationary mass.

The independent checker rederives all rational objects, tests detailed balance, the difference equation at every state, pairwise orthogonality, norms, and full nested coordinate sets. SymPy repeats 492 identities with symbolic positive alpha. Isolated replay requires byte identity. Hostile mutations repair the evidence payload hash while changing semantics, attack JSON/YAML parsers, and require optimized Python to fail closed.

Finite checks are not used as a proof of the general theorem.
