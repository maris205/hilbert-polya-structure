# Research question

For the conservative two-site inclusion chain with fixed mass N and rates

'x -> x+1' at '(N-x)(alpha+x)' and 'x -> x-1' at 'x(alpha+N-x)',

can one prove in one closed theorem the unique reversible law, every eigenvalue and eigenfunction, the full transition kernel, the sharp L2 rate, and the singular alpha-to-zero absorbing boundary with all limiting stationary laws?

The package answers yes for every integer N>=0 and every real alpha>0, with the zero-parameter face split explicitly. It does not infer the theorem from the finite grid.
