# Source and citation audit

## Verified primary sources

- H. E. Lomelí and J. D. Meiss, “Symmetry Reduction and Rotation Numbers for
  Poncelet maps,” `arXiv:2309.08013v1`,
  [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2309.08013](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2309.08013).
  The official arXiv record and 42-page PDF were checked on 2026-09-01.  The
  source gives the covering, formula, inverse-porism parameterization,
  monotonicity derivatives, and endpoints used here.  The authors' official
  research page lists it as submitted to *Nonlinearity*; this package does not
  call it peer reviewed or published.
- Shau-Jin Chang and Richard Friedberg, “Elliptical billiards and Poncelet's
  theorem,” *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **29** (1988), 1537–1550,
  [doi:10.1063/1.527900](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.527900).  The publisher DOI
  metadata was checked on 2026-09-01; it is primary support for the common
  closure/period statement.
- Rafał Kołodziej, “The rotation number of some transformation related to
  billiards in an ellipse,” *Studia Mathematica* **81** (1985), 293–302,
  [doi:10.4064/sm-81-3-293-302](https://doi.org/10.4064/sm-81-3-293-302).
  The journal's official record was checked on 2026-09-01; it is a primary
  rotation-number antecedent.

The local contribution is an exact theorem/evidence/firewall package, not a
literature-priority claim.  No quotation or unverified bibliographic item is
used.

## Collision and scope audit

HCS-C247 treats the circular billiard and its diameter/grazing faces.  It does
not own the two-eccentricity Jacobi formula or Poncelet inverse-porism atlas.
This package stops at elliptic caustics: hyperbolic-caustic non-Birkhoff
trajectories are not silently imported.  No prime table, Euler factor, root
number, automorphy claim, target divisor, functional equation, or
Hilbert–Pólya operator appears.
