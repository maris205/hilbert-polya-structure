# Research question

For the Euclidean billiard in the confocal ellipse `E(f)`, can every
positive-orientation orbit tangent to an inner elliptic caustic `E(e)`,
`0<f<e<1`, be put into one explicit action-angle coordinate that closes:

1. the exact rotation number and its modulus convention;
2. strict dependence on both eccentricities and both boundary values;
3. the least period of every rational caustic; and
4. the clean-periodic-family obstruction to an isolated-orbit determinant?

The scope is deliberately the elliptic-caustic sector.  Hyperbolic caustics,
non-Birkhoff trajectories, and ambient monodromy type require different
claims.  Proof status: **PROVABLE AS STATED** with this sector restriction.
