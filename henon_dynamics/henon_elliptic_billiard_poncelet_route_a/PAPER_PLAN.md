# Paper plan

**Title:** *Elliptic Billiards as Exact Poncelet Rotations: Monotonicity,
Porisms, and the Clean-Family Obstruction*

The paper is organized around one theorem-level step:

1. Freeze the confocal eccentricity convention and elliptic-caustic sector.
2. Construct the Jacobi covering and exact rigid-rotation lift.
3. Derive the strict two-parameter monotonicity and endpoint atlas.
4. Prove the reduced-rational minimal-period Poncelet porism.
5. Identify the restricted unit derivative and continuous-family A2
   obstruction without claiming ambient unipotence.
6. Report the executable receipt, ambient Dirichlet formal hint, failed
   same-clock/phase-weight gates, Route-A decision, and sector boundary.

Revision 0 contains items 1–2; Revision 1 adds items 3–4; Revision 2 adds
items 5–6 and the complete integrity firewall.
