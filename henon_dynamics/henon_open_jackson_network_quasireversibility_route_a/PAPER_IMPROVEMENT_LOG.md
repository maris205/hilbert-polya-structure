# Paper improvement log

## Round 0: product-form closure

The first manuscript froze the state process, allowed self-routing only as a
phantom service mark, proved the traffic inverse, and wrote the complete
state-by-state global-balance calculation. It did not infer the theorem from a
finite truncation.

## Round 1: reversal closure

The second manuscript added all three reversed parameter families. It checked
the augmented reversed routing row sums, showed that the original traffic
vector also solves the reversed traffic equations, and verified each visible
jump-pair rate. The notation explicitly distinguishes forward \(i\to j\) from
reverse \(j\to i\). The author-swapped audit further made explicit that the
reverse construction uses the natural extension admitting zero exogenous
rates and that the rate identification is for visible marked jumps; phantom
self-routing completions are state-preserving marks.

## Round 2: necessity and process-level closure

The final manuscript added the truncated-coordinate drift proof that critical
and overloaded networks are not positive recurrent. It then derived the
external departure theorem from future-input independence in reversed time,
stating the independence direction as “forward departures before \(t\) versus
the state at \(t\).” The final audit also inserted the standard irreducible,
conservative, countable-state CTMC lemma that turns an invariant probability
into positive recurrence, uniqueness, and full support before using the zero
state on the critical face. It also added all named boundaries, the exact
evidence ledger, source lineage, collision exclusions, declarations, and the
complete Route-A firewall.

No round claims that finite rational evidence proves the infinite-state or
point-process theorem.
