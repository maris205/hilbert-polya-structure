# Paper plan

## Title

Product Form, Time Reversal, and External Departure Flows in an Open Jackson
Network

## Mathematical arc

1. Freeze the row-vector traffic convention and the queue-length generator,
   including phantom self-routing completions.
2. Prove nonexplosion, irreducibility, and positivity of the traffic solution.
3. Establish the product law by a full incoming/outgoing global-balance
   calculation.
4. Obtain necessity of every strict load inequality from stationary truncated
   coordinate drifts, after invoking the irreducible conservative CTMC
   invariant-probability lemma.
5. Compute every reversed arrival, routing, and exit parameter with indices
   exposed, then verify reversed row sums and traffic equations.
6. Convert reversed future exogenous arrivals into forward past external
   departures to prove the Burke/quasi-reversibility clause, with zero reverse
   input rates and phantom self-routing marks handled explicitly.
7. Close singleton, independent, tandem, no-direct-exit, critical, and overload
   faces, then state the finite-evidence and Route-A boundaries.

## Substantive rounds

- Round 0: model, traffic, global balance, and product-form sufficiency.
- Round 1: exact time reversal, including row sums and reverse traffic.
- Round 2: stability necessity, external departure theorem, boundary atlas,
  evidence separation, sources, collision boundary, and Route-A firewall.

The final main.pdf is byte-identical to round 2.
