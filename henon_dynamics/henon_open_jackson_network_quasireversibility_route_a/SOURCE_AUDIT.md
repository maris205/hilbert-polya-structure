# Source audit

Access date for the web records below: 2026-09-03.

## Verified source lineage

1. **James R. Jackson (1957), “Networks of Waiting Lines,” Operations
   Research 5(4), 518--521.** INFORMS gives the title, author, volume, issue,
   pages, publication date, and DOI
   [10.1287/opre.5.4.518](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.5.4.518).
   This is the primary product-form source for the open network family.
2. **Paul J. Burke (1956), “The Output of a Queuing System,” Operations
   Research 4(6), 699--704.** The official INFORMS record is
   [10.1287/opre.4.6.699](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.4.6.699).
   Its abstract explicitly records the equilibrium output-process result and
   end-state independence for the classical queue.
3. **F. P. Kelly (1975), “Networks of Queues with Customers of Different
   Types,” Journal of Applied Probability 12(3), 542--554.** The Cambridge
   record verifies the open-network equilibrium and time-reversal lineage:
   [10.2307/3212869](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/abs/networks-of-queues-with-customers-of-different-types/9C346141E320D083C7F5F2D1D8DD30ED).
4. **F. P. Kelly (1979), Reversibility and Stochastic Networks.** The author's
   Cambridge Statistical Laboratory page hosts the 1979 text with permission:
   [book record and files](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~fpk1/BOOKS/kelly_book.html).
   Kelly's later author page on
   [networks of quasi-reversible nodes](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~fpk1/PAPERS/nqrn.html)
   says that the property was first identified by Muntz. Accordingly this
   package uses “quasi-reversibility” only as established lineage and does not
   assign invention or priority to the present reconstruction.

## Claim ownership

Jackson and the subsequent queueing literature own the product-form family;
Burke owns the classical output theorem; Kelly's work supplies the
time-reversal and quasi-reversible-network lineage. This package contributes a
source-local, convention-locked proof and independently executable exact
receipts. It makes no global novelty or priority claim.

## Evidence and citation boundary

The cited sources are not claimed to contain this package's JSON schema,
rational panel, mutation suite, PDF revisions, or release manifest. Those are
local implementation artifacts. Conversely, local computation is not used as
evidence of historical ownership or as a proof of the infinite-state theorem.

## Scope firewall

“Product form,” “traffic inverse,” and “normalizer” refer only to a stochastic
queueing law. They are not target Euler factors, arithmetic local data, root
numbers, automorphic objects, a target divisor, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
