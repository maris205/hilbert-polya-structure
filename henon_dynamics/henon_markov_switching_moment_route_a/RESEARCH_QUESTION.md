# Research question — C117

For a Markov-switched pair of polynomial Hénon maps with a common fixed point,
can the first and symmetric second tangent moments be assigned exact,
source-owned finite operators whose space, action, transition convention,
traces, and determinant polynomials are all explicit?

The question is deliberately local and finite.  It does not ask for a global
random dynamical transfer operator, a complete nonlinear orbit ledger, or any
arithmetic interpretation.
