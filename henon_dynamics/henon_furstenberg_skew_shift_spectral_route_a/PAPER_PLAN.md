# C169 paper plan

## Claim spine

1. State the all-parameter iterate and empty-periodic-orbit theorem.
2. Derive the exact Haar--Koopman Fourier action.
3. Resolve pure-point and Lebesgue sectors with multiplicity.
4. Establish reversibility and antiunitary symmetry.
5. Prove noncompactness, non-Schatten membership, and absence of an ordinary Fredholm determinant.
6. Give the conservative A0--A4 decision.

## Figure/table decision

No figure is needed: the invariant Fourier-sector decomposition is stated more precisely by equations, and a diagram would add no independent information. One compact route-evaluation table is used because it compares five exact gates.
