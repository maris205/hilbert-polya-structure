# C169 research question

For the full irrational Furstenberg skew-shift family, what can be proved exactly about iterates, periodic points, Artin--Mazur zeta, Haar--Koopman spectral type, reversibility, compactness, Schatten membership, and ordinary Fredholm determinant ownership?

The decision question is deliberately hostile: can a dynamically natural same-clock unitary compensate for an empty periodic-orbit ledger in Route A? The answer proved here is no. The spectral and symmetry structure is complete, but A1 fails because every positive iterate has no fixed point, so \(\zeta_{AM}=1\) has no primitive-orbit carrier.

Success for this note means an all-irrational-parameter theorem and a sharp obstruction, not a target match.
