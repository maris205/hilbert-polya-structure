# C169 narrative report

The paper makes decisive progress on a new dynamical subtype: an irrational parabolic skew product rather than another finite cellular or Walsh model. The progress is complete but negative for the orbit route. All positive iterates are solved, all fixed sets are empty, and the Artin--Mazur zeta is exactly one. In parallel, the natural Haar--Koopman operator is resolved completely into a pure-point horizontal sector and countably infinite Lebesgue multiplicity off that sector. An explicit involutive reversor supplies the correct antiunitary symmetry.

This contrast is the result. A natural unitary and exact time reversal do not imply a usable periodic-orbit determinant. The same operator is noncompact, lies in no finite Schatten class, and has no ordinary Fredholm determinant away from \(z=0\). Accordingly A4 is positive while A0--A3 fail, with A1 failing rather than being softened to weak.

The paper cites no literature and claims no novelty priority. All formulas are source-derived. Finite computation is only a regression sentinel, while the all-parameter result rests on the written proof.
