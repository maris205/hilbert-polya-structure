# Narrative report

The package advances from equilibrium criteria to a complete finite-time and spectral fluctuation statement. Its central move is to keep two related observables distinct: total entropy is exactly the logarithmic stationary path-density ratio, whereas medium entropy exposes the tilted-matrix transpose. The stationary endpoint term connects them only at SCGF scale.

This separation prevents three common overclaims: applying the detailed fluctuation theorem to the wrong observable, asserting a rate-function symmetry without an LDP/Legendre hypothesis, and interpreting a finite Markov characteristic polynomial as an arithmetic determinant. The two-state and one-way-edge boundaries show why each hypothesis matters.
