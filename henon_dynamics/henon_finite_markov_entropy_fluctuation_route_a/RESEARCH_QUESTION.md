# Research question

For an arbitrary finite irreducible continuous-time Markov chain whose support is bidirected, can stationary normalization, dissipation, exact finite-time reversal, and asymptotic fluctuation symmetry be proved in one convention-complete theorem?

The answer is yes. The decisive distinction is between total entropy, which includes the stationary endpoint term and has an exact finite-time detailed fluctuation theorem, and medium entropy, whose tilted generator has the transparent transpose symmetry. Their bounded difference yields the same long-time SCGF.
