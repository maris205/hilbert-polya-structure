# Assumptions
Circle measure is normalized to one. Pi keeps nonnegative Fourier modes. Physical time is unchanged. The source is i u_t=Pi(|u|^2u)+alpha(u|1), alpha real.

The main manifold is u=b+cz/(1-pz), c nonzero, |p|<1. Constants form a separate face. Q=|b|^2+|c|^2/d, M=|c|^2/d^2, d=1-|p|^2. Positive square roots use their positive branch. Complex inner products are linear in the first variable.

Sobolev weights are (1+n)^(2s), giving exact critical squared norm Q+M. No experimental cutoff appears in a theorem hypothesis.
