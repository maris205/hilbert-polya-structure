# Narrative
An isospectral nonlinear flow can transfer momentum to arbitrarily high frequencies while keeping its native determinant unchanged. Rank-one alpha-Szego dynamics makes this contrast exact.

One invariant manifold carries all complex phases and infinitely many Fourier modes. Mass, momentum and energy reduce radial motion to a sharp defect dichotomy. A second-order equation preserves uniqueness at the turn and yields the complete two-sided cascade law.

At fixed alpha and M, a pure Fourier mode is bounded while adding the square-root-alpha constant creates a cascade. Both have determinant 1-w. This is one coherent theorem story, not an expanded coefficient catalog. Classical ownership and target boundaries remain explicit.
