## Design objective

This round implements the user's large-step rule literally.  It does not turn
one calculation into five installments: the five frozen owners are a
Lie-projective oscillator flow, a noninvertible partition map, a positive-cone
normalization algorithm, a hyperplane face-semigroup Markov chain, and an
integer Diophantine descent.  Each paper closes an all-parameter theorem and
its sharp boundary before Route A is graded.

Finite censuses are used only as convention and regression oracles.  They do
not replace the arbitrary-forcing, all-`N`, all-matrix, all-arrangement, or
all-positive-solution statements.  `NEW` in the collision scan means only
that C1--C188 does not already own the same result; it is not a claim of
priority in the mathematical literature.

## Collision scan and hard pivots

- A generic heterogeneous Kuramoto proposal was killed because the exact
  Watanabe--Strogatz reduction requires common first-harmonic forcing.  C189
  survives by freezing that maximal exact class and closing collision strata
  and the constant-generator trichotomy instead of hand-waving beyond it.
- Another finite reversible permutation was rejected as too close to the
  tableau, sandpile, rotor and action--angle lines.  C190 survives because the
  full Bulgarian-solitaire map is noninvertible and the all-`N` recurrent
  theorem also determines the transient zero algebraic multiplicity of its
  Koopman pullback.
- A single positive-matrix convergence example was rejected.  C191 survives
  only as a boundary-complete all-matrix classification: support, total
  support, full indecomposability, positive Hilbert contraction, and the exact
  quotient local rate must remain one theorem.
- A generic finite Markov-spectrum paper was rejected as a repeat.  C192
  survives because the Brown--Diaconis face semigroup gives one theorem for
  every real hyperplane arrangement, including the nonseparating stationary
  simplex and the precise stopping-sampler ceiling.
- A modular Markoff graph was killed by the arithmetic-local-data firewall.
  C193 instead freezes the positive integer surface itself and must orient the
  entire Vieta graph, not merely enumerate low-height triples.  The Frobenius
  uniqueness conjecture remains outside the claim.

No credible candidate in this pool simultaneously supplied a rational-prime
primitive carrier, prime-power repetition and a logarithmic clock.  The round
therefore keeps exact mathematics and records strict stops instead of
manufacturing arithmetic semantics.

## Frozen independent theorem increments

### C189 -- Watanabe--Strogatz common-forcing Möbius dynamics

For every number of identical phase oscillators and every continuous common
first-harmonic forcing, lift the shared Riccati equation to one `SU(1,1)`
fundamental matrix and project it to a diagonal `PSU(1,1)` action.  Prove the
`N-3` generic cross-ratio quotient, every labelled collision stratum and its
orbit dimension, and the identity/elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic classification
of every constant generator.  The elliptic projected period is exact; a
rational strobe fixes positive-dimensional families and blocks a finite
isolated primitive-orbit census.

### C190 -- Bulgarian solitaire on every deck size

For every `N`, write `N=binom(k,2)+r` and transport Brandt's recurrent
classification to weight-`r` binary necklaces of length `k`.  Close all
positive-iterate fixed counts, Möbius least periods, primitive cycles, zeta,
the full Koopman characteristic polynomial including transient zero
multiplicity, recurrent reflection formulas, and the triangular boundary.
Complete transient trees, hitting laws, nilpotent Jordan sizes and a global
reversor remain explicitly outside the theorem.

### C191 -- Sinkhorn--Knopp dynamics on every support stratum

Classify alternating row--column normalization for every square nonnegative
matrix by support, total support and full indecomposability.  On the positive
stratum derive a data-dependent Hilbert-projective contraction and, by direct
log-coordinate differentiation at the doubly stochastic limit `S`, the exact
full-cycle Jacobian `S^T S` and quotient local rate `sigma_2(S)^2`.  Separate
limit uniqueness from factor gauges and local asymptotics from a false global
dimension-only rate.

### C192 -- Brown--Diaconis hyperplane chamber walks

For every finite real hyperplane arrangement and every face probability
measure, retain the complete flat-indexed spectrum, Möbius multiplicities,
characteristic polynomial, determinant and all power traces.  Close the
separating uniqueness criterion, weighted exact stationary sampler, coupling
bounds, and the full nonseparating stationary simplex.  A chamber-hitting
stopping construction is not promoted to a strict strong stationary time
without the missing independence statement.

### C193 -- the complete positive Markoff--Vieta descent tree

For every normalized positive integer solution of
`x^2+y^2+z^2=3xyz`, prove unique maximum, positivity and strict decrease of
the other Vieta root, strict ascent of both nonparent mutations, global
termination at `(1,1,1)`, reverse generation, and the rooted-tree theorem for
the full permutation quotient.  The integer surface gives a weak arithmetic
relation, but strict descent has no nonroot cycles and the tree theorem neither
uses nor proves Frobenius uniqueness for Markoff numbers.
