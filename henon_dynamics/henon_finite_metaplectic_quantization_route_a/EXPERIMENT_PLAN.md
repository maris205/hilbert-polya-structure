# Experiment plan

1. Freeze `N=7` and every Weyl/Fourier/chirp phase convention.
2. Verify unitarity in exact `Q(zeta_7,sqrt(7))` arithmetic.
3. Check all 49 Egorov identities and the antiunitary identities exactly.
4. Reconstruct `U^8`, traces, characteristic polynomial, and determinant.
5. Test the same half-phase convention at even modulus and state its exact
   scope without excluding other even-level conventions.
6. Compute real-torus fixed and primitive counts independently.
7. Expose rather than hide the finite-level aliasing mismatch.
8. Run independent numerical, SymPy, replay, mutation, PDF, font, and
   manifest checks.

No target spectrum is loaded or fit.
