# Hostile audit

- Changing the Fourier sign changes the implemented classical matrix.
- Removing the Weyl half-phase creates projective phase errors.
- The literal half-phase convention cannot be copied to `N=8`, because
  `2*h=1 mod 8` has no solution.  This does not reject separately defined
  doubled-phase or other even-level Weil conventions.
- Absorbing a scalar anomaly into `U` is forbidden; the frozen `U=C F^-1`
  passes exactly without such repair.
- Mod-7 stationary points are not identified one-for-one with real-torus
  periodic points.
- The periodic finite-level trace is an obstruction, not a target match.
- `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` does not imply `A4_ROUTE_B_READY`.
- No Hilbert--Pólya, automorphy, Euler-factor, or root-number claim is made.
- Headline phase, action, spectrum, determinant, exact-count, and convention
  fields are all covered by repaired-hash semantic mutations.
