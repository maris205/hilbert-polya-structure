# Narrative report

C128 moves the quantum coordinate from a formal hint to an actual operator.
The Hilbert space is `C^7`; the unitary is a fully specified chirp followed by
an inverse Fourier transform.  Exact Weyl covariance proves Egorov without a
semiclassical remainder, powers of the unitary retain the same discrete clock,
and `Theta=F K` implements time reversal.

The frozen half-phase convention also has a sharp boundary: it requires two
to be invertible modulo the level.  The control `N=8` proves that the same
formula is undefined at even level, without making a no-go claim for other
even-level Weil conventions.

The construction also prevents overinterpretation.  Its spectrum is a
seven-element subset of the eighth roots of unity, so its traces are periodic.
This exact aliasing cannot carry the exponentially growing classical
fixed-point count at every time.  Passing the natural-quantization gate is
therefore real progress but not evidence for target spectral matching.
