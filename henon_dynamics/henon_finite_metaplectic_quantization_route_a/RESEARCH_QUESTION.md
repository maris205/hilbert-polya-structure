# Research question

Does a concrete Hénon-type symplectic map admit a named Hilbert space,
explicit unitary, exact Egorov covariance, clock preservation, and an
antiunitary reversor—and what obstruction remains after those properties are
proved?

C128 answers this at the frozen finite level `N=7`, then compares only the
source-owned classical and quantum clocks.  Target spectral comparison is out
of scope.
