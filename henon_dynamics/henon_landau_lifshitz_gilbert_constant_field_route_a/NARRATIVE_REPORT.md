# Narrative report

The field-aligned LLG equation is a rare continuous model whose nonlinear
geometry collapses exactly in one chart.  Damping contracts the stereographic
radius and moves every interior point from the south to the north pole; pure
precession preserves latitude and creates a continuum of circles.  The same
formula therefore exposes, rather than hides, the sampled-time obstruction:
resonant pure precession and zero field have a two-dimensional fixed set,
whereas positive damping has only two fixed poles.

The result is a complete source theorem and an honest Route-A stop.  It does
not infer arithmetic labels from a rotation frequency or from a formal
complex coordinate.
