# Research question

Can the constant-field Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert flow be closed exactly on its
full parameter and pole boundary atlas without confusing its continuous
latitude families with isolated primitive cycles?

The answer is yes at the ODE level: a stereographic coordinate obeys a scalar
linear equation, the third component is a logistic/tanh trajectory, and the
sampled-time fixed set changes sharply between damping, pure precession and
identity faces.  The answer is no for a target arithmetic or Hilbert–Pólya
promotion, which is therefore explicitly rejected.
