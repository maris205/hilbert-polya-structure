# C234 paper artifacts

`main.tex` is compiled at revision rounds 0, 1 and 2 with a fixed
`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1787875200`.  `main.pdf` is byte-identical to
`main_round2.pdf`; the earlier PDFs are retained to demonstrate two
substantive revisions.  The round-0 and round-1 PDFs are archival,
superseded drafts: their pre-correction reference line contains the old issue
label and no DOI value.  They are not the release citation surface; the
round-2/`main.pdf` artifact is authoritative and carries the corrected
Lakshmanan metadata (369(1939), DOI `10.1098/rsta.2010.0319`).  The paper is a
source-local ODE theorem and records the Route-A rejection explicitly.
