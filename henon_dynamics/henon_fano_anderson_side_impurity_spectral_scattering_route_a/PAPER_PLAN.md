# Paper plan

## Title

**A Branch-Safe Spectral and Scattering Atlas for a Side-Coupled
Fano--Anderson Impurity**

## Structure and claim ownership

1. **Frozen Hamiltonian and Schur function.** Define the operator, branch, and
   exact impurity resolvent.
2. **Physical bound states.** Prove endpoint limits, strict monotonicity,
   simplicity, and the quartic filter.
3. **Full spectral type.** Use parity and a cyclic eventually-free Jacobi
   representation; fix the anti-Herglotz convention; use local-uniform Stone
   inversion, exterior meromorphy, and edge atom tests to derive the complete
   density/atom decomposition and normalization.
4. **Scattering atlas.** Derive amplitudes, `T+R=1`, and the if-and-only-if
   continuum Fano zero.
5. **Degenerate faces.** Treat `g=0`, `J=0`, band edges, coupling sign, and the
   nonuniform zero-coupling limit.
6. **Evidence and scope.** Distinguish exact receipts from proof, record sources
   and collisions, and reject Route A/lock Route B.

## Revision rounds

- Round 0: Sections 1--2, the branch-safe two-pole owner.
- Round 1: add Sections 3--4 and complete density/scattering proofs.
- Round 2: add Sections 5--6, bibliography, AI-use statement, and all scope
  firewalls.

No figure is required: the square-root branch and scattering formula are more
precise in equations, and the package forbids numerical plots from serving as
spectral evidence.
