# Hostile audit

The suite performs repaired-payload-hash mutations against identifiers,
baseline/epoch/scope, evaluator authority/version/hash, nested raw and semantic
YAML hashes, branch normalization, Schur sign, spectral type, physical-root
count, quartic filtering, density/residue formulas, all six fields of the
Stone/edge spectral-measure proof lock, scattering conservation, Fano
location, boundary faces, collisions, nonclaims, Route-A tuple, Route-B lock,
and forbidden flags.

It separately attacks duplicate and nonfinite JSON, non-object roots, stale
payload hashes, extra/missing keys, YAML duplicate keys, anchors, aliases,
merge keys, non-string keys, type changes, unknown keys, byte-only changes,
authority deletion/rewriting, evidence-status deletion/rewriting, and every
individual evaluator leaf with repaired carrier hashes.

Success means every hostile file is rejected by the independent checker.  It
does not certify the mathematics; the analytic proof and independent symbolic
lane retain that responsibility.
