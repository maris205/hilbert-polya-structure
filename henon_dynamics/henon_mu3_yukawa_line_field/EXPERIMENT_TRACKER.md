## 5. Live hazards

1. The chart algebra may have the right vector-space dimension but fail one
   of the four direct substitution remainders.
2. A main-chart solution is not automatically the global line scheme. The
   proof must use finite étaleness to make the chart open-and-closed, or an
   equally explicit properness argument.
3. Partial modular degree patterns do not prove irreducibility; the complete
   factors, multiplicities, derivative gcds, and subset sums are required.
4. Every \(W(E_6)\) permutation on the 27 lines is even in \(S_{27}\).
   Ordinary permutation sign therefore cannot exclude \(U\).
5. For this instance the degree-27 field \(E\) is non-Galois and distinct
   from the splitting/normal field \(K\).
6. Hochschild--Serre gives rank equality here, not an asserted integral
   equality of Picard groups.
7. No result about rational points, rationality, the Hasse principle,
   Brauer--Manin, motives, VHS, or Calabi--Yau realization is in scope.
