\section{The fixed surface and its Fano scheme}
\label{sec:surface-fano}

\subsection{The cubic}

In homogeneous coordinates \(u_0,u_1,u_2,u_3\), let
\begin{align}
F={}&75081586157u_0^3-28576620789u_0^2u_1
-122000922135u_0^2u_2-5364921951u_0^2u_3\nonumber\\
&+164150208636u_0u_1^2-415458334296u_0u_1u_2
+151070718312u_0u_1u_3\nonumber\\
&+1158143874300u_0u_2^2+114691988016u_0u_2u_3
+113572676646u_0u_3^2\nonumber\\
&+6898957820u_1^3+1132596902196u_1^2u_2
-30413540316u_1^2u_3\nonumber\\
&-2054867641020u_1u_2^2+151980984216u_1u_2u_3
+36794420832u_1u_3^2\nonumber\\
&+2646295985484u_2^3+560186573940u_2^2u_3
+706181383584u_2u_3^2+1884468968u_3^3,
\label{eq:cubic}
\end{align}
and set \(Y=V(F)\subset\PP^3_{\Q}\).  The coefficient content is one and the
first coefficient is positive, which fixes the common-scalar convention.
The current exact replay reconstructs all twenty sparse coefficient rows and
verifies projective smoothness over \(\Q\) by unit ideals on the four
standard affine charts.  It also verifies good reduction at
\(7,19,29,37\).

The equation is the only H\'enon/Yukawa input used below.  A rational
coordinate transformation in \(\operatorname{GL}_4(\Q)\), or multiplication
of \(F\) by an element of \(\Q^\times\), changes its presentation but not any
line-scheme or Picard conclusion; see \cref{cor:projective-invariance}.

\subsection{The line section}

Let \(\cS\) be the tautological rank-two subbundle on \(\Gr(2,4)\).
Restriction of \(F\) to a two-plane defines a section
\begin{equation}
  \sF\in H^0\!\left(\Gr(2,4),
             \operatorname{Sym}^3(\cS^\vee)\right),
  \qquad [S]\longmapsto F|_S .
\label{eq:line-section}
\end{equation}
Its zero scheme is the Fano scheme \(\FanoY\).  This is exactly the
construction in Kass--Wickelgren, Definition~41
\cite{KassWickelgren2021}.

Two distinct external inputs control its geometric size and reducedness.
The rank of the arithmetic count in Kass--Wickelgren, Theorem~2, is
\(15+12=27\), recovering the total geometric degree \(27\) for a smooth
cubic surface.  Corollary~53, on the other hand, says that the zero of
\(\sF\) corresponding to every line disjoint from the singular locus is
simple.  Since \(Y\) is smooth, every line satisfies that condition.
Corollary~54 separately records separability of the residue fields.  We do
not attribute the total count \(27\) to Corollary~53.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:fano-etale}
The scheme \(\FanoY\) is finite \(\etale\) over \(\Q\) of rank \(27\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The zero scheme of \(\sF\) is closed in the projective Grassmannian.  Over
\(\Qbar\), the total-degree statement gives \(27\) zeros and
Kass--Wickelgren Corollary~53 makes every zero simple.  Thus
\(\FanoY_{\Qbar}\) is a disjoint union of \(27\) reduced points.
Consequently \(\FanoY\) is zero-dimensional and proper, hence finite.
Geometric reducedness over the characteristic-zero field \(\Q\) makes the
finite scheme \(\etale\), with rank \(27\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
\label{rem:global-first}
\Cref{prop:fano-etale} is proved before any affine chart is used.  This order
is needed in \cref{sec:chart-scheme}: it makes every chart open in
\(\FanoY\) also closed, preventing a hidden assumption that one chart already
contains all lines.
\end{remark}
