\begin{abstract}
We determine the arithmetic of the \(27\)-line scheme of a specific smooth
cubic surface over \(\Q\), namely the surface cut out by the four-variable
Yukawa cubic associated with the fourth H\'enon core.  An exact Grassmannian
calculation produces a degree-\(27\) eliminant and a quotient with standard
monomial counts \((1,4,10,12,0)\).  Direct back-substitution, followed by a
scheme-theoretic open-and-closed argument, identifies the complete Fano
scheme with \(\Spec(E)\) for a degree-\(27\) number field \(E\).  Four
complete squarefree factorizations modulo \(7,19,29,37\) have degree patterns
\[
(3,3,3,3,3,6,6),\quad(1,4,4,6,12),\quad
(1,2,8,8,8),\quad(2,5,5,5,10).
\]
Their factor-degree subset sums intersect only in \(\{0,27\}\), proving that
the eliminant is irreducible.  The last Frobenius type, combined with an
exact enumeration of the \(51840\)-element Weyl group, excludes its
index-two Coxeter-even subgroup and gives
\(\Gal(K/\Q)\cong W(E_6)\) for the normal closure \(K\) of \(E\).
Consequently the geometric and arithmetic Picard ranks are \(7\) and \(1\).
No line is defined over \(\Q\); more generally, every finite extension
\(L/\Q\) over which a line is defined has degree divisible by \(27\).
The result concerns lines and Picard
ranks only: it makes no assertion about rational points, rationality,
Brauer--Manin obstructions, motives, or Calabi--Yau realizations.
\end{abstract}
