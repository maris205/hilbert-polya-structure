\section{Thermodynamic and operator context}
\label{sec:context}

\paragraph{Pressure and suspended zeta functions.}
For a mixing subshift of finite type and a positive H\"older roof \(\tau\),
thermodynamic formalism gives a unique real solution of
\(P(-s\tau)=0\).  It is the topological entropy of the suspension.  The same
transfer family controls the leading pole of the suspension Ruelle zeta
function; non-lattice periodic data exclude a vertical arithmetic progression
of competing boundary poles \citep{Bowen1975,ParryPollicott1990,Ruelle1976}.
These facts make pressure the mandatory control for any positive real signal
in an instability-weighted Euler product.

\paragraph{Bowen dimension.}
For a locally maximal hyperbolic set with one-dimensional unstable bundle,
the derivative is conformal on that bundle.  The unstable-slice Hausdorff
dimension is the unique zero of the pressure of the negative unstable
geometric potential.  This is the surface horseshoe formula of
McCluskey--Manning and its local conformal-basic-set formulation
\citep{McCluskeyManning1983,Manning1985Erratum,PesinSadovskaya2001,Pesin1997,Barreira2013}.
We verify local
maximality and the norm-gauge interface rather than assuming those
hypotheses.

\paragraph{Analytic pinning operators.}
Rugh-type pinning coordinates and mixed Cauchy kernels give powerful
dynamical determinants for analytic surface maps.  Baladi, Pujals, and
Sambarino prove chronological composition, order-zero nuclearity, and trace
identities under their analytic hyperbolic hypotheses
\citep{BaladiPujalsSambarino2005}.  The existing H6 domains specialize this
framework.  Those qualitative all-word statements are therefore prior art,
not a contribution of the present paper.  Our new object is the explicit
finite error certificate for the geometric pressure root.

\paragraph{Relation to the original H\'enon proposal.}
The original model studies an area-preserving H\'enon lift near a numerically
selected transition and compares quantum and dissipative solvers with zero
data \citep{Wang2026HenonModel}.  Here \(a=6\) is a target-free,
proof-selected hyperbolic control.  We use no Riemann zeros or primes, and our
conclusion concerns one local survivor rather than the full plane.
