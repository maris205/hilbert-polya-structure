# Paper plan: a certified Bowen-pressure gate for the H6 survivor

## One-sentence contribution

For the exact four-state survivor of the area-preserving Hénon map, we enclose
the geometric pressure root by a finite rational cylinder/Collatz certificate
and show that the prominent positive finite-section signal is consistent with
that root to certified resolution, sharply weakening its status as an
unexplained arithmetic anomaly without asserting finite-section convergence.

## Paper structure

1. **Abstract.** State the certified interval, the pressure/dimension
   identification, and the negative Hilbert--Pólya interpretation.
2. **Introduction.** Explain why a stable finite-section zero is not yet a
   spectral discovery and preview the pressure gate.
3. **Source-locked Hénon survivor.** Define the map, the four-state graph,
   the exact recurrence, and the inherited hyperbolic certificate.
4. **Intrinsic roof and cone sharpening.** Prove the explicit coboundary and
   the self-consistent slope/expansion bounds.
5. **Cylinder pressure enclosure.** Define the length-13 higher-block graph,
   interval roof bounds, rational elementary-function enclosures, and Collatz
   certificate.
6. **Pressure, zeta, and dimension.** Identify the unique pressure root,
   the leading zeta singularity, and the unstable Hausdorff dimension.
7. **Certified results and controls.** Give counts, interval margins,
   independent-checker results, mutations, and the comparison with the old
   finite-section value.
8. **Route-A implications.** Separate a valid geometric theorem from an
   arithmetic or Hilbert--Pólya claim.
9. **Conclusion.** Close the pressure lane and state the next genuine large
   gate: a canonical arithmetic fibre on this proven hyperbolic base.
10. **Appendices.** Full interval arithmetic, source hashes, reproducibility,
    and the exact slope/angle algebra.

## Claim boundary

The paper does not claim a Riemann-zero match, a functional equation, a
Riemann--von Mangoldt law, a prime correspondence, or a self-adjoint operator.
Generic BPS/Rugh all-word composition and order-zero nuclearity remain prior
art; they are background, not the novelty claim.
