# C170 verification plan

This is a finite-theory package, not a statistical or GPU experiment.

1. Freeze the marker indexing, departed-site clock, labelled-state normalization, and determinant convention.
2. Prove the \(N\)-step law and least-period dichotomy for every \(N\) and marker word.
3. Derive fixed counts, zeta, Koopman determinant, and root multiplicities from the cycle decomposition.
4. Construct the gauge, unfolding, reversor, and antiunitary; audit \(N=1\).
5. Enumerate every marker word through \(N=10\), checking cycles, fixed times, \(R^2=I\), and \(RTR=T^{-1}\); produce class rows through \(N=24\).
6. Run an independent checker, SymPy determinant checks, byte replay, and repaired/stale-hash mutations.
7. Preserve three materially improved PDFs and require deterministic final builds, embedded fonts, and clean logs.
8. Close the self-excluded manifest over exactly 27 payload files.

Falsifiers include a marker word with the wrong cycle type, a fixed-count mismatch, a reversal failure, an incorrect polynomial multiplicity, a promoted arithmetic claim, or a true Route-B flag.
