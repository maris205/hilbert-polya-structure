# C170 research question

Can every marker configuration of the reversible finite Kac ring be classified at every ring size by a single intrinsic invariant, with exact least periods, primitive cycles, all-time fixed counts, Artin--Mazur zeta, Koopman determinant and spectrum, and an explicit involutive reversor?

The paper asks a second Route-A question: does a complete primitive-cycle classification in a natural kinetic toy model provide an arithmetic bridge? The theorem answers the first question yes through the sign product \(\eta\), and the second no: A1 is weak because the cycle structure is finite and exactly reducible, while A0, A2, and A3 fail.
