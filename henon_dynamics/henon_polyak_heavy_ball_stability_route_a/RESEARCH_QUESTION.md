# Research question

For every real pair `(alpha,beta)` and every real SPD quadratic with spectrum
in `[m,L]`, can one give one exact dynamical classification containing the
full Jury stability region, exact endpoint root radius, the unique minimax
constant parameters, all defective/Jordan transients, the degenerate `m=L`
case, finite characteristic data, conformal symplecticity, and every
finite-order boundary?

The question is explicitly about quadratics and the two-step phase state.  It
does not authorize extrapolation to general nonlinear strongly convex
objectives or target arithmetic data.
