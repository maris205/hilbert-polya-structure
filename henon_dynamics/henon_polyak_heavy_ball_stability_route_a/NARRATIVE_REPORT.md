# Narrative report

Orthogonal diagonalization converts the full quadratic iteration into scalar
companion blocks.  Jury's three inequalities then produce an exact open
triangle for all real momentum, including a stable negative branch.  A disk
version of the same criterion yields the endpoint reduction and forces the
unique Polyak parameters through a one-variable inequality.

The hostile audit exposes a common but material overstatement.  At the
minimax endpoints the characteristic factors are squared, so generic orbits
carry a polynomial `k` transient.  The asymptotic root factor is `q`, but a
uniform `C q^k` bound is false.  The paper includes the explicit
`m=1,L=4` counterexample.

The map is conformally symplectic and becomes symplectic at `beta=1`, where
fixed matrices may have finite-order rotations.  This is a useful source
boundary, not arithmetic evidence.  The complete theorem remains a strict
Route-A rejection.
