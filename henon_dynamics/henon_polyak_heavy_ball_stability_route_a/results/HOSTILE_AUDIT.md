# C201 hostile audit

The checker rejects repaired-hash changes to the source lock, evaluator,
headline, Route tuple, forbidden claims, citation, negative-momentum stability,
Jury values, flip regime, minimax `q` and `alpha`, Jordan flag and sequence,
rotated determinant, finite order and both top-level and nested schema keys.
A separate unrepaired attack is rejected by the payload hash.

The theorem-level hostile example is `m=1,L=4`, `alpha=4/9`, `beta=1/9`,
standard endpoint initialization, for which

`e_k=(1+2k/3)3^(-k)`.

Thus the root factor is `q=1/3`, but no fixed constant gives a uniform
`C q^k` bound.  The paper also blocks extrapolation to general nonlinear
strongly convex functions.
