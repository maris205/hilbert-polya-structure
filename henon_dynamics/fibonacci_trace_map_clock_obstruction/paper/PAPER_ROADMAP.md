# Paper roadmap

## One-sentence contribution

Fibonacci discriminants carry physical-clock complexity that cannot be
compressed into ordinary renormalization-clock determinant coefficients: their
energy degree is \(F_{k+2}\), and at two exact escaping section energies their
value series has radius zero.
