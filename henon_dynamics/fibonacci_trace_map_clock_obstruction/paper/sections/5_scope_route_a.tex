\section{Operator scope and Route-A disposition}

The experiments and theorems above concern distinct candidates and parameter
regimes.  The incidence and zero-radius witnesses use \(\lambda=1\).  The
source-faithful Casdagli coding uses \(\lambda\ge16\), and we freeze
\(\lambda=16\) for the unweighted Route-A record.  The degree theorem is
coupling-independent.  Evidence is not transferred between these regimes
without an explicit theorem.

\subsection{Three objects that must remain separate}

\paragraph{HCS-C13-AM: unweighted Artin--Mazur zeta.}
For the frozen hyperbolic coding,
\begin{equation}
Z_{\AM}(z)=
\exp\left(\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{z^n}{n}\Tr A_{10}^n\right)
=\det(I-zA_{10})^{-1}
\label{eq:frozen-am}
\end{equation}
is an intrinsic closed-return function, normalized by \(Z_{\AM}(0)=1\).
For the direct candidate only, we freeze the literal formal-series identity
\[
\log Z_{\AM}(z)=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{d_k(E)}{k}z^k,
\]
equivalently \(\Tr A_{10}^k=d_k(E)\) in \(\C[E]\) for every \(k\).
This is refuted: \(\Tr A_{10}^k\) is an integer independent of energy, whereas
\(d_k(E)\) is a monic polynomial of degree \(F_{k+2}\).
The boundary series \eqref{eq:boundary-series} is a different marked object.
No alternative map from \(z\) to energy or to a target Riemann divisor is
implicit in \eqref{eq:frozen-am}.

\paragraph{HCS-C13P and HCS-C13G: scoped repair obstructions.}
HCS-C13P permits arbitrary finite dimensions \(N_k\) but freezes a uniform
local polynomial energy-degree bound; the dimension-independent theorem
refutes literal trace, boundary, and order-\(k\) determinant-coefficient
repairs in that class.  HCS-C13G removes polynomiality at the two exact
finite-approximant section energies and refutes literal coefficient or
logarithmic-trace matching
by every fixed scalar germ analytic at \(z=0\).  These are proved obstruction
records, not additional evidence layers of HCS-C13-AM.

\paragraph{HCS-C13R: general energy-dependent Fredholm proposal.}
A schematic expression such as
\begin{equation}
\left\langle u,(I-z\mathcal L_{E,\theta})^{-1}v\right\rangle
\label{eq:schematic-fredholm}
\end{equation}
does not define a mathematical candidate.  A function space, operator domain,
energy and phase dependence, weights, clock, determinant convention,
normalization, and boundary functionals are required.  Zeros of a defined
Fredholm determinant could arise through global cancellations and need not
make the marked point \(\ell_\lambda(E)\) periodic.  This is compatible with
the standard Ruelle determinant framework \citep{ruelle1976}.  HCS-C13R is
therefore rejected at the Route-A input gate as \texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}; it is
not assigned A1--A4 scores and is not a universal Fredholm no-go.

\subsection{The natural Hamiltonian is not a discrete spectral lift}

The infinite Fibonacci Hamiltonian is self-adjoint, but that fact alone does
not provide a Hilbert--P{\'o}lya lift.  S{\"u}t{\H{o}} proved zero-measure
Cantor spectrum in the Fibonacci model \citep{suto1989}, and Damanik--Lenz
proved absence of eigenvalues for every element of the Fibonacci hull
\citep{damanik2000}.  Hence the natural infinite operator does not supply a
discrete eigenvalue sequence.  This excludes that operator itself, not every
new operator that one might derive from the trace dynamics.
