## Reusable obstruction

In substitution/renormalization spectral problems, distinguish:

```text
marked initial state -> finite-time section incidence
periodic point       -> closed three-coordinate return
renormalization k    -> exponentially longer physical word q_k
```

A uniformly bounded-degree local polynomial weight contributes only bounded
energy degree per renormalization step.  If the target polynomial has degree
comparable to the physical word length, a short-clock trace, boundary
coefficient, or order-\(k\) determinant coefficient cannot reproduce it
without level-dependent target injection.  This remains true for arbitrary
finite state dimension \(N_k\).  Independently, the exact escape witnesses
satisfy \(|d_k(E_*)|^{1/k}\to\infty\), so their coefficient and
logarithmic-trace series have radius zero.  No scalar germ analytic at
\(z=0\) can realize either literal matching; fixed bounded resolvents and
standard analytic Fredholm determinants are included.

## Claim boundary

The modular audit does not refute all Fredholm determinants.  C13P covers
arbitrary finite \(N_k\) but only uniformly bounded polynomial energy degree
under ordinary short-clock multiplication.  C13G is dimension-free but only
addresses literal coefficient or signed logarithmic-trace realization by a
scalar germ analytic at zero.  Its escape routes are physical-time indexing
by \(q_k\), \(k\)-dependent or nonanalytic/zero-radius constructions,
singularity at a witness, composition or moving evaluation, and indirect
energy-divisor maps.  Infinite dimension alone is not an escape from C13G if
the claimed scalar object remains analytic at zero.

Casdagli's C13B identity is not asserted at coupling one: its source regime is
\(V_{\rm C}\ge8\), equivalently \(\lambda\ge16\).  The incidence, gcd, and
zero-radius witnesses are at \(\lambda=1\).  Their energies \(E=0,-1\) are
finite-periodic-approximant section energies, not asserted spectral points of
the infinite Fibonacci Hamiltonian.
