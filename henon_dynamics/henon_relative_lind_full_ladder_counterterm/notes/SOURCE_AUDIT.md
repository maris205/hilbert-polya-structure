# Source audit

The full two-shift reverse Lind zeta remains the formula from Young-One Kim,
Jungseob Lee, and Kyewon K. Park, *A zeta function for flip systems*, Pacific
Journal of Mathematics 209 (2003), Example 4.3, DOI
10.2140/pjm.2003.209.289.  The full H\'enon horseshoe coding boundary remains
the Devaney--Nitecki conjugacy theorem.

P73 imports its exact relative formula and scalar coefficients only from the
SHA-256-locked P71 and P72 repository artifacts.  The complex partial
fractions, raw pole-summability obstruction, genus-`m-1` regularization, and
normalized full-ladder identity are new derivations.  No source is credited
with an operator or arithmetic interpretation, and none is claimed.
