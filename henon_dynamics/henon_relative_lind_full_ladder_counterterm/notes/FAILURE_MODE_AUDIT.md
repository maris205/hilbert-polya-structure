# Failure-mode audit

- **Positive-only cancellation:** rejected.  Every level has `2m` complex
  roots, and the source remainder also has a negative first-channel
  power-exponential singularity at `1+sqrt(2)t=0`.
- **Raw pole product:** rejected.  The level-`m` absolute mass at the origin
  is `sqrt(2)|c_m|`, bounded below on infinitely many prime levels.
- **Changing the channel sum:** excluded.  All subtracted Taylor degrees
  vanish by exact root-of-unity orthogonality within each level.
- **Conditional-to-unconditional promotion:** excluded.  The genus grows as
  `m-1`, giving a geometric compact majorant and arbitrary pole-order
  independence.
- **Branch confusion:** the identity is stated only on components with
  compatible logarithm and square-root branches; the exponential pole
  factors themselves are single valued off their poles.
- **Operator promotion:** forbidden.  Copying the complete logarithmic ledger
  into a counterterm is not transfer-operator ownership.
- **Arithmetic promotion:** channel indices remain scalar repetition indices,
  not certified rational primes or prime powers.
