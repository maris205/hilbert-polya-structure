# Research question

Can the complete positive and complex singularity ladder left by P72 be
removed by one canonical pole-factor counterterm whose value does not depend
on the order of the scalar channels or their individual complex poles?

The decisive tests are predeclared.  The construction must expose every
complex root, prove an exact partial-fraction formula, diagnose the raw
pole-factor divergence, find a levelwise Weierstrass regularization that is
absolutely normally convergent, and cancel both the P72 tail and the remaining
negative first-channel source factor.  A determinant identity obtained by
copying the channel ledger is not counted as transfer-operator ownership.
