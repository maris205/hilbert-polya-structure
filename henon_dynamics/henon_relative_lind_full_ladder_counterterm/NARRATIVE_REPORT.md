# Narrative report

P72 proves that the first local H\'enon--Lind counterterm leaves one essential
singularity for every positive scalar channel.  The exact channel formula
also contains `2m` complex poles at level `m`; a counterterm that treats only
the positive ray therefore misses most of the divisor.

Splitting each rational channel into simple poles creates a new problem.
Although the signed poles recombine perfectly within one level, their raw
absolute mass is bounded below along every odd-prime level.  Consequently an
unordered infinite pole product is not defined.  The repair is intrinsic to
the level: subtract Taylor degrees below `m` from every level-`m` pole.  Root
orthogonality makes all subtractions cancel after the `2m` poles are summed,
whereas each individual regularized pole now starts at degree `m`.  This
gives an absolutely normally convergent, order-independent double series.

The resulting exponential is exactly the complete P72 tail.  One additional
source factor removes the negative first-channel singularity, and a scalar
normalization fixes the value at the origin.  The product with the relative
germ is identically one on compatible branches.  The theorem solves the
analytic renormalization problem, but it does so by reproducing every known
channel.  The next honest gate is independent operator ownership, not an
arithmetic interpretation of this copied ledger.
