# C147 paper plan

## Title

Clean primitive families and length collisions in the square billiard.

## Claims/evidence map

1. Primitive unfolding and length: geometric proof plus exact direction rows.
2. Clean cylinders: transverse-circle argument, scoped transverse measure, and
   a family-tangent unit eigenvalue in the full reduced Poincare derivative.
3. Exact census: Möbius formula and 979-row ledger.
4. Degeneracy: exact first symmetry-inequivalent witness at square 65.
5. Control: irrational-aspect rectangle eliminates all distinct collisions.
6. Natural quantization: clock-matched Dirichlet half-wave, antiunitary time
   reversal, reflection phase, and explicit missing trace bridge.
7. Route-A boundary: A1 weak, A2/A3 fail, A4 natural, Route B false.

## Section structure

1. Frozen unfolding and primitive bookkeeping.
2. Clean family and stability obstruction.
3. Exact census, collision, and aspect control.
4. Quantization, validation, and scope.
