# C147 research question

What exact Route-A information is supplied by an integrable billiard whose
rational periodic trajectories occur in clean families rather than as
isolated orbits?

For the unit square, unfolding makes every positive coprime absolute-direction
representative `(m,n)` intrinsic and reproducible.  Its four signed sectors
form two time-reversal pairs, and it has length
`2 sqrt(m^2+n^2)`, an exact one-dimensional fixed-family curve, and a unit
eigenvalue tangent to that curve in the full reduced Poincare linearization.
This gives a primitive-family ledger and a
natural Dirichlet quantization, but simultaneously makes the ordinary
isolated-orbit stability factor singular.

C147 also asks how much of the length collision structure is forced by square
symmetry.  Exact enumeration finds the first symmetry-inequivalent collision
at square 65 and an irrational-aspect rectangle control that removes all
distinct positive-direction collisions.

The scope excludes every target-data, arithmetic/local, and Route-B claim.
