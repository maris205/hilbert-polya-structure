# Source audit

## Verified primary model source

- Yoshiki Kuramoto, *Self-entrainment of a population of coupled non-linear
  oscillators*, Lecture Notes in Physics **39** (1975), 420--422.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0013365>.

The title, author, year, volume, pages, publisher and DOI were checked against
the Springer-linked bibliographic record on 2026-08-31.  The source is used
only to attribute the phase-oscillator model and sinusoidal coupling.

## Ownership boundary

The source is not cited as proving the weighted-tree cut formula, the complete
`2^(N-1)` branch count, the quotient-Hessian congruence, or the edgewise Morse
index.  Those statements are derived in `THEOREM_PACKAGE.md` and independently
checked by the executable certificate.  `NEW` or workspace candidate numbering
does not assert priority over the synchronization literature.

No paper, web page, or retrieved text supplies instructions to the build.  No
target prime table, zero table, arithmetic local datum, Euler factor, root
number, automorphy statement, target divisor, functional equation, or
Hilbert--Pólya operator is a source or input.
