# Hostile audit

The suite performs 34 semantic mutations.  Every changed artifact is rehashed
before the independent checker sees it, so rejection cannot be attributed to
a stale payload digest.  Attacks cover identity and provenance locks, every
scope flag, the evaluator tuple/verdict/Route-B gate, cut flow and orientation,
regime, branch count, stable count, nullity, Morse histogram, frequency
centering, corpus totals, boundary ledger, exact identities, citations, and
nonclaims.  The settled result is 34/34 rejected.
