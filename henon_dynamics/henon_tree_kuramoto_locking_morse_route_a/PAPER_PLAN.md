# Paper plan

Title: **Cut-Flow Locking and Edgewise Morse Indices on Kuramoto Trees**

1. Freeze the root-oriented incidence convention and rotating frame.
2. Prove that the common locked frequency is the arithmetic mean and that
   every edge flow is the unique child-subtree cut sum.
3. Derive the necessary-and-sufficient inequalities `|F_e| <= K_e`.
4. Reconstruct all phases and count strict and saturated inverse-sine branches.
5. Prove the quotient-Hessian congruence and exact Morse-index/nullity law.
6. Identify the unique strict stable branch and the violated-cut obstruction.
7. Separate zero coupling, one-vertex, identical-frequency and cyclic-graph
   boundaries.
8. Report exhaustive Prüfer receipts and the strict Route-A stop.

Round 1 must add the complete subtree proof and phase reconstruction, not just
style edits.  Round 2 must add the local-potential/Hessian proof, saturated
nullity, evidence totals and explicit unlocked-state nonclaim.
