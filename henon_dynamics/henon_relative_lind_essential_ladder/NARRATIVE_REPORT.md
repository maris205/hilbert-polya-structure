# Narrative report

P71 looked encouraging: one uniquely chosen power-exponential counterterm
made the packet/Lind ratio regular at the first entropy boundary. P72 asks the
global question rather than polishing that local success.

The key move is to regroup the primitive and repetition indices. The result
is one rational channel for each positive integer. Its coefficient is an
Euler product over the odd prime divisors of the channel index and can never
vanish. The first channel is exactly the P71 singularity; every later channel
creates a new essential singularity farther along the positive real axis.
These singularities accumulate at the unit circle.

Thus the local bridge is real but cannot be a conventional determinant on
the full disk. The surviving route is not another finite matrix: it is a
punctured-domain, infinite-rank, all-channel renormalization problem.
