# Research question

Does the unique P71 local counterterm turn the H\'enon reflection-packet/Lind
ratio into a meromorphic or Fredholm determinant on the unit disk, or do
later primitive/repetition channels force new singularities?

The predeclared decisive test is all-channel: regroup the exact P70 product,
prove whether every channel coefficient is nonzero, and classify the local
singularity at its first positive denominator zero.
