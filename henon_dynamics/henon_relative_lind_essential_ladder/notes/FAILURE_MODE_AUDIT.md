# Failure-mode audit

- **False cancellation:** impossible because
  `c_m=m^(-1) product_{p|m,p odd}(1-p)` is nonzero.
- **Pole collision:** at the positive `rho_m`, the equation
  `2 rho_m^(2j)=1` forces `j=m`.
- **Finite-prefix promotion:** the all-`m` proof is symbolic; the numerical
  ledger is only an audit.
- **Local/global confusion:** P71 remains a valid local theorem. P72 refutes
  only a unit-disk meromorphic globalization of that specific relative germ.
- **Overbroad operator no-go:** punctured, non-trace-class, or infinitely
  renormalized models remain open.
- **Arithmetic promotion:** channel indices are integers, not certified
  rational primes or prime powers.
