# Source audit

The only external formula used is the full two-shift reverse Lind zeta from
Young-One Kim, Jungseob Lee and Kyewon K. Park, *A zeta function for flip
systems*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 209 (2003), Example 4.3, DOI
10.2140/pjm.2003.209.289:

https://msp.org/pjm/2003/209-2/pjm-v209-n2-p06-p.pdf

The H\'enon/full-shift conjugacy boundary is from Devaney--Nitecki. The
channel regrouping, continuation formula, and essential ladder are new
derivations from the exact P68--P71 repository objects; no source is credited
with those results.
