# Hostile review round 1

**Attack:** later terms might cancel the pole at `rho_m`.

**Resolution:** for a positive `rho_m`, denominator vanishing in channel `j`
means `2^(1-j/m)=1`, hence `j=m`. The coefficient of that unique channel is
nonzero by its Euler product.

**Attack:** an infinite sum need not continue beyond its initial radius.

**Resolution:** after regrouping, `|c_m|<=1` and the terms are `O_K(r^m)` on
every compact punctured subset of `|t|<1`; normal convergence gives the
continuation.
