# Hostile review round 2

**Attack:** the no-determinant claim is too broad.

**Repair:** it is restricted to finite-dimensional determinants and
quotients of holomorphic trace-class Fredholm determinants on the whole unit
disk. Punctured, slit, non-trace-class, and infinitely renormalized models
remain open.

**Attack:** the essential ladder could be a numerical artifact.

**Repair:** the proof uses an all-integer Euler factorization. Computation
checks only the implementation and signs.
