# Methodology blueprint

The paper uses a proof-first, coefficient-independent architecture:

- start from the exact P70 primitive series and Euler repetition law;
- group by the product `m=kr`, not by a finite coefficient fit;
- factor the resulting divisor sum over the odd radical of `m`;
- remove only the `m=1` channel already paid by P71;
- prove normal convergence on compact punctured subsets of the unit disk;
- isolate each positive root, where no other channel has a denominator zero;
- exponentiate the nonzero log-pole and apply the meromorphic singularity
  classification.

Finite computation checks identities and signs but does not establish the
all-channel theorem.
