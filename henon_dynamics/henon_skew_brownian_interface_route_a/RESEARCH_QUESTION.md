# Research question

For the zero-drift skew Brownian SDE with symmetric local time, can one close
in one theorem package all objects that are usually confused at the
interface—Lebesgue density, speed density, generator matching, resolvent,
two-sided exit laws, and occupation time—while retaining `p=0,1/2,1`?

The answer is yes.  The exact kernel is discontinuous in its terminal
Lebesgue coordinate at zero unless `p=1/2`, but division by the piecewise
speed density produces a symmetric kernel.  One piecewise constant-slope
scale function closes all hitting probabilities; one hyperbolic transfer
matrix closes the discounted exit sides; and excursion thinning gives a
ratio of independent stable-`1/2` variables for occupation time.

The Route-A question is separate: do these source-local facts create
rational-prime primitive orbits or a target determinant?  They do not.  The
strict result is therefore a complete stochastic theorem together with a
proved Route-A obstruction, not a target spectral claim.
