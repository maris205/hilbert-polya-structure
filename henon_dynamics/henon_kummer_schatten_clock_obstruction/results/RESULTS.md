# Results

The exact grid distinguishes the Hilbert--Schmidt boundary
`sigma=1/2` and the trace-class boundary `sigma=1`.  The finite prime sums
are monotone at exponents 0.8, 1.0, and 1.2, as required.  Their finite size
does not certify convergence; the theorem follows from the classical prime
Dirichlet series criterion.

The resulting Fredholm determinant for `sigma>1` is mathematically valid but
uses an external prime damping absent from the unramified Kummer channel.
