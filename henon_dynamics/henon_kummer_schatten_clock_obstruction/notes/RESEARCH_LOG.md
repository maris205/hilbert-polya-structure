# Research log

C39 shows that phase cancellation without decay cannot even define a local
meromorphic divisor.  Prime damping repairs the operator theory completely:
there is an exact Schatten phase diagram.  The repair is nevertheless
noncanonical because the finite Kummer channel is unramified away from the
bad prime 3 and has no growing conductor.  The next project therefore moves
from an artificial block weight to an actual cubic arithmetic variety.
