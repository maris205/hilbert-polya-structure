# Narrative report — C123

Additive noise changes the object being iterated: a periodic orbit now carries
both a state cycle and a chronological noise word.  Because both branch maps
share a strict contraction, each word has a unique closing state.  This makes
an exhaustive short-word certificate possible without stochastic sampling.

The same model law defines a Markov operator on observables.  Degree at most
four is invariant under the affine branches and their exact average, producing
a 15-dimensional source-owned matrix.  Its stationary vector recovers a
positive covariance, while a nonzero negative fourth cumulant prevents a
Gaussian shortcut.  Removing the noise collapses the stationary covariance to
zero.

The orbit and moment results reinforce one another but remain finite local
structures.  They provide no prime-like target correspondence.  In
particular, the degree-four Markov determinant has no target-divisor match or
analytic bridge, and no global transfer-space theorem is inferred.
