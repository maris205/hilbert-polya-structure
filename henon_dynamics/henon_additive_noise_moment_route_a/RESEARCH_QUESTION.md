# Research question — C123

For a strictly contracting additive-noise Hénon recurrence, can periodic noise
words and stationary polynomial moments be certified by one exact,
source-defined Markov operator without sampling random trajectories?

The requested result is limited to noise periods at most six and polynomial
degree at most four.  Global orbit completeness, an analytic Fredholm owner,
prime-like target correspondence, target-divisor matching, arithmetic data,
and Route B are outside scope.
