# Research question

For the Schnakenberg reaction--diffusion system

\[
 u_t=d_u u_{xx}+a-u+u^2v,\qquad
 v_t=d_v v_{xx}+b-u^2v
\]

on `(0,L)` with homogeneous Neumann conditions and
`a,b,d_u,d_v,L>0`, can one classify exactly when the unique positive
homogeneous equilibrium is kinetically stable but linearly unstable to a
genuine finite-domain spatial mode?

The answer must distinguish the continuous dispersion window from the
discrete Neumann spectrum, count all unstable modes with strict endpoint
conventions, and state every neutral or singular boundary without promoting
linear instability to nonlinear pattern existence.
