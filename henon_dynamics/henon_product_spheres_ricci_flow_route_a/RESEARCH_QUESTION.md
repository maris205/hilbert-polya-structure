# Research question

For every finite product of round spheres, every list of factor dimensions
`d_i>=1`, and every positive diagonal product scale `a_i`, can one give a
single exact theorem that determines:

1. the maximal unnormalized Ricci-flow interval;
2. the complete tied first-collapse set and all curvature, volume, and
   diameter asymptotics;
3. the pointed Type-I singularity model;
4. the exact constant-volume normalization and its physical-to-normalized
   time change;
5. the sharp equivalence between partial collapse, finite normalized lifetime,
   and escape of survivor scales;
6. the sharp equivalence between full collapse, Einstein initial data, and a
   stationary forward-eternal normalized flow;
7. the `d_i=1`, all-flat, one-factor, tie, permutation, and common-scaling
   boundaries?

The source theorem is independent of Route A.  Only after closing it do we ask
whether the flow contains rational-prime carriers, isolated primitive cycles,
a target dynamical determinant, global target analytic structure, or a natural
same-clock quantization.  It contains none of them, so the correct result is a
large exact geometric theorem together with a Route-A rejection.
