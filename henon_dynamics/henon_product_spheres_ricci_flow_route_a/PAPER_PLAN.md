# Paper plan

## Round 0 — exact homogeneous flow

Define the product owner, derive the coefficient ODE, prove the maximal
interval, and close the scalar curvature, Riemann norm, volume, diameter, and
Einstein/homothety formulas.  State the all-flat and one-factor faces.

## Round 1 — singularity and normalization

Add the full tied-collapse theorem, Type-I residues, pointed parabolic blowup,
and the constant-volume conjugacy.  Prove the endpoint integral rather than
asserting a normalized lifetime from pictures.

## Round 2 — exact boundary and audit closure

Add normalized-time asymptotics, the partial/full equivalence theorem, the
complete boundary atlas, executable reconstruction, internal collision audit,
source-role audit, and strict all-fail Route-A evaluation.

The central result stays geometric.  There is no heat trace, spectral zeta,
regularized determinant, Schatten threshold, or arithmetic target data in any
round.
