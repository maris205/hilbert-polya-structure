# Narrative report

The paper makes a single large step for a nonsmooth hybrid subtype: it turns a
Schmitt-trigger relay convention into a complete event atlas. The phase
coordinate is piecewise affine, while the transverse variable has an exact
multiplicative cocycle. This separates geometric period from transverse
stability and makes the no-Zeno statement a direct lower bound.

The receipt spans contracting, neutral, zero-amplitude, small/large threshold,
and grazing faces. It is an A1 analytic pass only; no arithmetic origin or
target determinant exists.
