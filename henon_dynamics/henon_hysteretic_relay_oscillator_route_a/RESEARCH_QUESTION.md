# Research question

Can a fully specified two-threshold relay convention yield an exact global
event/return theorem, periodic-set classification, transverse multiplier,
grazing policy, and no-Zeno bound?

**Answer.** Yes. With h>0, every leg has duration 2h, the Poincare map is
P(y)=exp(-4 gamma h)y, and all boundary conventions are explicit. The
arithmetic origin is none, so the Route-A evaluator stops at A0.
