2. Independently recompute leg times, guard states, exponential decay, full
   return map, fixed set, and grazing labels.
3. Prove ten flow/composition/guard identities symbolically.
4. Replay the producer twice and require byte equality.
5. Mutate metadata, scope, route, and period cells; require rejection.
6. Build three fixed-epoch manuscript revisions and verify fonts, text, and

extension is inferred.
