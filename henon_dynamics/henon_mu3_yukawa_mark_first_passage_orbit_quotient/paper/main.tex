\begin{abstract}
We quotient the exact twenty-target first-passage atlas by the faithful
order-1920 label action inherited from the frozen closure model.  The
ambient lifted order 11520 is kept distinct.  Rebuilding the five named
label permutations and checking their action on all 65536 support indicators
produces sixteen target orbits: twelve singletons and four doubletons.  The
C88 first-passage laws and the C92 pivotal-label rank rows are transported
equivariantly at generator level.  This is a finite quotient result under
\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER}.
\end{abstract}

support gives a permutation of the twenty actual targets.  Theorem-level

\begin{theorem}
The effective action partitions the twenty target laws into sixteen orbits,
with orbit-size spectrum $12\times1+4\times2$.  For every generator $g$,
\[
 \Pr(T_H=k)=\Pr(T_{gH}=k),

and the pivotal-rank row for label $S_i$ at $H$ equals the row for $gS_i$ at
$gH$.  The C88 support indicators and C92 sensitivity rows satisfy these
identities exactly.
\end{theorem}
