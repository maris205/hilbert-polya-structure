# Research question

For the magnetic equation `D_t dot(gamma)=b J dot(gamma)` on the complete simply connected hyperbolic plane of curvature `-kappa^2`, with fixed speed `v`, can one classify all trajectories, locate the exact closed-orbit threshold, compute the primitive period, and describe every singular boundary without importing a classification label as a proof?

Yes.  For `v>0`, the signed geodesic curvature is `b/v`.  The ambient Lorentz frame has a constant generator satisfying `A^3=(kappa^2 v^2-b^2)A`.  Its elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic conjugacy types give circles, horocycles, and hypercycles/geodesics.  Only `|b|>kappa v` closes, with primitive period `2 pi/sqrt(b^2-kappa^2 v^2)`.  Equality is a nonzero nilpotent, nonperiodic horocycle.
