# ROUND2 clue

The next high-value problem is not period 17 factorization. It is an
all-period transversality criterion for the intersections

```text
Fix(J) intersect H^{-(m+1)} Fix(R).
```

A repeated root of `F_n` should translate into a monodromy degeneracy (an
eigenvalue or symmetry-line tangent condition). Establishing that equivalence
and testing whether the certified H6 cone bounds exclude it on the physical
survivor is the narrowest route to an effective dynatomic theorem. Roots
outside the local survivor must remain a separate population.
