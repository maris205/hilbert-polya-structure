# Hostile review round 1

The first review attacked the divisibility proof. Setwise inclusion of roots
would be insufficient without squarefreeness. The proof was therefore moved
to the quotient ring: the two exact reflection boundary equations make the
recurrence periodic modulo `F_d`, giving a literal zero remainder and hence
polynomial divisibility without any root-multiplicity assumption.

The review also required the term *formal entropy* everywhere the all-period
M\"obius divisor is used.
