# Research question

P59 proves that physical reflection necklaces grow at rate
`exp((n/2)log(phi))`. Does the algebraic mixed-axis reflection closure have a
different primitive degree rate, and can that rate be proved without assuming
that every formal dynatomic point is an effective minimal-period root?

The decision targets are:

1. derive the odd mixed-axis closure for every period;
2. prove its degree and divisor relation algebraically;
3. compute the M\"obius formal primitive degree and compare entropy with P59;
4. determine how far exact reducedness and irreducibility can be certified;
5. identify the precise theorem still required before a Galois pressure can
   use the degree law.
