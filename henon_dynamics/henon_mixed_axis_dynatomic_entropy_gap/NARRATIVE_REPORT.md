# Narrative report

P59 counted the physical reflection subsystem and found half the ambient
symbolic entropy. P60 asks whether algebraic multiplicity can supply a larger
reflection population.

The answer is positive at the formal-degree level. Odd mixed-axis closures
form an exact divisibility sequence with total degree `2^((n+1)/2)`. M\"obius
subtraction gives formal primitive degree asymptotic to the same leading term,
and hence entropy `(1/2)log(2)`. The excess over the local physical reflection
rate is strictly positive.

The result does not yet provide a Galois pressure. The formal divisor could,
in principle, contain excess multiplicity at lower minimal periods unless the
symmetry-line intersections are transverse. Exact computation rules this out
through period 15 and recovers P58's period-nine factor, but no all-period
transversality theorem is available. The next project should attack that
single gate rather than extend the factor table.
