# Methodology blueprint

1. Factor `H_6` into its two reversing involutions and parameterize the
   vertex symmetry line by `X`.
2. Generate the half-chain recurrence and define `F_n=q_(m+1)-q_m`.
3. Track leading degrees to prove `deg F_n=2^(m+1)`.
4. Work in `Q[X]/(F_d)`: the two reflection boundary equalities make the
   recurrence `d`-periodic, proving `F_d|F_n` for odd `d|n`.
5. Form the virtual M\"obius divisor and calculate its degree.
6. Recursively divide exact lower-period quotients through `n=15`; check
   squarefreeness and factor each quotient over `Q`.
7. Cross-lock the period-nine factor to P58 and the physical entropy to P59.
8. Keep formal degree, effective root counts and Galois heights as three
   separate claim levels.
