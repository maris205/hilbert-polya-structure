# Narrative report

C299 changes dynamical subtype from the nearby shear and nonlinear-diffusion papers to a planar viscous vortex with a nonlocal Biot--Savart closure.  The decisive geometric fact is that radial vorticity generates tangential velocity, so the apparently nonlinear advection term vanishes on the entire radial class.  This permits a full—not sampled—classification of regular finite-circulation forward-self-similar profiles.

The advance is bundled rather than sliced: one theorem determines the unique Gaussian, reconstructs the velocity, integrates every positive-radius particle path through an exponential integral, evaluates all even moments and finite \(L^p\) norms, proves exact enstrophy dissipation, and separates nine singular or asymptotic boundaries.  It also records the physically important mismatch between finite enstrophy and infinite whole-plane kinetic energy.

The result is deliberately stronger on completeness and weaker on scope: it covers all real circulations, all positive viscosities, all nonnegative initial ages, and every profile satisfying the declared radial-similarity hypotheses, but it says nothing about uniqueness for arbitrary vortices.  The finite JSON grid is regression evidence only.
