# Paper plan

## Headline

One short paper gives a complete classification of the regular finite-circulation radial forward-self-similar class, then turns the unique Gaussian profile into exact Eulerian, Lagrangian, dissipative, and singular-boundary formulas.

## Claim ledger

| Claim | Proof mechanism | Finite evidence role |
|---|---|---|
| Uniqueness in the declared similarity class | radial advection cancellation; one integration of the similarity ODE; origin regularity; circulation normalization | pointwise residual regression only |
| Exact velocity and particles | polar Biot--Savart; invariant radii; differentiated exponential-integral primitive | twelve quadrature comparisons |
| All moments and \(L^p\) laws | polar Gaussian integrals | rational receipts for \(k=0,\ldots,8\), \(p=1,\ldots,6\) |
| Enstrophy dissipation | exact enstrophy and palinstrophy integrals | eight parameter cases |
| Boundary atlas | weak convergence, asymptotics, norm monotonicity, far-field energy expansion | nine explicit boundary rows |
| Route-A rejection | evaluator rubric applied without scope escalation | strict YAML and claim flags |

## Revision architecture

- Round 0: classification, velocity, trajectories, moments, and dissipation.
- Round 1: add the complete singular/boundary atlas and sharpen the theorem class.
- Round 2: add finite-evidence traceability, collision boundaries, Route-A evaluation, reproducibility, and AI-use disclosure.

All three revisions are retained as distinct PDFs; `main.pdf` is byte-identical to round 2.
