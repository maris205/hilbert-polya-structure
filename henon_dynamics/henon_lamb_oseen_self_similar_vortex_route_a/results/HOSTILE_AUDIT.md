## Theorem attacks

1. **Overbroad uniqueness.**  Rejected: the theorem repeatedly names the \(C^2\), bounded-at-origin, absolutely finite-circulation radial forward-self-similar class and explicitly excludes arbitrary vortices.
2. **Hidden integration constant.**  Rejected: origin regularity forces the first-integral constant \(C\) to zero before solving the Gaussian equation.
3. **Unsigned-only proof.**  Rejected: circulation \(\Gamma\) is real; signed moments and absolute \(L^p\) norms are distinguished.
4. **Wrong exponential-integral sign.**  Rejected: both differentiation and 12 independent 90-digit quadratures verify \(\mathcal F_a'=1-e^{-a/\tau}\).
5. **Division at the origin.**  Rejected: \(r=0\) is handled by the continuous velocity extension; the angle formula states \(r>0\).
6. **Silent \(\nu=0\) substitution.**  Rejected: \(\nu>0\) is the theorem domain and the inviscid object is only a weak/off-origin limit.
7. **Particle return confused with fluid recurrence.**  Rejected: the obstruction uses strict finite \(L^p\) decay of the fluid state for \(p>1\).
8. **Energy coefficient error.**  Rejected: the radial integral includes the kinetic factor \(1/2\), producing \(\Gamma^2/(4\pi)\log R\).
9. **Finite evidence promoted to proof.**  Rejected: every artifact labels 213 cells as regression only; the similarity ODE and integrals prove the theorem.
10. **Scope or collision laundering.**  Rejected: collision-boundary text separates C206 and C207, while strict flags prohibit all target arithmetic and Hilbert--Polya claims.
11. **Angle treated only modulo \(2\pi\).**  Rejected: the trajectory theorem explicitly fixes a continuous real-valued polar-angle lift before stating logarithmic drift.
12. **Semantically altered but shape-compatible rows.**  Rejected: full nonclaim, boundary, and reference trees, exact row-key sets, and canonical rational strings are independently enforced.

No surviving theorem, parser, scope-escalation, or collision-boundary attack was found in the two improvement rounds.
