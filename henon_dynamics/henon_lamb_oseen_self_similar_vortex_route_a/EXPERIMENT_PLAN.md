# Exact-evidence plan

## Purpose

The theorem is analytic.  Computation is limited to deterministic regression, independent formula reconstruction, parser hardening, and release closure.

## Evidence grid

- Eight field cases span zero, positive, and negative circulation; small and large age; and narrow and wide viscous cores.
- Each field case uses nine exact similarity coordinates \(x=r^2/(4\nu\tau)\), nine moment orders, and six finite \(L^p\) powers.
- Twelve positive-radius trajectory cases compare the exponential-integral primitive with direct 90-digit quadrature.
- Nine boundary rows separate theorem domain, singular limits, recurrence obstruction, and infinite-energy behavior.
- Total audited cells: \(72+72+48+12+9=213\).

## Independent gates

1. The producer writes a canonical JSON payload with a self-hash.
2. A producer-free checker reconstructs every rational and transcendental receipt.
3. SymPy proves the similarity ODE, heat equation, curl relation, moments, norms, dissipation identity, trajectory primitive, and asymptotic coefficients.
4. Replay generates two fresh files and requires byte equality with the archived evidence.
5. Hostile mutations recompute payload hashes when appropriate, ensuring semantic checks—not merely stale hashes—reject corruptions.
6. The release closer checks strict JSON/YAML parsing, an exact 27-payload ledger, six isolated deterministic PDF builds, embedded fonts, text contracts, and rasterizability.

No numerical row is used to infer the global theorem.
