\section{The prime-graded determinant}
\label{sec:global-determinant}

Trace class is the exact region in which the block assembly gives an ordinary
Fredholm determinant without choosing an order of multiplication.

\begin{theorem}[Prime-order-independent Fredholm product]
\label{thm:global-det}
On

\[
 \halfplane\times\{z\in\C:\operatorname{Re}z>3\},
\]

the family \(\fL_{s,z}\) is holomorphic with values in
\(\Schatten 1(\cH_\oplus)\).  Therefore

\begin{equation}
 \fD(s,z,u)=\det_{\cH_\oplus}(I-u\fL_{s,z})
 \label{eq:global-det}
\end{equation}

is jointly holomorphic in \((s,z,u)\), and

\begin{equation}
 \fD(s,z,u)
 =\prod_{p\ \odd}\cD_p(s,up^{-z}).
 \label{eq:global-product}
\end{equation}

The product is locally normal and independent of the enumeration of the odd
primes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix a compact \(K\Subset\halfplane\) and a compact \(z\)-set on which
\(\operatorname{Re}z\ge3+\varepsilon\) for some \(\varepsilon>0\).  The
trace-norm upper bound from \cref{thm:sharp-local} gives

\begin{equation}
 \sum_{p\ \odd}\Norm{p^{-z}\cL_{s,p}}_{\Schatten 1}
 \le C_K\sum_{p\ \odd}p^{-1-\varepsilon}<\infty,
 \label{eq:S1-prime-majorant}
\end{equation}

uniformly in \(s\) and \(z\) on these compact sets.  Each block depends
holomorphically on \(s\), while \(p^{-z}=e^{-z\log p}\) is entire in \(z\).
The Weierstrass theorem for Banach-valued holomorphic series makes the direct
sum \(\Schatten 1\)-holomorphic.

The ordinary determinant is continuous and holomorphic on trace
class~\citep{simon2005}.  Finite prime truncations factor into the product of
their block determinants.  They converge to \(\fL_{s,z}\) in trace norm,
locally uniformly, so determinant continuity gives
\cref{eq:global-product}.  Absolute trace-norm convergence makes the direct
sum, and hence its determinant, invariant under every permutation of the
prime blocks.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Chronological trace expansion}

The global object keeps the exact local chronology rather than collapsing it
to a transition statistic.

\begin{proposition}[Wordwise prime coefficient]
\label{prop:chronological-trace}
In the domain of \cref{thm:global-det}, for every \(n\ge1\),

\begin{equation}
 \Tr\fL_{s,z}^n
 =\sum_{w\in\Gamma^n}A_{s,w}
   \sum_{p\ \odd}p^{-nz}\Theta_p(g_w).
 \label{eq:global-word-trace}
\end{equation}

Both sums are absolutely convergent, locally uniformly in \((s,z)\).  If a
primitive word \(w\) of length \(n\) is repeated \(r\) times, its fibre
factor is

\begin{equation}
 p^{-nrz}\Theta_p(g_w^r),
 \label{eq:repetition}
\end{equation}

not \(p^{-nrz}\Theta_p(g_w)^r\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Powers of a block-diagonal operator remain block diagonal, so

\[
 \Tr\fL_{s,z}^n
 =\sum_{p\ \odd}p^{-nz}\Tr\cL_{s,p}^n.
\]

Insert the source-locked word formula \cref{eq:local-word-trace}.  Since
\(|\Theta_p(h)|\le p^2\) and the scalar word trace has a locally uniform
absolute majorant, the absolute prime majorant is a constant times

\[
 \sum_{p\ \odd}p^{2-n\operatorname{Re}z},
\]

which converges already for \(n=1\) when
\(\operatorname{Re}z>3\).  Tonelli's theorem therefore permits the stated
reordering.  The repetition formula follows by applying the actual
representation to the chronological matrix power \(g_w^r\).
\end{proof}

For sufficiently small \(u\), the usual expansion

\begin{equation}
 \Log\fD(s,z,u)
 =-\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{u^n}{n}\Tr\fL_{s,z}^n
 \label{eq:global-log}
\end{equation}

then inserts \cref{eq:global-word-trace} without changing word order.

\subsection{Regularized hierarchy}

For an integer \(m\ge2\), \cref{cor:phase-diagram} gives

\begin{equation}
 \fL_{s,z}\in\Schatten m
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Re}z>\frac3m.
 \label{eq:regularized-region}
\end{equation}

Thus the regularized determinant \(\det_m(I-u\fL_{s,z})\) is available in
exactly that half-plane; its logarithm near the origin starts at \(n=m\).
In particular, \(z=2\) gives an operator in
\(\Schatten2\setminus\Schatten1\) and first permits \(\det_2\), while
\(z=1\) first permits \(\det_4\).  At \(z=0\) the block sum is noncompact,
so no finite-order regularized determinant applies.  These statements use
the standard regularized Fredholm convention~\citep{simon2005,britz2021}.
They do not continue the ordinary determinant across its trace-class wall.

The weight \(p^{-z}\) is therefore analytically effective and dynamically
external.  On a length-\(n\) return it contributes \(e^{-nz\log p}\), adding
\(z\log p\) to the original AGY roof for each return.  Prime damping retains
chronology but does not arise from the one-clock dynamical system itself.
