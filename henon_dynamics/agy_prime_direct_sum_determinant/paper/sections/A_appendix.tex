\subsection{Direct-sum compactness and determinant order}
\label{app:direct-sum}

For completeness, suppose \(T_p\) is compact on \(H_p\) and
\(T=\bigoplus_pT_p\).  If \(T\) is compact, choose unit vectors \(v_p\in H_p\)
with \(\Norm{T_pv_p}\ge\Norm{T_p}-p^{-1}\).  The vectors occupy mutually
orthogonal blocks.  Compactness of \(T\) forces \(\Norm{T_pv_p}\to0\), hence
\(\Norm{T_p}\to0\).  Conversely, if the block norms tend to zero, finite
block truncations converge to \(T\) in operator norm and are compact.  This
proves the compactness assertion used in \cref{thm:block-iff} without a
convention at \(q=\infty\).

For trace-class blocks, finite direct sums satisfy

\[
 \det\!\left(I-u\bigoplus_{p\le P}T_p\right)
 =\prod_{p\le P}\det(I-uT_p).
\]

If \(\sum_p\Norm{T_p}_{\Schatten1}<\infty\), finite block truncations
converge in trace norm.  Continuity of the Fredholm determinant then proves
the infinite product and removes the prime-order choice.  This is stronger
than conditionally converging a scalar logarithmic Euler product.

For \(m\ge2\), the convention used in \cref{eq:regularized-region} is

\[
 \det_m(I-T)=\det\!\left((I-T)
 \exp\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}\frac{T^j}{j}\right)\right),
 \qquad T\in\Schatten m.
\]

It deletes the first \(m-1\) trace terms near the origin and does not restore
the missing ordinary trace.

\subsection{Claim-status firewall}

The sharp Schatten bounds, weighted direct-sum equivalence, ordinary
determinant for \(\operatorname{Re}z>3\), regular-character limit, and
normalized positive-monoid collapse are theorem-level deductions.  The
P073 identities and 146-cycle census are exact finite controls.  The
150-signature conductor census is finite evidence only.  Exclusion of fixed
planes from every word of the C26 induced language, continuation toward
\(z=0\), and any nontrivial groupoid trace remain open.
