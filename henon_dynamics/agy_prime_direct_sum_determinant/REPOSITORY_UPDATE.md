## Claim boundary

The update proves a prime-graded Dirichlet--Fredholm family and its sharp
Schatten threshold.  It does not construct a genuine adelic Weil
representation, derive the new prime clock from the source dynamics, continue
the determinant to \(z=0\), produce a functional equation or Riemann divisor,
or define a self-adjoint Hilbert--Pólya operator.

C24-P073 is a full-Rauzy control and is not relabeled as a C26 induced branch.
The corresponding all-word fixed-plane gate for the C26 language remains
open.
