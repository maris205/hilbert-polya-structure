### HCS-C281 -- product-spheres homogeneous Ricci flow

`a_i(t)=a_i-2(d_i-1)t`.  It derives the complete scalar, Ricci, Riemann,
volume and diameter laws; retains every tied first-collapse factor; proves
sharp Type-I residues; and identifies every parabolic blowup as the product
of the tied shrinking spheres with an Euclidean survivor factor.

The volume-normalized time conjugacy is derived exactly.  Partial first
collapse occurs at finite normalized time with sharp vanishing, expansion and
curvature asymptotics.  Full collapse is equivalent to a positive-Einstein
initial product and becomes a stationary forward-eternal normalized
solution.  All-flat tori are separately eternal Ricci-flat fixed points, and
mixed flat/curved, one-factor, scaling, permutation and tie faces are closed.

The evidence has 14 cases and 218 analytic/boundary rows:
`14/68/66/12/36/14/8` across case, flow, normalized, collapse, asymptotic,
covariance and boundary families.  The checker closes **2,063** assertions,

## Citation, proof and scope integrity

The source audits use references only for equation, model, terminology or
direct-owner lineage.  In particular, C279 identifies the direct 1D
TV-flow/fused-lasso owners, while C283 identifies the exact published owner
of its positive spectral-zeta core.  C280's Jeffery and Bretherton sources,
C281's Hamilton and Chow--Knopf sources, and C282's Gerber--Shiu and
Drekic--Willmot sources are used at the stated narrow locations.  No
displayed theorem is outsourced to a citation, and no repository ownership is
presented as literature priority.

The final audit separates analytic proof from finite computation in every
paper.  Exact grids test conventions and implementation; they do not prove
the arbitrary-real, arbitrary-dimension, infinite-volume, stochastic-process
or operator statements.  Every producer has a producer-independent checker,
an independent symbolic reconstruction, a fresh-path byte replay, and
repaired-hash semantic mutations plus a stale-hash control.  The checker
strengthening in C280--C282 and C281's replacement package explicitly rejects
duplicate/drop-replace attacks and reconstructs complete boundary semantics.

All five evaluations set `route_b_invocation_allowed: false`.  No target
arithmetic local data, Euler factor, bad-prime datum, root number, automorphy
object, target divisor/counting law or functional equation, target zero
match, Hilbert--Pólya operator, or Route-B input is asserted.
