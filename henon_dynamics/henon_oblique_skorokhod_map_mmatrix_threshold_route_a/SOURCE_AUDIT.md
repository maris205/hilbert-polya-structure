# Source audit

## Authority records

1. J. M. Harrison and M. I. Reiman, “Reflected Brownian Motion on an
   Orthant,” *Annals of Probability* 9 (1981), 302–308, DOI
   `10.1214/aop/1176994471`.  This is the classical orthant reflection-matrix
   owner.
2. P. Dupuis and H. Ishii, “On Lipschitz Continuity of the Solution Mapping to
   the Skorokhod Problem, with Applications,” *Stochastics and Stochastics
   Reports* 35 (1991), 31–62, DOI `10.1080/17442509108833688`.  This is the
   source owner for Lipschitz Skorokhod-map analysis.

The records were checked against author, title, venue, year and DOI.  No
literature-priority claim is made.  The package contribution is a
convention-locked, self-contained two-dimensional theorem and executable
boundary audit.

## Claim boundary

- The theorem is deterministic and pathwise; it does not claim a reflected
  Brownian approximation or diffusion limit.
- Inputs are cadlag and start in the quadrant; complementarity evaluates the
  state after regulator jumps.
- The all-input theorem is analytic.  Rational event ledgers are regression
  evidence only.
- `det R=1-rho*sigma` is a source matrix threshold, not a dynamical or target
  determinant.
- No target arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy,
  divisor, functional equation, target-zero match, Hilbert–Pólya operator or
  Route-B input is asserted.
