# Research question

For every two-dimensional cadlag input beginning in the nonnegative quadrant,
when does the oblique reflection matrix

`R=[[1,-rho],[-sigma,1]]`, with `rho,sigma>=0`,

define a unique global Skorokhod state and minimal regulator?  Can one prove a
sharp necessary-and-sufficient threshold, a quantitative fixed-point
construction and Lipschitz bound, and close simultaneous jumps, triangular
couplings, critical nonuniqueness and supercritical failure in one theorem?

The frozen answer is yes: the exact chamber is `rho*sigma<1`.  The statement
uses post-jump Stieltjes complementarity; changing that convention changes the
jump problem and is outside this paper.
