## Package contribution and boundaries

The package integrates the componentwise semigroup theorem with a complete
all-principal-minor and characteristic-polynomial pseudoforest expansion,
then certifies every labelled signed simple graph and root set through four
vertices.  The exhaustive finite ledger is not an all-size proof.  This audit
is not a systematic review and makes no external-review or priority claim.
