# Research question

For every finite, static, undirected, simple signed graph—possibly
disconnected—with strictly positive edge weights, can one theorem determine
the signed-Laplacian kernel, exact semigroup limit and sharp spectral rate?
Can the same theorem go substantially further and expand every principal minor
and the complete characteristic polynomial by rooted trees and root-free
negative unicycles?

The formula must survive isolated vertices, mixed balanced/unbalanced
components and arbitrary root sets.  It must also expose why ordinary spanning
trees alone give the wrong principal minor in a connected graph containing a
negative cycle behind a bridge.  Directed graphs are a stated exclusion, not a
silent extension.

Finally, Route A is evaluated without promoting a finite graph determinant to
the target arithmetic determinant.
