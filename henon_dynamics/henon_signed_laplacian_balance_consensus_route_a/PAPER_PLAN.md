# Paper plan

## Central claim

Arbitrary disconnected positive-weight signed graphs admit one exact theorem
covering balance-controlled consensus, kernel/projector/rate, all principal
minors and the complete characteristic polynomial.

## Structure

1. Freeze undirected signed incidence conventions and exclusions.
2. Prove the componentwise balance/kernel/projector theorem.
3. Derive the exact spectral rate and all-static boundaries.
4. Prove the rooted-tree/negative-unicycle principal-minor theorem.
5. Sum root sets to obtain the full characteristic polynomial.
6. Give bridge-triangle and directed counterexamples.
7. Report exhaustive/symbolic validation and apply Route A.

No figure is required.  A compact mapping table and the explicit four-vertex
counterexample convey the essential combinatorics exactly.

## Revisions

- Round 0: signed consensus, kernel, projector and rate.
- Round 1: full principal-minor/characteristic pseudoforest formulas and sharp
  counterexamples.
- Round 2: exhaustive 11,894-root certificate, hostile closure, source/scope
  ownership, Route-A decision and disclosures.
