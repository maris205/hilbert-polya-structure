# C203 hostile audit

1. **Global-balance collapse:** balance and nullity are computed per connected
   component, isolated vertices included.
2. **Tree-only minor:** the bridge/negative-triangle cofactor is 7, decomposed
   as three trees plus a factor-4 negative unicycle.
3. **Missing characteristic terms:** every coefficient is reconstructed both
   from all principal minors and all admissible pseudoforests.
4. **Rate overstatement:** the equality uses symmetry and the smallest positive
   eigenvalue; when `L=0` the error is identically zero and `gamma` is omitted.
5. **Directed laundering:** an explicit nonsymmetric matrix has an oblique
   left/right-zero projection and is excluded.
6. **Weight overreach:** the finite ledger uses unit weights; independent
   symbolic identities and Cauchy–Binet carry arbitrary positive weights.
7. **Determinant promotion:** graph characteristic polynomials are not called
   the target arithmetic determinant.
8. **Hash-only defense:** repaired semantic mutations, unknown keys and a stale
   hash all fail independent checking.

All tested attacks are closed.  This is internal hostile analysis, not
external review or an acceptance score.
