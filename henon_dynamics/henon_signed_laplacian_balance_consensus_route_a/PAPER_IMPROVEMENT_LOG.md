# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — componentwise semigroup theorem

The initial manuscript proved the quadratic-form kernel criterion for every
balanced/unbalanced component, the exact orthogonal projector, semigroup limit
and sharp spectral norm rate, including isolated and all-zero boundaries.  It
is `paper/main_round0_original.pdf`.

## Round 1 — determinant-size expansion

The first substantive revision added the Cauchy–Binet classification of every
principal minor by one-root trees and root-free negative unicycles, then summed
root choices to obtain the complete characteristic polynomial.  It also added
the bridge-plus-negative-triangle and directed counterexamples.  It is
`paper/main_round1.pdf`.

## Round 2 — exhaustive and claim closure

The second substantive revision added all 760 graphs and 11,894 root-set
checks, arbitrary-weight SymPy identities, exact assertion/mutation counts,
source and scope locks, the Route-A decision and disclosures.  It is
`paper/main_round2.pdf` and equals `main.pdf`.  These are internal revisions,
not external review or scoring.
