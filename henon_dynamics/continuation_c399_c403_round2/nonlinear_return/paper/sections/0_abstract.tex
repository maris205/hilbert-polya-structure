We construct a finite matrix that represents every global residue trace of a
complex H\'enon map with a fixed polynomial coordinate weight. For
$H(x,y)=(p(x)-ay,x)$, where $p$ is monic of degree $d\ge2$ and $a\ne0$,
the weight at a point is $q(x)$ with $\deg q\le m$.
The cyclic fixed-point scheme at period $n$ has length $d^n$, but the matrix
constructed here has size $(B+1)^2$, where
$B=\lfloor\max(0,m-d+1)(d+1)/(d-1)^2\rfloor$ is independent of $n$.
Its entries are integer-coefficient polynomials in the parameters.
The proof expands a global residue into nearest-neighbour exponent flows
and bounds each local flow by a column-stochastic propagation argument.
The signed stability trace is $-\Tr(W^n)$, and the associated generating
function is $\det(I-zW)^{-1}$.
All identities remain valid at degenerate parameters when the trace is
defined by the full local-residue sum. For quadratic maps and quadratic
coordinate weights, we obtain an explicit factorization of degree at most
four. We also characterize when this trace sequence has an ordinary
trace-class representative: exactly when $W$ is nilpotent.
