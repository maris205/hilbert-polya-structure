# Research question

For the fixed periodic Airy equation

\[
u_t+u_{xxx}=0,\qquad x\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z),
\]

can one go beyond an isolated Talbot picture and give one complete theorem
for the full `L^2` unitary group: its minimum global return, every rational
strobe as a cubic finite Fourier transform of translations, its exact order
and fixed subspace, every finite-support state period, and the irrational
fixed-mode boundary?

Success means an all-time proof plus independent modular, DFT, symbolic,
replay, and hostile-mutation receipts.  It does not mean nonlinear KdV,
general boundary conditions, a closed evaluation of every cubic Gauss sum,
or a target arithmetic determinant.
