# Narrative report

C261 opens a linear-dispersive PDE owner that is absent from C1--C260.  The
advance is not one rational-time image: it starts from the all-time unitary
group, proves its least global return, and then classifies every rational
strobe by a cubic DFT together with its exact order and entire fixed
subspace.  The same theorem closes individual finite-spectrum periods and
the irrational fixed-space boundary.

The nearest workspace owners are C195 viscous Burgers, C206 Couette, C217
rotating shallow water, C221 cubic NLS, and C256 KdV cnoidal waves.  None owns
the periodic cubic-phase revival operator.  This is workspace collision
bookkeeping, not a claim of literature novelty.

The canonical self-adjoint `D^3` generator is a genuine source-local natural
quantization.  It is not a Hilbert--Pólya operator: its integer-cube spectrum
has no target-zero relation, while the unitary maps are noncompact and not
trace class.  A1 therefore remains weak and Route B remains disabled.
