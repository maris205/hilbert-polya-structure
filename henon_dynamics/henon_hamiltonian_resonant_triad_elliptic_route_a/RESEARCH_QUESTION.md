# HCS-C344 research question

For the canonically normalized Hamiltonian resonant triad, can one prove in one closed theorem the global flow, complete Manley--Rowe reduction to a real cubic, every regular Jacobi-elliptic intensity orbit, both symmetry-phase return integrals, and every zero-Hamiltonian or double-root boundary without confusing intensity recurrence with recurrence of the full complex state?

The answer proved here is yes.  The finite ledger is a regression certificate, not the proof of the parameter-continuum statement.  The Route-A question is answered separately and negatively at the arithmetic gate.
