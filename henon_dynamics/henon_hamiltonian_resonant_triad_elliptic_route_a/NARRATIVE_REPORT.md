# Narrative report

The decisive progress is not merely another elliptic formula.  The package distinguishes three different recurrence statements that are often conflated: periodicity of one intensity, return of all three intensities, and return of the full complex amplitudes.  Two independent torus phases survive the scalar reduction, so a regular complex orbit closes only when both accumulated phase shifts are rational multiples of `2*pi`.

The zero-Hamiltonian face exposes the distinction sharply.  For unequal Manley--Rowe invariants, the `sn^2` intensity has period `2K/sqrt(N_plus)`, whereas the signed `cn/sn` amplitudes require `4K/sqrt(N_plus)`.  At equal invariants, this family becomes a heteroclinic `sech/sech/tanh` separatrix of infinite period.

This is a substantial source-dynamics theorem but not a Route-A breakthrough: it has no intrinsic arithmetic carrier, isolated prime-indexed orbit dictionary, target determinant, or target analytic structure.
