# Source audit

## Verified source owners

1. J. A. Armstrong, N. Bloembergen, J. Ducuing, and P. S. Pershan, “Interactions between Light Waves in a Nonlinear Dielectric,” *Physical Review* **127** (1962), 1918--1939, DOI [10.1103/PhysRev.127.1918](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.127.1918).  The APS record explicitly owns coupled-amplitude solutions for three interacting waves and the optical Manley--Rowe setting.
2. J. M. Manley and H. E. Rowe, “Some General Properties of Nonlinear Elements---Part I. General Energy Relations,” *Proceedings of the IRE* **44** (1956), 904--913, DOI [10.1109/JRPROC.1956.275145](https://doi.org/10.1109/JRPROC.1956.275145).  This is the primary owner of the named energy relations.
3. D. J. Kaup, A. Reiman, and A. Bers, “Space-time evolution of nonlinear three-wave interactions. I. Interaction in a homogeneous medium,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* **51** (1979), 275--309, DOI [10.1103/RevModPhys.51.275](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.51.275).  This is an authoritative primary treatment of the broader resonant three-wave system.

## Claim boundary

The paper is a source-local reconstruction in one frozen canonical convention.  It makes no priority claim for the three-wave equations, Manley--Rowe invariants, elliptic-function integration, or nonlinear-optics interpretation.  Its implementation ledger, independent checker, adversarial parser tests, and exact separation between intensity and full-state periods are reproducibility artifacts rather than claims about what the cited authors computed.

The sources do not support any assertion about rational primes, target Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target functional equation, target zeros, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator; none is made here.
