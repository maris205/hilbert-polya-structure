# HCS-C48 narrative report

The normalized Hénon Euler product had reached \(\Re s>1/2\), but its generic
moment bound left no room beyond that line.  The second moment turns out not to
be generic: after exact projective stratification, all Tate-sized pieces cancel
and the residue is the Frobenius trace of a smooth genus-four curve.

That square-root cancellation moves the first analytic wall from \(n=2\) to
\(n=3\).  The Euler germ is now holomorphic and nonzero on \(\Re s>1/3\), and
the operator realization upgrades from a fourth-order to a sixth-order
regularized determinant relative to the normalized semifinite trace, with five
transparent counterterms.  This is not a classical Fredholm determinant on
that half-plane: the unregularized \(\tau\)-determinant begins at
\(\Re s>2\), while classical Hilbert trace class begins only at
\(\Re s>3\).  The latter multiplies each normalized logarithmic moment by
\(d_p\) and therefore encodes the ordinary Galois norm rather than its
canonical field-degree-normalized root.

This is a substantial Route-A advance because it links chronological Hénon
time, arithmetic geometry, and operator ideals in one exact theorem.  It is
still not a Riemann-divisor result: the next obstruction is the middle
cohomology of the six-step phase.
