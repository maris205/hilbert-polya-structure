provenance, Route-A status, scope, dynamics, the scattering theorem, exact
polynomial coefficients, total velocity, ballistic intercept, anchors,
`beta` signs, roots, velocities, Vieta sums, ODE residuals, asymptotic

duplicate/anchored/aliased/non-mapping YAML, Route-B, scope, typed-epoch and
non-tuple semantic-field attacks are rejected.  The checker fails closed
under `python -O`.  A semantics-preserving YAML byte mutation is rejected
by the raw-byte digest, while the parsed full-document digest locks every
semantic field.
