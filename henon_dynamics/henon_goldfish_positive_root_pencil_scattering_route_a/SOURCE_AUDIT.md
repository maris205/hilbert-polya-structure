# Source and ownership audit

## Primary ownership

F. Calogero, “The `neatest' many-body problem amenable to exact treatments
(a `goldfish'?),” *Physica D* 152–153 (2001), 78–84,
DOI `10.1016/S0167-2789(01)00160-9`, retains ownership of the standard
goldfish system and its solvable character.

F. Calogero, “Motion of poles and zeros of special solutions of nonlinear
and linear partial differential equations, and related `solvable'
many-body problems,” *Nuovo Cimento B* 43 (1978), 177–241,
handle `11573/11324`, supplies the polynomial-zero dynamics lineage.

M. C. Nucci, “Calogero's `goldfish' is indeed a school of free particles,”
*Journal of Physics A* 37 (2004), 11391–11400,
DOI `10.1088/0305-4470/37/47/008`, supplies free-particle linearization
context.

## Package boundary

This package contributes a self-contained closure of the strictly positive
real chamber: signed-time interlacing, global no-collision, and explicit
first inverse-time scattering coefficients.  It does not claim invention
of the equations, their polynomial-root solution, or priority for the
positive-cone observations.  It does not cover generic complex data,
mixed-sign global evolution, coincident particles, or a quantum theory.

No source supplies or licenses target prime data, Euler factors, root
numbers, automorphy, a target divisor, a target zero match, or Route B.
