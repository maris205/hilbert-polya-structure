- Generate 14 rational initial conditions in dimensions two through seven.
- Reconstruct `P`, `Q`, total velocity, weighted position, and ballistic
  intercept exactly over the rationals.
- Use exact Sturm isolation to certify simple real roots and the required
  intervals at seven signed times for every case.
- Recompute 413 root-time cells, their positive velocities, coefficient
  sum laws, goldfish residuals, 45 fixed anchors and positive `beta`
  coefficients independently.
- Test both time ends at magnitude one million against the first
  inverse-time expansions.
- Run strict JSON/YAML parsing, semantic reconstruction, symbolic checks,
  isolated replay, repaired/stale-hash attacks, optimized-Python fail
  closure, and deterministic three-round PDF builds.

Finite dimensions and times regress the implementation only.  The proof
owns every finite `N>=2` and all real time.
