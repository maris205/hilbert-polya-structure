# Research question

For every finite `N>=2`, let

\[
x_1<\cdots<x_N,\qquad v_i>0,
\]

and evolve the Calogero goldfish system

\[
\ddot z_i=2\sum_{j\ne i}\frac{\dot z_i\dot z_j}{z_i-z_j},
\qquad z_i(0)=x_i,\quad \dot z_i(0)=v_i.
\]

Can the polynomial-root solution be promoted to a global theorem on this
positive cone: exact all-time interlacing, strict no-collision and
monotonicity, followed by a two-ended scattering description with explicit
first inverse-time coefficients for all finite and ballistic roots?

The question is source-local.  It asks neither for priority over the
goldfish model nor for a canonical quantum or arithmetic interpretation.
