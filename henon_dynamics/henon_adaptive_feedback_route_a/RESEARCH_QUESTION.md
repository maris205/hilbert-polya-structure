# Research question — C122

Can an exact adaptive-parameter extension of a Hénon recurrence retain a
polynomial inverse and constant dissipation while creating a verifiable orbit
that disappears when the feedback channel is ablated?

The answer is sought only for the frozen map and named low-period witnesses.
It does not ask for orbit completeness, a transfer operator, analytic
continuation, prime-like target correspondence, target-divisor matching,
arithmetic data, or Route B.
