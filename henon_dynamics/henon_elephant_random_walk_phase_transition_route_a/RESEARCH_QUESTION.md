# Research question

For the one-dimensional elephant random walk, can one state and verify a single boundary-complete theorem covering every memory parameter `p` and initial bias `q`, including exact finite moments, the correct martingale normalization at `p=0`, the threshold `p=3/4`, the superdiffusive limit moments, and the endpoints `p=1`, `q in {0,1}`?

The package answers yes. Its main mathematical progress is the explicit unification of formulas whose naïve normalizations become singular at `p=0` or `p=3/4`. Its evidence question is narrower: do exact dynamic programming, full-history enumeration, and symbolic identities agree on a fixed rational test grid? They do, but this finite agreement is not used as a proof of a central limit theorem.
