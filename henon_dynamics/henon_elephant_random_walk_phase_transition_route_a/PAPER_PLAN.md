# Paper plan

The manuscript is organized around one boundary-complete theorem rather than five incremental notes.

1. Define the signed-copy memory process and derive the position kernel.
2. Solve the first- and second-moment recurrences, isolating `p=3/4`.
3. Prove both martingale normalizations and isolate `p=0`.
4. State the three limit regimes with their exact constants and identify what is imported from the cited martingale limit theory.
5. Give the first four superdiffusive limit moments and split `p=1` and the biased endpoints.
6. Document finite exact evidence, collision boundaries, and the Route-A rejection.

Revision 1 adds the complete asymptotic and endpoint layer. Revision 2 adds the auditable evidence and scope boundary.
