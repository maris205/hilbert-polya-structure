# Source audit

The source lineage was checked against authoritative publisher records and the authors' accessible manuscript.

- G. M. Schütz and S. Trimper, “Elephants can always remember: Exact long-range memory effects in a non-Markovian random walk,” *Physical Review E* 70 (2004), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevE.70.045101`. Used for the original model and finite-moment lineage.
- B. Bercu, “A martingale approach for the elephant random walk,” *Journal of Physics A* 51 (2018), DOI `10.1088/1751-8121/aa95a6`; accessible preprint `arXiv:1707.04130`. Used for the martingale phase theorem, fourth-moment convergence, and the first four superdiffusive limit moments.

No priority claim is made. Exact finite identities are rederived in the paper. The asymptotic statements are attributed rather than inferred from enumeration. Neither source is used to support arithmetic, Euler-factor, root-number, automorphy, divisor, functional-equation, zero, or Hilbert–Pólya claims.
