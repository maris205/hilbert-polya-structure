# HCS-C29 research question brief

## Topic area

Normalized finite-Weil traces, reversible Rauzy path groupoids and
identity-holonomy dynamical zeta functions.

## Primary research question

**Is the dimension-normalized finite-Weil determinant germ of the symmetric
cyclically non-backtracking groupoid generated by the frozen C26 AGY return
matrices nonconstant?**

## Scope boundaries

### In scope

- the frozen seven-state, fourteen-arrow C25 labeled Rauzy graph;
- the frozen C26 branch triple `A,B,C` and the exact bridge matrices `H,K`;
- formal inverse arrows with an explicit no-immediate-reversal rule;
- chronological symplectic holonomy in the fixed integral frame;
- full finite-Weil fibres over odd finite fields;
- the normalized `Log_0` determinant germ on a common small disc;
- gauge invariance, primitive/repetition bookkeeping and exact relation
  certificates.

### Out of scope

- claiming that the symmetric groupoid is the AGY natural extension;
- assigning an AGY roof to inverse arrows without a geometric derivation;
- importing C26 Bergman nuclearity to expanding inverse branches;
- a global Fuglede--Kadison determinant outside the proved germ;
- continuation, functional equation, Riemann divisor, self-adjointness, Route B
  or RH.

### Key assumptions

- the C25 statewise symplectic trivialization and later-on-the-left chronology
  are source locked;
- reverse arrows carry the inverse fixed-frame matrix;
- non-backtracking means that an arrow is never followed by its own formal
  inverse, including cyclic closure;
- the finite-Weil character limit from C28 is used only at fixed path length,
  where the path set is finite.

## Sub-questions

1. Does the genuine two-sided natural extension create any new forward
   identity-holonomy periodic word?
2. Does the symmetric C25/C26 groupoid contain a primitive cyclically reduced
   identity relation that is not a disguised `w w^-1` backtrack?
3. Does such a relation force a nonconstant locally uniform normalized
   finite-Weil determinant limit, with the correct power/repetition law?

## Sub-question bindings

1. Inherits the C26 induced alphabet, positive chronological cocycle and C26
   scalar transfer weights; no deviation.
2. Inherits the frozen C25/C26 matrices but deliberately replaces positive
   dynamics by the declared symmetric non-backtracking groupoid; this is the
   approved object change under the primary question.
3. Inherits odd primes, full finite-Weil fibres, fixed-length limit and common
   small determinant disc; no continuation claim.
