# HCS-C29 preliminary source and novelty boundary

## Local-program audit

- HCS-C15 already treats finite nonabelian voltage covers and Artin--Ihara
  aggregation.  C29 does not claim a new general Artin--Ihara factorization.
- HCS-C18 studies a different modular endpoint action groupoid and proves a
  coboundary obstruction.  Its projector-resolved paths have no primitive
  path law or determinant and do not duplicate the C29 Rauzy relation.
- HCS-C25 proves positive Rauzy path injectivity and monoid freeness.  C29 is
  compatible with it because every new relation necessarily uses inverses.
- HCS-C26 supplies a one-sided scalar nuclear operator.  C29 does not transfer
  that theorem to inverse branches.
- HCS-C27/C28 supply the finite-Weil character and normalized regular-character
  limit.  C29 changes the path language rather than scanning more primes.

## Non-novel claims

- group traces extract identity coefficients;
- finite graph Hashimoto/Ihara determinant identities;
- matrix or unitary twists of non-backtracking adjacency;
- two-sided symbolic extensions and group extensions;
- analytic `Exp(tau(Log))` determinant germs.

## Defensible project-specific delta

1. the exact theorem that the C26 positive regular-group trace-log germ is
   one, together with the semantic result that natural extension preserves
   the positive periodic products defining that germ;
2. two explicit primitive length-six C25 identity-holonomy loops surviving
   cyclic non-backtracking;
3. an exact braid relation producing a primitive length-24 identity word in
   the actual frozen C26 branch alphabet;
4. the locally uniform normalized finite-Weil determinant limit for this
   finite symmetric system;
5. the formal separation between algebraic reopening and the still-missing
   geometric reversible roof/trace theorem.
