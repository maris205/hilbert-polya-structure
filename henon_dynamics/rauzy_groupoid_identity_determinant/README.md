# HCS-C29: Rauzy groupoid identity determinant

## Phase-2 outcome

This project tests the large structural escape left open by HCS-C28: replace
the one-sided positive AGY monoid by a based, reversible path groupoid and ask
whether the normalized finite-Weil trace can see nonempty identity-holonomy
loops.

The original proposal had to be split into two mathematically different
objects.  Phase 2 has now certified both sides of that split.

1. **The genuine two-sided natural extension does not escape C28.** It adds
   past coordinates, not inverse letters to a forward periodic product.  The
   group-von-Neumann regular trace of every positive AGY power is zero, so the
   regular-group/periodic-product analytic determinant germ formed from those
   same products is exactly one.  No new trace-class operator on a two-sided
   extension space is asserted.
2. **A new symmetric non-backtracking Rauzy groupoid does escape the algebraic
   collapse.** Formal inverse arrows are added explicitly and immediate
   reversals are forbidden.  Exact C25 and C26 matrix relations give primitive
   cyclically reduced identity-holonomy loops.  Consequently its
   dimension-normalized finite-Weil determinant germ is nonconstant.

The second object is a deliberate change of dynamics, not a relabeling of the
AGY natural extension.

## Exact structural witnesses

In the frozen C25 seven-state Rauzy graph, the cyclic word

```text
0t  1b  (0t)^-1  0b  3t  (0b)^-1
```

is closed, primitive and cyclically non-backtracking.  The tree-arrow
holonomies are the identity and

```text
g_(1b) = g_(3t)^-1,
```

so the chronological holonomy of the six-edge word is exactly the identity.
A second disjoint six-edge relation supplies another unoriented primitive
cycle.  These two cycles and their reversals give at least 24 based oriented
identity contributions in the sixth non-backtracking moment.

The phenomenon is not confined to the ambient elementary graph.  Let `A` be
the C26 `gamma_star` return matrix, let `B=AHA` and `C=AKA` be the frozen
second and third C26 branch matrices, and put `Y=H^-1 K H`.  Exact rank-one
algebra gives

```text
K Y K = Y K Y.
```

This becomes a primitive cyclically reduced identity relation of length 24
in the symmetric alphabet generated by the actual C26 branches `A,B,C`.
Its 24 cyclic starts and the distinct inverse class give at least 48 marked
identity contributions in the twenty-fourth C26 return-groupoid moment.

## Determinant target

For a finite symmetric edge alphabet and odd prime `p`, let `B_p` be the
non-backtracking edge operator twisted by the full `p^2`-dimensional finite
Weil representation.  On a common small disc define

```text
D_p^norm(u) = exp[p^(-2) Log_0 det(I-u B_p)].
```

At every fixed moment there are only finitely many paths, and HCS-C28 gives

```text
p^(-2) Theta_p(g) -> 1_(g=I).
```

Hence `D_p^norm` converges locally uniformly to a group-trace determinant
germ whose logarithm counts the genuine reduced kernel of the chronological
symplectic cocycle.  The exact relations above prove that this limit is not
one.

## Current boundary

Phase 2 proves an algebraic reopening, not a Hilbert--Pólya construction.
Three large gates remain:

- derive an intrinsic positive roof for the reversible arrows;
- realize forward and inverse dynamics on a stable--unstable or anisotropic
  space with a valid trace theorem;
- determine whether the resulting von-Neumann/edge determinant has useful
  arithmetic analytic structure rather than only a combinatorial relation
  spectrum.

Unit edge length is a legitimate graph clock but is not the AGY roof.  The
inverse of a C26 contracting holomorphic branch is expanding on the C26
Bergman domain, so the C26 nuclearity theorem cannot be imported silently.
