# Research question

Can the three-branch Romik map support one convention-complete theorem that
simultaneously proves the unique parity-oriented primitive-Pythagorean tree
and the full quadratic-irrational periodic atlas, including exact monodromy,
multipliers, fixed counts, primitive counts, source zeta, and the two
parabolic endpoints?

The answer is yes, provided rational terminating states are never identified
with irrational periodic orbits and the two leg orientations remain explicit.
