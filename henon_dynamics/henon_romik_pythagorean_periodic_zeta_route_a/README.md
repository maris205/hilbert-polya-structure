# HCS-C330: Romik Pythagorean periodic zeta

This package separates two exact parts of the Romik system that are often
blurred together.  Parity-normalized rational points terminate and form the
Barning tree of primitive Pythagorean triples.  Irrational points carry the
periodic subsystem: every length-`n` word except the two pure parabolic words
has one quadratic-irrational fixed point, giving `3^n-2`, complete primitive
counts, and source zeta `(1-z)^2/(1-3z)`.

The rational and periodic ledgers are disjoint.  Thus the source has an
intrinsic weak Diophantine relation and a complete A1 orbit layer, but no
rational-prime orbit law or target determinant.  Scope is
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`; Route B is false.
