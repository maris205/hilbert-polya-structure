rejected before assertion-dependent work.

theorem contracts, full word coordinates, triples, matrices, cylinders,
quadratics, classifications, periods, multipliers, period counts, collision
and nonclaim ownership, Route-A branches, scope flags, and YAML hashes after
repairing the evidence payload.  It also attacks duplicate/nonfinite JSON;
duplicate, alias, merge, non-string, timestamp, unknown, missing, and typed
YAML.  The evidence checker owns exact key sets and exact coordinate ledgers,
so a repaired top-level payload digest cannot hide a semantic mutation.
