# Narrative report

The same ternary alphabet describes two different kinds of orbit data.  A
rational Euclid parameter has finite descent: after its parity orientation is
fixed, it reaches one terminal root and records a unique primitive
Pythagorean triple.  An irrational parameter never terminates.  Periodic
irrational itineraries instead correspond to hyperbolic Möbius words, apart
from the two pure parabolic endpoint words.

Keeping these ledgers separate yields the exact fixed count and zeta while
also exposing the Route-A limit.  The arithmetic triples are not periodic;
the periodic points are quadratic irrational.  The theorem therefore makes a
large source-dynamics step without inventing a rational-prime orbit law.
