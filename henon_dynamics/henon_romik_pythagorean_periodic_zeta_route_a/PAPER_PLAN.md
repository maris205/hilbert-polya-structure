# Paper plan

The paper's single contribution is a convention-complete split theorem:
parity-normalized rational states form a terminating Pythagorean tree, while
quadratic irrationals form a separate periodic atlas with exact zeta.

Round 0 freezes the coordinate, orientations, branch endpoints, and unique
rational descent.  Round 1 adds the contraction/cylinder proof, `3^n-2`,
primitive cycles, zeta, and multipliers.  Round 2 adds finite evidence,
literature/collision ownership, and Route-A limits.
