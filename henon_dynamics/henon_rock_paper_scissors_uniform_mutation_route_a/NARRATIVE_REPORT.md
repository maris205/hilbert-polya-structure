# Narrative report

HCS-C235 isolates a useful contrast inside one cyclic population flow.  With
zero mutation and positive cyclic rate, the antisymmetric replicator field
preserves the product \(xyz\); regular positive levels are closed curves surrounding the barycenter,
while the three coordinate edges form the heteroclinic boundary.  Eliminating
the two other coordinates gives a cubic turning equation and an exact period
integral.  The center limit is \(2\pi\sqrt3/a\), whereas the boundary limit
has divergent period.

Uniform additive mutation changes the geometry qualitatively but in a
controlled way: every boundary face points inward, the logarithmic product is
monotone by AM–HM, and LaSalle leaves only the barycenter.  The linearized
tangent pair records the residual cyclic rotation as an imaginary frequency.
The zero-cyclic-rate face is an exactly solvable contraction for positive
mutation, and the simultaneous zero-rate/zero-mutation face is the identity.

The certificate intentionally stops at this source-native theorem.  It does
not attach arithmetic labels to cycles, does not build a target determinant,
and does not invoke Route B.  See `results/TEST_REPORT.md` and the release
manifest for the reproducibility receipt.
