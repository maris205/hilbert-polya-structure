# Research question

For the exactly specified three-strategy cyclic vector field with *additive
uniform* mutation, which portions of the conservative periodic geometry
survive, and which mutation and degenerate faces admit a complete analytic
classification with a reproducible finite certificate?

The answer is deliberately bounded.  We prove the product-level period atlas
for \(\mu=0,a>0\), the inward/LaSalle theorem for \(\mu>0\), the tangent spectrum,
and the exact \(a=0\) and \(a=\mu=0\) faces.  We do not infer results for a
nonuniform mutation matrix, stochastic sampling, a target arithmetic object,
or a Hilbert–Pólya construction.
