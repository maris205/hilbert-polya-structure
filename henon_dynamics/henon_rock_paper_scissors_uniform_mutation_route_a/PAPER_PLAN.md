   conservation and positivity.
2. Set \(\mu=0,a>0\), derive \(H=xyz\), classify regular levels and the three-edge
   heteroclinic boundary, and derive the exact turning-point quadrature.
3. Evaluate the center limit and the small-product boundary limit, keeping the
   quadrature distinct from an orbit zeta.
4. For \(\mu>0\), prove interior entrance, the AM–HM product Lyapunov law,
   LaSalle convergence, and the tangent eigenvalues.
5. Close the \(a=0\), \(a=\mu=0\), and zero-coordinate faces, then report the
   independent ledger, symbolic checks, replay, hostile mutations, and strict
   Route-A boundary.
