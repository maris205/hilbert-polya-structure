# Research question

Can a genuinely nonlinear, strictly contracting Möbius iterated system own a
trace-class holomorphic transfer operator, an exact all-period fixed-point
trace identity, and a primitive Fredholm product while retaining the cyclic
order of its branches?

The required progress over the common-linear affine gate is explicit: equal
digit populations must no longer force equal orbit weights.  The repair must
come from the source dynamics itself, not from an externally attached phase.

Arithmetic Euler factors, root numbers, a target divisor match, automorphy,
and Hilbert--Pólya are outside the active scope.
