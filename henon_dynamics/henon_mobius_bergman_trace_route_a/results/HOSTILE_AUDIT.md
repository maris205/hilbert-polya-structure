# Hostile audit

- The same-count control words are not cyclic rotations; cyclic rotations
  would have the same matrix trace and multiplier and would be invalid.
- Matrix order represents function composition; composition-operator products
  reverse the written word.  The all-word sum is relabelled by the reversal
  bijection, not silently identified term by term.
- Trace class is proved from a convergent rank-one expansion, not inferred
  from compactness or numerical singular values.
- The branch derivative bound proves uniqueness of every word fixed point.
- Odd-length Möbius words reverse real orientation, so their multipliers can
  be negative; no absolute value is inserted into the trace formula.
- The raw primitive product is restricted to `|z|<1/2`.  Entire continuation
  belongs to the Fredholm determinant, not automatically to the raw factors.
- The 2,046 cases are regression sentinels for an analytic all-period proof;
  they do not create its universal quantifier.
- The primitive factors are dynamical and carry no arithmetic interpretation.
- Route B remains disabled and all forbidden scope flags remain false.
