# Narrative report

The prior common-linear affine determinant compressed a word to aggregate
digit data, so changing positions without changing counts could be invisible.
C132 repairs that gate without adding a label or phase.  Möbius composition is
noncommutative: its integer matrix retains cyclic order, while strict branch
separation gives each symbolic word an intrinsic geometric address.

The analytic advance is equally concrete.  Both branches map the unit disk
strictly into itself, and the Bergman basis exposes an absolutely summable
rank-one decomposition.  This makes the unweighted composition sum trace
class and gives an entire Fredholm determinant.  Every word then contributes
a quadratic-algebraic multiplier and an exact fixed-point trace weight; the
formula is valid at all periods, not only at the receipt cutoff.

The exact control pair `33366` and `33636` has identical populations and lies
in different cyclic classes.  Its traces `1344` and `1317` force different
multipliers and operator traces.  This is meaningful Route-A structural
progress, but it remains exploratory: there is no target divisor, functional
equation, arithmetic Euler factor, or natural Hilbert--Pólya operator.
