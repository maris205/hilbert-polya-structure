# Research question

Can one replace pointwise Hénon-style parameter probes by a single explicit
parameter rectangle on which hyperbolicity, symbolic coding, periodic-orbit
weights, a natural trace-class owner, and determinant control all hold
uniformly?

The deliverable is a theorem with exact constants, not a numerical stability
plot.  The target-facing A2/A3 questions are intentionally excluded by the
active scope firewall.
