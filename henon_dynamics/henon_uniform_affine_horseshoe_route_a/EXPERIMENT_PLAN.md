# Experiment plan

1. Freeze the parameter rectangle and exact rational audit grid.
2. Enumerate all binary words through period 8 and verify their exact periodic
   coordinates and itineraries.
3. Compute fixed and primitive counts through period 12.
4. Reconstruct stability traces from both the fixed-point formula and finite
   diagonal-mode sums with certified tails.
5. Check all four rectangle corners, edge midpoints, and the center.
6. Run an independent checker, a SymPy identity check, deterministic replay,
   and mutation tests.
7. Compile the paper twice, inspect warnings and embedded fonts, then close the
   release ledger.

No target spectrum or parameter fitting is permitted in this plan.
