# Hostile audit

- If `lambda` drops below 2, the two domain strips need not be separated.
- If `mu` rises above 1/2, the image strips need not be separated.
- Replacing the stability denominator by `(lambda*mu)^n` destroys the trace
  identity and is rejected.
- Treating `2^n` based words as `2^n/n` primitive cycles is rejected by Möbius
  inversion.
- Altering a displayed periodic coordinate, its source word, or the frozen
  sample parameter is rejected after independent coordinate reconstruction.
- The open zero-free radius `3/2` is uniform; the boundary can contain a zero
  at `(lambda,mu)=(3,anything)` and is not claimed zero-free.
- Internal Fredholm zeros are not compared with any target divisor.
- Route B remains disabled, and all forbidden arithmetic/spectral flags remain
  false.
