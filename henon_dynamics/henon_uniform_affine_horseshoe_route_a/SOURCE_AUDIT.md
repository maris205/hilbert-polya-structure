# Source audit

## Source-owned objects

- The two-strip piecewise-affine map and the rectangle
  `[3,4] x [1/5,1/3]` are defined in this package.
- Periodic points, primitive counts, stability weights, the Hilbert space,
  the trace-class operator, and its Fredholm determinant are derived from
  that map.
- All reported finite certificates are generated by the package producer and
  recomputed by an independent checker.

## External inputs

No external data set, prime table, zero table, fitted parameter, or downloaded
source is used.  Standard facts invoked with proofs or proof sketches are:
Möbius inversion for primitive words, the trace of a tensor product, and the
Fredholm product for a diagonal trace-class operator.

## Evidence boundary

The exact theorem is mathematical and all-period.  The JSON evidence checks a
finite audit grid and periods through 12; it is not presented as the proof of
the theorem.  Novelty against the literature has not been independently
verified.  No external-review score is claimed.
