# Research question

For a finite variable-model Lovasz Local Lemma instance, does the sequential Moser--Tardos resampling dynamics terminate under every legal violated-event selection rule with the sharp witness bounds `E[N_A] <= x_A/(1-x_A)`?

The package answers yes under the asymmetric witness condition.  The proof is reproduced from the independent resampling-table construction through the proper witness-tree probability lemma and the branching-process summation.  Exact binary examples audit conventions but do not replace the general proof.
