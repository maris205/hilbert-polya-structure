# Source audit

Primary ownership is Robin A. Moser and Gabor Tardos, “A constructive proof of the general Lovasz Local Lemma,” *Journal of the ACM* 57(2), Article 11 (2010), DOI `10.1145/1667053.1667060`; the author preprint is `arXiv:0903.0544`.

This package reconstructs the finite-variable sequential theorem and its proof.  It makes no priority claim.  It does not import lopsided, permutation, parallel, entropy-compression, or outside-criterion extensions.

Workspace neighbors are C192's fixed hyperplane chamber walk, C302's Quicksort cost contraction, and C317's deterministic Newton--Schulz iteration.  None owns adaptive shared-variable resampling tables or witness-tree termination.
