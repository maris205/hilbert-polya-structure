# Assumptions

The coupling parameter $\sigma$ is strictly positive; the potential is $-(\sigma^2+1/4)/x^2$. The configuration space is the positive half-line and the Hilbert measure is Lebesgue measure. Units are hbar = 2m = 1. The boundary coefficient ratio kappa has modulus one. Minimal and maximal domains are defined in the proof; there is no imposed boundary condition at infinity.

The classical flow stops at its collision endpoint. The quantum group is defined by the selected self-adjoint realization, not by a classical collision rule. Critical coupling, nonunit boundary phases, ultraviolet regularization, confining terms and target-fitting are excluded.
