# Narrative

The same local Hamiltonian expression admits a circle of self-adjoint quantum domains, yet its classical trajectories hit an excluded endpoint. A boundary phase turns continuous scale covariance into a discrete cycle and produces an infinite geometric ladder extending both to zero and to minus infinity. The continuum survives, scattering is unitary, and the physical quantum group is complete. None of this creates a bounded heat operator, compact resolvent, or a bilateral zeta convergence germ.

The paper develops this single distinction through three substantive drafts: domains and bound states; complete continuum and correctly normalized scattering; then the domain-scale cycle and rigorous global determinant obstruction. No arithmetical conclusion is obtained by changing the clock.
