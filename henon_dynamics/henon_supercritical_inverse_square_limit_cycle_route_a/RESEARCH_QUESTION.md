# Research question

Can the same strictly supercritical inverse-square source be closed at the level of every self-adjoint domain, both infinite spectral components, scattering, and dilation, while identifying exactly why a natural self-adjoint Hamiltonian does not yield an ordinary heat trace or global Fredholm determinant?

The required advance is one complete domain-sensitive source theorem, not five papers splitting eigenvalues, eigenvectors and plotting. The free boundary phase is not chosen from target data. The source has no arithmetic carrier.
