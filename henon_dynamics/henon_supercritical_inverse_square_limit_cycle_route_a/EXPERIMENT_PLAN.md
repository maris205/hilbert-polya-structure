# Executed experiment design

The exact producer uses Fraction arithmetic for 45 classical rows, 27 boundary rows and 15 algebraic reflection rows. The independent checker reconstructs these with SymPy without importing the producer. It recomputes 60 negative levels and 36 continuum rows through independently normalized Bessel coefficients at 110 digits; the producer works at 100 digits and stores 60.

The symbolic/special-function lane proves eight symbolic identities and numerically checks 108 Green boundary-jump cells, 12 Wronskians, 12 Bessel differential equations, 12 small-endpoint boundary matches, 3 normalization integrals and 36 log-period cells. No GPU, learned model, random seed or target spectral data is used.

Replay uses two unrelated working directories. Hostile tests repair content hashes before semantic attacks and separately test JSON serialization, YAML raw/typed closure and release-write refusal. Optimized Python is refused before assertions could be disabled. Smoke tests cover valid YAML, valid evidence and bool-versus-rational rejection.
