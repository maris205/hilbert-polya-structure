# Limitations

This is a reconstruction and audit of classical inverse-square structure, not a worldwide novelty claim. The complete wave-operator existence theorem is an explicitly delimited external input from Dereziński--Richard Proposition 6.10, with every hypothesis verified in the proof. Stone's theorem and endpoint classification are operator-theoretic inputs, not numerical findings.

Only the strictly supercritical self-adjoint family is closed. The critical logarithmic domain requires a different parameterization. Non-self-adjoint exceptional points, finite-radius models, confined spectra and relative or renormalized determinants are not included. Ordinary determinant obstruction does not assert that every possible relative determinant is impossible.

Finite exact and high-precision regressions do not prove completeness or certify special-function values by intervals. Local AI-agent review is not human peer review or independent cross-provider validation.
