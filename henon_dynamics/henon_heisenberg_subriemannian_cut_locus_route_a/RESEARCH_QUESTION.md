# Research question

For the standard real Heisenberg group with

`X=d_x-(y/2)d_z`, `Y=d_y+(x/2)d_z`, `[X,Y]=d_z`,

can one close, in one convention-stable theorem, every unit-speed normal
geodesic, the absence of nonconstant abnormal extremals, the exponential-map
Jacobian, the first conjugate and cut times, the cut locus from the identity,
and the exact Carnot--Caratheodory distance on both regular and degenerate
endpoint faces?

Yes.  The answer is limited to this standard `H^1` metric.  It does not assert
the same atlas for arbitrary Carnot groups.
