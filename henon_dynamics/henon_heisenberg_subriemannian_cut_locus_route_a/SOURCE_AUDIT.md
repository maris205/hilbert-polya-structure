# Source audit

Primary lineage:

- Bernard Gaveau, “Principe de moindre action, propagation de la chaleur et
  estimees sous elliptiques sur certains groupes nilpotents,” *Acta
  Mathematica* **139** (1977), 95--153,
  DOI `10.1007/BF02392235`.

The DOI, author, journal, volume, year, and page range were checked against the
publisher metadata.  The citation establishes classical nilpotent
sub-Riemannian provenance; this package makes no priority or novelty claim.

Every displayed sign and factor is derived internally from the frozen frame.
In particular, the bracket is `+[X,Y]=d_z`, the vertical coordinate contains
`1/(2 lambda^2)`, and the first cut/conjugate time is `2*pi/abs(lambda)`.

Excluded claims: arbitrary Carnot groups, heat-kernel asymptotics, general
contact manifolds, arithmetic local factors, automorphy, root numbers, target
divisors, and Hilbert--Polya identification.
