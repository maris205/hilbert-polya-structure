# Paper plan

The paper is organized around one theorem rather than a sequence of numerical
observations:

1. freeze the standard `H^1` convention and Hamiltonian;
2. integrate the normal flow and exclude abnormal extremals;
3. compute the exponential Jacobian and first conjugate time;
4. solve the planar Dido problem to obtain minimality, the Maxwell merger, and
   the complete identity cut locus;
5. derive the implicit-angle distance formula and both degenerate faces;
6. state exact computational receipts, Route-A rejection, and claim boundaries.

Round 0 already contains the Jacobian, Dido argument, and Route-A boundary.
Round 1 adds the convention/first-zero audit.  Round 2 adds the independent
Maxwell-equals-cut lemma, three-endpoint table, and displayed evidence ledger.
