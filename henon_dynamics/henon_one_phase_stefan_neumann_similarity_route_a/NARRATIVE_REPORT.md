# Narrative report

This paper closes one theorem for a free-boundary heat system rather than
splitting the classical Stefan problem into disconnected calculations. The
Neumann similarity substitution reduces the moving-front PDE to one scalar
root. Its derivative is strictly positive, giving existence and uniqueness
for every Ste>0. The same root controls both endpoint regimes: a five-term
inverse series at small Ste, and a rigorous Lambert-W enclosure (with an
explicit +1 correction) at large Ste.

The wall and interface fluxes are not merely reported numerically. Their
ratio is exactly exp(-lambda^2), and integrating the PDE gives an exact
ledger in which wall input equals sensible heat in the liquid plus latent heat
at the front. The zero-superheat, zero-diffusivity, and zero-latent limits are
kept as singular labels; in particular L=0 requires a separate rescaling and
is not presented as a finite-lambda interface solution.

This is a source-native parabolic/free-boundary result. It has no primitive
periodic-orbit owner, arithmetic clock, target determinant, or Hilbert--Polya
operator. The source heat clock is not target continuation/divisor/counting
law, so the analytic-target gate is A3_FAIL and Route A is rejected.
