# Research question

Can one close the classical one-phase Stefan free-boundary problem in a
single, auditable similarity theorem that remains informative at both Stefan
endpoints? For the normalized problem the answer is yes: one strictly
increasing scalar function gives a unique front parameter; its inverse has a
controlled small-Stefan series and a large-Stefan Lambert-W enclosure; and
the same parameter yields an exact flux and sensible-plus-latent energy ledger.

The result is intentionally source-local. It does not assert global
uniqueness for arbitrary non-similar data, and its singular limits are not
silently promoted to finite-interface solutions. It is not an arithmetic
determinant or a target-spectrum construction.
