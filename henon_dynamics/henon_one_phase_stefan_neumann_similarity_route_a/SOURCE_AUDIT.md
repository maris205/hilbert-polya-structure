# Source audit

Bibliographic fields are locked to publisher or journal records. These sources
establish the classical Stefan setting and asymptotic context; the
all-endpoint organization and deterministic ledger are source-local to this
package, with no priority claim.

- J. A. Addison, S. D. Howison, and J. R. King, Ray methods for Free Boundary
  Problems (2005), Oxford author PDF
  [smallstefan.pdf](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/howison/papers/smallstefan.pdf).
  Section 2 formulates the small-latent-heat Stefan limit and its free-boundary
  asymptotics.
- S. C. Gupta, *The Classical Stefan Problem: Basic Concepts, Modelling and
  Analysis*, Elsevier, North-Holland Series in Applied Mathematics and
  Mechanics, Volume 45 (2003), ISBN 978-0-444-51086-0. The publisher record
  identifies Gupta as the single author and lists the one-dimensional Neumann
  solution in the contents: [Elsevier book record](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-classical-stefan-problem/gupta/978-0-444-51086-0).
- L. I. Rubinstein, “Global Stability of the Neumann Solution of the Two-phase
  Stefan Problem,” *IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics* **28**(3), 287--299
  (1982), DOI [10.1093/imamat/28.3.287](https://doi.org/10.1093/imamat/28.3.287).
  The Oxford record labels this as a two-phase stability paper; it is used
  only for general background, not as a one-phase priority assertion.

The normalized equation sets the dimensional thermal diffusivity kappa and
wall superheat scale to one. The small-latent-heat analysis of Addison,
Howison, and King is the cited rescaling context; the zero-latent limit
(L=0, Ste=infinity) changes the scaling and is not a finite-lambda
continuation. Zero diffusivity
(dimensional kappa=0) collapses the similarity length, and zero superheat
makes the normalization singular. No source is used to infer a prime/zero
correspondence, functional equation, or Hilbert--Polya operator. In particular,
the source heat clock is not target continuation/divisor/counting law; the
erf/Lambert-W formulas are not an A3 analytic-target match.
