- **Conditional independence.**  The proof conditions on the unique first
  edge.  Only then are the relative orders on the two residual path edge sets
  asserted independent and uniform; their global interleaving is irrelevant.
- **All factorial moments.**  The `H_r` ODE includes both derivatives of
  `F^2` and the `r` derivatives falling on the factor `z`.  The checker uses the
  corresponding general falling-factorial identity, not just the first two
  rows.
- **Variance.**  SymPy verifies the displayed `H_2` ODE and Laurent
  coefficients.  Combining `H_2+H_1` with the squared coefficient sequence of
  `H_1` yields `e^{-4}n+2e^{-4}+o(1)`; it is not inferred from a decimal fit.
- **Support.**  Binary gap words prove both necessity and sufficiency.  Putting
  a chosen maximal matching first in the order proves positive RSA
  probability.  “Maximal” is never silently replaced by “maximum.”
- **Cycle boundary.**  The first accepted cycle edge leaves exactly `P_{n-2}`;
  hence the identity is `G_n=zF_{n-2}`, including `C_3,C_4`, and the same-size
  mean correction tends to `e^{-2}`.
