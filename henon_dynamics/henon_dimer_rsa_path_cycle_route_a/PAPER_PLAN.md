# Paper plan

## Round 0 — original mathematical core

Define the uniform edge-order process and the maximal/maximum distinction.
Prove the path first-edge PGF convolution, derive the Riccati OGF, solve the
mean equation, and state the cycle first-edge identity.  Include classical
ownership and a narrow claim boundary.

## Round 1 — substantive theorem closure

Add the all-order factorial-moment triangular ODE, explicit `H_2`, Laurent
singularity extraction, the variance coefficient and constant term, binary-gap
proofs of exact path/cycle support, and all small-domain/tie boundaries.

## Round 2 — audit and Route-A closure

Add the independent bitmask/order oracle, exact evidence scales, strict schema
and mutation design, source/collision audit, limitations, Route-A failure
table, reproducibility statement, and complete research declarations.  Make
explicit that finite computation is not the all-size proof and that the
classical theorem is reconstructed rather than claimed as new literature.

The final structure is: abstract; model and ownership; main theorem; first-edge
proof; factorial hierarchy and asymptotics; support and cycle boundary effect;
executable evidence; limitations and Route-A assessment; declarations;
references.
