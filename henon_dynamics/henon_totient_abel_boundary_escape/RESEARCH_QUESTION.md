# Research question

HCS-P51 proves that the source-native period-four packet series

\[
\sum_{n\ge3}u^nD_n
\]

has radius exactly one in the weighted tagged divisor space, and that direct
substitution at \(u=1\) diverges.  HCS-P52 asks the next non-micro question:

> Does this exact Hénon packet possess a canonical Abel boundary after
> renormalization, and if so, in which topology does the boundary live?

The frozen source is the primitive period-four orbit of
\(H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q)\), with positive multiplier

\[
L=289+24\sqrt{145}
\]

and inversion-fixed packet

\[
\beta_n=L^{-\varphi(n)/2}\Phi_n(L)\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\qquad
\|D_n\|=\log\beta_n.
\]

The project separates three possible outcomes:

1. a scalar Abel law for the source-native mass functional;
2. a nontrivial scaled-index boundary profile;
3. convergence or nonconvergence in the original tagged Banach topology.

No all-orbit interchange, Riemann-prime identification, determinant or
operator is assumed.
