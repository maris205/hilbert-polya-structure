# Narrative report

P51 reached the unit Abel circle but proved that direct substitution fails.
P52 identifies the first genuine boundary law.  On the exact period-four
orbit the inversion-fixed cyclotomic packet has logarithmic mass

\[
\log\beta_n=\frac12\varphi(n)\log L+O_L(1).
\]

The average order of Euler's totient function then forces a second-order Abel
pole.  After the canonical normalization, the scalar packet mass converges to
\(3\log L/\pi^2\).  This is an analytic theorem, not a finite fit.

The positive result has a precise topological boundary.  The normalized
tagged divisor vectors do not converge in the original weighted \(\ell^1\)
space: every fixed tag disappears while the total mass stays positive.
Blowing up the grading by \(x=\tau n\) resolves the escape and yields the
universal Gamma\((2,1)\) density \(xe^{-x}\,dx\).

The paper therefore advances A3 at one orbit while refusing the tempting
but false claim that scalar Abel convergence supplies a vector-valued
determinant.  The next project should test pressure-uniform all-orbit
interchange, where the orbit period and cyclotomic index diverge together.
