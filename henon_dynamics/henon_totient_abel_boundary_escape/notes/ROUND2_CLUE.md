# ROUND2 clue

The one-orbit boundary has a universal shape because its mass is asymptotic
to a constant times \(\varphi(n)\).  The natural next question is whether the
P51 pressure sum permits the limit

\[
\lim_{\tau\downarrow0}\tau^2
\sum_{\gamma}e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma}
\sum_{n\ge3}e^{-\tau n}\|D_{\gamma,n}\|.
\]

If every orbit has a leading coefficient determined by the logarithmic
Mahler height of its multiplier, pressure summation may produce either:

1. a finite weighted mixture with the same Gamma\((2,1)\) index profile; or
2. a second critical exponent where orbit growth prevents interchange.

This is an all-orbit pressure/Tauberian theorem, not another finite packet
factorization.  It should be the first candidate for HCS-P53.
