## Per-paper gates

### G0 -- frozen owner and convention

- State space, parameter chamber, source clock and normalization are explicit.
- C274 freezes canonical rather than kinetic momenta, field/potential signs,
  positive square-root conventions and the physical stability wall.
- C275 freezes the normalized eccentricities `0<f<e<1`, elliptic-caustic
  sector, oriented rotation number and elliptic-integral conventions.
- C276 freezes labeled maps, a uniform marked vertex, tail length before cycle
  entry, positive cycle length and first-repeat indexing.
- C277 freezes the Caputo derivative, Dirichlet sine normalization, spectral
  Sobolev scale and separates `0<beta<1` from `beta=1`.
- C278 freezes ordered peaks before collision and calls the `alpha` rule a
  declared finite-dimensional extension, not general PDE uniqueness.
- The nearest C1--C273 owners and exact differences are recorded.

### G1 -- theorem-scale closure

- C274 may not stop at normal-mode frequencies; the critical/unstable atlas,
  symplectic formula, Krein/action signs, resonances and boundary faces must
  close.
- C275 may not infer a full elliptical-billiard theorem from a circular limit;
  the elliptic-integral conjugacy, monotonicity, endpoints and Poncelet
  minimal-period statement must be proved in the stated sector.
- C276 may not report only expected components; the complete global count,
  marked joint law, collision identity and scaling limit belong to one proof.
- C277 must prove both endpoints and failure outside them, not merely an upper
  regularization estimate or one-way Schatten inclusion.
- C278 must derive the collision asymptotics and delimit the continuation;
  neither formal Lax integrability nor arbitrary weak-solution uniqueness is
  an acceptable substitute.
