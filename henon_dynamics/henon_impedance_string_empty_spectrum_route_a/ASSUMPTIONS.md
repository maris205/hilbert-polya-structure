# Frozen assumptions

Positive length L and speed c; finite real impedance eta nonnegative.
Homogeneous scalar wave equation on (0,L), fixed left displacement and right
condition u_x+(eta/c)u_t=0. Complex energy space H1 with left trace zero
times L2. Generator domain: H2 displacement, H1 velocity, both left traces
zero and the right impedance condition. The energy norm has no added
displacement L2 term and physical energy is one half of its square.

Physical time is unchanged. tau=2L/c and signed q=(eta-1)/(eta+1).
At q=0, q^0 means one in the transport formula. The theorem separately
handles transparent eta=1 and conservative eta=0. Infinite impedance, variable
coefficients, internal damping and target-dependent clocks are excluded.
