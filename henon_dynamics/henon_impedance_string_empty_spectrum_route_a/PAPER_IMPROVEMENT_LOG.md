## Round one

Substantive addition: exact transparent norm via the lowest separated
Sturm–Liouville eigenvalue; trigonometric, critical and hyperbolic branches;
positive-eigenfunction lowest-root selection; strict monotonicity and all
epsilon half-planes. Added Rayleigh/action/gauge evidence and explicit warning
that these half-planes are not asserted for nonzero reflection.
Review focus: the scalar root equation alone does not identify the lowest
singular value. The positive-eigenfunction proof supplies the missing choice.

## Round two

Substantive addition: complete half-integer singular sequence, HS but nontrace
for every spectral parameter, factorial Volterra powers, quasinilpotence,
explicit canonical-product det2, semigroup noncompactness before extinction,
reciprocal-impedance and physical-reversal controls.
Review focus: compactness does not give trace class, and a spectral
characteristic does not automatically give an ordinary Fredholm determinant.
The proof now separates these categories and excludes no unspecified
regularization. The strict Route-A verdict remains rejected.
