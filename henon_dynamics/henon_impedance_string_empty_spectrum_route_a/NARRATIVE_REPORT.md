# C396 narrative

The source is an infinite-dimensional autonomous wave system, not a selected
finite matrix. A perfectly absorbing endpoint empties the generator spectrum
but leaves a norm-one transient lasting one full round trip. This separates
spectral location, time evolution and resolvent norm inside one exact model.

The package progression is domain/clock/full spectrum, then the transparent
Sturm--Liouville resolvent norm, then operator-ideal and determinant boundaries.
The theorem covers every positive length and speed and every finite nonnegative
impedance. The finite ledger checks constants, signs and branch choices; it
does not prove completeness by sampling.

Classical ownership belongs to Driscoll and Trefethen's 1996 absorbing-wave
study. Package-level progress consists of a self-contained closed theorem and
independent, typed, adversarially tested reconstruction. No new-literature
priority, target arithmetic, target divisor, root number or Route B is claimed.

The two impedances one half and two have the same decay norm but opposite
signed round-trip coefficients and shifted spectral ladders. At impedance one
the Volterra resolvent is entire and Hilbert--Schmidt, not trace class. Its
explicit order-two regularized determinant is one; that fact does not supply
an ordinary Fredholm determinant or a target spectral function.
