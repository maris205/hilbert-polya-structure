# Research question

Can a natural infinite-dimensional wave generator simultaneously have empty
spectrum, a norm-one transient and a precise finite extinction time, and can
its resolvent and determinant categories be closed without changing the clock?

Answer: yes for transparent finite impedance. Exact unfolding, a complete
boundary-value inverse and a lowest-eigenfunction calculation close the source
theorem. The result does not supply an arithmetic orbit owner or target divisor.
This is one complete source paper, not a subcase of the previous singular
Schrödinger packages.
