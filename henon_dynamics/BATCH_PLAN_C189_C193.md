## Uniform paper contract

Each candidate releases 28 physical files: 27 content-addressed payloads plus
one self-excluded manifest.  The payload consists of eight theorem/writing
documents, seven executable code/manifest files, one evaluator YAML, seven
paper artifacts including three content-distinct revision PDFs and the final
PDF, and four result/evidence artifacts.

Every package must pass a deterministic producer, a producer-independent
checker, a separate symbolic reconstruction, canonical byte replay, semantic
repaired-hash mutations and a stale-hash mutation.  Every final PDF must be
rebuilt twice from `main.tex` alone at a fixed epoch, reproduce byte for byte,
embed all fonts, have no build warning or bad box, preserve extractable text,
and survive inspection of every rendered page.  Internal hostile reviews are
not external peer review.
