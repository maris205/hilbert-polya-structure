# C129 exact experiment plan

## Goal

Certify an all-order phase-twisted orbit/operator bridge and both sides of its
interpretation boundary using exact arithmetic only.

## Gates

1. **Source lock:** freeze `A,t,B,c`, the bidisc, the determinant convention,
   and the primitive fifth-root character.
2. **Geometry:** retain the rational strict-interior and strong-separation
   inequalities from the same frozen affine maps.
3. **Orbit owner:** enumerate rooted closed words and primitive necklaces
   through period eight, while proving the coding theorem for all periods.
4. **Twisted owner:** prove the phase-weighted Hardy operator trace class by
   compact-interior factorization and a summable total-degree majorant.
5. **All-order identity:** derive the exact trace, Fredholm lattice product,
   and primitive repetition formula.
6. **Exact finite receipt:** compute traces and Fredholm coefficients through
   order eight in `Q[Z/5]`, with both cyclotomic and trivial-character views.
7. **Finite sections:** independently build polynomial operator matrices
   through total degree three and compare their power traces.
8. **Controls:** permute the same translation centers across branches; require
   twisted separation and exact untwisted coincidence at every order.
9. **Falsification:** repair the evidence hash after each of 35 registered
   hostile mutations and require independent rejection, including exact
   operator, trace-formula, control, progress, and closed-schema cases.
10. **Release:** byte replay, fixed-epoch double build, embedded fonts, rendered
    page inspection, and a 27-payload content manifest.

## Success and failure

Success is the strict tuple
`(A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`. It requires explicit progress over
C124 and explicit denial of complete geometry recovery. A target-divisor
claim, natural-quantization claim, stale-hash-only mutation test, or compiler
warning blocks release.
