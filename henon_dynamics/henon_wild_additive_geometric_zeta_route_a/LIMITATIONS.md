# Limitations

Bridy's positive-characteristic additive-polynomial transcendence results are direct prior art. This package claims a source-complete exposition and reproducible audit, not new literature priority.

The all-extension formula has a finite-dimensional linear-algebra realization, already available abstractly in repository C204. The new owner is the infinite geometric union and its wild-multiplicity analytic boundary, not another fixed finite matrix.

Prime characteristic supplies arithmetic structure but not rational-prime owners of primitive cycles. The six mandatory A1 controls are not completed; target A2 and A3 fail, and no natural quantum lift is supplied. The evidence grid cannot validate the infinite proof by itself.
