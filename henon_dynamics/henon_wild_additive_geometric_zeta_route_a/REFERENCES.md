# Verified primary references

1. Andrew Bridy, Transcendence of the Artin–Mazur zeta function for polynomial maps of A1(Fbar_p), Acta Arithmetica 156 (2012), 293–300. Author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.0362 ; original text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.0362 . Theorem 2 treats x^(p^m)+a x for odd p and nonzero a in F_(p^m).
2. Andrew Bridy, The Artin–Mazur zeta function of a dynamically affine rational map in positive characteristic, Journal de théorie des nombres de Bordeaux 28 (2016), 301–324. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.941 ; publisher full text: https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.941.pdf . Theorem 1.3 covers the additive-polynomial transcendence/rationality distinction in positive characteristic.
