# Assumptions

The characteristic p is prime, the ground field is its algebraic closure, the phase space is affine rather than projective, and the source map is exactly x+x^p. All coefficients used by the linearized Euclidean argument are in F_p. Both n and r are positive integers; neither is assumed prime to p.

Geometric counts do not include nilpotent multiplicities. The complex normalization w=pz is a proof device, not a fitted clock. Local logarithms are fixed by value zero at the origin. A claimed boundary continuation means a meromorphic germ agreeing with the source on an inner overlap.

No normal-basis theorem, finite-grid completeness assumption, target zero table or unproved number-theoretic conjecture enters the proof.
