# Research question

Can the rational determinant obtained by counting the full lengths of fixed schemes represent the actual geometric primitive-orbit zeta of f_p=x+x^p?

Answer: no in the same variable. The exact inseparable multiplicity removes p^(p^vp(n)) from the degree count, and the resulting geometric zeta has a meromorphic natural boundary. The positive part of the result is a complete two-clock census and explicit analytic tail, not only a negative verdict.
