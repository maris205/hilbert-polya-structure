# Narrative report

The mathematical finding is a precise mismatch between two plausible trace ledgers. The degree p^n counts nilpotent fixed-scheme thickness. The autonomous map on geometric points instead has p^(n-p^vp(n)) fixed points, so its repetitions remember the p-adic divisibility of time.

The full extension gcd gives an arithmetic label without confusing residue degree with dynamical period. The valuation correction has an explicit Lambert expansion. Boundary residues of the zeta's logarithmic derivative are noninteger, which excludes a meromorphic local zeta germ and then gives the full natural boundary.

The package therefore preserves a large positive source theorem while refusing an invalid determinant promotion. It does not reinterpret the obstruction as an RH proof or claim a new phenomenon absent from Bridy's work.
