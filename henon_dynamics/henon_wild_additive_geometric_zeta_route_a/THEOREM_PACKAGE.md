# Theorem package

All statements below are PROVED for every prime p and positive integers n,r.

1. With e=p^vp(n), the fixed-scheme length is p^n, common point multiplicity p^e and geometric count p^(n-e).
2. The extension count is p raised to the degree of gcd((1+T)^n-1,T^r-1), with repeated factors retained.
3. Ordinary and double Möbius inversions give primitive cycles and exact dynamical-period/residue-degree points.
4. For n=em,m>1, the least splitting degree is e times the order of T modulo H_m=((1+T)^m-1)/T; when m=1 it is one.
5. The geometric zeta has an absolutely convergent primitive product inside |z|<1/p and a Lambert logarithmic derivative with a uniform interior tail.
6. At each primitive p-power boundary root the logarithmic-derivative residue lies in (0,1); this forbids meromorphic continuation of the zeta itself and its reciprocal across any boundary point.
7. Scheme-length counting gives 1/(1-pz), which is a different ledger and cannot repair the original geometric object.

The complete proofs and all edge cases are in proof/ANALYTIC_PROOF.md. No finite experiment is used to prove an infinite statement.
