# Research narrative

## Central result

The candidate HCS-C14 is an autonomous full-shift skew product on the dyadic
two-solenoid.  Its fibre maps are generated by

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}3&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both determinants equal the unit (8\in\mathbb Z[1/2]\), so their dual maps
are compact-group automorphisms.  Later symbols multiply on the left.  This
preserves the actual chronology of a switching system rather than replacing it
by one averaged matrix.

For every based word (w\) of length (n\), the return is expanding and

\[
\#\operatorname{Fix}(\alpha_{M_w})
=\operatorname{oddpart}\!\left(8^n-\operatorname{tr}M_w+1\right).
\]

Modulo two, the words with a nontrivial local correction are exactly the
cyclic words with no occurrence of `aa`; there are (L_n\) of them.  This
symbolic gate supplies the exact correction scale (8\varphi\).

The strongest orbit-resolved result occurs at primitive period five.  The
words `aabbb` and `ababb` have the same two `a` symbols and three `b` symbols,
but their return traces are (2734\) and (2727\).  Their fixed counts are
(30035\) and (15021\).  The first return zeta is rational; the second has a
natural boundary after the Bell--Miles--Ward theorem is applied to that single
compact-group automorphism.  Thus chronology changes analytic type even when
abelianized symbol data agree.

Global summation produces the complementary conclusion.  The archimedean
comparison is

\[
Z_\infty(z)=\frac{1-13z+33z^2}{(1-2z)(1-16z)}.
\]

If (\Delta_n\) is the dyadic correction, then

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\Delta_n^{1/n}=8\varphi.
\]

Consequently the exact solenoid zeta continues meromorphically to

\[
|z|<(8\varphi)^{-1},
\]

where it has one simple pole at (1/16\) and no zeros.  Therefore local
natural-boundary factors do not force a natural boundary at the full zeta's
first convergence circle.

## Relation to the source H\'enon program

The source paper proposes the area-preserving H\'enon family as a conservative
replacement for a dissipative logistic map and constructs numerical quantum
and Fokker--Planck spectra.  HCS-C14 keeps only the structural demand for an
invertible measure-preserving system.  It abandons numerical zero fitting and
smooth planar dynamics in exchange for exact intrinsic periodic groups,
chronological symbolic dynamics, and provable zeta statements.  The result is
not a refinement of the source paper's spectral fit; it is an independent test
of whether conservativity plus noncommutative chronology produces arithmetic
zeta structure.

## Interpretation

The arithmetic-dynamics result is positive: a small exact system supports a
cyclic symbolic valuation gate, a congruence transfer tower, and an
orbit-by-orbit analytic-type bifurcation.  The Hilbert--P\'olya result is
negative.  The dominant scales are (16\), (8\varphi\), and (8\); there is no
prime-period law, Riemann divisor, functional equation, or discrete
self-adjoint realization.  The Route-A ruling is therefore rejection for HP
and retention as a structural paper.
