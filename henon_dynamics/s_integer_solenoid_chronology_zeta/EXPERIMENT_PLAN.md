## Mandatory controls

1. **Uniform-sign divided-unipotent control.**  It retains word chronology but
   collapses to ((1-6z)/(1-8z)).
2. **Archimedean torus control.**  It uses the same ordered words before the
   (2)-adic odd-part operation and has an exact rational zeta.
3. **Abelianization control.**  The period-five witness has the same letter
   incidence but different trace, fixed count, and analytic type.
4. **Repetition control.**  Use (W(w,r)=\#\operatorname{Fix}(M_w^r)), never
   (W(w,1)^r).
5. **Independent implementation.**  Recompute counts with nested matrices and
   repetitions with the characteristic trace recurrence.
6. **Finite-prefix recurrence screen.**  Label it numerical evidence only;
   no theorem may depend on a Padé or Berlekamp--Massey prefix.

## Stopping rule

Stop the HP lane after the analytic structure is identified.  A lattice pole
generated by (2\times8), no prime clock, no xi divisor, and no natural
self-adjoint operator is a Route-A failure even when the arithmetic-dynamics
theorems are publishable.  The next system-level fallback is a nonabelian
congruence voltage-graph/Ihara tower with its trivial sector removed before
evaluation.
