# Breadth-first idea report: HCS-C14

Date: 2026-08-06

## Search decision

The previous Fibonacci short-clock lane ended in exact degree and zero-radius
obstructions.  The next search therefore changed system class rather than
tuning its parameters.  The selected question was whether ordered
noncommuting (S)-integer automorphisms could simultaneously provide:

1. intrinsic primitive periodic orbits;
2. nonarchimedean arithmetic weights;
3. a genuine chronological skew product; and
4. an exact dynamical zeta or a decisive obstruction.

No prior hypothesis about Hénon spectra or Riemann zeros was retained.

## Refinement history

### Candidate C14-0: chronology without aggregate survival

With (M_a=U/2,M_b=U^{\mathsf T}/2), individual primitive words of the
same Parikh vector have different fixed counts.  Nevertheless the exact
chronological sum is

\[
N_n=8^n-6^n,
\qquad Z(z)=\frac{1-6z}{1-8z}.
\]

This killed the idea that noncommutativity alone is an arithmetic zeta
mechanism.

### Candidate C14-1: genuine neutral-place variation

Integral cat generators on (widehat{\mathbb Z[1/2]^2}) activate wordwise
(2)-adic valuations and inherit natural-boundary faces.  This passed the
arithmetic gate, but the strongest easily proved multivariable no-go came by
specializing to a known one-generator face.  That was rigorous but not a
sufficient theorem delta.

### Candidate C14-2: analytic-type chronology

The selected determinant-(8) pair has a sharper separation.  Its global
first zeta circle is rationally controlled and admits a zero-free continuation
annulus, while two same-Parikh primitive base cycles yield respectively a
rational and a natural-boundary fibre zeta.  This turns chronology into an
analytic classification, not just an orbit-count discrepancy.

## Novelty status

`YELLOW / SCOPED`.

Established ingredients include solenoid fixed-point product formulas,
natural-boundary dichotomies for single compact-group automorphisms,
full-shift skew products, and golden-mean symbolic counting.  The project does
not claim these separately as new.  The scoped theorem delta is their exact
combination in one noncommuting chronological cocycle:

- mod-(2) language iff (2)-adic correction;
- same-Parikh primitive rational/natural-boundary split;
- exact global rational comparison and continuation past the first circle;
- chronological higher-congruence transfer tower.

No exact collision for this package was found in the directed primary-source
audit.  This is not a claim of exhaustive bibliographic novelty.

## Final decision

Preserve HCS-C14 as a mathematical negative/structural paper.  Do not compare
finite zeta roots with Riemann zeros.  The first pole (1/16) and secondary
scale ((8\varphi)^{-1}) are generated by transparent branching and symbolic
entropy, so Route B remains closed.
