# Refinement iteration 2: activate a neutral finite place

Integral hyperbolic (SL_2(\mathbb Z)) generators on the dyadic solenoid make

\[
\#\operatorname{Fix}(M_w)=
\operatorname{oddpart}(\operatorname{tr}M_w-2)
\]

genuinely chronology-sensitive.  A symmetric cat pair produced an unbounded
same-Parikh valuation gap and inherited the Bell--Miles--Ward natural boundary
on each one-generator face.

The finite-dimensional rational-pencil obstruction obtained by specializing a
bivariate zeta to one face was correct but hereditary: it did not require
noncommutativity.  The analytic behavior of the unweighted diagonal zeta also
remained open.

Decision: retain as an arithmetic control, but search for a pair where
chronology changes the analytic type of two mixed primitive returns.
