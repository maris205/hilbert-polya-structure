# Refinement iteration 1: kill noncommutativity alone

The first object used (U/2) and (U^{\mathsf T}/2) over
(mathbb Z[1/2]).  It passed an orbit-resolved chronology test: primitive
period-five words with identical incidence had fixed counts (769) and
(705).  It failed the aggregate gate exactly:

\[
N_n=8^n-6^n,
\qquad Z(z)=\frac{1-6z}{1-8z}.
\]

Decision: keep as a negative control.  Noncommuting word products are not by
themselves a useful zeta mechanism.
