# Limitations and stopping conditions

The strongest limitation is proved, not guessed: Fix(T) contains the entire
half-open diagonal segment. Every fixed-set point count is uncountable, so the
ordinary global Artin–Mazur germ is undefined. One fixed-scale finite-cycle
zeta is a distinct restricted object. Regularized traces on other spaces are
not ruled out, but none is constructed here.

The Koopman operator on probability L2 is unitary and noncompact; it is not
trace class. The source has no intrinsic rational-prime/log-prime carrier.
The A0/A1 grades remain weak and A2/A3 fail. A4 is formal only.

All main mechanisms are classical, with heavy Athreya–Cheung ownership.
Textual local dedup and reconstruction do not certify literature newness.
Finite checks cover N<=64, not the theorem's universal quantifiers.
