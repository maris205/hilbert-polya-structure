# Narrative report

One source, two clocks, and a continuous-family obstruction form the paper's
single technical story. Native coprime arithmetic completely solves the
periodic source ledger, but it also exposes why that ledger is not a discrete
prime carrier. The physical roof is forced by the lattice return; it is not
fitted to number-theoretic labels. Its exact cycle sum and its mean agree only
through an asymptotic period-ratio limit.

The boundary audit makes a material correction to naive phrasing: the exact
unipotent branch product is not a smooth derivative at included floor walls.
This distinction is necessary even before asking for an isolated-orbit trace.
The final source theorem retains all continuous periodic families and stops
at the undefined global cardinality germ. It does not hide them in a cutoff.
