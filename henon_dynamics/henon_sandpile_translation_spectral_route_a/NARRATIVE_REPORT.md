# C176 narrative report

This paper introduces a new project subtype: avalanche stabilization on a graph rather than a cellular automaton, toral map, or pre-existing finite permutation. Its core progress is the model-specific bridge. Recurrent stable height vectors are not replaced by an arbitrary finite abelian group; the burning/representative theorem identifies each reduced-Laplacian class with one physical recurrent configuration, and actual addition followed by toppling becomes translation by the chip-vector class.

Two formulas make that bridge computationally exact. Smith normal form resolves the order coordinate by coordinate, while the adjugate formula reduces it to one determinant and a joint gcd. Once the order `L` is fixed, every recurrent orbit has exactly that length. Fixed counts, primitive cycles, zeta, determinant and character multiplicities then follow without fitting. Group inversion supplies a source-native reversor, and the uniform finite Koopman operator is self-adjoint precisely at order one or two.

The most important boundary is equally explicit. Addition--stabilization on all stable height vectors need not be injective. The two-state path example collapses both stable states to the single recurrent state. The positive operator theorem therefore belongs to the recurrent phase space and cannot be laundered into a claim about the full stable space.

路线结论：复现临界群平移具有完整本原周期和自然酉 Koopman 算子，因此分别获得 `A1_WEAK` 与 `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`；但没有素数来源、目标除子或全局解析桥，整体仍为 `ROUTE_A_REJECTED`。本文不声明外部新颖性、优先权或外部同行评审。
