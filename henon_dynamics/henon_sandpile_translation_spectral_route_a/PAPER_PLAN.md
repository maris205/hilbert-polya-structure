# C176 paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

We prove that addition--stabilization on recurrent configurations of every finite connected undirected sink multigraph is an explicitly ordered critical-group translation, yielding uniform cycles, exact zeta/determinant/character spectrum, natural reversal, and a sharp boundary against the full stable-state map.

## Claim spine

1. Freeze graph, sink, stability, toppling, recurrent set and clock.
2. Prove the burning and unique recurrent representative bridge.
3. Derive Smith and adjugate order formulas.
4. Classify every recurrent orbit and fixed count.
5. Derive zeta, determinant and character spectrum.
6. Prove group-inversion reversal and self-adjointness iff `L<=2`.
7. Separate the recurrent theorem from all stable configurations with an exact path counterexample.
8. Apply the conservative A0--A4 decision and scope firewall.

## Figure/table decision
