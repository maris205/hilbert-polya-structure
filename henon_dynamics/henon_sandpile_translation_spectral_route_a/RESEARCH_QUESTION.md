# C176 research question

For an arbitrary finite connected undirected loopless multigraph with a sink, what exact orbit, fixed-count, zeta, determinant, spectrum, reversal, and self-adjointness laws follow when addition--stabilization is restricted to recurrent stable configurations?

The mathematical question has two necessary bridges. First, recurrent stable configurations must be identified class-by-class with the reduced-Laplacian critical group, rather than replaced by an abstract finite group. Second, the order of a concrete nonnegative chip vector must be computable from both Smith normal form and the adjugate of the reduced Laplacian.

The hostile Route-A question is whether this exact source translation acquires prime semantics merely because its zeta and character spectrum are closed. It does not. Intrinsic primitive cycles support `A1_WEAK`, while the recurrent permutation and inversion reversal support `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`; A0, target-divisor matching, and target global structure all fail.

Success means an all-graph source theorem with the all-stable-state boundary exposed, not a target match or external novelty claim.
