# C88 research question

For a uniformly random ordering of the sixteen frozen C75 labels, let `A_k`
be the first `k` labels and let `Phi(A_k)` be their generated subgroup.  For
each of the twenty actual subgroups `H <= Z/9 + Z/3 + Z/2`, determine the
complete exact law

```text
T_H = min{k : H <= Phi(A_k)}.
```

Can all permutation counts, reduced probabilities, survival probabilities,
expectations, and attainable time ranges be certified exactly?  Does actual
subgroup inclusion recover the pointwise and stochastic order of these
twenty first-passage times, and does the top target reproduce the complete
C83 assembly stopping law entry by entry?

This is an up-set hitting problem, not an exact-closure hitting or skip
problem.  It is finite and source-bound under
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
