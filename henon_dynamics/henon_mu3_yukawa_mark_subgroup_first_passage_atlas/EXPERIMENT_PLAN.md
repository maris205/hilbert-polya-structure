# C88 experiment plan

1. Raw-byte bind the final C75, C83, and C85 evidence and manifests.
2. Reconstruct the twenty actual subgroups and all `65536` named-support
   closures from the 54-point addition law.
3. For every target `H`, enumerate the hit up-set
   `{A : H <= Phi(A)}` and its counts `F_H(k)` by cardinality.
4. Compute the exact first-passage permutation counts both from consecutive
   CDF differences and from the pivotal-edge formula.
5. Store reduced probabilities, survival rows, expectation, attainable time
   range, the complete hit bitset, and every minimal hitting support.
6. Verify pointwise, CDF, survival, and expectation order for all `102`
   comparable target pairs, including reflexive pairs.
7. Compare the top target with every corresponding C83 assembly-atlas field.
8. Independently reconstruct every target hit up-set from its minimal support
   antichain, then compare the complete canonical evidence object.
9. Run a SymPy exact-rational check, clean-process replay, at least fourteen
   hostile mutations, two isolated deterministic LaTeX builds, and complete
   PDF and manifest audits.

The event throughout is subgroup containment.  Exact equality
`Phi(A_k)=H` and skips between exact closure states are outside C88.
