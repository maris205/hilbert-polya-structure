# Research question

Can the classical Kirchhoff–Love ellipse be turned into one complete,
all-mode theorem that identifies the exact first instability wall, orders
every later threshold, and quantifies the high-mode ladder without
overstating linear spectral information as nonlinear stability?

The answer is yes.  The dimensionless factorization reduces every mode to
two scalar factors.  One is positive and the other has a unique zero; a
one-line inter-mode identity orders those zeros.  The cubic factor gives
the sharp wall `gamma=3`, and a scaled-root limit gives the complete linear
asymptotic law.  The same analysis also makes clear why this strong fluid
theorem does not advance arithmetic Route A.
