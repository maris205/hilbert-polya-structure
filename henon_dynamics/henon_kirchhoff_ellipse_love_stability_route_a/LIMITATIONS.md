# Limitations

- Linear spectral mode stability is not nonlinear stability.  In particular,
  no Jordan-block, semigroup, orbital, or Lyapunov estimate is proved.
- A negative modal square identifies a linear growing pair but does not
  predict finite-amplitude filamentation, fission, saturation, or late-time
  morphology.
- Viscosity, walls, external strain, compressibility, stratification, and
  three-dimensional disturbances are excluded.
- The limit `delta->1` is singular: it is not a bounded positive-area
  ellipse in the theorem.
- At the circle, the continuation value of `Omega` is a gauge for the
  degenerating ellipse orientation; the circular patch shape itself is
  stationary.
- The continuous aspect family and mode labels do not create rational-prime
  dynamics, isolated primitive orbits, or a determinant bridge.
