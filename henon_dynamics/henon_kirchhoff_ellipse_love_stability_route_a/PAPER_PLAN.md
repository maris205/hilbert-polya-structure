# Paper plan

- **Round 0 — relative-equilibrium owner:** Euler patch definition, exact
  interior velocity, normal-velocity rotation proof, invariants, and the
  corrected patch/oriented-axis/circle period conventions.
- **Round 1 — complete modal-wall owner:** Love formula, dimensionless
  factorization, `m=1`, `m=2`, uniqueness and sign of every `m>=3` wall,
  strict threshold ordering, and the sharp `gamma=3` first instability.
- **Round 2 — asymptotic/boundary/route owner:** scaled threshold asymptotic,
  exact evidence, circle/zero/axis/strip atlas, literature boundary, collision
  audit, and the linear-only and Route-A firewalls.

The rounds accumulate into one theorem paper rather than splitting one idea
into several articles.
