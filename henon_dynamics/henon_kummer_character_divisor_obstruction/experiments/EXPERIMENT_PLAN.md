# Experiment plan

1. Encode cubic characters in the exact cyclotomic basis `(1,zeta)`.
2. Enumerate all virtual multiplicities in `[-6,6]^3`.
3. Require simultaneous vanishing at repetitions 1, 2, and 3.
4. Sample primes below 100,000 and record the nearest nontrivial-channel
   divisor distance `2*pi/(3*log p)`.
5. Attack the false surrogate “zero superdimension is enough” with
   `(1,-1,0)`, which vanishes at repetition 3 but not repetition 1.

The theorem uses Fourier inversion and analytic local finiteness, not a
finite extrapolation from the scan.
