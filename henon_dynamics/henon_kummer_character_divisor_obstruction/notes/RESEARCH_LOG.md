# Research log

C38 left monomial channel permutation as the smallest algebraic survivor.
Its eigencharacters do survive closed loops, but this immediately exposes a
stronger global problem: at critical normalization each local zero or pole
lies at distance `O(1/log p)` from the same interior point.  Graded
superdimension zero cancels only repetitions divisible by three and does not
repair the divisor.  The next minimal test is prime-dependent damping.
