# Paper contract

No manuscript is drafted at Stage 1.  The paper direction is selected only
after the T1--T3 decision gate.

## Positive branch

If a complete non-dihedral aggregate witness and the common nuclear operator
both survive, the paper will present:

1. the common rational-window hyperbolic survivor;
2. joint primitive-orbit and dihedral classification;
3. universal bare/signed collapse controls;
4. the intrinsic instability determinant;
5. the common ordered transfer operator and trace formula;
6. the exact limit of its Hilbert--Pólya relevance.

## Obstruction branch

If the complex operator gate fails after T1--T3, the paper will instead state
the strongest exact result among:

- chronology survives in real intrinsic weights but admits no certified
  common holomorphic realization;
- word-level chronology cancels after complete orbit aggregation;
- the proposed weight reduces to a finite-memory/cohomological control;
- global polynomial/residue identities force bare or signed-zeta collapse.

The manuscript will not present a numerical finite section as an operator
theorem and will not compare its roots with Riemann zeros.
