# Devil's advocate checkpoint 1

**Decision:** PASS TO FORMAL TESTING AFTER REFRAME
**Critical flaws:** none at the design level
**Major risks:** five
**Current status:** Stage 1 only; this document does not validate any theorem

## Strongest criticism

The candidate may be no more than a standard full symbolic shift carrying a
mildly perturbed Hölder potential.  Generic noncommutativity will then produce
small word-level multiplier differences, while the autonomous extension adds
an artificial factor of parameter entropy.  None of this supplies arithmetic
structure, a target divisor, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.

This criticism is potentially fatal to a positive RH narrative.  The project
may proceed only because its theorem gates can convert that criticism into a
sharp chronology classification or an exact obstruction.

## Major risk 1 -- reversal destroys a time-arrow claim

The common reversor gives

\[
F_{w^{\mathrm{rev}}}=RF_w^{-1}R.
\]

Pure instability and flat-trace weights are therefore equal under reversal.
Any narrative that the determinant detects the direction of time is false.

**Required response:** phrase the result as "order memory modulo the dihedral
symmetry," and make reversal equality a test that must pass.

## Major risk 2 -- a selected branch is not a zeta coefficient

A large difference between one state branch over two protocol words may
cancel after summing all state cycles above the base word.  This is precisely
the branch-to-aggregate danger tracked in the repository.

**Required response:** complete local aggregation at the tested period is a
hard pass condition.  The current numerical and finite-field witnesses are
pilots only.

## Major risk 3 -- the operator may not exist on one common domain

Real contraction does not imply a common holomorphic branch space, and a
finite branch matrix does not imply nuclearity.  If the two parameter letters
require incompatible complex domains, the advertised Fredholm determinant
has no foundation.

**Required response:** T5 is a kill gate.  Failure produces an operator
obstruction paper; it is not repaired with Ulam matrices or a fitted kernel.

## Major risk 4 -- algebraic trace identities may erase the signal

The periodic polynomial scheme appears to have word-independent total degree,
and global residue identities may make bare or signed traces universal.  A
rich-looking finite table could therefore be forced algebra.

**Required response:** prove the global collapse controls before interpreting
absolute or instability-weighted coefficients.  Separate the local survivor
from the full complex scheme.

## Major risk 5 -- prior art absorbs the broad claim

Nonautonomous Hénon hyperbolicity, periodic nonautonomous zeta functions,
random transfer operators, and operator cocycles already exist in the
literature.

**Required response:** claim only an explicit rational-window theorem,
chronology quotient, non-dihedral aggregate witness, or new exact operator
obstruction.  If none survives, close C22 as a duplication/negative screen.

## Additional controls demanded

- Keep the Paper-5 and additive Hénon coordinate conventions separate.
- Count primitive **joint** necklaces; separate canonicalizations are invalid.
- Separate base entropy from state dynamics.  The unweighted local symbolic
  adjacency is expected to be \(J_2\otimes A\), giving the control

  \[
  \det(I-z(J_2\otimes A))^{-1}
  =\frac{1}{1-2z-8z^3-16z^4}.
  \]

- Treat the periodic block operator as a monodromy diagnostic, not the main
  determinant.
- State whether every sum is local real, global complex, signed, absolute, or
  instability weighted.
- Do not use finite-field traces as evidence for real hyperbolicity.
- Do not begin Route B or compare with Riemann zeros.

## Checkpoint verdict

The broader "random Hénon zeta remembers chronology" proposal is rejected as
too vague and too close to prior frameworks.  The revised, testable question
passes Stage 1:

> determine exactly what survives in the intrinsic local instability
> determinant after cyclic, reversal, global-algebraic, and finite-memory
> collapses are removed.

Promotion to a paper requires at least T1 and T2 plus either a positive T5
operator theorem or a precise T5 no-go theorem.  A polished numerical cycle
table alone is not publishable under this checkpoint.
