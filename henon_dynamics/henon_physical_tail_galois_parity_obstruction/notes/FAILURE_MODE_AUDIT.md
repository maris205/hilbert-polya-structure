# Failure-mode audit

- **Finite alternation promotion:** rejected.  Four signs do not prove an
  eventual parity law.
- **One embedding equals field height:** rejected.  The physical trace is one
  root; the excess sums all other roots.
- **Decimal sign proof:** rejected.  Exact integer products carry both signs.
- **Degree equals primitive orbit count:** not assumed.  Reflection-axis
  multiplicity and imprimitive factors must be compiled separately.
- **Hölder no-go promotion:** rejected.  P58 concerns a proposed tail proof,
  not all Hölder potentials.
- **Route-B rescue:** rejected because Route A is not ready.
