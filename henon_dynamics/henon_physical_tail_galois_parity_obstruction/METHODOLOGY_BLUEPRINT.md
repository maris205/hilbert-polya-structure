# Methodology blueprint

1. Freeze the H6 recurrence and the P57 definition of Galois excess.
2. Separate the three reflection boundary types: vertex--vertex,
   edge--edge, and vertex--edge.
3. Generate each one-variable closure polynomial and select the unique
   primitive factor of the expected degree.
4. Multiply chronological derivative matrices, reduce the trace modulo the
   coordinate factor, and eliminate the coordinate by a resultant.
5. Prove modular irreducibility and isolate every real trace root by Sturm
   counts.
6. Identify the physical embedding with a rational coordinate box, a fixed
   sign itinerary, and trace monotonicity.
7. Sum `arcosh(|T|/2)` over nonphysical embeddings for diagnostics, but prove
   the discrepancy signs with integer products only.
8. Compare the local fixed-point contraction with the all-conjugate height
   and enforce a no-promotion firewall.

The reusable object is a symmetry-resolved reflection-ensemble compiler, not
a fit to observed discrepancy signs.
