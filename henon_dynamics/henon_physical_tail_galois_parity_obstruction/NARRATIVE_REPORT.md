# Narrative report

## One-sentence contribution

The negative-fixed-point stable tail controls one physical H6 orbit
embedding, whereas the Galois excess sums every nonphysical embedding; exact
period-eight and period-nine trace fields, together with
`Delta_6 < 0 < Delta_7`, prove that the former cannot by itself compile the
latter.

## Why this stage was necessary

P57 left an asymptotic suggestion: the reflection chains approach the
negative fixed point, so perhaps its stable eigenvalue predicts the ladder
discrepancies.  That suggestion concerns the physical real root of each
closure polynomial.  The assigned Galois excess is different: it is the sum
of instability lengths over every other real trace embedding.  P58 tests
whether these two scales can be identified without a new theorem.

## Exact result

Three reflection closures are reconstructed directly from
`q_{j+1}=1-6q_j^2-q_{j-1}`.  The vertex--vertex period-eight closure gives an
irreducible totally real trace field of degree 12.  The edge--edge closure
gives degree 6.  At period nine the vertex--edge closure gives one
irreducible totally real degree-28 field whose extreme embeddings are the
physical `A9` and `B9` cycles.

Sturm isolators and the elementary inequality

```text
log(a-1) < arcosh(a/2) < log(a),  a>2,
```

turn the next two ladder discrepancies into exact integer comparisons:

```text
Delta_6 = -185.5524168765298761... < 0,
Delta_7 =  300.0665139419694939... > 0.
```

The finite signs are not promoted to an eventual alternation theorem.  They
instead show that the nonphysical conjugate ensemble remains macroscopically
active while the selected physical chain is already controlled by a
positive stable multiplier.

## Claim boundary

P58 proves the physical/Galois interface obstruction and exact period 8--9
algebra.  It does not refute unrestricted Hölder data, prove an infinite sign
law, construct a Galois-weighted determinant, or produce arithmetic primes or
a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
