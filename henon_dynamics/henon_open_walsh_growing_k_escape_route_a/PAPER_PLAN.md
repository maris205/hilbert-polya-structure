# C153 paper plan

## Title

Saturated Image Escape and GCD Trace Clusters in a Growing Open Walsh Gate

## Claims-evidence map

| Claim | Proof object | Exact sentinel |
|---|---|---|
| all-`n,k` rank formula | quotient-remainder tensor normal form | 624 rank rows + direct source actions |
| macroscopic escape exponent | exact survival fraction and floor limit | 192 rational-alpha rows |
| full fixed-period cluster set | gcd trace formula + explicit infinite subsequences | divisor ledger through period 20 |
| normalized trace decay | finite cluster-set bound | exact cluster receipts |
| no general raw-trace limit | period-two parity split | two exact `Q(sqrt(3),i)` receipts |
| control separation | closed, order, and moved-hole characteristic polynomials | SymPy control checks |

## Section structure

1. Freeze the gate, clock, and normalization.
2. Prove the exact tensor normal form and rank law.
3. Derive the macroscopic signed and positive escape rates.
4. Classify fixed-period trace clusters and prove normalized decay.
5. Give the period-two obstruction, controls, validation, and strict Route-A
   boundary.

No figure is needed: the exact formulas and compact ledger counts are clearer
than a plotted finite approximation.
