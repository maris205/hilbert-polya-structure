\begin{abstract}
We compute the exact first-passage order statistics of target coverage in a
frozen sixteen-label, twenty-target subgroup model.  Decoding all twenty C88
hit bitsets on all 65536 supports gives the exact target-coverage histogram
at every prefix size.  For each $r=1,\ldots,20$, we determine the law and
first four raw moments of the first time $K_r$ at which at least $r$ targets
have been hit.  Every distribution is carried by exact support counts and
$16!$-weighted permutation counts.  The trivial target is retained, making
$K_1=0$ deterministically.  This is a finite result under
\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Coverage statistic}
Let $h_i(A)$ be the frozen C88 indicator that support $A$ hits target $H_i$,
and define
\[
 C(A)=\sum_{i=0}^{19}h_i(A),\qquad
 K_r=\min\{k:C(A_k)\geq r\}.
\]
Here $A_k$ is the support of the first $k$ labels in a uniform permutation.
The trivial subgroup is target zero and satisfies $h_0(A)=1$ for every $A$.
Thus $C(\varnothing)=1$ and $K_1=0$; all twenty targets remain in the model.

\section{Exact laws}
For $0\leq k\leq16$ and $1\leq r\leq20$, put
\[
 G_r(k)=\#\{A:|A|=k,\ C(A)\geq r\}.
\]
\begin{theorem}
The exact CDF and PMF of each coverage order statistic are
\[
 \Pr(K_r\leq k)=\frac{G_r(k)}{\binom{16}{k}},\qquad
 \Pr(K_r=k)=\Pr(K_r\leq k)-\Pr(K_r\leq k-1).
\]
Multiplying the reduced PMF by $16!$ gives a nonnegative integer permutation
count.  These counts sum to $16!$, and
\[
 \mathbb E[K_r]=\sum_{k=0}^{15}\Pr(K_r>k).
\]
Moreover $K_r\leq K_{r+1}$ pointwise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first $k$ positions of a uniform permutation have a uniform
$k$-element support.  Taking consecutive CDF differences gives the first-hit
law.  For $k\geq1$, each oriented support edge that first crosses rank $r$
has exactly $(k-1)!(16-k)!$ permutation completions; at time zero the empty
support has $16!$ completions.  The expectation is the standard finite
survival sum, and nested threshold events give rank monotonicity.
\end{proof}

\section{Atlas features}
The receipt partitions every one of the seventeen cardinality layers by all
twenty possible coverage counts and records four raw moments, mean, and
variance for every rank.  For example,
$\mathbb E[K_2]=17/12$, while
$\mathbb E[K_{10}]=15589/3960$.  The frozen support lattice admits coverage
jumps: ranks 13 through 20 have the same law, equal to the top-target law,
whose mean is $36499/3960$.  These equalities are observations of the exact
finite receipt, not a classification claim beyond it.
