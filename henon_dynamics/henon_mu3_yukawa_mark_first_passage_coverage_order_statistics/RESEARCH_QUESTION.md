# Research question

Under a uniform random ordering of the sixteen frozen labels, what is the exact distribution of the first prefix time at which at least `r` of all twenty C88 subgroup targets have been hit, for every `r=1,...,20`?

The answer must retain target 0 explicitly, enumerate all support-cardinality layers, convert exact CDF increments into `16!`-weighted permutation counts, and certify moments and rank monotonicity.
