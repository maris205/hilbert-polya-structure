# Narrative report

The equilibrium count and stability threshold close through two low-dimensional reductions.  First, `Omega_y=0` separates the axis from an off-axis constraint; on the axis, `Omega_x` is strictly increasing between collisions, while off axis the two inverse-cube weights must agree and force equilateral geometry.  Second, the rotating `4x4` determinant reduces to a quadratic in `lambda^2`.

The subtle point is equality.  A purely imaginary repeated spectrum does not imply stability.  At `mu_R`, the position pencil has one-dimensional kernel for each double eigenvalue at both `L4` and `L5`; the four exact point/eigenvalue rank cells all give rank three.  Each double eigenvalue therefore produces a Jordan block of size two and `t exp(plus_or_minus i t/sqrt(2))` growth.  The package labels equality defective and not stable.  Below the threshold, distinct imaginary eigenvalues keep the linear flow bounded even at resonant frequency ratios; only nonlinear resonance consequences are outside scope.

Equilibria are constant orbits and supply no intrinsic primitive-period structure, so A1 is `FAIL`, not weak.  The Hamiltonian formalism is only an A4 formal hint.  No target or arithmetic bridge exists.
