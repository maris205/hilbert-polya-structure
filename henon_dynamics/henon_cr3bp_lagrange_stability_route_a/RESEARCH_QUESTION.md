# Research question

Can one derive, from the normalized rotating potential rather than a memorized threshold, the complete equilibrium atlas of the planar circular restricted three-body problem: exactly five points, the linear type of all three collinear points, the full stability split of both triangular points, and the Jordan behavior on the threshold?

Yes.  The three collinear branches follow from a strictly increasing scalar equation and are all saddle-times-center because `S>1`.  The two off-axis roots are the equilateral points.  Their characteristic polynomial is `lambda^4+lambda^2+(27/4)mu(1-mu)`.  With `mu_R=(1-sqrt(23/27))/2`, the linearized flow is bounded elliptic for every `0<mu<mu_R`, including resonant frequency ratios; it is defective with linear growth at equality and has an unstable Hamiltonian quartet for `mu_R<mu<=1/2`.
