# Research question

Can the Grassmann double-commutator flow be solved globally—not merely near a
principal eigenspace—and can one give exact forward/backward limits, rates,
stability dimensions, and every repeated-spectrum equilibrium stratum without
assuming that eigenvalue subset sums are distinct?

Yes.  The flow is the quotient of `Y'=AY`; exterior powers expose every
Plücker exponent.  Simple eigenvalues permit a unique maximum supported basis
by matroid greedy exchange, even when unrelated subset sums tie.  Repeated
eigenvalues require an associated-graded limit rather than a fictitious unique
coordinate.  The same calculation yields the complete Morse--Bott atlas.

The separate Route-A question has a negative answer: exact spectral ordering
inside this source system supplies no arithmetic local data, periodic-orbit
bridge, arithmetic clock, target completion, or Hilbert--Pólya operator.
