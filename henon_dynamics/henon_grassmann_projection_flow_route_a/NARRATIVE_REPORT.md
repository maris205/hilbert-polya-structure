# Narrative report

C298 upgrades a familiar eigenspace-selection intuition to a global theorem
on the entire real Grassmannian.  The key observation is exact rather than
asymptotic: the nonlinear projector equation is the quotient of the linear
flow `Y'=AY`.  This gives every trajectory for all real time.

Exterior powers then turn the dynamics into diagonal exponential scaling.
The main subtlety is that simple eigenvalues do not force distinct subset
sums.  The proof avoids that false shortcut: nonzero Plücker indices form a
representable matroid, and greedy exchange gives a unique maximum supported
basis from distinct element weights.  Each Schubert cell therefore has its
exact coordinate-plane limit, while the actual second nonzero supported
weight—not an ambient spectral gap guessed in advance—sets the decay rate.

Repeated eigenvalues change the conclusion rather than breaking it.  The
whole tied top-weight component survives, and an eigenflag-adapted basis
identifies it with the associated graded.  Invariant projections form the
complete disjoint union of product Grassmannians; zero within-block modes are
their tangents and nonzero cross-block modes give the Morse--Bott normal
splitting and exact stable/unstable dimensions.

Finally `Tr(AP)` increases by the squared commutator norm.  This excludes all
nonconstant recurrence and makes the Route-A limitation explicit: the system
has no primitive-periodic repetition bridge.  Its spectral language remains
source-local, so only a formal A4 analogy survives.
