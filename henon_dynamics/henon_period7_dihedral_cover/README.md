# HCS-C20: dihedral closure and real multiplication of the period-seven Hénon curve

**Date:** 2026-08-08

Status: **exact (D_7), genus-eight, quotient, Jacobian, and real-multiplication theorems; Route A exploratory**

HCS-C19 proved that the adopted period-seven septic (P(\sigma,x)) has a
generic Hénon neighbor graph equal to one seven-cycle.  This project takes the
next nonlocal step.  It identifies the ordered-edge curve with the full
dihedral Galois closure, determines all three natural quotient curves, and
shows that Hénon chronology induces real multiplication on the genus-three
scalar Jacobian.

The general (D_7)-cover and real-multiplication mechanisms are classical.
The contribution here is their exact, source-locked realization on the
specific area-preserving Hénon period-seven curve, together with reproducible
branch and arithmetic certificates.  No novelty is claimed for dihedral
cover theory itself.

## Exact (D_7) and genus theorem

The seven scalar roots form a Galois-invariant cycle, so geometric monodromy
is contained in \(\operatorname{Aut}(C_7)=D_7\).  It is transitive.  The
discriminant

\[
\operatorname{Disc}_xP=(4\sigma-9)^2Q_6(\sigma)^3
\]

is not a square even over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}(\sigma)\), excluding the
only transitive proper possibility \(C_7\).  Thus geometric and arithmetic
monodromy are both exactly \(D_7\).

An ordered adjacent pair reconstructs all seven coordinates by the Hénon
recurrence.  Consequently (E) is the splitting curve and is geometrically
connected.  The only branch values of (E\to\mathbb P^1_\sigma) are the six
roots of (Q_6); each has seven points of inertia two.  Therefore

\[
2g(E)-2=14(-2)+6\cdot7=14,
\qquad \boxed{g(E)=8}.
\]

The quadratic extension over the scalar curve is exactly

\[
\mathbb Q(E)=\mathbb Q(C)(\sqrt{Q_6(\sigma)}).
\]

It branches at one scalar point over each root of (Q_6), and nowhere at the
finite node or infinity.  Modulo (Q_6), the six branch points have coordinate

\[
x=u(\sigma)=
-\frac{160\sigma^5-760\sigma^4+412\sigma^3+1120\sigma^2
 -111\sigma+166}{4}.
\]

## Jacobian and chronology-induced real multiplication

The rational permutation-character relation for (D_7) gives a
\(\mathbb Q\)-isogeny

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Jac}(E)
\sim_{\mathbb Q}
\operatorname{Jac}(B)\times\operatorname{Jac}(C)^2.
}
\]

This is not asserted to preserve the principal product polarization.

Let (q:E\to C).  The unnormalized push-pull correspondence

\[
A=q_*\tau^*q^*
\]

acts on (H^1(C)) as \(\tau+\tau^{-1}\).  It is Rosati self-adjoint and has
exact minimal polynomial

\[
m(T)=T^3+T^2-2T-1.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})
\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Q}^0(\operatorname{Jac}(C)).
}
\]

This proves real multiplication, not equality of the full endomorphism
algebra or absolute simplicity.

## Selected-prime good reduction and exact local factors

At \(p=5,11,13\), the genus-two model \(B:w^2=Q_6(\sigma)\) is smooth.
The connected unramified \(C_7\)-cover \(E\to B\) also has good reduction:
nontrivial vertical inertia would be totally tame and would force
\(\mu_7\subset\mathbb F_p(B)\), hence \(7\mid p-1\), which is false at all
three primes.  Purity then extends \(E\to B\) as a finite étale cover.
The order-two quotient by \(J\) is smooth because \(2\) is invertible.

A two-chart integral-closure argument identifies this quotient model of
\(C\) with the total normalization of the projective plane septic.  Exact
irreducible specializations of \(P\bmod p\) make the comparison birational
on each special fibre.  The unique affine ordinary node and the seven
rational infinity branches therefore give the actual formula

\[
\#C(\mathbb F_{p^r})=A_{p,r}+7+\epsilon_{p,r}.
\]

The first three genuine counts are

| \(p\) | \((N_1,N_2,N_3)\) |
|---:|---:|
| 5 | \((9,39,147)\) |
| 11 | \((19,167,1171)\) |
| 13 | \((16,242,2131)\) |

\[
L_{C,p}(T)=\operatorname{Norm}_{F/\mathbb Q}(1-a_pT+pT^2),

theorem.  Restoring orientation does not create a new ordinary Frobenius
spectrum: the genus-eight cohomology is the genus-two sign quotient plus two
copies of the scalar genus-three cohomology.  The nonzero time characters
appear as three conjugate rank-two factors over the real cubic field.
