# C241 paper plan

1. Define the classical Lüroth map, half-open partition, isolated endpoint,
   and the fact that each branch image is \((0,1]\), not \([0,1]\).
2. Derive the affine inverse composition for an arbitrary finite word and prove
   existence, uniqueness, itinerary, and multiplier.
3. Use least repetition periods and Möbius inversion to count primitive
   necklaces; explain why the countable alphabet gives countably infinitely
   many points at every positive period.
4. Derive the finite weighted generating identity and its primitive-factor
   formal series, then pass to the full convergence half-plane.
5. State sharply that absolute product/log convergence requires
   \(\Re(s)>1/2\) and \(|z|A(\Re(s))<1\), while the rational expression is a
   meromorphic continuation away from denominator zeros; treat \(s=1/2\) as
   divergent and \(s=1,z=1\) as the telescoping pole/boundary.
6. Report the exact receipt, independent checker, SymPy, replay, mutation, and
   PDF gates, followed by the Route-A boundary and explicit nonclaims.
