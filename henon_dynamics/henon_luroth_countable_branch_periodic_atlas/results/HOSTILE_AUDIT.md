# C241 hostile-audit checklist

The mutation harness changes branch slopes and endpoints, word fractions and
itineraries, primitive flags, necklace counts, weighted values and domains,
limit tails, formal-product coefficients, theorem strings, frozen map/clock,
route verdict, scope flags, citation DOI, schema, date, evaluator hash, row
ordering, and required rows.  Repaired-digest mutations recompute
`payload_sha256` before invoking the independent checker, proving that semantic
checks—not just stale hashes—reject them.

The suite must report every mutation caught and no uncaught case.  It also
checks the two subtle domain boundaries: full \(A(s)\) diverges at
\(\Re(s)=1/2\), and absolute primitive-product convergence is stricter than
the meromorphic continuation domain.  Any accidental target-prime/zero or
Euler/root-number language is outside the declared scope and is a release
failure.
