Orient the full positive Markoff--Vieta graph by unique largest-coordinate
descent, prove finite termination and rooted-tree completeness, and identify
the exact arithmetic and periodic-orbit boundaries for Route A.

2. Prove polynomial invariance and the Vieta root identities.
3. Prove unique maximum and the strict lower-root inequality.
4. Derive termination, complete reverse generation and unique-parent tree.
5. Separate the autonomous descent from the involutive generators.
6. Present exact tree/brute-force evidence as regression only.
7. Grade A0 weak and close A1--A4 without mod-prime or Frobenius claims.
8. State source ownership, limitations and declarations.
