# C193 narrative report

C193 gives the round an arithmetic but honestly weak Route-A candidate.  The
phase space is not fitted: it is the positive integer Markoff surface, and the
three transformations are its intrinsic Vieta involutions.  The paper's large
step is to orient this entire classical graph by a deterministic rule and
close the resulting dynamics globally.

The orientation is rigid.  Every non-root normalized triple has a unique
largest coordinate; replacing it by the other quadratic root produces a
positive solution of smaller height.  Height therefore certifies finite
termination, while the source-owned Vieta orbit theorem gives completeness.
The permutation quotient is a rooted tree with a unique parent at every
non-root vertex.

This progress also supplies the obstruction.  A terminating descent cannot
own nonconstant primitive periodic orbits.  The Diophantine equation does not
select rational primes, repetitions or logarithmic times.  Its arithmetic
origin is stronger than an arbitrary finite graph but still far below the A0
entry requirement.  The paper records that precise gap instead of importing
mod-prime graphs or invoking the open Frobenius uniqueness conjecture.
