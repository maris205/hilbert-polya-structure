multiplicities, component counts, invariance and denominator statements,
discarded-pattern witnesses, Koopman limitations, Route-A coordinates, and
claim flags.  Each payload hash is repaired before the independent checker is
expected to reject the attack; a stale-hash control is separate.  No external
reviewer is simulated.

matrix); train/test or parameter overfit **NOT APPLICABLE** (no fit);
confounding conventions **PASS** (left quotient and representatives frozen);
independence **PASS** (quadratic producer, direct-iterate checker, SymPy third
path); metric integrity **PASS** (horizontal mass and zero bin separated);
reproducibility **PASS** (byte replay and fixed-date build); claim inflation
**PASS** (quadratic-filter terminology, false pattern, target, arithmetic, and
