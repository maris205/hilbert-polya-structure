# C151 research question

For the frozen Heisenberg lattice automorphism of C146, which horizontal fixed
classes actually lift to fixed central fibres, and can their number be
expressed exactly at every iterate without assuming an unsupported finite
pattern?

C151 answers the classification question by deriving a representative-
invariant central rotation.  Zero rotation is necessary and sufficient for a
clean fixed circle; nonzero rotation gives no fixed point above that
horizontal class.  A universal denominator turns this test into a finite
root-of-unity projector at every iterate, while exact histograms through
iterate twelve guard the derivation.

The question is source-side only.  It asks for no target comparison,
arithmetic interpretation, isolated-orbit determinant, or Route-B step.
