# C151 paper plan

Title: **Central-rotation-resolved fixed fibres for a Heisenberg nilmanifold
automorphism**.

The paper proceeds from the left-quotient fixed equation to the exact
rotation, proves representative invariance, derives a universal denominator
and root-of-unity projector, reports the independently rebuilt `n<=12`
histograms, rejects a tempting finite pattern, and closes with the clean-
component and Route-A boundary.  The evidence table is summarized rather than
printed in full.  No external citation or figure is required for this exact
self-contained argument.
