# Experiment plan

1. Enumerate exact rational `L,c,n` modal energy and observation coefficients.
2. Reconstruct the same identity from the periodic d'Alembert representation
   in a checker which imports no producer and requires complete unique key
   sets rather than row counts alone.
3. Audit every rational subcritical window by a positive unobserved arc and a
   smooth-support construction contract.
4. Verify revival phases, absence of a zero mode, and the nonidentity half
   revival.
5. Run symbolic, double-fresh replay, repaired-hash contract/duplicate/drop
   mutation, deterministic PDF, and exact manifest gates.

Kill gates: confusing `L/c` with `2L/c`, losing the equality case, replacing
the infinite-dimensional pulse by a finite-mode Gramian, or using the wrong
`c^3` factor stops the paper.
