# Narrative report

The main progress is not another modal spectrum.  It is the exact equality at
the geometric round-trip time together with a counterexample for every
shorter window.  The periodic d'Alembert coordinate exposes both at once:
the endpoint observer samples precisely one full `2L` period at `2L/c`, and
strictly less than a period before then.

The function-space boundary matters.  The observed adjoint lives in the
energy space; an `L^2` Dirichlet control therefore acts on the dual
transposition state space.  No finite Galerkin controllability claim is used
to stand in for this statement.

At the critical time the control-adjoint observation is a scaled isometry
onto its closed range, not a surjection onto the whole temporal `L^2` space.
HUM uses the coercive Gramian `O_T^* O_T`; this is the operator that becomes
an isomorphism after Riesz identification.

The source has a natural self-adjoint wave generator but no arithmetic orbit
dictionary.  Its exact revival does not alter that failure.
