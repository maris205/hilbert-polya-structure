# Source audit and ownership boundary

Two primary SIAM records were checked at the publisher:

1. J.-L. Lions, “Exact Controllability, Stabilization and Perturbations for
   Distributed Systems,” *SIAM Review* 30(1) (1988), 1–68,
   DOI [10.1137/1030001](https://doi.org/10.1137/1030001).  This is the HUM
   owner and explicitly treats boundary and distributed controls.
2. C. Bardos, G. Lebeau, and J. Rauch, “Sharp Sufficient Conditions for the
   Observation, Control, and Stabilization of Waves from the Boundary,”
   *SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization* 30(5) (1992), 1024–1065,
   DOI [10.1137/0330055](https://doi.org/10.1137/0330055).  This owns the
   geometric-ray boundary criterion and notes the one-dimensional
   characteristics mechanism.

C218 owns Kelvin–Voigt damping, C157 a two-dimensional half-wave trace, C261
Airy revival, and C222 a finite double-integrator control.  None owns this
exact one-dimensional critical Parseval identity plus the every-shorter-time
pulse obstruction.  The result is a source-local reconstruction and
executable boundary closure, not a literature-level originality claim.
