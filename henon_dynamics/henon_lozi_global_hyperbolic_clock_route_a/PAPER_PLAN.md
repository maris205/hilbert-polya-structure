# Paper plan

One-sentence contribution: prove the complete bounded Lozi chamber and
show exactly what its derived instability clock cannot encode.

Round zero: source definition, all-bounded-orbit contraction and coding.
Round one: add invariant cones, all-period matrices, primitive counts and
reversal. Round two: add the separately normalized zeta/flat germ, sharp
scope of the unit-clock obstruction, computational receipts and limitations.
Each round is one revision of the same paper. English and Chinese abstracts
and six keywords accompany each version. No venue or novelty certification
is assumed. A small audit table suffices; no decorative figure is needed.
