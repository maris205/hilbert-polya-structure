# Finite audit plan

Freeze rational parameters $9/2,5,6$ and every binary word of length 1–7.
The producer solves a two-dimensional affine return; the independent checker
solves the full cyclic coordinate system by rational Gaussian elimination,
then separately evolves tangent vectors. Symbolic cone/repetition checks and
80-digit eigenvalue identities audit a different representation. Hostile
inputs repair their payload hashes, so rejection cannot rely on stale hashes.
No GPU, training/validation fit, target data or random seed is used.
