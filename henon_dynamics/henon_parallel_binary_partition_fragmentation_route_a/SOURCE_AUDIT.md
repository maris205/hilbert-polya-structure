# Source and collision audit

## Primary source checked

Persi Diaconis, C. Y. Amy Pang, and Arun Ram, “Hopf algebras and Markov
chains: two examples and a theory,” *Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics*
(2014), DOI `10.1007/s10801-013-0456-7`, arXiv `1206.3620`.

The source explicitly treats binary Hopf-power chains, an absorbing
rock-breaking chain on integer partitions, labelled-graph examples, spectral
diagonalization mechanisms, and absorption through coloring/chromatic
polynomials.  It is therefore a genuine nearest owner, not merely background.
This C301 package makes no priority claim for binary fragmentation, occupancy,
birthday asymptotics, or Hopf-algebraic Markov chains.

## What this package closes

The contribution is a narrow, self-contained labelled-set-partition contract:
the arbitrary-start semigroup kernel, the exact law on every labelled
partition, the block-count law and mean, the last-collision distribution, a
direct refinement-flag spectral proof, and executable exact validation are
placed in one paper.  The proof does not invoke the general Hopf machinery.

## Repository collision boundary

- **C194 (Holte carries/base-riffle):** owns carries and the riffle semigroup;
  C301 owns labelled partition refinement and all-label separation.
- **C215 (Kingman coalescent):** merges blocks asynchronously in continuous
  time; C301 refines them synchronously in discrete time.
- **C276 (uniform random mapping):** has a birthday collision along one marked
  functional-graph orbit; C301 asks when every label has a distinct bit word.
- The unlabelled integer-partition quotient is not claimed.  It collapses many
  labelled states and changes Stirling multiplicities.

## Scope firewall

No source supplies target arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers,
automorphy, target divisor laws, a target functional equation, a zero match, or
a Hilbert–Pólya operator.  The polynomial `det(I-zK_n)` is a finite source
Markov determinant only.  Route B remains locked.
