# Paper plan

## Claim spine

1. Define a new package-level dynamical subtype: synchronous fair-bit
   refinement on labelled set partitions.
2. Replace matrix iteration by a binary-word coupling and derive the arbitrary
   start `t`-step kernel.
3. Read the one-block law as finite occupancy to obtain all distributional and
   absorption formulas.
4. Use the refinement flag to prove the spectral factorization and close the
   diagonalizability step with a squarefree annihilator.
5. derive the critical birthday limit, explicitly preserving the dyadic phase.
6. Separate this finite combinatorial success from every failed Route-A rung.

## Manuscript structure

- Model and exact conventions (`n>=1`, labelled states, fresh fair bits).
- One all-parameter theorem containing kernel, law, absorption, spectrum, and
  critical limit.
- Binary-word and occupancy proof.
- Spectral-flag proof emphasizing why triangularity alone is insufficient.
- Birthday expansion and lattice warning.
- Exact certificate, closest-source boundary, Route-A audit, model mutations,
  and AI-use statement.

## Review progression

- **Round 0:** coupled kernel and probabilistic formulas, with the core proof.
- **Round 1:** full spectral flag/annihilator proof and lattice-safe asymptotic.
- **Round 2:** reproducibility counts, primary-source collision, hostile scope
  audit, mutation boundaries, and final disclosure.

Round 2 is the final manuscript.  The archived PDFs must differ by substantive
text rather than metadata alone.
