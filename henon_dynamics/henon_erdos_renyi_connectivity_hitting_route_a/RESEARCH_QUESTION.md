# Research question

For a uniform random ordering of all edges of the complete labeled graph,
what is the complete law of the first connected prefix, and how does that
exact finite law meet the classical connectivity window?

One paper must close both scales:

1. an all-`n,m` recurrence for connected labeled graph counts, including
   support and endpoint cases;
2. exact CDF, PMF, tails, and every finite raw moment of the hitting time;
3. a rigorous Gumbel weak limit at `m=(n/2)(log n+c)`, with both isolated
   vertices and all nontrivial component obstructions accounted for;
4. `n=1`, `n=2`, integer rounding, and terminal-edge boundaries;
5. explicit separation from `G(n,p)`, last-isolated pathwise identities, and
   unproved moment convergence.

The finite recurrence and asymptotic proof form one coherent monotone-dynamics
result rather than separate incremental papers.
