# Paper plan

## Round 0 — all finite laws

Define the without-replacement edge process, derive the vertex-1 component
recurrence, close support/endpoints, and turn connected counts into the exact
hitting CDF, PMF, tails, and all moments.

## Round 1 — Gumbel window

Add the exact isolated-vertex factorial moment, Poisson limit, and a genuine
small/intermediate component proof using required spanning trees plus absent
cross edges.  Deduce the connectivity and integer hitting-time Gumbel law.

## Round 2 — boundaries and audit

Add `n=1,2`, floor/range and terminal support; expose finite evidence and its
cutoffs; rule out `G(n,p)` substitution, pathwise last-isolated equality, and
moment convergence; record Route-A, scope, source ownership, and AI use.
