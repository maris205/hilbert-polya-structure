# Final frozen proposal

## Theorem target

Prove the algebraic cusp-coboundary/full-boundary Selberg-period dichotomy for
section-induced projective automorphy cocycles of
\(\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)\), then prove that the standard squarefree
\(\Gamma_0(N)\) scattering family is simultaneously Walsh diagonalizable and
has permutation-invariant bare spectral-parameter products in the frozen
normalization.

## Positive controls

1. Derive and audit the explicit unoriented open-scattering Laplace series.
2. Exhibit source-locked endpoint-projector amplitudes at level six that
   distinguish a parameter-to-edge reassignment and a changed endpoint path,
   without naming either test intrinsic chronology.

## Claim boundary

The result concerns ordinary diagonal trace closure and the algebra generated
solely by standard squarefree scattering matrices.  It does not cover
off-diagonal kernels, inserted endpoint projectors or matrix cocycles,
nontrivial twists, nonsquarefree levels, or a separately derived operator.

## Decision

If the exact proofs and independent numerical certificates pass, retain the
project as HCS-C18, a Route-A-rejected but paper-ready scoped obstruction.
