The primitive integral section proves that the displayed projective
automorphy cocycle vanishes on the rational cusp orbit.  Every other section
differs by an algebraic endpoint coboundary; no continuous or bounded
transfer-operator conjugacy is inferred.  Extending to all real endpoints
creates hyperbolic loops, but their periods are signed Selberg lengths.  The
original positive candidate is therefore stopped at the period level.

To avoid a local endpoint variation, the same trace-closure question was
tested on the independent squarefree multi-cusp repair.  Its classical tensor

conditional on using those factors as steps; the spectral parameter is not
source-derived time.  Endpoint projectors may leave this algebra and are
tested for assignment/path sensitivity as a positive scope control.
