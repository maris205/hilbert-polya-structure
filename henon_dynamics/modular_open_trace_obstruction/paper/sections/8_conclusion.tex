\section{Conclusion}

Retaining modular endpoints resolves the compositional defect of cusp double
cosets, but it changes the denominator height into an algebraically
gauge-trivial cocycle on the rational orbit.  Extending the endpoint space to
all real points creates nonzero periods only by recovering signed hyperbolic
translation lengths.  Retaining
all squarefree cusp channels also yields no order information in the frozen
bare-matrix diagnostic: the standard scattering family belongs to one
commutative Atkin--Lehner algebra and keeps the completed-zeta quotient
divisor.  This is not a claim that the spectral parameter is dynamical time.

These conclusions do not say that open scattering contains no dynamical
information.  The explicit open Laplace series is non-Selberg, and endpoint
projectors restore assignment- and path-sensitive amplitudes.  They show
instead that the frozen ordinary diagonal closure discards the endpoint
information that makes those objects open.  Any successor must freeze the
added off-diagonal state and
derive its primitive law and operator before invoking a determinant.

The result is limited to projective automorphy cocycles and standard
squarefree trivial-nebentypus scattering matrices.  Nonsquarefree or twisted
levels, matrix cocycles inserted between arrows, groupoid kernels with
additional fiber state, and separately constructed self-adjoint operators
remain outside the theorem.  Because current Kloosterman and modular-symbol
theory already occupies much of the nearest off-diagonal territory, the next
breadth-first experiment should switch systems rather than tune the modular
level.  A natural next target is the two-axis Frobenius--iterate H\'enon count,
which preserves arithmetic extension degree and dynamical time separately.
