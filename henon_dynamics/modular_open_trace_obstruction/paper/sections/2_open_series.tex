\section{A genuinely open arithmetic series}

Fix a sufficiently large compact-core height \(T_0>1\).  Pujahari and Satpathy classify
unoriented modular scattering geodesics by subsets of reduced rationals
\cite{PujahariSatpathy2025}.  If \(s_q\) denotes the number of solutions to
\(r^2\equiv-1\pmod q\), with \(s_1=1\), their result gives
\begin{equation}
n_q=\frac{\varphi(q)+s_q}{2},\qquad
\ell_q=2\log(qT_0).
\label{eq:count-sojourn}
\end{equation}
The special term \(q=1\) represents the rational endpoint zero.

\begin{proposition}[open scattering series]
For \(\Re s>1\), the Laplace series over the unoriented scattering geodesics
is
\begin{equation}
Z_{\rm sc}(s;T_0)
=\frac{T_0^{-2s}}2\left[
\frac{\zeta(2s-1)}{\zeta(2s)}+
\frac{\zeta(2s)L(2s,\chi_{-4})}{\zeta(4s)}
\right].
\label{eq:open-zeta}
\end{equation}
It has meromorphic continuation through the right-hand side, with
\begin{equation}
\Res_{s=1}Z_{\rm sc}=\frac{3}{2\pi^2T_0^2},\qquad
\Res_{s=1/2}Z_{\rm sc}=\frac{3}{8\pi T_0}.
\label{eq:open-residues}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The standard totient identity gives
\[
\sum_{q\ge1}\frac{\varphi(q)}{q^w}
=\frac{\zeta(w-1)}{\zeta(w)}.
\]
For the second coefficient, roots of \(-1\) lift uniquely through powers of a
prime congruent to one modulo four, do not exist at primes congruent to three
modulo four, and contribute the factor \(1+2^{-w}\) at two.  Thus
\[
\sum_{q\ge1}\frac{s_q}{q^w}
=(1+2^{-w})\prod_{p\equiv1(4)}
\frac{1+p^{-w}}{1-p^{-w}}
=\frac{\zeta(w)L(w,\chi_{-4})}{\zeta(2w)}.
\]
Substitute \(w=2s\) into \cref{eq:count-sojourn}.  The residues follow from
\(L(1,\chi_{-4})=\pi/4\) and \(\zeta(2)=\pi^2/6\).
\end{proof}

The object in \cref{eq:open-zeta} is a sum over open geodesics.  It has no
primitive-power law analogous to a closed-orbit Euler product.  That is not a
defect of the formula; it is the positive control against which trace closure
will be measured.  The coefficient audit later sets \(T_0=1\) only as an
analytic normalization of this Dirichlet series; that boundary value is not
presented as a positive geometric sojourn-time cutoff.
