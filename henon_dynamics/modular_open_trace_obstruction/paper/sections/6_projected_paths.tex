\section{Endpoint projectors restore assignment and path sensitivity}

The commutative-algebra result does not justify deleting cusp endpoints.  Let
\(P_a=e_ae_a^T\) project onto one cusp in the standard basis.  For three
scattering steps,
\begin{equation}
\tr\bigl(P_a\Phi(s_1)P_b\Phi(s_2)P_c\Phi(s_3)\bigr)
=\Phi(s_1)_{ab}\Phi(s_2)_{bc}\Phi(s_3)_{ca}.
\label{eq:projected-amplitude}
\end{equation}
The projectors need not commute with the scattering matrices.  Therefore
\cref{eq:order-blind} does not imply that
\cref{eq:projected-amplitude} is invariant when parameters are reassigned to
edges or when the endpoint path is changed.

Level \(N=6\) is the smallest squarefree level with two independent cusp bits
and three distinct nontrivial flip types.  Its four cusps can be
labelled by the two bits associated with \(p=2,3\).  The closed itinerary
\[
00\longrightarrow10\longrightarrow11\longrightarrow00
\]
uses a two-bit-local \(2\)-flip, a \(3\)-flip, and a simultaneous flip.
Assigning three distinct spectral parameters to these edge types and then
changing the parameter-to-edge assignment changes the product in
\cref{eq:projected-amplitude}.  The reproducibility certificate evaluates a
fixed nonpalindromic witness, as well as a different endpoint path, at
80-digit precision; an independent checker recomputes both from the local
blocks.

This assignment and path sensitivity is a genuine positive scope boundary:
off-diagonal endpoint resolution retains data suppressed by the unprojected
trace.  It does not yet establish intrinsic chronology, because no source
identifies the spectral parameter with a time step or defines the allowed
event reorderings.  It is not yet a positive Hilbert--P\'olya candidate.  A
promotion would need all of the following.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A source-derived rule specifying which projected paths compose and
which cyclic paths are primitive.
\item A repetition law compatible with the matrix weights and the chosen
endpoint fibers.
\item A trace-class or nuclear operator whose flat trace yields those paths,
not a finite product selected after the fact.
\item Meromorphic or entire continuation and a divisor different from the
already known scattering and Kloosterman factors.
\end{enumerate}

Kloosterman zeta functions and their twists already encode sophisticated
off-diagonal arithmetic \cite{Kiral2016,DiamantisFriedbergStromberg2026},
while relative scattering determinants are tightly related to Selberg zeta
and resonances \cite{BorthwickJudgePerry2005}.  The projected witness is
therefore recorded as a required next gate, not named as a new zeta.
