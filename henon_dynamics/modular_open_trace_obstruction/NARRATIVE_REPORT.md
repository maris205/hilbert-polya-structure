# Research narrative

## The question that survived HCS-C17

The modular scattering denominator is rich open-channel data but is not a
closed-orbit clock.  Two natural repairs remained: retain the endpoint so that
denominator increments compose locally, or retain every cusp channel so that
matrix multiplication records a path.  HCS-C18 tests both repairs without
assuming either one works.

## First turn: an open object really exists

Unoriented modular scattering geodesics of denominator \(q\) have sojourn
time \(2\log(qT_0)\) and multiplicity
\((\varphi(q)+s_q)/2\), where \(s_q\) counts square roots of \(-1\) modulo
\(q\).  Their Laplace series is an explicit sum of a totient zeta quotient
and a \(\chi_{-4}\) Euler product.  This object is genuinely open and does not
pretend to be a closed primitive-orbit determinant.

## Second turn: endpoint retention makes the height exact but gauge-trivial

The double-coset space itself has no well-defined multiplication.  Passing to
the endpoint action groupoid repairs composition.  A projective section gives
an exact additive automorphy cocycle.  On rational endpoints, however, the
primitive integral section makes that cocycle identically zero.  The familiar
\(2\log|c|\) formula in the affine section is precisely the difference of two
endpoint denominator gauges.

This is an algebraic/set-theoretic coboundary.  Its transfer function is
discontinuous and unbounded in the inherited real-boundary topology, so no
analytic transfer-operator conjugacy is inferred.

This resolves a tension in the previous round: endpoint retention does make
the formula composable, but it does not make the denominator an intrinsic
period.  A positive sojourn time is also invariant under reversing a
geodesic, whereas every real groupoid cocycle changes sign under inversion.

## Third turn: full-boundary period support is Selberg

Enlarging the endpoint space to the full real projective line introduces
loops.  Rational loops are parabolic and have zero cocycle period.  Every
other real loop fixes a quadratic irrational and comes from a hyperbolic
matrix.  At its two fixed points the automorphy period is plus or minus the
hyperbolic translation length.  Thus a loop-supported diagonal trace can draw
nonzero automorphy periods only from the Selberg closed-geodesic spectrum.
This classifies periods, not trace weights or a determinant.

## Independent matrix escape and its bifurcation

Squarefree \(\Gamma_0(N)\) has \(2^{\omega(N)}\) cusps, but its standard
scattering matrix is a tensor product of symmetric two-channel blocks.  A
fixed Walsh basis diagonalizes the whole meromorphic family.  Keeping a frozen
product at distinct spectral parameters shows that it is invariant under
reordering; no averaging is used.  This is a conditional modeling diagnostic,
because the spectral parameter is not source-derived time.

The negative result has a sharp boundary.  Cusp endpoint projectors inserted
between scattering events generally do not commute with the scattering
algebra.  Projected amplitudes can therefore retain parameter-to-edge
assignment and endpoint-path information.  They are a real positive scope
signal, but not yet intrinsic chronology; a single finite witness is not a
dynamical zeta, a Fredholm determinant, or a Hilbert--P\'olya structure.

## Outcome

HCS-C18 closes the two standard trace-closure repairs and records the precise
off-diagonal escape.  The result is valuable as a structural obstruction,
not as a positive construction and not as a claim of new scattering theory.
The next breadth-first round should leave modular scattering and test the
two-axis Frobenius/iterate H\'enon object.
