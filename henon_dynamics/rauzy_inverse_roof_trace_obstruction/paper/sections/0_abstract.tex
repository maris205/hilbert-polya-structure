\begin{abstract}
We test whether identity-holonomy cycles found in a symmetric extension of a
Rauzy--AGY symbolic clock can be promoted to genuine periodic dynamics of the
positive Rauzy natural extension.  The answer is negative for the three
source-locked witnesses under consideration.  We separate the genuine length
action $B^{-\mathsf T}$, the contravariant transfer action $B^{\mathsf T}$,
and a raw covariant homology control.  Exact integer Farkas certificates then
show that two length-six words and one length-twenty-four relation fail the
positive-cone test in every cyclic phase, for both the length and transfer
conventions, although their chronological raw products are exactly the
identity.  A real groupoid one-cocycle is antisymmetric under inversion, so an
intrinsic roof cannot be strictly positive on both orientations.  Matrix
identity is distinguished from the groupoid unit and from vanishing of an
arbitrary edge cocycle; a projective normalization cocycle vanishes only
conditionally, by telescoping on a domain where every prefix is defined.  On
the operator side, faithful bounded inverse branches on one
infinite-dimensional space obstruct compactness and nuclearity of the
non-backtracking block operator.  Enlarging the geometric domain instead
produces a full neutral fixed family, outside the standard isolated
hyperbolic flat-trace atom.  The finite group-trace Hashimoto determinant from
the preceding calculation nevertheless remains valid as a combinatorial
graph object.  Thus the result is an obstruction to dynamical promotion, not
a retraction of the finite algebraic signal.  A strictly positive symmetric
edge clock is possible, but it defines a new suspension in which formal
inverse labels are symbols rather than inverse arrows of the original
dynamical groupoid.
\end{abstract}
