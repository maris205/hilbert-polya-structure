\section{Evaluation and the next nonduplicative gate}
\label{sec:routea}

The purpose of the \RouteA{} protocol is to prevent a striking finite signal
from being promoted before its dynamical and analytic carrier exists.  For
the specific promotion tested in this paper, the evaluation is
\begin{equation}
 (\mathrm{A1}_{\mathrm{fail}},\mathrm{A2}_{\mathrm{fail}},
  \mathrm{A3}_{\mathrm{fail}},\mathrm{A4}_{\mathrm{formal\ hint}}),
 \qquad
 \boxed{\text{promotion rejected}.}
 \label{eq:route-a-decision}
\end{equation}
The four labels have precise meanings.

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=2.4cm]
\item[A1: fail.]
The proposed primitive objects do not exist on the tested positive
Rauzy/transfer domains: every cyclic phase of all three source-locked words is
infeasible.  The unit graph clock is a declaration for a new system, not a
recovered AGY return time or an arithmetic primitive law.

\item[A2: fail.]
An ordinary nuclear Hashimoto operator cannot carry faithful same-space
inverse branches.  Enlarging the domain instead produces a neutral identity
fixed family, not the standard isolated flat-trace atom.  The C29 finite
group trace remains valid as a different trace mechanism.

\item[A3: fail.]
Because the dynamical and operator carriers fail first, questions of global
analytic continuation, a functional equation, or a prime-orbit counting law
for this promotion are not yet meaningful.

\item[A4: formal hint.]
The exact symplectic kernel relations and normalized finite-Weil traces may be
useful as a fibre cocycle over a genuinely hyperbolic base.  They do not, by
themselves, furnish a self-adjoint Hilbert--P\'olya operator or a spectral
identification with arithmetic zeros.
\end{description}

No Route--B claim is made.  In particular, this work neither fits
Riemann-zero data nor infers spectral statistics from the coefficients in
\eqref{eq:C25-coefficient}--\eqref{eq:C26-coefficient}.

\subsection{What not to rebuild}

The associated H\'enon program already contains a certified hyperbolic local
control, denoted $H_6$, with mixed-domain polynomial map
\begin{equation}
 H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q),
 \label{eq:H6}
\end{equation}
rational domain margins, and an exact one-step stable--unstable pinning
kernel.  Analytic pinning and cross-map methods are the natural setting in
which stable and unstable variables are separated, as in the primary
construction of \citet{BaladiPujalsSambarino2005}; analytic flow zeta methods
likewise have a well-developed hyperbolic foundation \citep{Fried1995}.
Re-deriving generic local pinning machinery around \eqref{eq:H6} would
therefore duplicate an already closed local gate rather than address the
obstruction found here.

\subsection{The next large gate}

A nonduplicative next theorem should keep the hyperbolic base and move the
identity relation into a fibre, where trivial fibre holonomy need not make the
base return map the identity.  One viable target is the following conditional
program.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(P\arabic*),leftmargin=2.8em]
\item Construct all admissible iterated pinning compositions for the certified
  $H_6$ mixed domains and prove a common quantitative contraction bound,
  rather than only a one-step identity.
\item Place the resulting word operators on compatible polarized spaces and
  prove a summable nuclear-norm or flat-trace majorant uniform in word length.
\item Add the symplectic/finite-Weil cocycle purely in the fibre.  A relation
  word may have identity fibre holonomy while its base orbit remains an
  isolated hyperbolic periodic point.
\item Establish a common zero-free determinant disk and a repetition theorem
  before asking for arithmetic continuation or a Hilbert--P\'olya
  interpretation.
\end{enumerate}

This pivot is intentionally a large gate.  It attacks the missing all-word
composition and determinant-tail theorem rather than extending the failed
formal-inverse scan to longer words or more primes.  Success would evade both
parts of \Cref{thm:flat-dichotomy}: the base word would have a genuine
positive period and nonneutral derivative, while identity would occur only
in a finite or group-traced fibre.

\subsection{Scope of the novelty claim}

The mathematically new local content claimed here is limited to the
source-locked conjunction of: (i) exact all-phase cone certificates for the
three frozen words under both correct action conventions; (ii) their raw
covariant controls; and (iii) the resulting roof, repetition, and trace
semantic boundary.  The general cocycle sign identity, suspension formalism,
operator-ideal fact, and hyperbolic trace machinery are standard.  We make no
claim that every possible natural extension or every regularized groupoid
zeta is obstructed.
