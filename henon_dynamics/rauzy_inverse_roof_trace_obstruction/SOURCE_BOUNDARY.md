## Established primary-source framework

The following are not C30 novelty claims.

- Rauzy--Veech chronology, the length action, the invertible
  zippered-rectangle extension, and the positive AGY return roof are supplied
  by Avila--Gouëzel--Yoccoz, [*Exponential mixing for the Teichmüller
  flow*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511614), especially Sections 3.1.3,
  3.2, 3.3.1, and 4.1.
- Positive-roof symbolic suspensions and their periodic-orbit zeta functions
  are classical; see Parry--Pollicott, [*Zeta functions and the periodic
  orbit structure of hyperbolic dynamics*](https://smf.emath.fr/system/files/filepdf/AST_1990__187-188__1_0.pdf),
  Chapter 6.
- Adapted anisotropic spaces and meromorphic dynamical determinants for
  Anosov systems are prior art: Gouëzel--Liverani,
  [*Banach spaces adapted to Anosov systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405278),
  and Giulietti--Liverani--Pollicott,
  [*Anosov flows and dynamical zeta functions*](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2013.178.2.6),
  with the [2022 erratum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04917).
- Microlocal flat-trace proofs for Anosov flows are prior art; see
  Dyatlov--Zworski, [*Dynamical zeta functions for Anosov flows via microlocal
  analysis*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4203), Section 2.2.
- Analytic stable--unstable cross maps and pinning operators are prior art;
  see Fried, [*Meromorphic zeta functions for analytic
  flows*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02099469), and
  Baladi--Pujals--Sambarino, [*Dynamical zeta functions for analytic surface
  diffeomorphisms with dominated splitting*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307045).

These frameworks establish what hypotheses a successful escape would need.
They do not certify that a formal inverse of a positive Rauzy branch is a
second positive-time AGY branch.

## Search-bounded repository delta

The defensible C30 delta is limited to:

1. exact source-frame/raw-matrix replay of the C29 kernel words;
2. all-phase integer Farkas certificates for the source length and transfer
   actions;
3. raw covariant positive controls that prevent a chronology-category error;
4. the precise identity-arrow, identity-holonomy, and projective-period
   trichotomy for these words;
5. the scoped same-space nuclearity and flat-trace no-go theorems;
6. the resulting Route-A rejection of the C29-to-AGY promotion.

This is not a claim that no equivalent algebraic relation or abstract no-go
lemma appears anywhere in the literature.  The exact C25/C26 specialization
and complete cyclic-phase certificate are repository-specific; novelty beyond
that specialization remains search-bounded.
