# Devil's Advocate checkpoint 1

## Verdict: PASS WITH REQUIRED SCOPE CONTROLS

No critical defect blocks the experiment. Three major overclaim risks are
frozen before interpretation.

### Major issue 1: an algebraic loop need not be a dynamical orbit

The C29 graph word is admissible in the formal symmetric graph, but formal
inverse arrows can leave the Rauzy positive cone. The project must test the
complete common prefix domain for every cyclic basepoint before calling any
word a periodic orbit, and it must not substitute the raw covariant homology
action for the AGY \(B^{-\mathsf T}\) length action.

Resolution: exact positive-cone feasibility is the first gate, before roof or
operator construction.

### Major issue 1b: matrix holonomy identity is not a unit path

A reduced symmetric-path word may lie in the kernel of the matrix cocycle
without being the unit arrow of the free path groupoid.  Therefore an
arbitrary edge cocycle need not vanish on C1 or C2.

Resolution: the project records signed abelianization and claims zero period
only for a true unit arrow, for a cocycle known to vanish on the kernel, or for
the projective normalizer that telescopes through \(B_w=I\).

### Major issue 2: reversible does not mean positive time in both directions

The inverse of a groupoid time arrow carries the negative cocycle value. A
positive symmetric graph length may define a new geodesic graph flow, but it
cannot be silently identified with the AGY return-time cocycle.

Resolution: the project reports the two objects under separate names.

### Major issue 3: flat trace is not ordinary trace class

Anosov transfer operators can possess distributional flat traces on
anisotropic or microlocal spaces without being trace class. A compactness
argument alone therefore cannot rule out every dynamical determinant.

Resolution: the negative theorem combines compactness with the stronger
identity-word degeneracy (h_W=\mathrm{id}), and explicitly leaves general
clean-fixed-set regularization outside scope.

## Strongest counterargument

One could enlarge the phase space beyond the positive cone, choose a positive
symmetric graph clock, and use a
microlocal trace rather than a Bergman or Hölder-space Fredholm determinant.

Response: a symmetric graph clock is mathematically valid, but it declares a
new suspension in which inverse labels are symbols rather than negative-time
arrows.  Enlargement changes the empty-positive-domain branch into a word
whose full base map is the identity, with a nonisolated fixed set and
\(\det(I-Dh_W)=0\). A new regularization may exist, but it would not justify
the C29 isolated-periodic-orbit trace formula for the original AGY system.

## Frame-lock check

The preferred positive result would be a C29 promotion. The experiment is
therefore designed to make a negative outcome first-class, and its pivot is
fixed before any new spectral computation.
