# HCS-C30 validation report

## Validation target

The release validates a scoped negative theorem: the three exact C29
identity-holonomy witnesses cannot be promoted to positive-domain AGY orbits
or to the tested same-space nuclear/standard isolated-flat-trace realization.

## Independent gates

The checker reconstructs, rather than trusts, the following surfaces.

1. complete SHA-256 source lock and forbidden-data policy;
2. C25/C26 raw token matrices, inverse semantics, and later-on-left products;
3. source-locked closed-path frame result and direct raw identity replay;
4. genuine \(B^{-\mathsf T}\) length prefixes for every cyclic phase;
5. contravariant \(B^{\mathsf T}\) transfer prefixes for every cyclic phase;
6. every canonical Farkas descriptor, with zero-based step/coordinate indices;
7. raw covariant homology controls and their positive integer witnesses;
8. identity-holonomy versus unit-path scope and signed abelianization;
9. inverse roof antisymmetry and projective normalizer telescoping boundary;
10. same-space compact/nuclear ideal theorem hypotheses and conclusion;
11. empty-domain/full-neutral-family flat-trace dichotomy;
12. retained C29 group-trace scope, Route-A decision, and nonduplicative pivot.

The JSON comparison is recursive and type-strict.  Unknown keys, booleans in
integer fields, floats in exact fields, stale payload hashes, changed source
paths, changed chronology, and changed decision scope must fail closed.

## Mathematical validity controls

- All phase matrices use exact integer inversion of unimodular matrices.
- A phase is declared infeasible only from an explicit integer certificate.
- The covariant raw control prevents substitution of \(B\) for
  \(B^{-\mathsf T}\).
- Both final identity and intermediate-domain conditions are checked; neither
  is inferred from the other.
- The roof theorem distinguishes a unit groupoid arrow, a matrix-kernel word,
  and a cocycle that factors through matrix holonomy.
- The operator theorem is conditional on bounded faithful inverses on a common
  infinite-dimensional realization; no universal microlocal no-go is claimed.
