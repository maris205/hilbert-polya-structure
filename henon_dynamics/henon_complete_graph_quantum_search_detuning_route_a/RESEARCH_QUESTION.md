# HCS-C323 research question

For the continuous-time complete-graph search Hamiltonian

\[
H_g=-g|s\rangle\langle s|-P_W,
\qquad |s\rangle=N^{-1/2}\sum_{x=1}^N|x\rangle,
\qquad a=M/N,
\]

can one close, for every `N>=1`, every marked-set size `0<=M<=N`, and every
`g>=0`, the full spectrum and multiplicities, the initialized success law,
the exact perfect-search resonance, the off-resonance maximum, the critical
detuning window, the complete-graph adjacency convention, and every missing-
sector boundary without confusing finite evidence with proof?

The intended answer is analytic.  The target-free scope is
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`; no target zero matching, arithmetic local data,
Euler factor, root number, automorphy statement, Hilbert--Polya operator, or
Route-B input is allowed.
