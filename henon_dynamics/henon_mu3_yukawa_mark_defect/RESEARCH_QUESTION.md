# C65 research question

The C63 character map has the saturated integer kernel
(K=\langle z_1,z_2,z_3\rangle\subset\mathbb Z^{16}), while C64 supplies
an injective 16-type mark map (m).  What integral defect is created when the
new C63 exterior direction is added to the older control directions?

Define

[
L_{\rm old}=m\langle z_1,z_3\rangle,\qquad
L_{\rm all}=m\langle z_1,z_2,z_3\rangle,
]

[
\operatorname{Sat}(L)=(\mathbb QL)\cap\mathbb Z^{16}.
]

The target is the exact pair of Smith forms and the relative quotient
(\operatorname{Sat}(L_{\rm all})/(L_{\rm all}+operatorname{Sat}(L_{\rm old}))).
No subtraction of finite quotients of different ranks is used.
