# C65 source and novelty audit

C65 binds the C63 kernel evidence, the C64 mark evidence, and the C64 scoped
manifest by exact SHA-256.  It recomputes the character-kernel annihilation,
the gcd-one maximal minor of the displayed integer kernel basis, all relevant
determinantal divisors, and the normalized saturation bases.

Smith normal form, determinantal divisors, and lattice saturation are standard
tools.  The bounded contribution is the exact instance-specific dyadic jump
for the C63 (W(E_6)) support and the explicit relative quotient generated by
the four-versus-four direction.  No general SNF theorem or priority claim is
made.

The full Burnside ring, arithmetic resolvents, local fields, Euler factors,
root numbers, and Hilbert--Polya operators remain outside scope.
