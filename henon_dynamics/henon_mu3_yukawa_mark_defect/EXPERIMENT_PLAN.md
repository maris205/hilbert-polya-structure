# C65 experiment plan

1. Rebind C63/C64 evidence and the C64 manifest; reject any hash or scope
   drift.
2. Verify the C63 character kernel and its gcd-one maximal minor.
3. Multiply the C64 mark matrix by the old and full kernel bases.
4. Compute determinantal divisors by direct enumeration of all small minors and
   derive the two Smith forms.
5. Divide the mark vectors by their contents and verify gcd-one saturation
   bases and the relative index-two quotient.
6. Run an independent checker, clean replay, a library SNF cross-check, and
   hostile mutations before compiling the manuscript.

Kill gates include any changed predecessor hash, non-saturated kernel basis,
SNF mismatch, failed relative quotient, or scope leakage.
