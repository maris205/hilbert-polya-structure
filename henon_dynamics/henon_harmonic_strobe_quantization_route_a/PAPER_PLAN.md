# Paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

The all-angle planar harmonic strobe admits a complete classical,
invariant-Gaussian Koopman, and same-clock quantum spectral ledger, but its
rational continuum fixed sets and two non-trace-class unitaries rule out both
classical and ordinary Fredholm determinant ownership.

## Argument order

1. Freeze the oscillator, orientation, real-time parameter, classical
   \(2\pi\) projection, metaplectic double cover, and determinant conventions.
2. Prove the all-angle classical fixed-set law and zeta dichotomy.
3. Diagonalize the invariant-Gaussian Koopman operator.
4. Construct the natural quantum propagator on the same real-time clock and
   retain its \(2\pi\) global sign and \(4\pi\) period.
5. Prove the two noncompactness and ordinary determinant obstructions.
6. Record the Route-A tuple, heat-clock boundary, and nonclaims.

## Figure and table decision

No figure or comparison table is needed.  The decisive relations are one
rotation matrix, one Laguerre basis, and one Hermite spectrum; a diagram would
add less information than the displayed equations.  This figure-phase
decision is explicit rather than skipped.
