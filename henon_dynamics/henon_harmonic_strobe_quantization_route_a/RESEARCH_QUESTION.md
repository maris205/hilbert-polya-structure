# Research question and decision rule

For the all-angle stroboscopic family of the planar harmonic oscillator, can
one classify the classical fixed sets and then place the invariant-Gaussian
Koopman lift and the natural quantum propagator on the same physical clock,
while distinguishing the classical \(2\pi\) quotient from the quantum
metaplectic double cover, without confusing either unitary spectrum with an
ordinary Fredholm determinant or an arithmetic target?

## Hard gate

1. Prove the fixed-set law for every angle and every positive iterate.
2. Separate irrational angles, where the Artin--Mazur series exists, from
   rational resonances, where it fails by continuum cardinality.
3. Prove a complete orthonormal Laguerre--angular diagonalization on the
   invariant Gaussian space, including all multiplicities.
4. Freeze physical time on \(\mathbb R\), prove the Hermite spectrum, the
   exact \(2\pi\) sign and \(4\pi\) return, Egorov identities, and time
   reversal without suppressing global phase.
5. Decide compactness, all finite Schatten classes, and ordinary determinant
   ownership for both unitary lifts.
6. Keep heat damping, Wick rotation, truncation, target data, and Route B
   outside the frozen object.

## Decision

Every gate has an exact answer.  The answer is structurally useful but
negative for Route A: irrational angles have a one-factor source zeta,
rational angles have uncountable resonant fixed sets, and both natural
unitaries fail ordinary trace-class determinant ownership.  The quantum
propagator is a genuine same-clock A4 lift, but no A4 result can compensate
for the failed arithmetic, primitive-orbit, determinant-matching, and global
analytic gates.
