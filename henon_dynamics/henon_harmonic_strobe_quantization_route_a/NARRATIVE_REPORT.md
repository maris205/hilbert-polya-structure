# Narrative report

## Outcome first

C178 resolves every physical time of the planar harmonic oscillator in
one theorem chain.  Irrational angles fix only the origin at every positive
iterate and produce the elementary source zeta \((1-z)^{-1}\).  A rational
angle has finite order; at every multiple of that order the whole plane is
fixed.  The classical Artin--Mazur cardinality series therefore ceases to
exist precisely on the rational resonance locus.

The same source has two natural operator lifts, and both are explicit.  The
Gaussian Koopman operator diagonalizes in a Laguerre--angular basis.  Its
angular eigenvalues are dense for irrational angles and finite roots of unity
for rational angles, but radial indices give infinite multiplicity in every
case.  The natural quantum oscillator uses the same real physical time,
has the Hermite phases \(e^{-i\theta(j+1/2)}\), and implements the classical
rotation through exact Egorov identities.  Unlike the classical and Gaussian
families, it satisfies \(Q_{\theta+2\pi}=-Q_\theta\) and only returns exactly
at \(4\pi\).  This global metaplectic sign is retained.

## Why this is progress

The paper does more than repeat that a unitary is noncompact.  It aligns the
classical resonance transition, the full Gaussian spectral multiplicity, and
the quantum energy ledger under one frozen real-time clock while distinguishing
the classical quotient from its quantum double cover.  This exposes two different
obstructions: rational classical fixed counts become infinite, while both
operator lifts remain outside trace-class Fredholm theory.  Heat damping
would create a legal trace-class operator, but only by changing physical time
to an imaginary-time clock.

## Boundary

The quantum lift is a genuine `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` result and nothing
more.  It does not supply arithmetic origin, isolated prime-like cycles, a
target divisor, global target analytic structure, or a Hilbert--Pólya
operator.  Finite ledgers are reproducibility sentinels for independently
proved formulas, not evidence for an untested parameter.
