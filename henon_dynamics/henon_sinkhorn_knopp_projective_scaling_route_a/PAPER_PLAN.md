# C191 paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

Classify alternating matrix scaling on every nonnegative support stratum and
connect the positive Hilbert contraction to the exact local rate, while
proving that convergence supplies no Route-A primitive-orbit owner.

## Manuscript structure

1. Freeze the row-then-column clock and define support, total support and full
   indecomposability.
2. State the source-owned all-matrix convergence/scaling theorem and the exact
   uniqueness boundary.
3. Write the positive scaling-vector map and derive the Birkhoff coefficient.
4. Differentiate in log coordinates to obtain `S^T S` and
   `sigma_2(S)^2`.
5. Present exact zero-pattern and rational-matrix evidence as regression only.
6. Close the recurrence, rate, zero-pattern and Route-A boundaries.
7. Separate classical ownership, package derivations, limitations and
   declarations.

## Revision contract

- Round 0: support/total-support/full-indecomposability theorem skeleton.
- Round 1: projective contraction, local Jacobian and exact boundary examples.
- Round 2: source ownership, hostile evidence, recurrence proof and strict
  Route-A stop.

The final paper must retain both an English and Chinese abstract, verified
citations, a concrete progress sentence, explicit limitations, code/data
availability, AI-use disclosure and no external-review language.
