# HCS-C333 research question

For the relaxed randomized pairwise-gossip process on the complete graph,
can one close the full finite-dimensional second-moment dynamics, rather than
only bound an averaging time?  More precisely, the package asks for the
complete invariant-block decomposition and endpoint-aware spectrum of

\[
\mathcal T_\eta(A)=\binom N2^{-1}\sum_{i<j}W_{ij}AW_{ij},
\qquad W_{ij}=I-\eta(e_i-e_j)(e_i-e_j)^{\mathsf T},
\]

on symmetric matrices over the disagreement space, together with exact
covariance evolution, the sharp mean-square law, almost-sure consensus, and
all low-dimensional and endpoint faces.

The source owner is a stochastic distributed-averaging algorithm.  The
question does not ask for arithmetic local data, an Euler product, a target
zero match, or a Hilbert--Polya operator.
