# Source audit

## Verified owner

Stephen Boyd, Arpita Ghosh, Balaji Prabhakar, and Devavrat Shah,
"Randomized Gossip Algorithms," *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*
52(6) (2006), 2508--2530, DOI
[`10.1109/TIT.2006.874516`](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2006.874516).
The authors also maintain a bibliographic page and manuscript at
<https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/gossip.html>.

The paper is the source owner for randomized pairwise averaging and its
network interpretation.  The C333 manuscript derives the frozen complete-
graph, relaxed-parameter second-moment block formulas directly.  It makes no
claim that this representation-theoretic calculation is absent from the
literature and no priority claim for gossip algorithms.

## Registry collision boundary

- C183 owns the random-transposition chain on permutations.  It reappears only
  as the `eta=1` boundary, where gossip ceases to dissipate energy.
- C203 owns deterministic signed-Laplacian consensus and pseudoforest
  determinant identities, not iid rank-one edge products.
- C312 owns nonlinear state-dependent Hegselmann--Krause averaging and finite
  termination, not state-independent random pair selection.
- C322 owns Kac's continuous-angle collision operator and sharp kinetic gap,
  not relaxed coordinate averaging.

## Scope audit

The source contains no rational-prime labeling, target Euler factors, root
number, automorphy, target divisor/counting law, functional equation, target
zero match, or Hilbert--Polya operator.  The finite exact evidence is an
implementation receipt and cannot promote the source theorem across those
boundaries.
