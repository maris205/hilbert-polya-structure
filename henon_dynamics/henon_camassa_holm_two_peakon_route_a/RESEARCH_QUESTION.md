# Research question

For the Camassa–Holm momentum equation

```text
m_t + u m_x + 2 u_x m = 0,   m=u-u_xx,
```

what is the complete real dynamics of an ordered signed two-peakon ansatz,
including the exact same-sign scattering law, the opposite-sign collision
time and blow-up scale, and a transparent energy ledger for a declared
post-collision continuation?

The question freezes physical CH time, the kernel `exp(-|x|)`, the order
`q_1<q_2`, and the energy normalization
`E=p_1^2+p_2^2+2p_1p_2 exp(-(q_2-q_1))`.  It asks for a theorem on this
finite-dimensional invariant manifold, not a classification of arbitrary
PDE solutions.
