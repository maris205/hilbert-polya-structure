# Audited C22 closure and closed C23 baseline

## C22G decision after the final theorem audit

HCS-C22G is retained as a **conditional analytic blueprint / closure note**.
The earlier statement that all corrected gates G1--G5 passed is withdrawn.

The exact part that survives is:

- the common one-step lifted Hénon cross domains and correct BPS mixed-data
  convention;
- the rational pinning clearances, projective pole exclusion, and Jacobian
  bounds;
- the explicitly frozen tangent-fibre and product-contour conventions;
- the finite-dimensional block identity

  \[
  \det DR=-\partial_z h\,\det(I-DF),
  \]

  with its raw residue minus sign for the ordered orientation
  \(dx\wedge dm\wedge du\);
- the exterior identity and the corresponding candidate parity shift
  \(k\mapsto k+1\);
- exact code regressions for constants, graph metadata, block algebra,
  exterior algebra, and chronology mutations.

The following gates remain open:

- **G2:** all-word composition of the vector cross kernels, including the
  iterated half-inverse numerator, chronological cocycle, and contour signs;
- **G3:** an explicit enlarged-domain factorization proving nuclearity of
  order zero, the approximation property, and locally uniform nuclear-ideal
  holomorphy in \(s\);
- **G4:** equality of the canonical nuclear trace with the diagonal contour
  and exact fixed-point residues for periods one, two, repetitions, and
  general \(n\);
- **G5:** jointly entire Fredholm factors and a meromorphic continuation on
  \(\mathbb C^2\), which are conditional on G2--G4.

The symbolic producer does not close these functional-analytic gates.  Its
`pass` fields record finite algebra and declared metadata, not an all-period
nuclear theorem.

G0's conceptual-novelty gate remains negative: even if G2--G5 are later
proved, the mechanism is a quantitative Hénon implementation of classical
Ruelle--Rugh/Grothendieck--Lefschetz machinery, not a new construction of the
Riemann divisor.  No further C22 finite sections, memory windows, or contour
mutations are authorized without first supplying the missing theorem lemmas.

## HCS-C23: fixed-word cyclic-resultant baseline

The successor experiment is recorded in
`../henon_adelic_lefschetz_ramification/`.  It asked whether arithmetic
nontransversality of chronological Hénon fixed schemes survives full Galois
packetization and produces a new cross-period law.

For a primitive word \(w\), retain

\[
F_w=H_{a_{w_{n-1}}}\circ\cdots\circ H_{a_{w_0}}
\]

and its finite-free fixed algebra \(A_w\).  With
\(M_w=DF_w\), define

\[
L_{w,r}=\det(I-M_w^r),
\qquad
\Delta_{w,r}=\operatorname{Norm}_{A_w/R}(L_{w,r}).
\]

The exact packet multiplier polynomial

\[
P_w(X)=\operatorname{Norm}_{A_w/R}
\left(X^2-\operatorname{tr}(M_w)X+1\right)
\]

satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{w,r}=\operatorname{Res}_X(P_w(X),X^r-1)
}.
\]

Thus the complete repetition tower for each fixed word is a classical
cyclic-resultant sequence.  Its recurrence, reconstruction, and divisibility
properties are baseline consequences rather than a new Hénon arithmetic
mechanism.

Finite chronology separation nevertheless survives packetization:

- the period-seven same-bigram pair separates at \(\ell=11\);
- the period-eight same-trigram pair separates at \(\ell=3\).

These are exact finite arithmetic chronology certificates, not an
all-period cross-word law, a strong-divisibility theorem, or a Zsigmondy
theorem.  No explicit relation between multiplier polynomials of distinct
chronology words was frozen before the proposed broad ledger.

Under the preregistered fast-kill rule, the larger scan was therefore
cancelled and HCS-C23 is

\[
\boxed{\texttt{CLOSED\_AT\_CYCLIC\_RESULTANT\_BASELINE}}.
\]

No Euler product is authorized.  The data may be reopened only after a
falsifiable cross-word or cross-period formula is stated that is not forced
by the fixed-word cyclic-resultant sequence, reciprocal-polynomial controls,
or reversible-map controls.

## Final scope

C22 closes with exact analytic geometry and finite residue algebra but open
operator-theorem gates.  C23 closes with exact packetized chronology
separation but a classical fixed-word cyclic-resultant repetition tower.
Neither project supplies an arithmetic primitive law, the Riemann divisor,
