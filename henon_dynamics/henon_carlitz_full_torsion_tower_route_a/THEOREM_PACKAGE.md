# Theorem package

The complete derivation is proof/ANALYTIC_PROOF.md; the final self-contained manuscript is paper/main_round2.pdf.

Dependencies: ring-action construction and separability → cyclic torsion and exact multiplier clocks → annihilator proof of C_P reduction → prime-power Eisenstein and full Galois action → finite-prime ramification and disjoint composite conductors → compatible towers and complete lower ramification groups.

Different exponent at residue size Q and level k is Q^(k-1)[k(Q-1)-1], proved both by the ramification-group sum and the derivative of the primitive polynomial. The local integral-basis proof uses distinct valuations modulo e_k, so the ramification calculation is not a generator-only assertion without an integer-ring owner.
