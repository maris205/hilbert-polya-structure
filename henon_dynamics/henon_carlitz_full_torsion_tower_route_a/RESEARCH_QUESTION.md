# Research question

Can the intrinsic polynomial-prime arithmetic of the full Carlitz ring action be closed, in one independently auditable theorem package, from every finite annihilator stratum through every prime-power Galois layer to compatible infinite towers and their ramification?

Answer: the analytic proof gives cyclic modules, exact multiplier transients and cycles, critical Carlitz reduction, primitive Eisenstein polynomials, full finite and profinite Galois ownership, disjoint conductors and lower ramification/different. These classical source results do not answer the separate target Riemann spectral question.
