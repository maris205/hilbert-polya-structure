# Assumptions and normalizations

Let q be any prime power, A=F_q[theta], K=F_q(theta) exactly, and C_theta(X)=theta X+X^q. Conductors are monic and nonzero; P is monic irreducible; k>=1. Multipliers b range over A, including zero and nonunits. Generic characteristic is not characteristic zero.

Use v_P(P)=1, and v_L(lambda_k)=1 for the primitive local uniformizer. Conductor level k is not multiplier time n. The inverse tower uses C_P(lambda_(k+1))=lambda_k. The infinity place, maximal abelian extension, arbitrary base-field extensions and general rank-one twists are not added to the contract.
