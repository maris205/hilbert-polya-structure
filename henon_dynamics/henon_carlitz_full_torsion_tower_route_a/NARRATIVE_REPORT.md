# Narrative report

The source advances from a single additive polynomial to an intrinsic coefficient-ring action with all conductor modules. Exact annihilators explain both periodic and transient dynamics. The critical reduction lemma enables an Eisenstein polynomial at every prime-power level; this proves the full Galois action rather than only an injection. Local ramification separates the different prime towers and closes arbitrary conductors. Compatible roots and local integral bases then give the infinite unit tower, complete lower filtration and different.

The conclusion is source progress, not target promotion. Function-field irreducibles and their local ramification do not solve the rational-prime clock and target divisor problems. The overall exploratory judgement records a genuine source carrier while preserving A2–A4 failures.
