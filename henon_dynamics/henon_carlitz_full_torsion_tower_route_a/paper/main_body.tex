\begin{abstract}
We study the full Carlitz ring action over $\F_q(\theta)$, for every prime
power $q$, rather than one additive polynomial in fixed characteristic.
Every conductor torsion module is cyclic, and its annihilator strata give
the exact preperiod and eventual period of every multiplier. Fixed-point
counts and finite determinants follow without treating nonunit multipliers
as permutations. An annihilator argument proves the specific reduction
$C_P(X)\bmod P=X^{q^{\deg P}}$.
\ifnum\CRevisionRound>0
Primitive polynomials at every prime-power level are Eisenstein. Their
roots generate the full unit-group Galois action; finite-prime ramification
proves linear disjointness and the corresponding theorem at every conductor.
\fi
\ifnum\CRevisionRound>1
Compatible towers realize completed local unit groups. Explicit integral
bases give all lower ramification groups and two matching formulas for the
different. Independent finite-ring orbit enumeration audits 107 conductors
and 77 prime-power cases, including a nonprime constant field.
\fi
The source theorem is a synthesis of classical Carlitz cyclotomic theory,
not a literature-priority claim or a target Riemann spectral construction.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Carlitz module; torsion dynamics; annihilator strata;
prime-power towers; ramification; source determinant.

\section{Frozen source and the complete torsion dynamics}
Put $A=\F_q[\theta]$, $K=\F_q(\theta)$, and
$C_\theta(X)=\theta X+X^q$. The base field is exactly $K$.
For $a=\sum a_j\theta^j$, define $C_a=\sum a_j C_\theta^{\circ j}$.
Constants from $\F_q$ commute with $C_\theta$, so
$C_{a+b}=C_a+C_b$ and $C_{ab}=C_a\circ C_b=C_b\circ C_a$.
For monic nonzero $a$, induction gives $\deg_X C_a=q^{\deg a}$ and
$C_a'=a\ne0$. Thus $\Lambda_a=\ker C_a\subset K^{\rm sep}$ has exactly
$q^{\deg a}$ distinct points. Generic characteristic refers to the injective
coefficient map $A\to K$; the field still has characteristic $p$ when
$q=p^e$. Reduced torsion means multiplicity one, not Lyapunov stability.

The classical ring and cyclotomic framework is discussed in Conrad's
author exposition \cite{Conrad} and in the tower background of
Angl\`es--Bandini--Bars--Longhi \cite{ABBL}. The following proofs do not use
their Iwasawa main conjecture. Our contribution within this repository is
an all-conductor ring-action and ramification package: C384 studied a
single map $x+x^p$ and its wild geometric zeta, while general finite-map
determinants were already available in C204. Those finite formulas alone
are not credited as the new mechanism.

\paragraph{Round 0 advance.}
We close the full annihilator/clock classification and the critical
prime-reduction lemma, including nonunit and zero multipliers.

\begin{theorem}[All conductor modules and multiplier clocks]
For every monic nonzero $a$, $\Lambda_a\cong A/a$ as an $A$-module.
Points of exact annihilator $D\mid a$ have population
$\Phi(D)=|(A/D)^\times|$, with $\Phi(1)=1$.
For $b\ne0$, define
\[
 D_* =\prod_{P\nmid b}P^{v_P(D)},\qquad
 h_D=\max_{P\mid b}\left\lceil\frac{v_P(D)}{v_P(b)}\right\rceil .
\]
The empty maximum is zero. Under $C_b$, a point in this stratum has exact
preperiod $h_D$ and eventual period $\ord_{D_*}(b)$, with $\ord_1(b)=1$.
For $b=0$, every nonzero point reaches the fixed point zero in one step.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $P$ irreducible and $k\geq1$, put $Q=q^{\deg P}$.
There are $Q^k$ points in $\Lambda_{P^k}$ but only $Q^{k-1}$ in
$\Lambda_{P^{k-1}}$. Choose $\lambda_k$ in their difference. Its annihilator
ideal contains $(P^k)$ but not $P^{k-1}$, hence is $(P^k)$.
The injection $A/P^k\to\Lambda_{P^k}$ sending $r$ to $C_r(\lambda_k)$
is bijective by cardinality. If $u,v$ are coprime and $ru+sv=1$, then
$x=C_{sv}(x)+C_{ru}(x)$ splits $\Lambda_{uv}$ into its $u$ and $v$
torsion, with zero intersection by Bezout. Induction proves the CRT and
cyclicity. Exact-annihilator-$D$ points are the classes $(a/D)r$ with
$r\in(A/D)^\times$, giving their stated count.

The annihilator after one step is $D/\gcd(D,b)$, because
$C_c(C_bx)=0$ if and only if $D\mid cb$. After $j$ steps the exponent
at $P\mid b$ is $\max(v_P(D)-jv_P(b),0)$, whereas other exponents
are unchanged. A strictly decreasing annihilator cannot occur on a cycle.
Exactly at $h_D$ the remaining annihilator is $D_*$, where $b$ is a unit.
Return after $n$ steps is then equivalent to $D_*\mid b^n-1$.
The assertions for $b=0$, for $b=1$, and for the one-point module $a=1$
follow directly from $C_0=0$ and $C_1=X$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Finite source counts and determinant]
For $n\geq1$ and every $b\in A$,
\[
 F_n(a,b)=\#\operatorname{Fix}(C_b^n|\Lambda_a)
        =q^{\deg\gcd(a,b^n-1)},\qquad
 O_n=\frac1n\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)F_d.
\]
For pullback $U_b$ on all complex functions on $\Lambda_a$,
\[
 \det(I-zU_b)=\prod_{d\geq1}(1-z^d)^{O_d},\qquad
 \exp\left(\sum_{n\geq1}F_nz^n/n\right)=\det(I-zU_b)^{-1}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The kernel of multiplication by $c=b^n-1$ on $A/a$ consists of the
multiples of $a/\gcd(a,c)$, giving its size. Set $\gcd(a,0)=a$.
Every finite functional graph is a disjoint union of directed cycles with
incoming trees, so $F_n=\sum_{d\mid n}dO_d$ and M\"obius inversion gives
integer nonnegative $O_d$. Ordering vertices by distance to a cycle makes
the point-map matrix block triangular: the transient block is nilpotent,
and a length-$d$ cycle contributes $1-z^d$. Transposing gives the pullback
formula; expansion of $-\log(1-z^d)$ proves the formal zeta identity.
\end{proof}
In particular, a nonunit multiplier is not generally a permutation of the
whole torsion module. The two clocks here are multiplier iteration $n$ and
conductor depth $k$; neither is declared to be a rational-prime logarithm.

\section{The critical Carlitz reduction}
\begin{lemma}
For every monic irreducible $P$, reduction of coefficients modulo $P$ gives
$\overline C_P(X)=X^Q$, where $Q=q^{\deg P}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The reduced polynomial is monic of degree $Q$ and has derivative zero.
Suppose it has a nonzero root $\gamma$ over $\overline{\F_Q}$.
The map $a\mapsto\overline C_a(\gamma)$ has a proper annihilator ideal:
it contains $(P)$, while $C_1(\gamma)=\gamma\ne0$.
Maximality of $(P)$ identifies this annihilator as exactly $(P)$, so the
ring orbit has $Q$ distinct elements. Commutation makes them all roots
of $\overline C_P$. But a degree-$Q$ polynomial with derivative zero in
characteristic $p$ has every root of multiplicity at least $p$, and cannot
have $Q$ distinct roots. Thus zero is the only root; monicity gives $X^Q$.
\end{proof}
This proves a specific ring-action property. It is not an assumption that
an arbitrary additive polynomial reduces to Frobenius.

\section{All prime-power Galois towers and composite conductors}
\paragraph{Round 1 advance.}
We add primitive irreducibility, full Galois surjectivity, valuation
ownership, and a finite-prime proof of disjointness at arbitrary conductors.

\begin{theorem}
For $k\geq1$, let $e_k=(Q-1)Q^{k-1}$ and
\[
 \Psi_P=C_P/X,\qquad \Psi_{P^k}=\Psi_P\circ C_{P^{k-1}}.
\]
This is a monic $P$-Eisenstein polynomial of degree $e_k$ whose roots
are exactly the primitive $P^k$-torsion points. For any such root $\lambda_k$,
\[
 L_{P^k}=K(\lambda_k),\qquad
 \Gal(L_{P^k}/K)=(A/P^k)^\times,
 \quad \sigma_u(\lambda_k)=C_u(\lambda_k).
\]
The prime $P$ is totally ramified, $v_P(\lambda_k)=1/e_k$, and every
other finite prime is unramified. For all monic $a$,
$\Gal(L_a/K)=(A/a)^\times$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The identity $C_P(Y)=Y\Psi_P(Y)$ gives
$C_{P^k}=C_{P^{k-1}}\Psi_{P^k}$. All roots of the left side are simple,
so the quotient has precisely the primitive roots. Reduction gives
$\overline\Psi_{P^k}=X^{e_k}$, while its constant coefficient is exactly
$P$. Thus every nonleading coefficient is divisible by $P$, but the
constant coefficient is not divisible by $P^2$. Eisenstein proves
irreducibility. A primitive root generates the entire torsion module, so
its field is the splitting field. Galois acts faithfully by units; the
irreducible degree equals the number of units, proving surjectivity.

At a place over $P$, an Eisenstein root has positive valuation. Otherwise
the monic term is uniquely minimal. With positive valuation every
nonconstant, nonleading term has valuation greater than one; cancellation
forces $e_kv_P(\lambda_k)=1$. Its denominator forces ramification index
at least $e_k$, and the global degree bounds it above. Hence the prime
has one place, residue degree one, and total ramification.

At a finite $R\ne P$, the monic polynomial $C_{P^k}$ has derivative
$P^k$, a unit. Its residue roots are distinct in a finite residue extension.
Pass to the associated unramified extension of the complete field $K_R$.
Hensel lifting gives all the roots there, so the splitting field is
unramified at $R$.

Now form a compositum of distinct-prime towers inductively. At the next
prime $P$, the previous compositum is unramified, while the new tower is
totally ramified. Their intersection is both. A subextension of a globally
totally ramified extension has one place with local degree equal to global
degree; an extension both total and unramified has degree one. Hence the
intersection is $K$. The two Galois extensions are linearly disjoint.
Multiplying degrees and using the module CRT identifies the compositum
with $L_a$ and gives exactly $(A/a)^\times$.
\end{proof}
Infinity ramification and the full maximal abelian extension are outside
this theorem; constant extensions are not silently added.

\section{Compatible towers, lower groups, and the different}
\paragraph{Round 2 advance.}
We complete the infinite tower and local integer-ring argument, derive all
ramification jumps, and confront source-to-target promotion with controls.

Choose compatible primitive roots by solving $C_P(\lambda_{k+1})=\lambda_k$.
Such a root is killed by $P^{k+1}$ but not by $P^k$, since otherwise
$C_{P^{k-1}}(\lambda_k)=0$. Restriction of automorphisms is unit reduction
modulo $P^k$, which is surjective. Compatible restrictions glue on the union,
so as profinite groups
\[
 \Gal\left(\bigcup_{k\geq1}L_{P^k}/K\right)
 =\varprojlim_k(A/P^k)^\times=A_P^\times.
\]
The infinite quantifier follows from these compatible algebraic maps,
not from a finite truncation.

\begin{theorem}[Exact lower filtration and different]
Normalize $v_{L_k}(\lambda_k)=1$. For $u\ne1$ and
$s=v_P(u-1)<k$, the ramification displacement is
$v_{L_k}(\sigma_u\lambda_k-\lambda_k)=Q^s$. Consequently
\[
\begin{split}
 G_0&=(A/P^k)^\times,\\
 G_i&=1+P^s\pmod {P^k}
 \quad (Q^{s-1}\leq i\leq Q^s-1,\ 1\leq s<k),\\
 G_i&=\{1\}\quad(i\geq Q^{k-1}),\qquad
 d_k=Q^{k-1}\bigl(k(Q-1)-1\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $u-1=P^sv$ with $v$ a unit. Then
$\sigma_u\lambda_k-\lambda_k=C_v(C_{P^s}\lambda_k)$ is primitive
of level $k-s$, so its base valuation is $1/e_{k-s}$ and its $L_k$
valuation is $e_k/e_{k-s}=Q^s$.

We must justify testing ramification on one generator. The powers
$1,\lambda_k,\ldots,\lambda_k^{e_k-1}$ form a field basis. In an
expansion $x=\sum_j a_j\lambda_k^j$, nonzero summands have valuations
$e_kv_P(a_j)+j$, distinct modulo $e_k$. The minimum is unique. Thus $x$
is integral exactly when all $a_j$ are integral, proving
$\mathcal O_{L_k}=\mathcal O_{K_P}[\lambda_k]$.
The difference of any integral polynomial in $\lambda_k$ under $\sigma$
is divisible by $\sigma\lambda_k-\lambda_k$ with integral quotient.
The minimum difference valuation is attained on $\lambda_k$ itself.
The lower-group definition $v_{L_k}(\sigma x-x)\geq i+1$ for every
integral $x$ now gives the stated intervals and group sizes.

Summing their contributions gives
\[
 d_k=(e_k-1)+\sum_{s=1}^{k-1}(Q^s-Q^{s-1})(Q^{k-s}-1)
     =Q^{k-1}(k(Q-1)-1).
\]
Independently, differentiate
$C_{P^k}=C_{P^{k-1}}\Psi_{P^k}$ at $\lambda_k$ to get
$P^k=\lambda_1\Psi'_{P^k}(\lambda_k)$. The monogenic different formula
therefore yields $ke_k-Q^{k-1}$, the same answer. When $k=1$ the
intermediate intervals are empty; when also $Q=2$, the degree is one,
the group is trivial and the different exponent is zero.
\end{proof}

\section{Evidence, controls, and HEN-O373}
The exact grid covers $q=2,3,4,5$. Conductors have degree at most three
for $q=2,3$, and at most two for $q=4,5$: 107 cases in total.
Every residue multiplier is included, with its complete preperiod/period
population and fixed counts through time twelve. The 77 prime-power cases
use irreducibles of degree at most three for $q=2$ and at most two otherwise,
$1\leq k\leq3$, and $q^{k\deg P}\leq256$.
The nonprime field is $\F_4=\F_2[w]/(w^2+w+1)$.

The producer uses annihilator formulas and left composition. The independent
checker imports no producer code: it enumerates every finite functional
graph, constructs annihilators from residue classes, uses right composition,
and obtains primitive polynomials by exact division. It also enumerates
unit displacements for the ramification histogram. SymPy checks exact
polynomial, finite-matrix, field-table and different identities. Two fresh
working directories replay the same frozen producer source. Repaired-hash
mutations test semantic and exact-type failures; these are not statistical
tests or independent copies of the source tree.

Three structural controls locate the arithmetic carrier. The rank-zero
parent $C_a(X)=aX$ has no nonzero torsion. A distinct-prime conductor $PQ$
has a CRT product, while $P^2$ has a principal-unit filtration. Finally,
$\theta X+uX^q$ is geometrically conjugate to the frozen rank-one action:
choose $v$ with $uv^{q-1}=1$ and conjugate by $X\mapsto vX$.
This conjugacy need not descend to $K$, so arbitrary twists do not inherit
the base-field Galois theorem without further work.

The strict Route A tuple is structural arithmetic relation at A0, weak A1,
and failures at A2, A3 and A4; the overall judgement is exploratory.
The source owns polynomial primes, exact torsion and ramification. It
does not identify them with rational primes or construct logarithmic
rational-prime orbit clocks. None of the six mandatory strong-A1 controls
is complete. Source finite determinants have no target divisor ownership;
no target completion, functional equation, counting law, Weil compression,
quantization or Hilbert--Polya domain is supplied.

\section{Conclusion}
The full ring action, rather than an isolated additive-map census, makes
annihilator ideals and coefficient primes intrinsic to the source.
\ifnum\CRevisionRound>0
Primitive Eisenstein polynomials close the full finite Galois and
composite-conductor construction.
\fi
\ifnum\CRevisionRound>1

their ramification filtration.

The audit records exact finite regressions separately from the all-parameter
proof; no finite evidence or formal determinant upgrades the target claim.
