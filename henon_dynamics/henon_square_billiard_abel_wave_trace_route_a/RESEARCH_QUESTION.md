# C157 research question

Can the square's natural Dirichlet half-wave quantization be connected exactly
to the full clean-family length/repetition ledger by an Abel-regularized trace,
with the Weyl, axis, interior, and boundary terms retained separately and with
the analytic branch structure controlled?

C157 answers yes on the source side for `Re(s)>0` and its Abel boundary.  It
does not turn the clean families into isolated orbits, construct an
isolated-orbit determinant, or compare the trace with any target spectrum.
