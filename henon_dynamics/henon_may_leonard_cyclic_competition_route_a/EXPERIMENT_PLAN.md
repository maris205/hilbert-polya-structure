# Exact-evidence plan: HCS-C358

## Claim-driven lanes

1. Derive the exact `S`, `log(xyz)` and `log R` identities on a rational
   parameter/state panel spanning both cyclic orientations and all three
   `a+b` phases.
2. Reconstruct the coexistence eigenvalue signs and the exact boundary-cycle
   orientation.
3. On `a+b=2`, verify the normalized field preserves the simplex and `uvw`,
   and independently verify the scalar quartic behind the period quadrature.
4. Verify the logistic population chart and its critical time change.
5. Run a producer-independent checker, a SymPy derivation, isolated byte
   replay, strict parser tests, and repaired-hash hostile mutations.

## Analytic-only claims

The finite lanes do not prove positivity for arbitrary initial data, LaSalle
exhaustion, uniqueness of the stable manifold, the full boundary omega-limit
set, diverging residence times, or the periodic-leaf theorem.  Those claims
must remain in the numbered proof.

## Stop conditions

Release stops if the sign of `(log R)'`, the exceptional diagonal, either
cycle orientation, the critical time change, the quartic factor, a scope flag,
or either evaluator digest fails an independent lane.
