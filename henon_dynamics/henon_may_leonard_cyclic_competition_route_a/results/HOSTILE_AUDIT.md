# Hostile audit: HCS-C358

The repaired-hash hostile suite rejects 67 of 67 attacks.  Mathematical
mutations alter the normalized-product coefficient or sign, a phase label,
coexistence eigenvalues, critical conservation, quartic velocity, logistic
chart, edge orientation, and declared closure faces.  Contract mutations
alter the candidate or obstruction identity, source baseline, epoch,
evaluator digest, evidence role, strict tuple, Route-B lock, or a false scope
flag.

Parser attacks include duplicate JSON/YAML keys, nonfinite JSON constants,
YAML aliases, anchors, merges, implicit dates, unknown fields, and scalar type
changes.  Each JSON attack repairs its payload hash before checking, so a
rejection cannot be credited merely to stale outer hashing.

This audit is deliberately orthogonal to the analytic proof.  It establishes
that the executable certificate detects convention and contract corruption;
it does not turn a finite test panel into a global dynamics theorem.
