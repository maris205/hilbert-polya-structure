# C145 narrative report

Rule 90 supplies a different Route-A subtype: a local linear cellular
automaton with exact finite-volume spatiotemporal cycles.  Encoding a cyclic
configuration as a Laurent polynomial turns one time step into multiplication
by `x+x^{-1}`.  Clearing the denominator reduces every temporal fixed-point
problem to a polynomial gcd.

The key proof avoids the common squarefree trap.  For multiplication by `h`
on `k[x]/(f)`, the kernel is the ideal generated by `f/gcd(f,h)`, so its
dimension is the gcd degree even when `f` has repeated factors.  This directly
covers even circumferences.  Möbius inversion then separates exact-period
points from fixed points, and only afterward may exact points be divided by the
period to count cycles.

The `24 x 24` table exposes essential two-clock structure.  Minimum-area
claims are domain-qualified: area 3 in the full positive domain, area 6 after
excluding length/time one, and area 12 when nonzero exact-period content is
required.  A separate `L=5` control gives the same fixed count at times 3 and 6
but five versus zero primitive cycles.  Hence neither area nor one snapshot of
fixed points retains the ordered-clock geometry.

This is meaningful A1 progress, but also a precise A2 obstruction: there is no
single frozen circumference and clock from which a target determinant has
been constructed.  The conservative tuple therefore remains
`(A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)`.
