# C145 experiment plan

1. Freeze Rule 90, the ordered clocks `(L,n)`, and the distinction among fixed
   points, exact-period points, and temporal cycles.
2. Implement polynomial arithmetic over `F_2`, clear the Laurent denominator,
   and evaluate the gcd formula for `1<=L,n<=24`.
3. Apply exact Möbius inversion for every fixed circumference and verify
   nonnegativity and divisibility by `n`.
4. Search explicit bounded domains for minimum-area aspect-ratio witnesses and
   freeze every search-domain qualifier in the evidence.
5. Build an independent binary-matrix/rank checker, selected direct state
   enumeration, a full SymPy gcd path, byte replay, and at least twenty
   repaired-hash mutations plus one stale-hash mutation.
6. Draft and compile a paper, perform two genuine internal theorem/scope
   reviews, implement their repairs, and preserve all three PDF stages.
7. Close a self-excluded manifest over exactly 27 payload files.

The table cutoff validates the implementation and witnesses; it does not
restrict the all-positive-integer theorem.
