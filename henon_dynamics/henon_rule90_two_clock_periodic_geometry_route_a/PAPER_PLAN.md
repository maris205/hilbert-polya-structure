# C145 paper plan

## Title and thesis

**Exact Two-Clock Periodic Geometry of Cyclic Rule 90.**  The paper derives an
all-size gcd formula, exact temporal-cycle inversion, and explicit evidence
that neither area nor a single fixed-point count preserves the two-clock
geometry.

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| all-size fixed-point formula | Laurent clearance plus multiplication-kernel lemma |
| even non-squarefree lengths included | ideal proof and `L=6,n=2` sentinel |
| exact points and cycles | Möbius inversion and finite-orbit divisibility |
| tori counted by `Fix(L,n)` | time-zero-row bijection |
| aspect ratio matters | explicitly qualified area 3, 6, and 12 witnesses |
| one fixed count loses history | `L=5`, times 3 and 6 control |
