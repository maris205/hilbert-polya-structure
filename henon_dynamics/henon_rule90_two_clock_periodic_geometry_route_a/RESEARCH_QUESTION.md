# C145 research question

For Rule 90 on a cyclic binary lattice of circumference `L`, can every
spatiotemporal `L x n` torus and every primitive temporal cycle be counted by
an exact formula valid for all positive `L,n`, including even, non-squarefree
spatial moduli?  Does collapsing the ordered clocks `(L,n)` to their area, or
retaining only one fixed-point count, preserve the periodic geometry?

The frozen object is multiplication by `x+x^{-1}` on
`F_2[x,x^{-1}]/(x^L-1)`.  Fixed configurations, exact-period points, and
temporal cycles are kept distinct.  The theorem is all-size; the `24 x 24`
ledger is a replay cutoff only.

The intended progress is an exact two-clock cellular-automaton geometry and a
proved information-loss obstruction.  No single target determinant, target
divisor, arithmetic/local data, operator lift, or Route-B input is in scope.
