# Research question: HCS-C363

For the planar parabolic--elliptic Keller--Segel system

\[
\partial_t\rho=\Delta\rho-\nabla\!\cdot(\rho\nabla c),
\qquad -\Delta c=\rho,
\]

can one give a self-contained analytic route from conservation and free
energy to the exact mass `8 pi`, a quantitative supercritical obstruction,
and the critical stationary family, while keeping all regularity and moment
hypotheses visible?

The target theorem must distinguish statements valid for smooth decaying
solutions from those that additionally require a finite second moment.  It
must explain why the stationary critical profiles do not contradict the
zero-slope virial law, derive the radial cumulative equation, and state what
is not proved about weak continuation or subcritical convergence.

The Route-A audit asks separately whether this mass-critical PDE supplies
intrinsic prime data, a prime orbit clock, a determinant bridge, the target
analytic package, or a natural target-zero quantization.  No target-side data
may be imported.
