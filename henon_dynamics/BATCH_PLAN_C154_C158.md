## Frozen sequence and required progress

1. **C154 -- a single-orbit Thue--Morse/period-three heteroclinic
   interface.**  On the alphabet `{0,1,2,3,4}`, join a left period-three
   background to a right one-sided Thue--Morse tail and take the two-sided
   orbit closure of that single interface point.  Prove its exact
   decomposition into the Thue--Morse component, the period-three orbit, and
   the heteroclinic orbit.  Its complete two-sided `Z`-orbit is dense by
   construction, while the interface orbit is wandering and the
   nonwandering set is the union of the two limiting components.  Because
   the interface points are isolated, distinguish this full-orbit statement
   from standard forward (`n>=0`) topological transitivity and prove that the
   latter fails.  Prove for every `n>=1` that

   ```text
   #Fix(sigma^n)=3 if 3 divides n, and 0 otherwise,
   zeta_AM(z)=1/(1-z^3).
   ```

   Thus the periodic orbit belongs to one orbit closure generated by a
   single dense full orbit rather than a freely declared topological disjoint
   union, although the system is nonminimal, not forward transitive, and
   carries only one primitive cycle.
2. **C155 -- full-period concentration in the Mersenne Rule-90 family.**
   For `L=2^r-1`, `r>=2`, retain the frozen Rule-90 multiplier and its
   `2^(L-1)`-point periodic image.  For every nonidentity clock `j`, reduce
   its fixed space to `d=gcd(j,L)` and prove the dimension bound
   `dim Fix(a^j)<=2d<=2L/3`.  Deduce that the fraction of periodic states
   whose exact period is smaller than `L` is at most
   `2L*2^(-L/3)`.  If `C_L` is the number of temporal cycles, prove the
   Burnside estimate

   ```text
   abs(L*C_L/2^(L-1)-1) <= 2L*2^(-L/3).
   ```

   Hence a uniform periodic state has exact period `L` with probability
   tending to one and the average cycle length divided by `L` tends to one.
   The neighboring power-of-two nilpotent family remains the matched
   negative control.
3. **C156 -- the primary quadratic module of Heisenberg fibre rotations.**
   For the frozen C151 automorphism, prove the Fibonacci--Lucas factorizations

   ```text
   A^n-I = L_n [[F_(n+1),F_n],[F_n,F_(n-1)]]  for odd n,
   A^n-I = F_n [[L_(n+1),L_n],[L_n,L_(n-1)]]  for even n.
   ```

   Their quotient determinants are `-1` and `-5`.  Determine the resulting
   Smith types `(Z/L_n)^2` and `Z/F_n x Z/(5F_n)`, write the iterate cocycle
   as one explicit quadratic polynomial, and replace the coarse projector
   order `2D_n^2` by the proved divisor bound `L_n` for odd `n` and `5F_n`
   for even `n`.  Decompose the finite quadratic module into its coprime
   primary summands and prove multiplicativity of the zero-rotation count
   across those summands.  This is source-internal primary decomposition,
   not a target arithmetic factorization or horizontal-character formula.
4. **C157 -- a Dirichlet Abel trace for square-billiard clean families.**
   For `Re(s)>0`, define the genuine Dirichlet Abel half-wave trace

   ```text
   W_D(s)=sum_(j,k>=1) exp(-pi*s*sqrt(j^2+k^2)).
   ```

   With one fixed branch, prove by two-dimensional Poisson summation that

   ```text
   W_D(s)=s/(2*pi) sum_(m in Z^2)(s^2+4*abs(m)^2)^(-3/2)
          -1/4-1/(exp(pi*s)-1).
   ```

   Separate the Weyl and axis terms, then regroup the nonaxis lattice vectors
   by positive primitive direction and repetition.  For
   `L_(a,b)=2*sqrt(a^2+b^2)`, the resulting interior contribution must have
   coefficient `2s/pi` and denominator
   `(s^2+r^2*L_(a,b)^2)^(3/2)`.  Prove absolute regroupability in the declared
   half-plane and locate the Abel boundary branch singularities at the clean
   family lengths and repetitions.  No isolated stability determinant or
   external target trace is inferred.
5. **C158 -- full-cycle secular scaling for the open Walsh gate.**  Let the
   two nonzero one-site eigenvalues be `lambda_+` and `lambda_-`.  For
   `C_k=B_k^k=A^(tensor k)`, prove the complete degree-`2^k` factorization

   ```text
   det(I-z*C_k)=product_(j=0)^k
     (1-z*lambda_+^j*lambda_-^(k-j))^binom(k,j).
   ```

   Normalize the nonzero eigenvalues by their binomial multiplicities and
   prove an exact log-modulus mean `-log(3)/4`, variance
   `(log(abs(lambda_+)/abs(lambda_-)))^2/(4k)`, exponential concentration,
   weak convergence to the corresponding point mass, and the centered
   square-root-`k` Gaussian limit.  The paper must not claim a phase limit,
   self-adjoint realization, or target divisor.  Closed, projector-order, and
   moved-hole controls remain separate.
