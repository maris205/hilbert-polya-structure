# C156 research question

For the frozen C151 Heisenberg automorphism, can one replace the coarse
`2D_n^2` rotation denominator by the exact exponent of the horizontal fixed
class group, identify that group for every iterate, and turn the central
rotation into an orthogonal primary quadratic-module decomposition whose
zero count factors exactly?

C156 answers all structural parts affirmatively.  It does not claim a closed
formula for every zero count, an isolated-orbit determinant, or any
target-facing arithmetic factorization.
