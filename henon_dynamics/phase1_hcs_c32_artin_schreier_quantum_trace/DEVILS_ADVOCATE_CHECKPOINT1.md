# HCS-C32 Devil's Advocate Report -- Checkpoint 1

## Verdict: PASS TO PHASE 2 ONLY

The revised research question is answerable and the methodology addresses the
main logical failures.  This verdict authorizes a primary-source and novelty
audit.  It does **not** authorize implementation, an Euler product, a Route-A
promotion, or a paper claim.

## Critical Issues That Block Progression

No critical issue remains after the Stage-1 revision to one fixed
Artin--Schreier kernel, one nontrivial character sector, explicit

gate.

The candidate becomes blocked immediately if Phase 2 cannot identify a

## Major Issues

### 1. The strongest current theorem is mostly a generic cubic baseline

- **Type:** Novelty / alternative explanation.
- **Location:** Primary RQ; Methodology WP4--WP5.
- **Problem:** Fourier--chirp unitarity holds for any phase multiplier.  The
  rank \((3-1)^n=2^n\) and weight-\(n\) purity follow from the smooth highest
  cubic form and are shared by broad families of polynomials.  The equality
  \(2^n=2^n\) with the Hénon fixed-scheme length is not by itself a new
  correspondence.
- **Impact:** Without more, the project would be a routine specialization of
  Deligne's exponential-sum theorem with Hénon notation.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the uncoupled and matched-cubic controls.  Require
  the scheme-theoretic identity
  \(\operatorname{Crit}(\Phi_n)=\operatorname{Fix}(H_6^n)\), the cyclic Hill
  identity, and at least one additional Hénon-specific structural consequence
  that survives the controls.  A formal stationary-equation calculation alone
  is not sufficient for paper promotion.

### 2. A different cohomology group appears at every dynamical time

- **Type:** Scope / operator interpretation.
- **Location:** Primary RQ; Methodology WP4.
- **Problem:** Deligne's theorem produces
  \(V_{n,p}=H_c^n(\mathbb A^n,\Phi_n^*\mathcal L_{\psi_0})\), whose dimension
  changes with \(n\).  These are not powers of one fixed finite-dimensional
  Frobenius operator.
- **Impact:** A list of fixed-\(n\) local spectra would not yet be one
  dynamical zeta or Hilbert--Pólya spectrum.
- **Recommendation:** The revision to one raw kernel
  \(\mathcal K_p=\mathcal L_{\psi(S_6)}\) is mathematically coherent:
  !-convolution and diagonal compact-support trace recover the complexes
  \(V_{n,p}\).  Phase 2 must verify all shifts and signs.  This fixed-kernel
  packaging is standard Fubini/sheaf--function formalism and resolves logical
  compatibility, not novelty.  Do not claim one fixed cohomological spectrum
  across \(n\).

### 3. The local Weil statement is not a self-adjoint spectral theorem

- **Type:** Claim scope / Route-A bias.
- **Location:** RQ scope; Methodology limitations and decision rules.
- **Problem:** Purity puts normalized Frobenius eigenvalues on the unit circle
  after a complex embedding.  It does not provide a canonical positive inner
  product, Frobenius semisimplicity, a self-adjoint operator, or a canonical
  logarithm of the phases.  Duality pairs the \(\psi_0\) and
  \(\psi_0^{-1}\) sectors, not generally one sector with itself.
- **Impact:** Calling this a finite Hilbert--Pólya theorem or a functional
  equation would be false.
- **Recommendation:** Retain the current firewall.  Report the exact local
  determinant/reciprocal-determinant parity only.  Route B remains closed.

### 4. The sign, isotypic sector, and normalization are structural

- **Type:** Method / convention validity.
- **Location:** Methodology WP4--WP7.
- **Problem:** For one nontrivial character sector,
  \[
  E_{p,n}(r)=(-1)^n\operatorname{Tr}
  (\operatorname{Fr}_p^r\mid V_{n,p}).
  \]
  The full Artin--Schreier cover has \(p-1\) nontrivial sectors and a trivial
  contribution.  For odd \(n\), normalization by \(p^{n/2}\) is not a
  canonical half-Tate twist.
- **Impact:** Dropping the sign, using the full cover while retaining rank
  \(2^n\), or silently choosing a square root changes the object.
- **Recommendation:** Freeze geometric versus arithmetic Frobenius, the
  nontrivial character, the complex embedding, and determinant parity.
  Require fail-closed mutations for each convention.

### 5. Scheme length is not a rational orbit count or a canonical basis

- **Type:** Evidence / hasty generalization.
- **Location:** Critical-scheme rank comparison.
- **Problem:** The fixed scheme is finite flat of length \(2^n\), but
  \(\#\operatorname{Fix}(H_6^n)(\mathbb F_p)\) need not equal \(2^n\).
  Some good-characteristic fibres can also be Hessian-degenerate.
- **Impact:** One cannot label the \(2^n\) cohomology dimensions by rational
  periodic points or infer an orbit basis from dimension equality.
- **Recommendation:** Separate scheme length, geometric support, rational
  point count, étaleness, and cohomology dimension in every artifact.  Use the
  Hill identity to flag degeneracy rather than discard it.

### 6. The finite-field system is not the real HCS-C31 survivor

- **Type:** Scope / relevance.
- **Location:** Motivation.
- **Problem:** The construction uses the same integral Hénon polynomial but
  not the certified four-state real basic set or its positive instability
  roof.
- **Impact:** It is a legitimate Hénon system switch, not an arithmetic fibre
  placed directly over the C31 real survivor.
- **Recommendation:** Say so explicitly.  The real-side action Livšic gate may
  later supply a comparison, but no reduction/quantization equivalence should
  be assumed.

## Observations

- The fixed-kernel formulation is the correct large-door version: one
  integral phase produces the finite unitaries, their chronological powers,
  and all fixed-time trace complexes.
- The most promising extra theorem is a vanishing-cycle or local Fourier
  statement that links Hessian degeneracy and Hénon multipliers without
  inventing an orbit-indexed basis.
- If the arithmetic route reduces to a baseline, the universal
  \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) derivative-representation collapse is the strongest
  theorem-level negative pivot.  The real action Livšic certificate is a
  smaller positive auxiliary result.

## Strongest Counter-Argument

> This is a discrete Fourier transform times a cubic chirp.  Its path sum is
> the usual trace of a kernel power; its sheaf convolution is Fubini; its
> rank and purity are the generic Deligne theorem for a smooth leading cubic.
> Hénon appears only because the stationary equations were written in Hénon
> form.  No cross-prime compatible system, primitive Euler product, global
> divisor law, or self-adjoint operator has been constructed.

The project defeats this objection only if Phase 2 identifies a genuine
Hénon-specific theorem delta and Phase 3 certifies it independently.
