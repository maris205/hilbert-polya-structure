# C135 exact experiment plan

## Goal

Certify a transition-sensitive nonlattice suspension and identify exactly
what remains aggregated after passing from symbol counts to edge counts.

## Gates

1. Freeze the full binary shift, ordered roof matrix, clock, and determinant.
2. Reconstruct `Delta=1-x00-x11+x00*x11-x01*x10` independently.
3. Prove the rooted trace and primitive product at every period.
4. Enumerate periods one through ten only as an exact replay ledger.
5. Represent roof times by coefficient vectors in the basis
   `(1,sqrt(2),sqrt(3),sqrt(6))`; use no floating radicals.
6. Require exact separation of `000111` and `001011`.
7. Require exact retention of the `001011`/`001101` nonrotation collision.
8. Prove `N01=N10` and the invisibility of `tau01-tau10`.
9. Recover C130 as the destination-symbol roof control.
10. Require independent checker, independent SymPy, byte replay, 42 repaired
    semantic hashes, and one stale-hash mutation.
11. Save real round0/round1/round2 PDFs, double-build the final source at a
    fixed epoch, and require embedded fonts with zero layout/reference warning.
12. Close exactly 27 payload files under a self-excluded manifest.

Orbit-injectivity, off-diagonal orientation recovery, target matching,
arithmetic Euler factors, natural quantization, or Route-B claims block release.
