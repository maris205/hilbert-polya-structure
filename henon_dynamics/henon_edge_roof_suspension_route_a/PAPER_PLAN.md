# C135 paper plan

## Title

Edge-Count Separation and Residual Orbit Collisions in a Nonlattice Binary
Suspension

## Claims--evidence matrix

| Claim | Exact evidence |
|---|---|
| Edge-weighted full shift has an explicit determinant | direct `2x2` determinant in four variables |
| Trace and primitive product hold at every period | formal logarithmic determinant regrouping |
| The roof is nonlattice | fixed-cycle lengths `1` and `sqrt(6)` |
| Distinct edge vectors have distinct times | rational basis of `Q(sqrt(2),sqrt(3))` |
| `000111` and `001011` are separated | vectors `(2,1,1,2)` and `(1,2,2,1)` |
| Orbit injectivity still fails | primitive nonrotation pair `001011/001101` |
| Off-diagonal orientation is invisible | all closed words satisfy `N01=N10` |
| C130 is recovered as a negative control | destination-symbol roof makes the requested pair collide |

## Paper-writing phases

1. Edge-roof suspension and formal determinant.
2. All-period trace and primitive product.
3. Nonlattice edge-sector separation and requested pair.
4. Remaining collision and orientation-blindness theorem.
5. Validation, progress over C130, and strict Route-A boundary.
