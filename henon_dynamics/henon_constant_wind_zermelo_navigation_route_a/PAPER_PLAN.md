# Paper plan

Title: **Constant-Wind Zermelo Navigation Beyond the Randers Chamber**.

1. State the all-dimensional control model and one complete theorem.
2. Derive the exact-time ball and reduce every reachability question to one
   quadratic inequality.
3. Classify weak, critical, and strong wind, explicitly selecting the smaller
   strong root and deriving the entire attainable-time set.
4. Prove equality-case uniqueness of the constant saturated control.
5. Derive HJB, homogeneity, rotations, velocity scaling, and Mach-cone
   regularity; close zero-wind/cap/target faces.
6. Add evidence, hostile audit, collision/scope firewall, Route-A verdict,
   reproducibility, and AI-use statements.

Round 0 contains the reachability/value theorem. Round 1 adds optimizer,
HJB, regularity, time-set and degenerate-face proofs. Round 2 adds exact
evidence, hostile validation, collision and Route-A closure.
