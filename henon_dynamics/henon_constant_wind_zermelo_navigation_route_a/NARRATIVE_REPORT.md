# Narrative result

C305 takes a full step across a new continuous-time control subtype. Instead
of solving only the familiar weak-wind formula, it closes weak, critical,
and strong wind in one theorem. The strong chamber is qualitatively
different: a target has a finite window of attainable times, and minimum
time is the smaller of two positive roots. The theorem also proves unique
constant control, HJB, symmetries, cone regularity, and all zero-data faces.

The result is exact but source-local. Travel times and Mach cones provide no
arithmetic local data, primitive-orbit ledger, target functional equation,
or operator realization, so Route A is rejected in all five branches.
