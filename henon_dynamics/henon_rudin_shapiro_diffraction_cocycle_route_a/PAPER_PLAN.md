# Paper plan — HCS-C248

The paper is organized around one theorem-scale advance rather than five
independent slices:

1. Freeze the four-letter substitution and its ±1 factor.
2. Prove primitivity, uniform frequencies, and aperiodicity with an explicit
   desubstitution boundary.
3. Derive the dyadic Hadamard cocycle, exact energy identity, and unit-circle
   square-root bound.
4. Derive the four Laurent correlation recurrences and explain how their
   normalized van Hove limit gives \(\delta_0\) and Lebesgue diffraction.
5. Report exact receipts, independent controls, and the strict Route-A
   negative boundary (diffraction is not the full dynamical spectrum).

Revision 0 establishes definitions and the main identities.  Revision 1 adds
the primitivity/aperiodicity proof and correlation boundary.  Revision 2 adds
the exact certificate counts, hostile audit, reproducibility protocol, and
explicit nonclaims.  `main.tex` is the revision-2 source.
