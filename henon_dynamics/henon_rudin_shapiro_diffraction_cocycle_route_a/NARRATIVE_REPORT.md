# Narrative report — HCS-C248

The C248 object is a deterministic, primitive, aperiodic substitution whose
binary factor has unusually transparent harmonic control.  The Hadamard pair
\((P_k,Q_k)\) keeps total energy exactly equal to \(2^{k+1}\), so the usual
square-root estimate is an immediate norm consequence rather than a numerical
fit.  The same two-by-two structure closes after adjoining the four Laurent
products \(R,S,T,U\).  This gives an auditable route from substitution to
two-point correlations.

The certificate deliberately reports two different levels of evidence.  The
all-\(k\) algebraic identities are proved in the manuscript; dyadic rows,
frequency rows, correlation rows, and mismatch rows are finite exact receipts
that expose implementation details.  The checker reconstructs them from
literal rules, while SymPy expands the identities independently.

The standard two-sided hull has \(\delta_0\) autocorrelation and Lebesgue
diffraction under the stated averaging convention.  This does not imply a
purely absolutely continuous *dynamical* spectrum, nor does it create a
periodic primitive-orbit atlas.  Accordingly the Route-A verdict remains
negative despite the strong source-local theorem.
