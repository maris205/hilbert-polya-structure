# Research question — HCS-C248

Can one turn the Rudin–Shapiro constant-length substitution into an exact,
auditable Route-A dynamical certificate without confusing finite polynomial
receipts with periodic orbits or confusing diffraction with the complete shift
spectrum?

The answer established here is yes for the following source-local statement.
The substitution matrix is primitive (a positive third power), its invariant
frequency vector is uniform, and the ±1 factor obeys the two-polynomial
Hadamard recursion.  Expanding the four Laurent products gives a closed exact
correlation cocycle.  Under the explicitly fixed symmetric Cesàro/van Hove
convention, the standard Rudin–Shapiro autocorrelation is \(\delta_0\), so its
Fourier transform is Lebesgue measure.

The answer is deliberately no for the stronger target question: there is no
shift-periodic primitive-orbit atlas, no target divisor or zero-set match, and
no arithmetic or Hilbert–Pólya identification.  Thus A1, A2, and A3 are not
promoted by the finite receipts.
