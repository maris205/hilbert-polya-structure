# Source audit

The source-native normal-mode context was checked against primary publisher
records:

- A. R. Mohebalhojeh and D. G. Dritschel, “Hierarchies of Balance
  Conditions for the f-Plane Shallow-Water Equations,” *J. Atmos. Sci.* 58
  (2001), 2411--2426, DOI
  `10.1175/1520-0469(2001)058<2411:HOBCFT>2.0.CO;2`.
- R. Salmon, “Semigeostrophic theory as a Dirac-bracket projection,” *J.
  Fluid Mech.* 196 (1988), 345--358, DOI `10.1017/S0022112088002733`.

Mohebalhojeh--Dritschel supplies the f-plane doubly-periodic normal-mode
context.  Beta-plane/Rossby literature is deliberately not used: the frozen
model has constant f, and no equatorial conclusion is imported.  The finite
certificate proves the displayed algebra independently and makes no priority
or novelty claim.
