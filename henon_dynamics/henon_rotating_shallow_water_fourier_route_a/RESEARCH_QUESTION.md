# Research question

What is the complete same-clock spectral atlas of the constant-\f-plane
rotating shallow-water flow on a doubly periodic domain?  The answer is more
than a list of plane waves: one obtains an orthogonal zero/\(± iω\) spectral
resolution for every lattice mode, exact geostrophic and inertia--gravity
coordinates, arithmetic-free shell multiplicities, and sharp operator-class
boundaries.  The result also specifies precisely when a finite Fourier
superposition is periodic and when degeneracies change the stationary space.

The torus is (2π)-periodic and (f,c) are source-local real parameters.
This package does not identify the lattice spectrum with an arithmetic target.
