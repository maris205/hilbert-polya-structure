\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

An iterated splitting field can have many independent global generators
while acquiring very little ramification at a particular place. For
the cubic
\[
  f(X)=X^3+aX^2\qquad (\charac k=3,\ a\neq0),
\]
this distinction determines the entire generic inverse-image tower.
The finite critical point is fixed, and infinity is wildly ramified.
At height $n$, a quadratic radical step produces $3^n$
Artin--Schreier equations. Their classes are independent over the global
function field, but become proportional over each geometric completion
at infinity. Tracking these two ranks simultaneously yields both the
Galois group and the ramification at every height.

The groups that occur here are already known. Benedetto, Faber, Hutz,
Juul and Yasufuku introduced the signature-constrained ternary groups
$E_n$, computed their orders, and realized them for the cubic
$-2X^3+3X^2$ in a number-field setting; their geometric realization is
given in Corollary~1.3 of \cite{benedetto2017cubic}.
Theorem~2.3.1 of Bouw--Ejder--Karemaker \cite{bouw2021belyi}
places these groups in the wider theory of normalized dynamical Belyi
maps. Ejder \cite{ejder2022arithmetic} uses the same local product-sign
description in studying arithmetic monodromy. Thus neither $E_n$ nor
the formula for its order is a new group-theoretic object in this paper.

Those characteristic-zero Belyi realizations do not supply the wild
local calculation required here. Our map has the two geometric branch
values $0$ and $\infty$, with ramification divisible by the
characteristic at infinity. The elementary square-root and
Artin--Schreier normal form retains all constants in $a$, so the result
does not require a square root of $a$ or an initial extension of the
base field. This also makes it possible to descend the geometric group
calculation to every characteristic-three field.

Recent general monodromy results have different characteristic
hypotheses. The version of Adams--Hyde \cite{adams2025unicritical}
cited here treats unicritical power polynomials of degree prime to the
characteristic. The cited version of Hlushchanka--Lukina--Wardell
\cite{hlushchanka2025cubic} treats a number-field cubic setting and
discusses extension to characteristics prime to two and three.
Neither stated scope includes the present characteristic-three family.
These comparisons concern the specified sources and versions; they
are not an assertion of exhaustive literature priority.

The main results answer one question: what are the generic splitting
fields of $f^{\circ n}(X)-t$, together with their geometric branch data,
for all $n$? Three parts of the answer are useful to distinguish.
First, a single compatible labelling of the inverse-image tree gives
arithmetic and geometric group $E_n$ over an arbitrary base field.
Second, over algebraically closed constants, the square-class relations
and the two Artin--Schreier ranks are determined exactly. Third, the
resulting ramification indices give explicit different exponents and
genera. The substantive calculation is the simultaneous global/local
induction, not a list of orders or low-height examples.
