\section{Conclusion and scope}\label{sec:conclusion}

The cubic $X^3+aX^2$ realizes the classical group $E$ over every
characteristic-three field, without enlarging the constant field.
Its geometric ramification is determined at all heights by the
same induction that proves maximality of the tree group. The
zero-place parity vectors detect exactly the sibling square
relations. Distinct Kummer characters then separate all global
Artin--Schreier classes, while cancellation of their principal
parts makes the local class space at infinity one-dimensional.
The different and genus follow from this local information by
standard formulas.

The theorem is specific about its domain. The parameter $a$ is
nonzero, the characteristic is three, and the target $t$ is
transcendental. Specializing $t$, changing the characteristic,
or replacing the inverse-image height by forward iteration time
requires separate arguments. In particular, the result supplies
no formula for ordinary periodic points and no assertion that a
finite specialization has the full generic Galois group. The
geometric branch data likewise make no residue-degree assertion
over a non-algebraically-closed constant field.

Finally, an inverse-image Galois tower and its genera are source
arithmetic data. No target Euler factors, functional equation,
root number, correspondence with zeros, or Hilbert--P\'olya
operator is constructed here. The conclusion is the explicit
wild tower and the simultaneous global/local rank mechanism
proved above.
