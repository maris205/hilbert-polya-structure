Let $k$ be any field of characteristic three and let $a\in k^*$.
We determine the splitting fields of
$(X^3+aX^2)^{\circ n}-t$ over $k(t)$ for every inverse-image height
$n\geq1$, with $t$ transcendental. Their arithmetic and geometric
Galois groups coincide with the classical signature-constrained ternary
groups $E_n$, and have order
$2^{3^{n-1}}3^{(3^n-1)/2}$. The proof separates two different kinds of
independence. At each step the only relations among the relevant square
classes are the sibling relations, and all $3^n$ resulting
Artin--Schreier classes are globally independent. In contrast, after
extending the constants to an algebraic closure, these classes span only
one dimension at every completion above infinity. A simultaneous
induction on the tree group and this local rank gives the complete
geometric branch data: the ramification indices at zero and infinity
are $2^n$ and $2\cdot3^n$, and the corresponding different exponents are
$2^n-1$ and $3^{n+1}-2$. The geometric genus is
$1+\frac{|E_n|}{2}(\frac12-2^{-n}-3^{-n})$, giving genus $46$ at height
two. The result is a wild characteristic-three realization with
ramification data, not a new construction of the abstract groups
$E_n$. It concerns generic inverse images, not forward periodic points
or finite-field extension degrees.
