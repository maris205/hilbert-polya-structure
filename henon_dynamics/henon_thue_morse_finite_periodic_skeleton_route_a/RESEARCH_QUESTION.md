# C149 research question

What exact periodic layer is obtained by adjoining a finite, completely
controlled skeleton to the aperiodic minimal Thue--Morse component, and what
structural property must be surrendered?

Freeze the topological disjoint union
`Y=X_TM sqcup C_1 sqcup C_2 sqcup C_3 sqcup C_5`, with one componentwise
iterate as the clock.  The task is to count all fixed points and primitive
cycles at every period, derive the source Artin--Mazur zeta, and prove the
minimality obstruction for every nonempty finite disjoint attachment.  This
tests a deliberate symbolic-system subtype, not a target match.
