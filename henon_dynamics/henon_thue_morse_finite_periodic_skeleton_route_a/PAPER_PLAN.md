# C149 paper plan

## Title and thesis

**A Controlled Finite Periodic Skeleton Attached to an Aperiodic Minimal
Subshift.**  Exact periodic content can be added by a tagged disjoint union,
but every nonempty such attachment destroys minimality.

| all-period fixed formula | direct finite-cycle action |

| attachment destroys minimality | proper closed invariant cycle |
