# C149 narrative report

The preceding Thue--Morse certificate established that the minimal, uniformly
recurrent subshift has a complete periodic-orbit vacuum.  C149 asks the sharpest finite design
question: attach exactly four tagged cycles and account for every consequence.

The gain is exact.  A length-`ell` component contributes `ell` fixed points at
multiples of `ell`, one primitive cycle, and a zeta factor
`(1-z^ell)^(-1)`.  For lengths `1,2,3,5`, the complete source zeta is therefore
the product of four such factors.  No finite cutoff enters that derivation.

The cost is equally exact.  A tagged cycle is a proper nonempty closed
invariant subset of the union, so minimality is impossible.  The paper does
not soften this conclusion with an almost-minimal label, nor mistake the
attached points for elements of the Thue--Morse language.  The package thus
turns an informal repair into a controlled negative result: finite orbit
decoration is mathematically transparent but does not repair Route A.
