# Failure-mode audit

- Each primitive marked word receives its own factor.
- Repeated weights use q^(rS), not q^S.
- Primitive and repetition singularities are compared by strict binomial
  inequalities for all q>0.
- q=1 is locked to P68 coefficient by coefficient.
- Essential-singularity classification is local analytic, not numerical.
- No relative determinant, Lind identity, or arithmetic trace is claimed.
