# Methodology blueprint

1. Freeze P68's convention and P69's primitive moment polynomials.
2. Multiply one Euler factor per primitive marked reflection word.
3. Sum repetitions before taking any asymptotic.
4. Derive the two-variable primitive generating series.
5. Prove every nonleading primitive and repetition singularity lies farther
   from the origin.
6. Compare the resulting boundary to the mean-field boundary exactly.
7. Lock the q=1 specialization to P68.
