# Paper plan

## Central claim

All positive-parameter closed mass-action SIR flows are conjugate by physical
scaling to one phase portrait, whose integral simultaneously yields peak,
unique forward final size, branch selection, quadrature and sensitivity; a
strictly monotone coordinate closes global convergence and no recurrence.

## Structure

1. Freeze model, clock, data-free scope and nondimensionalization.
2. Prove global positivity and the phase integral.
3. Derive the peak and both final-size intersections, including `I0=0`.
4. Derive quadrature, sensitivity, equilibria and stability.
5. Present independent branch validation.
6. Apply Route-A and medical/target claim firewalls.

## Visual policy

No plot is needed.  A phase-plane drawing would be qualitative and less exact
than the invariant, branch inequalities and compact regime table.  Equations
and one table carry the essential relationships.

## Revisions

- Round 0: scaling, invariant, phase curve and peak.
- Round 1: global convergence, Lambert branch, quadrature, sensitivity and
  equilibrium boundary.
- Round 2: independent certificate, source ownership, infection-free branch,
  safety disclosure and strict Route-A evaluation.
