# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — theorem core

The initial manuscript defined the face product, stated the Brown--Diaconis
spectrum, and derived the characteristic polynomial, determinant, and trace
formulas.  It also gave the separation criterion and the principal mixing bound.

Hostile reading exposed two omissions: the nonseparating case was described too
briefly, and “strong stationary” could be read as asserting independence of the
stopped state and stopping time.

## Round 1 — probability and boundary repair

The revision added the full `A0`-component/stationary-simplex classification and
separated three distinct objects: the without-replacement exact sampler, the
with-replacement stationary stopping sampler, and the coupling time used in the
TV bound.  It explicitly declined the strict-SST independence claim.  The
oriented-matroid statement was confined to Section 6 of the primary source.

## Round 2 — ownership and release audit

The final revision added a claim-ownership table, made the finite-regression
role explicit, reported the independent checker/SymPy census, and stated every
Route-A gate and forbidden claim.  Tsetlin and coordinate walks remained
regression examples, not separate theorem increments.

The three retained PDFs are content-distinct.  Round 2 is the final paper and is
rebuilt twice under the fixed epoch before manifest closure.
