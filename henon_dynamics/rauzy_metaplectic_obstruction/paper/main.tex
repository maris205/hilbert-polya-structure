\begin{abstract}
We source-lock the labeled four-interval reversal permutation, reconstruct its
seven-state hyperelliptic Rauzy diagram in the stratum $\cH(2)$, and enumerate
its primitive labeled directed cycles through elementary length twelve while
preserving the chronological Kontsevich--Zorich cocycle.  There are 828
primitive labeled free cycles and 146 phase-invariant eventually-positive
labeled cycles.  Twenty-one of the latter have $\det(I-M)=0$, so the regular
point formula for the Weil distribution character cannot provide a finite
weight on the full selected labeled-cycle set.  We do not claim that all
codes are distinct primitive unmarked Teichm\"uller geodesics.  We also prove
that a nonzero absolutely norm-summable discrete
sum of metaplectic atoms is noncompact after central-sign-aware aggregation.
Together with an exact branch-compression lemma, this closes the unsmoothed
ordinary Fredholm-determinant form within either of two explicit realization
classes.  The application hypothesis on a particular canonical analytic
Zorich space remains open.  The result does not rule out a genuinely
distributional, semifinite, or continuously smoothed trace object.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and scope}

The proposed operator combines a Rauzy--Veech--Zorich base transfer operator
with the oscillator representation of its symplectic homology cocycle:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:operator}
  \cL_s^{\Mp}=\sum_h e^{-sr_h}K_h\otimes\mu(\widetilde B_h).
\end{equation}
Here $h$ is a chronological return branch, $r_h$ is its roof, and any
metaplectic lift must retain its central sign.  We ask only whether the
\emph{unsmoothed discrete operator} in \eqref{eq:operator} can be an ordinary
nuclear Fredholm object.  No prime or Riemann-zero data enter the construction.

The answer is negative in two natural and explicitly stated operator classes.
The conclusion must not be enlarged to flat traces, distribution characters,
semifinite determinants, or continuous group smoothing.

\section{The literal Rauzy system}

We fix
\[
 \pi_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&4\\4&3&2&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The prefix sets in its two rows disagree for every proper width, hence the
permutation is irreducible.  It is the four-letter central vertex of the
hyperelliptic Rauzy diagram described in \cite{amy}; no permutation is
substituted after inspection.

For a labeled permutation $\pi$, let $\Omega_\pi$ be the crossing matrix: an
entry is $+1$ when the ordered pair is top-forward and bottom-reversed, $-1$
in the opposite case, and zero otherwise.  At the base state,
\[
\Omega_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&1&1\\-1&0&1&1\\-1&-1&0&1\\-1&-1&-1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det\Omega_0=1.
\]
Thus $\operatorname{rank}\Omega_0=4=2g$.  The relation
$d=2g+s-1$ gives $s=1$, and the unique zero has order $2g-2=2$.
Consequently the system belongs to $\cH(2)$, with no relative-to-absolute
kernel.  These conventions follow \cite{yoccoz,zorich}.

If an edge has winner $w$ and loser $\ell$, we use the forward homology matrix
\begin{equation}\label{eq:edge}
 B_e=I+E_{\ell,w}.
\end{equation}
Later edges multiply on the left.  An open edge satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transport}
 B_e\Omega_{\mathrm{src}}B_e^T=\Omega_{\mathrm{dst}}.
\end{equation}
It generally does not preserve one fixed form.  A closed loop at $v$ instead
satisfies
\[
 B_w^T J_v B_w=J_v,\qquad J_v=\Omega_v^{-1}.
\]
Our exact reconstruction has seven states and fourteen directed edges and
checks \eqref{eq:transport} edge by edge.

\section{Chronological periodic ledger}

A cycle is encoded by directed edge tokens, quotiented by cyclic rotation but
not by reversal.  Proper powers are removed at the token level.  Entrywise
positivity is not invariant under a change of cyclic cut.  We therefore call
a free cycle eventually positive when every phase monodromy has a positive power.
The four-dimensional Wielandt bound makes this an exact finite test; in the
released range the largest observed exponent is three.

This selector also proves labeled Rauzy admissibility.  If
$M=B_{e_n}\cdots B_{e_1}$, the length-visitation product is $R=M^T$.  For a
positive Perron vector $\lambda_0$ of $R$, put
$\lambda_n=\lambda_0/\rho(R)$.  The identity
$\lambda_0=R\lambda_n$, factored edge by edge, gives
$\lambda_{i-1,w}=\lambda_{i,w}+\lambda_{i,\ell}>
\lambda_{i,\ell}=\lambda_{i-1,\ell}$ at every declared winner.  Thus the word
returns projectively with the registered chronology, and the normalized roof
telescopes to $\log(\|\lambda_0\|_1/\|\lambda_n\|_1)=\log\rho(R)$.

Throughout, ``primitive'' means not a proper power as a closed directed
edge-token word in the fixed-label Rauzy coding, modulo cyclic rotation only.
We do not audit the possible identifications that can arise after forgetting
labels, marked separatrices, or other marking data.  The counts below are
coded-orbit counts, not counts of pairwise distinct primitive closed
Teichm\"uller geodesics in unmarked moduli space.

the M\"obius--trace identity

\begin{proposition}[Fixed-vector singular cycles]\label{prop:singular}
Among the 146 eventually-positive primitive labeled cycles through length twelve,
exactly 21 satisfy $\det(I-M)=0$: six at length ten, six at length eleven, and
nine at length twelve.  For each of them,
$\det(I-M^r)=0$ for every $r\ge1$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The finite counts and factorizations are exact certificate statements checked
by an independent implementation.  Each listed reciprocal characteristic
polynomial contains $(x-1)^2$.  Hence $1$ remains an eigenvalue of every power,
which proves the all-repetition claim.
\end{proof}

\section{Two noncompactness theorems}

\begin{theorem}[Tensor essential norm]\label{thm:tensor}
Let $H_0,H_1$ be Hilbert spaces, let $K:H_0\to H_1$ be bounded, and let $U$
be unitary on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space $F$.  Then
\[
 \|K\otimes U\|_{\ess}=\|K\|.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix a unit vector $x\in H_0$ and an orthonormal sequence $(e_n)$ in $F$.
Then $x\otimes e_n\rightharpoonup0$.  A compact operator $C$ sends this
sequence to zero in norm, while
$\|(K\otimes U)(x\otimes e_n)\|=\|Kx\|$.  Hence
$\|K\otimes U-C\|\ge\|Kx\|$.  Supremizing over $x$ gives the lower bound;
the reverse inequality is immediate.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Branch compression]\label{cor:branch}
Under the assumptions of Theorem~\ref{thm:tensor}, let $X,Y$ be Banach
spaces and let $T:X\to Y$ be bounded.  If bounded maps
$J:H_0\otimes F\to X$ and $R:Y\to H_1\otimes F$ satisfy
$RTJ=aK\otimes U+C$ with $a\in\mathbb C$, $aK\ne0$, and $C$ compact, then
\[
 \|T\|_{\ess}\ge
 \frac{|a|\|K\|}{\|R\|\|J\|}>0.
\]
\end{corollary}

The next result removes the need for cylinder projections on analytic spaces.

\begin{theorem}[Discrete metaplectic atoms]\label{thm:atoms}
Let $d\in\mathbb N$, $d\ge1$, let $H_0,H_1$ be Hilbert spaces, and let
$\mu:\Mp(2d,\mathbb R)\to\mathcal U(L^2(\mathbb R^d))$ be the oscillator
representation.  Let $I$ be countable.  For each $h\in I$, let
$\widetilde g_h\in\Mp(2d,\mathbb R)$ and let $A_h:H_0\to H_1$ be bounded.
Suppose
\[
 T=\sum_{h\in I} A_h\otimes\mu(\widetilde g_h),
 \qquad \sum_{h\in I}\|A_h\|<\infty.
\]
Let $\pi:\Mp(2d,\mathbb R)\to\Sp(2d,\mathbb R)$ be the projection.  Choose
one lift $\widetilde g$ above every projected atom $g$ and write
\[
 \mu(\widetilde g_h)=\varepsilon_h\mu(\widetilde g),\qquad
 A_g=\sum_{\pi(\widetilde g_h)=g}\varepsilon_hA_h,
 \qquad \varepsilon_h\in\{\pm1\}.
\]
If some
$A_g\ne0$, then $T$ is noncompact.  More precisely,
\[
 \|T\|_{\ess}\ge
 \sup_{\|x\|=\|y\|=1}
 \left(\sum_g|\langle A_gx,y\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix unit vectors $x\in H_0$, $y\in H_1$ and slice the base variables.  It
remains to study
$V=\sum_g a_g\mu(\widetilde g)$ with
$a_g:=\langle A_gx,y\rangle$ and $(a_g)\in\ell^1$.  Choose a generic
phase-space vector $z$ outside the countable union of the proper subspaces
$\ker(g-h)$ for distinct atoms.  For a unit Schwartz vector $\phi$, put
$\phi_t=W(tz)\phi$, where $W$ is the Weyl representation.  Then
$\phi_t\rightharpoonup0$.

Metaplectic covariance gives
\[
 \mu(\widetilde g)W(tz)\phi
 =W(tgz)\mu(\widetilde g)\phi
\]
up to convention-dependent phases.  Weyl matrix coefficients vanish at
infinity, so off-diagonal inner products tend to zero because
$t(g-h)z\to\infty$.  The $\ell^1$ hypothesis dominates the double series and
yields
\[
 \|V\phi_t\|^2\longrightarrow\sum_g|a_g|^2.
\]
Compact operators send $\phi_t$ to zero in norm.  This proves the scalar
essential-norm bound; the input and output slices have norm one.
\end{proof}

\section{Distribution character versus ordinary trace}

For a unitary on an infinite-dimensional space, $|U|=I$ and
$\operatorname{Tr}|U|=\infty$.  Thomas \cite{thomas} defines the Weil
character distribution after smooth integration over the metaplectic group.
On the regular set $\det(g-I)\ne0$, that distribution has a smooth
representative whose amplitude is $|\det(g-I)|^{-1/2}$ and whose phase retains
the lift and Weil/Maslov data.  It is not the ordinary trace of the isolated
unitary.

Proposition~\ref{prop:singular} shows that the regular point formula cannot be
used on the entire selected labeled-cycle set.  Those 21 cycles are not to be
deleted or assigned a finite value by a cutoff.  Any surviving construction
must specify a genuine distributional or generalized trace operation.

\section{Decision}

Corollary~\ref{cor:branch} closes every bounded realization of
\eqref{eq:operator} that admits the stated nonzero branch compression, either
exactly or modulo compact operators.  Theorem~\ref{thm:atoms} also closes any
Hilbert-space analytic realization on
a half-plane where the branch norms are absolutely summable and one signed
aggregate is nonzero.  These hypotheses have not yet been verified on a
particular canonical analytic Zorich space.  Within either stated class,
noncompactness excludes all finite Schatten classes and ordinary or
finite-order regularized Fredholm determinants for this operator.

This is a scoped obstruction, not a Hilbert--P\'olya construction.  It does
not assert that $I-z\cL_s^{\Mp}$ is never Fredholm or invertible, and it does
not exclude flat traces, distribution characters, canonical semifinite
determinants, continuous group smoothing, or a different quantum fiber.
The Route-A verdict is
\[
 (\mathrm{A1\_WEAK},\mathrm{A2\_FAIL},\mathrm{A3\_FAIL},
  \mathrm{A4\_FORMAL\_HINT}),
\]
so Route B is not opened.

\section*{Reproducibility}

The producer emits the complete exact certificate.  A checker that does not
import the producer rebuilds the seven-word automaton and verifies the cycle
counts by M\"obius inversion.  Exact integers and rational root intervals are
authoritative; displayed decimals are diagnostics.  The accompanying
mutation suite rejects transposed edge matrices, reversed chronology,
move-word-only cycle identities, proper powers, phase-dependent filtering,
and finite weighting of the singular character locus.
