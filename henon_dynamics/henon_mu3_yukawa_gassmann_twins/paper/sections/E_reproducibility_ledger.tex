The promoted tuple has $15$ top-level payload keys and $10{,}412$ scalar
payload leaves.  It consists of $13$ code files and $8$ result files, with
$21$ live scoped entries and $20$ self-excluding manifest entries.  The
$48$-test suite passed.  The independent checker rebuilt the payload,
rejected $20{,}894$ one-leaf certificate mutations and eight evidence-rebind
mutations, and checked exact key sets and types.  These are machine-layer
facts, not a claim that the present manuscript has compiled or passed a
paper audit.

The finite computations use GAP~4.11.1 and TomLib~1.2.9
\citep{gap2021,tomlib2019}.  The Python checker is a separate replay path:
it consumes the promoted artifacts and reconstructs the certified
projections without calling the producer's theorem functions.  The source
and result manifest excludes itself, avoiding a self-hash cycle.  Formal
documents and this paper sit outside that historical machine tuple and
therefore require their own later audits.
