\section{The complete Gassmann table and field consequences}
\label{sec:gassmann}

\subsection{The complete collision table}

For a subgroup $H\leq G$, let
\[
 \chi_H=\Ind_H^G\one
\]
be the rational permutation character of $G$ acting on $G/H$.  The finite
calculation compares the complete $25$-entry character vector for each of all
$350$ subgroup conjugacy classes, not selected cyclic rows or sampled
Frobenius data.

The prior count of $350$ classes, $339$ profiles, and eleven collisions is
due to James \citep[\S3.1, p.~22]{james2022bayesian}.  The exact replay in
the fixed \texttt{U4(2).2} table yields the complete projection in
\cref{tab:collision-buckets}.

\begin{proposition}[Complete-table minimum]
\label{prop:table-minimum}
The equality $\chi_{H_+}=\chi_{H_-}$ holds on every conjugacy class of
$G$.  Among the eleven buckets in \cref{tab:collision-buckets},
$[301,303]$ is the unique bucket of minimum index, namely $320$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Grouping the $350$ complete character vectors by equality gives precisely
the eleven two-class buckets displayed in \cref{tab:collision-buckets}; all
other profiles occur once.  The common vector for rows $301$ and $303$ is
\[
(320,0,0,32,8,20,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0,16,0,0,0,0,4,0,0,0).
\]
The index entries in \cref{tab:collision-buckets} show that $320$ is strictly
smaller than the other ten bucket indices.  This proves uniqueness only
within the complete $W(E_6)$ table.
\end{proof}

The result is not inferred from a sample of good primes.  The exact carrier
contains all $350\times25$ character entries; its checksum-bound form and
the independent regrouping check are described in \cref{app:gassmann-audit}.

\subsection{Common normal closure and nonisomorphism}

\begin{lemma}[Normal closure from the core]
\label{lem:core-normal-closure}
For $F=K^H$, the normal closure of $F/\Q$ inside $K$ is
$K^{\Core_G(H)}$.  In particular, both $F_+$ and $F_-$ have normal closure
$K$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The conjugates of $K^H$ inside $K$ are $K^{gHg^{-1}}$.  Their compositum is
fixed by the intersection of their fixing groups:
\[
 \bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}=\Core_G(H).
\]
This compositum is the normal closure inside $K$.  Both cores are trivial by
\cref{tab:subgroup-invariants}, so both normal closures equal $K$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Common-normal-closure conjugacy criterion]
\label{lem:field-nonisomorphism}
The fields $F_+$ and $F_-$ are not isomorphic over $\Q$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Their subgroup abelianizations have invariant factors $[2,3]$ and $[2]$.
Thus $H_+$ and $H_-$ are not isomorphic and, in particular, are not
conjugate in $G$.  This subgroup distinction alone is not yet the desired
field statement.

Suppose instead that a $\Q$-isomorphism $F_+\to F_-$ existed.  Extend it to
an automorphism $\sigma$ of $\Qbar$.  By
\cref{lem:core-normal-closure}, both fields have the same normal closure
$K$, so $\sigma(K)=K$.  The restriction of $\sigma$ to $K$ belongs to $G$
and transports point stabilizers:
\[
 \sigma H_+\sigma^{-1}=H_-.
\]
This contradicts nonconjugacy.  Hence no such field isomorphism exists.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Artin formalism and the common zeta function}

For any subgroup $H\leq G$, Artin formalism gives
\begin{equation}
 \zeta_{K^H}(s)=L\!\left(s,\Ind_H^G\one\right).
 \label{eq:artin-zeta}
\end{equation}
This is the classical Gassmann--Perlis bridge
\citep[Theorem~1]{perlis1977equation}; permutation-character computations
in arithmetic equivalence are also treated by Bosma and de Smit
\citep[printed p.~68]{bosmadesmit2002small}.

\begin{proposition}[Zeta equality]
\label{prop:zeta-equality}
The two Dedekind zeta functions agree:
\[
 \zeta_{F_+}(s)=\zeta_{F_-}(s).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the split-prime noncollision bridge in
\cref{lem:split-noncollision}, $F_\pm=K^{H_\pm}$.  (The separate
\cref{cor:product-minimal} identifies the product-form minimal
polynomials.)  Substitute
$H=H_+$ and $H=H_-$ in \eqref{eq:artin-zeta}.  The full character identity
in \cref{prop:table-minimum} makes the two Artin $L$-functions identical.
\end{proof}

From the equality in \cref{prop:zeta-equality} alone we infer none of integral
permutation equivalence, isomorphic rings of integers, equality of class
numbers, local equivalence, adelic equivalence, or isomorphic idele groups.
The local tables in \cref{sec:local} separately disprove local and adelic
equivalence for the present pair.  Together,
\cref{prop:table-minimum,lem:core-normal-closure,lem:field-nonisomorphism,prop:zeta-equality}
prove \cref{thm:integrated}(ii)--(iii).
