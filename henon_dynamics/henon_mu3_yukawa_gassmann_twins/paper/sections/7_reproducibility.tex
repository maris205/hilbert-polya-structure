\section{Exact computation and the reproducibility boundary}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The proof separates finite premises from mathematical implications.  Exact
computation supplies embedded arrays, complete enumerations, and arithmetic
identities.  The preceding sections supply the bridges from those finite
facts to integrality, fixed fields, normal closures, zeta functions,
discriminants, and local products.  A machine Boolean is never substituted
for one of those implications.

\subsection{The G0--G7 evidence map}

The finite package is organized into eight gates.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2em,style=nextline]
\item[G0] Rebind the complete released C56 and C58 authority, including their
inventories, manifests, certificates, line carrier, and lower-filtration
arrays.
\item[G1] Reconstruct the primitive integral orbit sums, all $27$ lines and
four chart equations, the full Schlaefli automorphism set, and the split-prime
noncollision products.
\item[G2] Enumerate the complete $350$-class table, all full permutation
characters, the eleven buckets, and the durable subgroup invariants.
\item[G3] Bind the exact premises used by the fixed-field, common-normal-
closure, nonisomorphism, and Artin-formalism arguments.
\item[G4] Recompute the common orbit vector, signature, sign, conductor
exponents, support, and discriminant digest.
\item[G5] Enumerate every double coset and row in the \Tom{140} branch.
\item[G6] Prove the unique normal \Tom{140} inertia inside \Tom{206} and
enumerate the second complete local table without selecting a branch.
\item[G7] Enforce checker independence, exact schemas, mutation and source
rebound, scope leaves, and nonrelease status.
\end{description}

The producer and checker have disjoint theorem call graphs.  Common code is
limited to canonical input/output, fingerprints, and backend preflight.  The
checker rebuilds the complete scalar-leaf inventory, enforces exact key sets
and strict types, and compares independently generated projections of the
group and resolvent carriers.  Historical bounded pilots were used to select
the target, but they are not theorem authority and are not substituted for
the promoted G1 reconstruction.

\subsection{Finite audit totals}

The frozen code/results tuple contains $13$ source files and $8$ result
files, hence $21$ live leaves.  Its self-excluding scoped manifest binds $20$
entries.  The certificate payload has $15$ top-level keys and $10{,}412$
scalar leaves.  The independent checker rejected $20{,}894$ certificate
mutations and $8$ self-consistent evidence-carrier rebound mutations.  All
$48$ source tests passed.  A mandatory nonmutating replay and an independent
post-refresh audit read the same promoted bytes.

These counts certify a finite reconstruction envelope.  They do not prove
that the present prose is correct, that a TeX build succeeds, that a paper
has passed an independent manuscript audit, or that C59 has been promoted or
released.  The
principal artifact identities and the exact inventories appear in
\cref{app:reproducibility-ledger}.

\subsection{Software and conventions}

The group lane uses GAP~4.11.1, TomLib~1.2.9, SmallGrp~1.4.1, and
CTblLib~1.3.1.  These package and version strings are reproducibility
metadata.  The mathematical Python lane uses Python~3.12.3,
python-flint~0.9.0, SymPy~1.14.0, and NetworkX~3.5.  The table-of-marks
locators in this paper are meaningful only relative to that frozen version;
the exact $27$-point arrays remain authoritative.  The specific software and
table conventions used in the proof are those documented by official GAP and
TomLib sources
\citep{gap2021,tomlib2019}.

The literal firewall in \cref{thm:integrated} is part of the certificate as
well: a finite PASS creates no excluded analytic datum and constitutes
neither paper nor release approval.
