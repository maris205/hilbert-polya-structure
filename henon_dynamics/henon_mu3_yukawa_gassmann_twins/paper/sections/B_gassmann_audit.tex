\section{Complete restricted Gassmann audit}
\label{app:gassmann-audit}

The phrase ``complete'' in this appendix means complete in the fixed
$350$-class subgroup table, not complete among all finite groups or all
arithmetic-equivalence phenomena.  Each table row is represented by its
full rational transitive permutation character on the $25$ conjugacy
classes of $G$.  Equality of these vectors produces exactly the following
eleven collision buckets:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &[12,15], [17,21], [29,36], [31,39], [41,42], [46,48],\\
 &[57,58], [59,64], [112,120], [132,140], [301,303].
\end{aligned}
\]
Their indices, in the same order, are
\[
 12960,12960,8640,8640,6480,6480,6480,6480,3240,2880,320.
\]
Consequently the least index in the table at which two nonconjugate
subgroups have the same rational permutation character is $320$, and the
unique bucket at that index is $[301,303]$.  These are package positions
used to locate embedded representatives, and are therefore locators rather
than definitions.

For both $H_+$ and $H_-$, the common $25$-entry vector is
\[
 (320,0,0,32,8,20,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0,16,0,0,0,0,4,0,0,0).
\]
The complete $350$-row vector audit has digest
\[
 \hexsha{1738cb84d62b8011878d2af4c7929b894b24b1fb66a694c4a03266448bdc261c}.
\]
This is the object used in the proof of character equality; SmallGroup
identifiers are auxiliary cross-checks only.

For clarity, the logical test applied to a candidate pair is:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{B\arabic*.},leftmargin=3em]
\item compare all $25$ entries of $\Q[G/H]$;
\item require different conjugacy classes in the embedded group;
\item record subgroup order, index, core, normalizer order, and abstract
      identifier;
\item sort collision buckets by index and check that all $350$ rows were
      visited.
\end{enumerate}
The first two steps prove the Gassmann assertion, the third binds it to
durable invariants, and the fourth proves the stated restricted minimum.
The group-theoretic evidence file carrying these rows has digest
\[
 \hexsha{0b01f9d47e5141d2bff88fbe4d58ed049d88751cbf8ab1df5469009b684c4958}.
\]

The comparison with James's census is deliberately bounded.  That census
supplies an independent count of $350$ subgroup classes, $339$ rational
profiles, and eleven collisions in this group
\citep[\S3.1, p.~22]{james2022bayesian}.  The present audit adds a durable
identification of the degree-$320$ bucket in one labelled line action and
connects it to exact primitive resolvents and the two local branches; it
does not claim the first discovery of the abstract collision.
