\section{Global ramification ledger}
\label{app:global-ledger}

This appendix expands the arithmetic of \cref{sec:global}.  We order the
relevant subgroups as
\[
 (I_3,P_3,Q_3,I_5,P_5,C_3,C_2,C_\infty).
\]
The common orbit-count vector on the two degree-$320$ carriers is
\[
 (36,56,112,16,64,128,160,168),
\]
and the corresponding local subgroup locators are
\[
 (140,72,7,147,23,6,2,5).
\]
The locators identify rows in the frozen table; the embedded permutation
arrays, orbit counts, and filtration containments are the mathematical
data.

The lower-filtration formula of \cref{eq:permutation-conductor} gives
\begin{align*}
 v_3&=(320-36)+\frac{9}{18}(320-56)
        +6\frac{3}{18}(320-112)=624,\\
 v_5&=(320-16)+3\frac{5}{20}(320-64)=496,\\
 v_{C_3}&=320-128=192,\\
 v_{C_2}&=320-160=160.
\end{align*}
This displays the lower-filtration weights responsible for every exponent;
none is inferred from an expanded defining polynomial.

Let $q$ be the certified prime
\[
 q=14932047182473291995860108491583652133938007263719.
\]
The exact signed identity is
\begin{multline*}
 \Disc(F_\pm)=+
 3^{624}5^{496}181^{192}283^{160}997^{192}1801^{160}\\
 {}\cdot2346241^{192}q^{160}
 =+3^{624}5^{496}(181\cdot997\cdot2346241)^{192}
   (283\cdot1801\cdot q)^{160}.
\end{multline*}
Its unsigned no-newline decimal expansion has $11{,}658$ digits and digest
\[
 \hexsha{7f3ed0f731e5905f9af8254df2114ad15c2bb7d96cfa9a8b464a58ae8ea3ae70}.
\]
The decimal expansion itself is not part of the paper.

For completeness, the support proof has two inclusions.  The normal closure
$K$ is unramified outside
\[
 \{3,5,181,283,997,1801,2346241,q\},
\]
so its fixed fields are unramified outside this set.  Conversely, after
expanding the two grouped factors, every displayed prime occurs with a
positive field-discriminant valuation.  Therefore every one ramifies in
each $F_\pm$, proving equality with the displayed support.  The sign is
positive because the common signature is $(16,152)$.

Finally, Artin formalism compares the full permutation representations.  The
exact Dedekind zeta equality
\[
 \zeta_{F_+}(s)=L(s,\Q[G/H_+])
 =L(s,\Q[G/H_-])=\zeta_{F_-}(s)
\]
omits no bad place.  We have not computed and do not claim an explicit bad
Artin Euler polynomial or factor.  The literal scope flag
\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER} also excludes any decomposition
Frobenius, epsilon factor, root number, or Artin-holomorphy assertion.
