\section{Local branch ledger}
\label{app:local-ledger}

We give the exact one-line permutations behind the two local tables.  If
$[a_1,\ldots,a_{27}]$ is displayed, it denotes the permutation sending
$i$ to $a_i$.  These arrays define the local groups in the same labelled
degree-$27$ action as the arrays defining $H_\pm$.

\subsection{The ToM--140 branch}

The decomposition group is generated by
\begin{align*}
D_{140}=\langle
&[7,26,13,12,5,15,1,18,20,22,16,4,3,14,6,11,25,8,24,9,27,10,23,19,17,2,21],\\
&[17,2,21,4,18,1,15,5,20,3,11,12,22,14,25,16,6,8,19,23,10,27,9,24,7,26,13],\\
&[23,24,22,19,21,20,1,3,15,13,14,2,18,16,17,11,9,10,26,25,27,8,7,12,6,4,5]
\rangle .
\end{align*}
Here $I_{140}=D_{140}$.  The wild group $P_{140}$ is generated by
\begin{align*}
&[7,12,8,26,27,25,23,22,17,18,16,24,10,11,9,14,15,13,4,6,5,3,1,2,20,19,21],\\
&[25,12,18,26,22,15,20,13,1,5,16,24,21,11,23,14,7,27,4,17,8,10,6,2,9,19,3],
\end{align*}
and $Q_{140}$ is generated by
\[
[6,2,10,4,8,17,25,18,23,21,11,12,27,14,7,16,1,5,19,9,3,13,20,24,15,26,22].
\]
The containment audit certifies $Q_{140}\le P_{140}\le I_{140}\le
D_{140}$.  Applying
\[
 n=[D:J],\qquad e=[I:I\cap J],\qquad
 f=\frac{|D|\,|I\cap J|}{|I|\,|J|}
\]
to every double-coset representative $x$ with $J=D\cap xH_\pm x^{-1}$
gives exactly the rows of \cref{tab:c59-local-tom140}.  The representative
carrier was traversed completely: both sides have $36$ primes, each with
$f=1$.  Thus both sides have total residue degree $36$, as zeta equality
requires.  Their factor-degree multisets nevertheless differ because
$F_+$ has eight degree-one factors and $F_-$ has none.

Its inertia group is generated by

\end{align*}
The group $P_{206}$ is generated by the last two displayed generators of
$I_{206}$, and $Q_{206}$ by the last one.  The certified containments are
$Q_{206}\le P_{206}\le I_{206}\le D_{206}$.  The complete double-coset
traversal gives \cref{tab:c59-local-tom206}; both sides have $18$ primes,
each with residue degree $2$.  Their total residue degree is again $36$.
The separating invariant is different: $F_+$ has four degree-two
unramified factors, whereas $F_-$ has no factor of degree two.

\subsection{Why the tables are complete}

For each sign and each branch, the computation stores a canonical
representative for every double coset in $D\backslash G/H_\pm$, forms
$J=D\cap xH_\pm x^{-1}$, and records the quadruple
$(|J|,|I\cap J|,|P\cap J|,|Q\cap J|)$.  Aggregation is performed only after
the sum of the double-coset sizes has been checked against $|G|$.  The
reported totals also satisfy
\[
 \sum_x e_xf_x=[G:H_\pm]=320,
 \qquad \sum_x f_x=36,
\]
on both sides and in both branches.  The first identity checks the local
degree; the second reconciles the residue-degree information with the
ramified Euler factor.  The tables therefore distinguish the products of
local fields by their factor degrees without invoking any classification
of the individual local extensions.
