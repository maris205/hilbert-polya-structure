We construct two explicit degree-$320$ number fields from the labelled $27$
lines on one cubic surface: after scaling each line-eliminant root $d_i$ to
the algebraic integer $\alpha_i=Ld_i$, the fields are generated by quadratic
orbit sums supported on respectively $27+27$ and $81$ unordered pairs.
The known classification of Gassmann collisions in $W(E_6)$ identifies the
underlying pair abstractly, but it does not provide primitive generators in
the line coordinates or arithmetic that remains valid under the unresolved
$3$-adic decomposition-group choice.  We prove primitivity by exact support
stabilizers and $320$ distinct reductions at the completely split prime
$692717$, and we combine the complete $350$-class subgroup-character table
with double-coset calculations for both permitted decomposition groups.
The resulting nonisomorphic fields have the same Galois normal closure, equal
Dedekind zeta functions, and realize the unique minimum-index collision
within that complete $W(E_6)$ subgroup table.  Both fields have signature
$(16,152)$, exact finite ramified support consisting of eight primes, and
the same exact factorized positive discriminant.  Nevertheless, their finite
\'{e}tale $\mathbf Q_3$-algebras are nonisomorphic for each of the two
retained decomposition-group alternatives; no alternative is selected.
