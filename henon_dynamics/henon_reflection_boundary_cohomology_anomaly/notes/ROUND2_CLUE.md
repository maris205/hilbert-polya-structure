# ROUND2 clue

Let a period-`n` sampler use normalized weights `w_0,...,w_(n-1)`. Requiring
it to annihilate every coboundary for every function on the cycle should
force `w_j=w_(j-1)` and hence `w_j=1/n`. Prove this uniqueness and combine it
with P64 equidistribution to obtain a canonical gauge-invariant pressure for
every continuous observable.
