# Research question

For the one-dimensional Schrödinger operator with a single delta interaction,
can one close the self-adjoint realization, rank-one resolvent, complete
bound/scattering atlas, heat kernel, and relative heat trace in one convention,
including the attractive, free, repulsive, threshold, and pole boundaries?

The answer below is source-local.  It is not a claim that point interactions
or their explicit formulas are new, and it supplies no target arithmetic data.
