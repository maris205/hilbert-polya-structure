1. Generate exact rational resolvent, scattering, bound-state, and trace
   cells across attractive/free/repulsive chambers.
2. Make a duplicate-rejecting exact-schema checker reconstruct the interface
   equations and heat cells by inverse Laplace transformation of the
   resolvent, without importing the producer or copying its erfc formula.
3. Use SymPy for symbolic pole, jump, unitarity, normalization, and trace
   identities.
4. Require two isolated byte replays and repaired-hash semantic/structural,
   raw duplicate-key, missing/unknown/type, and stale-hash mutations.
5. Compile three retained manuscript rounds; for each round require two fresh
   deterministic LuaLaTeX builds, embedded fonts, and no unresolved layout or
   reference warnings.
6. Close the exact file ledger in a self-excluding release manifest.

Kill gate: any wrong factor two in the jump, heat kernel, bound-state norm, or
relative trace stops release.  A finite sample may not be presented as proof.
