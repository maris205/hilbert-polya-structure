# C166 research question

For every dyadic modulus `q=2^r` and every tower dimension `d>=2`, can the
Pascal skew map have its complete fixed-point clock, primitive cycles,
Artin--Mazur zeta, finite Koopman determinant, and a source-derived time
reversal determined exactly rather than inferred from a finite table?

The hard gate is an all-parameter proof.  A successful package must explain
why every fixed set is either empty or the entire state space, identify the
least clock `M=2^(r+floor(log_2 d))`, and prove the truncated-polynomial
substitution reversor.  A finite scan, a standalone `d=2` shear, or a product
of C161 cyclic rotations does not pass.
