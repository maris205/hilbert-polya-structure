# Narrative report

The Couette family is unusually suitable for an exact source theorem: after a
fixed Fourier transform, every mode follows an affine characteristic in
vertical frequency. Integrating the heat symbol along that characteristic
produces a positive cubic polynomial. Completing its square exposes both the
exact, sharp sector norm and the `t^3/12` enhanced-dissipation term. For
`nu*t>0` the norm value is approached by frequency-localized packets but is
not attained by a nonzero `L2` vector, because the unique maximizing frequency
is a null set. For `nu*t=0` the evolution is unitary and every nonzero vector
attains the norm.

The same formula also prevents category mistakes. At zero viscosity the
evolution is unitary and mixes only weakly; at positive viscosity no nonzero
`L2` periodic state survives. The streamwise zero mode has norm one on the
noncompact vertical line but no nonzero periodic vector. That same continuous
multiplier sector proves noncompactness, so no ordinary trace-class Fredholm
determinant exists.

The result is a complete continuous PDE theorem, but it has no intrinsic
rational-prime owner or isolated primitive cycles. Its honest outcome is
`(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)` and
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.

The finite certificate keeps exact rational exponents separate from decimal
display: exponential fields use 100 working decimal digits and are serialized
to 82 significant digits. This is a reproducibility contract, not an accuracy
claim of 100 displayed digits.
