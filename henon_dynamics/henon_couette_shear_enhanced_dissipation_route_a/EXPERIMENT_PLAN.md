# Executable certificate plan

1. Generate 675 rational Fourier cells covering five shears, three
   viscosities, five sectors, three times, and three vertical frequencies.
2. Generate 54 independent rational semigroup-composition cells.
3. Lock the exact characteristic shift, integrated frequency, completed
   square, dissipation exponent, minimum, multiplier, and sector norm.
   Evaluate exponentials at 100 working decimal digits and serialize the
   1,350 multiplier/norm fields to 82 significant digits.
4. Reconstruct every cell in a checker that imports no producer code.
5. Re-derive the Fourier PDE, square, strict quadratic curvature, minimum, and
   cocycle in SymPy; lock the exact norm-attainment boundary in the checker.
6. Require byte replay and rejection of repaired-hash semantic/schema attacks,
   including false norm-attainment and corrupted precision declarations, plus
   a stale-hash attack.
7. Build three substantively different fixed-epoch PDFs; require deterministic
   final bytes, embedded/subset fonts, extractable text, and clean logs.
8. Close exactly 27 payloads in a self-excluded release manifest.

Finite cells test signs and conventions. They do not prove a continuum
quantifier.
