# Hostile audit

Seventeen repaired-hash attacks alter source/evaluator/scope/route metadata,
the Fourier shift, cubic factor, heat term, multiplier, composition law,
periodic-state scope, trace-class stop, Fourier convention, citation, or
schema, including a false claim that a nonzero `L2` maximizer exists when
`nu*t>0` and a corrupted serialized-digit declaration. Every attack is
rejected after its payload hash is repaired. An eighteenth stale-hash attack
is rejected independently.

The mathematical attacks target the most dangerous errors: the sign of the
frequency characteristic, `1/12` versus `1/3`, loss of the streamwise heat
term, false norm attainment, false periodic states, and a fabricated
trace-class determinant.
