# Research question

For the continuous Couette advection–diffusion family on the infinite
periodic channel, can one obtain in one convention-locked theorem:

1. the exact Fourier semigroup at every parameter and time;
2. the exact, sharp sector operator norm, its attainment boundary, and cubic
   dissipation scale;
3. all inviscid, no-shear, zero-mode, and zero-time boundaries;
4. a complete periodic-state and trace-class stopping theorem; and
5. an honest Route-A judgment without importing arithmetic labels?

The answer is yes for the source theorem and no for Route A. The finite ledger
is a regression oracle only; the all-parameter statement is proved in the
manuscript and `THEOREM_PACKAGE.md`.
