# Source audit

## Frozen ownership

The package studies the classical linear Couette transport–diffusion equation
on `T x R`. Kelvin shear coordinates, inviscid damping, and Couette enhanced
dissipation predate this package. Bedrossian, Masmoudi, and Vicol,
*Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis* 219 (2016), 1087–1159,
DOI `10.1007/s00205-015-0917-3`, is cited only for modern Couette
enhanced-dissipation/inviscid-damping context.

## Package-owned derivation

After freezing the Fourier sign, the displayed characteristic formula,
completed square, exact and sharp sector norm with its attainment boundary,
semigroup identity, periodic-state classification, and noncompactness proof
are derived directly. No source title is used as a substitute for those
proofs.

## Claim boundary

No priority, nonlinear Couette stability, wall-bounded theorem, target
determinant, arithmetic local datum, Euler factor, root number, automorphy,
Hilbert–Pólya operator, external review, or literature-exhaustiveness claim is
made.
