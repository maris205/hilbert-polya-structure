# Research question

For a McKendrick age-transport equation with constant mortality and an Erlang
fertility kernel, can one distinguish every algebraic Euler–Lotka root from the
actual `L1` spectrum and close the resulting essential-edge phase transition?

The required theorem must give characteristics, the renewal/resolvent
denominator, all roots, the exact eigenvalue integrability gate, the dominant
rank-one regime, the distinct population-growth threshold, and the zero-birth
boundary.  Formal denominator roots below the shift edge may not be relabeled
as eigenvalues.

Proof status: **PROVABLE AS STATED** on `L1(R_+)` for `mu,gamma,beta>0` and
integer `k>=1`.
