# Theorem package: Erlang age transport and its spectral edge

## Frozen generator

On `X=L1(R_+,da)`, consider

\[
 \partial_t n+\partial_a n=-\mu n,
 \qquad n(t,0)=\int_0^\infty b_k(a)n(t,a)\,da,
\]

where

\[
 b_k(a)=\frac{\beta\gamma^k a^{k-1}e^{-\gamma a}}{(k-1)!},
 \qquad \mu,\gamma,\beta>0,\quad k\in\mathbb N.
\]

The generator is `Gf=-f'-mu*f` with domain consisting of `W^{1,1}` functions
satisfying the renewal boundary condition.  This Banach space and this
physical population clock are part of the owner.

## Main theorem

1. Characteristics give a positive strongly continuous semigroup and reduce
   the boundary birth rate to a scalar renewal equation.
2. Relative to the absorbing mortality shift, the resolvent difference is
   rank one.  Its denominator is

   \[
   \Delta(\lambda)=1-\beta
   \left(\frac{\gamma}{\gamma+\lambda+\mu}\right)^k.
   \]

   Hence the essential spectral bound remains `-mu`.
3. The algebraic denominator roots are

   \[
   \lambda_j=\gamma\beta^{1/k}e^{2\pi i j/k}-\gamma-\mu,
   \qquad 0\le j<k.
   \]

   Such a root is an `L1` eigenvalue exactly when
   `Re(lambda_j)>-mu`.  A root on or below the edge is not an `L1`
   eigenvalue.
4. If `beta>1`, the real root
   `lambda_0=gamma(beta^(1/k)-1)-mu` is isolated, simple, dominant, and has a
   positive stable-age eigenfunction.  In operator norm, the rescaled
   semigroup converges to its positive rank-one spectral projection.  If
   `beta<=1`, no isolated real pole lies to the right of the essential edge,
   so no uniform rank-one asymptotic is claimed.
5. For every nonzero positive initial density, population size grows, is
   critical, or decays according as

   \[
   \beta \gtrless \left(1+\frac\mu\gamma\right)^k.
   \]

   This threshold is distinct from the spectral-isolation threshold
   `beta=1`.  At `beta=0` the owner reduces to the mortality-weighted right
   shift.

## Proof

Along a characteristic, an individual of age `a` is transported to age
`a+t` with factor `e^{-mu*t}`; newborns fill the triangle `a<t`.  Substitution
at `a=0` gives the scalar renewal equation.

For `Re(lambda)>-mu`, solving `(lambda-G)u=f` gives

\[
 u(a)=e^{-(\lambda+\mu)a}u(0)
 +\int_0^a e^{-(\lambda+\mu)(a-s)}f(s)\,ds.
\]

The renewal boundary solves for `u(0)` with denominator `Delta(lambda)`.
The second term is the absorbing resolvent; the remaining term is a scalar
functional of `f` times one exponential, so the resolvent difference is rank
one.  Essential spectrum is therefore inherited from the mortality shift and
has boundary `Re(lambda)=-mu`.

The Erlang integral

\[
 \int_0^\infty b_k(a)e^{-(\lambda+\mu)a}\,da
 =\beta\left(\frac\gamma{\gamma+\lambda+\mu}\right)^k
\]

gives `Delta` and the displayed roots.  Their eigenfunction is
`e^{-(lambda+mu)a}`, which belongs to `L1` exactly when
`Re(lambda)>-mu`.  This proves the root/eigenvalue firewall.

Let `T(t)` be the renewal semigroup and let
`S_mu(t)f(a)=e^{-mu*t}f(a-t)1_{a>=t}` be the absorbing mortality shift.
For fixed `t>0`, characteristics write `T(t)-S_mu(t)` as the newborn term.
The boundary forcing generated by `f` on `[0,t]` is

\[
 g_f(s)=e^{-\mu s}\int_0^\infty b_k(s+u)f(u)\,du.
\]

Because `b_k` and its derivative are bounded and vanish at infinity, the
unit ball of `L1` is sent by `f -> g_f` to a bounded equicontinuous subset of
`C[0,t]`.  The Volterra renewal resolvent preserves compact sets, and the map
from the resulting boundary history to the newborn triangle is bounded into
`L1`.  Hence `T(t)-S_mu(t)` is compact for every `t>0`.  Since the essential
norm of `S_mu(t)` is `e^{-mu*t}`, the essential growth bound of `T` is exactly
`-mu`.

Write `rho=beta^(1/k)`.  The largest real part belongs uniquely to `j=0`.
It clears the essential edge exactly when `rho>1`.  Its eigenfunction and
dual residue are positive, so its spectral projection is positive and rank
one.  Compact perturbation plus the strict spectral gap gives the
quasi-compact decomposition

\[
 \|e^{-\lambda_0t}T(t)-P_0\|_{L^1\to L^1}\longrightarrow0.
\]

Its distance to the rest is bounded below by

\[
 \min\{\gamma(\rho-1),\;\gamma\rho[1-\cos(2\pi/k)]\}
\]

for `k>1`, with only the edge term for `k=1`.  For nonzero `f>=0`, strict
positivity gives `P_0f!=0`.  Finally `lambda_0` changes sign at
`rho=1+mu/gamma`, proving the positive-data population trichotomy.  When
`beta<=1`, the negative essential edge and absence of a nonnegative pole give
decay but not the asserted rank-one profile.
