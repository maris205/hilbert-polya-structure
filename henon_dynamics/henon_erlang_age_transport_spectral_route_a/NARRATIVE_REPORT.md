# Narrative report

The essential progress is a category distinction.  The Erlang renewal
denominator is a polynomial after clearing its denominator, so it always has
`k` algebraic roots.  The age generator lives on `L1`, however, and its
candidate eigenfunction has modulus `exp[-(Re(lambda)+mu)a]`.  Roots at or
below `-mu` therefore fail to be eigenvalues.  The same inequality creates an
exact phase transition at `beta=1`.

For each positive time the characteristic formula makes the renewal semigroup
a compact newborn perturbation of the mortality-weighted right shift.  Hence
the essential growth bound is exactly `-mu`, and once the real root clears the
edge, quasi-compact spectral decomposition gives operator-norm convergence to
the positive rank-one stable-age profile.  Its sign changes only later, at
`beta=(1+mu/gamma)^k`.  Separating isolation from population growth is the
paper’s main conceptual step.  The source spectrum remains a population
semigroup spectrum; it is not an arithmetic determinant.
