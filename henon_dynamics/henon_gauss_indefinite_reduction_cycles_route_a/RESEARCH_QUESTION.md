# HCS-C364 Research Question

For a fixed positive nonsquare discriminant \(\Delta\equiv0,1\pmod 4\), can the Gauss shift on primitive reduced real quadratic irrationals be closed as one exact finite dynamical system: phase-space bijection, complete cycles, period matrices and multipliers, a reversing involution, all fixed-point counts, and its finite Koopman determinant?

The normalization is frozen before any calculation. A state is

\[
\alpha=\frac{P+\sqrt\Delta}{Q},\qquad Q=2A>0,
\]

where \([A,B,C]=[Q/2,-P,(P^2-\Delta)/(2Q)]\) is a primitive integral form of discriminant \(\Delta\), \(\alpha>1\), and \(-1<\bar\alpha<0\). One clock tick is

\[
G(\alpha)=\frac1{\alpha-\lfloor\alpha\rfloor}.
\]

The target is a source-local theorem. It does not ask for a rational-prime orbit dictionary, a target Euler product, a target divisor, target zeros, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.

## Success criterion

One theorem must prove, for every admissible \(\Delta\), finiteness and bijectivity, primitive cycle words, hyperbolic stabilizer matrices and exact multipliers, reversal, fixed-count and determinant identities, and all square/zero/negative/congruence/imprimitive/norm-sign boundaries. Finite enumeration is permitted only as regression evidence.
