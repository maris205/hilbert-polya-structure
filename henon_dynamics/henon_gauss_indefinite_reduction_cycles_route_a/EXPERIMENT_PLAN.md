# HCS-C364 Exact-Evidence Plan

## Analytic owner

The proof owns the infinite family. It derives the pair update, constructs the inverse state, invokes the reduced-quadratic-irrational form of Lagrange periodicity only where its hypotheses are met, derives the period-matrix fixed polynomial and multiplier, proves the reversing identity, and computes the finite determinant cycle by cycle.

## Deterministic receipt

For every nonsquare \(5\leq\Delta\leq1000\) with \(\Delta\equiv0,1\pmod4\):

1. Enumerate primitive forms through exact integer divisibility and gcd tests.
2. Test the two strict radical inequalities without floating point.
3. Recompute every Gauss digit, successor, inverse permutation, and reversal.
4. Partition the entire state set into cycles.
5. Multiply every digit matrix; verify determinant parity, its fixed quadratic polynomial, and
   \(\operatorname{tr}(M)^2-4\det M=k^2\Delta\).
6. Verify \(R^2=1\) and \(RGR=G^{-1}\) state by state.
7. Check \(\#\operatorname{Fix}(G^n)\) for \(1\leq n\leq24\) from the full cycle inventory.
8. Record determinant exponents by cycle length.

Square discriminants from \(4\) through \(225\), \(\Delta=0\), negative discriminants, invalid residues modulo four, and the imprimitive \(\Delta=20\) representation of a primitive \(\Delta=5\) state are hostile boundary controls.

## Independence and failure closure

The checker enumerates coefficients \([A,B,C]\), not the producer's \((P,Q)\) loop. It has its own floor algorithm, cycle decomposition, matrix recurrence, strict JSON/YAML parsers, raw and semantic evaluation locks, and exact nested schemas. A SymPy lane checks algebraic identities. Two isolated directories must reproduce identical evidence bytes. Repaired-hash attacks target every semantic owner. All executable lanes must refuse both `python -O` and `python -OO`.

## Scope

The evidence is a regression receipt, not a proof by exhaustion and not evidence for any forbidden target-arithmetic claim.
