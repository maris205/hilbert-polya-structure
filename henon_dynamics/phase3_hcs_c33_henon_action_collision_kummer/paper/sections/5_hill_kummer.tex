\section{Hill--Kummer descent}

At a chronological orbit point \(x_i\), write
\begin{equation}
D_i=\begin{pmatrix}-2Ax_i&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M=D_4D_3D_2D_1D_0.                                        \label{eq:monodromy}
\end{equation}
Every \(D_i\) has determinant one.  Define the Hill value
\[
h_A(q)=\det(I-M)=2-\Tr M.
\]

The cyclic Hessian of \eqref{eq:action} has diagonal entries \(2Ax_i\) and
cyclic nearest-neighbor entries one.  Direct exact expansion gives the H\'enon
specialization of Hill's formula,
\begin{equation}
\det D^2\PhiA=h_A(q)
\quad\text{on }\GA(q)=0.                                  \label{eq:hill}
\end{equation}
The resultant
\begin{equation}
\Res_q(\GA,h_A)=-2^6A^{30}P_2^5P_5                        \label{eq:plus}
\end{equation}
is coprime to \(\PN\).  Thus both node branches are Morse and exclude
multiplier \(+1\).

Morse nondegeneracy does not exclude multiplier \(-1\).  Because \(\det M=1\),
\[
\det(I+M)=2+\Tr M=4-h_A.
\]
The separate exact gate is
\begin{equation}
\Res_q(\GA,4-h_A)=-2^6A^{30}B_6B_9,                       \label{eq:minus}
\end{equation}
where \(B_6,B_9\) are printed in Appendix~\ref{app:ledgers} and both are
coprime to \(\PN\).  Consequently neither branch has multiplier \(+1\) or
\(-1\).

Now reduce \(h_A(q)\) in the quadratic algebra
\(E=\K[q]/(g_2)\):
\[
h_A(q)\equiv\ell q+m.
\]
Let \(\bar q\) be the class of \(q\) in \(E\) and let \(\sigma\) denote
the exchange involution.  Using \(q_1+q_2=-b\) and \(q_1q_2=d\), the
quadratic norm of \(h=h_A(\bar q)\) is
\begin{equation}
\NH=\Norm_{E/\K}(h_A)=h_1h_2=\ell^2d-\ell mb+m^2.          \label{eq:NH}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Descent and gauge]\label{lem:gauge}
The square class \([\NH]\in\K^\times/\K^{\times2}\) is fixed by branch
exchange, by cyclic action coboundaries, by parameter-dependent action
translations or nonzero rescalings, and by a common Hill normalization.
Moreover,
\([h/\sigma(h)]=[h\sigma(h)]\) in \(E^\times/E^{\times2}\); over a
splitting field this reads \([h_1/h_2]=[h_1h_2]\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Branch exchange fixes the product.  A cyclic coboundary telescopes on a
closed orbit, while action translation or rescaling changes only the target
coordinate and not the return matrices.  A common change
\(h_i\mapsto\nu h_i\), with \(\nu\in\K^\times\), multiplies
\(h_1h_2\) by \(\nu^2\).  Finally,
\[
\frac{h/\sigma(h)}{h\sigma(h)}=\sigma(h)^{-2}.
\]
Independent branch-dependent rescalings are not common dynamical
normalizations and are excluded from the gauge group.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nontrivial Hill--Kummer class]\label{thm:kummer}
The element \(\NH\) is not a square in \(\K\).  Hence \(u^2=\NH\) defines a
nontrivial quadratic extension of the generic collision field.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Exact quotient arithmetic gives
\[
\NH=-\frac4{4827099043}Q_8(A)
\quad\text{in }\K,
\]
where \(Q_8\) is printed in Appendix~\ref{app:ledgers}.  The leading
coefficient of \(\PN\) is \(110592\), so the nonmonic norm formula is
\[
\Norm_{\K/\Q}(\NH)=
\left(-\frac4{4827099043}\right)^9
\frac{\Res(\PN,Q_8)}{110592^8}.
\]
It simplifies to
\begin{equation}
\Norm_{\K/\Q}(\NH)
=\frac{2^6\,13\,19^5\,41\,59^5\,5653^2}{3^5}.             \label{eq:norm}
\end{equation}
The rational number is not a square.  A square in \(\K\) would have square
rational norm, giving a contradiction.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:kummer} proves one nontrivial quadratic class.  It does not
prove independence of its nine parameter conjugates or a full
\(C_2\wr S_9\) Galois group.
