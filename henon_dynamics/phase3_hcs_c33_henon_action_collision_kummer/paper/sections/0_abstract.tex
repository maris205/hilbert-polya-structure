We prove that the exact period-five action map of the area-preserving H\'enon
family contains an arithmetic stability invariant that is absent from its
known orbit permutation cover.  Starting from the chronological recurrence,
we derive the six-sheet period-five normalization and its cyclic action
image.  The action discriminant has a coprime degree-nine factor \(P_9\).
Over \(K_9=\Q[A]/(P_9)\), the repeated action value has exactly two distinct
normalization points; their image is a transverse ordinary node.  Both
points have exact period five, their action Hessians are nondegenerate, and
their return matrices have neither multiplier \(+1\) nor \(-1\).  If
\(h_1,h_2\) denote their chronological Hill determinants, then the product
\(N_H=h_1h_2\) descends through branch exchange and defines a
normalization-invariant square class.  Its exact rational field norm is
\[
\Norm_{K_9/\Q}(N_H)
=\frac{2^6\,13\,19^5\,41\,59^5\,5653^2}{3^5},
\]
so \(N_H\) is not a square in \(K_9\).  Hence \(u^2=N_H\) is a nontrivial
quadratic Kummer extension of the generic collision field.  We also prove
\(\Gal(P_9/\Q)=S_9\) and release an independently checked exact
certificate.  The result is fixed-period arithmetic structure, not a
dynamical zeta function or a Hilbert--P\'olya operator.
