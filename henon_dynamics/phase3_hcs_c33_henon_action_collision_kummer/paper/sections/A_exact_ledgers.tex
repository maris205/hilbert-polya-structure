\section{Scope of the computational proof}

The certificate works over \(\Q\), \(\Q(A)\), and
\(\Q[A]/(P_9)\) using exact polynomial arithmetic.  Its finite-field rows
are derived afterward and serve only as controls.  The machine proof does
not query a prime table, a Riemann-zero table, or a numerical root finder.

The source-lock includes the staged research question, the prior-work audit,
the earlier C32 collision certificate, the chronological H\'enon producer
used as a convention control, and the foundational area-preserving H\'enon
manuscript.  No coefficient from the Phase-1 pilot is imported as a theorem
input.
