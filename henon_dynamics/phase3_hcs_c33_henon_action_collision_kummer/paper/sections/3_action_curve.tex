\section{Chronological action curve}

Set \((x_0,x_{-1})=(q,q)\) and iterate
\begin{equation}
x_{i+1}=1-Ax_i^2-x_{i-1}.                                  \label{eq:recurrence}
\end{equation}
The state after five steps is \((x_5,x_4)\).  Taking the gcd of
\(x_5-q\) and \(x_4-q\) over \(\Q(A)[q]\), removing the fixed-point factor
\(Aq^2+2q-1\), and primitive-scaling yields the sextic \(\GA(q)\) printed
in Appendix~\ref{app:ledgers}.  Its discriminant is
\begin{equation}
\Disc_q\GA=2^6A^{30}P_2P_5,                                \label{eq:discG}
\end{equation}
where
\[
P_2=16A^2-28A-19,
\quad
P_5=16A^5-108A^4+105A^3+27A^2-97A-47.
\]
Because five is prime, removing the fixed-point factor leaves exact period
five away from \(AP_2P_5=0\).

The cyclic action is
\begin{equation}
\PhiA=\sum_{i\bmod5}
\left(x_ix_{i+1}-x_i+\frac A3x_i^3\right).                 \label{eq:action}
\end{equation}
Its Euler--Lagrange equations are
\[
\partial_{x_i}\PhiA=x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}-1+Ax_i^2=0,
\]
which reproduce \eqref{eq:recurrence}.  Reducing \(3A^2\PhiA\) modulo
\(\GA\) gives a degree-five polynomial \(R(A,q)\).  We define the primitive
action image by
\begin{equation}
\WA(A,c)=A^{-30}\Res_q\bigl(\GA(q),3A^2c-R(A,q)\bigr).
                                                                    \label{eq:W}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Birational control]\label{prop:birational}
The plane curve \(\WA(A,c)=0\) and the normalization \(\GA(q)=0\) have the
same function field over \(\Q\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
At \(A=6\), reduction modulo \(37\) preserves degree six.  The coefficient
rows of \(\GA\) and \(\WA\), from highest to lowest degree, are
\[
(36,12,21,21,26,27,34),\qquad
(26,34,7,16,19,8,4),
\]
and exact finite-field factorization gives profile \((6)\) for each.
Consequently both generic sextics are irreducible over \(\Q(A)\).
The last nonzero subresultant of \(\GA(q)\) and \(3A^2c-R(A,q)\), viewed as
polynomials in \(q\), is linear:
\[
U(A,c)q+V(A,c).
\]
Its coefficient \(U\) is coprime to \(\WA\) over \(\Q(A)\).  Therefore
\(q=-V/U\) on a dense open set, proving
\(\Q(A,q)=\Q(A,c)\).  The released certificate stores and independently
checks the complete coefficient ledgers.
\end{proof}

This proposition is a negative control with a positive consequence.  It
rules out a new ordinary cover, but it also identifies singularities of
\(\WA\) as failures of the action coordinate to separate points on the
known normalization.
