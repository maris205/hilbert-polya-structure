The characteristic-zero norm \eqref{eq:norm} is the primary nonsquare proof.
Finite reductions test the specialization and catch branch, chronology, and
square-class errors.  We froze every prime dividing
\[
\PN(6)=61\cdot157\cdot3203\cdot21943
\]
before the Phase-3 theorem computation.  Table~\ref{tab:primes} reports the
complete control set.

The released producer derives all promoted polynomials from
\eqref{eq:map}--\eqref{eq:action}.  It serializes rational polynomials and
elements of \(\Q[A]/(P_9)\) canonically.  The checker imports no producer
module.  It reconstructs the recurrence, action, resultants, collision
fiber, tangents, derivative chronology, Hessian, field norm, and modular
controls, then enforces exact key sets and type-strict JSON equality.

The independent report passes 12 semantic gates.  Thirty-three adversarial and
regression tests alter the source hash, polynomial data, discriminant
exponent, irreducibility or Galois ledgers, collision fiber, tangent,
\(-1\)-multiplier gate, field norm, finite controls, post-hoc disclosure,
Route-A status, and scope.  Expected
mathematical mismatches are distinguished from unexpected checker errors.
The full project is frozen by a SHA-256 manifest and reproduced by a
read-only default runner.

These checks do not make two computer-algebra implementations logically
independent proof assistants.  The explicit derivation and mutation suite
instead provide a transparent, reproducible exact certificate whose main
identities can be checked outside the producer.
