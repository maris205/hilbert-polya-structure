# HCS-C33 Phase-3 research question

## Selected question

Let \(G_A(q)=0\) be the known six-sheet normalization of the exact
period-five reversor-line locus of

\[
H_A(q,p)=(1-Aq^2-p,q).
\]

Let \(c=\Phi_{5,A}\) be the cyclic generating action and let
\(W_5(A,c)=0\) be the plane image of \((A,q)\mapsto(A,c)\).  Does the
degree-nine factor \(P_9(A)\) in \(\operatorname{Disc}_c W_5\) carry a
genuinely new, gauge-stable dynamical invariant?

The precise test is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
P_9=0\text{ gives two distinct exact-period-five Morse points},\\
\text{their action-image branches form a transverse ordinary node},\\
[h_1h_2]\ne1\quad\text{in }K_9^\times/K_9^{\times2},
\end{gathered}}
\]

where \(h_i=\det(I-DH_A^5)\) and
\(K_9=\mathbb Q[A]/(P_9)\).

## Why this is a large-door test

The degree-six orbit cover itself is prior work.  A nontrivial symmetric
Hill square class would add information that is invisible to the bare
permutation cover and survives exchange of the two equal-action branches.
It would therefore give a concrete arithmetic decoration of chronological
Hénon dynamics without inventing an external prime weight.

## Falsification rule

Stop C33 if any of the following occurs:

1. \(P_9\) meets the fixed-point, marker-ramification, or \(\pm1\)-multiplier
   loci generically;
2. the repeated action value comes from one nonreduced point rather than two
   distinct normalization points;
3. the two image branches have the same tangent;
4. \(h_1h_2\) fails to descend under branch exchange or changes under a
   common admissible Hill normalization;
5. \(h_1h_2\) is a square in \(K_9\);
6. the conclusion needs the post-hoc prime \(61\) rather than a
   characteristic-zero proof.

## Promotion boundary

Success proves an arithmetic square class on one fixed-period collision
field.  It does not prove a full Kummer wreath group, a Picard--Lefschetz
representation, an all-period dynamical zeta, or a self-adjoint
Hilbert--Pólya operator.
