## Decisive prior-work boundary

### Exact Hénon orbit algebra

Endler and Gallas derive exact low-period Hénon orbit equations, orbital
sums, discriminants, reversibility classes, and symmetric Galois groups.
Brison and Gallas exhibit polynomial bridges between equivalent orbit
equations.  Therefore the period-five sextic normalization, its ordinary
permutation cover, and a birational relabeling by the action coordinate are
not new.

Primary locators:

- A. Endler and J. A. C. Gallas, “Reductions and simplifications of orbital
  sums in a Hamiltonian repeller,” *Physics Letters A* 352 (2006), 124--128,
  DOI `10.1016/j.physleta.2006.01.031`.
- O. J. Brison and J. A. C. Gallas, “Polynomial interpolation as detector of
  orbital equation equivalence,” *International Journal of Modern Physics C*
  29 (2018), 1850096, DOI `10.1142/S0129183118500961`.

### Generating actions and equal-action Hénon saddles

Kook--Meiss establish action principles for reversible symplectic maps.
Shudo and Shudo--Ikeda use a discrete quantum-Hénon action and equal-action
saddles in Stokes geometry.  C33 therefore does not claim the first Hénon
action or first equal-action Hénon pair.

Primary locators:

- H.-T. Kook and J. D. Meiss, “Periodic orbits for reversible, symplectic
  mappings,” *Physica D* 35 (1989), 65--86,
  DOI `10.1016/0167-2789(89)90096-1`.
- A. Shudo and K. S. Ikeda, “Stokes geometry for the quantum Hénon map,”
  *Nonlinearity* 21 (2008), 1831--1880,
  DOI `10.1088/0951-7715/21/8/007`.

### Maxwell and critical-value monodromy

Looijenga and the Lyashko--Looijenga literature make equal-critical-value
discriminants and their monodromy classical.  In the convention used here,
a Maxwell stratum means distinct Morse critical points with a common
critical value; no claim about equal global minima is made.  The generic
node mechanism and the abstract occurrence of symmetric monodromy are not
new.

Primary locators:

- E. Looijenga, “The complement of the bifurcation variety of a simple
  singularity,” *Inventiones Mathematicae* 23 (1974), 105--116,
  DOI `10.1007/BF01405164`.
- D. Zvonkine and S. Lando, “On multiplicities of the Lyashko--Looijenga
  mapping on discriminant strata,” *Functional Analysis and Its
  Applications* 33 (1999), 178--188, DOI `10.1007/BF02465202`.
- J. Yu, “Galois group of Looijenga--Lyashko mapping,” *Mathematische
  Zeitschrift* 232 (1999), 321--330, DOI `10.1007/s002090050517`.

### Hill and Kummer technology

The equality between a periodic action Hessian and a return-map determinant
is a specialization of the discrete Hill formula.  Kummer covers and braid
monodromy are established technology.  C33's potential increment is their
specific coupling on the exact Hénon action node.

Primary locators:

- S. Bolotin and D. Treschev, “Hill's formula,” *Russian Mathematical
  Surveys* 65 (2010), 191--257, DOI `10.1070/RM2010v065n02ABEH004671`.
- E. Artal Bartolo, J. I. Cogolludo-Agustín, and J. Ortigas-Galindo,
  “Kummer covers and braid monodromy,” *Journal of the Institute of
  Mathematics of Jussieu* 13 (2014), 633--670,
  DOI `10.1017/S1474748013000297`.
