# Paper plan: Hénon action nodes and Hill--Kummer descent

## One-sentence contribution

We prove that a degree-nine equal-action collision in the exact period-five
area-preserving Hénon family is an ordinary two-branch node whose symmetric
Hill determinant defines a nontrivial quadratic Kummer class.

## Claims--evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| The action curve is a singular embedding of the known period-five normalization | linear inverse subresultant and equality of function fields |
| \(P_9\) is an equal-action ordinary node | quadratic normalization fiber, nonzero tangent discriminant, distinct branch slopes |
| Both branches are exact-period-five and have no \(\pm1\) multiplier | fixed-factor removal plus three coprimality resultants |
| The Hill product descends and is gauge-stable | quadratic norm and branch/common-normalization lemmas |
| The Kummer class is nontrivial | exact nonsquare rational field norm |
| The collision parameters have \(S_9\) Galois group | exact modular factor types at 7, 17, and 23 |
| The result is not yet a zeta/HP structure | formal Route-A evaluation and fixed-period boundary |

## Section plan

1. **Abstract:** theorem, exact field norm, and boundary.
2. **Introduction:** start from the failure of bare orbit covers to retain
   stability; state the coupled action-node/Hill increment.
3. **Prior work and claim boundary:** exact Hénon orbit algebra, action and
   equal-value geometry, Hill/Kummer technology.
4. **Chronological setup:** recurrence, action, marker, action image, and
   birationality.
5. **The equal-action node:** discriminant, collision field, two-point fiber,
   tangent proof, and \(S_9\).
6. **Hill--Kummer descent:** chronological derivative, Hessian identity,
   \(\pm1\) gates, norm, nonsquare theorem, gauge lemma.
7. **Finite controls and reproducibility:** the four frozen primes, exact
   producer/checker architecture, and mutation gates.
8. **Discussion and Route-A boundary:** what the theorem adds and what an
   all-period reopening would require.
9. **Appendix:** full polynomial ledgers, group proof, norm correction, and
   certificate contract.

## Claim boundary

The manuscript must not claim a new period-five normalization, a generic
Maxwell mechanism, a full Kummer wreath group, Picard--Lefschetz monodromy,
an all-period zeta, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
