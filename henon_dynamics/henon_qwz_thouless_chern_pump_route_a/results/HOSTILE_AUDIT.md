# Hostile audit

Fifty-seven adversarial cases passed. They include repaired-hash identity,
schema/date/path, evaluator, model, theorem, analytic-degree, collision,
nonclaim, gap, Chern-sign, Dirac, lattice, route, scope, reference, and
enumeration mutations; duplicate/omit, truncation, and reordering attacks on
the mass and lattice coordinate ledgers; nested deletions and extra fields; a
stale-hash control; duplicate/nonfinite/wrong-root JSON; and authority,
evidence-status, Route-B, forbidden-flag, identity, duplicate-key,
anchor/alias, and non-string-key YAML attacks.

This suite is a tamper detector, not a mathematical proof. The independent checker and analytic proof own the theorem.
