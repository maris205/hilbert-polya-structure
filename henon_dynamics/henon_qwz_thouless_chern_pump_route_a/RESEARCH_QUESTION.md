# Research question

For the two-band Bloch pump
\[
H_m=d_m\cdot\sigma,\qquad d_m=(\sin k,\sin\tau,m+\cos k+\cos\tau),
\]
can one prove, for every real mass, the exact band gap, all gapped Chern phases, all gap-closing Dirac data, and the precise adiabatic transport statement?

Yes. The exact direct gap is twice the distance from `m` to `{-2,0,2}`. With orientation `dk wedge dτ` and
`c1=(2πi)^(-1) integral Tr(P[dP,dP])`, the lower-band values are `0,-1,+1,0` on the four chambers. Four local Dirac cones account for the jumps. A filled lower band pumps charge `c1` only under the usual gapped adiabatic hypotheses; no finite-speed exact quantization is asserted.

The work excludes rational resonance variants, target arithmetic, target zeros, Route B, edge-state bulk-boundary claims, disorder, interactions, and finite-rate error estimates.
