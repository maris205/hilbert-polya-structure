# Narrative report

The package advances from one cyclic spectral measure to a theorem about the entire Markov operator.  A complete sibling-wavelet decomposition exposes one exceptional radial Jacobi block and infinitely many free half-line Jacobi blocks; both are purely absolutely continuous on the same interval.  Separately, return words are classified by the number of Dyck excursions, retaining the otherwise easy-to-miss factor \(D^k\) for departures from the root.  The escape proof avoids conditioning on a non-stopping “last return”: it couples the radial chain to i.i.d. biased increments plus twice a boundary-flip count, then proves that count is almost surely finite.

The rank-one face is genuinely critical rather than a formal substitution: the spectrum becomes arcsine, return mass becomes one, speed vanishes, and Gaussian centering is replaced by half-normal radial scaling.
