# Source audit

## Verified lineage

- Harry Kesten, “Symmetric random walks on groups,” *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 92 (1959), 336–354.  Publisher DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1959-0109367-6>.  JSTOR DOI: <https://doi.org/10.2307/1993160>.  These are two identifiers for the same article, not independent sources.  The article is the primary lineage for symmetric walks on groups and the free-group spectral-radius result.
- Wolfgang Woess, *Random Walks on Infinite Graphs and Groups*, Cambridge Tracts in Mathematics 138, Cambridge University Press, 2000.  Official DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511470967>.  This is the authoritative monograph lineage for graph/group random walks.

## Ownership boundary

No priority claim is made.  This package is a source-local reconstruction that supplies its own resolvent algebra, whole-space pure-AC decomposition, Dyck enumeration, and probabilistic limit proof under one explicit convention.

## Claim boundary

The paper does not claim Kesten's general amenability theorem, nonuniform walks, free products, harmonic measure, entropy, or a sharp local-limit asymptotic.  Random closed words are not called primitive arithmetic orbits.  No zeta function, target determinant, Euler factor, root number, target zero, or Route-B object is introduced.
