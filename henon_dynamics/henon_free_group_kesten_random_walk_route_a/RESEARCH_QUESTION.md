# Research question

For the uniform generating-set walk on \(F_d\), can one close in one normalization-frozen theorem the full operator spectrum, the root spectral density, every finite return and first-return probability, transience, speed and Gaussian radial fluctuations, while proving rather than inferring the pure-AC statement and separating the \(d=1\) critical boundary?

The answer is yes.  Root resolvents, a complete rooted-tree Haar--Jacobi decomposition, weighted Dyck paths, and a finite-boundary-correction coupling supply the four proof engines.
