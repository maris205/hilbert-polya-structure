# Paper plan

The final paper is a compact theorem-and-proof article.

- Round 0 fixes the fourth-order model, the Ostrogradsky Hamiltonian, the canonical signs, and the rational/irrational orbit theorem.
- Round 1 adds the equal-frequency Jordan face and the complete zero/negative factor atlas, including every all-time bounded and periodic subspace.
- Round 2 adds the self-adjoint Hermite difference operator on the explicit maximal domain at `hbar=1`, the rational infinite-multiplicity lattice, the irrational simple dense pure-point spectrum, the Route-A evaluation, and evidence boundaries.

The main mathematical progress is one integrated classification, not five fragments of one calculation.
