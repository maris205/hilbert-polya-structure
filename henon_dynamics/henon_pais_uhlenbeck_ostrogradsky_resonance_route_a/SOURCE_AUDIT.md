# Source audit

## Verified source owners

1. A. Pais and G. E. Uhlenbeck, “On Field Theories with Non-Localized Action,” *Physical Review* **79** (1950), 145--165. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.79.145>. This is the original source owner for the higher-derivative oscillator family.
2. A. V. Smilga, “Benign vs. malicious ghosts in higher-derivative theories,” *Nuclear Physics B* **706** (2005), 598--614. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2004.10.037>. This is a primary source for higher-derivative ghost dynamics and its stability distinctions.
3. P. D. Mannheim / related literature is not used as a theorem owner here.  The Hamiltonian convention is compared instead with P. Bolonek and P. Kosiński, “Hamiltonian structures for Pais--Uhlenbeck oscillator,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0501024>.

The DOI resolver and arXiv record were checked on 2026-09-04.  An APS bot response may deny automated full-text access, but the DOI metadata and publisher target are stable.

## Claim boundary

The package reconstructs every displayed identity from the frozen Lagrangian.  Sources establish lineage; they do not substitute for the proof.  No claim of priority is made for the equation, Hamiltonian structures, resonance behavior, or quantization.

The quantum theorem is only for distinct positive frequencies and for the explicitly defined Hermite-diagonal closure.  No equal-frequency quantum limit, interacting stability result, full field theory, or alternative positive Hamiltonian is claimed.
