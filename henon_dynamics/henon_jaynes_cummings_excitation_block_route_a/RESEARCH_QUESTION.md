complete, convention-locked theorem: exact conservation, vacuum/block
decomposition, dressed energies, unitary propagators, transition laws,
finite-support revival equivalences, singular faces and full-Fock operator

also possesses the missing arithmetic owner and target analytic bridge.  It
does not.  `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` records only that the source system is
already naturally quantized; it does not create a target Hilbert--Polya
