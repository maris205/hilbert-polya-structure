# Narrative report

## What moved

C223 turns the familiar two-state Rabi picture into a complete excitation
atlas.  It fixes the often error-prone shift between block `n` and photon
number, separates the vacuum, derives every dressed block and exponential,
and closes probability, trace, determinant and unitarity identities.  The
finite-support revival statement is sharpened into an if-and-only-if
commensurability criterion, while population and full state-vector revival
are kept distinct.

The boundary work is part of the theorem rather than a footnote: zero
coupling, exact resonance, sign gauge, vacuum, finite truncation and the
noncompact full-Fock unitary are all explicit.  This prevents finite block
determinants from being silently promoted to an infinite-dimensional
Fredholm determinant.

## Evidence boundary

Thirty-two blocks and 64 time cases exercise all frozen faces.  They are
regression evidence, not a proof of the infinite family.  A separate checker
reconstructs the matrices and a 10-dimensional finite Fock commutator without
importing the producer.  SymPy, byte replay and hostile mutations provide
independent algebra, provenance and schema controls.

## Route-A result

The source is genuinely and naturally quantum, so A4 records
`A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`.  That does not cure A0--A3: excitation labels are
not rational-prime owners, the blocks are not primitive-orbit clocks, and no
target divisor or analytic target is produced.  The overall result is
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`, with Route B unauthorized.
