  *Proceedings of the IEEE* 51 (1963), 89--109,
  DOI: `10.1109/PROC.1963.1664`.
- Bruce W. Shore and Peter L. Knight, “The Jaynes-Cummings model,” *Journal
  of Modern Optics* 40 (1993), 1195--1238,
  DOI: `10.1080/09500349314551321`.

second reviews its exact dressed dynamics and collapse/revival context.  C223
does not claim priority for the model, dressed states or Rabi oscillations.
