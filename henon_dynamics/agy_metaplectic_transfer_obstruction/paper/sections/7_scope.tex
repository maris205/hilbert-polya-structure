\section{Route-A decision, limitations, and conclusion}
\label{sec:scope}

The ordinary-determinant proposal fails on both declared spaces.  On the raw
Banach space, Theorem~\ref{thm:c1-noncompact} excludes Banach nuclearity for
every \(\Re s>-\sigma _0\).  On the normalized Hilbert space,
Theorem~\ref{thm:l2-branch} excludes compactness and every finite Schatten
class for \(\Re s\ge0\); consequently neither the ordinary Fredholm
determinant nor a finite-order regularized Schatten determinant is available.
Theorem~\ref{thm:coisometry} makes the obstruction quantitative on the
imaginary axis.

Noncompactness alone does not imply that \(I-z\cP_s\) is never Fredholm or
invertible.  It also does not rule out a determinant defined by a flat or
dynamical trace, a Weil distribution character on its regular locus, a
semifinite trace forced by geometry, or genuine continuous group smoothing.
These constructions change the trace framework and require their own
well-posedness theorems.

A holomorphic or anisotropic completion is the most precise escape exposed
by the proof.  A nonzero holomorphic function cannot have compact support in
one real branch, so the bump/evaluation compression does not transfer to
that setting.  Conversely, scalar compactness on a holomorphic space does
not automatically persist after tensoring with an unsmoothed
infinite-dimensional unitary fibre.  A viable continuation must fix a common
complex domain, prove complex distortion and branch-norm estimates, retain
the chronological central sign, and establish a legal trace or determinant.

The quasi-H\"older space of \citet{aimino2017} illustrates the same need for
space-specific care.  It contains characteristic functions of some sets and
is a Banach algebra, but the cited paper does not prove that every return
cylinder has the boundary regularity needed for its indicator to belong to
the space.  We therefore leave that application conditional rather than
silently importing an \(L^2\) projection.

Under the project's Route-A rubric, the result is
\[
 (\mathrm{A1\_WEAK},\mathrm{A2\_FAIL},
   \mathrm{A3\_FAIL},\mathrm{A4\_FORMAL\_HINT}).
\]
The all-length decoder provides a natural reproducible orbit coding, but no
prime-like clock or arithmetic amplitude law.  The tested ordinary
dynamical determinant is obstructed before a zero comparison is meaningful.
There is no Riemann divisor, functional equation, gamma factor, continuation
of the metaplectic determinant, or Riemann--von Mangoldt law.  The
symplectic cocycle supplies a natural oscillator fibre, but no surviving
self-adjoint Hilbert--P\'olya operator.  Route B is therefore not authorized.

The appropriate next step is a system-space switch: either source-lock a
holomorphic no-localizer transfer family or construct a geometrically forced
generalized trace.  Extending the elementary periodic ledger beyond the
existing cutoff would not address the failed operator gate.
