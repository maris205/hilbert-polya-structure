\section{Auxiliary operator facts and reproducibility details}
\label{app:details}

\begin{lemma}[Tensor essential norm]
\label{lem:tensor-essential}
Let \(K:H_0\to H_1\) be bounded between Hilbert spaces and let \(U\) be
unitary on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space \(F\).  Then
\[
 \|K\otimes U\|_{\ess}=\|K\|.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For a unit vector \(x\in H_0\) and an orthonormal sequence \((e_n)\) in
\(F\), the vectors \(x\otimes e_n\) converge weakly to zero.  Every compact
operator \(C\) sends them to zero in norm, whereas
\[
 \|(K\otimes U)(x\otimes e_n)\|=\|Kx\|.
\]
Thus \(\|K\otimes U-C\|\ge\|Kx\|\).  Taking the supremum over \(x\) proves
the lower bound; the operator norm gives the reverse bound.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Why the branch norm contains a square root}

The normalized scalar branch is
\(K_{s,\gamma}f=p_\gamma e^{-sr_\gamma}f\circ h_\gamma\).  Applying
\eqref{eq:disintegration} once removes one of the two factors of
\(p_\gamma\):
\[
 \|K_{s,\gamma}f\|^2
 =\int_{D_\gamma}p_\gamma(Ty)e^{-2\Re(s)r(y)}|f(y)|^2\,d\mu(y).
\]
Hence the norm is the essential supremum of
\(\sqrt{p_\gamma(Ty)}e^{-\Re(s)r(y)}\), not of
\(p_\gamma(Ty)e^{-\Re(s)r(y)}\).  This factor is responsible for the
absolute-atom threshold \(\Re s\ge d/2\).

\subsection{Decoder invariants}

At every decoder step, the two outgoing edges exchange winner and loser.
For a valid image matrix, exactly one row difference is componentwise
nonnegative.  The checker records both differences, the selected edge, the
matrix before and after subtraction, and
\[
 \sum_{i,j}R_{ij}-\sum_{i,j}R'_{ij}
 =\sum_jR_{\ell j}>0.
\]
This trace detects three common bugs independently: using
\(B_1\cdots B_n\) instead of later-on-left chronology, peeling columns
instead of rows, and advancing the state before selecting the edge.

\subsection{Reproduction contract}

The producer and checker are separate Python programs.  The checker imports
neither producer functions nor producer constants.  Both reconstruct the
graph from the literal seed, while their move, multiplication, crossing-form,
decoder, and projective-derivative routines are separately written.  The
certificate stores a Material Passport, runtime versions, source hashes,
and exact JSON values.  The repository runner executes the producer,
checker, unit tests, mutation tests, and artifact-hash generation and
verification.  The paper has a separate clean-build command and compilation
report.
