We prove that the unsmoothed oscillator twist of an
Avila--Gou\"ezel--Yoccoz induced countable Rauzy transfer operator is noncompact on
two natural spaces.  The scalar construction is quasicompact on bounded
\(C^1\) functions, but adjoining the infinite-dimensional metaplectic fibre
creates an exact obstruction: a branch-supported \(C^1\) bump followed by
point evaluation compresses the full operator to a nonzero scalar multiple
of one oscillator unitary.  This proves noncompactness throughout the AGY
half-plane \(\Re s>-\sigma _0\).  Put \(\cF=L^2(\R^2)\).  After
invariant-density normalization, the operator is contractive but noncompact
on \(L^2(\mu;\cF)\) for \(\Re s\ge0\); on \(s=it\) it is a coisometry with
essential norm one.  We
note that this normalized \(L^2\) obstruction already occurs for the scalar
Perron--Frobenius operator and is therefore a space-level robustness test,
not an oscillator-specific novelty.  We
also give an all-length row-subtraction decoder showing that a Rauzy path
from a fixed labeled permutation is uniquely determined by its full
chronological matrix.  Thus distinct return branches cannot cancel through
equal matrices and opposite metaplectic signs in the four-letter full-rank
model.  A project-chosen deterministic length-128 word with eight complete
blocks fixes one neat strongly-positive AGY-admissible section, and an
independent implementation
replays its exact matrix and 35,420 finite decoder stress cases.  The result
rules out ordinary nuclear Fredholm determinants on the two stated spaces;
holomorphic no-localizer spaces and generalized traces remain separate open
candidates.
