## Hostile-review closure

The initial package versions had mathematically correct headline results, but
the independent review did not accept the initial evidence machinery at face
value.  Repaired-payload-hash attacks exposed unchecked semantic fields and
duplicate/drop-replace rows in C284, C287, and C288.  C285 separately admitted
Python's `False == 0` type confusion and numeric replacements for canonical
rational strings.  C288's first heat-kernel checker also repeated the
producer's closed form instead of reconstructing it from the
interface/Laplace-transform problem, while the C287 and C288 release scripts
initially audited archived PDFs without themselves performing the six fresh
builds.

Those are release blockers even when the formulas are correct.  The repaired
packages therefore reject duplicate JSON keys, enforce exact top-level,
nested, and row schemas with strict types and complete unique index sets,
check every theorem/proof contract, and include the demonstrated semantic
escapes in their mutation suites.  C285 now requires exact Boolean, integer,
list, and canonical-rational-string types.  Its visual repair also removes a
literal `cal S_N` token and an abstract sentence collision that the initial
render-count gate missed.  C288 independently reconstructs the heat layer and
integrated relative trace.  C287 distinguishes the observation norm identity
from the HUM Gramian isomorphism.  The strengthened manifests execute all
evidence routes and the complete fresh-PDF build contract before writing a
release receipt.

The hostile audit also rederived the C284 raw Cartesian Hessian and DFT sign,
the C287 critical Parseval constant and shorter-time missed-wave construction,
and the C288 heat/resolvent/relative-trace normalization off the archived
grids.  No review licenses a literature-priority claim; the source audits name
the classical owners and delimit the package-local reconstruction.

## Five theorem-scale advances

### HCS-C284 -- Thomson polygon point-vortex stability

For equal nonzero circulations on a regular `N`-gon, the paper derives the
relative-equilibrium angular speed from the pair Hamiltonian, reconstructs the
full Cartesian augmented Hessian, and diagonalizes it in moving
radial/tangential coordinates.  The exact Fourier block is governed by
`q_m=m(N-m)` and `sigma_m=2(N-1)-q_m`.  After removing the symmetry sector,
`N=3,...,6` is linearly elliptic, `N=7` has precisely the rank-one nilpotent
degeneracy in modes 3 and 4, and every `N>=8` has a real hyperbolic pair.
Translation, rotation, scaling, circulation/radius singularities, and the
small-`N` faces are separated; no nonlinear conclusion is drawn at seven.

Route-A verdict:
`(A0_FAIL,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)`,
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.

### HCS-C285 -- Gordon--Newell network condensation

For every finite irreducible closed single-server network, including
nonreversible routing, the paper proves the canonical product form with
`Z_N=h_N(w)`, all occupation derivatives, station throughputs, directed edge
currents, time reversal, and the positive-population reversibility criterion.
It then proves the complete bottleneck limit: nonmaximal queues converge
jointly to independent geometric variables; one maximal queue absorbs
`N-O(1)` customers; and tied maximal queues have a uniform
`Dirichlet(1,...,1)` limiting fraction after the nonbottleneck mass is removed.
Zero population, one station, equal weights, zero routing entries, self loops,
gauge changes, and singular/reducible faces are explicit.

Route-A verdict:
`(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)`,
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.

### HCS-C286 -- Coxeter numbers-game strong convergence

For a finite reduced crystallographic root system and every dominant starting
weight, the paper proves that every legal positive-coordinate firing order
terminates at the same antidominant point.  If `J` is the initial zero wall,
every legal word has length `|Phi^+|-|Phi_J^+|`; its cumulative element is
`w_0 w_J`, the unique shortest representative in `w_0 W_J` and the unique
maximal element of the corresponding parabolic quotient.  Strictly dominant,
wall, zero, rank-one, disconnected, and non-simply-laced cases are closed,
while affine, indefinite, noncrystallographic, and arbitrary Cartan extensions
remain outside the theorem.

Route-A verdict:
`(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FAIL)`,
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.

### HCS-C287 -- exact one-end wave-control time

For the Dirichlet wave equation on `(0,L)`, observed through `u_x(L,t)`, the
paper proves that the least full-energy-space revival and the sharp one-end
observability/control threshold both equal `2L/c`, including equality.  At
that time the exact identity is

`integral_0^(2L/c) |u_x(L,t)|^2 dt = 4 E(0) / c^3`.

Every shorter interval misses a nonzero smooth periodic traveling profile;
therefore no shorter observability inequality or dual exact control exists.
HUM duality is stated between the adjoint energy space
`H_0^1 x L^2`, an `L^2` Dirichlet boundary control, and the transposition
state space `L^2 x H^{-1}`.  Endpoint reversal, the absence of a zero mode,
critical equality, and scaling faces are explicit.

Route-A verdict:
`(A0_FAIL,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`,
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.

### HCS-C288 -- delta point-interaction spectral dynamics

For the full-line extension with
`psi'(0+)-psi'(0-)=alpha psi(0)`, the paper closes the quadratic form and
self-adjoint domain, rank-one resolvent, sign-dependent spectrum, unique
attractive bound state, even/odd scattering, exact heat kernel, and integrated
relative heat trace.  The heat correction is

`-alpha/4 exp(alpha(|x|+|y|)/2+alpha^2 t/4)
 erfc((|x|+|y|)/(2 sqrt(t))+alpha sqrt(t)/2)`,

and the relative trace is

`(exp(alpha^2 t/4) erfc(alpha sqrt(t)/2)-1)/2`.

The free face, attractive pole, repulsive chamber, zero/high energy, and
small/large-time boundaries use the same normalization.  The even Robin and
odd Dirichlet half-line channels make the absolutely continuous spectrum and
absence of singular-continuous spectrum explicit.

Route-A verdict:
`(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`,
`ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.
