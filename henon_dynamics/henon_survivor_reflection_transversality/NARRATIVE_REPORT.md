# Narrative report

P60 left two very different effectivity questions under one label.  A root
inside the certified survivor carries a hyperbolic monodromy matrix; an
arbitrary algebraic root of the same closure polynomial need not.  P61
separates these populations.

For `n=2m+1`, the closure is the intersection of
`H^(m+1)(Fix J)` with `Fix R`.  Pulling the latter axis back gives a second
involution `K_m`, and `H^n=J K_m`.  If the two axes were tangent, their common
tangent would be fixed by both involution derivatives and hence by `DH^n`.
Uniform hyperbolicity excludes multiplier `1`.  This proves transversality
without estimating a large polynomial derivative.

At odd period, the symbolic reflection action has one conjugacy class and
each primitive reversible necklace has one representative on the calibrated
axis.  The physical root count is therefore exactly P59's `R_n`.  Its entropy
is `(1/2)log(phi)`, below P60's formal degree entropy `(1/2)log(2)`.

The result is deliberately local.  It proves a genuine positive theorem, but
also shows that physical hyperbolicity cannot settle the exponentially larger
formal ambient population.  The next natural project is the critical
resultant/discriminant of the ambient closure sequence.
