# Source audit

- Kang, arXiv:1410.3997, supplies modern context for symmetric odd periodic
  points and fixed loci of involutions in reversible planar maps.  P61 does
  not import its global annulus theorems.
- Hutz, arXiv:1011.5155, concerns effectivity of dynatomic cycles for
  morphisms of projective varieties.  It does not automatically settle the
  birational Hénon symmetry-line slice.
- The H6 survivor's uniform hyperbolicity and coding are locked to the local
  R058/R059 theorem artifacts listed in the executable certificate.
- P59 supplies the exact odd reflection count; P60 supplies the closure and
  formal degree.  The reversor tangency lemma is proved directly here.
