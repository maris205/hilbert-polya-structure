# Methodology blueprint

- **Analytic layer:** products of involutions and hyperbolic periodic-point
  multipliers.
- **Symbolic layer:** the frozen four-state reversal and odd-axis Möbius
  count from P59.
- **Algebraic layer:** the P60 mixed-axis closure and formal divisor.
- **Exact finite layer:** rational root isolation, interval recurrence and
  interval Horner evaluation.
- **Independent layer:** Cartesian necklace enumeration plus 50-digit root
  classification.
- **Fail-closed boundary:** physical transversality is never promoted to
  ambient squarefreeness, Galois pressure, rational primes or an operator.
