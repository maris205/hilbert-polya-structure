\section{Ambient effectivity remains a separate theorem}

The local transversality proof cannot be applied to a root outside the
certified survivor: its real orbit may leave the isolating rectangles, or the
root may be complex, and no inherited hyperbolic splitting is available.
Thus Corollary~\ref{cor:hyperbolic} does not prove that every $F_n$ is
squarefree or that the formal M\"obius divisor is globally effective.

The narrow next object is the critical resultant
\[
\operatorname{Res}_X(F_n,F_n').
\]
A nonzero all-period formula would close ambient reducedness.  More
informatively, a divisor-level factorization could identify whether a first
zero comes from a genuine primitive tangency or inherited lower-period
intersection multiplicity.  That is an algebraic recurrence problem, not a
consequence of physical pressure.

Nor does simple-root incidence attach Galois heights, rational-prime labels,
a completed determinant or a self-adjoint operator.  The result strengthens
the physical analytic layer of Route A only.
