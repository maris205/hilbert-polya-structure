# Research question

Does the formal odd mixed-axis divisor of P60 count reduced primitive roots on
the certified physical H6 survivor, and how large is that physical population
relative to the formal algebraic degree?

The answer is exact on the survivor: every primitive physical intersection is
transverse and occurs with multiplicity one.  Its population has entropy
`(1/2)log(phi)`, exponentially below the formal entropy `(1/2)log(2)`.
Ambient effectivity remains open and is not inferred from this local theorem.
