# C85 research question

For the frozen sixteen-label closure model, does the complete C80 vector of
twenty subgroup-containment repair thresholds depend only on the generated
subgroup?  If so, can its zero coordinates recover that subgroup and can the
twenty resulting vectors recover the entire actual subgroup poset by reversed
coordinatewise order?

The question is finite and source-bound.  It concerns all `65536` named
supports and the twenty actual subgroups of `Z/9 + Z/3 + Z/2` under the scope
firewall `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
