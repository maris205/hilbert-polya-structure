# Narrative report

The large step in C352 is completeness.  The zero mode by itself is classical; this paper joins it to a uniform all-integer classification of every discrete energy, both continuum thresholds, and the reflectionless scattering mechanism under one fixed operator convention.

The decisive observation is that the two components of the squared Dirac operator are adjacent members of the same shape-invariant hierarchy.  The hierarchy does more than generate plausible states: intertwining sends any additional bound state down to the free problem, where none exists.  It therefore supplies both construction and exhaustion.  Applying the same raising chain to a constant produces the bounded non-`L^2` threshold state, while applying it to a plane wave produces a Jost solution with no reflected exponential.

This is also a clean negative Route-A result.  The candidate has a genuine natural quantization, so A4 is positive, but its integer parameter is not arithmetic origin and its finite scattering product is not a target Euler product.  The missing A0--A3 structure is stated rather than blurred.
