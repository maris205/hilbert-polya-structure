# Research question

For every positive integer kink height, can the one-dimensional Jackiw--Rebbi Dirac operator be classified in one theorem from operator domain through exact bound states, threshold behavior, and scattering, without turning a finite symbolic table into a continuum proof?

The answer established here is yes.  Supersymmetric factorization reduces the problem to the integer Pöschl--Teller hierarchy.  Shape invariance exhausts the bound states, a Darboux image of the free zero-momentum state identifies the non-square-integrable thresholds, and the same chain gives reflectionless Jost solutions.  The finite evidence checks formulas and conventions for `0 <= n <= 24`; the proof is uniform in every integer `n >= 1`.

The arithmetic research question is separate.  Natural self-adjoint quantization earns `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`, but the family has no structural prime clock, Euler factors, target divisor, or target-zero identification.  Route A is therefore rejected and Route B remains locked.
