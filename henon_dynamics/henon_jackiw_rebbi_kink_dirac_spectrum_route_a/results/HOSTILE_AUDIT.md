# Hostile audit

## Main failure modes tested

- off-by-one energy ladder or omitted bound pair;
- wrong sign in either Pöschl--Teller channel;
- doubled or misplaced zero mode;
- promotion of a threshold resonance to an eigenvalue;
- nonunit transmission factor or nonzero reflection amplitude;
- repaired evidence hash after a semantic alteration;
- altered evidence date or fixed build epoch under a repaired hash;
- duplicate/nonfinite JSON and ambiguous YAML;
- promoted Route-A verdict or forbidden target claim.

## Outcome

All declared attacks are rejected by the independent checker.  The analytic proof also closes the main non-computational risk: a finite table cannot establish spectral exhaustion, so exhaustion is proved by Darboux descent to the free operator.
