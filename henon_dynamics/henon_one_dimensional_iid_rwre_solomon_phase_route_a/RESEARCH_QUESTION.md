# Research question

For the one-dimensional nearest-neighbour walk in an iid strictly elliptic
static environment, can one package the quenched finite-interval scale function,
the full log-bias direction trichotomy, the annealed almost-sure velocity
including both transient zero-speed chambers, and the Beta and constant faces
in one convention-complete theorem?

The answer proved here is yes.  The theorem is all-parameter under
\(\mathbf E|\log\rho_0|<\infty\).  Exact finite enumeration is used only to
catch convention and implementation errors; the infinite-time result is owned
by the stationary-ergodic crossing-time proof.

The Route-A question is answered separately and negatively.  The environment
law supplies no intrinsic rational-prime taxonomy, logarithmic-prime clock,
target determinant, target divisor, or natural Hilbert--Pólya realization.
