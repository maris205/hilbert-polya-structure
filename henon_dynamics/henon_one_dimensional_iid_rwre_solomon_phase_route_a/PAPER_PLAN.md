## Round structure

- Round 0: model, probability-space distinction, scale theorem, and direction.
- Round 1: stationary crossing lemma, finite/infinite-mean time change, full
  velocity theorem, Beta and constant corollaries.
- Round 2: exact evidence ownership, all excluded faces, source audit,
  collision boundary, and Route-A firewall.
