# One coherent progress narrative

The a=1 Lyness identity closes an exceptional finite-order map, but does
not describe the generic source. The present package first eliminates
every positive-real geometry gap using the proper strictly convex log
integral. An oriented Abel ratio then gives the entire map on every oval,
so primitive families, repeats, reversal and stability share one clock.

The rational question is sharper than the real question. On a regular
rational-energy fiber the map translates by a rational elliptic point.
Periodic rational states therefore force a torsion translation; the
external positive-coordinate classification reduces allowable periods
to 1,5,9. In contrast, a nonempty gap between two classical real rotation
endpoints gives every sufficiently large prime-integer real period by a
one-line integer-interval argument. These real ovals cannot be relabeled
as rational-prime periodic carriers.

The stopping result is part of the progress: real resonances are continuous,
so ordinary point-cardinality zeta is undefined; the native finite-annulus
unitary is explicitly noncompact. This prevents inappropriate A2/A4 promotion.
The theorem package is owner-heavy and source-local, not a novelty certificate.
