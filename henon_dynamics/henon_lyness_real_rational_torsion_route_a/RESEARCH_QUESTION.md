# Research question

Can the full positive-real periodic spectrum of an integrable rational map
be treated as a rational prime-bearing orbit ledger?

The answer is no for the autonomous positive Lyness source. We determine
the entire oval map by one Abel ratio, then separate real rational-rotation
families from rational-coordinate points. Every sufficiently large prime
integer is a real least period at any fixed a>0, a≠1, but the explicit
classical rational classification allows only 1,5,9. Continuous resonant
families also prevent the ordinary point-cardinality zeta from having
finite coefficients.

This is a theorem-scale source reconstruction with credited classical
dependencies, not a claim that Lyness elliptic integrability is new.
