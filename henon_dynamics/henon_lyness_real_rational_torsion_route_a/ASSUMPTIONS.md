# Assumptions and conventions

- The source is autonomous and a>0. Periodically forced recurrences are excluded.
- States have x,y>0. Axes, projective base points and other real components
  are not positive states; no zero-totalization is applied.
- A rational orbit requires a,x,y in Q. A rational energy does not imply
  a rational point on its positive oval.
- One map step is the source clock. Hamiltonian T and tau only parametrize
  its oval action; rho=tau/T has an explicitly clockwise section.
- Cartesian det DF is (a+y)/x²; only the density dx wedge dy/(xy) is preserved.
- Every noncentral positive energy is smooth elliptic. The singular center
  is handled separately and is never passed to Mazur's theorem.
- BR2004 Propositions 6–7 supply two endpoint limits. GMX2012 supplies the
  positive-coordinate rational-period restriction. Mazur supplies the deep
  rational elliptic torsion theorem. These dependencies are not reproved.
- No global monotonicity or everywhere nonzero twist is assumed.
- The Koopman probability is normalized on a chosen compact regular annulus,
  not the full quadrant.

Period means least positive iterate count; a prime integer period is
distinguished from the historical synonym “prime period” for least period.
