# Limitations and stopping conditions

This is OWNER_HEAVY classical source-local reconstruction. It does not
certify literature novelty. BR rotation endpoints and GMX/Mazur arithmetic
results remain external theorem dependencies.

For rational a, positive rational periods are confined to 1,5,9, whereas
real large-prime periods are entire irrational-coordinate ovals. Neither
rational energy nor elliptic integrability repairs this arithmetic gap.
A nine-cycle at a=7 does not prove one exists at every rational a.
The orbit starting point need not be torsion; periodicity concerns the
translation point.

Every a>0 has an uncountable fixed set at some finite iterate. Consequently
the ordinary real point-cardinality zeta has no finite-coefficient germ.
The finite-annulus Koopman unitary is noncompact and supplies no canonical
target self-adjoint generator. A2 and A3 fail; A4 stays a formal hint.

The exact finite grid is not an infinite theorem. Ninety-digit Abel quadrature
is a control, not an interval certificate. Certified pi/cosine enclosures
only validate rational rotation intervals lying between classical endpoints.
The excluded a=0 map is globally six-periodic. The a=1 map is globally
five-periodic and has a different spectral type.
