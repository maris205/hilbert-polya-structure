- G. Bastien and M. Rogalski, “Global Behavior of the Solutions of Lyness'
  Difference Equation,” Journal of Difference Equations and Applications
  10 (2004), 977–1003.
  [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/10236190410001728104).
  Propositions 6 and 7 are the two endpoint inputs.
- F. Beukers and R. Cushman, “Zeeman's Monotonicity Conjecture,”
  Journal of Differential Equations 143 (1998), 191–200.
  [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/jdeq.1997.3359);
  [Utrecht primary preprint](https://dspace.library.uu.nl/handle/1874/2189).
  Positive prior owner, not a required theorem dependency here.
- A. Gasull, V. Mañosa and X. Xarles, “Rational periodic sequences for
  the Lyness recurrence,” Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems
  32 (2012), 587–604.
  [Publisher](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2012.32.587);
  [Primary arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/1004.5511v1).

  convention and exact a=7 nine-cycle; the classification is credited.
- B. Mazur, “Modular curves and the Eisenstein ideal,” Publications
  Mathématiques de l'IHÉS 47 (1977), 33–186.
  [Primary archive](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1977__47__33_0/).
  The deep rational torsion theorem is named explicitly, not reconstructed
