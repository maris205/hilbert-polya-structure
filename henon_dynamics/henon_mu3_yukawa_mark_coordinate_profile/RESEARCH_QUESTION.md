# C67 research question

C66 determines the invariant factors of the restricted mark map but does not
record how its finite cokernel sits in the named S_i mark coordinates. For
each standard coordinate vector e_j, what is the least positive integer
o_j for which n e_j is an integral mark vector? What is the corresponding
profile for the transpose map?

The answer is the pair of exact 16-entry denominator profiles below. It is
a coordinate-level refinement of C66, not a choice of Smith transformation
or a claim about the full Burnside ring.
