# Research question — C114

For the fixed polynomial Hénon germ

\[
F(u,v)=\bigl(u^2+\tfrac32u-\tfrac12v,u\bigr)
\]

at the origin, what exact operator data survive after passing to the order-four
local algebra \(A_4=\mathbb Q[u,v]/(u,v)^5\)?

The bounded question has four parts:

1. Is composition with \(F\) well defined on \(A_4\), and what is its exact
   15 by 15 matrix in the total-degree monomial basis?
2. How does the maximal-ideal filtration split the matrix into five
   associated-graded blocks?
3. What exact traces, determinant, characteristic polynomial, and
   \(\det(I-zK)\) follow from those blocks?
4. Which features come from the nonlinear term \(u^2\), and which spectral
   data agree with the linearized control?

The question explicitly stops at this finite quotient.  It does not ask for
a global orbit atlas, a global Koopman spectrum, a Banach-space realization,
nuclearity, a Fredholm determinant, analytic continuation, or Route-B data.
