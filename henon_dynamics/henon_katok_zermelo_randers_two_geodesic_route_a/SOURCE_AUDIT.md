## Verified owners

- A. B. Katok, *Ergodic perturbations of degenerate integrable Hamiltonian
  systems*, DOI `10.1070/IM1973v007n03ABEH001958`:
  <https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im2279>.  This is the historical construction
  owner; it is not cited as a line-by-line proof of this package.
- W. Ziller, *Geometry of the Katok examples*, DOI
  `10.1017/S0143385700001851`:
  <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/ergodic-theory-and-dynamical-systems/article/geometry-of-the-katok-examples/A791972A0BEBD1932F6B99AC8D0A334C>.
  This owns the geometric closed-geodesic analysis of the Katok examples.
- D. Bao, C. Robles, and Z. Shen, *Zermelo navigation on Riemannian
  manifolds*, DOI `10.4310/jdg/1098137838`:
  <https://projecteuclid.org/journals/journal-of-differential-geometry/volume-66/issue-3/Zermelo-navigation-on-Riemannian-manifolds/10.4310/jdg/1098137838.full>.
  This owns the navigation formulation and the constant-flag-curvature
  theorem used here.

## Claim boundary

The package supplies a source-local reconstruction with its own proof of the
special two-sphere theorem.  It makes no priority claim and does not attribute
the executable finite grid to the cited papers.  The finite rational ledger
checks conventions only; it is not evidence for the all-period irrational
exclusion.  No arithmetic prime correspondence, Euler factor, automorphy,
root number, target functional equation, target zero match, or
Hilbert--Polya statement is sourced or claimed.
