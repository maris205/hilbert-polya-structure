# HCS-C339 research question

For the Zermelo navigation metric on the unit round two-sphere generated by
the rotational Killing wind (W=\epsilon\partial_\phi), can one give a
complete, cutoff-free classification of prime closed oriented geodesics when
(\epsilon) is irrational, including their exact periods, transverse
Poincare data, rational-resonance face, and loss-of-convexity boundary?

The theorem is source-local.  It asks neither for target arithmetic data nor
for a target determinant, target zeros, or a Hilbert--Polya operator.
