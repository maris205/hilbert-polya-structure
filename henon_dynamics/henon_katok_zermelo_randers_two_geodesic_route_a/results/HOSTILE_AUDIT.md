# HCS-C339 hostile audit

The mutation lane rejects 54/54 attacks.  More than ten semantic attacks
repair the canonical evidence payload hash before checking.  Targets include
candidate/obstruction/date/source ownership, evaluator authority/version/hash,
YAML raw and semantic bindings, navigation convention, irrational theorem,
the exact \(SO(3)\) return equation and its two axes, rational face,
orientation, convexity wall, collision boundary, Route-A
tuple, Route B lock, scope flags, exact row coordinates, canonical rational
strings, nested additions, duplicate/omitted rows, references, and nonclaims.

A stale-hash control is rejected separately.  Parser attacks cover duplicate
and nonfinite JSON, nonmapping JSON, duplicate/non-string YAML keys, anchors,
aliases, merges, and nonmapping YAML.  YAML authority/status/source/Route-B
attacks are also tried with repaired YAML hashes in the evidence.
