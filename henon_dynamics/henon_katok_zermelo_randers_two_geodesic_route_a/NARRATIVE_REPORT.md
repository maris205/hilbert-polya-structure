# HCS-C339 narrative report

This package takes a genuinely nonreversible Finsler system and closes its
entire irrational periodic-orbit ledger.  The key step is not numerical orbit
search: navigation splits the flow into commuting round-geodesic and wind
rotations, so the non-equatorial closure question becomes the exact equation
\(R_z(\epsilon T)=gR_z(-T)g^{-1}\) on \(SO(3)\).  Its axes are \(e_3\) and
\(ge_3\), distinct off the equatorial frames.  Irrational wind removes every
generic resonance but
leaves the two invariant equators, whose periods and Jacobi determinants are
explicit.  The rational and zero-wind faces explain exactly where the finite
ledger ceases to hold.  This earns A1 analytic passage, but the absence of an
arithmetic carrier or target determinant keeps Route A rejected.
