# C45 results

The pressure-normalized roof

\[
\widehat\tau=h_*\tau,
\qquad P(-h_*\tau)=0,
\]

is positive, Hölder, non-lattice, and has suspension entropy one.  The
prime-orbit theorem therefore gives

\[
\#\{\gamma:\widehat\ell_\gamma\le T\}
\sim\frac{e^T}{T}.
\]

The normalized local label

\[
P_\gamma=e^{\widehat\ell_\gamma}
=|\Lambda_\gamma|^{h_*}
\]

produces exact logarithmic-derivative atoms, but is only a positive real
number.  No rational-prime interpretation is asserted.
