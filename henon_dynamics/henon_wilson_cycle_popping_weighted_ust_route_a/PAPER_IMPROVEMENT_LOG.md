# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — complete stochastic core

The first manuscript version froze labelled parallel edges and positive
conductances, proved the local diamond/strip abelian lemma, constructed the
Wilson legal order, and used the last-exit/Cramer's-rule telescope to derive the
weighted spanning-tree law and matrix-tree normalization.  This is already a
complete result rather than an outline.

## Round 1 — determinantal completion

The second version added the full all-orders transfer-current theorem.  It
introduced both \(H_{ef}=c_f b_e^TL^+b_f\) and its symmetric similar kernel,
proved root independence through the reduced inverse, derived the multivariate
conductance-perturbation determinant, and handled singleton, tree,
parallel-edge, root-change, and orientation boundaries.

## Round 2 — reproducibility and scope closure

The final version added exact evidence totals, clarified that finite stacks are
receipts rather than proof, attributed Wilson/Burton--Pemantle/Kirchhoff/Chaiken
ownership, separated C338 from C176 and C181, and made the Route-A rejection and
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER` firewall explicit.  It does not claim priority,
infinite-graph extensions, directed nonreversible extensions, or any target
Euler-factor, zeta, root-number, automorphy, zero, or Hilbert--Pólya statement.
