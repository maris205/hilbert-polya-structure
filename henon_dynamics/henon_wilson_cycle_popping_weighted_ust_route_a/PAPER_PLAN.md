# Paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

A single finite-network proof carries arbitrary-order infinite-stack cycle
popping through Wilson loop erasure to the weighted spanning-tree law,
matrix-tree normalization, and every transfer-current edge determinant, with
all root, singleton, tree, and labelled-parallel-edge conventions explicit.

## Structure

1. Freeze the conductance multigraph, stack law, incidence orientation,
   Laplacian, pseudoinverse, and nonsymmetric/symmetric kernel conventions.
2. State one theorem containing abelianity, almost-sure termination, Wilson
   equivalence, the weighted tree law, matrix-tree identity, and all (k)-edge
   determinants.
3. Prove local diamonds and the strip argument before invoking probability.
4. Build the canonical Wilson legal sequence and close almost-sure termination.
5. Use a labelled last-exit LERW formula and Cramer's-rule telescoping to derive
   the tree law and its normalization.
6. Use conductance perturbations and the determinant lemma to recover all
   transfer-current minors at once.
7. Close singleton/tree/parallel/root boundaries, report finite evidence, state
   source ownership and the Route-A firewall.

## Revision design

- Round 0: full abelian/Wilson/weighted-law proof and matrix-tree closure.
- Round 1: add the complete conductance-perturbation transfer-current proof,
  symmetric-kernel similarity, orientation invariance, and boundaries.
- Round 2: add the exhaustive evidence ledger, source ownership/collision
  boundary, and explicit Route-A rejection/nonclaims.
