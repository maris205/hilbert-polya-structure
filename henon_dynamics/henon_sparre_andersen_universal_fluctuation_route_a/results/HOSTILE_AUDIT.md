# Hostile audit

## Convention attacks

- Replacing strict positivity by nonnegativity is rejected.
- Treating a tied maximum as unique is rejected.
- Dropping continuity is rejected by the two-step simple-walk witness.
- Excluding time zero from the maximum-time definition would change endpoint
  masses and is not allowed.

## Formula attacks

- Central-binomial, first-descent, convolution, and individual arcsine-cell
  mutations are rejected.
- Histogram, history-count, tie-count, scaling-mass, and metadata mutations are
  rejected after their payload hashes are repaired.

## Claim attacks

Finite enumeration is not promoted to a proof of universality.  The primary
source is cited without a novelty claim.  Probability transforms are not
called Euler factors, arithmetic zeta functions, target divisors, functional
equations, or Hilbert–Pólya operators.  Route B remains false.
