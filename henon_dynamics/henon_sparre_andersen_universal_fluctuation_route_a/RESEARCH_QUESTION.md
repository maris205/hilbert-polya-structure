# Research question

For an iid real random walk with continuous increments symmetric about zero,
can one close in one theorem the survival law, first strict descent, positive
occupation count, unique maximum time, and arcsine scaling limit, while also
showing exactly where atomic increments invalidate the shared formulas?

The answer proved here is yes.  Continuity removes all ties.  Independence and
symmetry factor the unique maximum about its location, normalization forces the
central-binomial survival generating function, and the Sparre–Andersen
permutation-cycle lemma identifies the positive-count law with the same
arcsine convolution.  A two-step simple symmetric walk supplies the minimal
atomic failure witness.

The question is source-local probability theory.  It asks for no rational-prime
carrier, target local datum, Euler factor, root number, automorphy statement,
target divisor, functional equation, or Hilbert–Pólya operator.
