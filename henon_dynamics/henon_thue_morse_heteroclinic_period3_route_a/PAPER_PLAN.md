## Title and thesis

**A Period-Three Limit Orbit in a Thue--Morse Heteroclinic Closure.**  A
single dense full `Z`-orbit generates an intrinsic finite periodic limit
component, but its isolated interface points force wandering, nonminimality,
and failure of forward topological transitivity.
