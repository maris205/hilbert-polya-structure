# C154 research question

Can a nonempty periodic skeleton arise intrinsically in the closure of one
symbolic heteroclinic orbit, instead of being adjoined as a free topological
component, while retaining an exact all-period orbit ledger?

Freeze `x_j=(234)_j` for `j<0` and `x_j=t_j` for `j>=0`, where `t` is the
one-sided Thue--Morse fixed point, and put
`X=closure{sigma^n x:n in Z}`.  The task is to classify every limit point,
the wandering and nonwandering sets, all periodic points, and the source
Artin--Mazur zeta.  The distinction between a dense full `Z`-orbit and
standard forward topological transitivity is part of the frozen question.
