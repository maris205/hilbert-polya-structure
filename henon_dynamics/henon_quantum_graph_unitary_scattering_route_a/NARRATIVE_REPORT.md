# Narrative report — HCS-C133

C128 supplied a natural finite metaplectic quantization, but it did not supply
a scattering system whose secular determinant was owned by intrinsic metric
orbits.  C133 changes the subtype: a theta metric graph has a source-defined
directed-bond Hilbert space, Kirchhoff scattering, and metric propagation.

The central positive result is not merely that a finite matrix can be made
unitary.  The same edge lengths and vertex rule determine its antiunitary
reversal, its secular determinant, and the amplitude/phase assigned to every
primitive bond orbit.  The exact determinant factors after the physical
length specialization, while the formal orbit product retains repetitions and
signed scattering amplitudes at all orders.

Two source perturbations locate the boundary.  A wrong vertex normalization
is exactly nonunitary; a direction-asymmetric reverse length breaks the
physical bond-reversal identity.  Thus the A4 advance is conditional on a
genuine metric graph rather than post-hoc matrix fitting.

This closes a unitary/scattering structural gate only.  Nothing in the package
compares its secular divisor with an arithmetic or external spectral target.
