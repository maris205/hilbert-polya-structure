# Research question — HCS-C133

Can a finite metric graph supply a source-derived unitary/scattering object
whose clock, antiunitary reversal, secular determinant, and primitive orbit
expansion are all exact before any target-facing comparison is attempted?

The frozen candidate is the theta graph with vertices `L,R`, three undirected
edges of lengths `1,2,3`, and standard degree-three Kirchhoff conditions.  One
iteration means one scattering event together with the symmetric half-edge
propagation assigned to the incoming and outgoing directed bonds.

Success requires:

1. an explicit Hilbert space and real-`k` unitary family;
2. exact `Theta=J K` time reversal with the same metric clock;
3. a closed secular determinant and all-period trace/primitive product;
4. controls that destroy unitarity or physical reversal when a frozen source
   rule is changed;
5. a conservative Route-A classification with no target-divisor promotion.
