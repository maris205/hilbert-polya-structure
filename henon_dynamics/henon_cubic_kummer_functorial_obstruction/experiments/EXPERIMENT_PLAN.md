# Experiment plan

The experiment is a falsification test, not evidence for a theorem by finite
enumeration.

1. Freeze a cyclic action and a nonconstant cubic phase label.
2. Enumerate every pair of group elements and every base point.
3. Verify the cocycle law before applying representations.
4. Verify all three Kummer characters separately.
5. Verify the determinant channel and a nontrivial closed path.
6. Repeat with a different modulus and a quadratic phase label.

Any nonidentity closed holonomy would refute the claimed implementation. The
general theorem is proved independently in the paper.
