## Frozen five-paper round

The round consists of five complete papers, not five installments:

1. **C264 — finite abelian power maps.**  Prove the canonical periodic/nilpotent
   split, every fixed and primitive count, zeta, identical in-tree, tail
   layers, image ranks, full Koopman characteristic polynomial, and every
   zero-Jordan multiplicity; isolate `d=0`.
2. **C265 — exponential Hawkes process.**  Prove the affine transform,
   subcritical stationary Laplace/moment law, intensity and counting
   covariances, complete covariance atom, frozen Bartlett convention, window
   variance, cluster law, and all stability faces.
3. **C266 — skew Brownian interface.**  Prove the symmetric-local-time kernel,
   Lebesgue/speed measure distinction, generator/resolvent, complete
   two-sided exits, mean exit, occupation law, and endpoint reflections.
4. **C267 — Wannier--Stark lattice.**  Prove gauge conjugacy, ladder spectrum,
   Bessel eigenbasis and propagator, least Bloch return, shell law, and sharp
   compactness/Schatten boundaries, including `F->0`.
5. **C268 — constant-field Lorentz flow.**  Prove the invariant projector
   decomposition, all-time proper-time flow and position integral,
   Lorentz/determinant invariants, periodic/drift/growth atlas, and every
   electric, magnetic, null, and zero face.

## Per-paper gates

### G0 — frozen owner and convention

- State space, parameters, source clock, normalization, boundary policy, and
  determinant convention are explicit.
- C264 distinguishes `d=0`; C265 freezes predictable left-limit intensity and
  Fourier normalization; C266 freezes symmetric local time and reference
  measure; C267 freezes Fourier sign and full-line Hilbert space; C268 freezes
  metric signature, tensor index placement, and proper time.
- The nearest C1--C263 owners and exact differences are recorded.

### G1 — theorem-scale closure

- C264 must deliver the zero-Jordan theorem, not only cycles.
- C265 must separate intensity covariance, continuous counting covariance,
  and the complete measure with its Dirac atom.
- C266 must retain speed and Lebesgue kernels plus both exit labels.
- C267 must prove infinite-dimensional spectrum and operator ideals rather
  than infer them from finite matrices.
- C268 must retain the null-field polynomial face and must not infer a closed
  physical worldline from a periodic velocity.

producer-independent checker, an independent SymPy reconstruction, fresh-path
byte replay, repaired-hash semantic mutations plus a stale-hash control, and
generic/equality/singular boundary rows.  Finite rows are regression oracles,
not proofs of all-parameter theorems.

## Collision and nonclaim controls

Forbidden shortcuts include replacing C264 with its Dickson quotient,
collapsing C265's three covariance objects, using right local time in C266,
proving C267 only on a finite box, or using coordinate time/null-field
diagonalization in C268.  Ihara/Hashimoto, generic Grover/Szegedy, free
transport, and same-round risk/workload dual pairs remain rejected by the
collision audit.

Workspace ownership does not assert literature priority.  Primary references
must be existence/metadata/context checked.  No finite computation is
described as proof of an arbitrary-group, arbitrary-parameter, or
infinite-dimensional theorem.
