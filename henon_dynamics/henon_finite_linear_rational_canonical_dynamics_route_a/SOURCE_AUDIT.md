# Source audit

Only primary bibliographic sources are used for historical context:

- B. Elspas, “The theory of autonomous linear sequential networks,” *IRE
  Transactions on Circuit Theory* 6 (1959), 45--60,
  DOI `10.1109/TCT.1959.1086506`.
- G. Xu and Y. M. Zou, “Linear dynamical systems over finite rings,” *Journal
  of Algebra* 321 (2009), 2149--2155,
  DOI `10.1016/j.jalgebra.2008.09.029`.

The package does not assert literature priority.  Its theorem is proved
internally and every finite witness is reproduced from the checked ledger.
No source is used to identify these finite-field factors with target Euler
factors, root numbers, automorphy, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
