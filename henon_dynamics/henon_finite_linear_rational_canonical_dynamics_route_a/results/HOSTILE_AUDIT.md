# Hostile audit

Seventeen repaired-hash semantic corruptions are rejected: fixed formula,
Route-A overall token, Route-B invocation flip, exact points, periodic
dimension, predicted preperiod, Koopman zero multiplicity, zeta cycle factor, restriction
order, matrix entry, field order, GF(4)-to-`Z/4Z` substitution, GF(4) matrix,
scope literal, forbidden target claim, source commit, and evaluator hash.

One additional stale-hash corruption is rejected at the digest gate.  Thus the
audit distinguishes semantic detection after hash repair from ordinary
integrity detection; it does not describe the stale-hash attack as resealed.
