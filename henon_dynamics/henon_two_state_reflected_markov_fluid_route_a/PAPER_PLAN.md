# Paper plan

- Round 0: define the reflected PDMP, prove the sharp drift trichotomy by the Skorokhod map and an embedded Lindley chain, and state the stable law.
- Round 1: derive the stationary atom/densities, all moments, environmental marginals, and the stationary regulator rate.
- Round 2: add the complete closed-class zero-rate atlas, source and collision boundaries, exact-evidence semantics, and the Route-A firewall.

The target is a compact theorem paper.  The main claim is the closed all-parameter classification, not a sequence of incremental numerical observations.
