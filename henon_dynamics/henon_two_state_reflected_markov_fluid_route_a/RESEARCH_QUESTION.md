# Research question

For a two-state Markov-modulated fluid reflected at the origin, can one give a single exact theorem that (i) separates positive recurrence, null recurrence, and transience sharply; (ii) reconstructs the entire stable stationary law, its boundary atom, all moments, and the regulator rate; and (iii) remains correct on every zero-rate communication or drift face?

The frozen source model is
\[
  X_t=X_0+\int_0^t r_{J_s}\,ds+L_t,\qquad r_0=-d,\quad r_1=c,
\]
where `0 -> 1` has rate `a`, `1 -> 0` has rate `b`, and `L` is the minimal regulator at zero.  The irreducible core has `a,b,c,d>0`; all zero-rate faces are treated separately by closed environmental classes.

Finite exact panels audit normalization only.  The analytic proof, not sampling, owns the continuum theorem.
