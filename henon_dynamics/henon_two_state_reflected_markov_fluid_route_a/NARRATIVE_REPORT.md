# Narrative report

The decisive quantity is not a fitted decay rate but the stationary environmental drift `bar r=(ac-bd)/(a+b)`.  Sampling at full on/off cycles turns the reflected fluid exactly into a Lindley recursion.  This exposes a sharp three-way transition: negative mean produces a normalizable stationary law, zero mean produces recurrent returns without a finite invariant probability, and positive mean produces linear escape.

The stable law is unusually explicit.  Interior probability flux must vanish, forcing the two state densities to be proportional; the remaining transport equation fixes one exponential rate.  Boundary flow then fixes the atom, and normalization closes the law.  Consequently the positive workload is exactly exponential and every moment follows in one line.

The main robustness improvement is the closed-class boundary atlas.  When a transition rate vanishes, the environment is reducible and global uniqueness is generally the wrong concept.  The package therefore classifies each closed class and only asserts uniqueness when the dynamics actually select a single attracting closed class.

The closest workspace comparison is C346's deterministic two-dimensional oblique Skorokhod map.  Here reflection acts on a scalar fluid driven by a stochastic two-state Markov environment, so neither the deterministic path-map theorem nor its M-matrix threshold is reused.  C351's discrete Jackson network and C332's scalar deterministic play operator remain separate neighbors.
