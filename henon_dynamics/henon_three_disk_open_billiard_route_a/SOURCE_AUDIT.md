## Claim boundary

There is **no literature-priority claim** for open-billiard coding,
dispersing hyperbolicity, the three-disk repeller, or periodic-orbit zeta
methods.  The contribution of this package is a frozen, auditable theorem
contract joining the convex variational proof, exact symbolic ledgers,
boundary atlas, and conservative Route-A classification.

The collision-code zeta counts bounces.  It is not a geometric-length zeta,
an arithmetic target Euler product, or a completed target function.  Mention
of the exterior Dirichlet Laplacian records a natural quantization only; no
spectral identification, resonance-zero correspondence, root number,
automorphy statement, or Hilbert--Pólya operator is claimed.
