## Cross-audit clarification applied to every round

The final conditional source distinguishes a primitive oriented geometric
ray from its repeated traversals.  Cyclic word classes correspond to
periodic-ray iterates, whereas primitive cyclic classes correspond to
primitive rays.  In particular `[01]` and `[0101]` share geometric support
but have multiplicities one and two.  All three archived rounds were rebuilt
after this semantic repair.
