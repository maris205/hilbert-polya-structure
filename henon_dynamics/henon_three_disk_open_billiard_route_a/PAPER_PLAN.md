# Paper plan

The article is organized around one theorem rather than a sequence of
computational observations.

1. Define the exterior flow, cyclic reduction, orientation, clock, and strict
   no-eclipse chamber.
2. Prove the capsule-gap lemma and the convex variational coding theorem.
3. Derive specular non-grazing admissibility, converse uniqueness, isolation,
   the primitive-ray/traversal-multiplicity decomposition, and time reversal.
4. Prove the adjacency spectrum, fixed and Möbius ledgers, collision-code
   zeta, and length bounds.
5. Establish hyperbolicity from positive determinant-one optical blocks and
   exhibit period-two and period-three checks.
6. Give the exact boundary atlas and state what fails outside the chamber.
7. Separate theorem proof from reproducibility evidence and freeze the
   Route-A/nonclaim boundary.

Revision 0 contains the model, headline theorem, and core derivation.
Revision 1 adds the detailed variational/converse and monodromy arguments.
Revision 2 adds the evidence architecture, boundary table, source audit,
Route-A evaluation, and AI-use statement.
