consequences; it does not stand in for the geometric proof.

1. Freeze source commit, epoch, scope, evaluator hash, candidate ID, and
   obstruction ID in JSON, YAML, prose, and release script.
2. Produce exact fixed/primitive/orbit counts for periods 1–16, and directly
   enumerate all three-symbol words through period 10.
3. Expand the rational collision-code zeta through degree 16 and verify its
   logarithmic derivative against `F_n`.
4. Audit six exact integer geometries in the strict no-eclipse chamber, two
   symmetric rays, and 175 rational positive optical products.
5. Independently recompute every ledger without importing producer code;
   cross-check the determinant, series, Möbius, chamber, and monodromy
   identities in SymPy.
6. Replay the producer byte-for-byte and subject JSON/YAML contracts to
   repaired-hash semantic, duplicate-key, missing-key, unknown-key, and type
   mutations.
7. Compile three substantively different manuscript rounds twice each in
   fresh directories with fixed epoch; require byte identity, clean settled
   logs, embedded subset fonts, text contracts, and a final visual audit.
8. Close exactly 27 payload files plus one self-excluding release manifest.

Success requires every lane to pass.  Failure of the geometric argument
cannot be repaired by increasing an enumeration cutoff.
