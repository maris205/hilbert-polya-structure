# Research question

For three equal circular obstacles at the vertices of an equilateral
triangle, can one prove—uniformly for every period—that the elementary
three-symbol no-repeat shift is the exact periodic-ray dynamics, rather than
only a numerical surrogate?

The answer is yes in the sharp strict no-eclipse chamber
`d>4r/sqrt(3)`.  The main burden is geometric: existence, uniqueness,
physical admissibility, non-grazing isolation, and hyperbolicity must all be
proved without treating a finite word search as proof.  Once that bridge is
established, the exact fixed-point, primitive-orbit, zeta, reversal, length,
and monodromy statements follow.

The Route-A question is narrower.  Does this exact source system also supply
the missing arithmetic/local or target-spectral bridges?  It does not; only
A1 passes.
