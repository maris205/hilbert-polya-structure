### G0 -- independent ownership and source boundary

Workspace `NEW` means only that no prior local package owns the frozen theorem.
No paper presents a cited classical theorem or another package's dictionary as
its own novelty.

### G1 -- analytic completeness

All-period, all-prime, full-spectrum, exact-determinant and if-and-only-if
headlines must be proved under explicit hypotheses.  Finite evidence checks
normalization and implementation but never proves an unbounded theorem by
sampling.  Native source determinants remain distinct from target A2.

non-string keys, aliases, anchors, merges, implicit dates, unknown fields and
type substitutions.  Candidate ID, complete baseline, epoch, evaluator hash,
strict tuple, Route-B lock, obstruction ID and scope flags are invariant.
