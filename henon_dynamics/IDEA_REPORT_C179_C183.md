## Design objective

The user requested five genuinely large, independent papers rather than five slices of one manuscript. This round therefore changes the mathematical owner every time: varying-modulus arithmetic return dynamics, holomorphic orbifold dynamics, deterministic graph routing, ultradiscrete integrability, and stochastic representation theory.

A candidate survived only if one paper could close an all-parameter theorem with an explicit source clock, exact operator/orbit consequences, and a sharp Route-A stopping boundary. Finite tables are regression evidence, never the basis for an infinite claim.

## Collision scan and pre-freeze pivots

- **Gauss/Farey and Pell reduction:** killed because the modular/continued-fraction owner already has extensive repository coverage and the proposed determinant would repeat that geometry.
- **Toral automorphisms, odometers, rowmotion, and interval exchanges:** killed as near-neighbors of existing cat-map, solenoid/2-adic, necklace, and Rauzy/AGY lines.
- **Calogero and nonlinear oscillator extensions:** deferred because they would sit too close to C178's harmonic-strobe clock in the same adjacent round.
- **Sierpiński spectral decimation:** retained as a future pivot, but not selected after the random-transposition package supplied a cleaner stochastic/operator subtype with a complete all-size theorem.
- **Double-bracket/Brockett flow:** retained as the hard fallback for C182. It was not needed after the periodic box--ball internal-symmetry sectors closed exactly.

This is a repository collision and theorem-ownership audit, not a universal literature novelty certification.

## Frozen candidates and independent theorem increments

### C179 -- Zsigmondy congruence first-return tower

Freeze every coprime pair (a>b\ge1). A rational prime (p\nmid ab) is a primitive divisor of (a^n-b^n) exactly when the marked point (1) first returns at time (n) under multiplication by (ab^{-1}\pmod p). One paper must include the exact Bang--Zsigmondy exceptions, the full prime-power order lift, every finite-fibre cycle/zeta/spectrum/reversor, and the global-owner no-go. The large advance is that rational primes arise from an intrinsic first-return predicate; the strict boundary is that the clock is (n), not (log p), and the fibres do not select a unique global phase-space owner.

### C180 -- Lattès three-channel and Lefschetz collapse

Freeze every complex elliptic curve (E), every integer multiplication (m\ge2), and the quotient (E/\{\pm1\}\simeq\mathbb P^1). For every iterate, split fixed points into the (m^n-1), (m^n+1), and two-torsion branch channels; classify their multipliers; derive the total count, Artin--Mazur zeta, primitive cycles, and exact holomorphic Lefschetz cancellation. The all-moduli branch/multiplier theorem is essential: a bare count (m^{2n}+1) would be too close to an expanding-map corollary.

### C181 -- rotor-router on all strongly connected digraphs

Freeze every finite strongly connected directed multigraph and every cyclic local rotor order. Directed tree numbers (t_v), their gcd (M), and the weighted sum (L=M^{-1}\sum_v d_v^+t_v) must determine every recurrent unicycle orbit, visit frequency, fixed iterate, zeta, finite Koopman determinant, and the existence of a cycle-origin-dependent reversor; no source-canonical reversal is claimed. Eulerian graphs appear only as the (L=|E|) specialization. The theorem centers deterministic traversal and non-Eulerian stationary frequencies, not sandpile translation.

### C182 -- periodic box--ball action--angle theorem

Freeze all binary periodic box--ball states with at most half filling and the whole commuting carrier family (T_\ell). Soliton content and internal symmetry sectors must give finite angle-variable lattice quotients; every (T_\ell) becomes translation by the vector with coordinates (min(j,\ell)). Smith normal form then supplies every component period, fixed iterate, primitive cycle, zeta, and finite Koopman determinant, followed by exact aggregation over all sectors. Generic-period formulas alone were forbidden; the internal-symmetry multiplicities and boundary sectors had to close.

### C183 -- random-transposition full spectrum

Freeze the lazy ordered-pair transposition clock on every (S_n). Every partition (lambda\vdash n) gives an exact character-ratio eigenvalue with regular multiplicity (d_\lambda^2), producing the full finite determinant, trace and return laws, exact (L^2) density distance, spectral gap, and bottom sector. On frozen (S_n), (P_n) is not a deterministic map and its determinant is not an unweighted Artin--Mazur determinant. After changing to the weighted directed-edge path owner, the reciprocal determinant does have a canonical primitive-cycle product; that lift changes the object and repairs neither A0 nor A1.
