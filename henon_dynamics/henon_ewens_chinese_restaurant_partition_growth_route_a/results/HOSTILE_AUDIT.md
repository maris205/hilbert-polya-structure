# Hostile audit

## Attacks

- shifted insertion denominator or altered EPPF;
- wrong cycle multiplicity or unsigned-Stirling coefficient;
- changed Bernoulli probability or `K_n` distribution;
- false finite independence or incorrect factorial-moment correction;
- broken normalization and parameter boundaries;
- repaired evidence checksum after a semantic mutation;
- altered evidence date or fixed epoch under a repaired checksum;
- omitted, duplicated, or schema-extended rows in each of the seven ledgers;
- duplicate/nonfinite JSON and ambiguous YAML;
- promoted arithmetic gate, target claim, or Route-B flag.

## Outcome

All 60 declared attacks are rejected.  The proof separately addresses the non-computational risk: finite exact tables do not imply strong laws, central limit theorems, or Poisson limits, so each asymptotic statement has an analytic probability argument.
