# Narrative report

C353 takes a larger step than an isolated sampling-formula derivation.  It follows the process from one-step stochastic growth to its complete finite partition law, then splits the asymptotic analysis into two genuinely different observables.

The total number of blocks is governed by innovation times.  Their conditional probabilities do not depend on the current partition, so the indicators are independent, which immediately exposes the exact PGF and makes the strong law and CLT transparent.  Fixed small-block counts are not independent at finite `n`; their limit requires a separate mixed-factorial-moment calculation.  Keeping those mechanisms distinct prevents the common overclaim that all finite occupancy counts are independent.

The Route-A conclusion is intentionally negative.  Rising factorials and unsigned Stirling numbers are combinatorial structures, but they do not manufacture arithmetic origin, prime-indexed local factors, or target spectral data.
