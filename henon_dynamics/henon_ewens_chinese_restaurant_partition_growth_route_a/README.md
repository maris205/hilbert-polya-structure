# HCS-C353: Ewens--Chinese-restaurant partition growth

This package proves a single finite-to-asymptotic theorem for the one-parameter Chinese-restaurant growth process.  It derives the exchangeable Ewens partition law, the exact occupancy-vector distribution, an independent-Bernoulli representation of the total block count, its strong law and central limit theorem, and the joint independent-Poisson limit of every fixed collection of block sizes.

Primary artifacts:

- `paper/main.pdf`: final manuscript.
- `THEOREM_PACKAGE.md`: claims and proof dependencies.
- `results/c353_ewens_crp_evidence.json`: 5,238-row exact certificate.
- `C353_RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`: 27-payload release ledger.
- `evaluations/route_a/HCS-C353/2026-09-03.yaml`: Route-A evaluation.
