# Research question

Can the local Chinese-restaurant insertion rule be promoted, in one proof, to a complete exact finite partition law and to sharp asymptotic laws for both the total number of blocks and every fixed vector of small-block counts?

The package answers yes.  A labelled-partition induction proves exchangeability and the Ewens EPPF.  Counting labelled partitions gives the exact occupancy law.  Deterministic conditional innovation probabilities imply an independent-Bernoulli representation of the total block count, yielding a strong law and Lindeberg central limit theorem.  A uniform mixed-factorial-moment identity yields the independent-Poisson small-block limit.

This combinatorial clock is not a prime clock.  The finite block-count generating polynomial is not an arithmetic Euler product, and no natural target-zero quantization is present.  All five Route-A gates fail and Route B remains locked.
