# Paper plan

## Thesis

The same insertion rule has two complementary exact factorizations: one over labelled blocks, producing the Ewens law, and one over innovation times, producing independent Bernoulli variables.  Mixed factorial moments then bridge the exact finite law to independent Poisson limits.

## Structure

1. Freeze the Markov growth convention and notation.
2. State the finite and asymptotic atlas.
3. Derive the EPPF and occupancy-vector law.
4. Factor the total-block PGF and prove strong law plus CLT.
5. Derive the full mixed-factorial-moment formula and Poisson limit.
6. Separate `theta=1`, `theta down to 0`, and `theta to infinity` boundaries.
7. Document evidence, sources, and the Route-A obstruction.

## Revision ladder

- Round 0: exchangeability and exact finite laws.
- Round 1: innovation factorization, strong law, and CLT.
- Round 2: joint Poisson limit, boundary atlas, evidence, and scope.
