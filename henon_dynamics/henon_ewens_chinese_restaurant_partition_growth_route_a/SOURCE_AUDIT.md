# Source audit

## Verified sources

- Warren J. Ewens, “The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles,” *Theoretical Population Biology* 3 (1972), DOI `10.1016/0040-5809(72)90035-4`.  Primary sampling-formula lineage.
- Fred M. Hoppe, “Pólya-like urns and the Ewens' sampling formula,” *Journal of Mathematical Biology* 20 (1984), DOI `10.1007/BF00275863`.  Urn-growth and Ewens-formula lineage.

## Ownership boundary

No priority claim is made.  The repository contribution is a self-contained, notation-frozen reconstruction that joins exact finite laws, two asymptotic regimes, strict evidence boundaries, and a Route-A evaluation.

## Exclusions

The source audit does not use the Poisson--Dirichlet ranked-frequency theorem, because the paper does not claim it.  It also does not reinterpret the block-count PGF as an Euler product or customer time as prime time.
