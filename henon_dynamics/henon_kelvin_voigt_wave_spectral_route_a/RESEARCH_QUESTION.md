# Research question

How does Kelvin--Voigt strain-rate damping change the complete spectral and
semigroup atlas of a Dirichlet wave?  On the normalized interval `(0, pi)` the
answer is exact: each sine mode has two quadratic-pencil roots, only finitely
many modes can be underdamped, critical values create Jordan blocks, and the
overdamped slow roots accumulate at `-1/b` without an eigenvector.  These facts
combine into a closed formula for the spectral-abscissa gap and a unique best
damping, while the energy identity remains at the physical clock.

The result is source-native continuous-time analysis, not an arithmetic target
construction.
