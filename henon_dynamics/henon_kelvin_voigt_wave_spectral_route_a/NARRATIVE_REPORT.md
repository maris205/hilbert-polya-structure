# Narrative report

The central advance is the mixed hyperbolic--parabolic spectral geometry.  A
single damping coefficient moves low modes through underdamped and Jordan
faces, but sends high modes to a slow essential accumulation point and a fast
branch at minus infinity.  Comparing these two mechanisms gives an exact,
globally optimized spectral-abscissa gap.  The proof keeps the physical clock
and the energy identity visible, while explicitly refusing to turn a spectral
gap into an unsupported uniform semigroup bound.

The finite ledger controls the theorem numerically; it is not an arithmetic
or target-operator construction.
