# HCS-C311 research question

For the standard planar Brusselator with `A>0`, `B>=0`, can one prove a
single all-parameter theorem containing positive-quadrant global existence,
the entire node/focus/Hopf linear atlas, and a convention-explicit first
Lyapunov coefficient with leading cycle amplitude and frequency?  Does the
result supply more than a weak source-local orbit signal for Route A?
