# Research question

For the finite asymmetric nearest-neighbor Hatano--Nelson chain, can one give a single exact theorem that covers positive OBC hopping, canonical left/right modes, skin amplitude versus biorthogonal density, propagators and resolvents, the PBC Fourier spectrum, and every zero-hopping boundary face without importing topological or arithmetic claims?

The answer in C308 is yes.  OBC is treated for every `N>=2`; the standard oriented-ring PBC statement is for `N>=3`, with the coincident-neighbor `N=2` convention isolated rather than hidden.
