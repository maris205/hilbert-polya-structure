# HCS-C47 narrative report

C46 forced a change of determinant category.  C47 proves that the change is
mathematically substantive rather than rhetorical.  The complete prime block
is not \(\tau\)-trace class near the Riemann critical abscissa, but it lies in
the fourth \(L^q(\mathcal M,\tau)\) ideal exactly on the C45 half-plane.
Three low chronological moments supply the canonical regularization
counterterms, and the remaining trace logarithm is a genuine fourth-order
graded determinant.  This is a normalized semifinite-trace construction, not
a classical Fredholm determinant.

The analytic phase comes from the supertrace.  A positive
Fuglede--Kadison determinant would retain only modulus and cannot replace it.
The next paper attacks the second counterterm geometrically; a genus-four
curve may supply enough prime decay to enlarge the Euler half-plane.  For the
same positive-ideal block, however, enlargement to \(\Re s>1/3\) raises the
minimal regularization order to six; lowering the order would require a new
compressed operator.
