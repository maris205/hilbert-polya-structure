\section{Sharp Schatten thresholds}

Form the product algebra
\[
 \mathcal M=\prod_{p\equiv1\ (3)}\mathcal M_p,
\]
represented on
$\mathcal H=\bigoplus_p(\mathcal H_p^+\oplus\mathcal H_p^-)$.  On positive
elements $A=(A_p)_p$, set $\tau(A)=\sum_p\tau_p(A_p)$; this is a faithful
normal semifinite trace.  Form the block Dirichlet family
\[
 X_s=\bigoplus_{p\equiv1\ (3)}p^{-s}W_p,
 \qquad \Gamma=\bigoplus_p\Gamma_p.
\]
Because $W_p$ is unitary, $|p^{-s}W_p|=p^{-\RePart s}I$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lq}
 \tau(|X_s|^q)
 =\sum_{p\equiv1\ (3)}\frac{8p+4}{3}p^{-q\RePart s}.
\end{equation}
For every $q>0$, the prime series in \eqref{eq:lq} converges exactly when
$q\RePart s>2$; at equality it contains the divergent prime harmonic series.
More explicitly, convergence for $q\RePart s>2$ follows by comparison with
the integer series of exponent $q\RePart s-1>1$.  In the other direction the
summand dominates a constant multiple of $1/p$, and the Euler--Dirichlet
theorem gives $\sum_{p\equiv1\ (3)}p^{-1}=\infty$.

Thus
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\toprule
\text{ideal}&\text{exact domain}\\
\midrule
L^1&\RePart s>2\\
L^2&\RePart s>1\\
L^3&\RePart s>2/3\\
L^4&\RePart s>1/2\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]
As a bounded operator on $\mathcal H$, $X_s$ is compact exactly when
$\RePart s>0$; it is bounded and noncompact at $\RePart s=0$, and unbounded
for $\RePart s<0$.  Since $|\Gamma X_s|=|X_s|$, the grading cannot improve
any positive ideal threshold.  This excludes an unregularized
$\tau$-determinant on most of the C45 half-plane.

The canonical Hilbert-space trace gives a different threshold.  Since
\[
 \dim(\mathcal H_p^+\oplus\mathcal H_p^-)
 =d\,\tau_p(I)=\frac{(p-1)(4p+2)}3,
\]
one has
\[
 \Tr_{\mathcal H}(|X_s|^q)
 =\sum_p\frac{(p-1)(4p+2)}3p^{-q\RePart s},
 \qquad X_s\in S^q(\mathcal H)\Longleftrightarrow q\RePart s>3.
\]
Thus standard Hilbert trace class begins only at $\RePart s>3$, and its
graded moments are $C_{p,n}=d_pc_{p,n}$: they encode the ordinary Galois norm,
not the field-degree-normalized root.
