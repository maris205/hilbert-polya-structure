\section{Trace expansion}\label{sec:traceproof}

For one real Galois class $[a]$, the signed trace of the $n$th power is
\begin{align*}
&2\Tr T_{a,0}^n+2\Tr T_{-a,0}^n\\
&\quad-\Tr T_{a,1}^n-\Tr T_{a,2}^n
      -\Tr T_{-a,1}^n-\Tr T_{-a,2}^n.
\end{align*}
This is exactly the sum of the augmentation moment for $\psi_a$ and its
inverse character.  Summing over all $[a]$ takes the field trace from
$\mathbf Q(\zeta_p)^+$ to $\mathbf Q$.  Dividing by
$d=(p-1)/2$ gives $c_{p,n}$, proving \cref{prop:moment}.
