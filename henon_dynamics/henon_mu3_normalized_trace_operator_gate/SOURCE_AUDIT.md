# HCS-C47 source audit

Schatten ideals, regularized determinants, normalized traces, and
Fuglede--Kadison determinants are established operator theory.  C47 claims no
new general determinant theory.  Its project-specific result is the exact
realization of the full Galois-graded Hénon block, its sharp threshold
\(q\Re s>2\), and the equality of the C45 germ with a fourth-order graded
determinant plus three forced local chronological counterterm series.

C27--C28 studied unnormalized prime direct sums whose matrix dimension grows
like \(p^2\).  C47 has a different full-kernel block and a field-degree
normalized trace; nevertheless it explicitly reuses their lesson that signed
trace cancellation cannot prove positive Schatten membership.  C40 uses an
external damping clock; C47 keeps the source-native prime clock.

The classical Hilbert-space trace of the same block is not a competing
realization of the normalized root: its block dimension is larger by \(d_p\),
its Schatten threshold is \(q\Re s>3\), and its graded moments recover the
ordinary Galois norm.

Novelty verdict: `MEDIUM_HIGH_SEARCH_BOUNDED` for the Hénon identification,
not for the underlying operator-theory machinery, as of 2026-08-13.
