We determine the stability product formed from all finite real periodic points
of the generalized Boole map $T(x)=ax-b/x$, for every $a,b>0$ in the original
iteration clock. The physical periodic set excludes infinity, so its product
differs from the classical projective-circle determinant even when the omitted
orbit has measure zero. A rational fixed-point index identity gives the exact
weighted sums in the subcritical, parabolic and supercritical regimes. We use
them to classify every zero and pole of the subcritical product and prove that
it is entire precisely at the parameters
$a=(1-2a)^{2m}$, $m\ge1$, in $(0,1/2)$. At $a=1$ the normalized germ is
$(1-u)^{1/2}\exp(\Li(u)/2)$, where $\Li$ is the dilogarithm, and has no
meromorphic continuation through $u=1$.
The subcritical germs converge to it locally uniformly on $|u|<1$; the
supercritical germs have the same limit after deleting the complete
stability factors of the two newborn real fixed orbits, whereas the unreduced
family does not. Uniform bounds on all
iterate weights justify both limits. The classical phase geometry is included
to fix the periodic set and its multipliers. The resulting products do not
identify a natural transfer operator on the punctured real domain or a target
arithmetic spectrum.
