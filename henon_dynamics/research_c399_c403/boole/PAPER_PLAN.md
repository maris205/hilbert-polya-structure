## One-sentence contribution

Classify the complete finite-real stability product of `T(x)=a*x-b/x`, including
every subcritical divisor cancellation and the compensated two-sided limit at
the parabolic transition, while distinguishing it from the classical circle
transfer determinant.

## Frozen object and scope

- Parameters `a,b>0`; physical domain is the real line with all prepoles deleted.
- Original integer iteration; infinity is not a physical periodic orbit.
- Positive real stability denominator `(T^n)'(x)-1`; germ normalized by `D(0)=1`.
- No target primes, zero data, natural physical transfer domain, or Route-B entry.
- Existing `PROOF_PACKAGE.md` is the complete author argument, not a source of
  publication-priority claims. Its original reviewed bytes remain intact.
