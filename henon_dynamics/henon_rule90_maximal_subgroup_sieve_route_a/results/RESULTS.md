- Exact maximal-subgroup inclusion--exclusion holds for every Mersenne
  circumference.
- Finite replay covers `2<=r<=10`, 27 nonempty subset cells, and 38 divisor
  cells, with exact sieve/Möbius equality in every row.
- Mersenne-prime sentinels `L=7,31,127` have one fixed state and every other
  periodic-image state at full period.
- The all-parameter prime-subfamily theorem gives short-period probability
  exactly `2^(-(L-1))` and the complete finite zeta.
