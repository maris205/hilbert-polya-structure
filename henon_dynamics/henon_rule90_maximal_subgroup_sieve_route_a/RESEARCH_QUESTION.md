# C160 research question

Can C155's Mersenne Rule-90 concentration theorem be sharpened from a union
over all proper clock times to an exact subgroup-resolved law, and does a
natural source subfamily admit a complete closed cycle distribution?

The hard gate requires a theorem valid beyond the finite replay range.  A
smaller numerical error bound alone is insufficient.  The answer is yes:
maximal-subgroup inclusion--exclusion is exact for every Mersenne length, and
every Mersenne-prime length greater than three has precisely one fixed state
and all remaining states of full period.

This is finite-source orbit geometry.  It does not authorize target-facing
arithmetic or Route B.
