1. For `2<=r<=10`, factor only the source length `L=2^r-1`.
2. Enumerate all nonempty subsets of its distinct factors.
3. Compute every intersection fixed dimension by independent `F_2`
   polynomial gcd arithmetic.
4. Sum inclusion--exclusion and compare it with the full Möbius formula.
5. Record the Mersenne-prime sentinels `L=7,31,127` and the `L=3` exception.
