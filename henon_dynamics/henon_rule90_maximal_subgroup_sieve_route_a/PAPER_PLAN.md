# C160 paper plan

1. Restate the periodic-image lemma only to establish notation.
2. Prove that every proper orbit lies in a maximal subgroup fixed space.
3. Compute all intersections and derive exact inclusion--exclusion and
   Bonferroni bounds.
4. Specialize to Mersenne-prime source lengths, prove the order-three root
   exclusion, and derive all state/cycle counts and the finite zeta.
5. Display the `L=3` exception, no-infinitude boundary, source-only meaning of
   circumference factors, validation receipts, and declarations.

The compact two-page theorem certificate needs no decorative figure; its
comparison is expressed by exact formulas and one finite sentinel table.
