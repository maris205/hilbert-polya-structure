# Research question

What is the exact convergence basin of the Newton–Schulz/Hotelling matrix inverse iteration, and how does it change for singular rectangular matrices when the target is the Moore–Penrose inverse?

The package replaces the familiar sufficient condition `||I-AX_0||<1` by an iff spectral-radius condition for invertible matrices, identifies the sharp Jordan-block rate, and proves that rectangular convergence requires both a compressed residual in the open unit spectral disk and the exact support compatibility `X_0=QX_0P`. It then closes the canonical scalar corridor, its equality face, and the zero-rank exception.
