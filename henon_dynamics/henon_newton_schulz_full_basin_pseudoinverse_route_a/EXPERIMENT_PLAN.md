# Experiment plan

## Analytic targets

1. Derive exact dyadic residual powers and the square spectral-radius iff basin.
2. Expand arbitrary Jordan blocks and recover the sharp polynomial-times-double-exponential rate.
3. In SVD coordinates, prove necessity and sufficiency of Moore–Penrose support compatibility.
4. Resolve the canonical `alpha A*` corridor and every boundary face.

## Exact regression matrix

- 14 square cases spanning nilpotent, interior, unit-circle semisimple, unit-circle defective, and exterior spectra.
- Six compatible rectangular cases, including tall, wide, singular square, nilpotent compressed residual, and rank zero.
- Six off-support cases activating `C`, `D`, `E`, and combined SVD-coordinate blocks.
- 19 canonical-alpha cases spanning zero, two safe points, sharp equality, outside, negative, repeated maxima, and rank zero.
- Six dyadic snapshots for positive cases and five for incompatibility witnesses.

## Gates

An independent checker reconstructs every frozen matrix without importing the producer. A separate symbolic lane proves generic residual, Jordan, SVD-block, and alpha identities. Byte replay, repaired-hash mutations, strict JSON/YAML parsing, semantic YAML locking, optimized-Python rejection, deterministic warning-free PDF builds, font checks, and rasterization are mandatory.
