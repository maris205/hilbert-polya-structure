- A. Ben-Israel, “An iterative method for computing the generalized inverse of an arbitrary matrix,” *Mathematics of Computation* 19 (1965), DOI `10.1090/S0025-5718-1965-0179915-5`; cross-checked against the Rutgers publication record. Generalized-inverse iteration lineage.
