# Source Audit

## Verified source lineage

1. P. J. Hurtado and A. S. Kirosingh, “Generalizations of the ‘Linear Chain
   Trick’: incorporating more flexible dwell time distributions into mean
   field ODE models,” *Journal of Mathematical Biology* 79 (2019),
   1831–1883. Official open-access publisher page:
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00285-019-01412-w
   (DOI 10.1007/s00285-019-01412-w). The publisher abstract and equations
   support the Erlang/ODE linear-chain provenance.
2. F. G. Boese, “The stability chart for the linearized Cushing equation
   with a discrete delay and with gamma-distributed delays,” *Journal of
   Mathematical Analysis and Applications* 140 (1989), 510–536. Official
   publisher record:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022247X89900814
   (DOI 10.1016/0022-247X(89)90081-4). This supplies primary gamma-delay
   stability context, not our exact specialized formulas by citation.
3. N. MacDonald, *Time Lags in Biological Models*, Lecture Notes in
   Biomathematics 27, Springer, 1978. Official publisher record:
   https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-93107-9
   (DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-93107-9). The record explicitly describes
   distributed lags and reduction to extended ODE systems.

## Claim boundary

The sources establish terminology, history, and model provenance.  The
specific threshold, transversality constant, full root count, discriminant
factorization, Jordan classification, and propagator atlas are independently
derived and machine-checked in this package.  No claim of literature
priority is made for the linear-chain trick, gamma-delay stability, cubic
Routh--Hurwitz criterion, or their assembly here.

The source audit used direct DOI/publisher records.  No manuscript content
was sent to an external model.  No target arithmetic or zero data were used.
