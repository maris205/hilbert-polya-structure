# Research Question

For scalar negative feedback filtered by a normalized Erlang shape-two
memory, can one give a single exact theorem that (i) proves the delay-to-ODE
equivalence, (ii) identifies the necessary-and-sufficient stability and
imaginary-crossing threshold, (iii) counts unstable roots, and (iv) closes
every repeated-root and Jordan boundary?

The frozen domain is \(a,b\geq0\), \(r>0\), with compatible fading-memory
histories.  Nonlinear feedback, discrete delay, other gamma shapes, target
arithmetic, and Route B are outside the question.
