# Hostile Audit

The hostile lane repairs the self-excluding evidence hash after semantic
mutations, so rejection cannot be attributed merely to a stale checksum.
It mutates theorem constants, kernel normalization, chain ordering, Routh
classification, crossing speed, unstable-root count, Jordan sizes,
compatible-history wording, collision boundaries, source identifiers,
Route-A tuple, Route-B lock, and forbidden flags.

Parser attacks include duplicate and nonfinite JSON, an array root, missing
and extra keys, a stale-payload-hash control, duplicate/non-string/merged
YAML keys, anchors, aliases, YAML root arrays, scalar-type changes, and
whitespace drift.  Every evaluator leaf is also rewritten with repaired
raw and semantic carrier hashes.  Dedicated attacks delete or rewrite
evaluator authority and evidence_status.
