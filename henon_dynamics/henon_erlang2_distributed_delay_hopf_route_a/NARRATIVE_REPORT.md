# Narrative Report

The central result is a complete analytic atlas, not a parameter sweep.
Erlang-2 memory closes exactly to a three-state linear system.  Its remaining
Routh determinant reduces to one affine expression in feedback strength,
giving a sharp stability threshold and a transverse imaginary crossing.

The substantive extra step is the full degeneracy classification.  The
cubic discriminant factors into the zero-feedback face and one admissible
positive-feedback surface.  A cyclic-vector calculation then determines all
Jordan sizes, allowing exact semigroup formulas even on defective faces.
The manuscript explicitly refuses the tempting but unsupported upgrade from
a linear spectral crossing to existence of nonlinear periodic orbits.
