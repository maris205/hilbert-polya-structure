payloads plus one self-excluded manifest.  The payload comprises eight
theorem/writing documents, seven executable code/manifest files, one evaluator
YAML, seven paper artifacts including three content-distinct revision PDFs and
the final PDF, and four result/evidence artifacts.

Every package must pass a deterministic producer, a producer-independent
checker with recursive exact-schema closure, a separate symbolic

content revisions.  Every final PDF must reproduce byte for byte in two fresh
fixed-epoch LuaLaTeX builds, embed and subset every font, have clean logs and
extractable text, and survive visual inspection of every page.  Internal
artifact cross-checking is not external review or an independent error
