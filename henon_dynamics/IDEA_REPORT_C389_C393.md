## Retained and ranked by the user's priorities

1. **Carlitz full ring-action torsion tower (C389).** Strong intrinsic arithmetic
   priority. The all-conductor annihilator law, Eisenstein prime-power towers and
   compatible ramified Galois groups exceed a single additive-map periodic count.
   Classical Carlitz theory is an explicit owner, not a new discovery claim.
2. **Generic quadratic arboreal tower (C393).** Strong intrinsic arithmetic
   priority with nonabelian full binary wreath growth. New critical branch values
   give the full kernel at every height. C374's power-map affine tower is not this
   mechanism. Generic Q(t) and number-field specialization must not be conflated.
3. **Lüroth meromorphic Hardy family (C392).** A genuine infinite-operator step
   beyond C241. Complete spectrum alone is not the full contract: continuation
   has operator poles at every s=(1−l)/2 for integers l≥0, including square-zero residues invisible
   to the scalar determinant at nonpositive integers. Ruelle/Mayer/Bandtlow–
   Jenkinson are credited for the general analytic-operator framework.
4. **Lyness real/rational contrast (C390).** Goes beyond C173's globally
   five-periodic a=1 slice to positive oval dynamics and elliptic rational-torsion
   restrictions. Bastien–Rogalski, Beukers–Cushman and Gasull–Mañosa–Xarles are
   strong prior owners. Rational-period classification is cited with its hypotheses.
5. **Supercritical inverse-square limit cycle (C391).** Imaginary Bessel order,
   nonunique self-adjoint boundary, fall to the centre and a two-sided geometric
   negative ladder differ from C383's fixed Friedrichs real-order AB source.
   Dereziński–Richard own the complete classical spectral model.

Ranking is a qualitative project-priority judgment, not literature novelty certification.

## Feasibility and limits

The ring and tree contracts give actual arithmetic sources, but do not identify
rational-prime orbit clocks for the target. The analytic and scattering contracts
include explicit obstructions instead of silently changing determinants.
Search results can establish nearby owners, not certify no prior theorem exists.
Final actual test outcomes and any narrowed or corrected statements will be
recorded in the five source audits and the batch review.

## Actual outcome

All five retained complete-paper contracts were achieved; no sixth candidate
or silent subtype replacement was used. The stronger Carlitz all-ring/all-tower
contract and the Lüroth operator-residue contract were retained in full, so
neither collapses back to the eliminated scalar/finite-prefix mechanism.
The generic quadratic package also closes the ordered prime-limit periodic
fraction theorem; the real/rational and self-adjoint-domain boundaries remain
explicit in the other two papers.

Two packages (C389/C393) retain structural A0 and weak A1 as exploratory
arithmetic sources. None passes target A2. The exact theorem ownership,
external dependencies, corrected implementation failures and release evidence
are recorded in [the completed batch review](BATCH_REVIEW_C389_C393.md).
Five final papers total 22 pages; complete theorem progress is not a
literature-novelty certificate, and publication status is not asserted.
