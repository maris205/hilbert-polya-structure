# C64 research question

Let (G=W(E_6)) and let (X_i=G/S_i) be the 16 ambient-conjugacy subgroup
types frozen by C62.  C63 computed the rational permutation-character map
(chi:mathbb Q{X_i}	ooperatorname{Class}_{mathbb Q}(G)) and found the
primitive relation (R_4) in its kernel.  Does the integral Burnside mark map
separate this relation on the same finite support?

The target is a single exact statement: reconstruct
[
mathsf M_{ij}=|(G/S_j)^{S_i}|
=\frac{\#\{g\in G:g^{-1}S_i g\subseteq S_j\}}{|S_j|},
]
prove (operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}\mathsf M=16), certify
(detmathsf M=2^{23}3^3), and compute (mathsf M R_4) with a nonzero
mark witness.

No full (B(G)) claim is licensed: only the 16-type submodule generated by
the C62 dictionary is in scope.
