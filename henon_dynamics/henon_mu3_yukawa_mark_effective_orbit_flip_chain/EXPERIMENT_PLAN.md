# C86 experiment plan

1. Byte-bind every directly read C75, C76, C78, C81, and C82 evidence and
   manifest artifact.
2. Generate the faithful label group from the five C76 generators and
   partition all 65536 support masks into orbits.
3. Toggle each of the sixteen labels from every orbit representative, verify
   the same target multiplicities at every point of that orbit, and publish
   the sparse quotient.
4. Check row sums, weighted detailed balance, orbit and arc spectra, repair
   flows, and the C82 distance-one full-core correlation.
5. Prove the quotient spectrum through invariant Walsh orbit sums and verify
   its degree multiplicities independently.
6. Run an independent checker, a SymPy radial-spectrum cross-check, clean
   replay, hostile mutations, two isolated LaTeX builds, and the manifest
   audit.
