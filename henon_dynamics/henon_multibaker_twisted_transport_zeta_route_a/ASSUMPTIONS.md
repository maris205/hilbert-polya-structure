# Assumptions

The phase space is an L-cell ring times two coordinates in (0,1) with dyadic
rationals removed; L is any positive integer. The branch symbol is floor(2x),
the displacement is 2s-1, and time/cell length equal one. Non-dyadic tails
make coding unique and preserve the anti-symplectic reversal exactly.

Twist phi is real for the absolute-convergence statement. The geometric
phase is exp(i phi W), where W is total closed displacement divided by L.
The primitive weight is inverse unstable multiplier 2^(-q). It is not the
inverse of the two-dimensional flat-trace denominator.

The diffusion law uses invariant internal Lebesgue data on the integer-cell
lift. Ring relaxation concerns the finite source transport matrix, not every
phase-space observable. On an even ring, the two-step relaxation norm acts on
the mean-zero subspace of each parity class.
