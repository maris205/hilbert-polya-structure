# Limits and corrected statements

The domain matters. A standard half-open square does not support every
homogeneous periodic code and the same exact reversal convention. We exclude
both homogeneous corner codes on an invariant full-measure domain and retain
their explicit correction in the transport product.

The all-period source proof is complete under that convention. Dyadic
boundary orbits under other extensions are outside it. The infinite-dimensional
Perron operator is not assigned the finite transport spectrum, and its
trace-class property is not asserted. No quantum multibaker is constructed.

For even L the transport has eigenvalue -1. Uniform mixing at every integer
time is false. L=1 has no nontrivial cell relaxation mode; L=2 has no residual
parity-class mode. Diffusion concerns the unbounded lift, not the bounded
cell index modulo L.

Neither source continuation nor source exact arithmetic implies an RH-like
target. This is a complete proof-and-audit paper package, not an assertion of
literature novelty, journal acceptance, or a solved Route-A arithmetic gate.
