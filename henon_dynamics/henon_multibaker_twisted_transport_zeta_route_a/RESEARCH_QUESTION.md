# Research question

Can one make the entire reversible finite-ring multibaker dictionary exact
in a single convention: primitive symbolic and geometric cycles, winding and
powers, the transport determinant, its periodic product, and diffusion?

Answer: yes, with a compulsory correction for the two homogeneous symbolic
cycles. The geometric and symbolic ledgers disagree precisely at those
cycles, and the transport weight must be kept distinct from flat-trace
weights. This exact closure still supplies no intrinsic prime mechanism.
