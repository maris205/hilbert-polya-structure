# Paper plan

Round 0: boundary convention, coding/reversal proof, exact rational fixed
points, necklace lift, winding, powers and complete census.

Round 1: add the full twisted transport spectrum and Chebyshev determinant;
prove the geometric correction and explain the flat-trace weight boundary.

Round 2: add exact diffusion, odd/even relaxation, one/two-cell degeneracies,
lazy control, independent executable evidence and the strict Route-A audit.

The conditional LaTeX source changes substantive theorems and its abstract,
title and conclusion by round. Every round includes bilingual abstracts and
six keywords in each language. The final paper has no conference page limit.
