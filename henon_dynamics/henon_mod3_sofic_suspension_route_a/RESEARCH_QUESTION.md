# C140 research question

Can Route A move from full shifts to a genuinely strictly sofic suspension and
still retain an exact intrinsic primitive-orbit zeta, provided the finite-to-
one presentation's exceptional orbit is corrected rather than silently
counted with cover multiplicity?

For the mod-three gap shift the answer is yes.  The minimal three-state cover
is one-to-one on every label sequence containing `1`; only the all-zero label
fixed point has three lifts, forming one cover orbit of least period three.
This yields an exact fixed-point correction for every period and the rational
intrinsic zeta.

The question is source-side.  It does not ask for a target divisor, global
target identity, arithmetic interpretation, natural Fredholm owner on label
space, quantum lift, or Route B.
