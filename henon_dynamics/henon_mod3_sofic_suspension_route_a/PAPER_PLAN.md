# C140 paper plan

## Working title

**A Strictly Sofic Mod-Three Gap Suspension and Its Exact Fischer-Cover Correction**

## Claims/evidence narrative

1. Define the label shift and prove strict soficity before using the cover.
2. Prove follower-set minimality and the exact cover determinant.
3. Isolate lift uniqueness and the exceptional all-zero point.
4. Derive the all-period correction, then the intrinsic rational zeta and
   primitive product.
5. Explain the nonlattice suspension specialization.
6. Report finite sentinel totals only after the infinite proof.
7. Close with the Fredholm-owner and Route-A boundaries.

## Layout target

Two to four pages, one compact fixed-count table, no external citations or
figures.  Round 1 audits the exceptional-orbit proof and cover/label notation.
Round 2 audits strict-soficity quantifiers, source/target boundaries,
reproducibility language, and rendering.  No review score or acceptance
prediction is used.
