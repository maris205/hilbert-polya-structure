# C103 research question

For every ordered pair of the twenty frozen C88 first-passage targets, what
are the exact laws of
\(U_{ij}=\min(T_i,T_j)\) and \(V_{ij}=\max(T_i,T_j)\), and which tail and
expectation identities can be certified directly from the C90 joint survival
receipt?  The question is finite combinatorics only and uses the scope
firewall `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
