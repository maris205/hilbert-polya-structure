# Batch review: HCS-C44 through HCS-C48

Date: 2026-08-13

## Batch question

Can the conjugate-paired full Fourier--cubic Hénon kernel be promoted from a
placewise analytic germ to a coefficient-stable arithmetic object and then
to a genuine global operator determinant with a larger normal-convergence
domain?

## Paper outcomes

1. **HCS-C44 — fixed coefficient field.**  Every paired first moment
   generates \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)^+\); the degrees \((p-1)/2\) are unbounded,
   so a fixed-coefficient compatible system is impossible.
2. **HCS-C45 — normalized Galois norm.**  The ordinary norm has unbounded
   virtual rank, but its canonical field-degree-normalized logarithmic root
   gives a holomorphic nonzero Euler germ on \(\Re s>1/2\).
3. **HCS-C46 — local branch obstruction.**  The exact \(p=7\) norm is not a
   cube; its normalized root has finite local orders \(\pm2/3\), ruling out an
   ordinary single-valued rational/Fredholm determinant promotion.
4. **HCS-C47 — normalized-trace operator.**  In the correct graded
   semifinite block algebra, the C45 germ is exactly a fourth-order
   \(\tau\)-regularized determinant with three source-native chronological
   counterterms on \(\Re s>1/2\).
5. **HCS-C48 — genus-four second moment.**  The second chronological moment
   is \(-14-2a_p\) for an explicit smooth genus-four curve; the Weil bound
   extends the Euler germ and a sixth-order \(\tau\)-regularized determinant to
   \(\Re s>1/3\).

## Strongest advance

The strongest result is not merely another obstruction.  It is the exact
chain

\[
\text{Hénon chronology}
\longrightarrow
\text{Galois-normalized supertraces}
\longrightarrow
\text{regularized graded determinant}
\longrightarrow
\text{genus-four Frobenius cancellation},
\]

ending with a canonical holomorphic nonzero normalized-semifinite
operator-determinant germ on \(\Re s>1/3\).  This is the first result in
this branch where arithmetic
geometry improves the analytic abscissa of the same source-locked Hénon
operator rather than merely diagnosing a failed normalization.

## Main obstruction

The construction is still a family over arithmetic fibres, not a primitive
orbit zeta of one real Hénon system.  It has no global functional equation,
Gamma factor, Riemann--von Mangoldt law, exact Riemann divisor, or
self-adjoint Hilbert--Pólya generator.  Analytically, the first remaining
wall is the third chronological moment.

## Next batch

The most valuable successor is HCS-C49: projectively stratify the six-step
phase, isolate the non-Tate middle cohomology of the resulting \((2,3)\)
threefold, and decide whether \(c_{p,3}\) gains a further \(p^{-1/2}\).  A
positive result would move the germ toward \(\Re s>1/4\) and require an
eighth-order regularized determinant; a negative result would make
\(\Re s=1/3\) a structurally explained wall.  Only after that gate should the
program attempt a compatible global functional equation or self-dual
spectral generator.
