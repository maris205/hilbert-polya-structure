## Uniform paper contract

Each candidate releases exactly 28 physical files: 27 manifest-listed
payloads and one self-excluded release manifest.  The payload set contains
the research question, theorem/source/narrative/audit documents, executable
producer/checker/symbolic/replay/mutation/manifest code, evaluator YAML, three
content-distinct revision PDFs plus the final PDF, and result/evidence reports.

Every package must pass a deterministic producer, a producer-independent
recursive schema checker, an independent symbolic or algebraic reconstruction,
clean-process byte replay, repaired-hash/semantic/unknown-key/stale-hash
mutations, and manifest hash closure.  Each paper receives two substantive
revisions.  The final PDF must reproduce byte-for-byte in two fresh fixed-epoch
LuaLaTeX builds, embed and subset every reported font, have clean settled logs
and extractable text, and pass a page-level visual audit.  Internal checks are
artifact validation, not external peer review.

## Proof and evidence boundaries

- C229's finite transition rows do not replace the all-parameter affine and
  Feller proof; boundary atoms and zero-rate faces stay explicit.
- C230's finite numerical rows do not assert a periodic-orbit zeta; scattering
  and action--angle coordinates remain source-local.
- C231's Pöschl--Teller calculation is a spatial Hessian theorem; it is not a
  nonlinear stability, Hopf, or target spectral assertion.
- C232's finite quadrature ledger supports the endpoint treatment but does not
  discretize the continuum of energy ovals into primitive cycles.
- C233's finite PMF window is an audit oracle, not an all-state truncation
  theorem; the Fredholm product is explicitly source-local.

## Integrity and stopping gates

The ARS implementation-integrity and failure-mode audit covers source
ownership, citation context, implementation bugs, hallucinated results,
shortcut reliance, bug-as-insight risk, methodology fabrication, and frame
lock.  No package may introduce target-zero or prime tables, arithmetic local
data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target divisor/counting law or
functional equation, a Hilbert--Pólya operator, or Route-B input.  Strict
tuples are source-local decisions and do not become positive arithmetic claims
because a source has a spectrum, action, or generating function.
