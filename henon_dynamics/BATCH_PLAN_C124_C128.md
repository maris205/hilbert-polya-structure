## Frozen sequence and explicit progress

1. **C124 -- graph-directed Hardy--Fredholm bridge.**  A three-state affine
   Hénon IFS on three Hardy bidiscs has strongly separated branch images,
   infinitely many primitive admissible/geometric cycles, a trace-class
   operator, the all-order law
   \[
   \operatorname{Tr}\mathcal L^n=
   \frac{\operatorname{Tr}W^n}
   {(1-8^{-n})(1-16^{-n})},
   \]
   and an entire primitive-repetition Fredholm product.  This closes the
   joint structural gap left between C119's global owner and C123's finite
   recurrent word prefix.  A translation control proves that the determinant
   is exactly blind to orbit locations.
2. **C125 -- Anosov orbit zeta and natural-owner obstruction.**  The toral
   automorphism generated by
   \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)
   has a complete all-period fixed/primitive census and exact Artin--Mazur
   zeta \((1-z)^2/(1-3z+z^2)\).  Its natural Koopman action on
   \(L^2(\mathbb T^2)\) is unitary but noncompact and belongs to no finite
   Schatten class.  The paper therefore identifies, rather than conceals,
   the mismatch between rich recurrent dynamics and an ordinary
   determinant-class natural owner.
3. **C126 -- Chebyshev contracting skew product.**  For
   \(F(x,y)=(4x^3-3x,y/4+x)\), the base iterate is \(T_{3^n}\), every one of
   its \(3^n\) simple real fixed points has one period-preserving fiber lift,
   and all primitive counts, stability multipliers, orientations, and
   repetition laws are exact for every period.  This advances a real critical
   polynomial system from finite symbolic evidence to a complete geometric
   atlas.
4. **C127 -- uniform affine horseshoe rectangle.**  A two-strip affine
   horseshoe is treated simultaneously for
   \((\lambda,\mu)\in[3,4]\times[1/5,1/3]\).  Uniform separation,
   hyperbolicity, full-shift coding, periodic coordinates, a trace-class
   stability-mode operator, its Fredholm product, a zero-free disk, and
   trace-norm Lipschitz constants hold on the whole rectangle.  This replaces
   neighboring-point controls by a genuine parameter-region theorem.
5. **C128 -- exact finite metaplectic lift and aliasing obstruction.**  The
   toral linear Hénon map
   \(A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)
   has an explicit level-seven chirp--Fourier unitary.  Exact arithmetic proves
   all 49 Weyl Egorov identities, clock preservation, and antiunitary time
   reversal, reaching `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`.  The same proof gives
   \(U^8=I\), an exact finite-level aliasing obstruction, and a
   convention-scoped proof that the literal half-phase formula has no direct
   even-modulus extension, so no Hilbert--Pólya or Route-B promotion follows.
