# C69 hostile audit

The checker rejects all 23 semantic mutations, including changes to source
authority, scope, moduli, retraction formula or matrix, `RM`, `RU`, complement
basis or index, presentation or Smith form, group orders, `Hom` exponents,
row-solution counts, total complement count, and noncanonical-claim flags.

The test specifically prevents a mod-2-only check from replacing the required
mod-8 first row and prevents the congruence kernel from being confused with the
integer kernel of `R`.
