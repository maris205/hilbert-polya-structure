```text
D = <[u1],[u2],[u3]> subset C = Z^16 / M Z^16
```

certified in C68, does there exist a homomorphism `rho:C->D` whose restriction
to `D` is the identity?  If so, construct an explicit complement, compute its
exact invariant factors, and count all retractions/complements.  If not, give
an exact obstruction to splitting.

for this embedding; the required evidence must verify the congruences for an
actual retraction.
