# HCS-C21 argument blueprint

## Central thesis

Preserving the exact Hénon time order on an ordered periodic cover does not
guarantee a nontrivial weight-one time spectrum: the period-six chiral cover
is a connected genus-one (D_6)-curve on which the exact order-six generator
acts as a cohomologically trivial translation.  The first nontrivial sector
in the narrowly certified (n\le7) chiral sample occurs at period seven, and
the most conspicuous cross-period marker coincidence is only a period-one
alias.

## Claim--evidence--reasoning chains

### C1. The ordered object is intrinsic

- **Claim:** a valid ordered edge generates the full splitting field.
- **Evidence:** the Hénon recurrence generates all six coordinates and their
  alternating sum recovers \(\eta\).
- **Reasoning:** no external radical label is needed; the ordered state itself
  separates the two conjugate cubic sheets.
- **Strongest objection:** the cover may be a disconnected formal labeling.
- **Resolution:** absolute irreducibility gives geometric (S_3), and the
  twelve-state action is free and transitive after adjoining the sheet
  involution.

### C2. Point chronology collapses on (H^1)

- **Claim:** (g(E_6)=1) and the exact order-six time acts trivially on
  (H^1(E_6)\).
- **Evidence:** the certified branch ledger gives genus one; the exact fixed
  field (\mathbb Q(A,\eta w)\) also has genus one.
- **Reasoning:** the degree-six quotient has zero total ramification, so time
  is a torsion translation; translations induce the identity on weight-one
  cohomology.
- **Strongest objection:** projective fixed points may invalidate the
  translation argument.
- **Resolution:** equal genus in Riemann--Hurwitz proves that no such
  ramification exists.

### C3. The (n=7) threshold is real but narrowly scoped

- **Claim:** among source-identified and repository-certified chiral ordered
  components through period seven, the smallest period admitting at least
  one nontrivial weight-one time sector is seven.
- **Evidence:** the published class count has no chirality below six; C21
  gives dimension zero at six; HCS-C20 gives dimensions (16-4=12) at seven.
- **Reasoning:** the predicate is nontrivial chronological cohomology, not
  chirality or nonzero genus.
- **Strongest objection:** one adopted period-seven component cannot support
  a classification.
- **Resolution:** the theorem is explicitly existential and repository
  scoped.

### C4. The quadratic cross-period signal is imprimitive

- **Claim:** the period-six reversible marker and period-seven chiral marker
  share a field only because both pull back the fixed-point marker.
- **Evidence:** (D_6^{\rm mark}(s_6)=4D_1(s_6/2)\) and
  (C_7^{\rm mark}(s_7)=D_1(s_7-2)\); the fiber product splits into two
  affine graphs.
- **Reasoning:** a common quotient field already present at period one cannot
  certify a primitive chronology-preserving bridge between full covers.
- **Strongest objection:** a multivalued correspondence may still exist.
- **Resolution:** the paper excludes only dominant single-valued faithful
  clock maps; multivalued and boundary correspondences remain open.

| C4 | Strong for the marker alias; negative interpretation scoped | Qualified | Medium |

## Negative constraints

- Do not turn the targeted novelty audit into an absolute priority claim.
- Do not identify (D_6^{\rm mark}) with the chiral ordered cover (E_6).
- Do not average the chronological phase or identify it with period,
  Frobenius degree, or the radical.
- Do not promote fixed-period algebra to a cross-period determinant or
  Hilbert--Pólya operator.
