1. Freeze the substitution, one-step clock, fixed-point convention, and zeta
   convention before computation.
2. Generate the exact languages for widths 1 through 16 using the four
   adjacent dyadic block types.
3. Audit circulated approximants `w_k^infinity` for levels 2 through 12,
   recording least period, balance, local seam defects, and macroscopic stress
   defects without treating them as points of `X_TM`.
4. Emit explicit odd-popcount multiples and forbidden-window bounds for
   proposed periods 1 through 32 as replay sentinels for the all-period proof.
5. Validate by an independent recurrence/language implementation, a SymPy
   substitution-polynomial path, byte-identical replay, and at least twenty
   repaired-hash semantic mutations plus one stale-hash mutation.
6. Draft, compile, internally review, repair, and recompile the paper twice.
7. Close a self-excluded manifest over exactly 27 payload files.

The finite cutoffs validate implementation; they do not delimit the theorem.
