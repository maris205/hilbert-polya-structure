# C144 research question

Can a minimal, uniformly recurrent symbolic system still fail Route A at its
first layer because it has no periodic points at all?  For the two-sided
Thue--Morse subshift generated by `0 -> 01`, `1 -> 10`, the package asks for a
self-contained proof of nonemptiness, minimality, and complete periodic-orbit
vacuum, followed by an exact audit of the periodic words obtained by
circulating finite substitution approximants.

The frozen clock is one shift iterate.  `Fix(n)` means points fixed by the
`n`th shift power.  The Artin--Mazur convention is

```text
zeta_TM(z)=exp(sum_(n>=1) Fix(n) z^n/n).
```

The intended progress is negative but structural: prove that rich recurrence
does not itself supply a primitive-periodic-orbit source.  No target divisor,
arithmetic/local data, operator lift, or Route-B input is in scope.
