# C144 paper plan

## Title and thesis

**A Minimal Uniformly Recurrent Subshift with a Periodic-Orbit Vacuum.**
The paper proves that Thue--Morse recurrence is dynamically rich but supplies
no periodic points and hence no primitive-orbit Route-A source.

## Claims-evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `X_TM` is nonempty and minimal | dyadic block identity and uniform-recurrence proof |
| `X_TM` has no periodic point | odd-popcount multiple and forbidden-window proof |
| the Artin--Mazur zeta is one | all positive fixed-point counts vanish |
| finite approximants do not repair A1 | exact local/stress defect ledger and theorem boundary |
| Route A fails at A1 | Route-A YAML and explicit nonclaims |

## Structure

1. Frozen language subshift.
2. Uniform recurrence and minimality.
3. All-period aperiodicity theorem.
4. Finite periodic approximants and validation.
5. Route-A boundary.

No bibliography or figure is needed; the proof and compact exact table are the
primary content.
