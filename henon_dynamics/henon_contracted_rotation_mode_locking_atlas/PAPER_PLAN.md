   and the exact scope of the finite census.
2. Derive the affine composition formula and the unique fixed point attached to
   a word; prove that the recorded linear inequalities are necessary and
   sufficient for admissibility.
3. Define primitive cyclic representatives and the carry rotation number.
   State the literature boundary carefully: local word uniqueness is proved,
   while no unqualified global one-cycle theorem is used.
4. Present the exact \(\delta\)-interval and endpoint-equality atlas for three
   rational slopes through length 12, together with grouped mode-locking
   components and direct-iteration controls.
5. Explain the source-local derivative factor and why it is not a target
   determinant; close with Route-A A0/A2/A3/A4 boundaries, reproducibility, and
   limitations.
