# Research question

For the one-discontinuity contracted rotation
\(f_{\lambda,\delta}(x)=\{\lambda x+\delta\}\), can a finite primitive
itinerary atlas be made exact enough to expose mode-locking components while
retaining the half-open branch convention at every equality boundary?

The frozen answer is yes at a declared cutoff: for three rational slopes and
all primitive binary words through length 12, affine composition gives a unique
candidate point per word and Fraction arithmetic gives its exact admissible
parameter interval.  Direct iteration independently recovers the certified
suffix words at interior and endpoint probes.  The result is a source-local
piecewise-contraction theorem, not an arithmetic spectral construction.
