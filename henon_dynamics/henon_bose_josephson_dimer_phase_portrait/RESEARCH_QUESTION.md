# Research question — HCS-C243

For the nonlinear two-mode Bose--Josephson dimer, can one give a single
coordinate-safe theorem that classifies the fixed points and \(\Lambda=1\)
pitchfork, reduces every regular energy level to a quartic in \(z\), evaluates
crossing and self-trapped periods by complete elliptic integrals, and treats
the \(H=1\) homoclinic and \(\Lambda=0,2\) boundaries exactly?

Answer: the source-local phase portrait and component criteria are certified;
regular levels remain a continuum, so A1 is WEAK and no discrete primitive
