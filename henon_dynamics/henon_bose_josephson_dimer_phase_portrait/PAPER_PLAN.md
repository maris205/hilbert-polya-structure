# C243 paper plan

**Question.** What is the exact phase portrait of the normalized
Bose--Josephson dimer across the pitchfork, separatrix, pole, and self-trapping
boundaries?

**Theorem spine.** Fix the Hamiltonian and canonical sign convention; pass to
Bloch coordinates; classify all fixed points and linear stability; eliminate
the phase to obtain the quartic and its roots; derive complete-\(K\) periods;
prove the sech homoclinic and component-level reverse criterion.

**Evidence spine.** The receipt has 14 fixed-point rows, 8 Bloch-pole rows,
13 level rows, and 5 criterion rows. Independent symbolic identities and three
elliptic quadratures check the formulas; replay and repaired-hash mutations
check reproducibility.

**Integrity spine.** Smerzi et al. (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.79.4950,
arXiv:cond-mat/9706221) and Raghavan et al. (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.59.620,
arXiv:cond-mat/9706220) support the two-mode convention. A0/A2/A3 fail; A1 is
WEAK because regular levels are continuous; A4 is NATURAL_QUANTIZATION only.
