# C243 narrative report

The advance is a full nonlinear phase-portrait theorem rather than a single
numerical trajectory.  In Bloch coordinates the field is smooth at the poles,
while the canonical chart gives the conserved Hamiltonian and an exact quartic
for \(z\).  Its two roots divide regular levels into a one-component crossing
regime \((-1<H<1)\) and two sign-preserving self-trapped components
\((1<H<h_{\max})\) when \(\Lambda>1\).

The finite ledger covers all requested boundaries: rigid rotation at
\(\Lambda=0\), pitchfork/parabolic coalescence at \(\Lambda=1\), the sech
homoclinic at \(H=1\), and the coordinate-pole turning at \(\Lambda=2\).
Complete elliptic-integral periods are checked against independent quadratures;
the reverse condition is stated at the level of connected energy components.

This is source-local Bose--Josephson dynamics.  It intentionally does not
invent a discrete primitive orbit set, arithmetic labels, or a target zeta.
