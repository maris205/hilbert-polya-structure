# Paper plan

## Working title

**Exact Symplectic Flow and the Complete Stability--Resonance Atlas of the
Ideal Penning Trap**

## Claim hierarchy

1. Main theorem: exact six-dimensional canonical symplectic flow for every
   real signed field and every nonnegative axial frequency.
2. Full parameter atlas: bounded chamber, magnetic threshold, critical Jordan
   face, unstable chamber, zero field, zero axial frequency, free limit, and
   field-sign conjugacy.
3. Stable normal form: exact frequencies, actions, negative magnetron
   Krein/energy sign, and positive axial/cyclotron signs.
4. Orbit closure: active-mode resonance iff closed, primitive minimal period,
   and strobe fixed-space dimensions on all boundary faces.
5. Evaluation boundary: finite evidence as regression only; strict Route-A
   rejection despite natural canonical quantization.

## Section architecture

1. Frozen symmetric-gauge Hamiltonian and theorem.
2. Rotating-frame integration and canonical symplecticity.
3. Signed actions and Krein signature in the stable chamber.
4. Boundedness, Jordan, unstable, and degenerate boundary audit.
5. Active-mode resonance, minimal periods, and strobe fixed spaces.
6. Deterministic evidence and Route-A/nonclaim boundary.
7. Primary-source lineage.

## Revision design

- Round 0 proves the exact flow, stable normal form, and basic parameter atlas.
- Round 1 adds an explicit critical/zero-field/zero-axial/sign-reversal boundary
  lemma and fixes all limiting conventions.
- Round 2 adds the active-mode minimal-period and strobe-fixed-space proof,
  quantitative evidence table, and the natural-quantization nonclaim.

The three versions must therefore differ in mathematical content rather than
cosmetic wording.
