# Limitations

The theorem is local over Q_p. It does not classify complex periodic points or periodic points outside the unit polydisc. A residue cycle is not a genuine periodic point. Algebraic hit sets are finite or all, but no uniform last-hit time or universal finite identity-test algorithm is supplied. Orbit closures are not asserted to be algebraic curves or Zariski dense.

The stated dyadic threshold is a sufficient condition with a general sharp-boundary control; failure for every out-of-threshold double shear is not asserted. The zero parameter is excluded. The natural Koopman operator is unitary and noncompact, which does not rule out all imaginable relative regularizations but does not give an ordinary trace-class determinant.

Poonen's analytic interpolation method and the dynamical Mordell--Lang and Strassmann methods have classical owners. Recent effective certificate work is credited. This package is not a novelty certificate, external peer review, target zero computation, or Hilbert--Pólya construction.
