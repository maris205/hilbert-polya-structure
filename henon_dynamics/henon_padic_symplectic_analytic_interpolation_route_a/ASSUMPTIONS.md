# Assumptions

The field is Q_p and the phase space is Z_p squared. The prime p is arbitrary. The parameter a is nonzero with c=v_p(a)>=1 for odd p and c>=2 for p=2. All time interpolation uses this exact map and its original integer clock. The zero parameter is a control, not an admissible theorem point. The maximum norm and v_p(p)=1 are fixed.

An algebraic target means a subvariety of affine two-space over Q_p, cut out by finitely many polynomials. It does not mean an arbitrary closed or measurable subset. Finite residue cycles are not identified with genuine periodic points. The method needs coefficientwise near-identity, not merely pointwise identity over F_p.
