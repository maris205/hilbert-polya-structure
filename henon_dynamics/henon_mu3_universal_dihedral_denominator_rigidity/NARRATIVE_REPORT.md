# HCS-C54 narrative report

HCS-C53 explains why the fourth moment row behaves differently after rational
descent.  At a good split rational prime, the two equal local factors over
\(K=\mathbf Q(\rho)\) turn the inherited exponent \(2/n\) into \(4/n\)
on one rational factor.  That construction proves that row four clears its
half-power.  It does not classify every row, and it does not determine whether
source symmetry can isolate a smaller integral piece.

HCS-C54 resolves that classification problem from the source equations
outward.  A projective monomial ideal stabilizer must preserve both the cubic
and quadratic equation lines.  Once normalized, it is a dihedral permutation
of the chronological cycle decorated by cubic-root phases.  One edge
recurrence controls every phase.  Its alternating closure condition keeps
exactly the even rotations and odd reflections, with three phase lifts per
support.  This exhaustive calculation gives

\[
\operatorname{PMonStab}(C_n,Q_{n,\rho})
=\operatorname{Dih}(C_{3n})
\]

for every \(n\ge2\).  The statement is full in the projective monomial
category and says nothing about nonmonomial projective automorphisms.

The group does not descend as \(6n\) separate rational automorphisms.
Transport through the HCS-C53 semilinear reversal sends

\[
r\longmapsto r^{-1},\qquad s\longmapsto rs.
\]

The resulting rational object is a nonconstant finite etale group scheme of
rank \(6n\), split by \(K\).  Two congruences show that exactly two geometric
elements are rational in every row.  This group-form theorem, like the source
group theorem, is unconditional equation algebra.

The arithmetic classification begins only after a row is packet-admissible.
The two rational compatible realizations have distinct pure weights and ranks

\[
e_n=\frac{4^n+5}{3},\qquad
o_n=\frac{2(4^n-4)}3=2(e_n-3).
\]

HCS-C53 supplies those packet data for rows \(2,3,4\), but it does not prove
semisimplicity.  In the necessity proof, one fixes a coefficient prime,
restricts to \(K\), and takes semisimplifications.  This preserves traces,
characteristic polynomials, ranks, and purity.  Chebotarev density and
Brauer--Nesbitt then produce an equality in the semisimple Grothendieck group.
Purity separates the equality into the two weight rails.  Their ranks force

\[
n\mid4e_n,\qquad n\mid4o_n.
\]

The relation between the ranks reduces the problem to \(n\mid24\), and a
complete divisor check leaves only \(n=2,4\).  Conversely, when \(n\mid4\),
direct copies of both rails match every Frobenius power trace.  The result is
therefore an if-and-only-if theorem for the complete split-local factor, not
merely a necessary rank test.  Later rows remain conditional on construction
of the required packet data.

The third row illustrates why both weights are essential.  Its scaled total
rank is the integer \(84\), so a one-number check would accept it.  Its
separate scaled ranks are \(92/3\) and \(160/3\).  HCS-C54 strengthens that
obstruction by computing the complete
\(\operatorname{Dih}(C_9)\)-character over the common geometric source field.
No nonzero central source-isotypic sector has multiplicity divisible by three
on both rails.  The calculation includes the residue determinant ratio in the
Cayley action and packages the coefficient-conjugate two-dimensional
representations correctly.

There is one rational descent caveat.  The Fermat packet in its standard
rational form and the complete-intersection packet with \(M_3\)-descent
should not be called representations of one rational group scheme.  A common
\(\mathscr G_3\)-equivariant rational formulation twists the Fermat descent
datum.  The no-go does not depend on that choice because it already holds
after restriction to \(K\).

Quadratic twists provide the last apparent loophole.  A virtual class such as

\[
\mathbf1-\chi_{K/\mathbf Q}
\]

is a nonzero class invisible at split primes, so restriction is not injective
on virtual rational classes.  Here the Grothendieck group is the
fixed-\(\ell\) category generated by finite-dimensional continuous
semisimple realizations of the in-scope compatible systems, unramified outside
one common finite set, rather than all \(G_{\mathbf Q}\)-representations.
Every such kernel class restricts to zero, has rank zero, and cannot change a
\(K\)-rail rank or source-isotypic multiplicity.  It may change inert traces
and the rational extension, but it cannot clear the denominator while
preserving the frozen split Euler object.

The positive result is universal but sharply scoped: a full
projective-monomial source group, its rational group form, and a complete
split-factor ordinary-realization classification.  At inert primes,

\[
P_{K,v}(U^2)=P_p(U)P_p(-U)
\]

is generally not a square.  HCS-C54 constructs no global root, no new smooth
row, no automorphy, no continuation, no functional equation, and no RH
statement.
