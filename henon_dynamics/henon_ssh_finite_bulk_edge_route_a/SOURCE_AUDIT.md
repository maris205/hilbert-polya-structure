# Source and collision audit

## Authoritative lineage

- W. P. Su, J. R. Schrieffer, and A. J. Heeger, *Solitons in
  Polyacetylene*, Physical Review Letters 42 (1979), DOI
  `10.1103/PhysRevLett.42.1698`.  This owns the SSH/polyacetylene model
  lineage.
- W. P. Su, J. R. Schrieffer, and A. J. Heeger, *Soliton excitations in
  polyacetylene*, Physical Review B 22 (1980), DOI
  `10.1103/PhysRevB.22.2099`.  This owns the soliton and localized gap-state
  lineage.
- J. K. Asbóth, L. Oroszlány, and A. Pályi, *A Short Course on Topological
  Insulators*, chapter 1, DOI `10.1007/978-3-319-25607-8_1` (also arXiv
  `1509.02295`).  This is the authoritative pedagogical source for chiral
  form, winding, and SSH bulk--boundary exposition.

No novelty or priority claim is made for the SSH model, its winding number,
bulk--edge correspondence, or finite edge splitting.  C318's package-level
contribution is the unified, boundary-complete, independently falsifiable
finite theorem.

## Repository collision boundary

- C308 is the nearest neighbor: a one-site non-Hermitian nonreciprocal
  Hatano--Nelson chain studying skin spectra and boundary sensitivity.  It
  explicitly excludes topology.  C318 is balanced, Hermitian, bipartite,
  chiral, and owns the finite hyperbolic threshold and two-ended edge pair.
- C267 is an infinite uniform-field Wannier--Stark ladder with Bessel
  propagation, not a finite dimerized bulk--edge chain.
- C297 is a two-site non-Hermitian PT dimer with an exceptional point, not a
  many-cell Hermitian topological system.
- C138 studies flux winding in a metric quantum-graph primitive-walk
  determinant, not Bloch-band winding or SSH hybridized edge modes.

To keep the boundary visible, C318 does not reuse C308's resolvent,
pseudospectral, conditioning, or skin-effect axes.

## Scope audit

All inputs are source lattice data.  The finite characteristic polynomial
is not called an Euler factor; lattice energy levels are not called target
zeros; and no root number, automorphy, target divisor/counting law, target
functional equation, Hilbert--Pólya operator, interaction, disorder, or
many-body dynamical phase transition is claimed.
