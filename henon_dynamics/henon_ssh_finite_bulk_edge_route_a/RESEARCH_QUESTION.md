# Research question

For a balanced finite Hermitian SSH chain with `M` cells and real
nonnegative intracell/intercell hoppings `(v,w)`, can one give one theorem
that simultaneously and without thermodynamic-limit shorthand:

1. computes the exact open-chain characteristic polynomial;
2. distinguishes the periodic bulk transition `w/v=1` from the finite open
   hyperbolic-edge threshold `w/v=(M+1)/M`;
3. supplies exact edge energies and eigenvectors, including the threshold
   taper and exponential localization;
4. resolves finite-ring zero-mode parity, separates the continuum and
   sampled spectral gaps, and treats every singular hopping face;
5. gives a propagator formula that remains meaningful at zero singular
   values; and
6. states exactly when a gapped Bloch quench has a zero mode amplitude,
   without confusing a continuum momentum with a sampled finite-ring mode?

The secondary audit question is whether any of those exact source results
passes Route A.  The answer is no: only the natural-quantization axis is
positive, and no target arithmetic or spectral correspondence is supplied.
