# Paper plan

## Claim sentence

The positive Lyness map has a globally exact five-cycle law, but that same
law forces uncountable periodic fixed sets and an infinite-multiplicity
order-five Koopman spectrum, excluding both the classical Artin--Mazur zeta
and the ordinary trace-class Fredholm determinant.

## Argument order

1. Freeze the map, clock, positive domain, and no-target scope.
2. Display all five rational iterates and classify periods globally.
3. Turn the period law into the precise Artin--Mazur obstruction.
4. Prove invariant log-symplectic measure and reversal.
5. Derive the cyclic Koopman projections with the checked sign.
6. Prove all five eigenspaces have infinite multiplicity and extract the
   compactness, Schatten, Fredholm, and self-adjointness consequences.
7. Give the A0--A4 verdict and declarations.

## Figure and table decision

No figure is needed.  The central relationship is a five-line exact iterate
chain, and a diagram would be less precise.  No comparison table is needed
because there is one frozen model and one theorem chain.  Equations carry
the information directly.

## Paper contract

- Standalone English and Simplified Chinese abstracts.
- Six English and six Chinese keywords.
- No bibliography; citation population zero; no novelty claim.
- Explicit limitations and six declaration categories.
- Compact two-page target with embedded fonts and no layout warnings.
