# Research question

For the bounded double integrator, can one give a convention-complete global
minimum-time synthesis rather than a phase-plane sketch: an exact value on
every state, a proof that its two arc lengths are admissible, a sharp global
lower bound, a nonsmooth HJB certificate, all singular boundaries and an
independent executable audit?

The secondary Route-A question is deliberately strict: does this classical
Hamiltonian control structure supply an intrinsic rational-prime carrier,
primitive source clock, target determinant/analytic structure or natural
same-clock Hilbert--Polya lift?  It does not.  The Pontryagin Hamiltonian is
recorded only as `A4_FORMAL_HINT`.
