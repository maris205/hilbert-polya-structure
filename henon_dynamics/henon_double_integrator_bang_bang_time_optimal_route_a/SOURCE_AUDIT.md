# Source audit

## Frozen source owner

- Marcello Romano and Fabio Curti, “Time-optimal control of linear time
  invariant systems between two arbitrary states,” *Automatica* 120 (2020), 109151,
  DOI: `10.1016/j.automatica.2020.109151`.

This source treats minimum-time bounded normal LTI systems, their reduction to
an origin-transfer problem, and the double-integrator context.  C222 claims no
priority for bang--bang control, Pontryagin's principle or the value formula.
The article title and DOI were checked against the publisher record.

## Package increment

The release contribution is a convention-locked synthesis: the sharp endpoint
moment interval and sufficiency proof, whole-plane formulas, all boundary
branches, independent exact/high-precision evidence, hostile schema tests,
three substantive paper revisions and a strict Route-A evaluation.  `NEW`
inside the batch means absent from the workspace, not new to the literature.

## Citation and claim boundary

The DOI resolves to the named *Automatica* article and is used only for the
classical owner.  The paper's all-state theorem is derived in the artifact;
finite regression rows are not cited as proof.  No source is recruited for an
arithmetic, target-zero, determinant or Hilbert--Polya claim.
