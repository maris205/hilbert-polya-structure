# Research question — C119

Can one choose an exact linear Hénon-type Euclidean contraction whose standard
bosonic second quantization is provably trace class, and then derive its
Fredholm determinant, trace sequence, Taylor prefix, and full zero divisor
without numerical fitting?

The question is deliberately source-owned. It does not ask whether this
determinant matches an external target divisor. It also tests a sharp tradeoff:
strict contraction makes the Fock operator analytic but destroys all
nontrivial periodic dynamics.
