# Experiment plan — C119

1. Freeze the rational linear Hénon-type matrix and Fock convention.
2. Compute eigenvalues, determinant, `A^T A`, and exact contraction inequalities.
3. Prove that every finite-period point is the origin.
4. Use symmetric tensor powers to enumerate Fock eigenvalues and singular values.
5. Prove trace class, derive the trace formula and Fredholm product.
6. Apply the Newton recurrence to compute determinant coefficients through
   degree eight and solve the exact zero-multiplicity counting problem.
7. Run an independent checker, symbolic reconstruction, byte replay, and
   hostile mutation audit.
8. Compile twice in isolated fixed-date environments, inspect fonts/logs, and
   close a 26-file hash manifest.
