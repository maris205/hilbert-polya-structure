## Outcome

This round delivers five independent theorem packages from baseline
`1ccbfe2d759fe007c6b53c9646e1ab031878b34a`.  Each paper has its own
research question, analytic theorem/proof package, executable evidence,

  ordered Type-I cylinder limits with residue `1/2`; strict area dissipation;
  and the minimal product's complete separated Jacobi count, with index

  affine weight-two `SL(2,C)` covariance and modular-generator permutations;
  Chazy and discriminant transport; and every pair-collision branch,
  including the reciprocal family, nonzero diagonal, all three coordinate
  axes, and the cusp/pole boundaries.
- **C321:** all-order fixed-vertex rising-factorial moments in the initial-edge
  preferential-attachment tree; almost-sure and every-finite-`Lp`
  convergence after square-root scaling, with explicit moments and Carleman
  determinacy; and, on the separate population scale, the `L2` law
  `N_k(n)/n -> 4/[k(k+1)(k+2)]` for every fixed degree.
- **C322:** the exact gap `(N+2)/[2(N-1)]` for `N(I-Q_N)` on Kac's energy
  sphere, proved by conditional slicing and a complete `TT*/T*T` block-Gram
  spectral transfer that also closes continuous and approximate spectrum;
  the unique centered-quartic slow mode for `N>=3`; sharp `L2` decay; and
  all two-particle and zero/positive-energy faces.
- **C323:** the full marked/unmarked bright--dark spectrum of complete-graph
  continuous-time quantum search; exact success probability; perfect search
  if and only if the driver is resonant; the exact first hit, off-resonance
  maximum and square-root detuning window; graph-adjacency phase equivalence;
  and every empty/full-marking, one-vertex and zero-driver boundary.

## Citation, proof, and scope integrity

The source audits assign the Clifford-family evolution to the isoparametric
mean-curvature-flow literature, Darboux--Halphen and its theta identities to
Ohyama and the modern modular reconstructions, preferential attachment to
Barabasi--Albert and rigorous degree-sequence/limit work, the Kac gap to Kac
and Carlen--Carvalho--Loss, and continuous-time search to Farhi--Gutmann.
Repository packaging is never used as evidence of literature priority.  The
collision map was checked against the C1--C318 registry before drafting.

The C319 theorem stays inside the two-factor Clifford family.  C320 separates
its generic theta chart from every discriminant-zero branch and does not
claim an exhaustive meromorphic classification.  C321 does not infer a
maximum-degree theorem or silently switch to a self-loop convention.  C322's
finite polynomial matrices are not used as its infinite-dimensional lower
bound: the final proof explicitly transfers the whole nonzero spectrum with
`TT*/T*T` and accounts for the `1/N` accumulation point.  C323's finite
Hamiltonian remains an oracle-model theorem and makes no external complexity
claim.

All five evaluations set `route_b_invocation_allowed: false`.  No target
arithmetic local datum, Euler factor, bad-prime datum, root number, automorphy
object, target divisor/counting law or functional equation, target zero
match, Hilbert--Polya operator, or Route-B input is asserted.  C320's `E2`
divisor sums are native source arithmetic only; C319 and C322 retain formal
source-operator hints only; C323's `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` belongs solely
to its finite Hermitian source Hamiltonian.
