# Hostile audit

The suite repairs the receipt hash after each semantic mutation, preventing a
checksum-only checker from passing as validation.  It rejects mutations of:

- schema, candidate, scope, precision, normalization, determinant convention,
  and cutoffs;
- renewal coefficients, triangular exponents, traces, primitive counts, and
  primitive weights;
- trace norms, determinant theorem, entire order, and negative-control
  classification;
- every promoted Route-A or forbidden-claim flag.

Totals: 24 repaired-hash semantic mutations plus one independent stale-hash
sentinel, all rejected.

The strongest hostile conceptual control is not synthetic: the constant
advance system has a simple rational formal renewal series but a noncompact
natural operator.  Therefore the positive result cannot be reduced to scalar
series manipulation.
