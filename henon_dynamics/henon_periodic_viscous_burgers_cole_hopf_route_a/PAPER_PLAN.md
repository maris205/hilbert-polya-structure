# Paper plan

## One-paper increment

The manuscript is not one lemma split into several outputs. It packages one complete
global phase portrait for a distinct continuous PDE owner: periodic viscous Burgers
on every fixed-mean Sobolev leaf and all \((\nu,L,m)\).

## Structure

1. Freeze the PDE, physical clock, Sobolev leaf, positive projective cone, and signs.
2. State the global Cole--Hopf/projective drift--heat conjugacy.
3. Prove global smoothing and convergence, then exclude every nonconstant equilibrium,
   periodic point, and recurrent point.
4. Prove the exact first-active-mode expansion and logarithmic decay exponent.
5. Give the complete linearized spectrum with its domain and fixed-mean restriction.
6. Separate the finite exact oracle from the analytic proof.
7. Record Hopf--Cole ownership and the negative Route-A disposition.

## Revision ladder

- Round 0: correct global conjugacy and global solution formula.
- Round 1: add the full recurrence theorem, exact leading asymptotics, spectral-domain
  statement, and their proof mechanisms.
- Round 2: harden fixed-coordinate versus Galilean language; add the executable
  certificate, independent-check boundary, classical ownership, and scope/route audit.

The final paper must remain compact but self-contained, with no novelty claim for the
classical transformation and no arithmetic upgrade from a generic PDE linearization.
