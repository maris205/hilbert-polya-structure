# Narrative report

The paper's single large step is an exact finite-state stochastic atlas.  The
selection ratio appears in a closed fixation formula, while the same
tridiagonal generator yields every occupation time and expected absorption
time as rational data.  Detailed balance supplies a reversible symmetrization,
which is a structural result rather than a numerical fit.

The eight receipts cover neutral, advantageous, disadvantageous, weak, and
strong selection at several population sizes.  The result is an A1 analytic
pass but A0 fails: there is no arithmetic origin or target orbit owner.
