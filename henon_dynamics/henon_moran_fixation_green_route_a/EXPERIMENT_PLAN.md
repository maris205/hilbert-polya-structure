# Experiment and verification plan

1. Generate eight exact rational Moran rows and tridiagonal generators.
2. Independently solve the fixation and absorption-time recurrences and invert
   the transient generator over the rationals.
3. Verify detailed balance and neutral/selection limits symbolically.
4. Replay the producer twice and require byte equality.
5. Mutate metadata, route, scope, and fixation cells; require rejection.
6. Build three fixed-epoch manuscript revisions and audit fonts, text, and
   manifest closure.

No Monte Carlo estimate is promoted to a theorem.  The beta=0 and N=1 faces
are recorded as boundaries, not silently inverted.
