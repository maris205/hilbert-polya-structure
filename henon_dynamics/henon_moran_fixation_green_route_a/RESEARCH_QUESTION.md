# Research question

Can a fixed-size stochastic population process be closed exactly—rather than
simulated—at the level of fixation, occupation Green matrix, absorption time,
reversibility, and parameter faces?

**Answer.** Yes.  The finite tridiagonal generator gives a rational Green
matrix, the selection-ratio formula gives fixation, and the neutral, frozen,
and singleton faces are separated.  Arithmetic origin is none, so A0 stops
the Route-A evaluation.
