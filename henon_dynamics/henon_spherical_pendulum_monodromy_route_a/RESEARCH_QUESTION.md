# Research question — HCS-C244

Can a coordinate-safe reduction of the spherical pendulum turn the qualitative
focus-focus picture into a reproducible analytic atlas?  The answer proved in
this round is yes at the level of the reduced cubic: its discriminant and
double-root parameterization delimit the interior elliptic critical branch,
while independent quadrature receipts describe regular Liouville tori.  The
isolated top focus-focus value carries an integral monodromy receipt
\(M=\left[\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right]\) after fixing
both loop orientation and the column convention.

The result deliberately stops before any discrete arithmetic matching.  A
regular torus is a continuum, and the certificate does not manufacture a
primitive-orbit or target determinant.
