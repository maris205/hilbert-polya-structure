# C244 hostile audit

The mutation suite repairs the payload hash after changing source/evaluator
locks, route and scope flags, endpoint labels, critical parameterization,
fixed-point type, regular inputs, roots, all three quadratures, row counts,
monodromy matrix/basis, theorem prose, identity count, and citation DOI.  An
unknown top-level key and a stale hash are also tested.  The independent
checker rejects all 34 mutations (34/34); no semantic mutation is accepted.
