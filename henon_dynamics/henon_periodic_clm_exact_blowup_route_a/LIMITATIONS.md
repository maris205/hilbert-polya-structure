# Limitations

- This is the inviscid, nonadvective CLM model, not generalized CLM with advection or dissipation.
- The theorem concerns classical smooth solutions only up to the first denominator pole; it selects no weak continuation.
- At a pole point, `ω` can remain zero while `Hω` diverges. “Breakdown” therefore means loss of smooth CLM evolution, not necessarily pointwise divergence at the zero itself.
- The local self-similar vorticity profile assumes `ω'_0(x*)≠0`.
- A global two-sided `L∞` rate is claimed only when every point attaining the first time is simple.
- Tangent and higher-order zeros may have different scales; no rate is asserted for them.
- Exact singularity formation in this toy model implies nothing about three-dimensional Euler singularities.
- No global literature novelty, arithmetic target, determinant, or quantum claim is made.
