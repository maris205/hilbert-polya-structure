# Paper artifacts

- `main_round0_original.pdf`: convention, periodic Tricomi closure, conserved mean, and both Möbius flows.
- `main_round1.pdf`: adds both necessary-and-sufficient forward clocks and the complete one-mode phase diagram.
- `main_round2.pdf`: adds transverse profiles, conditional global rate, boundary atlas, and Route-A closure.
- `main.pdf`: byte-identical to round 2.
- `main_round0.tex`, `main_round1.tex`, and `main_round2.tex`: checked-in build wrappers fixing the round gate explicitly.

Every round is compiled from its wrapper twice in fresh directories and audited for deterministic bytes, settled warnings, embedded Latin and CJK fonts, gated bilingual abstracts and keyword lists, text extraction, and rasterization.
