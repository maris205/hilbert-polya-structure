# Scope

## In scope

- Real smooth `2π`-periodic initial data.
- The inviscid nonadvective equation `ω_t=ωHω`.
- The Fourier convention `H(e^{ikx})=-i sign(k)e^{ikx}` and `H1=0`.
- Arbitrary conserved mean `μ`.
- Exact flow up to the first denominator pole.
- Necessary-and-sufficient forward clocks, one-mode thresholds, and local profiles at simple first zeros.

## Out of scope

- Advection, dissipation, forcing, weak continuation after blow-up, or numerical regularization.
- An unconditional vorticity profile at tangent/higher-order zeros.
- Any implication for singularity formation in three-dimensional Euler.
- Target arithmetic data, Euler products, root numbers, target zeros, a Hilbert–Pólya operator, or Route B.

Scope literal: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
