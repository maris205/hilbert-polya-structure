# Research question

Can the periodic Constantin–Lax–Majda equation be closed exactly for smooth real data of arbitrary mean—with one immutable Hilbert-transform convention—so that global solutions, finite-time denominator poles, first forward times, one-mode thresholds, and valid self-similar rates are all separated without silently assuming zero mean or simple zeros?

The theorem must answer:

1. which complex variable produces the correct Riccati sign;
2. how the zero- and nonzero-mean Möbius formulas differ;
3. whether initial zeros are exactly equivalent to forward breakdown when the mean is nonzero;
4. which branch of `arccot` gives the first time;
5. what replaces that criterion at zero mean;
6. which transversality hypotheses are necessary before claiming an inverse-time vorticity profile.

It must also apply the strict Route-A evaluator without turning a fluid toy model into an arithmetic or quantum claim.
