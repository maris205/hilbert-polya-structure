# Paper plan

## Round 0 — exact arbitrary-mean flow

Freeze the Fourier sign, prove the periodic Tricomi identity and mean conservation, derive `z_t=iμz+z²/2`, solve both mean strata, and recover real vorticity formulas.

## Round 1 — complete first-pole clock

Prove the nonzero-mean zero-set iff theorem, fix the principal `arccot` branch, prove the zero-mean signed-zero criterion, and close every one-mode regime.

## Round 2 — valid rates and strict boundary

Derive explicit local profiles at simple first poles, prove the global inverse-time rate only under simultaneous simplicity, isolate tangent/higher-order zeros and post-pole continuation, then close sources, collisions, and Route A.

Each round is a compiled standalone theorem increment; the final PDF contains all three.
