pairwise symmetrization, upper Dini derivatives of spatial and velocity
diameters, and the monotone barrier

$$V(t)+K\int_{X(0)}^{X(t)}(1+s^2)^{-\beta}\,ds.$$

produces the exact first integral

$$u(r)=u_0-K\int_{r_0}^{r}(1+s^2)^{-\beta}\,ds.$$
