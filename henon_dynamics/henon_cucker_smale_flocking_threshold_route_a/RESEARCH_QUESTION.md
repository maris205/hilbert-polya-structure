# Research question

For the normalized continuous-time Cucker--Smale system

$$
\dot x_i=v_i,\qquad
\dot v_i=\frac K N\sum_{j=1}^N(1+|x_j-x_i|^2)^{-\beta}(v_j-v_i),
$$

which source-intrinsic quantity gives a rigorous all-particle confinement and
alignment threshold, does the long-range endpoint `beta=1/2` belong to the
unconditional chamber, and in what precise sense is the short-range threshold
sharp?

The answer must separate three statements that are often conflated:

1. a sufficient tail-integral barrier for arbitrary `N` and dimension;
2. unconditional flocking for `0 <= beta <= 1/2`;
3. a necessary-and-sufficient scalar outward two-body classification for
   `beta>1/2`, including the equality trajectory with vanishing relative speed
   but unbounded separation.

No claim is made that the sufficient barrier is necessary for every
many-particle configuration.
