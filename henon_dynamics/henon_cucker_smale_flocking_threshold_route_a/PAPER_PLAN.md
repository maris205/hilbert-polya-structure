   of flocking.
2. Prove global existence, convex-hull control, conserved mean velocity, and
   the exact variance identity.
3. Prove the diameter comparison and tail-integral barrier; extract explicit
   confinement and exponential alignment.
4. Evaluate the communication tail and retain `beta=1/2` in the unconditional
   chamber.
5. Solve the outward scalar two-body problem exactly and distinguish the
   subthreshold, equality, and superthreshold regimes.
6. Close degenerate faces, source provenance, collision boundaries, evidence
   semantics, and the Route-A firewall.
