# Research question

Does the explicit P72 singularity ladder determine a unique all-channel
counterterm, or does cancellation leave an unconstrained holomorphic gauge?

The predeclared tests are:

1. freeze the channel-log multiplier class and prove coefficientwise
   necessity from the isolated P72 poles;
2. replay the uncancelled negative source boundary and determine its exact
   power-exponential pair;
3. classify the remaining holomorphic freedom;
4. compare two source-ordered primary-factor normalizations; and
5. test whether any finite Taylor-jet condition removes that freedom.

No operator, arithmetic labeling, or Route-B conclusion is inferred from an
analytic counterterm classification.
