# Source audit

## External sources used

1. Young-One Kim, Jungseob Lee, and Kyewon K. Park, *A zeta function for
   flip systems*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 209 (2003), Example 4.3,
   DOI `10.2140/pjm.2003.209.289`.  This owns the full two-shift reverse-flip
   Lind zeta formula.
2. Robert L. Devaney and Zbigniew Nitecki, *Shift automorphisms in the Hénon
   mapping*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 67 (1979), DOI
   `10.1007/BF01221361`.  This supplies the classical Hénon/full-shift
   horseshoe context.
3. Conway and Remmert are cited only for standard one-variable facts:
   Weierstrass normal convergence, holomorphic logarithms on simply connected
   domains, and unconditional convergence of normally convergent products.
4. Simon is cited only to delimit what an actual infinite determinant would
   require.  No theorem of Simon is used to construct the P74 counterterm.

## Repository-owned inputs

P72 owns the exact formula

    log C_rel=H_rel-sum_(m>=2)c_m Phi(t^m)

and the coefficient/radius formulas.  P73 owns the order-independent
genus-`m-1` all-channel construction that P74 compares with the next genus.
P74 hash-locks both the P72 and P73 proof packages, certificates, and
compiled PDFs.

## New derivations in P74

The full complex partial fraction orbit, coefficientwise multiplier
rigidity, negative source replay, gauge-torsor classification, two primary
regularizations, Möbius product residual, and finite-jet nonuniqueness are
internal derivations.  No external source is credited with those statements.
