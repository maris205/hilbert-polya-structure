# Failure-mode audit

- **Raw/relative sign confusion:** the relative log is negative and the
  multiplier log is positive; the forced equality is `d_m=c_m`.
- **Pole collision:** strict radial separation gives one channel owner for
  every complex pole.
- **Illicit interchange:** the declared condition
  `sum |d_m|r^m<infinity` gives compact normal convergence before local
  principal parts are compared.
- **Source-branch ambiguity:** the negative source theorem is stated on a
  local slit, and nonzero extension fixes the exponent.  If zeros/poles are
  allowed, the weaker integral-exponent conclusion is recorded.
- **False absolute uniqueness:** the residual is explicitly an
  `O(D)^x` gauge torsor.
- **Universal-triviality overclaim:** only the genus `m-1`, `G=0`, final
  scalar-normalized convention trivializes; genus `m` supplies an explicit
  nontrivial counterexample to a universal claim.
- **Residual sign error:** both exact coefficient implementations require
  `-2c_m t^m`.
- **Finite-jet promotion:** explicit gauges preserve arbitrary finite jets.
- **Slit monodromy promotion:** nontrivial logarithmic monodromy is extra
  regularization data and violates the globally exponential nowhere-zero
  class unless it vanishes.
- **Operator promotion:** analytic pole products are not identified with a
  Fredholm or regularized determinant.
- **Arithmetic promotion:** integer channel indices have no certified
  rational-prime or von-Mangoldt semantics.
- **Route-B promotion:** authorization remains false.
