# C163 hostile audit

## Theorem attack

The most dangerous shortcut is to infer irrational phase from decimals.  The
release instead proves that `r+r^(-1)` has primitive irreducible integer
polynomial `3x^4-19x^2+27` and monic rational minimal polynomial
`x^4-(19/3)x^2+9`.  Its nonintegral coefficient excludes algebraic
integrality, so a root-of-unity ratio is impossible.  A second attack notes
that marginal phase and modulus limits do
not imply independence.  The exact mixed transform is supplied and checked,
so the joint product limit is a separate theorem.

## Control attack

The moved-hole model has phase ratio `i`, so it must not be advertised as
Haar.  Exact residue ledgers and the finite Fourier bound certify the moving
four-coset limit.  The closed parent has three surviving phases and is kept
outside the binary proposition.
