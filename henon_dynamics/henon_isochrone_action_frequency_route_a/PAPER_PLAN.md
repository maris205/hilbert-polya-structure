# Paper plan

## Central claim

Give a complete action–frequency and closed-orbit atlas for the planar Hénon isochrone Hamiltonian, with the allowed energy domain and all degenerate faces stated as part of the theorem.

## Reader contract

The paper must let a reader verify the result without trusting the evidence generator.  Classical provenance is explicit; no literature-priority, arithmetic, target-determinant, target-zero, or Hilbert–Pólya claim is permitted.

## Structure

1. **Model, conventions, and source boundary.** Define \(\ell=|L|\), physical time, action convention, and classical ownership.
2. **Circular boundary and energy domain.** Derive \(s_c,r_c,E_c\); prove \(E_c\leq E<0\) is exact.
3. **Quadratic substitution.** Derive \(Q(x)\), its root sum/product, and the radial period.
4. **Action–frequency theorem.** Integrate \(\partial_EJ_r\), invert the action, differentiate the Hamiltonian.
5. **Apsidal angle and closure.** Use partial fractions and shifted root products; prove the rational return criterion and primitive period.
6. **Boundary atlas.** Separate circles, central equilibrium, center crossings, signed angular momentum, escape, and the noncommuting Kepler collision corner.
7. **Finite certificate.** Explain exact quadratic-field encoding, independent quadratures, strict schemas, replay, mutations, and why the grid is not the proof.
8. **Route-A and quantization.** Prove only the natural bounded-potential Schrödinger realization; freeze the conservative tuple.
9. **Limitations and declarations.** State all nonclaims and standard reproducibility declarations.

## Three substantive versions

- **Round 0:** complete circular-domain, substitution, period, and action proof.
- **Round 1:** add independent apsidal derivation, closure theorem, and every exceptional boundary.
- **Round 2:** add certificate construction, Route-A interpretation, natural quantization, source-priority limits, and declarations.

The three archived PDFs must be textually and bytewise distinct; the final `main.pdf` must equal round 2.
