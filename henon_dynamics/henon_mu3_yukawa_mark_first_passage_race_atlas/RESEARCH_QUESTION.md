# C91 research question

For the frozen C88 first-passage variables `T_H` attached to the twenty
actual subgroup targets, what is the exact race law for every unordered pair
of targets that is incomparable in the subgroup-inclusion poset?

The finite answer records, for each of the 108 incomparable pairs, the number
of uniform sixteen-label permutations with `T_left < T_right`, with equality,
or with `T_right < T_left`.  The certificate also resolves those counts by
the common first-passage rank and by the pivotal boundary edges that generate
the permutations.  This is a finite combinatorial result under the scope
firewall `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
