# C148 paper plan

## Working title

**Exact Traces and Escape Ranks for a Three-Symbol Open Walsh Gate**

## Claims/evidence narrative

1. Freeze the DFT, projector order, tensor shift, and one-step clock.
2. Lead with the corrected one-step versus `k`-step rank theorem.
3. Prove the exact Gram defects and the tensor-cycle identity.
4. Derive the gcd trace formula and Newton recurrence before presenting finite
   coefficient data.
5. State the signed/complex closed-walk and primitive product with its local
   raw-product domain.
6. Use closed, projector-order, and hole-location controls to distinguish
   matrix geometry from spectral sensitivity.
7. End with finite-`k`, non-self-adjoint, no-target, and no-Route-B boundaries.

## Layout target

Three to five pages, no figures, two compact tables.  Round 1 audits the rank
quantifiers, zero algebraic multiplicity, and primitive-product convergence.
Round 2 audits control logic, coefficient reproducibility, and claim scope.
No external reviewer, numerical score, acceptance prediction, or cross-model
independence claim is used.
