\begin{abstract}
We enumerate the complete support geometry of sixteen frozen named
coordinates in the finite abelian core
$Q\cong\Z/9\oplus\Z/3\oplus\Z/2$.  A support is a subset of the labels and
its closure is the subgroup generated by the selected coordinates.  The
11520-element C75 lifted symmetry has an order-six kernel on labels, so the
faithful effective action is generated by five explicit permutations and has
order 1920.  Its action partitions all $2^{16}=65536$ supports into 3024
orbits.  Exact closure tracking finds 98 single-deletion-minimal supports in
34 orbits; imposing closure equal to all of $Q$ leaves 25 supports in seven
orbits, all of cardinality three.  The computation is source-bound to the C75
evidence and records the ambient kernel explicitly.  It is a finite named
support result, with no arithmetic, local, Euler-factor, root-number,
Burnside-ring, or Hilbert--Polya claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Finite support object}
Let
\[
Q=\Z/9\oplus\Z/3\oplus\Z/2
\]
and let $(x_i)_{i=1}^{16}$ be the frozen C75 named coordinates.  Write
$L=\{S_1,\ldots,S_{16}\}$ for the labels.  For a support $A\subseteq L$,
define
\[
\Phi(A)=\left\langle x_i:S_i\in A\right\rangle\leq Q,
\qquad \Phi(\varnothing)=\{0\}.
\]
The complete support space therefore has $2^{16}=65536$ elements.  C76
records both the generated subgroup and the orbit of each support; it does
not sample supports or replace the named presentation by an abstract
isomorphism type.  The scope firewall is
\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER}.

\section{The effective label group}
The C75 lifted object retains an ambient automorphism in each pair and has
order 11520.  Its ambient order-six factor is a kernel for the induced action
on the sixteen labels.  C76 removes only this action kernel and works with
the faithful permutation group $H\leq\Sym(L)$ generated by
\[
\begin{split}
z_5&=(S_5\ S_6\ S_{10}\ S_{13}\ S_{14}),\\
z_2&=(S_5\ S_6),\\
a&=(S_3\ S_{11}),\qquad c=(S_7\ S_{15}),\\
u&=(S_1\ S_{16})(S_3\ S_4)(S_{11}\ S_{12}).
\end{split}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Effective label action]
Direct permutation closure gives $|H|=1920$.  Its element-order
distribution is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}
\text{order}&1&2&3&4&5&6&10&12&20\\\hline
\text{count}&1&311&20&584&24&460&264&160&96.
\end{array}
\]
GAP identifies the compatible direct-product structure as
$C_2\times S_5\times D_8$ (equivalently $S_5\times C_2\times D_8$).
\end{theorem}

The C6 factor is therefore a recorded boundary condition, not an additional
generator of support orbits.  This distinction is essential: using the
11520-element ambient lift would collapse to the same support partition but
would misstate the effective group and its stabilizers.

\section{Complete support orbit atlas}
The effective group acts on supports by
\[
 h\cdot\{S_i:i\in A\}=\{h(S_i):S_i\in A\}.
\]
The producer and checker enumerate every orbit and choose its smallest mask as
representative.

\begin{theorem}[Orbit partition]
The 65536 supports form exactly 3024 $H$-orbits.  Their size spectrum is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrrr}
|O|&1&2&4&5&8&10&16&20&40&80&160\\\hline
\#O&128&256&416&128&192&384&16&672&608&208&16.
\end{array}
\]
The number of orbits by support cardinality $k=0,\ldots,16$ is
\[
1,7,27,73,151,252,352,424,450,424,352,252,151,73,27,7,1.
\]
The size-weighted orbit sum is 65536.
\end{theorem}

The closure atlas contains all twenty subgroups of $Q$.  Aggregating rows by
subgroup order gives the following independent view of the same partition:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
|\Phi(A)|&1&2&3&6&9&18&27&54\\\hline
\#\text{supports}&32&32&256&256&2080&2080&30400&30400\\
\#\text{orbits}&6&6&24&24&150&150&1332&1332.
\end{array}
\]
The evidence keeps the finer subgroup-index counts, so this aggregation does
not discard distinctions between subgroups of equal order.

\section{Minimal supports}
Call $A$ closure-minimal when $A=\varnothing$ or
\[
\Phi(A\setminus\{S_i\})\neq\Phi(A)
\quad\text{for every }S_i\in A.
\]
This is a single-deletion definition and is checked directly for all 65536
supports.

\begin{proposition}[Closure-minimal atlas]
There are 98 closure-minimal supports, with cardinality counts
\[
\{0:1,\ 1:11,\ 2:48,\ 3:38\}.
\]
They form 34 $H$-orbits with spectrum
\[
\{1:10,\ 2:8,\ 4:14,\ 8:2\}.
\]
Among them, 25 supports generate the full 54-point core $Q$.  Every such
support is a triple; the 25 supports form seven orbits with spectrum
\[
\{1:1,\ 2:2,\ 4:3,\ 8:1\}.
\]
Their canonical representative masks are
\[
261,\quad265,\quad321,\quad324,\quad385,\quad448,\quad33025.
\]
\end{proposition}

The masks are retained rather than replaced by informal label descriptions so
that the result can be compared byte-for-byte with the canonical evidence.
For example, with the least significant bit assigned to $S_1$, mask 261 is
the support $\{S_1,S_3,S_9\}$ and mask 33025 is $\{S_1,S_9,S_{16}\}$.

\section{Reproducibility and scope}
The source-bound producer and independent checker recompute the subgroup
extension table, effective group, orbit partition, closure atlas, and both
minimality filters.  GAP, clean replay, and hostile semantic mutation tests
are separate gates; they must preserve the C6-kernel boundary and the exact
source hashes.  The resulting object is an exact finite support-orbit atlas
for the named coordinates.  It is not a full table of marks or Burnside-ring
calculation, and it carries no arithmetic/local, Euler-factor, root-number,
automorphy, or Hilbert--Polya interpretation.
