## Ranked retained ideas

### 1. C258: mixed congruential full-period atlas

- Hypothesis: prime-power return valuations plus CRT give a complete
  all-modulus full-period classification and an exact primitive ledger.
- Contribution: theorem, arithmetic control, and natural source Koopman lift.
- Risk: medium; close neighbors C172/C204 require an explicit owner
  distinction.
- Minimum validation: all affine pairs through modulus 96, local valuation
  controls, symbolic cycle determinants, replay, and hostile mutations.
- Status: retained and executable.

### 2. C257: quadratic Newton--Cayley global dynamics

- Hypothesis: the Newton map for `z^2-a^2`, `a != 0`, is globally conjugate on
  the Riemann sphere to squaring, closing basins, Julia boundary, rates,
  periodic and preperiodic classification, multipliers, invariant measure,
  and source zeta in one theorem.
- Contribution: complete complex root-finding dynamical atlas.
- Risk: medium; binary periodic counts overlap neighboring doubling owners, so
  the headline must remain the full Newton root-finding geometry.
- Minimum validation: exact Cayley recurrences, Möbius iteration, cyclotomic
  order census, high-precision basin and measure controls.
- Status: retained.

### 3. C256: KdV cnoidal--soliton traveling-wave atlas

- Hypothesis: the three-root first integral classifies every bounded real
  traveling profile and all root-collision faces.
- Contribution: nonlinear dispersive-PDE theorem with exact elliptic period,
  mean, Galilean covariance, harmonic and soliton limits.
- Risk: medium; nonlinear stability of arbitrary PDE solutions is excluded.
- Minimum validation: symbolic ODE identities, independent elliptic
  quadrature, root-grid controls, limits, replay, and mutations.
- Status: retained.

### 4. C254: Monod chemostat threshold theorem

- Hypothesis: exact total-mass relaxation reduces the all-parameter dynamics
  to washout, critical, and survival chambers with global convergence.
- Contribution: complete ecological ODE phase atlas, implicit invariant-leaf
  solution, transcritical exchange, and critical algebraic rate.
- Risk: low; the main challenge is keeping global and invariant-leaf arguments
  distinct.
- Minimum validation: exact phase-chamber identities, Jacobian spectra,
  implicit differentiation, boundary controls, replay, and mutations.
- Status: retained.

### 5. C255: Euler--Poincare--Suslov heteroclinic atlas

- Hypothesis: the constrained reduced energy circle has an explicit
  hyperbolic-angle solution, two heteroclinic branches, clean reconstructed
  rotations, and a singular Poisson boundary.
- Contribution: complete nonholonomic mechanical theorem including principal
  and zero-energy faces.
- Risk: medium; the clean full-space periodic families must not be erased by
  the reduced heteroclinic description.
- Minimum validation: exact constrained Euler equations, energy, hyperbolic
  solutions, endpoint rates, reconstruction periods, Poisson density,
  reversor, replay, and mutations.
- Status: retained.

## Pilot policy and execution order

These are theorem-first projects, so a GPU pilot is inapplicable.  Each idea
instead receives an exact executable pilot before manuscript finalization:
symbolic identities, an independent implementation, deterministic replay, and
repaired-hash semantic mutations.  The execution order is C258 first because
its arithmetic gate is strongest, C257/C256 next for their intrinsic periodic
structures, then C254/C255 for complete global and obstruction atlases.
