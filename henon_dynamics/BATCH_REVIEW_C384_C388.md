## Five complete mathematical increments

**C384: wild additive geometric dynamics.** For every prime p, including 2,
the map `x -> x+x^p` on the algebraic closure has, at iterate n,
`p^(n-p^v_p(n))` geometric fixed points, each with local multiplicity
`p^(p^v_p(n))`. Linearized polynomial Euclid gives every extension-field
count, including nonsemisimple cases where p divides the extension degree.
Two Mobius inversions retain dynamical least period and residue degree as
different clocks. The least splitting degree is determined, including its
exceptional singleton case. A convergent Lambert expansion and controlled
radial limits give noninteger logarithmic-derivative residues at all
nontrivial p-power roots of unity. This proves a meromorphic natural
boundary for the geometric zeta itself at `abs(z)=1/p`, not only for a
formal derivative. The rational fixed-scheme length zeta changes the counted
object. A zero-free entire prefactor cannot remove the boundary in the same
variable. Bridy's prior additive-map transcendence results are credited;
this package does not claim priority for the general mechanism.

**C385: the entire bounded Lozi set and its clock exclusion.** For every
real `a>4`, an explicit contraction on two-sided bounded sequences both
constructs all binary itineraries and exhausts every bounded geometric
orbit of `F_a(x,y)=(1-a abs(x)-y,x)`. A uniform sign margin avoids the
nonsmooth switching line; parameter continuity is quantitative. Projective
cone contraction closes variable-matrix hyperbolicity, signed reciprocal
periodic multipliers, all primitive periods, repetition and reversal.
The elementary full-shift zeta and the stability-weighted holomorphic germ
are separate objects; the latter has an explicit interior tail but no
asserted trace-class owner or continuation outside that disk. At integer
`a>=5`, each unstable multiplier is a quadratic algebraic unit, excluding
every exact positive rational rescaling of its logarithm to `log(m)` for
an integer `m>1`. The derivative roof defines an instability suspension,
not the physical map-step clock. The sufficient chamber is not called sharp.

**C386: complete rank-one alpha-Szego threshold and exact cascade.** Direct
Fourier convolution gives the full invariant-manifold ODE and conserved
quantities. Off the energy threshold, an explicit positive distance from
the pole boundary gives relative compactness; every real-alpha and
rank-zero boundary is handled separately. On the positive-alpha threshold,
the second-order equation remains valid at the turning point and yields
the exact two-sided sech-squared envelope. The high-Sobolev exponent is
`(s-1/2) kappa`, with critical norm and high-frequency momentum escape
separated from finite-time blowup. At a single fixed positive alpha,
`u0=z` and `u0=sqrt(alpha)+z` have the same native shifted-Hankel determinant
`1-w` but opposite bounded/cascading behavior. This is an exact invariant
blindness result. The antilinear K is not assigned a complex determinant;
the determinant belongs to the positive linear rank-one operator K squared.
Xu owns the classical threshold; the proof makes its rank and turning-point
hypotheses explicit rather than claiming a new general PDE theorem.

**C387: compact Heisenberg nilflow returns and full spectrum.** On the
integer left quotient, the normalized vector field `X+beta Y+gamma Z`
has an exact global flow. Irrational beta gives no closed orbit; rational
`beta=p/q` has the circle-valued invariant
`theta=gamma q+p x-q y+pq/2`. A rational theta of denominator d has least
period dq, and every return and least-period stratum is a counted clean
two-torus family. The return derivative is unipotent with rank-one defect.
A global section and elementary quadratic Weyl argument establish unique
ergodicity of the irrational flow, but not its time-one map. A signed
Weil--Brezin isometry covers the entire Hilbert space, and an explicit chirp
fixes the self-adjoint domain and full spectral decomposition: the torus
eigenblock coexists with a countably infinite Lebesgue multiplicity block.
An explicit lattice-compatible reversor is valid at all gamma. Continuous
spectral subspaces prove noncompactness of the native evolution, resolvent
and auxiliary heat, with infinite extended positive heat trace. Neither a
countable isolated periodic atlas nor an ordinary global determinant follows.

**C388: connected rank-two fixed groups and the integer-lattice correction.**
For the connected principal `1+u+v` action, each finite-index HNF lattice
has its exact fixed compact group from the Smith form of `I+S1+S2`.
The two root-of-unity zero characters give the already-classical resonance
criterion. In the singular case, the number of connected components is the
nonzero singular-value product divided by the covolumes of the two integer
kernels. The three-colour kernel yields the explicit factor `3/N^2`.
At the smallest index-three quotient, the matrix is all ones: its nonzero
eigenvalue product is 3, but its fixed group is one connected two-torus.
This supplies a direct counterexample to an uncorrected finite-lattice
formula in a verified preprint version. On that two-torus all directional
third iterates are the identity, obstructing every rank-one ordinary
cardinality zeta. The intrinsic Mahler entropy and its Dirichlet L-value
are derived with a controlled series tail and attributed to the classical
entropy and Smyth source owners, not labelled a target Euler construction.
