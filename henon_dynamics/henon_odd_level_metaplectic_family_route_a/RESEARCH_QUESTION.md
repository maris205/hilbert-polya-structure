# Research question

For the toral Hénon automorphism generated by
`A=[[3,-1],[1,0]]`, does the frozen level-seven chirp--Fourier quantization
extend, without a phase repair, to every odd modulus?  Can one prove one
uniform antiunitary reversal and a level-dependent interval on which the
quantum adjoint action has not yet aliased to the identity?

Target spectral matching, cross-level projective compatibility, semiclassical
trace asymptotics, and Hilbert--Pólya are outside the active scope.
