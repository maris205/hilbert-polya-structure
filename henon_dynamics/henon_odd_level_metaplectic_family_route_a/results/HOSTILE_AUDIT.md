# Hostile audit

- Replacing odd `N` by an even modulus destroys the inverse-of-two used by
  this literal phase convention; it does not rule out other conventions.
- Reversing the Fourier sign changes the implemented classical matrix.
- Removing the Weyl half-phase leaves a projective phase error.
- The antiunitary identity is exact; no scalar anomaly is silently absorbed.
- `A^n != I mod N` is used only to exclude scalar adjoint-action aliasing.
- The growing window is not a semiclassical trace or orbit-count match.
- Certificates at nine levels are sentinels for an analytic all-odd proof,
  not the source of its universal quantifier.
- Exact key sets are enforced at the evidence top level, every certified
  receipt, the check map, and the scope map.  Renamed or added keys are rejected
  after the content hash is repaired.
- Cross-level projective compatibility and Route B remain disabled.
- All forbidden arithmetic and spectral claim flags remain false.
