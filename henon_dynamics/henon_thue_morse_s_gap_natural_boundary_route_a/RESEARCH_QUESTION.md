# C159 research question

Can the one-pass symbolic interface of C154 be replaced by a genuinely
recurrent symbolic dynamics with a nontrivial all-period orbit theorem and a
new analytic conclusion, without importing any target-facing data?

The hard gate is not met by another finite word table or by another aperiodic
periodic-point vacuum.  It requires all of the following:

1. recurrent interaction in the source system, preferably mixing;
2. nonzero periodic structure at arbitrarily large periods;
3. an exact all-order zeta identity; and
4. a proved analytic statement that is not inferred from a finite cutoff.

The Thue--Morse S-gap renewal shift clears all four.  Its obstruction is also
explicit: a source natural boundary is not a target divisor or a natural
operator construction.
