# C159 claim-driven experiment plan

## Hard gate

- Reject a candidate if its orbit theorem repeats C144's periodic vacuum.
- Require a full recurrence/mixing proof, exact all-order zeta, and analytic
  continuation boundary independent of a finite computation.

## Deterministic evidence

1. Generate the first 256 Thue--Morse bits and the induced code lengths.
2. Enumerate every cyclic binary word through period 18 and test every gap.
3. Recover exact-period points and primitive cycles by Möbius inversion.
4. Rebuild `P`, renewal, zeta, and denominator coefficients through degree 48.
5. Verify the logarithmic-derivative fixed-point identity coefficientwise.
6. Certify the entropy root with rational endpoints and a geometric tail.
7. Record dyadic boundary sentinels without treating them as the proof.

## Independent validation

- A standard-library checker uses a separately written cyclic-word decoder.
- SymPy reconstructs the formal product and derivative identities.
- Replay requires canonical byte equality.
- Repaired-hash and stale-hash mutations must all be rejected.
- Fixed-epoch double PDF builds, embedded fonts, manifest closure, and a
  warning-free log are release gates.

The all-parameter proof, not the degree-48 or period-18 ledger, carries the
claim.
