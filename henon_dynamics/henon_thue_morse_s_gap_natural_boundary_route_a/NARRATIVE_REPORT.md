# C159 narrative report

C154 produced one intrinsic period-three limit orbit, but its interface was
visited only once and wandered.  The first C159 idea repaired recurrence by
adding a periodic clock to a Sturmian system; hard review rejected it because
its periodic vacuum and zeta one were too close to C144.

The replacement turns the Thue--Morse digits into admissible zero gaps.  It
changes the qualitative dynamics: interfaces between ones and zero runs recur
throughout concatenated code words, lengths two and three force mixing, and
periodic points are dense.  The renewal parsing is strong enough to derive the
complete zeta, not just a period table.  The same Thue--Morse source supplies
an infinite product whose dyadic boundary zeros force a natural boundary for
the meromorphic continuation of that zeta.

This is clear source-structural progress but not target-facing success.  The
natural boundary is an obstruction, not a critical-line claim.  No arithmetic
factorization or operator lift is present, so the release remains
`ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY` and Route B remains off.
