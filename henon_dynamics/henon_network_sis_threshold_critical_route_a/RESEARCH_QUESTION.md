# Research question

For the irreducible heterogeneous network SIS equation

\[
\dot x=\beta\operatorname{diag}(1-x)Ax-Dx,
\]

can one close the complete threshold phase portrait, including the equality
case that is often hidden inside a non-strict stability statement?  The frozen
answer must include global extinction or persistence, uniqueness and stability
of the endemic equilibrium, and the normalized critical limit.  It must not
identify an epidemic threshold, Perron vector, or Jacobian with arithmetic
primitive-orbit data or a target spectral operator.

Proof status: **PROVABLE AS STATED** under `A>=0` irreducible, `beta>0`, and
strictly positive diagonal recovery matrix `D`.
