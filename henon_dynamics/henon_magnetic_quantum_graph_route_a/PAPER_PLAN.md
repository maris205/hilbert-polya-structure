# Paper plan

**Working title:** Gauge-Invariant Magnetic Holonomy in Exact Theta-Graph Scattering

**Type:** short exact theory/release paper

**One-sentence contribution:** an exact magnetic theta-graph family owns its gauge torus, antiunitary reversal, oriented orbit phases, and complete Laurent secular determinant.

## Claims–evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| exact unitary magnetic family | rational Kirchhoff matrices and diagonal unitary phases | real `k,alpha` |
| common-shift gauge and antiunitary | matrix identities with `D_c`, `J`, and conjugation | antiunitary changes `alpha` sign |
| complete Laurent determinant | independent `3×3` block determinant and coefficients | source determinant only |
| orientation-sensitive ledger | exact winding monomials and reverse IDs through period eight | full determinant remains inversion-even |
| robust controls | zero, common, `pi`, `pi/2`, wrong Kirchhoff, asymmetric reverse length | no target data |

## Structure

1. State the magnetic family-level advance and strict scope.
2. Freeze the graph, phase convention, gauge quotient, and antiunitary.
3. Derive the complete Laurent determinant.
4. Relate oriented walks to Laurent monomials without cosine collapse.
5. Present exact positive/negative controls and release receipts.
6. State the Route-A boundary.

## Figure/table phase

No standalone plot or illustration is needed.  Exact block matrices, formulas, and a compact ledger count table are more informative.  The 28-file release contract therefore contains no `figures/` directory; this is the explicit Phase-2 outcome.

## Citation plan

The release paper makes no historical or novelty claim and is self-contained, so it contains no unverifiable bibliography.

## Review gates

Round 1 audits phase splitting, gauge algebra, and the antiunitary parameter map.  Round 2 audits Laurent completeness, orientation/full-determinant distinctions, negative controls, and forbidden claims.
