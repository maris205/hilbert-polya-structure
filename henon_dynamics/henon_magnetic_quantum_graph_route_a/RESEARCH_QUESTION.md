# Research question

Can the exact theta-graph scattering model be upgraded to a nontrivial magnetic-flux family that simultaneously has:

- exact common-phase gauge invariance;
- a correctly parameter-changing antiunitary reversal;
- orientation-sensitive primitive-orbit phases;
- a complete exact Laurent secular determinant;
- and negative controls that distinguish full-determinant evenness from individual orientation data?

The smallest source-owned test uses the existing edge lengths `(1,2,3)` and three phases modulo a common shift.  Success must preserve `q_i/q_j` in oriented ledgers, while proving that the full determinant pairs each phase with its inverse.

No external target comparison is part of the question.
