# C138 hostile audit

## Attacks rejected

- Replacing common-phase invariance with mere covariance.
- Dropping the `alpha -> -alpha` parameter change from the antiunitary identity.
- Claiming that an individual oriented orbit phase is even or replacing `q_i/q_j` by a cosine.
- Removing or altering any `rho^0,rho^2,rho^4,rho^6` Laurent coefficient after repairing the payload hash.
- Faking zero-flux recovery, the `pi` determinant change, or the `pi/2` reversal defect.
- Changing a walk digest, primitive count, signed amplitude, winding vector, or reverse identifier.
- Restoring unitarity to the wrong Kirchhoff control or reversal to the asymmetric-length control.
- Enabling Route B, changing the A4 verdict, renaming a false scope flag, or leaving a stale hash.

## Residual boundary

The full determinant is even under `alpha -> -alpha`; this follows from reverse-orbit pairing and does not erase orientation from the primitive ledger.  The A4 coordinate is a finite-dimensional unitary/scattering candidate.  It is not a self-adjoint target-spectrum realization, target divisor match, arithmetic Euler product, root number, automorphy theorem, or Hilbert–Pólya operator.
