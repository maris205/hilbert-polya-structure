# Narrative report

C133 supplied an exact unitary theta-graph scattering system at zero magnetic flux.  C138 turns that point into a genuine family without adding an external phase label by hand: phases live on directed bonds, a common shift is gauge, and two independent flux differences remain.

The family has an exact antiunitary statement, but it is parameter-changing.  Bond reversal plus conjugation maps `alpha` to `-alpha` and the evolution to its inverse.  The `pi/2` control is designed to catch the tempting false statement that the same identity holds at fixed nonzero flux.

The determinant is not presented as a black-box symbolic expansion.  A `3×3` block reduction gives all four even powers of `rho`; each coefficient is a Laurent polynomial in the phase variables.  Common scaling cancels, while inversion pairs reverse orientations.  At zero flux the factorization is exactly the C133 polynomial, and a nontrivial `pi` flux changes the determinant by a visible coefficient.

The orbit ledger keeps what the full determinant hides.  Each directed primitive walk retains a signed Kirchhoff amplitude and the monomial `q^m`; reversing the walk sends `m` to `-m`.  Through period eight, 14,760 rooted walks and 1,905 primitive cycles are exact audit sentinels.  Full-determinant evenness is a paired statement, never a license to erase individual direction phases.

This closes a meaningful A4 unitary/scattering coordinate.  It does not advance A2 or A3 and does not create an arithmetic or Hilbert–Pólya identification.
