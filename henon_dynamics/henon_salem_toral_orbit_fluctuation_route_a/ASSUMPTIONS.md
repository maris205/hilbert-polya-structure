# Assumptions and conventions

All integer parameters a>=1 except the separately classified a=2. The phase space is the full four-torus, the original map is its integral companion automorphism, and the clock is integer iteration.

Clock: One original toral iterate; no inserted logarithmic roof.
Normalization: Normalized Haar probability, oriented cycles without reversal quotient, absolute fixed counts and separately signed Lefschetz indices.
Determinant: Artin-Mazur zeta (1-z)^4 Q_a(z)/P_a(z)^2; not a trace-class Koopman determinant.

Excluded target data: No intrinsic log-prime original clock or target divisor relation.
All-parameter assertions are carried by the full proof, not the finite cutoff.
