## Claim boundary

The explicit transform, coupling, stationary statistics, and finite-filtration proof are rederived here.  No priority is claimed.  The references do not support—and this package does not make—a full \(L^2\) spectral claim, a target Euler product, arithmetic local data, a functional equation, a zero match, or a Hilbert–Pólya operator.
