# HCS-C335 research question

For the positive affine PDMP
\[
dX_t=-\gamma X_t\,dt+dJ_t,
\]
where \(J\) is a rate-\(\kappa\) compound-Poisson process with independent exponential marks of rate \(\beta\), can one close the all-time transition semigroup, invariant law and correlations, and the generator spectrum on every finite polynomial filtration?

The answer proved here is yes for \(\gamma,\kappa,\beta>0\).  The deliberately sharp boundary is that the filtration spectra are not promoted to a claim about the full \(L^2\) spectrum.
