# Narrative report

The main advance is an all-time exact closure for a genuinely jump-driven affine process.  One formula simultaneously produces the Markov semigroup, stationary Gamma law, convergence, and correlations.  The generator then closes every finite polynomial degree, where a triangular matrix gives an exact simple spectrum.

The important restraint is spectral: finite polynomial restrictions do not determine the full \(L^2\) spectrum.  Route A is therefore rejected.  There is no prime owner, no isolated periodic ledger, no orbit determinant, no target analytic bridge, and no natural Hilbert–Pólya lift.
