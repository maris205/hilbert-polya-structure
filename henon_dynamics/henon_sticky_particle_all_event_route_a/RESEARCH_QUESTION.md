# Research question

For arbitrary finite one-dimensional positive-mass data, can one construct
and prove unique the entire forward perfectly sticky trajectory—including
initial coincidences, multi-cluster contacts, and several simultaneous
contact locations—and identify it with both weighted isotonic regression and
a lower convex hull, while closing all conservation, exact energy loss, and
the pressureless-Euler weak formulation?

The frozen answer is **yes**, after the explicit convention that initially
coincident labels are premerged by mass and momentum.  Zero masses and
backward uniqueness are excluded.  The all-data proof is analytic; finite
receipts only audit branches and constants.
