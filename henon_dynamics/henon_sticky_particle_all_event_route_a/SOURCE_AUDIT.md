# Source and claim audit

## Verified direct owners

- Luca Natile and Giuseppe Savaré, *A Wasserstein approach to the
  one-dimensional sticky particle system*, arXiv:0902.4373.  The arXiv record
  verifies title/authors and owns the monotone-cone projection and sticky
  semigroup context: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.4373>.
- Yann Brenier, Wilfrid Gangbo, Giuseppe Savaré, and Michael Westdickenberg,
  *Sticky particle dynamics with interactions*, arXiv:1201.2350.  The arXiv
  record verifies title/authors and owns Lagrangian pressureless-flow and
  discrete sticky approximation context:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2350>.
- Ryan Hynd, *Lagrangian Coordinates for the Sticky Particle System*, SIAM
  Journal on Mathematical Analysis 51 (2019), 3769–3795,
  DOI 10.1137/19M1241775.  The publisher DOI verifies the bibliographic record
  and weak pressureless-Euler ownership:
  <https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1241775>.

## Claim boundary

The paper does not claim literature-level originality for sticky particles,
isotonic projection, or the pressureless-Euler connection.  Its claim is the
workspace-local convention, proof, evidence, and release closure.  The finite
scenarios are regression evidence only.  No target arithmetic, Euler factor,
root number, automorphy, target counting law, functional equation, zero
correspondence, or Hilbert–Pólya operator is inferred.
