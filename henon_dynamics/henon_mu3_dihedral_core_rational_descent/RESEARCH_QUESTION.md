# HCS-C53 research question

## Primary question

Can the C51 moment sources over \(K=\mathbf Q(\rho)\) be descended without
changing their chronological closing edge, and does that descent convert
the fourth-row half-power into an ordinary rational local factor while
retaining the C52 rank-10 dihedral core?

## Resolved questions

1. **Equation descent.** Yes, for every \(n\ge2\): the semilinear reversal
   \(M_n\) and fixed basis \(B_n\) give explicit rational equations.
2. **Motivic scope.** For the certified smooth rows \(n=2,3,4\), the even
   and odd packets descend to \(\mathbf Q\) with total ranks \(15,63,255\).
3. **Fourth-row exponent.** At every good split rational prime, the two
   equal \(K\)-place factors turn exponent \(1/2\) into exponent one on a
   single rank-255 rational factor.
4. **Core descent.** The order-24 Reynolds graph sum is Galois-stable and
   descends to a rank-10 rational Chow summand; its complement has rank 158.

## Questions deliberately left open

- Is the rank-10 Frobenius polynomial irreducible at one good prime?
- Can a full rank-10 polynomial be computed efficiently without building a
  finite trace-table paper?
- Can the split-local repair be extended through inert and bad places to a
  meaningful global analytic statement?
- Does the rank-10 motive admit a geometric Calabi--Yau/Prym realization?

These are future-route questions. They are not prerequisites for the C53
descent theorem.
