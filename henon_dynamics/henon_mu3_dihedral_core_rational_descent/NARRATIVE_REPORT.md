# HCS-C53 narrative report

C51 exposed a parity obstruction: the fourth moment row naturally carried
a half-power over \(K=\mathbf Q(\rho)\), and no honest half-rank \(K\)-system
could realize it. C52 then isolated an unexpectedly small, rank-10 middle
core inside the order-24 dihedral invariants, but it still lived in the
quadratic coefficient-field presentation.

C53 changes the arithmetic category explicitly. A semilinear reversal of
the chronological cycle, decorated by alternating cubic-root phases, is a
Weil descent datum for every row. Solving its fixed vectors produces a
closed rational basis and rational equations. Nothing is averaged away: the
weighted closing edge is preserved through the coordinate change.

For the certified smooth rows, the entire moment packet is therefore a
rational motive. At a split rational prime, its two base-changed \(K\)-place
factors coincide. The fourth-row half-power of their product is exactly one
rational local factor of rank 255. This is the quadratic counterpacket that
C51 allowed but did not construct. The same calculation explains why the
third row remains fractional: its exponent becomes \(4/3\), not an integer.

The C52 core survives the descent for a subtler reason. Galois conjugation
does not fix the 24 automorphisms individually; it permutes them through a
twisted dihedral form. Their Reynolds graph sum is fixed, and rational
restriction/corestriction descends the projector. Thus the fourth rational
packet decomposes by ranks \(87+10+158\), with a rank-10 summand of
Calabi--Yau-threefold Hodge type after one Tate twist.

The boundary is as important as the positive theorem. At inert primes,
Frobenius eigenvalues are squared rather than duplicated, so the split
square identity disappears. C53 therefore proves no global square root,
functional equation, or automorphy. It also does not manufacture a
Calabi--Yau threefold or a flat Prym model. The clean gain is algebraic and
theorem-sized: an all-row rational equation family, a certified rational
packet in rows two through four, exact fourth-row split-local exponent
clearing, and a rational rank-10 dihedral core.
