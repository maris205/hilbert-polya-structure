# HCS-C53 paper plan

## Working title

**Rational descent and the dihedral core of the fourth Hénon moment packet**

The title deliberately avoids claiming an all-\(n\) compatible-system
theorem: all \(n\) applies only to the explicit equation descent.

## Section plan

1. **Introduction and source chronology.** State the C51 parity obstruction,
   the C52 order-24 rank-10 core, and the rational-counterpacket question.
2. **The semilinear reversal.** Prove the symbolic identities for \(M_n\).
3. **An explicit rational form in every row.** Construct \(B_n\), prove the
   determinant formula, and derive \(C_{n,0},Q_{n,0}\).
4. **Certified rational moment packets.** Restrict smooth/motive statements
   to \(n=2,3,4\) and prove the rank formulas.
5. **The twisted dihedral group scheme.** Show Galois transport of all 24
   elements and descend the Reynolds cycle by transfer.
6. **The rank-10 rational core.** Descend \(\pi_5e_G\), prove the
   \(10+158\) split, and state the Hodge realization.
7. **Compatible local polynomials.** Give the correspondence-trace argument
   and the separate integrality step.
8. **Quadratic Artin formalism.** Prove split exponent \(4/n\), exact
   \(n=4\) clearing, and the inert polynomial identity.
9. **Limits and future routes.** Record the irreducibility criterion with
   its realization/phantom scope and the flattened-quadric future gate.

## Central theorem hierarchy

- Theorem A: explicit rational equations for every \(n\ge2\).
- Theorem B: rational packets for certified smooth \(n=2,3,4\).
- Theorem C: exact fourth-row split-local half-power repair.
- Theorem D: rational rank-10/158 middle decomposition at \(n=4\).

## Excluded paper shapes

- no finite Frobenius trace table;
- no Fano-plane classification theorem;
- no direct Prym theorem without an independent flatness theorem;
- no global analytic conclusion from split-prime algebra.
