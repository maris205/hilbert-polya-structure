# Research question

Can a genuinely nonlinear threshold cellular automaton be closed at all
cyclic lengths rather than treated through a short periodic census?

We freeze the labelled cycle $X_n=\{0,1\}^n$ and the one-tick majority map
$F_n$ (elementary rule 232).  The question has four source-local parts:

1. derive an exact evolution law for domain walls;
2. classify every periodic state, including the even-length alternating
   boundary;
3. prove a sharp finite-time bound to the attractor set;
4. count fixed states and transient-depth populations by independent transfer
   matrices.

The arithmetic and target-spectrum gates are intentionally outside scope.
The finite receipts are checks of an all-$n$ theorem, not a replacement for
it.
