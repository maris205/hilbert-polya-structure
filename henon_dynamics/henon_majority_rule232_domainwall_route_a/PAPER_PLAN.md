# Paper plan

**Title:** Domain-wall erosion and the complete periodic atlas of cyclic
majority rule 232

1. **Introduction.** Explain why a nonlinear threshold CA gives a distinct
   Route-A owner from additive and conservative traffic rules.
2. **Frozen dynamics.** Define $F_n$, cyclic labels, primitive temporal
   period, and the degenerate small-$n$ faces.
3. **Wall reduction.** Prove the exact Boolean wall identity and block-erosion
   lemma.
4. **Attractor theorem.** Classify fixed words and the unique alternating
   two-cycle; prove the sharp transient bound and no-longer-periodic result.
5. **Exact enumeration.** Construct $M$, derive
   $\operatorname{tr}(M^n)=L_n+2\cos(n\pi/3)$, and construct the parity-twisted
   $B_m$ matrices for transient-depth counts.
6. **Independent evidence.** Describe exhaustive ($n\le14$) receipts,
   transfer rows ($n\le64$), symbolic checks, replay, and mutation audit.
7. **Route-A boundary.** State the honest rejected tuple and all nonclaims.
