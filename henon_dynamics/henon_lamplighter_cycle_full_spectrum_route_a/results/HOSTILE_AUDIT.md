# Hostile audit

All 59 attacks were rejected. The suite contains more than ten repaired
self-hash semantic attacks and separately targets identifiers, source/date,
scope, evaluator authority and SHA, raw/semantic YAML bindings, the switch
convention, theorem contract, collisions, nonclaims, source tokens, Route-A
tuple, Route B, forbidden flags, cycle/block/direct rows and enumeration
digests. Omission, duplication and nested-extra-key attacks exercise every
coordinate ledger.

Strict-parser attacks cover duplicate and nonfinite JSON, non-object JSON,
duplicate YAML, anchors, aliases, merges, non-string keys, implicit timestamps,
unknown fields, type mutation and non-object YAML. Repaired YAML-binding
attacks and an unrepaired stale-payload-hash control were also killed.
