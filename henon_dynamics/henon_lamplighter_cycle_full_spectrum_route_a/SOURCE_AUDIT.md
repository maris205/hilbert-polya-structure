# Source audit

## Verified lineage

- Franz Lehner, Markus Neuhauser and Wolfgang Woess, *On the spectrum of
  lamplighter groups and percolation clusters*, **Mathematische Annalen** 342
  (2008), 69--89. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-008-0222-7>; author preprint:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.3135>.  This is the primary source for the
  switch--walk--switch/percolation spectral correspondence.
- Franz Lehner, *On the eigenspaces of lamplighter random walks and
  percolation clusters on graphs*, **Proceedings of the American Mathematical
  Society** 137 (2009), 2631--2637. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-09-09869-4>; author preprint:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0867>.  This supplies the graph-level eigenspace
  lineage.

Metadata were checked against the authors' institutional publication pages,
the DOI records, and the arXiv records on 2026-09-03.

## Ownership boundary

This package is a source-local, finite-cycle reconstruction.  It proves the
specialized block formula and all finite-cycle consequences directly and does
not claim priority for the general lamplighter/percolation correspondence.
The exact gap, multiplicity and \(n=1,2\) conventions are owned here only as
the consequences of the frozen finite model.

The sources contain no authorization to reinterpret these characteristic
polynomials as target Euler factors or their zeros as target arithmetic zeros.
No such claim is made.
