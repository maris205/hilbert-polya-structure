# Research question

For the fair rerandomizing switch--walk--switch chain on
\(\mathbb Z_2^{C_n}\times C_n\), can one determine the complete finite-volume
spectrum, all multiplicities and eigenfunctions, and the sharp \(L^2\) decay
for every \(n\), including the one- and two-cycle conventions?

The answer proved here is yes.  The key question is operator-theoretic rather
than asymptotic: identify every Fourier block exactly and then diagonalize the
path components created by deleting the lamp-character support.

The research boundary is equally explicit.  We do not replace fair
rerandomization by deterministic toggling, pass to an infinite wreath product,
assert a percolation phase transition, or infer target arithmetic information
from the finite characteristic polynomial.
