# Hostile audit

Twenty content mutations are applied one at a time and the attacker then recomputes the evidence payload hash.
Targets include source/epoch/scope/evaluator, Route-A tuple and Route-B gate, forbidden claims, kernel phase,
shell probability, second moment, eigenvector and energy, counts, compactness, resolvent threshold, trace class,
gauge/eigen contracts, and minimal period.  The independent checker rejects all 20 repaired-hash artifacts.

Residual boundary: high-precision cells are regression sentinels, not a numerical proof of completeness,
pure-point spectrum, minimal return, noncompactness, or Schatten membership.  Those statements have explicit
analytic proofs in the theorem package and paper.
