# Research question

For the full-line Wannier–Stark lattice with nonzero field, can one give a single all-parameter theorem that
simultaneously identifies its unitary normal form, spectrum, eigenbasis, exact dynamics, recurrence clock,
delta-source transport law, and sharp operator-ideal boundaries—and separate the singular zero-field owner?

Yes.  The theorem is exact for all `F != 0`, `J in R`, includes the `J=0` face, and treats `F=0` only as a
changed-owner boundary.  It does not infer an infinite-dimensional statement from a finite truncation.
