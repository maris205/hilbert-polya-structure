# Narrative report

The decisive step is a global unitary normal form, not a truncation.  Fourier transformation turns the linear
potential into circle momentum and hopping into a cosine.  A periodic phase gauge removes that cosine exactly,
so the complete spectral and dynamical closure follows from the integer Fourier basis.  This simultaneously
explains the equally spaced ladder, exact recurrence, bounded Bloch oscillation, and compact resolvent.

The operator-ideal boundary is intentionally separated: compact resolvent does not imply compact time evolution.
Every `U(t)` is unitary on an infinite-dimensional space and hence noncompact, while the resolvent singular
values decay harmonically and lie in `S_p` precisely for `p>1`.  The zero-field limit is singular: the discrete
ladder becomes the continuous free-lattice band, so it is documented as a changed owner rather than inserted
into the nonzero-field theorem.
