\begin{abstract}
We regularize the primitive direction families of the unit square billiard by
a positive squared-length heat transform.  M\"obius inversion gives an exact
theta factorization, and an elementary quarter-disk count plus Stieltjes
integration gives its small-time law.  Coincident lengths retain direction
multiplicity.  The construction is not a wave trace, isolated-orbit
determinant, or target identity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Transform and factorization}
For ordered positive coprime pairs, coordinate swap retained and axes
excluded, set
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm prim}(t)=\sum_{\substack{m,n\geq1\\(m,n)=1}}
 e^{-4t(m^2+n^2)},\qquad t>0.                    \tag{1}
\end{equation}
The exponent is $-tL_{m,n}^2$ for square-billiard length
$L_{m,n}=2\sqrt{m^2+n^2}$.  With
$\theta_+(u)=\sum_{k\geq1}e^{-uk^2}$, the coprimality identity gives
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm prim}(t)=\sum_{d\geq1}\mu(d)\theta_+(4td^2)^2.      \tag{2}
\end{equation}
The interchange is absolute because monotonicity gives
\[
\theta_+(4td^2)\leq\int_0^\infty e^{-4td^2x^2}\,dx
=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4d\sqrt t},
\]
so the absolute transformed sum is bounded by
$\frac{\pi}{16t}\sum_{d\geq1}d^{-2}$.  Each ordered primitive direction
appears once, so different pairs of the same length add their multiplicities.

\section{Primitive density and small time}
For real $R\geq0$, let $Q(R)$ count ordered positive lattice points in the
quarter disk and let $N(R)$ count the coprime ones.  Unit-square comparison
gives
\[
Q(R)=\frac{\pi R^2}{4}+O(R+1).
\]
M\"obius inversion then yields
\begin{equation}
N(R)=\sum_{d\leq R/\sqrt2}\mu(d)Q(R/d)
    =\frac{3R^2}{2\pi}+O(R\log R).               \tag{3}
\end{equation}
Indeed, absolute Dirichlet convolution and the Basel sum give
$\sum_{d\geq1}\mu(d)d^{-2}=1/\zeta(2)=6/\pi^2$; its omitted tail is
$O(R^{-1})$, while the boundary errors sum to
$O(R\sum_{d\leq R}d^{-1})$.
Stieltjes integration by parts gives
\[
H_{\rm prim}(t)=8t\int_0^\infty r e^{-4tr^2}N(r)\,dr.
\]
Substitution of (3) gives
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm prim}(t)=\frac{3}{8\pi t}
 +O\!\left(t^{-1/2}\log(1/t)\right),\qquad t\downarrow0.  \tag{4}
\end{equation}
The remainder follows directly from
$t\int_0^\infty r^2\log(2+r)e^{-4tr^2}\,dr$ after the change
$u=\sqrt t\,r$.

\section{Exact validation and boundary}
If $b_s$ counts every ordered positive representation $s=a^2+b^2$ and
$c_s$ the primitive ones, (2) is coefficientwise
$c_s=\sum_{d^2\mid s}\mu(d)b_{s/d^2}$.  Producer and independent checker
agree for every $s\leq20000$ and on Euclidean radius counts through $200$;
SymPy independently verifies initial coefficients and the leading integral.

This is a direction-label heat transform.  It is not a clean wave trace, a
Gutzwiller or isolated-orbit determinant, or the spectral heat trace of the
Dirichlet Laplacian.
