# C152 research question

Can the divergent raw census of clean square-billiard direction families be
replaced by an intrinsic positive transform with an exact factorization and a
controlled small-time law, while preserving collision multiplicities and
without pretending that the result is an isolated-orbit or spectral trace?

C152 answers yes for the squared-length Gaussian transform.  Möbius inversion
isolates primitive directions, a quarter-disk count supplies the leading
density, and Stieltjes integration transfers it to the heat asymptotic.  The
construction is strictly source-side and leaves the isolated-stability and
target-facing Route-A failures unchanged.
