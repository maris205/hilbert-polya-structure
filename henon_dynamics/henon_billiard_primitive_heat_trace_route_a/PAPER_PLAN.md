# C152 paper plan

Title: **A primitive-direction heat transform for square-billiard families**.

Sections: source convention and absolute convergence; exact Möbius/theta
factorization; quarter-disk primitive count; Stieltjes small-time law; exact
coefficient validation; spectral and Route-A boundary.  The proof is
self-contained, so no external citation or generated figure is needed.  A
compact exact count table replaces a plot and keeps multiplicity conventions
auditable.
