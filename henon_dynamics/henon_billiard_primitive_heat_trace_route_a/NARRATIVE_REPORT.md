# C152 narrative report

## Outcome

C152 advances the square-billiard branch from a divergent family census to a
convergent all-direction transform with an exact source-internal combinatorial
factorization and a proved small-time scale.

## Progress

Möbius inversion yields an absolutely convergent theta factorization for every
positive time.  The ordered positive convention retains length collisions.
An elementary quarter-disk estimate gives primitive density `6/pi^2`; after
Stieltjes integration the heat transform has leading term `3/(8 pi t)` with a
controlled logarithmic remainder.

## Evidence

Every formal-series coefficient through squared direction size 20,000 is
reconstructed independently.  Exact radius counts through 200 agree with
scaled Euclidean Möbius inversion.  The first fourfold ordered coefficient is
at square 65, consistent with the two inequivalent direction pairs and their
coordinate swaps.

## Boundary repair

The result is deliberately named a direction heat transform.  It is neither a
wave trace nor the heat trace of the Dirichlet Laplacian, and it does not cure
the clean-family isolated-stability obstruction.  The natural square
half-wave remains only an A4 quantization boundary; Route B stays disabled.
