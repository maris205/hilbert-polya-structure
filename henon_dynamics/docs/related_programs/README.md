## Research Philosophy

The exploration follows the chain:

```

Dynamical System
|
v
Periodic Orbits / Symbolic Dynamics
|
v
Weighted Dynamical Zeta Function
|
v
Transfer Operator / Spectrum
|
v
Possible Spectral Interpretation

```

A numerical observation alone is not considered evidence for a Hilbert–Pólya
construction. Each project is evaluated using the Route-A and Route-B criteria.

## 5. henon_frobenius_scheme_obstruction

### Topic

Fixed-period periodic-point schemes for the integral area-preserving Hénon
family, with Frobenius degree and chronological iterate kept as independent
clocks.

### Main results

- the universal cyclic fixed scheme over \(\mathbb Z[A,A^{-1}]\) is finite
  flat of rank \(2^n\);
- every finite fixed-\(n\) local zeta is a nilpotent-blind finite-permutation
  determinant, so recurrence and rationality in Frobenius degree are
  universal rather than distinctive Route-A evidence;
- ordinary rectangular traces do not determine the joint action even among
  matched reversible finite controls;
- the \(a=6,n=5\) \(S_6\) sextic is an exact collision with
  Endler--Gallas (2006).

### Relation to HP search

This is a scoped obstruction, not a claim that global Artin zeta functions
have no nontrivial zeros.  It closes the fixed-\(n\) local-rationality lane and
redirects breadth-first work toward source-locked parameter-varying
exact-period quotient curves or genuinely new dihedral-compatible Galois
theorems.

## 6. fibonacci_trace_map_clock_obstruction

### Topic

Chronology-preserving Fibonacci Schrödinger cocycles, trace-map symbolic
dynamics, and the mismatch between renormalization time and physical word
length.

### Main results

- finite spectral-section hits are not three-coordinate trace-map returns;
- Casdagli's source-faithful ten-state marked-band series differs from the
  closed Artin--Mazur zeta, with a separately verified decorated unweighted
  six-state quotient;
- arbitrary finite state dimension \(N_k\) cannot evade the \(O(k)\)
  energy-degree bound when local polynomial degree is uniformly bounded,
  whereas \(\deg_E d_k=F_{k+2}\);
- at two exact finite-approximant section energies,
  \(|d_k(E_*)|^{1/k}\to\infty\), so literal coefficient and logarithmic-trace
  series have radius zero and cannot be analytic germs at the orbit-variable
  origin.

### Relation to HP search

This is a Route-A negative theorem package and source-faithful symbolic
control, not a Hilbert--Pólya construction.  It closes literal short-clock
coefficient models while retaining physical-time determinants,
level-dependent/nonanalytic models, and explicitly defined indirect divisor
maps as distinct future classes.

## 7. s_integer_solenoid_chronology_zeta

### Topic

An autonomous full-shift skew product of noncommuting automorphisms of the
dyadic two-solenoid, with chronological word products and intrinsic
\(2\)-local fixed-point weights.

### Main results

- the fibre fixed count is the odd part of
  \(8^n-\operatorname{tr}M_w+1\);
- the words with a nontrivial dyadic correction are exactly the cyclic
  golden-mean language;
- two primitive period-five words with the same letter counts have rational
  versus natural-boundary return zetas;
- the full zeta nevertheless crosses its first convergence circle, and
  \((1-16z)Z_2(z)\) is holomorphic and zero-free for
  \(|z|<(8\varphi)^{-1}\);
- the natural Koopman lift contains a continuous Bernoulli spectral component.

### Relation to HP search

This is an exact arithmetic-dynamics result and a Route-A rejection, not a
Hilbert--Pólya construction.  It shows both why chronological order must be
preserved and why orbit-level natural boundaries cannot be naively promoted
to the full dynamical zeta.

## 8. nonabelian_voltage_zeta_obstruction

### Topic

Chronology-preserving nonabelian voltage covers, representation-resolved
Artin--Ihara factors, finite-memory roof determinants, and conductor-new
Heisenberg congruence sectors.

### Main results

- the canonical regular-minus-trivial local factor depends on holonomy only
  through element order;
- a primitive non-dihedral \(H(\mathbb F_7)\) word pair has identical full
  cyclic bigram counts but distinct same-order central holonomies;
- every fixed finite-state, finite-memory, finite-roof twisted determinant
  has only \(O(T)\) zeros in a disk or fixed strip;
- a conductor-new irreducible Schrödinger sector in
  \(H(\mathbb Z/3^m\mathbb Z)\) has top eigenvalue tending to four, so the
  frozen uniform Ramanujan/new-sector-gap rescue fails.

### Relation to HP search

This is a scoped Route-A obstruction package, not a new Artin factorization,
Harper operator, or Hilbert--Pólya construction. It leaves open nuclear
infinite-rank determinants, separately renormalized tower limits, and
nonamenable property-\(\tau\) towers.

## 9. s_arithmetic_height_clock_obstruction

### Topic

A compact quaternionic \(S\)-arithmetic quotient with a real geodesic clock,
a Bruhat--Tits tree clock, periodic flat tori, and a canonical Weil-height
scalarization.

### Main results

- an explicit projective \(S\)-unit centralizer has signed clock lattice
  \((m,n)\mapsto(mA+nC,n)\) with nonzero determinant;
- primitive directions approach the real Weyl wall while escaping in tree
  length, forcing failure of the real-only Euler-product specialization;
- the absolute Weil height gives the exact proper roof
  \(H=\ell_\infty+\log13\,\ell_{13}\), with a quadratic primitive-direction
  count on the flat;
- the geometric periodic objects are non-isolated flat families requiring
  centralizer/regulator weights;
- direct bounded self-adjoint Hecke perturbations retain the compact-surface
  \(T^2\) Weyl law.

### Relation to HP search

This is an explicit arithmetic-clock calculation and scoped Route-A
rejection, not a new general higher-rank zeta or Hilbert--Pólya operator. It leaves open
proper regulator-weighted Lefschetz zetas, conductor-growing sectors,
scattering and a separately derived unbounded Weil-height operator.

# Evaluation

All projects should eventually be evaluated by:

- Route-A evaluator:
  - periodic orbit structure
  - weighted zeta construction
  - spectral consistency

- Route-B evaluator:
  - operator realization
  - self-adjointness possibility
  - trace formula compatibility

A project may fail one route and still provide useful information.
Negative results are preserved as part of the exploration record.
