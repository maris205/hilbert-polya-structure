# Methodology blueprint

## Frozen object

Use

\[
H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q),\qquad P_6(q)=2q^3-q,
\]

the standard adelic additive character, self-dual Haar measure, and the
unitary

\[
\mathcal U_H=\mathcal F_{\mathbb A}\mathcal M_{P_6}.
\]

Use the full nonzero-rational Poisson map \(E_\times\), not the
positive-integer real half-model; the cubic chirp does not preserve the even
sector required by the latter shorthand.

The arithmetic clock is not fitted: it is the scaling-site clock
\(\ell(C_p)=\log p\).

## Phase 1: mother-route lock

1. Prove the local spherical vectors are fixed.
2. Prove \(\Theta\mathcal U_H=\Theta\).
3. Pull back the double-zero Poisson domain and identify its scaling range.
4. Retain the simpler-parent control \(P_6=0\).

This phase is complete.

## Phase 2: kill the wrong determinant

1. For a \(p\)-dimensional unitary, derive all local zero strings of
   \(\det(I-p^{1/2-s}U_p)\).
2. Prove they accumulate at \(s=1/2\).
3. For the actual \(p\)-adic chirp, prove the exact ball-integral recurrence
   \(S_{p,m}=p^2S_{p,m-1}\).
4. Use the normalized ball tower to prove
   \(\mathcal M_{P_6}-I\) is noncompact.

This phase is complete. It prevents a false Fredholm construction from
consuming further work.

## Phase 3: scaling-covariance obstruction

1. Work on the fixed input space \(\mathcal S_0\), not the transported
   Hénon-adapted space.
2. Quotient both ranges by the common image of
   \[
   W=\ker(ev_0)\cap\ker\Lambda_0\cap\ker\Lambda_{-P}.
   \]
3. Prove the static algebraic quotient bound two.
4. Conjugate the chirp by dilation and derive
   \(P_a(x)=2a^3x^3-ax\).
5. Prove the boundary orbit is infinite-dimensional before applying the
   Poisson map.

This phase is complete and refutes a direct dynamic two-channel inference.

## Phase 4: Poisson anomaly or crossed product

1. Use the exact Poisson boundary identity
   \[
   E_\times(\widehat g)=IE_\times(g)
   +|x|^{-1/2}g(0)-|x|^{1/2}\widehat g(0)
   \]
   to isolate the common outgoing distributional defect mode and determine
   whether it belongs to the chosen scaling Hilbert completion.
2. Construct an actual Hilbert bundle/cocycle over the scaling site and prove
   its chronological holonomy and repetition law.
3. Determine the kernel/closure of the scaled boundary orbit after the
   Poisson map.
4. If it remains infinite, build a canonical crossed-product trace and test
   determinant class.
5. Define \(\Delta_H(s)\) only after the same object retains the clock
   \(\log p\).

## Phase 5: one-shot Route-A gates

The next computation must test all of the following at once:

- meromorphic continuation and exact domain;
- conjugation symmetry;
- \(\Delta_H(s)\Delta_H(1-s)=1\);
- absence or presence of extra zeros and poles;
- zero-free finite-order representation \(e^{g_H(s)}\);
- whether the same quotient retains the scaling clock \(\log p\);
- whether the induced boundary form has a positivity interpretation.

## Stop/go protocol

- Coboundary/trivial anomaly: record a rigidity theorem and pivot.
- Extra divisor: record an obstruction and pivot.
- Zero-free reciprocal nonconstant factor: promote the same object to a
  full analytic Route-A audit.

The project will not return to small-prime scanning, finite-zero fitting, or
independent local determinant products.
