# Paper route

Working title:

**An Adelic Hénon Kick and the Scaling-Covariance Obstruction at the Riemann
Poisson Boundary**

The paper should be written only after the scaling-covariant anomaly gate is
decided. Its intended theorem chain is:

1. adelic quantization and theta stabilization;
2. raw local-product and same-space noncompactness obstructions;
3. the static rank-two range-pair theorem and infinite scaling-orbit
   obstruction;
4. an exact Poisson-renormalized/crossed-product construction or no-go;
5. a Route-A verdict from the factor's divisor and reciprocal symmetry.

If the factor is trivial or has extra zeros, the paper becomes a rigidity or
obstruction paper. If it is nonconstant and zero-free, it becomes the first
analytic Route-A candidate in this Hénon program.
