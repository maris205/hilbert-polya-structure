# Research question

## Bold hypothesis

The adelic quantization of the area-preserving Hénon map may define a
nontrivial anomaly of the Poisson/Tate scaling system after a canonical
scaling-covariant renormalization. The completed determinant should have the
form

\[
D_H(s)=\xi(s)\Delta_H(s),
\]

where the Hénon increment is a crossed-product or renormalized-Poisson
determinant.

The Route-A promotion conjecture is

\[
\Delta_H(s)\Delta_H(1-s)=1,
\qquad
\Delta_H(s)=e^{g_H(s)}
\]

for an entire \(g_H\). This would preserve the full Riemann divisor while
placing the nonlinear Hénon quantization, the prime clock, the functional
equation, and the determinant in one object.

## What is already proved

- The integral H6 map has a canonical restricted adelic unitary
  \(\mathcal U_H=\mathcal F_{\mathbb A}\mathcal M_{2q^3-q}\).
- It fixes the rational theta distribution and every unramified finite
  spherical vacuum.
- On an adapted test space it presents the standard Tate/Connes scaling
  range exactly using the full adelic \(\mathbb Q^\times\) Poisson map; this
  is a transport theorem, not Hénon essentiality.
- The raw product of finite-field Hénon unitary determinants has an interior
  zero accumulation point and is therefore stopped.
- The direct local difference \(\mathcal M_{P_6}-I\) is noncompact.
- For each fixed phase, the two algebraic ranges differ by at most one
  direction on each side; any common closed Hilbert realization has
  projection difference of rank at most two.
- Scaling moves the cubic boundary functional through an
  infinite-dimensional family, so the static rank bound does not give a
  finite-channel dynamical scattering matrix.
- Exact Poisson summation sends the failure of inversion at every fixed scale
  into the same outgoing asymptotic mode \(|x|^{1/2}\); only its coefficient
  functional varies. Membership of this boundary mode in the scaling Hilbert
  completion is open, so this is a concrete compression mechanism, not yet a
  bounded finite-rank operator or determinant.

## Decisive next question

Construct a genuine scaling-site Hénon bundle/cocycle and decide whether the
Poisson map or a canonical crossed-product quotient turns the family

\[
\{\Lambda_{-P_a}:a>0\},
\qquad P_a(x)=2a^3x^3-ax,
\]

into a determinant-class anomaly. There are three meaningful outcomes:

1. the anomaly is a removable coboundary: the route closes by rigidity;
2. a zero-free reciprocal determinant exists: promote toward a genuine
   Route-A analytic candidate;
3. no determinant-class quotient exists, or it has an extra divisor: record
   an obstruction and pivot.

No further low-period scans are needed before this gate is decided.
