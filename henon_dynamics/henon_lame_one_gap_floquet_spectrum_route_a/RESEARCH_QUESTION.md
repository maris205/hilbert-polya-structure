# HCS-C340 research question

For the real one-gap Lame Hamiltonian

\[
H_m=-\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+2m\,\operatorname{sn}^2(x\mid m),
\qquad 0<m<1,
\]

can one prove the complete real-line spectrum, spectral type, precise open-gap
count, band-edge modes, commuting operator, spectral curve, and both endpoint
degenerations without replacing the infinite Floquet argument by a finite
coefficient calculation?

The question is source-local and contains no target arithmetic or Route B.
