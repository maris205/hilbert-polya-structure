- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Chapter 29, *Lame

- P. Kuchment, *An overview of periodic elliptic operators*, *Bulletin of the
  American Mathematical Society* 53 (2016), 343--414, DOI
  `10.1090/bull/1528`: <https://doi.org/10.1090/bull/1528>.  Its
  one-dimensional Hill-operator discussion and Floquet-transform/direct-
  integral sections supply the general theorem that a real periodic
  one-dimensional Schrödinger operator decomposes into quasi-periodic fibers
  and has purely absolutely continuous band spectrum.  It is not used to
  source the Lam\'e-specific cubic curve.

## Claim boundary

The package reconstructs the degree-one theorem in a fixed
(m=k^2\) convention and proves spectral completeness from a commuting
operator, its algebraic relation, and the cited general Floquet theory.  It
makes no priority
claim.  The rational-modulus ledger is an implementation receipt only and
cannot establish that every higher gap is closed.  The finite-gap curve is
not a target Euler product or target determinant.  No target local data,
root number, automorphy, functional equation, divisor, zero match, or
Hilbert--Polya operator is claimed.
