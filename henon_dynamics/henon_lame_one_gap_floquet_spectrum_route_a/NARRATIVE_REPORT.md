# HCS-C340 narrative report

This package takes a smooth elliptic Hill operator and proves its entire
real-line spectrum rather than reporting a handful of special eigenfunctions.
The band-edge modes determine the cubic curve, but completeness comes from a
third-order skew commuting operator with the explicit fiber domain
\(D(A_\theta)=H^3_\theta([0,2K])\): quasi-periodic endpoint phases cancel
in its boundary form, and its polynomial relation excludes the
wrong sign chambers, while simultaneous Bloch eigenvectors and their
Wronskian put every allowed energy back on the unit Floquet circle.  The
result is exactly two spectral bands and one finite open gap.  The self-adjoint
Hamiltonian earns a natural A4 quantization, but no arithmetic carrier or
target spectral identification exists, so Route A remains rejected.
