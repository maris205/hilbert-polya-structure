# Exact evidence plan

Three frozen strongly connected directed multigraphs exercise loops, unequal
outdegrees and distinctly labelled parallel arcs.  For each graph, every legal
arc-labelled path from the frozen start through length eight is enumerated.
Each row records its edge counts, grouped rising-factorial probability,
Dirichlet-moment probability and posterior predictive normalization.

The evidence additionally records every Dirichlet row mean, variance and
within-row covariance, plus three rational fixed environments.  Their vertex
stationary distributions and labelled edge occupation flows are solved exactly
and checked against balance, normalization and flow conservation.

The independent checker reconstructs the entire coordinate ledger without
importing the producer.  SymPy separately verifies the beta-ratio identities
and stationary systems.  Replay runs two isolated temporary-directory
producers.  Hostile mutations repair payload and YAML binding hashes after
semantic changes, attack strict JSON/YAML parsing, drop or duplicate paths,
collapse parallel arcs, alter departure counts, and include a stale-hash
control.

These finite computations test conventions only.  Almost-sure convergence for
all finite strongly connected graphs is proved analytically.
