# Research question

On a finite strongly connected directed multigraph with at least one outgoing
labelled arc at every vertex and positive initial weights on all arcs, does
linear edge reinforcement admit a complete static representation and an exact
learning limit?

The theorem proved here answers all parts simultaneously: the finite path law,
partial exchangeability, independent-row Dirichlet mixture, posterior after
every history, and almost-sure transition/vertex/edge occupation limits.  The
graph may contain loops and parallel arcs, but its arc labels are never
collapsed. In particular, the one-vertex graph must carry at least one loop;
the vacuous zero-arc singleton is excluded because its normalization is
undefined.

The question explicitly excludes undirected edge reinforcement, vertex
reinforcement, infinite graphs, nonpositive parameters, and target arithmetic
interpretations.
