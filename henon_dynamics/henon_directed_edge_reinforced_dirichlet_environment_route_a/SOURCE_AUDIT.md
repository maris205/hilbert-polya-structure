## Ownership and negative boundary

The paper reconstructs the positive finite strongly connected specialization
directly from beta integrals and the finite-state ergodic theorem.  It claims no
priority for the general reinforced-walk/random-environment correspondence.
It does not invoke the undirected ERRW magic formula, time reversal identities,
infinite-volume directional transience, or any target arithmetic object.
