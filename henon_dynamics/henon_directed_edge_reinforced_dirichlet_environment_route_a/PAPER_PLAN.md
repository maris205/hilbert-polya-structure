# Paper plan

1. Freeze labelled directed multigraphs with nonempty outgoing rows, departure
   counts, positive parameters and the augmented non-Markov state.
2. Group sequential transition factors by departure vertex and arc to obtain
   the exact rising-factorial path law.
3. Integrate the same monomial against independent Dirichlet rows, proving the
   annealed environment representation without relying on an abstract de
   Finetti theorem.
4. Apply the same beta-integral ratio to obtain the posterior and predictive
   rule after every finite history.
5. Condition on the positive environment and apply the finite irreducible
   Markov ergodic theorem to prove all almost-sure learning and occupation
   limits.
6. Close deterministic rows, parallel arcs, single-vertex loops, reducibility,
   zero-weight, collision, source and Route-A boundaries.

The three PDFs add, respectively, mixture closure; posterior and limit closure;
and exact evidence plus source and claim firewalls.
