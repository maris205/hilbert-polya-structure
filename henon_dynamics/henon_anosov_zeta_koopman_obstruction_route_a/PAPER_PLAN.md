2. Prove the complete all-period fixed and primitive orbit theorem.
3. Resum the Artin--Mazur zeta exactly.
4. Define the natural Koopman action and prove its determinant-class
   obstruction.

6. State progress over C119/C121 and the strict Route-A boundary.

exact evidence more clearly than a decorative plot.  No bibliography is
included because there is no external or novelty claim.
