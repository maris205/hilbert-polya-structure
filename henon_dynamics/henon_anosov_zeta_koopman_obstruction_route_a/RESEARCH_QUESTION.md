# Research question — C125

For the frozen toral automorphism

\[
T_A([x])=[Ax],\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\]

can one derive the complete all-period orbit census and Artin--Mazur zeta
exactly, then determine whether the most natural global Hilbert-space action
owns that zeta as an ordinary Fredholm determinant?

The bounded question has six parts:

1. Why is every \(\operatorname{Fix}(T_A^n)\) finite and why is its exact
   cardinality \(|\det(A^n-I)|\)?
2. Can the fixed counts and exact least-period orbit counts be proved for all
   \(n\), with a deterministic replay through period twelve?
3. Does the primitive product have the closed form
   \((1-z)^2/(1-3z+z^2)\)?
4. How does the natural Koopman operator act on the Fourier basis of
   \(L^2(\mathbb T^2)\), and is it compact or determinant class?
5. What fails for a parabolic parent, after deleting the absolute value in
   the fixed-point formula, or after imposing finite cyclic Fourier aliasing?
6. What has progressed relative to C119 and C121, and which target-facing
   Route-A obligations remain absent?

The project does not ask for a prime-like orbit dictionary, a target divisor
comparison, a functional equation, arithmetic local factors, a Hilbert--Pólya
operator, or Route B.  The internally exact zeta and the natural Koopman
operator are kept as different objects unless a determinant-class theorem
identifies them; C125 proves that the ordinary trace-class identification
fails.
