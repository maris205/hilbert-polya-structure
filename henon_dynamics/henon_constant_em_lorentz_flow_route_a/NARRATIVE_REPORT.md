# Narrative report

The main advance is a single invariant functional calculus that survives every electromagnetic field type.
The two squared spectral scales split spacetime into a boost plane and a rotation plane; their analytic limiting
functions automatically expose the secular directions at electric-like and magnetic-like boundaries.  The
nonzero null field is not obtained by dividing by a vanishing spectral gap: its cubic nilpotency is handled by a
separate exact polynomial.

Periodicity also requires object separation.  A four-velocity may be periodic in proper time, especially in a
magnetic-like field, while the physical worldline cannot close because future coordinate time increases strictly.
This prevents a common but material overstatement.
