# Research question

Can constant electromagnetic Lorentz motion be classified for every invariant field type in one exact theorem,
including the propagator, integrated worldline, periodicity, degenerations, and the nonzero null field, without
confusing proper time with coordinate time or periodic velocity with a closed physical worldline?

The answer is yes.  The proof is matrix-invariant and the selected `E/B` convention is independently checked
against the three-vector Lorentz force.
