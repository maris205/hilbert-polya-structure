# Research question

Can a discrete-time, spatially inhomogeneous coined walk supply a natural
Route-A unitary with exact time reversal and a signed primitive-path
determinant that detects the order of local dynamical ingredients?

The test is source-internal.  Two five-site coin arrangements have the same
coin population but are not related by a dihedral symmetry.  The paper asks
whether their exact secular polynomials remain different without fitting any
external spectrum.

## Frozen answer

Yes.  With rational reflection coins `C0`, `C1`, flip-flop shift `S`, and
coin-then-shift convention `U=SC`, both walks are exactly unitary and satisfy
antiunitary reversal with `Theta=CK`.  Their determinant difference is

```text
(196/4225) z^2 (z-1)^2 (z+1)^2 (z^2+1).
```

Population averaging destroys unitarity, so it is not a valid replacement.
