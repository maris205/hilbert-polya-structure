# Paper plan

## Title

Order-Sensitive Secular Determinants for an Inhomogeneous Coined Quantum Walk

## Sections

1. Freeze the five-cycle, two reflection coins, and coin-then-shift clock.
2. Prove unitarity, antiunitary reversal, and the classical shadow.
3. Derive the signed primitive path product.
4. Compare the two equal-population arrangements exactly.
5. Reject population averaging and state Route-A limitations.

## Claims--evidence matrix

| claim | proof | sentinel |
|---|---|---|
| unitary and reversal | involution algebra | exact 10-by-10 identities |
| path ownership | determinant logarithm | signed paths through clock 10 |
| order sensitivity | explicit polynomials and factorized difference | two independent determinant constructions |
| averaging failure | exact orthogonality defect | mutation coverage |
