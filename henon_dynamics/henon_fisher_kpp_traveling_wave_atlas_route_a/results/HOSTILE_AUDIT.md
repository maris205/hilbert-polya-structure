3. **Incomplete trapping boundary.**  Check `V=0`, `U=1` and `V=-qU`; on the

4. **Finite shooting as theorem.**  Treat all 25 trapping rows as regression

5. **Focus-tail positivity.**  Force a subcritical focus and verify that every
   nontrivial tail has a nonzero sinusoidal coefficient pair and changes sign.
6. **Stationary-front confusion.**  Distinguish the `0<h<1/6` Hamiltonian ovals

7. **Critical-root factor error.**  Verify the generalized-eigenvector law and
   `W''=(r/D)e^{sqrt(r/D)z}U^2>0` at `c=2sqrt(Dr)`.
8. **Special-speed overreach.**  Differentiate the Ablowitz--Zeppetella member
   exactly, while refusing to infer a generic closed form from it.
9. **Translation-locator drift.**  Reject a truncated late-start KPP range;
   lock the official table-of-contents range to `242--270`.
10. **Schema and hash bypass.**  Repair the payload hash after semantic edits,
    inject unknown keys at every finite-row layer, and test one stale hash.
11. **Route laundering.**  Mutate each fail gate, scope flag, nonclaim and
    Route-B boolean while preserving syntactically valid JSON.

## Outcome

and symbolic paths agree with the producer, byte replay is exact, and the
strict five-fail Route-A result survives.  This is an internal hostile audit,
not external review or an independent error process.
