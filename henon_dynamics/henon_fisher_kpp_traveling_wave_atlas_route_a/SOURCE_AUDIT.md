## Proof and attribution boundary

The all-speed front theorem is classical.  This package supplies a
convention-locked phase-plane proof synthesis: a forward-invariant triangle
for supercritical and critical speeds, unstable-manifold uniqueness, the
focus obstruction below threshold, the Hamiltonian stationary boundary,
reflection, and local tail expansions.  It claims neither historical priority
nor a new shooting theorem.

The exact and high-precision rows are finite implementation sentinels.  They
do not prove that a separatrix remains trapped for a continuum of speeds, do
not replace asymptotic ODE theory, and do not certify global literature
novelty.
