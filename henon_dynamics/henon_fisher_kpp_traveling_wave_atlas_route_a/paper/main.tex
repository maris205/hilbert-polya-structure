speed in the Fisher--KPP traveling-wave equation.  A dimensionless
phase-plane triangle proves existence and translation uniqueness of positive
monotone fronts exactly when $c\ge2\sqrt{Dr}$.

\section{Frozen equation and exact scaling}
Fix $D,r>0$ in physical time and consider
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pde}
 u_t=D u_{xx}+r u(1-u).
\end{equation}
Fisher introduced this logistic diffusion model and its wave argument
~\cite{F37}; Kolmogorov, Petrovskii and Piskunov established the classical
minimal-speed front theory~\cite{KPP37}.  With $z=x-ct$, $u(x,t)=U(z)$,
\eqref{eq:pde} becomes
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical}
 D U_{zz}+cU_z+rU(1-U)=0.
\end{equation}
Set
\[
 \xi=\sqrt{r/D}\,z,\qquad s=c/\sqrt{Dr},\qquad V=U_\xi .
\]
The complete parameter family is conjugate, with time orientation retained,
to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:plane}
 U_\xi=V,\qquad V_\xi=-sV-U(1-U).
\end{equation}
The equilibria are $(1,0)$ and $(0,0)$.  The first is always a saddle; the
zero-state characteristic polynomial is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zerochar}
 \lambda^2+s\lambda+1.
\end{equation}

\section{The positive-speed front theorem}
Assume $s\ge2$ and define the slow positive slope
\[
 q=\frac{s-\sqrt{s^2-4}}2,\qquad q^2-sq+1=0.
\]
Consider the compact triangle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:triangle}
 \mathcal T_q=\{(U,V):0\le U\le1,\ -qU\le V\le0\}.
\end{equation}
On $V=0$, $V_\xi=-U(1-U)<0$, while on $U=1$ one has
$U_\xi=V\le0$.  On the slanted boundary put $G=V+qU$; substitution of
$V=-qU$ gives the decisive identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:inward}
 G_\xi=U\{q(s-q)-1+U\}=U^2>0.
\end{equation}
The decreasing branch of the one-dimensional unstable manifold of $(1,0)$
enters $\mathcal T_q$.  Since the divergence of~\eqref{eq:plane} is the
negative constant $-s$, a nonconstant periodic omega limit is impossible.
The energy identity below also prevents return to the saddle; hence the branch
converges to $(0,0)$.  It is a profile with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:front}
 0<U<1,\quad U_\xi<0,\quad U(-\infty)=1,\quad U(+\infty)=0.
\end{equation}
Conversely, every orbit with~\eqref{eq:front} must leave the saddle along this
same unstable branch.  Reparametrizing one orbit changes only the origin of
$\xi$.  Thus for every $c\ge2\sqrt{Dr}$ the front exists and is unique modulo
translation.

\section{Every remaining speed}
If $W(\xi)=U(-\xi)$, then $W$ solves~\eqref{eq:plane} with speed $-s$.
Therefore every $c\le-2\sqrt{Dr}$ has a unique reflected positive profile,
increasing from $0$ to $1$.

When $0<s<2$, the roots of~\eqref{eq:zerochar} are
\[
 -\frac{s}{2}\mathbin\pm i\frac{\sqrt{4-s^2}}2.
\]
Asymptotic linearization at this hyperbolic focus makes every nontrivial tail
approaching zero rotate and change the sign of $U$.  It cannot be a
$[0,1]$ front.  Reflection gives the same obstruction for $-2<s<0$.

At $s=0$, system~\eqref{eq:plane} conserves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H0}
 H_0(U,V)=\frac{V^2}{2}+\frac{U^2}{2}-\frac{U^3}{3}.
\end{equation}
For every $0<h<1/6$, the bounded component of $H_0=h$ is a periodic oval
around the center $(0,0)$; it crosses negative $U$ and is not a positive
front.  More decisively, $H_0(1,0)=1/6\ne0=H_0(0,0)$, so no stationary
$1$-to-$0$ heteroclinic exists.  This closes the full real speed axis.

\section{Energy, no cycles, and three leading-edge laws}
In physical variables,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:energy}
 E=\frac D2U_z^2+r\left(\frac{U^2}{2}-\frac{U^3}{3}\right),
 \qquad E_z=-cU_z^2.
\end{equation}
Consequently no nonconstant periodic profile exists for $c\ne0$; this is
also Bendixson's conclusion from divergence $-c/D$.

At the back of a positive front,
\[
 1-U(z)\sim C e^{\alpha z},\qquad C>0,\qquad
 \alpha=\frac{\sqrt{c^2+4Dr}-c}{2D}>0,\quad z\to-\infty.
\]
At the leading edge $z\to+\infty$, three distinct laws occur:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:super}
 c>2\sqrt{Dr}:\qquad
 U(z)\sim B e^{\lambda_+z},\qquad B>0,\qquad
 \lambda_+=\frac{-c+\sqrt{c^2-4Dr}}{2D}.
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:critical}
 c=2\sqrt{Dr}:\qquad
 U(z)\sim(Az+B)e^{-\sqrt{r/D}\,z},\qquad A>0.
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sub}
 \begin{split}
 0<c<2\sqrt{Dr}:\qquad
 U(z)&=e^{-cz/(2D)}\{A\cos(\omega z)+B\sin(\omega z)+o(1)\},\\
 \omega&=\frac{\sqrt{4Dr-c^2}}{2D}.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Here $(A,B)\ne(0,0)$ for a nontrivial tail.
For~\eqref{eq:super}, the triangle excludes the fast eigendirection.  At the
critical speed, $W=e^{\sqrt{r/D}z}U$ obeys
$W''=(r/D)e^{\sqrt{r/D}z}U^2>0$, giving the positive
generalized-eigenvector coefficient $A$.  Equation
\eqref{eq:sub} is the announced sign-changing obstruction, not a numerical
shooting observation.
