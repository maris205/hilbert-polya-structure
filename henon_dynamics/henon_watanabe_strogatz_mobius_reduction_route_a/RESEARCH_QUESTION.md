# C189 research question

For every number of identical phases and every common time-dependent
first-harmonic forcing, can one prove in one theorem that the flow is a single
`PSU(1,1)` action, identify all generic Watanabe--Strogatz invariants, retain
the collision and synchronization strata, and classify every constant
generator including the exact elliptic projected period?

The large-step requirement is the whole common-forcing family.  A simulation
of one Kuramoto parameter, three oscillators, or one constant elliptic orbit
would not qualify.  The theorem must include the degenerate zero generator,
the parabolic boundary, repeated phases, and the distinction between a matrix
period and its projective period.

The Route-A question is separate: does this exact group action intrinsically
encode rational primes, a logarithmic prime clock, or a target divisor?  The
frozen answer is no.  The useful positive result is an exact group-action
owner and a clean explanation of why elliptic resonances fix continua rather
than a finite isolated-orbit census.

Scope is exactly `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.  Target zero/prime tables,
arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, target
functional equations, Hilbert--Pólya operators, and Route B are excluded.
