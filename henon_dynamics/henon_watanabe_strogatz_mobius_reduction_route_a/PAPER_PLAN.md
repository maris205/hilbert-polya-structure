# C189 paper plan

## Thesis

Every identical common first-harmonic phase system is one `PSU(1,1)` flow.
Three landmarks determine the moving frame, `N-3` cross ratios determine a
generic leaf, collision partitions remain exact strata, and a single
quadratic generator identity closes the constant elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic
atlas.

## Claims--evidence matrix

| claim | analytic owner | executable sentinel |
|---|---|---|
| arbitrary common forcing gives one Möbius flow | `SU(1,1)` fundamental matrix proof | 96 exact local Riccati jets |
| generic quotient has `N-3` invariants | three-point rigidity and cross-ratio proof | exact rational configurations `N=4,...,10` |
| collision strata are invariant | injectivity and stabilizer proof | five collision partitions under four maps |
| constant generators have a complete trichotomy | `A^2=-Delta I/4` | eight exact constant rows |
| finite isolated-orbit zeta stops | clean fixed-set argument | elliptic period-factor sentinels |

## Structure

1. Freeze the phase, Riccati, `SU(1,1)`, and cross-ratio conventions.
2. State and prove the arbitrary-forcing group-action theorem.
3. Prove generic independence and all collision-stratum dimensions.
4. Derive the complete constant-generator atlas and projected period.
5. Report the independent exact certificate and hostile mutations.
6. Separate formal group linearization from arithmetic, determinant, and
   Hilbert--Pólya claims.

The paper is theorem-first.  Aggregate audit counts are allowed; raw finite
rows never replace an all-parameter proof.
