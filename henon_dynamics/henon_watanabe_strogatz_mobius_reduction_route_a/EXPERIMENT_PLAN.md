# C189 exact-verification plan

## Claim-driven tests

1. Generate exact rational local Riccati jets from three real common
   frequencies, four complex common forcings, and eight rational circle
   points: 96 rows.  Check circle tangency, phase velocity, and the Riccati
   identity in every row.
2. Apply four exact disk automorphisms to generic labelled configurations for
   every `N=4,...,10` and to five collision partitions.  Check every image,
   circle equation, collision partition, orbit dimension, and independent
   cross ratio with rational arithmetic.
3. Reconstruct each eligible projective map from three distinct landmarks in
   the independent checker; do not import producer functions.
4. Cover identity, three elliptic, two parabolic, and two hyperbolic constant
   generators.  Check the generator square, boundary fixed roots, and exact
   coefficient of the elliptic projected period.
5. Run a separate SymPy derivation, canonical byte replay, repaired-hash
   semantic mutations, and one stale-hash mutation.

## Interpretation

The rational rows detect signs, conjugations, cross-ratio ordering, collision
handling, and factors of two.  The proof in `THEOREM_PACKAGE.md` establishes
the infinite family.  No finite grid, regression score, or numerical fit is
reported as proof.

## Release gates

The paper must have three content-distinct rounds, two clean fixed-epoch
fresh-directory builds that are byte-identical, embedded fonts, no unresolved
references or layout warnings, rendered-page inspection, and a 27-payload
self-excluded manifest.
