# C46 results

For every (n\ge1), the scaled fixed algebra is finite free over
(\mathbb Z) of rank (2^n).  Every coordinate (x_i), every monodromy
entry, and the trace (t_\gamma) are algebraic integers.  Since

\[
\lambda^2-t_\gamma\lambda+1=0,
\]

both λ and λ⁻¹ are algebraic integers.  Every H6 periodic multiplier is
therefore an algebraic unit.  Exact Gröbner and trace sentinels pass through
period 10, and period one recovers

\[
X^4-4X^3-22X^2-4X+1.
\]
