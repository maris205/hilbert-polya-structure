# Research log

The unscaled H6 coordinates obscure their arithmetic type.  The canonical
change (x=6q) turns every cyclic recurrence monic and every derivative step
integral.  This yields an all-period theorem, not a finite pattern: the raw
multipliers are algebraic units.  The result is positive arithmetic structure
and simultaneously a warning against raw rational-prime labels.
