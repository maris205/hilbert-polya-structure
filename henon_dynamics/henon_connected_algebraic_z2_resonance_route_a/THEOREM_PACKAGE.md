# Theorem package

1. The source is compact connected, dual to the torsion-free localization
   `Z[u+-1,(1+u)^-1]`.
2. Every lattice fixed group is the kernel of the exact integer convolution
   matrix `I+S1+S2`; Smith factors give its complete compact-group type.
3. The only unit-torus zeros are the two primitive third-root conjugate modes.
   The fixed group is finite unless `3|a,b=c mod3`, when its torus dimension
   is exactly two.
4. For any integer matrix the torsion cokernel order equals its nonzero
   singular-value product divided by the two integer kernel covolumes.
   Here the common Gram determinant is `N^2/3`, so the component correction
   is `3/N^2`.
5. At `K={i-j=0 mod3}`, the fixed group is exactly a two-torus. Apart from
   three constant arrays, every point has stabilizer K and primitive orbit
   size three. Its specified tangent action has third-root phases.
6. Classical joint entropy is `3sqrt(3)/(4pi) L(chi_-3,2)`; the finite paired
   Dirichlet series has an explicit positive rational tail bound.
7. Ordinary lattice-cardinality and every rank-one Artin--Mazur zeta fail
   because a finite index/iterate already has uncountably many fixed points.
