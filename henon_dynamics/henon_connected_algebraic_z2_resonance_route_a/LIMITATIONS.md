# Limitations

The source model, HNF resonance condition, finite-versus-torus dichotomy,
entropy theorem and Smyth value have substantial classical ownership. This
package does not certify literature novelty. It gives a full source-local
reconstruction and an exact finite-lattice formula with independently checked
integer certificates.

The displayed finite-lattice formula in the accessed arXiv version
0912.5169v1 fails the index-three all-ones-matrix control. This version-specific
statement does not establish the status of later corrigenda. It does not
refute normalized entropy asymptotics: the correction has logarithm O(log N).

The finite 142-lattice grid, 4900 denominator-labelled torus grid records and eight rational
Dirichlet tail bounds are finite checks. The proof, not their size, supports
all finite-index lattices. Eighty/ninety-digit floating controls are not
certificates. There is no all-prime orbit correspondence, target determinant,
analytic target divisor, preferred scalar quantum generator or Route B.
