# Scope and firewall

`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER` is literal. Every target local-data, Euler-factor,
root-number, automorphy, target-divisor, functional-equation, target-zero,
Hilbert--Polya and Route B claim flag is false.

The all-lattice theorem is about a source compact group. A Smith presentation
classifies its fixed groups, not an arithmetic Frobenius fibre. The two-torus
tangent multipliers are not the monodromy of an isolated orbit in an
infinite-dimensional phase space. Coordinate exchange swaps generators; it
is not a same-clock reversor for every direction.

The native Koopman actions are commuting Haar unitaries, which give only
`A4_FORMAL_HINT`. A scalar L-value gives only a weak arithmetic relation.
Ordinary point-count zeta is obstructed, but component-count, measured and
regularized constructions remain outside the negative statement.
