# Assumptions and conventions

The acting group is the whole integer lattice in rank two. Circle coordinates
are additive modulo one. HNF columns are `(a,0),(b,c)` with positive `a,c`
and `0<=b<a`; index is `N=ac`. Quotient coordinate order is lexicographic
in `0<=i<a, 0<=j<c`. A repeated neighbor contributes its full integer
multiplicity to the quotient matrix, including index-one and index-three faces.

Characters use forward shifts, not their conjugates. Nonzero singular-value
products include character multiplicities. Kernel covolumes are calculated in
their real spans with the Euclidean metric inherited from all N coordinates.
Smith factors are positive and divisibility-ordered, with zeros last.

The entropy identity uses the principal-action theorem of Lind--Schmidt--Ward.
The Dirichlet value is rederived by Jensen and an integrated Fourier series.
No prime table, target zero data, adjustable roof, physical scalar time, or
quantum generator is assumed.
