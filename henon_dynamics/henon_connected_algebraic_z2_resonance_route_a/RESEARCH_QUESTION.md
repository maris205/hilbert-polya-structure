# Research question

For the connected principal algebraic action associated to `1+u+v`, which
finite-index lattice fixed groups are finite, which contain continuous tori,
and what integer-lattice correction turns a singular Fourier product into
the exact number of connected components?

The second question is whether its classical arithmetic entropy supplies an
ordinary orbit-cardinality zeta. The answer is negative: the index-three
fixed torus contains uncountably many primitive three-point orbits, and the
third iterate of every rank-one restriction fixes that torus.

The exact residual contribution is an auditable all-HNF reconstruction, the
covolume proof and minimal version-specific counterexample, not new discovery
of the classical resonance condition or Mahler special value.
