# Narrative report

A connected arithmetic-looking action need not have a discrete periodic
ledger. Here the Fourier diagonalization has just two possible zero modes,
but those modes change fixed points into entire tori at infinitely many
lattices. Omitting zero eigenvalues produces a finite number, yet that number
still overcounts connected components by the integer kernel discriminant.

The proof isolates the exact mechanism. The saturated kernel basis has Gram
matrix `N/3 [[2,1],[1,2]]`. A general integer-image covolume lemma therefore
forces the factor `3/N^2`. At the smallest resonant quotient the matrix is
all ones, its nonzero eigenvalue is three, and its torus kernel is connected.
This is an exact counterexample to the displayed uncorrected formula in the
accessed arXiv v1, not a numerical anomaly or an entropy refutation.

The same index-three torus has uncountably many primitive three-point orbits.
Its classical joint entropy nevertheless has the Smyth character-modulo-three
value. The paper therefore separates a genuine native arithmetic scalar from
a missing discrete prime-carrying orbit trace. Neither target grading nor
literature novelty is inflated.
