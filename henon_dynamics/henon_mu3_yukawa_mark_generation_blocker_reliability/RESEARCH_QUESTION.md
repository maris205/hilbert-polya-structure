# C73 research question

Use the complete C72 named-support atlas to determine the obstruction and
reliability geometry of retaining generation of the universal complement core
under coordinate deletion:

1. identify a structural hypergraph model for the 25 minimal generating
   supports;
2. classify every inclusion-minimal destructive deletion set;
3. enumerate all destructive and surviving deletions by size;
4. derive homogeneous and heterogeneous exact reliability formulae;
5. distinguish worst-case, protected, and existential deletion robustness;
6. compute coordinate importance in the associated monotone generation game.

A mere reversal of the C72 generating polynomial is insufficient: the paper
must prove the multipartite geometry and heterogeneous formula.

Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
