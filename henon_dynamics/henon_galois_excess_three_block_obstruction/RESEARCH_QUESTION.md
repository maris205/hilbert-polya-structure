# Research question

## Primary question

Does the P54 Galois-excess assignment on the frozen mixing H6 survivor satisfy
the exact periodic-sum constraints of a locally constant potential, and what
is the smallest symbolic memory not ruled out by exact short orbits?

## Secondary question

What condition would genuinely test an unrestricted Hölder realization,
given that finitely many periodic orbits can always be interpolated?

## Frozen scope

- Same H6 map, Markov survivor and symbolic labels as HCS-P31.
- Same Mahler-height splitting and excess definition as HCS-P54.
- No target primes, Riemann zeros or fitted potential values.
- Exact symbolic cycles through period five and exact algebraic elimination.
- General Hölder realizability is not inferred from finite rows.

## Answer obtained

The Galois excess violates the shortest width-three block-incidence relation,
so every locally constant realization needs at least four consecutive
symbols.  The finite witness is interpolable at width four.  A general Hölder
realization must instead satisfy exponentially decaying discrepancies along
every sequence of higher-block incidence relations.
