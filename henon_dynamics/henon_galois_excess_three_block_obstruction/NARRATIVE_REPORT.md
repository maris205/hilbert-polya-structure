# Narrative report

P54 reached a clean but genuinely arithmetic gate: the physical instability
roof has an all-period pressure pole, whereas the remaining Mahler amplitude
is the sum of nonphysical Galois contributions.  P55 asks whether that excess
is local enough for ordinary thermodynamic formalism.

The answer is negative at the first meaningful finite-memory scales.  The H6
graph contains exact cycle relations invisible to orbit-by-orbit fitting.  At
three-symbol resolution, a period-three cycle plus a period-five cycle has
exactly the same cyclic block inventory as two period-four cycles.  Any local
potential must assign equal total weight to the two sides.  Exact multiplier
algebra shows that the Galois excess does not: the period-five excess alone is
already larger than the nonzero period-four excess.

This proves a four-symbol lower bound for locally constant models.  It does
not close the broader Hölder question, because a long enough cylinder
function can fit any finite orbit table.  The reusable gain is therefore a
quantitative program: find higher-block relations at growing lengths and
measure whether the excess discrepancy decays at a Hölder-compatible
exponential rate.  That is the next bridge, not another unconstrained finite
interpolation.
