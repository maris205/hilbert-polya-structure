## Claim-to-source boundary

Livshits supplies the distinction between periodic-sum cohomology and an
arbitrary assignment.  It is not cited as an extension theorem for the P54
Galois excess.  Bowen and Parry--Pollicott supply the standard regularity and
zeta context; the block-incidence criterion, period-five elimination,
finite-interpolation theorem and quantitative Hölder gate are proved in this
paper.

the relevant algebra and inequalities are fully reproduced in the manuscript
