# C309 evidence plan

The theorem is analytic; computation is used only to make convention errors
hard to hide.

1. Enumerate 16 rational spectra spanning both fixed points, the heteroclinic
   chamber, repeated eigenvalues, one- and two-sided chart poles, pole ties,
   and dimensions one through five.
2. At six signed times, record the scalar flow, denominator, vector field,
   and Lyapunov derivative whenever the probe avoids a pole.
3. Record every Loewner factor at a nonzero time and all 44 equilibrium
   strata for `1<=n<=8`.
4. Reconstruct the ledger in a producer-independent checker; verify symbolic
   Möbius, gradient, rate, and dimension identities in SymPy; demand two-run
   byte replay; reject repaired-hash semantic and malformed-JSON attacks.
5. Compile three substantive manuscript rounds twice at a fixed epoch, check
   warnings, text, pages, rasterization, and embedded/subset fonts, then close
   an exact self-excluding manifest.
