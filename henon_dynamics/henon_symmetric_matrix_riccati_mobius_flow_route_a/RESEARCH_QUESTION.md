# HCS-C309 research question

For the all-dimensional symmetric matrix Riccati equation

\[
\dot X=I-X^2,\qquad X(0)=X_0=X_0^{\mathsf T},
\]

can one close, in one theorem, the exact flow, its maximal chart, finite-time
blow-up, forward limits, the complete Morse--Bott equilibrium atlas, and the
Fréchet geometry of the time map?  After that source-local theorem is proved,
what survives the Route-A ownership test under
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`?

The question does not ask for target arithmetic data or a Hilbert--Pólya
operator.  The block linear lift is tested only as a source-native formal
operator hint.
