# C161 narrative report

C161 made a deliberate model switch.  The attempted Heisenberg continuation
did not clear its local-equivalence proof gate, so it was not packaged.  For a
finite cyclic rotation, the quadratic Birkhoff sum remains quadratic at every
iterate.  This turns the complete orbit amplitude into a fully evaluable odd
Gauss sum: a gcd condition gives exact vanishing, and completion of the square
gives phase, sign, and magnitude in the nonzero branch.

The strict new progress is all-parameter rather than tabular.  Equation (6) in
the theorem package also exposes a concrete discriminant transition for the
pure quadratic observable.  A finite weighted Koopman unitary realizes the
same-clock amplitude as `Tr(U^n K^(-n))`.  This remains source mathematics and
an explicit involutive antiunitary reverses the weighted unitary.  This remains
source mathematics and does not establish a target trace formula.
