# C161 research question

For the finite rotation `R_q` and quadratic phase `phi_(a,b)`, can the complete
iterate amplitude

`G_(q,n)(a,b)=sum_(x mod q) exp(2*pi*i*S_n phi_(a,b)(x)/q)`

be evaluated for every odd modulus and every iterate, including exact
vanishing conditions and zero-level counts?  The success criterion is an
all-parameter theorem, not a finite table.
