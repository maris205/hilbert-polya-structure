\section{An exact five-block obstruction}

We first turn coarse trace isolators into a rigorous separation.

\begin{lemma}[Elementary logarithmic bracket]\label{lem:log}
For every real \(u>2\),
\[
 \log(u-1)<\acosh(u/2)<\log u.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The expanding root of \(z+z^{-1}=u\) is
\(\lambda=(u+\sqrt{u^2-4})/2\).  Since
\(u-2<\sqrt{u^2-4}<u\), one has \(u-1<\lambda<u\).
Taking logarithms proves the claim.
\end{proof}

For \(A_5\), the physical trace is the root in \((-7607,-7606)\).
The other five locked intervals are
\[
 (-711,-710),\ (-590,-589),\ (390,391),\ (770,771),\ (4445,4446).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:log} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A5lower}
 \Egal(A_5)>
 \log(709\cdot588\cdot389\cdot769\cdot4444).
\end{equation}
For \(A_6\), Proposition~\ref{prop:A6} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A6upper}
 \Egal(A_6)<\log(1095\cdot5138).
\end{equation}
P56 proved
\(\Egal(B_6)=\acosh(9031-2676\sqrt7)\) and
\(1950<9031-2676\sqrt7<1951\).  Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B6upper}
 \Egal(B_6)<\log3902.
\end{equation}
The required product comparison is exactly
\begin{align}
 709\cdot588\cdot389\cdot769\cdot4444
 &=554208972546768,\label{eq:lower-product}\\
 1095\cdot5138\cdot3902
 &=21953081220,\label{eq:upper-product}\\
 554208972546768-21953081220
 &=554187019465548>0.\label{eq:margin}
\end{align}
It follows that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stronger}
 \Egal(A_5)>\Egal(A_6)+\Egal(B_6).
\end{equation}
Moreover \(\Egal(B_7)>0\), since the irreducible degree-fourteen field has
nonphysical expanding trace embeddings.  Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta-positive}
 \Delta_5=\Egal(A_5)+\Egal(B_7)-\Egal(A_6)-\Egal(B_6)>0.
\end{equation}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
The \(m=5\) member of \eqref{eq:ladder-intro} is
\[
 \Nblk_5(A_5)+\Nblk_5(B_7)=\Nblk_5(A_6)+\Nblk_5(B_6).
\]
Every width-five potential must therefore give \(\Delta_5=0\), contrary to
\eqref{eq:delta-positive}.  A potential of any shorter width is also a
width-five potential after ignoring the extra coordinates.  This excludes
all widths one through five.
\end{proof}

The high-precision diagnostic value is
\[
 \Delta_5=139.7325728699720846164890241354\ldots,
\]
but its only logical role is reproducibility; equations
\eqref{eq:A5lower}--\eqref{eq:margin} certify the sign.
