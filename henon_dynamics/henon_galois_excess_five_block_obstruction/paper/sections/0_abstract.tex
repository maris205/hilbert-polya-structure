\begin{abstract}
For the area-preserving H\'enon recurrence
\(q_{j+1}=1-6q_j^2-q_{j-1}\), a previously constructed pair of primitive
symbolic families satisfies an exact cyclic block-incidence identity at every
width.  We resolve the next metric member of this ladder.  Reversibility
reduces the period-six orbit \(A_6\) to a degree-six coordinate equation and
the period-seven orbits \(A_7,B_7\) to two real embeddings of one
degree-fourteen equation.  Chronological monodromy elimination gives an
irreducible cubic trace polynomial for \(A_6\) and an irreducible totally real
degree-fourteen trace polynomial shared by \(A_7,B_7\).  Sturm intervals and
an elementary logarithmic comparison prove
\[
 \Egal(A_5)+\Egal(B_7)-\Egal(A_6)-\Egal(B_6)>0,
\]
with numerical value \(139.73257\ldots\).  The sign proof reduces to a
strict integer-product comparison, independent of floating-point root
separation.  Hence no locally constant potential depending on at most five
consecutive states realizes all primitive-orbit Galois excesses.  A
determinant-one width-six incidence minor interpolates the cumulative finite
witness, so the unrestricted H\"older question remains open.  We state the
precise asymptotic discrepancy theorem still needed and preserve the
physical-subsystem/arithmetical claim boundary.
\end{abstract}
