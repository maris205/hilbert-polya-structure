\section{Cubic and degree-fourteen trace fields}

\begin{proposition}[The \(A_6\) trace field]\label{prop:A6}
Modulo \(f_6(a)\), the chronological trace is
\[
 T_6(a)=-2(1364688a^4-94608a^3-379080a^2+56808a+689).
\]
Its resultant with \(f_6\) is the square of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:F6}
 F_6(T)=T^3+48342T^2-334511988T+306994257352.
\end{equation}
The polynomial \(F_6\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\), has three real
roots, one in each interval
\begin{equation}\label{eq:F6intervals}
 (-54575,-54574),\quad(1094,1095),\quad(5137,5138),
\end{equation}
and the first is the physical trace of \(A_6\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Direct matrix multiplication followed by polynomial remainder gives the
displayed trace.  Elimination gives \(F_6^2\); the square corresponds to the
two choices of reflection-axis coordinate for each orbit.  Reduction modulo
5 is irreducible.  Exact Sturm counts give one root in each interval in
\eqref{eq:F6intervals}, exhausting the degree.  The rational coordinate box
from Section~3 maps strictly inside the first trace interval.
\end{proof}

The reciprocal multiplier polynomial is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A6multiplier}
 z^6+48342z^5-334511985z^4+306994354036z^3
 -334511985z^2+48342z+1,
\end{equation}
and is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\).

\begin{proposition}[The shared \(A_7/B_7\) field]\label{prop:P7}
Eliminating \(a\) from the period-seven trace and \(f_7(a)\) gives an
irreducible degree-fourteen polynomial \(F_7(T)\).  It has fourteen real
roots, isolated by the intervals in Appendix~\ref{sec:ledger}.  The physical
embeddings satisfy
\[
 T(A_7)\in(-390512,-390511),\qquad
 T(B_7)\in(230985,230986).
\]
Thus \(A_7\) and \(B_7\) are physical real embeddings of one totally real
degree-fourteen trace field.  Its reciprocal multiplier polynomial is
irreducible of degree twenty eight.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The exact reduced trace is printed in the certificate.  Its resultant is the
polynomial \(F_7\) in Appendix~\ref{sec:ledger}; reduction modulo 37 is
irreducible.  Sturm counts give one root in each of fourteen disjoint integer
intervals.  The two coordinate boxes of Section~3 map inside the claimed
trace intervals and carry the required sign words.  Substitution
\(T=z+z^{-1}\), followed by multiplication by \(z^{14}\), gives the
reciprocal degree-twenty-eight polynomial; exact factorization over
\(\mathbb Q\) is irreducible.
\end{proof}
