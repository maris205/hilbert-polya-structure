# Experiment plan

## Exact experiment A: reflection elimination

- generate `b,c,d` from one rational polynomial variable;
- factor both closing equations;
- select the primitive degree-six and degree-fourteen factors;
- reduce every recurrence residual modulo the selected factor.

## Exact experiment B: trace fields

- multiply chronological `SL_2` derivative matrices;
- reduce traces and compute resultants;
- certify irreducibility modulo 5 and 37;
- Sturm-isolate all 3 and 14 real roots;
- identify physical `A6/A7/B7` embeddings by rational boxes and signs.

## Exact experiment C: obstruction

- evaluate the Galois excess at 90 digits for diagnostics;
- prove its sign independently with the integer logarithmic comparison;
- mutate every interval, degree, sign, determinant and route claim.

## Exact experiment D: finite sharpness

- verify the width-five left-kernel vector `(1,1,-1,-1)`;
- compute the four-row determinant `-1` width-six minor;
- compute the cumulative nine-row determinant `+1` minor.

No Riemann zeros, rational-prime tables or fitted potentials enter any
experiment.
