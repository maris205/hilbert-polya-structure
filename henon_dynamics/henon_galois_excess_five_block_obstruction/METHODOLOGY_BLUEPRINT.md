# Methodology blueprint

1. Freeze the P31/P54/P55/P56 survivor, excess and incidence conventions.
2. Use reversibility to reduce `A6` to `(a,b,c,d,c,b)` and `A7/B7` to
   `(a,b,c,d,d,c,b)`.
3. Generate `b,c,d` recursively from `a`, factor the closing equation, and
   retain the primitive degree-six or degree-fourteen factor.
4. Multiply chronological derivative matrices, reduce the trace modulo the
   coordinate polynomial, and eliminate `a`.
5. Prove irreducibility modulo a small prime and isolate every real trace
   root by Sturm counts.
6. Identify physical embeddings by rational coordinate boxes, signs and
   trace intervals.
7. Compute Galois excess diagnostically at high precision, but certify the
   sign of `Delta_5` with elementary exact logarithmic bounds and one integer
   product comparison.
8. Check the width-five relation rank and exhibit width-six unimodular
   minors for both the four-row and cumulative witness sets.
9. Separate the finite-memory theorem from the still-open Hölder asymptotic
   gate, then evaluate Route A and Route B under their frozen rubrics.
