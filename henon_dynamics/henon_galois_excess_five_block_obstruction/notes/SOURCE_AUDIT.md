# Source audit

No new external theorem is needed for the P57 algebra.  The H6 map,
survivor, trace convention and Galois-excess definition are dependency-locked
to P31/P54/P55/P56.  Polynomial elimination, modular irreducibility, Sturm
counts, integer incidence and logarithmic inequalities are derived in the
project.

The contextual H\"older and zeta statements use the same standard sources as
P56: Bowen for symbolic hyperbolic dynamics, Parry--Pollicott for periodic
orbit zeta formalism, and Liv\v{s}ic for cohomological scope.  The manuscript
does not attribute the new trace polynomials or obstruction to those
sources.
