# Route-B scope

P57 contains no Route-B object.  The trace fields are finite algebraic
invariants of selected classical periodic orbits.  They do not define a
Hilbert space, self-adjoint operator, compact resolvent, spectral measure,
von-Mangoldt trace formula or completed determinant.

Route-B evaluation is retained only to prevent accidental promotion.  Its
verdict is `ROUTE_B_NOT_TESTABLE`.
