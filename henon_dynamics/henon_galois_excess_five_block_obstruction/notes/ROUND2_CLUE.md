# ROUND2_CLUE

P57 should end the strategy of proving one new finite-memory width per paper.
The exact compiler is now strong enough to attack the whole discrepancy
sequence.

Next project:

1. identify the negative fixed point approached by the long zero runs;
2. linearize the recurrence and both reflection boundary conditions there;
3. derive the leading stable eigenmode for `A_m` and `B_m` trace/excess data;
4. predict the sign and decay rate of `Delta_m`;
5. compute `B8` (and only the minimum additional exact data) as an
   adversarial falsifier;
6. prove either a compatible exponential law or a subsequential lower bound
   incompatible with every one-sided H\"older rate.

The fixed-period degree growth is a tool, not the target.  Cross-family
analogues remain deferred by the one-session/one-family rule.
