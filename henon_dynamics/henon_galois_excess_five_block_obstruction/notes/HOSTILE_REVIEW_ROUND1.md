# Hostile review round 1

## Objection 1: the reported `Delta_5` is only numerical

**Finding:** valid concern in the first draft.

**Repair:** the theorem now uses `log(u-1)<acosh(u/2)<log u`, exact Sturm
intervals and an integer product margin `554187019465548`.  The decimal is
diagnostic only.

## Objection 2: the degree-fourteen resultant may mix several physical words

**Finding:** the field does contain several real embeddings; this is a
feature that must be typed.

**Repair:** rational coordinate boxes and sign patterns separately identify
`A7` and `B7`; the statement is precisely that they are two embeddings of
one field, not the same orbit.

## Objection 3: a five-block failure proves no H\"older potential exists

**Finding:** false promotion.

**Repair:** width-six unimodular minors are stated next to the obstruction,
and the H\"older claim remains an asymptotic open theorem.

## Objection 4: the `A6` resultant square means the trace polynomial is wrong

**Finding:** no.  Each orbit is parametrized from either reflection-axis
fixed coordinate.

**Repair:** the square multiplicity is explicitly recorded; the cubic factor
and its three real embeddings are the trace polynomial.

Major revision completed; proceed to independent reconstruction.
