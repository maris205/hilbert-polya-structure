# Research question

For the exact P56 incidence ladder, does the first untested discrepancy

\[
\Delta_5=E(A_5)+E(B_7)-E(A_6)-E(B_6)
\]

vanish?  If it does not, can its sign be certified without trusting decimal
root approximations, and is the corresponding finite witness sharp at width
six?

The project also asks for the smallest reusable algebraic description of
`A6`, `A7`, and `B7`, because these are the first data needed for a later
asymptotic study of \(\Delta_m\).
