## Selected theorem-level delta

The observable is the whole accumulation set of normalized native
prime-orbit counting, `N pi_f(N)/Lambda^N`, for every positive-entropy FAD
system with a unique dominant root. Its classical detector representation
is explicitly treated as input. With at least one active distortion prime,
the proposed theorem proves that this set is perfect and totally
disconnected, hence Cantor, and gives the finite-prime quantitative bound

`N_epsilon(L_f) <= C(1+log(1/epsilon))^(2d)`.

The genuinely additional proofs are:

1. `L` centers need only `1+(p-1)LK` adaptive valuation atoms at depth `K`,
   yielding the polylogarithmic covering count.
2. The Fourier coefficient of each active radial kernel is strictly
   negative at every nontrivial conductor. Among finitely many real
   exponent types one dominates asymptotically, so a positive summable
   combination of integer translates cannot be constant.
3. Applying that Fourier lemma in every cylinder upgrades the classical
   negative-integer slice choice already used by BCH. Coprime exponent
   periods supply an active center in every admissible ball, excluding
   isolated image values. The slice choice itself is not new.

This does not rely on an unjustified claim that continuous images of Cantor
spaces are Cantor, does not assert detector injectivity, and does not
promote continuum cardinality to topological classification. The same
proof covers genuine two-prime, wild, and mixed examples recorded in
`REALIZED_EXAMPLES.md`.
