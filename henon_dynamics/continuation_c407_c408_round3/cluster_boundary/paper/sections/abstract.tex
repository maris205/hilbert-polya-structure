\begin{abstract}
For an odd integer $k\geq3$, consider the finite cyclic relation algebra
defined over $\C$ by $x_i^k+1=x_{i-1}x_{i+1}$, with indices modulo $n$.
We determine its complete local Artin length at every alternating-zero
point, for every even clock $n=2m$. These points have a common local model
given by the logarithmic gradient of a formal dilogarithm potential.
Intersection additivity expresses its length as a sum over proper cyclic
subsets with run weights $1$, $k-1$, and $2k-1$, together with a full-cycle
term equal to $1$ unless $4\mid m$, when it is $4k+1$.
The proof resolves the one-dimensional cancelled path kernel and the
two-dimensional cycle kernel, including the exceptional cubic coefficients.
If $\ell_m(k)$ is the resulting local length and
$D_k(t)=1-t-(k-1)t^2-t^3-2kt^4$, then
\[
 \exp\!\left(\sum_{m\geq1}\frac{\ell_m(k)}m t^m\right)
 =\frac1{(1-t)D_k(t)(1-t^4)^{k-1}}.
\]
The alternating support is already known from rank-two cluster geometry;
the calculation here concerns its nonreduced thickness in the unsaturated
cyclic relation algebra. It neither classifies the other boundary supports
nor identifies the displayed series with an ordinary dynamical zeta function.
\end{abstract}
