# Primary-source ownership audit for the revised alternating-zero contract

2026-09-06. Scope: the new PROOF_PACKAGE.md, not a global novelty certificate.
No C-number or paper admission is assigned by this document.

## 1. Direct owner of the support: Beyer--Muller

James Beyer and Greg Muller, *Deep Points of Cluster Algebras*, IMRN 2025,
issue 4, rnaf027. Publication metadata was verified on the journal page.
The entire rank-two Section 4 of arXiv v1 was actually read, including the
proofs of Lemmas 4.1, 4.3, 4.4 and Theorem 4.5, and Corollary 4.6.

Their coefficient-free classification gives exactly the alternating pattern
`(0,alpha,0,alpha^(-1))`, and its other phase, with the stated root condition.
This owns the deep-point support. Our additional finite-clock root restriction
is elementary and is not presented as an independent contribution.
The inspected section does not calculate the local length of the unsaturated
finite cyclic relation algebra. Its ambient cluster variety is a different
scheme from that zero-dimensional relation algebra.

[Journal original](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnaf027),
[arXiv original v1, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.15589v1#S4).

## 2. Direct owner of ambient rank-two singularities: Benito--Faber--Mourtada--Schober

Angélica Benito, Eleonore Faber, Hussein Mourtada and Bernd Schober,
*Classification of singularities of cluster algebras of finite type II:
coefficients*, arXiv:2401.06758v2, revised 22 September 2025; accepted journal
metadata and DOI were verified from the arXiv record. The entire Section 5
was actually read, including Theorem 5.2.3 and its Jacobian proof.

For nonzero rank-two exponents over C their singular-locus equations give an
empty singular locus. Thus the relevant ordinary cluster surface is smooth.
This is a prior fact, not a result of our local-thickness calculation.
The theorem classifies singularities of the ambient cluster variety and its
coefficient fibers; it does not identify the finite unsaturated cyclic
relation algebra with a fixed scheme on that surface. The embedding-dimension
distinction in our package explicitly prevents that conflation.

[arXiv original v2, Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06758v2#S5),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/jca/117).

## 3. Direct dynamical owner: Grigorev--Kalidindi--Quintero Santander--Roeder

Andrei Grigorev, Krishna Chaitanya Kalidindi, Andres Quintero Santander and
Roland Roeder, *Complex dynamics perspective for birational maps of the plane
arising from cluster algebra mutations*, arXiv:2607.08125v2.
The primary metadata, displayed mutation maps, stable-model passages and
Remark 5.13 were inspected this round. The previous round's more extensive
source-reading boundary remains recorded in its frozen geometry report.

The diagonal mutation composition is F_k squared. Stable models, their
non-biregularity, Picard actions, dynamical degree and entropy are prior
inputs. None is claimed as new here. The new package makes no torus count
from a Picard trace. No local alternating-zero cyclic-algebra thickness
formula was found in the portions inspected; this is only a bounded search
finding.

[Primary original v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08125v2).

## 4. Very recent neighboring source: Faber--Schober

Eleonore Faber and Bernd Schober, *Singularities of star cluster algebras*,
arXiv:2609.01501v1, submitted 1 September 2026. The abstract, introduction,
Theorems A and B, and the defining star-shaped-quiver setup were read.
Its full proofs were not read or used.

It studies singularities of star cluster varieties and counts resolution
centers for intersections of compatible A1 hypersurfaces. This is relevant
recent context for combinatorial singularity classifications, but the stated
objects and invariants differ from our cyclic-relation Artin lengths.
No exclusion of an overlap beyond those inspected statements is claimed.

[Primary original v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01501v1).

## 5. Classical tools versus the proposed increment

The following are classical tools, and the package proves the particular
uses needed rather than claiming ownership of the general theory:

- formal implicit-function elimination of a nonsingular Hessian block;
- additivity of zero-dimensional complete-intersection multiplicity under
  replacing a product by its factors;
- gradient-colengths and the ordinary path/cycle adjacency spectra;
- labeled cyclic-word enumeration by a marked separator.

The proposed increment is their specific combination with the cyclic
exchange relations to obtain the all-odd-k, all-even-clock excess thickness
at every alternating-zero point. The nontrivial local calculations are the
cancelled path kernel (leading degree 2k, including coefficient -23 at k=3),
the two-dimensional cyclic kernel, and the exact aggregation of intersecting
coordinate/gradient branches. The rational multiplicity-generating function
is a consequence in the same contract, not another candidate.

The all-support boundary classification remains unresolved. These thicknesses
are not ordinary periodic-point multiplicities in the original torus domain.
