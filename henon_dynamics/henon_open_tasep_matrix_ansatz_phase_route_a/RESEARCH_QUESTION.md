# HCS-C220 research question

Can one give one source-locked, all-parameter theorem for the open totally
asymmetric exclusion process (TASEP), including its finite-state generator,
matrix-product stationary law, exact finite-size current, thermodynamic phase
boundaries, and absorbing parameter faces, while keeping the finite
reproducibility ledger separate from the analytic thermodynamic statement?

The frozen owner is the continuous-time chain on binary occupations
\(\eta\in\{0,1\}^L\).  Particles enter at the left boundary at rate
\(\alpha\), hop right at unit rate when the destination is empty, and leave
at the right boundary at rate \(\beta\).  The bulk rate is fixed to one, so
\(\alpha,\beta\) are dimensionless physical rates.  The source theorem is
classical and no priority or novelty claim is made.

The decisive checks are exact rational enumeration, an independently rebuilt
generator and nullspace, an independent DEHP-algebra evaluator, and a
symbolic divided-difference check at \(\alpha=\beta\).  The package records
the Route-A failure boundary explicitly: site labels and reservoir rates do
not supply an arithmetic primitive owner, target divisor, or Hilbert--Polya
operator.
