## Frozen conventions

Source/code lock for this package: `86c7bb8a39cdd1b8e941e45833b068170ca06287`.

The state is a binary word with site one on the injection side, \(D\) denotes
occupied and \(E\) empty.  Bulk hopping is exactly one.  Rates are continuous
time and non-negative.  The divided-difference form of \(Z_L\) is used so the
equal-rate diagonal is an explicit branch, not an unevaluated \(0/0\).

## Scope firewall

The literal scope is NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER.  No prime or zero table,
target label, fitted rate, Euler factor, root number, automorphy statement,
target divisor, or Hilbert–Polya operator is read or produced.  The matrix
Ansatz is not promoted to a target determinant.  Finite rational rows are
reproducibility sentinels only and do not establish the thermodynamic phase
law or a novelty/priority result.
