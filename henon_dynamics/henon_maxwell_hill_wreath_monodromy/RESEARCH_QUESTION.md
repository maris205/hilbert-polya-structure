# Research question

Let \(P_9(A)\) be the C33 period-five equal-action collision polynomial,
let \(K=\mathbb Q[A]/(P_9)\), and let \(\beta=N_H\in K^\times\) be the
symmetric product of the two Hill determinants above the generic Maxwell
node. If \(L\) is the splitting field of \(P_9\) and
\(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_9\) are the conjugates of \(\beta\), determine the
exact rank of

\[
\langle[\beta_1],\ldots,[\beta_9]\rangle
\subset L^\times/L^{\times2}.
\]

Equivalently, determine whether the normal closure of
\(K(\sqrt\beta)/\mathbb Q\) has the maximal possible quadratic wreath
group \(C_2\wr S_9\), or whether a hidden global relation collapses its
Kummer base.

The question is dynamical rather than a generic number-field exercise:
\(\beta\) is the gauge-invariant Hill decoration of a transverse
equal-action collision of two exact period-five Hénon orbits.
