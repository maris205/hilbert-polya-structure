\section{Full wreath monodromy}

Let
\begin{equation}
 M=L(\sqrt{\beta_1},\ldots,\sqrt{\beta_9}).                 \label{eq:M}
\end{equation}
Because the \(S_9\)-action permutes the \(\beta_i\), this field is normal
over \(\Q\).  It is the normal closure of the tower
\(\Q\subset K\subset K(\sqrt\beta)\): any normal extension containing
\(K(\sqrt\beta)\) contains every conjugate of \(K\), hence \(L\), and
then every \(\sqrt{\beta_i}\).

\begin{theorem}[Maxwell--Hill wreath group]\label{thm:wreath}
There are isomorphisms
\[
 \Gal(M/L)\cong C_2^9,
 \qquad
 \Gal(M/\Q)\cong C_2^9\rtimes S_9=C_2\wrp S_9.
\]
In particular, \(|\Gal(M/\Q)|=185{,}794{,}560\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Characteristic-zero Kummer theory identifies
\(\Gal(M/L)\) with the dual of the span of the square classes
\([\beta_i]\).  Theorem~\ref{thm:rank-nine} therefore gives
\(\Gal(M/L)\cong C_2^9\).

Restriction to \(L\) gives an exact sequence
\[
 1\longrightarrow C_2^9
 \longrightarrow\Gal(M/\Q)
 \longrightarrow S_9\longrightarrow1.                     \label{eq:exact}
\]
The standard quadratic normal-closure theorem embeds the middle group into
the imprimitive wreath product \(C_2\wrp S_9\), acting on the signed set
\(\{\pm\sqrt{\beta_i}:1\le i\le9\}\)
\citep{barquero2023wreath}.  No splitting of \eqref{eq:exact} is assumed.
The exact sequence gives
\[
 |\Gal(M/\Q)|=2^9 9!=|C_2\wrp S_9|.
\]
An embedded subgroup with the full ambient order is the entire wreath
product.  Equality supplies the semidirect-product description afterward.
\end{proof}

The associated norm polynomial is
\begin{equation}
 F_{18}(U)=\Norm_{K/\Q}(U^2-\beta)
          =\prod_{i=1}^{9}(U^2-\beta_i),                   \label{eq:f18}
\end{equation}
up to primitive integral scaling.  The full wreath group is transitive on
its eighteen signed roots, so \(F_{18}\) is irreducible over \(\Q\).  The
released certificate also verifies this independently modulo \(7\): Rabin's
test has gcd degrees zero for the prime divisors \(2,3\) of \(18\), and the
\(7^{18}\)-Frobenius remainder is zero.  The primitive coefficient ledger
appears in Appendix~\ref{app:ledgers}.

The signed set should not be misread as eighteen independent branchwise Hill
classes.  Each \(\beta_i\) is already the exchange-invariant product of the
two branch Hill determinants above one Maxwell node.  The Kummer base has
rank nine, not eighteen.
