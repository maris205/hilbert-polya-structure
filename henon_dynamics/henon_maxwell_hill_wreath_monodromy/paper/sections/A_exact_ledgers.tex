\paragraph{Scope of the exact computation.}
All promoted identities are over \(\Q\), finite quotient algebras over
\(\Q\), finite fields, or the exact Newton valuation ledger.  The producer
uses no floating-point root finder, Riemann-zero table, or prime-fitting
objective.  The finite primes \(7\) and \(19\) certify irreducibility and a
local square-class row, respectively; they are not assigned to primitive
H\'enon orbits.  The certificate concerns the nine symmetric two-branch
products \(\beta_i\), not eighteen independently normalized Hill values.
