# Narrative report

Perfect shuffling is a deterministic positional dynamics rather than a
randomization model.  For a \\(k,n\\) deck, the nonzero residues modulo
\\(M=kn+1\\) are multiplied by \\(k\\).  The congruence \\((k^r-1)i\\equiv0\\pmod
M\\) immediately gives every positive-iterate fixed count, while gcd strata
reduce each card position to a smaller modular order.  Möbius inversion then
separates least-period points from repetitions and division by the period
counts oriented cycles.

The resulting atlas is complete for every listed parameter pair and the
formula is uniform for all integer \\(k,n\\), including composite moduli.  A
finite permutation matrix supplies a source-local zeta factorization and
Koopman characteristic polynomial.  This is a substantial, reproducible
combinatorial theorem, but the deck modulus and shuffle clock carry no
intrinsic rational-prime labels or logarithmic arithmetic weights.  The
Route-A decision therefore stops at the arithmetic gate while preserving the
exact finite dynamics.
