# Research question

Can the entire \\(k,n\\)-multiway perfect-shuffle family be described by one
exact primitive/repetition ledger, including composite-modulus gcd strata and
the associated finite zeta and Koopman factors, without confusing modular
position arithmetic with an arithmetic prime clock?

Yes at the source-dynamics level.  Multiplication by \\(k\\) modulo \\(kn+1\\)
is a permutation; its fixed congruences, local multiplicative orders and
Möbius inversion give all cycles uniformly, and direct enumeration certifies
the declared grid.  No intrinsic prime/zero correspondence is present, so a
target arithmetic or Hilbert--Pólya promotion is explicitly rejected.
