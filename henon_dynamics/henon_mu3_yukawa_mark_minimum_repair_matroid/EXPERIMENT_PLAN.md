1. Bind C75, C76, C79 evidence and manifests by literal SHA-256.
2. For each deletion mask `D`, identify the fully deleted direction blocks,
   construct the loop/coloop/truncated-partition matroid, enumerate its bases,
   and verify the basis-exchange axiom directly.
3. Aggregate the ten `(rho,W)` templates and the five unlabeled exchange-graph
   types; record vertices, edges, degree spectra, radii, diameters, and pair
   distances.
4. Independently rebuild the C75 point-set closure table.  For every `D`, test
   restoration subsets in increasing cardinality, compare the resulting
   minimum witness family with the proposed matroid bases, and re-run exchange.
5. Expand C76's seven full-core-minimal orbits under the effective order-1920
   label action and compare their 25 masks with the bases for `D=L`.
6. Use SymPy to expand the block-state support polynomial and to check the
   25-by-25 line-graph adjacency matrix independently.
7. Require canonical replay, at least fourteen hostile semantic mutations,
   two isolated deterministic LaTeX builds, embedded-font and reference-log
   checks, text extraction, and visual inspection before freezing.
