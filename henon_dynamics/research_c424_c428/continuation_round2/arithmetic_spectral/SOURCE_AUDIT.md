`2010.07364v2`, and `2403.13383v2` did not provide usable full text in

`46118` was tried while seeking Raulf's paper and was not used as proof
evidence. One publisher-link click was rejected by the tool interface;
repeating with the retrieved page identifier reached the CMT metadata.
These are access limitations, not evidence of absent literature.
