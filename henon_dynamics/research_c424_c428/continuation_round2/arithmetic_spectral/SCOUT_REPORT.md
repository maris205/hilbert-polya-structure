## Why no helper was promoted

The maximal-order problem has genuine native arithmetic, but its
closest counting mechanism is already in primary literature. A short
Mellin/logarithmic-derivative consequence, a CRT obstruction, or a standard
covering identity cannot be substituted for the frozen all-level
analytic question. This is distinct from the retired finite U/support
calculation and C420's cusp-scattering question; those predecessors were
not reopened.

Browkin I is a genuinely different signed $p$-adic return mechanism.
Nevertheless the general recurrence and boundedness criterion are
already owned by
[Capuano–Murru–Terracini](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.07364v1#S4).
The real-embedding and digit-distribution limitations in
[Romeo (2025)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11139-025-01264-7)
cannot be removed by assumption. General periodic-fraction varieties
are not a proof that the actual frozen algorithm follows those digits.
No long unrepeated prefix, source abstract, or Haar-almost-every claim
was used to fabricate a negative quadratic case.
