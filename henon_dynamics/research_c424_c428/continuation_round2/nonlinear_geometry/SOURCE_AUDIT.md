## Primary sources actually inspected this round

1. **Eunju Shin, Character varieties on a four-holed sphere.**
   [Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16614),
   [v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.16614v3).
   The newest listed version is dated 1 June 2026; the first preprint
   was submitted 31 August 2023. The accepted-manuscript metadata lists
   Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 154 (2026), 4091–4106, DOI
   `10.1090/proc/17770`. Actual v3 reading: introduction and Theorems
   1.1–1.3, definitions of the generators and height graph, the initial
   low-coordinate formulas in Section 2.2, and Lemma 2.2 with its proof.
   Parts of Lemma 2.3's proof were inspected, not its complete proof or
   all of Section 3. Graph figures were not independently analyzed.
   The source supplies group-orbit equivalence and conditional
   fundamental-domain statements. It is not quoted as deciding
   periodicity of the single word `s_z s_y s_x`. The first open used
   [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.16614v2); after current metadata was
   inspected, v3 was opened and used for current theorem numbering.

2. **Serge Cantat, Bers and Hénon, Painlevé and Schroedinger.**
   [arXiv 0711.1727v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0711.1727v2), dated
   5 December 2007, 42 pages. Actual reading: introduction; Sections
   2.1–2.6, especially the full coefficient family, involution matrices
   and Proposition 2.2; Theorem 3.1 and its displayed proof; Proposition
   3.2 and its proof; Corollary 3.3 with its proof; initial Section 3.2
   definitions. Other search/open excerpts are not a whole-paper read.
   The inference used here is confined to algebraic stability and
   compact containment away from forward/inverse basins at infinity.
   No unseen quantitative radius or effective integer atlas is imported.
   The PDF's displayed `K(f)=K^-(f) intersect K^-(f)` is not adopted;
   the two-sided set here is expressly `K^+ intersect K^-`.
   The author's `bhps.pdf` URL returned **403 Forbidden**. The arXiv
   version was successfully inspected as the alternative; no bypass of
   that denied endpoint was attempted. The author's publication page
   search result locates the 2009 Duke article; it is a metadata lead,
   not a claim to have checked the final published theorem numbering.
