## Three nearby primary-source checks

### S1. Veech: toral periodic-data regularity

William A. Veech, *Periodic points and invariant pseudomeasures for toral
endomorphisms*, Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems **6** (1986),
449–473, DOI `10.1017/S0143385700003606`.
[Publisher PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5AF4A715A07BF8E0AC967645A282DA24/S0143385700003606a.pdf/periodic_points_and_invariant_pseudomeasures_for_toral_endomorphisms.pdf).

Actual body access: the introduction and displayed Theorems 1.1–1.2,
Corollary 1.4, Proposition 1.5 and surrounding regularity discussion.
The PDF identifies the real torus and complex Fourier framework. Its
periodic-data theorem yields smooth transfers for smooth observables over
ergodic toral endomorphisms; the weaker differentiability statement can
fail. This provides an analogy, not a polynomial theorem over an
algebraic closure of a finite field. The mathematical hypotheses do not
match PC424-L.

A subsequent attempt to locate/read Section 5 returned a no-match error
and then a timeout. No Section-5 proof read or full 25-page audit is
claimed. The printed 1986 date governs, not newer online/crawl labels.
The separate DOI open failed. The metadata is instead supported by the
publisher PDF and independently repeated in S2's reference 15; S2's
introductory discussion also describes the smooth toral result.

### S2. De la Llave–Saprykina: analytic families over an integrable base

Rafael de la Llave and Maria Saprykina, *Noncommutative coboundary equations
over integrable systems*, Journal of Modern Dynamics **19** (2023),
773–794, DOI `10.3934/jmd.2023020`.
[Publisher record and abstract](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/jmd.2023020),
[author-uploaded text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374575937_Noncommutative_coboundary_equations_over_integrable_systems).

Actual body access: author-uploaded Section 1.1 definitions, Theorem 3,
the following proof outline and remarks; not its complete proof. The
base is an angle–action torus system and the cocycle is an analytic
parameter family with identity initial value. The conclusion is an
analytic transfer on a possibly smaller complex domain. These are real/
complex analytic hypotheses, not the frozen algebraic finite-field ones.
In particular, existence of a formal solution is not permission to assume
that the chosen formal series itself converges.

The publisher record independently supplied the authors, affiliations,
volume/pages, DOI, received/revised dates and October 7, 2023 online
publication date. Its abstract matches the author text's scope. The
initial DOI request failed on a redirected URL; directly opening the
publisher path then succeeded. This is not recorded as a successful
DOI-resolver check. The displayed NSF support was observed; no full
funding/COI audit was conducted.

### S3. Farmer–Horadam: different polynomial group-cohomology problem

D. G. Farmer and K. J. Horadam, *A polynomial approach to cocycles over
elementary abelian groups*, Journal of the Australian Mathematical
Society **85** (2008), 177–190, DOI `10.1017/S1446788708000876`.
[Publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/polynomial-approach-to-cocycles-over-elementary-abelian-groups/9955D096780D776AFECD65C9B80D96BB).

Actual access is limited to the publisher-indexed abstract and metadata
returned by the search, plus a landing-page attempt; a later focused
landing-page request timed out. The DOI redirect and its direct product
endpoint also failed. No article proof or PDF was read.

The abstract concerns bivariate group 2-cocycles and coboundaries on an
elementary abelian finite group. Its finite-dimensional polynomial
formulae are not the dynamical difference operator of PC424-L. It is
retained only as an explicitly excluded terminology match, not as a
regularity input or a theorem proved over the wrong object.
