## AS424-I — repeat-stable cyclic kernels in mixed-prime isogeny paths

Parameters: any prime p; an integer M >= 3; a finite nonempty set L of
distinct primes; gcd(M,p product(L))=1 and p not in L. Vertices are
isomorphism classes of supersingular elliptic curves over algebraic closure
F_p with a full ordered M-torsion basis eta. An l-edge is a degree-l
isogeny from (E,eta) to (E',phi eta), modulo isomorphism. Full level removes
automorphisms fixing the marked object. Choose representatives only to write
matrices; no conclusion may depend on those choices.

Native clock: one isogeny edge. Retain color variables x_l and the separately
intrinsic cumulative logarithmic degree, sum log l = log(degree phi).
No degree is replaced by a desired target coefficient or an adjustable roof.

Observable: the generating series of based closed chronological words w for
which the endomorphism alpha_w has cyclic geometric kernel under every
positive repetition. Equivalently, the primitive Euler product is over cyclic
rotation classes of such words, with reversal not identified. A one-pass
cyclic kernel and a graph-theoretic nonbacktracking word are different tests.

Question: construct a finite source-defined transfer realization for every
parameter tuple and prove its exact primitive/repetition multiplicities,
including words omitting some colors. The proposed state records one line
in E[l] for each actually active l. An inclusion-exclusion over allowed
colors must precede summing supports. A naive trace on memories for all L
is not accepted: unused colors can carry several fixed lines of holonomy.

Cheap decisive test: rank-one torsion linear algebra must prove that a
closed word has exactly one surviving closed memory for each active prime
if and only if every repetition has cyclic kernel. Then compare against
Arpin et al. (2025 v2/2026 publication), Lau et al. (2025 v1), ordinary
Ihara determinants, and Hecke operators with level structure. Finite-state
rationality alone is not automatically a substantial new paper.

## AS424-D — ordinary spectral trace of a rational-Witt orbit packet

Use Deninger's construction for X_0=Spec Z, without changing the space:
X_0^dyn = check-X_0(C) x_{Q_{>0}} R_{>0}, where check-X is the colimit of
the injective Frobenius maps on the finite-kernel character locus of the
rational-Witt complex points. The flow multiplies the positive-real
coordinate by exp(t). Closed points p have compact packets Gamma_p of
periodic orbits, all of native period log p, with transverse compact group
G_p = (product_{q != p} Z_q^*) / closure(<p>).

The exact bounded question is whether a single packet, on its ordinary
Haar L2 realization, has a trace-class smoothed Koopman operator with a
nonzero invariant Fourier component. It is not the global conjectural
cohomological trace formula and does not authorize assigning packet mass
one and calling it the arithmetic trace. The cheap test is the dimension
of L2(G_p) inside the zero-frequency invariant subspace. A short ordinary
trace obstruction is not a replacement proof of the global trace formula.
