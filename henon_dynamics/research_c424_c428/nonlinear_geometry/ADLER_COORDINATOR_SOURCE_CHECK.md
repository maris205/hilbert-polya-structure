  construction in Section 5. The general two-involution torsion
  restriction and existence inputs are positively source-owned. The
  source is used as a published existence dependency, not as an
  independently recomputed rank table. Its institutional metadata and
  alternative PDF access failures were checked by the smooth reviewer,
  not rerun here. Not all Poncelet configuration tables were audited.
- [Adler, Construction of triad maps, arXiv nlin/0606056v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/nlin/0606056v1):
  the coordinator read the introduction, construction theorem and
  examples through the body of the nine-page preprint; the terminal
  reference list was not fully read. Generic compositions of invariant
  involutions and QRT-type constructions are prior mechanisms. No
  all-rational, factorwise-native Adler classification was inferred
  from an unseen statement or from this preprint's title.
- The exact Yang–Baxter transfer, polynomial invariants and native versus
  extended transfer distinction retain the Veselov/Kassotakis source

  The coordinator read that primary text in the initial pass; this
  follow-up does not count it as a second fresh search or read.
