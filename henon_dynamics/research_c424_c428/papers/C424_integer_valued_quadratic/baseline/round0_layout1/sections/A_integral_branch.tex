\section{The precisely imported monic integral classification}
\label{app:integral}

This appendix states the exact prior result used for even $m$.
It is Theorem 1.1 and Tables 1--2 of the anonymous internal
manuscript C412~\cite{c412}; its proof is not claimed as part
of the present increment. The accompanying file
\path{evidence/C412_main.pdf} is an exact copy of that complete
manuscript, whose proof contains its own analytic reduction and
finite-complement evidence.

\begin{theorem}[C412, complete monic integral theorem]
\label{thm:imported}
Let $\alpha,\beta\in\Z$ and
$H_{\alpha,\beta}(x,y)=(y,y^2+\beta y+\alpha-x)$.
Write uniquely $\beta=2h+e$, $h\in\Z$, $e\in\{0,1\}$, and put
\begin{equation}
 D=\alpha-h^2+(2-e)h.
 \label{eq:C412_parameter}
\end{equation}
Every rational periodic point is integral.
The complete rational periodic cycles are obtained by taking
Table~\ref{tab:C412_even} for $e=0$ or
Table~\ref{tab:C412_odd} for $e=1$, and subtracting $h$ from
every listed coordinate. At overlapping parameters take the
union, counting a coincident cycle only once.
All exact periods are in $\{1,2,3,4\}$, and there are at most
eight rational periodic points. Equality holds exactly when
$e=1$ and $D=-4$.
\end{theorem}

The translation underlying this statement is
$(x,y)\mapsto(x+h,y+h)$, which sends
$H_{\alpha,\beta}$ to $H_{D,e}$ by direct substitution.
For the even branch of Theorem~\ref{thm:whole_class} use
$\alpha=\kappa r$, $\beta=n-\kappa$ and
\eqref{eq:C412_parameter}. A displayed coordinate $w$ then
corresponds to the original coordinate
\begin{equation}
 x=\frac{w-h}{\kappa}.
 \label{eq:even_recovery}
\end{equation}
All small-index clauses in the imported tables remain necessary
after this affine recovery. They should not be confused with
Table~\ref{tab:families}, where both three-cycles have exact
period three even at $k=0$.
