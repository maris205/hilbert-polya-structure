## 3. External sources and exact versions/access

### S1. Complete period-four algebra: closest direct owner

Antônio Endler and Jason A. C. Gallas, *Arithmetical signatures of the
dynamics of the Hénon map*, Physical Review E **65** (2002), 036231,
DOI `10.1103/PhysRevE.65.036231`.
[Publisher record](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.65.036231),
[author-hosted printed article](https://inaesp.org/PublicJG/pre_EG02.pdf).

The publisher metadata and abstract were read. A browser open of the
author PDF timed out. Two later read-only `curl | pdftotext` streams
succeeded, both with exit zero: requested extracted lines 1--290, followed
by lines 135--245 to recover the portion obscured by combined-output
truncation. The actually used body is the introduction, §II and §II.A,
printed pages 036231-1--3, especially equations (2)--(11). The rest of
the eleven-page article was not fully read.

The source map is `(u,v) -> (A-u^2+Bv,u)`. The rational linear change
`(u,v)=(-y,-x)` identifies our map with `A=-c, B=-1`. The source's
conservative orbital-sum factorization and repeated scalar factor
already own the period-four algebra. Our explicit splitting-field
comparison is a short consequence/reconstruction. It is not claimed as
a newly discovered period-four mechanism. Repeated scalar coordinates
are not nonreduced affine phase points.

set*, [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02204),
[v3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.02204v3).

The record reports v1 on 2024-05-03, v2 on 2024-05-15, and v3 on

This is an applicability comparator, not a proof dependency: its

question here. No equivalence or reduction between the two is claimed.
