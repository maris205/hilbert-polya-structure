## Object, parameters, domain, clock and arithmetic carrier

Let `c` be transcendental over the rational numbers, put
`K = Q(c)`, and fix exactly

\[
 H_c(x,y)=(y,y^2+c-x),\qquad R(x,y)=(y,x).
\]

The domain is all of `A^2(overline K)`. No affine point is removed. The
Jacobian is one, and `R H_c R = H_c^{-1}`. One application of `H_c` is one
native time step. Reversal is a separately defined symmetry, not an
identification of time or of points.

For every integer `n >= 1`, let `P_n` be the ordinary geometric points of
exact least period `n`. Let `L_n/K` be the field generated by both
coordinates of every point in `P_n`, and set

\[
 K_N=L_1L_2\cdots L_N,\qquad
 G_N=\operatorname{Gal}(K_N/K),\qquad N\geq1.
\]

The observable is the actual permutation action of `G_N` on the disjoint
union of `P_1,...,P_N`, together with the restriction maps for increasing
`N`. The integer `N` bounds the arithmetic layers being observed; it does
not replace the native iterate clock. Coordinates, periods, and incidence
under `H_c` and `R` are retained. Scheme length is used only to prove that
these ordinary points exhaust a finite reduced scheme.

The arithmetic carrier is the compatible tower of these splitting fields
over `Q(c)`, including their intersections and constant fields. It is not
an inverse arboreal tree: `H_c` is invertible. The problem is generic in the
parameter, not a claim about every rational specialization of `c`.

## Full question and required output

Determine `G_N` and its indicated permutation representation for **every**
`N`, by one structural description with proved restriction maps. Equivalently,
determine the closed image of the absolute Galois group of `K` on all
periodic points, with an explicit description of each finite projection.

A complete answer must identify the relations coupling the reversal
types within a period and the different period fields, not merely list
the possible centralizer containing the image. It must prove both the
upper constraints and attainment of the proposed image, including the
constant-field issue. An unbounded sequence of separate elimination/Galois
computations is not the prescribed structural answer. Neither a theorem
at one frozen `c`, nor a result with a different Jacobian parameter, closes
this question.

An initially tempting **hypothesis to test**, not part of the question's
definition, was that the group on each `P_n` is the whole centralizer of
the native dihedral action. The period-four hand test disproves it. The
full determination question survives unchanged; its answer is not presumed
to be maximal.

## Cheap decisive test and its actual result

The chosen finite diagnostic is a **hand calculation at exact period four**:
separate the two reversal types, compute their splitting fields, and test
whether those fields can vary independently. Failure of independence kills
the full-centralizer hypothesis immediately; it does not settle the
correct all-period image.

The test finds a shared quadratic field `Q(c)(sqrt(-c))`. The actual group
on all exact period-four points has order eight, while the dihedral
centralizer has order sixteen. The [complete hand proof](PROOF_PACKAGE.md)
records the radical independence, the exact action, and the source deduction.
No numerical parameter value, selected prime, random sample, period census,
or computer Galois call is used.

The only infinite step obtained is also written there: a simultaneous
formal expansion at parameter infinity proves generic reducedness,
regularity of every finite joint splitting field, and a single
symbol-complement inertia involution. This is an auxiliary classical-method
result, not the missing global image theorem.
