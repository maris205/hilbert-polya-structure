# Research question

Can one make the autonomous Coulomb oscillator mathematically unambiguous at
zero velocity and then close its complete stick–slip capture atlas, including
the exact boundary, arbitrary signed initial velocity, and the harmonic
zero-friction face?

The answer is yes for the explicitly frozen maximal-monotone/viability law.
The result is deliberately narrow: no stochastic friction, delayed state,
external forcing, impact reset, or general differential inclusion is inferred.
