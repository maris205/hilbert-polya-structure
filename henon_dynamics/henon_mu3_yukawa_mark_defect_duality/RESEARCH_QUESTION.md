# C68 research question

How does the C65 mark-saturation defect sit inside the finite cokernel of the
C64 restricted mark map, and what is its exact row-side annihilator?

The answer is a source-bound integral quotient theorem.  It must determine
the embedded subgroup `D`, the quotient `C/D`, and the matching quotient of
the explicit congruence lattice on the transpose side without choosing a
canonical Smith basis.
