# Research question

How does the faithful finite label symmetry compress the 400 ordered pairs of
C88 first-passage targets, and does the full C90 joint law descend exactly to
that quotient?

C97 answers this by reconstructing the order-1920 label group, inducing its
diagonal action on ordered targets, and certifying each orbit against every
joint-survival cell, mixed moment, and covariance in C90.  The answer is an
exact finite quotient, not an arithmetic, spectral, or full table-of-marks
statement.
