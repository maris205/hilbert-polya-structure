# Paper Plan

## Title

Tagged Prime-Ideal Packets and the Rational Pushforward Obstruction for Hénon
Monodromy

## Central claim

The inversion-fixed cyclotomic half packets of finitely many signed H6
monodromies admit an exact tagged prime-ideal divisor assembly.  Rational
norm pushforward is exact but noninjective; good-characteristic packet atoms
carry an intrinsic cyclotomic residue-order clock.

## Sections

1. Motivation: why HCS-P49 leaves ideals rather than scalar primes.
2. Signed H6 trace fields and inversion-fixed half packets.
3. Finite tagged divisor assembly and exact norm pushforward.
4. Good-characteristic residue-order theorem.
5. Exact H6 collision certificate and the kernel-rank obstruction.
6. Source audit and the all-orbit theorem that remains open.
7. Strict Route-A evaluation and conclusion.

## Claim discipline

- Use `PROVED` only for the finite algebraic assembly, norm identity, order
  lemma, and free-kernel calculation.
- Use `NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED` for the exact finite H6 counts and collisions.
- Use `STOP_SCOPED` for injective rational-prime scalarization.
- Use `OPEN` for pressure-weighted all-orbit convergence, von Mangoldt
  weights, analytic continuation, and any Hilbert--Pólya operator.

## Adversarial controls

- Preserve the negative period-three multiplier branch.
- Reject the order claim when the residue characteristic divides the index.
- Reconstruct every rational norm from residue degrees and ideal valuations.
- Exhibit separate witnesses for orbit, index, and prime-ideal tag loss.
- Check the free-kernel rank rather than infer noninjectivity from anecdotes.
