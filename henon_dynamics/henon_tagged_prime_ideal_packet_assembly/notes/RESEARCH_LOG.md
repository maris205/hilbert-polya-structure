# Research Log

## 2026-08-14 — question selection

HCS-P49 stopped full-field norm scalarization but left trace-field half norms
and prime ideals open.  The selected next question was the smallest
source-native assembly problem: build a finite tagged divisor object before
asking for all-orbit convergence.

## Algebraic construction

For each signed H6 control, the cyclotomic half packet was reconstructed as a
polynomial in the trace variable \(t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}\).  Exact integral
bases were used for \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt7)\), \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), and
\(\mathbb Q\).  Principal ideals were factored into ramified, inert, and
split trace primes.  Hensel lifting recovered split-prime valuations.

The first important correction was conceptual: exact residue order belongs
to a multiplier-field prime above the trace prime.  The paper now keeps
\(\mathfrak q\) and \(\mathfrak P\) separate.

## Certificate

The frozen range \(3\le n\le20\) gives 54 orbit/index rows and 125 tagged
atoms.  All rational norm exponents reconstruct exactly.  Good
characteristic produces 105 exact-order atoms; 20 bad-characteristic atoms
remain outside the theorem.

The rational-prime collapse has 95 image coordinates and kernel rank 30.
The strongest finite witnesses are:

- \(p=29\): orders 7, 14, and 15;
- \(p=109,n=11\): two split primes for period one and one for period three;
- \(p=38039\): orders 13 and 19 from different orbits.

## Claim correction

The initial phrase “minimal tagged ledger” was rejected because no category
of encodings had been defined.  The final claim is “canonical and lossless
relative to the fixed finite source data.”  Likewise, noninjectivity of the
rational pushforward is not promoted to impossibility of all scalar
statistics.

## Decision

The result is a genuine finite algebraic bridge section, not an analytic
Route-A success.  The next natural theorem is a pressure-cutoff convergence
theorem for an all-orbit vector-valued tagged divisor measure.  Rational
pushforward should be derived only after that source object is controlled.
