# Hostile Review

No external reviewer or subagent was used.  Both rounds were performed as
claim-by-claim adversarial audits against the proof package, certificate, and
Route evaluators.

## Round 0: object and theorem audit

### Issues

1. The draft called the construction the “first exact” assembly without an
   exhaustive novelty theorem.
2. Calling the tagged group “lossless” risked making a definitional choice
   sound like a difficult theorem.
3. The phrase “minimal ledger” would have required a category of encodings
   and a universal property that were not defined.

### Fixes

- Removed the priority claim.
- Added a remark separating the definitional retention of tags from the
  substantive H6 coefficient, norm-pushforward, and residue-order results.
- Replaced global minimality by “canonical relative to the fixed signed
  source data.”

## Round 1: obstruction and quantifier audit

### Issues

1. A positive free-kernel rank could, by itself, rely on signed differences
   and might overstate loss on effective packet data.
2. The exact-order statement had to distinguish the trace-field prime
   \(\mathfrak q\) from a multiplier-field prime \(\mathfrak P\) above it.
3. The bad-characteristic boundary was too weakly phrased as “order can
   drop.”

### Fixes

- Added the direct effective-cone witness at \(p=109,n=11\): three distinct
  effective degree-one atoms map to the same \([109]\).
- Kept \(\mathfrak q\) and \(\mathfrak P\) separate throughout the theorem
  and proof.
- Strengthened the boundary: if \(p\mid n\), exact order \(n\) is impossible
  because a finite-field multiplicative group has order prime to \(p\).

## Round 2: asymptotic and source audit

### Issues

1. Finite collision counts might be misread as evidence for an asymptotic
   collision density.
2. Noninjectivity might be misread as excluding every weighted scalar
   statistic.
3. Primitive-divisor sources might be imported beyond their fixed-sequence
   coefficient classes.

### Fixes

- Added an explicit finite-only paragraph: the certificate is an exact
  counterexample to injectivity, not an asymptotic law.
- Repeated that useful weighted pushforwards remain open; only lossless
  identification stops.
- Added a source-scope section distinguishing fixed cyclic, Lucas/Lehmer,
  quadratic-unit, and fixed-map dynamical results from the missing
  varying-orbit H6 theorem.
- Added a reproducibility paragraph binding the JSON ledger, generated table,
  and eleven adversarial tests.

## Final verdict

`PASS` within the stated boundary.

- Finite tagged assembly: proved.
- Good-characteristic exact clock: proved.
- Rational-prime-only injectivity: refuted on the exact H6 source data.
- All-orbit convergence and analytic Route A: open.
- Route B: not authorized.
