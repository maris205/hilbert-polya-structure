# Source Audit

## Primary sources checked

1. Christopher J. Hillar, “Cyclic Resultants,” *Journal of Symbolic
   Computation* 39 (2005), 653--669,
   [arXiv:math/0401220](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401220),
   DOI `10.1016/j.jsc.2005.01.001`.
   This supplies cyclic-resultant reconstruction context.  It does not
   construct the H6 tagged divisor ledger.

2. Christopher J. Hillar and Lionel Levine, “Polynomial Recurrences and
   Cyclic Resultants,” *Proceedings of the AMS* 135 (2007), 1607--1618,
   [arXiv:math/0411414](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0411414),
   DOI `10.1090/S0002-9939-06-08672-2`.
   This supplies recurrence context for a fixed polynomial.  It does not
   address varying primitive H6 orbits, signed branches, or pressure weights.

3. Anthony Flatters, “Primitive Divisors of Some Lehmer--Pierce Sequences,”
   *Journal of Number Theory* 129 (2009), 209--219,
   [arXiv:0708.2190](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2190),
   DOI `10.1016/j.jnt.2008.05.008`.
   Its quadratic-unit theorem supports the period-four boundary recorded in
   HCS-P49.  It is not an all-orbit packet assembly theorem.

4. Yuri Bilu, Guillaume Hanrot, and Paul M. Voutier, “Existence of Primitive
   Divisors of Lucas and Lehmer Numbers,” *Journal für die reine und
   angewandte Mathematik* 539 (2001), 75--122,
   [DOI 10.1515/crll.2001.080](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.2001.080).
   The coefficient classes are Lucas/Lehmer sequences.  The result cannot be
   substituted for a theorem about the varying H6 trace fields and orbit
   weights.

5. Patrick Ingram and Joseph H. Silverman, “Primitive Divisors in Arithmetic
   Dynamics,” *Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical
   Society* 146 (2009), 289--302,
   [arXiv:0707.2505](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2505),
   DOI `10.1017/S0305004108001795`.
   Its object is an orbit of a fixed rational map.  It does not identify
   prime ideals in monodromy multiplier fields across distinct H6 periodic
   orbits.

6. Joseph H. Silverman, “Primitive Divisors, Dynamical Zsigmondy Sets, and
   Vojta's Conjecture,” [arXiv:1209.3491](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.3491).
   This is useful for the dynamical-Zsigmondy landscape but remains a
   wrong-object source for the present tagged monodromy packets.

## Source-backed versus elementary content

The main P50 theorem does not require a deep external attachment.  It uses
standard facts about ideal factorization, ideal norms, cyclotomic
factorization, and separability.  HCS-P49 supplies the specialized H6
integrality statement and signed multiplier data.  The exact finite H6
certificate is independently executable.

The cited literature is used only to locate the result in cyclic-resultant
and primitive-divisor theory and to state what those sources do **not**
provide.

## First source-level gap

No checked source proves a pressure-weighted vector-valued divisor limit
over all primitive H6 orbits with:

- the actual signed multiplier branch;
- orbit and cyclotomic-index tags;
- trace-field prime ideals, residue degrees, and valuations;
- an exact derived rational pushforward;
- convergence strong enough to form a trace or determinant.

That missing theorem is the next analytic gate.  Primitive divisors in one
fixed sequence do not fill it.
