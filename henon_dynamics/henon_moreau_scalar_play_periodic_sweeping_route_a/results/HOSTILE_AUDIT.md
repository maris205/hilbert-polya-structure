# Hostile audit

Attacks cover identity/date/source changes, evaluator authority and hashes, Route-A layers, forbidden flags, theorem/model extras, noncanonical rationals, chamber swaps, fixed-set changes, broken idempotence, omitted initial/path nodes, reparameterized-path changes, false variation receipts, duplicated case coordinates, collision/source drift, enumeration extras, duplicate/nonfinite JSON, and YAML timestamp/alias/merge/non-string/unknown/missing-field attacks.

Every evidence attack is tested both with its stale hash and after repairing the canonical payload hash. All are rejected.
