# Research question

For the scalar Moreau play operator of radius `r >= 0`, driven by a periodic `W1,1` input that makes one monotone rise from its minimum to its maximum and one monotone return per period, can one close the exact segment solution, Poincaré map, all three excursion chambers, finite-time entrainment, order/nonexpansion, admissible time-reparameterization invariance, variation and dissipation, including plateaus, corners, `r=0`, and `D=0`?

The frozen answer is yes. No differentiability beyond absolute continuity and no post-event weak continuation are needed.
