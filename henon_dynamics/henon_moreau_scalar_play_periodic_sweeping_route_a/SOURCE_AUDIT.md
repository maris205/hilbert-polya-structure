# Source audit

## Verified authority records

1. J.-J. Moreau, “Evolution Problem Associated with a Moving Convex Set in a Hilbert Space,” *Journal of Differential Equations* 26 (1977), 347–374, DOI `10.1016/0022-0396(77)90085-7`. This is the moving-convex-set evolution owner.
2. M. A. Krasnosel'skii and A. V. Pokrovskii, *Systems with Hysteresis*, Springer, 1989, DOI `10.1007/978-3-642-61302-9`. This is an authoritative play/hysteresis source.
3. M. Brokate and J. Sprekels, *Hysteresis and Phase Transitions*, Springer, 1996, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4612-4048-8`. This is an authoritative scalar-play, convexity, and dissipation source.

The DOI records were checked against title, authors, publisher or venue, and year. We claim no priority for Moreau sweeping or the play operator. The package contribution is a convention-locked reconstruction of the single-excursion periodic theorem and its executable audit.

## Claim boundary

- The result covers scalar `W1,1` single-excursion forcing only.
- Plateaus and corners are included; discontinuous forcing and vector/nonconvex sweeping are not.
- The Poincaré clamp is not an Euler product or target determinant.
- No target local data, root number, automorphy, functional equation, divisor, zero match, or Hilbert–Pólya operator is asserted.
