# Paper plan

## Round 0 — determinant and full spectrum

Freeze the killed generator, derive the sine kernel, and prove the
Karlin--McGregor/Slater theorem with dimension-count completeness and an
explicit positive ground determinant.

## Round 1 — absorption and immortal conditioning

Add the entire survival/CDF/density/moment atlas, leading two-energy
asymptotics, unique QSD, Doob rates, invariant `h^2` law, and relaxation gap.

## Round 2 — hostile boundaries and release evidence

Close `k=1`, full occupancy, `L=k=1`, time zero, and long time; separate
killing from reflecting exclusion; expose evidence cutoffs, Route-A tuple,
scope/nonclaims, source ownership, and AI use.

The final paper is intentionally short: one coherent theorem package rather
than several incremental papers.
