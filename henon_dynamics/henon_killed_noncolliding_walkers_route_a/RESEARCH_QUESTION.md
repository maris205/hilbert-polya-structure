# Research question

For `k` independent continuous-time symmetric nearest-neighbour walks on
`{1,...,L}`, killed at a boundary or first coincidence, can one close the
entire finite-state law rather than only a transition determinant?

The required closure is simultaneous:

1. diagonalize the one-particle Dirichlet chain;
2. turn antisymmetry into a complete `binom(L,k)`-mode chamber basis;
3. recover survival, absorption density, and all lifetime moments as exact
   finite sums;
4. identify the positive ground state, unique QSD, Doob rates, invariant law,
   and relaxation gap;
5. close `k=1`, `k=L`, `L=1`, `t=0`, and long-time faces without importing
   reflecting-exclusion conventions.

The answer is affirmative.  The analytic owner is exterior-power spectral
theory plus the Karlin--McGregor cancellation.  The finite atlas is a
regression certificate, not the proof for unbounded `L`.
