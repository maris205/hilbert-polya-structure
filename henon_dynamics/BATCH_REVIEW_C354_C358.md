  boundary-flip coupling proves the almost-sure radial speed and CLT without
  using a non-stopping last-return time.  The recurrent \(F_1\cong\mathbb Z\)
  arcsine and half-normal boundary is closed separately.
- **C356:** the complete QWZ--Thouless mass-chamber atlas.  The direct gap
  closes exactly at \(m=-2,0,2\); the lower-band projector defines a smooth
  line bundle in every other chamber; and a frozen \(dk\wedge d\tau\)
  orientation gives Chern values \(0,-1,+1,0\), with all four Dirac jumps
  accounted for.  For a filled lower band and unit positive particle charge,
  \(J=\partial_kH\) and
  \(Q=-(2\pi)^{-1}\int\Omega_{k\tau}=c_1\) only in the gapped adiabatic
  limit.  Electron charge adds \(-e\); finite-speed exact quantization is not
  asserted.
- **C357:** the complete two-stiffness bilinear oscillator theorem.  Every
  nonzero classical trajectory is bounded and has one energy-independent
  least period exactly when both one-sided frequencies are positive.  The
  action is linear in energy, the punctured plane has a global \(C^1\)
  piecewise-analytic action--angle chart, and the full-return map and
  monodromy are the identity; the chart is not \(C^2\) at the seam unless the
  frequencies agree.  The Friedrichs quantization has compact resolvent and
  simple discrete spectrum characterized exactly by the plus-sign
  parabolic-cylinder interface Wronskian.  Equal-stiffness, zero-energy,
  one-flat-side and free faces are explicit.
- **C358:** the complete May--Leonard strict cyclic-chamber trichotomy.  The
  normalized product \(R=xyz/(x+y+z)^3\) is the global source Lyapunov
