# C185 research question

For every real symmetric matrix with simple spectrum and every strict diagonal
target `N`, can the Brockett double-bracket equation be closed as one all-size
dynamical theorem—global flow, exact Lyapunov law, every equilibrium and tangent
mode, generic sorting, recurrence obstruction, and repeated-spectrum
boundary—and does that closure cross any Route-A gate?

The mathematical answer is yes for the all-size dynamical theorem.  The
Route-A answer is no.  The spectra are arbitrary ordered real lists, not an
intrinsic arithmetic source; the strict Lyapunov law rules out every
nonconstant periodic orbit; no source-owned dynamical determinant or target
analytic structure is produced.  The state-dependent skew Lax generator gives
only an A4 formal hint.

The question deliberately excludes a full Bruhat/Schubert cell-closure theorem
and a classification of repeated-spectrum critical manifolds.  Those are not
needed for the generic convergence statement and would exceed the source lock.
