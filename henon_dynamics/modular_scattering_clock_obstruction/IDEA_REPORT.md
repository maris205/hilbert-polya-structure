# Breadth-first idea report for HCS-C17

## Search mandate

HCS-C16 ended with a compact rank-two arithmetic clock but the wrong periodic
geometry and a compact spectral count of order \(T^2\).  The next round was
therefore required to change the spectral mechanism, not merely the
quaternion algebra or split prime.  The foundational area-preserving Hénon
model remains the local motivation, but no claim from it was assumed.

## Selection

The modular one-cusp system was selected because it simultaneously provides:

1. a source-locked noncompact dynamical system;
2. exact arithmetic multiplicities \(\varphi(c)\);
3. a completed-zeta quotient in a natural scattering operator;
4. a countable symbolic/transfer-operator positive control;
5. a yes/no compatibility question that can be settled without a zero table.

The frozen question is:

> Can the denominator that indexes modular cusp scattering channels define a
> nontrivial total clock on closed hyperbolic conjugacy classes while
> preserving the standard primitive/repetition law?

This formulation keeps the strongest arithmetic signal while making the
open-channel/closed-orbit bridge explicit and falsifiable.

## Kill and continuation criteria

The candidate is killed as a closed-clock proposal if any of the following is
proved:

- final-denominator data is not cyclic/conjugacy invariant;
- every denominator-only total clock satisfying repetition is trivial;
- the only canonical stable closure is the already known Selberg length;
- the scattering quotient cannot become a single entire target under the
  source-allowed normalization class.

It would continue toward Route B only if a clock-preserving Fredholm or
self-adjoint construction survived those gates with an intrinsic primitive
object and a controlled divisor.

All four kill conditions occur.  The result is therefore retained as HCS-C17,
an obstruction project, rather than extended through small parameter sweeps.

## Next high-risk escapes

The no-go theorem deliberately leaves two qualitatively different, large-step
directions:

- an endpoint-extended or local cocycle whose cyclic Birkhoff sum retains
  cusp arithmetic without depending only on the final denominator;
- a genuinely multi-cusp matrix scattering/groupoid determinant whose
  primitive objects are open channels rather than closed hyperbolic orbits.

Either revival must freeze its category first.  It must not identify powers
of a double coset with repeats of one open scattering trajectory, and it must
not replace chronological cocycles by averaged transitions.
