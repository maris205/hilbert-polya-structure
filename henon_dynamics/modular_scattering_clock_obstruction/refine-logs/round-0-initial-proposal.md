# Research Proposal: Open--Closed Clock Separation on the Modular Surface

## Problem Anchor

- **Bottom-line problem:** determine whether the noncompact modular surface supplies a source-faithful dynamical route from cusp returns to the Riemann scattering divisor and a Hilbert--Pólya structure, without treating a classical zeta ratio as a new construction.
- **Must-solve bottleneck:** the same intrinsic clock must support primitive-cycle repetition, a dynamical determinant, and the claimed Riemann arithmetic.  It is not enough for these properties to occur in different parts of the modular theory.
- **Non-goals:** no proof of RH; no direct zero fitting; no claim that Gauss/Mayer, Selberg zeta, Eisenstein scattering, or cuspidal acceleration is new; no replacement of chronological products by an averaged transition matrix.
- **Constraints:** use the fixed source object \(\Gamma=\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)\), its cusp stabilizer \(\Gamma_\infty\), exact matrix chronology, and standard normalizations.  Riemann-zero data are forbidden in candidate selection and may be used only for a post-theorem divisor illustration.
- **Success condition:** prove a normalization-stable theorem deciding whether the cusp-denominator weight that produces \(\zeta(2s-1)/\zeta(2s)\) can be the periodic roof of the same return dynamics; provide exact witnesses, independent code checks, and a scoped Route-A ruling.

## Technical Gap

The classical modular theory contains two rigorous but categorically different mechanisms.

1. Parabolic Eisenstein scattering groups open cusp channels by their lower-row denominator \(c\), giving
   \[
   \sum_{c\geq1}\frac{\varphi(c)}{c^{2s}}
   =\frac{\zeta(2s-1)}{\zeta(2s)}.
   \]
2. Periodic Gauss words and hyperbolic conjugacy classes use the additive geodesic length
   \(\ell(\gamma)=2\log\lambda_\gamma\), giving the Mayer--Selberg determinant.

The unresolved Route-A question is not whether either identity exists.  It is whether the first clock can be promoted to the closed periodic-orbit clock of the second without losing chronology or inserting a compensating zeta divisor.  Existing transfer-operator work establishes the Selberg side but does not make the parabolic denominator a conjugacy-invariant, repetition-additive closed-orbit roof.

## Method Thesis

- **One-sentence thesis:** the Riemann zeta ratio in modular scattering is generated by an open-channel double-coset height that fails both conjugacy/cyclic invariance and periodic repetition, whereas the unique standard closed-geodesic repair restores the Selberg rather than the Riemann determinant.
- **Why this is the smallest adequate intervention:** the claim is decided by exact \(2\times2\) matrix identities and the source-derived double-coset decomposition; no new dynamical system or fitted operator is needed.
- **Why no frontier-model component is used:** this is an exact mathematics problem.  An LLM, learned operator, or data-driven surrogate would weaken source provenance rather than solve the clock mismatch.

## Contribution Focus

- **Dominant contribution:** an open--closed clock separation theorem for \(\Gamma_\infty\backslash\Gamma/\Gamma_\infty\) versus primitive hyperbolic conjugacy classes, including a universal power-law obstruction
  \[
  c(\gamma^k)=c(\gamma)U_{k-1}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{tr}\gamma}{2}\right).
  \]
- **Supporting contribution:** a zero-free-normalization no-go showing that the completed scattering coefficient retains two shifted nontrivial divisors and cannot become a single entire \(\xi\) determinant by cusp normalization.
- **Explicit non-contributions:** the Eisenstein constant-term formula, the totient Dirichlet series, Mayer's Fredholm identity, Selberg zeta continuation, and scattering unitarity are classical inputs/controls.

## Proposed Method

### Complexity Budget

- **Frozen/reused backbone:** \(\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)\), \(\Gamma_\infty=\langle T\rangle\), standard Eisenstein normalization, Gauss inverse branches, and the standard hyperbolic length.
- **New components:** one exact source ledger comparing open double cosets, chronological matrix products, cyclic representatives, and repetitions; one independent checker.
- **Tempting additions intentionally excluded:** congruence levels, multi-cusp twists, finite-dimensional colors, new transfer-operator spaces, and numerical root fitting.

### System Overview

```text
primitive bottom rows (c,d)
        |
        v
parabolic double cosets ----------------> totient Dirichlet series
        |                                      |
        | exact chronology test                v
        +------------------------------> scattering coefficient

periodic Gauss words ------------------> hyperbolic conjugacy classes
        |                                      |
        v                                      v
cyclic/repetition tests ----------------> geodesic length -> Selberg/Mayer
```

The experiment never replaces a product \(g_1\cdots g_n\) by a mean matrix.  Every word, cyclic shift, conjugate, and repeat is retained as an exact integer matrix.

### Core Mechanism

1. Prove that nontrivial parabolic double cosets are indexed by \(c>0\) and \(d\in(\mathbb Z/c\mathbb Z)^\times\), hence their height partition sum is the totient Dirichlet series.
2. Prove that \(|c(\gamma)|\) is not invariant under full \(\Gamma\)-conjugacy.  Record an exact smallest witness and a periodic Gauss-word cyclic-shift witness.
3. Apply Cayley--Hamilton to prove
   \[
   \gamma^k=U_{k-1}(t/2)\gamma-U_{k-2}(t/2)I,
   \qquad t=\operatorname{tr}\gamma,
   \]
   and therefore the lower-left-entry formula above.
4. Show that \(2\log|c(\gamma^k)|=k\,2\log|c(\gamma)|\) cannot hold simultaneously at \(k=2,3\) for any \(c(\gamma)\ne0\).  Thus the scattering height cannot be the integral of a roof along a repeated periodic return.
5. Contrast with \(\ell(\gamma)=2\operatorname{arcosh}(|t|/2)\), which is conjugacy invariant and power additive and is precisely the Selberg/Mayer clock.

### Supporting Divisor Mechanism

Write
\[
\Phi(s)=\frac{\Lambda(2s-1)}{\Lambda(2s)}.
\]
For every nontrivial zeta zero \(\rho\), \(\Phi\) has a pole at \(\rho/2\) and a zero at \((1+\rho)/2\), with no cross-cancellation.  A one-cusp scaling changes \(\Phi\) only by a zero-free exponential factor.  Therefore no allowed cusp normalization or other entire zero-free prefactor can turn \(\Phi(as+b)\) into the single entire function \(\xi(s)\).

### Failure Modes and Diagnostics

- **Double-coset count is mis-normalized:** enumerate primitive bottom rows independently and compare exactly with Euler's totient.
- **The matrix witness lies outside the chronological coding:** provide both a group-conjugacy witness and a positive Gauss-branch cyclic-shift witness.
- **Power failure is only exceptional:** prove the Chebyshev identity for all \(\gamma\in\mathrm{SL}_2\) with \(c\ne0\), then use \(k=2,3\) for a universal contradiction.
- **A different cusp scaling repairs the clock:** track the scaling factor and prove it is zero-free and cannot alter either the repetition defect or the scattering divisor.
- **The result is overstated as a universal no-go:** restrict the conclusion to the standard modular scattering coefficient and any chronology-preserving closed-cycle realization that uses its literal denominator height.

### Novelty and Elegance Argument

Mayer, Series, Lewis--Zagier, Pohl--Wabnitz, and the classical scattering literature completely cover the two endpoints.  The residual contribution is a Route-A compatibility theorem: the arithmetic object with the Riemann ratio and the object with valid primitive periodic cycles use inequivalent clocks.  The proof is deliberately elementary because the bottleneck is categorical, not numerical.  The claim is a scoped obstruction and synthesis, not a new scattering or transfer-operator formula.

## Claim-Driven Validation Sketch

### Claim 1: the scattering denominator is not a periodic-orbit roof

- **Minimal experiment:** exact double-coset/totient census; exact conjugacy and cyclic-shift witnesses; exact repetition ledgers through several powers; symbolic verification of the Chebyshev formula.
- **Controls:** geodesic length on the same matrices; digit-product and trace-only surrogates; multiple positive Gauss words.
- **Metric:** zero exact-identity failures; all denominator roofs fail at least one of cyclic invariance or repetition, while geodesic length passes both.
- **Expected evidence:** a proof-backed certificate, not statistical evidence.

### Claim 2: the scattering quotient cannot be normalized into one \(\xi\)

- **Minimal experiment:** generate a formal divisor ledger and high-precision illustrations at frozen zeta zeros only after the theorem is proved.
- **Controls:** verify \(\Phi(s)\Phi(1-s)=1\) and unit modulus on the physical line; distinguish poles/resonances from \(L^2\) eigenvalues.
- **Metric:** exact affine locations and multiplicities of the paired shifted divisors; numerical residuals used only as regression checks.
- **Expected evidence:** an analytic no-go under zero-free normalization.
