# Narrative report

P67 answered *which* sampler is intrinsic. P68 asks the next macroscopic
question: what determinant-like object that sampler actually supports.
There is a natural answer in the open disk—a packet Euler product whose
logarithmic derivative has the correct primitive/repeated combinatorics.

The same computation exposes its limitation. At the half-entropy boundary,
the logarithm has a simple pole, so the product itself has an exponential
essential singularity. This is stronger information than a finite zeta fit:
it both constructs a canonical analytic germ and proves why that germ is not
yet a meromorphic Fredholm determinant. The next paper must recover the
individual exponential moments erased by periodwise averaging.
