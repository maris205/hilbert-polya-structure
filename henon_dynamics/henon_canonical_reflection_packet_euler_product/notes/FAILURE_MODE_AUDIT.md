# Failure-mode audit

- **Primitive/repeated mixing:** blocked by the exact divisor-sum derivative.
- **Gauge dependence:** blocked by P67's finite telescoping theorem.
- **Lind-zeta relabeling:** blocked by the source definition and explicit
  odd/even/full-subgroup mismatch.
- **Finite-series continuation:** replaced by a local analytic decomposition
  at the first positive boundary point.
- **Orbit-resolution overclaim:** explicitly excluded; one aggregate mean is
  used per period.
- **Arithmetic promotion:** certificate freezes `arithmetic_advance=NO`.
