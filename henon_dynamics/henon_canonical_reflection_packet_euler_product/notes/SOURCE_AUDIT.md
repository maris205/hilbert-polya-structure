# Source audit

- Kim, Lee and Park (2003) define flip systems as infinite-dihedral actions,
  define their Lind zeta by finite-index subgroups, and prove the decomposition
  into the ordinary zeta and odd/even flip fixed-point series. This is the
  controlling definition for the Lind-zeta firewall.
- Ryu (2017) extends the finite-order reversal formalism and gives matrix
  formulas for shifts of finite type and sofic shifts.
- Devaney–Nitecki and Arai supply the inherited Hénon/full-shift conjugacy
  context; P64–P67 supply the actual packet and sampler used here.

No source is cited as proving the P68 packet product. Conversely, P68 does
not rename the standard Lind zeta or claim novelty for group-action zeta
theory.
