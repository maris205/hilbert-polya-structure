# ROUND2 clue

For individual canonical orbit weights `a_{n,j}`, the periodwise exponential
moment is `D_n^{-1} sum_j exp(-s n a_{n,j})`, whereas P68 retains only
`exp(-s n b_n)`. Their logarithms agree at first order in `s` and separate at
second order by the within-period variance. The next project should prove the
exact cumulant identity and decide whether the variance remains nonzero for a
natural P65 symmetry-defect potential.
