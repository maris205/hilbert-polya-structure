# Methodology blueprint

1. Freeze the P64/P67 packet counts and canonical sampler by SHA-256.
2. Define one explicit Euler convention before inspecting coefficients.
3. Expand its logarithm and derivative formally and analytically.
4. Recompute primitive counts by two independent Möbius procedures.
5. Recover the logarithmic derivative from product coefficients alone.
6. Separate the restricted packet product from the source-defined Lind zeta.
7. Classify the first boundary singularity exactly rather than extrapolating
   from finite coefficients.
8. Reject arithmetic, Fredholm, and Route-B promotions in the certificate.
