# Paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

The natural two-channel Mellin scattering symbol of the inhomogeneous H6
cubic boundary satisfies reciprocity and critical-line unitarity exactly,
but a certified simple even-channel zero creates an off-critical divisor in
the open strip and rejects the unrenormalized Route-A candidate.

## Claims--evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence | Location |
|---|---|---|
| exact analytic symbol and symmetries | contour rotation, hypergeometric continuation, parity diagonalization | Sections 2--3 |
| one simple off-line strip zero | Arb enclosures, analytic second-derivative majorant, Rouch\'e inequality | Section 4 |
| no local or Riemann-target cancellation | full-ball lower bounds for mirror, odd, linear-parent, and completed-\(\xi\) factors | Section 4 |
| no ordinary Fredholm determinant | multiplication-operator compactness lemma | Section 5 |
| next large pivot is homogeneous anomaly/index | explicit gamma--trigonometric homogeneous symbol and coboundary warning | Section 6 |

## Section plan

1. Introduction: symmetry is necessary but not sufficient.
2. Context: C35 boundary orbit, Mellin reduction, and prior art.
3. Exact symbol: continuation, recurrence, parity scattering.
4. Certified obstruction: Rouch\'e disc and no-cancellation theorem.
5. Operator and Route-A interpretation.
6. Homogeneous pivot: anomaly or closure.
7. Conclusion and limitations.
8. Appendices: majorant and reproducibility contract.
