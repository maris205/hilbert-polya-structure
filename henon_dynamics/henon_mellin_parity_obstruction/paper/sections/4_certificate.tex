\section{A certified off-critical divisor}

Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:disc}
c=0.7286922241147175+1.6054479123346985i,
\qquad r=10^{-12},
\qquad \Disc=\{z:|z-c|\le r\}.
\end{equation}
Both coordinates of $c$ and the radius are stored as exact rationals.

\begin{theorem}[Certified H\'enon Mellin obstruction]\label{thm:obstruction}
The even symbol $A$ has exactly one zero, counted with multiplicity, in
$\Disc$. That zero is simple. The disc lies in
$0<\RePart z<1$, is disjoint from $\RePart z=1/2$, and satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nonzero}
\inf_{\Disc}|A(1-z)|>3/10,
\quad \inf_{\Disc}|B(z)|>4/5,
\quad \inf_{\Disc}|B(1-z)|>13/10.
\end{equation}
For
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi}
\xi(z)=\tfrac12z(z-1)\pi^{-z/2}\Gamma(z/2)\zeta(z),
\end{equation}
one also has \(\inf_{\Disc}|\xi(z)|>9/20\).
Consequently $\det S_H$ has one pole in $\Disc$ and one zero in
$1-\Disc$, plus their conjugates. All four are off the critical line in the
open strip and are additional to the Riemann divisor.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Certified ball evaluation gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:center-bounds}
|A(c)|<10^{-16},\qquad |A'(c)|>2/5.
\end{equation}
The contour estimate in \cref{app:majorant} gives
$\sup_{\Disc}|A''|<1200$. On $|z-c|=r$,
\begin{align*}
|A(z)-A'(c)(z-c)|
&\le |A(c)|+\tfrac12\sup_{\Disc}|A''|r^2\\
&<10^{-16}+600\cdot10^{-24}
<\tfrac25\,10^{-12}
\le |A'(c)(z-c)|.
\end{align*}
Rouch\'e's theorem compares $A$ with the linear function
$A'(c)(z-c)$, proving the zero count. Count one also proves simplicity.
Complex-ball evaluation on the full input discs proves
\eqref{eq:nonzero}. Direct Arb evaluation of \eqref{eq:xi} on the full disc
gives the stated \(9/20\) lower bound; no Riemann-zero table is read. The
functional equation gives the corresponding nonvanishing on \(1-\Disc\).
The determinant identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:detS}
\det S_H(z)=\frac{A(1-z)B(1-z)}{A(z)B(z)}
\end{equation}
then gives the local divisor statement. Real-type symmetry gives the
conjugates.
\end{proof}

The natural linear reference does not cancel the pole. Its even symbol is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:linear}
A_{\mathrm{lin}}(z)=2(2\pi)^{-z}\Gamma(z)\cos(\pi z/2),
\end{equation}
and the certificate proves $\inf_{\Disc}|A_{\mathrm{lin}}|>7/10$.
No Riemann-zero data enter this conclusion.
