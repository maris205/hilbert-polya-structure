\section{Rotated-contour majorant}\label{app:majorant}

After the contour rotation, differentiation under the integral gives
\begin{align}
\kappa_\sigma''(z)
=e^{\sigma i\pi z/6}\int_0^\infty
&e^{-4\pi t^3+2\pi e^{-\sigma i\pi/3}t}t^{z-1}\notag\\
&\times\left(\log t+\frac{\sigma i\pi}{6}\right)^2dt.
\label{eq:second}
\end{align}
On the certified disc,
$\RePart z>18/25$ and $|\Im z|<161/100$. Use
$\pi<22/7$, $e^\pi<24$, and
$e^{\pi(161/100)/6}<3$. For $0<t<1$, discard the negative cubic term and
bound the remaining logarithmic moment by
\begin{equation}
\int_0^1 t^{18/25-1}\left(|\log t|+\pi/6\right)^2dt<8.
\end{equation}
For $t\ge1$, the exponent satisfies
$-4\pi t^3+\pi t\le-9t$, and
\begin{equation}
\int_1^\infty e^{-9t}(t+1)^2dt<1
\end{equation}
dominates the remaining factor. Each sign in \eqref{eq:second} is then
bounded by 579. Summation yields $|A''|<1158<1200$ on the disc.

The bound is deliberately coarse. Its role is to produce a transparent
analytic inequality, while Arb supplies the much sharper center and
companion enclosures.
