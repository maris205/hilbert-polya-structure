# HCS-C36 certified results

The independently replayed certificate proves that the even Mellin symbol

\[
A(z)=\kappa_+(z)+\kappa_-(z)
\]

for the Hénon chirp `P6(u)=2u^3-u` has exactly one simple zero in the closed
disc of radius `10^-12` centered at

\[
0.7286922241147175 + 1.6054479123346985 i.
\]

The disc lies strictly inside `0 < Re(z) < 1` and strictly to the right of the
critical line. The proof combines independent Arb enclosures with the exact
Rouché inequality

\[
10^{-16}+600(10^{-12})^2 < (2/5)10^{-12}.
\]

On the same registered discs, the checker certifies that `A(1-z)`, `B(z)`,
`B(1-z)`, the linear-phase parent factor, and the completed Riemann \(\xi\)
function are all nonzero. The \(\xi\) lower bound is \(9/20\) and is obtained
directly by Arb, without a zero table. Consequently, the unrenormalized
formal parity scattering determinant has a genuine extra
pole on this disc and a reflected zero on `1-D`, together with the conjugate
pair. This blocks the unrenormalized candidate; a post-hoc deletion of the
zero is not admissible.

The result is deliberately local and negative. It neither supplies a global
zero census nor constructs an operator determinant. The next large-door
candidate is the homogeneous cubic Hénon phase, where the Mellin symbols are
explicit Gamma factors and the remaining question is whether a Poisson
boundary quotient carries a nontrivial anomaly or merely a removable scaling
coboundary.
