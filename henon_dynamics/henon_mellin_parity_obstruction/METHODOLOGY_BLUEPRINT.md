# Methodology blueprint

## Claim map

### Primary claim

The most natural Mellin/parity scattering symbol generated by the H6 cubic
Poisson boundary has the right reciprocal/unitary symmetry but an extra
off-critical divisor in the zeta-relevant even channel.

### Supporting claim

The natural linear-phase scaling parent does not contain the same nonreal
factor, so a cancellation is either a genuinely new coupled-parent theorem
or a removal of the Hénon content itself.

### Anti-claim

Critical-line unitarity plus \(z\leftrightarrow1-z\) reciprocity does not by
itself give an RH-compatible divisor.

## Experiment blocks

### B1. Exact symbol derivation — must run

Derive the rotated-contour gamma series, hypergeometric formula, conjugation
law, recurrence, pole parity, and the parity diagonalization of \(K(z)\).

### B2. Certified local zero — passed

Use complex-ball arithmetic or a Rouché/Krawczyk inequality on a small disc
around \(z_0\). Independently evaluate \(A\) by:

1. the rotated-contour integral;
2. the absolutely convergent gamma series;
3. the grouped \({}_1F_2\) formula.

Certify that \(A\) has exactly one simple zero in the disc and that the disc
does not meet \(\operatorname{Re}z=1/2\).

### B3. No-cancellation gate — passed

Certify that \(A(1-z)\), \(B(z)\), and \(B(1-z)\) are nonzero on the same
disc. This promotes the local zero to an extra pole of \(\det S_H\).

### B4. Parent controls — passed

Compare with

\[
A_{\rm lin}(z)=2(2\pi)^{-z}\Gamma(z)\cos(\pi z/2)
\]

and certify the completed Riemann \(\xi\) function on the same disc. The
linear divisor cannot cancel the nonreal \(z_0\), and direct nonvanishing of
\(\xi\) proves that the Hénon point is genuinely additional.

### B5. Global strip census — appendix

Use interval argument principle on nested rectangles. This is not required
to kill the candidate; one certified extra divisor already suffices.

## Stop/go protocol

- Certified extra divisor with no parent factor:
  `STOP_UNRENORMALIZED_ROUTE` — triggered.
- Independently derived parent factor cancels it: audit whether the entire
  Hénon contribution becomes a coboundary/trivial quotient.
- Post hoc Hadamard removal or zero table division: `FORBIDDEN`.

The project will not scan Riemann zeros or fit a spectral affine map.

The next large-door object is the homogeneous phase \(P_0(q)=2q^3\). Its
gamma--trigonometric Mellin symbol has no open-strip divisor; the only
remaining source of nontriviality is a quotient index/anomaly.
