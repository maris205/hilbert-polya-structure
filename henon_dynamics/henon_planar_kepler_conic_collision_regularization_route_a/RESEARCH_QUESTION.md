# Research question (HCS-C216)

For the planar attractive Kepler Hamiltonian with \(\mu>0\), can one close a single, convention-locked theorem package that simultaneously handles:

1. the invariants \(E,L\) and Runge–Lenz vector \(A\), including both algebraic identities;
2. the complete \(L\ne0\) conic classification for \(E<0\), \(E=0\), and \(E>0\);
3. the exact Kepler period, radial action with its closed-cycle normalization, and hyperbolic scattering angle;
4. the finite-time \(L=0\) collision incompleteness;
5. the Levi–Civita change \(q=u^2,\;dt=|u|^2d\tau\), its fixed-energy oscillator equation and constraint; and
6. the obstruction to ordinary isolated Artin–Mazur counting on resonant negative-energy shells?

The answer is yes at the classical planar identity/configuration level.  It is not a Route-A arithmetic or Hilbert–Pólya construction: the coefficients and energy shells carry no intrinsic prime carrier, and a time-period resonance fixes a positive-dimensional continuum rather than isolated primitive orbits.
