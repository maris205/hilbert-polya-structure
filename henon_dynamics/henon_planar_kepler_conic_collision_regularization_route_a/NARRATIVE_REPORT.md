# Narrative report

## Progress made

This paper advances the Kepler line by closing the boundaries that are usually left implicit in a short orbit classification.  The Runge–Lenz identities connect the algebraic invariants to the polar conic for every energy sign.  The negative-energy period and radial action are normalized on the same radial cycle, while the positive-energy branch receives an explicit scattering angle.  The zero-angular-momentum branch is not silently included in the conic formula: it reaches the removed collision point in finite physical time.

The Levi–Civita substitution then explains exactly what can be continued.  The fixed-energy \(\tau\)-equation is a linear oscillator/repulsor according to the sign of \(E\), and its energy constraint remains explicit.  At \(u=0\), the configuration equation is smooth but the physical time density vanishes.  This is a useful regularization boundary, not permission to claim a global canonical map.

Finally, Kepler's period depends only on energy.  For every integer resonance \(T=mP(E)\), a time-\(T\) map fixes the entire collision-free three-dimensional energy shell, so an isolated Artin–Mazur primitive-orbit count stops at the source dynamics itself.
