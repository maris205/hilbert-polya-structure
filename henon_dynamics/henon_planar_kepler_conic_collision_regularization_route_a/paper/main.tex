\begin{abstract}
We close a convention-locked theorem package for the attractive planar Kepler
Hamiltonian.  Runge--Lenz identities classify every nonzero-angular-momentum
orbit as an ellipse, parabola, or hyperbola; the negative-energy period and
radial action and the positive-energy scattering angle are normalized exactly.
The zero-angular-momentum branch reaches the removed collision in finite
physical time.  Levi--Civita's fixed-energy change of variables gives a smooth
configuration equation, while a time-period resonance fixes a continuum rather
than isolated primitive orbits.

\section{Frozen Hamiltonian and invariants}
Identify the plane with \(\C\) only when the Levi--Civita map is introduced.
For \(q\in\R^2\setminus\{0\}\), \(p\in\R^2\), and \(\mu>0\), set
\begin{equation*}
 H(q,p)=\frac{|p|^2}{2}-\frac{\mu}{r},\qquad r=|q|.
\end{equation*}
Hamilton's equations are \(\dot q=p\) and \(\dot p=-\mu q/r^3\).  Along a
collision-free solution define
\begin{equation}
 E=H,\qquad L=q_1p_2-q_2p_1,\qquad
 A=(|p|^2-\mu/r)q-(q\!\cdot\!p)p .                         \tag{1}
\end{equation}
Differentiation gives \(\dot E=\dot L=\dot A=0\).  Taking the scalar product
and norm of \(A\) gives the two identities
\begin{equation}
 A\!\cdot q=L^2-\mu r,\qquad |A|^2=\mu^2+2EL^2 .          \tag{2}
\end{equation}

\section{All-energy conics, period, action, and scattering}
Suppose \(L\ne0\), let \(e=|A|/\mu\), and measure \(\theta\) from \(A\).
Equation (2) is equivalent to
\begin{equation}
 r(\theta)=\frac{L^2/\mu}{1+e\cos\theta},\qquad
 e^2-1=\frac{2EL^2}{\mu^2}.                                \tag{3}
\end{equation}
Thus \(E<0\) is an ellipse (a circle when \(e=0\)), \(E=0\) a parabola, and
\(E>0\) a hyperbola.  In the bound case put \(a=-\mu/(2E)\) and
\(r_\pm=a(1\pm e)\).  Kepler's period and the radial momentum are
\begin{equation}
 P(E)=2\pi\mu(-2E)^{-3/2},\qquad
 p_r^2=2\left(E+\frac\mu r\right)-\frac{L^2}{r^2}.          \tag{4}
\end{equation}
The normalization is part of the theorem, not a convention left implicit:
\begin{equation}
 J_r=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint p_r\,\dd r
 =\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{r_-}^{r_+}p_r\,\dd r
 =\frac{\mu}{\sqrt{-2E}}-|L|,\qquad E<0 .                \tag{5}
\end{equation}
The turning-point substitution also gives \(\partial_EJ_r=P(E)/(2\pi)\).
For \(E>0\), the asymptotic directions of (3) have \(\cos\theta_\infty=-1/e\),
so the unsigned hyperbolic deflection is
\begin{equation}
 \chi=2\arcsin(1/e),\qquad e>1 .                          \tag{6}
\end{equation}

\section{The radial collision boundary}
When \(L=0\), the inward branch obeys
\begin{equation}
 \dot r^2=2(E+\mu/r),\qquad
 t_{\rm coll}(r_0)=\int_0^{r_0}\frac{\dd r}{\sqrt{2(E+\mu/r)}}<\infty, \tag{7}
\end{equation}
for every admissible starting radius.  The endpoint \(r=0\) is absent from the
physical phase space, hence the physical flow is incomplete.  With
\(\alpha=-E>0\), \(u=\arcsin\sqrt{\alpha r_0/\mu}\) gives
\[
 t_{\rm coll}=\frac{\mu}{\sqrt2\alpha^{3/2}}
 (u-\sin u\cos u);
\]
for \(E=0\) it is \(2r_0^{3/2}/(3\sqrt{2\mu})\), while for \(E>0\), with
\(u=\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{Er_0/\mu}\), it is
\[
 t_{\rm coll}=\frac{\mu}{\sqrt2E^{3/2}}
 (\sinh u\cosh u-u).                                       \tag{8}
\]
These formulas separate the collision boundary from the \(L\ne0\) conic
classification; no collision is silently counted as a periodic orbit.

\section{Levi--Civita equation and what is (not) extended}
Write \(q=u^2\) with \(u\in\C\), and set \(\dd t=|u|^2\dd\tau\).  On a fixed
energy level, direct substitution gives
\begin{equation}
 u''=\frac E2u,\qquad 2|u'|^2-E|u|^2=\mu,\qquad
 L=2\operatorname{Im}(\bar u u').                           \tag{9}
\end{equation}
The equation in \(\tau\) is smooth through \(u=0\), so (9) supplies a
configuration-level collision continuation.  The physical time density then
vanishes at the collision.  We make no claim of a global canonical
transformation, a full Ligon--Schaaf symplectomorphism, or a three-dimensional
regularization theorem.

\section{Fixed-time resonance and strict stop}
Every collision-free ellipse at energy \(E<0\) has the same period \(P(E)\).
Consequently a time-\(T\) map has the fixed continuum
\[
 \{H=E,\ L\ne0\}\quad\text{whenever}\quad T=mP(E),\qquad m\in\mathbb N. \tag{10}
\]
This set has dimension three in the four-dimensional phase space, so ordinary
isolated Artin--Mazur primitive-orbit counting is undefined on the resonant
shells.  Parabolic and hyperbolic noncollision trajectories escape and have no
finite period.
