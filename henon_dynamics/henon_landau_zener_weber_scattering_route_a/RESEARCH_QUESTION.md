# Research question

Can one obtain a theorem-scale, source-local scattering invariant from a
nonautonomous avoided crossing while keeping the analytic and arithmetic
claim boundaries explicit?

We study the two-level Landau–Zener–Weber system
`i psi_dot=((v t/2) sigma_z+g sigma_x) psi`, with `v>0`, real `g`, and
physical time.  The target deliverable is one complete result: Weber scalar
reduction, exact infinite-time connection law, Stokes/Gamma phase, unitarity,
monotonicity and singular limits, plus independently reproducible finite-window
ODE controls.  Finite-window numerics are validation data, not a replacement
for the asymptotic theorem.

The Route-A question is deliberately negative as well as constructive: does
this natural scattering structure provide a primitive orbit owner, target
divisor, or arithmetic carrier?  The answer is no on source-local evidence,
so Route B is not invoked.
