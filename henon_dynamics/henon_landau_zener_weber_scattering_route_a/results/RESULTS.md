# Results

The canonical receipt contains 5 exact asymptotic scattering rows, 15
finite-window matrices, and 6 boundary rows.  The five cases span reference,
fast/weak, slow/strong, negative-coupling, and `g=0` limits.  Each scattering
row satisfies `P_diabatic=exp(-2*pi*g^2/v)`, the Gamma/Stokes phase formula,
the derivative identity, and the displayed SU(2) invariants.

The finite-window rows are deterministic 2048-step RK4 integrations on
`[-T,T]` for `T∈{2,4,8}`.  They report all four complex matrix entries,
`P_window`, discrepancy from the asymptotic law, and the Gram residual.  The
largest residual is below `1.6e-5`; discrepancies are intentionally retained
as finite-window effects and are not presented as theorem errors.

The receipt payload hash is regenerated by the producer and checked by the
independent checker, symbolic cross-check, replay, and mutation suite.  The
Route-A tuple is `(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,
A4_UNITARY_OR_SCATTERING_CANDIDATE)` with overall `ROUTE_A_REJECTED`.
