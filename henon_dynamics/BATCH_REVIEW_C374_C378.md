## Outcome

This round delivers five independent theorem packages from frozen baseline
`f58422d8f03235329863f946654981ecb5d4dc97`.  Their owners are a Kummer
arboreal tower, a quaternionic arithmetic Cayley-graph family, a compact

## Independent theorem increments

- **C374:** the complete basepoint-two Kummer arboreal image and root-prime
  law.  For every `n>=3`, the pure radical and `2^n`-cyclotomic fields meet
  exactly in `Q(sqrt(2))`.  The shared quadratic field cuts the affine Galois
  image by `(-1)^b=(2/a)`; all adjacent restrictions are onto with kernel
  four, and the inverse limit is the corresponding closed index-two
  `2`-adic affine subgroup.  Solving the affine fixed-index congruence gives
  the complete fixed-root spectrum, excludes exactly one and four roots, and
  yields Chebotarev density
  `7/24+1/(3*4^(n-1))`.  The finite Koopman actions are canonical, but the
  paper does not promote them beyond an A4 formal hint.
- **C375:** the exact norm-five LPS nonbacktracking atlas.  The Legendre
  symbol `(5/q)` selects the connected `PSL_2(F_q)` nonbipartite or
  `PGL_2(F_q)` bipartite chamber for every eligible prime.  With `n` graph
  vertices, the six-regular Bass--Hashimoto identity is
  `det(tI-H)=(t^2-1)^(2n)det(t^2I-tA+5I)`.  It determines every trace,
  orientation-distinguished primitive cycle, and finite-graph Ihara product.
  The LPS bound puts all nontrivial quadratic Hashimoto roots on
  `|mu|=sqrt(5)`, with the `+/-6` adjacency exceptions stated separately;
  the two arithmetic chambers have conditional prime density one half.
- **C376:** the complete classical and quantum flat magnetic-torus closure.
  Every positive-energy orbit at nonzero field has least period
  `2*pi/|B|`, with maximally clean returns and torus orbit quotient.  A
  magnetic line bundle exists exactly at integral flux
  `N=BL_xL_y/(2*pi)`.  Its physical Bochner Hamiltonian has energies
  `|B|(n+1/2)` of multiplicity `|N|`, while fixed positive division vectors
  retain orientation through
  `UV=zeta_|N|^(sgn N) VU`.  Heat trace, Hurwitz spectral zeta,
  `det_zeta H_B=2^(|N|/2)`, scalar revival at one classical period, identity
  revival at two, and the metric-normalized zero-field closure boundary are
  all explicit.
- **C377:** the arbitrary-mean periodic Constantin--Lax--Majda solution and
  exact first-pole atlas.  The frozen Hilbert sign and periodic Tricomi
  identity give `z_t=i*mu*z+z^2/2`; invariance of the strictly negative Hardy
  subspace proves the reduction in both directions.  Exact zero- and
  nonzero-mean Moebius formulas give necessary-and-sufficient forward
  breakdown clocks and the complete one-mode phase diagram.  At every simple
  first zero the paper proves a transverse inverse-time profile.  The global
  two-sided `L^infinity` rate is asserted only when every simultaneous first
  zero is simple, leaving tangent and higher-order zeros inside the exact
  breakdown theorem but outside the rate claim.
- **C378:** the all-finite-`N` beta-two Dyson--Ornstein--Uhlenbeck spectral
  theorem.  Trace-metric matrix covariance gives unit eigenvalue noise,
  reciprocal-gap drift, and linear confinement without hidden rescaling.
  The Karlin--McGregor determinant has exact Vandermonde mass, so its shifted
  Doob transform is conservative, reversible for the ordered GUE law, and
  noncolliding from every interior start.  Divided Hermite Slater
  determinants form a complete orthogonal basis indexed by partitions,
  with eigenvalues `-|kappa|/2`, exact norms, multiplicities `p_N(k)`, sharp
  gap `1/2`, partition-product heat trace, source Fredholm determinant, and
  the beta-two free-fermion oscillator transform.

## Author-swapped corrections and integrity

the strict evaluator showed that a fixed-root law is not a complete
all-level primitive/repetition atlas with orientation, phase, weights,
stability, and the six mandatory A1 controls.  Its finite permutation

mixed composites with at least two distinct prime factors and no
single-prime Frobenius owner.  Evidence, checker, symbolic, mutation,
evaluation, manuscript, and release layers were regenerated and re-reviewed.
