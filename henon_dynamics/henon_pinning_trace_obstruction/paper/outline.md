# Paper route: a negative-result research note

## Working title

**Exact Pinning Kernels for an Area-Preserving Hénon Map: Domain
Certification and Two Operator Obstructions**

## Intended status

This is a rigorous project note and possible short negative-result paper, not
a Hilbert--Pólya paper. Submission promotion remains on hold until an
external novelty review decides that the scoped semantic obstruction is of
independent interest.

## Central question

Can finite-window endpoint localization for the area-preserving Hénon
recurrence be promoted, without changing the dynamical clock, into a natural
finite-memory approximation of an exact pinning transfer operator with
signed periodic trace?

## Main theorem sequence

1. **Explicit mixed-domain theorem.** The rational disks \(Y_t\times X_r\)
   support all six allowed signed square-root branches, with strict target
   inclusion, clearance \(1/360\), and derivative bound \(2/\sqrt{66}\).
2. **Exact-kernel identification.** These branches define the standard BPS
   one-step mixed Cauchy kernel on four exterior/interior Banach blocks.
3. **Window-semantics theorem.** C02C length-\(N\) endpoint functions are
   precisely the iterated pinning data of chronological word summands of
   \(\mathcal L^N\) on the common domain. They do not truncate an
   infinite-memory coefficient of the one-step kernel.
4. **Trace-sign theorem.** Under the frozen BPS pinning contour convention,
   the raw kernel has trace \(-T_n\), whereas the BPS orientation twist gives
   the absolute denominator.
5. **Repetition obstruction.** No ordinary scalar multiplicative edge
   cocycle converts each raw orbit residue to the desired signed residue for
   both primitive cycles and their repetitions. Aggregate-only accidental
   cancellation between distinct orbits is not ruled out.

## Explicit nonclaims

The note does not claim:

- that every history-space or alternative transfer operator is impossible;
- that a reciprocal Fredholm determinant is entire;
- a new graded/Lefschetz determinant mechanism;
- a new general finite-rank approximation theorem;
- a global description of the Hénon nonwandering set;
- a prime-orbit correspondence, completed zeta, self-adjoint operator, RH, or
  Hilbert--Pólya realization.
