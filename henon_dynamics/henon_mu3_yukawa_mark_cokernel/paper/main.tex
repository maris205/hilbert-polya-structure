\begin{abstract}
The preceding finite-group computation gives a nonsingular $16\times16$
table-of-marks matrix for sixteen core-free subgroup types of $W(E_6)$.  We
compute its full integral Smith form, refining the determinant to an exact
congruence invariant.  The Smith invariants are
$(1,2^{\times10},4^{\times3},24,144)$, so the cokernel is
$(\mathbb Z/2)^{10}\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^3\oplus\mathbb Z/24\oplus\mathbb
Z/144$.  This is a theorem about the explicitly restricted 16-type mark map;
no full Burnside-ring or arithmetic claim is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and scope}
Let $G=\W$ be the order-$51840$ permutation group fixed by the preceding
source package.  The C62 atlas and C63 dictionary provide sixteen
ambient-conjugacy subgroup types $S_1,\ldots,S_{16}$, and C64 defines
transitive sets $X_j=G/S_j$ and their self-mark matrix
\[
 M_{ij}=|(G/S_j)^{S_i}|.
\]
C64 proved $\operatorname{rank}_{\Q}M=16$ and
$\det(M)=226492416=2^{23}3^3$.  C65 instead studied the rank-three image of
the C63 character kernel.  Here we ask for the full integral cokernel of $M$.

The scope is the lattice map generated by these sixteen types only.  The
firewall is
\[
\texttt{NO\_BAD\_EULER\_OR\_ROOT\_NUMBER}.
\]
We do not claim a full table of marks, a full Burnside-ring classification,
resolvents, local fields, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, or a
Hilbert--Polya operator.

\section{Exact integral calculation}
For an integer matrix $A$, its Smith form is obtained by unimodular row and
column operations and has diagonal entries $d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots$.  The
product $d_1\cdots d_k$ is the $k$th determinantal divisor.  Thus the Smith
form records all integral congruence defects, whereas the determinant records
only their product.

The C64 matrix is reconstructed from frozen evidence rather than inferred
from subgroup orders.  Its rows and columns are ordered by the fixed labels
$S_1,\ldots,S_{16}$.  The producer performs exact integer Euclidean
row/column reduction.  A separate checker repeats the reduction, and a clean
SymPy integer Smith implementation supplies a third check.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted mark-map Smith form]
For the frozen sixteen C64 subgroup representatives, the self-mark map
$m:\Z^{16}\to\Z^{16}$ has
\[
\operatorname{SNF}(M)=
 (1,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,4,4,4,24,144).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\operatorname{coker}(m)\cong
 (\Z/2)^{10}\oplus(\Z/4)^3\oplus\Z/24\oplus\Z/144.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Certificate]
The direct mark reconstruction gives a $16\times16$ integer matrix of rank
16.  Exact Smith reduction returns the displayed diagonal.  The successive
determinantal divisors are
\[
\begin{split}
&1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512,1024,4096,\\
&16384,65536,1572864,226492416.
\end{split}
\]
Their successive ratios are precisely the displayed invariant factors, and
the final divisor equals $2^{23}3^3$, the independently recorded C64
determinant.  The two independent checkers and library cross-check reproduce
all reported invariants.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Primary decomposition]
The 2-primary part of the cokernel is
\[
(\Z/2)^{10}\oplus(\Z/4)^3\oplus\Z/8\oplus\Z/16,
\]
and its 3-primary part is $\Z/3\oplus\Z/9$.  No other prime occurs.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Factor each nontrivial invariant factor in the theorem.  The exponents sum to
$23$ at $2$ and $3$ at $3$, matching the determinant.
\end{proof}

\section{Relation to C65 and limitations}
C65 certifies the distinct rank-three image lattices
\[
\operatorname{SNF}(m\langle z_1,z_3\rangle)=(2,8),\qquad
\operatorname{SNF}(m\langle z_1,z_2,z_3\rangle)=(2,2,8),
\]
with relative saturation quotient $\mathbb Z/2$.  C66 checks these values as
an upstream compatibility condition, but does not identify that relative
class with a canonical direct summand of $\operatorname{coker}(m)$: such an
identification would require an additional explicitly constructed map.

The word ``restricted'' is essential.  The full table of marks of $G$ has
more subgroup classes, and this paper neither computes nor classifies that
larger object.  Likewise, an integral mark cokernel carries no assertion
about arithmetic fields or bad-prime factors.

\section{Reproducibility}
The canonical evidence binds C64 mark evidence, its prefreeze manifest, and
C65 saturation evidence by SHA-256.  The producer, structural checker,
clean-process replay, library SNF cross-check, and ten hostile mutations all
pass.  The evidence status is \texttt{PREFREEZE\_G3\_PASS}; release closure is
separate from the theorem certificate.

\section{Conclusion}
The C64 determinant $2^{23}3^3$ decomposes into a precise sixteen-factor
integral obstruction.  This Smith-invariant theorem is the complete
integer refinement available from the frozen 16-type mark matrix, while its
scope remains deliberately narrower than the full Burnside ring.
