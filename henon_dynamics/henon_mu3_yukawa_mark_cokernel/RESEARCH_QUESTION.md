# C66 research question

The C64 mark matrix proves that the 16-type character relation is visible to
marks and has determinant `2^23 3^3`.  C65 studies the rank-three image of the
C63 character kernel and finds a dyadic saturation jump.  C66 asks the
different, full-rank question:

> What is the exact integral cokernel of the restricted 16-type mark map?

The answer is tested by the complete Smith invariant list, not by its
determinant alone.  The project is intentionally finite-group and integral;
it makes no arithmetic or full-Burnside claim.
