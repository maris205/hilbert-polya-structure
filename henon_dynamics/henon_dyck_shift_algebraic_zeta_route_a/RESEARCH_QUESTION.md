# Research question

Can one turn the algebraic zeta function of the one-vertex edge-type Dyck
shift into a complete all-`N`, all-period orbit theorem, while auditing the
often ambiguous distinction between periodic words and cyclic orbits?

Yes.  The zeta logarithmic derivative gives every origin-marked fixed count;
Möbius inversion then gives primitive points and division by `n` gives
primitive orbits.  The same closed form resolves the `N=1` rational boundary,
the `N>1` dominant double pole, quadratic branchpoints, nonrationality, and
exponential orbit growth.  This symbolic achievement does not pass the target
arithmetic or operator gates of Route A.

The source-locked Markov-Dyck entropy theorem further gives
`h_top(D_N^E)=log(N+1)` from the proved periodic-point growth rate.
