# Hostile audit

Eighteen repaired-hash semantic corruptions are rejected: odd/even fixed
counts, primitive points, primitive orbit division, direct word count, entropy,
source pagination, `N>1` pole order/location and nonrationality, both
periodic-word conventions, Route-B invocation flip, Route-A verdict, scope
literal, forbidden target claim, source commit, and evaluator hash.

One additional stale-hash corruption is rejected at the digest gate.  This
separates semantic rejection after hash repair from ordinary integrity
rejection; the stale attack is not described as resealed.
