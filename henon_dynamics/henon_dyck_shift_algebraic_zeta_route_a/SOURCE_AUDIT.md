# Source audit

The model and zeta formula are anchored to primary sources:

- W. Krieger and K. Matsumoto, “Zeta functions and topological entropy of the
  Markov-Dyck shifts,” *Münster Journal of Mathematics* 4 (2011), 171--184;
  arXiv `0706.3262`.  The official Münster volume PDF is the controlling
  pagination record; an arXiv journal-reference entry reports 171--185.
- G. Keller, “Circular codes, loop counting, and zeta-functions,” *Journal of
  Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 56 (1991), 75--83,
  DOI `10.1016/0097-3165(91)90023-A`.

The source paper's Proposition 3.1 is the lock for identifying Markov-Dyck
entropy with periodic-point growth.  The package makes no literature-priority
claim.  All coefficient, singularity,
word-convention, and mutation checks are performed internally.  No symbolic
zeta factor is called a target Euler factor, root number, automorphic object,
or Hilbert--Pólya determinant.
