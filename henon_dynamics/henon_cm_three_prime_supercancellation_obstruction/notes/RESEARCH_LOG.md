# Research log

C41 supplied a real arithmetic object, so the next question was whether
graded cohomology could cancel its non-Riemann factors while retaining some
H\'enon content.  Comparing only the first logarithmic Euler coefficient at
two inert primes and one split prime gives a nonsingular system.  The unique
solution deletes both `H^1(E)` and the degree-two Tate factor.  This closes
the entire finite three-generator category before any global fit is tried.
