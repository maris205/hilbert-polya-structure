# Paper plan

The paper is organized around one theorem rather than five small claims.

- Fix the full GKSL model and dephasing normalization.
- Solve the channel and diagonalize the Liouvillian on every parameter face.
- Derive the normalized Choi matrix and prove the exact PPT/EB criterion.
- Prove the unique faithful-thermal threshold, including the closed form when
  pure dephasing vanishes.
- Compute the sharp trace-distance contraction coefficient and recurrence
  classification.
- Give a complete boundary atlas.
- Separate classical source ownership and neighboring repository models from
  the new assembled all-parameter certificate.
- End with the honest Route-A tuple and the `HEN-O287` obstruction.

Round 0 contains the exact channel and EB theorem.  Round 1 adds the sharp
contraction result and complete degeneracy atlas.  Round 2 adds the independent
evidence contract, collision audit, proves-too-much counterexamples, and
scope-locked Route-A conclusion.
