# Source and ownership audit

The package uses authoritative original owners only for standard background:

- Gorini, Kossakowski, and Sudarshan, *Completely positive dynamical
  semigroups of N-level systems*, J. Math. Phys. 17 (1976),
  DOI `10.1063/1.522979`.
- Lindblad, *On the generators of quantum dynamical semigroups*, Commun.
  Math. Phys. 48 (1976), DOI `10.1007/BF01608499`.
- Choi, *Completely positive linear maps on complex matrices*, Linear Algebra
  Appl. 10 (1975), DOI `10.1016/0024-3795(75)90075-0`.
- Horodecki, Shor, and Ruskai, *Entanglement breaking channels*, Rev. Math.
  Phys. 15 (2003), DOI `10.1142/S0129055X03001709`.
- Horodecki, Horodecki, and Horodecki, *Separability of mixed states:
  necessary and sufficient conditions*, Phys. Lett. A 223 (1996),
  DOI `10.1016/S0375-9601(96)00706-2`.

We make no priority claim for the GKSL form, Choi criterion, PPT theorem, or
entanglement-breaking terminology.  The package contribution is the explicit,
auditable assembly of the all-rate solution, sharp contraction coefficient,
finite threshold equation, and complete degeneracy atlas under the repository
Route-A protocol.

## Collision boundary

- C223 is closed unitary Jaynes–Cummings block dynamics.
- C224 is nonautonomous unitary Landau–Zener scattering.
- C237 is a classical Kramers Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process.
- C243 is nonlinear Hamiltonian Bose–Josephson Bloch-sphere motion.
- C297 is non-CPTP PT-symmetric gain/loss ray dynamics.
- C298 is a Grassmann projection gradient flow.

C303 instead evolves density matrices by a trace-preserving completely
positive dissipative semigroup and proves a Choi/PPT entanglement-breaking
threshold.  None of the six neighbors is being relabeled.

No claim is made about external peer review or exhaustive literature novelty.
