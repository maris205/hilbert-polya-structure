# Research question

For the complete nonnegative rate cone
`gamma_down,gamma_up,gamma_phi >= 0`, arbitrary real `omega`, and all
`t >= 0`, can the thermal amplitude-damping plus pure-dephasing qubit GKSL
semigroup be solved and classified exactly— including its Choi separability
transition and all singular parameter faces—without converting a
finite-dimensional quantum-channel calculation into an arithmetic claim?

The answer is yes.  The full theorem is analytic.  Finite enumeration is used
only as an independently checked regression certificate.

The Route-A question has a negative answer: neither exponential physical time,
nor a four-dimensional Liouvillian characteristic polynomial, nor the Choi
determinant supplies arithmetic prime-power weights, a target global analytic
object, or a same-clock Hilbert–Pólya realization.
