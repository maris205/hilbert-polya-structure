# C141 paper plan

## Title and claim

**A quadratic inverse-branch Ruelle operator: exact stability traces and a scoped primitive product**

The short paper will prove one headline theorem for \(F(z)=z^2-6\) on \(H^2(\mathbb D_4)\), not survey general transfer-operator theory.

## Structure

1. Freeze branches, disk, Hardy normalization, clock, and determinant sign.
2. Prove strict geometry and the explicit nuclear decomposition.
3. Prove all-period exhaustion and the weighted fixed-point trace formula.
4. Derive the \(m=0,1\) controls and explain why \(m=2\) is the first nontrivial stability weight.
5. State the exact trace/coefficients receipt and primitive product.
6. Separate raw-product convergence from global Fredholm entireness.
7. Report reproducibility, negative control, Route-A classification, and nonclaims.

## Presentation

Target length is 2–4 pages. One compact table will carry the exact prefix. No decorative figure is needed: the proof dependencies are clearer as formulas and a table.
