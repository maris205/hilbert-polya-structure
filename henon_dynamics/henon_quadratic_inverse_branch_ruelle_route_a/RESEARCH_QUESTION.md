# HCS-C141 research question

For the frozen quadratic map

\[
F(z)=z^2-6,
\qquad \mathbb D_4=\{z:|z|<4\},
\]

do the two holomorphic inverse branches on \(\mathbb D_4\) define an unconditional trace-class Hardy-space transfer operator whose traces exhaust every periodic point of \(F\), and whose first nontrivial stability weight \(m=2\) admits exact low-order receipts and a rigorously scoped primitive-orbit product?

The source lock is the principal square root on \(D(6,4)\subset\{\Re w>0\}\),
\(\psi_\pm(z)=\pm\sqrt{z+6}\), the Hardy space \(H^2(\mathbb D_4)\), and

\[
(\mathcal L_m f)(z)=\sum_{\epsilon=\pm}(\psi_\epsilon'(z))^m f(\psi_\epsilon(z)),
\qquad m\in\{0,1,2\}.
\]

The headline is \(m=2\). The \(m=0,1\) cases are exact controls, not separate candidates. The theorem has no period cutoff; periods \(1\) through \(6\) are a deterministic evidence prefix.

## Decision boundary

Success means source-side nuclearity, all-period point exhaustion, the exact weighted trace identity, the \(m=0,1\) collapse, and the \(m=2\) primitive product with its convergence disk. It does **not** mean a target zero/divisor match, arithmetic/local information, a functional equation, a natural quantization, or a Hilbert--Pólya construction.
