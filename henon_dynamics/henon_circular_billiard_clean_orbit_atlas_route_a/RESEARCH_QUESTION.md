# Research question — HCS-C247

Can the circular billiard be closed as one exact, orientation-aware
primitive-family theorem, including the clean return geometry that is lost
when a family is incorrectly treated as an isolated orbit?  In the frozen
angle chart the answer is yes: the map is a rigid translation, rational
rotation fractions are classified by a gcd condition, and the length, caustic,
action, repetition, and unipotent-kernel ledgers follow for every parameter.

The endpoint faces are part of the theorem: the diameter is a single
endpoint-equivalent family, while grazing has two one-sided orientation
limits.  The result is analytic and source-local; it deliberately makes no
arithmetic target or spectral matching claim.
