# HCS-C50 narrative report

C49 reached \(\Re s>1/4\), but that boundary was not a single local defect.
The second moment contributed a genus-four Frobenius trace of Weil size,
and the generic tail began again at the fourth moment. A pointwise estimate
alone could not remove both walls.

The first large step is structural. The C48 cyclic cubic curve has three
explicit \(K\)-rational automorphisms generating \(C_2\times S_3\). Its
differentials split as the two standard \(S_3\)-representations with
opposite central signs. Rational group-algebra idempotents therefore give

\[
\operatorname{Jac}(C)\sim_K E_+^2\times E_-^2.
\]

This explains the squared quadratic local factors seen in exact finite-field
controls and, more importantly, makes the entire second chronological
counterterm resum exactly:

\[
\exp(-\ell_2/2)
=\zeta_K(2s+1)^7L(H^1(C/K),2s+1)H_2(s).
\]

The residual \(H_2\) is holomorphic and nonzero on \(\Re s>0\).
Caraiani--Newton modularity continues the two elliptic factors. Their zeros
are retained rather than hidden behind a continued logarithm.

The second large step keeps the genuine eight-step phase. Radial
stratification produces a cubic sixfold, a split quadric sixfold, and a
\((2,3)\) fivefold. An exact recurrence Gröbner basis proves the latter
smooth in characteristic zero. The prime \(181\) supplies a useful negative
control: one split reduction is singular, so the theorem must have a finite
bad set.

The surviving primitive traces have ranks \(86\) and \(168\). Their weights
give

\[
c_{p,4}=O(p^{-1/2}),
\]

so the fourth moment is no longer a quarter-abscissa obstruction. After the
second-moment resummation, the first untreated generic repetition is
\(n=5\). The same Hénon object therefore continues holomorphically to
\(\Re s>1/5\), now represented by a normalized-semifinite
tenth-order graded determinant.

The advance is substantial but sharply scoped. There is still no
prime/primitive-orbit dictionary, full functional equation, Gamma factor,
Riemann divisor, or self-adjoint spectral generator. The next major gate is
not another isolated moment count: it is whether the extracted elliptic
functional equations and higher cohomological factors can be assembled into
a coherent global completion without destroying the original prime clock.
