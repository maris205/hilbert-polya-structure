# HCS-C50 paper plan

## Proposed title

**Elliptic Resummation and a Fourth-Moment Fivefold in a Cubic Hénon Euler
Germ**

## Claim architecture

1. Freeze the C48 curve, the split-prime clock, and normalized chronology.
2. Verify the three \(K\)-rational automorphisms and the
   \(C_2\times S_3\) relations.
3. Derive the two standard representation blocks and rational idempotents.
4. Prove \(\operatorname{Jac}(C)\sim_K E_+^2\times E_-^2\).
5. Match the complete second logarithm with
   \(\zeta_K^7L(H^1(C/K))H_2\), including sign and split-prime multiplicity.
6. Invoke Caraiani--Newton only for the two elliptic \(L\)-functions.
7. Derive the ordered eight-step projective direction formula.
8. Prove characteristic-zero smoothness by the exact recurrence Gröbner
   basis and record \(p=181\) as a bad-reduction counterexample.
9. Compute primitive ranks, Frobenius weights, and the exact fourth moment.
10. Prove continuation to \(\Re s>1/5\) and distinguish
    \(\tau\)-Det10 from classical \(S^{15}\).
11. Evaluate Route A and state the next global-completion gate.

## Manuscript sections

1. Introduction and the paired quarter-abscissa obstruction.
2. Frozen Hénon chronology and main theorem.
3. \(K\)-rational elliptic decomposition of the C48 curve.
4. Exact second-counterterm resummation and modular continuation.
5. Projective geometry of the fourth moment.
6. Smoothness, Betti ranks, and Frobenius bounds.
7. Fifth-abscissa continuation and the tenth-order determinant.
8. Route-A evaluation, limitations, and outlook.
9. Appendix: exact identities, Singular transcript, and bad-prime witness.

## Mandatory claim firewalls

- The Jacobian statement is a \(K\)-isogeny, not a polarized isomorphism.
- Jiménez is prior art for related complete decompositions, not a theorem
  about the present curve.
- Moonen does not place the present monodromy family on his special list.
- \(H_2\) is zero-free on \(\Re s>0\); the full continued second factor is
  not.
- Characteristic-zero smoothness gives finitely many bad primes, not all
  split primes; \(p=181\) is explicitly bad.
- The full Hénon Euler object has no proved functional equation.
- The determinant is normalized-semifinite and graded, not classical
  Fredholm.
- Route B remains unauthorized.
