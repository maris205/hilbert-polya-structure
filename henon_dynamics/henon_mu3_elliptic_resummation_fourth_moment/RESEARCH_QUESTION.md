# HCS-C50 research question

The previous papers reached \(\Re s>1/4\) but exposed two distinct gates:
the genus-four second moment sat exactly on that wall, while the untreated
fourth moment could also recreate it. The central question is:

> Can source-derived arithmetic structure remove the complete second
> logarithm and prove square-root decay for the ordered fourth moment, so
> that the same normalized Hénon Euler object continues to \(\Re s>1/5\)?

This divides into five theorem-level questions.

1. Does the frozen C48 curve admit a \(K\)-rational low-rank Jacobian
   decomposition, rather than only a finite-prime factorization pattern?
2. Does that decomposition turn
   \(\exp(-\ell_2/2)\) into an integer-power product of standard global
   \(L\)-functions with no fractional-root branch?
3. Does the ordered eight-step phase admit a projective stratification whose
   non-Tate part has a true cohomological square-root gain after division by
   \(d_p=(p-1)/2\)?
4. Is the resulting \((2,3)\) fivefold smooth over characteristic zero, with
   bad reductions honestly isolated rather than silently discarded?
5. Does the combined result yield a canonical holomorphic continuation
   and a correctly typed regularized operator determinant on
   \(\Re s>1/5\)?

The answer is positive, with two important qualifications. The continued
Euler object may have zeros inherited from elliptic \(L\)-functions, and no
functional equation for the full Hénon product is proved. The result is a
Route-A analytic advance, not a Hilbert--Pólya construction.
