# C44 results

For one primitive orbit,

\[
\partial_s\log(1-|\Lambda|^{-s})
=\sum_{r\ge1}\ell\,|\Lambda|^{-rs},
\qquad \ell=\log|\Lambda|.
\]

This is exactly the prime-power atom when (|\Lambda|=p).  Globally,
however, the inherited H6 estimate gives absolute convergence for

\[
\Re s>\frac{\log\varphi}{\log J_*}=0.35598\ldots,
\]

so the critical line lies strictly inside the ordinary absolute domain.  The
all-prime von Mangoldt series is not absolutely convergent there.  Therefore
the raw instability clock cannot give an all-prime termwise bridge.
