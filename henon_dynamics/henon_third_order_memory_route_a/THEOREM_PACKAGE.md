# Theorem package — C113

For (G(x,y,z)=(x^2-55/16-y-z/2,x,y)), the fixed locus consists of the two
points ((5/4\pm\sqrt5)^3).  The exact primitive period-two orbit is

\[
(-7/4,1/4,-7/4)\mapsto(1/4,-7/4,1/4)\mapsto(-7/4,1/4,-7/4).
\]

Its ordered Jacobian product has determinant (1/4), trace (-15/4), and

\[
\det(\lambda I-M_2)=(4\lambda^3+15\lambda^2-2\lambda-1)/4.
\]

These exact statements are restricted to periods one and two; no completeness
or analytic operator theorem is asserted.
