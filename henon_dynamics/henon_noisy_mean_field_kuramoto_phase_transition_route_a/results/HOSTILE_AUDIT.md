# Hostile audit

The hostile suite repairs the evidence payload hash after semantic tampering; rejection therefore demonstrates field ownership rather than merely checksum sensitivity.

## Attacked surfaces

- candidate, obstruction, date, baseline, scope, evaluator authority/hash, and YAML relative path;
- (D>0,K\geq0), PDE sign, transition threshold, Fourier spectrum, critical coefficients, and continuum-proof boundary;
- collision descriptions, nonclaims, source identifiers, Route-A tuple/verdict, Route-B lock, and forbidden flags;
- extra, omitted, duplicated, and altered rows in every ledger;
- missing top-level keys and an unrepaired stale-hash control;
- duplicate/nonfinite/root-type JSON;
- duplicate, anchor, alias, merge, non-string-key, implicit-timestamp, unknown-field, wrong-type, authority, source, Route-B, verdict, theorem-status, artifact-path, evidence-role, and root-type YAML;
- validly reparsed YAML mutations with matching evidence hash changes.

All 71 attacks must be rejected. Optimized execution is separately tested for every executable because validation must not disappear under `-O` or `-OO`.
