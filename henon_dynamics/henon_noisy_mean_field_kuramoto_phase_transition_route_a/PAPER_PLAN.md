# Paper plan

## Central contribution

One short paper closes the complete identical-frequency noisy mean-field Kuramoto atlas under a single fixed normalization. The contribution is not split into separate formula notes: well-posed probability flow, Lyapunov identity, stationary exhaustion, sharp transition, linear spectrum, and critical amplitude are one theorem.

## Logical order

1. Freeze the circle, density, drift sign, and (D,K) conventions.
2. State the all-parameter theorem and its exclusions.
3. Prove global flow, mass, strict positivity, and the free-energy identity.
4. Use constant stationary flux to exhaust stationary densities by von Mises profiles.
5. Prove strict Bessel quotient monotonicity and the Turán inequality from positive coefficients.
6. Deduce the sharp (K=2D) transition.
7. Diagonalize the uniform linearization in Fourier modes.
8. Derive the analytic critical expansion.
9. Explain exact evidence, boundaries, sources, and Route-A rejection.

## Three substantive rounds

- **Round 0:** frozen model, main atlas, global probability flow, free energy, and stationary-flux proof.
- **Round 1:** adds the self-contained coefficient-pairing/Turán lemma, sharp phase threshold, and complete uniform Fourier spectrum.
- **Round 2:** adds the rigorous implicit-function expansion, exact-evidence architecture, hostile verification, boundary atlas, source ownership, and Route-A firewall.

The final PDF is byte-identical to round 2.
