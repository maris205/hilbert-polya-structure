# C74 research question

Determine the actual affine symmetry of the frozen C72 named coordinate
realization

\[
Q=\mathbb Z/9\oplus\mathbb Z/3\oplus\mathbb Z/2,
\]

and compare it with the abstract 16-vertex generation hypergraph studied in
C73.  The question has four exact parts:

1. enumerate `Aut(Q)` and `Aff(Q)` in the displayed coordinates;
2. compute the affine stabilizer of the 16-occurrence named multiset and of
   its 10-point underlying set;
3. give the complete multiset- and set-overlap distributions, including the
   nearest nontrivial affine maps;
4. state precisely what the comparison does and does not say about the C73
   hypergraph automorphism group.

The overlap statistic is occurrence-based:

\[
 O(\varphi)=\sum_{q\in Q}\min(m_M(q),m_{\varphi(M)}(q)),
\]

where `m_M` is the multiplicity function of the 16 named occurrences.  It is
not a pointwise fixed-label count and is not the cardinality of the distinct
point intersection.

Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
