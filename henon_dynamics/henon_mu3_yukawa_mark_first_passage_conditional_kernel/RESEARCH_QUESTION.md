# Research question

For every ordered pair of frozen C88 targets, what is the exact conditional
law of the response first-passage time after conditioning on each attainable
value of the first target time?

C98 asks additionally whether all reverse kernels satisfy Bayes balance and
whether each pair gives exact laws of total expectation and total variance.
Empty conditioning events are part of the question: their rows must remain
undefined (`null`) rather than receive arbitrary values.
