This is an internal artifact-bound audit, not external peer review and not an independent error process.

The mutation suite repairs the payload hash after each attacked field. It rejects 57 repaired-hash changes covering candidate/date/commit identity, scope, evaluator path/version/hash, every source-lock field, finite cutoffs and row counts, partitions and conjugates, hook dimensions, character data, eigenvalues, multiplicities, traces, returns, \(L^2\) data, determinant multiplicities and factor strings, gap, every Route-A qualification, Route B, target-data flags, every owner-boundary sentence, source key/title/authors/year/DOI/role, and nonclaims. A separate stale-hash mutation is also rejected.

Five previously accepted attacks now fail: identifying the weighted path owner with frozen \(S_n\), replacing a factor string by `BROKEN`, asserting `A1_PASS_CANONICAL_ORBITS`, changing the source year to 1900, and changing `n_max` to 999.

The mathematical audit also corrected the former absolute factorization claim. The valid boundary is narrower: no deterministic map on frozen \(S_n\) owns \(P_n\), while the changed weighted path space has a canonical primitive-cycle product for the reciprocal determinant. This does not repair A0 or provide frozen primitive orbits carrying an A0 arithmetic payload.

The determinant-degree edge case remains explicit: zero eigenvalue sectors contribute a unit factor, so the polynomial degree is the nonzero spectral multiplicity, not automatically \(n!\).
