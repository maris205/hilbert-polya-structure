# C183 research question

For the lazy random-transposition chain on every symmetric group \(S_n\), can one place the complete representation-theoretic spectrum, finite determinant, return traces, exact \(L^2\) mixing law, and the frozen-owner versus weighted-path-owner boundary in one source-locked Route-A theorem?

The chain chooses \((i,j)\in[n]^2\) uniformly and right-multiplies by \((ij)\), with \((ii)=e\). The question is stronger than computing a few eigenvalues: it asks for every partition sector and every deck size, then distinguishes the Markov operator on frozen \(S_n\) from the weighted edge/path shift whose primitive-cycle product represents the reciprocal determinant. No target table or arithmetic local datum is permitted.

The frozen expected answer is positive at the source-theorem layer and negative at Route A. The full spectrum and weighted path-cycle identity are exact, but the chain has no intrinsic rational-prime origin, and no primitive orbit in the frozen source carries an A0 arithmetic payload.
