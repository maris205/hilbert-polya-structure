# C183 source and ownership audit

## Verified classical source

Persi Diaconis and Mehrdad Shahshahani, “Generating a random permutation with random transpositions,” *Zeitschrift für Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie und Verwandte Gebiete* 57(2), 159–179 (1981), DOI `10.1007/BF00535487`.

The title, authors, report provenance, and model definition were checked against Stanford's technical-report record (`EFS NSF 149`, April 1980) and the DOI metadata. The paper is the ownership source for the representation-theoretic random-transposition method and the classical cutoff theorem.

## Claim-level boundary

- Classical: the random-transposition chain, character analysis, spectrum/mixing method, and cutoff at order \(\tfrac12 n\log n\).
- Package proof: one source lock joins all partition eigenvalues and multiplicities to a finite determinant, return traces, exact \(L^2\) identity, and direct word enumeration.
- Package owner boundary: on frozen \(S_n\), \(P_n\) is not induced by a single-valued deterministic map; the weighted path-space primitive-cycle product is derived from the finite-matrix trace--log identity and explicitly recorded as a different owner.
- Not claimed: priority for any classical formula, absence of every primitive factorization after a lift, external peer review, journal acceptance, or a literature-wide novelty certificate.

The bibliography population is one. No external source supplies target zeros, prime tables, arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, or automorphy.
