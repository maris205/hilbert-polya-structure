# Research question

For the unit-sphere magnetic equation with charge fixed by an integral Chern class, can one classify every classical trajectory and, for the same bundle, derive the complete covariant-Laplacian spectrum, multiplicities, heat trace, sign involution, and all zero-charge/zero-energy boundaries without importing any target arithmetic data?

The frozen answer is yes. The source-local theorem is analytic for every `q in Z`, every `E >= 0`, and every spectral level. The finite certificate is only a convention and implementation receipt.
