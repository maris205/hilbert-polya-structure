# Narrative report

The decisive simplification is that the Lorentz force on the round sphere hides a constant ambient angular momentum. Once `K=x cross xdot+(q/2)x` is found, the entire classical phase portrait becomes one rigid rotation theorem. The same half-integer charge convention then enters the bundle Casimir calculation, so the classical period and quantum spectrum are audited under one normalization.

The quantum bridge is now explicit rather than implicit: the Chern class identifies the degree-`q` bundle, equal curvature and `H^1_dR(S^2)=0` identify its unitary gauge class, and the closed smooth-section energy form fixes the Friedrichs realization before the homogeneous `SU(2)` calculation begins.

The progress is theorem-scale: all positive-energy trajectories, all integral charges and fixed-curvature unitary connections, every spectral level, multiplicities, heat trace, sign reversal, and every declared degeneracy are closed. The obstruction is equally explicit. Integer Chern degree is not rational-prime ownership; clean circle families are not isolated hyperbolic primitive cycles; and a natural self-adjoint Laplacian is not automatically a Hilbert–Pólya operator.
