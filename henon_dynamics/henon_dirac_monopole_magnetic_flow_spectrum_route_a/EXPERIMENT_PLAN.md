# Experiment plan

The experiment is a deterministic theorem receipt, not empirical support for the proof.

1. Enumerate 39 classical rows from `q=-6,...,6` and three positive rational speeds.
2. Check five quarter-turn Rodrigues samples per row, the unit-sphere equation, the Poincaré plane, period, height, and radius.
3. Enumerate 357 exact spectral cells for `q=-10,...,10` and `n=0,...,16`.
4. Evaluate 36 degree-80 heat-trace partial sums and first omitted eigenvalues at four positive times.
5. Audit 25 Chern rows for `q=-12,...,12`.
6. Audit 18 exact time-reversal pairings with charge and initial velocity both reversed.
7. Exact-lock the Chern-class/H¹ gauge bridge, Friedrichs realization, essential self-adjoint closure, and their boundary-atlas entries; attack each with repaired payload hashes.
8. Run an independent checker, exact SymPy lane, two isolated byte replays, repaired-hash hostile mutations, optimized-Python refusal, three deterministic PDF rounds, and the self-excluding release manifest.
