# Hostile audit

The mutation lane attacks identity/date/source fields, evaluator authority and digests, Route-A verdicts, forbidden flags, static theorem text, the Chern-class/H¹ gauge bridge, Friedrichs and essential-self-adjoint realizations, the matching boundary atlas, rational canonicalization, nonfinite decimals, classical coordinates, quarter samples, quantum multiplicities, heat traces, Chern rows, row duplication/omission, enumeration, source DOI, duplicate JSON keys, nonfinite JSON constants, YAML timestamps, aliases, merges, non-string keys, nested evidence statuses, artifact paths, and Route-B authorization.

Every attack is rejected; evidence mutations are also rerun after repairing the canonical payload hash. This demonstrates field ownership rather than hash-only checking.
