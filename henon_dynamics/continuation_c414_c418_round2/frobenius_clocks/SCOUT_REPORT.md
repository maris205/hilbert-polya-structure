## Residual joint series and why it does not fill a slot

When $d$ is not a power of the characteristic, put
$\alpha=\log_q d$, which is irrational. Summing the owned count
formally introduces two elementary floor series. With
$a=q^2v$, $b=qv$,

$$
F(u,v)=\frac{du\,b}{(1-du)(1-b)}
+\frac{\sum_{n\ge1}u^n a^{\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor+1}}{1-a}
-\frac{\sum_{n\ge1}(du)^n b^{\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor+1}}{1-b}.
$$

This points to classical irrational-rotation/Hecke–Mahler analysis,
not a new point-count mechanism. On a fixed generic $v$ slice the
combined periodic weight has Fourier coefficients proportional to

$$
\frac{\log q}{(L+2\pi i j)(L+\log q+2\pi i j)},\qquad L=\log b.
$$

Their absolute summability gives the familiar dense radial-singularity
mechanism. Possible joint meromorphic continuation would still need
its own precise slice/axis/polar-divisor statement. No complete new
joint-boundary theorem is claimed or admitted in this scout report;
even a closed version would inherit the already owned count and the
classical analytic mechanism. This residual does not justify occupying
one of the two missing substantial contracts.
