The read grades below mean precisely: **primary text excerpt** = the
specified theorem/section was actually returned and read, not the entire
paper; **primary metadata/abstract** = no theorem-proof reading implied;
**inherited record** = a sealed prior access receipt, not a fresh reread.
Search-result snippets and secondary hosts were discovery aids only.
