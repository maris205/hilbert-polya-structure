\begin{abstract}
We classify every rational periodic point of the conservative H\'enon map
$h_d(x,y)=(y,-x+s_d(y))$, where $s_d$ is the integer-valued
discrete-sine polynomial of any odd degree $d\ge3$. The result gives the
exact multiplicity of every ordinary primitive cycle, including both
degree-dependent central phases and the unique growing boundary cycle
when $d\equiv1\pmod6$. The total number of rational periodic points is
$(3d^2-4d+152)/3$, $d^2+6d+24$, or $d^2+8d/3+14$, according as
$d\equiv5,1,3\pmod6$. A nonarchimedean argument first proves integrality;
a second-difference estimate then gives a uniform escape box. Inside its
core, affine residue cells yield exact clipped multiplicities. Outside the
core, a signed first-return graph with a complete endpoint complement
provides the exhaustion. All formulas include the least admissible
degrees. The resulting finite-cycle zeta is an immediate corollary of
the ordinary cycle classification.
\end{abstract}
