\begin{abstract}
We determine the forward-plus-backward canonical-height distribution
of every polynomial point for every constant-coefficient H\'enon map
$H(x,y)=(y,f(y)-a x)$ over $\mathbb F_q$, with $\deg f=d\ge2$ and
$a\ne0$. The distribution depends only on $q$ and $d$, including in
inseparable cases. A unique degree valley in each nonconstant orbit
gives two disjoint classes of representatives with exact multiplicities.
Summing their integer time shifts yields an explicit height Dirichlet
series, absolutely convergent exactly for $\Re s>1$ and meromorphic
for $\Re s>0$. We determine every pole after combining the sectors:
all are simple except for a specified lattice of double poles on
$\Re s=1$. The surviving pole lattices accumulate at every point
of the imaginary axis, which is a natural boundary even for meromorphic
continuation. Direct counting gives
$\#\{P:\widehat h(P)\le B\}=
\frac{d-1}{d+1}(q-1)q^{\lfloor B\rfloor+1}B+O_{q,d}(q^B)$
for arbitrary real $B$. The fractional-part oscillation is retained
in the leading term. The domain is $\mathbb F_q[t]^2$ and the
series counts heights, not periodic orbits.
\end{abstract}
