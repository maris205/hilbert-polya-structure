Let $p$ be an odd prime, $q=p^e$ with $e\ge3$, and let
$g\in\F_q[y]$ have degree $2p$. For every $a\in\F_q^*$ we determine
the ordinary geometric fixed points of every iterate of
$S=H^{-1}\Phi_q$, where $H(x,y)=(y,y^q+g(y)-ax)$ and $\Phi_q$ is
coordinatewise $q$-Frobenius. The answer has two coefficient-support
branches. If $g$ has a nonzero term of degree strictly between $p+1$
and $2p$, a two-term degree block gives the count. If all such terms
vanish, a different recurrence arises from a semilinear operator on
a perfected polynomial ring. A two-term invariant controls every
fractional and mixed remainder and descends to an ordinary polynomial,
including arbitrary coefficients outside the prime field. Writing
$w=p^{v_p(n)}$, both branches yield an explicit integer $D_w$ and the
formula $\#\Fix(S^n)=q^{2n-w}D_w$. The relevant fixed schemes are
finite and reduced. The resulting dynamical zeta function, and every
positive integral power of it, has the circle $|t|=q^{-2}$ as a
meromorphic natural boundary.
