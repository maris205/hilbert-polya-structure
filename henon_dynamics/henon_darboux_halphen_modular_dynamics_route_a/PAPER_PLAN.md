variables, exact Jacobi series, and prove the coefficientwise ODE.

Prove the affine weight-two covariance law, record `S,T` permutations, derive
the scalar Chazy equation and the relative discriminant law.

and all three coordinate-axis equilibrium branches,
state cusp and modular/logarithmic-derivative poles, report independent
evidence, distinguish registry neighbors, and give the strict Route-A
verdict and nonclaims.
