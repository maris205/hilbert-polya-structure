# C320 source audit

## Primary lineage

- Y. Ohyama, *Differential Relations of Theta Functions*, Osaka J. Math.
  32 (1995), 431--450, historical and theta-function account:
  <https://ocu-omu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2009467/files/111F0000002-03202-12.pdf>.
- D. V. Chudnovsky-related/generalized Darboux--Halphen geometry is surveyed
  in the modern primary article, DOI
  <https://doi.org/10.3842/SIGMA.2018.003>, arXiv
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09682>.
- J. Harnad and J. McKay, modular solutions and triangle-function lineage,
  arXiv <https://arxiv.org/abs/solv-int/9902012>.

These sources own the classical system, modular/theta solution tradition,
and broader solution theory.  This package's contribution is a convention-
locked derivation and audit artifact, not a priority claim.
