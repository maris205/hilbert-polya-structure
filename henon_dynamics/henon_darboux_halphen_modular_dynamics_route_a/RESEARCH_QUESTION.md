# C320 research question

Can one freeze a single polynomial/nome/theta convention for the classical
Darboux--Halphen system and close, in one source-local theorem, its theta
solution, exact formal series, full PSL(2,C) covariance, Chazy reduction,
discriminant transport, invariant collision strata, cusp, and modular poles?

The question is deliberately stronger than another numerical theta example:
all three convention-sensitive interfaces--the polynomial ODE, the `Q`-series
normalization, and the `S,T` generator laws--must agree exactly.  It is
deliberately weaker than a classification of every meromorphic solution.

Route A is evaluated separately.  Intrinsic modular coefficients give only a
weak arithmetic relation; they do not supply target-prime ownership, an orbit
Euler ledger, a target determinant, or a Hilbert--Polya operator.
