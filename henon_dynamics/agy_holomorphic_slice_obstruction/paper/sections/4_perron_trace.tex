\section{Perron arithmetic of chronological trace atoms}
\label{sec:perron-trace}

The order convention matters before any spectral reduction.  Let
\(\boldsymbol\beta=(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n)\) list AGY returns in forward
time, from earliest to latest.  Rauzy matrices multiply later on the left:
\begin{equation}
 B_{\boldsymbol\beta}
 =B_{\beta_n}\cdots B_{\beta_1},
 \qquad
 A_{\boldsymbol\beta}=B_{\boldsymbol\beta}^T
 =A_{\beta_1}\cdots A_{\beta_n}.
 \label{eq:chronological-matrices}
\end{equation}
The inverse projective map and the corresponding term in \(L_s^n\) are
\begin{equation}
 h_{\boldsymbol\beta}
 =h_{\beta_1}\circ\cdots\circ h_{\beta_n}
 =p_{A_{\boldsymbol\beta}},
 \qquad
 T_{s,\beta_n}\cdots T_{s,\beta_1}.
 \label{eq:chronological-operators}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, if \((\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_n)\) indexes the operator product
\(T_{s,\gamma_1}\cdots T_{s,\gamma_n}\), then
\(A=A_{\gamma_n}\cdots A_{\gamma_1}\).  This equivalence prevents a
transpose from silently reversing time.

Every \(A_{\boldsymbol\beta}\) is a strictly positive matrix in
\(\SL(d,\mathbb Z)\).  Let \(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}>0\) be its Perron
root and put
\begin{equation}
 \chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}(t)
 =\det(tI-A_{\boldsymbol\beta})\in\mathbb Z[t].
 \label{eq:word-characteristic}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Perron--characteristic trace formula]
\label{thm:perron-trace}
The map \(h_{\boldsymbol\beta}\) has one fixed point in \(\Omega\), the
normalized positive Perron vector of \(A_{\boldsymbol\beta}\), and
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \tr\bigl(T_{s,\beta_n}\cdots T_{s,\beta_1}\bigr)
 =\frac{\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}^{-(s+1)}}
 {\chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}'(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta})}.}
 \label{eq:perron-atom}
\end{equation}
For every fixed \(n\),
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \tr L_s^n
 =\sum_{\boldsymbol\beta\in\Gamma^n}
 \frac{\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}^{-(s+1)}}
 {\chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}'(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta})},}
 \label{eq:word-trace-sum}
\end{equation}
and the sum converges absolutely and locally uniformly for
\(s\in\halfplane\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Strict positivity gives a simple Perron root and a positive eigenvector.
The corresponding normalized vector belongs to the real AGY base because
\(A_{\boldsymbol\beta}\) retains the fixed positive left factor after the
projective composition.  Compact containment gives uniqueness of the fixed
point in \(\Omega\).

Let \(x_0\) denote that fixed point and follow the inverse periodic history
in the order in which the maps in \eqref{eq:chronological-operators} act.
At each step write
\[
 A_{\gamma_k}x_{k-1}=q_kx_k,
 \qquad q_k=\ell(A_{\gamma_k}x_{k-1})>0.
\]
Multiplication around the closed history gives
\[
 A_{\boldsymbol\beta}x_0
 =\left(\prod_{k=1}^nq_k\right)x_0,
 \qquad
 \prod_{k=1}^nq_k=\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}.
\]
All intermediate normalizers are positive real numbers, so no principal-log
winding occurs.  The product of the scalar weights is therefore
\begin{equation}
 W_{s,\boldsymbol\beta}(x_0)
 =\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}^{-(s+d)}.
 \label{eq:telescoped-weight}
\end{equation}

Let \(V=\ker\ell\), the tangent space of \(H_\C\).  At a normalized Perron
vector \(x\), direct differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 Dp_A(x)v
 =\lambda^{-1}\bigl(Av-x\ell(Av)\bigr),
 \qquad v\in V.
 \label{eq:projective-derivative}
\end{equation}
The map \(V\to\C^d/\C x\), \(v\mapsto[v]\), is an isomorphism.  Under this
isomorphism \eqref{eq:projective-derivative} is the quotient action of
\(A/\lambda\).  Hence, without assuming diagonalizability of the other
eigenvalues,
\begin{equation}
 \det_{\C}(I-Dp_A(x))
 =\frac{\chi_A'(\lambda)}{\lambda^{d-1}}.
 \label{eq:projective-denominator}
\end{equation}
The fixed-point trace formula for holomorphic weighted composition
operators~\citep{bandtlow2008ruelle,bandtlow2008explicit} divides
\eqref{eq:telescoped-weight} by
\eqref{eq:projective-denominator}.  Since the Jacobian exponent is \(d=4\)
and the projective dimension is \(d-1=3\), the quotient is
\(\lambda^{-(s+1)}/\chi_A'(\lambda)\).

It remains to justify the countable trace interchange.  The branch series
converges absolutely in trace norm by \eqref{eq:branch-trace-norm}; therefore
\[
 \sum_{(\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_n)\in\Gamma^n}
 \|T_{s,\gamma_1}\cdots T_{s,\gamma_n}\|_1
 \leq
 \left(\sum_\gamma\|T_{s,\gamma}\|_1\right)^n<\infty.
\]
Taking the continuous Hilbert-space trace term by term proves
\eqref{eq:word-trace-sum}.  Local uniformity follows from the compact
\(s\)-set bound in \Cref{lem:complex-weight-sum}.
\end{proof}

The monic polynomial \(\chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}\) has constant term one,
so its Perron root is an algebraic unit.  The denominator
\(\chi'(\lambda)\) records separation from the other eigenvalues, but it is
not automatically a number-field discriminant: the characteristic
polynomial may be reducible or nonminimal.  For nonintegral complex \(s\),
the value \(\lambda^{-(s+1)}\) is likewise not asserted to be algebraic.

In the present full-rank four-dimensional model, the word matrix is
conjugate to a symplectic matrix, so
\begin{equation}
 \chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}(t)
 =t^4-a_{\boldsymbol\beta}t^3+b_{\boldsymbol\beta}t^2
 -a_{\boldsymbol\beta}t+1,
 \qquad a_{\boldsymbol\beta},b_{\boldsymbol\beta}\in\mathbb Z.
 \label{eq:reciprocal-polynomial}
\end{equation}
The atom depends on this polynomial and its Perron root, but
\eqref{eq:word-trace-sum} still counts every marked word.  Spectral
collisions do not quotient the symbolic dynamics.

Finally, the trace-class determinant identity gives
\begin{equation}
 -\log D(s,u)
 =\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{u^n}{n}
 \sum_{\boldsymbol\beta\in\Gamma^n}
 \frac{\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta}^{-(s+1)}}
 {\chi_{\boldsymbol\beta}'(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\beta})}
 \label{eq:orbit-log}
\end{equation}
for sufficiently small \(|u|\), locally uniformly on compact subsets of
\(\halfplane\).  The Fredholm determinant, not the orbit-log series, gives
the entire continuation in \(u\).  In particular, evaluation of
\eqref{eq:orbit-log} at \(u=1\) requires an additional convergence argument.
