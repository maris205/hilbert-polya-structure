\section{Exact certificate and independent replay}
\label{sec:certificate}

The infinite theorems above do not depend on a periodic cutoff.  A finite
certificate nevertheless fixes the source section, catches chronology and
dimension mutations, and supplies a rational essential-norm witness.  The
selected neat strongly positive loop is
\begin{equation}
 \gamma_*=t^{64}(tbttbtbb)^8,
 \qquad |\gamma_*|=128.
 \label{eq:gamma-star}
\end{equation}
Later elementary arrows multiply on the left, giving
\begin{equation}
 B_{\gamma_*}=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 18540&1210580&11430&27373\\
 24020&1568410&14783&35450\\
 50233&3279928&31130&74253\\
 38803&2533625&24020&57343
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det B_{\gamma_*}=1.
 \label{eq:gamma-star-matrix}
\end{equation}
All entries are positive.  Put \(R_{\gamma_*}=B_{\gamma_*}^T\) and
\begin{equation}
 x_0=
 \frac{(131596,8592543,81363,194419)}{8999921}.
 \label{eq:exact-x0}
\end{equation}
The exact normalizer and projective Jacobian are
\begin{align}
 S_*(x_0)
 &=\one^TR_{\gamma_*}x_0
 =\frac{15076979616018}{8999921},
 \label{eq:exact-normalizer}\\
 J_{\gamma_*}(x_0)
 &=S_*(x_0)^{-4}
 =\frac{6560769639033108250634950081}
 {51672252134321473356696529937672668896230786946152976}.
 \label{eq:exact-jacobian}
\end{align}
The exponent four in \eqref{eq:exact-jacobian} is the homogeneous
projective-Jacobian exponent, not the affine dimension three.

Keeping only this atom in \eqref{eq:evaluation-bound} gives
\begin{equation}
 \|\cL_s^{\rm Mp}\|_{\ess}
 \geq
 \frac{S_*(x_0)^{-(\Re s+4)}}
 {\|E_{x_0}\|\,\|J\|}.
 \label{eq:exact-essential-floor}
\end{equation}
At \(\Re s=0\), the numerator is exactly
\eqref{eq:exact-jacobian}.  Its numerical size, approximately
\(1.2696891\times10^{-25}\), is irrelevant: strict positivity is enough to
exclude compactness.

The positive-prefix geometry also has exact sentinels.  Normalizing the
columns of \(P=R_{\gamma_*}\) gives
\begin{equation}
 \delta_P
 =\min_{i,j}\frac{P_{ij}}{\sum_kP_{kj}}
 =\frac{14783}{1642663}>0.
 \label{eq:delta-prefix}
\end{equation}
The maximum Birkhoff cross ratio is
\begin{equation}
 \theta(P)
 =\frac{12206150825}{12121793906},
 \qquad
 \tanh\!\left(\frac{\log\theta(P)}4\right)
 \approx0.0017337504976364321.
 \label{eq:birkhoff-prefix}
\end{equation}
These numbers are not needed by the complex-cone proof, but they independently
verify strict positivity, precompactness, and a strong real contraction
margin.

For the second row the forward matrix and operator-factor order are
\[
 B_{2}=B_{R_2}B_G,
 \qquad (R_2,G).
\]
Reversal changes this matrix, but not its characteristic polynomial:
the two invertible products \(B_{R_2}B_G\) and \(B_GB_{R_2}\) are similar.
Thus this is a contravariant matrix-order sentinel, not a spectral one.
The third row is noncyclic: its forward matrix is
\(B_3=B_{R_3}B_{R_2}B_G\), with operator factors \((R_3,R_2,G)\).
Reversing forward order gives \(B_GB_{R_2}B_{R_3}\), whose reciprocal
coefficients are exactly
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a_{\rm rev}&=78{,}462{,}676{,}839{,}072{,}961{,}074{,}051{,}275{,}858{,}065,\\
 b_{\rm rev}&=193{,}788{,}302{,}599{,}985{,}746{,}036{,}399{,}604{,}537{,}872{,}487{,}742{,}569{,}559.
 \end{aligned}
\]
They differ from the third-row \((a,b)\), so the three-return example is a
genuine spectral chronology sentinel.

projective derivative agrees with
\(\chi'(\lambda)/\lambda^3\) to the errors shown in the table.

The producer and checker independently reconstruct seven Rauzy states,
fourteen edges, the complete 128-arrow word, all three Perron examples in
\Cref{tab:perron-certificate}, and every rational value above.  The checker
does not import the producer and uses a different derivative computation.
A separate length-20 mutation sentinel finds 13,528 first returns and 13,528
distinct matrices.  That finite replay tests code; it is not evidence for
all-length injectivity or completeness.  Full commands and artifact hashes
are recorded in \Cref{app:reproducibility}.
