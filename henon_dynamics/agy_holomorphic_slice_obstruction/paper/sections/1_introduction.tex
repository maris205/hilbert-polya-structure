\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Analytic contraction of a scalar base does not compactify an
infinite-dimensional unitary fibre.  This elementary warning becomes a
decisive operator theorem for the countable induced Rauzy system constructed
by Avila, Gou\"ezel, and Yoccoz (AGY)~\citep{agy2006}.  The scalar system has
the geometry required by holomorphic transfer theory, while the literal
oscillator twist remains noncompact on the very same complex domain.

The operator-space question is subtle because the obvious real-variable
localizer disappears after complexification.  In a bounded \(C^1\) space, a
bump supported in one inverse-branch cylinder can isolate one unitary fibre
operator.  A nonzero holomorphic function cannot have that support.  It is
therefore not enough to transfer a real noncompactness proof to a Bergman or
Hardy space.  Conversely, scalar nuclearity theorems for compactly contained
holomorphic branches do not automatically apply to operator-valued weights;
the compactness criteria of \citet{bonet2012} likewise retain an explicit
fibre condition.

We resolve both sides without changing the dynamics.  AGY's branch grammar
makes each return either the fixed strongly positive path \(\gamma_*\) or a
minimal path \(\gamma_*\gamma_0\gamma_*\)
\citep[Section~4.1.3 and Lemma~4.4, pp.~162, 165]{agy2006}.  Later-on-the-left
Rauzy multiplication and transposition then give our factorization
\[
 A_\gamma=B_\gamma^T=P C_\gamma,
 \qquad P>0,\quad C_\gamma\geq0.
\]
The fixed map \(p_P\) sends the closure of a canonical complex cone strictly
inside that cone, whereas every \(p_{C_\gamma}\) preserves it.  An enlarged
intermediate domain \(\Omega\) therefore satisfies
\[
 \bigcup_{\gamma}h_\gamma(\Omega)\Subset\Omega
\]
for the complete countable return family.  The real AGY exponential-tail
estimate then controls the complex weights through one right-half-plane
logarithm.  The Bergman results of
\citet{bandtlow2008ruelle,bandtlow2008explicit} yield a legitimate scalar
Fredholm determinant and an ordinary fixed-point trace formula.

The scalar periodic atoms simplify further.  A later-on-the-left return word
has a positive integral matrix \(A_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\), a simple Perron
root \(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\), and a monic characteristic polynomial
\(\chi_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\).  Normalizers telescope to the Perron root,
while the projective derivative is the quotient action of
\(A_{\boldsymbol\gamma}/\lambda_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\).  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \tr T_{s,\boldsymbol\gamma}
 =\frac{\lambda_{\boldsymbol\gamma}^{-(s+1)}}
 {\chi_{\boldsymbol\gamma}'(\lambda_{\boldsymbol\gamma})}.
 \label{eq:intro-trace-atom}
\end{equation}
This formula is arithmetic in a precise but limited sense: the Perron root
is an algebraic unit attached to a chronological integer matrix.  No
prime-orbit law, automorphic interpretation, or field-discriminant identity
is inferred.

The infinite fibre is tested after the scalar construction is complete.
Let \(U_\gamma\) be the metaplectic operator obtained by composing the
declared edge lifts in true time order on
\(\cF=L^2(\R^2)\); standard phase-space conventions are recalled from
\citet{folland1989}.  Constants and evaluation at a real interior point
compress the vector-valued Bergman operator to
\[
 \sum_\gamma w_{s,\gamma}(x_0)U_\gamma.
\]
The projected symplectic atoms are pairwise distinct, and coherent-state
separation gives
\begin{equation}
 \|\cL_s^{\rm Mp}\|_{\ess}
 \geq
 \frac{\bigl(\sum_\gamma|w_{s,\gamma}(x_0)|^2\bigr)^{1/2}}
 {\|E_{x_0}\|\,\|J\|}>0.
 \label{eq:intro-essential}
\end{equation}
No branch-supported holomorphic function is used.

The contributions are therefore concrete and falsifiable:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item We construct one bounded complex domain for every inverse branch of
 the countable AGY return map using only the fixed positive prefix.
 \item We prove scalar Bergman trace class, trace-norm word interchange, and
 the exact Perron--characteristic formula~\eqref{eq:intro-trace-atom}.
 \item We prove the same-domain noncompactness bound
 \eqref{eq:intro-essential} for the unsmoothed oscillator twist.
 \item We provide exact and independently replayed arithmetic for a
 length-128 source branch, a two-return matrix-order sentinel, and a
 noncyclic three-return spectral-order sentinel.
\end{enumerate}

The paper separates inherited inputs from new deductions.  AGY supply the
branches, real summability, and positive-path grammar~\citep{agy2006}; the
fixed-left-factor identity is our chronological algebraic deduction.  Prior
project theorems supply all-length branch
matrix injectivity and the abstract discrete-metaplectic atom estimate;
\Cref{app:operator-proofs} restates the needed form.  The common complex
domain, scalar trace theorem, Perron reduction, and same-domain evaluation
application are the present results.  \Cref{sec:complex-domain} constructs
the domain, \Cref{sec:scalar-transfer,sec:perron-trace} establish the scalar
determinant, and \Cref{sec:oscillator} proves the obstruction.  Exact
certificates and the Hilbert--P\'olya assessment follow in
\Cref{sec:certificate,sec:route-a}.
