# HCS-C26 devil's-advocate checkpoint 1

## Verdict

**PASS for the point-evaluation no-go theorem.**  The question is narrow,
falsifiable, and directly closes an escape explicitly left open by HCS-C25.
The stronger scalar-nuclearity half is **HOLD** pending a uniform complex
domain proof.

## Strongest objection

The evaluation theorem may look almost formal once C24 and C25 are
available.  That objection is correct about proof length but not about
scope: C25's branch-supported bump does not exist in a holomorphic space,
whereas the constant/evaluation compression exposes the full countable
operator without a localizer.  The result is useful only if the paper states
this dependency and does not advertise the C24 atomic theorem as new.

## Potential fatal errors

1. **Projected collisions.**  Full Rauzy matrices being distinct would not
   suffice in a class with relative-homology kernel.  Here C25 separately
   proves that the `H(2)` crossing form has full rank, so the fixed symplectic
   conjugation loses no information.
2. **Central signs.**  Coefficients cannot be replaced by absolute values
   after aggregating equal projected atoms.  The decoder removes equality of
   projected atoms; pathwise lifts preserve the actual sign.
3. **Unbounded evaluation.**  Some anisotropic distribution spaces have no
   continuous point evaluation.  They are outside the main theorem and must
   not be claimed as closed.
4. **Non-literal realization.**  A correlated base--fibre completion might
   fail to contain constants or might not realize the branch series
   pointwise.  Such a construction is a new operator model, not a
   counterexample to the theorem.
5. **Scalar/vector confusion.**  A trace-class scalar weighted composition
   operator does not make its tensor with an infinite-dimensional unitary
   trace class.  Scalar nuclearity, if proved, strengthens the contrast but
   cannot rescue the oscillator twist.
6. **Complex-domain overreach.**  Uniform real Hilbert contraction does not
   by itself prove one bounded complex domain with uniform compact
   containment for countably many branches.

## Required wording constraints

- Say “all standard point-evaluative realizations satisfying the displayed
  boundedness assumptions,” not “all holomorphic or anisotropic spaces.”
- Say “ordinary Hilbert/Banach determinant-class realization fails,” not
  “no generalized zeta can exist.”
- Treat Bargmann--Fock as a unitary reformulation, not a new smoothing
  mechanism.
- Treat heat/Hermite damping as a changed cocycle unless a dynamical source
  forces it and proves an exact cocycle law.

## Pivot criterion

Once the main no-go theorem, exact witness, and independent replay pass,
additional variations of Hardy/Bergman/RKHS norms are small doors.  The next
large candidate should change the fibre itself:

\[
g_\gamma\bmod q\in\operatorname{Sp}(4,\mathbb F_q),
\qquad
\rho_q(g_\gamma)\in U(q^2),
\]

for odd primes `q`.  This finite Weil tower preserves chronology, admits an
ordinary finite trace, and introduces genuine arithmetic Gauss sums.  It is
not to be sold as a limit that recovers the nonexistent ordinary trace of
the infinite oscillator representation.
