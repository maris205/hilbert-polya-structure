# C196 research question

For the repulsive rational Calogero--Moser Hamiltonian

\[
H=\frac12\sum_jp_j^2+\sum_{j<k}\frac{g^2}{(q_j-q_k)^2},
\qquad N\ge2,\quad g>0,
\]

can one close, in one all-parameter theorem and paper, a physical-time
Hermitian-pencil solution with the exact signs and factor of two, all-time
simple spectrum and global completeness, every trace integral, both
scattering ends with ordered-rank reversal, a globally invertible intercept
atlas, absence of bounded nonconstant periodic motion, and a strict Route-A
verdict without prime/zero/local or Route-B input?

The answer is yes.  The analytic closure uses the rank-one commutator,
Hermitian eigenvalue perturbation, and the classical Calogero--Moser/Moser
solution.  A deterministic 18-system oracle checks conventions only; the
infinite quantifier does not come from finite computation.
