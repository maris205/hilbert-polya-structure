# C196 paper plan

## Title and contribution

*The Free Rational Calogero--Moser Flow as a Hermitian Pencil: Global
Scattering, Ordering Reversal, and the Route-A Boundary*

One rank-one commutator closes the all-parameter repulsive rational flow from
collision-free evolution through a global two-ended scattering atlas and
proves that no bounded periodic dynamics remain to support Route A.

## Actual improvement rounds

- **Round 0:** Hamiltonian/Lax signs, physical clock, simple pencil, exact
  Newton coefficient, completeness, and trace integrals.
- **Round 1:** ordered `lambda`, gauged intercepts, the complete forward
  formula, inverse global atlas, and a no-symplectic-overclaim boundary.
- **Round 2:** both ends, rank reversal, preserved line intercepts,
  aperiodicity, evidence totals, degenerate/model boundaries, natural quantum
  realization, declarations, and the strict Route-A tuple.

The source changed and was recompiled between stages.  The commutator, atlas,
and two asymptotic formulas communicate the key relationships more precisely
than a decorative figure.
