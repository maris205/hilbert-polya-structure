# C213 narrative report

The decisive advance is an all-mode semigroup theorem rather than a short
simulation: the circular telegraph generator is an explicit two-by-two block
for every integer Fourier mode.  Squaring the centered block gives a scalar,
so one formula covers hyperbolic, oscillatory and Jordan regimes.  The same
identity yields the telegraph PDE and the exact eigenvalue atlas.

The gap result is deliberately stated as a sharp spectral-abscissa gap.  At
critical blocks a polynomial transient can occur, so no constant-free
operator-norm decay is asserted.  The \(|k|\to\infty\) limit proves the
essential-norm boundary and explains why positive flip rate does not make the
semigroup compact.  Degenerate speed and flip-rate faces are retained.

The negative result is equally precise: Fourier characteristic polynomials are
finite source algebra, not target determinants or arithmetic data.  Route A is
therefore rejected despite the complete Markov/PDE theorem.
