# Research question

Can a genuinely infinite-dimensional Markov semigroup with a finite internal
velocity state be closed in one exact theorem, including its singular speed,
flip-rate, critical-Jordan and noncompactness boundaries?

For the circular telegraph process the answer is yes.  Fourier diagonalization
reduces the generator to a two-by-two family, and the Cayley--Hamilton identity
gives a uniform matrix exponential for every mode.  The answer is no as an
arithmetic Route-A candidate: no prime carrier, target divisor or isolated
primitive-orbit owner is present.  This separation is the scientific boundary
of C213.
