# C172 research question

For multiplication by a primitive element on every finite field, can one
derive the complete periodic-orbit inventory, Artin--Mazur zeta, Koopman
spectrum/determinant, time reversal and exact self-adjoint boundary uniformly
in the prime power \(Q\), while determining whether prime-power cardinality is
enough to pass the Route-A arithmetic gate?

## Decisive progress criterion

The result must be all-parameter rather than a list of examples: it must prove
the unique nonzero cycle, every fixed-point count, both determinant identities,
the inversion reversor and self-adjointness if and only if \(Q\leq3\).  It must
also compare controls that share the same cycle type.

## Failure and pivot rule

If the nonzero dynamics depended on unresolved finite-field representation
choices or if only prime fields worked, the model would be rejected.  Primitive
coordinates reduce the entire problem to translation on \(\mathbb Z/(Q-1)\),
so the theorem closes for all prime powers.  The same reduction also exposes
genericity and prevents promotion beyond a weak A0 verdict.
