# C172 narrative report

The paper advances a genuinely different dynamical subtype: algebraic
permutation dynamics over every finite field.  Primitive coordinates turn
multiplication into one cyclic translation, yielding an exact orbit inventory,
all fixed counts, a closed Artin--Mazur zeta, the complete Koopman spectrum and
determinant, an inversion time reversor, and the sharp self-adjoint threshold
\(Q\leq3\).  Every statement is uniform in the prime power and primitive
generator.

The arithmetic assessment is deliberately stricter than the algebra.  Finite
fields give an intrinsic prime-power source, so A0 is not a total failure.
However, the single nonzero orbit has period \(Q-1\), not a derived rational
prime label or \(\log p\) clock.  An abstract composite cyclic surrogate and a
random permutation with the same cycle type reproduce every displayed zeta
and spectral invariant.  Therefore the result establishes only a weak
arithmetic relation and remains exploratory.

The Koopman operator is a natural same-clock finite unitary lift, justifying
`A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION`.  It is not self-adjoint beyond \(Q=3\), and nothing
in the proof supplies target divisors, global analytic structure, arithmetic
local data or Route-B readiness.
