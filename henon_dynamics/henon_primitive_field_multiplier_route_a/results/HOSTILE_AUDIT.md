## Seven failure modes

1. **Citation hallucination:** no bibliography, citation or priority claim;
   registered population is zero.
2. **Invented experiment:** only exact deterministic reconstruction is
   reported.
3. **Finite-to-general overclaim:** finite sentinels are not the proof; the
   exponent-coordinate argument covers every prime power.
4. **Fabricated review:** no external reviewer, score or acceptance forecast.
5. **Determinant conflation:** Artin--Mazur zeta and Koopman determinant are
   defined separately before their reciprocal identity is proved.
6. **Arithmetic leakage:** composite cyclic and same-cycle permutation controls
   reproduce the formulas; no rational-prime/logarithmic weight is inferred.
7. **Operator leakage:** Koopman is a natural unitary, but self-adjointness is
   claimed only for \(Q\leq3\); no target Hamiltonian is inferred.
