# Hostile audit

All 75 attacks were rejected.  The 54 evidence-JSON attacks include repaired-hash specimens that alter the integer
spectral theorem, Friedrichs proof contract, channel eigenvalues, heat
values, multiplicities, counts, source-zeta values, flux reductions, and
the line-Laplacian a.c. multiplicity from two to one.  Structural specimens
duplicate/drop every cell layer.  Raw specimens inject duplicate top/nested
keys, `NaN`, and a non-object root.  Exact-type specimens test JSON booleans
where integers are required, integers where strings are required, and
noncanonical rational strings.

The other 21 evaluation-YAML attacks cover raw duplicate top/nested keys,
unknown/missing top or nested keys, source/evaluator and theorem locks,
tuple/overall/Route-B changes, scope literal/flag drift, axis changes,
bool/int confusion, and a non-object root.  A duplicate-rejecting safe loader
precedes exact recursive schema and canonical semantic-hash validation.

The checker imports no producer code.  It reconstructs heat cells by summing
individual Fourier–Hermite levels, enumerates factor pairs and odd divisors,
and checks the double-index zeta sum independently.  The finite receipt is
not used as a substitute for the operator proof.
