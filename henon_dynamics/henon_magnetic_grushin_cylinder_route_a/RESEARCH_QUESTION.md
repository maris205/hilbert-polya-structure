# Research question

Can the magnetic Baouendi–Grushin cylinder be closed at theorem scale using
only its Friedrichs form: all flux channels, the noninteger compact-resolvent
regime, the integer resonant free channel with its full spectral type, the
embedded oscillator point spectrum, exact heat traces, odd-divisor
multiplicity, a source-local zeta series, and the logarithmic Weyl law?

The frozen answer is **yes**.  Essential self-adjointness on a smaller core
is not asserted.  At integer flux the phrase “one free Fourier channel” must
not be confused with spectral multiplicity one: the line Laplacian has a.e.
absolutely continuous multiplicity two for positive energy.
