# C92 research question

For each of the twenty frozen subgroup targets (H) and each named label
(S_i), what is the exact uniform-random-order contribution of (S_i) to
the first-passage time (T_H)?  Can the labelwise pivotal rank laws recover
the C88 mean and second moment without sampling?

The answer is a finite sensitivity atlas over all (20\times16) target-label
pairs.  It is not a new group action and does not claim arithmetic or
operator structure.
