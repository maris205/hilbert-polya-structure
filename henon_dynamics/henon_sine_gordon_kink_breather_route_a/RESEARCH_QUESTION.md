# Research question

Can the one-dimensional sine--Gordon equation be closed exactly on a useful
coherent-family atlas—travelling kink/antikink heteroclinics, rest/boosted
breathers, conserved energy--momentum, topological charge, and the rest-kink
linear spectrum—while keeping the non-classification and non-arithmetic
boundaries explicit?

Yes for the declared families.  The kink ODE has only the subluminal Lorentz
profiles, the breather formula is exact for $0<\Omega<1$, and the rest-kink
Hessian is the factorized Pöschl--Teller operator.  The answer is no for an
isolated arithmetic primitive orbit: the breather family is continuous and the
receipt makes no target, Euler, root-number, automorphy, or Hilbert--Pólya
claim.
