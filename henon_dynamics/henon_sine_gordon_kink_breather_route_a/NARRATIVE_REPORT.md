# Narrative report

The sine--Gordon equation exhibits two complementary exact geometries.  A
monotone travelling heteroclinic is forced by its first integral to be a
Lorentz kink or antikink; its energy and momentum form a relativistic mass
shell.  The same PDE also supports a zero-charge, continuously parameterized
breather family.  Boosting transports the energy--momentum ledger, but it
transports the internal clock as a proper/comoving clock rather than creating a
period at a fixed laboratory point.

At the rest kink, translation is the only discrete neutral mode of the
Pöschl--Teller Hessian; the remainder starts at the essential edge $1$.  These
closed identities are a substantial PDE advance, while the continuous
breather parameter and lack of an isolated primitive owner force an honest
Route-A stop.  No arithmetic labels are extracted from the frequency or mass
shell.
