# Paper plan

1. Freeze the minimal nonexplosive branching chain, physical time and
   one-ancestor normalization.
2. Derive the Riccati backward equation and both M\"obius clocks.
3. Prove the semigroup by exact M\"obius matrices.
4. Expand the one-ancestor zero-modified geometric law.
5. Derive the arbitrary-`z` survivor-binomial/conditional-negative-binomial
   transition and state its collapse exceptions.
6. Differentiate the PGF for the all-rate mean and variance.
7. Close critical, pure-birth, pure-death, zero-rate, zero-population and
   zero-time degeneracies.
8. Prove exact conditional-semigroup invariance of the subcritical geometric
   quasi-stationary law, its attraction, the critical Yaglom limit and the
   supercritical martingale atom/gamma limit separately from the finite
   regression.
9. Document independent checking, symbolic reconstruction, byte replay and
   hostile mutations.
10. Apply the strict Route-A and claim firewalls, followed by reproducibility,
    ethics, contribution and AI-use declarations.

No figure is required: the central objects are exact formulas and limit
decompositions, all of which are clearer in equations and a boundary table.
