# Hostile audit

The mutation harness attacked the frozen source commit, evaluator hash, scope
literal, headline, PGF naming convention, Route-A tuple and verdict, Route-B
authorization, forbidden-claim flags, both source identifiers, `p0`, `beta`,
M\"obius coefficients, transition coefficients, survivor weights, moments,
semigroup composition, summary totals, and unknown top-level/nested keys.

For 22 semantic/schema attacks, including direct corruption of the exact QSD
conditional-semigroup invariance field, the harness recomputed the payload
SHA-256 before invoking the checker. Every repaired-hash attack failed
semantically. A twenty-third attack changed a variance while retaining the
stale hash and also failed. The two unknown-key attacks demonstrate recursive
schema closure rather than permissive JSON parsing.

The audit also forced two theorem-wording checks:

1. the arbitrary-population transition must retain the binomial number of
   surviving ancestral lines and the conditional negative-binomial sum; it is
   not uniformly one negative-binomial family, although special parameters
   can collapse the mixture; and
2. the supercritical limit must retain the extinction atom, the initial-`z`
   binomial weights and the gamma shape dependence.

Result: **23/23 hostile mutations rejected**. This is an internal development
control, not external review or an acceptance score.
