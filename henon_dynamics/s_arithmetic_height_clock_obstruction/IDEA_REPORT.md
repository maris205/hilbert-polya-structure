# Candidate-selection report

## Candidate screen

This round sought a structural change rather than another modification of a
finite-state determinant.

| Candidate | Structural feature | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Compact quaternionic $\Gamma\backslash(\mathbb H\times T_p)$ | independent real/$p$-adic coordinates and a compact Laplace--Hecke setting | selected for an explicit arithmetic example; the tested rank-one baseline meets periodic-flat, one-flat convergence, and Weyl-order obstructions |
| Noncompact arithmetic scattering with a countable-state return map | scattering and cusp terms can change the compact-surface spectral mechanism | retained only as a future direction; no construction or novelty claim is made here |

## Why HCS-C16 was selected

The explicit $S$-arithmetic product offered three ingredients:

1. a non-Archimedean local displacement;
2. a natural fixed-prime Hecke correspondence;
3. a compact automorphic spectral baseline.

For the chosen generators, the signed clock matrix satisfies

\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}A&C\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=A\ne0.
\]

This is a concrete positive arithmetic calculation. It does not by itself
define a dynamical determinant or establish novelty for higher-rank zeta
theory.

## Main finding

The real-place projection of the rank-two lattice is nonproper. Primitive
$S$-unit directions approach the real Weyl wall while escaping in the tree
direction. Consequently, the real-only one-flat class product fails a
necessary convergence condition.

The absolute Weil height retains both local contributions through

\[
H=\ell_\infty+(\log13)\ell_{13}=2h(r).
\]

This identity provides a proper scalar clock on the explicit centralizer, but
it does not replace the periodic-flat geometry or supply a global trace
formula.

## Why the height clock does not complete Route A

- The centralizer quotient of the minimum flat is a flat torus, and the
  corresponding periodic geodesics are not isolated.
- Higher-rank flat counting, Lefschetz weights, and Hecke trace structures have
  substantial prior art, including Dang--Li's work on periodic flat tori.
- The full compact Laplace--Hecke baseline retains $T^2$ Weyl order.
- No global regulator-weighted determinant, canonical sparse projection, or
  unbounded height operator is derived.

The project is therefore positioned as an explicit arithmetic example with a
scoped Route-A assessment.

## Future-system boundary

A future noncompact scattering/countable-return study would require a fresh
source audit before implementation. Classical modular scattering already
contains Riemann-zeta ratios, so merely repackaging those identities would not
constitute a new construction. No claim about such a future system is included
in HCS-C16.
