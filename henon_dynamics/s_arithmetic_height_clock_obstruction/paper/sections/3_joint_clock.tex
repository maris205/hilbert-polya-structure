\section{An exact two-place clock}

For $a+b\sqrt3\in K$, multiplication in the basis $(1,\sqrt3)$ is
\[
M(a,b)=\begin{pmatrix}a&3b\\b&a\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det M(a,b)=a^2-3b^2.
\]
Set
\[
\cA=2\log(2+\sqrt3),
\qquad
\cC=\log\frac{4+\sqrt3}{4-\sqrt3}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[joint-clock certificate]\label{thm:joint-clock}
After orienting the real axis and the $13$-adic apartment, the signed Cartan
map on the centralizer lattice is
\[
\mathbf c(\gamma_{m,n})=(m\cA+n\cC,n).
\]
In particular,
\[
\ellinf(\gamma_{m,n})=|m\cA+n\cC|,
\qquad
\ellp(\gamma_{m,n})=|n|,
\]
and the clock has rank two, with covolume
\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}\cA&\cC\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=\cA>0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At the two real embeddings, the eigenvalue ratios are
\[
\frac{2+\sqrt3}{2-\sqrt3}=(2+\sqrt3)^2,
\qquad
\frac{4+\sqrt3}{4-\sqrt3},
\]
with logarithms $\cA$ and $\cC$.  At $13$, use the Hensel lifts of the two
roots of $X^2-3$ congruent to $\pm4\pmod {13}$.  The components of $\eps$
are units.  The components of $\pi$ have valuations $0$ and $1$.
Translation length on the Bruhat--Tits tree is the absolute
eigenvalue-valuation difference.  Additivity on the torus proves the
formula.
\end{proof}

There is a useful invariant check that avoids numerical $p$-adic
diagonalization.  At the real place let the element be
orientation-preserving hyperbolic with positive reduced norm $N>0$; at the
finite place let it be $\Q_p$-split regular.  If its reduced trace is $t$
and $\Delta=t^2-4N$, then the respective local formulae are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:invariant-length}
\ellinf=2\operatorname{arcosh}\frac{|t|}{2\sqrt N},
\qquad
\ell_p=\max\{0,v_p(N)-v_p(\Delta)\}.
\end{equation}
Indeed, if the two eigenvalue valuations are $a<b$, then
$v_p(\Delta)=2a$ and $v_p(N)=a+b$; equal valuations give zero projective
translation.  For $\eps$ and $\pi$ the triples $(t,N,\Delta)$ are
$(4,1,12)$ and $(8,13,12)$, respectively.

\begin{remark}
The determinant in \cref{thm:joint-clock} is the first positive gate.  It
rules out a fake coupling in which the tree displacement is a fixed multiple
of the real length.  Global product-formula cancellation occurs between
conjugate local components; it does not identify the two place coordinates.
\end{remark}

Primitive joint boxes already exhibit the rank-two geometry.  Visible
lattice-point density gives, as $X,Y\to\infty$ with $X/Y$ bounded away from
$0$ and $\infty$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:box-count}
\#\{\gamma_{m,n}:\gcd(m,n)=1,
\ellinf\le X,\ellp\le Y\}/\{\pm1\}
\sim\frac{12XY}{\pi^2\cA}.
\end{equation}
The high-precision reproducible counts for $X=Y=10,40,160,320$ are
$48,742,11841,47349$; the last ratio to the main term is
$1.001685\ldots$.
