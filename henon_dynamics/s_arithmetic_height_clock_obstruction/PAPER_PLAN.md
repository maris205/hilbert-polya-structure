# Paper plan and claim--evidence map

## Working title

**An Explicit Quaternionic $S$-Arithmetic Height Clock: A Scoped Route-A
Assessment**

## Central thesis

One explicit compact real/$p$-adic quaternionic system has an independent
two-place clock. Its real projection is nonproper on primitive centralizer
directions, while the absolute Weil height gives a proper scalarization. The
same example remains periodic-flat dynamics, and its direct full compact
Laplace--Hecke baseline has $T^2$, rather than $T\log T$, spectral counting.

## Proof versus computation

The theorem statements rest on algebraic, geometric, Diophantine, height, and
spectral arguments. The finite tables are high-precision reproducible
numerical checks. They do not prove asymptotic constants, divergence, novelty,
or a global prime-geodesic statement.

## Red-line wording

Do not claim:

- invention of periodic-flat, higher-rank, or Hecke--Ruelle zeta theory;
- a global regulator-weighted determinant for the full quotient;
- that the one-flat class product is a dynamical determinant;
- a global prime-geodesic theorem from the finite direction counts;
- a universal no-go theorem for $S$-arithmetic, conductor-growing, scattering,
  sparse-projection, or unbounded systems;
- a Hilbert--Pólya operator;
- exact finite transcendental-boundary counts when the computation is
  high-precision numerical.
