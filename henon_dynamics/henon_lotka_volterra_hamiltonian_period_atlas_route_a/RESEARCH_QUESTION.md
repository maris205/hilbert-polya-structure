# Research question

Can one obtain a global period/action atlas for the positive two-species
Lotka--Volterra flow from a single first integral, while retaining exact
quadrature and cycle averages at the same physical clock? The answer here is
yes for the positive parameter family: logarithmic coordinates expose a
strictly convex proper Hamiltonian, so every positive level is one periodic
oval. Lambert-W branches turn the oval into an explicit one-dimensional
quadrature, and coarea gives the action derivative.

The result is intentionally a source-native continuous-time theorem. It does
not identify the period family with any arithmetic target, and it does not
assert period monotonicity or an unproved large-energy expansion.
