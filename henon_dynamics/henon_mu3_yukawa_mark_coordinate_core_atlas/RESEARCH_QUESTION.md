# C72 research question

Let `8C ~= Z/3 + Z/18` be the universal complement core found in C71 and
let `8[S_1],...,8[S_16]` be its named coordinate classes.  Determine:

1. an explicit coordinate realization of all sixteen classes;
2. every subgroup generated by a named support;
3. whether named supports reach the complete subgroup lattice of `8C`;
4. the exact subgroup-type distribution for all `2^16` supports;
5. the complete minimal named generating complex and its rank distribution.

The named-support rank is presentation dependent and must not be identified
with the abstract generator rank of `Z/3 + Z/18`.

Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
