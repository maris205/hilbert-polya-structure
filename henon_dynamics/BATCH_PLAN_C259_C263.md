# Route-A independent cross-subtype batch plan: C259--C263

## Frozen five-paper round

The round consists of five complete papers, not five installments of one
paper:

1. **C259 — tree Kuramoto locking and Morse atlas.**  Prove the exact subtree
   cut-flow chamber, all `2^(N-1)` inverse-sine branches, branch mergers,
   quotient Morse indices, unique stable branch, and violation/nullity faces.
2. **C260 — projective Möbius dynamics over finite fields.**  Prove the four
   `PGL_2(F_q)` types, full cycle/fixed/primitive/zeta/Koopman ledgers,
   characteristic-two classifier, reversors, and exact all-order type census.
3. **C261 — periodic Airy cubic-Talbot revivals.**  Prove the unitary group,
   least global time return, every rational translated-copy formula, exact
   strobe order, fixed subspaces, finite-support periods, and irrational face.
4. **C262 — two-segment square-wave Hill oscillator.**  Prove the unified
   entire segment matrices, monodromy discriminant, complete Floquet chamber,
   Chebyshev iterate closure, Jordan band edges, and every zero/constant face.
5. **C263 — multicolor Pólya urn and Dirichlet directing measure.**  Prove the
   exchangeable word law, all finite count/marginal/factorial-moment formulas,
   martingale and de Finetti limit, plus iid, zero-mass, and one-color faces.

## Per-paper theorem and evidence gates

Each package must satisfy all of the following before batch release.

### G0 — frozen source owner

- State space, parameters, one-step or physical-time clock, normalization,
  boundary policy, and determinant convention are explicit.
- The theorem is independent of the other four candidates.  No proof may
  borrow another candidate's evaluator coordinate to improve its own tuple.
- The closest C1--C258 owners and the exact distinction are written in the
  source audit and paper.

### G1 — theorem-scale closure

- C259 closes every locked branch and its quotient inertia, not just the
  stable solution or a numerical phase portrait.
- C260 closes every projective element type and every iterate for arbitrary
  prime powers, not just sampled fields.
- C261 closes every rational time and every sampled fixed space, not a gallery
  of low denominators.
- C262 closes the whole sign/band/band-edge atlas and exact iterates, not only
  one stable parameter rectangle.
- C263 closes arbitrary factorial moments and the limiting directing measure,
  not merely mean/variance or a simulation.

Finite regression rows are implementation oracles and never substitute for
the all-parameter proof.

### G2 — executable certificate

Every package contains:

- a deterministic producer with canonical JSON output and an internal payload
  SHA-256;
- a producer-independent checker that imports no producer implementation;
- an independent SymPy reconstruction of generic identities and stored-row
  controls;
- canonical byte replay from a fresh output path;
- repaired-hash semantic hostile mutations plus a stale-hash control;
- exact counts for the enumerated parameter, state, word, tree, field, strobe,
  or Floquet rows appropriate to the owner.

The checker must exercise equality and singular faces, not only generic
interior parameters.  Mutation targets must include metadata, source/evaluator
locks, scope, Route-A tuple, Route-B flag, central theorem cells, and boundary
policies.

### G3 — complete research and paper package

Each candidate carries exactly these 27 manifest payloads before the
self-excluded manifest is written:

1. eight root documents: `README.md`, `RESEARCH_QUESTION.md`,
   `THEOREM_PACKAGE.md`, `SOURCE_AUDIT.md`, `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`,
   `NARRATIVE_REPORT.md`, `PAPER_PLAN.md`, and `PAPER_IMPROVEMENT_LOG.md`;
2. seven code documents/scripts: `code/README.md`, producer, independent
   checker, SymPy cross-check, byte replay, hostile mutation, and release
   manifest generator;
3. one evaluator YAML under `evaluations/route_a/HCS-Cxxx/2026-08-31.yaml`;
4. seven paper artifacts: `paper/README.md`, `paper/COMPILE_REPORT.md`,
   `paper/main.tex`, round-0 PDF, round-1 PDF, round-2 PDF, and final PDF;
5. four result artifacts: evidence JSON, `results/RESULTS.md`,
   `results/TEST_REPORT.md`, and `results/HOSTILE_AUDIT.md`.

The release generator then writes the self-excluded content-addressed manifest,
giving 28 physical files.  No LaTeX sidecar, bytecode cache, or undeclared file
may remain inside a package.

### G4 — two substantive manuscript revisions

- Round 0 freezes the owner and central theorem.
- Round 1 adds a genuine theorem layer: branch/inertia, census/reversor,
  sampled-fixed classification, Chebyshev/Jordan closure, or all-moment
  formulas as appropriate.
- Round 2 closes proof gaps, singular faces, collision audit, exact executable
  counts, and the strict Route-A boundary.

All three PDFs must have different hashes.  `paper/main.pdf` must equal
`paper/main_round2.pdf` byte-for-byte.  An improvement log must describe the
actual content delta; cosmetic-only revisions do not pass.

### G5 — deterministic PDF and visual audit

- Compile with LuaLaTeX under `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1788048000`,
  `FORCE_SOURCE_DATE=1`, `TZ=UTC`, and a fixed trailer ID.
- Use two passes in fresh build trees for every retained revision.
- Rebuild the final source in two additional fresh trees and require byte
  equality with each other and with `paper/main.pdf`.
- Require extractable text, embedded/subset fonts, correct page count, working
  DOI links where citations are used, and no overfull/underfull boxes,
  undefined references/citations, duplicate destinations, missing assets, or
  layout warnings in the settled pass.
- Render and visually inspect every page.  A successful compiler exit alone is
  not a visual-quality certificate.

### G6 — content-addressed release closure

The release script reruns producer, checker, SymPy, replay, and mutation; checks
the evaluator/source/scope locks; verifies all scope flags are false; checks
round-PDF distinction and final equality; audits fonts and extracted text;
hashes every declared payload; excludes only its own manifest and named build
sidecars; and requires exactly 27 payloads plus 1 manifest.

The batch review will record for each candidate:

- evidence payload hash and evidence-file hash;
- round and final PDF hashes;
- checker, symbolic, and hostile-mutation counts;
- page and embedded-font counts;
- final manifest hash;
- strict Route-A tuple, verdict, and `route_b_invocation_allowed: false`.

## Collision and nonclaim controls

The following shortcuts are forbidden:

- replacing C259 by delayed consensus, which merely combines C203 and C210;
- replacing C260 by rowmotion, whose CSP/zeta paper shape collides with C187
  and C209, or by another shuffle adjacent to C171/C183/C239;
- describing C261 as nonlinear KdV or borrowing C256's traveling-wave claims;
- describing C262 as C110's nonautonomous Hénon prefix, C178's constant strobe,
  or C252's state-triggered relay;
- conflating C263 counts, current normalized masses, empirical proportions,
  and the Dirichlet directing vector, or defining `alpha=a/c` when `c=0`;
- reviving a small Burgers shock/rarefaction paper next to C195;
- substituting Hawkes without first closing the stationary intensity-versus-
  counting covariance and Bartlett-spectrum convention audit.

Workspace ownership never asserts literature priority.  Primary references
must be existence/metadata checked and used only for the claim they support.
No package may state that regression enumeration proves the arbitrary-parameter
theorem.

## Scope firewall and Route-A policy

Every artifact carries the literal `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.  The round
does not use or claim:

- target arithmetic local data or target prime/zero tables;
- Euler factors or root numbers;
- automorphy;
- a target divisor/counting law or target functional equation;
- a Hilbert--Pólya operator;
- Route-B input or authorization.

C260's intrinsic finite-field arithmetic, primitive source cycles, finite zeta,
and finite Koopman unitary remain candidate-local.  They may support an
exploratory C260 tuple but cannot be combined with C261's natural unitary or
any formal coordinate from C259/C262/C263.
