# Narrative report — C107

C107 turns the frozen H6 local symbolic interface into an open-system pilot by
removing state 3 before every iterate.  The resulting three-state matrix has
the exact determinant (1-z-z^3), and its trace/primitive ledger is certified
through period twelve.  The paper treats this as a discrete escape certificate
only: a geometric hole-to-rectangle theorem and an analytic open Fredholm
operator remain future gates.
