# Research question

For the ideal incompressible spherical-cavity Rayleigh model, can one state a
single theorem that remains valid on every pressure-sign and radius boundary?
The answer is yes on the positive-radius phase space: a conserved weighted
kinetic-plus-pressure energy gives an exact monotone collapse/expansion
branch; the collapse clock is a universal Beta constant times
`R0 sqrt(rho/Pi)`; and the terminal radius, wall speed, wall acceleration,
volume, and `L^p` thresholds follow from one endpoint expansion.  The
zero-pressure equilibrium and the `R0=0` nonclassical face are kept separate.

This is a source-native hydrodynamic theorem and regression certificate, not an
arithmetic determinant or a target-spectrum construction.
