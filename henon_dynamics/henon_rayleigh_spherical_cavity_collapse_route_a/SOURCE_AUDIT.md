# Source audit

Bibliographic metadata were checked against publisher or archival records.
The sources establish the classical model and its physical context; the
all-parameter organization and regression ledger below are our source-local
certificate and make no priority claim.

- Lord Rayleigh, “VIII. On the pressure developed in a liquid during the
  collapse of a spherical cavity,” *The London, Edinburgh, and Dublin
  Philosophical Magazine and Journal of Science* **34**(200), 94--98 (1917),
  DOI [`10.1080/14786440808635681`](https://doi.org/10.1080/14786440808635681).
  This is the original empty-cavity pressure calculation.
- M. S. Plesset and A. Prosperetti, “Bubble Dynamics and Cavitation,” *Annual
  Review of Fluid Mechanics* **9**, 145--185 (1977), DOI
  [`10.1146/annurev.fl.09.010177.001045`](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.fl.09.010177.001045).
  This is the standard source review for the Rayleigh--Plesset setting.
- M. P. Brenner, S. Hilgenfeldt, and D. Lohse, “Single-bubble
  sonoluminescence,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* **74**, 425--484 (2002), DOI
  [`10.1103/RevModPhys.74.425`](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.74.425).
  The review discusses the terminal `2/5` law and the physical effects that
  eventually regularize the ideal singularity.
- D. Obreschkow, M. Bruderer, and M. Farhat, “Analytical approximations for
  the collapse of an empty spherical bubble,” *Physical Review E* **85**,
  066303 (2012), DOI
  [`10.1103/PhysRevE.85.066303`](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.85.066303).
  The APS record states the frozen ODE, initial data, dimensionless collapse
  profile, and the absence of an elementary closed form for `R(t)`.

The source model neglects gas pressure, surface tension, viscosity, and liquid
compressibility.  The `Pi<0` branch in this package is a mathematical sign
control of the same ODE, not a claim about the original cavitation experiment.
No source is used to infer a prime/zero correspondence, a functional equation,
or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.  In particular, the source Beta clock is not
target continuation/divisor/counting law; the inverse-Beta profile is not
evidence for the Route-A A3 analytic-target gate.
