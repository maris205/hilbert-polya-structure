# Limitations

A transcendental generic basepoint is not every numerical specialization. Each fixed height has finitely many bad primes; no prime stays good at every height. The limiting argument fixes height before taking the prime limit, then sends height to infinity.

No generic equality at every rational specialization, uniform-in-height Chebotarev error, quantitative rate in the prime limit or log-prime periodic clock is asserted. Finite leaf unitaries are not a global self-adjoint realization.

This is a project-level source completion with strong classical ownership,
not a certified globally novel theorem or publication-readiness assessment.
Reviews are current-team internal mathematical/code reviews, not human,
external-model or cross-model peer review.
