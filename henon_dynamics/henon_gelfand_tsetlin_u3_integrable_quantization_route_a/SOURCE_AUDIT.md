# Source audit and ownership boundary

## Primary lineage

- V. Guillemin and S. Sternberg, “The Gelfand--Cetlin system and quantization of the complex flag manifolds,” *Journal of Functional Analysis* 52 (1983), 106--128, DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1236(83)90092-7>. This is the primary lineage for the flag-manifold integrable system and its quantization.
- V. Guillemin and S. Sternberg, “Geometric quantization and multiplicities of group representations,” *Inventiones Mathematicae* 67 (1982), 515--538, DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01398934>. This is primary lineage for geometric quantization and multiplicity/branching principles.

## What is reconstructed here

The package independently derives the (U(3)) polytope, an explicit arrow completion, the commuting regular torus, the rational-rank closure classification, and the exact pattern count. The sources establish lineage; they are not substituted for missing proof steps. We make no priority claim.

## Convention and claim boundary

The symplectic form is ω_A([X,A],[Y,A])=-i Tr(A[X,Y]); effective circle periods are (2\pi); prequantum curvature is (-i\omega); and quantization uses no half-form correction, hence no ρ-shift. The quantum triples are unshifted GT branching labels; no eigenvalue formula for unspecified Casimir or quantum action operators is asserted. Principal-minor characteristic polynomials are source-local linear algebra. The branching lattice is not a rational-prime clock, the finite count is not a target divisor, and nothing here supplies target Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target functional equation, target zeros, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
