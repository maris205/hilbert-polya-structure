# Theorem package: the $U(3)$ Gelfand--Tsetlin system

## 1. Frozen conventions

Let $\lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\lambda_3$ and

\[
\mathcal O_\lambda=\{A=A^*: \operatorname{spec}A=\{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3\}\}.
\]

For skew-Hermitian $X,Y$, tangent vectors are $[X,A]$ and

\[
\omega_A([X,A],[Y,A])=-i\operatorname{Tr}(A[X,Y]),\qquad
f_X(A)=-i\operatorname{Tr}(AX).
\]

Thus $\iota_{X_{f_X}}\omega=df_X$ and $X_{f_X}(A)=[X,A]$. Effective circle parameters have period $2\pi$. For quantization, $\lambda$ is a dominant integral $U(3)$ weight, the prequantum curvature is $-i\omega$, and there is **no half-form correction** and hence no $\rho$-shift.

Write $B=A^{(2)}$ for the northwest $2\times2$ minor, let $y_1\geq y_2$ be its eigenvalues, and set $x=A_{11}$. Put $F(A)=(y_1,y_2,x)$.

## 2. Main theorem

**Theorem.** The following statements hold.

1. The image of $F$ is exactly
   \[
   \Delta_\lambda=\{\lambda_1\geq y_1\geq\lambda_2\geq y_2\geq\lambda_3,
   \quad y_1\geq x\geq y_2\}.
   \]
   If $\lambda_1>\lambda_2>\lambda_3$, the six-dimensional orbit has, over the strict interior of $\Delta_\lambda$, an effective Hamiltonian $T^3$-action generated by $y_1,y_2,x$; its fibers are exactly the Lagrangian $T^3$-orbits.
2. For $H_\omega=\omega_1y_1+\omega_2y_2+\omega_3x$, the regular action-angle flow is $\theta(t)=\theta_0+t\omega\pmod {2\pi}$. Define the annihilator subgroup
   \[
   K_\omega=\{\theta\in\mathbb R^3/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^3:
   k\cdot\theta=0\pmod {2\pi}\text{ for every }k\in\mathbb Z^3
   \text{ with }k\cdot\omega=0\}.
   \]
   Its orbit closure is the coset $\theta_0+K_\omega$, and
   $\theta_0+K_\omega=K_\omega$ if and only if $\theta_0\in K_\omega$.
   Its dimension is $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}\{\omega_1,\omega_2,\omega_3\}$. The flow is fixed when $\omega=0$. For $\omega\ne0$ it is periodic exactly when $\omega=\alpha m$, where $\alpha>0$ and $m\in\mathbb Z^3$ is primitive; then its least positive period is $2\pi/\alpha$.
3. If $\lambda$ is dominant integral, no-half-form Borel--Weil quantization has a multiplicity-free Gelfand--Tsetlin branching basis indexed without shift by the integral patterns
   \[
   \lambda_1\geq m_{21}\geq\lambda_2\geq m_{22}\geq\lambda_3,
   \qquad m_{21}\geq m_{11}\geq m_{22}.
   \]
   The joint branching labels are $(m_{21},m_{22},m_{11})$, and
   \[
   \#\mathrm{GT}(\lambda)=
   \frac{(\lambda_1-\lambda_2+1)(\lambda_2-\lambda_3+1)
   (\lambda_1-\lambda_3+2)}2.
   \]

## 3. Exact image: interlacing and arrow completion

Cauchy interlacing gives every displayed inequality. Conversely fix a point of $\Delta_\lambda$. A Hermitian $2\times2$ matrix with eigenvalues $y_1,y_2$ and first entry $x$ is

\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}x&r\\r&y_1+y_2-x\end{pmatrix},\qquad
r=\sqrt{(y_1-x)(x-y_2)}.
\]

Diagonalize $B$, and in that basis form

\[
A'=\begin{pmatrix}y_1&0&z_1\\0&y_2&z_2\\
\bar z_1&\bar z_2&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad
c=\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3-y_1-y_2.
\]

For $P(t)=\prod_{j=1}^3(t-\lambda_j)$, choose

\[
|z_1|^2=-\frac{P(y_1)}{y_1-y_2},\qquad
|z_2|^2=\frac{P(y_2)}{y_1-y_2}.
\]

Interlacing makes these nonnegative. The leading coefficient and the values at $y_1,y_2$, together with the trace choice, force $\det(tI-A')=P(t)$. Conjugating the first two coordinates back preserves $A_{11}=x$. Coincident endpoints follow by continuity, or by setting the vanishing arrow coordinate to zero. This proves surjectivity without enumeration.

## 4. Thimm action and orbit closures

On the locus where $B$ has simple spectrum, differentiating $y_j$ gives its spectral projector in the northwest block. Its Hamiltonian flow is conjugation by the associated one-parameter unitary and has period $2\pi$. The $x$-flow is conjugation by $\exp(itE_{11})$. Spectral functions on the same $U(2)$ block commute, and the $U(1)\subset U(2)$ invariant function commutes with them; this is the nested-group argument. Strict interlacing makes the three infinitesimal actions independent.

The regular fiber has no hidden component. In the arrow construction strictness makes $r,z_1,z_2$ nonzero. After fixing an eigenvector gauge, their three phase choices give one $T^3$ and exhaust every matrix with the prescribed $x,y_1,y_2$. Hence the whole fiber is that compact three-dimensional orbit. It is half-dimensional and isotropic, so it is Lagrangian.

The linear flow is translation on $\mathbb R^3/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^3$. The closure through the origin is the compact subgroup $K_\omega$ annihilated by $\{k\in\mathbb Z^3:k\cdot\omega=0\}$; translation by the initial phase shows that the general closure is the coset $\theta_0+K_\omega$. A coset equals $K_\omega$ exactly when its basepoint lies in $K_\omega$; the basepoint need not be zero. For example, if the frequency has rational rank three, then $K_\omega=T^3$ and every basepoint gives the same subgroup. For $\omega=(1,2,3)$, the nonzero basepoint $(\pi/2,\pi,3\pi/2)$ also belongs to $K_\omega$, whereas $(0,0,\pi)$ does not. Character duality gives $\dim K_\omega$ as the rational rank. Rank one yields $\omega=\alpha m$ with primitive $m$, and $t\omega\in2\pi\mathbb Z^3$ first occurs at $t=2\pi/\alpha$.

## 5. Quantization and exact lattice count

Under the frozen convention, Borel--Weil realizes the quantization as the irreducible $U(3)$ module of highest weight $\lambda$. Restriction along $U(3)\supset U(2)\supset U(1)$ is multiplicity free at each step: an integral $U(2)$ highest weight $(m_{21},m_{22})$ occurs exactly under the first interlacing row, and $m_{11}$ occurs exactly under the second. Iterating gives one branching vector per displayed pattern, with unshifted joint labels. No spectrum of unspecified Casimir or quantum action operators is asserted.

Set $a=\lambda_1-\lambda_2$ and $b=\lambda_2-\lambda_3$. Translating $\lambda_3$ to zero does not change the count. Summing the $m_{11}$ interval length gives

\[
\sum_{u=0}^{a}\sum_{v=0}^{b}(b+u-v+1)
=\frac{(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)}2.
\]

This is the Weyl dimension. Negative $\lambda_3$ supplies a determinant twist and does not change the count.

## 6. Boundary atlas

- A facet of the regular polytope is singular: at least one effective circle acquires stabilizer. No uniform torus-fiber topology is claimed at facet intersections.
- If $\lambda_1=\lambda_2>\lambda_3$ or $\lambda_1>\lambda_2=\lambda_3$, the orbit is four-dimensional, the image is a triangle, and only the regular-interior $T^2$ statement survives.
- If $\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\lambda_3$, the orbit is a point and there is one pattern.
- For a nonintegral spectrum, the classical image and flow theorem remain valid, but this package makes no prequantum or Borel--Weil assertion.

## 7. Evidence and scope

The evidence exhausts the 169 gap pairs $0\leq a,b\leq12$ and commits every enumerated pattern through per-weight canonical digests; it also checks exact rational-rank examples, three basepoint/coset-equality cases, and boundaries. It is regression evidence, not the proof.

The Route-A tuple is `(A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION, A1_WEAK, A2_FAIL, A3_FAIL, A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`. The package does not claim arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target divisor or functional equation, a target-zero match, a Hilbert--Pólya operator, or Route B.
