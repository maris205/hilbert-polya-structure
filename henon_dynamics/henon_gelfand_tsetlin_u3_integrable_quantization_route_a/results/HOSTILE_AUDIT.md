# Hostile audit

The suite killed 86 of 86 attacks. Seventy-one attacks repaired all affected section and payload hashes after changing identity, evaluator locks, KKS sign, period, half-form and ρ conventions, theorem clauses (including a regression to the false zero-basepoint-only criterion), source/collision boundaries, route verdicts, flags, weight coordinates, pattern counts/digests, frequency ranks/periods, basepoint witnesses, facet claims, exact ledgers, or bool/int/float leaf types. One stale-hash mutation was rejected. Two strict-JSON and twelve strict-YAML attacks were also rejected.

This demonstrates semantic ownership of the artifact rather than mere digest consistency.
