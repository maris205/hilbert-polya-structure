# Frozen assumptions

H=H²(|z|<2), normalized boundary norm, e_j=(z/2)^j. Positive inverse branches h_n=(z+n)/(n(n+1)), n>=1, slope a_n=1/(n(n+1)). One real map step is the clock. Initially Re(s)>1/2; ordinary stability products also require |u|A(Re(s))<1. All complex powers use the real logarithm of a_n. Continuation is a trace-class meromorphic family, not a convergent branch series outside the initial domain.

The isolated real point 0 is excluded from the derivative-weighted branch object. At s=0,-1,... the continued operator is singular although its determinant extends holomorphically. D(1,s) has unavoidable poles at 1/2-m; no zero-free entire multiplier removes them.
