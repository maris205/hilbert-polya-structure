# Research narrative

The countable Lüroth transfer family has a complete Hardy spectrum and a whole-plane meromorphic continuation whose nonpositive-integer operator poles have nonzero square-zero residues invisible to its determinant.

C241 stopped at the scalar word identity and the right-half-plane convergence boundary. Here a specific Hilbert space owns the full operator, not just a formal triangular matrix. Taylor coefficients in a centered branch parameter supply meromorphic continuation with finite-rank residues. Even residues are spectrally visible; odd ones square to zero and disappear from the determinant's singularity set. The final native determinant still has exact poles, so its source completion sharpens an obstruction rather than passing target A2.
