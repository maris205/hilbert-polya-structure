# Source audit

The manuscript uses three verified, narrowly scoped references.

1. J. Monod, “The Growth of Bacterial Cultures,” *Annual Review of
   Microbiology* **3** (1949), 371–394,
   [DOI 10.1146/annurev.mi.03.100149.002103](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.mi.03.100149.002103).
   The publisher page verifies author, title, volume, year and pages.  It owns
   the named saturating growth-response context, not the present proof.
2. S. B. Hsu, S. Hubbell and P. Waltman, “A Mathematical Theory for
   Single-Nutrient Competition in Continuous Cultures of Micro-Organisms,”
   *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* **32** (1977), 366–383,
   [DOI 10.1137/0132030](https://doi.org/10.1137/0132030).
   The SIAM page verifies the continuous-culture dynamical context.
3. H. L. Smith and P. Waltman, *The Theory of the Chemostat*, Cambridge
   University Press (1995),
   [DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511530043](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511530043).
   The publisher page verifies the monograph and its simple-chemostat scope.

The package makes no literature-priority claim.  Positivity, the `Q`
reduction, thresholds, spectra and transient formulas are derived in the
artifact and independently checked.  No experimental dataset, parameter fit,
clinical/ecological recommendation, prime table or target spectrum appears.
