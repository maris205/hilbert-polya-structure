# Research question

For the ideal one-substrate, one-species chemostat with constant yield and
Monod response,

```text
S' = D(S_in-S)-mu(S)X/Y,
X' = (mu(S)-D)X,
mu(S)=mu_max S/(K+S),
```

can one prove, in one convention-complete theorem, the global fate of every
nonnegative initial state for every positive parameter tuple; resolve the
washout equality without extrapolating a hyperbolic formula; and derive an
exact transient on the attracting total-nutrient leaf?

The associated Route-A question is deliberately separate: does this monotone
continuous-culture flow possess an intrinsic arithmetic primitive-orbit owner
or target determinant?  The answer is no.  The source theorem is retained,
while A0 through A4 fail and Route B remains unauthorized.
