# Narrative report

C254 changes the dynamical owner to a dissipative ecological flow and takes
one full theorem-scale step.  Its useful idea is not a numerical phase plot:
after scaling biomass by the yield, total nutrient obeys an exact scalar
linear equation.  The remaining equation is asymptotically one-dimensional.
This gives a proof-level washout/survival classification for every positive
parameter tuple and every nonnegative initial state.

The equality `D=mu(S_in)` is retained as its own nonhyperbolic regime.  On the
attracting nutrient leaf, separation gives a closed logarithmic transient; at
equality it degenerates to an exact reciprocal-log law with algebraic decay.
Thus the paper advances beyond an equilibrium calculation without splitting
one result across several manuscripts.

The Route-A outcome is nevertheless negative.  The same monotonic structure
that makes the global theorem clean eliminates nonconstant recurrence.  A
growth threshold is not an arithmetic prime clock, and the implicit transient
is not a determinant.  All mathematical progress remains source-local.
