2. **Orbit-growth promotion:** rejected. The exponential term \(b^n\) is generic topological degree, and the rational zeta carries no target divisor.

5. **Fredholm overreach:** rejected. The infinite-multiplicity isometry is non-Schatten, so no ordinary \(\det(I-zU_b)\) exists for \(z\ne0\).

7. **Edge-case leak:** exposed. \(b=1\) has infinite fixed sets and is excluded; negative/noninteger slopes are not silently included.
8. **Citation/independence overclaim:** rejected. Citation population is zero, no priority is claimed, and internal audits are not external review.
