# C177 narrative report

C177 makes a larger step than another fixed-point computation. It treats the entire degree family at once and connects three layers that are often left separate: primitive orbit combinatorics, the exact Koopman/Perron operator model, and quantitative mixing for smooth observables.

The periodic side is completely explicit: fixed grids have size \(b^n-1\), Möbius inversion resolves every exact period, and the zeta is \((1-z)/(1-bz)\). The operator side is equally exact: the Fourier basis splits uniquely into chains indexed by integers not divisible by \(b\), yielding one constant plus countably many unilateral shifts. That ownership immediately identifies the adjoint, spectrum, lack of compactness and Schatten membership, and absence of an ordinary Fredholm determinant. The same Fourier ledger yields a sharp \(b^{-ns}\) correlation inequality.

The obstruction is also stronger. Prime and composite degrees are dynamically indistinguishable at every proved layer. The integer label therefore does not become an arithmetic carrier merely because it appears in exponential growth. The natural extension provides only a formal unitary hint after the phase space changes. This justifies A1 weak but A0, A2, and A3 fail.

The package makes no novelty-priority or external-review claim. Exact computation is a regression sentinel; the written proof owns the all-parameter theorem.
