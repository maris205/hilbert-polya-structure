# C177 paper plan

## Claim spine

1. Solve all fixed grids and exact-period counts for every \(b\ge2\).
2. derive the rational Artin--Mazur zeta and primitive-cycle product.
3. Resolve Haar--Koopman into constants plus countably many unilateral Wold chains.
4. Identify the Perron adjoint and prove the operator-ideal boundary.
5. Prove the sharp \(b^{-ns}\) correlation law.
6. Apply the evaluator conservatively: a complete generic degree ledger does not pass A0.

## Figure/table decision

The paper-figure phase is deliberately `N/A`: exact equations expose the chain decomposition and decay factor more precisely than a schematic. The single Route-A gate table is the necessary comparison visual.

Round 2 adds the sharp correlation theorem, prime/composite control, bilingual abstract, evidence boundary, and declarations. These are internal drafting rounds, not external review.
