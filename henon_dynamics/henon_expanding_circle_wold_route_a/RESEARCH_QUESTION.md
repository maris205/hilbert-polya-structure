# C177 research question

For the full family of integer expanding circle endomorphisms \(T_b(x)=bx\pmod1\), \(b\ge2\), can periodic coordinates, primitive-cycle counts, Artin--Mazur zeta, Haar--Koopman Wold chains, Perron action, operator-ideal ownership, and smooth-observable correlations be resolved in one exact all-parameter ledger?

The hostile Route-A question is whether the integer degree \(b\), its exponential orbit growth, and a natural dilation can create an intrinsic arithmetic carrier. The theorem proves a complete dynamical ledger but answers no: the same formulas hold uniformly for prime and composite \(b\), the orbit zeta has only the generic degree pole, and the source Koopman operator is a proper isometry rather than a self-adjoint determinant owner.

Success for this package means a strictly larger exact theorem and a sharper obstruction. It does not mean a target match or an external novelty claim.
