1. Freeze the full integer family, source clock, Haar normalization, Fourier convention, zeta convention, and A0 gate.
2. Prove the fixed grid, Möbius exact-period law, cycle integrality, rational zeta, and coefficientwise primitive product.
3. Prove the unique \(m=rb^j\) factorization and complete Wold decomposition.
4. Derive the Perron adjoint, proper-isometry, spectrum, compactness, Schatten, and ordinary-determinant boundaries.
5. Prove the sharp homogeneous-Sobolev correlation bound from Fourier coefficients.
6. Generate exact finite rows; independently reconstruct them, run SymPy identities, byte replay, and repaired-hash mutation tests.
7. Compile three materially distinct manuscript rounds and two clean final builds; require embedded fonts and clean layout/glyph/reference logs.
8. Close a self-excluded manifest over exactly 27 payload files.

Falsifiers include one wrong fixed count, nonintegral primitive-cycle count, duplicate/missing Wold chain, wrong Perron image, correlation factor weaker than claimed sharpness, or any true scope flag.
