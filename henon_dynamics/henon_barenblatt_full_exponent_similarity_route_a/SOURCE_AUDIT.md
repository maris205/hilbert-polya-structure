The executable evidence records exactly these two sources.  The paper claims
neither invention nor priority for the formulas, and it does not advertise
the source audit as exhaustive novelty research.

## Ownership boundary

The contribution of this certificate is the convention-complete synthesis:
one-dimensional all-`m>0` profile derivation, exact mass and moment formulas,
the `m=1/3` boundary, pressure/free-boundary statement, rescaled stationary
form, explicit nonlinear and heat free-energy branches with applicability
conditions for dissipation, and independent executable checks.  Classical
mathematical content remains owned by its original literature.

No source is used to support arithmetic local data, Euler products, root
numbers, automorphy, a target divisor, a functional equation, or a
Hilbert–Pólya operator; none is claimed.
