# Paper plan

1. Freeze the one-dimensional equation, physical clock, mass, centered
   profile class, local absolute continuity of `F^m`, the almost-everywhere
   zero-flux law, and similarity normalization.
2. Derive the integrated profile equation without dividing through the
   singular boundary `m=1`.
3. State and prove the unique mass-normalized compact, Gaussian, and
   algebraic profiles for every `m>0`.
4. Evaluate exact Beta/Gamma mass and absolute-moment formulas and classify
   finite, logarithmic, and power-divergent moments.
5. Isolate the `m=1/3` second-moment boundary.
6. Derive porous-medium pressure, support radius, and free-boundary speed.
7. State the rescaled stationary equation; define the `m!=1` and heat energy
   branches; derive their first variations and conditional dissipation with
   its precise applicability boundary.
8. Separate proof from executable regression, then record source ownership,
   Route-A failure, nonclaims, and disclosure statements.

Round 0 contains items 1–3.  Round 1 adds items 4–7.  Round 2 adds the exact
certificate, Route-A decision, source/claim firewall, and disclosures.
