# Narrative report

P72 exposed an infinite scalar-channel ladder, but only after setting the
weight to one.  P75 returns to the natural P70 family without inventing a
weighted Lind zeta.  The correct move is to split the orbit monomial into two
fugacities: `z` records zero-energy steps and `w` records defect-energy steps.
The physical positive-weight slice is simply `w=qz`.

This lift makes the algebra unexpectedly rigid.  The all-reflection transfer
function is `2w/(1-z^2-w^2)`.  Primitive Möbius subtraction and Euler
repetition therefore regroup into the same nonzero coefficient `c_m` as in
P72, multiplying one rational channel for each integer `m`.  Its denominator
defines `z^(2m)+w^(2m)=1`.

Inside the bidisk these hypersurfaces are smooth and locally finite, and the
channel sum is normally convergent off them.  Returning to `w=qz` gives a
strictly ordered family of radii and a closed principal coefficient at every
complex root.  This is a complete moving-divisor theorem, not yet a
natural-boundary theorem and not an operator construction.
