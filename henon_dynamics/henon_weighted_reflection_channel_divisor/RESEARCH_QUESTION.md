# Research question

Does P72's scalar-channel compiler extend from the unweighted slice `q=1`
to P70's full positive-weight family in a way that controls both variables,
the complete moving singular divisor, and every local principal coefficient?

The decisive object is the two-fugacity lift obtained from `w=qz`.  The
predeclared test is exact: derive its channel series from P69's primitive
polynomials and P70's repetition law, prove normal convergence away from a
locally finite bidisk divisor, and isolate each denominator root on every
fixed positive-weight fiber.  Dense accumulation and natural-boundary claims
are explicitly deferred.
