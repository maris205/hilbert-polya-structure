# Source audit

The frozen mathematical inputs are repository results HCS-P69, HCS-P70, and
HCS-P72:

- P69 supplies `F_(2h+1)(q)=2q(1+q^2)^h` and primitive dilation M\"obius
  inversion.
- P70 supplies the orbit-resolved product and exact Euler repetition law for
  every `q>0`.
- P72 supplies the already proved coefficient
  `c_m=m^(-1) sum_(k|m,k odd) k mu(k)` and its odd-radical factorization on
  the unweighted slice.

The H\'enon/full-shift coding boundary is cited to Devaney--Nitecki and Arai,
using the verified bibliography already present in P70.  No Lind formula is
used in P75: in particular, the reverse-shift Lind zeta from P71--P72 is not
promoted to `q!=1`.  The two-fugacity lift, hypersurface divisor, normal
continuation, and moving principal coefficients are new derivations from the
locked repository formulas.
