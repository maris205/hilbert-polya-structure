# Hostile review round 1

**Attack:** the formula in two variables may be an arbitrary analytic
reparameterization rather than the original weighted packet.

**Resolution:** every factor is lifted coefficientwise by the identity
`z^n q^S=z^(n-S)w^S`, and restriction to `w=qz` returns the P70 factor
exactly.  The lifted all-word function is independently derived as
`2w/(1-z^2-w^2)`.

**Attack:** infinitely many hypersurfaces may invalidate normal convergence.

**Resolution:** on any compact sub-bidisk, `r_z^(2m)+r_w^(2m)<1` for every
sufficiently large `m`.  Only finitely many hypersurfaces remain, and the
tail is dominated by a geometric series in `|w|`.
