# ROUND2 clue

For fixed `q>0`, channel `m` has all `2m` equally spaced roots on the circle
of radius `rho_m(q)`, and the radii approach `min(1,q^(-1))`.  The next exact
question is whether the union of these roots accumulates at every point of
that limiting circle and whether the already proved nonzero local principal
parts forbid meromorphic continuation there.  This angular-density and
natural-boundary theorem is reserved for P76; it must not be replaced by a
finite root plot.
