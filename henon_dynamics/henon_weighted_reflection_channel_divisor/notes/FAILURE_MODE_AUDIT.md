# Failure-mode audit

- **Wrong weighted lift:** `w=qz` gives exactly
  `z^n q^S=z^(n-S)w^S`; all exponents remain nonnegative because `0<=S<=n`.
- **Lost repetition weight:** both fugacity exponents dilate under repetition,
  so grouping `m=kr` preserves the factor `1/r` and produces the same `c_m`
  as P72.
- **Nonuniform denominator bound:** local finiteness separates finitely many
  early hypersurfaces; only the tail uses the uniform `1/2` bound.
- **False smoothness at a coordinate axis:** the gradient can vanish in one
  coordinate but not both on `z^(2m)+w^(2m)=1`.
- **Fiber pole cancellation:** strict monotonicity of `rho_m(q)` excludes an
  inter-channel collision for fixed `q>0`, and `c_m` never vanishes.
- **Joint collision overclaim:** intersections `H_m cap H_j` are not
  classified.
- **Natural-boundary promotion:** the radius limit is proved, but density of
  complex roots and noncontinuation across every limiting point are not P75
  claims.
- **Lind promotion:** the source-defined Lind comparison exists only on the
  earlier unweighted slice; no weighted Lind formula is asserted.
- **Operator/arithmetic promotion:** the scalar divisor is neither an
  operator construction nor a rational-prime labeling.
