# Paper plan

1. Freeze the P69 observable, primitive polynomial, and P70 Euler product.
2. Introduce the two-fugacity orbit lift and derive its all-word function.
3. Regroup primitive and repetition indices into weighted scalar channels.
4. Prove smoothness and local finiteness of the bidisk hypersurface divisor.
5. Establish normal convergence of the logarithmic continuation off it.
6. Restrict to fixed `q>0`, prove strict radius separation, and compute every
   exact local principal coefficient.
7. State the collision, natural-boundary, Lind, operator, and arithmetic
   firewalls, then lock the algebra with independent code.
