# Paper plan

1. Define the linear-rate AIMD process and distinguish pre-jump from post-jump
   values.
2. Derive the hazard integral and the exact (2a/rho) square-affine chain.
3. Prove contractive coupling, the stationary perpetuity, its q-product, the
   continuous stationary Laplace-generator identity, and the all-order
   squared-moment recursion.
4. Derive the generator recurrence and stationary Markov-renewal/Palm reward
   occupation law; audit it on a rational hazard skeleton.
5. Treat (beta=0,1), (a=0), and (rho=0) boundary faces, then close the
   Route-A and `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER` boundaries.
