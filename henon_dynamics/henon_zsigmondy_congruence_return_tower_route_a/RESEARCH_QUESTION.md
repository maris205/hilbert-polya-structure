# Research question and decision rule

Can the intrinsic divisibility sequence \(a^n-b^n\) be realized as an
all-parameter first-return tower on finite congruence dynamics, including
exact prime-power lifting and complete finite-fiber orbit data, and does
that tower itself determine one global periodic-orbit determinant owner?

## Hard gates

1. For every coprime \(a>b\geq1\) and every \(n\geq2\), identify primitive
   divisors with least marked return, while attributing classical existence
   and its exceptions correctly.
2. For every odd primitive return prime \(p\) and every \(k\geq1\), prove
   the exact order on the \(p^k\)-fiber rather than extrapolating from a
   finite table.
3. For every admissible \(N\), classify all cycles, all fixed counts, the
   finite Artin--Mazur factor, the finite Koopman determinant, and inversion
   reversal.
4. Compare a disjoint-union globalization and the profinite inverse-limit
   globalization on the same source clock.
5. State exactly what these theorems do and do not establish for Route A.
6. Keep target tables, arithmetic local factors, logarithmic prime time,
   prime-weighted global products, and Route B outside the object.

## Decision

The first three gates close uniformly.  The fourth produces a rigorous
nonuniqueness result: the disjoint union and inverse limit have incompatible
positive-time fixed ledgers.  Therefore the finite tower does not select one
global determinant owner without an extra construction principle.  This is
meaningful A0/A1 progress, but A2 and A3 fail because no target divisor or
global target analytic comparison is present.
