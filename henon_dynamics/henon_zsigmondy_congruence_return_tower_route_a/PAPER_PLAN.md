# Paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

The divisibility sequence \(a^n-b^n\) has a uniform congruence-return
realization with an exact prime-power lift and complete dynamics on every
finite unit fiber, while two natural globalizations prove that those fibers
alone do not select a unique global determinant owner.

## Draft architecture

1. Bilingual abstract and frozen arithmetic dynamics.
2. Primitive returns and explicit classical attribution.
3. Prime-power lift theorem with valuation proof.
4. Complete finite-fiber cycle, zeta, determinant, and reversal theorem.
5. Disjoint-union versus inverse-limit globalizations.
6. Exact audit, Route-A decision, limitations, declarations, and references.

The final manuscript must distinguish a prime as a return modulus from a
prime-labeled primitive orbit, and must distinguish global nonselection from
absolute nonexistence.
