# Narrative report

## Outcome first

C179 turns the full divisibility family \(a^n-b^n\) into a congruence
first-return tower.  For every coprime \(a>b\geq1\), primitive divisor
status at time \(n\) is exactly least return of the marked point on the
corresponding prime fiber.  Classical Zsigmondy existence supplies a return
prime outside its two precise exception forms.  If the base valuation is
\(e\), the entire tower above that prime has return length
\(np^{\max(0,k-e)}\) at level \(p^k\).

The result then leaves the prime fibers and resolves every admissible
composite modulus.  Translation makes all cycles have the same length, so
fixed counts, the Artin--Mazur factor, the finite Koopman determinant, and
inversion reversal are exact for every \(N\).  This is one all-parameter
theorem chain rather than five papers cut from a single example.

## The global step

Two natural ways to assemble the finite congruence system disagree.  The
disjoint union has \(a^n-b^n\) fixed points at time \(n\), rational source
zeta \((1-bz)/(1-az)\), and a closed Möbius formula for primitive cycles.
The inverse-limit translation has no positive-time fixed points and source
zeta 1.  This is a sharp global owner result: finite fibers alone do not
choose one global periodic-orbit determinant.  It is intentionally not
phrased as absolute nonexistence after arbitrary additional structure.

## Why this is Route-A progress

Unlike a nonarithmetic control, primes and prime powers arise internally
from return arithmetic, so A0 moves from failure to a weak arithmetic
relation.  A1 is also weak because each finite primitive orbit ledger is
complete.  The owner ambiguity prevents a stronger verdict.  No target
divisor or target analytic structure is tested, so A2 and A3 remain failed.
The finite permutation and profinite Haar Koopman lifts justify natural A4,
but A4 does not improve the other coordinates.

## Boundary

No imported prime table, local arithmetic factor, root number, automorphy
claim, target-zero comparison, logarithmic prime roof, prime-weighted global
product, Hilbert--Pólya claim, or Route-B authorization enters the package.
