# Analytic theorem package

T1 -- Complete periodic atlas. Every T^n has exactly 2^n fixed points. Primitive necklaces give P_n=(1/n) sum_(d|n) mu(d)2^(n/d). The all-zero orbit is the unique neutral orbit; every other primitive multiplier is positive and at least two. All repetitions preserve the derivative-power and original-time laws.

T2 -- Complete inducing and tail. All y in Y return, the disjoint left-open/right-closed branches h_n(Y) partition Y, and nonneutral primitive T cycles correspond bijectively to induced cycles. With a_m=g^m(1/2), Leb{tau>n}=a_(n-1)/2~1/(4n). The reciprocal recurrence proves the tail, rather than a numerical fit.

T3 -- Infinite-branch trace class. g preserves D(1,1), all h_n map Omega=D(1,3/4) into D(1,1/2), and sup|h_n'|<=C*/n^2. The monomial rank-one expansion gives ||h_n' C_hn||_1<=3sup|h_n'|. Hence L_zeta is trace class for |zeta|<=1, continuous in trace norm on the closed disk and holomorphic inside.

T4 -- Exact two-clock primitive determinant. Delta is entire in u and has factors 1-u^(r_p) zeta^(N_p)/Lambda_p^k, k>=1, in the stated absolute log-product domain. r_p is return period, N_p is original period. No additional primitive-cycle multiplicity is inserted.

T5 -- Genuine boundaries. The defining branch series fails for |zeta|>1. On uninduced L1 the operator is noncompact. The zero-integral vectors v_e=f_e-f_(2e) have norm one and ||(P-I)v_e||_1<=12e. Contraction and telescoping give ||(P^n-I)v_e||_1<=12ne, so for every fixed integer n>=0 the restricted power norm is exactly one. This excludes uniform exponential operator-norm decay. The unique neutral orbit gives a singular would-be stability trace denominator.
