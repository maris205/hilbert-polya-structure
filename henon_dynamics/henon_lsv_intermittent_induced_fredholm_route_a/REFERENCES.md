# Primary references

1. C. Liverani, B. Saussol and S. Vaienti, *A probabilistic approach to intermittency*, Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems 19 (1999), 671--685. DOI 10.1017/S0143385799133856. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0143385799133856 . Author copy: https://www.mat.uniroma2.it/~liverani/Logrono/live9901.pdf
2. H. H. Rugh, *Intermittency and Regularized Fredholm Determinants*, arXiv:chao-dyn/9610011 (1996). https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9610011 . This is background lineage, not an unchecked theorem used to establish this package's common domain or nuclearity.
3. B. Simon, *Trace Ideals and Their Applications*, second edition, Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 120, AMS (2005). https://bookstore.ams.org/SURV/120 . Standard trace-ideal/Fredholm background.

The package derives its alpha-one branch domain and summability directly. It does not assert that a cited finite-measure statistical theorem automatically extends to the alpha-one endpoint.
