# Research question and answer

Can the neutral obstruction of the alpha-one LSV map be separated from a complete first-return system without replacing an infinite-dimensional theorem by finite branch data?

Yes: one explicit complex disk contains the entire inducing scheme, a telescoping derivative identity gives uniformly summable branch weights, and a Hardy rank-one expansion proves a trace-class two-clock operator. The original uninduced L1 evolution has no uniform exponential operator-norm relaxation on zero-integral densities. These conclusions belong to the declared source and spaces, not a target arithmetic spectrum.
