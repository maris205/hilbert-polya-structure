# Limitations and excluded inferences

The explicit convergence constant 1250 exp(625 pi^2/6) is deliberately coarse. It proves summability but is not advertised as a useful numerical cutoff certificate.

Failure of the original branch series for |zeta|>1 does not prove absence of analytic continuation through the unit circle. Entire continuation in u does not imply the primitive Euler product converges for every u.

The unnormalized reference return tail and its divergent mean are not a theorem about an unstated invariant density. L1 noncompactness by disjoint supports is not special to intermittency; the separate neutral approximate-eigenvector argument gives the no-uniform-gap conclusion.

The neutral orbit cannot be inserted into a stability trace with denominator Lambda-1. The unweighted neutral factor, induced weighted determinant and uninduced transfer operator are different objects.

Finite dyadic certificates audit normalization, not all-branch completeness. Classical LSV dynamics, inducing and Fredholm techniques have prior owners. No publication acceptance, external review or literature-first claim is made.
