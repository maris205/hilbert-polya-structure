# HCS-C62 source and novelty boundary

The general ingredients are prior art: Gassmann/Perlis arithmetic
equivalence, Burnside-ring linearization, and the standard character formulas
for exterior and symmetric squares.  C62 does not claim those mechanisms.

The bounded contribution is the exact instance-specific `W(E_6)` result for
the released C61 pair: complete lambda-square orbit atlases, the exact field
dictionary, and a certified separation or collapse of the plus/minus shadows.

Closest internal comparators are C59's Gassmann pair and C61's tensor
products.  C62 changes the operation from multiplication in the Burnside
ring to the degree-two lambda operations.  Cubic tensor powers are explicitly
deferred as a same-mechanism/salami risk.

No web-literature claim is used as theorem authority in this target-lock
record; external novelty citations must be refreshed before paper release.
