# HCS-C327 research question

For the infinite periodic delta-comb Hamiltonian

\[
H_{a,g}=-d^2/dx^2+g\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\delta(x-na),
\qquad a>0,\quad g\in\mathbb R,
\]

can one give, in a single convention-locked theorem package, all of the
following without using a merely formal distributional operator or numerical
plots as proof?

1. A closed lower-semibounded quadratic form and its exact matching domain.
2. The transfer monodromy and its continuation through zero and negative
   energy.
3. The Floquet criterion, pure absolutely continuous spectral type, and Bloch
   multiplicity/edge classification.
4. The complete attractive/free/repulsive band-gap atlas, including the exact
   `ga=-4` zero threshold.
5. Openness of every Bragg gap for nonzero coupling and a controlled
   high-energy gap-width expansion.
6. The integrated and ordinary density of states with correct band indexing,
   gap constants, edge limits, and free degeneration.

The answer is analytic and covers all real `g`.  The target-free scope is
`NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`: no target zero matching, arithmetic local data,
Euler factor, root number, automorphy claim, target divisor, functional
equation, Hilbert--Polya operator, or Route-B input is allowed.
