# HCS-C327 exact-evidence plan

## Analytic claims under test

1. Form closure, lower semiboundedness, matching conditions, and self-adjoint
   ownership for both signs of the delta coupling.
2. Determinant-one monodromy and
   `Delta(E)=cos(ka)+g sin(ka)/(2k)`, with the exact zero limit and hyperbolic
   negative-energy branch.
3. `spectrum iff |Delta|<=1`, pure absolute continuity, interior Bloch
   multiplicity two, and simple/double edge classification.
4. The `g>0`, `g=0`, and three `g<0` low-energy regimes separated by
   `ga=-4`.
5. Exact positive-band indexing, noncollapse of all nonzero-coupling Bragg
   gaps, and the `2|g|/a` high-energy width.
6. The unwrapped-phase IDS and DOS, including edge and free limits.

## Deterministic lanes

- The producer writes canonical self-hashed JSON with 72-digit receipts: five
  attractive low-energy cases, nine low-edge cases, 216 Bragg edges, 150
  transfer matrices at three scales, and 70 band-indexed IDS/DOS samples.
- The checker does not import the producer.  It owns exact schemas, all nested
  fields, raw and semantic evaluator hashes, factor equations, transfer
  matrices, every band index, all density formulas, and all counts.
- SymPy separately verifies the monodromy determinant/trace, positive and
  negative factorizations, zero series, energy derivative, and high-energy
  implicit expansion before a broad exact substitution grid.
- Replay requires two isolated fresh producers to match each other and the
  checked-in evidence byte for byte.
- Hostile tests repair the payload hash after semantic attacks and also attack
  duplicate/nonfinite JSON, duplicate/anchored YAML, evaluator authority,
  every `evidence_status`, Route-B locks, and nested rows.
- Every executable lane must refuse optimized Python explicitly.

## Paper gates

- Round 0: quadratic-form owner, transfer/Floquet theorem, pure spectral type,
  and the negative/zero atlas.
- Round 1: the complete positive atlas, all open gaps, controlled gap
  asymptotic, and IDS/DOS.
- Round 2: evidence scope, verified source ownership, collision boundary,
  strict Route-A result, and forbidden-promotion statement.

Every round is built twice from a fresh directory by LuaLaTeX at epoch
`1788393600`.  Release requires exact bytes, warning-free logs, no layout or
reference defects, embedded/subset fonts, extractable text, and successful
page rasterization.
