# Paper plan

## Title

An Exact Band--Gap Atlas for the Delta-Comb Kronig--Penney Hamiltonian

## Claim/evidence map

| Claim | Analytic owner | Executable control |
|---|---|---|
| self-adjoint operator | trace form bound and representation theorem | scale-separated transfer rows |
| Floquet spectrum/type | direct integral and nonconstant bands | discriminant/monodromy reconstruction |
| negative atlas | two hyperbolic factorizations and monotonicity | five threshold-straddling cases |
| positive atlas | parity-free partner equation and factor signs | 216 ordered Bragg rows |
| gap asymptotic | analytic implicit-function expansion | scaled width-error sequence |
| IDS/DOS | unwrapped Floquet phase and differentiation | 70 band-indexed density rows |
| edge multiplicity | monodromy eigenspaces | fixed/shifted edge residuals |

## Revision architecture

- Round 0 proves the operator/discriminant owner, Floquet spectral theorem,
  and complete negative/zero classification.
- Round 1 adds the positive-band atlas, every open gap, a two-term width
  expansion with controlled remainder, and IDS/DOS.
- Round 2 adds evidence closure, source ownership and non-priority wording,
  C288/C308/C318/C323 separation, and the strict Route-A/nonclaim boundary.

No figure is needed: plots would obscure the exact threshold and band-index
conventions, whereas the factor equations fully determine the geometry.
