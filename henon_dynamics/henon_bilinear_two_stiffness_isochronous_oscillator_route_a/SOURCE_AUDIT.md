# Source audit — HCS-C357

## Verified sources

1. J. Dorignac, “On the quantum spectrum of isochronous potentials,” *Journal
   of Physics A* 38 (2005), 6183--6210,
   DOI [10.1088/0305-4470/38/27/007](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/38/27/007).
   The primary paper explicitly includes the asymmetric parabolic well among
   its isochronous examples. It supports model lineage and the warning that
   classical isochrony does not force quantum equispacing.
2. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions,
   [Chapter 12, Section 12.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/12.2). This official
   reference owns the Weber equation, \(D_\nu\) convention, values and
   Wronskians used in the matching formula.
3. G. Teschl, *Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics*, 2nd ed., AMS GSM
   157, 2014, DOI [10.1090/gsm/157](https://doi.org/10.1090/gsm/157).
   The AMS source supports the self-adjoint one-dimensional Schrödinger
   framework.
