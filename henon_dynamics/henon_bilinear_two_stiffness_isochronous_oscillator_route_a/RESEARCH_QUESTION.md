# Research question — HCS-C357

For a one-dimensional oscillator whose positive and negative displacements have
different quadratic stiffnesses, can one prove a global all-energy isochrony
theorem, construct the seam-compatible action-angle chart, and characterize
the complete quantum bound spectrum by one exact interface equation?

The package answers yes for two positive frequencies. It also proves that
positivity on both sides is necessary for the all-nonzero-orbit bounded-periodic
property and closes the one-sided-flat and free boundaries.
