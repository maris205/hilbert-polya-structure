# Research question

Can one close an all-parameter, source-owned spectral theorem for a genuine
delay semigroup without confusing characteristic roots with a dynamical zeta?

The frozen answer is yes at the functional-differential level: Lambert-W
branches, method-of-steps, eventual compactness and the exact Hopf boundary
are unified.  The answer is no as an arithmetic Route-A candidate because the
model has no intrinsic rational-prime carrier or isolated primitive-orbit
owner.  This separation is the main scientific boundary of C210.
