## Design objective

One paper again means one independent theorem-scale advance.  The frozen
owners are a singular hydrodynamic ODE, a driven interacting-particle Markov
chain, a dispersive Hamiltonian PDE, a nonsmooth optimal-control synthesis and
a quantum-optical unitary system.  Their phase spaces, clocks and proof tools
are genuinely different; no coordinate, spectrum or Route score is imported
from another package.

`NEW` means only that this convention-locked owner is absent from the existing
workspace packages.  It is not a literature-priority claim.  Classical
results retain their source attribution; the retained increment is a complete
parameter/boundary synthesis, independently replayable evidence, finished
paper and strict Route-A decision.

## Collision scan, kills and pivots

- Rayleigh collapse shares a finite-collision endpoint with C216, but its
  owner is radial fluid inertia with a moving free boundary, not celestial
  angular momentum or Levi--Civita regularization.  It also differs from the
  externally imposed impacts of C212 and the porous-medium support front of
  C207.
- Open TASEP is retained despite C175's Rule-184 traffic model: C175 is a
  deterministic periodic cellular automaton, whereas C220 is a stochastic,
  boundary-driven, nonreversible exclusion chain with reservoir phases and a
  matrix-product stationary state.
- The NLS soliton Hessian has no prior owner.  It is a conservative dispersive
  PDE with phase and translation kernels, unlike the dissipative Fisher--KPP
  front of C202 or finite-dimensional oscillator packages.
- The double integrator is retained as a global minimum-time synthesis rather
  than another damping or impact model.  Its switching parabola and
  Pontryagin/HJB certificate do not overlap C201's heavy-ball stability atlas.
- Jaynes--Cummings is limited to its conserved-excitation decomposition and
  exact two-level blocks.  Earlier Fock/quantum packages do not own the
  atom--field exchange law.  Infinite-dimensional determinant language is
  explicitly excluded.
- M/M/1, Kramers--Ornstein--Uhlenbeck and Cahn--Hilliard candidates remain
  reserves.  Autonomous dry friction was rejected because a complete result
  first requires a frozen Filippov sticking selection law.  The selected set
  gives a cleaner five-way subtype separation.

No retained system intrinsically labels primitive trajectories by rational
primes, realizes prime powers as their repetitions, or produces `log p` as a
source clock.  Beta functions, matrix-product normalizations, Hessian spectra,
value functions and dressed energies remain model-local objects.

## Frozen independent theorem increments

### C219 -- Rayleigh spherical-cavity collapse

For `R R''+(3/2)(R')^2=-Pi/rho`, close all three pressure-sign branches.  For
positive pressure derive the first integral, exact incomplete-beta clock and
finite collapse time, the `2/5` endpoint law, sharp velocity/acceleration
integrability thresholds, volume law and finite liquid kinetic-energy limit.
Zero pressure, expansion under negative pressure, and the zero-radius external
boundary are separate branches.  The source Lagrangian is recorded without
turning this solvability into a Route-A analytic bridge.

### C220 -- open TASEP matrix ansatz and phases

For every finite chain length and positive injection/extraction rates, prove
irreducibility and the unique stationary distribution, close the DEHP
matrix-product weights, normalization and current ratio, and independently
match them to the exact generator nullspace.  Then state the low-density,
high-density, maximal-current, coexistence and critical thermodynamic regimes
with finite-data evidence clearly separated from proof.  Every vanishing-rate
absorbing boundary and the zero/one-site cases remain explicit.

### C221 -- cubic NLS soliton Hessian

For the focusing cubic line NLS, derive the full bright-soliton family, mass,
Hamiltonian and Vakhitov--Kolokolov slope.  Factor and classify both scalar
Hessians: the phase operator has the soliton kernel and no negative mode,
while the amplitude operator has one negative eigenvalue, the translation
kernel and continuum threshold.  The Morse index, symmetry origins,
zero-frequency limit and defocusing no-bright-soliton boundary are closed.

### C222 -- time-optimal double integrator

For bounded acceleration, derive the switching parabola and a closed minimum-
time value function on the whole state plane.  Give the direct-braking and
one-switch control laws, prove their arc lengths are nonnegative and reach the
origin exactly, and certify global optimality with the affine Pontryagin
switching function and the off-curve HJB equation.  Reflection, scaling,
origin and zero-acceleration boundaries are part of the theorem.

### C223 -- Jaynes--Cummings excitation blocks

Prove conservation of total excitation and decompose the rotating-wave
Hamiltonian into one vacuum state and exact two-dimensional blocks.  Close
each dressed pair, propagator and transition probability for arbitrary
detuning and coupling, and give the exact finite-support commensurability
criterion for population revival.  Resonance, zero coupling, block-index,
coupling-sign and finite-cutoff boundaries are audited independently.
