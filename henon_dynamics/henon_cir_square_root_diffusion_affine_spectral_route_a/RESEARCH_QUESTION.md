# Research question — C229

Can one close a single theorem for the entire nonnegative CIR parameter cone,
including the Feller boundary and singular zero-rate faces, while retaining an
exact affine transform and a useful spectral statement on the nondegenerate
face?

Answer: yes.  The released theorem distinguishes (i) entrance/inaccessible
zero when `2*kappa*theta >= sigma^2`, (ii) regular instantaneously reflecting
zero when the strict inequality is reversed, (iii) absorbing dimension-zero
faces, and (iv) deterministic/constant faces.  For positive parameters it
also proves the Gamma reversible law, Laguerre diagonalization, exact gap
`kappa`, and quantitative `L2`, chi-square and total-variation contraction.

This is a Route-A dynamics experiment only.  No arithmetic labels, prime or
zero tables, Euler factors, target divisor, or Hilbert--Pólya construction are
part of the object.
