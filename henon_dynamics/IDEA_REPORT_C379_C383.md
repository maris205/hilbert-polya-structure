## Collision decisions before freezing

- A multinacci beta-transformation paper was killed.  The symbolic-dynamics
  programme already records the generic beta/negative-beta lane as internally
  occupied, and the classical periodic-point/zeta mechanism would make the
  proposed specialization a corollary rather than a new paper.
- A standard Bost--Connes reconstruction was killed.  Its zeta partition
  function and KMS structure have a direct primary owner, while the proposed
  package had no intrinsic isolated primitive-orbit atlas satisfying A1.
- A PGL3 Ramanujan-complex gallery zeta was deferred because no small explicit
  arithmetic quotient with independently verified links and chamber incidence
  had been supplied.  No executed quotient-construction preflight is recorded;
  this is an implementation-risk decision, not an experimental failure or an
  impossibility theorem.  Replacing it by a hand-built complex or by an
  underlying graph would violate the theorem contract.
- A Hecke-triangle transfer paper was not used because the repository already
  has several modular/Selberg boundary owners.  A new paper would first need
  a genuinely different accelerated correction theorem, not a change of
  triangle parameter.
- Plane-wave NLS instability was kept as a reserve only.  The nearby C221
  soliton Hessian package makes its owner distance smaller than the
  Aharonov--Bohm scattering problem selected below.

## Frozen candidates

### C379 -- reversible multibaker transport and twisted dynamical zeta

**Owner.**  The finite-ring, area-preserving multibaker map with a binary
Markov partition, signed cell displacement, winding on the ring, and Bloch
twists of the transport transfer operator.

**Large step.**  Close the entire symbolic-to-geometric dictionary: cyclic
binary words, least periods, powers, reversal, windings, reciprocal unstable
and stable multipliers, and direct fixed-point reconstruction.  Derive the
Bloch-block traces and determinant, recover the primitive weighted product,
and extract the hydrodynamic branch, exact finite-ring gap and diffusion
constant.  The one- and two-cell degeneracies are part of the theorem.

**Nearest collisions.**  Open Walsh bakers concern escape and resonance, C127
owns an affine horseshoe, and finite graph packages own nonbacktracking walks.
None owns a reversible area-preserving multibaker with winding-refined
primitive words and a twisted transport determinant.

**Expected strict tuple.**
`(A0_FAIL,A1_PASS_ANALYTIC,A2_FAIL,A3_PARTIAL_ANALYTIC_STRUCTURE,A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION)`.
The determinant and orbit product are source-native; they are not a target
divisor or target-zero correspondence.

### C380 -- finite Blaschke circle dynamics and exact Ruelle spectrum

**Owner.**  The nonlinear degree-two circle endomorphism
`B_a(z)=z(z-a)/(1-a z)` in one explicitly proved expanding real-parameter
chamber, acting through analytic inverse branches on a frozen annular Hardy
space.

**Large step.**  Give all-period fixed and primitive counts, nonconstant
multipliers and orientation data; prove the annular inverse-branch operator
is nuclear; specialize the complete transfer spectrum and Fredholm product;
and close the linear-map limit and loss-of-expansion wall.  Exact resultants
and an independently convergent finite-section calculation audit the theorem.

**Nearest collisions.**  C177 is the linear integer expanding circle, while
C141 is a two-branch polynomial inverse system on a different domain.  The
finite Blaschke inner map, its nonlinear multiplier ledger and its explicit
holomorphic transfer spectrum are not owned by either package.

**Expected strict tuple.**
`(A0_FAIL,A1_PASS_ANALYTIC,A2_FAIL,A3_PARTIAL_ANALYTIC_STRUCTURE,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`.

### C381 -- the alpha-one LSV intermittent map and its induced determinant

**Owner.**  The Liverani--Saussol--Vaienti map
`T(x)=x+2x^2` on the left branch and `T(x)=2x-1` on the right, with its neutral
fixed point separated from the uniformly expanding first-return dynamics on
`(1/2,1]`.

**Large step.**  Prove uniqueness of every binary periodic itinerary,
separate the neutral orbit from every hyperbolic primitive necklace, and
record all powers and derivative multipliers.  Construct the countable
first-return branches, prove their uniform contraction and inverse-linear
return tail at the endpoint exponent, and state the exact convergence domain
of the induced dynamical/Fredholm series.  The paper also proves why the
uninduced Perron operator does not inherit a trace-class spectral-gap claim.

**Nearest collisions.**  Finite shifts, the integer circle map and the
fixed-discriminant Gauss reduction do not own a neutral full-branch interval
map together with a countable hyperbolic inducing scheme and a parabolic
factor.  The classical LSV statistical theorem is cited as an owner, not
presented as a new result.

**Expected strict tuple.**
`(A0_FAIL,A1_PASS_ANALYTIC,A2_FAIL,A3_PARTIAL_ANALYTIC_STRUCTURE,A4_FAIL)`.

### C382 -- CM elliptic Frobenius phases and all-degree closed points

**Owner.**  The curve `E:y^2=x^3-x` over every good odd prime, with a frozen
primary Gaussian-prime convention in the ordinary chamber and the separate
supersingular chamber.

**Large step.**  Determine the Frobenius trace and phase in both congruence
classes, prove the extension-field recurrence, recover every degree of
primitive closed point by Moebius inversion, and close the complete native
`H^0/H^1/H^2` determinant, functional equation and critical circle.  Exact
point enumeration, recurrence and product coefficients are checked through
independent lanes.

**Nearest collisions.**  Flow papers 4 and 6 retain the universal closed-point
and cohomological determinant dictionary.  C382 claims only the CM
all-good-prime phase specialization, ordinary/supersingular split, sign
normalization and complete all-degree consequences.  It does not relabel the
native curve zeta as a Riemann-target determinant.

**Expected strict tuple.**
`(A0_STRUCTURAL_ARITHMETIC_RELATION,A1_PASS_ANALYTIC,A2_FAIL,A3_PARTIAL_ANALYTIC_STRUCTURE,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`.
The native curve-zeta determinant is reported separately; the strict A2 target
gate remains failed.

### C383 -- Friedrichs Aharonov--Bohm scattering on the punctured plane

**Owner.**  The nonnegative Friedrichs realization of the scalar
Aharonov--Bohm Hamiltonian on the punctured plane for one flux cell
`beta in [0,1)`, including its singular-origin boundary condition.

**Large step.**  Close the partial-wave decomposition, Bessel generalized
eigenfunctions, absolutely continuous spectral resolution, energy-independent
channel scattering matrix, distribution-sensitive forward direction,
away-forward cross section, heat kernel, gauge periodicity and the exact
time-reversal faces.  Infinite-volume heat traces and non-Friedrichs point
interactions are explicitly excluded.

**Nearest collisions.**  Existing magnetic packages treat compact monopole or
uniform-field Landau problems, and C293 has shifted oscillator channels on a
Grushin cylinder.  None owns a flat singular connection on the punctured plane
with continuous spectrum and a topological scattering matrix.

**Expected strict tuple.**
`(A0_FAIL,A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_UNITARY_OR_SCATTERING_CANDIDATE)`.
The exact scattering theorem is a deliberate Route-A obstruction: the
classical exterior motion is free and supplies no nontrivial primitive ledger.

## Round decision

The five systems are retained because they use different phase spaces and
proof mechanisms: hyperbolic transport, analytic circle dynamics,
nonuniformly hyperbolic inducing, arithmetic Frobenius action, and topological
quantum scattering.  C379--C382 test progressively stronger A1/A2 ingredients;
C383 is an independently useful negative control.  Coordinates and
determinants remain candidate-local.  Route B is disabled throughout, and no
package may claim target local arithmetic data, target Euler factors, bad
prime data, root numbers, automorphy, a target divisor or counting law, target
functional equations or zeros, or a Hilbert--Polya operator.
