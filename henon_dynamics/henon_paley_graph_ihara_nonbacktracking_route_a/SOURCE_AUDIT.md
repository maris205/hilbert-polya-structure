# Source and ownership audit

## Verified lineage

1. R. E. A. C. Paley, “On Orthogonal Matrices,” *Journal of Mathematics and
   Physics* 12 (1933), 311–320, DOI
   [10.1002/sapm1933121311](https://doi.org/10.1002/sapm1933121311).
   This is the primary Paley construction lineage.
2. Ki-ichiro Hashimoto, “Zeta Functions of Finite Graphs and Representations
   of p-Adic Groups,” *Advanced Studies in Pure Mathematics* 15 (1989),
   211–280, DOI
   [10.1016/B978-0-12-330580-0.50015-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-330580-0.50015-X).
   This owns the directed-edge zeta/operator lineage.
3. Hyman Bass, “The Ihara--Selberg zeta function of a tree lattice,”
   *International Journal of Mathematics* 3(6) (1992), 717--797, DOI
   [10.1142/S0129167X92000357](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129167X92000357).
   This owns the determinant formula lineage.

## Claim ownership

Paley parameters and spectrum, the Hashimoto operator, and Bass elimination
are classical.  This package makes a source-local reconstruction: it combines
them with an explicit orientation convention, complete trace-to-primitive
ledger, extension-field representation lock, and `q=5` boundary.  It makes no
priority or novelty claim for those classical ingredients or their immediate
combination.

## Collision audit

- C15 studies Heisenberg congruence voltage and Bass roots.
- C161 studies quadratic Gauss sums for cyclic Birkhoff sums.
- C260 classifies projective Möbius permutation cycles over finite fields.
- C269 classifies finite-field Chebyshev functional graphs.

None owns Paley quadratic-residue adjacency together with Hashimoto
nonbacktracking cycles.  The distinction is structural, not merely a changed
parameter.

## Prohibited inference

The source Ihara Euler product is not a target Euler factor.  The source Paley
Ramanujan inequality is not the Riemann hypothesis, a root-number statement,
or a target zero theorem.  No automorphy or Hilbert--Pólya operator is claimed.
