# Narrative report

The finite-field character does two jobs without fitted data.  It makes the
Paley graph strongly regular, and it diagonalizes adjacency by additive
characters.  Bass elimination then transports those three adjacency
eigenvalues to every root of the nonbacktracking operator.  The trace log does
the remaining combinatorics: it separates based repetitions into oriented
primitive cycles exactly.

This is a substantial source theorem, but it exposes its own obstruction.
The arithmetic label `q` selects a finite-field family; it is not generated as
a rational-prime orbit.  Nonbacktracking length is an integer word clock, not
`log p`.  The determinant is the finite graph's Ihara determinant, not a
target local factor.  That is why the result earns a strong A1 and only a weak
A0.
