# Research question

For every finite field of odd order `q=1 mod 4`, can the quadratic-residue
Paley graph be treated as a completely explicit nonbacktracking dynamical
system: all primitive oriented cycles, repetitions, multiplicities, source
Ihara determinant, and operator roots included, with the arithmetic and
Hilbert--Pólya boundaries stated without ambiguity?

The answer proved here is yes for the source graph theorem and no for any
promotion to a rational-prime orbit law or target spectral statement.
