# Evidence plan

1. Derive the scalar flow, area identity, collapse times, and minimal Jacobi
   spectrum analytically.
2. Enumerate all `1<=p,q<=10`, six nonminimal leaves per dimension pair, and
   the `0<=ell,m<=5` product spectrum.
3. Recompute every row in an independent checker and verify the defining
   identities separately in SymPy.
4. Replay the evidence byte-for-byte and require all hostile mutations to
   fail, including repaired-payload attacks.
5. Preserve three substantive paper rounds and rebuild each twice with a
   fixed epoch.  Require clean logs, embedded fonts, rasterization, and a
   self-excluding 27-file manifest ledger.

The finite enumeration checks conventions; it is not the proof of the
all-dimensional theorem.
