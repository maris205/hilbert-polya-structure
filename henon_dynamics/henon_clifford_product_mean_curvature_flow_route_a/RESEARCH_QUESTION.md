# Research question

For every pair of positive dimensions, can the full mean-curvature-flow
dynamics of the Clifford products

`S^p(cos(theta)) x S^q(sin(theta)) in S^(p+q+1)`

be closed in one theorem, including both ancient branches, exact focal
collapse times, Type-I tangent cylinders, area dissipation, and the complete
Jacobi index/nullity of the separating minimal product?

The answer proved here is yes inside this two-factor isoparametric family.
The question does not ask for a classification of arbitrary spherical
hypersurfaces and does not posit an arithmetic or Hilbert--Polya connection.
