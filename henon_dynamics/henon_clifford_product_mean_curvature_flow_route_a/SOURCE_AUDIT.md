# Source audit

## Primary ownership

The mean-curvature flow of parallel isoparametric hypersurfaces and its focal
collapse are sourced to dos Reis and Tenenblat, *The mean curvature flow by
parallel hypersurfaces*, Proc. AMS 146 (2018), DOI
`10.1090/proc/14178`.  The broader isoparametric-flow setting is sourced to
Liu and Terng, DOI `10.1215/00127094-2009-009`.

## Package contribution and non-priority statement

No priority is claimed for Clifford products, their principal curvatures, or
the cited isoparametric-flow facts.  The package contribution is a
convention-locked, proof-complete synthesis of the exact two-factor lifespan,
both Type-I tangent cylinders, strict area law, and a direct product-spectrum
derivation of index `p+q+3` and nullity `(p+1)(q+1)`.

## Claim boundary

The theorem is only for `p,q>=1` and the declared parallel family.  Endpoint
focal submanifolds are not regular hypersurface leaves.  The Jacobi operator
is a source stability operator, not a target spectral realization.  No target
arithmetic local data, Euler factor, root number, automorphy, target divisor,
functional equation, target-zero match, or Hilbert--Polya operator is claimed.
