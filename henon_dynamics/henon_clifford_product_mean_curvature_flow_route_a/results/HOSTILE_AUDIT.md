# C319 hostile audit

The adversarial lane attacks identity and source pins, a forbidden scope
flag, the Route-A verdict, the minimal leaf, index, nullity, collapse time,
cylinder radius, normalized area, and a spectral eigenvalue.  Every evidence
mutation is tested both with a stale digest and after adversarial digest
repair.  YAML attacks cover identity, Route A, scope, artifact-list content
and type, and duplicate-key parsing.  Repaired-digest attacks also add an
unowned theorem field, an extra branch field, and a duplicate branch
coordinate, the evaluation date, and the enumeration schema.  All 39
mutations are rejected.

The independent checker does not import producer code.  In particular, it
recomputes the area ratio and proves at each witness that
`d(log Area)/dt=-H^2<0`; this closes ownership of a field that is not merely
protected by the evidence digest.
