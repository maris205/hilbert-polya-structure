- The lattice correction satisfies the exact Heisenberg cocycle identity.
- The embedded central circle is a clean fixed component for every iterate.
- `det(I-DPhi^n)=0` and `L(Phi^n)=0` for every positive `n`.
- The transverse toral control has `L_(2n)-2` isolated fixed points; exact
  values are frozen through `n=20`.
- At `n=2`, `(1/5,2/5)` is a horizontal fixed class with fibre obstruction
  `-4/5`; the naive all-fibres component count is refuted.
- Haar preservation gives a natural Koopman unitary with the same iterate
  clock, but no clean-family orbit-weight bridge.
- Route-A tuple: `(A1_FAIL,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`.

The full nilmanifold fixed-component count is deliberately not stated.
