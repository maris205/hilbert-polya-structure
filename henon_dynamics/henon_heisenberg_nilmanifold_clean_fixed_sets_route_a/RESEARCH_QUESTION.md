# C146 research question

For a genuine compact Heisenberg nilmanifold automorphism whose horizontal
action is hyperbolic but whose center is fixed, what exact periodic structure
survives, and where does the usual isolated-orbit determinant mechanism fail?

The frozen answer is an all-iterate obstruction.  The central circle is fixed
pointwise by every positive iterate, so the fixed set is clean and
positive-dimensional, `det(I-DPhi_A^n)=0`, and the Lefschetz number is zero.
The horizontal torus supplies a sharp negative control with
`L_(2n)-2` isolated fixed points.

The stronger proposed count—one fixed circle above every horizontal fixed
class—is false for the standard integer lattice automorphism.  C146 records an
exact period-two counterexample and therefore reports only the certified
central component, not a fabricated total component ledger.

The Route-A boundary is structural.  No target data, target divisor,
arithmetic/local factor, quantum operator, or Route-B conclusion is requested
or authorized.
