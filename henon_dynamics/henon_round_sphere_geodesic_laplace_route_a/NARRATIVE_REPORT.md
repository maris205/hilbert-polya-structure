# C313 narrative report

The round sphere is maximally periodic rather than generically chaotic.  An
ordered orthonormal pair `(x/R,v)` spans an oriented two-plane, and geodesic
time simply rotates that frame.  This simultaneously proves the common least
period, the oriented-Grassmann quotient, and the identity linearized return.

The quantum side is equally explicit but carefully scoped.  Harmonic
polynomials give every Laplace eigenvalue and multiplicity.  Adding the
geometric scalar `(d-1)^2/(4R^2)` completes the square, so the half-wave has a
single phase after one classical period and becomes the identity after two.
This is an exact source classical/quantum alignment.

It still does not clear Route A: the orbit family is positive-dimensional,
the radius varies continuously, and the natural Laplacian has no target-zero
identification.  The paper records `A1_WEAK` and
`A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` without promoting either to an arithmetic bridge.
