# C313 source and claim audit

The source owner is the fixed round metric on `S_R^d`; its unit tangent flow,
spherical harmonics, and Laplace--Beltrami operator are all derived from that
same geometry.  Colin de Verdière's survey
[`10.5802/aif.2339`](https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.2339) supplies historical
periodic-geodesic/spectral context, not a novelty claim.

The package proves the embedding-space flow, least period, fixed sets,
reversor, Liouville preservation, oriented-Grassmann orbit quotient, maximal
clean return, spherical-harmonic eigenvalues/multiplicities, exact heat
series, and the scalar/identity revivals of the completed-square half-wave.

`A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` refers only to the source Laplacian.  It is not a
Hilbert--Pólya operator, and its eigenvalues are not matched to target zeros.
The continuum of great circles earns only `A1_WEAK`, because it is maximally
nonisolated.  No target Euler factor, root number, automorphy, divisor law,
functional equation, or arithmetic local datum is asserted.
