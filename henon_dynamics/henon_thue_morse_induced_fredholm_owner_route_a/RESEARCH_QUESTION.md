# C164 research question

Can the recurrent Thue--Morse renewal zeta proved in C159 be owned by a
source-natural trace-class operator construction, rather than merely by a
formal scalar denominator, while retaining an honest statement about why
the original one-step adjacency is not trace class?

The hard gate requires all of the following:

1. source return branches must be actual Hilbert coordinates;
2. the operator series must converge holomorphically in trace norm on the
   full open unit disk;
3. every trace power, not just one determinant, must recover renewal words;
4. the uninduced one-step adjacency must be audited on every diagonal
   weighted `l2`, not on one convenient norm; and
5. the source natural boundary must constrain operator continuation.

The frozen first-return family clears this gate.  It does not clear the
separate unitary or self-adjoint gate: the owner is induced and nonunitary,
while every bounded diagonal Hilbert realization of the time-one adjacency
is noncompact.
