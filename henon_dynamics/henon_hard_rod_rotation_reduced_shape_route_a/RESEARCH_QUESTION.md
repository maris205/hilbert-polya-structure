# Research question

For `N>=1` equal unit-mass hard rods of length `a>0` on a circle of
circumference `ell>N*a`, what is the exact all-event dynamics after quotienting
global spatial rotation and rod labels?  Does excluded-volume compression with
free length `L=ell-N*a` give a genuine conjugacy, and what are the necessary
and sufficient periodic-return conditions when velocities are distinct or
repeated?

The rotation quotient is essential.  The corresponding claim for the complete
physical phase space is false already for one moving rod and is recorded as an
obstruction rather than silently assumed.
