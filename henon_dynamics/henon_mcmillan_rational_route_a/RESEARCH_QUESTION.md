# Research question

For the rational McMillan/QRT-type map

\[
M_\mu(x,y)=\left(\frac{2\mu x}{1+x^2}-y,x\right),\qquad \mu=-2,
\]

which algebraic and low-period dynamical statements can be certified exactly
without promoting a local monodromy polynomial into a transfer determinant?

The bounded target is to verify:

1. the rational inverse and the reversor \(S(x,y)=(y,x)\) on their common
   domains;
2. \(\det DM=1\) and exact preservation of
   \(I=x^2y^2+x^2+y^2-2\mu xy\);
3. the fixed locus and a genuine real primitive two-cycle;
4. explicit removal of roots lying on \(x^2+1=0\);
5. the ordered period-two monodromy and a fixed-point control.

The package does not ask for a complete orbit atlas, global level-set
classification, transfer operator, Fredholm determinant, or any arithmetic
interpretation.
