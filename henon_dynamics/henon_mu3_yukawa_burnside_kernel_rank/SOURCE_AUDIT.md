# C63 source and novelty audit

The source boundary is the released C61 `W(E_6)` group presentation plus the
complete C62 orbit/stabilizer and fixed-field-type artifacts. C63 does not
reuse a pilot value as theorem authority: its producer rebinds the C61
generators, rechecks the C62 input schemas and digests, reconstructs ambient
conjugacy classes, and recomputes all fixed-coset characters from subgroup
element sets.

General Burnside linearization, permutation characters, Gassmann relations,
and lambda-ring identities are prior art and are not claimed as novel. The
bounded contribution is the exact instance-specific rank-13 / nullity-3
calculation for the 16 C62 `W(E_6)` subgroup types, with the primitive
four-term relation exposed by the exterior-square difference and its placement
inside the kernel. The order-4 relation `Y10-Y9` is an inherited C60
collision and is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim.

No absolute priority or literature-completeness claim is made in this local
source audit. Any external novelty survey belongs to a later paper review.
