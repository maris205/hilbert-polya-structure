# C47 results

Every nonzero rational function satisfying

\[
R(X^r)=R(X)^r\quad(r\ge1)
\]

identically on \(\mathbb G_m\) is \(R(X)=X^k\) for an integer \(k\).
Since C46 proves that every H6 multiplier is an algebraic unit, every such
label remains a unit and cannot be a rational prime.

On the unstable ray \(X>1\), continuous positive repetition-compatible
labels are \(X^c\) with \(c\in\mathbb R\).  Thus the pressure label
\(X^{h_*}\) is the exact survivor, not an exception hidden inside the
rational category.  Trace and fixed-point determinant controls fail the
square repetition law explicitly.
