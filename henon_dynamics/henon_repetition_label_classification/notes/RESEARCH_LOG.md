# Research log

C46 showed that raw multipliers are algebraic units.  C47 asks whether a
clever rational scalar transform could turn a unit into a prime while
preserving the exact Euler repetition law.  The divisor of a rational
function makes the answer rigid: repeated square roots force every finite
zero or pole away from 0 and infinity into an infinite support.  Only
monomials survive, and monomials of units remain units.  The pressure power
is nonrational and therefore becomes the unique scalar large-road survivor.
