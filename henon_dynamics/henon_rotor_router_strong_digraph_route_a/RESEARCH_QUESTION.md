# Research question

Given an arbitrary finite nonempty strongly connected directed multigraph with distinguished arcs and a cyclic order at every vertex, can the recurrent rotor-router dynamics be classified by graph invariants alone?

The target is one all-graph theorem, not five papers about one graph. It must determine:

- the exact recurrent phase space;
- the number and common length of all rotor-router orbits;
- the vertex-visit and individual-arc traversal frequencies on every orbit;
- all fixed counts, the Artin--Mazur zeta, the finite Koopman determinant, and spectral multiplicities;
- the Eulerian degeneration;
- the exact point where Route A stops.

The formulation must remain the sinkless rotor-router permutation on unicycles. A sandpile critical-group translation is outside the frozen source.
