# Hostile audit

## Strongest positive result

One vector of arborescence counts controls every recurrent orbit, its length, all vertex visits, every individual-arc frequency, the zeta, determinant, spectrum, and Eulerian degeneration for every finite strong directed multigraph and every rotor ordering.

## Strongest failure

The classification is combinatorially complete but contains no natural rational-prime labels, prime-power repetition law, target amplitude, or target analytic structure. The finite unitary Koopman model is exact but does not override A0 failure.

## Seven-mode integrity audit

1. **Implementation bug:** cleared by exhaustive enumeration, independent algorithms, CAS, replay, and repaired-hash mutations.
2. **Citation identity/context:** the Pham source metadata, Theorem 1 exact-length/count role, and primitive-return proof mechanism are explicit; no priority inflation.
3. **Invented result:** all reported finite counts exist in the evidence; external review and acceptance rates are not reported.
4. **Shortcut reliance:** the regression materializes rotor states and orbits rather than checking formulas alone.
5. **Bug reframed as insight:** no mismatch is accepted as a theorem; any mismatch blocks release.
6. **Methodology drift:** the chip clock, advance-then-move convention, distinguished arcs, and unicycle phase remain frozen.
7. **Early-frame lock:** loops, parallel arcs, non-Eulerian graphs, and cyclic-order changes are explicit controls.

## Sandpile collision control

No sink or stabilization appears in the frozen dynamics, and no recurrent sandpile group acts on its phase space. Flow balance determines only a positive multiple of the primitive Laplacian-kernel vector. Exact primitivity is instead source-locked to Pham's Theorem 1: its proof deletes the chip basepoint's outgoing arcs, uses chip addition on the remaining acyclic rotors, and computes the deleted row-class order in a quotient. C181 uses that mechanism only to justify exact return; it does not recast the frozen unicycle permutation as a sandpile translation.

## Time-reversal control

Every abstract finite cycle admits a reversor after choosing an origin, but the graph does not canonically choose one origin on each recurrent orbit. C181 therefore claims a natural finite unitary quantization but not a source-canonical reversal operator.

## Decision

Retain C181 as a complete rotor-router classification and natural finite quantization. Reject Route A at A0 and make no arithmetic local-factor, Euler-factor, root-number, automorphy, Hilbert--Pólya, or Route-B claim.
