2. Reconstruct all three Uhlenbeck integrals, both linear identities, their time derivatives, all three pairwise Dirac brackets, and two resolvent/Lax probes per state using exact fractions.

4. Record repeated-spectrum $SO(2)$ boundaries with an exact commuting-pair
   bracket, energy identity, and differential-wedge independence witness, and
   record isotropic great-circle boundaries without applying singular
   distinct-spectrum formulas.
5. Use a producer-independent checker, a separate symbolic lane, isolated byte replay, repaired-hash hostile mutations, and strict JSON/YAML parsing.
