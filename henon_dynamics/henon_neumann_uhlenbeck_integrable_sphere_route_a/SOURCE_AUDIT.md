## Claim boundary

The paper is a source-local reconstruction. It makes no literature-priority claim for the Neumann system, Uhlenbeck integrals, Lax representation, quadrics correspondence, or Liouville integrability. The analytic proof in this package owns its exact conventions and boundary bookkeeping.

The cited sources do not support, and this package does not claim, rational-prime orbit labels, target Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target divisor or functional equation, a target-zero match, a Hilbert--Polya operator, or Route B. For fixed $\hbar>0$, the natural Schrödinger operator is the quantization of the declared sphere Hamiltonian only. The repeated-spectrum bracket, energy relation, and independence witness are source-local derivations rather than priority claims.
