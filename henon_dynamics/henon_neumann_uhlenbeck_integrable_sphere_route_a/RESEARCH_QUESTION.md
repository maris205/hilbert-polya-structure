# Research question

For the classical Neumann oscillator on the round two-sphere with three distinct quadratic frequencies, can one prove in one convention-locked theorem global completeness, the Uhlenbeck commuting integrals, their resolvent/Lax generator, the Liouville-torus return criterion, and every axial, coordinate, repeated-spectrum, and isotropic boundary, while keeping the natural quantum Hamiltonian separate from any target-arithmetic claim?

The answer proved here is yes. On each double-spectrum face the replacement
integrals are explicitly commuting and independent on a nonempty open set;
the natural quantum clause fixes $\hbar>0$. The finite artifact checks exact
rational identities and conventions; it does not replace the all-state
analytic proof.
