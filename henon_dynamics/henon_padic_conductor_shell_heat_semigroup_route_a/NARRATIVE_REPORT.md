# Narrative report

HCS-C283 closes unusually cleanly because the compact p-adic group has two
exact descriptions of the same scale geometry.  Characters group into
conductor shells, while conditional expectations group functions by residue
resolution.  Freezing the multiplier directly makes both descriptions
unambiguous and turns every claimed endpoint into a geometric-series question.

The positive result is a complete operator theorem.  The spectrum has one
constant mode and a geometric ladder with exact multiplicities.  A telescoping
sum of conditional-expectation generators proves positivity and conservation;
it is not inferred merely from positive eigenvalues.  Superexponential heat
damping gives trace class at positive time.  The mean-zero zeta is a rational
function of \(p^{-\alpha s}\), so its entire vertical pole lattice, residues,
value and derivative at zero, and primed determinant are exact.  The eigenvalue
count is a literal staircase, with distinct liminf and limsup, and the
resolvent Schatten boundary includes the divergent equality face.

The negative Route-A conclusion is equally exact.  A local p-adic carrier is
an honest weak arithmetic relation, but a fixed p is not the set of rational
primes.  Shell conductors do not carry \(\log q\), von Mangoldt weights,
primitive-orbit data, bad Euler factors, or root numbers.  Replacing p by a
composite branching number keeps the complete shell algebra, so the formulas
prove too much to identify target arithmetic.  The pole lattice and power-law
count also have the wrong target geometry.  Hence A1–A3 fail and A4 remains a
formal self-adjoint hint only.

The executable layer mirrors the proof rather than sampling an unsupported
claim.  It covers 438 deterministic cells, quotient orders up to 4096, an
independent checker with over a thousand assertions, exact rational SymPy
matrices, fresh-path byte replay, and repaired-hash hostile mutations.  The
three paper revisions add, in order, the operator/Markov core, the sharp trace
and scale endpoints, and the zeta/boundary/Route-A closure.
