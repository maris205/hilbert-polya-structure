# Paper plan

The manuscript is a compact theorem certificate rather than a survey.

1. Freeze \(\mathbb Z_p\), Haar normalization, dual conductors, and the Fourier
   multiplier; state explicitly that no named-operator normalization is used.
2. Reconstruct the multiplier from residue conditional expectations and prove
   the positive conservative semigroup.
3. Count characters to obtain spectrum, compact resolvent, heat trace, and
   all positive-time Schatten classes.
4. Sum the mean-zero spectral zeta, list the complete pole lattice and
   residues, and compute the primed determinant.
5. Prove the exact counting staircase, liminf/limsup oscillation, log-periodic
   heat profile, and sharp resolvent Schatten condition.
6. Give a boundary atlas for \(\alpha=0\), \(\mu=0\), \(t=0\),
   \(\alpha\to\infty\), and \(p=2\).
7. Describe the finite-quotient DFT certificate and close with the honest
   Route-A obstruction and composite-branching control.

Revision 0 contains items 1–2.  Revision 1 adds items 3 and 5.  Revision 2
adds items 4, 6, and 7.  Thus the archived PDFs are substantively different,
