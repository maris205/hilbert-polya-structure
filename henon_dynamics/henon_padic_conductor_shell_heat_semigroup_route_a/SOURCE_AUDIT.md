## Registry collision audit

- **C277:** time-Caputo Dirichlet heat is a physical-memory non-semigroup.
  C283 is a spatial, compact p-adic, true Markov semigroup.
- **C184:** the finite Sierpiński pre-gasket uses graph spectral decimation.
  C283 uses the infinite compact group and conductor filtration.
- **C174:** the deterministic 2-adic parity return map has a different owner,
  and only prime 2.  C283 is a self-adjoint Fourier multiplier for arbitrary
  fixed prime p.
- **C28:** the weighted direct sum ranges over all primes.  C283 is expressly
  local at one p and forbids promoting that p to all-prime target data.
- **C221/C256 and neighboring PDE owners:** NLS Hessians and KdV/Lax systems
  have neither this ultrametric filtration nor this spectrum.  The same is
  true of Euclidean fractional heat multipliers.

No candidate package in C1–C278 uses this compact p-adic owner, but the direct
external owner above already contains its core positive spectral/zeta data.
The retained source-local closure is narrower: adjoining and separating the
constant zero mode, reconstructing Markov positivity through conditional
expectations, recording exact staircase envelopes and the locally uniform
log-periodic heat profile, proving the sharp fractional-resolvent Schatten
gate and primed determinant, closing all parameter faces, and binding the
normalization to an independent finite-quotient DFT certificate.  This is an
integrated proof/reproducibility residual, not a literature-priority claim.

## Normalization and nonclaim audit

The phrase “conductor-shell p-adic fractional heat” is descriptive.  Every
theorem is derived from the displayed character multiplier.  We identify its
positive mean-zero spectral data with the precise Taibleson example just
cited, while making no claim of identity with every restriction or
normalization called a Vladimirov operator.  We do not claim literature
novelty.  The source-local spectral zeta is not a target Euler product or
target zero divisor.
