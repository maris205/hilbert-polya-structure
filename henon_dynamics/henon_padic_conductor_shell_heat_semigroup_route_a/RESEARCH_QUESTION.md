# Research question

For every fixed prime \(p\), \(\alpha>0\), and \(\mu\ge0\), can an explicit
conductor-shell Fourier multiplier on \(L^2(\mathbb Z_p)\) be taken from its
character definition all the way to a complete and independently executable
spectral-semigroup theorem, including every degeneration, without smuggling in
a convention-dependent pseudodifferential normalization or target arithmetic
data?

The answer proved here is yes.  The finite-dimensional conductor spaces give
exact multiplicities; the conditional expectations onto residue classes give
a second reconstruction and a positive Markov generator; the resulting
geometric series gives the entire meromorphic continuation of the mean-zero
spectral zeta.  The same shell geometry gives a sharp staircase Weyl law,
log-periodic scaling, and an if-and-only-if Schatten threshold.

The arithmetic question is deliberately answered in the negative.  Fixing one
local prime creates a genuine p-adic source, hence an A0 weak relation, but it
does not create an all-prime primitive-orbit ledger, Euler product, target
functional equation, or same-clock Hilbert–Pólya construction.  A composite
branching filtration has the same operator formulas and is the strongest
“proves too much” control.
