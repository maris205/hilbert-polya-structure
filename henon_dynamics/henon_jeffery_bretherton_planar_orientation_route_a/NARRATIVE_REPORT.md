# Narrative report

The decisive reduction is projective.  If `q'=Bq`, then
`p=q/||q||` satisfies the Jeffery–Bretherton director equation.  For an
incompressible planar gradient, the nonzero block obeys

`B2^2 = delta I`,

where

`delta=lambda^2(a^2+((b+c)/2)^2)-((b-c)/2)^2`.

This identity is the complete atlas.  Negative `delta` gives a projective
oscillator: equatorial head–tail directors close after `pi/sqrt(-delta)`,
whereas mixed directors need twice that time.  Positive `delta` gives three
eigen-directors and an exact source–saddle–sink stratification.  The saddle's
stable and unstable sets are `RP1` projective lines with, respectively, the
source and sink endpoints excluded.  At zero
`delta`, a nonzero generator is nilpotent, an entire `RP1` is fixed, and all
other trajectories converge algebraically to its unique horizontal
image/kernel direction.  A zero generator fixes all of `RP2`.

For nonzero simple shear, the theorem recovers the aspect-ratio frequency
`|gamma| r/(r^2+1)` and resolves the often-hidden factor two between a
head–tail equatorial director and a nonvertical oriented vector or mixed
director; the vertical vector is fixed.  At `r=1`, this statement concerns a
marked material director, since an unmarked sphere has no intrinsic axis.

The result is a substantial source-dynamics classification but fails Route A
at entry.  Elliptic periodic points occur in clean continua and depend on
continuous flow/shape parameters; no prime dictionary or logarithmic clock
appears.  The remaining chambers align or are fixed rather than recurrent.
