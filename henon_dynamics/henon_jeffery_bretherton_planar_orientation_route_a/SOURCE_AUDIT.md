all-planar-flow `RP2` sign atlas, genuine minimal periods, complete strobe
fixed sets, nilpotent projective line, hyperbolic cell decomposition, and
executable release closure under one convention.  “New” means only that no
C1–C279 owner in this workspace owns that exact theorem.  It is not a claim
of priority in the literature, and the paper expressly does not claim to have
invented Jeffery’s equation.

describes a marked material director, not an intrinsic axis of an unmarked
