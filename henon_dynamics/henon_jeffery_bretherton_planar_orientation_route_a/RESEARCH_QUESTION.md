# Research question

For the Jeffery–Bretherton equation of a passive axisymmetric spheroid in a
prescribed steady incompressible planar linear flow:

1. can every director trajectory be reconstructed from one linear flow before
   normalization;
2. can a single exact invariant classify all periodic, aligning, nilpotent,
   and stationary parameter chambers;
3. can true head–tail minimal periods, all stroboscopic fixed sets, complete
   hyperbolic `RP1` stable/unstable manifolds with endpoint exclusions, and
   rod/disk/marked-sphere boundaries be stated without
   confusing `S2` vectors with `RP2` directors; and
4. does any resulting recurrence clear the arithmetic entry gate of Route A?

The answer to the first three questions is yes.  The answer to the fourth is
no: the elliptic chamber is filled by clean periodic continua, the remaining
chambers have no nontrivial recurrence, and the clock and invariant contain no
rational-prime carrier.

The frozen owner excludes Brownian rotation, particle inertia and interaction,
spatially varying or time-dependent gradients, and feedback of the particle
on the fluid.  At `r=1`, it tracks a marked material director; an unmarked
sphere has no intrinsic orientation state.
