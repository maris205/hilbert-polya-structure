# Paper plan

## Core narrative

The apparent nonlinear director equation becomes a linear projective flow.
For a planar incompressible gradient the active block is traceless, so its
square is a scalar.  One discriminant consequently controls every orbit type.

## Revision structure

- **Round 0:** freeze the physical/projective convention, derive the linear
  lift, and prove the elliptic/hyperbolic classification.
- **Round 1:** add the nilpotent and identity faces, complete source–saddle–sink
  limits with `RP1` stable/unstable endpoint exclusions, and derive both
  nonzero simple-shear periods with the head–tail factor.
- **Round 2:** add all strobe fixed sets, rod/disk/sphere boundaries,
  executable receipt, source traceability, collision audit, and Route-A
  obstruction.

## Figure/table phase

The essential visualization is a compact theorem table mapping the sign of
`delta` to fixed sets, limits, and recurrence.  A second data table records
receipt sizes and independent gates.  Both are generated from the frozen
formulas/evidence and embedded directly in LaTeX; a decorative figure would
not add information and is therefore omitted.

## Claim boundary

Jeffery and Bretherton are cited only for model lineage and the spheroidal
shape parameter.  Every displayed classification and proof is local.  The
paper claims neither invention of the source equation nor literature priority,
and it draws no target arithmetic or spectral conclusion.

The `r=1` row uses a marked material director; no orientation is attributed to
an unmarked sphere.
