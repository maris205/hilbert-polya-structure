# Hostile integrity and failure-mode audit

## Rejected corruptions

The mutation gate rejects changes to the source commit, epoch, scope literal,
evaluator hash, proof status, Route-A tuple, Route-B lock, forbidden claim
flags, declared counts, exact discriminants, regime labels, normalized
directors, semigroup identity, shear periods, strobe times/fixed sets, complete
key sets, marked-sphere semantics, and hyperbolic endpoint exclusions.  Twenty-
four semantic mutations receive repaired payload hashes; a twenty-fifth
changes text without repairing the hash.  All are rejected.

The exact parameter-key comparison retains rational values without integer
coercion.  In particular, a consistently recomputed row at `a=-5/2` cannot
alias the frozen `a=-2` grid point; that repaired-hash attack is part of the
24 semantic trials.

## Seven research failure modes

1. **Phantom target:** absent; the theorem concerns only the frozen source.
2. **Post-hoc fitting:** absent; no primes, zeros, or fitted parameters enter.
3. **Train/test leakage:** inapplicable; the complete formulas precede the
   finite regression grid.
4. **Computation as proof:** prevented by a separate arbitrary-parameter proof.
5. **Citation laundering:** prevented by claim-level source use and verified
   publisher metadata.
6. **Hidden singular boundary:** prevented by the marked-material sphere
   convention and explicit nilpotent, identity, rod, disk, vertical-vector,
   and zero-shear faces.
7. **Cross-owner theorem assembly:** absent; no result from C189, C234, C274,
   or another package is imported as a premise.

chambers align or remain fixed.  Periods vary with continuous fluid and shape
parameters and carry neither all rational primes nor logarithmic prime time.
