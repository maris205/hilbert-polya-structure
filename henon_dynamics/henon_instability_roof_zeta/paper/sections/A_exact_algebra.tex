\section{Exact algebra}
\label{app:algebra}

\subsection{The period-four orbit}

Let \(q=1/\sqrt6\) and
\[
 \mathbf q=(-q,-q,q,q).
\]
Because \(6q_i^2=1\), the H\'enon recurrence becomes
\(q_{i+1}=-q_{i-1}\), which the sequence satisfies.  Its cyclic state word is
a rotation of
\[
 (--,\,+-,\,++,\,-+),
\]
and all four points lie strictly inside the corresponding certified
rectangles.  The three components of the action satisfy
\[
 \sum_iq_iq_{i+1}=0,\qquad
 \sum_iq_i=0,\qquad
 \sum_iq_i^3=0.
\]
Thus \(\sum_iS_6(q_i,q_{i+1})=0\).

Up to cyclic conjugacy, its exact monodromy is
\[
 M_4=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 601&-48\sqrt6\\
 48\sqrt6&-23
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
with trace 578 and determinant one.  Hence
\[
 L_4=289+24\sqrt{145},\qquad
 L_4^2-578L_4+1=0.
\]

\subsection{Degree of powers of the fixed multiplier}

For \(q_*=-(1+\sqrt7)/6\), the trace is \(t_+=2+2\sqrt7\) and
\[
 L_1+L_1^{-1}=t_+.
\]
Eliminating \(\sqrt7\) gives the reciprocal polynomial
\[
 P_1(X)=X^4-4X^3-22X^2-4X+1.
\]
To prove irreducibility, work over \(K=\Q(\sqrt7)\).  The discriminant of the
quadratic for \(L_1\) is
\[
 t_+^2-4=28+8\sqrt7.
\]
Its norm to \(\Q\) equals \(336\), which is not a rational square.  The
discriminant is therefore not a square in \(K\), so
\[
 [K(L_1):K]=2.
\]
Moreover,
\(\sqrt7=(L_1+L_1^{-1}-2)/2\in\Q(L_1)\), hence
\(K\subset\Q(L_1)\) and \(K(L_1)=\Q(L_1)\).  The tower law now gives
\([\Q(L_1):\Q]=4\).

Set
\[
 U(t)=\frac{t+\sqrt{t^2-4}}2\quad(t>2),\qquad
 M=U(2\sqrt7-2).
\]
The conjugates of \(L_1\) are
\[
 L_1,\ L_1^{-1},\ -M,\ -M^{-1}.
\]
Since
\[
 L_1>M>1>M^{-1}>L_1^{-1}>0,
\]
their absolute values are distinct.  Raising them to any positive integer
power preserves distinctness, including when the signs of the last pair
coincide.  Thus \([\Q(L_1^m):\Q]=4\) for all \(m\ge1\).  Since
\(L_4^n\in\Q(\sqrt{145})\), an equality \(L_1^m=L_4^n\) is impossible.

\subsection{Orientation-twisted unit control}
\label{app:orientation-control}

At \(s=0\), the instability lengths disappear from the orientation-twisted
section.  From \eqref{eq:positive}, the one-step unstable factor
\(a_j=-12x_j-rm^u(z_j)\) has sign \(-\sgn x_j\).  Thus
\[
 \sigma_p=(-1)^{n_p}\prod_{j=0}^{n_p-1}\sgn x_j.
\]
In the state order \(--,-+,+-,++\), this sign character weights the outgoing
states by \(B=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1)A\).  Direct expansion gives the
exact symbolic determinant
\[
 \det(I-zB)=1-z+z^3-z^4=(1-z^2)(1-z+z^2).
\]
It has the tautological root \(z=1\), corresponding to \(s=0\), and only
cyclotomic-looking roots.  This finite-state sign control is a warning:
elementary symbolic characters can produce superficially arithmetic patterns
without a prime correspondence.
