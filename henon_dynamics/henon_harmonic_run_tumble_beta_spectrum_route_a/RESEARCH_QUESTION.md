# Research question

For a particle driven by symmetric telegraph noise in a one-dimensional harmonic trap, can one close in a single theorem the stationary component law, every moment, the exact time correlations, and the nonnormal algebra of every finite polynomial filter?

The answer is yes for `mu,v,lambda>0`.  Scaling by `y=mu x/v` produces a beta density on `[-1,1]`; the first-moment matrix gives the complete stationary correlation matrix; and the polynomial generator is bidiagonal after parity splitting.  The latter reveals an exact odd/even resonance dichotomy.

The theorem expressly does not classify the full `L2` generator.
