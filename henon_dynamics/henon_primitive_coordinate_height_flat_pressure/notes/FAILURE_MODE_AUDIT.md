# Failure-mode audit

- **Target leakage:** none; no prime or zero data are read.
- **Finite-to-infinite promotion:** avoided; finite irreducibility is not
  claimed all-period.
- **Coordinate ambiguity:** fixed algebraic rescaling is treated explicitly;
  period-dependent scaling remains outside scope.
- **Multiplicity error:** excluded by locked P62 squarefreeness.
- **Open/closed bound error:** period one is an exact equality at the root
  bound and is handled separately in the independent checker.
- **Clock mismatch:** the refuted pressure omits period by definition; the
  corrected extensive pressure is explicitly a new object.
- **Route-B inflation:** prohibited; no operator is proposed.
