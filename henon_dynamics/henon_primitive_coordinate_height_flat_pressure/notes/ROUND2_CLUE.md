# ROUND2_CLUE

Define the packet-average logarithmic Mahler mass

\[
a_n=\frac1{D_n}\log M(\widetilde\Psi_n)
=\frac1{D_n}\sum_{\widetilde\Psi_n(\alpha)=0}\log^+|\alpha|.
\]

The complete root set is an exact reflection slice of the full two-shift.
Test whether its empirical root measures converge to the pushforward of the
Bernoulli maximal-entropy measure.  Such a theorem would give
`a_n -> integral log^+|x| dmu` and turn the extensive packet weight
`exp(-s*n*a_n)` into a genuine linear pressure.  It would still not supply a
prime trace; the next paper must keep that boundary explicit.
