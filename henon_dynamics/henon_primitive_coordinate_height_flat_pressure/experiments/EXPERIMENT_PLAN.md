# Experiment plan

## Claim-driven checks

1. Reconstruct odd closure and primitive quotient polynomials independently.
2. Apply `T=6X` and verify monic integral coefficients exactly.
3. Factor exactly through odd period 11.
4. Compute 50-digit real roots and factor Weil heights as diagnostics.
5. Verify exact rational root isolators through period 9 against
   `1+sqrt(7)`; treat period one as endpoint equality.
6. Sample `s=-2,-1,0,1,2` and verify the theoretical height sandwich.
7. Run 24 adversarial mutations and ordinary/optimized unit tests.

## Non-claims

Finite irreducibility is not promoted to all periods.  Numerical heights are
not used to prove the pressure limit.  No arithmetic labels or Riemann data
are fitted.
