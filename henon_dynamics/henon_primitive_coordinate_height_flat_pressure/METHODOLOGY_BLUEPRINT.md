# Methodology blueprint

1. Import only the locked P46 integrality theorem and P62 full-horseshoe
   algebraic exhaustion theorem.
2. Apply a maximum-coordinate argument directly to the integral cyclic
   recurrence.
3. Convert the coordinate bound into a uniform absolute Weil-height bound.
4. Sandwich the weighted root count between period-independent multiples of
   its exact degree.
5. Use the P62 degree asymptotic to identify the pressure.
6. Rebuild finite primitive polynomials independently, check integral
   scaling, factorization, exact root intervals, and numerical heights.
7. Reject mutations that change the object, scaling, bound, degree, pressure,
   or claim boundary.

The finite calculations diagnose the theorem but do not prove it.  No prime
or zeta-zero data are allowed.
