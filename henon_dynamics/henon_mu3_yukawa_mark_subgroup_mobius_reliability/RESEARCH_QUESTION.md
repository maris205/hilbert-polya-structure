# C77 research question

Given the sixteen frozen C76 labels and the actual twenty-subgroup lattice of
`Q = Z/9 + Z/3 + Z/2`, what is the exact reliability law for each possible
generated closure under independent label deletion?

The question is deliberately more refined than the C76 orbit census:

1. For each subgroup `H`, determine `n_H`, the number of named cyclic label
   closures contained in `H`.
2. Compute the cumulative polynomial
   `P_{<=H}(q)=q^(16-n_H)` from first principles.
3. Compute the subgroup-lattice Möbius function `mu(K,H)` and invert to obtain
   `P_{=H}(q)` for all twenty subgroups.
4. Independently enumerate every support and form
   `sum_A 1_{Phi(A)=H}(1-q)^{|A|}q^{16-|A|}`.
5. Require exact equality between the inverted and directly enumerated
   polynomials, including the top subgroup and the C73 reliability law.

Here `Phi(A)` is the subgroup generated by the retained named coordinates,
and the empty support generates the zero subgroup.  The variable `q` is a
deletion probability; no asymptotic or numerical approximation is involved.

Boundary: the C76 effective label action is not replaced by the 11520-element
ambient lift, and this finite calculation makes no arithmetic/local,
Euler-factor, root-number, automorphy, full Burnside-ring, or Hilbert--Polya
claim.  Scope firewall: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
