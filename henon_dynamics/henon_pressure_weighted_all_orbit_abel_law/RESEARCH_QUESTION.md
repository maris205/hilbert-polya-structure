# Research question

HCS-P51 constructed the all-primitive-orbit Abel-graded packet germ, but the
ungraded boundary diverges.  HCS-P52 found the exact Abel renormalization on
one period-four orbit and left a single large question:

> Can the Abel boundary be interchanged with the complete pressure-weighted
> primitive-orbit sum without assuming a uniform orbitwise cyclotomic
> remainder?

The key issue is that nonphysical algebraic conjugates of an H6 multiplier
need not all be hyperbolic.  Unit-circle conjugates can make
\(\log|\Phi_n(\lambda)|\) fluctuate.  P53 asks whether those fluctuations
remain below the \(n\)-scale needed by the totient Abel law and whether the
crude P51 packet envelope is already strong enough for all-orbit dominated
convergence.

The answer is yes in the certified P51 right half-plane.  The exact leading
coefficient is the full Mahler spectral height, not merely the logarithm of
the physical unstable multiplier.
