\section{Source packets and Mahler spectral height}

Let $H_6(q,p)=(1-6q^2-p,q)$ be restricted to the source-locked four-state
survivor.  Its adjacency matrix is
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}
 1&0&1&0\\
 1&0&0&0\\
 0&1&0&1\\
 0&1&0&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
For a primitive orbit $\gamma$, let $m(\gamma)$ be its symbolic period and
$M_\gamma\in SL_2$ its return derivative.  Write
\[
 \Lambda_\gamma=|\lambda_\gamma|>1,
 \qquad t_\gamma=\lambda_\gamma+\lambda_\gamma^{-1},
 \qquad F_\gamma=\mathbb Q(t_\gamma).
\]
The signed branch is part of the source data.  In particular, taking an
absolute value before forming $t_\gamma$ is forbidden.

For $n>2$, cyclotomic reciprocity and the integral monodromy construction
give \citep{WangP49}
\begin{equation}
 \beta_{\gamma,n}
 =\lambda_\gamma^{-\varphi(n)/2}\Phi_n(\lambda_\gamma)
 \in\mathcal O_{F_\gamma}.
 \label{eq:beta}
\end{equation}
Let
\[
 D_{\gamma,n}
 =\sum_{\mathfrak q}v_{\mathfrak q}(\beta_{\gamma,n})
 [\gamma,n,\mathfrak q]
\]
in the weighted source-tagged space $\Btag$.  The ideal norm formula is
the source mass identity of the all-orbit packet construction
\citep{WangP51}:
\begin{equation}
 b_{\gamma,n}:=\|D_{\gamma,n}\|_{\rm tag}
 =\log|N_{F_\gamma/\mathbb Q}\beta_{\gamma,n}|.
 \label{eq:mass}
\end{equation}

Put $K_\gamma=\mathbb Q(\lambda_\gamma)$.  Inversion is a nontrivial
automorphism and its fixed field is $F_\gamma$.  Every embedding
$\sigma:F_\gamma\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ therefore has two extensions to
$K_\gamma$, sending $\lambda_\gamma$ to the reciprocal roots of
\[
 X^2-\sigma(t_\gamma)X+1.
\]
Choose the root $\rho_\sigma$ of modulus at least one.

\begin{lemma}[Mahler-height identity]
Let $f_\gamma$ be the monic minimal polynomial of
$\lambda_\gamma$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \Hspec_\gamma:=\log M(f_\gamma)
 =\sum_{\sigma:F_\gamma\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}
 \log|\rho_\sigma|.
 \label{eq:height}
\end{equation}
Moreover $\Hspec_\gamma\ge\log\Lambda_\gamma>0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The conjugates of the algebraic unit $\lambda_\gamma$ occur in reciprocal
pairs $\rho_\sigma,\rho_\sigma^{-1}$.  Their contribution to the Mahler
measure is $|\rho_\sigma|$.  Multiplying over the pairs proves
\eqref{eq:height}.  The physical real embedding contributes
$\log\Lambda_\gamma$.
\end{proof}

No $\rho_\sigma$ is a root of unity.  If one were, irreducibility would make
the minimal polynomial of $\lambda_\gamma$ cyclotomic, contradicting the
physical root of modulus greater than one.

\begin{remark}
$\Hspec_\gamma$ is the unnormalized logarithmic Mahler measure of the monic
minimal polynomial.  It is not the degree-normalized absolute Weil height.
No hidden factor $[K_\gamma:\mathbb Q]^{-1}$ is present in the Abel
coefficient.
\end{remark}

The pressure length is
\[
 \widehat\ell_\gamma=h_*\log\Lambda_\gamma.
\]
The symbolic census and uniform unstable expansion inherited from the
certified survivor give
\begin{equation}
 \#\{\gamma:m(\gamma)=m\}\le3\phi^m,
 \qquad
 \widehat\ell_\gamma\ge h_*m\log J_*.
 \label{eq:orbit-envelope}
\end{equation}
These bounds will be used only after the orbitwise arithmetic asymptotic has
been proved.
