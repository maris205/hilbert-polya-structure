\section{Elementary Laplace estimates}

We record the two elementary estimates used by the boundary proof.  The
classical summatory totient formula is
\[
 S_\varphi(x):=\sum_{n\le x}\varphi(n)
 =\frac3{\pi^2}x^2+O(x\log(2x));
\]
see, for example, \citet{Apostol1976}.  Partial summation gives
\[
 \sum_{n\ge1}\varphi(n)e^{-\tau n}
 =\tau\int_0^\infty S_\varphi(x)e^{-\tau x}\,\dd x.
\]
The main integral equals $6/(\pi^2\tau^2)$.  Scaling $y=\tau x$ in the
error integral gives
\[
 \tau^2\sum_{n\ge1}\varphi(n)e^{-\tau n}
 =\frac6{\pi^2}+O(\tau\log(2/\tau)).
\]

For the orbitwise remainder, split the sum at $n\le\tau^{-1}$ and compare
the tail with an integral, or directly scale $x=\tau n$.  One obtains
\[
 \tau^2\sum_{n\ge3}\sqrt n(1+\log n)^2e^{-\tau n}
 \ll\tau^{1/2}(1+|\log\tau|)^2.
\]
Both estimates are uniform for $0<\tau\le1/2$.  Finally,
\[
 \tau^2\sum_{\tau n\ge R}ne^{-\tau n}
 \ll(R+1)e^{-R}
\]
follows from the corresponding exponential integral and is the scaled-index
tightness estimate used in Section~5.
