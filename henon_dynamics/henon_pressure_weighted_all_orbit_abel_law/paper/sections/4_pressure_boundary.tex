\section{Pressure domination and the all-orbit boundary}

The orbitwise error constant in \eqref{eq:orbit-asymptotic} is not uniform
in $\gamma$, and we do not need it to be.  The source construction gives a
separate positive estimate.  If $m=m(\gamma)$, then
\begin{equation}
 0\le b_{\gamma,n}\le K_m n,
 \qquad
 K_m=2^m(a+bm),
 \label{eq:crude}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 a=\log(2\sqrt3),
 \qquad b=\frac12\log(3+2\sqrt7).
\]
For $0<\tau\le1$,
\begin{equation}
 \tau^2\sum_{n\ge3}ne^{-\tau n}
 \le\tau^2\frac{e^{-\tau}}{(1-e^{-\tau})^2}\le4.
 \label{eq:n-laplace}
\end{equation}
Combining \eqref{eq:crude}, \eqref{eq:n-laplace}, and
\eqref{eq:orbit-envelope}, one obtains, for $\sigma>\sigma_0$,
\begin{equation}
 12\sum_{m\ge1}(a+bm)
 \left(2\phi e^{-\sigma h_*\log J_*}\right)^m<\infty.
 \label{eq:majorant}
\end{equation}

Let $\mathfrak m:\Btag\to\mathbb C$ be the norm-one logarithmic mass
functional.  For $\tau>0$ put
\begin{equation}
 Z(s,\tau)
 =\sum_{\gamma}e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma}
 \sum_{n\ge3}b_{\gamma,n}e^{-\tau n}.
 \label{eq:Z}
\end{equation}
This is $\mathfrak m$ applied to the previously constructed Banach-valued
germ at $u=e^{-\tau}$.

\begin{theorem}[Pressure-weighted all-orbit Abel law]
For $\Re s>\sigma_0$,
\begin{equation}
 \tau^2Z(s,\tau)\longrightarrow
 \Aamp(s):=\frac3{\pi^2}
 \sum_{\gamma}e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma}\Hspec_\gamma
 \label{eq:all-orbit}
\end{equation}
locally uniformly in $s$.  The series defining $\Aamp$ converges normally,
so $\Aamp$ is holomorphic in this half-plane.  Equivalently,
\begin{equation}
 (1-u)^2\mathfrak m(\mathcal G(s,u))\longrightarrow\Aamp(s)
 \qquad(u\uparrow1).
 \label{eq:u-boundary}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{cor:one-orbit-abel} gives the limit orbit by orbit, while
\eqref{eq:majorant} dominates the normalized terms.  Dominated convergence
proves \eqref{eq:all-orbit}.  For local uniformity, choose a common lower
bound for $\Re s$ on a compact set, make the period tail of
\eqref{eq:majorant} uniformly small, and then handle the finite orbit prefix.

The limit in the one-orbit theorem and the bound \eqref{eq:n-laplace} show
that $\Hspec_\gamma$ is bounded by a fixed multiple of $K_m$.  Thus the
series for $\Aamp$ has the same normal majorant, proving holomorphy.
Finally $(1-e^{-\tau})/\tau\to1$ proves \eqref{eq:u-boundary}.
\end{proof}

The source-locked pressure estimate $h_*\ge0.277980$ gives the numerical
safe condition
\begin{equation}
 \Re s>3.125206884004728\ldots .
 \label{eq:sigma-numeric}
\end{equation}
The theorem does not assert convergence at equality or continuation beyond
this sufficient domain.

\begin{remark}
The holomorphic function $\Aamp(s)$ is not identified with a logarithmic
derivative of a Ruelle or Fredholm determinant.  In particular, no theorem
here realizes $\Hspec_\gamma$ as a H\"older potential, supplies repetition
weights, or produces the trace-log combinatorics required by a determinant.
\end{remark}

The use of two estimates is structural.  The sharp arithmetic asymptotic
identifies the limit for each orbit.  The crude positive estimate justifies
the all-orbit exchange.  Trying to make the two-logarithm constant uniform
over all multiplier fields would solve a stronger and unnecessary problem.
