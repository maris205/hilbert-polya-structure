# Route-B scope

Route B is not authorized.  P53 produces a scalar holomorphic amplitude and
a probability boundary law.  It does not specify a Hilbert space, operator
action, dense domain, boundary conditions, self-adjointness theorem, compact
resolvent, completed-xi determinant or von Mangoldt trace identity.

The Mahler-height pressure series is an arithmetic thermodynamic observable,
not an operator spectrum.  B1--B5 are therefore `NOT_TESTABLE`, and
`hilbert_polya_claim_allowed` is false.
