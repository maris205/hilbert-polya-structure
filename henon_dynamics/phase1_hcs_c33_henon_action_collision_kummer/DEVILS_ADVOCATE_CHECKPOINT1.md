## Major issues

### 1. The normalization is old

- **Type:** novelty / scope.
- **Problem:** \(W_5(A,c)=0\) has the same function field as the old
  Endler--Gallas/C12B period-five marker cover.  Calling the action cover or
  its ordinary Galois group new would be false.
- **Impact:** a paper framed as a new period-five cover would repeat prior
  work.
- **Binding correction:** claim novelty, if it survives search, only for the
  singular action embedding, the degree-nine equal-action node locus, and its
  Hill Kummer decoration.

### 2. A projected self-intersection may be generic

- **Type:** alternative explanation.
- **Problem:** a primitive observable on a six-sheet cover can generically
  create plane self-intersections.  The existence of \(P_9\) alone does not
  prove exceptional Hénon or arithmetic structure.
- **Impact:** the bare node divisor may have limited conceptual novelty.
- **Binding correction:** center the theorem on the exact coupling to the
  intrinsic Hill determinant and state the general projection mechanism as
  classical/background rather than new.

### 3. Gauge dependence must be eliminated

- **Type:** invariant definition.
- **Problem:** generating actions admit additive constants and coboundary
  changes.  A coordinate polynomial in \(c\) is not intrinsically normalized.
- **Impact:** an unqualified discriminant could be an artifact of the chosen
  action coordinate.
- **Binding correction:** prove invariance of the equal-action relation under
  additive parameter functions, cyclic coboundaries, and common nonzero
  rescaling.  State explicit coefficients only in the frozen normalization.

### 4. The Kummer class must descend without choosing a branch

- **Type:** logical/algebraic validity.
- **Problem:** \(h_1/h_2\) is inverted by branch exchange.
- **Impact:** a branch-dependent square class would not define a canonical
  object on the collision locus.
- **Binding correction:** use
  \([h_1/h_2]=[h_1h_2]\), since their quotient is \(h_2^{-2}\).  The symmetric
  norm \(N_H=h_1h_2\) is the primary invariant.

### 5. The C32 prime is post-hoc

- **Type:** confirmation bias.
- **Problem:** \(p=61\) was discovered before C33 was frozen.
- **Impact:** presenting it as a prediction would overstate the evidence.
- **Binding correction:** label it a regression control.  Make the generic
  field-norm nonsquare theorem primary and include the nonsquare
  \(p=3203\) row as an adversarial matched control.
