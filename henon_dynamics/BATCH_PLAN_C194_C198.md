## Uniform paper contract

Each candidate releases exactly 28 physical files: 27 content-addressed
payloads plus one self-excluded manifest.  The payload consists of eight
theorem/writing documents, seven executable code/manifest files, one evaluator
YAML, seven paper artifacts including three content-distinct revision PDFs and
the final PDF, and four result/evidence artifacts.

Every package must pass a deterministic producer, producer-independent
checker, separate symbolic reconstruction, canonical byte replay, semantic
repaired-hash mutations and a stale-hash mutation.  Each paper receives two
substantive improvements.  Every final PDF must reproduce byte for byte in two
fresh fixed-epoch builds from `main.tex` alone, embed all fonts, have clean
logs and extractable text, and survive visual inspection of every page.
Internal hostile review is not external peer review.
