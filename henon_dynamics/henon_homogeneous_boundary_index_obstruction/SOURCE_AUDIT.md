1. Tate's adelic Fourier/Poisson framework and the Connes--Consani scaling
   architecture are imported.  A stable primary locator is A. Connes and
   C. Consani, *The Scaling Hamiltonian*, Journal of Operator Theory 85
   (2021), 259--278, DOI 10.7900/jot.2019oct30.2265,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14368>.
2. The compact-commutator/VMO implication is classical.  The primary
   locator used here is A. Uchiyama, “On the compactness of operators of
   Hankel type,” Tohoku Mathematical Journal 30 (1978), 163--171,
   DOI 10.2748/tmj/1178230105.
   Some real-line sources call the relevant closure class CMO.  The exact
   shrinking-interval witness violates the shared local vanishing
   condition, so the compactness conclusion does not depend on notation.
3. The restricted-unitary/determinant-line language is standard
   Grassmannian/Toeplitz theory; no novelty is claimed for it.
4. The gamma and trigonometric identities in the Mellin shadow are standard.
