# Research question

For the homogeneous area-preserving Hénon map

\[
H_0(q,p)=(-6q^2-p,q),\qquad P_0(q)=2q^3,
\]

does the scalar chirp define a nontrivial scaling-site/Poisson-boundary
index, or is every canonical lift a removable coboundary?

The target is not another zero computation.  It is the following exact
fork.

1. Construct a nonzero, gauge-invariant index whose determinant is intrinsic
   to the same scaling clock \(\ell(C_p)=\log p\); or
2. prove that the functorial scaling lift is equivariantly trivial and that
   the standard Hardy-polarized anomaly is unavailable.

The second alternative is what survives.  A deliberately nonfunctorial
Poisson quotient is not ruled out, but it must supply its own bounded
operator, kernel calculation, and index theorem.  None is inherited from
C35 or C36.
