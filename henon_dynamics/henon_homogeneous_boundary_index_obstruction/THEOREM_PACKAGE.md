## Theorem 1. Simultaneous equivariant trivialization

Let \(\phi(x)=\psi(2x^3)\) and

\[
c(a,x)=\phi(ax)\phi(x)^{-1}.
\]

The twisted \(\mathbb Q^\times\)-descent and the full idele-scaling lift on
the associated line bundle are simultaneously conjugate to the untwisted
actions by

\[
T(x,v)=(x,\phi(x)^{-1}v).
\]

Hence the transformation-groupoid \(H^1\)-class and equivariant line-bundle
class are zero.  Every functorial descendant has trivial Hénon holonomy.

## Corollary 1. Prime repetitions carry unit Hénon weight

For every prime \(p\) and \(r\ge1\), the scalar Hénon cocycle around the
length-\(r\log p\) scaling loop has holonomy one.  Thus the homogeneous
scalar decoration does not change the inherited scaling Euler factor.

## Theorem 2. The intrinsic pre-Poisson boundary-pair index is zero

Let

\[
K_0=\ker(ev_0)\cap\ker\Lambda_0,
\qquad
K_a=\ker(ev_0)\cap\ker\Lambda_{-P_a},
\]

with \(a>0\).  The two boundary functionals are independent and, for
\(W=K_0\cap K_a\),

\[
\dim(K_0/W)=\dim(K_a/W)=1.
\]

On any Hilbert completion where the functionals have nonzero Riesz vectors,
the difference of the two hyperplane projections is trace class and has
trace zero. Equivalently the compression from one hyperplane to the other
has Fredholm index zero. Multiplication by the chirp
does not preserve \(K_0\), so it does not induce an intrinsic boundary-
quotient automorphism without an additional splitting.

No claim is made that the image-pair index remains zero after a noninjective
Poisson map.

Indeed this limitation is sharp: take
\(V=K\oplus\mathbb Ce_1\oplus\mathbb Ce_2\),
\(M=K\oplus\mathbb Ce_1\), \(N=K\oplus\mathbb Ce_2\), and let \(E\)
be the identity on \(K\), with \(E(e_1)=0\) and
\(E(e_2)=f\perp K\).  The source essential codimension is zero, but
\(E(M)=K\) and \(E(N)=K\oplus\mathbb Cf\), so the image essential
codimension is \(-1\).  A post-Poisson conclusion therefore requires an
explicit kernel/equal-collapse theorem.

## Theorem 3. Standard Hardy determinant-line obstruction

For \(b(x)=e^{4\pi i x^3}\), there are intervals \(I_n\) with
\(|I_n|\to0\) for which

\[
\frac1{|I_n|}\int_{I_n}|b-b_{I_n}|^2\,dx>\frac{51}{100}.
\]

Thus \(b\notin\mathrm{VMO}\).  Consequently the commutator with the
standard Hardy projection is noncompact and \(M_b\) is not in the
restricted unitary group.  The standard Hardy/Grassmannian determinant-line
index is unavailable.

## Theorem 4. Strip-safe Mellin shadow is gauge-trivial

The exact homogeneous channels are

\[
\kappa_\pm^{(0)}(z)=
\frac13(4\pi)^{-z/3}\Gamma(z/3)e^{\pm i\pi z/6}.
\]

Their parity factors have no zero or pole in the open critical strip.  After
removing the fully derived homogeneous gamma--trigonometric kinematics, both
normalized channels equal one.  This is compatible with Theorem 1 and does
not define a new Fredholm determinant.
