\section{The homogeneous H\'enon lift}

Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:generating}
  S_0(q,Q)=qQ+2q^3.
\end{equation}
With \(p=-\partial_qS_0\) and \(P=\partial_QS_0\), this gives
\eqref{eq:H0}.  Moreover
\[
  D\Hp(q,p)=\begin{pmatrix}-12q&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad \det D\Hp=1.
\]
Thus the pivot remains an authentic area-preserving H\'enon system, rather
than a separate arithmetic model attached after the fact.

Let \(\psi:\A/\Q\to\C^\times\) be the standard additive character and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi}
  \phi(x)=\psi(\Pz(x)),\qquad \Pz(x)=2x^3.
\end{equation}
The function \(\phi\) is nonvanishing and unitary.  Multiplication by it
preserves the Schwartz--Bruhat space.  For \(a\in\A^\times\), define the
half-density dilation \(D_af(x)=|a|^{1/2}f(ax)\).  Conjugation gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cocycle}
  D_aM_\phi D_a^{-1}M_\phi^{-1}
  =M_{c(a,\cdot)},
  \qquad
  c(a,x)=\frac{\phi(ax)}{\phi(x)}
         =\psi\!\left(2(a^3-1)x^3\right).
\end{equation}

We distinguish three levels throughout:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item the transformation groupoid before Poisson summation;
  \item static boundary hyperplanes in a chosen completion;
  \item images or compressions under a possibly noninjective Poisson map.
\end{enumerate}
Only constructions that preserve equivariant isomorphisms are called
\emph{functorial descendants}.  This convention prevents a choice of
noninvariant compression from being mistaken for a cocycle invariant.

For the boundary calculation, write
\[
  \Lambda_Q(f)=\int_{\A}\psi(Q(x))f(x)\,dx,
  \qquad V=\ker(ev_0).
\]
The distributional identities hold on \(\Sc(\A)\).  Whenever orthogonal
projections are used, we explicitly assume a Hilbert completion on which
the relevant functionals are continuous and nonzero.  Evaluation and
integration are not asserted to be bounded on raw \(L^2\).
