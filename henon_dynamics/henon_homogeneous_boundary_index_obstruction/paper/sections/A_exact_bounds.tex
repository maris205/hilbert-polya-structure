\section{Exact oscillation bounds}\label{app:bounds}

For completeness, the uniform error in \eqref{eq:phase-expansion} is
maximal at \(n=2\), \(y=1\).  Its coefficient of \(\pi\) is
\[
  \frac1{96}+\frac1{27648}=\frac{289}{27648}.
\]
Consequently
\[
  \left|\frac{\pi}{12n^3}y^2+
  \frac{\pi}{432n^6}y^3\right|
  \le \frac{22}{7}\frac{289}{27648}<\frac1{30}.
\]
The ideal half-turn average has modulus
\[
  \left|\int_0^1e^{i\pi y}\,dy\right|=\frac2\pi<\frac23.
\]
The triangle inequality therefore gives the rational bound \(7/10\), and
the exact mean-square identity for a unit-modulus function gives
\[
  1-\left(\frac7{10}\right)^2=\frac{51}{100}.
\]
The certificate records every rational numerator and denominator and the
independent checker reconstructs them rather than trusting the verdict.
