# Assumptions and conventions

- (p,q) are odd coprime integers satisfying (3\le p<q). Pairwise
  coprimality of ((2,p,q)) is used in every connectedness and minimal-period
  statement.
- The link is cut out inside the unit sphere. The contact form is exactly
  (alpha=(i/(4\pi))\sum a_j(z_jd\bar z_j-\bar z_jdz_j)), not an unspecified
  positive multiple.
- A point fixed at time (T) means a phase-space point fixed by the Reeb flow,
  not merely a closed unparametrized image.
- The exceptional-orbit CZ formula uses the ambient missing-coordinate
  complex-line trivialization and is stated only before the first degenerate
  cover.
- The principal RS index uses the standard Milnor-fiber capping
  trivialization. It is not numerically identified with an index in an
  undeclared framing.
- Empty fixed sets use dimension (-1) only as a serialization convention.
- The evidence grid has (q\le101); the analytic theorem has no such cutoff.
- Standard Brieskorn contact and Seifert facts are source-owned; no literature
  priority claim is made.
