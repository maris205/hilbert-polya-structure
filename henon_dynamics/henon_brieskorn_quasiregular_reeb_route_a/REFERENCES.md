# References

1. Myeonggi Kwon and Otto van Koert, “Brieskorn manifolds in contact
   topology,” *Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society* 48 (2016),
   173–241. DOI: [10.1112/blms/bdv088](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/bdv088).
2. Otto van Koert, “Contact homology of Brieskorn manifolds,” *Forum
   Mathematicum* 20 (2008), 317–339. DOI:
   [10.1515/FORUM.2008.016](https://doi.org/10.1515/FORUM.2008.016).

The first is the principal survey source for weighted Brieskorn contact
geometry. The second records the periodic orbit-stratum and index framework.
C370 uses these sources for ownership and conventions, reconstructs its
specialized formulas explicitly, and does not perform the contact-homology
calculation suggested by the second title.
