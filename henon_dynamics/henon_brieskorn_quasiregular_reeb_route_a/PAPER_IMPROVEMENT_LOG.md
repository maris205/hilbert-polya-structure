# Paper improvement log

## Round 0: normalization and primitive periods

The first manuscript freezes the sphere radius, (1/(4\pi)) contact-form
factor, exact Reeb vector field, and the four support/period types. It proves
the two-coordinate loci are connected circles rather than silently assuming
one orbit per support.

## Round 1: fixed strata and transverse indices

The second manuscript adds the all-real-time fixed-set formula and a direct
Morse--Bott kernel proof. It declares the ambient missing-coordinate
trivialization before stating rotations, determinants, degeneracy orders, or
CZ indices. Degenerate covers are excluded from the nondegenerate CZ formula.

## Round 2: quotient, principal index, and scope

The final manuscript adds the Seifert quotient, orbifold Euler characteristic,
and principal Robbin--Salamon sign theorem. The RS normalization is derived
from the ambient diagonal winding minus the defining-polynomial normal and is
tied to primary literature; it is not inferred from the finite table. The
finite receipt, collision audit, Route-A tuple, continuum obstruction,
contact-homology limitation, and forbidden-claim firewall are explicit.

Each round is a distinct conditional PDF. The release gate compiles every
round twice in fresh directories at the frozen epoch and requires identical
bytes, settled warning-free logs, embedded/subset fonts, clean extracted text,
and complete rasterization.
