# Source and collision audit

## Primary lineage

1. Kwon and van Koert, *Brieskorn manifolds in contact topology*, BLMS 48
   (2016), DOI `10.1112/blms/bdv088`: standard weighted contact form, periodic
   Reeb-flow, orbit strata, and contact-topology context.  Section 5.3,
   formula (14), and Proposition 5.9 supply the precise principal-index
   convention: the complex normal block has one winding and one stationary
   radial/Reeb summand, while the Milnor-fiber trivialization fixes the
   capping convention.
2. van Koert, *Contact homology of Brieskorn manifolds*, Forum Math. 20
   (2008), DOI `10.1515/FORUM.2008.016`: Brieskorn orbit families and index
   conventions. The present package uses only the elementary orbit/index
   layer and performs no contact-homology calculation.

The exact theorem package is reconstructed under its displayed normalization;
workspace novelty is not represented as literature priority.

## Workspace ownership

- C242 owns irrational ellipsoid Reeb dynamics with two isolated coordinate
  orbits. C370 instead has a weighted-homogeneous link, three exceptional
  fibers, a principal Morse--Bott family, and an exact Seifert quotient.
- C313 owns clean round-sphere geodesic and Laplacian dynamics. C370 is a
  contact Reeb circle action on a singularity link, not a geodesic flow.
- C339 owns Katok--Zermelo Finsler closed-geodesic dynamics. It has neither the
  weighted link nor the three Seifert isotropy orders.
- C349 owns the Neumann integrable system on a sphere. Its invariant tori and
  Lax/Uhlenbeck mechanism are distinct from a quasiregular contact action.

The full registry search found Brieskorn only as an explicitly deferred clue
in the C359--C363 idea report; no earlier candidate owns this theorem.

## Claim firewall

The source exponents are intrinsic integers, but they are not rational-prime
labels and Reeb time is not a logarithmic-prime clock. The principal orbits
have a two-dimensional continuum quotient, so no ordinary discrete primitive
product is available. The contact linearization is only a formal A4 hint.

No target arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy,
target divisor or functional equation, target-zero matching, Hilbert--Pólya
operator, Route B invocation, or contact-homology extrapolation occurs.
