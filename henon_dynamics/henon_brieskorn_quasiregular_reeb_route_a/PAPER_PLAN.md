# Paper plan

## Title

Quasiregular Reeb Dynamics on Pairwise-Coprime Brieskorn Three-Manifolds

## Narrative

The weighted homogeneous equation supplies an unusually transparent periodic
contact flow. The paper first fixes the normalization rather than quoting
periods up to scale. It then proves a complete support/divisibility atlas for
fixed points. The same support decomposition exposes the missing-coordinate
contact line, giving exact rotations, return determinants, degeneracy orders,
and CZ indices. Finally the Seifert quotient turns the principal
Robbin--Salamon index into twice the common period times the orbifold Euler
characteristic, yielding a sharp sign theorem.

## Sections

1. Frozen link, contact form, and normalized Reeb field.
2. Primitive orbit types and the complete fixed-time Morse--Bott atlas.
3. Transverse rotations, determinants, and nondegenerate CZ indices.
4. Seifert quotient and an explicit derivation of the principal RS index.
5. Exact finite receipt, limitations, collision firewall, and Route-A result.

## Required boundaries

- exceptional circles versus the continuum of principal fibers;
- ambient missing-coordinate versus Milnor-fiber capping trivializations;
- nondegenerate exceptional iterates versus the degenerate principal return;
- finite verification (q\le101) versus the all-parameter proof;
- weak intrinsic weight arithmetic versus forbidden target arithmetic claims;
- fixed-stratum and index results versus an unperformed contact-homology
  computation.
