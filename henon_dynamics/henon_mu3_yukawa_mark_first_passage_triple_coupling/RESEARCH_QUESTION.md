# C101 research question

For each unordered triple of the twenty frozen C88 first-passage targets,
what is the exact joint law of the three stopping times on uniform
permutations of sixteen labels?  Can its 17-by-17-by-17 PMF, all pair and
single marginal recoveries, and exact third-order interaction statistics be
certified from the frozen C88 and C90 finite receipts?  This is a finite
combinatorial question under `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
