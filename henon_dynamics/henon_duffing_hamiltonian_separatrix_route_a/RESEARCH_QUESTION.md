# Research question — C232

Can one close the full phase topology and action/period boundary of the
conservative Duffing family, including the double-well separatrix, while
keeping source Hamiltonian recurrence distinct from an arithmetic primitive
orbit construction?

Answer: yes for the stated `beta>0` family. The theorem gives the complete
energy-component atlas and exact quadratures; the Route-A arithmetic gate
remains failed because the recurrent family is continuously energy-indexed.
