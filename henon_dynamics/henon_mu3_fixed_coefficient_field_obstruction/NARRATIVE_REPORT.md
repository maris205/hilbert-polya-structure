# HCS-C44 narrative report

## What was attempted

HCS-C43 left a serious arithmetic possibility: pairing the additive character
with its inverse repairs the most immediate failure of complex conjugation.
The natural next question was whether these paired chronological moments form
the local traces of one pure self-dual compatible system over a fixed number
field.

## What was discovered

The first moment already decides the question.  Its Galois stabilizer can be
read from a paired finite-field value histogram.  Two exact power moments force
that stabilizer to be precisely \(\{\pm1\}\) for every split prime.  The trace
field is therefore the entire maximal real cyclotomic field, of degree
\((p-1)/2\).

This is stronger than a growing finite ledger: it is a uniform theorem on all
primes congruent to one modulo three.

## Scientific decision

The fixed-coefficient-field compatible-system bridge is closed.  Continuing to
Hankel-rank calculations would be local detail after a more fundamental
necessary condition has failed.

Galois trace and norm are the two minimal invariant descents not ruled out;
they are not an exhaustive classification of possible descents.  The trace
collapses to \(-6\); C45 therefore studies the norm of the full local
determinant and its divisor growth.
