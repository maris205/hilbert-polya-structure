## Independent theorem increments

- **C359:** a complete Pais--Uhlenbeck distinct-frequency, resonance,
  degeneration, and natural-quantization atlas.  For two unequal positive
  frequencies, an explicit real Ostrogradsky canonical transformation gives
  one positive and one negative oscillator.  The modal support determines all
  periods: every two-mode orbit is periodic exactly at rational frequency
  ratio, while an irrational two-mode orbit is dense in its invariant
  two-torus.  Equal positive frequencies give one size-two Jordan block at
  each of \(\pm i\omega\) and generic secular growth; zero and negative
  squared frequencies have their full all-time bounded and periodic
  subspaces stated separately.  With \(\hbar=1\), the oscillator-difference
  operator is self-adjoint on its maximal diagonal domain.  Rational ratios
  have infinite spectral multiplicities; irrational ratios have simple dense
  eigenvalues, pure-point spectral measures, and spectrum \(\mathbb R\).
  The energy and spectrum are unbounded in both directions, and no
  Hilbert--Polya interpretation is made.
- **C360:** a complete Berger-\(SU(2)\) Ricci-flow cone and singularity atlas.
  A frozen Maurer--Cartan convention yields
  \(A'=-8+4C/A\), \(C'=-4C^2/A^2\), the ratio equation
  \(r'=8r(1-r)/A\), and the two-chamber first integral
  \(Ar/\sqrt{|1-r|}\).  Elementary `atanh`/`atan` formulas give maximal
  lifespans and separate all ancient and finite-backward branches.  Forward
  extinction is genuinely Type I, with
  \((T-t)R\to3/2\) and \((T-t)K_{13}\to1/4\); the horizontal sectional
  curvature wall \(r=2\) is retained.  The volume-normalized flow is forward
  complete and converges exponentially to the round metric.
- **C361:** a finite-state Markov entropy theorem from stationarity through
  fluctuations.  Matrix-tree cofactors construct the stationary law;
  current--affinity decomposition proves nonnegative entropy production, with
  equality exactly at detailed balance and equivalently at vanishing cycle
  affinities.  Stationary total entropy is the correctly oriented
  forward/reversed path Radon--Nikodym logarithm and obeys the finite-time
  detailed fluctuation relation.  For the medium tilt,
  \(L_\lambda^{\mathsf T}=L_{1-\lambda}\), so the entire characteristic
  polynomial and Perron SCGF have Gallavotti--Cohen symmetry.  The rate
  antisymmetry is asserted only when the stated full LDP and
  Legendre--Fenchel identification hold.  Reducible and one-way support are
  explicit non-theorem boundaries.
- **C362:** the complete Cucker--Smale communication-tail threshold at every
  particle number and dimension, together with a sharp two-agent converse.
  Global flow, mean-velocity conservation, exact variance dissipation, and a
  diameter comparison yield a Lyapunov barrier, explicit confinement radius,
  and exponential alignment rate.  Flocking is unconditional throughout
  \(0\le\beta\le1/2\), including the endpoint.  For \(\beta>1/2\), the
  one-dimensional two-agent reduction gives the exact subthreshold,
  equality, and superthreshold trichotomy; at equality relative velocity
  tends to zero while separation still diverges, and above threshold the
  limiting relative speed is positive.
- **C363:** a planar Keller--Segel critical-mass theorem with matching energy
  and virial thresholds.  Under explicit classical regularity and
  integrability hypotheses, mass and barycenter are conserved, free energy
  dissipates, and mass-preserving dilation changes it by
  \(2M(1-M/(8\pi))\log\lambda\).  Finite second moment satisfies
  \(I'=4M(1-M/(8\pi))\), giving the supercritical classical-persistence bound
  \(T_* = 2\pi I(0)/(M(M-8\pi))\).  The translated and scaled critical
  stationary family has mass \(8\pi\) but logarithmically divergent second
  moment, preventing an invalid merger with the virial theorem.  The exact
  radial cumulative-mass equation and its regular origin boundary are also
  derived.
