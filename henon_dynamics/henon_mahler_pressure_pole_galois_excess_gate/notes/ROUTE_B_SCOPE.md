# Route-B scope

Route B is not authorized for HCS-P54.  The project has a rigorous dynamical
zeta only for the physical suspension subsystem and a scalar arithmetic
amplitude for the full Mahler coefficient.  It does not define:

- a Hilbert space and dense operator domain;
- a self-adjoint or even closed candidate operator;
- boundary conditions or a canonical quantization;
- a target spectral type or compact-resolvent theorem;
- a von Mangoldt-weighted exact trace formula;
- a zeta-regularized determinant equal to the completed Riemann function.

Accordingly B1--B5 are `NOT_TESTABLE`, every Route-B gate remains open, and
`hilbert_polya_claim_allowed` is false.  The physical suspension zeta is not
an operator spectrum, and the conditional Hölder completion would still be a
weighted dynamical-zeta theorem rather than a Route-B realization.
