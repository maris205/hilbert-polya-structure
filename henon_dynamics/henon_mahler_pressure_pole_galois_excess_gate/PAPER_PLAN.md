# Paper plan

## Working title

**A Physical Pressure Pole and the Galois-Excess Gate for Hénon Mahler
Heights**

## One-sentence contribution

Decompose the all-orbit H6 Mahler amplitude into a source-backed physical
pressure pole and a positive arithmetic excess, prove the simplest
cohomological completion impossible, and state the exact Hölder theorem that
would close the full critical boundary.

## Sections

1. Introduction and relation to HCS-P53.
2. Frozen H6 suspension and Mahler height.
3. Physical/Galois decomposition.
4. Suspension-zeta logarithmic derivative and pressure residue.
5. Exact Galois witnesses and scalar-roof obstruction.
6. Excess abscissa trichotomy.
7. Conditional two-parameter Hölder completion.
8. Finite certificate, Route-A assessment and limitations.
9. Appendix: repetition-tail analyticity and exact trace-root formulas.

## Claim firewall

The manuscript must use `CONDITIONAL_THEOREM` every time the full Galois
excess is represented by a Hölder observable.  Only the physical summand has
an unconditional pressure-pole theorem.
