\section{Exact Galois witnesses and a cohomological no-go}

For a real reciprocal pair with trace \(t\), \(|t|>2\), the outside root
has logarithmic modulus
\begin{equation}
L(t)=\operatorname{arcosh}(|t|/2).
\label{eq:pair-log}
\end{equation}
The exact inherited trace polynomials yield Table~\ref{tab:excess}.

Numerically, the triples
\((\ell_\gamma,\Hspec_\gamma,\Egal_\gamma)\) are
\begin{align*}
m=1:&\quad(1.9673466291,\ 3.0501161905,\ 1.0827695614),\\
m=3:&\quad(4.8820992058,\ 8.9056092911,\ 4.0235100852),\\
m=4:&\quad(6.3595708754,\ 6.3595708754,\ 0).
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}[No scalar pressure retuning]
There do not exist a constant \(c\) and a function \(u\) on the symbolic
survivor such that every primitive cycle satisfies
\begin{equation}
\Hspec_\gamma
=c\ell_\gamma+S_{m(\gamma)}(u-u\circ\sigma).
\label{eq:scalar-coboundary}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Periodic sums of a coboundary vanish.  The period-four orbit has
\(\Hspec_4=\ell_4>0\), so \eqref{eq:scalar-coboundary} forces \(c=1\).
The period-one orbit then contradicts \(\Egal_1>0\).
\end{proof}

Thus the full arithmetic height cannot be obtained by merely retuning the
pressure parameter.  This theorem does not exclude a new, non-proportional
H\"older potential.
