\section{Mahler height and the Galois excess}

Let \(f_\gamma\in\mathbb Z[X]\) be the monic minimal polynomial of the
signed unstable multiplier \(\lambda_\gamma\).  Its conjugates occur in
reciprocal pairs.  From each pair choose \(\rho\) with \(|\rho|\ge1\).
The P53 embedding formula is
\begin{equation}
\Hspec_\gamma=\log M(f_\gamma)
=\sum_{\rho}\log|\rho|.
\label{eq:height-embedding}
\end{equation}
The physical real pair contributes \(\log\Lambda_\gamma=\ell_\gamma\).

\begin{definition}
The \emph{Galois excess} of \(\gamma\) is
\begin{equation}
\Egal_\gamma
=\sum_{\substack{\rho\ \mathrm{nonphysical}\\|\rho|\ge1}}
\log|\rho|
=\Hspec_\gamma-\ell_\gamma.
\label{eq:excess}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Canonical splitting]
For every primitive orbit,
\begin{equation}
\Hspec_\gamma=\ell_\gamma+\Egal_\gamma,
\qquad \Egal_\gamma\ge0.
\label{eq:split}
\end{equation}
Consequently, wherever \eqref{eq:total-amplitude} converges absolutely,
\begin{equation}
\Aamp=\Aamp_{\rm phys}+\Aamp_{\rm Gal},
\label{eq:amp-split}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{align}
\Aamp_{\rm phys}(s)
&=\frac3{\pi^2}\sum_\gamma
\ell_\gamma e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma},
\label{eq:physical}\\
\Aamp_{\rm Gal}(s)
&=\frac3{\pi^2}\sum_\gamma
\Egal_\gamma e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma}.
\label{eq:galois}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every term in \eqref{eq:height-embedding} is nonnegative.  Isolating the
physical reciprocal pair proves \eqref{eq:split}; substitution proves
\eqref{eq:amp-split}.
\end{proof}

This splitting is intrinsic.  It does not choose a fitted observable or
change the pressure clock.  It also shows why replacing
\(\Hspec_\gamma\) by \(\ell_\gamma\) without an error ledger would discard
arithmetic information.
