\section{The frozen pressure-normalized survivor}

Let \(\Sigma_A\) be the four-state mixing subshift conjugate to the certified
locally maximal H6 survivor.  On \(\Sigma_A\), the adapted unstable Jacobian
has a positive H\"older logarithm \(\tau\), unique at the periodic-sum level
up to a H\"older coboundary.  Its pressure root is defined intrinsically by
\begin{equation}
P_{\Sigma_A}(-h_*\tau)=0,
\qquad
0.277980<h_*<0.277987.
\label{eq:pressure-root}
\end{equation}
Set \(\widehat\tau=h_*\tau\).  The suspension over \(\Sigma_A\) with roof
\(\widehat\tau\) has entropy one.  The inherited exact multiplier comparison
proves that the roof is non-lattice, hence the suspension is weak mixing.

For a primitive orbit \(\gamma\) of symbolic period \(m(\gamma)\), choose a
point \(x_\gamma\) on the cycle and write
\begin{equation}
\ell_\gamma=S_{m(\gamma)}\tau(x_\gamma)
=\log\Lambda_\gamma,
\qquad
\widehat\ell_\gamma=h_*\ell_\gamma.
\label{eq:lengths}
\end{equation}
All objects in \eqref{eq:pressure-root}--\eqref{eq:lengths} are fixed before
the arithmetic Mahler coefficient is examined.

The normalized suspension zeta is
\begin{equation}
\zeta_{\widehat\tau}(s)
=\prod_{\gamma\ \mathrm{primitive}}
\left(1-e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma}\right)^{-1}.
\label{eq:zeta}
\end{equation}
The prime orbit theorem gives
\begin{equation}
\#\{\gamma:\widehat\ell_\gamma\le T\}\sim e^T/T
\qquad(T\to\infty)
\label{eq:pot}
\end{equation}
by \citet[Theorem~6.9]{ParryPollicott1990}.  This is a theorem about
dynamical primes.  No rational-prime identification is used in the present
paper.
