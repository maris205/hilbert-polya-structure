\section{Conditional H\"older completion}

We now state the precise theorem that would complete the pressure-critical
bridge.  Assume that there exists one real H\"older function
\(\psi:\Sigma_A\to\mathbb R\), fixed independently of the orbit, such that
\begin{equation}
\Egal_\gamma=S_{m(\gamma)}\psi(x_\gamma)
\quad\text{for every primitive }\gamma.
\label{eq:holder-hypothesis}
\end{equation}
This is not obtained by finite interpolation.

Consider the two-parameter zeta
\begin{equation}
\zeta(s,z)=\prod_\gamma
\left(1-e^{-s\widehat\ell_\gamma+z\Egal_\gamma}\right)^{-1}.
\label{eq:two-param}
\end{equation}
With \(f=\widehat\tau\), \(g=0\), \(c=1\), and \(k=\psi\),
\citet[Theorem~6.4 and Corollary~6.4.1]{ParryPollicott1990} gives
\begin{equation}
\left.\partial_z\log\zeta(s,z)\right|_{z=0}
=\frac{\int\psi\dd\mu}{\int\widehat\tau\dd\mu}
\frac1{s-1}+G_\psi(s),
\label{eq:weighted-principal}
\end{equation}
where \(\mu\) is the equilibrium state of \(-\widehat\tau\) and
\(G_\psi\) is holomorphic near one.

Here the source convention is \(P(g-cf)=0\) and
\[
 \mathfrak Z(s,z)=
 \exp\!\left(
 \sum_{n\ge1}\frac1n\sum_{\sigma^nx=x}
 e^{S_ng-csS_nf+zS_nk}
 \right).
\]
Thus the above choices give exactly \eqref{eq:two-param}; in particular the
source denominator \(c\int f\,\mathrm d\mu\) becomes
\(\int\widehat\tau\,\mathrm d\mu\).  We use only the resulting local
meromorphic germ at \((s,z)=(1,0)\).

\begin{theorem}[H\"older-excess completion---conditional]
Under \eqref{eq:holder-hypothesis}, the full Mahler amplitude has a
meromorphic simple-pole germ at \(s=1\), with
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\operatorname*{Res}_{s=1}\Aamp(s)
=\frac3{\pi^2}
\frac{\int(\tau+\psi)\dd\mu}
{\int h_*\tau\dd\mu}}.
\label{eq:full-residue}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Differentiating the Euler factors in \eqref{eq:two-param} shows that the left
side of \eqref{eq:weighted-principal} equals
\(\sum_{\gamma,k\ge1}\Egal_\gamma
e^{-sk\widehat\ell_\gamma}\).  If
\(a=\min\widehat\tau>0\), then
\(m(\gamma)\le\widehat\ell_\gamma/a\), and hence
\(|\Egal_\gamma|\le
\|\psi\|_\infty\widehat\ell_\gamma/a\).
The \(k\ge2\) weighted tail is therefore absolutely and normally dominated
near one by the repetition tail of Lemma~4.1.  The primitive excess series
has the same residue.  Adding the physical residue from Theorem~4.2 gives
\eqref{eq:full-residue}.

For real \(s>1\), the differentiated excess Euler product has nonnegative
coefficients because every \(\Egal_\gamma\ge0\).  Multiplying
\eqref{eq:weighted-principal} by \(s-1\) and letting \(s\downarrow1\) shows
\(\int\psi\,\mathrm d\mu/\int\widehat\tau\,\mathrm d\mu\ge0\).
The physical residue is strictly positive, so the residue in
\eqref{eq:full-residue} is strictly positive and the full pole cannot cancel.
\end{proof}

Equation \eqref{eq:holder-hypothesis} remains open.  The minimal-polynomial
conjugates are global arithmetic data, whereas a H\"older potential is local
on the physical symbolic survivor.  An asymptotically additive replacement
could also be useful, but would require its own weighted-zeta theorem and
uniform error ledger.
