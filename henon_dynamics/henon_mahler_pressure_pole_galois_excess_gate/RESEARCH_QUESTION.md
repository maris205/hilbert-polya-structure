# Research question

HCS-P53 proved that the all-orbit packet boundary is governed by

\[
\mathcal A(s)=\frac3{\pi^2}\sum_{\gamma\ \mathrm{primitive}}
e^{-s h_*\log\Lambda_\gamma}\,\mathcal H_\gamma,
\qquad
\mathcal H_\gamma=\log M(f_\gamma),
\]

but only in a crude safe half-plane.  HCS-P54 asks the smallest
thermodynamic question that can move this object toward the normalized
pressure line \(\Re s=1\):

> Which part of \(\mathcal H_\gamma\) is already generated by the H6
> Hölder instability roof, what exact singularity does that part carry, and
> what additional theorem is necessary for the nonphysical Galois part?

The paper does not assume that the full Mahler height is a fitted symbolic
observable.  It first proves the canonical decomposition into physical and
Galois terms, then tests the simplest cohomological completion and states the
remaining Hölder realization as a conditional theorem.
