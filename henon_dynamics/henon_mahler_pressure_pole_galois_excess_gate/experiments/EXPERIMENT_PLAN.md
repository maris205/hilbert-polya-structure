# Experiment plan

The experiment is an exact finite audit, not a pole fit.

1. Lock P31, P45, P48 and P53 source artifacts by SHA-256.
2. Reconstruct the exact trace roots for periods 1, 3 and 4.
3. Compute physical length, full Mahler height and Galois excess independently.
4. Certify the pressure-pole residue interval from the rational bounds on
   \(h_*\).
5. Verify finite Euler log-derivative identities, including all repetitions.
6. Test the scalar-roof/coboundary contradiction.
7. Run an independent checker and adversarial claim mutations.

No experiment is permitted to fit a pressure pole, a rational-prime label, or
a Riemann zero.
