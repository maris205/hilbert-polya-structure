# Narrative report

P53 reached a pressure-weighted all-orbit Abel law but stopped in a safe
half-plane because the full multiplier Mahler height was bounded by a crude
algebraic-degree envelope.  P54 identifies why that bound mixes two different
objects.

Every Mahler height contains the ordinary physical instability length.  That
length is already a Hölder periodic sum on the certified H6 survivor.  After
pressure normalization, its primitive Dirichlet series is the first-
repetition part of the logarithmic derivative of the standard suspension
zeta.  The classical source theorem therefore supplies a genuine simple pole
at the entropy-one line, with residue \(3/(\pi^2h_*)\).

What remains is a nonnegative Galois excess produced by nonphysical conjugate
pairs outside the unit circle.  Exact periods one and three have positive
excess, while period four has none.  This immediately disproves the tempting
idea that Mahler height is just a constant rescaling of instability time up to
a coboundary.

The resulting road map is sharp.  If the excess has abscissa below one, the
physical pole is already the full pole.  If it lies on one, a weighted
thermodynamic theorem is needed.  If it lies above one, the defining positive
series stops before the physical pressure line.  Under an exact Hölder
periodic-sum hypothesis, Parry--Pollicott's two-parameter zeta theorem closes
the critical pole and gives its residue.  Establishing or refuting that
hypothesis is the next large problem.
