## Closest primary sources

### 1. Constant-derivative PCF families are classical

Alon Levy, *An Algebraic Proof of Thurston's Rigidity for Maps With a
Superattracting Cycle*, author preprint dated 23 September 2014.
Primary author PDF:
https://people.kth.se/~alonlevy/rigidity4.pdf

Read scope: abstract/introduction and the relevant constant-derivative
examples on PDF pp. 1–5, plus the beginning of Section 2 on pp. 6–7.
The example `z^(mp)+tz` and the more general constant-derivative rational
families show that a positive-dimensional non-isotrivial family with
constant multiplier data is not a new discovery. The read material does
not supply the complete ordinary-count ramification tower for PC-A.

### 2. Dynamically affine zeta classification is not a theorem for PC-A

Andrew Bridy, *The Artin–Mazur Zeta Function of a Dynamically Affine
Rational Map in Positive Characteristic*, Journal de théorie des nombres
de Bordeaux 28 (2016), no. 2, 301–324, DOI 10.5802/jtnb.941.
Primary journal record:
https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/jtnb.941/
Primary PDF:
https://www.numdam.org/article/JTNB_2016__28_2_301_0.pdf

Read scope in this scout: journal metadata/abstract and the PDF's
introduction/definition and displayed scope of the conjecture, as
returned by the primary-source search. It treats dynamically affine
maps. Its discussion distinguishes algebraic closures of finite fields
from transcendental coefficient fields. The generic-parameter examples
here are not solutions of its finite-field separable-map conjecture.
No full fresh reading of all 24 pages is claimed in this screen.

### 3. The abstract signature-constrained group is already owned

Irene I. Bouw, Özlem Ejder and Valentijn Karemaker, *Dynamical Belyi maps
and arboreal Galois groups*, Manuscripta Mathematica 165 (2021), no. 1–2,
1–34, DOI 10.1007/s00229-020-01204-3.
Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10086
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10086
Author publication list verifying the bibliographic record:
https://www.uni-ulm.de/mawi/algzt/mitarbeiter/bouw/forschung-research/

Read scope: the stated theorem and scope around Theorem 2.3.1 and
Lemma 2.3.2; the reduction definitions/Proposition 1.2.4; the relevant
source relationships in Ejder's sequel below. Their `E_n` and its order
are not new objects in this scout. Their dynamical realization concerns
normalized single-cycle Belyi maps with three branch points and their
number-field arithmetic/specialization results. The current cubic has
two geometric branch points and wild characteristic-three ramification.
The tame, three-branch-point result is not silently applied to it.

Özlem Ejder, *Arithmetic Monodromy Groups of Dynamical Belyi Maps*,
Contemporary Mathematics 779 (2022), 91–102,
DOI 10.1090/conm/779/15677.
Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09005
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.09005
Primary publisher contents (author verification):
https://bookstore.ams.org/CONM/779

Read scope: PDF pp. 1–6, in particular Definition 2.1, Definition 2.2,
and Theorems 4.1–4.2. These explicitly define the same local product-sign
group and identify its classical Belyi realization. The ambient field
in the geometric/arithmetic dynamical statements is a number field.
An aggregator returned an erroneous author attribution to David Cressy;
that attribution is not used. The primary PDF and AMS contents both
identify Özlem Ejder.

### 4. Recent unicritical classification excludes degree divisible by p

Ophelia Adams and Trevor Hyde, *Profinite Iterated Monodromy Groups of
Unicritical Polynomials*, arXiv:2504.13028 (17 April 2025).
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13028
Primary author PDF:
https://pages.vassar.edu/thyde/files/2025/04/Adams-Hyde-Profinite-iterated-monodromy-groups-of-unicritical-polynomials.pdf
Primary author publication list:
https://pages.vassar.edu/thyde/research/

Read scope: abstract, contents, and the related-work discussion exposed
by the author PDF. The stated family is `aX^d+b` with `d` coprime to
the characteristic. It does not include `X^3+aX^2` in characteristic
three. The author's current list marks it as to appear in Algebra &
Number Theory (2026); no volume/pages or final published theorem text
are invented here.

### 5. Recent cubic-PCF work does not assert the wild result

Mikhail Hlushchanka, Olga Lukina and Dean Wardell, *Profinite geometric
iterated monodromy groups of postcritically finite polynomials in
degree 3*, arXiv:2507.05033 (7 July 2025).
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05033
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.05033

Read scope: abstract and PDF introduction through Theorem 1.5 and the
following characteristic-scope remark on pp. 1–5. The paper works with
cubic polynomials over number fields with two distinct finite critical
points and the stated portrait condition. Its remark anticipates an
extension to characteristics coprime to two and three. It does not
assert a characteristic-three theorem for the present map.

### 6. Correspondence framework is classical, not itself an increment

The primary journal result *A graph aided strategy to produce good
recursive towers over finite fields*, DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2016.07.008,
was inspected through its displayed introduction and definitions:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2016.07.008

It supplies the established correspondence/recursive-tower/graph
framework; the equal-bidegree setting displayed there is not the same
as the unequal-degree PC-C. No claim of an exact theorem collision is
made. PC-C is rejected because its proved increment is only the short
degree/transversality lemma, independently of an exact-title match.

## Search scope and limitations

### Additional PC-E ownership sources

Karl-Olof Lindahl and Juan Rivera-Letelier, *Optimal cycles in
ultrametric dynamics and minimally ramified power series*, Compositio
Mathematica 152 (2016), no. 1, 187–222,
DOI 10.1112/S0010437X15007575.
Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.4478
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.4478

Read scope: minimal-ramification definition in the introduction/PDF
p. 5; the explicit `z+z^2` statement on PDF p. 6; the iterative-residue
definition and Theorem E on PDF p. 19; the related displayed definition
and criterion returned by the primary PDF search. This already owns
the complete ramification sequence used in PC-E. Theorem E even points
to Rivera-Letelier's earlier 2003 example for the odd-characteristic
multiplier-one case. No full reading of the 37-page preprint is claimed.

Mikhail Ershov, *On subgroups of the Nottingham group of positive
Hausdorff dimension*, Communications in Algebra 35 (2007), no. 1,
193–206, DOI 10.1080/00927870601041532.
Primary author PDF: https://m-ershov.github.io/Research/dimzero.pdf
Author bibliography: https://m-ershov.github.io/Research/

Read scope: introduction, filtration/dimension definitions and
Section 3, especially the statement that all abelian pro-`p` groups
are Nottingham-small, Theorems 3.1–3.2, and the stated nilpotent
consequence (PDF pp. 1–5). The paper proves the stronger upper-box
zero statement; the simple orbit-evaluation consequence used to reject
PC-E must not be claimed as a new dimension theorem.

### First-level owner for the bounded rational-map alternative

John Conway, John McKay and Allan Trojan, *Galois groups over function
fields of positive characteristic*, Proceedings of the American
Mathematical Society 138 (2010), no. 4, 1205–1212,
DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-09-10130-2.
Primary institutional copy of the journal PDF:
https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/977522/1/S0002-9939-09-10130-2.pdf

Read scope: first-page bibliographic data and introduction, recalling
Serre's 1988 `X^(q+1)-tX+1` projective-special-linear example and its
appearance in Abhyankar's appendix. The read scope does not prove an
all-height theorem for iteration of `X^p+1/X`; it does settle ownership
of the first-level observation in the bounded alternative screen.

### Overall limitations

Searches included combinations of positive characteristic, constant
derivative, ordinary periodic counts, nonlinear skew products,
Artin–Schreier correspondences, wild iterated monodromy, the exact
polynomials `X^p+aX^2`/`X^3+X^2`, cubic characteristic three, signature
constraints, and dynamical Belyi groups. Several broad results used
“wild” for a Cantor-action invariant rather than wild field ramification;
those were not conflated with the present question. Secondary snippets
were used only to locate the primary records above.

The limited search has not located a direct all-height owner for the
particular characteristic-three realization in `WILD_CUBIC_PROOF.md`.
That is not a proof of novelty. The working claim difference is narrow:
the abstract group is classical; the proposed new local package proves
that this wild polynomial family realizes it with the stated complete
Kummer/AS ranks and branch data. Independent review must check that
difference and the actual proof before admission.
