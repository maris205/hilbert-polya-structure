## Verdict and exact scope

No mathematical blocker was found in T1–T3 as stated. The proposed theorem
concerns the splitting fields of `(X^3+a X^2)^n-t` over `k(t)`, where
`char(k)=3`, `a!=0`, `t` is transcendental and the exponent denotes
composition. It concerns inverse-image depth, not periodic-point counts.
The abstract signature-constrained group E and its size are classical.

## Independent reconstruction of the potentially fragile steps

1. The cubic normal form is consistent: in characteristic three,
   `f(a(z^2-1))=a^3(z^3-z)^2`. Translating z by the three prime-field
   elements yields exactly the three expressions in (2.2). Their
   differences recover z, so the splitting-field equality is two-sided.
   The discriminant is `a^3 alpha` and the product of the three roots is
   alpha. Constants in the square-root normal form require no extension
   of k.
2. The all-level sign constraint is an actual tree constraint. The
   squared Vandermonde products differ by `a^6`; independent odd
   reorderings one level lower can enforce the unsquared identities
   without changing already chosen upper relations. Applying a field
   automorphism then gives the local sign equation at every parent.
   The homomorphism imposing the top equation is surjective (a root
   transposition with trivial sections suffices). Thus the upper bound
   and the order ratio are both justified before equality is proved.
3. At zero, the root cover is tame, with maximal index `2^n`; over
   algebraically closed constants the Galois-closure index is the lcm,
   also `2^n`. All `2^n` roots reducing to zero have normalized valuation
   one. Their supports form an embedded binary subtree, so every bottom
   sibling triple has an even parity vector.
4. The span argument uses only the induction hypothesis at height n.
   An independent bottom 3-cycle changes the nonzero even vector of
   one triple and fixes the other triples. Subtracting the valuation
   vectors isolates that triple. Its cyclic translates span its full
   two-dimensional even space; transitivity reaches every triple.
   The annihilator is precisely the space generated by disjoint sibling
   triples. All these candidate relations hold because
   `product(alpha/a^3)=eta/a^9=(beta_eta/a^3)^2`.
5. Weight-one and weight-two vectors are not in that relation space.
   Therefore the Kummer characters of different beta variables are
   nontrivial and pairwise distinct. Character projection is valid in
   characteristic three because the Kummer group is a two-group.
   It remains valid after quotienting by the Artin–Schreier image, since
   this quotient is an F3 representation of the same group.
6. The infinity induction is not inferred from global independence.
   Previous-level index `2*3^n` gives root valuation -2. Every Kummer
   generator is already a square in the geometric completion, so the
   Kummer extension splits there and beta has valuation -1. This proves
   that each global AS class is nonzero; the distinct characters now
   prove their global independence.
7. In that same completion, ratios of root leading coefficients have
   `3^n`-th power one and hence are one. Ratios of beta leading
   coefficients are consequently +/-1. Subtraction removes their only
   pole. Every integral element is an AS image by the algebraically
   closed residue field and Hensel's lemma with derivative -1. Thus the
   local rank is exactly one, not `3^n`, and infinity index grows by
   exactly three. This supplies the next ramification induction step
   independently of the full global degree.
8. The two computed relative degrees multiply to the known E order
   ratio. Combined with the already proved upper bound this establishes
   equality at the next level without circularity. Over arbitrary k,
   geometric degree equals the upper bound already valid over k, so
   the arithmetic degree cannot be larger or drop on extending
   constants. This gives the claimed regularity and group equality.
9. The local reciprocal map is `h(u)=u^3/(1+au)` and its derivative has
   order three. The composition chain rule gives root-field different
   `3(3^n-1)/2`. The geometric Galois completion has degree two over
   that root completion, hence is tame with relative different one.
   Transitivity gives `3^(n+1)-2`. Riemann–Hurwitz then gives exactly
   the displayed genus, including `g_1=0` and `g_2=46`.

## Boundaries retained

The argument does not prove specialized surjectivity, a forward-period
formula, any result for p>3, or a target-prime/zero correspondence. The
group and genus are one theorem package and must not be split into
separate batch papers. Finite symbolic checks can catch constants and
arithmetic slips but are not evidence for the all-height induction.
