## 3. Contract-specific recommendations

### 3.1 Finite-adelic coefficients and the actual FAD consequence

The [coefficient theorem](PROOF_PACKAGE.md) starts with

\[
 F(z)=\sum_{n\ge1}a_nz^n,\qquad
 a_n=\left(\sum_jc_j\eta_j^n\right)r_n
 \prod_{p\in S}p^{-s_{p,n}v_p(n)-t_{p,n}p^{v_p(n)}}.
\]

Its finite active-fibre test is applied **after** root-of-unity phase grouping
and aggregation of actual Fourier coefficients. Abstract complex coefficients
need not be nonnegative integer fixed counts and are not automatically
dynamically realizable. The ordinary generating function `F` is not itself
the Artin–Mazur zeta function.

For an actual FAD map with `f_n=#Fix(f^n)`, the source conventions are instead

\[
 Z_f(z)=\sum_{n\ge1}f_nz^n,
 \qquad
 \zeta_f(z)=\exp\left(\sum_{n\ge1}f_nz^n/n\right),
 \qquad z\zeta_f'(z)/\zeta_f(z)=Z_f(z).
\]

The corollary fixes positive entropy, the exact FAD gcd-sequence hypotheses,
nonnegative distortion exponents, and periods coprime to their own primes.
The abstract theorem's wider allowed periods cannot silently replace those
dynamical hypotheses. The [Salem-times-additive realization](REALIZED_EXAMPLE.md)
has `f_n=|det(A^n-I)| p^(n-p^v_p(n))`, native integer time, and entropy
scale `Lambda=p*lambda`. It is a genuine product self-map, not an asserted
single algebraic-group realization over one characteristic.

Prospective A0: `A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION` is defensible for the actual
finite-prime valuation source. Neither the finite distortion-prime set nor
the additive characteristic supplies an all-rational-prime primitive owner,
`log p` roof, or target repetition amplitude. Abstract chosen radial data
alone cannot receive stronger arithmetic-origin credit.

Prospective A1: `A1_WEAK` is the conservative target-facing recommendation.
Intrinsic source least periods and Möbius inversion are analytic count
subchecks; they do not provide a uniform smooth stability convention across
the FAD class or the missing target arithmetic labels and phases. The new
natural-boundary theorem is stronger source analysis than the earlier
limit-set topology, but it does not repair those A1 fields.

Determinant distinction: the displayed source scalar zeta and its
meromorphic natural boundary are proved mathematical content. No ordinary
trace-class operator is constructed to own that zeta; the finite matrix `A`
inside the fixed-count formula is not such an operator by naming convention.
Source natural-boundary analysis is not `A2_ANALYTIC_DETERMINANT` for the
Riemann target and is not target analytic continuation under A3.

Its group `E_n`, regularity, branch data, one-step Kummer/AS ranks, and genera

periodic-orbit repetition formula.

Prospective A0: there is genuine source arithmetic geometry, not target-data
fitting. A target-facing `A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION` is the safe ceiling
on the supplied evidence. Do not import the stronger rational-prime

forward-period ledger is **not supplied**. If height were directly submitted

be an analytic A1 pass. A finite permutation representation of a selected
Galois element is not the forward dynamics of `f`.

Determinant distinction: no Artin–Mazur zeta of `f`, all-extension-degree

Generic regularity also gives no assertion that every `t0` specialization
has the same Galois group.

### 3.3 Rectangular common returns

The [statement](RECTANGULAR_RETURN_PROOF.md) fixes

\[
 R_{a,b}(x,y)=\sum_{n,m\ge1}
 \gcd(a^n-1,b^m-1)x^ny^m.
\]

For independent bases the open absolute-convergence domain is the bidisc.
For `a=c^r,b=c^s`, with coprime positive `r,s`, it is
`|x|<1, |y|<1, c^(rs)|x|^s|y|^r<1`; the function then has meromorphic
continuation to the bidisc with the stated genuine interior pole hypersurfaces.
In both cases the full bidisc boundary blocks **joint** meromorphic
continuation. The identically zero axis slices do not contradict this joint
statement, and no claim of a natural boundary for every slice is permitted.

Prospective A0: `A0_WEAK_ARITHMETIC_RELATION` is defensible for exact source
gcd/order arithmetic. The arithmetic of two integral endomorphisms does not
select rational primes as primitive orbit owners. The classical two-exponent
S-unit gcd input remains source arithmetic, not a target explicit formula.

Prospective A1: `A1_WEAK` for exact rectangular common-return data. A count
of points fixed by two specified powers is not a primitive one-map census,
and the proof does not classify all higher-rank orbit stabilizers.

Determinant distinction: `R` is an ordinary bivariate generating function,
not an exponential orbit product. Primitive integer rays `(i,j)` used in
its meromorphic expansion are primitive **index vectors**, not proved
primitive periodic orbits with those weights. Exponentiating, inserting
`1/(nm)`, replacing `(n,m)` by `nm`, or setting `x=y` would define a
different observable; none is authorized as a same-owner target determinant.
Ward's rectangular counts, volume zeta, Lind's subgroup-index zeta, and the
synchronous/diagonal counts were deducted in the source audit, not identified
with this two-variable function.

### 3.4 All monic integral conservative quadratic Hénon maps

Use the [main statement](../nonlinear_geometry/PROOF_INTEGER_HENON.md)
together with the [odd-branch statement](../nonlinear_geometry/ADDENDUM_INTEGER_HENON_ODD.md).
For `b=2q+e`, `e` in `{0,1}`, the integer translation `(x,y)->(x+q,y+q)`
gives `H_(A,e)`, where `A=a-q^2+(2-e)q`. This is an actual conjugacy
preserving rational/integer points and exact periods. The two branches
answer one classification question; the seven-point even maximum does not
replace the full family's sharp eight-point maximum.

For each fixed `(a,b)`, let `c_l` count its distinct rational cycles of
exact length `l`, with coincident words counted once and only cyclic
rotation quotiented. Then the complete source ledger gives

\[
 \#\operatorname{Fix}(H_{a,b}^n,\mathbb Q)
 =\sum_{\ell\mid n}\ell c_\ell,
 \qquad
 \zeta_{a,b,\mathbb Q}(z)
 =\prod_{\ell=1}^4(1-z^\ell)^{-c_\ell}.
\]

Prospective A0: rational-point integrality and the exact parameter
classification give genuine source arithmetic restrictions, supporting at
most a weak arithmetic relation for the target route. Treating the finite
cycle product alone as an arithmetic-origin mechanism would fail A0: the
same cycle-to-determinant algebra applies to an arbitrary finite permutation.

Prospective A1: this is the strongest **native unweighted count subcheck**
of the retained four: all parameters, all rational periodic points, exact
least periods, and multiplicities are classified. A separately documented
count-only native evaluation could support analytic A1 credit. This note
does not assign `A1_PASS_ANALYTIC` to the full target route: the proof does
not supply target arithmetic labels, target phases/amplitudes, a complete
stability-weighted trace, or the mandatory control panel. A conservative
target-facing recommendation remains `A1_WEAK` with that analytic source
subcheck prominently retained.

Determinant distinction: on the classified finite rational periodic set,
the one-step permutation `P_(a,b)` satisfies the elementary source identity
`det(I-z*P_(a,b))=product_l(1-z^l)^c_l`, the reciprocal of the displayed
zeta. This finite-permutation interpretation is a direct classical corollary,
not a new determinant contract. It does not identify the Koopman operator on
the full real phase space, a quantization of the nonlinear map, or a target
zero divisor. Jacobian `+1` alone establishes none of those identifications.
