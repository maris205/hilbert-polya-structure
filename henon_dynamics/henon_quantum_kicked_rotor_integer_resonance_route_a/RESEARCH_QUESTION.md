# HCS-C337 research question

For the source Floquet ordering

$$
U_\tau=\exp(-i\tau\widehat n^2/2)\exp(-i\kappa\cos\theta)
$$

on $L^2(\mathbb T,d\theta/(2\pi))$, what is the complete all-time propagator and momentum law on the integer sheet $\tau=2\pi\ell$?  In particular, does the parity of the positive integer $\ell$ alone separate ballistic quantum resonance from exact operator antiresonance, uniformly for every $\kappa\in\mathbb R$ and every momentum seed $|m\rangle$?

The question is deliberately source-local.  General rational resonance, Bloch quasimomentum, detuning, localization away from resonance, target arithmetic data and Hilbert--Polya matching are outside scope.
