# C337 paper plan

## Title

Integer Quantum Resonance and Antiresonance of the Kicked Rotor: An Exact Parity Sheet

## Structure

1. Freeze the Hilbert space, Floquet ordering and integer-resonance domain.
2. Establish the free-phase parity lemma and unitary ownership.
3. Prove the even-$\ell$ Bessel propagator and ballistic moment theorem.
4. Prove the odd-$\ell$ half-turn factorization and exact two-kick recurrence.
5. Audit $m$, $\kappa=0$, parity and operator-order boundaries.
6. Separate proof from finite evidence and apply the Route-A firewall.

## Revision design

- Round 0: operator ownership, parity lemma, Bessel kernel, and involution proof.
- Round 1: add the exact characteristic function and moments through order six.
- Round 2: add full boundary/collision/source/evidence audits and the strict Route-A decision.

Each round is a complete readable paper.  Later rounds add theorem-bearing material rather than cosmetic wording.
