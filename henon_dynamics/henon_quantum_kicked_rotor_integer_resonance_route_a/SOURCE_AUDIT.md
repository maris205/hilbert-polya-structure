# C337 source audit

## Verified primary and official records

1. I. Dana, E. Eisenberg and N. Shnerb, “Antiresonance and localization in quantum dynamics,” *Physical Review E* **54** (1996), 5948--5963. Official APS record and DOI: https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.54.5948 and https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.54.5948 . The APS abstract identifies quantum antiresonance with exactly periodic recurrences. It does not certify this package's particular operator ordering or formulas.
2. I. Dana and D. L. Dorofeev, “General Quantum Resonances of the Kicked Particle,” *Physical Review E* **73** (2006), 026206. Author preprint and journal DOI: https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0509035 and https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.73.026206 . The source studies general quantum resonances and reports exact momentum-distribution/kinetic-energy formulas in main-resonance settings. This package uses it as historical/source context, not as a substitute for proof.
3. C. Ryu, M. F. Andersen, A. Vaziri, M. B. d'Arcy, J. M. Grossman, K. Helmerson and W. D. Phillips, “High-Order Quantum Resonances Observed in a Periodically Kicked Bose-Einstein Condensate,” *Physical Review Letters* **96** (2006), 160403. Official APS record and DOI: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.160403 and https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.160403 . The APS abstract supports the experimental existence of rational-Talbot-time resonances and ballistic momentum transfer; it does not own the present exact proof.

These records were checked on 2026-09-03.  Direct publisher/arXiv identifiers, author lists, titles, venues, years and stated abstract-level claims were verified.  No literature-priority claim is made for the parity reduction, Bessel identities, or their assembly here.

## Collision boundary

- C110 is a nonautonomous classical Hénon Floquet owner, not a quantum rotor on an infinite momentum lattice.
- C143 is an inhomogeneous coined quantum walk; C148 is an open Walsh quantum baker.
- C178 is a harmonic metaplectic strobe, not a cosine-kicked rotor.
- C224 owns nonautonomous two-level Landau--Zener scattering.
- C318 owns a static SSH lattice and C323 a finite continuous-time search Hamiltonian.

None owns the exact $\tau=2\pi\ell$ resonance/antiresonance sheet.  “Owner” here means only the workspace's theorem package; it never asserts priority in the literature.

## Exclusions

The package does not address general rational $\tau/(2\pi)$, Bloch quasimomentum, detuning, noisy kicks, nonlinear rotors, dynamical localization, quasienergy spectral type away from this sheet, or any target arithmetic identification.
