# Research question — C121

For the frozen polynomial automorphism

\[
H(x,y)=(x^2-4-y,x),
\]

what exact global algebraic information follows from its projective extension,
and how can that information be kept separate from unsupported analytic or
operator claims?

The bounded question has five parts:

1. What are the forward and inverse homogeneous formulas and their
   indeterminacy points?
2. Where does the projective exceptional line go, and does its orbit force a
   degree cancellation in an iterate?
3. Can \(\deg H^n=2^n\) be proved for every \(n\), with a reproducible
   prefix through \(n=8\), without expanding a degree-256 polynomial?
4. Which fixed points and primitive real two-cycle can be certified exactly,
   and what is the cycle's tangent monodromy?
5. Do nearby parameters \(c=-3,-5\) preserve the particular frozen
   two-cycle, or is \(c=-4\) essential to that witness?

The question does not request a complete periodic-orbit atlas, a prime-like
orbit-to-target correspondence, a target divisor, a Markov partition, an
entropy theorem, a transfer operator, a Fredholm determinant, an analytic
bridge to a target function, or any arithmetic or Route-B structure.  Their
absence is scored as a Route-A failure at the relevant evaluator layer rather
than hidden behind a nonstandard “not addressed” label.
