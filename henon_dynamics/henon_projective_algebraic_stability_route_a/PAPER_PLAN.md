## Structure

1. Define the affine automorphism and projective extensions.
2. Locate the indeterminacy points and exceptional-line orbit.
3. Prove degree doubling and algebraic stability using the iterate recurrence.
4. Give the replay prefix and distinguish dynamical degree from entropy.
5. Certify fixed points, the two-cycle, monodromy, and parameter controls.
6. State reproducibility and the exact Route-A boundary, distinguishing
   structural evidence from the absent prime-like correspondence, target
   divisor, and analytic bridge.

No figure is needed.  A compact degree table and exact formulas communicate
the result more faithfully than a decorative diagram.  No bibliography is
added because the manuscript makes no external or novelty claim.
