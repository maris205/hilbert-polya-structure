# Narrative report

The decisive simplification is not enumeration but prime support. Each cyclic invariant factor splits into the part coprime to (d) and the part supported on primes dividing (d). On the first part multiplication by (d) is invertible; on the second it is nilpotent. This makes every functional graph component a periodic cycle in the automorphic factor decorated by the same transient tree from the nilpotent factor.

That decomposition simultaneously settles three layers that are often treated separately. Möbius inversion of exact fixed counts gives every primitive cycle and the complete finite zeta product. Kernel growth on the nilpotent factor gives every tail level. Image shrinkage gives the ranks of all powers of the full-function composition operator; second differences of those ranks recover every zero Jordan-block size.

The singular exponent (d=0) is intentionally separated. It is a constant map, so importing positive-exponent prime-support notation would obscure rather than simplify the boundary. The identity exponent (d=1) remains inside the main theorem and has no transient or zero sector.

The evidence spans 646 maps and is directly re-enumerated by an independent checker. It includes map-table hashes, but no empirical extrapolation is used in the proof. The exact finite evidence, symbolic matrix checks, fresh replay, and repaired-hash hostile tests together establish a replayable source-local certificate.
