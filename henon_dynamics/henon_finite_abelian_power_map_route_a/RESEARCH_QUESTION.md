# Research question

For an arbitrary finite abelian group
\[
G\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^r C_{n_i}
\]
and integer (d\ge1), can the entire functional graph of the power map (P_d(g)=g^d) be described in one exact parameter-uniform theorem, including all periodic and primitive counts, all transient levels, the finite dynamical zeta function, and the complete zero-Jordan structure of the full-function Koopman operator?

The answer proved here is yes. Write (n_i=a_i b_i), where ((a_i,d)=1) and every prime divisor of (b_i) divides (d). Then (G=A_d\times B_d); (P_d) is an automorphism on (A_d) and nilpotent toward the identity on (B_d). This split owns both the graph atlas and the operator atlas. The singular exponent (d=0) is not forced into formulas containing prime support or multiplicative order; it is proved separately.

The intended progress is theorem-scale: this is not another finite-permutation census. It classifies every finite abelian group and every positive exponent and then verifies a wide finite corpus independently.
