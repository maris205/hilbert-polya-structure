# Source and claim audit

## Primary context

Claudio Qureshi and Lucas Reis, “On the functional graph of the power map over finite groups,” *Discrete Mathematics* **346** (2023), 113393. Primary publisher record: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2023.113393.

This source establishes that finite-group power maps and their functional graphs are an existing mathematical subject. It is used for context, not as a substitute for the proofs in this package.

## Claim discipline

- The package claims ownership only of the HCS-C264 workspace certificate and its exact implementation.
- It does not claim that the underlying group-theoretic facts, functional-graph viewpoint, or any displayed identity are new to the literature.
- The arbitrary-finite-abelian theorem is proved directly from invariant factors, Chinese remaindering, finite automorphisms, nilpotence, Möbius inversion, and rank-nullity.
- The exact checker imports no producer. SymPy checks are additional small-instance matrix witnesses, not proof of the all-parameter theorem.
- Nearest internal owners differ materially: C172 and C176 concern other finite maps; C204 is a finite-state atlas; C258 concerns a different finite permutation family; C260 classifies Möbius transformations on a projective line. None owns the arbitrary finite-abelian power-map split together with the full zero-Jordan atlas.

## Scope audit

No arithmetic local datum, bad Euler factor, root number, automorphy statement, target divisor, functional equation, or Hilbert–Pólya operator is claimed. “Zeta” always means the finite source dynamical zeta function. “Koopman” always means composition on the full finite function space.
