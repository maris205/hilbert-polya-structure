   `p<=100000`.
2. Enumerate every `(a,b)` satisfying `(-1)^b=(2/a)` at each level and
   solve `(a-1)j=-b` by the exact gcd criterion.
3. Recompute the whole ledger in an independent checker that imports no
   producer code.
4. Use SymPy for independent polynomial, discriminant, character, density,
   and group-closure checks.
5. Rebuild evidence twice in isolated temporary directories and demand
   byte identity.
6. Repair payload hashes after hostile semantic mutations and demand
   rejection; separately test duplicate and nonfinite JSON.
7. Run A0 controls: prove the neighboring-basepoint-3 trivial intersection,
   enumerate the uncut full-affine parent and its restored four-root stratum,
   and exactly split odd composites below 100 into five prime powers retained
   as `Frob_p^r` repetition controls and twenty mixed composites lacking a
   single-prime Frobenius owner.  Give finite empirical density no A0 credit.

   orientation/phase, monodromy/stability, and six mandatory control lanes;
   these force `A1_WEAK`.

   because no family-wide antiunitary time reversal, nontrivial phase/weight
   preservation law, or global self-adjoint Hamiltonian owner is proved.

   fixed epoch; demand byte identity, embedded subset fonts, readable text,
   rasterizable pages, CJK font embedding, bilingual independently written
   abstracts, separate 5--7 item keyword lists, and no warning/layout or
   later-round-leak sentinel.
