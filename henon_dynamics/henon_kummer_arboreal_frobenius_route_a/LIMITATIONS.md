# Limitations

- The theorem treats the monomial map `z^2` at basepoint `2`; it is not a
  classification of arbitrary quadratic maps or basepoints.
- The finite-level images lie in an affine subgroup far smaller than the
  full automorphism group of a binary rooted tree.
- The density concerns roots of the source polynomial `x^(2^n)-2` modulo
  primes.  It is not target arithmetic local data.
- Prime-indexed Frobenius classes do not constitute one real-time
  autonomous dynamical system.
- The package proves fixed-root multiplicities but does not enumerate an
  all-level primitive/repeated orbit atlas or record orbit orientation,
  phase, multiplicity weights, monodromy, and stability.  The six mandatory
  A1 orbit controls remain uncompleted, so A1 is weak.
- Finite permutation unitaries do not yield a global self-adjoint operator.
- No target Euler product, functional equation, divisor, counting law, or
  zero correspondence is constructed.
