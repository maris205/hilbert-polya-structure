# Paper plan

**One-sentence contribution:** the iterated preimages of `2` under `z^2`
have an exactly computable, compatibly entangled index-two affine Galois
image whose fixed-point spectrum gives a closed Chebotarev root-density
formula at every level.

## Claims--evidence matrix

| Claim | Analytic evidence | Executable receipt |
|---|---|---|
| intersection and degree | Capelli plus nonsquare lemma | degree/formula sentinels |
| exact image and restrictions | shared `sqrt(2)`, cardinality, lifts | exhaustive levels 3--12 |
| fixed-root spectrum | linear congruence and 2-adic valuations | 5,592,400 pairs |
| Chebotarev density | fixed spectrum plus Chebotarev | 95,910 prime cells |
| route boundary | evaluator criteria | strict YAML and false flags |

## Three substantive rounds

- **Round 0 — field and image theorem:** define the tree; prove the
  radical--cyclotomic intersection, degree, exact `H_n`, and restriction
  maps.
- **Round 1 — Frobenius law:** add the complete fixed-root distribution,
  forbidden multiplicities, exact Chebotarev densities, and limit `7/24`.
- **Round 2 — inverse-limit and audit closure:** add `H_infinity`, the
  finite prime criterion and evidence ledger, the finite Koopman realization
  with its `A4_FORMAL_HINT` boundary, three explicit A0 controls, collision
  ownership, and the strict `A1_WEAK`/`ROUTE_A_EXPLORATORY` firewall.

No decorative figure is warranted: the essential relationship is the
short proof-dependency diagram and an exact table, both typeset directly in
the manuscript.

Every round includes an English abstract and an independently written
Chinese abstract with separate 5--7 keyword lists.  Frozen wrapper builds
must reject any conclusion or evidence that belongs only to a later round.
