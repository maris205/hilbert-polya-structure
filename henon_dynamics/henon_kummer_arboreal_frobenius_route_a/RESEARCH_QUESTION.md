# Research question

For `f(z)=z^2`, basepoint `2`, and
`K_n=Q(z:z^(2^n)=2)`, determine the Galois action on every level of the
binary preimage tree and answer all of the following without extrapolating
from finite levels:

1. What is the exact radical--cyclotomic intersection?
2. What is the exact image in `AGL_1(Z/2^n)` and how do adjacent levels
   restrict?
3. How many level-`n` roots can a Frobenius element fix?
4. What is the natural density of odd primes at which `x^(2^n)=2` has a
   root, and what is its limiting value?
5. Which Route-A layers are genuinely earned, and which remain closed?
