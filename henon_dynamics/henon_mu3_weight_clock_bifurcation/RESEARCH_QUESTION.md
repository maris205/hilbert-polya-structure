# Research question

## Locked question

C50 continued the normalized Hénon Euler germ to \(\Re s>1/5\), but only
its extracted second-moment factors acquired a standard functional equation.
The C51 question is:

> Do the source-native cohomological packets of the \(n=2,3,4\)
> chronological moments admit one completion with a common center while the
> prime clock \(u=ns+j\), the split-prime trace, and the exact Galois
> normalization remain unchanged?

## Finer questions

1. Can the exact moment coefficient be separated uniformly into pure
   weight-zero and weight-one Frobenius traces?
2. Is the rank identity \(4^n-1\) geometric, or merely a fit to three rows?
3. What standard \(L\)-variable is forced by
   \(1/(p-1)=\sum_{j\ge1}p^{-j}\)?
4. Which sector centers coincide after mapping the standard pure-weight
   reflection to the Hénon variable \(s\)?
5. Can integral or formal half Tate twisting repair a center mismatch?
6. Can the fractional powers \(2/3\) and \(1/2\) come from a semisimple
   direct source-native \(K\)-compatible system retaining the two-weight
   decomposition and unchanged split-prime trace?
7. What survives after denominator clearing?
8. What does the \(\chi_y\) polynomial say about a possible low-rank
   projector inside \(O_4\)?

## Stop/go gates

- **GO:** the odd leading rail has one exact common center and yields a
  denominator-cleared formal expected-completion skeleton.
- **STOP for full alignment:** the even leading rail or any \(j\ge2\)
  denominator term has a different mapped center.
- **STOP for direct finite-rank roots:** the required weightwise ranks are
  nonintegral.
- **GO to C52:** a \(K\)-rational algebraic projector whose realizations
  are \(\ell\)-compatible separates the rank-two extreme Hodge piece of
  \(O_4\).
- **PIVOT:** if no such projector exists, prove a monodromy or endomorphism
  obstruction rather than accumulating more finite-prime traces.

The question does not authorize chronology averaging, a hidden critical
shift, or inference of \(n=3,4\) Hasse--Weil functional equations from
finite data.
