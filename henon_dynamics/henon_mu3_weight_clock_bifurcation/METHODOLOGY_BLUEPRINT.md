# Methodology blueprint

## Source lock

1. Work over \(K=\mathbf Q(\rho)\), where \(\rho^2+\rho+1=0\).
2. Retain the exact \(2n\)-variable chronological phase for \(n=2,3,4\).
3. Keep \(d_p=(p-1)/2\), the clock \(\log p\), and both degree-one primes
   of \(K\) over each split rational prime.
4. Never replace chronological dynamics by an averaged transition matrix.

## Mathematical route

1. Partition the affine zero fibre into projective radial strata.
2. Express its point count using a Fermat cubic \(S_n\), a split quadric
   \(Q_n\), and the \((2,3)\) intersection \(X_n\).
3. Cancel all Tate polynomial terms and isolate the deviations
   \(A_{p,n}\) and \(B_{p,n}\).
4. Package those deviations into weight-zero \(E_n\) and weight-one \(O_n\).
5. Derive ranks from the Jacobian ring and the complete-intersection Chern
   class.  Apply the source theorem only to \(n=2,3,4\).
6. Expand \(1/(p-1)\) exactly with the canonical index \(j\ge1\).
7. Compare the leading logarithm with standard local \(L\)-factor
   logarithms and bound the residual.
8. Map the standard weight-\(w\) center through \(u=ns+j\).
9. Test twist invariance and finite-rank divisibility before proposing any
   completed object.
10. Clear the denominators \(2,3,4\) only after the factorwise calculation.
11. Compute \(\chi_y(X_4)\) and the Hodge types of \(O_4\).

## Evidence hierarchy

- **Theorem:** exact algebraic identities, purity, rank formulas, center
  mapping, direct-system divisibility obstruction, and the \(n=4\) Hodge
  ledger.
- **Inherited theorem:** \(n=2\) continuation/functional equation and C50's
  continuation/\(\operatorname{Det}_{10}\) realization.
- **Motivic expectation only:** the completed \(n=3,4\) odd factors and
  their individual functional equations.
- **Machine replay:** exact rational arithmetic, finite coefficient
  expansions, schema gates, and mutation tests.

## Large-door decision

The paper is successful if it either produces a coherent completed
source-native object or proves a structural obstruction.  The exact
weight--clock bifurcation supplies the latter while retaining one positive
odd-sector skeleton.  C52 therefore targets a projector theorem, not
another broad prime scan.
