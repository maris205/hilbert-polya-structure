# Narrative report

The useful advance is the simultaneous closure of an exact classical invariant and the complete quantum bound-state problem.  The action normalization is explicit, so its derivative recovers the physical period without a hidden \(2\pi\).  On the quantum side, the same dissociation energy that bounds the classical well is the bottom edge of the essential spectrum.  The strict Laguerre termination inequality is indispensable: equality produces a formal zero-energy solution but not an \(L^2\) state.

This is strong analytic and natural-quantization evidence, but it fails Route A overall.  The integer \(n\) is a consecutive excitation label, not an intrinsic prime owner; periodic wells are continuous invariant families; and no source orbit determinant is identified with a target function.
