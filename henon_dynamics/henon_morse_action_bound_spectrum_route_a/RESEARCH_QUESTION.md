# HCS-C334 research question

For the Morse Hamiltonian
\[
H=\frac{p^2}{2m}+D(e^{-2ax}-2e^{-ax}),\qquad m,D,a>0,
\]
can one close, in a single theorem, both the exact classical action on every bounded energy shell and the complete point spectrum of its canonical Schrödinger quantization, including the dissociation and zero-energy boundaries?

The answer proved here is yes.  The classical action and period are elementary, the negative quantum spectrum is a finite generalized-Laguerre ladder, and the strict endpoint condition excludes the tempting but non-square-integrable zero-energy state.  The result is source-local: it supplies neither prime ownership nor a target determinant.
