# Source audit

The mathematical classification and all proofs in this package are self-contained and source-local. These primary publisher records anchor only the classical surrounding literature; no priority claim is made.

- E. Sakzad, M.-R. Sadeghi, and D. Panario, “Cycle structure of permutation functions over finite fields and their applications,” *Advances in Mathematics of Communications* 6 (2012), 347--361. DOI: `10.3934/amc.2012.6.347`. Relevant ownership: finite-field permutation cycle structure.
- C. Forsyth, J. Gurev, and S. Shrima, “Metacommutation as a Group Action on the Projective Line Over F_p,” *Proceedings of the AMS* 144 (2016), 4583--4590. DOI: `10.1090/proc/13126`. Relevant ownership: projective-line PGL action and cycle characterization.
- G. E. Wall, “Conjugacy classes in projective and special linear groups,” *Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society* 22 (1980), 339--364. DOI: `10.1017/S0004972700006675`. Relevant ownership: projective-linear conjugacy classes over finite fields.

The package neither imports a class table nor treats finite enumeration as proof of the arbitrary-prime-power theorem. The exact evidence is regenerated from matrices, finite-field arithmetic, and direct projective permutations.
