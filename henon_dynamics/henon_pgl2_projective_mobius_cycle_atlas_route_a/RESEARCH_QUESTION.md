# HCS-C260 research question

For an arbitrary prime power `q=p^r`, classify every element of `PGL_2(F_q)` by its permutation dynamics on `P^1(F_q)`. The requested closure is simultaneous: complete cycle type, all fixed-point counts, primitive-point and primitive-cycle ledgers, finite Artin--Mazur zeta, finite Koopman determinant and spectrum, exact projective reversors, element counts by type and projective order, and the characteristic-two decision rule.

The theorem is uniform in every prime power. The finite computation is only an independent regression oracle on 18 representative fields, including nonprime fields of degrees 2--5.

Scope is `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`. Nothing here identifies rational primes with primitive orbits, supplies arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root numbers, automorphy, a target divisor or functional equation, or a Hilbert--Polya operator. Route B is not authorized.
