# Paper plan

The paper is a theorem-scale atlas, not a split fragment.

1. Freeze the arbitrary-prime-power owner and projective normalization.
2. State one four-row theorem giving cycle types, fixed counts, primitive counts, zeta, and type/order census.
3. Prove the four cases from rational eigenlines and the eigenvalue-ratio order.
4. Give a separate characteristic-two classifier via the absolute trace of `det/tr^2`.
5. Prove exact projective reversibility in a cyclic basis and close the finite Koopman spectrum/determinant.
6. Report independent exhaustive permutations over 18 fields and hostile controls.
7. End with the strict Route-A tuple and forbidden-claim boundary.

Revision 1 adds the characteristic-two and involution/census proof faces. Revision 2 adds the exact reversor and strengthens evidence ownership and Route-A limitations.
