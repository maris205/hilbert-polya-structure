## Seven failure modes

1. No target object is inferred from the source transform.
2. No parameter is fitted to primes or zeros.
3. There is no training/test data split or hidden target data.
4. Finite rows are explicitly not called a proof.
5. Both references have verified metadata and narrow claim-level use.
6. Critical, adverse, no-claim, zero-reserve, and excluded zero-premium faces
   are explicit.
7. The queue/workload duality is a local corollary, not cross-owner theorem
   assembly.
