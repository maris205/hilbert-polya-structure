# Source and originality audit

## Verified primary records

1. H. U. Gerber and E. S. W. Shiu, “On the time value of ruin,” *North
   American Actuarial Journal* **2** (1), 48–72 (1998), DOI
   `10.1080/10920277.1998.10595671`.  The DOI/university record verifies title,
   authors, venue, volume, issue, year, and article pages, and identifies the
   expected discounted penalty as the paper’s subject.
2. S. Drekic and G. E. Willmot, “On the density and moments of the time of ruin
   with exponential claims,” *ASTIN Bulletin* **33** (1), 11–21 (2003), DOI
   `10.2143/AST.33.1.1036`.  Cambridge’s primary PDF verifies the complete
   metadata and exponential-claim/time-to-ruin context.

## Exact source use

- Gerber–Shiu is cited for the discounted-penalty framework and classical
  first-passage context only.
- Drekic–Willmot is cited for exponential-claim ruin-time lineage only.
- The root selection, joint deficit factorization, loading-wall synthesis,
  conditional means, critical cusp, supremum mixture, evidence, and Route-A
  decision are all derived locally.

## Originality boundary

The package claims a complete frozen-convention synthesis and executable
closure, not invention of the Cramér–Lundberg model, Gerber–Shiu function, or
literature priority.  “New” means no C1–C281 workspace package owns this exact
theorem.

## Collision audit

C225 treats a finite M/M/1/K birth–death generator, stationary spectrum, and
mixing.  C282 treats a continuous surplus with downward compound-Poisson jumps,
strict absorbing first passage, joint discounted deficit, all safety-loading
chambers, and no finite-state spectrum.  The dual supremum/workload identity is
reported as source-local and does not import C225 as a premise.
