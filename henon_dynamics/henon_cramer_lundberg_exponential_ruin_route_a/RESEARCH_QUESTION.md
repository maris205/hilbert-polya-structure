# Research question

For

`U_t=u+c t-sum_{i=1}^{N_t}Y_i`,

where `N` has rate `nu` and `Y_i~Exp(beta)`, can a single exact object resolve:

- the joint discounted law of ruin time and deficit;
- ultimate ruin in positive, zero, and negative mean-drift chambers;
- the conditional first mean of ruin time and its critical divergence;
- the adjustment martingale and dual all-time supremum law; and
- every no-claim, zero-reserve, and convention boundary?

Yes.  The inhomogeneous first-jump equation reduces to a quadratic root.  For
positive discount boundedness selects it; at zero discount in the profitable
chamber the vanishing probability boundary at infinite reserve makes the
selection, while the no-claim face is handled directly.  Exponential
memorylessness fixes the coefficient and the positive-intensity overshoot
law.  The resulting theorem is source-local.  After defining the killed owner
`X_t=U_t` before ruin and `X_t=Delta` thereafter, its Markov semigroup has no
intrinsic deterministic, enumerable primitive-periodic-orbit owner,
arithmetic primitive carrier, or target determinant, so it fails Route A at
A0 and A1.
