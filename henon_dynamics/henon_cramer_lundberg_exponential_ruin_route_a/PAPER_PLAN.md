# Paper plan

## Thesis

The exponential-claim risk process is not merely one ultimate-ruin formula.
One joint discount–deficit transform contains the complete loading atlas,
overshoot independence, the conditional first mean of ruin time, and a
supremum law.

## Revisions

- **Round 0:** freeze the strict-ruin convention, derive the inhomogeneous
  first-jump equation, and prove the joint transform.
- **Round 1:** add defective/certain ruin, conditional mean times on both sides
  of the drift wall, the critical infinite-mean singularity, and all local
  boundary faces.
- **Round 2:** add adjustment-martingale/supremum duality, executable receipt,
  claim-level source audit, nearest-owner collision audit, and strict Route-A
  obstruction.

## Figure/table phase

Two formula-driven tables replace a redundant plot: one maps the sign of
`c beta-nu` to ruin probability and mean time; the other records independent
certificate counts and hashes.  The critical square-root cusp is stated
analytically, so plotting sampled values would add no proof content.
