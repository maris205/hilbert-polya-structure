# C184 paper plan

## One-sentence contribution

We present a source-locked all-level theorem that carries the complete
2/5/6 spectral genealogy of the finite Sierpiński-gasket Dirichlet
Laplacian through exact characteristic, determinant, heat, and finite-zeta
consequences, then proves that the inverse-branch tree is refinement rather
than physical time.

## Narrative

1. Freeze the graph, Dirichlet boundary, unnormalized Laplacian, and level
   convention before displaying any spectrum.
2. State the complete birth-and-continuation theorem, with the exceptional
   values and forced \(6\mapsto3\) step visible.
3. Give the local elimination formula and the rank/kernel count that owns
   the three multiplicity series.
4. Close the total dimension and derive the monic characteristic recurrence
   with its exact \((t-2)\) cancellation.
5. Evaluate at zero for the determinant and derive heat-trace and finite
   spectral-zeta identities from the same lineage ledger.
6. Separate graph-refinement genealogy from autonomous time and record the
   strict Route-A failure.
7. Report exact executable validation, finite boundaries, source ownership,
   and explicit nonclaims.

Equations and one compact lineage table carry the argument.  No decorative
figure is needed because the central relationship is an exact mapping from
birth series to descendants, multiplicities, and analytic consequences.
