# C184 paper artifacts

`main_round0_original.pdf`, `main_round1.pdf`, and `main_round2.pdf` are
content-distinct internal drafting rounds.  Round 0 freezes the complete
lineage and exceptional-value theorem, round 1 adds polynomial/determinant
closure, and round 2 locks the finite-zeta and physical-time boundaries into
the release.  `main.pdf` is byte-identical to round 2.

The final build uses LuaLaTeX with a fixed source epoch, embedded fonts,
clean logs, and two independent fresh-directory byte reproductions recorded
in `COMPILE_REPORT.md`.  The drafting and visual checks are internal
workflow artifacts, not external peer review.
