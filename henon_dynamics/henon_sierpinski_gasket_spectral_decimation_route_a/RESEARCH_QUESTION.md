# C184 research question

For the standard unnormalized Dirichlet graph Laplacian on every finite
Sierpiński pre-gasket, can one put the complete spectral-decimation
genealogy, exceptional branches, multiplicity closure, characteristic
polynomial, determinant, heat trace, and finite spectral zeta into one
all-level theorem while keeping the refinement clock distinct from physical
time?

The question is deliberately stronger than diagonalizing a few finite
graphs.  It asks for every level \(m\ge1\), every allowed birth generation,
and every admissible inverse word, with the exceptional values \(2,5,6\)
treated exactly.  Finite matrices may be used only as regression sentinels;
they cannot prove the all-level claim.

The source-theorem answer is positive.  The Route-A answer is negative.
Level and spectral-branch labels have no intrinsic rational-prime origin,
and the branch tree is a renormalization correspondence rather than a
physical-time primitive-orbit owner.  The exact finite determinant and
finite spectral zeta therefore count as partial analytic structure only;
they are not a target divisor or functional equation.
