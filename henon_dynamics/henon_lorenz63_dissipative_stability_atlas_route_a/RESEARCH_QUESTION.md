# Research question

For the Lorenz-63 vector field

\[
\dot x=\sigma(y-x),\qquad
\dot y=x(\rho-z)-y,\qquad
\dot z=xy-\beta z,
\]

can one close, in a single convention-locked theorem, the global forward
dissipativity and every equilibrium's linear stability for all
\(\sigma,\beta>0\) and \(\rho\in\mathbb R\), while also resolving the
\(\sigma=0\), \(\beta=0\), \(\rho=1\), and Hopf boundary faces?

The question is deliberately smaller than a global classification of Lorenz
attractors and larger than a numerical study at \((10,8/3,28)\).  It asks for
an exact parameter atlas, not for a slogan that “the Lorenz equations are
chaotic.”

The separate Route-A question is whether this source-native progress supplies
an arithmetic primitive owner, orbit clock, determinant bridge, target
analytic structure or natural unitary lift.  It does not; the registered
verdict is `ROUTE_A_REJECTED` and Route B is not invoked.
