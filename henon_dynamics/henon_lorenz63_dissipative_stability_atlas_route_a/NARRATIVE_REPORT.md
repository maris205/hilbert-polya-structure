# Narrative report

The useful progress is a full positive-parameter theorem, not a new picture
of the classical \((10,8/3,28)\) attractor.  A carefully shifted quadratic
form cancels both cubic terms and the remaining cross term.  Completing one
square gives an explicit scalar differential inequality, a global forward
bound and a computable absorbing ellipsoid for every
\(\sigma,\beta>0\) and real \(\rho\).

The same parameter domain admits a complete equilibrium atlas.  The origin's
quadratic factor closes its stability at \(\rho=1\).  For the symmetric wing
pair, the cubic Routh–Hurwitz determinant becomes affine in \(\rho\).  Its
sign either stays positive forever or crosses once at the exact surface
\(\rho_H=\sigma(\sigma+\beta+3)/(\sigma-\beta-1)\).  At the crossing the
cubic factors and exposes the imaginary frequency exactly.

The theorem then steps outside its main domain deliberately.  At zero
\(\sigma\) or zero \(\beta\), isolated equilibria become curves and a tangent
zero eigenvalue appears.  These controls explain why the positive-parameter
absorbing result cannot be extended by continuity as a blanket claim.

The evidence file has ten exact atlas rows and five independent dissipation
rows.  A producer-independent checker makes 231 assertions; SymPy verifies 14
generic identities; clean replay is byte exact; and all 17 hostile mutations
are rejected.  These computations audit a proof rather than substitute for
one.

Route A still fails at every axis.  A dissipative ODE with a locally explicit
stability surface is not a rational-prime carrier, complete periodic-orbit
owner, target determinant, target analytic structure or natural unitary
operator.
