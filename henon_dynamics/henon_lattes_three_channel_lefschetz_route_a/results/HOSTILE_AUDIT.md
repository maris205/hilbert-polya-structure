# Hostile audit

## Strongest positive result

The branch-aware three-channel theorem is global in elliptic modulus, multiplication factor, and iterate. It retains multiplier information that a fixed-total or zeta-only computation erases, and the Wold theorem closes the natural-operator question.

## Strongest failure

Every unweighted fixed count is \(m^{2n}+1\), independent of \(\tau\). The primitive ledger has no intrinsic rational-prime labels or von-Mangoldt-type weights. The exact zeta is elementary, and the natural Koopman operator is a proper noncompact isometry. These are structural failures, not missing computation.

## Seven-mode integrity audit

1. **Implementation bug:** cleared by independent reconstruction, CAS checks, replay, and repaired-hash mutations.
2. **Citation identity/context:** one arXiv source anchor; author, title, identifier, and use were checked; no theorem-number or priority inflation.
3. **Invented result:** all finite metrics are generated on disk; no external review or acceptance statistic is reported.
4. **Shortcut reliance:** direct torsion sets and an independent checker supplement closed-form algebra.
5. **Bug reframed as insight:** no failed computation is promoted; any mismatch would block release.
6. **Methodology drift:** the phase space, clock, quotient, normalization, and determinant convention remain frozen.
7. **Early-frame lock:** the package deliberately tests the natural Koopman lift and line-bundle alternative, then records why neither upgrades Route A.

## Proves-too-much control

Varying \(\tau\) leaves all listed counts unchanged. This universality is mathematically useful but maximally hostile to arithmetic specificity. The branch multiplier census survives the control, while the hoped-for arithmetic entry gate does not.

## Decision

Retain C180 as a Lattès fixed-class/Lefschetz/Wold theorem and obstruction control. Do not infer an arithmetic local factor, Euler factor, root number, automorphy, Hilbert--Pólya operator, or Route-B permission.
