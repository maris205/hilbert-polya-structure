# Narrative report

C180 takes a genuine one-paper step: instead of recording only the familiar fixed total \(m^{2n}+1\), it resolves the entire quotient fixed set over every complex elliptic curve into three geometrically different channels. The branch channel is not cosmetic. Its size changes from one to four with the parity of \(m^n\), its multiplier is squared, and precisely this correction makes the holomorphic Lefschetz sum equal to one.

The resulting theorem is uniform in both moduli and multiplication factor. It recovers the exact Artin--Mazur zeta and primitive-period ledger, then pushes further to a complete Wold classification of the natural Haar Koopman operator. Even Fourier modes form countably many multiplication chains plus constants, so the operator is a proper noncompact isometry rather than a trace-class determinant owner.

The negative Route-A conclusion is as explicit as the positive dynamics theorem. Moduli disappear from every fixed count; no rational-prime semantics appear; the zeta is elementary; and the only canonical operator is not a Fredholm-determinant realization. Thus C180 is retained as a full Lattès/Lefschetz/Wold classification and a sharp stopping theorem, not as a disguised arithmetic claim.

Exact evidence contains 108 formula rows, 16 direct torsion-union enumerations materializing 25,290 torsion points, and 5,880 Wold-mode rows. An independent checker made 43,184 assertions, SymPy made 18,065 checks, replay was byte-identical, and all 23 repaired-hash plus one stale-hash mutations were rejected.
