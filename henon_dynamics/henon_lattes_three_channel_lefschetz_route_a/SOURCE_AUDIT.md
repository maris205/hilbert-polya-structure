# Source and ownership audit

## Primary source anchor

John Milnor, “On Lattès Maps,” arXiv:math/0402147, <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0402147>.

The author, title, arXiv identifier, and subject are locked to the arXiv record. The source supports the classical construction and structural setting of Lattès maps. This package does not claim novelty for the Lattès construction, torsion descriptions of periodic points, the holomorphic Lefschetz formula, Fourier analysis on a torus, or the Wold decomposition theorem.

## Contribution boundary

The package contribution is a self-contained synthesis with an unusually strict ledger: over the full complex elliptic moduli family and all integers \(m\ge2\), it keeps the two regular torsion channels separate from the quotient branch channel, attaches the three exact multipliers, verifies the Lefschetz identity, and places the resulting zeta and Koopman model into the repository’s Route-A contract. The finite evidence and software integrity layer are new artifacts; they are not presented as a proof or a literature-priority claim.

## Collision audit

Repository search shows earlier packages involving toral maps, finite quotients, and fixed-count zetas. C180 is separated by the conjunction of: all complex elliptic moduli, the quotient by \(\pm1\), a parity-dependent branch overlap, a three-multiplier census, a holomorphic Lefschetz identity, and an even-Fourier Wold theorem. A package containing only \(m^{2n}+1\) and its rational zeta would be too close to prior finite-count work and is explicitly not the claimed unit.
