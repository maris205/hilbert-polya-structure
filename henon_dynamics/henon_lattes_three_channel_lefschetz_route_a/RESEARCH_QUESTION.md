# Research question

Let \(E_\tau=\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+\tau\mathbb Z)\), let \(\pi:E_\tau\to E_\tau/\{\pm1\}\cong\mathbb P^1\), and let \(f_{m,\tau}\) be defined by \(f_{m,\tau}\pi=\pi[m]\) for an integer \(m\ge2\).

Can one give, uniformly for every \(\tau,m,n\), a complete fixed-point classification that retains multiplier data at the quotient branch locus, prove the resulting holomorphic Lefschetz identity, recover exact primitive-orbit and Artin--Mazur data, and determine the natural Haar Koopman operator strongly enough to make an honest Route-A decision?

The required answer is global in \(\tau\) and \(m\), not a split sequence of one-parameter examples. Finite torsion and Fourier ledgers are regression checks only.

The success criterion is a single theorem with:

- the exact \(+\), \(-\), and branch channels;
- their exact multipliers and parity-dependent overlap;
- a Lefschetz sum equal to one;
- exact-period and zeta formulas;
- an explicit Wold decomposition and determinant boundary;
- the strict Route-A tuple and forbidden-claim firewall.
