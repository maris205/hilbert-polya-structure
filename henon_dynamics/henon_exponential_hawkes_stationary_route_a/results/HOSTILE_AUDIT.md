# Hostile audit

Twenty-seven semantic mutations are applied after recomputing the internal
payload hash, followed by one stale-hash control.  Targets include:

- candidate/source/evaluator/epoch/scope locks;
- predictable pre-jump intensity;
- Fourier normalization and the primary DOI;
- Route-A tuple, verdict, and Route-B flag;
- affine and Laplace equations;
- intensity covariance, counting covariance, Dirac mass, and spectrum;
- a stationary moment and a window coefficient;
- rooted-tree cluster data and boundary classification.

All 28 mutations are rejected.  This proves sensitivity of the deterministic
certificate to the declared cells; it does not prove that mutation testing is
complete or that the source theorem is novel in the literature.
