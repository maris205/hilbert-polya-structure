# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — proof core

The initial manuscript freezes predictable pre-jump intensity, proves the
joint affine transform, constructs the subcritical stationary law, derives its
Laplace ODE, and closes every stationary moment.  It does not yet claim a
complete second-order point-process atlas.

## Round 1 — substantive covariance revision

The first revision adds three separate named objects: intensity covariance,
complete counting covariance with its Dirac atom, and the Bartlett spectrum
under the no-`1/(2*pi)` convention.  It derives finite-window count variance
and the long-window spectral slope.  This is a new theorem layer, not a prose
or formatting edit.

## Round 2 — genealogy, faces, and claim audit

The second revision adds the Poisson Galton--Watson construction and Borel
law, the `a=0`, `nu=0`, critical, and supercritical faces, exact executable
counts, nearest-owner collision controls, and the strict Route-A boundary.
The wording was tightened from an unrestricted nonexistence claim to the
proved finite-intensity stationary obstruction.  The final paper makes no
literature-priority, target arithmetic, or Hilbert--Pólya claim.
