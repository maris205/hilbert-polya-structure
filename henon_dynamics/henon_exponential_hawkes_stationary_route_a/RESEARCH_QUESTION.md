# Research question

For the univariate exponential Hawkes process with baseline `nu>=0`, jump
height `a>=0`, decay `b>0`, and predictable pre-jump intensity, can one close
in a single all-parameter theorem:

1. the joint affine transform of event count and intensity;
2. the exact subcritical stationary law and every intensity moment;
3. intensity covariance, complete counting covariance, Bartlett spectrum, and
   finite-window count variance without conflating their conventions;
4. the Poisson-cluster total-progeny law and the Poisson, empty, critical, and
   supercritical boundary faces;
5. the strict Route-A obstruction?

The answer proved here is yes.  `NEW` means that C1--C264 contains no package
owning this frozen theorem; it is not a literature-priority claim.  Hawkes's
1971 primary paper supplies the source definition and spectral convention,
not a novelty assertion for the formulas.
