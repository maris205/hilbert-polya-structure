# Scope

## In scope

- Rectangular flat torus `R^2/(Lx Z ⊕ Ly Z)`, `Lx,Ly>0`.
- Twisted classical symplectic form and `H_cl=|p|^2/2` for `B≠0`.
- Positive-energy classical shells and the zero-energy boundary.
- Degree-`N` Hermitian line bundles with curvature `-iB dx∧dy`, where `N=BA/(2π)∈Z\{0}`.
- The positive Bochner Hamiltonian `H_B=(Π_x^2+Π_y^2)/2` with arbitrary flat holonomies.
- Exact signed finite magnetic translations for fixed positively oriented division vectors, heat trace, spectral zeta, zeta determinant, and propagator revivals.

## Out of scope

- Interacting or many-body quantum Hall systems.
- Boundaries, disorder, electric potentials, or curved metrics.
- A target dynamical zeta, Euler product, target functional equation, target divisor, target zeros, or Hilbert–Pólya claim.
- Route B.

Scope literal: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
