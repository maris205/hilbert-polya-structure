# Limitations

- The torus is flat and rectangular and the field is constant. The proof does not cover variable curvature, disorder, boundaries, or electric potentials.
- The one-particle theorem is not a many-body quantum Hall result.
- Continuous clean orbit families are not isolated periodic orbits, so they do not meet the strong orbit side of Route A.
- First-Chern integrality is a topological quantization condition, not arithmetic provenance and not rational-prime input; consequently A0 fails rather than receiving partial credit.
- The Hurwitz spectral zeta is not claimed to be a dynamical zeta, Fredholm determinant, Euler product, or target function.
- Nonintegral flux has a valid classical twisted flow but no global line-bundle quantization with the stipulated curvature.
- `B=0` is a distinct flat-flow/flat-Laplacian face and is not covered by the Landau ladder theorem. Its classical closure condition is lattice-normalized, not a raw Euclidean-slope test.
- Literature sources establish standard ingredients; this package claims no global novelty. Its owner is only the displayed joint theorem and audited closure.
