# Paper plan

## Round 0 — global classical geometry

Derive the lifted solution, prove the common least period, compute every fixed set and clean tangent space, identify the orbit quotient, then prove the sharp flux-integrality theorem constructively.

## Round 1 — exact quantum mechanics

Freeze the physical Bochner normalization, prove the ladder spectrum and `|N|` multiplicity for every holonomy, and construct the signed finite magnetic-translation clock–shift representation on every level using one fixed positively oriented pair of division vectors.

## Round 2 — spectral closure and stop rule

Sum the heat trace and Hurwitz zeta, evaluate the zeta determinant, distinguish the least scalar and identity revival times, close every boundary face with the exact lattice-normalized zero-field criterion, and apply the strict Route-A evaluator without invoking Route B.

Each round is a standalone compiled paper with a distinct theorem payload.
