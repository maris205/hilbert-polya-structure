# References

1. M. H. Al-Hashimi and U.-J. Wiese, “Discrete Accidental Symmetry for a Particle in a Constant Magnetic Field on a Torus,” *Annals of Physics* 324 (2009), 343–360. DOI: `10.1016/j.aop.2008.07.006`; arXiv: `0807.0630`.
2. E. Onofri, “Landau levels on a torus,” *International Journal of Theoretical Physics* 40 (2001), 537–549. DOI: `10.1023/A:1004115827959`; arXiv: `quant-ph/0007055`.
3. I. Burban and S. Klevtsov, “Algebraic Geometry of the Multilayer Model of the Fractional Quantum Hall Effect on a Torus,” *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 406 (2025), article 97. DOI: `10.1007/s00220-025-05267-9`.

The first two sources support the torus Landau problem, flux quantization, finite degeneracy, holonomies, and magnetic translations. The third provides modern line-bundle and finite-Heisenberg context. It is not used to replace the physical Bochner operator by a differently shifted holomorphic number operator. The heat/zeta/determinant and two-time revival consequences are derived transparently from the frozen spectrum in this package.
