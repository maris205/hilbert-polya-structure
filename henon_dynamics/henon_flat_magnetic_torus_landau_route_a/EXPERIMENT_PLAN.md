# Exact-computation plan

The theorem is analytic. Computation has six adversarially useful roles:

1. enumerate 256 rational lifted cyclotron cases through all four quarter turns and verify energy, center, period, and full return;
2. enumerate 128 signed flux cases and all 16,512 level labels through level 128;
3. enumerate all 4,160 signed basis cells of clock–shift representations of orders 1 through 64 for both flux orientations;
4. certify 1,024 exact geometric heat-series cells and 64 determinant controls;
5. check 129 spectral phases at both revival times and seven boundary cases, with exact axial, stationary, closed nonaxial, and dense nonaxial zero-field faces;
6. run an importing-independent checker, SymPy identities, isolated byte replay, repaired-hash mutations, smoke tests, manifest closure, and two fresh builds of each PDF round.

Acceptance requires exact equality, stable hashes, rejection of all mutations, embedded Latin and CJK fonts, no settled LaTeX warning, byte-identical fresh wrapper builds with strictly increasing page counts, and a closed 38-payload ledger.
