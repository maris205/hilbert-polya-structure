# Hostile audit

The mutation suite rewrites canonical section and payload hashes after semantic attacks. Mutations cover identity fields, source and evaluator pins, all physical conventions, each theorem-contract clause, grid counts, collision owners, sources, every forbidden-claim flag, Route-A status, both signs of the finite-translation commutator, the stationary/axial/nonaxial zero-field boundary, representative rows from every exact ledger, stale hashes, duplicate/nonfinite JSON, and strict-YAML attacks.

Success means every repaired-hash corruption is rejected by independently reconstructed mathematics, not merely by checksum mismatch. The release gate additionally attacks optimized Python, undeclared sidecars, stale template tokens, TeX warnings, missing fonts, and nondeterministic PDFs.
