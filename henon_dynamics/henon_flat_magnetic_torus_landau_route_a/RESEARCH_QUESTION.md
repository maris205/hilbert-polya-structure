# Research question

For a charged particle on a compact flat two-torus with uniform magnetic curvature, which classical and quantum statements can be closed exactly in a single convention, and do those statements satisfy the strict Route-A gates?

The intended theorem-scale answer must simultaneously settle:

1. the global positive-energy classical return and clean fixed-set geometry;
2. necessity and sufficiency of flux integrality for a global quantum line bundle;
3. the full physical Bochner spectrum and multiplicity for every nonzero integral flux and flat holonomy;
4. the exact signed finite magnetic-translation representation for one fixed positively oriented pair of division vectors;
5. heat, spectral-zeta, zeta-determinant, scalar-revival, and identity-revival formulas;
6. the zero-energy, sign, unit-flux, nonintegral-flux, and zero-field boundaries, including axial and stationary zero-field motion.

The question explicitly excludes any attempt to identify this source system with target arithmetic data or target zeros.
