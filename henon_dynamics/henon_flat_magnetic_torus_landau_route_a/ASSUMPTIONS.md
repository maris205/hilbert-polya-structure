# Assumptions and conventions

1. `Lx,Ly>0`, `A=LxLy`, and the main theorem assumes `B≠0`.
2. Units are `ℏ=q=m=1`.
3. The twisted form is `dx∧dp_x+dy∧dp_y+B dx∧dy`; with `J(u,v)=(-v,u)`, Hamilton's equations are `qdot=p`, `pdot=BJp`.
4. Quantum curvature is `F_∇=-iB dx∧dy`; kinetic momenta obey `[Π_x,Π_y]=iB`.
5. The Hamiltonian is the physical positive Bochner operator `H_B=(Π_x^2+Π_y^2)/2=(1/2)∇*∇`. An unshifted Dolbeault number operator is not silently substituted.
6. For magnetic translations, the ordered division vectors are always the positively oriented pair `(Lx/M,0),(0,Ly/M)`. Thus their commutator retains the flux sign: `UV=ζ_M^{sgn N}VU`.
7. The spectral determinant is `exp[-ζ'_H(0)]` over the full positive spectrum.
8. “Least period” concerns a phase-space trajectory, “least scalar revival” allows one common phase, and “least identity revival” requires that common phase to be `+1`.
9. Finite exact ledgers are regression receipts; they are not proofs by sampling.
