# Narrative report

The decisive classical observation is global rather than perturbative: the cyclotron center `c=q+Jp/B` is conserved on the universal cover. Momentum rotates rigidly, and position is reconstructed from the same rotation. On a positive-energy shell, a phase-space return therefore occurs exactly when the momentum rotation is the identity. The entire shell returns at once, and the derivative is the identity; this is a maximally clean family, not an isolated orbit list.

Compact quantization introduces one genuinely discrete datum. Integrating curvature shows that `BA/(2π)` must be the first Chern number. At those and only those fluxes, the physical Bochner Hamiltonian exists globally. Ladder algebra fixes the equally spaced levels, while genus-one Riemann–Roch fixes the finite multiplicity. For the fixed positively oriented pair of division vectors, the lifted translations retain orientation through `UV=ζ_M^{sgn N}VU`; this signed finite Heisenberg representation explains the multiplicity without erasing negative flux.

The exact spectrum then closes three additional questions without asymptotics: a geometric heat trace, a scaled Hurwitz spectral zeta, and a zeta determinant `2^{|N|/2}`. Adjacent phase ratios separate two times often conflated in informal accounts: one classical period gives the scalar `-I`, and two give the identity.

This is a substantial source theorem but a strict Route-A rejection. Flux integrality is topology rather than arithmetic provenance, so A0 fails; clean families are not isolated arithmetic orbits, and a Hurwitz spectral zeta is not a target dynamical determinant. The result stays valuable precisely because the positive mathematics and the negative Route-A boundary are both explicit.
