\section{The normalized logarithmic root}\label{sec:normalized}

On $|z|<1$, all local sector determinants are nonzero.  There is therefore a
unique analytic logarithm $\Logzero N_p$ with value zero at the origin.  Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Gp}
 G_p(z)=\exp\!\left(d_p^{-1}\Logzero N_p(z)\right),
 \qquad G_p(0)=1.
\end{equation}
Its logarithmic moments are
\[
 \log G_p(z)=-\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{c_{p,n}}n z^n,
 \qquad c_{p,n}=C_{p,n}/d_p.
\]
In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:c1}
 c_{p,1}=-\frac6{d_p}=-\frac{12}{p-1}.
\end{equation}

The smooth-leading cubic Artin--Schreier estimate inherited from C43 gives,
for every embedding of the paired moment,
\[
 |B_{p,n}^{\sigma}|\le4\cdot4^n.
\]
Averaging over all $d_p$ embeddings yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bound}
 |c_{p,n}|\le4\cdot4^n.
\end{equation}

Equations \eqref{eq:c1} and \eqref{eq:bound} have different analytic roles.
The exceptional first moment has an extra factor $p^{-1}$; the uniform bound
starts contributing only at $n=2$.  Their intersection is the half-plane in
\cref{thm:main}.  The detailed normal-convergence proof is in
\cref{sec:convergence}.

\begin{remark}
No parameter is adjusted to place the boundary at $1/2$.  The exponent is
forced by the exact trace-field degree and the fact that the first nontrivial
uncontrolled repetition is $n=2$.
\end{remark}
