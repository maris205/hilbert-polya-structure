# HCS-C45 narrative report

C44 appeared to close the arithmetic globalization route by proving unbounded
trace fields.  Galois descent instead revealed a sharper dichotomy.

The ordinary norm is rational but pays for rationality with virtual rank
\(2(p-1)\), so it cannot define a bounded-rank local system.  Dividing its
canonical logarithm by the field degree is more effective: the first moment
shrinks from \(-6\) to \(-12/(p-1)\), while all higher moments retain a
uniform Deligne bound.  This advances the global Euler germ from
\(\Re s>1\) to \(\Re s>1/2\).

The gain is real but incomplete.  The normalized root may have fractional
local divisor multiplicities.  Moreover, under the canonical clock those
divisors lie on \(\Re s=0\); the newly reached Riemann critical abscissa is an
abscissa of normal convergence, not yet a local divisor boundary or
continuation theorem.  The next paper resolves the normalized local root
exactly at \(p=7\): its perfect-power and branch orders decide
whether an ordinary determinant promotion survives before any
normalized-trace operator category is proposed.
