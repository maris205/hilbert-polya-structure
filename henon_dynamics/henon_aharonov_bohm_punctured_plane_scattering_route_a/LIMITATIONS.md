# Limitations and stopped claims

The singular Friedrichs extension is only one extension. Alternative point interactions can change the spectrum and scattering, and are not covered. The source Hamiltonian has continuous spectrum rather than a discrete target-zero sequence. Classical formula novelty is not asserted.

The cross section is an ordinary density only away from the forward ray. The delta distribution is retained in the scattering operator and never squared. A finite angular cutoff cannot define the ordinary infinite scattering determinant: two equally exhaustive windows disagree. Relative and renormalized determinant constructions are neither disproved nor constructed here.

The heat kernel converges locally, but the positive global trace is infinite. The high-precision heat checks have no interval enclosure and are therefore numerical regression. The tail bound is analytic and explicitly assumes the fundamental flux cell, or a general-flux cutoff beyond the absolute flux.

The physical time-reversal result is for integer gauge times conjugation at fixed spatial point. Reflection-conjugation exists for every flux and is not mislabeled as the position-preserving operation. No prime mechanism or isolated periodic-orbit ledger exists in the frozen subtype. Further within-owner phase tables would not clear this Route-A obstruction.
