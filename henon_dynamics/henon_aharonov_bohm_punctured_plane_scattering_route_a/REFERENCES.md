# References and ownership

1. Y. Aharonov and D. Bohm, “Significance of Electromagnetic Potentials in the Quantum Theory,” Physical Review 115 (1959), 485–491. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.115.485). Owns the physical AB effect; cited as classical lineage, not as a source for newly claimed package discoveries.
2. K. Pankrashkin and S. Richard, “Spectral and scattering theory for the Aharonov–Bohm operators,” arXiv:0911.4715 (2009). [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.4715), [HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/0911.4715v2). Lemma 7 establishes existence and completeness; the original AB channel calculation fixes its scattering phases. Their potential convention yields order |m+alpha| and is translated by reflection to ours.
3. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, [§10.17](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.17) and [§10.22](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.22). Named Bessel asymptotics, Hankel transform/inversion, and [Weber exponential integral 10.22.67](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.22.E67). These are explicit analytic inputs.
