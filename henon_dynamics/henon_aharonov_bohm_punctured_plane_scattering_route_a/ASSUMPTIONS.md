# Assumptions

The Hilbert space is plane-area L2, represented with radial measure r dr and angular measure dtheta. Hbar and twice the particle mass are one. The operator is the Friedrichs closure of the positive magnetic kinetic-energy form on smooth compact support away from the origin. Flux is real; the principal numerical and scattering cell is the half-open interval from zero to one.

Angular functions are exp(i m theta)/sqrt(2 pi), m integer. The scattering angle is outgoing minus incoming direction; the square root of i is exp(i pi/4). The reference operator is the ordinary free plane Laplacian. The amplitude is interpreted distributionally before restricting away from forward angle.

Physical time reversal is restricted to a single-valued integer gauge factor times position-space conjugation, fixing spatial points and reversing mechanical momentum. A reflection-conjugation operation is evaluated separately. Other singular extensions, spin couplings, uniform fields, confinement, finite-radius solenoids, and renormalized determinants are excluded.
