  polynomial automorphisms of C² are polynomially conjugate*, Bulletin
  of the London Mathematical Society 56 (2024), 3745–3751;
  DOI 10.1112/blms.13164. The record gives issue 12 and first publication
  on 26 September 2024; the bibliography does not need either extra field.
- The final example of §2.2 and its full-centralizer explanation were read
  in the research stage and again during drafting.

  The manuscript explicitly credits it. The analogous all-place failure
  for two independently chosen maps is identified as a consequence of
  that mechanism together with Wang, not as a theorem quoted from the
  source or a novel general conjugacy mechanism.
- No use of the paper's analytic rigidity proof is needed for our theorem.
