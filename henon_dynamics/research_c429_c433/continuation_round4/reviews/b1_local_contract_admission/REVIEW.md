### Object, family, domain, and native clock

For every odd prime \(p\), fix

\[
k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p,\qquad R=k[[s]],\qquad K=k((s)),\qquad v(s)=1,
\qquad P_s(z)=(1+s)z+z^2.
\]

All fields are embedded in one fixed separable closure of this \(K\).
For every integer \(e\geq1\), set

\[
Q_e(z)=\frac{P_s^{\circ p^e}(z)-z}
                 {P_s^{\circ p^{e-1}}(z)-z}.
\]

Let \(M_e\in R[z]\) be the unique monic degree-\(p^e\) Hensel factor of
\(Q_e\) reducing to \(z^{p^e}\), with complementary factor a unit at zero
after reduction. Its root set \(S_e\) is the canonical small ordinary
period-\(p^e\) cycle in the positive-valuation disk. It is not an arbitrarily
chosen numerical branch or the full global dynatomic curve.

The clock is **one application of \(P_s\)**. Neither Frobenius nor a
relabeled \(p\)-step map replaces that generator. The arithmetic observable
is the absolute-Galois action on \(S_e\), and then its degree-\(p\) character
with this fixed native orientation. No determinant or Euler-factor
convention is asserted or required for this local contract.

### Complete intended conclusion

For all odd \(p\) and all \(e\geq1\), \(M_e\) is irreducible over the
specified \(K\). For any root \(\alpha_e\), the field
\(L_e=K(\alpha_e)\) is its splitting field and is totally ramified cyclic
of degree \(p^e\). The designated generator is

\[
\sigma_e(\alpha_e)=P_s(\alpha_e).
\]

For \(e\geq2\), the first degree-\(p\) characters agree with that of level
two as oriented characters of \(G_K\). Equivalently, with

\[
w_e=\frac{\alpha_e^{p^e-1}}{M'_e(\alpha_e)},\qquad
y_e=-\sum_{i=0}^{p^e-1}(i\bmod p)\sigma_e^i(w_e),\qquad
a_e=y_e^p-y_e,

the normalization is \(\sigma_e y_e-y_e=+1\), and

\[
[a_e]=[a_2]\text{ in }K/\wp(K),\qquad
F_e=F_2,\qquad L_1\cap L_e=K\quad(e\geq2).
\]

Here \(F_e\) is the unique degree-\(p\) subfield of \(L_e\), and
\(\wp(b)=b^p-b\). Embedded-field equality and the oriented AS-class
equality are part of the conclusion; equality of raw Laurent-series
resolvents is not.
