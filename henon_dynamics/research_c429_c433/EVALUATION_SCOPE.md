## C433 / FCP10: ordinary multiplicative finite detection

For every prime p, k=algebraic closure of F_p, every f in k[x]
of degree d>=2 with f'=0, and every g=A/B with nonzero coprime
A,B in k[x], put m=max(deg A,deg B). The one-step skew map
(x,y) -> (f(x),g(x)y) is defined on

cycles admit all required iterates. No global automorphism is claimed.

Ordinary CP says all but finitely many primitive affine cycles avoid
the support and have product of g equal to one, counting each distinct
point once, including characteristic-divisible periods. Set
b=ceil(m/(d-1))+1, D=2b^2, N=6b^2+1,
F_n=f^n-x, H_n=product_{i=0}^{n-1} A(f^i(x)) minus
product_{i=0}^{n-1} B(f^i(x)), and S_*=product_{r=1}^D F_r.

Coefficients, nonmonic maps, p=2, m=0 and support multiplicities
are unrestricted within that definition. Test degrees can reach d^N.

The period bound D applies to each cycle visible in some cleared
H_n, not to the total number of exceptional points. A cycle meeting

pointwise F_n'(x)H_n(x), where e_n is the return-root multiplicity.
Here F_n'=-1 makes all return roots simple, so no ordinary visible
