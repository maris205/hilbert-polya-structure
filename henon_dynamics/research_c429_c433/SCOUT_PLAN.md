## Exchange and selection

Each mathematical lane first reads its closest local proof/source records, then
performs targeted primary-source searches. Before a substantial new proof or
program, it states its exact claim and success/failure boundary in its lane.
Its report must separate the frozen original question, any proved auxiliary
statement and the remaining gap. No all-input result comes from a finite table.

An actionable intermediate result is sent to the coordinator immediately with:
source locator; assumptions; exact mathematical output; potential consumer;
missing compatibility condition; and status (proved/conjectural/refuted).
The coordinator forwards it to named consumers. A receiver must establish the
compatibility condition, not merely cite the originator's conclusion.

At the first checkpoint, integrate results and assign independent proof/source
checks to the strongest surviving contracts. Concentrate subsequent effort on
those with a specific remaining lemma; do not widen every lane indefinitely.
Only five independent, complete, reviewed questions may become C429–C433.
If fewer qualify, state the deficit. No C434 without further authorization.
