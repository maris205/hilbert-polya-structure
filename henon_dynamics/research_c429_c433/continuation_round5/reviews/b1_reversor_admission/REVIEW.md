## 2. Unchanged complete question and candidate

For every number field $K$, every finite word
$$
F=H_r\circ\cdots\circ H_1,\quad r\ge1,\qquad
H_i(x,y)=(y,f_i(y)-a_ix),
$$
with $a_i\in K^\times$ and $f_i\in K[Y]$ of degree at least two,
does polynomial reversibility over **every** completion $K_v$ imply
polynomial reversibility over $K$?
$$
\left[\forall v\in\Omega_K\ \exists R_v\in
\operatorname{Aut}_{K_v}(\mathbb A^2),\quad
R_vFR_v^{-1}=F^{-1}\right]
\Longrightarrow
\left[\exists R\in\operatorname{Aut}_K(\mathbb A^2),\quad
RFR^{-1}=F^{-1}\right].
$$
All finite, real and complex places are included. The automorphisms and
their inverses are polynomial. No degree/order bound, affine restriction,
involution restriction, common local formula, extension of the original
fields, or replacement by an iterate is allowed. The native clock is one
application of the full $F$. The observable is existence of a reversor;
the ordinary Jacobian's necessary sign condition is not a sufficiency
criterion or a target-arithmetic invariant.

The one selected candidate is
$$
K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt7),\qquad
F=H_{4,7}\circ H_{1/16,15}\circ H_{16,15}\circ H_{1/4,7},
\qquad H_{c,d}(x,y)=(y,cy^d-x).
$$
Its proposed local reversors are
$$
R_t(x,y)=(t^{-1}y,tx),\qquad t^8=16.
$$
Success requires these identities at all original completions and the
exclusion of **every** $K$-polynomial reversor. Failure of this affine
ansatz globally is not enough. A single verified counterexample answers
the universal question; a classification of all words is not an additional
completion requirement.

The scout's uniform $F_b$ power criterion is part of the proposed same
mechanism, not an extra paper or a substitute for the original question.
The rejected local-periodic-existence formulation and C2's unrelated
period spectrum are outside this audit.

## 4. Strongest classical-corollary argument and its exact limit

Cantat–Dujardin's final §2.2 example uses
$f=(y,x+y^{m+1})$, $g=(y,x+Dy^{m+1})$, scalar conjugacy with
$\alpha^m=1/D$, and the full cyclic-by-finite centralizer to exclude a
conjugacy over the original field. **Inference from those source inputs:**
taking $m=8$, $D=16$ and the classical Wang field gives an all-place
conjugacy counterexample immediately; a global conjugator would again
force an eighth root in $K$. Thus “everywhere local field-of-conjugacy
failure for Hénon maps” is already a short consequence of checked prior
work. It is not, by itself, the candidate's residual advance.

For the proposed candidate, the strongest merger argument would be:

1. Choose an aperiodic palindromic degree word so its monic realization
   has the swap reversor, while cyclic shifts cannot yield a shorter
   commuting component.
2. Apply the constructive old normal-form mechanism to reduce the whole
   centralizer to diagonal scalars and native powers; the displayed
   monomial degrees then impose the finite root-of-unity group.
3. Conjugate by an alternating diagonal scale. The resulting reversor
   coset has a power equation over $K$, of the same root-torsor descent
   form as the prior conjugacy example.
4. Insert Wang's already everywhere-soluble/non-global power class.

If the author proof consists exactly of these specializations with no
further structural obstruction, that is the strongest reason to retain

reversibility/classification line. An explicit degree pattern or a

independent paper merely because the final equation is newly displayed.

The limit of that argument must not be concealed. An arbitrary pair
$f,g$ in the earlier example does not satisfy $g=f^{-1}$, and a
