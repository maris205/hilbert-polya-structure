## Exact comparison target

The statement audited is Theorem (T1)–(T3) in the assigned [A3 proof package](../a3_eventual_quotient_tower/PROOF_PACKAGE.md). The coordinator supplied the identifier SHA256 b20fc9dbfe32ca492a373bc86ced69738518f7cabd295f6903c3bd52d5b77c68; no new hash execution was performed here.

For every odd $p$, with $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, $K_0=k((s))$, and $P=(1+s)z+z^2$, the canonical small cycle $\Pi_e$ gives the native character

$$
\rho_e:G_{K_0}\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/p^e\mathbb Z,\qquad
g\alpha=P^{\circ\rho_e(g)}\alpha.
$$

The new interface is a continuous surjection $\chi_\infty:G_{K_0}\to\mathbb Z_p$ satisfying

$$
\forall j\ge1\;\exists E_j\ge j\;\forall e\ge E_j:
\quad \rho_e\bmod p^j=\chi_\infty\bmod p^j
\quad\text{as characters on the same }G_{K_0}.
$$

The degree-$p^j$ subfield of every sufficiently late $L_e=K_0(\Pi_e)$ is therefore the same actual subfield $K_j$ of the chosen separable closure. The $K_j$ are nested. The native convention is one application of $P=+1$, not a freely chosen unit multiple. The claim does not assert that the full $L_e$ are nested, that $E_j=j$, or a formula for the thresholds.

R4 all-anchor contact and compact-odometer inputs are accepted dependencies. The coordinator reports the separate mathematical review accepted this extension without repairs. This report does not reproduce that review.

## Keating 2009: compatible towers are an antecedent

Kevin Keating, *Wintenberger's functor for abelian extensions*, *Journal de Théorie des Nombres de Bordeaux* 21(3) (2009), 665–678, Theorem 1.1. [Published source](https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.693.pdf); [accessible author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.2932).

This equivalence fixes a **finite** residue field. Objects include abelian extensions $E/F$ with varying $F$; morphisms allow embeddings and finite separable changes of base. The §3 essential-surjectivity proof was read through (3.7): compact candidate sets of finite extensions and norm-compatible uniformizers admit a compatible inverse-limit choice, producing a nested tower.

That is an important antecedent for tower construction, but it does not identify every sufficiently late member of a prescribed sequence with one finite quotient field. Nor does it attach the R5 character to the original $G_{k((s))}$ with $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. The fixed-base comparison cannot be replaced by existence of some tower. This is a statement-scope conclusion, not a denial of possible further deductions from norm-field theory.

## Recent close mechanism: Debaisieux 2026

Martin Debaisieux, *Lubin's conjecture for height-one $p$-adic dynamical systems over cyclo-tame extensions*, arXiv:2603.03873v2; inspected PDF dated 23 April 2026, with arXiv version stamp 22 April. Preprint status is retained. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.03873v2).

The main theorem assumes finite $K/\mathbb Q_p$ with $(e_K,p^2-p)=1$, a commuting noninvertible/invertible pair $(f,u)$, nonzero reduction of $f$, simple iterate roots, exactly $p$ roots of $f$, $f'(0)$ a uniformizer in $\mathbb Z_p$, and nontorsion $u'(0)\in\mathbb Z_p^\times$.

The relevant §2.1 Propositions 2.1–2.3 and proofs were read: a cell of **$f$-consistent root sequences** has a simply transitive $u^{\mathbb Z_p}$-action; comparison with Galois action produces a character. The construction initially uses $G_{L_\pi}$ and subsequently a finite extension $L/K$. This owns a related character-extraction mechanism, but does not supply the equal-characteristic, single-polynomial, eventually stable quotients of the prescribed cycles. No claim is made that the torsor-to-character step itself is new.

## What is subtracted, and what remains distinct

The classical completion theorem, extension of the Galois isometric action, compact finite-quotient arguments, the centralizer of a translation, profinite inverse limits, and finite/infinite Galois correspondence are not counted as independent novel results. The accepted R4 contact and odometer theorems are also not counted again.

The model-specific interface being compared is the **uniform transfer of every fixed native finite quotient to all sufficiently late original cycles**, over the same $K_0$, hence equality of their oriented characters and actual fixed fields. “A tower exists,” “the fields are abstractly isomorphic,” “an intersection is large,” and “a compatible subsequence can be selected” are not substitutes for that statement. This describes the assigned claim, not a new proof of it.

The result remains an extension of the same UL4 contract, not another paper or target-arithmetic advance. It gives no full Witt-vector formula, all-level ramification formula, global dynatomic classification, Euler factors, root numbers, or Hilbert–Pólya realization.
