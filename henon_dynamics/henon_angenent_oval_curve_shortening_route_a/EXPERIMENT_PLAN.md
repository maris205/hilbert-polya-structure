- Evaluate 20 exact rational values of `r=exp(t)` and 220 points on the
  upper halves of their ovals.
- Independently recompute the level equation, constant gradient norm,
  curvature/speed equality, arrival time, and arrival PDE.
- Compare direct high-precision area and length quadratures with
  `-2*pi*t` and the complete elliptic integral formula.
- Regress eight extinction scales and seven backward scales with 35
  translated tip samples.
- Run a producer-independent strict JSON/YAML checker, symbolic identities,
  two-run byte replay, repaired-hash and stale-hash attacks, optimized-Python
  fail closure, and deterministic three-round PDF builds.

Finite rows check the implementation only; the analytic proof owns all
`t<0`.
