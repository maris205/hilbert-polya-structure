provenance, Route-A status, scope flags, the implicit equation, the
foliation theorem, time, width, area, length, curvature, gradient norm,
arrival PDE, speed, extinction and Grim limits, and enumeration counts.

duplicate/anchored/aliased/non-mapping YAML, Route-B, scope and typed-epoch
attacks are also rejected.  A semantics-preserving YAML byte mutation is
