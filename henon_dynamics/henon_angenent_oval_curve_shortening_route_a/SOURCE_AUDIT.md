# Source and ownership audit

## Primary ownership

S. B. Angenent, “Shrinking Doughnuts,” in *Nonlinear Diffusion Equations and
Their Equilibrium States, 3*, Progress in Nonlinear Differential Equations
and Their Applications 7, Birkhäuser, 1992, 21–38,
DOI `10.1007/978-1-4612-0393-3_2`, records the explicit curvature-pressure
solution together with its two Grim-Reaper ends and round extinction, and
therefore retains ownership of the Angenent oval formula used here.

P. Daskalopoulos, R. Hamilton, and N. Šešum, “Classification of compact
ancient solutions to the curve shortening flow,” arXiv:`0806.1757`, retains
ownership of the compact convex ancient classification.  T. Bourni,
M. Langford, and G. Tinaglia, “Convex ancient solutions to curve shortening
flow,” arXiv:`1903.02022`, retains ownership of the modern full convex
classification and records the Cartesian equation used here.

## Package boundary

The package contributes a convention-complete proof chain and executable
closure of the explicit formula.  It uses the one-dimensional special
solution recorded in the 1992 source but does not rely on that paper's
higher-dimensional torus theorem.  This package does not claim the example, the
classification theorem, or their historical formulas as new.  It does not
cover nonconvex, immersed, noncompact, or higher-dimensional ancient
solutions.

No source supplies or licenses target prime data, Euler factors, root
numbers, automorphy, a target divisor, a target zero match, or Route B.
