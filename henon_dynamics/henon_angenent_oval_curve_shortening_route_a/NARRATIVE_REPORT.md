# Narrative report

Writing `r=e^t` exposes the decisive cancellation.  On the central
component of `F=cos(x)-r cosh(y)=0` in `|x|<pi/2`, the Hessian is
`-cos(x) I` and
`|grad F|^2=1-r^2`.  Thus the same one-line computation proves strict
convexity and that outward normal velocity is minus curvature.

The global geometry is unusually complete: horizontal and vertical spans
are elementary, area is exactly `-2*pi*t`, and length is a complete elliptic
integral.  The function `T=log(cos x)-log(cosh y)` turns the whole family
into an arrival-time foliation of the punctured strip and satisfies its
level-set PDE identically away from the extinction point.

The central-component qualifier is essential: the unrestricted periodic
zero set is the disjoint union of `2*pi`-translates of this oval.

At the two temporal ends the same formula yields different universal
models: parabolic scaling gives the unit circle at extinction, whereas
translations of the upper and lower tips give opposite Grim Reapers in the
ancient limit.  These are classical source-local structures assembled into
one auditable theorem, not a priority claim.
