# Research question

For the Angenent family

\[
\Gamma_t=\{(x,y):\cos x=e^t\cosh y,\ |x|<\pi/2\},\qquad -\infty<t<0,
\]

can one give a single convention-safe proof that it is a complete compact
ancient curve-shortening solution, close its global geometry and
arrival-time equation exactly, and derive both its round extinction limit
and its two translated Grim-Reaper limits?

The strip condition selects the central connected component.  Without it,
the periodic level set is the disjoint union of the translates
`Gamma_t+(2*pi*k,0)` and is not a single compact curve.

The question is source-local.  It asks neither for a new classification of
ancient solutions nor for an arithmetic or target spectral interpretation.
