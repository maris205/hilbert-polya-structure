# Source audit

The theorem uses standard primary sources for the finite nonperiodic Toda
lattice and Jacobi isospectral geometry.  DOI links are recorded in the
machine-readable receipt and in the manuscript bibliography.

| Key | Source-local claim | Persistent DOI |
|---|---|---|
| Flaschka1974 | Lax variables and conserved integrals | [10.1103/PhysRevB.9.1924](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.9.1924) |
| Moser1975 | finite exponential-particle inverse scattering and norming data | [10.1007/3-540-07171-7_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-07171-7_12) |
| Tomei1984 | compactification/topology of tridiagonal isospectral manifolds | [10.1215/S0012-7094-84-05144-5](https://doi.org/10.1215/S0012-7094-84-05144-5) |

These references support the classical source-local identities only.  They do
not support an arithmetic correspondence.  The receipt is generated from
rational \(a,b\) sentinels; \(q\)-gaps are reconstructed by logarithms and
\(p=-2b\), with no fitted target data.

The scripts read no prime list, zero list, Euler factor, root number,
automorphy label, target divisor, or Route-B input.  The finite characteristic
polynomial is explicitly distinguished from a dynamical zeta/Fredholm
determinant.  Positive-edge rows are regular; \(a_j=0\) and repeated-root
rows are boundary controls only.
