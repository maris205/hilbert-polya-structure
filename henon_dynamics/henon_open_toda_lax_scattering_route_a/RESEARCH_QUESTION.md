# C230 research question

Can one close, in one fixed convention, the global finite open Toda theorem
from its Hamiltonian equations through the Jacobi Lax flow, including the
simple-spectrum norming coordinates and the sorting/scattering boundary, while
keeping the singular repeated-root edge explicit?

The answer in this package is yes for the source-local dynamics.  The proof
chain establishes global existence and positivity, isospectrality and all
trace invariants, the exact two-particle sech solution, the finite-chain
sorting theorem, and the normalized exponential evolution of norming weights.
It also records why the positive open-chain isospectral leaf is a noncompact
scattering chamber rather than a compact real torus; torus language is reserved
for the phase-compactified extension.  This is a classical integrability result
only.  No arithmetic origin, primitive-orbit owner, target determinant, or
Hilbert--Polya operator is inferred.
