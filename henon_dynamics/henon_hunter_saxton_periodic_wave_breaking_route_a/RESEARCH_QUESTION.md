# Research question

For periodic `C2` initial data, can the entire maximal pre-breaking interval of the Hunter--Saxton flow be determined exactly from the initial slope, including every first-breaking label and the pointwise blow-up coefficient?

The answer proved here is yes.  With `E=int u0_x^2`, `m=min u0_x`, and `M=max u0_x`, the characteristic factor is explicit and the interval is
`(T_minus,T_plus)`, where
`T_plus=2/sqrt(E) atan(sqrt(E)/(-m))` and
`T_minus=-2/sqrt(E) atan(sqrt(E)/M)`.

The formulation is the once-integrated classical equation.  This avoids imposing the extra derivative needed to read the third-order PDE pointwise.  No claim is made after the characteristic Jacobian first vanishes.
