# Narrative report

The decisive change of variables is not spectral.  Along characteristics, the slope satisfies a Riccati equation with the conserved energy as its constant term.  Writing its denominator as `F` simultaneously linearizes the slope, the characteristic Jacobian, and the energy push-forward.  The first zero of `F` is monotone in the initial slope, so the complete future breaking set is exactly the global-minimum set; reversing time replaces the minimum by the maximum.

The nonconstant periodic condition automatically gives `min w0<0<max w0`.  Thus both classical endpoints are finite.  At every first future label, `F` has a simple time zero and `2F_t/F` has residue two, producing the universal `-2/(T_plus-t)` law independent of the shape or multiplicity of the minimum.

The asymmetric receipts are essential: a single harmonic has `max=-min` and cannot detect a swapped time direction.  The profile `cos(theta)+cos(2theta)/2` has energy `5/8`, maximum `3/2`, and minimum `-3/4`; its negative swaps these magnitudes and exposes such an error.
