# Paper plan

The manuscript is one theorem-scale paper joining two genuinely different scales.

1. Freeze the one-edge, no-self-loop attachment convention and separate `D_i(n)` from `N_k(n)`.
2. Derive conditional rising-factorial closure and the all-order gamma quotient.
3. Construct the normalized fixed-vertex martingale; prove almost-sure/all-`Lp` convergence, explicit limit moments, and Carleman determinacy.
4. Derive the lower-triangular degree-count recursion and prove the global profile in `L2`.
5. Explain the microscopic/macroscopic contrast: fixed old vertices have square-root growth while the empirical profile has cubic tail.
6. Close finite exact evidence, convention collisions, boundary cases, and the Route-A rejection.

Revision round 0 contains the frozen model and exact finite factorial theorem.  Round 1 adds both asymptotic scales and the complete analytic proofs.  Round 2 adds exact evidence, source/collision audit, and the scope firewall.
