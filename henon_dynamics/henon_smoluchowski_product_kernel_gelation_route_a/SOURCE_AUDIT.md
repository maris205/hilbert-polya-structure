in the **Quarterly Journal of Mathematics** 13 (1962), 119–128 and 193–205,
DOIs `10.1093/qmath/13.1.119` and `10.1093/qmath/13.1.193`, are primary
classical sources for the infinite coagulation system and exact-solution
analysis.  Ziff and Stell, DOI `10.1063/1.440502`, treat polymer-gelation
kinetics and postgel conventions (**Journal of Chemical Physics** 73(7),
3492–3499).  Normand and Zambotti, DOI
`10.1016/j.anihpc.2010.10.005`, **Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré C**
28(2), 189–215, address global Smoluchowski and Flory solution theory.  Norris,
DOI `10.1214/aoap/1029962598`, **Annals of Applied Probability** 9(1), 78–109,
is the explicit
uniqueness/nonuniqueness and hydrodynamic-limit boundary.

Accordingly, no priority is claimed for Cayley-tree coefficients, the gel
time, the critical exponent, or either classical postgel convention.  The
local contribution is a convention-locked, independently executable synthesis
that prevents a common branch substitution error.
