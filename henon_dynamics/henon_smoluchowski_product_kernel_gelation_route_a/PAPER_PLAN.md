# Paper plan

1. Freeze the discrete product-kernel normalization and monodisperse initial
   condition.
2. Prove the Cayley recurrence and exact coefficient law using rooted and
   unrooted tree functions.
3. Derive number, mass, second and third moments and locate the gel point.
4. Prove the critical \(k^{-5/2}\) law and the subcritical exponential tail.
5. Construct and directly verify the postgel Smoluchowski/Stockmayer branch.
6. Construct the gel-reactive Flory branch, its Lambert-W root and moments.
7. Show precisely why the two branches coincide at \(t=1\) but solve different
   loss terms afterward.
8. State uniqueness, truncation, source-priority and Route-A boundaries.
