# Research question

For monodisperse discrete coagulation with multiplicative kernel
\(K(i,j)=ij\), can one close in one theorem the exact coefficient and
generating-function law before gelation, the moment blow-up and critical tail,
and the genuinely different postgel laws obtained when the gel is excluded
from or included in the finite-cluster loss term?

The key risk is a convention error: the familiar coefficient formula
\(k^{k-2}t^{k-1}e^{-kt}/k!\) remains a valid gel-reactive Flory continuation
after \(t=1\), but it is not the postgel solution of the Smoluchowski loss
\(-k c_k M_1(t)\).  This package treats that model boundary as a theorem, not
as a footnote.

The separate Route-A question also remains negative.  Cluster size is not a
rational-prime owner, the tree generating function is not a target Fredholm
determinant, and no target analytic or unitary bridge is obtained.
