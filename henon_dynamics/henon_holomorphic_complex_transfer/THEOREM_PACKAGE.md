# Theorem package

For (F(z,w)=(w,w^2-z/4)), the fixed-point polynomial is

\[
x(5-4x)/4,
\]

and the period-two resultant is

\[
\!-x(4x-5)(4x^2+3x+3)/16.
\]

The exact weighted traces are

\[
L_1=0,qquad L_2=-1664/1725,
\]

so the formal determinant begins

\[
D(z)=1+\frac{832}{1725}z^2+O(z^3).
\]

The inverse pullback sends a coordinate polynomial of degree (1) to degrees
(2,4,8) after three iterations. Hence no finite polynomial-degree subspace
is invariant under this pullback without an additional analytic construction.

The checker and SymPy script independently verify every displayed identity.
