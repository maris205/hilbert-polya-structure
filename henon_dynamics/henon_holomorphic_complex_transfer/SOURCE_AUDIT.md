  determinant (1/4); it is not identified with the real H6 survivor.
- Period-one/two equations are solved from the map itself by resultants.
- The finite cycle ledger does not use prime or zero tables and does not read
  the C62--C103 mark arrays.

  Galerkin determinant to be a Fredholm determinant.

Novelty is `UNVERIFIED`; this is a reproducible ownership pilot, not a
literature claim.
