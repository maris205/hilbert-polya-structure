bounded-curvature paths to Dubins, the chemical model to Prigogine--Lefever,
the Hopf convention to Kuznetsov, one-dimensional HK convergence to
Bhattacharyya--Braverman--Chazelle--Nguyen, and periodic-geodesic/spectral
context to Colin de Verdiere.  Repository packaging is never used as
evidence of literature priority.  The collision map was checked against the
C1--C308 registry after drafting.

arithmetic local datum, Euler factor, bad-prime datum, root number, automorphy
object, target divisor/counting law or functional equation, target zero
match, Hilbert--Polya operator, or Route-B input is asserted.  C309's block
lift and C310's Pontryagin Hamiltonian are source-side formal hints only;
C313's `A4_NATURAL_QUANTIZATION` belongs solely to the round-sphere
Laplacian.
