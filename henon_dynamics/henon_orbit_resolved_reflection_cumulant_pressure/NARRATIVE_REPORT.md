# Narrative report

P68 constructed the first canonical Euler germ but deliberately collapsed all
weights in one period to their mean. P69 measures exactly what was erased.
For the simplest natural cross-axis potential, reflection plus odd decimation
turns the packet into a one-dimensional equality model with a two-state
transfer matrix.

The outcome is global, not perturbative. Orbit resolution changes the affine
pressure by one half log cosh(s) for every real s. This is a rigorous
cumulant obstruction and supplies exact primitive moment polynomials for the
next full Euler product.
