# Failure-mode audit

- Distance-two versus nearest-neighbor indexing is fixed by an explicit
  cyclic permutation.
- Primitive subtraction scales both period and energy; ordinary unscaled
  Möbius inversion is not used.
- The pressure theorem holds for each fixed real s; no uniform complex-s
  continuation is claimed.
- Strict Jensen separation is proved by a closed formula and finite
  primitive witnesses.
- No prime or operator semantics are inferred from a transfer matrix.
