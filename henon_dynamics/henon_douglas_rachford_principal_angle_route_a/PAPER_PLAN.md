# Paper plan

## Core claim

Every relaxed Douglas--Rachford map for two finite-dimensional real subspaces
has a complete orthogonal principal-angle block atlas.  This single atlas owns
the fixed space, all relaxation regimes, the sharp rate, shadow convergence,
endpoint periodicity, and trace/determinant formulas.

## Section logic

1. Freeze the operator, owner and clock.
2. State and prove the canonical decomposition and generic block.
3. Derive convergence, optimal relaxation and endpoint/instability boundaries.
4. Derive determinant and trace consequences.
5. Explain the independent exact certificate.
6. Apply the Route-A gates and state nonclaims.

## Evidence placement

No figure is needed: the central relationship is more precisely expressed by
one block matrix and one determinant factorization.  A compact table compares
the four relaxation regimes.  Counts and mutation outcomes appear only after
the theorem so they cannot be mistaken for its proof.

## Revision objectives

- Round 0: mathematical core and first exact certificate.
- Round 1: add the mismatch/fixed-space boundary, optimality and complete
  trace/determinant consequences.
- Round 2: strengthen source ownership, independent validation, endpoint
  periodicity and the exact Route-A firewall.
