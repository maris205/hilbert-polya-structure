# Exact validation plan

## Claim-to-test map

| Claim | Primary proof | Executable sentinel | Failure injection |
|---|---|---|---|
| principal-angle block | reflection multiplication | 28 exact blocks | change a sign/off-diagonal |
| modulus/rate | block determinant | direct Fraction determinant | corrupt determinant |
| fixed/mismatch factors | four intersection spaces | 21 composite models | alter scalar block |
| all power traces | spectral/block recurrence | direct matrix powers 0–8 | corrupt a trace |
| determinant product | block diagonalization | exact degree-eight polynomial | corrupt coefficient |
| scope and Route tuple | evaluator application | literal gates | repaired-hash scope attack |

## Independent paths

The producer evaluates the closed block theorem.  The checker instead builds
`P_U,P_V`, forms both reflections and multiplies matrices.  It then takes
direct powers and builds composite determinants.  The SymPy path separately
derives the symbolic block modulo `c^2+s^2=1` and reconstructs all evidence
rows.  No checker imports producer functions.

## Regression grid

- Four rational Pythagorean angles.
- Seven relaxation values: two unstable, identity, two interior nonclassical,
  classical `lambda=1`, and orthogonal `lambda=2`.
- Three pairs of angles in eight dimensions, each with a two-dimensional
  fixed space and a two-dimensional mismatch space.
- Powers zero through eight and the full determinant polynomial.

## Integrity gates

Canonical JSON is hashed without its own hash field.  Replay requires byte
identity.  Twelve semantic attacks repair that hash before invoking the
checker; a thirteenth attack leaves it stale.  The paper requires two actual
content revisions and two clean fixed-epoch rebuilds from `main.tex` alone.

## Stop rule

Any failure of the independent block reconstruction, exact rate, scope flags,
or manifest closure stops release.  Numeric tolerance is not an allowed repair.
