# Paper improvement log

## Round 0 — theorem skeleton

The original manuscript fixed `T_lambda`, derived the generic principal-angle
block, and separated contraction, identity and orthogonal boundaries.  Its PDF
is preserved as `paper/main_round0_original.pdf`.

## Round 1 — full dynamical closure

The first substantive revision added all four intersection subspaces, the
exact off-fixed operator-norm rate, uniqueness of the uniform optimum
`lambda=1`, the shadow limit, divergence outside `[0,2]`, and the complete
trace/determinant factorization.  This changed both theorem scope and proof,
not merely wording.  The result is `paper/main_round1.pdf`.

## Round 2 — evidence and claim-boundary closure

The second substantive revision added explicit classical ownership, the
producer/checker/SymPy distinction, regression and mutation counts, the
finite-order criterion at `lambda=2`, and a gate-by-gate Route-A/nonclaim
firewall.  It also records that rational-angle regression does not prove the
all-subspace theorem and that the orthogonal endpoint is not a Hilbert--Pólya
operator.  The result is `paper/main_round2.pdf`, byte-identical to `main.pdf`.
