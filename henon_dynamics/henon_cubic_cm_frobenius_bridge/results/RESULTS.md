# Results

Every tested inert prime has trace zero and every tested good prime satisfies
the Hasse bound.  The exact quadratic-extension enumerator reproduces the
Frobenius recurrence at `p=5,7,11,13`.  Sample traces are

```text
a_5=0, a_7=-4, a_11=0, a_13=2, a_19=8.
```

These are intrinsic local factors of a cubic-symmetry elliptic curve.  They
are not claimed to be H\'enon orbit factors or Riemann local factors.
