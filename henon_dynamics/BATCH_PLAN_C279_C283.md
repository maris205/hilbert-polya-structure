## Per-paper gates

### G0 -- frozen owner and convention

- C279 fixes the unweighted path, Euclidean vertex norm, continuous
  maximal-monotone clock, and ordinary graph total variation.
- C280 fixes a constant incompressible planar velocity gradient, an
  axisymmetric spheroid, and projective director space; prescribed-flow and
  coupled suspension models are not mixed.
- C281 fixes finite products of unit round spheres, positive diagonal product
  scales, physical unnormalized Ricci-flow time, and the declared
  constant-volume normalized clock; circles have infinite collapse clocks.
- C282 fixes positive premium speed, compound-Poisson intensity, exponential
  claim rate, strict first passage below zero, and the deficit convention.
- C283 defines the Fourier multiplier by exact conductor shell; it does not
  inherit an unspecified p-adic Laplacian normalization.

### G1 -- theorem-scale closure

- C279 may not stop at abstract well-posedness: block velocities,
  no-splitting, all simultaneous collisions, the event bound, finite
  consensus, and the ROF identity must all close.
- C280 may not stop at the classical orbit equation: the projective lift,
  signed discriminant, every dynamical chamber, true projective periods,
  strobe sets, and degeneracies must close.
- C281 may not stop at the affine scale ODE: tied first collapse, curvature,
  volume and diameter residues, Type-I pointed blowup, normalized-time
  conjugacy, positive-Einstein full collapse, finite partial collapse, and
  flat/mixed/tie boundaries must all close.
- C282 may not report only ultimate ruin probability: the joint transform,
  loading boundaries, conditional first mean, critical cusp, overshoot,
  martingale, and supremum mixture must be consistent consequences.
- C283 may not promote a formal geometric series: positivity, domains,
  multiplicities, heat/Schatten convergence, complete zeta poles,
  determinant signs, and every parameter face must be proved.

## Collision and nonclaim controls

Path TV is not generalized to arbitrary graphs and its direct 1D prior owners
are credited; the Jeffery equation is not claimed as new or coupled to
Navier--Stokes; product Ricci flow is a source-local geometric synthesis, not
a new PDE; the exponential ruin model and its workload analogy remain
source-local; and the exact prior owner of C283's positive spectral-zeta core
is credited.  The five packages explicitly screen their nearest internal and
external owners and state the surviving difference.

Workspace ownership does not assert literature priority.  Every reference
must pass existence, metadata, context, and ghost-citation checks.  No finite
grid, edge-order enumeration, parameter table, transform sample, or quotient
DFT is described as proof of an arbitrary-real, arbitrary-size,
infinite-volume, stochastic-process, or operator theorem.
