# Source audit

Primary source: Young-One Kim, Jungseob Lee and Kyewon K. Park, A zeta
function for flip systems, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 209 (2003),
289--301, DOI 10.2140/pjm.2003.209.289. Example 4.3 states the exact full
n-shift reverse formula used here. The publisher PDF is:

https://msp.org/pjm/2003/209-2/pjm-v209-n2-p06-p.pdf

Ryu's finite-order reversal work confirms the finite-index subgroup scope.
No source is credited with the P71 relative subtraction or uniqueness
theorem.
