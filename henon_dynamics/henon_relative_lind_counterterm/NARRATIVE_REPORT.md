# Narrative report

P70 solved the packet Euler boundary but left open whether its essential
exponential was simply the familiar full flip-zeta singularity. P71 answers
that comparison exactly. The odd packet accounts for only part of the source
Lind boundary. Full subgroup data adds coefficient 3/4 and a square-root
branch.

These two entries determine a unique local relative counterterm. The resulting
germ crosses the positive entropy boundary without a zero or pole, but this is
only a local analytic reconciliation. Global continuation and operator
ownership remain the next hard gates.
