# Research question

For the Dirichlet Laplacian on `(0,pi)`, how completely can the Caputo
time-fractional heat equation be solved as an infinite-dimensional operator
family, including the exact point where its memory clock ceases to behave like
a semigroup and the sharp spatial and Schatten regularity thresholds?

The theorem must cover every `0<beta<=1`, prove the spectral Mittag–Leffler
formula and inverse-stable subordination, and distinguish the `beta<1`
algebraic-memory phase from the classical heat face.  “Smoothing” must mean a
bounded map on the Dirichlet spectral Sobolev scale; an unbounded multiplier
may not be reported as a gain.

Proof status: **PROVABLE AS STATED** on `L2((0,pi))`, with `t>0` on all
regularity and operator-ideal statements.  Smoothing powers are declared on
`theta>=0`; negative powers are bounded because `A>=I` but are outside that
domain.
