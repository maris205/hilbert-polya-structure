# Paper plan

One-sentence contribution: an exact spectral solution of Dirichlet Caputo
subdiffusion yields a single sharp theorem joining subordination, failure of
the semigroup law, exactly-two-derivative smoothing, the `p=1/2` Schatten
boundary, and an operator-norm resolvent asymptotic.

Section order:

1. Freeze the interval, Dirichlet operator, Caputo clock, and spectral scale.
2. Derive the Mittag–Leffler solution and inverse-stable subordination.
3. Prove the non-semigroup boundary and sharp smoothing/Schatten theorem,
   explicitly quantifying the smoothing claim on `theta>=0` and separating
   the bounded but out-of-domain negative powers.
4. Prove the scaled long-time resolvent limit and separate `beta=1`.
5. Report the executable certificate and Route-A limitation.

Figure decision: no figure.  The decisive thresholds are exact exponent
comparisons (`2theta-2` and `2p`), so a plot would be less informative than
the formulas and boundary table.
