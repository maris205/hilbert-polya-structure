# Narrative report

The main progress is a sharp memory-versus-smoothing classification.  The
Caputo equation retains the Dirichlet sine basis, but exponential heat
multipliers are replaced by Mittag–Leffler multipliers with an `n^-2` tail.
That one change simultaneously destroys the semigroup law, limits fixed-time
smoothing to exactly two derivatives, fixes the Schatten threshold at
`p=1/2`, and replaces exponential relaxation by a scaled resolvent limit.

Inverse-stable subordination preserves the heat flow's positivity and
contraction without erasing this category difference.  The endpoint
Within the declared `theta>=0` domain, `A*S_beta(t)` is genuinely bounded and
every higher power is genuinely unbounded.  For `theta<0`, `A>=I` instead
makes `A^theta S_beta(t)` bounded; negative powers are outside the smoothing
domain.  The `beta=1` face is therefore not a routine substitution but a
singular structural boundary with all-order smoothing and exponential decay.

These statements concern a source-local memory equation.  Neither its
trace-ideal membership nor its Dirichlet resolvent is promoted to target
arithmetic or Hilbert–Pólya data.
