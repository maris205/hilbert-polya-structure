# Narrative report

The attempted direct Mahler-gap proof met a real infrastructure boundary:
the current full-horseshoe source gives hyperbolicity but no explicit global
cylinder oscillation constant for `log^+|x|`. Rather than promote finite
decimals, P65 asks which symbolic channel can separate the measures exactly.

One-sided data cannot do so at all. The first centered cross-axis comparison
does, and it creates pressure slopes `-1` and `-1/2`. This supplies an exact
calibration axis for future rigorous cylinder work and reveals the next
structural question: whether boundary pressure is invariant under symbolic
coboundaries.
