# ROUND2 clue

For invariant measures, a coboundary `u-u∘sigma` integrates to zero. The
marked boundary law is not invariant. Taking `u=chi` gives

```text
int u d eta_J = 1,
int u∘sigma d eta_J = 1/2.
```

Thus two cohomologous symbolic potentials may produce different marked-axis
packet pressures while their orbit-averaged pressures agree. This should be
proved and classified before treating marked packet pressure as canonical.
