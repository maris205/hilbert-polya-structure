# Proof package

Let `eta_J` be generated by iid fair bits `xi_k`, `k>=0`, with
`s[-k]=s[k]=xi_k`, and let `mu_B` be fair two-sided Bernoulli measure.

## One-sided no-go

For every `r`, the vector `(s[0],...,s[r])` has the same uniform law under
`eta_J` and `mu_B`. Hence every finite observable supported entirely on one
side of the marked reflection axis has equal expectations under the two
measures. The same holds on the nonpositive side.

## Minimal separator

Define `chi(s)=1{s[-1]=s[1]}`. Then

```text
int chi d eta_J = 1,       int chi d mu_B = 1/2.
```

Thus a centered radius-one, genuinely two-sided window is sufficient and,
in the preceding support sense, minimal.

For an odd full palindrome of length `n`, the axis value is exactly one. If
the center is chosen uniformly, the paired coordinates coincide
deterministically only at the reflection center; at every other center they
are independent. Therefore the full-family orbit mean is

```text
1/2 + 1/(2n).
```

Removing lower periods perturbs this by at most `tau(n)2^(-n/3)`.

## Pressure laws

With primitive degree `D_n`, define

```text
Z_axis,n(t)=D_n exp(-tn),
Z_orbit,n(t)=D_n exp(-tn c_n),
```

where `c_n` is the primitive orbit mean. Since
`n^(-1)log D_n -> (1/2)log2` and `c_n->1/2`,

```text
P_axis(t)  = (1/2)log2-t,
P_orbit(t) = (1/2)log2-t/2.
```

Combining with P64's marked and orbit Mahler averages gives

```text
P_axis(s,t)  = (1/2)log2-s*kappa_J-t,
P_orbit(s,t) = (1/2)log2-s*kappa_max-t/2.
```

Their transverse derivative gap is exactly `-1/2`, even though
`kappa_J-kappa_max` remains unproved.

This is a symbolic calibration theorem. It proves no rational-prime label,
von Mangoldt amplitude, determinant, operator, or RH statement.
