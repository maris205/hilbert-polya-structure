# Research question: HCS-C360

For the Berger family

\[
g=A(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2)+C\sigma_3^2,
\qquad A,C>0,
\]

can one classify every maximal unnormalized and volume-normalized Ricci-flow
trajectory, with exact lifespans and all geometric boundary regimes, and then
decide Route A without importing target-side information?

## Frozen answer sought

The work must derive the Ricci and sectional curvatures in one declared
Maurer--Cartan convention, reduce the PDE to a two-dimensional ODE, integrate
both anisotropy chambers, prove round Type-I extinction, identify which
solutions are ancient, close the volume-normalized flow, and record every
degenerate face.  A finite ledger may audit formulae; it cannot replace the
global argument.

The Route-A question is separate: do these intrinsic geometric data supply an
arithmetic source, a prime-power orbit clock, a determinant bridge, the target
analytic package, or a natural target-zero quantization?  The frozen evidence
must answer each checkpoint independently.

## Exclusions

No claim is made for the full three-axis left-invariant cone, surgery through
extinction, spectral convergence, arithmetic local data, Euler factors, root
numbers, automorphy, target divisors, target zeros, Hilbert--Pólya operators,
or Route B.
