# Paper improvement log: HCS-C360

## Round 0 -- geometric reduction

The original manuscript fixed the Maurer--Cartan convention, derived all
curvature entries and the reduced ODE, and proved the ratio equation and
anisotropy first integral.  This removed normalization ambiguity before any
global claim was made.

## Round 1 -- maximal-interval closure

The first revision introduced separate squashed and stretched coordinates,
integrated both elementary primitives, and distinguished ancient squashed
solutions from finite-backward stretched solutions.  It added the shared
round Type-I forward asymptotic and exact endpoint times.

## Round 2 -- normalized and adversarial closure

The final revision added volume-normalized forward completeness and
exponential round convergence, curvature-sign walls, degenerate metric faces,
finite-quotient scope, explicit evidence limitations, source lineage, and the
five-checkpoint Route-A decision.  Final cross-review made the Type-I claim
strict by recording nonzero scaled scalar and sectional-curvature limits and
added the previously implicit `r=2` horizontal Ricci wall to the evidence
atlas.  It also records that the full unequal-axis
cone and post-extinction weak flow are outside the theorem.

The three retained PDFs are substantive products of one conditional source.
The release gate checks distinctness, deterministic double builds, warning-free
settled logs, embedded/subset fonts, clean text extraction, page rasterization,
and exact payload membership.
