## One-sentence contribution

An exact chart by `atanh` and `atan` turns the positive Berger Ricci-flow cone
into a complete maximal-interval atlas and simultaneously proves universal
round Type-I extinction and normalized round convergence.

## Manuscript sequence

2. Derive curvature, ODE, ratio monotonicity, and first integral.
3. Integrate the squashed and stretched chambers and prove endpoint claims.
4. Close the normalized flow and geometric boundary walls.
5. Separate finite receipts from proof and apply Route A.
