# Source audit: HCS-C360

## Verified sources

1. Richard S. Hamilton, *Three-manifolds with positive Ricci curvature*,
   Journal of Differential Geometry 17 (1982), 255--306,
   [doi:10.4310/jdg/1214436922](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214436922).
   Role: foundational Ricci-flow source and normalization context.
2. James Isenberg and Martin Jackson, *Ricci flow of locally homogeneous
   geometries on closed manifolds*, Journal of Differential Geometry 35
   (1992), 723--741,
   [doi:10.4310/jdg/1214448265](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214448265).
   Role: homogeneous-reduction context.

The DOI records were checked before drafting.  The paper makes no priority
claim.  Every coefficient, first integral, endpoint formula, and boundary
classification used here is rederived in the package's declared convention.

## Ownership and collision boundary

The candidate registry contains no prior Berger or homogeneous SU(2) Ricci
flow owner.  Nearby surface Ricci flow, Bianchi cosmology, mean-curvature
flow, and rigid-body packages use different phase spaces and equations.

## Claim discipline

The citations support lineage, not the new package's exact finite ledger.
No source is cited as evidence for arithmetic data, an Euler product,
automorphy, a target functional equation, target zeros, or an operator
realizing them.
