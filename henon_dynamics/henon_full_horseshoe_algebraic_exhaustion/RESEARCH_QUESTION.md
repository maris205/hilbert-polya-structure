# Research question

## Primary question

Can the formal odd mixed-axis dynatomic divisor from HCS-P60 be promoted, for
every odd period, to a reduced effective divisor of actual H6 periodic
points?

Equivalently, can one exclude all three ambient failure modes left by P60
and P61:

1. nonreal complex closure roots;
2. repeated closure roots;
3. lower-period roots surviving in the primitive quotient?

## Minimal positive target

Prove that every complex fixed point of every iterate of `H6` is already one
of the real hyperbolic points supplied by a full two-shift.  This one theorem
would simultaneously settle total reality, simplicity, and primitive
effectivity for the reflection slice.

## Claim boundary

The project asks an all-period algebraic/dynamical question.  It does not ask
for a prime labeling, an arithmetic trace formula, a completed Riemann
determinant, or a Hilbert--Pólya operator.
