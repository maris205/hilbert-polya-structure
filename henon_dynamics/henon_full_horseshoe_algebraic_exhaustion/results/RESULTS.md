# Results

## Theorem-level result

For every `n>=1`, `Fix_C(H6^n)` consists of exactly `2^n` distinct real
hyperbolic points.  For every odd `n`, the mixed-axis closure is totally real
and squarefree, and its primitive quotient is a reduced exact-period divisor.

## Interpretation

HCS-P60's formal entropy `(1/2)log(2)` is now an actual all-period real-root
entropy.  HCS-P61's physical entropy `(1/2)log(phi)` remains exponentially
sparse.  No arithmetic or Hilbert--Pólya status is promoted.
