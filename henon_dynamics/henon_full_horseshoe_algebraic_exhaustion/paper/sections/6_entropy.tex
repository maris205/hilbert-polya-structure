\section{Primitive reflection entropy}\label{sec:entropy}

Taking degrees in \eqref{eq:primitive-quotient} and applying M\"obius
inversion gives an actual root count, not merely a formal divisor degree:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Dn}
D_n:=\deg\Psi_n
=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)2^{(d+1)/2}.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:entropy}
Along odd integers,
\[
D_n=2^{(n+1)/2}+O\!\left(n2^{n/6+1/2}\right),
\qquad
\lim_{\substack{n\to\infty\\ n\,\mathrm{odd}}}
\frac1n\log D_n=\frac12\log2.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The $d=n$ term in \eqref{eq:Dn} is $2^{(n+1)/2}$.  Every proper divisor of
an odd $n$ is at most $n/3$.  There are at most $n$ divisors, so the absolute
value of the remaining sum is bounded by $n2^{n/6+1/2}$.  The entropy limit
follows.
\end{proof}

The earlier small-content survivor contains a distinguished physical
reflection population with count $P_n=\Theta(\varphi^{n/2})$.  Theorem
\ref{thm:primitive-effectivity} changes the interpretation of the ratio
\begin{equation}\label{eq:density-gap}
\frac{P_n}{D_n}=\Theta\!\left((\varphi/2)^{n/2}\right).
\end{equation}
Previously the denominator was only a formal degree.  It is now the count of
a reduced all-real hyperbolic population.  The physical population is
therefore genuinely exponentially sparse inside the ambient reflection
slice.

This does not make the ambient population arithmetic.  It only replaces an
effectivity obstruction by a clean height-pressure question on a certified
sequence of totally real finite \emph{\'etale} algebras.
