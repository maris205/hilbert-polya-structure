\section{Transporting the full horseshoe}\label{sec:horseshoe}

Let $\mathcal R_a$ denote the real chain recurrent set of $H_{a,-1}$.

\begin{theorem}[Full horseshoe at $a=6$]\label{thm:full-horseshoe}
The restriction $H_{6,-1}|_{\mathcal R_6}$ is uniformly hyperbolic and
topologically conjugate to the full shift on two symbols.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At $a=10$, inequality \eqref{eq:DN-threshold} holds, so the
Devaney--Nitecki theorem identifies the real invariant set with the full
two-shift.  By \eqref{eq:arai-endpoint}, every parameter in $[6,10]$ has a
uniformly hyperbolic chain recurrent set.  Arai's $R$-stability statement
provides conjugate chain recurrent dynamics along this connected path.
Therefore the full-shift conjugacy at $10$ continues to $6$.  Uniform
hyperbolicity at $6$ is also part of Arai's theorem.
\end{proof}

Transporting by \eqref{eq:scaling} gives the same conclusion for $H_6$.
The fixed points of the $n$th iterate of the full two-shift are precisely the
$2^n$ binary words of length $n$ with a marked origin.  Hyperbolic conjugacy
therefore gives the following count without any orbit-finding tolerance.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:real-count}
For every $n\ge1$, $H_6^n$ has exactly $2^n$ distinct real fixed points, and
all of them are uniformly hyperbolic.
\end{corollary}

Every real periodic point is chain recurrent, so no real periodic point lies
outside the set covered by Theorem~\ref{thm:full-horseshoe}.  The number of
points of exact least period $n$ and the number of primitive orbits are,
respectively,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primitive-full-shift}
P_n=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)2^d,
\qquad
O_n=\frac{P_n}{n}.
\end{equation}
