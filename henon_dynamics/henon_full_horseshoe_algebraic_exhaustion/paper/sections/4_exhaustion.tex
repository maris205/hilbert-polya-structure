\section{Complex algebraic exhaustion}\label{sec:exhaustion}

We now compare the real symbolic count with the complex intersection count.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:algebraic-exhaustion}
For every $n\ge1$, the complex fixed-point scheme of $H_6^n$ is reduced and
is supported on the $2^n$ real hyperbolic points from
Corollary~\ref{cor:real-count}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The map $H_6$ is a cyclically reduced degree-two polynomial automorphism.
The $n$th iterate has dynamical degree $2^n$, and the Friedland--Milnor
fixed-point theorem gives total complex algebraic multiplicity $2^n$.

Corollary~\ref{cor:real-count} supplies $2^n$ pairwise distinct real fixed
points.  Each contributes complex local intersection multiplicity at least
one.  Their total contribution is therefore at least $2^n$, already equal
to the complete Friedland--Milnor count.  No other complex fixed point can
exist, and every listed local multiplicity must equal one.  Hyperbolicity was
proved in Corollary~\ref{cor:real-count}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The comparison is multiplicity-sensitive.  Equality of a topological entropy
with $\log2$ would not suffice.  The proof uses an exact symbolic fixed-point
count on one side and an exact complex intersection count on the other.
\end{remark}

The same argument also identifies the exact-period scheme.  M\"obius
inversion of the fixed-point counts gives \eqref{eq:primitive-full-shift};
because every fixed-point scheme is reduced, this is an actual count of
distinct exact-period points rather than a virtual degree.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:no-neutral}
No periodic point of $H_6$ has multiplier $+1$ or $-1$ on the unit circle.
In particular, every complex periodic point is a real transverse hyperbolic
point.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Every complex periodic point is real by
Theorem~\ref{thm:algebraic-exhaustion}, and every real periodic point lies in
the uniformly hyperbolic chain recurrent set.  Uniform hyperbolicity excludes
unit-modulus multipliers.
\end{proof}
