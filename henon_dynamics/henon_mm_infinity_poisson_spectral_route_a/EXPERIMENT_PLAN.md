# Experiment and validation plan — C233

- Freeze five rational rate pairs, four times, four initial populations and a
  24-cell PMF window.
- Reconstruct Poisson masses, binomial–Poisson kernels, Charlier modes and
  source trace factors independently.
- Check the generator eigen-equations symbolically and verify the coupling
  bounds against the finite PMF window.
- Replay bytes, exercise repaired-hash/stale-hash/schema mutations, compile
  three revisions twice at a fixed epoch, and inspect fonts/logs/pages.

The finite window is an oracle with an explicit tail mass; no truncation is
promoted to an all-state theorem.
