# Research question

Can an integral zero-dimensional scheme provide a genuinely arithmetic,
fully provable primitive-orbit/determinant dynamics without pretending that
its prime-indexed fibers already form a global Hilbert--Pólya mechanism?

For `x^4-x-1`, the answer is yes at the source-local level: its `S4`
splitting field supplies every possible four-point Frobenius permutation,
Chebotarev supplies the densities, and the inherited C12A finite-permutation
mechanism exactly reconstructs the full repeat ledger.  C369 owns this
quartic-specific `S4` atlas, not the universal mechanism.  The answer remains
no at the global target bridge: the prime fibers have no single autonomous
clock or determinant-class direct sum.
