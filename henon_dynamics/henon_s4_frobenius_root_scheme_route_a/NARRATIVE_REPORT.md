# Narrative report

HCS-C369 makes a larger Route-A move by changing the dynamical subtype.  An
integral root scheme has rational-prime fibers intrinsically; its Frobenius
is therefore arithmetic rather than a prime label pasted onto an unrelated
map.  The quartic `x^4-x-1` is especially clean: its sole bad prime is 283,
its Galois group is `S4`, and Chebotarev forces the complete five-class
permutation atlas.

Every good fiber is a four-point dynamical system.  Its factor degrees are
its primitive cycle lengths, so the fixed-point repeat ledger and the
finite determinant are not analogies: they are exact identities.  This
clears A0 structurally and A1 at the finite-fiber analytic level.  The
permutation action also supplies a natural unitary fiber operator.

The universal reason for the trace/determinant identity is not a C369
novelty: C12A already owns it for reduced zero-dimensional Frobenius fibers.
C369's distinct step is the complete `x^4-x-1` package---`S4` Galois
closure, all five all-good-prime classes with fixed/primitive/density data,
the prime-283 boundary, and a convention-locked executable ledger.

The same precision exposes the remaining gap.  Different primes are
different fibers, not successive times of one autonomous owner.  Without
new source structure there is no determinant-class cross-prime sum, target
Euler product, or target divisor.  Thus A2 and A3 fail, and the positive
result is reported as an arithmetic candidate rather than promoted to a
target spectral mechanism.
