# Scope

The theorem covers the integral quartic root scheme, every rational prime
away from the discriminant, every Frobenius iterate, all five `S4` cycle
types, their natural densities, the exact primitive-orbit inversion, and the
fiberwise determinant and Koopman operator.

The universal reduced-zero-dimensional Frobenius permutation and finite
zeta/determinant mechanism is inherited from C12A and is outside C369's
ownership claim.  C369's ownership is limited to the theorem package and
executable atlas for the fixed quartic `x^4-x-1`.

Finite evidence covers every prime at most 10,000 except 283 and iterates
1 through 12.  It is an exact implementation audit of the formulas.

The scope literal is `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.  No target arithmetic
local data, target Euler factor, root number, automorphy, target functional
equation, target divisor, target zero correspondence, Route-B construction,
or Hilbert--Pólya operator is in scope.
