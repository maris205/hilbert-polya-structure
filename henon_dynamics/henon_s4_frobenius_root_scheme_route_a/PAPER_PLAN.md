# Paper plan

- **Round 0, quartic arithmetic step:** define the root scheme, fix arithmetic
  versus geometric Frobenius, compute the discriminant, prove
  irreducibility and `S4`, and establish the factor-orbit dictionary.
- **Round 1, convention-locked specialization:** classify all five cycle
  types, give exact witnesses, specialize C12A's universal finite-permutation
  trace/determinant mechanism to every good fiber and every iterate, and
  display the finite evidence counts.
- **Round 2, boundary-and-route closure:** prove Chebotarev densities, isolate
  the non-étale prime 283, identify the canonical fiber Koopman family, and
  state the ownership and target-bridge firewalls.

The final paper must remain a single theorem-scale unit rather than five
fragments.
