# Limitations

- The prime label indexes separate four-point systems; it is not the time of
  one autonomous flow.
- The finite determinants are not assembled into a cross-prime Fredholm
  determinant.  No trace-class weighting is introduced.
- Chebotarev densities are asymptotic source densities.  The finite counts
  up to 10,000 are not advertised as error bounds.
- The exceptional fiber at 283 is nonreduced and is not forced into the
  good-fiber permutation atlas.
- The construction is attached to this quartic number field, not to a
  target L-function and not to Hénon chronology.
- A canonical unitary exists on each fiber, but types `3+1` and `4` are not
  self-adjoint, and no global self-adjoint owner is claimed.
