```text
5,1,2,1,2,1,1,1,2.
```

Their internal label permutation factor is

```text
5! * 2! * 2! * 2! = 960.
```

Exactly 12 of the 108 automorphisms of `Q` preserve the complete weighted
closure profile. They are generated by `r=(2,0,0,2)` of order 6 and
`s=(2,1,2,1)` of order 2, which commute. Direct enumeration of all compatible
pairs yields

```text
12 * 960 = 11520.
```

The independently generated group has the same 11,520 elements. GAP uses a
faithful representation on 54 ambient points plus 16 labels and returns
`C2 x C6 x S5 x D8`.

The action on the twenty subgroup lattice alone has image order 18 and kernel
order 6. It is a diagnostic quotient, not the lifted C75 symmetry object.
