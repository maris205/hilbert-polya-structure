# C75 research question

For the frozen C72 named coordinates in

\[
Q=\mathbb Z/9\oplus\mathbb Z/3\oplus\mathbb Z/2,
\]

what is the exact symmetry of the incidence relation between the sixteen
labels and the cyclic subgroups they generate?

The question has five exact parts:

1. Enumerate the twenty ambient subgroups and the nine cyclic-subgroup fibers
   hit by the sixteen named coordinates.
2. Define the weighted stabilizer of the fiber profile under `Aut(Q)` and
   determine its exact order and generators.
3. Lift each compatible ambient map by every label permutation inside the
   corresponding closure fibers, and determine the exact total order.
4. Independently distinguish the lifted group from the non-faithful action on
   the twenty-subgroup lattice.
5. Identify a finite-group structure candidate, while keeping it explicitly
   separate from the incidence enumeration itself.

The closure relation is

\[
 I(i,C)=1\iff C=\langle x_i\rangle.
\]

A lifted symmetry is a pair `(alpha, pi)` satisfying

\[
 \langle x_{\pi(i)}\rangle=\alpha(\langle x_i\rangle)
 \quad (1\le i\le16).
\]
