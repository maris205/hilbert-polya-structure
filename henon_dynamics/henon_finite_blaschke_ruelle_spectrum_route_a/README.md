# HCS-C380: nonlinear Blaschke orbit--spectrum closure

This package proves a complete native orbit, transfer-spectrum and determinant chain for `B_a(z)=z(z-a)/(1-az)`, `0<=a<1`. It also proves quantitative finite-product tails and separates the two parameter faces. The explicit spectral mechanism is classical and attributed, not a new discovery claim.

The final manuscript is [paper/main.pdf](paper/main.pdf); the unabridged proof is [proof/ANALYTIC_PROOF.md](proof/ANALYTIC_PROOF.md). Each of three internal drafts adds a theorem layer; they are revisions of ONE paper, not three batch papers.

Strict Route A: `(A0_FAIL,A1_WEAK,A2_FAIL,A3_FAIL,A4_FORMAL_HINT)`, overall `ROUTE_A_REJECTED`. A complete source determinant does not satisfy target A2. Scope: `NO_BAD_EULER_OR_ROOT_NUMBER`.
