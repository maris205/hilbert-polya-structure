# Assumptions and conventions

- Real parameter `0<=a<1`; the wall `a=1` is a separately cancelled rational map.
- Integer iteration is the clock. Angular derivatives, not complex derivatives at arbitrary nonfixed points, are the stability convention.
- Hardy annulus `r<|z|<1/r`, `r=(1+a)/2`; target extension parameter `t=(r+q_a(r))/2<r` means a larger annulus.
- Inverse branches are local only; the weighted sum is globally single valued.
- Fredholm convention `det(I-u P_a)`. Primitive product starts at stability exponent `j=1`.
- Quantitative logarithmic tail requires `R*a^(N+1)<1`. Quotients extend across common zeros.
- Rational fixed-point index at infinity is computed in local coordinate `1/z`.
