# Limitations

The main transfer spectrum was known before this package. The contribution here is a complete attributed specialization with explicit native orbit proof, annular certificate, quantitative tails, singular faces and independently executable audit; no priority claim or publication-readiness judgment is made.

The exact grid is finite and audits formulas only. The theorem is justified analytically. No same-clock unitary quantization, arithmetic prime carrier, target zero comparison or target Weil compression is supplied. The unit-parameter wall changes both degree and operator class.
