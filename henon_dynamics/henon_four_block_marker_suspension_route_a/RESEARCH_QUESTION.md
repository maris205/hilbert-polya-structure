# C139 research question

Can one pass beyond directed-edge clocks with the smallest explicit forward
memory refinement that separates a genuine primitive pair invisible to every
one-, two-, and three-block population, while retaining an exact all-period
determinant and stating the next collision rather than claiming injectivity?

The frozen answer is yes for the coding-relative statement.  Adding
`sqrt(5) N_0011` separates `001011` from `001101`; an eight-state de Bruijn
matrix owns the exact determinant and primitive product.  The stronger orbit
injection statement fails already at period seven because `0101111` and
`0110111` share the full five-component vector.

Route-A question boundary: this asks only for intrinsic source-side orbit and
determinant structure.  It does not ask for, or authorize, a target divisor,
global target identity, arithmetic interpretation, natural quantum lift, or
Route B.
