# C139 paper plan

## Working title

**A Minimal Four-Block Refinement of a Directed-Edge Nonlattice Suspension**

## Claims/evidence narrative

1. Freeze the three-block presentation and state the exact determinant.
2. Prove the all-period trace and primitive product before showing any finite
   enumeration.
3. Establish rational independence of the five clock components.
4. Present the equal-through-width-three period-six pair as the precise new
   progress theorem.
5. Immediately disclose the period-seven residual collision and the
   coding-relative boundary.
6. Close with reproducibility totals and the strict Route-A tuple.

## Layout target

Two to four pages, no figures, one compact witness table, equations kept
within the text width.  Round 1 will audit proof completeness and
claim/evidence alignment.  Round 2 will audit notation, source/target
separation, reproducibility, and page rendering.  No external review score or
acceptance prediction is used.
